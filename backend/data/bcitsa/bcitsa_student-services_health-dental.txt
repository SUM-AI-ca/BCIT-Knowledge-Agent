### Health & Dental Plan Overview
## Your Student Health & Dental Plan
The BCITSA Extended Health & Dental Plan offers essential protection and security, helping to mitigate the financial impact of injuries or ailments while you’re a student. Enrollment is automatic for students in programs designated as full-time by BCIT that are at least 16 weeks in length. If you meet these eligibility criteria, the Student Association (SA) Health fee will be automatically included in your tuition when you register.
Approved by a student referendum, these health and dental plans are now a mandatory part of your enrollment at BCIT, stemming from your membership in the Student Association. For specific details and information, you can visit the BCITSA Student Plan Website.
### MyStudent Plan
## Your BCITSA Health & Dental Plan
As a BCIT student, your health is a priority, and the BCITSA Health and Dental Plan is here to support you. Designed to complement your provincial health plan, our program provides robust coverage for a range of services often not included in basic plans. This includes assistance with the cost of prescription drugs, routine and restorative dental care, and a variety of other extended health services. Our goal is to provide peace of mind and financial security, helping you stay healthy while pursuing your academic goals.

## Healthy Smile Dental Hygiene Services
Get a brighter, healthier smile right here on campus! As a student covered by the BCIT Student Health & Dental Plan, you can visit our on-campus dental hygienist located conveniently in the Student Association Centre in SE2. Healthy Smile dental services make it easy to take care of your oral health. A professional cleaning can give you the confidence of a radiant smile for a job interview, presentation, or everyday life. Booking your appointment is simple. You can either book online or visit us on the second floor of the Student Association Centre (SE2).

## Mental Health & Wellness Support
MyVirtualDoctor offers students unlimited virtual healthcare, providing 24/7 access to medical professionals from anywhere in Canada. This program allows you and your dependents to address primary health needs through a smartphone, tablet, or computer. It’s a convenient and accessible way to receive medical advice and care, ensuring you have professional support no matter where you are in the country.
