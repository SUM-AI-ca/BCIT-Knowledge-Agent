### About
## Support You Can Count On!
The BCITSA is dedicated to supporting your academic and professional journey. We offer a wide range of services, including wellness initiatives, financial aid, and advocacy, to help you succeed.
We also provide dedicated career and entrepreneur services, along with a comprehensive health and dental plan, ensuring you have the resources to thrive throughout your time at BCIT and beyond.
