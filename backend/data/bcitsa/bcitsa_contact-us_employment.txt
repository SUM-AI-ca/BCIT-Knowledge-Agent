### About the Role
The Store Manager oversees overall store operations. You are responsible for hiring front-line staff, managing schedules, and resolving staffing issues to ensure adequate coverage. You ensure all staff deliver friendly, efficient service with strong product knowledge and accurate payment processing. You implement strategies, support process improvements, supervise staff, and address issues beyond the scope of associates and supervisors. You ensure all perishable and non-perishable items are properly stocked and handled according to policies. You are also responsible for cash and safe management, including resolving discrepancies, as well as overseeing ordering, receiving (Point of Sale) system updates, merchandising, cleaning, and stocking.

### Our Commitment
## Working at the BCITSA
BCITSA is committed to fostering a safe, inclusive, and equitable workplace culture that supports and promotes equity, respect, and accountability among employees and BCIT students. We strive to cultivate a community that recognizes equity and diversity as fundamental to achieving inclusive excellence in all our programs and services. The following values encompass how BCITSA upholds our vision and mission, and guide how we provide our services:
### Unique Perspectives
We recognize the talent and unique perspectives, including seen and unseen qualities, our employees bring to our culture.
### Culture
We expect our workplace culture to be one that is inclusive, respectful, fair, and safe for the diverse people within our organization to be their most authentic selves.
### Flexibility
We advocate for flexibility, adaptability, and creative solutions to best support our employees, enabling them to thrive and successfully meet their individual needs.
### Community
We actively foster a workplace where clear communication, transparency, and mutual accountability are prioritized to ensure trust and success for all team members.
