### About Our Centre
## Learn About the BCITSA Childcare Centre
The BCITSA Childcare Centre, which opened in January 2013, was built to meet the recognized need for on-campus childcare for the BCIT community. The initiative was a collaboration between the BCIT Student Association and the BCIT leadership team, supported by student fees. This was a crucial step to ensure that parents with young children could access the excellent educational opportunities at BCIT. Because of this, placement priority is given to BCIT students, faculty, and staff, but families from the greater community are also welcome as space permits. Once enrolled, a child will not be displaced by the enrollment needs of a BCIT community member.
Our Rooms
The centre has three welcoming and distinct rooms designed to support the development of different age groups:
- The Little Acorn Room caters to toddlers aged 18 to 36 months.
- The Little Duckling Room and the Ladybug Room are both for children aged 3 to 5 years.
Our Philosophy and Environment
At the BCITSA Childcare Centre, we strive to promote your child’s emotional, social, physical, and intellectual development. We believe every child has the right to learn at their own pace in a safe, secure, and encouraging environment. Our childcare offers a play-based program that incorporates fun ways for children to learn math, science, language, music, and art. The centre is located on the picturesque BCIT campus alongside Guichon Creek and features a fully fenced and landscaped play area with an outdoor sandbox, climbing wall, slide, bike track, and covered areas for rainy days.

### Monthly Fees
## BCITSA Childcare Centre Fee Structure
For the BCIT academic year of September 1, 2026 to August 31, 2027
The Governments of British Columbia and Canada are partnering with child care providers to make child care more affordable for families through the Child Care Fee Reduction Initiative (CCFRI). This initiative provides a subsidy so that children under 36 months will have the nominal fee reduced by $900 per month, and children over 36 months in our 3-5 center will have the nominal fee reduced by $545 per month. In the infrequent circumstance where the net fee after the application of the CCFRI subsidy would reduce the fee to below $200 per month, the BC government has directed Childcare providers to have a minimum fee of $200 per month as this aligns with the BC Government policy of $10 per day Childcare. For parents who need additional help covering the costs of child care, extra support is available through the Province’s Affordable Child Care Benefit. The CCFRI subsidy and the Affordable Child Care Benefit work together to lower the costs of childcare fees for families and can be “stacked” to maximize the amount of savings. For more information please go to How to Access Child Care – Province of British Columbia ( gov.bc.ca ) .
As long as the BC Government is providing a childcare fee subsidy in the form of the Child Care Fee Reduction Initiative (CCFRI), children under 36 months will have the childcare fee reduced by $900 per month and children over 36 months will have the fee reduced by $545 per month.
