## 2026 Referendum on Permanent Event Funding
BCIT’s campus should be more than a classroom; it should be a community.
### The Results are in:
We’re excited to announce that our Events Funding Referendum passed overwhelmingly. Of the 1,496 people who voted, 1,030 voted YES !
### About
You came to BCIT to build your future, but you also deserve a campus that helps you connect, unwind, and enjoy your time here.
Over the past two years, BCITSA has delivered more than 175 events on campus, including pub nights, trivia, craft markets, food vendors, petting zoos, contests, giveaways, and more. These events are designed to create the kind of campus experience you have told us you want—one that brings people together and makes BCIT feel like a community.
We want to keep building that experience, but to do so, we need a permanent source of funding.
This February, you will have the opportunity to vote for a permanent source of funding for BCITSA campus events. By voting YES, you unlock a full year of events across campuses for less than the price of one drink per semester. Most importantly, by voting YES, you are supporting a more vibrant campus community, one that encourages connection, supports student wellbeing, and makes BCIT feel like a place to belong, not just a place to study.
### Voting is Closed
### Feb 23-27, 2026
### What does a yes vote mean for you?
### Dedicated Staff
A permanent events team means more reliable planning, professional-quality events, and sustainable year-round programming .
### Club Funding
Clubs will have their event funding doubled so they can host their own events.
### Student Choice
Each year, students will have the opportunity to tell us what kind of events they want to see, ensuring events reflect diverse interests, schedules, and needs.
### Accessible Fun
Students are under real financial strain, and campus events offer free access to experiences that would normally cost money.
### Places to Connect
Events offer opportunities for students to build friendship, a sense of belonging, and have fun.
