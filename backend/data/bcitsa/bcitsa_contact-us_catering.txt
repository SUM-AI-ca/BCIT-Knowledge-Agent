### Custom Catering
## About our Service
BCIT Student Association Catering offers an outstanding culinary experience. Whether it’s a brief meeting, an intensive workshop, or a week-long conference, our catering services are ready to meet your needs.
We pride ourselves on crafting diverse and delectable menus to suit every palate and dietary preference. From energizing breakfast options to kickstart your day to mouthwatering lunches that keep you fueled and focused, our team prepares each dish with care and precision. We use fresh, locally sourced ingredients whenever possible, ensuring every meal is a delightful and sustainable experience.
## Our Commitment to You
Our commitment to quality extends beyond delicious food. Our dedicated team is passionate about providing exceptional, seamless service, ensuring your dining experience is memorable. From a quick coffee break to a grand banquet, our Catering Services team handles every detail, making your event a success.
When you choose BCITSA Catering, expect a top-notch culinary experience tailored to your needs. Our diverse menu includes vibrant fruit platters, assorted sandwiches and wraps, hearty breakfasts, fresh pastries, flavorful dips, a selection of salads and pastas, and tantalizing main dishes featuring chicken, beef, pork, or fish. Finish your meal with desserts and fresh coffee or tea. Reach out today to plan your special event with our experienced team and discover culinary excellence.

## Looking for a Space?
While our catering team is happy to support clients across the BCIT Campus and beyond, we also have rentable venues available for your next board meeting or holiday party.
For groups of fifty and fewer, we have the bright renovated BCITSA Council Chambers available, which can be set up in a variety of configurations.
Looking for a larger space? Book the Habitat Pub for your next gathering of up to 300 people. Available for rentals most weekends, the Habitat Pub offers a large patio, a central bar, and a robust audio system. This is the ideal location for holiday parties, casual wedding receptions, and more.
