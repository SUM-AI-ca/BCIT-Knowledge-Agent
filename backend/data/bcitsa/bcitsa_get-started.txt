## Online Startup Training
### Build a Strong Business Model
If you’re developing a business idea and want expert support, the BCITSA can help you transform your concept into a validated and strategic business model. Our resources and guidance will help you build a solid foundation, ensuring you’re prepared to launch and grow effectively.
These flexible digital tools will help you engage deeply with entrepreneurship at your own pace.
### About the Lean Canvas
The Lean Canvas is a widely used business-planning tool that helps entrepreneurs capture the essential elements of their business on a single page. It acts as a clear blueprint, guiding you through the key assumptions, challenges, and opportunities behind your idea. Completing a Lean Canvas will help you assess feasibility, identify what you’ll need for success, and ensure your concept is properly tested and validated.
To support you, we’ve developed an easy-to-use Lean Canvas template designed for straightforward mapping and validation of your business model. You’ll also receive access to a digital tool that brings your financial picture to life and helps you better understand the numbers behind your idea.

## Business Plan Review
### Guidance for writing a business plan
If you’re starting a business, developing a clear business model and putting your ideas into a business plan is an important step for many entrepreneurs. A strong business plan can help you:
- Clarify the direction of your business
- Set realistic goals and milestones
- Align your financial plans with your overall strategy
- Prepare for funding opportunities
This service offers step-by-step guidance, practical resources, and easy-to-use templates to help you create a business plan with confidence.
### A Self-Paced Program
This self-paced program gives you access to practical resources and guidance across the following key areas of business planning:
- Module 1: Your Business Model
- Module 2: Defining Your Business
- Module 3: Understanding Your Market
- Module 4: Selling Your Product or Service
- Module 5: Business Operations
- Module 6: Managing Your Business
- Module 7: Financial Planning
- Module 8: Bringing Your Plan Together
- Module 9: Using Your Business Plan
### Learning Objectives
Creating a professional business plan provides a clear roadmap for your venture and can strengthen your chances of securing funding.
After completing these modules, you will be able to:
- Identify the seven core sections of a business plan
- Clearly define your business using a concise concept statement
- Evaluate whether your idea has sufficient market demand
- Develop realistic financial forecasts for your business
- Create a comprehensive business plan that clearly explains all aspects of your company
- Understand how to effectively use your completed business plan
