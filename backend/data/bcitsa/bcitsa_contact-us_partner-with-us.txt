## Key Opportunities
We have a variety of opportunities available, including customized annual sponsorships, on-site vending activations, space rentals, and various advertising solutions.
Contact us to explain your business goals, and we’ll work with you to decide how we can support you while enhancing student life. The best part? All the proceeds you spend go right back to students, ensuring they have an excellent campus experience.
### Annual Sponsorship
Looking to connect with BCIT students and achieve specific goals? Work with us to develop a custom, creative solution that perfectly meets your long-term needs and objectives.
### Vending & Events
Want to visit campus, interact with students, or benefit from a popular event? Whether you’re hiring, sampling products, or sharing information, we’re here to facilitate your connection.
### Advertising
With many print and digital opportunities available, we’ll discuss what best suits your needs. We offer an annual student agenda, a bi-monthly magazine, digital screens in high-traffic locations, and much more!
### Career Services
Consider connecting with our Career Services department to learn more about custom options. Promote your business at a Career Fair, set up a booth in a targeted location, or advertise in our Annual Career Resource Guide.
