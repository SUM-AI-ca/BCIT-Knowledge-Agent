## Mentorship and Informational Interviews
### Access Expert Guidance and Industry Insight
Building a business is easier when you don’t have to do it alone. We help connect you with experienced entrepreneurs and industry professionals who can offer practical guidance, informed perspectives, and real-world insight.
Through our network, you can be matched with a mentor who provides ongoing support over the course of a few months, or connected with industry experts for one-time informational meetings focused on specific questions or challenges. Both options are designed to help you move forward with confidence—whether you’re launching a new business, refining your strategy, or scaling an existing business. These connections support you in setting clear, meaningful goals and making steady, informed progress toward achieving them.
### Program Details and Requirements
Whether you are booking a one-time chat or starting a long-term mentorship, these sessions are designed to be high-impact, conversational, and focused entirely on your growth. You don’t need to have every detail figured out before you show up; these meetings provide a supportive environment to stress-test your ideas and bridge the gap between academic theory and the realities of the local business landscape. By the end of each session, you will gain a clearer sense of direction and the confidence to take your next professional steps.
- Get a “no-filter” look at your industry and advice that goes beyond what you learn in the classroom.
- Bring your specific business hurdles or “how-to” questions and get direct feedback from someone who has been there.
- Walk away with 1–2 clear, actionable goals so you know exactly what to work on next.
- Build a professional connection that helps bridge the gap between being a student and becoming a business owner.
