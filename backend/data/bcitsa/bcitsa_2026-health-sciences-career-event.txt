## 2026 Health Sciences Career Expo
Your career in health sciences starts here. Come by to meet employers who are hiring BCIT students and grads.
### About
In collaboration with BCIT, the BCIT Student Association is connecting students with employers through a day of career-focused programming, including a career fair, workshops, a friendly CPR competition, and an interactive VR experience.
Make the most of the opportunity to meet 1-on-1 with Health Authorities, Healthcare companies, professional individuals, and guest speakers. These events are a great chance to learn about the healthcare industry, explore current job opportunities for BCIT students and grads, and gain valuable insights into the hiring process, employer needs, and various health care topics.
Parking Note: For all events on campus, paid parking is in effect. For more information on parking fees and campus maps, please visit Campus Parking.
This is a free event located inside the Health Sciences Building (NW-04), open to BCIT students, alumni, and professionals in the healthcare industry.
