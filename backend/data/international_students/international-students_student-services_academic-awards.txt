Title: Academic Awards

================================================================================

## Academic Awards
## International student award
Awarded to international students who achieve the highest GPA in the first year of their program while maintaining a 100% course load. Students will be notified of results. No application is necessary.
## BCIT scholarships
Awarded to students who achieved the highest average in the first year of their program while maintaining a 100% course load. Students will be notified of results in the fall of their second year. No application is necessary.
## Achievement awards
Awarded to students with outstanding marks in specific courses or demonstrated excellence in qualities such as leadership, service and class participation. No application is necessary.
## Graduating awards
Awarded to students who achieve the highest average in the final year of their program while maintaining a 100% course load. Students will be notified at graduation. No application is necessary.
