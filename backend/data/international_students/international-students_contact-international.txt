## International students

If you have questions, please contact us at one of the email addresses below.

Are you a current BCIT student or alumni?

- Email: isc@bcit.ca
- Visit our Virtual Drop-In Service or In-Person Drop-In Service at the Burnaby and Downtown Campuses (Check for up-to-date drop-in hours)
