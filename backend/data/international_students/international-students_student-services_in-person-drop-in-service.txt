### Tired of waiting in line?
Join the virtual queue and wait wherever you want.
To join the queue:
- Visit Comm100
If you don’t have a cell phone, you can still join the queue using the self-serve kiosk at Student Information and Enrolment Services, SW1 – First Floor.
If you are not present when your number is called, we will serve the next person in line.

### ISC Virtual Drop-In Service
Can’t visit us in-person? The ISC is pleased to offer virtual drop-in services to international students via Zoom.
### Topics that we may be able to help with
- Accessing BCIT & BCITSA services for students
- Study permits & Co-op work permits
- Academic status at BCIT (full-time versus part-time)
- Updating your status in Canada documents
- Medical insurance
- Working in Canada & post-graduation work permits
- International Letters of Verification
- Mental and physical wellbeing resources
- Adjusting to life in Canada
### Have other types of questions?
- Information for prospective international students (contact international@bcit.ca)
- Advising regarding which program or courses to enrol in (see Program Advising )
- International Credential Evaluations Services (contact ICES )
- Course registration, refund & withdrawals (contact Student Information & Enrolment Services )
- Immigration applications (contact an authorized immigration consultant or lawyer )
