## ISC Events


Pre-Arrival Prep


Workshops that are of particular interest to international students who plan to study at BCIT but have not yet arrived in Canada.


International Orientation


Fun and educational events that are part of our International Orientation programming.


Workshop Series


Explore the full list of upcoming workshops from the International Student Centre's weekly series.


Social Activities


Fun activities designed to give you an opportunity to meet other students from around the world!


IRCC-Related


Workshops to help you gain a better understanding of immigration-related topics such as study permits, co-op work permit, post-graduation work permits, etc.


Health & Well-being


Events designed to boost your well-being and to explore health-related tips and resources.


Career Focus


Workshops that are designed to help international students learn more about opportunities for working in Canada and developing career skills.


All ISC Events


View the complete list of all upcoming events run by the International Student Centre.
