## International Peer Mentoring
We are pleased to offer International Peer Mentoring to help new international students transition to life at BCIT and make important social connections. See the information below if you may be interested in participating.
On this page:
- Overview
- Schedule
- New student registration
- Volunteer as a mentor
## Overview
International Peer Mentoring is a free peer-to-peer social support program that connects new international students starting their first term at BCIT with a mentor who is a continuing student in good academic standing. By registering as a mentee, you and up to 6 other new international students will be matched with an International Peer Mentor before your program starts and through the first month of the term. Mentors will be able to share their perspective and knowledge of student life at BCIT and the Metro Vancouver area. They will also be available to answer your questions or refer you to appropriate resources. Your mentor will likely be a different nationality and in a different program of study than you. Note that this mentoring program is not academic in focus.
This program is intended to provide:
- Peer support and guidance for new students
- A valuable leadership opportunity for mentors
- Social connections and networking opportunities for all participants

### *mandatory for all participants*
The Kickoff Event brings together all program participants and attendance is required. BCIT staff who coordinate this program will introduce themselves and go over a few important points for everyone before giving each mentor and their mentees time to enjoy some fun activities and good conversation!
- When: September 4 (exact time TBD)
- Where: Burnaby campus (the exact location will be sent to students who register)
- Complimentary refreshments will be provided!
During the first weeks of the term, mentors will initiate a Cultural Exchange Activity to be done virtually as a group email thread with all of their mentees.
In this activity, we will ask each participant to share links to some online videos that showcase some aspect of your culture. This could include music, cultural traditions, unique holidays, popular travel destinations, etc. More detailed information will be provided during the Kickoff Event.
Each mentor will plan an interactive group activity to do with their mentees on campus. This will be a great opportunity to get to know the others in your group better and to have some fun together!
Each mentor will communicate details about their group activity to their mentees including the exact date, time and location.
In the lead-up to term start and throughout the first month of the term your mentor will be available if you have any questions, issues, or simply want to connect with a fellow student.
However, understand that this program is not academic in focus and mentors are not able to help with course work (visit Learning Commons for information about Peer Tutoring and academic support).
## New student registration
New international students accepted into eligible programs starting at BCIT in Fall 2026 can expect to receive an invitation email through your myBCIT inbox several weeks before term start with registration instructions.
Capacity is limited for this program and so registration is required and space is filled on a first-come-first-serve basis.
## Volunteer to be an International Peer Mentor
Continuing BCIT students in good academic standing are invited to apply to become an International Peer Mentor. It is a unique opportunity to volunteer in a leadership role within the BCIT community and to make a real difference in the lives of new international students – all while having fun and enjoying fantastic benefits!
### Expectations & mentor benefits
The total time commitment for mentors is estimated to be around 6-7 hours in total. View the program schedule for more details and ensure that you can attend the mandatory events. Each mentor will receive receive:
- BCITSA gift card
- official letter of recognition from BCIT
- Complimentary food
- BCIT gear
- free International Confirmation of Graduation Letter (for international students only – a $20 value)
### Apply now!
***Applications are now being accepted for the Fall 2026 term***
To apply to become an International Peer Mentor, send an email to international.mentoring@bcit.ca with:
- Your name
- Your BCIT student number
- A brief explanation of why you are interested to serve as an International Peer Mentor (maximum 100 words)
Note that preference for mentor position selection may be given based on a combination of the factors below:
- Stated reasons for wanting to become a mentor
- Overall GPA (marks in previous terms)
- Academic status (i.e. full-time or part-time course load)
- Number of terms completed at BCIT
- Previous experience with International Peer Mentoring at BCIT
