## Flexible Learning Programs
Refer to the list below for all Flexible Learning programs that are available to international students. Note that there is a separate list for Full-time Cohort Programs .
Use the filter feature to search for programs that may be eligible for a post-graduation work permit (PGWP) , If you aim to apply for a PGWP upon completion of your program, make sure to review all PGWP eligibility criteria on the IRCC website. Understand that issuance of a PGWP is at the sole discretion of IRCC.
For help determining which program at BCIT may be the best fit for you, contact Program Advising .
