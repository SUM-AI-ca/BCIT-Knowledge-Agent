## International Student Centre
From your acceptance to BCIT to graduation and beyond, the International Student Centre (ISC) is an essential resource for international students at BCIT.
ISC services provided for international students at BCIT:
- International Orientation for new students
- Providing helpful, online International Student Guides
- Virtual Drop-In Service
- In-Person Drop-In Service
- Issuing International Letters of Verification
- Educating students about and verifying students’ study permits and medical insurance
- International Peer Mentoring for new students
- Workshops and events specifically designed for international students
- Providing helpful, up-to-date information on the BCIT International Student website including on key topics such as Academic Status at BCIT, Work Eligibility during studies, and Post-Graduation Work Permits
- International Student Update monthly newsletter sharing current news of interest to international students
- Supporting and advocating for international students in the community
- International Student Lounge at Burnaby campus (SW1–1160) available for students to study or relax
