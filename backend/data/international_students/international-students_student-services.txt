## International Student Centre
Many of the important services for international students at BCIT are offered through the International Student Centre. Learn more about these services below.
### International Orientation
Explore International Orientation resources and events.
### International Peer Mentoring
Learn more and register for this special program designed for new students, or apply to volunteer as an International Peer Mentor.
### Virtual Drop-In Service
Connect with International Student Centre staff through video chat from anywhere in the world!
### In-Person Drop-In Service
Visit us on campus for support during drop-in hours.
### International Workshops & Events
Take advantage of the many workshops and events put on by the International Student Centre each term.
### International Letters of Verification
Learn more about how to request a verification letter to support your application to IRCC.
### International Student Guides
Browse through online guides and checklists on a variety of important topics for international students at your convenience.
### ISU Monthly Newsletter
Check your myBCIT student email inbox for the International Student Update, a monthly newsletter filled with current news of interest to international students.
