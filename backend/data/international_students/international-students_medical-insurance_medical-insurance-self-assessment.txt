## Medical Insurance Self-Assessment
## BCIT's medical insurance requirement
International students must submit proof of adequate medical insurance coverage before starting a program at BCIT, and must maintain medical insurance coverage at all times while a student at BCIT.
Select the scenario below that best describes your personal situation for detailed information to help you understand your responsibilities regarding medical insurance versus what actions BCIT may be taking on your behalf.
If you have additional questions after reviewing the information and relevant links shared below, please contact us at international.medical@bcit.ca and include your BCIT student number. We will be happy to assist!

### Medical insurance scenarios for new international students
You may submit proof of MSP enrolment or another kind of basic medical insurance by email to international.medical@bcit.ca . If you successfully meet BCIT’s medical insurance requirement, a $180 credit (from your EPAS first term prepaid fees) will remain on your student account and can be used towards future tuition.
### When to submit your copy of medical insurance
If you are currently outside of Canada : as soon as you have a confirmed travel plan to Canada.
If you are currently inside Canada : immediately after receiving your Letter of Acceptance.
Submit your proof of medical insurance immediately after receiving acceptance to your program in order to satisfy BCIT’s medical insurance requirement and avoid being charged a fee by BCIT for private medical insurance. Typically the deadline to “opt out” by providing your own insurance is 90 days before your program starts.
If you are already enrolled in BC Medical Services Plan (MSP), you may submit your proof of MSP enrolment .
If you have another kind of basic medical insurance to cover your first months in Canada, you may submit a copy by email to international.medical@bcit.ca for approval. Note that even if you have your own private medical insurance, you will still need to apply for MSP once you arrive in BC and submit proof of MSP enrolment to BCIT.
Contact us at international.medical@bcit.ca as early as possible if you or your home institute plan to provide medical insurance to cover your time here at BCIT.
In your email to us, include:
- your student number
- a statement explaining that you will be a visiting 1-term Exchange or 1-term Study Abroad student
- a copy of your medical insurance information, OR a statement declaring that your home institute will be arranging medical insurance for you.*
*If you do not yet have a copy of your medical insurance information to provide, you can send it at a later date. By notifying us that your home institute will be arranging your medical insurance, it lets us know NOT to have BCIT charge you for medical insurance in Canada.
If you have any questions or concerns about medical insurance, you may contact us at international.medical@bcit.ca .

If you are not yet in Canada, then you have the option to purchase temporary medical insurance coverage through Guard.me’s medical insurance portal . The advantage of purchasing your own Guard.me policy is that you can customize the coverage dates. Be sure to purchase the insurance policy starting from your date of travel to Canada and through the end of your MSP coverage wait period . If you purchase Guard.me insurance through the portal linked above, Guard.me will notify us of your policy so you won’t need to send a copy of it to us.
