## Programs & delivery models at BCIT
BCIT offers a wide variety of programs all the way from English upgrading to master’s degrees. Academic programs are offered through two distinct program delivery models: Full-time cohort studies and Flexible Learning . Some programs are are offered through both, while others are only available through one specific program delivery model.
Compare the features of the two program delivery models below, and consider which may be a better fit for you.
In Full-time cohort programs at BCIT:
- Students are automatically registered into a block schedule of courses each term
- You can earn a major credential such as a diploma or degree as quickly as possible with an intensive full-time course load
- Immerse yourself in BCIT campus life by studying day classes throughout the week
- Create lasting connections with fellow students in your set as you progress through the program together
- Many full-time diploma and degree programs have a regularly scheduled summer break
- Tuition fees are charged by term, and are cheaper per credit compared to Flexible Learning
- Graduates from select programs may be eligible for a post-graduation work permit (PGWP)
In Flexible Learning programs at BCIT:
- Programs have three intakes each year: winter, spring/summer, or fall
- Select for courses each term and study at your own pace
- Maintain a full-time academic status by registering in at least 9 credits every term
- Most courses are offered on weekday evenings or on weekends
- Tuition fees are charged per course
