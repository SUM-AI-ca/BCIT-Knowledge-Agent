## Virtual Drop-In Service
We at the International Student Centre (ISC) are pleased to offer virtual drop-in services to international students via Microsoft Teams.
When you follow the link to access virtual drop-ins during service hours, you will be placed in a waiting room until an ISC staff member is able to assist you. Please have your BCIT student ID number ready as well as any other documents that are related to your questions (study permit, co-op work permit, etc.). You may also contact us in-person, via email or telephone.
## International Student Centre Virtual Drop-In Service
Virtual drop-in hours: 12:00 - 2:00 pm Pacific Time, Monday - Friday (excluding statutory holidays). 

Please note that Virtual drop-in service is closed Wednesday, June 10, 2026.
### Topics that we may be able to help with
- Accessing BCIT & BCITSA services for students
- Study permits & Co-op work permits
- Academic status at BCIT (full-time versus part-time)
- Updating your status in Canada documents
- Medical insurance
- Travel to Canada
- Working in Canada & post-graduation work permits
- International Letters of Verification
- Mental and physical wellbeing resources
- Adjusting to life in Canada
