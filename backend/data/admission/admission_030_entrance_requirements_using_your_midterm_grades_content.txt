Title: Using Your Midterm Grades
================================================================================

Step 2: submit your documents

### Canadian high school students

Submit one of the following: Second term or mid-year report card Midterm Grade Submission form [PDF] A letter from your institution on official letterhead that includes: Your name Name and address of your institution Course name, subject and number, length in weeks, and start and end dates. Midterm grade (percentage, if possible) Instructor’s name, phone number, and email address The following statements: Signed by the student: “BCIT reserves the right to contact the instructor to verify the information provided on this form. I have read and understand what a midterm grade is for the purposes of my admission and I understand that if I submit false information my application will be cancelled.” Signed by the instructor: “I verify that this student has completed 50% of the gradable content at the grade specified and the midpoint of the course has passed.”

### International high school students

Submit one of the following: Second term or mid-year report card that includes: Name of school Address and country of school Name of high school graduation credential you are working towards Grade level of each course (e.g. Grade 11, Senior Year 2) Grading scale and minimum passing grade Number of terms per year Midterm Grade Submission form [PDF] A letter from your institution on official letterhead that includes: Your name Name and address of institution Course name, subject and number, length in weeks, and start and end date. Midterm grade (percentage, if possible) Instructor’s name, phone number, and email address The following statements: Signed by the student: “BCIT reserves the right to contact the instructor to verify the information provided on this form. I have read and understand what a midterm grade is for the purposes of my admission and I understand that if I submit false information my application will be cancelled.” Signed by the instructor: “I verify that this student has completed 50% of the gradable content at the grade specified and the midpoint of the course has passed.”

## Grade audit

If you apply with midterm grades or final Grade 11 grades with proof of registration in Grade 12, you may be required to show proof of your final grades. Admissions will contact you if you are selected for a grade audit.
