Indigenous Gathering Place The Indigenous Gathering Place located on the Burnaby Campus serves as a welcoming place for Indigenous students, creating a sense of inclusion and belonging through cultural and educational activities. Students and staff from across BCIT are invited to participate in ceremonies and events that celebrate the diversity and richness of Indigenous culture. Indigenous Initiatives is committed to building communities, connecting students and providing support by offering a range of services such as advising, mentoring, gatherings, cultural ceremonies, and Elders on Campus.
