## Equivalencies
Is a course you’ve completed equivalent to a BCIT entrance requirement? View our requirement equivalencies to find out.
Search our program catalogue to check your program’s entrance requirements, then review the course equivalencies below.
- BC and Yukon high school equivalencies
- Canadian high school equivalencies
- International high school equivalencies
- Post-secondary course equivalencies
Note: High school graduation is a preferred requirement for admission. BCIT strongly recommends it for career success. For most programs, applicants who have not graduated from high school will be considered for admission if they meet or exceed program entrance requirements.
