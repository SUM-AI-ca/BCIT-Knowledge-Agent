## Policy update

Changes to Policy 2300 -Tuition, Fees, and Refunds will take effect in Fall 2026. Information on this page is subject to change.

BCIT’s refunds and withdrawals policy You may withdraw from your course or program and be eligible to receive a tuition refund if you voluntarily withdraw by the refund deadline. Different deadlines, fees, and rules apply depending on your course or program type. See below for details on the withdrawal and refund processes as they relate to: Full-time programs Flexible Learning courses Apprenticeship programs International Students with changing immigration statuses How to get a refund

Full-time programs

### How to withdraw

New students If you are a new, direct entry, or readmitted student withdrawing from your first term (or first term back) in a full-time program: If it is before the refund deadline, contact Admissions at 604-432-8899 or admissions@bcit.ca with your BCIT student number, program name, and program start date. If it is after the refund deadline, please follow the instructions below for continuing and current students. To withdraw from a full-time course, contact your program administrator. Continuing and current students If you are withdrawing from a full-time program after completing one or more terms: Contact your program area to obtain a signature on the applicable form below: Trades Withdrawal [PDF] Full-time Technology Withdrawal [PDF] Then, submit the completed form to Student Records at records@bcit.ca. Note: If BCIT requires you to withdraw for reasons of discipline or unsatisfactory progress after the refund deadline, you are not eligible to receive a refund.

### Refund eligibility deadlines

For most programs, your latest acceptable withdrawal date to receive a refund is 10 business days after the start of class. Please note that all commitment fees are non-refundable. International students currently pay a commitment fee of $6,000 in full-time programs. See the table below for the commitment fee amount deducted from your refund. Program type Commitment fee deducted from your refund Refund deadline Trades programs Domestic students: $200 International students: $6000 10 business days after the start of class Airline and flight operations Domestic students: $1000 International students: $6000 10 business days after the start of class Technology programs Domestic students: $500 International students: $6000 10 business days after the start of class Degree programs Domestic students: $500 International students: $6000 10 business days after the start of class U-Pass BC fees are collected on behalf of Translink and are not covered by BCIT’s refund policy.

Flexible Learning courses Note: Due to high volume, please allow 7 to 10 business days for Student Information and Enrolment Services to process your course withdrawal request. Please do not send multiple emails for the same withdrawal request.

### How to withdraw

All Flexible Learning (Part-time Studies) course withdrawal requests must be submitted using the official Flexible Learning Course Withdrawal form [PDF]. If you are submitting your completed form by email, please send it to PTSwithdrawals@bcit.ca. You must use your myBCIT email address so that the Registrar’s Office can verify the legitimacy of your request. Incomplete forms or forms submitted using a non-myBCIT email address cannot be processed. Withdrawals will be processed within 2-3 business days. Log in to myBCIT and check under Student Self Service to confirm withdrawals and account balances. Even if you have missed the refund or withdrawal deadline, it is very important to notify BCIT of your intent to withdraw. Late withdrawals are subject to program area approval, and will not be considered without applicable documentation. Remember to formally withdraw to ensure your permanent academic record, your GPA, and your ability to qualify for future awards and recognition are not affected. Please visit Flexible Learning Withdrawals for more information.

### Refund eligibility deadlines

Refund information, including deadlines, will be provided when you register for a course. Special fees for some courses are non-refundable, and different refund requirements may apply. Students who withdraw or transfer 31 days prior to the course start date will receive a 100 percent tuition refund, and there is no charge to transfer. Students in a continual entry/start any time course that is two weeks or less are not eligible to receive a refund.

### Transferring between courses

Contact Student Information and Enrolment Services to request a transfer from one course to another. The transfer is subject to a $16 fee and must be requested and processed by the refund deadline. No transfer fee will be charged if the transfer is requested 31 days or more before the start date of the course.

Apprenticeship programs

### How to withdraw

Up to and including the third day of class, contact Apprentice Services. After the third day of class, complete a Trades Withdrawal form [PDF] and submit it to Apprentice Services. No refund is issued for any withdrawal less than nine days before the start of class.

### Refund eligibility deadlines

Cancellation notice received: Refund: Up to 41 days prior to the start of class Tuition fee minus $200* 40-21 days prior to the start of class 50% of tuition fee 20-10 days prior to the start of class 25% of tuition fee 9 days or less prior to the start of class No refund *Note: $200 commitment fee is non-transferable and non-refundable.

International students Note: International students in Canada on a study permit should be aware that withdrawing from a program or courses may have immigration implications. If unsure, students are encouraged to consult with the International Student Centre before withdrawing. Regular refund and withdrawal processes apply to international students enrolled in part-time or full-time studies. However, processing fees may differ for international students. If your status in Canada has changed or your study permit has been denied, BCIT has specific processes available for International students.

### Changing immigration status

If you paid international tuition fees and provide proof of domestic status in Canada to the International Student Centre before the refund deadline for all courses in the term, we will refund you the difference between the domestic and international tuition fees.

### Study permit denial

Study permits might be denied. What to do depends on if you are studying in the International Student Entry Program (ISEP) or, other full-time or part-time programs. 1. International Student Entry Program (ISEP) If your study permit application is denied by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), you can request a 100% refund of all tuition fees paid (including the commitment fee). Submit the following documents to isepinfo@bcit.ca at least 10 days before your program starts: A scanned copy of the original study permit denial letter from IRCC ISEP – Request to Withdraw Authorization for Disclosure of Personal Information to a Third Party (optional) 2. Other full-time and part-time programs Please see the Study Permit page for information on refunds due to study permit denials.

How to get a refund If you are eligible for a tuition refund, you must fill out the refund request form. Your refund request will be assessed in accordance to BCIT refund and withdrawal policies and issued accordingly. In order for your request to be eligible for a refund, you must submit your request within two calendar years of the start date of the course(s). If BCIT received your payment by Flywire, your eligible refund will be made through Flywire. All other payment forms will be refunded by cheque. Refunds take approximately six weeks to process, plus mailing time. Thank you for your patience and understanding. To assist in a timely processing of your refunds, please ensure your mailing address is up to date and includes the Unit/Suite #, if applicable. For any cheque not received due to outdated or inaccurate address, it may take up to eight weeks for the new cheque to be issued and mailed. To update your mailing address: Log in to your myBCIT account Select Student Self Service Select Student Personal Information Select Update Your Personal Information Select Contact Information Select Update Mailing Address Click Update My Profile at the bottom of the page For refund inquiries for all programs and courses, please contact Student Information and Enrolment Services. Canada Post has suspended delivery or is reporting delays to many international destinations. If your destination address is included on the International Suspensions list, please contact BCIT_FIN_Student_AR@bcit.ca list to determine an alternate refund method.
