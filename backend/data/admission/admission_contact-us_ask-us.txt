## Common questions
Here are some of the most common questions we hear from you. Let us help you find the answers:
### Full-Time Programs
Tuition for most full-time programs will be posted in your myBCIT account under Student Self Service approximately 1-2 months before the program start date. Prior to posting, you can calculate your tuition using our fee schedules. Select your program type at the top of the Tuition & Fees page, then select the applicable tuition fee schedule PDF for your program. Please note there are no taxes on tuition.
### Part-Time Courses
Part-time course fees are paid a course-by-course basis. Fees for each course are listed on each course registration page; find your course using our online course catalogue . For additional details, visit the Flexible Learning Fees page.
Flexible Learning courses for International students carry 3.0 times the posted fee. Distance & Online Learning courses also carry 3.0 times the fee.
Please note there are no taxes on tuition.
You can apply to a program as long as you meet that program’s entrance requirements. Different requirements, guidelines, and processes apply depending on what you choose to study. BCIT provides a general step-by-step checklist for each of its program application options, including applications for advanced placement. Visit the How to Apply page to find your checklist.
Please note that you may only have one active application at a time.
### Full-time Studies
At BCIT, entrance requirements are program-specific. Find your program and refer to the program entry details. In addition to submitting documents to show that you meet the academic requirements, you may be required to have work experience, submit assessment forms, or attend an interview.
If you have questions regarding the entrance requirements , check our entrance requirements page or contact Program Advising .
### Flexible Learning
Whether it is a listed requirement for your program or not, it is strongly recommended that you have English proficiency . English requirements are program-specific. Find your program and refer to the program entry details.
Many courses have prerequisites listed in the course description. Before registering, it is your responsibility to ensure you have completed the prerequisites or provide proof that you have equivalent skills and knowledge, if requested.
If you have questions regarding entrance requirements, contact Program Advising .
