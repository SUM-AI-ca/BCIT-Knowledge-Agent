## Why BCIT
With an astounding 97% employment rate for degree graduates, engaging, hands-on training and passionate instructors, BCIT is so much more than an education—it’s the first step towards your major career milestone.

Learn why students choose to study here, and how we can help you thrive.
## Program Availability
We offer some of the most in-demand programs in the province—many of which are unique to BCIT. Each is designed to set you up for the workplace on graduation.

Check out our Program Availability to see if your preferred program is currently accepting applications.

Meeting the requirements for admission is the first step toward studying at BCIT. Learn what it takes to enroll, explore your options for upgrading, and determine if your previous experience will count toward your program.
