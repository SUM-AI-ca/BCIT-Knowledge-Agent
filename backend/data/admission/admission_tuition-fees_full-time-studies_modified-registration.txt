## Modified Registration
Full-time technology and degree studies students who are registering on a course-by-course basis pay tuition according to the tables under each field of study. Tuition for a term is calculated by the number of credits multiplied by the amount per credit, until the maximum tuition is reached.
BCIT Student Association and ancillary fees are assessed in addition to tuition when you register on a course-by-course basis.
