## After You Apply
You’ve chosen what you want to study and submitted your application to BCIT. What happens after you’ve applied?
## Step 1: Access your myBCIT
Once your application has been received and processed, you’ll receive an email with your BCIT ID and instructions to login to your myBCIT account for the first time. The email is your key to accessing all important communications about your application, including your Welcome to BCIT e-letter.
Learn more about communicating with BCIT
## Step 2: Take action in myBCIT
Your myBCIT account is the hub for managing your application. Here’s what you can do:
- Upload transcripts and supporting documents to complete your application
- Check your application status
- View important communications from Admissions
- Complete or update your personal information
## Step 3: Wait for an admission decision
The timing of your admissions decision depends on:
- The type of assessment model for your chosen program
- How complete your application is
- Review of non-academic requirements by the program area
Rest assured, Admissions will notify you as soon as a decision has been made.
Learn more about your application status
## Step 4: Prepare to start
Congratulations! Welcome to BCIT. Preparation is key to thriving on your educational journey. Here’s how to ensure you have a smooth start.
- Read the next steps for new students
- Connect with Financial Aid and Awards
- Attend upcoming Kick Start events
- Register for a campus tour
