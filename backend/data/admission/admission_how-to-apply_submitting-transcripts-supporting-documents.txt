Title: Submitting Transcripts & Supporting Documents

================================================================================

## Submitting Transcripts & Supporting Documents
Your application will remain incomplete until Admissions receives proof that you have completed or are actively completing all published admission requirements. To ensure your application can be processed, required documents must be uploaded with your online application or through your myBCIT account after you apply. Upload only transcripts and documents that support one or more of your program’s entrance requirements. Do not submit anything that is not relevant to your application.
Your application completion date is the date Admissions receives all required documents needed to assess your eligibility for admission, which may differ from the date you submitted your application. If Admissions does not receive the required documents by the published application deadline, or by your assigned deadline to submit outstanding requirements, your application will be closed.
## Before you apply
- Review your program’s entrance requirements. Each program at BCIT has unique requirements. Review the ‘ Entrance Requirements ’ section of your program page and note the documents you must provide.
- Gather your documentation. Collect the transcripts and supporting documents that prove how you satisfy the entrance requirements for your program (e.g., official transcripts, proof of English Language Proficiency [ELP], questionnaires, reference letters, portfolios, or other program-specific requirements).
- Prepare your files. All documents must be saved and uploaded as PDF files . After your files are ready, you can submit your application through EducationPlannerBC .
## For academic requirements
Note: BCIT may request the original official transcripts or other academic documentation at any time.
If your transcript or other official academic documentation does not meet your program’s English Language Proficiency (ELP) requirement, you must upload a copy of your completed ELP assessment results with your application and select BCIT as a score-receiving institution where available.
Learn more about alternative ELP equivalencies >
Submit one or more of the following (as applicable):
- Official high school transcript showing final grades
- Official post-secondary transcript showing completed courses and/or conferred credential
Official transcripts must:
- Display the institution’s name and address, and
- Bear the original signature, seal, and/or stamp of the issuing institution, or
- Be issued on institute security paper, or
- Bear an official notary stamp and/or seal.
If your transcript comes in a sealed envelope, open it to scan and upload with your application.
Note: If you are requesting transfer credit , your official transcripts must be sent directly from the institution(s) you previously attended or dropped off at BCIT in the original sealed and stamped envelope.
If you are currently completing required courses, upload one or more of the following:
- BC Diploma Verification report
- Second term or mid-year high school report card
- Midterm grade submission
- Final Grade 11 report card and confirmed Grade 12 timetable (current Canadian high school applicants only)
- In-progress post-secondary transcript showing current enrolment
Review additional information for BC, Canadian, and international high school applicants >
Note: Current BC high school applicants must upload their BC Diploma Verification report directly with their application. BCIT does not receive transcripts automatically through the Student Transcript Service.
If your transcript is not in English, upload:
- The original official transcript (PDF), and
- An official English translation by a certified translator.
Translation costs are the applicant’s responsibility.
Note: You may need a basic or comprehensive International Credential Evaluation Service (ICES) report for any assessment of post-secondary equivalent coursework. Check your program page for exact requirements.
## For non-academic requirements
Some programs require additional documentation for non-academic requirements (e.g., driver’s license, résumé or curriculum vitae [CV], letter of reference, portfolio, forms, assessments, or questionnaires) that must be submitted with your application.
Some programs also have additional non-academic requirements that must be completed after you receive a conditional acceptance (e.g., immunization records, criminal record checks, proof of certifications, licenses, or other clearances). These requirements will be communicated once conditional acceptance is issued.
Check your program page to confirm any additional requirements.
## Preparing your documents (PDF format)
You must upload your documents as PDF files with your online application. What you need to know:
- One PDF per document upload
- Multipage documents combined into a single PDF
- Front and back of every page for official documents (including blank pages)
- All text and images are clear and readable
- Maximum file size: 10MB (EPBC) or 2MB (myBCIT)
### How to create PDF files
Scanning produces the clearest and most reliable PDFs.
You can scan documents at:
- BCIT Student Association’s Campus Print & Copy
- BCIT’s Libraries or other public libraries
- Home scanner or photocopier
- Most commercial print/copy centres (small service fee may apply)
You can use your smartphone as long as the final file that is uploaded is a PDF. Tips for best results:
- Use a mobile scanning app (e.g., Adobe Scan, Microsoft Lens, iOS/Android built-in scan features)
- Capture images in bright, even lighting
- Ensure the document is in focus and aligned
For files you create (e.g., résumé, questionnaires, written statements):
- Use Save as PDF or Export to PDF in your software
- Search online for instructions for your specific device or software if needed
If your program requires fillable BCIT forms, download the form and save it to your computer. Then, follow the steps below:
- Open it with Adobe Reader or Adobe Acrobat .
- Complete all required fields.
- Save your file.
- Close and reopen it to confirm your content has saved correctly.
- Upload the completed PDF with your application.
