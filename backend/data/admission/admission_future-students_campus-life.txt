## Campus Life
## Make the most of your experience
BCIT offers the support and services for you to get the most out of life at BCIT. There are spaces for you to learn, work, get assistance, and socialize. This means you can build a network of connections and join supportive groups that help you thrive.
Explore BCIT Student Services
### Recreation Services
As a BCIT student, you’ll have free access to recreation facilities, including the gym, and fitness centre. You’ll also have discounted access to intramural sports leagues, squash courts, and more.
### Health and wellness
Confidential medical appointments and walk-in visits are available through Student Health Services. Counselling and Student Development are available both in-person and virtually.
### Academic support
Peer tutoring and writing support is available through the Learning Commons. The Trades Learning Centre provides support in math, science, and blueprint reading. MediaWorks offers design and production assistance.
### Accessibility Services
Connect with Accessibility Services about accommodations that may reduce and remove barriers related to disabilities during your studies, visible and invisible, permanent or temporary.
### Financial Aid & Awards
Funding your education starts here. Your finances shouldn’t stand in the way of your education. Let us help you determine your funding options for your BCIT program.
## Indigenous Gathering Place
The Indigenous Gathering Place located on the Burnaby Campus serves as a welcoming place for Indigenous students, creating a sense of inclusion and belonging through cultural and educational activities. Students and staff from across BCIT are invited to participate in ceremonies and events that celebrate the diversity and richness of Indigenous culture.
Indigenous Initiatives is committed to building communities, connecting students and providing support by offering a range of services such as advising, mentoring, gatherings, cultural ceremonies, and Elders on Campus.
Connect with Indigenous Initiatives
On our Burnaby Campus, Student Housing is a great way to share experiences with people from different backgrounds, participate in activities, and become involved in campus life.
