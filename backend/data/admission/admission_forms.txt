## Forms
On our forms, BCIT collects demographic information, including birth date, from prospective students, applicants, and enrolled students to generate unique, unduplicated student numbers, and to meet reporting requirements for the Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills. The information is kept in a secure system and can be accessed only by authorized BCIT personnel. For more information, please read BCIT’s Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Policy .
The documents below are in PDF format, which requires Adobe Acrobat Reader. Download the latest version of Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) .
