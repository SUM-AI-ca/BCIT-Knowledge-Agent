## How to Apply
## Application steps
Follow the step-by-step application process to help you identify the program that is right for you. Find the entrance requirements to your program of choice, prepare your application, submit your application in a timely manner, and track your application status online.
## Program entry options
Students attending BCIT may have different application processes available depending on their unique backgrounds. Let us provide you step-by-step application processes depending on how you choose to apply to BCIT:
- High School Applicants
- International Applicants
- Apply Using Your Midterm Grades
- Apprenticeship Applicants
- Advanced Placement
- Flexible Learning Program Declaration
## Applicant priority
Some programs are competitive, while other programs accept students on a first-qualified, first-accepted basis.
Learn more about Applicant Priority for your program type.
## Submitting documents
Submitting transcripts and supporting documents is a key step in the application process. Let us provide you tips on how to make this process fast and easy.
