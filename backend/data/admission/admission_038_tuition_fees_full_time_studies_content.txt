## Policy update

Changes to Policy 2300 -Tuition, Fees, and Refunds will take effect in Fall 2026. Information on this page is subject to change.

## Estimate your Full-Time Tuition and Fees

You can view or print the estimate cost for your chosen full-time program using the Tuition Estimator tool; see estimated tuition and fees for the entire program, and broken down by term.

Tuition increases Each year BC post-secondary institutions may raise tuition by 2 per cent. In April each year, BCIT posts the new rates that take effect on August 1. If you pay your tuition prior to April using the old rates, you are required to pay the difference within 10 business days from the start of your program. Please see the Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills’ Tuition Limit Policy for more information. Commitment fee Once offered admission, a newly admitted student must pay a commitment fee to confirm acceptance. Commitment fee amounts may vary by program of study and the applicant’s residency status and is applied to tuition upon registration. The commitment fee is forfeited and retained by BCIT under the following circumstances: the offer is declined by the applicant after payment is made, or the applicant does not register for any course in their start term The commitment fee may be deferred under limited circumstances outlined in BCIT Policy 5003 – PR1 when an admission deferral is approved. Domestic students $200 commitment fee is required to confirm acceptance in full-time trades programs except for Airline and Flight Operations Commercial Pilot program ($1,000). All other programs require a $500 commitment fee. International students $6,000 commitment fee is required to confirm acceptance in all full-time programs.

Costs included in 2025-2026 full-time studies tuition & fees

### Ancillary fees

Ancillary fees are mandatory fees that help fund many campus and student services and are included with your total tuition. These fees are paid by most students so that all students benefit from having access to a broad range of supports and services. For specific fee amounts, see the fee breakdown for your program or course. Ancillary fees are used to finance the following services and facilities: Learning Resources Supports academic services and educational materials that enrich the learning experience for students and faculty. Specifically, this fee helps fund learning resources including online databases, e-books, books, journals, standards & codes, multimedia, videos, and course materials; and support services including academic research support, peer tutoring, writing support, and interlibrary loans. Rates usually vary between $33.67 to $89.63 per term, depending on the program. Student Services Supports services that enhance student success and the overall BCIT student experience. Specifically, this fee helps fund services including a broad range of mental health supports including counselling, accessibility, and health services; supports for students at risk or in crisis, including early intervention systems/processes and financial aid programs; and student programming to increase engagement and well-being such as recreation services, peer supports, and orientation and transition activities. Rates vary between $18.62 to $55.91 per term with the exception of trades programs which is assessed at a weekly rate of $1.51. There is no Student Services fee applied for co-op terms. Student Technology Supports technology embedded in the student experience such as applications and software used for learning and teaching, including remote access to those applications and software, and remote access to well-being services and supports. The rate is $24.96 per term. There is no Student Technology fee applied for co-op terms. Building Supports new construction and/or improvements to the existing physical infrastructure for academic and non-academic student spaces/buildings and informal learning spaces. This fee also supports the associated cost and operations of buildings, spaces and facilities used by the Institute community. Rates vary between $9.29 to $13.94 per term with the exception of trades programs which is assessed at a weekly rate of $0.77. There is no Building fee applied for co-op terms. Funds from Government and donations from industry partners and donors contribute significant funding towards new capital projects. Student ID Card Used to generate and maintain student ID cards required by students for integration and connection to campus services, both virtual and in-person. In addition, these cards may be used to gain access to labs and classrooms as well as to verify identification for exams or other testing. The rate is $2.50 per term. There is no Student ID Card fee applied for co-op terms.

### BCIT Student Association fees

BCIT student association fees include the following: Extended Heath & Dental Plan Fee* Student Association Activity Fee Capital Levy Fee SA Centre Building Fee Learn more about BCIT Student Association fees for full-time programs *Full-time, eligible students in technology, trades, or technical studies programs running for 16 weeks or longer are automatically covered by the BCITSA Student Health and Dental Plan. If the student is already covered for comparable extended health and dental benefits, they have the option to opt-out of their student plan. Important: The Opt-Out and Family Add-On deadlines are 30 days from the start of their program, once per 12 months. After the deadline, Opt-Outs and Add-Ons cannot be processed.

### Accident insurance and insurance with WorkSafeBC

Accident insurance BCIT has a Student Accident Insurance Program that covers everything from a cracked tooth to a broken leg. Insurance under WorkSafeBC Employers pay the WorkSafeBC premium for all students participating in a required practicum at a recognized work site. Student apprentices are also covered during classroom, lab, and shop instruction.

### Tool deposit and lab or flying fee

Total tuition for Trades and Technical studies programs also includes a tool deposit and a lab or flying fee.

Additional costs not included in full-time studies tuition & fees

### U-Pass BC

The total tuition cost listed in the full-time tuition schedules does not include the U-Pass BC fee. The U-Pass BC fee will be charged upon registration and is a mandatory fee for all eligible students.

### Books and supplies

The cost of textbooks and study materials is additional to program and other mandatory fees. There are a few exceptions that are outlined in the program or course description. Check the BCIT Bookstore for most books and supplies. Students may be required to supply their own coveralls for use in some programs.

### International health insurance fee

New international students may be charged an International health insurance fee. For details about this fee and the medical insurance requirement for international students, visit the Medical Insurance page for international students.

### Computer hardware and software needs

Although it’s not required for most programs, it is recommended that you possess a personal computer capable of running the current version of MS Office and a web browser, as well as have an account with an Internet Service Provider (ISP) of your choice.

Transfer credit If you are granted transfer credit in your full-time program, your course load will be lighter; however, your full-time program tuition may not be reduced. Refer to Modified Registration for individual course fees. Paying full-time tuition Once you receive your offer of admission, BCIT will reserve a seat in the program for you when you submit your commitment fee. Fees are payable on a term basis. First-term tuition is due 60 days prior to the start of your program. Returning and continuing students must pay their tuition in full within 10 business days from the start of classes or have an approved fee deferral in place. We accept the following forms of payment: Debit card (in person only) Cheque or money order payable to BCIT – please include your BCIT ID (student number) Online banking (bill payment) through your financial institution using BCIT – Tuition as the payee and your BCIT ID (student number) as your account number. Please allow up to 3 business days for online bill payments to be posted to your account. Flywire – Go to bcit-main.flywire.com. Bank wire transfer or Credit Card option available, please note service fee will apply. Letter of sponsorship authorization for invoicing by BCIT You can view and print your receipt through your myBCIT account.
