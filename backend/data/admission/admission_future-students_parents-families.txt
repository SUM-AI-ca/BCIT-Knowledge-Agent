## Parents & Families
Choosing a post-secondary school is an important decision for your family. If you’re looking for employable skills that will lead your child to a rewarding career, look no further. A BCIT education will give your child an edge in the job market.
Why should your family choose BCIT?
- Our BCIT grads get hired , with a 93% employment rate for degree grads.*
- We offer cohort learning and flexible study options to fit your child’s needs.
- Financial aid , bursaries, and awards made available to eligible students.
- Extensive academic support , as well as health and wellness services.
- Hands-on learning taught by industry experts to help your child stay competitive in an evolving workforce.
Everywhere you look, BCIT grads are in demand and making a difference. Your child can too.
*Source: BC Student Outcomes, prepared by BC Stats (2024)
