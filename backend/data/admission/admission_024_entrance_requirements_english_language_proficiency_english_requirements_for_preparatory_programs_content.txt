Title: English Requirements for Preparatory Programs
================================================================================

### Business Fundamentals

If your program is Business Fundamentals, you must meet at least one of the English Language Proficiency assessments listed below. Test results must be dated no more than two years before the start date of the program you’re applying to. Assessment Score/Mark BCIT Technical Academic English (TAE) 3 International Student Entry Program (ISEP) 50% BCIT English Competency Assessment: COMM 0015 – English Competency Assessment and COMM 0033 – English Speaking and Listening Assessment “U” and placement in COMM 0071, COMM 0003, COMM 0004, or COMM 0005 Duolingo English Test 90 International English Language Testing Systems (IELTS) Note: IELTS Academic only Overall score 5.0 Pearson Test of English Academic (PTE Academic) Overall score 45 Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL iBT) BCIT Destination code: 9110 Overall score 35 ILSC Education Group Level 3 (Intermediate) 70% overall Royal Bridge College Level 4 (Intermediate) 75% overall VGC International College University Preparation (Intermediate) 70% overall

### Technology Entry

If your program is Technology Entry, you must meet at least one of the English Language Proficiency assessments listed below. Test results must be dated no more than two years before the start date of the program you’re applying to. Assessment Score/Mark BCIT Professional English Competency Assessment: COMM 0004 – Technical Communication Skills for EAL Students 3 or COMM 0005 – Technical Communication Skills for EAL Students 4 and COMM 0029 – Speaking and Listening Skills 1 for EAL Students 50% and S grade BCIT English Competency Assessment: COMM 0015 – English Competency Assessment and COMM 0033 – English Speaking and Listening Assessment 50% and 60% BCIT Technical Academic English (TAE) 4 International Student Entry Program (ISEP) 50% Duolingo English Test 100 International English Language Testing Systems (IELTS) Note: IELTS Academic only Overall score 6.0 Pearson Test of English Academic (PTE Academic) Overall score 52 Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL iBT) BCIT Destination code: 9110 Overall score 82 ILSC Education Group Level 3 (Intermediate) 70% overall Royal Bridge College Level 5 (Lower-Advanced) 75% overall VGC International College University Preparation (Intermediate) 80% overall
