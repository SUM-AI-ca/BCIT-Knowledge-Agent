## Build Your Credentials
Start with a certificate, finish with a degree. At BCIT, you can build your education to suit your needs, one step at a time.
## How it works
Some BCIT programs allow you to advance your education by building on one credential into another— shortening your overall classroom time and getting you into your career more quickly.
## Credentials
Certificates are relatively short but specialized training programs that combine applied theory and practical skills to work in a particular field. Certificates can be completed one or multiple courses at a time, over a period of months or years. You’ll graduate with an associate certificate or certificate credential.
See all Certificate Programs
Diplomas are more intensive, longer programs of study, which prepare you for employment in technological, technical, paraprofessional and professional occupations. These programs are usually full time, with some part-time options available.
See all Diploma Programs
BCIT’s Advanced Studies options are designed for college, technical institute or university graduates. We offer highly specialized, advanced training programs that build on your prior education and experience.
See all Advanced Certificate Programs
See all Advanced Diploma Programs
A degree from BCIT gives you the best of both worlds: a recognized bachelor’s credential, and practical training to step right into a job. All BCIT degrees include academic specialty courses, management studies, and liberal studies. Many programs can be done by either full-time or part-time study.
We offer the following bachelor’s degrees:
- Bachelor of Accounting
- Bachelor of Architectural Science
- Bachelor of Business Administration
- Bachelor of Engineering
- Bachelor of Health Science
- Bachelor of Interior Design
- Bachelor of Science
- Bachelor of Science in Nursing
- Bachelor of Technology
See all Bachelor’s Programs
Microcredentials are short specialized modular programs that help you quickly gain new skills and knowledge essential in Canadian industries. These programs align with industry needs, positioning you to be adaptable as new opportunities arise. Advance your career and build your resume with these affordable and flexible options.
View microcredentials
Graduate certificates are for people with a bachelor’s degree who are looking for a mix of practical skills as well as theory. These programs provide students with rigorous learning experiences that blend theoretical knowledge with practical applications.
See all Graduate Certificate Programs
All of BCIT master’s degrees include both a coursework and a research component. They resemble traditional university and college models in their quality and rigour, but students take a practical approach, solving real industry problems as part of their thesis or Master’s project.
We offer six unique master’s degree:
- Master of Applied Science in Building Engineering/Building Science
- Master of Digital Media
- Master of Engineering in Building Science
- Master of Engineering in Smart Grid Systems and Technology
- Master of Science in Applied Computing
- Master of Science in Ecological Restoration
