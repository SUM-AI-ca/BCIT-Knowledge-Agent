Title: Credential Evaluation
================================================================================

If you studied outside of Canada, the United States, or the United Kingdom and need your education recognized for admission to BCIT, you may need a credential evaluation.

BCIT accepts evaluations from International Credential Evaluation Service (ICES) or any member of the Alliance of International Credential Evaluation Services of Canada (ACESC). These evaluations assess your formal, for-credit education and determine how it compares to Canadian standards.

For some BCIT programs with a post-secondary entrance requirement, applicants will be required to submit a Comprehensive Report. Please review your program’s entrance requirements to confirm if this applies to you. Once you receive your Basic or Comprehensive Report, convert your evaluation to PDF and upload it with your application.
