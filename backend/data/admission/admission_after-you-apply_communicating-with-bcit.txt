## Communicating with BCIT
## Getting started with myBCIT
When you apply, declare a program , or request apprentice training at BCIT, all official communications will be channelled through your myBCIT account. Once your application is processed, you’ll receive an email with instructions on how to access your myBCIT account.
Your myBCIT portal is your key to navigating the next steps in your application:
- Viewing important communications from BCIT;
- Uploading transcripts and supporting documents to complete your application;
- Checking your application status;
- Ensuring your personal information is complete and up-to-date.
## Viewing important communications
You’ll receive an alert to your personal and BCIT email for every official communication sent by BCIT. Some messages are time-sensitive and require action, so it’s important to regularly check your inbox or myCommunication.
Log into your myBCIT account > Student Self Service > myCommunications to view and print all official BCIT letters.
## Uploading transcripts and documents
Easily submit your transcripts and any additional supporting documents needed to complete your application.
Log into your myBCIT account > Student Self Service > Check Application Status or Upload More Documents.
Find out how to submit transcripts and supporting documents >
## Checking your application status
Monitor your application status anytime.
Log into your myBCIT account > Student Self Service > Check Application Status or Upload More Documents.
Find out what your application status means >
## Updating your personal information
It’s important to ensure your personal information is current and accurate so you don’t miss important communications from BCIT.
Login to your myBCIT account > Student Self Service > Update Personal Information.
Find step-by-step instructions for updating your information >
## Questions?
Student Information and Enrolment Services is your first point of contact with BCIT.
