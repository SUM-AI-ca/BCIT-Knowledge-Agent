Title: Application Steps
================================================================================

Step 1: Choose your program Browse the range of programs and courses and find a program that fits your interests. Take note of key details, including start dates, application deadlines, and program type (competitive vs. first-qualified, first-offered). Before applying to a Flexible Learning program, you may be eligible to register for individual courses to try out the program. Learn more about Flexible Learning options 

Step 2: Meet the admission requirements Review the admission requirements for your chosen program to make sure you are eligible. Meeting the minimum requirements does not guarantee admission to all programs. While Flexible Learning programs may have more flexible requirements, specific courses or skills may support your success. Check the ‘Entrance Requirements’ section to see what is recommended for success. If you have education from outside of Canada, you may be required to have your credentials assessed by the International Credential Evaluation Service (ICES) to ensure equivalency. Learn more about International Credential Evaluation Services Missing or in-progress requirements? If you have any in-progress courses, you can apply using your midterm grades. Learn how to apply using your midterm grades You also have several options to help you qualify for your chosen program, even if you don’t meet the admission requirements initially. Upgrading courses Assessment testing English Language Proficiency Equivalencies If you don’t meet the published admission requirements, you might still qualify for your chosen program through our alternate admissions pathway. This option values individuals whose work experience, non-formal education, cultural knowledge, and other relevant experiences have prepared them for their chosen program. Learn more about meeting BCIT’s entrance requirements 

Step 3: Prepare your application First-qualified, first-offered programs don’t have posted deadlines to submit applications and can fill quickly. We recommend you apply and submit you supporting documentation when the program application opens. Learn more about application dates 

Step 4: Submit your application online Applications are available through EducationPlannerBC (EPBC). Use the Apply Now or Declare Now button on your program’s page to be directed to the EPBC platform. Apply through EPBC You can select up to two programs within a single application. Your first choice should be your preferred program, a competitive entry or a declaration program. Your second choice is optional and will only be considered if you’re not eligible for your first choice, and only upon request and availability. After you apply through EPBC, you’ll receive an email from BCIT activating your BCIT ID and email account, complete with instructions for accessing your myBCIT account. Learn more about what happens after you apply
