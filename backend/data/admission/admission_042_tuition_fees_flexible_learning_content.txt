## Policy update

Changes to Policy 2300 -Tuition, Fees, and Refunds will take effect in Fall 2026. Information on this page is subject to change.

Whether you plan to declare for a Flexible Learning program or study self-paced, you can find the cost for your course by searching in our online course catalogue. Tuition increases Each year BC post-secondary institutions may raise tuition by 2 per cent. Please see the Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills’ Tuition Limit Policy for more information. Course tuition and fees The cost of a Flexible Learning program is the total of individual course fees. In the event that multiple course sections are offered at different campuses, the total course fees may vary due to various rates assessed for BCIT Student Association Student Centre Building fee, passed through a 2017 BCIT Student Association referendum. ### Books and supplies

The cost of textbooks and study materials is additional to program and other mandatory fees. There are a few exceptions that are outlined in the program or course description. Check the BCIT Bookstore for most books and supplies.

### International health insurance fee

New international students may be charged an International health insurance fee. For details about this fee and the medical insurance requirement for international students, visit the Medical Insurance page for international students.

### Computer hardware and software needs

Although it’s not required for most programs, it is recommended that you possess a personal computer capable of running the current version of MS Office and a web browser, as well as have an account with an Internet Service Provider (ISP) of your choice.

### Incidental fees

View incidental fees that may be incurred as part of the application process or for services at BCIT.

Paying for Flexible Learning courses Fees for Flexible Learning courses must be paid within 10 business days or students may be dropped. Students registering in Flexible Learning courses within 3 weeks of the regular start of term (September, January, and April) must pay their fees at the time of registration. Fees for Flexible Learning courses may be paid using one of the following methods: Debit card (in person only) Cheque* or money order payable to BCIT – please include your BCIT ID (student number) Online banking (bill payment) through your financial institution using BCIT – Tuition as the payee and your BCIT ID (student number) as your account number. Please allow up to 48 hours for online bill payments to be posted to your account. Flywire – Go to bcit-main.flywire.com. Bank wire transfer or Credit Card option available, please note service fee will apply. Letter of sponsorship authorization for invoicing by BCIT *If your cheque has been returned as ‘NSF’ (Non-Sufficient Funds), your registration will be cancelled. You can view and print your receipt through your myBCIT account.
