Please visit this website(https://www.bcit.ca/admission/tuition-fees/refunds-withdrawals/refund-request-form) and note the following important details before you submit your refund request:

Prior to submitting a refund request, ensure you have dropped or withdrawn from your course registration first.
Your account will be reviewed by Financial Services to determine refund eligibility.
If you are not eligible for refund, you will be contacted by Financial Services.
If you are eligible, refunds take approximately six weeks to process, plus mailing time.
Payments received by Flywire will be refunded through Flywire. All other payments will be refunded by cheque and mailed to the address on myBCIT account.
Log in to your account and verify that you have a credit balance available.
Ensure your address is current in your myBCIT account. Include the Unit/Suite #, if applicable. To update or verify your address, please refer to the article Updating myBCIT contact information.
