## Estimate your Full-Time Tuition and Fees

You can view or print the estimate cost for your chosen full-time program using the Tuition Estimator tool; see estimated tuition and fees for the entire program, and broken down by term.

With hands-on apprenticeship training at BCIT, you can offset tuition and related fees because you’re learning while you’re working. Tuition increases Each year BC post-secondary institutions may raise tuition by 2 per cent. In April each year, BCIT posts the new rates that take effect on August 1. If you pay your tuition prior to April using the old rates, you are required to pay the difference within 10 business days from the start of your program. Please see the Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills’ Tuition Limit Policy for more information.

Costs included in 2025-2026 apprentice tuition

### Commitment fee

A non-refundable, non-transferable commitment fee of $200.00 is required upon registration to hold your training seat. Your commitment fee is applied to your tuition.

### Ancillary fees

Ancillary fees are mandatory fees that help fund many campus and student services and are included with your total tuition. These fees are paid by most students so that all students benefit from having access to a broad range of supports and services. For specific fee amounts, see the fee breakdown for your program or course. Ancillary fees are used to finance the following services and facilities. Student Services Supports services that enhance student success and the overall BCIT student experience. Specifically, this fee helps fund services including a broad range of mental health supports including counselling, accessibility, and health services; supports for students at risk or in crisis, including early intervention systems/processes and financial aid programs; and student programming to increase engagement and well-being such as recreation services, peer supports, and orientation and transition activities. The rate is $1.51 per week. Building Supports new construction and/or improvements to the existing physical infrastructure for academic and non-academic student spaces/buildings and informal learning spaces. This fee also supports the associated cost and operations of buildings, spaces, and facilities used by the Institute community. The rate is $0.77 per week. Funds from Government and donations from industry partners and donors contribute significant funding towards new capital projects. Student ID Card Used to generate and maintain student ID cards required by students for integration and connection to campus services, both virtual and in-person. In addition, these cards may be used to gain access to labs and classrooms as well as to verify identification for exams or other testing. The rate is $2.50 per term.

Additional fees not included in tuition

### U-Pass BC

The total tuition cost listed in the full-time tuition schedules does not include the U-Pass BC fee. The U-Pass BC fee will be charged upon registration and is a mandatory fee for all eligible students.

### Books and supplies

The cost of textbooks and study materials is additional to program and other mandatory fees. Check the BCIT Bookstore for required books. Most apprentices are also required to supply their own CSA-approved steel-toed boots, coveralls, eye protection, and other personal protective equipment.

### Incidental fees

View incidental fees that may be incurred as part of the application process or for services at BCIT.

Paying for apprenticeship training Once you register and pay your $200.00 commitment fee, BCIT will reserve your seat in your apprentice training program. Your commitment fee can be paid directly to BCIT by credit card. The balance of your tuition is due, in full, 60 days before the start of your program. Although you can pay your $200 deposit with a credit card, the remaining balance must be paid using one of the following methods: Debit card (in person only) Cheque or money order payable to BCIT – please include your BCIT ID (student number) Online banking (bill payment) through your financial institution using BCIT – Tuition as the payee Flywire – Go to bcit-main.flywire.com. Bank wire transfer or Credit Card option available, please note service fee will apply. If paying by online banking, please use your BCIT ID (student number) as your account number. Please allow up to 3 business days for online bill payments to be posted to your account. Note: If your employer is sponsoring your training costs, have your employer contact BCIT Apprentice Services.
