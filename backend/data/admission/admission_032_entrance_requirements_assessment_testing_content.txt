BCIT Entrance Requirements and Acceptable Tests

### Grade 12 Requirements

English Requirement Acceptable Test at BCIT English Studies 12 or English First Peoples 12 COMM 0015 – English Competency Assessment and COMM 0033 – English Speaking and Listening Assessment or BCIT English Trades Entry Assessment Acceptable for trades programs ONLY Science Requirement Acceptable Test at BCIT Anatomy and Physiology 12 BHSC 0012 – Biology 12 Challenge Exam Chemistry 12 CHEM 0120 – Chemistry 12 Challenge Exam Physics 12 PHYS 0120 – Physics 12 Challenge Exam Math Requirement Acceptable Test at BCIT Pre-Calculus 12 or Foundations of Math 12 MATH 0120 – Math 12 Competency Test BCIT Math Trades Entry Assessment Acceptable for trades programs ONLY

### Grade 11 Requirements

English Requirement Acceptable Test at BCIT English: Grade 11 level COMM 0015 – English Competency Assessment and COMM 0033 – English Speaking and Listening Assessment or BCIT English Trades Entry Assessment Acceptable for trades programs ONLY Science Requirement Acceptable Test at BCIT Life Sciences 11 BCIT test/assessment not currently available for Biology 11 Chemistry 11 CHEM 0110 – Chemistry 11 Challenge Exam To prepare for the exam, consider taking CHEM 0108 – Chemistry 11 Topics Refresher. Physics 11 PHYS 0110 – Physics 11 Challenge Exam Math Requirement Acceptable Test at BCIT Pre-Calculus 11 or Foundations of Math 11 or Workplace Math 11 MATH 0110 – Math 11 Competency Test OPMT 0198 – Business Math Assessment Test Acceptable for School of Business programs ONLY BCIT Math Trades Entry Assessment Acceptable for trades programs ONLY

### Grade 10 Requirements

Math Requirement Acceptable Test at BCIT Foundations and Pre-Calculus Math 10 or Workplace Math 10 BCIT Math Trades Entry Assessment Acceptable for trades programs ONLY
