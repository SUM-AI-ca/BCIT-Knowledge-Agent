## Mixed Registration
The following full-time degree programs require completion of courses that students are responsible for scheduling via Flexible Learning. Students who fail their full-time course and will retake it via Flexible Learning will be subject to additional Flexible Learning course tuition. Flexible Learning course fees are not included in the full-time tuition estimates .
