## High School Applicants
Learn more about BCIT’s application process.
## Application options based on high school location
You may be able to apply with in-progress grades or final grades, depending on your program’s requirements.
Some BCIT programs do not accept in-progress grades. Programs requiring final grades only will include a disclaimer on their Entrance Requirements page. Always review your program’s Entrance Requirements carefully before applying.
### Option 1: i n-progress grades
You may apply with in-progress high school grades if your program allows it.
You must meet one of the following:
A. Grade 11 grades and proof of Grade 12 registration
If you are registered in the required Grade 12 course and are less than halfway through, apply by submitting your report card , official transcript , or diploma verification showing your:
- Final Grade 11 grade and
- Confirmed Grade 12 registration with expected completion date.
Your final grade for the Grade 11 prerequisite course must meet the percentage grade specified in your program’s Entrance Requirements page.
B. Midterm grades
If you are at least halfway through the required Grade 11 or 12 course, you may apply by submitting:
- Your official midterm grade for the required course.
Your midterm grade must meet the percentage stated in your program’s Entrance Requirements page.
Note: Admissions may request your Grade 12 or final grade once available. If you are admitted using in-progress grades and you do not complete the Grade 12 requirement or do not meet the required grade, you must contact Admissions immediately to discuss your options.
### Option 2: final grades
If you have already completed the required Grade 11 and/or 12 course(s), apply by submitting your diploma verification, transcript, or other official academic record.

### Option 1: midterm grades
If you are at least half way through the required Grade 11 or 12 course, apply by submitting your midterm grade * for the required course.
* Note: Your midterm grade must meet the percentage grade specified in your program’s entrance requirements.
### Option 2: final grades
If you have completed the required Grade 11 or 12 course, apply with your final grade for the required course.
Submit an official transcript that includes:
- Name of school and/or examination board
- Address and country of school and/or examination board
- Name of high school graduation credential
- Grade level of each course (e.g. Grade 11, Grade 12, Senior Year 2, Senior Year 3)
- Grading scale and minimum passing grade
Wondering if your course is acceptable? Look up international high school course equivalencies .
## All high school applicants
If you apply with midterm grades or final Grade 11 grades with proof of registration in Grade 12, you may be required to show proof of your final grades. Admissions will contact you if you are selected for a grade audit.
## Missing one or more requirements?
We provide several pa thways to help you qualify for your chosen program, even if you don’t meet the admission requirements initially.
- Upgrading courses : register for courses at BCIT or through your local school district.
- Assessment testing : demonstrate subject proficiency through assessment.
- English language proficiency equivalencies : explore options to satisfy the English language proficiency requirement.
If you don ’ t meet the published admission requirements , you might still qualify for your chosen program through our alternate admissions pathway. This option values individuals whose work experience, non-formal education, cultural knowledge, and other relevant experiences have prepared them for the ir chosen program .
Learn more about alternate admissions.
## Apply with your midterm grades
You may apply to a BCIT program using your midterm grades unless otherwise indicated on the Entrance Requirements page.
