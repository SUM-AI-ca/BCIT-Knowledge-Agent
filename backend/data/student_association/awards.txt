1. Student Initiative Fund (SIF)

What it is

Funding for professional development opportunities.

BCITSA covers 75% of the total cost, up to $500 (reimbursement).

You can get funding for one initiative per academic year.

Who is eligible

You must be currently enrolled at BCIT (full-time or part-time) in the current term.

The initiative must:

Be related to your field of study at BCIT.

Clearly support your professional development.

Funding is only for registration costs (no travel, hotel, etc.).

Examples of eligible initiatives

Industry conferences, webinars, events (in-person or virtual).

Professional networking events.

Industry-related training/learning resources:

e.g., non-credit online courses, coding practice platforms, etc.

Skills like communication, time management, studying tools, teamwork, leadership, etc.

Funding is NOT available for

Competitions, co-ops, or internships.

LinkedIn subscriptions.

Professional memberships / associations / club fees.

Certifications or accreditations (course/exam fees), including First Aid.

Personal hobbies or anything not related to your BCIT program.

Textbooks or course materials.

Courses for credit (BCIT or other institutions, credit or non-credit).

Application & approval process (summary)

Submit application + signed FOI form by 4:00 pm on the intake deadline.

Committee reviews applications the week after the deadline.

You get an email in about 10–12 days:

Approved or not approved (you can re-apply if rejected).

If approved, you must submit within 2 weeks:

T4A info

Bank details / void cheque

Receipt (proof of payment)

They process and reimburse 75% up to $500 by direct deposit.

If your documents are:

Late within same intake → payment delayed to next intake (2+ months delay).

More than 8 weeks late → application expires and no payment.

2. BCIT Student Competition Fund (SCF)

What it is

Funding for student clubs to participate in competitions.

Maximum request: $5,000 or 75% of competition costs, whichever is less.

Max 20 students funded per club.

Max $250 per student.

For non–School of Business clubs (School of Business has its own funding).

What it can cover

Travel expenses (flights, etc.).

Accommodation costs (hotels).

Only up to 50% of flights + hotels.

Registration fees, materials, etc. are mostly the club’s responsibility.

Eligibility basics

You must:

Be a BCIT student (part-time or full-time).

Attend the competition in your attending year(s).

Respect caps:

≤ $250 per student.

≤ 20 students per club.

Not eligible

Funding is NOT for:

Registration fees.

Hosting a competition.

Materials and supplies.

Meals.

Competitions against only BCIT students.

School of Business students/competitions (they apply through their School).

SCF deadlines (same pattern as SIF)

All by 4:00 pm:

Sept 29, 2025

Nov 17, 2025

Jan 19, 2026

Mar 2, 2026

Apr 13, 2026

May 18, 2026

Application is via a Smartsheet form (“Apply here” button).

3. Annual Achievement Awards (summary)

All of these are $500 each, deadline April 20, 2026 by 4:00 pm, and use:

A PDF: “BCITSA Awards Application Process 2025 – read first”

A Smartsheet application form.

3.1 BCITSA Volunteer Award

For students who have:

Volunteered significantly with BCITSA services, events, and initiatives.

Helped build a vibrant, inclusive, engaged campus community.

Two students per year, $500 each.

3.2 Laurie Jack Award

In memory of Laurie Jack, former BCITSA President (1971–72).

For graduating students who:

Have made outstanding contributions to BCIT.

Created a lasting positive impact on student life.

Two students awarded $500 each.

3.3 Distinguished Set Rep Award

For Set Reps who:

Strongly support their peers.

Take initiative to grow and support their set.

Seven awards of $500:

One for each BCIT School.

One for Downtown campus.

One for specialty campuses.

3.4 Distinguished International Student Award

For international students who:

Improved student life for other international students.

Encouraged connection and mutual support.

Two students, $500 each.

All four awards share:

Application Deadline: April 20, 2026, by 4:00 pm

Buttons:

“Application Process – read first” (PDF)

“Application Form” (Smartsheet)
