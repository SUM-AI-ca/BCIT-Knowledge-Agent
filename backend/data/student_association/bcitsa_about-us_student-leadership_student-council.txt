### Recruitment
## Become a Councillor Today!
The Student Council, made up of the Executive Board and Councillors, is responsible for overseeing the Student Association and advocating for students. Set Reps help bring forward student ideas and concerns. These students contribute their time and effort towards improving campus life for their peers and moving our organization forward.
Want to learn more about what they are doing for students? Find out about upcoming Council meetings and read about what occurred during past meetings here
## How to Become a Councillor
Along with having the desire to improve student life at BCIT, those interested in becoming a Councillor must follow these steps.
### Step
## 1
### Submit an application form
Applicants must fill out and send the application form below.
### Step
## 2
### Have application reviewed
The Chairs review applications and reach out to candidates for interviews.
### Step
## 3
### Get notified
Applicants are notified if they’ve been selected.
### Step
## 4
### Attend training
New councillors attend a training session (end of September).

### Council Meetings – Open Forum Rules
BCITSA Council has established new guidelines for Open Forum to ensure meetings remain focused on relevant student issues while respecting the legislative process. These rules prioritise member participation and provide a structured three-minute window for speakers to address matters within Council’s jurisdiction.
