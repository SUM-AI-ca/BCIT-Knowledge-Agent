BCITSA HEALTH & DENTAL PLAN

The BCITSA Extended Health & Dental Plan provides protection and security to minimize the effect of injuries or ailments during your studies.

Automatic enrollment:
You are automatically enrolled in the plan if:
• Your program is designated full-time by BCIT, and
• Your program lasts at least 16 weeks.

Fees:
If you are eligible for coverage, the SA Health fee is automatically included in your tuition at the time of registration.

Referendum and requirement:
The health and dental plans were approved by student referendum.
As a result, they are now a requirement of your enrolment at BCIT through your membership in the Student Association.

For more specific details, students are directed to continue reading the sections below or visit the BCITSA Student Plans website.

DENTAL SERVICES – HEALTHY SMILE

On-campus dental care for eligible students:

On-campus dental hygienist services covered by the student healthcare plan (for students in the BCIT Student Health & Dental Plan).

Location: Student Association Centre (SE2), in the heart of campus.

Purpose:
To help students maintain a bright, healthy smile, useful for things like job interviews and presentations, and to boost self-confidence.

Booking:

You can book an appointment online, or

Visit the Student Association Centre (SE2, 2nd Floor).

BCIT STUDENT HEALTH SERVICES

BCIT Student Health Services provides confidential medical services to:

All BCIT students (full-time and part-time).

Important notes:

Walk-ins are NOT allowed.

Entry is by appointment only.

Students are asked not to come to campus if they are sick or required to self-isolate.

Appointments:

Book an appointment online, or

Call the clinic: 604-432-8608

Appointment type:

In-person, or

Virtual (phone or video), depending on the medical issue.
