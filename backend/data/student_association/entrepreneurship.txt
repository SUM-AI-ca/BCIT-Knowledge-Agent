Title: Entrepreneurship
================================================================================

BCITSA Entrepreneurship Overview
BCITSA Entrepreneurship is a service provided by the BCIT Student Association to support students and alumni interested in starting or growing a business. The initiative focuses on developing an entrepreneurial mindset, helping individuals explore and test business ideas, and connecting them with relevant resources, programs, and communities on and off campus. It is open to BCIT students and graduates from any program or background.

Location and Hours
The physical home of BCITSA Entrepreneurship is the Summit Centre at BCIT’s Burnaby Campus. It is located in SE14 (Library), rooms 212 and 214, at 3700 Willingdon Ave, Burnaby, BC. Typical office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 4:00 pm. The space is designed as a hub where students can learn about entrepreneurship, access support, and connect with staff or mentors.

1-on-1 Appointments (Virtual and In-Person)
BCITSA Entrepreneurship offers 1-on-1 appointments for students and alumni who want personalized guidance about starting or growing a business. In these meetings, staff help individuals understand available programs, supports, and community resources related to entrepreneurship, and guide them toward next steps. Appointments can be booked virtually or in person at the Burnaby campus through the online booking links provided on the website.

Online Startup Training Program
The Online Startup Training program is a self-paced training resource that helps participants move from an initial idea to a validated business model. It incorporates lean startup principles and uses digital tools and templates to structure the learning process. Participants learn how to test assumptions, refine their value proposition, and understand what is needed to launch or grow a venture. The training is designed for flexibility so students can work through it on their own schedule.

Business Plan Review Support
BCITSA Entrepreneurship provides a Business Plan Review service for students and alumni who are developing a business plan. Participants can receive feedback on the structure, clarity, and feasibility of their plan, regardless of whether they are just starting or already have a draft. The service helps improve the quality of the business plan and prepares individuals for funding applications, pitches, or launching their business. Access is requested through an online form linked from the website.

Mentorship Program (Mentees)
The Entrepreneurship Mentorship Program matches BCIT students and alumni who are starting or growing a business with experienced mentors. The program typically runs for about five months and is mentee-driven: mentees set goals, schedule meetings, and prepare agendas. Mentors provide guidance, share experience, and help mentees work through challenges related to their venture. Meetings can take place virtually, by phone, or in person, and there is no cost for BCIT students and alumni to participate.

Mentorship Program (Mentors)
Professionals and experienced entrepreneurs can apply to serve as mentors in the BCITSA Entrepreneurship Mentorship Program. Mentors commit to meeting with their mentee regularly over the program period to provide support, ask critical questions, and share insights from their own business or industry experience. The program emphasizes a collaborative relationship where mentors help mentees clarify their goals and make informed decisions about their ventures. Interested mentors can learn more and apply through the dedicated mentor information page linked from the site.

Newsletter and Communications
BCITSA Entrepreneurship publishes a regular newsletter for students and alumni interested in entrepreneurship. The newsletter shares information about upcoming events, workshops, competitions, funding opportunities, and other resources relevant to starting or growing a business. Individuals can sign up for the newsletter via a subscription form linked on the BCITSA website. The goal is to keep the entrepreneurial community informed and connected to new opportunities.

Resources and External Connections
The BCITSA Entrepreneurship website includes a Resources section that curates links and tools for different stages of the entrepreneurial journey. These resources may include templates, guides, funding information, and connections to external organizations that support entrepreneurs. The aim is to help students and alumni quickly find reliable information and tools relevant to idea development, validation, planning, and growth. The resources complement BCITSA’s other programs such as training, mentorship, and advising.

Peak Leadership and Related Programs
BCITSA Career Services also offers the Peak Leadership program, which is highlighted alongside entrepreneurship resources. Peak Leadership is designed to develop leadership skills, teamwork, and professional competencies that are valuable in both employment and entrepreneurship. Participants learn from industry professionals and gain experience that supports their career and business goals. The Entrepreneurship page directs students to a separate information page where they can learn more and apply.

Contact Person: Entrepreneurship Manager
The Entrepreneurship initiative is managed by an Entrepreneurship Manager at BCITSA. The manager’s role includes overseeing programs, supporting students and alumni, and building connections with community partners and mentors. The website provides direct contact information (email and phone number) for the manager, allowing interested individuals to reach out with questions about programs, eligibility, or how to get involved. The manager has a background in social enterprise, community economic development, and startup support.
