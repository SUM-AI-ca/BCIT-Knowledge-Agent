CAMPUS PRINT & COPY CENTRE

The Campus Print & Copy Centre has a team of three dedicated print and copy professionals available during business hours to assist with all print needs.

To place an order:

Email your files to: print@bcitsa.ca

In your email, include:
• A PDF file of your document
• Instructions on how you want your file printed and finished
• The number of copies you need
• The time you would like to pick up your project
• Any other relevant details

If the print team requires more information, they will contact you directly.

The Campus Print & Copy Centre offers a wide range of services to support student projects on campus. Whether you are printing large or small projects, you can expect high-quality results and fast turnaround times. Many projects can be completed on the spot.

Services include:

Large and small format printed projects

Booklets, pamphlets, and magazines

Pocket-size study guides

Business cards

Capstone project printing (templates available under the Templates tab)

Drafting and drawings (CAD drawings must be saved as PDF)

Project finishing (folding, cutting, etc.)

Binding (Spiral, Cerlox, and Coil)

Laminating

STUDENT BUSINESS CARDS

The Campus Print & Copy Centre offers official BCIT student business cards, ideal for making a strong first impression at events.

To order:

Fill out the student business card order form.

Include:
• The number of cards you need
• The correct spelling of your name
• The template you would like to use

If there are any questions about your order, the team will contact you.

Pricing:

36 Business Cards: $5.00 + tax

60 Business Cards: $8.00 + tax

Business Card Holder: $10.00 + tax

120 Business Cards & BCIT Business Card Holder: $17.50 + tax

240 Business Cards & BCIT Business Card Holder: $27.50 + tax

IMPORTANT INFORMATION (FILE PREPARATION)

Convert your files to PDF to ensure the formatting of your work is preserved
(various free programs are available online to create PDFs).

Include a half inch margin on all sides if printing files larger than 12" x 18".

CAD files should:
• Be in greyscale
• Be exported to PDF
