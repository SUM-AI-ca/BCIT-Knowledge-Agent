## Become a Student Leader at BCIT!
As a Set Rep , you’ll advocate for your classmates’ needs. Join the Student Council to represent fellow students regarding campus-wide decisions. Or, take on a leadership role as a Student Executive and play a pivotal part in shaping the future of the BCIT Student Association.
Step out of your comfort zone—gain valuable leadership experience, build your network, and contribute to an inclusive campus community.
