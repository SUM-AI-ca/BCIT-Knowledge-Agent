### Student Medical Plan
You will be automatically enrolled in the plan if your program is designated full-time by BCIT and it lasts at least 16 weeks. Fees for the plan are automatically included by the institution with registration. If you are unsure about whether you are eligible, please visit or contact the BCITSA Health & Dental Plan Office (in building SE2). It is also mandatory for you to have basic medical coverage such as a BC Care Card (MSP/Services Card/Personal Health Number), Student Guard (Guard Me), or comparable coverage for your country or province of origin. Learn more about your health and dental plan here (Link to health plan page)

### U-Pass
U-Pass BC is available to students who meet all of the following criteria:
- Accepted into and remain declared in eligible full-time studies programs.
- Registered into courses which are delivered in Metro Vancouver.
- Registered into a minimum of (3.0) credits or whose tuition exceeds $170.00 per month of study.
- Assessed a Student Association Fee.

## Breakdown of Expenses
### Services & Governance
51.2%
### Administration
48.8%
*based on 2022 financials
*Administration includes all Accounting, HR, Marketing and Communications, and other staff support and expenses related to our Retail Operations, Pub, Campus Print and copy, and all support related to Services and Governance.
### Resource Allocation
Student Fees cover 83% of the BCITSA administrative and services expenses (based on 2017 financials). The remaining expenses are covered by revenues from other sources. What this means is that for every dollar we spend on our administration and services, we only need to collect $0.83 in student fees. Our retail, pub, print shop, and sponsorship revenues make up the difference. Please review our Annual Management Report and Audited Financial Statements for further information.
