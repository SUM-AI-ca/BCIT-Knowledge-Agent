## About Test Centres

Welcome to the BCIT Test Centre, your comprehensive solution for a wide range of invigilation services. Our Test Centre offers a secure, accessible, and invigilated testing experience. With our modern facilities, including private rooms with comfortable setups, video camera monitoring systems, privacy screens on all monitors, and secure cell phone lockers, we prioritize your comfort and confidentiality. We are a proud member of Canadian Higher Education Testing Association (CHETA) and the British Columbia Higher Education Testing Association (BCHETA).
