## Non-BCIT Individuals
At the BCIT Test Centres you can book a proctored exam that requires examination or invigilation for a course, application, or certification.
## Test Centre locations and registration process
The Test Centres at BCIT offers assessment services at various times and locations throughout the Greater Vancouver Area. .

- Please schedule your exam through our online registration system, where you will be able to choose your preferred testing location ( Downtown Campus or Burnaby Campus ), date, and time.
- Have your home institution’s contact information (name, email, and phone #) ready before registering. If your home institution requires your exam to be returned by courier, and they do not cover the costs, additional fees will apply based on the courier destination. You will be expected to make payment for courier fees on the date of your exam.
- To complete your online registration process, you are required to pay an exam invigilation fee to reserve your seat. This fee is non-refundable, non-transferable, and no change of date is permitted. Payment must be made via MasterCard or VISA only. Visa Debit and Amex is not accepted. The invigilation fee for exams that are for 3-hours or less is $65 + GST. For exams that are over 3-hours, there is an additional charge of $15 per hour (or part thereof) + GST.
Important message: Effective April 1, 2026, examination fees for non-BCIT students will increase to $75.00 plus GST for exams lasting three hours or less. Exams exceeding three hours will incur additional fees.
- Once you have booked your exam, you will receive a confirmation email with the details of your exam session. This includes proctor information, Test Centre location, and the date and time of your exam booking.
## Exam day guidelines
Exam booking is a crucial step in preparing for your upcoming exams. It’s essential to familiarize yourself with the process and gather all the necessary information.
