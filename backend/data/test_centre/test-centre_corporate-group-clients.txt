## Registration process
Contact BCIT Test Centres by phone or email to book an exam session for your organization. The information that you will need to provide is:
- Number of participants Required testing delivery format Additional assessment details, such as required date, time, testing delivery format, specific instructions and permitted materials allowed during the exam should be communicated to the Test Centre as early as possible, in accordance with our change/cancellation policy as outlined in your signed booking confirmation
- Review our Assessment Services Fee Schedule
- If the group requires a full room or private accommodation, then it is the group rate, not the individual rate. Please contact our test centre coordinators by phone or email to access customizable rates
- For special cases like multi-campus exams, off-hours, please contact Nadia Rusan, Test Centre supervisor, at 604-412-7537 or nrusan@bcit.ca
- After scheduling a meeting, you will receive a confirmation email from our Assessment Administrator specifying all the details and all additional information you should know for the exam session.
Exam taker lists are shared only with necessary Test Centre staff involved in the invigilation of an exam. Any exam follow-up is promptly provided to institution-designated individuals. Our staff can coordinate certain accommodations upon registration.
