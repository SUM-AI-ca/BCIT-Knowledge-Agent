## Test Centre Locations

Meet our eight individual Test Centres conveniently located throughout the Greater Vancouver Area at four campuses:

## The Burnaby Test Centres offer:

- Four test centres, with a total of 113 student computers
- Privacy screens on all monitors
- Audio monitoring system
- Video monitoring and recording system
- Computer monitoring software (NetSupport School)
- Soundproof offices
- Sit/Stand desks – one per Test Centre
- Corporate/group client testing

## Parking information:

Parking is available at the Annacis Island Campus at no charge on a first come, first served basis. Overflow parking is on the street.

Exam fees are non-refundable and non-transferable. Once you book an exam, you cannot change the time, date, or test centre. BCIT Test Centres do not transfer exams between test centres. If you miss an exam, it will be returned to your home institution. To reschedule due to medical reasons, contact the BCIT Test Centre invigilation office and provide necessary documentation.
