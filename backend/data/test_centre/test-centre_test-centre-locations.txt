## Test Centre Locations
Meet our eight individual Test Centres conveniently located throughout the Greater Vancouver Area at four campuses:
## The Burnaby Test Centres offer:
- Four test centres, with a total of 113 student computers
- Privacy screens on all monitors
- Audio monitoring system
- Video monitoring and recording system
- Computer monitoring software (NetSupport School)
- Soundproof offices
- Sit/Stand desks – one per Test Centre
- Corporate/group client testing
British Columbia Institute of Technology NE1, room 292, 291, 290 , and NW6, room 205 3700 Willingdon Avenue Burnaby, BC V5G 3H2
Phone: (604) 456-1186 Email: bbytestctr@bcit.ca
- View Map
- Translink
- Monday to Friday: 7:30 am to 2:00 pm
- Hours vary at each Test Centre location at Burnaby Campus, contact us for further information.
For parking information for all campuses please see Student and Visitor parking information .
Please be aware that you will be considered a “student” as well as a “visitor” in terms of parking at BCIT.
- In Student Parking lots daily rates apply. In Visitors Parking lots hourly rates apply.
- Permits must be purchased on site at the ticket kiosks.
Please refer to the Burnaby campus map [PDF] for valid Burnaby Campus parking locations.
TIP: At certain times of day, especially mornings, some of the Burnaby Campus parking lots are full. Always allow additional time to find and pay for parking prior to your scheduled exam time.

Parking is available at the Annacis Island Campus at no charge on a first come, first served basis. Overflow parking is on the street.
Exam fees are non-refundable and non-transferable. Once you book an exam, you cannot change the time, date, or test centre. BCIT Test Centres do not transfer exams between test centres. If you miss an exam, it will be returned to your home institution. To reschedule due to medical reasons, contact the BCIT Test Centre invigilation office and provide necessary documentation.
