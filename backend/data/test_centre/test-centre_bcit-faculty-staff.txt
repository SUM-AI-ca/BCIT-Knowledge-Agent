## BCIT Faculty & Staff
Our Test Centres support BCIT faculty and staff with streamlined processes, boosting efficiency, and embracing convenience with our user-friendly platform. We provide invigilation and testing services that are integrated with the Learning Hub, offering you a seamless experience.

Please let us know if you need further assistance with booking exam sessions for your students.
- Approval process for full-time trades If this is the first time your program will use the Test Centre, the program head should contact the Test Centre supervisor for approval. If your program level has used the Test Centre before, you don’t need to go through the approval process again. Contact your assessment administrator .
- Schedule your exam session . Once your exam session is approved by the Test Centre supervisor, you may schedule your exam sessions by contacting the assessment administrator of the Test Centre you have been assigned to.
## Exam day considerations
Familiarize yourself with our exam day considerations to properly prepare the upcoming session for your students. We are here to support you every step of the way.
