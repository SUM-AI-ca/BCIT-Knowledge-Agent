## Media Inquiries
BCIT’s Marketing and Communications department specializes in telling the BCIT story . We are the providers of strategic and creative marketing and communications solutions for the BCIT community, across various mediums and platforms.
## Contact
On a deadline or seeking a subject matter expert?
The BCIT Media Relations team is here to help. We’ll work with you to find a suitable expert or come up with a story that fits your assignment and deadline. We might even be able to find the perfect image or b-roll to complete your story. Our experts at BCIT are available to comment on current events, issues, and a wide range of topics such as those related to:
- Aerospace Computing Cybersecurity Engineering Food technology Forensic sciences Health sciences Indigenous education Innovation and technology in education Legion Military Skills Conversion program Railway STEM education Sustainability (across many industries) Tourism Trades Wildlife and environmental conservation Work-integrated learning
Contact Amy Chen , Media & Communications Specialist, or Jocelyne Leszczynski , Communications Manager.
BCIT has a variety of publications available to the public, including course and program catalogues, our Viewbook, Strategic Plan, blogs, statistical research and more.
