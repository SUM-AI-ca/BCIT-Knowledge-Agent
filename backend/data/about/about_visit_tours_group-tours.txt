## Group Tours

Counsellors, teachers and organizations can easily bring their students to the BCIT campus on a group tour. Tours are a great way for your students to learn about BCIT’s diverse educational offerings and its unique path of learning.

Thank you for your interest in BCIT’s campus tours. We will try to respond to your request within 3 business days.
