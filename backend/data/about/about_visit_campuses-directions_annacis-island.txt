## Annacis Island Campus
BCIT’s Annacis Island Campus is located in Delta. The 142,000-square-foot facility is home to our motive power programs. These programs train heavy-duty mechanics, transport trailer mechanics, diesel mechanics, commercial transportation mechanics, railway conductors and forklift operators.
