## Our Impact
## Message from BCIT
At BCIT, we connect learners, industry, and communities to create impact that extends beyond classrooms, shops, and labs. With our provincial mandate to deliver applied education for workforce development, BCIT is preparing British Columbians for meaningful careers that drive prosperity while building sustainable and resilient communities.
Our partnerships with industry, alumni, researchers, First Nations, and government strengthen innovation and academic excellence, expand diverse pathways to education, and help eliminate systemic barriers for equity-deserving groups. Through the new BCIT Strategic Plan 2025–2030: Future Ready: Student Success for a Complex World , we continue to empower people, shape BC, and inspire global progress. Together, we are shaping a future where everyone can flourish and lead with purpose.
Michael Bonshor (Gixkan’dzi) , Chair, Board of Governors, BCIT Dr. Jeff Zabudsky , President, BCIT

## Awards
We are proud of our BCIT students, faculty, and staff on all their success this year. This is a snapshot of some of the high-profile, external recognition earned in the past year.
### Advancing life sciences in BC
BCIT and CASTL, alongside provincial and federal partners, are revolutionizing life sciences training in BC through the first-of-its-kind BC Biomanufacturing Training Facility.
### A sustainable community
The BCIT South Campus Infrastructure Renewal is transforming public spaces and improving accessibility to create a vibrant, inclusive, sustainable, and climate-resilient campus environment.
### Soaring high with aviation training
The arrival of two state-of-the-art flight simulators provides students with immersive and safe hands-on training that mimics real-world aviation scenarios and settings.
### Creativity takes the lead
The newly launched Bachelor of Creative Industries is the first interdisciplinary program of its kind in Western Canada that integrates knowledge and skillsets in technology, creativity, and business.
### “BCIT is a vital source of real-world ready graduates that has supported our company’s growth for many years.”
