The largest BCIT campus is home to hundreds of specialized learning spaces, including classrooms, shops, labs, simulators, broadcast studios, green roofs, energy grids, forests, and waterways.

## Getting here
The BCIT Burnaby Campus is located just off the Trans-Canada Highway at the corner of Willingdon Avenue and Canada Way:
3700 Willingdon Avenue Burnaby, BC V5G 3H2

## Parking
There are five visitor parking lots on the Burnaby Campus, offering 24/7 paid parking.

## Getting around the Burnaby Campus
Find buildings, campus services, food outlets and accessible routes around campus using the interactive Burnaby campus map
The BCIT Burnaby Campus is split up into four quadrants:
- Northwest (NW) Quadrant
- Northeast (NE) Quadrant
- Southwest (SW) Quadrant
- Southeast (SE) Quadrant
Building names start with two letters to indicate their quadrant location (NW, NE, SW, or SE), followed by a number.
Office hours vary for buildings across campus, so please check with your program before visiting.
