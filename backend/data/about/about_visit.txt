## Visit Us

Get to know BCIT by visiting our Burnaby campus, spending a day shadowing a student, or attending an information session related to your program of interest.

## Information sessions

Information sessions are a simple way to figure out the next step along your career path. Learn more about the programs that interest you.

Learn more about information sessions


Big Info


Big Info is the largest program expo and information session at BCIT. It’s your chance to find out about all our programs – from business, computing, and health to engineering, trades, and applied sciences.

Learn more about Big Info.

## Campus tours

Tours run weekdays September to May, on our Burnaby campus. See BCIT in person and get a taste of campus life.

Learn more and book a tour

## Spend a Day visits

Sometimes it’s hard to imagine your life as a student. You can experience it firsthand, by spending a half-day shadowing a current student. You’ll see inside classrooms, workshops, labs and see for yourself what it’s like to interact with our faculty and instructors.

Learn more about Spend a Day
