## Campus tours update

The BCIT Campus Tours program is available on Monday, Wednesday and Friday from September 15th, 2025 until December 5, 2025. If you have questions, please email tours@bcit.ca

## Book a campus tour

If you are a prospective student or visitor who is interested in learning more about student life at BCIT, we invite you to join us for a guided tour of our Burnaby Campus. Our student-led one-hour campus tour, will introduce you to our various campus spaces and give you the inside scoop on both the academic and social sides of life at BCIT.

Campus tours are offered at select times throughout the year, excluding statutory holidays. Reservations are required for all in-person tours.

Personal tours are provided for individuals and families, while group tours are provided for community or school groups (25 people max per tour group).

What to expect


At this time we are currently offering our general Technology Tour in-person at the Burnaby Campus. This tour covers the south side of the Burnaby Campus and is perfect for anyone who would like a general overview of what we have to offer. You’ll visit many key areas on campus such as Advising and Admissions, the book store, fitness centre, Health Services, library, and the Student Association building. Please note that we do not offer campus tours at any of our other campuses.


Already accepted at BCIT?
