## Big Info
Big Info
Big Info is the largest program expo and information session at BCIT. It’s your chance to find out about all our programs – from business and media, computing and IT, engineering, health sciences, trades and apprenticeships, and applied and natural sciences. It’s free. It’s lively. It’s a night of possibilities. Join us in person on Wednesday, October 14th, 2026 from 4:00pm – 7:00pm at our Burnaby campus.
