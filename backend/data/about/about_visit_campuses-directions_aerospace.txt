## Aerospace Technology Campus
Our state-of-the-art 285,000-square-foot Aerospace Technology Campus is home to BCIT’s aerospace programs. The campus is centred on a 40,000-square-foot hangar housing BCIT’s fleet of light piston, turboprop, corporate, and jet transport aircraft, including light and medium helicopters.
