## Spend a Day
## Be a BCIT student for a day
Spend a day shadowing a BCIT student. Pair up with a current student to see what a normal day is really like. You’ll experience the campus, sit in on lectures and labs, and get your questions answered by an expert in BCIT life: a real student! Or join a program on project day to find out how the skills come together in real-world results.
Spend a Day visits require at least one full week’s notice. Dates may vary, depending on program availability.
