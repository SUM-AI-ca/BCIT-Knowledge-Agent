## Be a BCIT student for a day

Spend a day shadowing a BCIT student. Pair up with a current student to see what a normal day is really like. You’ll experience the campus, sit in on lectures and labs, and get your questions answered by an expert in BCIT life: a real student!

Spend a Day visits require one full week’s notice. Dates may vary, depending on program availability.

## Participating Programs

So you can get the most out of your experience, all Spend a Day visits are tailored to a specific BCIT program. Select a program from the list below to find out more and choose the focus of your visit.
