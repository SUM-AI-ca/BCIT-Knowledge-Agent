## Policies
Policies are developed to support BCIT’s mandated goal of providing quality applied education. All policies are approved by either the Board of Governors or the Education Council. All policies are provided as PDFs.
BCIT policies are reviewed in accordance with BCIT Policy 1000: Policy Development and Maintenance (PDF) .
Questions? Contact the Policy Management Office .
