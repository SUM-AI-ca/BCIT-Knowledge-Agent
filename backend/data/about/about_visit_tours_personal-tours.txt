## Personal Tours

Led by our experienced Student Ambassadors, personal tours are provided for individuals and families interested in learning more about the day in the life of a student at BCIT.

Please note: due to limited our availability, individual tours may be combined with other groups.

Thank you for your interest in campus tours. We will try to respond to your request within 3 business days.
