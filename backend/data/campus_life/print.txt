The BCITSA Campus Print & Copy Centre is here for you. Our team of three dedicated, talented, and helpful print specialists strive to provide quality products at a student-friendly price. With all our team members being graduates of BCIT, we can provide great advice on how to turn your hard work into a beautifully printed project. Campus Print & Copy provides a variety of paper options, and we ensure we recycle all paper and printing products.

Let us bring your project to life!

We offer both in-person and self-serve print and copy options for you. No matter where you are, you can email your project to our staff and visit our centre in SE2 to pick it up in 24-hours. If you are on campus, you can stop by any of our self-serve stations to print your project immediately or come talk about your options with our dedicated printing team.

Email your files to print@bcitsa.ca (mailto:print@bcitsa.ca)

How to order:

Simply click the button below, create a Canvas account and you are on your way to adding your photos to a prebuilt template. We will contact you when it is printed and ready for pickup.

We have a team of three dedicated print & copy professionals who are available during business hours to assist with all print needs. Simply email your files to print@bcitsa.ca (mailto:print@bcitsa.ca). In your email, provide the print team with a PDF file, information about how you want your file printed and finished, and how many copies you need. Also, let them know what time you would like to pick the project up and any other relevant details. If our team needs more information, they will contact you directly.

The Campus Print & Copy Centre offers a wide range of services to meet the needs of student projects on campus. Whether you are printing large or small projects, you can be sure you will receive high-quality pieces that will make your reports shine. We have a fast turnaround time and are often able to get your projects finished right on the spot. Have a look at some of our services below:

• Large and small format printed projects
• Booklets, pamphlets, & magazines
• Pocket-size study guides
• Business cards
• Capstone project printing (check out our templates tab for downloadable templates)
• Drafting & drawings* (ensure you save your CAD drawings as PDF)
• Project finishing (folding, cutting, etc.)
• Binding (Spiral, Cerlox, & Coil)
• Laminating
