## Advertise With Us

The BCITSA Career Services offers opportunities for student engagement through both print & digital placements. Connect your brand identity to student culture, attract emerging talent to your team, and engage students at high-traffic locations around campus.

STUDENT AGENDA

Our annual student Agenda is distributed at 50+ locations across all 5 BCIT campuses. The agenda is a key resource free to all students on and off-campus. There are opportunities for strategic engagement through coupons, discount codes, and targeted placement.

9,000 copies printed annually | Year-long advertisement

LINK MAGAZINE

Link is the only monthly student publication on campus. Content is produced by student writers, designers, and artists, for the BCIT community and beyond. We produce 8 issues each year and distribute to 50+ locations across all 5 BCIT campus.

8000 copies printed annually | September to May advertisement

linkbcit.ca (http://linkbcit.ca)

CAREER WORKBOOK

The Career Workbook is an invaluable resource for BCIT students and alumni in their career journey. The workbook is used throughout all of our 100+ skill-building workshops. This is your opportunity to advertise to career-driven and job-seeking students and alumni.

700+ copies printed annually | Year-long advertisement

DIGITAL SCREENS

Our 5 digital screens offer key advertising in high-traffic locations at BCIT Burnaby campus. This is an impactful way to maximize your reach on campus, as our screens run 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Let us customize a special campaign that meets your timelines.
