## Explore a Culinary Delight on Campus!
Making sure students have opportunities to grab food on campus is one of our top priorities as the Student Association of BCIT. That is why we offer a variety of meal choices and convenient bites on campus. By doing so, we can ensure all your needs are met as you spend time at BCIT.
Explore specialty and seasonal drinks from our Stand Stores. Pick up a latte or baked goods before class for an extra kick of energy. Or, treat yourself to a delicious meal at Habitat Pub. Our diverse culinary offerings ensure that your unique taste preferences are catered to flawlessly.
