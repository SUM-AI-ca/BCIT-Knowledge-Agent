## Page Content

### About Us

BCIT Student Association Catering is an outstanding culinary experience that not only tantalizes your taste buds but also plays a pivotal role in advancing the overall student experience at BCIT. Whether you find yourself at BCIT for a brief meeting, an intensive workshop, or a week-long conference, our Catering Services stand ready to cater to your culinary desires.

We take pride in crafting a diverse and delectable menu that suits every palate and dietary preference. From energizing breakfast options that kickstart your day to mouthwatering lunches that keep you fueled and focused, our catering team meticulously prepares each dish with care and precision. We use fresh, locally sourced ingredients whenever possible to ensure that every meal is a delightful and sustainable experience.

### Enhancing Student Life!

### Our Commitment

Our commitment to quality doesn’t stop at the food itself. Our dedicated staff is passionate about delivering exceptional service, ensuring that your dining experience is seamless and memorable. Whether you require a small coffee break during a meeting or a grand banquet for a special event, our Catering Services team is here to make it happen, down to the finest detail.

### What to expect

When you choose catering services from the BCIT Student Association, you can be confident that you’ll receive a top-notch culinary experience tailored specifically to your needs. Our diverse menu offers a glimpse of the extensive options available through BCITSA Catering:

- Fresh Fruit and Vegetable Platters: Vibrant, crisp, and colourful assortments of seasonal produce that tantalize your taste buds.
- Assorted Lunch Items (Sandwiches & Wraps): A delightful variety of sandwiches and wraps to satisfy your midday cravings.
- Full Breakfast (Eggs, Sausage, Hash Browns): A hearty morning feast featuring classic breakfast favourites like eggs, sausage, and crispy hash browns.
- Fresh Pastries: Flaky, buttery, and mouthwatering pastries that add a touch of sweetness to any event.
- Dips & Spreads (Hummus, Bruschetta, Tzatziki, etc.): Flavorful dips and spreads that elevate your appetizer game.
- Salads, Pastas, and Side Dishes: A selection of salads, pasta dishes, and side options that complement your main course perfectly.
- Tantalizing Main Dishes (Chicken, Beef, Pork, Fish): Delectable main course options, including succulent chicken, savoury beef, tender pork, and fresh fish.
- Delectable Desserts, Coffee & Tea: A sweet finale to your meal with an array of mouthwatering desserts, accompanied by freshly brewed coffee and tea.

Reach out to us today, and let our experienced catering team help you plan and execute your special event with exceptional attention to detail and a commitment to culinary excellence. Your event deserves the best, and BCITSA Catering is here to deliver just that.
