### Who We Are!
## Your Campus Print & Copy Centre
At the BCITSA Campus Print & Copy Centre, our small but mighty team is dedicated to bringing your projects to life. As a fellow BCIT graduate, our print specialist offers quality products at student-friendly prices and expert advice for all your print needs, from large formats and booklets to business cards and pocket-size study guides. We prioritize sustainability by recycling all paper and printing products.
For your convenience, we offer both in-person and self-serve options with fast turnaround. Simply email your PDF files to print@bcitsa.ca with your specifications, quantity, and desired pickup time; your project will be ready at our SE2 centre, often the same day. Whether you need immediate printing at our self-serve stations, personalized assistance for Capstone projects (check our templates tab!), or finishing services like binding and laminating, we’re here to ensure your reports shine.
