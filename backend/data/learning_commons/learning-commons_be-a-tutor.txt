## Be a Tutor


Peer tutors


Peer tutors are high-achieving, upper-level BCIT students. They help other students understand course concepts and develop learning skills in difficult academic subjects.

If you are interested, review the documents below.

Inquire at LearningCommons@bcit.ca

Peer Tutor Job Posting [PDF] Peer Tutor Application Process [PDF] Application Form Link Instructor Referral Link Frequently Asked Questions [PDF]


Writing Tutors


Writing tutors work one-on-one with students to help them develop the capacity to plan, organize, write and revise various types of assignments and assist in areas such as correct and concise language and citations.

If you are interested, review the documents below.

Inquire at LearningCommons@bcit.ca

Writing Tutor Job Posting [PDF] Writing Tutor Application Process [PDF] Application Form Link Instructor Referral Link

To apply for both content and writing peer tutoring you need only submit one application package (the peer tutor package), but also include a referral from a Communication instructor.
