## About Us
The Learning Commons helps students succeed at BCIT by offering drop-in peer tutoring and Writing Centre services, asynchronous online writing help through WriteAway, and individualized learning skills support from a Learning Strategist. We are a service provided by the BCIT Library.
If you have questions or feedback, email us at learningcommons@bcit.ca .
