## Writing tutors
Drop in with a writing tutor to get help with your course-related writing. Tutors can’t proofread or edit your work, but can answer questions about all stages of writing: pre-planning, drafting, and proofing.
Writing tutors can provide guidance and feedback on:
- Business and technical writing – proposals, recommendation reports, user manuals, letters, memos
- Academic writing – essays, research papers, thesis statements
- Grammar and punctuation
- Style and format – main ideas, overall structure, paragraphing, transitions and flow; conciseness, specificity, clarity, reader access
- Citation – APA, MLA, avoiding plagiarism
Tutors do not help with:
- Writing that is not course-related (e.g. resumes, cover letters, grad school applications)
- Proofreading or editing
- Commenting on grades

## WriteAway
WriteAway is free online writing help available to all BCIT students.
Submit a draft of your assignment and get feedback from qualified tutors at participating post-secondary schools across BC. Response time during the semester is 24-48 hours.
