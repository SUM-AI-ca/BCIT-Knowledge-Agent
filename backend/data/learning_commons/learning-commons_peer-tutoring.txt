## Peer Tutoring
For Trades math and physics, please drop in with our engineering tutors .
Peer tutors are current students who have strong grades and understand the pressures and challenges of being a student at BCIT. They can help you review and apply course concepts and share learning strategies that have helped them succeed.
Peer tutoring is free and available to all BCIT students.

### Online tutoring
Online peer tutoring sessions are on the Learning Hub and use Class for Teams.
Chrome or Edge browsers are recommended on all devices. Please check the system and browser requirements and test your microphone and/or camera ahead of time.
- Log into the Learning Hub .
- Scroll down to My Courses and find BCIT Peer Tutoring .
- In the navigation bar, click on Activities and choose Class for Teams .
- Click on the program name (e.g. Nursing), and click the Launch button for the day’s session.
Don’t forget to turn on your mic and/or camera after you enter the tutoring room.
## Tutoring FAQs
Business math : most business tutors (except New Media and D3) can help with business math and business statistics.
Trades math : most of our engineering tutors can help with trades math and physics. We recommend finding a tutor in a program similar to yours if they’re available. For example, an electrical trades student would go to an electrical engineering (ECET) tutor.
Peer tutors can:
- Review and apply concepts taught in class
- Offer feedback on non-graded assignments
- Direct you to other student support services if needed
Peer tutors will not:
- Help with graded assignments
- Tell you what will be on exams
- Explain course material that you haven’t looked at on your own
Hiring for the academic year (Fall and Winter terms) begins in the spring. Check the Become a Tutor page for information about requirements and deadlines.
