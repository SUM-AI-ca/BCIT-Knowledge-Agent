## Learning Skills and Strategies

Becoming a confident, accomplished and successful student requires sound academic skills and effective study habits. Learning skills and strategies are tools and techniques that can help you be an effective and efficient learner. The Learning Commons offers services to support your development of these important cognitive skills.

Get support for:

- Time management
- Organizational skills
- Task prioritization (including term planning)
- Motivation and avoiding procrastination
- Managing stress and anxiety in your learning
- Quiz/test/exam preparation
- Reading strategies
- Note-taking strategies
- Memorization
- and much more!

## Read skills and strategies guides

Become a more effective learner at your own pace with these handy resources from the online study skills guide.

## Drop-in study skills sessions

Drop-in to one of our study skills sessions for support from one of our faculty members or visit a peer tutor. Both offer excellent advice and support for managing the challenges at BCIT.

## Book an appointment with a Learning Strategist

Learning Strategists are BCIT faculty members who provide individualized support to help you develop skills and strategies to learn effectively. Our goal is to make you a more confident and efficient learner.

Please note that Learning Strategists do not help with course content (check out peer tutoring for course-specific help).

We offer in-person appointments at the Burnaby Campus Library and virtual appointments. Book an appointment with a Learning Strategist.
