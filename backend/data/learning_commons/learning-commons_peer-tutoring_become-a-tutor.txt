## Become a Tutor
## When do we hire?
We hire peer tutors in the spring for the upcoming academic year (Fall and Winter terms) to provide drop-in support to students at four campuses and writing support at the Burnaby Campus Library.
## What are peer tutors?
Tutors are student employees in at least the second year of their program who have strong grades and enjoy helping others learn.
### Peer tutors
Peer tutors help students understand course concepts and develop learning skills for their courses.
### Writing tutors
Writing tutors help students plan, organize, write, and revise their assignments, and can answer questions about citations and language use.
Applicants must meet the following criteria to be considered:
- Be a current BCIT student in at least a two-year (four-term) diploma or degree program
- Complete two terms * of your program before tutoring starts
- Be registered in classes while working as a tutor
- Available to work in person for at least two terms (minimum 2-3 hours a week)
- Have two referrals from BCIT instructors Note: for writing tutors, at least one referral must be from a COMM or LIBS instructor
- Legally entitled to work in Canada
* The second term can be in progress when you apply, as long as your unofficial transcript shows your current courses.
- Strong grades, with an average of at least 80%
- Able to work 2-3 hours per week for at least two terms
- Good communication, problem solving, and time management skills
- Initiative and ability to work independently
- Tutor a minimum of 2 hours per week during scheduled drop-in times
- Be available for at least 1 hour per week of one-on-one tutoring support, if assigned
- Complete paid training (full day in-person session plus online training)
- Fill out admin paperwork on time
- Pay rate of $19.43 per hour
- Five sick days per year
- Opportunity to take on additional responsibilities as a Welcome Desk tutor
- Represent the Learning Commons at on-campus events
- Help your fellow students and be part of the academic support community
- Develop career-related skills in leadership, teamwork, and communication
## Ready to apply?
If you meet the criteria above, please prepare the following in PDF format and submit them with your application.
- Unofficial transcript
- Current resume/CV
