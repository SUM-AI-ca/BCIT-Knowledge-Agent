## Learning Strategies
Learn skills and strategies to make you a more effective and efficient learner.
- Book an appointment with a Learning Strategist.
- Drop in to a study skills session.
- Explore online study skills guides .
## Book an appointment
A Learning Strategist provides one-on-one support to help you become a more confident and efficient learner.
Skills and strategies they can help with include focus and productivity, reading and note-taking, test preparation, managing your workload, learning with ADHD, and reducing anxiety.
Book an appointment to meet with a Learning Strategist at the Burnaby Campus Library or virtually.
## Drop-in study skills sessions
Get support with study skills from a faculty member or visit a peer tutor from your program.
Study skills drop-in is available on Wednesday afternoons at the peer tutoring Welcome Desk on the main floor of the Burnaby Campus Library. Please check the peer tutoring schedule for any changes.
