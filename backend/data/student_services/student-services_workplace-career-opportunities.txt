Title: Workplace & Career Opportunities

================================================================================

## Workplace & Career Opportunities
There are many services in place to help support you to connect your academic world to your career world. These services will help you feel confident and prepared to enter your field after graduation. From applied learning to job searches, BCIT has you covered when it comes to moving forward in your career path.
## Centre for Workplace Education
Connect with the Centre for Workplace Education to get practical experience and hands-on learning outside of the classroom. Learn about BCIT’s co-op programs, which link knowledge and skills gained from the classroom with applications in business and industry. The Centre for Workplace Education hosts co-op, and eJobs .
Contact Centre for Workplace Education by phone or email .
## Working in Canada - International Students
Make sure you’re eligible and prepared to work in Canada, so nothing catches you by surprise. The International Student Centre has you covered – find all you need to know about working in Canada.
Connect to International Student Centre by email at isc_info@bcit.ca .
