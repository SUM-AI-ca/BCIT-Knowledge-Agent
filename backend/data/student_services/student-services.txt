## We're here for you

BCIT is committed to our students’ success – academic, professional, and personal. BCIT Student Services is here to support your well-being and development, and to help you reach your goals. We want you to thrive, not just in your chosen profession, but throughout your life.

- Workplace & career opportunities

## Set yourself up for success

BCIT cares about your success.

Student supports and services are available to you throughout your BCIT journey, and are designed to help you succeed. Don’t wait until you’re feeling overwhelmed to reach out; connect with the supports you need early, and let us help you set yourself up for success so you can hit the ground running on day one.

Check out the BCIT Secrets for Success for advice from our community!
