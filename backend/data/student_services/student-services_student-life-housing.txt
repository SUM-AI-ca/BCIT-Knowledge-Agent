## Student Life & Housing

From the moment you receive your admissions letter, to your graduation day, the Student Life Office plays an integral role supporting and connecting you to resources and services that enhance your well-being and enable you to thrive throughout your student life cycle. Find out all you need to know about the Student Life Office, and other aspects of life as a BCIT student.

## Early Assist

Early Assist is a confidential service that provides BCIT faculty, staff and students an opportunity to identify students who may be struggling personally or academically, and offer them appropriate support and services at an early stage.

## Housing

BCIT’s on-campus housing is located on the Burnaby Campus, adjacent to the sports field and campus recreation facilities.

## Student Life Office

The Student Life Office plays an integral role in connecting students to resources and services that enhance their well-being and enable them to thrive throughout their time at BCIT. Visit the Student Life Office to find out more about BCIT’s wide variety of services and resources, or to learn about the Eight Dimensions of Well-being.

## Student Life Ambassadors

Student Life Ambassadors play an important role in shaping the student experience at BCIT. Check out their initiatives or consider joining the team!
