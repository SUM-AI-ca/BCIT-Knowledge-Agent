Title: Technology

================================================================================

## Technology
You can stay in touch with BCIT from anywhere, at any time; stay up-to-date with BCIT’s online portals and app. Having trouble connecting? We can help with that, too!
## AppsAnywhere
AppsAnywhere is BCIT’s software delivery platform for academic software. Through AppsAnywhere, you’ll be able to access all the software you need to do your coursework anywhere, anytime.
## Educational Technology Support (ETS)
Many of your classes will rely on education technology, in one form or another. For helpful tech-related supports and “how-to” details, visit Education Technology Support’s Student Resources.
## IT Services
From technology-related how-to resources to details on campus computer labs and more, if you have a question about BCIT’s technology, chances are IT Services has already answered it.
## myBCIT
Your myBCIT account is your one-stop-shop for communications from BCIT, and self-service access to your course schedule, account balance and receipts, grades and transcript requests, and much more. Make sure to log in regularly to stay up-to-date
## Safety Wise
BCIT’s Safety Wise app can help you with way-finding on-campus and connect to Campus Security with the touch of a button. Make sure you enable push notifications, as this app will keep you in the loop during an emergency.
