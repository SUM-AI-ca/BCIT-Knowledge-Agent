## Welcome

BCIT is committed to making the transition to Metro Vancouver and BCIT as simple and stress-free as possible. The International Student Centre offers many resources, including information on:

- Study and work permits
- Peer mentoring
- Working in Canada
- Medical insurance

Resources are available online or in person at both the Burnaby and Downtown campuses.

Connect with the International Student Centre by email at isc_info@bcit.ca.
