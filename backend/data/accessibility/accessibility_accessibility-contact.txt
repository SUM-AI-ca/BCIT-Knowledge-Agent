## Disability-designated parking stalls

– in Burnaby campus

Individuals with a valid “SPARC pass” may park in any disability-designated parking stall on campus 24/7 with no restrictions to specific areas.

Students may park for as many hours as they have paid for at the Students and Staff parking lots. Visitor lots may have time restrictions. Please check and then adhere to these restrictions.

Please clearly display your SPARC pass and proof of parking payment.

Don’t forget to check our new Elevator Status Notification System if you’re planning to use elevator(s) in Burnaby campus.

Check BCIT Burnaby campus Accessibility Map [PDF]

Do you need a special parking permit? SPARC BC can help.
