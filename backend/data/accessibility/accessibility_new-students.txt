## Success starts with good planning

Planning ahead is key, especially if you know, or think, you may require accommodations. Connecting with Accessibility Services early allows enough time for the implementation of your accommodations, ensuring they are ready as soon as possible.

Review the Accessibility Services registration process to learn about the required medical documentation and recommended timelines.

Require accommodations for program admission criteria.

Need accommodations for a program/course for which you have applied/registered.

Have recently been accepted to a BCIT course or program and will require accommodations.

Are returning after a long break and have registered with Accessibility Services in the past.

## Finding the right program for you

BCIT offers an extensive array of educational options across various fields and study formats. Understanding the course components and the essential learning outcomes of your desired program is vital in determining if it’s the right fit for you.

## Transitioning from secondary

If this is your first experience at post-secondary, you will discover some distinct differences from secondary school. Take the time to understand these differences and how you will manage your accommodations and access other helpful resources.

## Well-being and success at BCIT

BCIT has an excellent reputation for graduating skilled, employable professionals. Full-time programs are intense with most carrying seven to eight courses per term. However, there are different options for you to find a right fit that will allow you to balance a proper wellness regime during your studies.

## Need assistance?

If you have questions, comments or are unsure about your next steps to access accommodations, please contact Accessibility Services
