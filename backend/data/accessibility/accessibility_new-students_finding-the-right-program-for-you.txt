## Finding the right program for you

BCIT offers a broad range of educational options, including Bachelor of Technology degrees, Diplomas of Technology, and Trades & Technical Training, in a variety of fields with different study formats, to provide you with a broad canvas to paint your future.

We encourage you to explore our available courses and programs to learn more about full-time and flexible learning options. We also recommend reviewing the academic dates & datelines and the Flexible Learning Key Registration Dates for term schedule information.

## BCIT’s career guiding resources

Still have questions or need further guidance? We have a variety of options to help you. Visit the Contact Us page of the program you’re interested in or join one of the following events to gain a deeper insight:


Spend a Day


Immerse yourself in BCIT's vibrant atmosphere by spending a day with a current student in your chosen program.


Information session


Dive deeper into your chosen program and related career paths. Attend one information sessions to get your questions answered.


Campus Tour


Discover what life at BCIT looks like on our one-hour campus tour.


Big Info


Attend one of BCIT’s bi-annual events for an extensive overview of the courses and programs available.


Program Advising


Get personalized information about the program you are interested in by booking a session with a program advisor.
