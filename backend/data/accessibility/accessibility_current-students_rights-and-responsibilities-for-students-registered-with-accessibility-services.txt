Title: Rights and Responsibilities for Accessibility Services students

================================================================================

## Rights and Responsibilities for Accessibility Services students
The BCIT student conduct outlines the policies and procedures students are to follow to ensure that all BCIT students are entitled to a healthy, safe, secure and respectful learning environment.
As a student registered with Accessibility Services, it is crucial for you to know your fundamental rights and responsibilities, which are outlined below:
## Student rights
- Obtain equitable access to learning at a post-secondary institution to which you have been accepted.
- Receive reasonable accommodations when an assessment of your functional limitations has determined they are necessary to address learning barriers resulting from your disability.
- The commitment to privacy and confidentiality concerning your disability. You only need to disclose information to those who need to know for the purpose of implementing your required supports(e.g., Accessibility Services).
- Be treated with dignity and respect.
## Student responsibilities
- Meet your program or course prerequisites.
- Review and follow your registration process with Accessibility Services.
- Sign the Student Consent to Release of Information as a part of your online registration process.
- Contact Accessibility Services to book a meeting with a Vocational Rehabilitation Specialist (VRS).
- Be aware of the BCIT policy relating to Accommodation for Students with Disabilities and the procedures for Requests for Accommodations .
- Renew your Individual Accommodation Plan each term to receive your accommodations.
- Be actively involved in arranging your accommodations. (e.g., submitting Exam Accommodation Request form).
- Contact Accessibility Services at your first opportunity if you require any changes to your accommodations.
- Maintain institutional standards for academic achievement.
- Maintain your own schedule and attendance.
- Self-advocate for your rights and needs.
- Acknowledge when you are experiencing difficulties and ask for assistance.
