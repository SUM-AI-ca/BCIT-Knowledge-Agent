## Course Description
Presents communication skills for experienced house inspectors or for persons considering the house inspection field. Teaches practical techniques for planning, selecting and presenting information effectively and professionally. Includes correct format for memos, letters, instructions, procedures, and house inspection reports.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Create documents that follow the conventions of correct grammar, spelling, mechanics, and punctuation.
- Generate, draft, and revise a memorandum, short business letter, facsimile, instructions, procedures, and house inspection reports following correct business conventions with respect to format, structure, style, and content.

## Course Goals
The course goals enable the writer to substantially develop and improve technical and business writing skills for documents typical of the construction industry including letters, memorandums, email, instructions, technical descriptions, and house inspection reports. Students will focus on key issues of format, structure, style, content, grammar, spelling, mechanics, and punctuation.

Assignment Details

In marking your assignments, your instructor will carefully review the following:

1. Complete development of your ideas including specific examples where appropriate. Analysis and discussion of materials must be logical and thorough. As everyone has an individual style of expression, your instructor cannot give the number of words expected for each assignment.

2. Correct document format, paragraph construction, sentence structure, grammar, punctuation, mechanics, diction, and spelling.

3. Detail and accuracy.

4. Clear and concise expression and appropriate style.

5. An indication that you have listened to, read, and thought about the concepts introduced in the course.

6. Submission on or before the due date.
