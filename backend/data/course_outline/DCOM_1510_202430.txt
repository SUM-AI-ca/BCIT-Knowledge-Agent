## Course Description
​This course provides the foundation required for understanding resistive electronic circuits, including basic electrical principles and components. The concepts of the quantities of charge, voltage, current, resistance, energy and power are developed. Ohm's Law, Kirchhoff's Voltage Law and Kirchhoff's Current Law are used to analyze series, parallel and series-parallel direct current (DC) circuits. DC test equipment will be used for measurements. General troubleshooting strategies and techniques are introduced, with emphasis on methods used to isolate faults in an efficient and logical manner. Analysis of capacitors and inductors in DC circuits is covered. Electromagnetism and the operation of relays is introduced. Theory is reinforced with hands-on practice.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Define charge, voltage, current, resistance, energy and power.
- Interpret colour band and alpha-numeric coding for resistors.
- Identify fundamental schematic symbols and relate them to their physical components.
- Measure resistance, voltage and current using DC test equipment.
- Use metric prefixes to express electronic values.
- Use Ohm's Law, Power Law, Kirchhoff's Voltage Law and Kirchhoff's Current Law in DC resistive circuits.
- Build, measure and analyze series, parallel and series-parallel DC resistive circuits.
- Interpret capacitor labelling.
- Build, measure and analyze resistor-capacitor (RC) DC circuits.
- Explain the basic principles of magnetic fields, electromagnetism and electromagnetic induction.
- Analyze resistor-inductor (RL) DC circuits.
- Build, measure and analyze a relay switching circuit.
- Troubleshoot and isolate faults within basic electrical circuits.​
