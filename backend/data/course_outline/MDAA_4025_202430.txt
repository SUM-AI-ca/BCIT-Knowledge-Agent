## Course Description
​In this course students will work on developing their demo reel based on their proposal and planning created in the previous term. Under the supervision and direction of the instructor, students create, test, revise and deliver their final professional demo reel based on industry standards. Students meet with the instructor (in person or online) and review the progress, receive feedback, and implement any revisions necessary for creating an industry standard demo reel.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Present their work in progressive steps.
- Apply advanced character and prop animation techniques to 3D models.
- Utilize industry standard methodology for compositing the rendered sequences.
- Receive, analyze and implement feedback for each task.
- Problem solve and regress the issues with their demo reel.
- Track progress based on the proposed plans.
- Deliver and present an industry standard demo reel.
