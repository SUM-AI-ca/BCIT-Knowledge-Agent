## Course Description
​Students in this course will focus on the techniques and methodology for drafting and prototyping a demo reel. Under the direct supervision of their instructor, students will create a rough draft piece that shows the alignment of their demo reel to their plan. Students will present their animatic to their instructor and receive feedback and guidance for their plan and ultimately their capstone project. Reference material will need to be gathered and approved. Students will need to finalize and have approved their concept for their demo reel capstone project. By the end of the semester, students will have completed and refined the camera moves and edit of the final demo reel and will be ready to replace assets with their final versions.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Create a plan and schedule to ensure completion of a final demo reel.
- Gather reference material for their capstone project.
- Identify key objects and animation components in the project.
- Analyze each scene of the proposed demo reel and identify proper proxy objects.
- Create animations for objects and camera for layout.
- Incorporate feedback received from instructors into their animatic.
- Present a final layout and edit of their expected demo reel.

## Course Goals
The goal is this course is to prepare students for their Final Capstone Project in term 5. Through regular reviews and mini lessons, the instructors will guide the students through the ideation stage to a complete pre-production package and proof of concept (aka Final Project). A core element of this class is to match student career goals with their project concepts.
