## Course Description
A comprehensive practicum in cardiac rhythm device implantation and follow-up at an approved cardiac pacemaker implantation centre. Students will spend a minimum of 625 hours (18 weeks) at the clinical site, first observing and subsequently learning and refining their skills in patient assessment, device performance evaluation and programming. Students will log their experiences assessing and programming a variety of single chamber and dual chamber pacemaker/ICD devices. Specific goals of consistency in competency requirements must be met before the student completes the practicum. More generic skills such as computer skills, teamwork, communication, problem solving and patient care are woven throughout the course.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

## Cardiac Rhythm Device procedures:
- Successfully interpret pacemaker/ICD device data for at least 60 patients (of the 60 cases, 10 are observed, 20 are assisted, and 30 are performed independently).
- Perform direct programming of pacemaker/ICD for at least 60 patients (of the 60 cases, 10 are observed, 20 are assisted, and 30 are performed independently).
- Successfully interpret at least 30 intracardiac EGM tracings.
- Perform at least 15 lead testing and monitoring during pacemaker/ICD implant (of the 15 cases, 5 are observed, 5 are assisted, and 5 are performed independently).

## General clinical skills:
- Recognize abnormalities and correlate information obtained from the device analysis with other clinical data.
- Take patient history.
- Document care delivery.
- Perform physical assessment of patient.
- Maintain confidentiality of patient records.
- Maintain sterility, asepsis, and infection control.
- Ensure safety and care of electrical equipment and connections to patient.
- Exercise independent judgement, problem-solving skills, application of knowledge of cardiac rhythm management.
- Communicate effectively and appropriately with patients and staff.

## Course Specific Requirements
The practicum must be supervised by a physician and an associated professional (AP) mentor with proven experience and competence in Cardiac Rhythm management Devices (CRMDs)

. The student will also be evaluated on their knowledge of and skill in the following: . Taking a patient history . Documenting care delivery . Physical assessment of the patient . Maintaining confidentiality of patient records . Maintaining sterility, asepsis and infection control . Safety and care of electrical equipment and connections to patients . CPR (BCLS certification) . Communicating effectively and appropriately with patients and staff . Recognising the effects of cardiac drugs on the patient and the ECG

## Other information
. Evaluation . Students will be undergo continuous evaluation throughout the program and receive both verbal and written feedback from the preceptor.

Each student must successfully meet the performance criteria identified in the detailed course description above and cannot successfully complete the course before logging a minimum of 625 hours of clinical exposure. Because the opportunity to observe and evaluate all of the required cases may not occur during 625 hours (4.5 months) at the clinical site, and because students may need exposure to more than the minimum number of cases to achieve the required level of competency, the length of the clinical may exceed 625 hours. The time limit to complete the practicum is 900 hours (6 months).

Students must maintain a complete record of their clinical experience in logbook issued to them. This record must be submitted to the preceptor and clinical coordinator on completion of the course.

Students will be graded as either satisfactory (S) or unsatisfactory (U) on completion of the course. Students with an unsatisfactory grade may be granted an extension by the clinical coordinator/program head in consultation with the preceptor.

Any student deemed unsafe at the clinical site will immediately be withdrawn and will fail the course. A student may challenge this decision through the normal appeal of grade procedure but in the meantime shall not be allowed to work in the clinical area.
