## Course Description
Second course of a two course series on mineral processing. The focus of the course is on concentration/enrichment processes. Classification of minerals is discussed. Gravity concentration methods, dense media separation and flotation theory and practice along with dewatering processes explained.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to competently cover the following topics;

- Describe the operation of mechanical classifiers, settling cones and hydrocyclones [1].
- Describe the operating principles in hydrocyclones [1,2].
- Describe the basic principles of mineral separation in a slurry under the influence of gravity [1,2,3].
- Explain the application of heavy medium separation in mineral processing [1,2].
- Describe the beneficiation of coal, galena, gold, diamonds etc., by the unit operations of jigging and flowing film concentrators such as shaking tables, pinched slurries, Reichert cones, etc., [1,2,3].
- Explain surface chemical phenomena as applicable to mineral separation by froth flotation [1].
- Describe mechanism by which various reagents such as collectors, frothers and modifiers work in froth flotation [1].
- Draw flotation circuits for simple and complex ores [4,7].
- Perform bench and pilot scale flotation experiments including overall and component material balances [3].
- Describe typical equipment utilized during thickening of metallurgical slurries [1].
- Describe the area principle for settling of solids in a slurry and from batch settling data and calculate unit area & height of compression zone in full scale thickeners [1,2,3].
- Describe various types of industrial pressure and vacuum filters [1].
