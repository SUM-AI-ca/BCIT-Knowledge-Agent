## Course Description
This course will explore the future of new media with the emergence of new technologies in print, web and mobile. Students will participate in group discussions, case studies and choose a specific field of research regarding new media leadership, or the implementation of new tools, platforms, software and hardware as it relates to new media and the general public. Students will experience a combination of fieldtrips, guess speaker discussions, and will be assessed by a combination of team and individual projects.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Research emerging hardware and software technologies, explaining how it will affect the new media industry.
- Research new and emerging tools, explaining how it will affect society at large.
- Explain the impact of new technology in marketing, advertising and communications.
- Explain how technology trends are being applied in new media.
- Analyze a case study and present findings to support how an emerging technology or trends will address particular issues in industry.
- Development a plan to follow trends and emerging technologies in New Media.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: As set out in BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations, regular attendance in lectures and labs is integral to student success. Therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Students are responsible for communicating with instructors proactively about both potentially approved and unexcused absences. It is the student’s responsibility to work with their instructor to find an effective means to complete work missed due to both approved and unexcused absences. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate. For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation. If absences are the result of a medical or other accommodation, notification from Accessibility Services personnel will be accepted in lieu of a doctor’s note, on an interim basis.
