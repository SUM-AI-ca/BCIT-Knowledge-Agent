### Course Description
This course is the first of two designed to introduce the theoretical and practical foundations in accounting. Topics include accounting concepts and principles; income measurement; business transactions; adjusting and closing entries; financial statement preparation; the accounting cycle; merchandising operations; and accounting systems.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate the nature and purpose of accounting information including its limitations and basic concepts.
- Apply generally accepted accounting principles in the analysis and recording of business transactions.
- Apply the basic principles of double-entry bookkeeping to the recording of transactions for both a service and a merchandising business.
- Prepare a trial balance.
- Analyze and record adjusting entries for both service and merchandising operations.
- Complete a worksheet.
- Generate financial statements (Income Statement, Statement of Retained Earnings and the Balance Sheet) for a service and a merchandising business in compliance with Accounting Standards for Private Enterprises (ASPE).
- Close the books.
- Describe and differentiate the three forms of business organization.
- Evaluate Accounting information systems and appreciate the need for internal control.

### Attendance Requirements
Attendance and participation are integral for success in this course. Students are responsible for reading and understanding BCIT's attendance policy included in this course outline.
Students are required to complete all course requirements at the scheduled times. There will be no make-up assignments or assessments. If you miss an assignment or assessment, you will receive zero marks. Exceptions may be made for approved excused absences, academic concessions, and accommodations, in accordance with the information below and BCIT Procedure 5101-PR1.
Excused Absences, Academic Concessions, and Accommodations
A student who misses an assignment or assessment due to a medical reason or other unavoidable causes will be provided with an opportunity to make up for that missed exam or evaluation, but only under the following conditions:
- The student must notify the instructor that they will miss the assignment or assessment as soon as possible, preferably prior to the time when the assignment or assessment is scheduled to occur. Timely notice should be as soon as possible, but never more than 7 calendar days after the missed event.
- For medical-related absences, the student must provide a BCIT-approved medical certificate, obtained at the student’s own expense, to show that an assignment or assessment was missed due to medical reasons. The BCIT Student Medical Certificate is available at the following URL: https://www.bcit.ca/files/healthservices/pdf/studentmedicalcertificate.pdf
- For other unavoidable absences the student should be prepared to provide supporting documentation.
When the program department has validated that the conditions previously stated have been met, the program department will employ one of the following options:
- The program department will provide the student with another opportunity to write the same or replacement assignment or assessment, or perform some other form of evaluation, or
- The program department will devise another means of judging the student’s mastery of the learning outcomes, or
- The program department will calculate an aegrotat grade for the student if the conditions for aegrotat have been met.
Consistent with these policies, students are advised that in this course:
- The only acceptable medical certificate is the BCIT Student Medical Certificate that is available at the following URL: https://www.bcit.ca/files/healthservices/pdf/studentmedicalcertificate.pdf
- Students who fail to notify their instructor or submit to their instructor the BCIT Student Medical Certificate in a timely manner, will be awarded zero marks on the missed assignment or assessment. Timely manner means as soon as possible, but never more than 7 calendar days after the missed event.
- The policy of the program department is that should a student miss an assessment, the BCIT Student Medical Certificate and the academic accommodation requested by the student must be validated by all three of the Instructor, the Program Head, and the Associate Dean, before an accommodation plan will be considered. In the case of missed assignments, the Instructor may alone validate the Student Medical Certificate and approve an accommodation plan.
- Students who miss an assessment due to an excused absence may have to schedule and write the same or replacement assessment at BCIT’s Test Centre. Students are responsible for covering any invigilation fees incurred.
