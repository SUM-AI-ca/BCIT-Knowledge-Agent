## Course Description
​This course covers the key business processes in high-end ERP systems such as SAP and SYSPRO. The focus is to explain key concepts in business processes such as sales, procurement, and production and how they apply to ERP systems. The Integration between business processes including accounting, inventory management, and material planning is discussed. This course is an excellent resource for SAP certification exams. This course will also introduce students to new technologies to make affordable process automation tools for small businesses such as Microsoft Power Automate.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Outline key business processes and their integration points.
- Identify the key business benefits of enterprise systems.
- Compare the organizational, transactional, and master data.
- Plan how to extract information and create reports.
- Propose the main steps in fulfillment, procurement, and production.
- Analyze the key concepts in material planning process.
- Contrast financial accounting and management accounting.
- Plan SAP S4/HANA to execute business processes and create reports.
- Develop process automation apps using Microsoft Power Automate.​
