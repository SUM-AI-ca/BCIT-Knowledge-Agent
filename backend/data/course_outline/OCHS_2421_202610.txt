### Course Description
Introduces legislation regulating hazardous materials used, transferred, and stored in the workplace and the environment. Fully explores Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) and Transportation of Dangerous Goods (TDG) requirements. Investigates lead abatement and asbestos management options in the workplace. Discusses the education and training requirements for hazardous materials. (This course is equivalent to OCHS 2420.)
### Learning Resources
Online access to OHS regulator websites
### Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
1. Develop and maintain a WHMIS management program in a workplace.
2. Develop and maintain a TDG management program in a workplace.
3. Develop and maintain asbestos manage-in-place and abatement programs in a workplace.
4. Develop and maintain a management program for the storage and disposal of waste chemicals in a workplace.
5. Develop and manage toxic mould abatement programs.
6. Develop and manage exposure control plans for the following agents in a workplace:
- ammonia in refrigeration systems
- lead in coatings and other sources
- ozone in disinfection systems
- chlorine in disinfection systems
- hydrogen sulphide from various sources
- silica from various sources

### Course Specific Requirements
The intent of this course is to bring the student to:
- a WHMIS Trainer level of knowledge
- a TDG Trainer level of knowledge
- a level of skill that will allow the student to manage asbestos, lead, ammonia, silica, hydrogen sulphide, ozone, chlorine, mold and other hazardous material programs for an employer
