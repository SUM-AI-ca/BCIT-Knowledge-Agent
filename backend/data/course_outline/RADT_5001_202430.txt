## Course Description
The clinical orientation week introduces the student to treatment and planning procedures in the radiation therapy department. Students will be scheduled to planning and treatment units to observe procedures and to interact with patients. Students will review BC Cancer Agency/PHSA and Clinical Program policies and procedures in order to perform in a safe and professional manner.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Comply with BC Cancer Agency/PHSA policy and procedures for safe and professional conduct.
- Comply with clinical program policy and procedure for safe and professional conduct.
- Recognise the major roles and responsibilities of radiation therapists, oncologists and other health care personnel in the planning and delivery of treatment.
- Introduce self to the patient and to health care personnel.
- Ensure identity of the patient before commencing with a planning and treatment procedure.
- Practice proper body mechanics when lifting and moving patients and equipment
- Communicate effectively with patient and health care personnel in a clear, professional manner
- Use technical and medical terminology as it is applied in the radiation therapy department.
- Identify accessories and equipment commonly found in the radiation therapy department.
- Identify equipment and procedures used for emergency response.
- Participate in routine planning and treatment activities under supervision.
