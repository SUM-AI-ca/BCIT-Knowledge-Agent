## Course Description
Project Management skills are in high demand and can provide a competitive edge in today’s workplace. This intensive course gives you the practical skills you need to deliver a winning project on time and on budget, with a marketing frame of reference. This course teaches you how to create a project life cycle, statement of work, work breakdown structure, schedule, budget, and risk management plan. Working in a team, you will apply your Project Management skills to a real-life marketing project. This course will prepare you to make a meaningful contribution to any project.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course you should be able to:

- Describe project management terminology.
- Define a project life cycle.
- Write a statement of work.
- Create a work breakdown structure.
- Estimate project costs and duration.
- Create project schedules and budgets.
- Track the progress of a project.
- Identify and quantify project risks.
- Identify problems and take corrective action early in a project.
- Establish effective team communications.
- Manage stakeholder expectations.
- Use resources effectively.

## Course Goals
To provide students with the principles and techniques to plan, execute, and manage complex projects. This will be accomplished through lectures, examples, group discussion and case studies. Topics include workflow analysis, quality control, and performance evaluation.

## Other information
Each student will submit a marketing project of their choice to be approved as their individual term project. Ideally, the project should be one that would be characteristic of the student's work environment and have an expected duration of 6 - 12 months of elapsed time. If a project cannot be identified within the student's work environment, another project of interest can be submitted for approval. A short writeup of the project is due by the end of class of Week 4.
