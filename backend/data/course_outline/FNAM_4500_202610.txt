### Course Description
Topics will include governance issues related to aboriginal rights, and the delegation of power among local, provincial and federal governments. Important legal principles and terms will be discussed to aid students in the interpretation of recent decisions. Students will be able to describe the role courts and administrative boards play in the interpretation of government policy and legislation. The link between the various professional associations, and role ethics and professionalism play in interpreting job responsibilities, will also form a component of the course. Students will obtain the skills necessary for researching and reviewing recent court and administrative board decisions that have the potential to impact their day to day work activities. The course also introduces students to the current structure of aboriginal organizations and government agencies involved with aboriginal affairs. Important resource management issues as they related to aboriginal organizations will be discussed. The course will also review current forest policy and tenure throughout the province. Students will work on the creation of an integrated resource management plan.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Contrast the scope of power of provincial and federal jurisdictions related to resource management.
- Provide an overview of the provincial and federal court system.
- Discuss how the different branches of law relate to resource management.
- Discuss the role the court system has played in the interpretation of aboriginal rights as they related to resource management.
- Explain key legal concepts related to resource management, and the review of case briefs.
- Discuss the principles of environmental and professional ethics, and their impact of day to day activities.
- Discuss the interrelationships between the responsibilities of various professional associations related to resource management in the province.
- Use legal research tools to find current updates to statutes and regulations.
- Discuss the significance of recent decisions that directly impact day to day work activities in resource management.
- Describe important BC Aboriginal organizations and groups found throughout the province.
- Describe Aboriginal concerns, issues, and projects related to resource management in the province.
- Describe how recent court decisions are influencing current legislation and resource management activities.
- Describe the historical development of forest management policy in the province.
- Provide detailed descriptions of various forest land tenures in the province.
- Participate in the creation of an integrated group forest management plan.
- Describe the roles and responsibilities of various provincial government ministries related to forest management.
