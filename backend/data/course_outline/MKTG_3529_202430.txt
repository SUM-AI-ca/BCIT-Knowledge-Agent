## Course Description
​In Earned Marketing Opportunities, students will explore a variety of concepts, tools, and techniques to generate publicity in an increasingly complex media world—including building and leveraging relationships with the press and traditional/broadcast media, influencers, and customers—for B2B, B2C, and not-for-profit organizations. Students will analyze and plan PR campaigns across communication channels, considering established digital and social platforms, emerging media environments, events and experiential marketing. As part of a cross-course team project (with Brand and Campaign Strategy, Consumer Experience Marketing, Owned Marketing Communications, and Paid Media Opportunities), students will follow a systematic approach to the analysis of earned media using a current marketing communications case study.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop customized pitches for various media types
- Collect and evaluate earned media metrics
- Identify potential influencers and create outreach plans (including link building)
- Implement best practices for online engagement and building community
- Compare tools and techniques for social media monitoring and social listening
- Create reputation management plans, including generating reviews and referrals
- Develop event and experiential marketing plans
- Develop selection criteria for sponsorship opportunities
- Discuss current trends and issues in earned media
- Analyze the earned media strategies and tactics of a real-world IMC campaign
- Develop team collaboration skills
- Develop strong written and oral presentation skills

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.
