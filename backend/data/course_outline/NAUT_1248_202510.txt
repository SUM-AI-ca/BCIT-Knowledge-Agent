## Course Description
​This course covers the principles of physical science and problem-solving as they apply to navigation. The course consists of two major parts: (1) waves, sound, and light and (2) magnetism and electricity. Topics include vectors and forces; motion in a straight line; Newton’s laws of motion; rotational motion; work, energy, and power; friction; simple machines; fluids at rest and in motion; Archimedes’ principle and flotation; solids, liquids, and gases and changes of state; heat and transmission of heat; and refrigeration. ​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Solve static problems using graphical and trigonometric methods.
- Solve problems involving moment and equilibrium, motion in a straight line, rotational motion, and Newton’s laws of motion.
- Describe the factors affecting frictional resistance to motion.
- Solve problems involving Hooke’s law and the modulus of elasticity.
- Solve problems involving work, power, energy, and simple machines.
- Calculate the density of objects and buoyant force using Archimedes’ principle.
- Calculate the change in pressure resulting from a change in velocity using Bernoulli’s equation.
- Describe common temperature scales of Celsius, Kelvin, and Fahrenheit, and convert temperature between different scales.
- Define specific heat capacity and solve problems involving heat energy.
- Solve problems involving linear and volumetric expansivity.
- Solve problems involving Boyle’s and Charles’ laws.
- Describe the heat transfer by conduction, convection, and radiation.
- Solve problems involving changes of state and latent heat.
- Read a wet and dry bulb hygrometer and use tables to determine the relative humidity.
- Sketch and describe a simple refrigeration circuit.
- Explain the theory of waves and its applications.
- Describe electromagnetic radiation.
- Explain electromagnetic waves and interference.
- Explain simple harmonic motion.
- Explain refraction and reflection.
- Explain sound waves and the Doppler effect.
- Explain electrical safety and its applications.
- Describe electrical principles and the basic components of electrical circuits.
- Explain magnetism.
- Describe electromagnetic induction and its applications.
- Describe alternating voltage and current.
- Explain the functioning and applications of transformers, electric generators, and motors.
- Explain the distribution of power and protective devices and their applications.
- Explain the functioning of electrical measuring instruments and their applications.
- Describe electrochemistry. ​
