### Course Description
Motorcycle technicians work primarily on 2 and 3-wheeled motorcycles and other units such as motor scooters. Topics for level 3 include​ using tools, equipment and documentation. Maintaining chassis and components, suspension systems , wheels and tires, two-stroke and four-stroke engines, transmissions, final drive systems, electrical systems, fuel and exhaust systems.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use diagnostic tools and equipment
- Maintain frames
- Maintain steering systems for multi-wheeled motorcycles
- Maintain chassis standard and accessory components
- Maintain front suspension components
- Maintain front suspension components for multi-wheeled motorcycles
- Maintain rear suspension components
- Maintain spoked wheels
- Apply principles of engines and engine construction
- Maintain cylinder heads
- Maintain valve systems on two-stroke engines
- Maintain valve trains on four-stroke engines
- Maintain cylinders and pistons
- Maintain crankshaft assemblies
- Maintain counterbalance assemblies
- Maintain engine cases
- Maintain lubrication systems
- Maintain cooling systems
- ​Maintain constant mesh transmissions
- Maintain final drive shafts and gears
- Apply electrical and electronic principles
- Maintain electrical standard and accessory components
- Maintain wiring harness systems
- Maintain ignition systems
- Maintain fuel tanks and fuel delivery components
- Maintain air delivery systems
- Maintain carburetor systems
- Maintain exhaust systems
