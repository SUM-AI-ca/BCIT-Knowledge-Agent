### Course Description
Explores psychological and sociological concepts, research and application of relevance to the clinical practice of radiation therapy. Topics to assist students to develop the communication and coping skills required of a health care professional include critical skills for effective, professional communications, challenges and opportunities presented by cultural and other diversity in the workplace, developmental differences, work teams, conflict resolution, harassment and discrimination, abuse and ethics.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Integrate effective interpersonal communication skills in order to foster rapport and trust.
- Apply concept of empathy to create respectful and supportive relations with clients and co-workers.
- Perform various types of assertion according to appropriateness and desired result.
- Implement psychological strategies to prevent distress and uncooperative behaviour in patients.
- Explain common cultural differences relating to interpersonal communication styles, patient perceptions, experiences and expectations.
- Demonstrate respect for diversity by interacting with patients from various cultures appropriately and respectfully.
- Manage effectively various types of harassment and discrimination found in the workplace.

### Attendance Requirements
This course includes in-person classes at BCIT on Wednesdays at 10:30AM and asynchronous learning activities on Friday mornings. Students will use our Friday class time to prepare and read materials and complete journal reflections, group work and practice-based activities assigned each week. Students are encouraged to read the course outline carefully for due dates, and course expectations.
Please contact your Instructor if you are unable to attend class. Missed classes without prior discussion/approval will result in a deduction from your participation grade.
### Other information
Exams (Midterm 20%, Final 25%): Exams will be administered during class time on the date and time specified in the course outline. Exams may be administered online using Integrity Advocate (or other remote-proctoring software). Students will be provided instruction and support with remote proctoring procedures, if necessary. Missed Exams: Failure to take an exam at the scheduled time will result in a grade of “0” for that exam, unless a physician’s note is provided or other arrangements were made with the instructor prior to the day of the exam. The instructor reserves the right to utilize an alternative assessment method (which may be different from the original) in the case of a missed exam.
Presentation (25%) : Students will complete a short presentation in small groups. Groups will be assigned by the instructor. More information about the presentation will be provided in class.
Interpersonal Communication Assignment (20%): Students will write a brief paper reflecting upon an interpersonal communication issue and developing a strategy to address the situation, utilizing course-related skills and themes. More information about the assignment will be provided in class.
Reflection to Practice Assignment (5%): Upon completion of the BCIT MOOC Indigenous Awareness course , students will complete a short reflection connecting course learning to their practice in clinical settings. More information will be provided in class.
Participation (5%) : Participation marks are awarded at the end of the term at the discretion of the Instructor. Criteria considered when determining this mark include:
Attendance at Class | Students are expected to attend all classes, arrive on time, and stay for the entire class. Missed classes without prior discussion/approval will impact your participation mark.
Engagement during Class | Students are expected to actively participate during class. Are you responding to questions posed by the instructor or your classmates? Are you verbal and nonverbal communication skills to demonstrate that you are actively listening and participating? Are you asking questions?
Role-Plays/ Group Work | Are you sitting in class but not involved? Full participation in activities, discussions, and group work is required for this class.
Questions | Demonstrate active listening by asking questions of peers during class sessions and posing questions for the instructor.
Contribution | Your perspective and/or answer to questions about class materials is highly valued. Take the discussion to the next level by sharing your experiences.
Respectful Conduct | Some topics may be sensitive and/or invite debate of different perspectives. Do this with use of respectful language and conduct.
Use of Skills | Demonstration of skills we have learned in class indicates you are able to apply your learning in a real-life interactive setting.
Preparation for Class | Prepare for class by doing your readings ahead of time; it will be easier to absorb and discuss materials discussed in class.
