## Course Description
In this course, a dairy pasteurizing process is used to demonstrate how various types of equipment work together to achieve a finished product that meets food safety, quality, and regulatory requirements. Students will develop a preventive control plan (PCP), including a risk assessment, for the pasteurizing process. The various Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI) standards will be compared and a workplan for meeting the criteria of a given standard will be developed. The documentation system needed for implementing a food safety program will be introduced and students will write SOPs and Work Instructions for a subset of the various processes being covered in this course. Students will also learn about the role of auditing for verifying a food safety and quality program, and a GMP audit will be developed and performed.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Outline the equipment needed to produce a finished product that meets food safety, quality and regulatory requirements for a given food process.
- Calculate a formula for a given food product
- Perform a food safety risk assessment for a food safety or quality-related problem.
- Fill in the SFCR preventive control plan templates for a given food product
- Identify and perform a set of appropriate tests for measuring the important food safety and/or quality attributes of a given food product.
- Develop a work instruction for a test that measures the important food safety and/or quality attributes of a given food product.
- Write a SOP for addressing a given section of criteria of a GFSI food safety standard.
- Develop training materials for personnel hygiene preventive controls.
- Explain auditing and the role of auditing in a food safety and quality program.
- Perform an internal audit focusing on GMPs.​
