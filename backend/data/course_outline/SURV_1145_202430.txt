## Course Description
Covers fundamental concepts and principles of surveying; datums, principle of differential leveling, bench mark and detail leveling, peg-test, grades, use of levels, theodolites and steel chains; linear measurements; angle measurements and reductions, directions, bearings, coordinate systems; gradients and cut and fill; field note keeping and reductions; care, maintenance and adjustment of equipment.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Explain the fundamental concepts and principles of surveying, errors, accuracy and precision [1].
- Describe the basic datums used in surveying [1].
- Perform the field test to evaluate the condition of a surveyor's level [2,5,6,7].
- Carry out differential leveling of benchmarks and detail and compute adjustments of levelling circuits [2,3,5,6,7].
- Conduct measurement of vertical and horizontal angles with a total station [5,6,7].
- Evaluate and adjust data from level and total station observations.
- Perform calculations involving grades for construction purposes [2,3].
- Keep proper notes for survey fieldwork [7,8,9].
- Perform fieldwork in a safe and efficient manner [8].

## Learning Resources
Required:

- Lab and lecture handouts, protractor and metric scale, straight edge and pencil.
- Scientific hand calculator & waterproof cover (e.g. sandwich bag)
- Waterproof field book and field book paper.
- Permanent felt marker (Sharpie fine point or equivalent).
- Pocket tape (at least 3 meters).
- Rain gear and suitable footwear.

Recommended:

- ‘Surveying Principles and Applications’ 9th Edition (2015) by B.F. Kavanagh. Prentice Hall. https://tnigatu.files.wordpress.com/2012/04/surveying-edn5-kavanagh-bird.pdf (5th Ed)
- https://tnigatu.files.wordpress.com/2012/04/surveying-edn5-kavanagh-bird.pdf (5th Ed)
- ‘Elementary Surveying an Introduction to Geomatics’ 13th Edition (2011) by Wolf and Ghilani, Prentice Hall.
- Surveying for Mining I. This is a basic surveying manual. Instructor to distribute as softcopy
- Any other recent elementary surveying book from the BCIT library.

Safety equipment or protective clothing

- Enclosed footwear required for field labs (boots with ankle support recommended).
- Hi-Visibility safety vest.
- Weather appropriate clothing recommended (i.e. warm clothing and rain gear).
