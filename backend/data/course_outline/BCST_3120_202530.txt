## Course Description
This modular course is designed to build upon the visual storytelling skills that students developed in previous production courses. Through the various modules the students will learn aesthetic and technical production requirements to produce single-camera narrative productions, live-streamed and live-recorded multi-camera switched productions, and local television newscasts including news-gathering camera operation and editing. Focus is given to the preparation and planning, teamwork, and technical knowledge and ability (lighting, camera operation, audio, post-production, technical facility set-up, etc.) that goes into these various types of production.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Discuss the role of aesthetic elements in the effectiveness and impact of single-camera narrative video projects and multi-camera productions.
- With artistic guidance, create a shooting plan that demonstrates creative pre-visualization of a short script or script segment.
- With limited instructor supervision, create breakdowns of elements necessary to execute a faithful visual interpretation of a short script or script segment.
- With instructor guidance, plan, organize, execute, and manage single-camera narrative productions and multi-camera productions to a deadline, including preparation of all necessary production documentation.
- Use aesthetic elements such as lighting, direction, camera operation, audio, and post-production techniques in the creation of narrative video projects that present story structure and mood, demonstrating an intermediate level of technical and creative skills.
- Use aesthetic elements such as lighting, direction, camera operation, and audio in the creation of multi-camera productions, demonstrating an introductory level of technical and creative skills.
- Plan and execute casting calls and auditions without instructor supervision.
- Individually and as a member of a production crew, execute a complete production plan to produce a single-camera narrative production, with direct instructor supervision.
- Under direct instructor supervision, operate to an introductory level all studio and mobile production equipment associated with a multi-camera production.
- Participate in a television production crew and, with limited supervision, demonstrate the relationships between the various roles involved in producing a local television production.
- Under limited supervision, prepare and troubleshoot the technical facilities of a television studio or small mobile production facility (flypack) to produce a local television production.
- Perform under instructor supervision, and to an intermediate level of proficiency, a number of production crew positions required to produce a multi-camera production or a local television newscast.
- Work as a team member in a multi-camera production environment, or local television production environment, to solve problems or find creative solutions as needed in order to ensure the successful creation of a multi-camera production or local television newscast.
- Using professional cameras, compose proper framing.
- Using professional cameras, record broadcast quality video demonstrating correct exposure and focus.
- Choose the most appropriate microphone and microphone placement to capture broadcast quality audio.
- Using current professional news-gathering cameras, record broadcast quality audio from various sources.
- Visually tell a story by recording and editing sequenced shots.
