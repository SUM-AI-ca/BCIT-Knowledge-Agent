## Course Description
Using knowledge of relational practice and an ethic of care, this course will focus learners on furthering the development and integration of their relational practice in complex practice situations across diverse contexts. Learners will explore being in-relation with clients experiencing significant issues (e.g., violence, addiction) through a lens of trauma informed practice and harm reduction. Additionally, learners will explore being in-relation with clients, colleagues, and policymakers as they learn and practice skills of change (e.g. influence, negotiation, conflict resolution, engendering partnership and facilitating empowerment) and critically analyze opportunities and challenges in these situations. This is a blended course, both online and experiential, where learners will examine relevant theories, methods, and research as well as participate in interactive activities, reflective exercises, discussions and practice experiences designed to deepen and consolidate their skills of communication and collaboration.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will meet the following competencies:

Advocate

- Examines and challenges the influence of power within organizational, social, economic and political contexts that influence health and well-being within the context of nurses’ role in the delivery of care
- Promotes acceptance of diversity and supports client empowerment to reduce stigma

Collaborator

- Demonstrates the ability to collaborate with diverse clients, adapt relational approaches appropriately, and accommodate varying contextual factors in diverse practice situations
- Works to prevent and resolve conflict, and to negotiate solutions to conflict situations in interactions with clients and members of the healthcare team

Communicator

- Communicates effectively with clients and healthcare team members to support safe, high quality, client-centred health care in diverse situations and contexts

Decision Maker

- Independently plans nursing care with clients, within the context of critical inquiry and relational practice, that integrates knowledge from nursing, health sciences and other related disciplines as well as knowledge from practice experiences, client knowledge, patterns and preferences, and influences within the health care context
- Effectively manages complex clinical scenarios and time sensitive situations

Global Citizen

- Practices culturally safe and equitable nursing care in diverse contexts and situations

Leader

- Promotes teamwork and garners commitment and participation of others that will optimize nursing practice and client care
- Facilitates organizational change initiatives

Professional

- Displays initiative, confidence and self-awareness in nursing practice
