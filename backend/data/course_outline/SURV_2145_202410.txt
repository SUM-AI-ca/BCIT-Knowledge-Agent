## Course Description
Covers computations and adjustments of integrated traverses; triangulation; trigonometric leveling; horizontal curves; road and building layouts, drill pattern layouts; cut and fill, area and volume calculations; introduction to total stations; introduction to GPS theory and applications.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Use a total station to measure angles and distances [5,6,7,8].
- Complete traverse observations; perform associated computations and make adjustments [1,2,3,6,7].
- Perform cross sectioning [2,3,4,6,7].
- Perform area and volume calculations [2,3,4,6,7].
- Carry out basic GPS surveys [5,6,7,8,11].
- Keep proper notes for survey fieldwork [7,8,9].

## Learning Resources
Required: 1. Course notes will be provided by the instructor. 2. Pocket calculator with trigonometric functions, square roots, and h.hhhh to h.m.s conversions (DMS <> DD). 3. Basic personal field gear, such as 2H/3H pencils, straight edge, safety vest, sturdy boots with ankle support. Warm and weather resistant clothing is highly recommended. 4. Field note book (loose leaf waterproof type) columns on one side and grid on reverse preferred.

Recommended: Elementary Surveying: an Introduction to Geomatics, 12th or 13th edition, P. Wolf & C. Ghilani, Prentice Hall. Fundamentals of Surveying by M.O. Schmidt and K.W.Wong. P.S.W. Publishers. Surveying 4th Edition by J. McCormic. Prentice Hall, New Jersey. Surveying Principles and Applications 5th Edition (2000) by B.F.Kavanagh and S.J.Glenn Bird. Prentice Hall, New Jersey. Surveying for Mining I, (2016) Weston.

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to: - Perform calculations concerning directions, including compass bearings. - Compute coordinates by bearing and distance. - Use a surveyors level to measure and transfer elevations. - Use a total station to measure basic horizontal and vertical angles, and to take distance measurements. - Carry out traverse observations and complete associated computations and adjustments. - Perform area calculations. - Perform volume calculations, including cross-sections. - Understand the basics of GPS. - Conduct survey operations in a safe and efficient manner, consistent with current.

## Attendance Requirements
It is very important for all students to attend the field labs as work will be done in teams. Advance notice must be given for any absence from a field lab.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
- Students are required to wear a high visibility vest and proper outdoor footwear during field labs.
- A water resistant jacket is highly recommended for outdoor labs. Labs run regardless of weather.

## Other information
This course emphasizes safe work practices. Special safety protocols are in place for the course field labs. We will discuss safety further as we approach the first scheduled field lab, including any required PPE, safe distancing, sanitizing, as well as the more common safety hazards surveyors may encounter in the field.

The course also expects the prompt completion of any work assigned. Labs and other assignments shall be submitted to the LH dropbox and submitted by the due date.
