## Course Description
​Provides practical and theoretical information required to inspect existing houses for visible defects. This covers 6 systems of a house that must be inspected as per the standards. It is the first of a series of courses that meet the academic requirements for house inspection and the qualifications for House Inspector Licensing Regulations under Consumer Protection BC (CPBC).​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Have a basic knowledge about the first 6 systems in a house to be reviewed as per the “Standards of Practice" for a house inspector.
- Understand the responsibility of a house inspector and their limitations.​

## Course Goals
The course goal is to provide students with an understanding of the methods of inspecting existing houses through a basic knowledge of the various components that make up a house. It will cover the first six systems required by the Standards of Practice for house inspections. The remaining systems will be covered in House Two. This course is also valuable for those individuals in the housing industry who do not wish to pursue a career as a house inspector but want to know more about identifying issues related to houses. This course meets the qualifications for House Inspector Licensing Regulations under Consumer Protection BC (CPBC) .

Note: BLDG2915 & BLDG2925 (House 1 & 2) can be taken at the same time but some students have found the workload too much for one term and have asked the instructor to pass this on to future students. Obviously this will vary from student to student based on each individuals background.
