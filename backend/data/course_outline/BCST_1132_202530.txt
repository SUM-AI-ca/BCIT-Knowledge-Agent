## Course Description
This course explores the fundamentals of audio storytelling. Students will use broadcast equipment and editing software to produce engaging short and long-form content. Class time will be divided between classroom instruction and hands-on lab experience. Journalistic standards and principles apply for all content in this course.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
​Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify the basic functions of a soundboard.
- Demonstrate simple audio mixing techniques.
- Apply effective sound gathering and field recording techniques.
- Apply an effective understanding of audio editing.
- Construct focused and engaging story pitches.
- Apply basic interviewing skills.

## Learning Resources
Students will require access to a smartphone with a data plan. Students will also need a microphone and cable adapter for voice and field recordings. Suggested microphone models and cable requirements will be provided in class.
