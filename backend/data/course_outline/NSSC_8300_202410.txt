## Course Description
This on-line course offers students the opportunity to develop their leadership knowledge and skills within the context of specialty nursing. Leadership is explored from multiple perspectives with an emphasis on contemporary theories and frameworks. Topics that are examined include followership, contextual influences, power, navigating change and transitions, and teambuilding. Students engage in a work related leadership project to apply leadership knowledge and skills to their practice.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
As a healthcare practitioner, you are expected to follow professional standards of practice throughout all SN programs and courses. In this course, you will have opportunities to use the processes of critical thinking, systematic inquiry, communication, collaboration, leadership, professionalism, and clinical decision-making. Upon successful completion of this course, you should be able to:

- Analyze contemporary theories of leadership.
- Demonstrate knowledge of the complementary roles between leaders, followers, and managers.
- Analyze contextual influences on leadership.
- Identify personsal leadership strengths.
- Articulate personal sources of power related to nursing.
- Demonstrate effective communication and collaboration with stakeholders in the context of a leadership project.
- Apply relevant change and transition strategies to a leadership project.
- Critically reflect on teambuilding abilities.
- Articulate ethical aspects of leadership.
- Critically reflect on leadership development.
