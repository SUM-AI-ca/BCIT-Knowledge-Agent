## Course Description
This course allows those with little or no knowledge of financial management to study the various methods of optimizing the economic position of a firm. It provides students with a conceptual understanding of the financial decision-making process in a business context and an appreciation of the impact that financial decisions have on value creation by focussing on the management and allocation of financial resources within the firm.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify the structure of capital markets and their function within the Canadian economy.
- Describe the concept of shareholder value maximization.
- Identify the role of valuation in the capital markets using a basic securities valuation model.
- Appraise a firm's financial performance using ratios based on financial statements.
- Evaluate projected financial statements to make managerial decisions.
- Examine the trade-offs involved in current asset management.
- Identify the characteristics of different sources of short term and long term financing.
- Construct a capital budgeting framework and interpret its results.
- Apply cash budgeting as a manager in a business entity.
- Analyze cash flows in a valuation context using time value of money concepts.

## Course Goals
This course is designed to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of key financial concepts and their application within the context of capital markets and business decision-making. By the end of the course, students will be able to identify the structure and function of capital markets in the Canadian economy, as well as understand the principle of shareholder value maximization. They will gain proficiency in securities valuation models and financial ratio analysis to assess a firm's performance. The course will also provide students with the skills to evaluate projected financial statements for managerial decision-making, while exploring the trade-offs in current asset management. Additionally, students will learn about various short- and long-term financing sources, construct and interpret capital budgeting frameworks, and apply cash budgeting strategies. Finally, they will be able to analyze cash flows using time value of money concepts to inform valuation and business management decisions.
