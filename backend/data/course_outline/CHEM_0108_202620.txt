### Course Description
​This is a four-week course that provides the student with a review of the core topics covered in BC Chemistry 11. This course is ideal for students preparing to write the BCIT Chemistry 11 Challenge Exam (CHEM 0110), students who already have Chemistry 11 credit but need a review before entering a BCIT program, or anyone who wants to review the material in BC Chemistry 11. An online tutorial is scheduled with every offering of this course; the specific day/date and time the tutorial will be held is indicated within the Notes section of each offering. Instructor assistance by email or video-conference will also be available. Please note: This course is intended for those who have already taken BC Chemistry 11 or the equivalent. This course is not for credit and does not provide Chemistry 11 transfer credit or prerequisite status. If a student requires Chemistry 11 upgrading or prerequisite credit for a BCIT program they will need to write the Chemistry 11 Challenge Exam (CHEM 0110). Students must review their program entrance requirements to determine the minimum acceptable exam grade.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand basic chemical principles and theories.
- Solve chemistry numerical problems using calculations and equations.
- Connect chemical principles to real world applications.

### Course topics
The course is presented in eight learning modules: 1. Math review for chemistry (including SI units, unit conversions, significant figures in measurement) 2. The Nature of Matter (physical and chemical properties and changes, classificaiton of matter, naming of inorganic compounds) 3. The Mole concept (including molarity and dilution) 4. Chemical Changes: A Closer Look (writing and balancing chemical equations, types of chemical reactions, predicting products of some chemical reactions) 5. Stoichiometry (cacluations involving chemical reactions, including reactions in solution) 6. Atoms and the Periodic Table (including nuclide notation the quantum model of the atom, electron configurations, and Lewis structures) 7. Organic Chemistry (A brief introduction to this important branch of chemistry) 8. Green Chemistry (A special topic relevant to many industry applications)
