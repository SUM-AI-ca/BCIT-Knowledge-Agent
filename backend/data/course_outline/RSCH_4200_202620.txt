### Course Description
In this course, students will explore and critically evaluate published research on chosen topics in the field of medical imaging. Students will also learn various research methodologies, how to employ an evidence-based approach to healthcare and the principles of research ethics. Students will conduct their own systematic literature review to apply the theoretical knowledge learned from this course. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify and explain the research processes used in evidence-based practice implemented in health care settings.
- Identify basic principles of research ethics (Tri-Council Policy Statement)
- Critiquing and analyzing research characteristics.
- Search for relevant sources related to research topic using appropriate databases.
- Critically evaluate, summarize, and synthesize research sources relevant to research topics
- Apply discipline-specific citation style when writing to avoid plagiarism.
- Produce a systematic literature review.

Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools
AI (artificial intelligence) tools may be used in a limited way to complete assignments in this course. See each assignment for details on acceptable use (e.g. brainstorming, editing) or inquire with your instructor in advance of submission. Assignments are designed to help you build skills and understanding. Please note that AI tools cannot learn for you.
AI tools offer benefits when used wisely. You must use AI tools responsibly and ethically and acknowledge use in all work submitted for a grade. Please note that AI output may include incorrect or misleading information, fabricated authors/dates/publications, or faked works. You are always responsible for accuracy and quality. Material directly copied from any source (e.g. websites, books, AI tools) must be cited. Other uses (e.g., ideas, brainstorming, designing, paraphrasing) must be acknowledged including the sources. Undeclared use of AI is a violation of the academic integrity policy as it presents work or ideas created by tools or other people as your own.
