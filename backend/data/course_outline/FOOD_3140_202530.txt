## Course Description
​This Food Analysis course will introduce students to good laboratory practices, representative samples, and to a set of methods used to monitor and/or verify in a food manufacturer is producing to meets their food safety, quality, and regulatory requirements. Basic data analysis techniques will also be performed.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate safe laboratory practices.
- Demonstrate appropriate food sampling techniques to create a representative sample.
- Measure water activity in various food types after calibration of the instrument has been checked.
- Perform and analyze a proximate analysis of a food; measure moisture, ash, fat, protein, and calculate carbohydrates.
- Measure moisture, pH and titratable acidity.
- Describe enzymes and their significance in food systems and food analysis.
- Utilize linear regression techniques for analyzing data.
- Explain how food analysis techniques are integrated into the monitoring and verification activities of food safety and quality programs.
