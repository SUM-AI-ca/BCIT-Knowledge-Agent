### Course Description
BCIT Aviation corporate training solutions can be customized to meet your organization’s workforce training needs. BCIT can deliver a range of training from one-hour workshops to multi-day sessions. Accommodations can also be made to train one individual or a full team at a BCIT campus, at your workplace, or online. Learn from BCIT corporate training instructors who are experts in their field. Let’s work together to provide your staff with gap training and life-long learning opportunities for them to excel in your organization.​

### Course Goals
This course is designed to provide students with the theoretical understanding and practical skills needed to identify, install and remove rivets.
### Evaluation Criteria
Criteria | % | Comments
Practical Project | 100 | The passing grade for this course is pass or fail. To achieve pass the student must demonstrate competency in assembly and disassembly of various rivets based on instructor assessment.
Attendance | 100 | Attendance is mandatory to receive the Certificate of Completion. Any absences must be reported to MTU and the BCIT Instructor
### Safety equipment or protective clothing
BCIT approved coveralls, safety glasses/safety goggles, light duty work gloves (nylon woven gloves with rubberized palms, not leather!).
### Course Schedule and Assignments
Four eight-hour days with each day divided in five ninty-minute sessions with fifteen-minute breaks in between the sessions, and a one-hour long lunch break between the second and the third sessions. For the first three days, training days are broken down to; three hours of theory and four hours and thirty minutes of shop time. The fourth day will be spent entirely in the shop.
### Course topics
1. Solid Rivets
1.1 Drilling
1.2 Rivet Layout Rules
1.3 Material Clamp Up
1.4 Clecos
1.5 Rivet Gun Types
1.6 Rivet Sets
1.7 Rivet Snap Force
1.8 Bucking Bars
1.9 Rivet Squeezers
1.10 Solid Rivets Installation and Removal
1.11 Inspection Standards
2 Blind Fasteners and Their Types
2.1 Blind Fasteners Identification
2.2 Blind Fasteners Installation and Removal
2.3 NAS 523 Rivet Coding System
