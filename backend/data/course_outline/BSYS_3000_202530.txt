### Course Description
​In this course, students will engage in an Integrated Entrepreneurial Project (IEP) which will be continued in BSYS 4000 Cloud Business Development 2. From the creation of a business idea to the construction of an e-commerce-enabled website, students will create a Cloud-based Internet presence which will culminate in a viable online business. Using the Lean Startup methodology, students will develop a business plan, a business model, and a technical design. In addition, students will research and manage business and regulatory requirements. Web design and development skills such as markup languages (HTML), dynamic Web application frameworks, and scripting languages will be used to build an online business web application.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Design and create an online business web application.
- Prepare a business plan and business model for a Cloud-based business.
- Gather business requirements for an Internet business presence.
- Determine and manage business, regulatory, and financial requirements for their business application.
- Create the technical design for a Cloud-based e-commerce-enabled website.
- Build an operational online business web application.

### Course Specific Requirements
Students will be expected to keep up with all required readings, projects, and assignments. Late assignments will not be accepted for marking. If a student is ill, a medical note will be required to obtain credit for the missed assignment. Assignments must be done on an individual basis unless otherwise specified by the instructor.
This course features a major team-based project. All team members usually receive the same mark. However, marks can be redistributed if one or more team members contribute less than their fair share. This may include one or more of the following:
- frequently absent from team meetings or classes
- submitting plagiarized or low effort work
- missing team deadlines or class deadlines
- disruptive behavior during team meetings
Your team will need to document infractions, escalating the issue to your instructor before the assignment is due. The instructor will then guide your team through the mark redistribution process. In this process, the team will have two options:
Option 1: If all members of the group agree that grades should be reallocated, up to 40% will be taken off one student’s score and redistributed to the other students.
Option 2: If group members do not reach agreement on how marks should be reallocated, each team member’s contributions will be marked separately.
