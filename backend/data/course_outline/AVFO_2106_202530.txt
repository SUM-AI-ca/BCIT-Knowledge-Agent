### Course Description
The focus of this course is a comprehensive study of the applicable security regulations in the Canadian Aeronautics Act (Aerodrome and Air Carrier measures) and its implications in designing and managing air carrier and airport security systems. The following topics will include access control, screening techniques, investigative procedures, customs and immigration procedures, limitations of authority and legal responsibilities.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the national and international legal standards for aviation security.
- Locate and interpret the various regulations of the Aeronautics Act and Criminal Code used for security.
- Describe the principles governing the security requirements of an air carrier.
- Processes involved in handling a security threat.

### Course topics
- MODULE 1 - Development of National and International Aviation Security Regulations and Law
- MODULE 1A - 9/11
- MODULE 2 - Canadian Aviation Security Regulations
- MODULE 3 - U.S. Aviation Security Regulations
- MODULE 4 - European Aviation Security Regulations
- MODULE 5 - Ground Security Measures
- MODULE 6 - Airport Security Threats and Breaches
- MODULE 7 - Weapons and Sabotage Devices at Airports
- MODULE 8 - Detection and Handling of Illicit Narcotic Drugs
- MODULE 9 - Air Carrier Security and Security Measures
- MODULE 10 - Airport Security Planning
