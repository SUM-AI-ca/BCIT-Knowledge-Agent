## Course Description
​Students will complete a team project that involves preparing and presenting a technical report on a proposed mining project. This report will be centered around an actual mineral deposit/property and will integrate collected field and laboratory data with additional project information provided to the students. Topics to be studied may include aspects of: deposit geology, exploration methods, resource estimates, mine planning and design, mineral processing, site layout, environmental sustainability, project cost estimates and economics. Each team is responsible for the preparation of a succinct, well written, and technically accurate project report. Teams will also complete an oral presentation and discussion of their study results.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Work as members of a team preparing, presenting and discussing a technical report on a mining project.
- Demonstrate teamwork, project management and research skills.
- Apply interdisciplinary knowledge to solve complex engineering problems.
- Demonstrate engineering knowledge in application of economic, health and safety, environmental sustainability among other interdisciplinary concepts.
- Evaluate and describe the geology for the project.
- Design mine and site layouts for the project.

## Course topics
Deliverables: Submission of Technical Report and Oral Presentation

1) The team will deliver a completed study report. It must contain all relevant information generated during the study period about the project and the team’s conclusions on its technical.

2) Each team member(s) will be responsible for producing the section of the report relevant to his/her role on the team. Some sections such as costing may require the contributions of ALL team members.

3) You will be required to give an oral presentation on the results of your study to a panel made up of members of BCIT staff and other invitees.
