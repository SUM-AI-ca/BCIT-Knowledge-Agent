## Course Description
This course examines the use of rapid methods for measuring the presence of chemical constituents in various food products. Students will learn how to incorporate these methods into a food manufacturer's food safety and quality program. Lipid rancidity mechanisms and measurements will be discussed. A non-microbial shelf life validation study, building on the techniques learned in the microbiologically based shelf life study performed in FOOD 2020, will be performed. . Students will write a report that presents scientific results to the management team of a food plant.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Utilize an enzymatic method to measure the level of a vitamin in a food product.
- Perform a rapid method for detecting the presence of an allergen.
- Perform and document a shelf life validation study examining lipid degradation.
- Explain the role of these methods in food safety and quality programs.
- Develop a presentation documenting the findings of the shelf life validation study that is appropriate for a management team.
