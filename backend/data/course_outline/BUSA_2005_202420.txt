## Course Description
Presents both traditional and contemporary perspectives of modern management examining management and management roles in teams, projects, departments and the organization as a whole. Strategic planning, operational planning, leading, organizing and controlling for performance will be addressed. Through actual business scenarios, cases and exercises, participants gain experience in decision-making and applying theory to real world organizations.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the changing role and functions of management in business.
- Critically evaluate the role of managers in today's business.
- Assess the impacts of alternative management approaches on an organization.
- Analyze and evaluate business situations involving management issues and develop appropriate responses to improve the situation by:
- Researching and interpreting business situations
- Applying commonly used models and methodologies for analysis
- Developing appropriate alternatives and solutions
- Presenting recommendations and an action plan.
- Apply a decision-making methodology to improve the quality of management decisions.
- Work effectively as a team member.
- Make effective verbal and written presentations.

Once the course is completed, 4 posts from the various topics will be evaluated as shown below. You will have the opportunity to select 2 of the posts that will be assessed, and I will select the other 2 randomly. ALL posts must be completed. Missing a topic’s release time means a zero on that specific discussion (also classified as not attended); You may prepare the discussion in word, then past back into the board before the window closes. Individual submissions due by NOON and peer commenting before the 8PM cut-off for the week. See schedule, activites tab and class activities each week.

o Contribution Level One (50-60%): Basic sharing of your thoughts on the question in 2 or 3 paragraphs for a given post; the minimal level expected. If the post is incomplete (content and or comments), that will reduce this mark proportionally.

o Contribution Level Two (60-70%): Level One and includes additional use of course concepts and theories being closely tied into your posts. Use and knowledge of course language is key!

o Contribution Level Three (70-80%): Level Two and with deeper discussion with peers above the minimum requirements

o Contribution Level Three (80-90%+): Levels One + Two +3 and includes the recommendation of URLs or business articles that you have found that are related to the topic of discussion.
