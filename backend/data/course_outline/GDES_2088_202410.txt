## Course Description
In today’s changing creative environment, designers must do more than simply ‘design things.’ Designers apply visual communication principles to express their client's message, but they must also work within the context of a business strategy and goals to be fully successful. This course examines how design supports and reinforces a client’s business goals by applying critical components of the design process: research, analysis, concept development and evaluation. You will also explore standard business practices and how to work effectively with clients. Principles, concepts, and techniques will be applied through the comprehensive development of a large and major campaign project. You will analyze and evaluate course work through discussion, critiques, and verbal and written presentations.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Conduct basic research required for establishing project goals.
- Identify and use a range of research approaches.
- Identify resources for implementing more comprehensive research.
- Define a project’s goals and target audience in the context of a business’ goals.
- Write a professional business research report.
- Write a creative proposal for a presentation.
- Write key messages for campaigns.
- Work effectively with a client by implementing a process to achieve project goals.
- Develop creative concepts that are in line with business marketing strategy.
- Analyze and evaluate a design concept within a strategic campaign through development of a concept proposal.
- Implement standard business practices and ethics relating to the design profession.

## Course Goals
• To provide students with an understanding of how research fits into the design process and give them the skills to conduct research • To provide students with the skills that will allow them to design strategically, taking into consideration the client, project context and goals, and the client’s audience • To provide students with basic information about business and professional practices common in the field of graphic design
