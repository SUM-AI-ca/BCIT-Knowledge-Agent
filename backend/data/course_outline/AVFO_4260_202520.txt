## Course Description
This course is the final instructions preparation for flight test to standards (TP9939E) in group 1 (multi engine). The student will also complete all licensing requirements of the Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL).

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
The student will be able to:

- Apply the knowledge and skills to fly in instrument conditions and apply the theoretical knowledge to the multi IFR flight test.
- Demonstrate the required knowledge to prepare for an instrument flight test.
- Demonstrate the required knowledge to pass the Transport Canada Flight Test.
- Complete all licencing requirements and be issued a commercial pilot licence
