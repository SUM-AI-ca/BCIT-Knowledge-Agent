### Course Description
This is the first course in a series of eight. This course introduces you to an integrated and holistic approach to maintenance and asset management. It provides a perspective and structure to apply to an organization to see how the organization performance can be improved through areas of maintenance and asset management. It will introduce: the Maintenance Excellence Pyramid, how the parts of the Maintenance Excellent Pyramid are linked and a simple tool and more detailed one to evaluate and better understand an organizational position relative to the areas of the Maintenance Excellence Pyramid. Individuals who successfully complete all eight courses are eligible to apply to PEMAC Asset Management Association of Canada, for the “Maintenance Management Professional" (MMP) certification and the right to use “MMP" designation behind their name. Renewal requirements to maintain the certification include demonstrating a minimum standard of continuing professional development (CPD), and payment of recertification fees.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- ​Understand the key concepts of maintenance and asset management and how they work together to support organizational goals.
- Recognize the importance of aligning maintenance strategies with broader business goals to optimize organizational success.
- Gain an overview of the Maintenance Excellence Pyramid and its role in improving maintenance processes.
- Explore how data and systems, like CMMS and EAM, can support better maintenance decisions and asset reliability.
- Understand how strategy shapes maintenance and asset management decisions and impacts cost and performance over the asset lifecycle.
- Explore fundamental maintenance tactics like preventive and predictive maintenance to keep assets reliable and reduce failures.
- Get introduced to the basics of work management, including how to plan, schedule, and execute maintenance tasks effectively.
- Gain an understanding of tools like CMMS and ERP for organizing maintenance processes and improving efficiency.
- Get familiar with Reliability Centered Maintenance (RCM) as a proactive method to address asset reliability and performance.
- Learn about continuous improvement approaches, such as Preventive Maintenance Optimization and Root Cause Analysis, to enhance maintenance practices over time.
- Understand the importance of evidence-based decision-making in asset management and its role in improving strategies and outcomes.
- Learn about the role of information management and governance in ensuring accurate, reliable data to support maintenance and asset management decisions.
- Recognize asset management as a coordinated effort across the organization, focusing on long-term value, sustainability, and alignment with standards like ISO 55000.​ ​​​
