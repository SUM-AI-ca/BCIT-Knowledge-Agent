## Course Description
Examines in detail the design, planning and operation of underground mines. Mining methods for different materials will be discussed with the emphasis on hard rock mining. The criteria for correct selection of underground mining methods will be presented in the context of orebody characteristics, required productivity and safety issues. Topics will include: general mine design, ground support requirements and techniques, sizes and types of underground openings, production planning, development and production cycles, mining equipment, mine ventilation and mine infrastructure.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Select an appropriate underground mining method for mineral deposits with different ore body characteristics [1,2].
- Determine the best methods of access to underground workings and the major development required [1,2]
- Design simple underground mine layouts [[1,4,5]
- Determine production capacities for different mining methods [1,2]
- Select appropriately matched mining equipment for underground mines [1,3]Calculate equipment productivities and fleets sizes [1,2,5]
- Design a simple blast pattern for an underground mining method and correctly time the blast [1,4,5]
- Interpret simple ventilation networks and calculate ventilation requirements for safe working conditions [1,3].
- Identify the different contaminant gases within a mine environment [1,9].
- Select correct gas testing methods and equipment [1,3].
- Determine the mine services required for an underground mine and their design and function [1,3,6]
- Select appropriate materials handling methods and perform simple calculations for hoisting and conveying ore [1.2,4].
