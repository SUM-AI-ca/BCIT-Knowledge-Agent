### Course Description
This course investigates the response and design of structures to earthquake loading. Topics include earthquake ground motion and response of single-degree-of-freedom systems to earthquake loading, response spectra and the response spectrum analysis of multi-degree-of-freedom systems; seismic design philosophy and analysis methods per the National Building Code of Canada (NBCC); and application of capacity design.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the seismicity and ground motion parameters used in Canada for design. [4]
- Explain the behaviour of single-degree-of-freedom systems under earthquake ground motions. [1, 2]
- Develop response spectra for various earthquake time histories using software. [2, 5]
- Determine dynamic characteristics of multi-degree-of-freedom systems. [2, 5]
- Analyse structures using the modal analysis procedure in the NBCC seismic design provisions. [2, 5]
- Perform seismic analysis of single and multi-storey buildings using the equivalent static method and dynamic methods in the NBCC seismic design provisions. [2, 5]
- Explain the procedure of seismic design of steel and reinforced concrete frame buildings. [4]
- Perform design and detailing of reinforced concrete frames and shear walls for seismic loading according to CSA A23.3 seismic requirements. [4]
- Explain the seismic retrofit strategies for steel and concrete buildings. [4, 12]
