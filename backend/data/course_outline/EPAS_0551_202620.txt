### Course Description
​​​​This course is designed for students with the English proficiency at the Independent/Proficient User level (CEFR High B2 - Low C1). This course is aimed at supporting learners in transitioning successfully into their BCIT programs. The course also provides opportunities for individuals to improve their reading and writng skills for successful participation in the workplace. This course consolidates the language competencies learned in BCIT programs , such as those in trades, sciences, business and technology environments in the program and raises the stakes in terms of how effectively learners use the language.Specifically, learners will read and evaluate a variety of advanced, authentic technical academic texts for comprehension and write a variety of genres typical of their target mainstream learning environment at BCIT and in the workplace. Learners will read course documents, instructor communications, and lengthy textbook material so as to identify course expectations and synthesize content for assignments.Learners will use advanced research skills to produce an essay/report related to their target discipline within a 21st-century workplace context​. In the last half of the course, learners will collaborate to produce a formal proposal for a volunteer service learning project by evaluating past technical initiatives, developing and pitching an idea, and then collaborating F2F/online to write a detailed proposal as a team. Experiential learning, intercultural competence, and academic study strategies are embedded in the course. Learners will increase the range of B2-C1 vocabulary and grammatical features they understand and will use B1-B2 level features accurately in their writing.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Read a complex, formal text in order to identify essential information about project work expectations, including implicit as well as explicit instructor attitudes, emotions and opinions
- Scan quickly through several informational and argumentative technical academic texts (articles, reports, websites, books, etc.) in order to identify relevance and usefulness of particular sections of texts
- Demonstrate detailed understanding of complex informational and argumentative texts of 1200 words or more with opportunities to re-read difficult sections and use dictionaries or other reference materials
- Write a descriptive-evaluative report of 500 words in response to a specific question, issue, or initiative
- Write a short description of a process, system, strategy, or idea
- Participate with classmates in a project requiring collaborative writing and redrafting using web-based tools that require negotiation, explanation of details, and the following and relaying of instructions to reach a goal
- Describe and evaluate the viewpoints and practices of own and other social groups, showing awareness of how judgments and prejudices are frequently based​

### Other information
Generative AI Use
Generally, the use of AI is not allowed for assignments in this course. If the use of AI tools (e.g. Chat GPT) is permitted for a part of an assignment, I’ll indicate that in the assignment instructions. When AI tools are permitted and you use them, you are responsible for ensuring that the content you submit under your name adheres to the course’s expectations. You must also acknowledge use of AI tools, e.g.
I acknowledge the use of [name the tool] in the preparation of my assignment.
Using AI tools without authorization is a violation of BCIT’s Student Code of Academic Integrity .
### Course Schedule and Assignments
Scenario 1 Main Tasks:
- Write a personal narrative on the challenges you have faced and overcome so far using the Hero's Journey structure.
- Write a brief on course expectations based on information in a course outline and from research online.
- Read textbook chapters and write a 1-page summary of a chapter excerpt for a study group.
Scenario 2 Main Tasks:
- Create a personal vision statement.
- Read an excerpt of a report on the future of work and answer related questions.
- Write a report on the alignment of your own job pathway with future workplace needs.
Scenario 3 Main Tasks:
- Research and discuss a range of work-integrated learning (WIL) projects that address real-world workplace, community, social or sustainability-related needs.
- Do a variety of vocabulary, grammar and writing exercises that will help to create the main written assignment
- Collaboratively plan, write, and revise a proposal for a WIL project to address a real-world workplace, community, social or sustainability-related issue, using online collaboration tools.
