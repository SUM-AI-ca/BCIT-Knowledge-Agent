## Course Description
Introduces basic inorganic chemistry. Topics include chemical bonding, stoichiometry, formula writing, solution preparation, oxidation and reduction, acid-base theory, titration calculations and buffer solutions. Laboratory exercises consist of qualitative and quantitative analysis. Good laboratory techniques including WHMIS are emphasised.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Identify specific ionic or molecular compounds with their formulas.
- List name and charges of common cations and anions.
- Describe the types of bonding that can exist between atoms.
- Relate the position of elements in the periodic table to physical and chemical properties.
- Write balanced equations for simple chemical reactions including reduction-oxidation reactions.
- Solve for quantities of product given quantities of reactants.
- Calculate the concentration of solutions using mass and volume values.
- Explain the concept of acids and bases.
- Calculate the acidities of solutions of acids, bases and salts.

## Course Goals
In this class you will:

- gain an understanding of basic chemical principles and methods
- acquire the knowledge and skills to perform chemical calculations.
- acquire essential laboratory skills in liquid and solid measurements, volumetric and gravimetric analysis, basic chemical reactivity, and acid/base chemistry.
- gain an appreciation of the influence in chemistry on Food Technology and in your daily life
- learn to think critically about chemical issues

## Course topics
The Study of Chemical Measurements · significant figures and units · dimensional analysis
The Structure of Atoms · atomic structure · atomic number and mass number · isotopes and atomic weight · electronic structure of atoms · electronic configurations
Chemical Compounds · Lewis theory of chemical bonding · ions and ionic compounds · molecular compounds · polar covalent bonds and electronegativity · polar molecules · binary molecular compounds and acids
Stoichiometry · formula and molecular weights · Avogadro’s number and the mole . types of chemical reactions . chemical equations · calculations using balanced equations · limiting reactants and excess reactants · percent yield
Solutions · solutes and solvents · the solution process · solubility · factors affecting volubility · solution concentrations (molarity, percent, parts per million, normality) · ions in solutions: electrolytes · equivalents and milliequivalents · colligative properties
Acids and Bases · properties of acids and bases · Arrhenius and Brønsted-Lowry definitions · autoionization of water · pH scale, working with pH · strong and weak acids · strong and weak bases · dissociation constants . buffer solutions · acid-base titrations · acid and base equivalents · acidity and basicity of salt solutions
Properties of Liquids and Solids · phase changes · intermolecular forces · viscosity and surface tension · vapor pressure · crystalline and amorphous solids
Oxidation and Reduction (Electrochemistry) · oxidation numbers · oxidizing and reducing agents · balancing redox equations · normality in redox reactions · redox titrations
