## Course Description
Introduces credit and collections to persons who contemplate employment in credit and collections or need to understand the credit function. Topics include determining credit risk, credit instruments and collateral security, types of consumer credit and credit cards, sources of consumer credit information, collections, credit department management.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will:

- Assess the purpose of credit, for both the users and providers of credit.
- Evaluate the history of credit, its impact on society, and future trends involving credit.
- Evaluate a company's credit and collections requirements and develop appropriate policies and procedures.
- Evaluate credit applications from both consumers and mercantile customers and make appropriate decisions regarding the applicant.
- Determine and implement the appropriate steps for successful collections.
- Assess the functioning and efficiency of a company's credit and collections departments.

Term Paper Guidelines

This paper is intended to provide you with an opportunity to apply the concepts learned in this course. You are asked to identify a company or other organization which you can work with to discuss its credit and collection policies. Preferably, you will arrange to interview a representative from the company to discuss their policies. If that is not possible, then you should identify a company or other organization about which you can find sufficient information to complete this paper.

The paper should include the following:

- The purpose of the report

- The name of the company, the person you interviewed and their position with the company

- The type of the business – mercantile or consumer, wholesale or retail, etc.

- The credit policy, including terms and conditions, of the company. If possible, compare the company’s policy to the standard policies of their industry.

- Sources of information used by the company for its credit decisions.

- The credit application form used by the company. Preferably, you will include a sample application form. Please discuss the information on the form, especially anything you consider unusual.

- The company’s collection system:

- Outline their overall collection policy

- When do they start calling?

- When do they follow up?

- What additional steps do they take?

- What tools do they use?

- The structure of the credit department – number of staff, their positions and their responsibilities.

- How does the company measure the performance of the credit department?

- Your constructive criticism of the company’s credit and collection systems (both positive and negative) and the overall performance of the credit department.

Please note that:

- The paper should be at least 700 words long.

- It should be typed.

- Late assignments will not be accepted.
