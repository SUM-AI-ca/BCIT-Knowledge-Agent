## Course Description
The students must demonstrate they have mastered the practical applications of the program by performing a major industry project in their final term. Industry sponsors provide the real-life environment and allow the students to demonstrate the skills developed through a mix of general business and industry specific courses such as marketing, international trade, logistics, transportation, computer application, customs, management and business communications. The projects require the students to identify opportunities and utilize critical analysis and problem solving techniques toward the recommendation of cost effective solutions.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Apply the criteria for planning and conducting a thorough and professional project study.
- Develop a project plan and estimate time requirements.
- Develop a Gantt Chart.
- Develop a Terms of Reference and appropriately modify it as the scope, objective(s) and criteria of evaluation evolve from data collection.
- Develop and implement an effective method of ongoing communications (including meetings) with the project sponsor and faculty advisor.
- Demonstrate sound project management principles. (management of time, advisor relations, client relations, professionalism).

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the student will have:

· An understanding of the key functions of planning, scheduling and controlling projects

· Used a decision-making methodology to solve problems in typical business situations

· Worked in teams to analyze and present business solutions in a group setting

· Developed skills in written and verbal presentations

· Developed skills in working effectively as a member/leader in a team

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. Identify those activities which are required to successfully complete a project.

2. Analyze and evaluate business situations involving management issues and develop appropriate responses to improve the situation by:

· Researching and interpreting business situations

· Applying commonly used models and methodologies for analysis

· Developing appropriate alternatives and solutions

3. Develop an effective project plan

4. Use the basics of Gantt charts, timelines, project software in the planning, tracking and execution of projects

5. Design, prepare and negotiate a Terms of Reference.

6. Deliver innovative oral/visual presentation of information using multimedia techniques approaches on an organization

Special Case: JDC West Participation:

IBMP program students have the option to use their participation in the JDC West Competition as their T3 BCP project.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
