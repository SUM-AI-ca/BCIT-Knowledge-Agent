### Course Description
Students will explore the principles and practice of digital image formation, display, data processing, archiving, and the use of digital networks in the context of nuclear medicine applications. Emphasis is placed on decision-making to optimize image quality and evaluate the diagnostic and technical quality of images and data. Students will also gain experience appraising professional literature topics regarding image quality to assess relevance to practice.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Relate knowledge of computer systems and binary logic to applications for digitizing and storing medical images.
- Utilize digital networking and archiving systems for medical images and health information communication.
- Assess performance of medical image display devices.
- Perform image display manipulations with recognition of benefits and limitations.
- Perform image data post-processing with recognition of benefits and limitations.
- Select acquisition, reconstruction, post-processing and display parameters to optimize images and data for Nuclear Medicine procedures.
- Evaluate diagnostic quality of images and data, and respond.
- Evaluate technical quality of images and data, and respond.
- Critically appraise professional literature topics regarding image quality to assess relevance to practice.
