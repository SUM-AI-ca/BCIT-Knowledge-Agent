## Course Description
​This course continues from Molecular Genetics 1 in its coverage of the principles of genetic analysis. Includes recombinant DNA technology, gene editing, regulation of gene expression, genetics of the cell cycle, genetic development and extranuclear DNA. Also covers advanced topics in Molecular Genetics, such as its application in cancer biology.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- apply molecular biology to the study of gene regulation.
- describe and discuss genetic engineering, gene editing, and genomics.
- describe the genetic control of development.
- explain the molecular genetics of cancer.
- describe extranuclear inheritance.
- sequence DNA.
- Isolate and reverse transcribe RNA.
- perform real-time PCR and gene editing.
- perform basic bioinformatics techniques.

## Attendance Requirements
Biotechnology Attendance Requirements

Students are expected to attend all lecture and lab sessions unless they have a serious illness or other medical condition. ## Course Schedule and Assignments
Assignment Details

- Assignments will be due on assigned dates
- Assignments must be submitted by the indicated due date or a penalty of 10% per day (24 hour) will be deducted from the assignment grade
- Assignments must be done on an individual basis unless otherwise specified by the instructor.
- The lab quizzes are designed to test your understanding of the lab exercises and procedures covered in the laboratory component of the course. Lab quizzes will be administered periodically at the start of lab sessions over the course of the term.
- 5% of the term mark will be based on lab participation, which will be recorded by the successful completion of a lab skills checklist. This checklist will be initialed by the student and the instructor during each lab session. It is the responsibility of the student to ensure their checklist is initialed before leaving the lab.
- The examinations are not comprehensive and will cover all the material presented in the course since the last examination.

Use of generative AI tools (eg ChatGPT, etc.)

- Generative AI (GenAI) tools may be used in a limited way for completing assignments for this course, according to the following guidelines. It is the student’s responsibility to ensure the quality and accuracy of any GenAI output prior to submitting assignments to the instructor. Note that analysis of lab data is an important part of learning and using GenAI for data analysis does not build adequate skills and understanding. Therefore, use of GenAI for data analysis is strongly discouraged. Any use of GenAI for completion of exams and quizzes is prohibited.
- If you use a GenAI tool for any course work, such as assignments, you must acknowledge that you have used it. Follow these guidelines:
- Cite and number each use, like this [1, ChatGPT, (date)]
- Mark all generative AI output, by using “quotes” for using unedited genAI output, and ‘quotes’ for using paraphrased genAI output
- For all generative AI content, student must include primary source verification i.e. original research journal articles or chapters from traditionally published textbooks.
- Undeclared use of GenAI is a violation of academic integrity as it presents work or ideas created by tools or other people as your own. Failure to fully declare the use of GenAI will be considered a violation of BCIT academic policy. .
