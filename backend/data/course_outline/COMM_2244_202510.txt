## Course Description
Students are introduced to Standard Operating Procedure format and to the process of reviewing literature on a Food topic. This course expands the scope, complexity, and format of communication skills learned in first term. The course will cover compiling a professional career package (resume, application letter, and answers to typical job interview questions), writing reports in the workplace, and giving oral presentations. You will continue to develop your ability to work effectively as a member of a team: solving workplace problems, resolving conflicts, and running and taking part in meetings.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Plan and write business/technical reports that meet professional standards of organization, style, and presentation.
- Use an appropriate and correct writing style.
- Explain the parts and structure of a Standard Operating Procedure.
- Plan and write reports that effectively present information.
- Identify and analyze problems.
- Propose and evaluate solutions.
- Use the most effective organizational pattern for various types of informational and analytical reports.
- Explain the process for conducting a literature review on a Food topic.
- Make correct and effective use of graphics and illustrations in reports.
- Follow appropriate conventions in preparing and packaging a formal report.
- Prepare and deliver an informative and persuasive oral report.
- Prepare a professional resume and letter of application.
- Prepare and participate in an effective business meeting.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.

## Course Goals
When you have successfully completed this course, you should be able to:

- Design an effective resume and application letter to obtain job interviews.
- Analyze job interview questions and plan your responses to highlight your best skills and qualities.
- Ask good questions, practice active listening, and ask appropriate follow-up questions.
- Plan and deliver informative oral presentations using professional quality visuals.
- Organize information and write effective reports in a food industry setting.
- Plan, conduct, and participate in productive meetings.
- Collaborate on team reports and presentations.
- Adapt communication style for different audiences.
- Respect and respond appropriately to different team member personality styles.
