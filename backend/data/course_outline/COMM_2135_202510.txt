## Course Description
This course teaches the communication skills students need to plan, manage, and complete their Term-4 project reports in Chemical and Environmental Technology. Students learn how to prepare a variety of project-related documents, including project proposals and work plans, progress reports, and written and oral final project reports. They also acquire the teamwork, meeting, and interpersonal communication skills they need to collaborate effectively on a team project. Students also write job application letters and resumes and practice job interviews.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Prepare a detailed project proposal and work plan.
- Prepare a detailed literature review.
- Write an effective, job-specific resume and application letter for a position in the student’s field.
- Prepare for job interviews, and do peer and self-assessments of performance.
- Write clear technical descriptions.
- Prepare effective graphics.
- Write short, informal reports, including progress reports.
- Write short, informal reports, including progress reports.
- Deliver a professional-level oral presentation before a technical audience, including fielding questions and handling objections.
- Plan, conduct, and participate effectively in a meeting.
- Collaborate as part of a team to complete a group writing project.
- Participate effectively on a team.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.

*Professionalism: Homework and attendance are worth 10 percent of your course grade. Completing all professionalism assignments and attending classes will maintain your full professionalism mark (i.e. you will receive a ‘10-out-of-10’ for professionalism). Missing more than 10 percent of classes will result in deductions to professionalism. Not completing an assignment marked professionalism will also result in a deduction from your professionalism for that assignment. The amount deducted for each missed assignment is listed in the course schedule above. E.g. if an assignment worth 3 professionalism is not completed, 3 marks will be deducted from the 10 course marks allotted to professionalism. Academic integrity issues (e.g. inappropriate use of AI) can also affect the professionalism mark.
