### Course Description
This course guides participants on the use of International Code of Signals for coding and decoding messages and procedures to be applied for communication by visual signaling; on the use of Radio Aids to Marine Navigation for ascertaining facilities and services; and on extracting necessary information from the Annual Notices to Mariners.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Recognize international code flags.
- Describe procedures for flag signals as a communication means.
- Use International Code of Signals for coding and decoding messages.
- State the International code signals for distress.
- Use Radio Aids to Marine Navigation.
- Extract information from the Annual Notices to Mariners.
### Learning Resources
Recommended Text(s):
- International Code of Signals by the International Maritime Organization
- Radio Aids to Marine Navigation
- Annual Notices to Mariners

In addition, students must achieve a minimum of 50% of the marks allocated for course work and 50% of the marks allocated for the final exam. Students who fail to achieve 50% of the allocated marks in either component cannot receive a final grade greater than 69%. The minimum attendance for this course, in compliance with the Transport Canada requirement, is 90%.
Note that the wording pertains to only two components: (1) the final examination and (2) course work. It is therefore NOT required that each sub-component, such as a quiz, assignment, etc., has to be passed. For course work, it is up to the student, with the instructor’s support, to meet the 50% bar through the sub-components of possible make-up quizzes, alternative assignments, etc.
