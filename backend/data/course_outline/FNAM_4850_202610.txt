### Course Description
Urban and Interface Silviculture will address integrated vegetation management strategies and treatment methods to achieve desired, stable, natural vegetation communities in B.C. Treatments studied will include site preparation, regeneration, brushing and weeding, cleaning, and thinning. Strategies will be designed to cost-effectively and with minimal undesirable environmental impact achieve desired species composition and dominance. Strategies may also be designed to conserve biodiversity, control invasive species, minimize the risk of catastrophic wildfire, promote windfirmness and maintain pleasing appearance. Case studies will be employed to convey as much of the material as possible. Students will be required to prepare a treatment regime for a specific site and set of objectives.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Assess an urban or peri-urban natural area with an objective to establish a treed, stable, vegetation community dominated by native species.
- Present plausible predictions of secondary succession pathways for specific sites including: species composition, structural stage sequence, and community replacement.
- Design integrated vegetation management strategies (treatment regimes) for specified sites and objectives to promote stable vegetation communities dominated by desired species.
- Assess alternative species for inclusion in the IVM regime
- Assess potential benefits from site preparation and develop a site preparation prescription.
- Assess potential benefits from natural or artificial regeneration and develop a planting prescription.
- Assess potential benefits from brushing and develop a brushing prescription.
- Assess potential benefits from density control treatments.
- Assess potential benefits from fertilization.
- Write site plans and treatment regimes that describe the site, objectives and constraints and specify treatments in enough detail that they can be implemented and evaluated.
- Plan and implement a regeneration treatment and assess its productivity, quality and safety.
