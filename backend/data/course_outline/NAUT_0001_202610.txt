### Course Description
Students are to become familiar with BMC policy, procedures, and the roles of key figures. They will be able to effectively understand Co-op employment procedures, and be prepared to apply for a Co-op job by having the ability to write cover letters and a resume. Students will also be given the skills to conduct an effective job interview.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this orientation, students will:
- Identify key people at BMC and their roles — Associate Dean, Chief Instructors, Co-op Coordinator, and Co-op Program Assistant.
- Identify key locations of the BMC Campus.
- List key services offered from the Student Association.
- Identify key features of BMC Policy pertaining to students.
- Get a clear understanding of Co-op employers and hiring process/ranking process.
- Write an effective cover letter for a job application.
- Write an effective resume to apply for Co-op jobs.
- Identify key requirements of Transport Canada pertaining to the program.
- Understand the role of the Co-op office.
