### Course Description
BCST 4410 expands on the day to day operation of the radio station ‘Evolution’ 107.9FM and Evolution 1079.com initiated in BCST 3310 in Term 3. The course focuses on two learner selected specialties encompassed within the daily operation of the radio station and its web component. This specialization allows students to concentrate on the areas they hope to gain employment in the field. Term four is divided into two phases with learners focusing on one of their two chosen specialties within each phase. The aim is to gain on-the-job experience at BCIT prior to starting BCST 4409 - Practicum 2, conducted in industry.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course, in their chosen areas of specialization, the student will be able to:
- Apply specialized operational and multi-platform production skills.
- Assume responsibility for departmental administration and operation (includes radio station, social media and website related duties).
- Self-assess their individual skills according to provided guidelines.
- Supervise the effectiveness of collaborative departmental initiatives.
- Supervise the effectiveness of collaborative departmental initiatives.
- Comply with CRTC, RTDNA and ASC regulations.
- Implement effective troubleshooting techniques to solve operational problems.
- Evaluate effectiveness of ongoing programming elements.
- Strategize in departmental groups to operate a radio station according to industry standards.

### Course Goals
BCST 4410 expands on the daily operation of the Evolution Storytelling Company and provides a unique opportunity for students to hone their skills in a specific area.
Concept : The lab is designed to provide a higher level of learning, increase the challenges, and create the capacity to expand the student's skills by providing in-depth practice in a specific area of interest. By arranging this term with greater flexibility in choice, the lab's intent is to create an environment where the student is inspired to accomplish their goals and achieve high performance and productivity standards. Thus, we expect the work to reflect deeper learning and greater proficiency .
Phases : There are three phases of four weeks each. Each phase is consistent. For example, a student may be scheduled for on-air from 1 - 5 pm and will complete four weeks in this role. In the next phase, the student may choose to do a podcast and complete four weeks of that specific role, etc. The goal is to provide consistency for the student to practice a specific skill for a determined period.
Mentorship : The student has the opportunity to meet weekly with an assigned faculty mentor every week in all three phases. In the meeting, the student will receive weekly feedback as a method to enhance their abilities. This provides the student the opportunity to hone their skills and use the feedback and knowledge gained in those sessions to perform at an optimal level throughout the phase. Mentorship meetings are optional .
Overall, it is expected that the student’s work reflects a high level of learning . Three marks along with three essays are accumulated throughout the term. The lab expectation is that the student participates in setting goals and will work closely with their faculty mentor for guidance in achieving high standards.

A student who has ceased to attend a course without explanation before the withdrawal deadline (two-thirds of the way through the course) will receive a Vanished (V) grade. These requirements are set out in accordance with both BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations and BCIT Policy 5103 Student Evaluation More information regarding withdrawal deadlines is available at Academic Dates & Deadlines . Academic Integrity and Use of Artificial Intelligence : The unauthorized use of Artificial Intelligence tools (such as ChatGPT) for any part of student work in this course is prohibited, and, when identified by your instructor, will result in an academic integrity violation. - A failing grade on the work submitted; or
- An academic misconduct letter being placed on the student’s record.
