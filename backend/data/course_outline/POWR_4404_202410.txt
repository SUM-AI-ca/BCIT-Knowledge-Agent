## Course Description
This course prepares power engineering students for a management role in a small to medium size power plant. Topics covered include plant organization, training, and safety; administration techniques; records, supervision, and personnel; scheduling, estimating, and report writing; interest, equivalence, and present worth methods; economy studies; and budgeting and labour relations.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:

- Take charge of a small to medium size power plant as chief engineer.
- Hire qualified engineers as shift and assistant shift engineers.
- Prepare operations and maintenance budgets.
- Upgrade equipment and controls.
- Maintain costs within budget.
- Train engineers for higher positions.
- Co-ordinate inspections with the boiler and pressure vessel branch.
- Promote safety.
- Promote good labour relations.
- Develop project management positions.
- Prepare for higher management positions.

## Course Goals
Section 1 – The Making of a Good Supervisor

- Self-assess management potential
- List and describe personal characteristics of good managers
- List principle duties of supervisors
- Understand group organizational trees

Section 2 – Planning

- Definition and creation of effective goals

Section 3 – Project Management

- Definition and creation of Gantt charts
- Definition and creation of PERT charts
- Determination of critical paths and slack time

Section 4 – Staffing

- Calculation of staff requirements
- Describe interview process and list things to avoid
- Create new employee orientation training package

Section 5 – Operations

- Describe steps in the control process
- Describe principle of management by exception
- Assess good control strategies

Section 6 – Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)

- Create awareness of Canadian injury statistics
- List and describe employer responsibilities under OHS
- List and describe supervisor responsibilities under OHS
- List and describe employee responsibilities under OHS
- Describe requirements and functions of a Joint committee
- Discuss consequences of failing to abide by OHS legislation
- Define and discuss due diligence

Section 7 – Problem Solving

- Describe 8-step problem solving process

Section 8 – Employee Appraisal and Motivation

- Define and describe employee appraisal process
- Define Maslow’s hierarchy of needs

Section 9 – Communication

- Describe typical communication processes
- List and describe 11 principles for effective communication
- Describe active listening

Section 10 – Problem Employees

- List and describe the four principles of “Hot-stove” discipline
- Discuss a six step process for behavior modification

Section 11 – Grievances

- List and describe a seven step grievance handling process
