## Course Description
In this course, students use computers to control physical devices. Students create CNC programs, manufacture products on CNC machines, design circuits using schematic design software, and manufacture circuit boards. The course prepares students to teach secondary school courses.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Explain the advantages of CNC in manufacturing applications.
- Write a point-to-point CNC program.
- Prove-out a CNC program on a CNC machine.
- Create electronic schematic diagrams.
- Create custom parts that link schematic diagrams to circuit board designs.
- Design printed circuit boards.
- Create circuit boards to control physical devices.
