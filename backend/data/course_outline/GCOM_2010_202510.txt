## Course Description
Students will examine how the creative process interlaces imagination with the materials and mechanical considerations of the graphics industry. Through individual and group projects, students will create production dockets, complete with layouts and production prototypes. The Lab will be used to study Lean Manufacturing concepts as they relate to the printing industry. Students will become familiar with materials management, outsourcing and customer service in graphic communications.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Select appropriate imaging and finishing technologies based on design and production outcomes.
- Provide solutions to production constraints through creative design, sample making, and strategic planning.
- Create production layouts and dockets for case studies and live projects.
- Apply the principles of lean manufacturing as it is applied in the commercial print industry.
- Describe the relationship between design, manufacturing, and operations management.
- Effectively manage client expectations and demands in a production environment.
- Select appropriate materials based on availability, physical characteristics, and sustainability.
- Utilize common mathematical formulas for production and paper procurement applications.
- Articulate the requirements for effective inventory management.
- Describe the relationship between manufacturers, paper merchants, and third-party trade vendors and their roles in the commercial print industry.​

## Course Goals
At the end of this course the student will be able to:

- Define the relationship between the print buyer, printer and user
- Create print specifications to fit the job to the budget and end user requirements
- Identify and explain contractual issues, common trade practices and the basics of copyright laws as it applies to the printing industry
- Process a job from an estimate to a final job docket, set-up purchases for paper and subcontracted work through shipping and billing
- Provide examples of job specification changes that will optimize production and add value for both the company and customer
- Use pricing strategies, such as price lists, program structures, individual projects and rebate offerings that are common in the industry.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
The Graphic Communication program utilizes a unique print and imaging lab facility. The equipment in the GCOM lab was donated by the generous business owners in our community. Students must respect the equipment in the lab at all times, comply with all rules and take reasonable care of themselves and others:

- Loose hair or long beards must be tied back – or contained in a hat or hair net
- Remove jewelry, scarves, coats or loose clothing
- Do not engage in recreational gaming or web browsing
- Do not bring food or drink into the lab(closed water bottles are allowed at desks only)

These rules are for the safety of all students using the equipment in the lab. Failure to comply with lab rules will not be tolerated. The degree of punitive action may range from being issued a verbal or written warning, to being barred entry to the lab or required to leave (which may result in course failure), to withdrawal from the program.
