## Course Description
​This course presents the fundamentals of laser-scanning and reality capture technologies, introduces proper planning and data collection methods, and explores a variety of products and deliverables created from massive point cloud datasets collected by laser-scanning technology. Students are exposed to industry-standard hardware and software solutions employed by the geomatics industry to collect, process, and model point cloud datasets.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Explain the concepts of laser-scanning and reality-capture technology and their application in geomatics.
- Differentiate among the various laser-scanning methods.
- Identify the purpose and required deliverables of a particular laser-scanning project.
- Process and visualize point cloud data using industry-standard software.
- Determine the survey and control network strategy required to collect data in a given scenario.
- Transfer and review collected data for quality-control purposes using industry software tools.
- Perform registration, such as visual alignment, cloud-to-cloud alignment and cloud-to-target alignment techniques, using industry software tools.
- Generate derived product deliverables, such as topography plans, analysis for the quality of BIM/ REVIT models, and 3D visualizations of point cloud data.​
