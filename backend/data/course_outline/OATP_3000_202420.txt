## Course Description
This workplace education provides students with the opportunity to augment their classroom training and demonstrate learned technical and social skills. Students will acquire and further refine their skills through real-world work experiences working in an administrative related position.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Utilize professional skills - such as effective communication, conflict resolution, stress management, and team skills in an office environment.
- Practice business skills and expand knowledge of popular software applications.
- Identify possible and preferable future career directions and opportunities within an area of interest.
- Collect a personal library of solutions to industry challenges (“lessons learned”).

## Evaluation Criteria

The student’s progress on the job is monitored by a BCIT Instructor and their performance on the job is supervised on an ongoing basis by the student’s work term employer. Students must complete the minimum required work hours (280 hrs.) to receive credit.

The Work Term course consist of the following evaluation components: - The Employer’s Evaluation of the Work Term Students Performance.: The following course grades may be assigned: - Satisfactory: All course requirements fulfilled. - Unsatisfactory: Course requirements not fulfilled. Students must receive Satisfactory on the work term evaluation to pass this course.
