## Course Description
This is a practical, hands-on course in the use of occupational hygiene instruments and procedures for the collection of data on chemical and biological hazards commonly encountered in the workplace. Students will learn to use a variety of instruments which are currently used to evaluate a wide range of chemical and biological hazards.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

Demonstrate an understanding of the uses, principles of operation, calibration (where applicable) and limitations of the following:

- Personal sampling pumps
- Critical orifice meter
- Calibration equipment: Soap Film Flow Meters, Dry Cal, Calibrated Rotameter
- Detector tube pumps and tubes
- Sorbent tubes
- Passive diffusion samplers
- Impingers
- Filter Cassettes (37mm & 25mm)
- Single gas monitors (various models)
- Multi-gas monitors (various models)
- Particulate analyzers (various models)
- Cyclones
- Andersen Cascade Impactors (1, 2, 6, & 8 stage)
- IOM, Button Samplers & other particle size selection instruments
- RCS Centrifugal Impactor
- Spore Traps
- Environmental Surface Swabs
- Sticky tape
- RODAC Plates
- Settling Plates
- Anemometers (Thermal & Rotating Vane types)

Interpret data including lab reports generated by the use of the above equipment.
