## Course Description
Study skills and learning techniques are aspects that can be transferred to your future career. You will have the opportunity to assess your learning style and be introduced to strategies through practice (e.g. time management, exam preparation, note-taking, reading) that will aid you in your studies. A discussion of student success will include topics such as responsibilities in the classroom and student professionalism

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Be aware of and know how to access major Institute resources.
- Feel comfortable with the Institute's environment and culture.
- Discuss student professionalism and policies that apply to your time at BCIT.
- Assess your personal learning style and approach to learning.
- Practice using different study skills and techniques.
- Prepare and give a group presentation.
- Discuss the characteristics of successful students.
