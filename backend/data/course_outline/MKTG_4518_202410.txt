## Course Description
​The ability to clearly communicate your thinking and convey ideas is essential to success in the marketing communications industry. In this course students will hone their oral pitching skills and formal and informal writing skills. All work will be individual to ensure that every student is learning these critical skills (vs. benefiting from one or two proficient team members in group projects). This course is for students to strengthen their public speaking and writing skills, and their authentic voices as professionals.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Learn how to tailor communications to different audiences
- Apply the principles of logical reasoning in structuring communications
- Connect authentically with an audience
- Create compelling, high-impact presentations and communications
- Communicate insights, with meaningful conclusions and recommendations
- Present information simply and clearly in slides, reports, emails, and other formats
- Develop strong active listening skills
- Develop team collaboration skills
- Develop strong written and oral presentation skills

## Attendance Requirements
As per the course outline, attendance is required by policy at BCIT. In the case of unavoidable absence, you must email the appropriate instructor before class.

In addition, absences will be deducted from your final course grade as follows:

- A minimum of 1% will be deducted for each lab hour missed without a valid doctor's note (as labs for this course are two hours, missing lab will result in a 2% deduction).
- Attendance will be taken at some lectures. If a student is absent from a lecture when attendance is taken, the instructor will deduct a minimum of 1%.
- If you are absent from in-person learning when attendance is taken (whether that is at the beginning, middle, or end of a lecture or lab), you will be marked absent and a minimum of 1% of your grade will be deducted.
- For online synchronous classes, failure to complete online attendance checks (in the form of on-screen surveys) or guest lecture questions will result in you being marked absent and a deduction of 1%.

Any student missing more than 10% of scheduled classes (which, in the case of this course, is 3 class hours of unexcused absences) may result in the assignment of a failing grade and/or removal from the course.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.
