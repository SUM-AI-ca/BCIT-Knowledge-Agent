## Course Description
Covers survey fundamentals and quantity take-off of civil engineering projects pertaining to roadwork, water, sewer and building construction. Topics include quantity take-off, productivity rates, labour and material costing, construction equipment and machinery, and project cost control. .

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Read and interpret typical civil construction drawings.
- Identify common construction equipment and their applications.
- Identify fundamental construction activities for a typical construction project.
- Prepare portions of a contractor's estimate.
- Use basic project cost control on a project.

## Course Goals
By the end of this course you will develop the following skills - Ability to read and interpret civil drawings and associated tender information - Identify equipment required for a typical civil project - Develop basic rst principle estimates - Have a general understanding of most costs that will apply to a typical civil tender.

## Course Specific Requirements
Please ensure you have awareness of typical geometry, such as area, volume of shapes common in the construction industry. In addition, be able to convert from metric to imperial and vice versa as well as use specific gravity for establishing material weights. Math and basic financial understanding is key to being an excellent estimator.
