## Course Description
This course teaches the communication skills students need to plan, manage, and complete their Term-4 project reports in Chemical and Environmental Technology. Students learn how to prepare a variety of project-related documents, including project proposals and work plans, progress reports, and written and oral final project reports. They also acquire the teamwork, meeting, and interpersonal communication skills they need to collaborate effectively on a team project. Students also write job application letters and resumes and practice job interviews.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Prepare a detailed project proposal and work plan.
- Prepare a detailed literature review.
- Write an effective, job-specific resume and application letter for a position in the student’s field.
- Prepare for job interviews, and do peer and self-assessments of performance.
- Write clear technical descriptions.
- Prepare effective graphics.
- Write short, informal reports, including progress reports.
- Write short, informal reports, including progress reports.
- Deliver a professional-level oral presentation before a technical audience, including fielding questions and handling objections.
- Plan, conduct, and participate effectively in a meeting.
- Collaborate as part of a team to complete a group writing project.
- Participate effectively on a team.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.

## Other information
Grading Policy:

90-100% An outstanding assignment. The work is perfect or nearly perfect. The assignment will be complete and easily accessible. The English language, grammar, and punctuation will be nearly flawless.

80-89% A superior assignment. Smoothly written and effectively organized, it leads the reader clearly and easily through the material. The purpose of the paper is clear, and the organization, tone and style are adapted to the audience. The assignment may show originality in word choice, in the selection or development of reader benefits or examples, or in the effective use of presentation strategies.

65-79% An assignment that does several things well. It has only a few minor errors that do not interfere with getting the job done. It shows tact and good judgement. It is well written and well organized. Its main points are developed and supported.

50-64% A document which meets the basic assignment demands. Errors in content, organization, style and tone do not seriously interfere with the communication. It may be an otherwise acceptable document with a major flaw or a number of minor flaws.

0-49% An assignment that fails to meet the basic demands of the assignment, which has a great many minor errors or several major errors, or which has a major, unacceptable flaw. The document would not achieve its intended purpose in the workplace.
