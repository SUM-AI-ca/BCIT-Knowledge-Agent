### Course Description
This non-credit self-paced online course is designed to help students meet the challenges of the math courses in their BCIT programs. This course is recommended for students entering the Applied Data Analytic Certificate (ADAC), engineering technology programs or preparing for the Math 0120 Competency Exam. Math 0060 is customizable depending on your pathway. Topics include algebra, functions, graphs, equations, word problems, geometry, trigonometry, logarithms, exponents, intro stats topics and intro linear algebra.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Manipulate and simplify algebraic expressions including fractional expressions.
- Solve simple, quadratic and simultaneous linear equations.
- Solve geometric and trigonometric problems.
- Graph algebraic functions.
- Solve exponential and logarithmic problems.
- Solve basic probability problems as seen intro statistics (optional depending on pathway).
- Solve basic linear algebra problems (optional depending on pathway).

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Assignments (self-paced):
Assignment 1: Basic Algebra Review
Assignment 2: Geometry
Assignment 3: Functions and Graphs
Assignment 4a & 4b: The Trigonometric Functions
Assignment 5a & 5b: Systems of Linear Equations: Determinants
Assignment 6: Factoring, Fractions and Quadratics
Assignment 7a & 7b: Vectors and Oblique Triangles
Assignment 8: Exponents and Radicals
Assignment 9: Exponential and Logarithmic Functions
Assignment 10: Matrices: Systems of Linear Equations
Assignment 11: Variation
Assignment 12: Introduction of Statistics
Assignment 13: Introduction to Linear Algebra
Pathways:
- ADAC Students should complete Assignments 1, 3, 4a, 5b, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, and 13
- Engineering Students should complete Assignments 1, 2, 3, 4b, 5a, 6, 7b, 8, 9, and 11
- Health Science Students or General Students should complete Assignments 1, 2, 3, 4b, 5a, 6, 7a, 8, 9, and 11
- Automotive Students must complete Assignments 1, 2, 3, 4a, 4b, 6, 7a, 7b, 8 and 11 and then wirte an assessment test at the Burnaby campus in order to get credit for the course.
Note: Students may chose to do more than 10 assignments but 10 is the minimum to complete the course.
