### Course Description
Evaluate global strategic concepts that will enable successful leadership in a complex and rapidly changing global environment. You will analyze company strengths and weaknesses, assess risks and opportunities, and develop strategies for international companies. Through collaborative and individual learning activities, you will apply behavioural models to implement strategic change and overcome resistance to organizational strategic change. The objective of this course is to enhance leadership of strategy and to provide tools to develop effective business strategies and global goals to become an effective leader of strategic change. Competencies may be demonstrated through a blend of individual and collaborative assignments, skill demonstrations, and major papers.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate the global dimensions of strategy to ensure appropriate leadership and control of organizations.
- Critically analyze the complexity of global business environments, including external and internal forces, and their impacts on the strategic decision-making process.
- Critically evaluate current strategic management tools, techniques and concepts to determine their effectiveness in a global construct.
- Develop alternative responses related to external business forces by assessing current developments within a variety of industry environments.
- Analyze and evaluate frameworks to assess and lead strategic change.
- Analyze organizations to determine how to effectively integrate the desired strategic changes within a comprehensive and responsive leadership system.
- Create strategies and design approaches to ensure successful implementation of strategic change.
- Create effective strategic plans for business problems that include people and performance measurement metrics.
- Develop teamwork and critical thinking skills for formulating solutions to strategic problems.

### Course Goals
This course’s primary aim is the design, implementation and evaluation of corporate global strategies. It introduces a range of concepts, theories and frameworks that aid understanding global strategic decision-making and its impact on organizations. These techniques will be applied on “live” organizations to ensure teaching and learning is current. The aim of this course will be on the critical nature of global strategic decision making, management and leadership in the current and increasingly competitive and globalized business environment. It examines how, in a dynamic environment, competitive advantage might be developed through strategic planning while being exploited in a cost-effective manner. Through the use of a variety of case studies, it will emphasize the value of structured models to develop practical solutions in a rapidly evolving and fast paced global business environment.
