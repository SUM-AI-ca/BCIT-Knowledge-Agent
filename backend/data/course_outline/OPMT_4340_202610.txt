### Course Description
Building on several courses, this course explores the Lean Body of Knowledge in greater detail. Lean (or Just in Time (JIT)/Toyota Production System (TPS)) principles have been incorporated into today’s popular management philosophies aimed at achieving excellence. Students will learn to apply Lean tools to business process improvement.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain the principles of Lean.
- Describe how Lean affects a company’s competitive position.
- Describe process improvement tools used in the implementation of Lean strategies.
- Apply waste elimination methods to business processes.
- Discuss the benefits of inventory reduction in a Lean environment.
- Relate Lean and product quality.
- Analyze the application of pull systems to limit work-in-process (WIP) and queue times.
- Analyze the application of strategies to reduce lot sizes.
- Analyze visual workplace/management strategies to facilitate engagement.
- Apply the principles of the 5S approach to workplace organisation.
- Analyze the principles of Total Productive Maintenance.
- Describe strategies to involve people in the development and implementation of Lean.
- Describe strategies to effect improvement through supplier relationships and supply chain management.
- Relate Lean to MRP II and the Theory of Constraints.
- Identify key performance measures integral to Lean implementation.
- Assess the synergies and differences between Lean, TOC and Six Sigma
