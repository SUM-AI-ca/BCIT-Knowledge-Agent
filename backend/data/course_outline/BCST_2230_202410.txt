## Course Description
Involves the student in identifying, researching and gathering of news material in an organized manner. The student is introduced to beat and filing systems, the courts, the police, organized labour, business, and politics.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Utilize resource material such as Google, Facebook and Twitter in news reporting.
- Recognize and report on various police procedures.
- Function effectively in political news coverage.
- Outline the functions and procedures of the BC Coroner’s Service.
- Research and conduct proper interviews for radio and TV news.
- Identify and report on a wide variety of newsworthy issues and events.
- Explain and implement the steps in organizing a news event from a public relations perspective.
