## Course Description
​This course will enable nuclear medicine students to communicate effectively with colleagues, patients, the public, and the wider medical profession, focusing on writing skills, presentation skills, and teamwork. Students will learn methods of organizing information to ensure communication is clear, concise, accessible, and suitable for the target audience. Students will also have the opportunity to work in interprofessional teams with other health professional students to develop interprofessional competency in role clarification. Finally, students will develop a resume and cover letter.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Use various forms of communication to provide and obtain relevant, accurate, and complete information. (2.1.1, 2.1.2, 2.1.3, 2.1.4, 2.1.7, 2.2.1)
- Write clear and concise emails. (2.1.2)
- Use a style and tone appropriate for purpose and audience. (2.1.3)
- Apply discipline-specific citation styles to prevent plagiarism. (6.2.3, 6.3.1, 6.3.3)
- Deliver a well-organized oral presentation. (2.1.3)
- Create a resume and cover letter. (2.1.2)
- Work effectively with a team: dividing responsibilities, evaluating work, making decisions, resolving conflicts, and giving, soliciting, and receiving constructive feedback. (2.1.4, 2.1.9, 3.1.3, 3.1.4, 3.2.1)
- Describe the roles of members of the healthcare team, including the patient, family, and community, and their contributions to safe healthcare. (HSIP 0100 – 3.1.1, 3.1.3, 3.1.4)​
