## Course Description
Examines health and safety hazards and controls in a variety of work environments. Includes building and plant layout, lighting, ventilation, automated lines, systems, and processes, sanitation and personnel facilities, personal protective equipment, manual materials handling, and electrical safety. Explains how to successfully eliminate or reduce the hazards and risks associated with several work processes. Reinforces the hierarchy of controls - engineering, administrative, and personal protective equipment. Discusses both historical and current issues. (This course is equivalent to OCHS 2320.)

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

-Discuss the engineering – administrative – personal protective equipment control hierarchy and indicate how and why this hierarchy is employed in legislation and in practice

-Apply a duly diligent method to select controls for reducing the risk of an identified workplace hazard

-Explain the concept and benefits of safety through design

-Recognize the application of machine design standards in overall OHS programs

-Recognize the application of building system codes in overall OHS programs

-Describe how building maintenance and sanitation is used to promote worker health and safety

-Describe the regulatory and technical requirements for personal facilities in a workplace

-Describe the regulatory and technical requirements for emergency washing facilities in a workplace

-Describe the types of personal protective equipment typically used in the workplace and the regulations and standards that govern the design and use of PPE

-Develop and effective PPE selection and management program for a workplace, including a respirator fit testing program

-Describe the common sources of electrical, mechanical and pneumatic energy in a workplace

-Discuss typical electrical injuries and their causes

-Describe safe practices for testing, using, maintaining and repairing electrical equipment (high and low voltage) in the workplace

-Demonstrate an ability to develop a de-energization and lockout program for a workplace

-Describe modern materials warehousing and handling practices in industry

-Demonstrate an ability to teach safe manual materials handling techniques

-Describe current standards and regulations regarding automated systems and processes

-Define common lighting terms and interpret lighting regulations and guidelines

-Use lighting to enhance safety and security in the workplace

-Demonstrate the use of basic photometers for evaluation of lighting systems

-Identify common dilution ventilation and local exhaust ventilation systems used in the workplace

-Define common industrial ventilation terms and interpret industrial ventilation regulations and guidelines

-Demonstrate the basic use of common ventilation system evaluation instruments and techniques
