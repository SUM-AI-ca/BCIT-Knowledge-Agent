### Course Description
​This course examines the technical factors that influence electric vehicle (EV) efficiency, driving range, and long-term performance. Participants will explore charging infrastructure, energy-efficient driving strategies, and mechanical considerations that affect vehicle efficiency. Key topics include battery and thermal management systems, regenerative braking, maintenance practices that reduce mechanical drag, and specification factors that influence vehicle selection. Through a combination of theory and applied exercises, students will develop a practical understanding of how driving behavior, charging practices, and vehicle design impact overall EV performance.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- ​Evaluate EV Charging for speed, cost and infrastructure constraints.
- Apply smart EV driving techniques to get more range.
- Analyze your EV to improve efficiency through correct maintenance and alignment.
- Compare specifications to help choose the right EV when purchasing.
- Describe battery and cabin temperature management systems and why it matters for range.​
