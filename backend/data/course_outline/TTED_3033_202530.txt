### Course Description
This course introduces students to the basic hand tools and processes as well as basic machines and processes used to manipulate wood and plastics for practical constructions. Students will learn how to select, set-up and use appropriate tools and equipment for specific operations. Basic nomenclature and specifications will be outlined and applied to the school-based context.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate competence in the following areas:
- Hand tool identification and nomenclature;
- Hand tool selection;
- Hand tool sharpening and set-up;
- Correct hand tool use;
- Basic hand tool techniques in stock lay out, squaring and sizing;
- Basic elements in wood joint selection, lay out, cutting, fitting and gluing;
- Basic finishing of a small wooden article;
- Use and modification of wood hand tools for plastic applications;
- Basic preparation, planning and delivery of a classroom demonstration.
- Safely operate the major portable and stationary machines commonly found in typical high school shops.
- Demonstrate safe work procedures and conduct in the woodwork shop.
- Use machines and equipment to size rough material.
- Use machines and equipment to produce common wood joinery, applied to a project.
- Create handouts and informational material suitable for high school students clarifying safety and procedure issues.
- Apply appropriate techniques to complete a small project, including mechanical and abrasive surface preparation, as well as application of a simple finish.
- Effectively demonstrate a specific machine operation to a group of students.
