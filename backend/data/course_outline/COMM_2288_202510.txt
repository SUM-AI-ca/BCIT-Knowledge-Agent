## Course Description
T​This course builds on the skills you learned in COMM 1188. It covers a professional career portfolio, job interview skills, OCHS-focused literature search skills, persuasive writing and presentations, and report writing. You will also research, design, and “sell" a proposal for change on an OHS topic.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Compile a professional career package.
- Prepare and practice for a job interview.
- Create and deliver persuasive messages in written and oral format.
- Conduct research using appropriate sources for OCHS.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.
- Write a proposal.
- Use formal report format.
- Give a persuasive presentation based on the written proposal​.

## Other information
In this course, some assignments will allow the use optional of AI tools, and some won’t. When the use of AI tools is permitted, the assignment instructions will provide details on exactly what is allowed.

Note that AI output may include incorrect or misleading information, fabricated authors/dates/publications, faked works, or may incorporate discriminatory ideas. It is your responsibility to ensure the quality, accuracy and appropriateness of the work you submit in this course.

If you use AI tools (such as ChatGPT) to generate any text for a draft or final version of any part of an assignment, you must submit a short paragraph declaring what tools you used and to what extend you used them. You must also save any prompts and generated text from these tools in case it is requested.

Material taken from any source (e.g. websites, books, AI tools) must be cited. Undeclared use of AI is a violation of academic integrity as it presents work, or ideas created by tools or other people as your own. Using any AI tool without authorization or failing to fully declare the use of any AI tool will be considered a violation of BCIT’s Student Code of Academic Integrity.
