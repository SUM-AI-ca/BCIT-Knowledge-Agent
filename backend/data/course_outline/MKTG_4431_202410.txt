## Course Description
Internet Marketing continues to play an increasingly important role in the success of every organization. Marketing students need to understand how to develop and implement online marketing strategies and tactics in this constantly evolving digital world. The course covers internet-specific strategy development and marketing campaign planning and execution in many areas including, but not limited to: website design, search engine optimization, paid search, advertising, social media marketing, mobile marketing, e-mail marketing and online lead generation. This course will provide students with the required knowledge to build online marketing campaigns to increase customer acquisition, conversion, retention and growth. The course culminates in a final capstone project, in which students will build an Internet Marketing Plan for an actual business.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop effective public speaking skills by delivering oral presentations (individual and team) to audiences of diverse sizes.
- Identify and apply various online marketing strategies, including value propositions, objectives, revenue models and growth strategies.
- Build online marketing campaigns that focus on customer acquisition, conversion, retention, and growth objectives.
- Explain the key factors that contribute to a successful search marketing strategy.
- Make detailed recommendations on how to drive qualified traffic to a website with particular emphasis on Search Engine Optimization, Paid Search and Social Media.
- Identify the key attributes of a successful website; critically evaluate various website designs and content as to their ability to create customer value and encourage conversions; make specific recommendations to improve website design.
- Explain how Social Media Marketing can be an efficient marketing tool; research and define an online community for a business; define and apply the tactics and platforms that fit with an organization’s objectives; make specific recommendations on how an organization can use blogs, micro-blogs, social networks, professional networks, video and content sharing to engage consumers while building and retaining relationships.
- Explain Mobile Marketing essentials including Mobile Websites, Mobile Search, Mobile Applications, Mobile Marketing Tactics, Mobile e-commerce and the Mobile Wallet; apply the appropriate mix of these mobile tools to a business.
- Build an e-mail marketing campaign, including developing campaign objectives, writing effective e-mail copy and creating a landing page to optimize conversions and response rates.
- Describe the best practices for consumer and business to business online lead generation campaigns, as well as not-for-profit direct and interactive online fundraising initiatives.
- Discuss the relevant sections of the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act that impact a business' ability to collect and use personal information for marketing purposes.
- Explain and define the key internet marketing metrics.
- Apply detailed knowledge from this course to build a comprehensive Internet Marketing Plan for a business.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
