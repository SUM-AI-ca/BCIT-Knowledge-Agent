## Course Description
In this course students will integrate what they have learned in prior courses on the various functional areas of managing a brokerage. Topics include Strategic Leadership, Financial Management, Human Resources, Inside the Brokerage, Sales and Service, Marketing Strategies, Strategic Communication, and Growth, Valuation and Perpetuation.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Analyze a particular setting with respect to local and regional factors from the perspective of the establishment of an insurance brokerage.
- Develop a comprehensive strategic plan for an insurance brokerage, including the effects on the financial statements, human resources, and valuation.
- Communicate the strategic plan to the stakeholders involved, including:
- Documents for distribution,
- Presentation materials.

Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence.
