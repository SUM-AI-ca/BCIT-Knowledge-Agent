## Course Description
Teaches the process by which a story idea is transformed into a television news story, how to gather visual materials that tell the story, to write a script that works with the visuals, and to edit visuals together with script to create a comprehensible television news story. Some attention is given to newscast make-up and presentation.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Propose, organize, shoot and edit a variety of basic video-based news elements and complete stories.
- Develop storytelling capabilities in a TV News environment.
- Work safely and professionally in a variety of television newsgathering circumstances.
- Demonstrate the operational requirements of a wide variety of television newsgathering, editing, and broadcast equipment.
- Recognize various newsroom positions and demonstrate understanding of basic newsroom operations.
- Operate basic functions of television newsroom software.
- Work effectively in groups including time and resource management to facilitate visual productions.
