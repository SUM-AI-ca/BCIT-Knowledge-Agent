## Course Description
This course will provide you with the latest techniques used to effectively gather online information for investigative purposes relating to persons, companies, and other assets. You will be introduced to the methods of gathering online intelligence through social media platforms, search engines, and dark web searches. In addition, you will learn how to assess potential counter-intelligence concerns. Particular emphasis will be placed on heightened awareness and the “paper trail” investigators may leave behind when using internet investigation tools. Approaches to maintain privacy and security when carrying out online investigations will also be explored and a variety of online search methodologies introduced.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Outline the basis of Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) and Social Media Intelligence (SMINT).
- Apply advanced internet searching tools and techniques to conduct online investigations.
- Apply advanced web browser usage, and apply techniques for searching the surface, deep, and dark web surfaces.
- Develop an internet search plan for operational use.
- Assess operational challenges that face the user when using online information.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Class Introductions and Course Expectations (Lecture) Topic: Social Media Protection Strategies Sub-Topics Covered: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, WhatsApp Training Overview: Of all the online activities one can engage in, social media is the commanding tool for online communications, intelligence, and investigations. Regrettably, it is also heavily exploited by those either seeking to do harm or others failing to understand the consequences of boundary blurring between personal and professional activities. Online investigators must understand their own privacy thresholds in order to better understand their target’s social media usage. This online facing session will encourage attendees to: Student Outcomes: 1.Conceptualize that online situational awareness requires constant updating. 2.Maintain the appropriate privacy settings of their professional and personal social media accounts. 3.Utilize online tools to expedite the rate and reach attendees can acquire social-media based information Topic: Creating Discreet Social Media Accounts Sub-Topics Covered: Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, Mobile Apps Training Overview: An investigator must practice constant Operational Security (OpSec). When using social media to supplement an investigation, the creation of a discreet social media account is mandatory. Such accounts aren’t exclusive to “deception operations”, they can also be used for privacy and OpSec for an investigator, journalist, and penetration tester. OpSec online not only protects the investigator, but it also protects the target in the case that the evidence provided leads nowhere. This online facing session will encourage attendees to: Student Outcomes: 1.Articulate why creating discreet accounts is necessary for the protection of the agency, investigator, and the target. 2.Demonstrate the steps required to maintain discreet accounts. 3.Create accounts for use within this online learning environment. Team Assignment Preparation
Topic: Search Engine Optimization (Surface Web Queries) Sub-Topics Covered: Google, Bing, Yandex, Duck Duck Go Search Engines Training Deliverables: Attendees will learn to better understand how to acquire online information expediently with investigative techniques and tactics. Additionally, attendees will review the importance of proper note taking and source documentation best practices as it pertains to online information and intelligence gathering. This 03- hour online facing session will encourage attendees to: Student Outcomes: 1.Utilize search operators to narrow the scope of online investigations. 2.Utilize reverse image searching techniques. 3.Utilize advanced features and settings within a search engine. Topic: Deep and Dark Web Researching Sub-Topics Covered: Internet Archive, Ahmai, TOR, various Deep Web Sites Training Deliverables: The Deep Web is the part of the Internet that isn’t necessarily malicious, but is simply too large and/or obscure to be indexed due to the limitations of crawling and indexing software. The Dark Web is the part of the non-indexed part of the Internet that is used by those who are purposely trying to control access because they have a strong desire for privacy, or because what they’re doing may be perceived as unlawful, unethical, and immoral. This online facing session will encourage attendees to: Student Outcomes: 1.Safely incorporate usage of Deep and Dark web tactics. 2.Locate and investigate previously deleted or amended online information. 3.Employ protective strategies when investigating the Dark Web. Team Assignment Preparation
Topic: Legal Studies Sub Topics Covered: Legislation, Statutes, and Case Law Training Deliverables: Open Source does not mean “all access” any longer. Privacy legislation, case law, and poor practice are paving the way for how law enforcement looks at using this powerful tool. This session will showcase the successes and pitfalls of open-source information acquisition. It will be a thought provoking and potentially sobering experience for students, and demonstrate the importance of good research, analysis and operational planning in using new information tools. This online facing session will encourage attendees to: Student Outcomes: 1. Articulate their lawful authority to conduct online investigations. 2. Understand the continuum of conclusion when engaged in an intelligence probe or an investigation. Topic: Real -Time Online Monitoring Sub Topics Covered: Twitter, Snapchat, Instagram, Facebook, various Websites Training Deliverables: There are a small host of social media platforms that continue to allow for real- time monitoring. Manual based online “Area Monitoring” allows for illuminating events using open-source information vetted into actionable intelligence. This online facing session will encourage attendees to: Student Outcomes: 1.Utilize Twitter and Snapchat to geo-locate user and event activity. 2.Utilize Hash-tags to locate crowd sourced social media information. 3.Utilize Google Alerts to keep current on emerging events and trends Team Assignment Preparation
Topic: Future Monitoring Sub Topics Covered: Google Analytics, IP Logging Training Deliverables: 1. Learn how to construct a simplistic Google Sites page with embedded analytics 2. Learn how to use IP logging to track entities. Student Outcomes: 1. Create a Google Sites page with embedded analytics 2. Use IP logging to track entities Team Assignment Preparation
