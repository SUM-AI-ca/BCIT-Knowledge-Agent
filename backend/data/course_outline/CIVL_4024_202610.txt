### Course Description
This course provides an introduction to the creation and management of projects and associated drawings using land development software. Topics include basic operations with land development software, terrain modeling, parcel definition and horizontal and vertical alignment. The course includes a component of field survey work to obtain input data for subsequent land development modeling.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use land development software to re-create engineering plans.
- Create a prototype drawing template.
- Create, manage and analyze surfaces.
- Create and manage digital land modelling projects.
- Apply industry standards to land modelling software.
- Interpret data for import to land modelling software.
- Create field data collection standards as part of a land modelling project.
- Produce Industry Standard engineering and survey plans.
- Explain various forms of property ownership, charges, liens and interests.
- Create subdivide plans of land using a variety of methods.
- Demonstrate a field topographic survey in as part of a land development modeling project using a total station and a data collector.
- Use field codes as part of land development field data collection.
- Create industry standard total station field notes.

### Course Goals
This course introduces concepts, theories, and applications of industry standard land development software. Includes data sources, data management, data standards, data sharing, terrain modeling, and parcel definition (subdivision of land). The course culimates with a field survey Term Project where students work in teams and employ standard field surveying equipment such as total stations, digital levels, and electronic field data collection. Survey computations, including determining reference coordinates and leveling adjustments, are integral to the Term Project . Adhering to Worksafe BC safety standards, in the context of field survey operations, are expected in the term field project and closely monitored. Students must follow these important safety procedures or will be asked to leave the project site. Property rights within BC and an overview of Global Navigation Satellite Systems (such as GPS) in the Land Development environment are also covered.
