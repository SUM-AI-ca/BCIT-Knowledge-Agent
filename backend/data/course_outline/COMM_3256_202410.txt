## Course Description
This course continues to develop the skills required for effective communication in other courses of the Architectural and Building Technology program, and in the workplace. This course will support both the elective course and practicum course.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Plan, organize, and write professional formal reports.
- Conduct thorough research that includes professional journals and scientific research studies, not just commercial Internet sites.
- Create, manipulate, insert, and embed informative, accurate, and honest graphics.
- Design effective presentations and deliver them with confidence.
