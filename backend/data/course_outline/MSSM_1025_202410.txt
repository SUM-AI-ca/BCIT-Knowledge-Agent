## Course Description
This 2-day course meets Transport Canada first aid requirements for Marine Basic first aid and includes AED.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate and explain emergency scene management in relation to a responsive and unresponsive casualty.
- Describe how to deal with shock, unconsciousness, and fainting in a casualty.
- Demonstrate and explain the first aid procedures for a conscious choking adult, choking adult who becomes unconscious, choking adult who is pregnant or larger than the rescuer, choking adult found unconscious, and self-administered abdominal thrusts.
- Demonstrate and explain the procedures for performing artificial respiration on an adult, child, and infant using a jaw thrust with and without head tilt.
- Demonstrate and explain cardiovascular emergencies on an adult (1-Rescuer CPR, and 2-Rescuer CPR).
- Understand and demonstrate how to use an automated external defibrillator.
- Understand and discuss different potential medical conditions.
- Describe and discuss rescue carries.
- Demonstrate how to make narrow and broad bandages.
- Demonstrate and explain how to control severe bleeding on a casualty.

Day 1 – Online training will be taught by Primary Response. Prior to the course, students will be emailed the details on how to access the online training. This training must be completed prior to taking the in-class and examination portion of the course, and receive a Transport Canada, training certificate.

Day 2 – Students will be required to take the in-class two hour course and examination at Primary Response (address located under Course Specific Requirements). Students will be notified in advance of their exam date and time.

## Course Specific Requirements
In order to be eligible to work in the Marine Industry, you must pass a Transport Canada Medical Examination.

For information regarding training requirements for marine certification, it is recommended that you contact the Transport Canada Marine Safety Branch. In the Vancouver area, call 604-666-0834 or visit their website at www.tc.gc.ca.
