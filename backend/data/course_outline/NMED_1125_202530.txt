## Course Description
​This course introduces the fundamental knowledge, skills and safety measures required for best practices in a simulated nuclear medicine environment. Topics include an overview of common safety principles as set out by the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission (CNSC), WHMIS, and industry guidelines related to radiopharmacy operations, handling of biohazardous materials and sharps, chemicals, and radioactivity. Students will gain theory and practical experience in basic radiopharmacy applications, including elution of 99Mo/99mTc radionuclide generators; calculation, preparation, and dispensing of radiopharmaceuticals; quality-control procedures; venipuncture technique; and maintenance of records according to CNSC.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the field of nuclear medicine with reference to its historical, present, and emerging applications in the healthcare system. (2.1.5, 6.2.1) ​​​
- Perform handling and disposal of biohazardous substances, chemicals, syringes and sharps according to universal precautions, WHMIS, and general lab safety guidelines. (4.4.3)
- Outline how and when safety equipment and safety procedures are used in a nuclear medicine environment. (NM 1.1, 1.3, 1.5, 1.7, 1.8)
- Demonstrate safe work practices according to CNSC regulations and the ALARA principle in a simulated nuclear medicine environment. (NM 1.1, 1.3, 1.5, 1.7, 1.8, 2.3)
- Calculate predicted generator elution yield and describe factors that affect elution efficiency. (NM 4.2)
- Explain the process of generator elution and the purpose of required quality control on the eluate. (NM 4.3, 4.4)
- Perform generator elution and required quality control on the eluate using ALARA principles and aseptic technique. (NM 4.3, 4.4)
- Describe the chemical process of radiolabeling with reference to chelation, hydrolysis, and the oxidation states of Technetium. (NM 4.5)
- Calculate radiopharmaceutical preparation requirements to meet a simulated clinical patient schedule. (NM 4.5)
- Perform radiopharmaceutical kit reconstitution and dispense doses safely and accurately, according to manufacturer or best-practice guidelines. (NM 4.5, NM 4.9)
- Describe how radiochemical purity is determined for a given radiopharmaceutical with reference to its polarity/solubility characteristics. (NM 4.6)
- Perform basic radiopharmaceutical quality control according to manufacturer or best-practice guidelines. (NM 4.6)
- Classify radionuclides as diagnostic or therapeutic agents using decay schemes and ALARA principles. (NM 1.5, NM 1.6, NM 3.1, NM 5.5)
- Safely perform venipuncture. (NM 6.1)​
