### Course Description
Covers basic components and requirements for sound estimating procedure emphasizing method and accuracy and in preparation for applying material and labour prices.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Interpret construction drawings and specifications for all divisions 1 through 16 for quantity take-off.
- Determine scopes of work for quantity take-off.
- Lay out industry-standard spreadsheet quantities.
- Take off into categories for costing.
- Use Excel as a computer spreadsheet application for data entry, data manipulation and summary consolidation.
### Learning Resources
Required:
- Laptop or computer with MS Excel installed, to be brought to every class for in-class lab exercises.
- Printed or digital set of course reference drawings (Common Exchange Building — provided by the instructor at the start of term).
- MS Excel (Office 365 is available to BCIT students for no charge: https://kb.bcit.ca/student/software-licensing-student-resources-172/ )

- Calculator
Recommended:
- Optional Textbooks: Method of Measurement of Construction Works, 8th Edition, CIQS Estimating in Building Construction, Second Canadian Edition, Dagostino Fundamentals of Construction Estimating, 4th Edition, Pratt
### Course Goals
Introduction to measurement of construction work, with a brief review of reading construction drawings and specifications. Specific study of methods of measurement techniques applicable to site work (excavation), concrete, and reinforcing steel quantity take-offs. Covers basic components and requirements for sound estimating procedures, emphasizing method and accuracy and using the spreadsheet format to take-off quantities in preparation for applying material and labour prices.

### Attendance Requirements
Students are expected to attend all scheduled in-class sessions. This is a once-weekly, on-campus course with 3-hour sessions combining lecture and lab. Regular attendance is essential; much of the learning occurs through in-class exercises, demonstrations, and instructor feedback that cannot be replicated outside the classroom.
Vacation requests will not be accepted during exam weeks (Midterm week 6, Final week 12). If you have vacation planned during these weeks and are not willing or able to attend the exam as scheduled, you must notify the instructor and program head ahead of time.
Students are responsible to make up any assignments missed due to absences before they are returned marked to the rest of the class. Submissions after assignments have been returned will receive a zero mark for that assignment.
Students unable to write the midterm or final exam due to illness must notify the instructor and submit a medical note to the instructor at the time of absence , not at the end of the course. The note must state the time period in question and state that the student was unable to attend class due to medical reasons.
### Course Specific Requirements
This course is delivered on campus once per week in a computer lab. Each 3-hour session combines a lecture portion and a structured lab exercise. Students are expected to:
- Arrive on time and remain for the full duration of the session.
- Engage actively during both the lecture and lab portions of each class.
- Use lab computers for course-related work only during class time.
- Save and back up all in-class Excel work to a personal drive or cloud storage, the instructor is not responsible for lost lab computer files.
