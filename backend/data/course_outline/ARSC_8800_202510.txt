### Course Description
This ARSC 8800 Graduating Project continues the project initiated in ARSC 8280 Graduating Project Proposal. The course is structured in 2 distinct parts. Part 1 focuses on refinement of the predesign brief developed in ARSC 8280. Part 2 will focus on the development of the design project. Students will develop a comprehensive design which fully describes the building including its organization, building systems, sustainable design, and materiality through the development of 2 and 3 dimensional models and drawings including plans, sections, elevations, and conceptual construction details. The Comprehensive Design exercise is intended to demonstrate the extent of the student’s ability to understand and synthesize technical, functional and other requirements of their selected project and how the design can be influenced by an integrated design process. The course is structured as a simulated work experience for the graduating project.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Present the site in plan, photos, models and drawings to the review committee.
- Modification of concepts based on modified programmatic requirements.
- Communicate these concepts in drawing and models, as well as verbally.
- Integrate observation and criticism productively as the project evolves.
- Develop design work to an appropriate level of detail, incorporating understanding of site, program, technology, structure, systems, materials, codes and sustainability issues.
- Incorporate the complete range of design concerns involving client, programme, concept, site, environment, technology, materials, structure, systems and codes.
- Resolve and communicate multi-disciplinary design issues at appropriate scales, including schematic proposals, plans, sections and details.
- Work effectively with limited supervision.
- Produce design development documentation on design based on predecessor course.
- Produce documentation output with digital skills.
- Provide differing viewpoints and solutions of building construction details.
- Prepare a document package that demonstrates process and documentation of both phases of the graduating project.

### Attendance Requirements
Attendance is mandatory during all ARSC classes, presentations and learning events. Students are expected to be present for any and all learning activities scheduled during ARSC program hours (8:30-5:30 Monday to Friday). Graded course work is conducted during this time and may be impossible to make up. Partial attendance will be considered cumulative absence. Poor attendance may render the student unable to complete the course.
In case of absence, course instructor/s and Program Head should be notified with a minimum 24 hours notice unless medically unavoidable. Documentation for the date of absence is to be provided via email within one week's time. In case of undocumented absence the following protocol will be followed:
1) first undocumented absence will receive a warning from instructor
2) second undocumented absence will be referred to program head
3) third undocumented absence will be referred to AD
