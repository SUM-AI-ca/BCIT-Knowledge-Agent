## Course Description
This course, a continuation of Human Behaviour 1, further explores concepts of behavioural science relevant to the electroneurophysiology technologist's concerns. Topics include: a review of and expansion on human interaction skills, ethical and legal issues, human development, and common patient psychosocial issues.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Use advanced active listening skills to clarify and obtain information from patients and colleagues.
- Develop strategies to effectively manage various types of harassment and discrimination in the workplace.
- Adjust communication strategies for developmental differences and impaired cognitive functioning.
- Identify indicators of abuse and respond within ethical and legal guidelines.
- Utilize de-escalation techniques to effectively communicate with patients who are in angry and/or in distress.
- Explain how mental health conditions, such as depression, anxiety, and schizophrenia, may impact interactions with patients and adapt communication strategies accordingly.
- Analyze approaches to managing negative feelings in self and clients.
- Describe the consequences and psychosocial impact of sleep deprivation and sleep disorders.
- Discuss ethical principles in healthcare as applied to practice as an electroneurophysiology technologist.
- Implement psychological strategies and skills to quickly achieve rapport and prevent distress in patients.

Assignments (15% total): Students will complete an Identity, Worldview, and Health assignment (further instructions to be provided)

Exams (Midterm 20%, Final 30%)

Missed exams and late arrival to exams: Failure to take an exam at the scheduled time will result in a grade of “0” for that exam, unless a physician’s note is provided or other arrangements were made with the instructor prior to the day of the exam. The instructor reserves the right to utilize an alternative assessment method (which may be different from the original) in the case of a missed exam or in the case of students arriving more than 20 minutes late to a scheduled exam. If a student misses an exam or presentation, she/he must contact the instructor by email no later than the following day. Failure to do so may result in a grade of zero.

Research Paper (25%): Students will write a paper summarizing the literature on a selected topic relating to a psychosocial issue for patients in ENPY. Papers must include specific recommendations for practice in ENPY. Students will select their own topics. It is recommended that students talk to instructor to ensure the topic is appropriate in both nature and scope. More information to be provided in class.

Participation (10%): Participation marks are awarded at the end of the term at the discretion of the Instructor. Criteria considered when determining this mark include attendance, demonstration of active listening and classroom engagement, and participation in group discussions and small group activities.
