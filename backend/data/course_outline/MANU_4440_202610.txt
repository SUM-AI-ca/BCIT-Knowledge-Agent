### Course Description
The course builds on the fundamentals of physics to provide students with a theoretical framework of selected topics in fluid mechanics, thermodynamics and heat transfer applicable to manufacturing engineering. Topics covered include: Hydrostatic pressure; buoyancy; Pascal’s Law; conservation of mass and the continuity equation; power conversion devices (compressors pumps, turbines, cylinders); heat capacity; thermal expansion; conduction; convection, and; radiation. Practical aspects of fluid power systems and their application in industry are covered.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use the Principle of Archimedes to calculate the buoyancy force on a partially or fully immersed object.
- Use Pascal’s law to analyse hydrostatic fluid systems.
- Use the continuity equation to analyse steady flow in simple flow systems.Apply heat conduction, convection and radiation laws to analyse basic heat transfer configurations
- Use specific heat capacity to determine temperature changes of substances undergoing heat transfer.
- Calculate linear, areal and volumetric expansion of substances undergoing to temperature changes.
- Identify and describe the function of the different components of pneumatic and hydraulic systems.
- Read the symbolic diagrams of basic fluid power circuits and identify their flow control objectives.
- Analyse a fluid power system with reference to energy consumption and efficiencies.
