### Course Description
The course focuses on the development of current sustainable development concepts and principles, and how these principles impact and relate to the day to day SRM technologists tasks in for those working in BC. Discussion includes an overview of the historical development of SRM concepts, environmental ethics, global sustainable development issues, and some of the challenges related to the implementation SRM principles. A review of the various government ministries, administrative and quasi-judicial organizations used to encourage sustainable resource management practices will be discussed. The biogeophysical characteristics, industrial structure, and socio-economic characteristics within each BC’s major development regions will be described. Included will be an introduction to First Nation communities, and organizations involved in BC resource management. Online BC data sources and computer applications will be used to analyze and present an overview of the current status of various resources management industries.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
​Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Outline the concepts of sustainable resource management.
- Outline the role and importance of how technologist level employment tasks play in implementing sustainable development.
- Analyze how significant historical government policies that have influenced the development of BC industry and community structure.
- Outline the role natural resources play in shaping the current BC economy.
- Outline important ministries, departments, and Acts associated with various BC resource industries.
- Access important sources of BC socio-economic information.
- Create Excel spreadsheets and Access databases to record, summarize, and display data.
- Use relevant MsWord tools for creating reports.
- Outline important issues related to population demographics and employment trends found in various BC communities and regions.
- Describe the biogeophysical characteristics of BC's development regions.
- Describe the impact of European contact and colonization on First Nations societies in Canada and BC.
- Outline aboriginal “Rights and Title” in a historical, political and legal context.
- Compare the historical and contemporary First Nations approaches to land and resources management.
- Outline Aboriginal organization’s concerns, issues, and projects related to resource management in the province.​

### Course topics
The major course topics include:
- The development of sustainable resource management principles.
- BC sustainable resource management issues.
- An introduction to BC Indigneous communities
- Illustration of how SRM principles impact planning and field activities.
- A review of: The locations of BC cities and towns. BC lakes and rivers. Biogeophysical regions. Site assessment guides Potential impacts of climate chanage on BC resource management and communities BC land use patterns. Overview of forest land tenures BC geohazards. Administrative regions and their economic characteristics. The structure of the BC forest industry, and forest related issues. Other major BC resourse industries, and interelationships between them and the forest industry.
- The use of Excel to analyse data.
- The use of Access to organize and present data.
- Initroduction to the use of computer modeling for resource management issues
