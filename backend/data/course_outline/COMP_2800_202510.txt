## Course Description
Students work in teams of four or five to complete an advanced mobile-friendly web application in a real-world domain. Teams will proceed through the majority of the software development lifecycle, from requirements gathering to specification, UI/UX design, to implementation and delivery. The course is focused on developing teamwork and project management skills as well as an understanding of the development lifecycle. The project will simultaneously allow students to apply their previously-developed technical knowledge. Students are challenged to effectively apply new technologies such as AI and cloud computing to achieve project goals. Prerequistes: COMP 1510, COMP 1537, COMP 1800 Co-requisite: COMP 2537

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Determine and document project requirements.
- Plan and schedule project work tasks in agile manner.
- Work effectively with a team to meet stated tasks and milestones.
- Design a system that meets the documented requirements.
- Implement a system that has been designed.
- Test and deliver a working system.

## Attendance Requirements
Regular attendance is seen as critical to student success, and will be monitored by faculty. Unless otherwise stated, all labs/lectures, scrums, and client meetings are assumed to be mandatory. Consider this course as training for a full-time job. The standard institute policy on attendance applies. Exemptions for health-related situations may be considered with official documentation submitted to the Lead Instructors and Program Head.

Your individual contribution to the group project will, in part, be evaluated on your professional behavior, which includes regular attendance, punctuality, and participation in scrum and client meetings. Being 5 minutes late for a scrum will be considered as an absence.

Failure to attend mandatory meetings may result in your individual final calculated grade being scaled down:

- 1 missed session = no adjustment
- 2 missed sessions = 5% penalty
- 3 missed sessions = 10% penalty
- 4 missed sessions = 20% penalty
- 5 missed sessions = a grade of U
