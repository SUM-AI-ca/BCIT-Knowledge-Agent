## Course Description
This course introduces practical applications of video for acquisition and display of data. Fundamentals of analog and digital video raster scan displays are developed. The types of equipment used in image creation, transmission, and storage are discussed. Principles of illumination are introduced and the characteristics and construction of light sources and modifiers are discussed. Principals of photography and photographic equipment are explored. Methods of image capture are investigated. Image processing and manipulation topics are developed using appropriate application software. Applications specific to Biomedical Engineering, such as endoscopy, microscopy, and digital radiology will be presented. Topics in the application of digital image creation, acquisition, processing and display will be presented as lab activities.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Build, test and analyze video signal processing, digitizing, image storage, data display and display circuitry. Analyze devices and circuits used in illumination engineering. Analyze and manipulate images with software used in PC-based electronic display systems.

## ELECTRONIC IMAGE DISPLAYS
- Understand the operation, advantages and disadvantages of common display types covering a range of electronic image display technologies.•Cathode Ray Tube (CRT) displays.•Liquid Crystal Display (LCD) displays.•Organic Light Emitting Diode (OLED) displays.•Plasma displays.•Electronic paper.

## DIGITAL VIDEO PROCESSING
- •Describe basic video characteristics including frame rate, resolution, and compression.•Describe the principles of video capture and storage.•Describe and practice video compression techniques.

## DIGITAL IMAGE PROCESSING
- Describe the application of software to enhance, manipulate and present images for photographic and medical applications. •Describe grayscale and color mapping. •Describe brightness and contrast control methods. •Describe image reorientation, magnification and level shifting methods. •Describe the need for windowing and colorization. •Describe histogram equalization. •Describe filtering and image calibration. •Perform image manipulation using one or more image processing software tools.

## PHOTOGRAPHIC METHODS FOR BIOMEDICAL ENGINEERING
- •Describe principles of photography, lens systems and lighting for photography.•Describe camera types, CCD, CMOS, 3D technology, chromatic and geometric aberration of images.

## ILLUMINATION ENGINEERING AND COLOR
- Calculate the basic parameters of illumination engineering. Performed lighting measurements and identify light sources by type and application •Describe light characteristics and human eye response.•Describe various light sources and their effects on materials and display devices.•Perform lab experiments on light sources and take measurements to determine applicability of sources.•Be able to make calculations from experiments on light sources.•Describe and measure the effects that filter or modify materials have a light.•Examine the transport of light for medical imaging and optical fibres.

## VIDEO SIGNAL
- Identify and describe video signals including:
- VGA, Composite, S-Video, DVI, HDMI, DisplayPort

## FURTHER APPLICATIONS
- Study applications of electronic display systems used in the field of Biomedical Engineering.•Endoscopy, MRI, ultrasound, CT, and digital radiology
