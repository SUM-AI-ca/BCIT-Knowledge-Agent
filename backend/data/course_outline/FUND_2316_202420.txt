## Course Description
This is a practical introduction to the elements of proposal and grant writing: purpose and responsibility, organizational readiness, research, mechanics of preparation, development of a formal proposal/grant, presentation, follow-up, and post grant reporting. Students gain skills through hands-on practice identifying potential funders, and developing and refining their proposal.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Undertake a grant search to identify potential funding sources for his/her project.
- Evaluate the fit between his/her project and funder interests and priorities.
- Apply appropriate concepts and formats to grant letters and proposals.
- Prepare a project proposal for a granting agency.
- Use research/data to create a project rationale and needs statement.
- Prepare a detailed project work plan.
- Prepare a project budget.
- Prepare an outline for an evaluation plan for a project's grant application.
- Describe key characteristics of an effective grant proposal.
- Describe how funders assess grant proposals.
- Evaluate a project proposal based on grant criteria.
- State the expectations and responsibilities of grantees.

## Learning Resources
Writing For A Good Cause, by Joseph Barbato and Danielle S. Furlich, Simon and Schuster, 2000

## Course Goals
This course will orient students to the essentials of grant, proposal, and sponsorship research, writing and presentation. Students will define a program or project for which they are seeking funding, research a list of potential funders, and develop a full proposal by the end of the course. They will learn how to locate potential funders or sponsors, find a match for their project, develop various application formats, outline project and evaluation plans, and prepare for the application assessment. Students will be exposed to the granting process, and learn how requests are considered by funders, and what to expect if their proposal is successful. Strategies for success will be combined with practical examples, case studies, resources, and reference materials.

## Course topics
Module 1: Introduction to Course

Content Topics: Research and Organizational Readiness

- Overview of funding organizations: foundations, corporations, service clubs, government agencies.
- Researching prospective grantors and sponsors: sources of information, communications approaches, finding the “fit”.
- Planning your approach: understanding the grant cycle and application process.

Learning Outcomes:

- Prepare for grant writing.
- Prepare for sponsorship development.
- Identify potential funding sources.
- Evaluate the fit between project and funder.
- Use effective research methods and sources.
- Describe the grant cycle and application process.

Module 2: Content Topics: Grant components start to finish

- Overview of Grant Components: letter of inquiry, grant proposal, site visits, project reports, thank yous and acknowledgements.
- Overview of sponsorship development.

- Planning a proposal: the grant cycle and application process.
- Developing relationships with funders.

Learning Outcomes:

- Plan for grant writing.
- Describe the components of the grant cycle.
- Describe the components of a sponsorship plan.
- Explain the importance of the grantee/grantor relationship.
- Illustrate approaches to developing relationships with funders.

Module 3: Content Topics: Organizing your proposal and your information

- Letters of Inquiry, Letters of Intent
- Sponsorship requests
- Application forms and formats
- Components of grant proposals: Introduction of organization Background information Statement of need Goals, objectives, workplans Budgets, financial statements Evaluation, dissemination plans
- Introduction of organization
- Background information
- Statement of need
- Goals, objectives, workplans
- Budgets, financial statements
- Evaluation, dissemination plans

Learning Outcomes:

- Describe the elements of the letter of inquiry and the grant proposal.
- Prepare a project budget.
- Organize information into a grant application or sponsorship proposal.
- Plan for adapting grant and sponsorship proposal components for use with multiple applications.

Module 4: Content Topics: Project planning approaches and methodologies

- Goals, objectives, strategies
- Workplans; action plans
- Logic frames
- Theory of change

Learning Outcomes:

- Articulate project goals and objectives.
- Prepare project descriptions in various formats.
- Apply appropriate concepts and designs to grant proposals.

Module 5: Content Topics: Evaluation concepts and strategies

- Types of evaluations
- Data collection strategies
- Evaluation planning
- Ethical, financial and other considerations

Learning Outcomes:

- Describe basic evaluation concepts and techniques.
- Apply relevant evaluation strategies to grant proposals.

Module 6: Content Topics: Refining the Proposal

- Use of language, formatting, supporting materials
- Characteristics of a compelling proposal

Learning Outcomes:

- Describe the characteristics of a compelling grant application or proposal.
- Review and refine a grant proposal.
- Apply appropriate formatting and lay-out to content of grant proposals.
- Incorporate stories into a grant proposal.
- Prepare appropriate attachments for final proposal submission.

Module 7: Content Topics: The Grant Assessment Process

- What to expect from the grantor.
- Site visits
- Submitting the proposal

Learning Outcomes:

- Prepare for funders’ interviews and site visits.
- Provide constructive feedback on a grant or sponsorship proposal.
- Describe a variety of funders’ approaches to proposal assessment.

Module 8: Content Topics: The Grant Application Follow-up

- The Grant Application Follow-up
- Unsuccessful submissions; successful submissions.
- Acknowledgement, recognition, project monitoring and reports.

Learning Outcomes:

- Describe strategies to deal with unsuccessful grant applications.
- Describe a variety of project monitoring activities.
- Explain basic elements of a grant report.
- Illustrate a variety of funder acknowledgement and recognition activities.
