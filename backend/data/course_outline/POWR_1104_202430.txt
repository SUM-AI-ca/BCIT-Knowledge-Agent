## Course Description
This course covers basic electricity, electrical problem solving, and introduces electrical machines and devices found in industrial plants. Topics for study include atomic theory, circuit fundamentals, circuit analysis, conductors, insulation, magnetism, and motors and generators. This course also prepares students to meet the requirements of the Interprovincial 4th class syllabus.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:

- Analyze electric circuits and perform basic calculations to solve electrical circuit problems.
- Troubleshoot reading, measuring and calculating electrical problems.
- Demonstrate how knowledge of electrical principals aids in troubleshooting problems occurring in the field.
- Demonstrate safe work practices around electrical equipment.
