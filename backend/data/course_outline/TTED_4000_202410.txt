## Course Description
This course builds on the fundamental skills and principles of visual literacy and design experienced in the foundation courses and applies them to the preparation of working drawings for product manufacture. A study in interior design and architecture will also provide a basis for aesthetic and structural synthesis. Students will apply the basics of formal drafting techniques, standards and conventions in both electronic and free hand formats.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe mechanical objects using hand drawn lines.
- Apply drawing set-up standards.
- Create multi-view drawings.
- Manage drawing organization through assembly, sub-assembly and detail drawings.
- Create reference data by reverse engineering and measuring an existing product.
- Create auxiliary views.
- Generate and recognize threads and fastener representation.
- Describe the basic physical arrangements of western platform construction.
- Consider architectural space planning and methods when developing a design.
- Interpret architectural floor plans and sections.
- Calculate building loads and timber spans.
- Specify framing materials using the BC Building Code.
- Apply drafting concepts, techniques, and terminology using CAD software.
- Generate drawings that conform to applicable standards using CAD software.
- Maximize efficiency and speed using tools available in CAD software.
