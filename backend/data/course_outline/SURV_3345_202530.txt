### Course Description
SURV 3345 builds on the foundations of SURV 2145 and covers the technical aspects of surveying in a mining environment. This includes the electronic data collection of survey data using Total Stations and the subsequent survey computations.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use a total station for mine surveys.
- Complete a survey traverse.
- Use electronic data collection.
- Perform a field survey resection.
- Layout drill patterns.
- Survey existing drill holes.
- Conduct a field survey for a digital surface model.
- Calculate a mining excavation volume.
- Keep industry standard field notes.
- Conduct survey operations in a safe and efficient manner.
### Learning Resources
- Lab and lecture handouts, protractor, metric scale, straight edge and pencil.
- Scientific hand calculator & waterproof cover (e.g. sandwich bag).
- Waterproof field book and field book paper.
- Permanent felt marker (Sharpie fine point or equivalent).
- Pocket tape (at least 3 meters)
- Rain gear (highly recommended) and suitable footwear.
- Safety is paramount. For outdoor field labs it is essential to wear a high visibility vest, protective clothing, and footwear.
