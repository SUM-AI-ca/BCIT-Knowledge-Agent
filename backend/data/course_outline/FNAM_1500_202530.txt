### Course Description
​Safety is everyone’s responsibility. Knowing and understanding what creates a safe working environment is crucial to your long-term professional career. This comprehensive course provides the essential training and certifications needed to launch a successful and safe career in the natural resource sector. Through a combination of classroom instruction, hands-on training, and field exercises, participants will gain vital workplace safety knowledge, practical skills, and industry-recognized certifications. This includes developing essential digital literacy skills to effectively utilize computer applications and manage files for data organization and communication in the field.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Obtain industry-recognized safety certifications such as: S-100/S-185 (fire suppression and safety), Basic First Aid, WHMIS, and industry training in Indigenous Awareness and Wildlife Awareness principles.
- Apply field safety practices including the correct use of personal protective equipment (PPE), use of safety tailgate forms, adhering to check-in/check-out protocols, and utilizing communication plans.
- Explain workplace safety regulations including employment safety standards, worker rights, and legal obligations relevant to the natural resource sector.
- Operate and maintain essential natural resource management tools (such as chainsaw or brushsaw) safely and according to industry best practices.
- Apply basic computer applications and file management techniques for effective data organization and communication.​
