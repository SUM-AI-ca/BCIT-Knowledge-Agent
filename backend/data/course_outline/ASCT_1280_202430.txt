## Course Description
Through the study of project management and controls, this subject area endeavors to develop the Student's: (1) Awareness to financial considerations to project development. (2) Decision making ability in evaluating economic considerations. Ability to analyze & discuss issues related to project development.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify the factors affecting economic consideration in construction.
- Identify the principles of land development, & land ownership.
- Employ the use of the computer to provide spread sheet calculations.
- Prepare & analyze Project financial planning practices and statement preparation.
- Develop Capital Budgets, feasibility studies of land development, Pro Forma Breakdown.
- Interpret project control mechanisms: cost, planning, cash flow analysis.
