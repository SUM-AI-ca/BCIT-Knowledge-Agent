## Course Description
In this course students are immersed in a radio/TV/online newsroom environment. Students will learn to function in a working environment across platforms with deadlines and incorporating industry ethical and legal standards in their work. Students will operate the Evolution newsroom, produce BCIT Magazine and produce multi-media for the Journalism website www.bcitbroadcastnews.ca. Students will also contribute to our social media such as Facebook, Twitter and Instagram.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Find, research and produce effective television/radio/online news and current events stories including original stories and ongoing stories with original angles.
- Apply industry standards to writing for video, audio production and online stories.
- Incorporate a variety of photojournalism techniques to create engaging compositions.
- Apply legal and ethical standards to radio/television/online content creation.
- Demonstrate effective use of sound in radio/television/online.
- Demonstrate effective industry standard teamwork skills and individual initiative.
- Demonstrate effective audio and video presentation skills.
- Research and conduct an engaging longer-form interview.
- Utilize a variety of digital tools to create alternative storyform content for online distribution.

- a microphone + adapter for mobile phone
- cell phone + data plan

- access to a car of public transit while chasing stories

Professionalism: marks are determined by the following criteria:

- Attendance at all classes
- Punctuality and adherence to deadlines
- Communication with instructor and classmates
- Journalistic integrity
- News awareness

- Work Ethic: initiative and willingness to contribute to class discussion
- Reviewing feedback and seeking clarification when needed

- Understanding of and adherence to the BCIT Academic Misconduct policy*
- Understanding of and adherence to BCIT Sexual Harassment and Discrimination policies

*Academic Misconduct: In addition to BCIT’s policy, Academic Misconduct within the Journalism Program may also be defined as…

- Deliberately inaccurate or misleading reporting
- Misrepresentation of facts or individuals
- Conflicts of interest

Note on Academic Misconduct and use of AI:

“The unauthorized use of Artificial Intelligence products (such as ChatGPT) for any part of student work in this course is prohibited, and when identified by your instructor, will result in an academic integrity violation. Refer to policy 5104 - Student Code of Academic Integrity.”

IMPORTANT: A lack of familiarity with the regulations cannot be used as a defence by those accused of academic misconduct. It is the student's responsibility to become familiar with the regulations.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
