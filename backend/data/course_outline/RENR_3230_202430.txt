## Course Description
This course is designed to provide students with experience developing research objectives and methods related to projects in the fields of fish, wildlife, and recreation. As a team, students will design a project in collaboration with a community sponsor, complete background research, and begin collecting data. As part of this course, students will develop project goals and objectives, create an appropriate study design, begin writing a technical report, develop safety and communication plans, and incorporate supervisor feedback on iterative submissions. Content and skills learned in the first year of the program will be used throughout this course for in‐field data collection and written data summaries.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Create a project schedule, data workflow, and management plan for the project
- Develop and implement safety and communication plans specific to project activities, client, and site
- Develop goals and objectives specific to the project
- Develop and implement appropriate study design and methodology specific to project
- Identify and manage gear/equipment required for project
- Prepare preliminary sections of a draft technical report for the project
- Integrate feedback and edits in an iterative series of report submissions through term
- Demonstrate safe, respectful, and professional behaviour that meets industry standards and expectations

## Learning Resources
Learning resources for this course are provided in the online course under the module Resources. Additional past reports completed by FWR students are available upon request. Requirements for resources and equipment and availability of past reports and data will vary from project to project. Please note that some projects may require permits for field work. Contacts and links will be made available as needed for each project. Please discuss project resources, field gear needs and permit requirements with your project supervisor.

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the students will be able to:

- Develop and use a database/data management system
- Create/identify project purpose, goals and objectives
- Design/apply field methods to meet purpose, goals and objectives of study.
- Develop safety and communication plans specific to project activities, client and site.
- Identify, apply for and secure necessary permits/permissions for study.
- Conduct field study: identify and manage gear/equipment, complete field safety checks, collect and manage field data.
- Construct framework and complete sections of study report.
- Integrate feedback and complete necessary edits in iterative series of submissions through term.
- Work effectively both independently and as members of a team.
