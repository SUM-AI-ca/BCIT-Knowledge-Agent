### Course Description
In this hands-on course students research and produce their own video content using ethical storytelling principles. They also have opportunities to be on-air hosts and show producers. Students with a minimum average of 70% have the opportunity to participate in an industry placement as part of this course.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate collaboration during show production.
- Produce elements for a live TV show.
- Apply storytelling principles to create focused video content.
- Produce content to deadline.
- Demonstrate effective videography skills using professional video cameras.
- Demonstrate effective video editing skills using digital editing software

Journalistic integrity: In addition to BCIT’s policy, academic integrity violations within the journalism program may also be defined as…
- Deliberately inaccurate or misleading reporting
- Misrepresentation of facts or individuals
- Conflicts of interest (including but not limited to interviewing family, friends, roommates, or individuals at places you work or accepting a gift or monetary compensation for coverage, excluding exchanges as part of cultural traditions)
- Falsifying audio, images, or video, including the use of artificial intelligence
Additional note regarding lab evaluation: evaluation includes a compilation of all work a student submits for vetting or marking while in a specific role. Therefore, penalties for violations of academic or journalistic integrity will be deducted from a student’s overall mark in that role.

Academic Integrity and Use of Artificial Intelligence : The unauthorized use of Artificial Intelligence tools (such as ChatGPT) for any part of student work in this course is prohibited, and, when identified by your instructor, will result in an academic integrity violation. See BCIT Policy 5104 Student Code of Academic Integrity. A violation of academic integrity may result in one or more of the following consequences:
- A written warning;
- Required resubmission of the work in question for equal or lower grade value;
- A lower grade on the work submitted (i.e. a penalty applied);
- A failing grade on the work submitted; or
- An academic misconduct letter being placed on the student’s record.
