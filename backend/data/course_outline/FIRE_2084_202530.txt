## Course Description
In this course, students are introduced to the basics of fire alarm systems, their operation, and the correct method to conduct periodic inspections and tests. Completion of inspection reports will be covered, along with applicable Building Code and Fire Code requirements.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will have a basic, working understanding of:

- Describe fire alarm systems components, functions and operation.
- Discuss codes, standards and regulations applying to fire alarm systems.
- Identify and describe applicable inspection and test procedures.
- Conduct inspections and tests to ensure compliance.
- Complete appropriate documentation and reports.
