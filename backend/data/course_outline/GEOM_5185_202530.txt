## Course Description
​This course examines current remotely piloted aircraft systems (RPAS) platforms, acquisition systems, mission-planning, and image capture and processing. The course focuses on the structure-from-motion (SfM) approach to photogrammetry, and topics include ground control points, accuracy assessment, payload selection, and common applications of data outputs. Students learn the topics through lectures and hands-on labs.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify and describe common RPAS remote sensing datasets and their applications.
- Explain proper data collection and processing techniques involved in RPAS remote sensing projects.
- Select appropriate RPAS remote sensing solutions matched to project specifications and deliverables.
- Conduct RPAS airborne mission-planning.
- Apply appropriate ground control points to improve absolute spatial accuracy.
- Illustrate the geometry and characteristics of optical camera(s) used in RPAS photogrammetric surveys.
- Compare and contrast various types of resolutions in data quality captured by cameras.
- Describe concepts, limitations and benefits of basic image-processing, enhancement, and data-manipulation techniques.
- Apply mathematical models relating sensor space to object space, including camera calibration.
- Describe and explain the use of optical and range data for 3D reconstructions and modelling.
- Apply DEM and orthoimage generation.
- Discuss common LiDAR systems, including their benefits and limitations.
- Describe, explain, and illustrate the geometry and characteristics of laser scanner sensors.
- Conduct mission-planning for an RPAS LiDAR/mobile mapping project.
- Illustrate the relevant calibration processes for LiDAR and mobile mapping systems.
- Process an RPAS LiDAR project using industry-standard software and generate project deliverables.​
