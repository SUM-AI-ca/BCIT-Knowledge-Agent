## Course Description
In Communication 4442, you will build on the skills you learned in Communication 3342. You will plan and write a progress report and a formal report for your industry-sponsored project, as well as any related documentation for the project as needed. You will also design and create a static display and present the project orally with professional visuals. You will also prepare a professional functional resume and letter of application.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Write and edit a major industry-oriented formal technical report. [7]
- Design and incorporate effective graphics, including drawings. [7]
- Incorporate complex calculations effectively into written formats. [7]
- Prepare and present periodic progress reports. [7]
- Design a static display poster for an industry project. [7]
- Present an oral explanation of your project, enhanced with visuals. [7]
- Prepare a professional resume and application letter [7]
- Research and report on professional development activities [12]

## Attendance Requirements
Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Most/ all assignments have an attendance requirement component in the evaluation criteria. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable cause of absence, the student must communicate as soon as possible with his/ her/ their instructor, indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT approved medical certificate submitted to the department, substantiating the reason for absence. For other absences, the student should be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation if required. Please see Policy 5101 - Student Regulations, and accompanying procedures.
