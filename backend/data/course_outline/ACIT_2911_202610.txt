### Course Description
This course provide students with hands-on experience developing application software using an Agile methodology such as Scrum. Collaboration and teamwork are used to continuously develop software over a series of iterations. Students learn to cope with changes, to identify and manage multiple work tasks, and to focus on maintaining a potentially releasable product throughout the project.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe a modern agile development framework.
- Describe the roles and responsibilities on an agile development project.
- Plan and schedule work using agile development techniques.
- Create the artifacts associated with an agile development project.
- Maintain a potentially releasable product throughout agile project.
- Use modern version control applications to manage project source code and related artifacts.
- Use continuous integration with unit testing, to maintain software quality.
- Use functional testing tools to assure proper project behaviour.
- Use modern collaboration software throughout an agile development project.
- Participate in the various project meetings associated with an agile development project.

### Course Goals
Students will undertake a team project, extending previous technology and adding skills for the development of software based systems. They will apply the core technical knowledge acquired in the program so far, completing the project by collaborating with their team members.

### Attendance Requirements
Attendance in lectures, labs, team meetings and presentations (supervised/unsupervised) is mandatory. Students are required to attend lectures, supervisor meetings and presentations with your team members during regular school hours. Attendance will be taken in all scheduled online lectures (no makeup lectures). Students are required to attend and participate all scheduled meetings with team members.
### Course Specific Requirements
This course includes mandatory group activities. Failure to participate may result in failing the course.
Course Website: All course materials are available on the Learning Hub. Please check regularly for announcements, assignment deadlines, and other updates.
Group Composition: Each team will consist of a maximum of 6 members. Teams are pre-assigned by the instructors at the start of the course and will be posted on the Learning Hub. Students are required to remain in their assigned groups unless they withdraw from the course. Instructors reserve the right to reassign students to different groups to ensure the successful completion of the project.
Group Communication: Each group is responsible for establishing an online communication channel, such as Discord or Slack, to facilitate remote collaboration among team members.
Quizzes: There are three quizzes, based on online learning materials, lecture content, and lab practice. A score of zero will be given to any student who misses a quiz. Please note that there are no makeup quizzes.

### Course topics
Project topics and technologies will be covered in instructional seminars every week. Students are required to attend each instructional seminar (Check your weekly schedule on the Learning Hub) on time. All teams will conduct a weekly check-in meeting with their respective supervisors. In addition, the students will have unsupervised collaborative time to complete their group tasks.
The project that the students undertake will be completed using Agile development practices. There will be four one-week iterations that produce required project artifacts or feature deliverables starting in Week 1.
The individuals and teams will conduct iteration reviews between iterations, and a project review at the end of the course. These will be assessed by the instructors. Additional comments and guidance will also be provided.
