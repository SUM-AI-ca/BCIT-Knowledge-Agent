## Course Description
Examines the planning stages involved in starting a new business including market, financial and legal feasibility requirements. The student develops a full business plan.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Assess their entrepreneurial potential and increase their understanding of the relationship between personal planning, level of commitment, and the development of an entrepreneurial venture.
- Formulate, design and structure a new venture.
- Develop a comprehensive business plan which can potentially be put into operation upon completion of the course.
- Describe the character, problems, environment and opportunities that exist for the small business sector in Canada.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.
