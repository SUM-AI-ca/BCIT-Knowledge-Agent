## Course Description
​CPIM 8.0 combines key concepts from the field of operations management. It details eight domains of operations management. These include: [1] Align the Supply Chain to support the Business Strategy, [2] Conduct Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP) to Support Strategy, [3] Plan and Manage Demand, [4] Plan and Manage Supply, [5] Plan, Manage, and Execute Detailed Schedules [6] Plan and Manage Inventory, [7] Plan and Manage Distribution, [8] Manage Quality, Continuous Improvement, and Technology. This comprehensive training course provides the student with the information and techniques to thoroughly understand the APICS body of knowledge for the Certiﬁed in Planning and Inventory Management (CPIM) exam. This course is the only single resource available that is designed specifically to help candidates pass the ASCM/APICS CPIM 8.0 exam. The APICs learning system materials provided in this course include printed handouts, knowledge progress checks, practice quizzes, practice exams and case studies. ​​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify the topics covered in the ASMC/APICS CPIM 8.0 certiﬁcation exam.
- Use the ASCM/APICS CPIM online learning portal to comprehend the CPIM exam topics.
- Identify the topics covered in the ASCM/APICS CPIM 8.0 version Understand the business environment in which an organization operates, align business and functional strategies, and make decisions to support competitive advantage.
- Evaluate supply and demand at an aggregate level, reconcile product/service portfolios, demand, supply, and financial plans while considering trade-offs.
- Understand the markets in which an organization chooses to operate, and how to align an organization's resources and business strategies.
- Learn how to manage inventory in support of an organization's resource availability, business and functional strategies, and tactical planning.
- Understand how to implement both material and capacity plans, including the adjustment of schedules, queues, and work prioritization to meet demand and inventory goals.
- Learn how to design a distribution network while considering the various investment cost and service trade-offs, and competitive priorities.
- Understand the use of appropriate technology tools and emerging technologies that support supply chain processes.​​​ ​

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
