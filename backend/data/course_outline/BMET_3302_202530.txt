## Course Description
Consists of four modules: • Biomedical Engineering Practice: discussion of o The hospital and medical device industry environment o The functions of the biomedical engineering technologists in R & D and technical support settings o Principles and practice of healthcare technology management • Medical Device Standards and Regulations, which includes o The regulatory context in Canada o Relevant safety standards and inspection procedures o Risk management o Design control • Troubleshooting Techniques: introduces formal and practical approaches to problemsolving and troubleshooting • Preparation for Biomedical Engineering Project: preparation of a project proposal to be carried out in BMET 4402. Laboratory sessions, projects and assignments reinforce the theory and allow you to gain experience in electrical safety measurements and troubleshooting techniques.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Explain the function of a biomedical engineering department in a health care environment.
- Discuss the role of a biomedical engineering technologist.
- Explain the main principles and practice in healthcare technology management.
- Locate relevant information in medical device standards and regulations.
- Carry out medical device safety tests in accordance with applicable standards.
- Explain the key elements of a product development process that is compliant with medical device regulations.
- Use different troubleshooting techniques and approaches to solve problems related to biomedical engineering.
- Prepare a project proposal for developing a small assistive or medical device.

## Attendance Requirements
Students are expected to complete all course works, and attend all classes including laboratory sessions. Excessive absences may result in failure or withdrawal from this course.

Laboratory works or assessments (such as lab exams, mid-term exam, or final exam) must be completed within the scheduled period. No marks will be assigned to such work completed outside of the scheduled period.

No make-up lab or assessment will be provided, even after an excused absence (as described below). However, if the absence is excused, the missed lab will not count toward the final grade. Students are encouraged to come to the lab during office hours for learning purposes.

An absence may be excused in case of illness or other unavoidable cause of absence. In this case, the student must communicate as soon as possible with the course instructor. Prolonged illness must be substantiated by a medical certificate. Absence other than illness must be supported by valid documentation. Based on the information provided, the instructor will determine if the absence is deemed to be excused.

## Course Specific Requirements
AI-based content generation tools, or AI tools, are permitted for assignments and project work. However, they are not allowed during class assessments (e.g., in exams). Note that AI output may include incorrect or misleading information, fabricated authors/dates/publications, faked works, or may incorporate discriminatory ideas. It is your responsibility to ensure the quality, accuracy and appropriateness of the work you submit in this course.

If you use ChatGPT (or a similar tool) to get ideas and/or partial answers for an assignment and/or to generate any text for a draft or final version of any part of an assignment, you must declare that you have used it, with a short paragraph describing the extent to which it was used, and you must save any generated text from this tool in case it is requested. You will not be penalized for using this tool, but the instructor may ask you to provide the generated text to help with grading decisions.

Material directly copied from any source (e.g. websites, books, AI tools) must be cited. Undeclared use of AI is a violation of academic integrity as it presents work, or ideas created by tools or other people as your own. Failure to fully declare the use of this tool will be considered a violation of BCIT academic policy.
