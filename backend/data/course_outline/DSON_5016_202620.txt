### Course Description
This course prepares the student for entry into the clinical environment. The student clinical role and responsibilities will be discussed including professional behavior, clinical evaluation and performance expectations. Professional awareness topics including safety, emerging issues in sonography and professional standards are also covered. These topics will be covered through lectures and exploration of clinical scenarios.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Adhere to relevant provincial and federal legislation and regulations. (2.1a)
- Adhere to relevant professional scope of practice, code of ethics, and institutional policies and procedures. (2.1b, 2.1c)
- Demonstrate awareness of professional liability. (2.3m)
- Comply with requirements of the provincial regulatory body, including applicable Standards of Practice and sexual abuse prevention guidelines. (2.1d)
- Identify the importance of consistently managing time and workload efficiently, maintaining a clean and orderly work area, critically appraising performance, and setting goals for self-improvement. (2.3g, 2.4a, 6.1a)
- Recognize hazardous conditions and adverse events in the work area; disclose and respond accordingly. (2.3l, 6.1b)
- Given a scenario, evaluate clinical behaviours related to patient informed consent, negligence of care, and patient right to privacy.
- Describe how to find information at each worksite on fire and disaster plans, as well as where to locate emergency equipment. (6.1c, 6.1d)
- Recognize the need for a chaperone, when to ask for guidance, and determine the need for additional personnel to assist in the examination. (2.2e, 2.3k, 3.1g)
- Practice within the limits of personal knowledge, skill, and judgment. (2.2d)
- Demonstrate how to share knowledge with patients, patient's relatives, colleagues, students, and other members of the health care team. (2.3j, 2.3e, 1.1i, 1.1j)
- Using CompTracker, create and explain the use of evaluation forms in the clinical environment.
- Demonstrate awareness of the need for continuing professional development. (2.4c)
- Using student-led presentations, describe current and emerging issues in health care relevant to the practice of sonography. (2.3n)
- Follow standardized procedures for handling and disposing of sharps and contaminated and biohazardous materials. (6.2d)
- Accept responsibility for decisions and actions. (2.2i)
- Provide care for a patient in a manner that is respectful of individual diversity. (2.3b) ​

### Course Goals
The course is designed with the purpose of providing students with an understanding of sonography professional expectations and environment as well as to introduce the performance and evaluation requirements for the clinical portions of the program. Strategies for optimizing the clinical experience, contributing positively within a sonography department and effective communication with other health care professionals will be discussed.

### Course topics
Describe the sonography student's role in the clinical environment, including responsibilities, challenges, performance, and assessment criteria of professional behavior. Support People and Sites The Learning Hub and CompTracker Student Performance Tracking Expectations in the Clinical Environment Professional Behaviour Achieving Competence Providing and Receiving Feedback Being an Active Member of the Team Managing Time and Workload The Clinical Evaluation Process -Awareness and Goal Setting
Describe occupational health and workplace safety, including privacy, confidentiality and hazardous materials and conditions. Pre-placement Training Privacy and Confidentiality Professional Liabilities Safety Hazards and Emergency Response Safety in the Clinical Environment
Describe how professional practice guidelines impact a sonographer's professional practice, including licensure, code of ethics, code of conduct, polices, position statements and practice guidelines. Professional Accountability Expectations of Clinical Students in the DMS Program Professional Manner and Conduct Professional Practice Guidelines Regulation and Legislation Discrimination and Abuse Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Knowledge, Skill, and Judgment
Describe the credentialing process and the importance of liability insurance and continuing education. Credentialing Professional Associations and Credentialing Bodies Sonography Canada and ARDMS Competency Profiles and Accreditation Pathways Liability Insurance and Continued Professional Development
Describe a sonographer’s communication expectations in the clinical environment, including communications with patients, families, and healthcare professionals. Competent Communication Sharing Knowledge Communication Tools Non-Verbal Communication and Self-Regulation Empathy and Sympathy Building Rapport with Patients and Their Support People Verifying Patient Identity Verifying Medical Orders Knowing When to Seek Support Medical Consent De-escalation Techniques
