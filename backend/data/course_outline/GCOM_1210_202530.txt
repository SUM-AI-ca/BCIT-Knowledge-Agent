## Course Description
This course commences with the basics of digital imaging, including the principles of electro-photography and other related technologies. There will be a review of different manufacturer’s equipment, their capabilities, and quality. Media issues will be discussed as well as imaging engine speeds, resolution, quality, and job costing features. Students will practice troubleshooting different media and calibrate the printer to ensure consistent colour reproduction. The course also covers many of the other considerations for reproducing files correctly on a digital imaging engine. Digital imaging for dye sublimation will be discussed and demonstrated relative to specially, item marketing and garment design and manufacturing.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the principles of electro-photography.
- Explain the different technologies encompassed within the digital imaging market.
- Choose the appropriate printing equipment for a media project.
- Distinguish between particular printer capabilities.
- Choose the appropriate media for a media project.
- Troubleshoot different media substrate issues.
- Explain how printer speeds, waste and running costs affect the total job cost.
- Configure calibration software to ensure consistency in colour reproduction.
- Set up jobs for output media size and related finishing options.
- Structure and package files for output to digital imaging engines, both internally and from remote locations (i.e., create PDF and PostScript files).
- Identify common design issues and their solutions with colour management, imposition crossovers, solid graphics, and gradation.
- Organize pages of a design with imposition software.
- Describe an ICC profile and describe its application in digital imaging.
- Print with total ink coverage for optimum results.​

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
The Graphic Communications program utilizes a unique print lab facility, with equipment donated by the generous business owners in our community. Students must always respect the equipment in the lab, and comply with the following rules:

- Loose hair or long beards must be tied back or contained in a hat or hair net.
- Remove jewelry, scarves, coats, or loose clothing.
- Gaming, videos, or loud music that can cause distraction are not permitted.
- Mobile devices are not permitted when working around machinery.
- No food or drink in the lab (closed water bottles are allowed at desks only).
- PPE is required to be worn when working with chemicals.
- No video recording or photography is permitted in the lab.

These rules are for the safety of all students using the equipment in the lab and are overseen at the discretion of all faculty. Failure to comply with lab rules will not be tolerated and may result punitive action including dismissal from class, formal warning, course failure, or withdrawal from the program
