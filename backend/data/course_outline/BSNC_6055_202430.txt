## Course Description
​This course builds on the concepts of relational practice covered in previous communication courses. The focus of this course is on effectively communicating with the individual client in the context of providing nursing care in acute medical/surgical settings. Learners will explore the "lived experience" of clients and practice the skills of interpersonal communication. Specific topics of focus include emotional intelligence; mindfulness; language and stigma; defusing anger and hostility; conflict, empowerment and power; negotiation; manipulation; and reluctance and resistance. Additionally, learners will identify and discuss the impact of personal values and biases on the provision of client care. Learning will occur using active learning strategies.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will meet the following competencies:

Communicator

- Communicates effectively with clients and healthcare team members in increasingly complex contexts or situations to support safe, high quality, client-centred health care with minimal assistance
- Demonstrates a strengths-based participatory approach to developing and maintaining therapeutic partnerships
- Engages in relational practice through a variety of approaches that demonstrate caring behaviors appropriate for clients
- Assists clients to identify and access health and other resources in their communities
- Demonstrates effective public speaking
- Educates clients to maintain and promote health
- Uses information and communication technologies to support information synthesis in accordance with professional and regulatory standards in the delivery of client care

Decision Maker

- Uses critical Inquiry and relational practice to Incorporate knowledge of a particular client, client patterns of responses; personal clinical knowledge of similar clients, and empirical knowledge, in the planning and provision of client care
- In relation to specific clients, identifies credible, accurate, appropriate and current information, resources, services and/or relevant technologies

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance: As student success is tied to regular attendance of classes, the BSN program strongly recommends that students attend class regularly. Attendance at all nursing theory classes is an expectation of the BSN program.

In all learning environments, students are socialized into their professional role and are thus required to demonstrate knowledge and application of professional nursing standards and ethics. Multiple absences may jeopardize a student’s development of the knowledge base required for competent professional nursing practice and successful completion of course requirements. As adult learners, students are ultimately responsible for their own learning; however, it is also recognized that repeated absences have a negative impact on collaborative learning experiences.
