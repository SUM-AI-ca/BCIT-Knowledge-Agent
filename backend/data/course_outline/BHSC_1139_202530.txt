## Course Description
Study the theoretical foundations for understanding the immune response and immune disorders, as well as the use of immune products as both therapies and laboratory reagents. The innate immune response and both humoral and cellular adaptive responses will be examined, including discussion of the impact of cytokine signaling and inflammation in the immune response. The use of immune products will be investigated as both reagents and analytes in a range of laboratory analyses, and students will apply this knowledge to the analysis of clinical and immunological data. The underlying basis and clinical manifestations of immunological disorders such as immunodeficiency, hypersensitivity and autoimmune diseases will also be examined. Learning is assessed through a range of written assignments and exams.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Describe the components and major mechanisms for the innate, humoral and cell mediated immune responses to pathogens.
- Describe how immune products such as antibodies can serve as both reagents and analytes in immunostaining and laboratory analyses in the context of viral and bacterial serotyping, serological analysis, transplant immunology, blood typing and oncology.
- Explain how vaccines and immunotherapies function to prevent and treat disease
- Explain the role of cytokine signaling and inflammation in host defense.
- Identify signatures of pathology and disease progression from clinical and immunological data.
- Describe the underlying basis and clinical manifestations of immunological disorders such as immunodeficiency, hypersensitivity and autoimmune diseases.

## Other information
AI Use: AI-based content generation tools, or AI tools, are permitted for assignments. However, they are not allowed during formal assessments (e.g. midterms and final exams). Note that AI output may include incorrect or misleading information, fabricated authors/dates/publications, faked works, or discriminatory ideas. It is your responsibility to ensure the quality, accuracy, and appropriateness of the work you submit in this course.

If you use ChatGPT (or a similar tool) to get ideas and/or partial answers for an assignment and/or to generate any text for a draft or final version of any part of an assignment, you must declare that you have used it, with a short paragraph describing the extent to which it was used, and you must save any generated text from this tool in case it is requested. You will not be penalized for using this tool, but the instructor may ask you to provide the generated text to help with grading decisions.

Material directly copied from any source (e.g. websites, books, AI tools) must be cited. Undeclared use of AI is a violation of academic integrity as it presents work or ideas created by tools or other people as your own. Failure to fully declare the use of this tool will be considered a violation of BCIT academic policy.
