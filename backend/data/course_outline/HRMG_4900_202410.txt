## Course Description
Designed to give students practical experience in completing a special human resource assignment in an organizational setting.

## Learning Resources
Each consulting team's resources are contingent on the nature and deliverables of the project.

## Evaluation Criteria
Criteria: %: Comments
Project Results Achieved: 30
Presentation: 20
Project Report: 20
Project Content: 20
Stakeholder Relationship Management: 10
Total: 100%

The business consulting project counts as 100% towards the grade. Each project is evaluated by the consulting team's client, a primary faculty advisor, and a second faculty advisor in consultation with other stakeholders, if applicable. Student team members may be given differential grading based on their faculty advisors' assessments. Faculty may use a peer assessment to determine each student's individual contribution to the project.

## Course Specific Requirements
- Students are ambassadors of BCIT and are expected to conduct themselves accordingly. Students shall adhere to BCIT’s Student Code of Conduct (Policy No. 5102) while working on their Business Consulting Project (BCP), whether they are working on-site or virtually.
- Students work on their projects under the general guidance of the organization, i.e., their client's representative, referred to as the host sponsor, and an assigned primary faculty advisor. Their primary faculty advisor is behind the scenes and provides support and advice to the team.
- Hours of work are determined by the client. Students are expected to work full-time on their projects on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.
- Research and surveys conducted as part of the industry/capstone project BCP must conform to the relevant sections of BCIT’s research and ethics policies (Policy #6500 and #6600) and the relevant sections of BCIT’s standards for academic integrity (Policy #5104).
- Each consulting team's presentation must be presented to their sponsor/client in the presence of their BCIT primary faculty advisor and second faculty advisor.
- Students should refer to Learning Hub for more information about project deliverables and grading criteria.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
