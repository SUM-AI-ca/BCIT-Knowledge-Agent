## Course Description
This course will give students a basic understanding of how operations management is applied in modern food manufacturing and service industries to improve operational performance. The student will do field studies in the food industry to evaluate how an existing company defines, plans, measure and manages productivity and other key performance indicators.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the strategic importance of operations and how operations can provide a competitive advantage in the marketplace.
- Explain the decisions that operations managers make.
- Explain how to achieve a good layout for plant processes.
- Explain how a focus on quality can improve organization performance.
- Explain the principles of Total Quality Management (TQM) and its implementation.
- Describe the fundamentals of the ISO 9000 quality systems.
- Identify aspects of quality management for purchasing supplies, materials and equipment.
- Describe the aspects of quality management which are unique to service industries.
- Describe the functions of inventory and basic inventory models.
- Apply a systematic approach to problem solving and diagnostics for improving business processes, including the use of graphical tools for recording and analyzing data
- Appreciate the techniques for improving business performance and productivity.
- Explain how to implement and sustain change in an organization.
