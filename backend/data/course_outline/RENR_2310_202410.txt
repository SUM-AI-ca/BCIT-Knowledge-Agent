## Course Description
​This course focuses on use of geographic information system (GIS) technology for the natural resource field. Course content covers access to, and use of, a variety of data types and sources, basic GIS editing, geoprocessing, and analysis tools. Students will learn how to use GIS to present results in online environments, presentations, and technical reports and communications. Students will also be introduced to foundational concepts such as digital data, scale and map projections, data management, and workflow.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe fundamental concepts of GIS and databases.
- Describe, use, source, and integrate different types of data sets used in GIS applications.
- Discuss the application and use of GIS tools for survey design, field work, and data collection.
- Create data management and workflow plan for GIS datafiles.
- Apply appropriate geoprocessing environment settings for project or area of interest.
- Use GIS application to complete basic tasks for working with and displaying GIS datasets.
- Use GIS applications to create maps, graphs, and reports.
- Use geoprocessing, analysis, and modelling tools with elevation and 3D datasets to assess sites.
- Demonstrate respectful and professional behaviour that meets industry standards and expectations.
