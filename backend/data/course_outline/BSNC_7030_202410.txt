## Course Description
This course builds on Practice of Nursing 6, focusing on adult and older adult clients experiencing complex and chronic health challenges across the health-illness continuum. It includes a rotation in a home health setting and a sub-acute setting through the lens of continuity and coordination of care. Students' clinical reasoning and clinical judgment increases in depth, and with minimal guidance. Students continue to build clinical reasoning skills demonstrated through increasingly accurate and refined health assessments. They identify and prioritize client issues and respond using a collection of appropriate interventions that are guided by best evidence, safe practice, professional standards, ethics, and legislation. Students gain confidence in their emerging professional practice and as participating members of the nursing and interprofessional health care teams. Through their experiences in the practice setting, students continue to evolve their professional identity in relation to becoming a registered nurse, and exhibit autonomous scope of practice by self-assessing strengths and needs to take appropriate actions to meet course competencies. Through development and strengthening of effective time management skills, including organization, prioritization, and delegation, students develop therapeutic relationships with individuals and families, and communicate and collaborate effectively with the health care team to ensure safe, appropriate and timely client care. During the home health rotation, students will engage with the theoretical and conceptual foundations of community health nursing. This course includes an integrated and applied learning experience in a simulated/practice environment.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

Advocate

- Examines and challenges the influence of power within organizational, social, economic and political contexts that influence health and well-being within the context of nurses’ role in the delivery of care
- Advocates for policies and programs that effectively address the determinants of health
- Promotes acceptance of diversity and supports client empowerment to reduce stigma

Collaborator

- Works to prevent and resolve conflict, and negotiate solutions in interactions with clients and the healthcare team

Decision Maker

- Effectively manages complex clinical scenarios and time sensitive situations.
- Monitors the effectiveness of clients’ outcomes to inform future care planning within all contexts and situations
- Articulates clinical reasoning process in support of nursing practice

Global Citizen

- Practices culturally safe and equitable nursing care in diverse contexts and situations
- Recognizes and addresses factors that influence the socio-political, economic and historical factors that lead to inequalities in healthcare
- Integrates knowledge of emerging community and global health issues, population health issues and related research into nursing practice

Leader

- Collaborates with and acts as a resource for members of the healthcare team to meet client needs
- Promotes teamwork and garners commitment and participation of others that will optimize nursing practice and client care

Lifelong Learner

- Collaborates with the interprofessional team to implement evidence based practice

Communication

- Communicates effectively with clients and healthcare team members to support safe, high quality, client-centred healthcare in diverse situations and contexts

Professional

- Displays initiative, confidence and self-awareness in nursing practice
- Provides knowledge translation to support professional development of other learners and healthcare team members
- Independently assesses own practice and undertakes activities to improve practice and meet identified learning goals
