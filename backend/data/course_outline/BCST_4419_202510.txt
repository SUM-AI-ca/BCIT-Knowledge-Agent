## Course Description
This course offers students an opportunity to apply their learned skills in industry, in a practical setting. In this four-week industrial experience students visit a workplace to observe, practice, work, and learn in actual industry situations. With an employer of their choice, students arrange a practicum in which they will apply the skills learned in the Diploma in Television and Video Production Program on a daily basis. This placement could be with a television station, a production house, a post production house, or in a business which services the television and video production industry. All placements are reviewed and approved by the instructor prior to the student attending the industry placement. This real-world experience complements the training and experience students have received through the course of the program, providing them with final preparation and signifying that they are ready to begin their careers.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Actively participate in the company’s day-to-day operation according to industry standards.
- Solve common problems and respond to day-to-day challenges working both independently and in collaboration with others.
- Communicate effectively as a team member, by demonstrating the ability to take direction from supervisors and show initiative in the work place.
- Apply a variety of skills, appropriate to the demands of the given tasks, timelines and workplace environment.
- Use technology effectively according to the needs of the placement.
- Complete assigned tasks in a timely fashion.
