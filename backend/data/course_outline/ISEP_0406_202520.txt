### Course Description
​This course is designed for students with English proficiency at the Independent User level (CEFR High B2). This course aims to improve English skills in reading, writing, grammar, and vocabulary, while also developing the collaborative skills needed to function effectively in BCIT programs. Experiential learning, intercultural competence, and academic study strategies are key components of the course. In this course, students will read and write increasingly authentic technical academic texts and participate in tasks that reflect those they will face in their BCIT programs. Specifically, learners will read a greater volume of material, including parts of a textbook, pre-lab/workshop material, multiple case studies, and multiple articles connected to student life and technical academic topics of interest. They will write texts with a set purpose, genre, and rhetorical organization where significant precision, accuracy, and coherence is necessary (e.g. writing a lab/workshop report or synopsis). They will also write longer, goal-oriented texts that require effective and impactful language use (e.g. essay, student success plan). Learners will increase the range of B2 vocabulary and grammatical features they understand and will use B1 and some B2 level features accurately in their writing.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Read quickly through long and complex texts to evaluate and prioritize content based on technical and academic tasks and goals
- Obtain information, ideas, and opinions from complex technical academic texts
- Demonstrate understanding of long technical academic articles while using dictionaries or other materials to check meaning
- Write notes or short responses, descriptions or summaries in order to communicate facts, ideas or procedures
- Write up a lab or workshop report to describe concepts, observations, and relevant theories; outline procedure or methodology; discuss data analysis; and report results
- Write a report that describes problems or challenges and compares their methods, procedures, solutions, and/or outcomes
- Participate in online discussion boards, linking own contributions to previous ones in the thread, understanding cultural implications and reacting appropriately
- Describe and evaluate the viewpoints and customs of own and other social groups, showing awareness of how judgments and prejudices are frequently based

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Scenarios
Main Tasks
1. Reviewing main grammar points that are needed in this course and at BCIT
2. Using Information from Textbooks
Read excerpts from an introductory textbook (front matter, several chapters)
Write responses to a variety of questions and prompts by restating, paraphrasing, and synthesizing factual, conceptual & procedural knowledge
3. Writing a Lab Report
Read about lab reports & the value of doubt in science
Complete a simulated lab report
Carry out and write up a lab report that compares two household items
