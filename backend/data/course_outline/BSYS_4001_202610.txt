### Course Description
In this course, students will continue to develop the skills from BSYS 3001 Artificial Intelligence Management 1. The business use cases of deep learning will be evaluated, and students will compare supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement machine learning models. The different types of neural networks, constraint and optimization problems, and search techniques will also be investigated. Other deep learning tools will be explored as the student applies these techniques to business process management. The focus will be on artificial intelligence/machine learning (AI/ML) business solutions that can be implemented and managed without unnecessary technical complexity. Upon completion of the course the student will be able to manage AI/ML implementation projects.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Contrast supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning
- Define, discuss, and evaluate the ethics of recommendation systems
- Learn about Machine Learning Algorithms such as Decision Trees, K-means, K-nearest neighbours, and Markov Chains
- Understand the pros and cons of different Machine Learning Algorithms for making decisions
- Understand the key components of Optimization, and how continuity, differentiability and convexity help determine the correct algorithm
- Analyze optimization problems using Hill Climbing, Simulated Annealing, and Genetic Algorithms
- Evaluate Automated Planning, Adversarial Search, Probabilistic Models, and Pattern Recognition techniques
- Develop artificial intelligence applications as a project team and present these applications to stakeholders

### Course Specific Requirements
This course is about AI, so AI use is encouraged. On the first day we will discuss ethics and citations, and how to successfully vibe code. Based on our discussions, we will collectively create an AI contract and grading rubrics.
There will be times when you are not allowed to use AI at all - exams and many quizzes will be on paper and technology free,
Also, you may be required to explain how Generative AI works - which is something you should know by then end of the class.
Students may use AI for specific task components if they critically evaluate AI outputs. | Students may compare output by different AI tools and then integrate it into their final work. Submitted work must include the prompt and AI-generated output. The critical analysis or reflection on the limitations and quality of output is assessed. Full citations are critical!
Instructor may use AI for specific components | There may be AI generated data sets (mostly so I can get fake data) Exams and Assignments will include labelled AI generated code output, and students will be expected to critique and explain this. Debugging and Tracing AI generated code will be a built in component of the class.
### Other information
This course features a major team-based project. All team members usually receive the same mark. However, marks can be redistributed if one or more team members contribute less than their fair share. This may include one or more of the following:
- frequently absent from team meetings or classes
- submitting plagiarized or low effort work
- missing team deadlines or class deadlines
- disruptive behavior during team meetings
Your team will need to have a team charter and document infractions, escalating the issue to your instructor before the assignment is due. The instructor will then guide your team through the mark redistribution / Reassign group member process. In this process, the team will have the following options:
Option 1: If all members of the group agree that grades should be reallocated, up to 40% will be taken off one student’s score and redistributed to the other students.
Option 2: If group members do not reach agreement on how marks should be reallocated, each team member’s contributions will be marked separately.
Option 3: If a member is removed from the group, they will be responsible for finishing the project on their own, and will be graded on the same rubrics.
