## Course Description
Studies the basic operation and hardware involved in imaging systems used in medicine. Covers the fundamental principles, functional building blocks and quality assurance of radiography, nuclear medicine and ultrasound imaging equipment. Introduces concepts in radiation protection, other imaging modalities such as MRI and PET as well as the fundamentals of digital radiology and PACS. Reinforces lectures with practical laboratory sessions.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
- Explain the principles of operation of basic diagnostic x-ray, nuclear medicine and ultrasound imaging equipment.
- Identify and understand the functional building blocks of diagnostic x-ray, nuclear medicine and ultrasound imaging equipment.
- Describe the principles involved in the production of images including digital and photographic negative records.
- Explain the principles of common medical imaging systems such as radiographic fluoroscopy, computer tomography, magnetic resonance imaging and positron emission tomography.
- Describe the precautions to be taken for radiation safety and protection.
- Perform quality assurance tests on some aspects of diagnostic x-ray and nuclear medicine equipment.

## Course topics
Part 1: X-ray

- X-ray production & interactions with matter
- Factors determining radiographic image quality
- Functional building blocks of diagnostic X-ray equipment
- Digital Radiography – acquisition technologies (CR & DR) and grayscale processing
- Introduction to tomography (CT)

Part 2: Nuclear Medicine

- Nuclear medicine physics (basic concepts)
- Gamma radiation detection and spectrometry
- Building blocks of a gamma camera system
- Principles of SPECT and PET
- Radiation safety and protection

Part 3: Ultrasound

- Ultrasound (U/S) physics (basic concepts)
- U/S transducers and the sound beam
- Basic U/S imaging systems
- Factors determining U/S image quality
- Building blocks of diagnostic U/S systems
- Doppler U/S basics
- U/S performance assurance and safety

Part 4: Magnetic Resonance Imaging

- Nuclear spin physics (basic concepts)
- MRI system components
- MRI signal acquisition and image formation
- MRI infrastructure and facility context
- MRI performance and safety issues

Part 5: Digital Image Communication and Storage Systems

- Information systems: HIS, RIS, LIS
- Image Management systems: MIMPS
- Communication systems: HL7, DICOM
