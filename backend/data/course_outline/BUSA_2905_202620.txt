### Course Description
This highly interactive course is designed to identify and manage conflict in the workplace. Case studies, role playing and student experiences will contribute to the development of a specific body of knowledge for contemporary managers.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
To enhance existing communication skills and identify appropriate processes/techniques to prevent and/or manage conflict.
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Identify common sources/costs of conflict.
- Examine your own response to conflict and your colleagues’ responses to conflict.
- Develop effective communication in conflict situations.
- Describe effective/appropriate responses to the resolution of issues in the workplace.
- Develop confidence, in a group setting, in discussing and presenting difficult and sensitive issues including harassment and discrimination.
- Evaluate the necessity/requirement for third party intervention.
- Developing an environment for prevention.
- Describe/apply appropriate conflict resolution models to a variety of case studies.
- Design a communication/conflict resolution program.
- Relate corporate goals/planning in the union and non-union environment.
- Utilize a risk based approach to identify opportunities for prevention of conflict.

DISCUSSION ASSIGNMENTS GRADING CRITERIA
Details (55% of Course Grade)
Your grade will be determined by the quality of your discussion assignments. To receive full marks for the discussion assignments, you must respond to each module discussion question AND respond to two other students for each module question. You are expected to participate in discussions through analysis and critique of the course readings and related material, by contributing your comments, experiences and insights and facilitating discussion through comments and questions. Your postings should demonstrate that you have read and understood all components of the discussion question and/or student postings and you are able to relate your comments to the topics in this course. When responding to a classmate’s posting, you must say more than “I agree” or “Good point”. You must state your reasons if you agree or disagree with a comment or statement and/or offer insight based on your own experience and knowledge of the course materials.
Each discussion activity is worth 10 points except for the student introduction which is automatically awarded 2 points and discussion 8 which is worth 15 points.
Posts are due on Sundays and replies to classmates are due on Tuesdays. Late posts are penalized 10% per day and will not be accepted for grading more than 2 days past the due date. Late replie are not graded. Students must submit their discussion assignments by the late deadline to avoid being recorded as absent for that week of the course.
To receive credit for your discussion assignment, you must use specific concepts and terms from the course text, and other materials in the relevant learning module, in your post and the course terms must be highlighted in bold.
The unauthorized use of outside sources, including Artificial Intelligence products (such as ChatGPT), for discussion assignments is prohibited, and when identified by your instructor, will result in an academic integrity violation. Refer to policy 5104 - Student Code of Academic Integrity
Excellent (8 to 10 points)
- Post addresses ALL components of the discussion assignment questions
- Demonstrates strong analysis and insight into the question and the related course materials (text, videos, articles etc.) listed in the relevant learning module
- Relevant terms, theories and concepts from the course learning materials are applied as appropriate
- Shares relevant experiences and references current events that relate to the discussion topic
- Replies to classmates add value to the discussion
- Responds to questions posed by other students and/or the instructor
Satisfactory (5 to 7.9 points)
- Post addresses most components of the discussion question
- Demonstrates some analysis and insight into the question and the related course materials
- A few relevant course terms, theories and concepts from the course learning materials are applied as appropriate
- Shares a little relevant experience but does not reference current events that relate to the discussion topic
- Reply to classmate is completed but adds limited value to the discussion
- Does not always respond to questions posed by other students and/or the instructor
Unsatisfactory (0 to 4.9 points)
- Post does not address most components of the discussion question
- Demonstrates little to no analysis and insight into the question and the related course materials
- Does not apply relevant course terms, theories and concepts from the course learning materials
- Does not share relevant experiences and does not reference current events that relate to the discussion topic
- Reply to classmate not completed
- Does not respond to questions posed by other students and/or the instructor
- Uses disrespectful and/or discriminatory language when interacting with others
