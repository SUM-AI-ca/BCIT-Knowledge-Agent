## Course Description
Introduces the student to the Canadian legal system including its development, constitutional law, the Charter, torts, contracts and business relationships.

## Learning Resources
Business Law in Canada, 13th Edition by Yates et al. (Pearson)

A copy of the 12th edition is also suitable for this class.

Available here (choose eTextbook edition): https://www.pearson.com/en-ca/subject-catalog/p/business-law-in-canada/P200000010229/9780137597819

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion of the course, a student will be able to 1. Describe how the legal system is implicated in occupational health and safety. 2. Describe the laws applying to various business situations. 3. Recognize legal issues as they arise. 4. Help clients and employers to avoid potential legal problems. 5. Communicate more effectively with lawyers and regulators. 6. Design business practices and transactions to avoid legal problems.
