## Course Description
​This is the second Biomedical Computer Networks and Data Communications course. Students will examine the computer networks with applications in healthcare and in greater depth. The course introduce students to the Wide Area Networks. Topics include IP address sub-netting and super-netting, virtual LAN, IPv6, virtual private network and network security. Theory and laboratory sessions include medical application of infusion pump (wired and wireless), telemetry, physiological monitoring systems, Picture Archiving Communication Systems (PACS) and medical device integration. Healthcare information system standards will be discussed as well as network security and data integrity in the medical network environment.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Apply the network knowledge to troubleshoot inter-connected medical device.
- Describe the function and operation of physiological monitoring networks, and telemetry systems.
- Configure and test physiological monitoring networks, and telemetry systems.
- Configure wireless enabled infusion pumps.
- Describe the network technology aspects of Picture Archiving and Communication Systems (PACS) in terms of its architecture and operation.
- Operate a PACS review station to archive, transfer, and manipulate diagnostic images.
- Describe the function and operation of databases and servers.
- Describe the HIS (Hospital Information System), including LIS (Laboratory Information System), RIS (Radiology Information System), CIS (Clinical Information System), and ADT (Admission Discharge Transport).
- Describe the Healthcare IT standards: such as HIPPA, HL7, and DICOM.
- Identify the Healthcare IT Standard agencies and regulations.
