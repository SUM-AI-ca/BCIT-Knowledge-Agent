### Course Description
Motorcycle technicians work primarily on 2 and 3-wheeled motorcycles and other units such as motor scooters. Topics for level 4 include performing work practices, using communication and mentoring techniques. Maintaining brake systems, two-stroke and four-stroke engines, transmissions, vehicle management systems, fuel and exhaust systems, and electric motorcycles.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Conduct safety inspections
- Use mentoring techniques
- Maintain braking control systems
- Maintain cylinder heads
- Maintain valve trains on four-stroke engines
- Maintain cylinders and pistons
- Maintain crankshaft assemblies
- Maintain counterbalance assemblies
- Maintain engine cases
- Maintain lubrication systems
- Maintain constant mesh transmissions
- Use specialized equipment
- Interpret diagnostic trouble codes (DTC) results
- Maintain system circuitry and components
- Update software
- Maintain fuel injection systems
- Implement specific safety protocols for electric motorcycles
- Maintain electric motorcycles
