## Course Description
This subject area is an introduction to the practices and issues involved in operating, managing, and understanding the day to day operations of an office/practice. It will provide an opportunity to discover the roles, responsibilities, and expectations involved in the typical office situations. Emphasis is placed on the student's ability to identify issues & demonstrate their awareness of the profession.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify the roles, issues, & structure of a typical office environment.
- Demonstrate their awareness of "business principles".
- Identify the role of specific management forms & control systems.
- Identify the phases of a project & the related services provided by the profession.
- Identify & define, the relationship of the Architect to the various consultants & specialists.
- Define the roles of the Project Team & review the topics of "ethics" in the construction industry.
