## Course Description
Covers general theories of measurement and pricing of construction work, emphasising sound estimating procedures and accuracy. Introduces recognition of work, specific methods of measurement, estimating forms, and common techniques used for quantity take-off with a focus on concrete foundation related work, earthwork, masonry, above-grade concrete, wood frame, and structural steel work items.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Break down quantities of work according to MasterFormat.
- Utilize standard estimating Quantity Take-off forms to process a number of items, their measurements and extensions, and input them for future pricing and evaluation.
- Minimize the possibilities for estimating errors by using systematic methodologies of data evaluation and inputting.
- Perform quantity take-offs related to the following MasterFormat Divisions:
- Division 03 Concrete
- Division 04 Masonry
- Division 05 Metals (Structural Steel)
- Division 06 Wood, Plastics, and Composites
- Division 31 Earthwork
- Describe the basics of quantity take-offs related to the following MasterFormat Divisions:
- Division 22 Plumbing
- Division 23 Heating and Ventilation
- Division 26 Electrical
- oDivision 31 Earthwork

## Course Goals
To understand the role of an estimator and estimating in the construction industry.

To gain basic skills necessary for performing a quantity takeoff and use this skill on simple foundation plans to prepare quantity takeoff related to concrete, excavating and backfilling items of work.

To improve estimating skills while preparing takeoffs for more demanding masonry, concrete, wood and structural steel work items.
