### Course Description
Lumber manufacturing has evolved significantly over the last several decades and includes complex processes and technology. The main driving forces behind this evolution are: an increased effort to achieve sustainable use of forest resources, implementation of automated processes and computerized controls to achieve higher levels log utilization, and a changing lumber market. This course provides an overview of the lumber industry landscape with specific emphasis on aspects that have a more direct impact on the processes and technology employed in sawmill plants. The topics explored include: production resources, sawmill operation units, wood products, and industry regulatory environment. Students engage in their learning through online activities. Students' knowledge is assessed through online discussions, quizzes, and the final exam as well as through practical projects.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the main aspects of the wood processing industry.
- Describe the harvesting practices commonly used in the wood industry.
- Identify the main products and by-products of the wood processing industry.
- Describe the function of such processing units as log merchandizing, sawmill, kiln drying, planing / processing.
- Describe the key attributes and measurements used to describe wood products.
- Describe lumber regulations (US/Canada).

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Module 1: Wood is Good
Week 1
In this module, you will introduce yourself, examine innovative engineering applications of wood, and describe relevant geographical implications for the lumber industry in your region.
Things you will do…
Read the Module 1 commentary. Submit Discussion Post 1.1: Introduce yourself. Review a curated list of resources examining the innovative uses of wood. Submit Discussion Post 1.2: Wood is Good. Create a short report that describes the geographical features and the lumber industry in your local region .
Module 2: Forest Management
Week 2
In Module 2, we will discuss the Basic Wood Processing Steps from logs to lumber. By the end of this module, you will be able to describe the sequence of processes required to convert trees to lumber. You will also be able to describe each process and identify the equipment used in each phase of processing. This module also discusses how logs are measured and assessed for value. You will be able to measure the volume of a tree and apply basic standards to estimate the relative value of the lumber it yields. Finally, we will introduce the concept of safety and discuss the priorities that underscore safety protocols within Forest Management.
Things you will do…
Read the Module 2 commentary. Submit Discussion Post 2.1: Lumber Regulations in our Context. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Phases of Industrial Wood Processing. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Key Technical Terms. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Scaling. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: IWP Phase ID. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: IWP Equipment ID. Module 2 Project: Harvesting Systems and Phases. Math Activity! Calculate the volume of a log. Safety Part 1.
Module 3: Wood Science
Week 3
In this module, we will discuss the main components of a tree, how they function, and in general, how they relate to industrial wood processing. We will discuss the basic components and internal structure of a tree. This module also provides an understanding of the unique visual identification features of the individual species as well as the various inherent wood properties that are so important in manufacturing.
Things you will do…
Read the Module 3 commentary. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Tree components. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Label the parts of a trunk. Project: Tree Identification Project. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Tree species ID. Test #1 (Based on Modules 1, 2, & 3.)
Module 4: Sawmills
Week 4
In this module, we will briefly outline the processes that are typically found onsite at a large industrial Sawmill. This will include all aspects of how the log is handled and processed directly from the log yard to the final stages of the finished products, where they are ready to be shipped out to market. We will outline the main facilities involved, and then provide details of the Sawmilling process itself and describe the main components of a Sawmill, where whole logs are cut in to lumber.
Things you will do…
Read the Module commentary. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Sawmill Facilities. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Sawmill Components. Project: Sawmill Photo Journal.
Module 5: Kilns
Week 5
In this module we will discuss the rationale for drying wood in greater detail and the different types of kilns used in industrial wood processing. This module will also consider heat or fuel sources in kiln drying and the way kilns are controlled for different types of wood fiber.
Things you will do…
Read the Module commentary. Read a ‘Kiln 101’ article and complete a comprehension task. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Kilns. Project: Kiln Project.
Module 6: Planers
Week 6
In this module, you will consider the general reasons or rationale for planing. Also, you will describe the flow pattern of a typical Planer Mill, describing each phase by referencing the innovative technologies and equipment within them. Finally, you will complete the third and final Safety Topic in this course and discuss safety priorities in this final processing phase.
Things you will do…
Read the Module commentary. Submit Discussion Post 6.1: Planer Upgrade Rationale. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Lumber Defects. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Lumber Stamp Literacy. Project: Planer Mill Flow Plan. Submit Discussion Post 6.2: Incident Report. Test #2 (based on Modules 4, 5, and 6).
Module 7: Lumber Products
Week 7
In this module, we will discuss four main lumber product uses in the market place: residential home structural framing, engineered, appearance, and expendable. You will apply these concepts in a project. We will introduce aspects of lumber measurement and volume calculations in both Imperial and Metric systems. We will also discuss how count loss is related to wood processing along with the idea of nominal and actual measurements. Finally, you will consider how production volumes provide an overview of how sales volumes are projected.
Things you will do…
Read the Module 7 commentary. Submit discussion Post 7.1: US Housing Starts vs Price of Lumber. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Lumber Uses. Submit discussion Post 7.2: Lumber Products in your Community. Complete a Concept Check Quiz: Volume Calculation.
Module 8: Markets
Week 8
In this module, you will read about dimensional lumber products typically used in North America and their applications. You will learn about the North American lumber markets and how the supply and demand affects production in all North American countries. There is a brief introduction to a few key Off-shore/International markets as well. The final project asks you to research, create and deliver a presentation about lumber products and Off-shore/International or domestic markets for your Sawmill .
Things you will do…
Read the Module commentary. Concept Check 8.1: The North American Lumber Market Project: Markets Test #3. (Based on Modules 7 and 8.)
