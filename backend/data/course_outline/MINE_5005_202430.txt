## Course Description
Mine 5005 examines different methods for data analysis and computer modeling of mineral deposits for resource estimation. Topics will include interpretation of drill results, geologic controls and continuities, geostatistics, outliers, and interpolation methods. . The primary parameters controlling the calculating and classifying of mineral resources are reviewed, including cut-off grade analysis, deposit type and drill hole spacing. Lab assignments and design work will focus on data compilation, integration, and analysis, geological and ore body modelling, and mineral resource estimating methodologies.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Assess data quality and certainty based on factors including drill hole spacing, nugget effects, lithological control and variability [3,4].
- Evaluate and combine large datasets of subsurface geological data (core logs, assay data) [3,5].
- Develop geologically realistic models of ore deposits [1,4,5].
- Perform statistical analysis that is used to calculate mineral resources [1,3,4,5].
- Generate mineral resource estimates and evaluate their limitations [4,5,10,11].
- Evaluate mineral resource models and mineral resource estimates to reflect changing economic conditions (i.e., commodity price drop) or other external factors [11].
- Understand the Canadian regulatory framework surrounding mineral resource estimates and apply recommended best practices and ethical standards for resource evaluation [8, 10].
