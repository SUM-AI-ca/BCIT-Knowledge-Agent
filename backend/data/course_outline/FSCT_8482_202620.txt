### Course Description
This course will explore crime and intelligence analysis that relates specifically to ideologically motivated national security issues. Using various Canadian case studies, you will examine analytical methodologies used during investigations relating to terrorism, criminal extremism, foreign influence, and insider threats. You will then conduct applied research and apply structured analytical techniques to prepare reports on individuals, groups, and networks who pose a threat to national security. The course provides an overview of the Canadian intelligence national security community. The purpose of this course is to give an overview of the subject matter, after which you apply your analytical skill set to a real-world topic.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate open source intelligence relating to national security issues.
- Assess which analytical methodologies should be used in investigations and intelligence assessments relating to terrorism, criminal extremism, foreign influence, and insider threats.
- Identify trends relating to Canadian national security interests.
- Assess a mitigation strategy that can be applied domestically and internationally.
- Develop and present a threat assessment on a national security topic.

### Course Goals
This course is designed to expose students to understanding analysis in the context of national security issues as a whole. Students will conduct applied research and apply analytical methods already learned, to prepare reports on individuals or groups who may pose a threat to national security. The course discusses investigative approaches/strategies as well as an overview of the Canadian intelligence community. An introduction to the subjects of terrorism, extremism, foreign influence, and insider threats amongst others will be covered. The purpose of this course is meant to provide an overview of the subject matter with application of students’ skills in analysis to a real-world topic.
1. Analyze historical and current trends in national security.
2. Assess national security interests and strategy within context of real-world scenarios.
3. Think strategically about national security as it pertains to Canada and its five eyes’ partners.
4. Work collaboratively with other students to produce a product relating to national security.
5. Communicate analytical findings in an oral and/or written product.
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate open source intelligence relating to national security issues.
- Assess which analytical methodologies should be used in investigations and intelligence assessments relating to terrorism, criminal extremism, foreign influence, and insider threats.
- Identify trends relating to Canadian national security interests.
- Assess a mitigation strategy that can be applied domestically and internationally.
- Develop and present a threat assessment on a national security topic.
