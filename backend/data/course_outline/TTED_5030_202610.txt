### Course Description
This course applies advanced design and construction skills to practical constructions in wood. The history and trends in furniture design will be analysed, concluding with contemporary expressions. Design evaluation will consider technical execution, ethical use of materials, planning and time management. Value-added, found and recycled materials will be encouraged for specific assignments. Exercises in advanced machining operations, techniques and set-ups will broaden the range of possible design solutions. CNC machine control will be incorporated.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Extend machine capability by advanced set-up, jigs and fixtures.
- Perform basic maintenance and set-up on common woodworking machinery.
- Perform basic operations using the wood lathe.
- Manage a unique project from inception to completion within restraints of time, materials, budget, size, technique and facility.
- Lead young learners safely through senior level technology activities in wood.
- Safely and efficiently use the shop, tools, equipment and machines in the shop.

### Course Goals
This course introduces students to advanced processes and techniques in woodworking, aimed at core competencies suitable for grades 8-12 of the BC public school technology education curriculum. Note that this course does not address a focus on trades-training in carpentry.
Students design, adapt, document and construct projects specific for the identified competencies and resources available for the specific grades.
In addition, students learn about machine set-up and maintenance and participate in active demonstrations concerning advanced specific set-ups and techniques.

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Assignments may be undertaken in any order during shop time.
Lectures and Demonstrations:
Week 1:Cabinet construction details
Week 2: Thickness Planer set-up and maintenance
Week 3: Band saw set up and maintenance
Week 4: Table saw set up and maintenance
Week 5: Jointer set up and maintenance
Week 6: Mortice and tennon joinery techniques and strategies
Week 7: Carcas dovetails
Week 8: Cove cutting on the table saw -- techniques and applications
Week 9: Rail and Stile cutters for cabinet doors
Week 10: Cutting tapers on the table saw and Jointer
Week 11: Spring Break - no classes scheduled
Week 12: Indigenous Woodwork: Steambending, Carving and related content. Discussion: how to respectfully include Indigenous content in curriculum
Week 13: Good Friday - no scheduled classes
Week 14: Overarm router techniques and applications.Shaper techniques and applications
Week 14: Vacuum clamping applications, Marquetry and Parquetry techniques applicable to the high school woodshop
Week 15: Spray finishing techniques and applications.
Week 16: Class discussion and exercise -- creating a community of safety in the woodshop.
Week 17: Class discussion and exercise -- maintaining a healthy and sustainable sign up and subscription to elective course programs
Week 18: Practical techniques for teaching woodshop
Week 19: Finish and display all project work
Week 20: Flex day
