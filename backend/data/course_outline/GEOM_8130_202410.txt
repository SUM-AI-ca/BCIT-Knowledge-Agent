## Course Description
This course is the third of three courses required for students to complete a comprehensive independent project in a selected area of Geomatics. Prior to graduating from the Geomatics Degree program participants will undertake a major independent project in a selected area of Geomatics technology relating to the project they planned during the GEOM 6130 – Geomatics Project Planning course, and started in GEOM 7130 – Geomatics Project 1. In consultation with industry sponsors and faculty advisors, participants will solve technical problems, demonstrate thorough knowledge of the project topic and present their final project results using a poster presentation, by writing a technical report and making an oral presentation. The final report will build on the draft report and poster submitted in earlier courses. Emphasis should be placed on the explanation, validation and verification of the results and a critical appraisal of the processes and techniques used. Students will present the findings of their project and answer questions during an oral presentation to peers and faculty members.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate an ability to think critically, analyze and solve a significant technical issue/problem related to the Geomatics work environment.
- Apply resources, data and personal organization techniques to manage a project.
- Maintain effective and constructive communication with industry and faculty on a regular basis.
- Maintain a project schedule, utilizing appropriate project management techniques when faced with unexpected issues.
- Demonstrate a high level of competence in producing a professional report through written and presentation skills.
- Record project observations and results in a logical and concise manner.
- Verify, validate and discuss the results and information collected.
- Analyze the results and draw meaningful conclusions and suggestions from the research conducted.
- Present an overview of the final research project to peers and faculty and defend the methodology and results during an oral presentation.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Students should submit their completed final project submissions to the GEOM 8130 course instructor in accordance with the following criteria:

- A pdf file version of a mid-term progress report due week 8 submitted to Learning Hub dropbox
- A pdf file of the final Project Report and any associated digital files to the Learning Hub dropbox (or via USB thumb drive if a high amount of data is involved) expected the final week of the course
- A pdf file of the final Poster (48 x 36 inch format created from a single Powerpoint slide) to the Learning Hub dropbox expected the beginning of week 15 (week prior to exam week).

Note - copies of the project report and poster once received are considered to be the property of the BCIT Geomatics Department and will not be returned to the student.

Deadline Dates and Poster Presentation Event

A poster presentation event is being planned, which will allow students to present the findings of their project and answer questions from peers, faculty and industry professionals. The presentation event is expected the week of April 8-12, 2024 but will be communicated to students once confirmed - please note final exams are scheduled for April 15-19, 2024.

The final deadline dates for submission of the poster and report to be confirmed once the presentation event details have been finalized.
