## Course Description
​Modeling for different industries will be the main theme in this course. Students will go through the entire 3D workflow to model, texture, light, render and present props consistently throughout the course. Students will learn the constraints and freedoms for modeling for different industries. Presentation techniques will be emphasized throughout student submissions to ensure the work is effectively being displayed. Still renders and image sequences will both be examined. Tight deadlines will force students to examine what is important to the model and will increase the speed of their work to get them ready for industry. Modeling to concept design is a skill that will be developed over the course of the semester. In addition, students will have an ongoing project throughout the semester to create a high quality demo reel piece.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Model low poly and high poly props.
- Increase work speed to meet deadlines.
- Create models that would be suitable for mobile games, AAA games, film, or TV.
- Present renders effectively to market their skills.
- Match models to concept art.
- Use the entire 3D workflow to create and present work.
