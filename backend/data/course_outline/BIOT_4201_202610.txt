### Course Description
​(UBC BIOT 206) This course explores the molecular biology and genetics of microorganisms, focusing on DNA replication, transcription, translation, and gene regulation. Key topics include genetic mutations, DNA repair, recombinant DNA techniques, and the role of viruses in microbial pathogenesis. Students also learn foundational immunological principles necessary to interpret host–microbe interactions. Laboratory exercises emphasize practical application of molecular and microbiological techniques, including PCR, sequencing, bacterial transformation, and assays for mutation and DNA repair.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the molecular mechanisms of microbial DNA replication, transcription, translation, and gene regulation.
- Describe processes of mutation and DNA repair in microorganisms.
- Determine microbial identity using 16S rRNA sequencing.
- Assess the effects of carcinogen-induced mutations in microorganisms.
- Evaluate the impact of DNA damage and repair pathways in microbial systems.
- Perform bacterial transformation to introduce recombinant DNA.​

### Attendance Requirements
In the biotechnology program, laboratory attendance is of the highest priority since lab experience is what sets this program apart from other undergraduate programs and what prepares students for co-op work terms. Furthermore, lab work is frequently done in pairs and students are therefore expected to be present to support their partners. You are expected to attend all in-person lecture and lab sessions unless you have a serious illness or other medical condition.
For some lab-based courses, participation marks will be given for completion of laboratory activities and these marks will be worth up to 5% of the student’s final course grade. A student must be on time for start of the lab period to avoid losing participation marks. These lab activities involve generating lab specific materials and/or data, learning, acquiring and/or practicing new skills and/or procedures.
Please notify your instructor and the Biotechnology program head of any absences by email as soon as possible.
If exams, quizzes, or in-class assignments are missed for reasons of illness, medical notes will be required to reschedule.
