## Course Description
This course covers economic factors affecting development and construction, principles of land development, valuation techniques for real property, cost planning and control, cash flow analysis, and feasibility analysis of land development.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:

- Identify economic forces affecting the development and construction industry.
- Perform a title search on a parcel of land and identify various claims against it.
- Outline the steps of the land development process.
- Apply the appropriate method, viz. market comparison, cost, income, and residual value, in the valuation of various types of real property.
- Identify various options available for financing a building development project, and apply the most appropriate.
- Calculate the payment schedule for financing a project, and determine the balance remaining at any point in time
- Prepare a cash flow statement and pro-forma for a land development project.
- Analyse the profitability of an investment in real property
- Perform a sensitivity analysis on the project's profitability as a risk management tool.
- Assist in taking a project through the development permit, rezoning, and subdivision processes.
