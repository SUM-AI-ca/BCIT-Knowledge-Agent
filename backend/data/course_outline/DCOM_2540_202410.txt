## Course Description
​This course introduces the applications, concepts and implementation of VoIP (Voice over IP) networks. The operation of a VoIP will be compared and contrasted with that of traditional telephony networks. Students will configure a VoIP telephony system on a PC connected to a VoIP switch. Troubleshooting with a network protocol analyzer will be introduced. Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) phones will be introduced and used in the configured PBX. WAN networks and bandwidth considerations will be discussed with reference to the CODECs necessary for implementation​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Compare and contrast traditional telephony with VoIP networks.
- Install and configure a PC-based VoIP system.
- Connect a VoIP switch to an Ethernet LAN.
- Perform administrative tasks required to manage a VoIP system.
- Troubleshoot VoIP systems using a network protocol analyzer.
- Configure SIP phones for VoIP operation.
- Calculate bandwidth requirements for VoIP calls and correctly determine CODEC requirements.​
