### Course Description
​This course provides a comprehensive overview of graphic design within the context of digital advertising. Emphasis is placed on creating visually compelling designs and developing effective strategies for promoting products and services across various media channels. Students will explore fundamental design principles, strategic thinking, and the integration of design elements into diverse advertising formats. The course fosters skills in both creative and strategic aspects of advertising, preparing students to adapt to evolving digital landscapes. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop persuasive and engaging copy that resonates with target audiences through strategic storytelling and clear messaging.
- Apply digital tools and platforms to create, manage, and assess the effectiveness of advertising campaigns, focusing on principles rather than specific technologies.
- Use analytical insights to refine and improve campaign performance, making informed decisions based on data-driven evaluation.
- Apply UX and UI principles to create visually appealing and user-friendly designs that drive engagement and conversions
- Work collaboratively to develop comprehensive advertising strategies and designs that align with client or organizational objectives.
- Recognize and address ethical considerations in advertising, including issues related to privacy, representation, and responsible messaging.
- Create advertising materials that maintain brand consistency and effectively communicate the brand's identity and values across different media.

### Evaluation Criteria
Criteria | % | Comments
A00 Advertising Through Time | 5 | This assignment is designed to provide students with a foundational understanding of historical advertising trends and their impact on graphic design.
A1. Research & Strategic Campaign Objective | 25 | This foundational assignment ensures students grasp that effective advertising design stems from rigorous research and a precise campaign objective.
A2: Establishing Roadmap - Crafting the Client Brief | 25 | This comprehensive brief requires synthesizing robust research and strategic planning into a persuasive proposal for an advertising campaign, encompassing crucial elements for its successful execution and measurement.
A3: Developing Visual Direction - Moodboard & Reasoning | 15 | This assignment marks a critical transition from strategy to creative execution. Students will translate their research and strategic objectives into a cohesive visual direction for their advertising campaign.
A4: Creating Advertising Materials that Maintain Brand Consistency | 25 | This assignment shifts focus to the core creative output of the course: the development of actual advertising materials. Building on their previously established strategy, visual direction, and client brief, students will design and produce a set of cohesive advertising assets
A5: Final Presentation | 5 | This culminating assignment provides students with the opportunity to synthesize and present their complete advertising campaign strategy and creative execution.
### Course Specific Requirements
To replicate the collaborative environment of a professional creative agency, this course requires active, real-time participation. Students are expected to have their cameras turned on during all live sessions to facilitate meaningful discussion, peer feedback, and in-class activities.
