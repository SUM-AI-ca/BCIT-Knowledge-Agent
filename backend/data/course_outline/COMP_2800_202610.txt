### Course Description
Students work in teams of four or five to complete an advanced mobile-friendly web application in a real-world domain. Teams will proceed through the majority of the software development lifecycle, from requirements gathering to specification, UI/UX design, to implementation and delivery. The course is focused on developing teamwork and project management skills as well as an understanding of the development lifecycle. The project will simultaneously allow students to apply their previously-developed technical knowledge. Students are challenged to effectively apply new technologies such as AI and cloud computing to achieve project goals. Prerequistes: COMP 1510, COMP 1537, COMP 1800 Co-requisite: COMP 2537
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Determine and document project requirements.
- Plan and schedule project work tasks in agile manner.
- Work effectively with a team to meet stated tasks and milestones.
- Design a system that meets the documented requirements.
- Implement a system that has been designed.
- Test and deliver a working system.

## Grading Notes
- An individual student’s group marks may be adjusted at mid and final checkpoints： Scaling is based on individual teamwork contributions, project contributions towards the MVP, and overall aspects of professional behaviour Scaling is assessed by the Faculty Supervisor AND by peer evaluation
- Students may receive a Fail (F), or “Unsatisfactory” (U) status under the following circumstances: Not contributing significantly or participating equally in all project iterations Not participating in a meaningful and non-trivial way in the Final Presentations Not professionally participating in team meetings, lacking professional behaviour towards teammates, clients, or instructors
- Teams may receive a failing grade under the following circumstances: Not delivering a functional Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Not completing a proper and professional handoff of the project to the client
- Final Course Grades are assigned as follows: 95% Exceptional (Above and beyond expectations; Exemplary quality) 85% Great (Meets all the requirements; High quality) 75% Good (Meets most requirements; Some omissions and mistakes) 50% Weak (Numerous omissions; Poor quality) Grades over 85% will be reserved for students and groups who exceed the requirements and go beyond the expectations.
- Use of generative AI is encouraged, but students must demonstrate personal understanding of the code that they are adapting, with citations and acknowledgment of all sources. The faculty team reserves the right to interview individual students about their code or to perform random code walk-throughs to assess understanding.
## Checkpoint Grades (Midpoint & Final):
Grades at the project checkpoints are based on your team’s overall performance in key areas of software development and project execution.
Daily Expectations
- Participate actively, professionally, and punctually in meetings and team collaboration
- Demonstrate teamwork through enthusiasm, reliability, and helpfulness
- Use project management tools effectively to create, prioritize, and complete SMART tasks on time
- Consistently track and communicate individual and team progress
End-of-Sprint Expectations (Weekly)
- Make meaningful progress toward key deliverables and the MVP
- Submit professional, well-formatted deliverables that align with project goals
- Use appropriate technology choices to support project requirements
- Thoroughly test deliverables and document evidence of testing
- Reflect on sprint outcomes and apply lessons learned to improve future work
### Attendance Requirements
Regular attendance is seen as critical to student success, and will be monitored by faculty. Consider this course as training for a full-time job. The standard institute policy on attendance applies. Exemptions for health-related situations may be considered with official documentation submitted to the Lead Instructors and Program Head.
Your individual contribution to the group project will, in part, be evaluated on your professional behaviour, which includes regular attendance, punctuality, and participation in scrum and client meetings. Being 5 minutes late for a scrum will be considered as an absence.
Failure to attend mandatory meetings, or daily check-in may result in your individual final calculated grade being adjusted down:
- 1 missed session = no adjustment
- 2 missed sessions = 5% penalty
- 3 missed sessions = 10% penalty
- 4 missed sessions = 20% penalty
- 5 missed sessions = a grade of U
