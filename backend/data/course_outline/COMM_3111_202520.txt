## Course Description
Building on the techniques learned in COMM 1111, students will apply their business writing skills to longer documents and develop strategies for persuasion. The course covers career communications, short reports, persuasive writing and speaking (suggestions, requests, proposals), team work, and the use of web-based tools for collaboration. The first class will include a language assessment to enable students who need extra support to get it.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Write an effective letter of application and resume
- Demonstrate effective behaviour in a job interview
- Apply strategies for persuasive writing
- Write short reports and an informal proposal
- Demonstrate effective team behaviours to work collaboratively
- Use web-based collaboration tools to work as a team
- Prepare and deliver a persuasive oral presentation as part of a team

## Evaluation Criteria
Criteria: %: Comments
Persuasive Message: 10: You will complete a first draft (2.5) and final version (5) of a persuasive email. You will also create a short video to explain how you applied the lessons from the course in your message (2.5).
Timed Writing Assignment: 10: You will write a timed persuasive email in class.
Workplace Proposal: 20: You will write a workplace proposal in a small team. This will include an outline (2.5), elevator pitch (2.5), first draft (5), and a final version (10).
Persuasive Pitch: 15: You will pitch an original idea in a small team. This will include an outline & draft slides (2.5), a live oral pitch (10), and a pitch reflection (2.5).
Job Application Module: 35: You will complete a resume (draft 5, final 5); a cover letter (draft 5, final 5); a video to explain how you applied the lessons from the course in your resume and cover letter (5); a personal pitch (5); and a mock interview activity (5).
Reflective Learning Activities: 10: You will complete four personal writing or speaking activities. These include a self-introduction video (2.5), the Story of Me assignment (2.5), a course reflection (2.5), and a professionalism self-assessment (2.5).
