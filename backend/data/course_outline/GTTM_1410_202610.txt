### Course Description
​Introduces the student to the international Harmonized System of classification of commercial goods. Develop students' ability to complete certain types of trade documentation required for importations and exportations. Familiarize students with the Canada-United States-Mexico Agreement (CUSMA) regulations, Rules of Origin, and current trade agreements coming into effect.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Understand all aspects of importation and exportation of commercial goods.
- Complete commercial documents used in the International Trade environment.
- Understand impact of CRA on other Government Departments and Agencies.
