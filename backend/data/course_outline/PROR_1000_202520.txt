### Course Description
​In this course, students will learn technical skills related to vacuum forming various thermoplastics for the fabrication of orthotic devices. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use topic-specific equipment and materials properly and safely
- Apply an understanding of thermoplastics and other orthotic materials and vacuum forming in the creation of orthotic devices
- For a device described in a prescription/tech sheet, select and measure plastic for the given vacuum forming process
- Prepare a plaster cast for a vacuum forming (such as wicking, sealing pipe, dry cast, using string to create straight seams, etc.)
- Based on a provided prescription, execute the application of transfer paper to thermoplastic plastic prior to ankle-foot orthosis forming
- Demonstrate understanding of internal reinforcements and external posting for an orthotic device (such as where they are needed and how they are executed)
- Demonstrate understanding of different techniques for creating corrugated reinforcements and spacers within a thermoplastic orthotic device
- Demonstrate critical thinking and self-reflection towards the quality of the student projects

### Course Goals
The PROR 1000 - Thermoplastic Forming Techniques for Orthotics micro-credential course covers techniques used in thermoforming and vacuum forming orthotic devices. The forming techniques covered in this micro-credential course are meant to provide students with a greater understanding of different forming techniques and their different applications. This course will provide students with tools in their skill toolbox, so that they will be able apply these techniques to best suit the outcome needs of the orthotist and the patient. Orthotic fabrication can differ from clinic to clinic and even from country to country. This course is not intended to introduce every technique or suggest that the techniques introduced are the only ways to thermoform or vacuum form orthotic devices. The techniques introduced will provide a proven and repeatable stepwise strategy for students looking to increase their rate of successful orthotic fabrication. It is recommended that students take what they need from this course and integrate practices that make sense for them and their workplace.
