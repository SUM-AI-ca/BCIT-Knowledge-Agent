## Course Description
​This course will introduce students to various current forms of money laundering committed within Canada. You will discuss various methods of criminal operations required to carry out money laundering by individuals, as well as domestic and transna​tional organized crime groups. You will learn of several best practices to detect, investigate, report, and deter money laundering.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Assess the legal statutes, case law and crime theory​ that relate to open source money laundering cases.
- Apply crime theory to the prevention, detection, and investigation of money laundering.
- Discuss the role of FINTRAC and other sources of intelligence and evidence relevant to money laundering.
- Apply money laundering investigation techniques as to the placement, layering and investment of illicit funds​.

## Course Goals
This course provides students with an overall understanding of money laundering within Canada. Discussions around identifying criminality surrounding money laundering patterns and trends using an investigative approach will enable students to detect, investigate, report and prevent money laundering. Through several exercises and assignments, and working with scenarios, students will study a case and provide a report in the final week in order to successfully complete the course.

## Course topics
Week 1 - Proceeds of Crime / Money Laundering - money laundering typologies - casinos, money services businesses, hawalas, trade based money laundering, real estate, stock market. Legal issues include PCMLTFA legislation, Criminal Code legislation and Canadian Case law.

Week 2 - Money Laundering and Monitoring Systems - Identifying indicators, sources of information, crime theories, detecting and preventing money laundering.

Week 3 - Canadian Environment - public / private partnerships, designataed non-financial businesses and professions, FINTRAC, Civil Forfeiture and Canada Revenue Agency.

Week 4 - Investigative Strategies - special and common strategies used in investigations and varying approaches across financial institutions and community sectors.
