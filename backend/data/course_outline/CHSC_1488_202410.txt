## Course Description
Covers test procedures for mechanical properties: nondestructive testing and failure analysis, the basic concepts of engineering materials including metals, alloys, plastics, and ceramics.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Perform hardness, tensile and impact tests on materials.
- Identify causes of failure in materials.
- Identify method of manufacture of machine components.
- Perform heat treatments on steel.
- Describe and list advantages of common non-destructive testing methods.
