## Course Description
​The course is designed to provide an overview of what companies must initiate to build customer loyalty. Topics will include customer retention, customer centricity, customer satisfaction metrics, and customer loyalty programs. Emphasis will be placed on practical methods of implementing loyalty tactics for business-to-business selling roles.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop effective public speaking skills by delivering oral presentations (individual and team) to audiences of diverse sizes.
- Develop a B2B sales strategy to increase customer satisfaction, retention and loyalty.
- Plan methods, systems & programs to ensure consistent, cost-efficient customer experiences.
- Design a superior customer service to create value and build brand.
- Identify key touchpoints of a business and describe common methods to improve a touchpoint.
- Explain customer evaluation standards, benchmarks and KPIs.
- Discuss the advantages of common customer evaluation methods.
- Develop a simple customer complaint evaluation system.
- Identify VOC/strategic listening channels.
- Create key questions for an Annual Service Review.
- Describe social media tactics that deliver superior customer experiences.
- Describe the concept of customer experience (CX) and how it correlates with customer loyalty in B2B.
- Identify the keys to creating a customer experience that generates loyalty and positive WOM.
- Provide ideas for implementing a WOW Factor in B2B sales and service.
- Explain the benefits of a customer loyalty program.
- Describe elements of an effective loyalty program/reward program.
- Identify benefits and challenges of subscription-based business models.
- Detail the key steps in winning back a dissatisfied client.
- Describe the fundamentals of Six Sigma in terms of its impact on improving customer loyalty.
- Evaluate a Gap Analysis in measuring customer service delivery.
- Identify 3 different models of firing customers.
