### Course Description
Familiarizes the technical writer with formats and conventions of documents used in the construction industry. Also includes a review of basic English grammar and sentence structure as well as an introduction to public speaking. Emphasis is placed on writing with clarity, directness, and brevity. 3.0 credits 12 weeks.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Create documents that follow the conventions of correct grammar, spelling, mechanics and punctuation.
- Generate, draft, and revise a memorandum, short business letter, and facsimile following correct business conventions with respect to format, structure, style and content.
- Make notes at job-site or office meetings, and then draft and revise an accurate record of such meetings following correct business conventions with respect to format, structure, style and content.
- Prepare an agenda for a job-site or office meeting following correct business conventions with respect to format, structure, style, and content.
- Communicate verbally with increased clarity and confidence.

### Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the student should be able to:
· Write & edit memos, emails, business letters and SVRs
· Understand grammar constructs such as the Imperative
· Use advanced style elements such as parallelism, given-new repetition, etc.
· Write with improved clarity, conciseness, and cohesion
· Recognize the ten most common logical fallacies in writing/argumentation
· Use inclusive and neutral language to communicate respectfully
· Make documents accessible using tools such as Indices, Alt Texts, etc.
· Produce correct in-text and bibliographical citations using IEEE style guide
· Effectively give and receive verbal feedback
· Apply basic public speaking techniques
· Successfully design and use audiovisual elements in a presentation

### Course topics
Students will learn the formats and conventions of memos, emails, letters, and site visit reports used in the construction industry as well as skills and techniques for oral communication. English grammar study will include punctuation, the active vs passive voice, the Imperative, etc. We will discuss advanced style elements such as sentence and paragraph structure, parallelism, given-new repetition, and emphasis. Students will practice verbal communication skills and public speaking techniques. Additional themes include in-text and bibliographical citations, document accessibility, inclusive language, neutrality, and avoiding logical fallacies in writing. The course centers on clear, concise, cohesive, complete, and accessible writing.
