### Course Description
An understanding of programming is essential for an Information Technology Business Solution Designer. In this course, the Python programming language will be used to introduce students to the concepts of computer programming in order to provide a foundation for systems development and data processing.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the definition of a computing algorithm.
- Provide an explanation of the problem solving process.
- Utilize debugging in response to common programming errors.
- Identify variables, expressions and statements.
- Create and utilize functions.
- Understand selection and iteration and create simple programs to demonstrate these concepts.
- Utilize strings and lists to manage data.
- Create files and dictionaries to extend the capabilities of programs.

Lab Participation
Modern coding is a collaborative endeavour. Students should become comfortable with paired/group programming and reviewing the code of their peers. To reflect this importance, we will spend a portion of each week's labs working in pairs or small groups to complete coding challenges related to that week's learnings. Each lab, students will be graded on the following scale.
Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3
Student did not collaborate with their peers | Student was somewhat prepared for lab activity and/or partially collaborated with their peers. | Student was fully prepared for lab activity and fully collaborated with their peers.
If a student will not be present in lab they should notify their instructor ahead of time to have their participation grade redistributed to other labs.
### Course Specific Requirements
Students will be expected to keep up with all required readings, projects, and assignments. Late assignments will not be accepted for marking unless special arrangements are made before the due date.
Assignments must be done on an individual basis unless otherwise specified by the instructor.
AI Usage
AI can be a highly useful tool for software developers. However, it is critical that AI is used responsibly and safely. To do this, students need foundational skills to be able to critique AI results, debug errors and properly test code.
To facilitate development of these skills, AI will generally not be permitted for use on graded assessments , unless explicitly stated otherwise by the instructor.
