## Course Description
​Continuing from BLDG 2810, this course covers the advanced concepts of Autodesk Revit in an architectural environment. The course gives students greater familiarity with the breadth of Revit Architecture's feature set.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Allows students to design and develop their own 3D and 2D objects (families) from the provided templates.
- Understand different coordinate systems within Revit​
- Teaches students to customize common object types such as railings and stairs.
- The course will give students the essential methods of administrating and coordinating Revit in an architectural office environment.
- Delivery will concentrate on real-world approaches and methodology rather than step-by-step procedures.
