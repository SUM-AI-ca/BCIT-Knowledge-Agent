## Course Description
Students learn how to write letters, memos, and routine email messages. They learn to write clear and concise sentences and paragraphs and to make documents easily accessible through headings, lists and white space. They learn how to avoid some common grammatical errors in their writing. They also learn to give an oral report based on library and online research on a new development in their technology. Students also learn how to collaborate as part of a team to accomplish work related communication tasks.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Determine the purpose and audience for specific written communication tasks. [7]
- Select, organize, and develop information to meet your purpose and the audience's needs. [7]
- Make documents easily accessible by using headings, lists and white space. [7]
- Write clear and concise sentences and paragraphs, and edit and revise your writing to an acceptable industry standard. [7]
- Use appropriate e-mail, memo and letter formats. [7]
- Create, format and integrate graphics in technical written or and oral/visual documents. [7]
- Locate and assess information from various sources and reference appropriately. [7, 12]
- Write an informative memo-style report on a new development in civil engineering technology. [7]
- Plan, organize and deliver an oral presentation on an engineering-related topic. [7]

## Other information
Grading Guidelines:

90-100% An outstanding assignment. The work is perfect or nearly perfect. The assignment will be complete and easily accessible. The English language, grammar, and punctuation will be flawless.

80-89% A superior assignment. Smoothly written and effectively organized, it leads the reader clearly and easily through the material. The purpose of the paper is clear, and the organization, tone and style are adapted to the audience. The assignment may show originality in word choice, in the selection or development of reader benefits or examples, or in the effective use of presentation strategies.

65-79% An assignment that does several things well. It has only a few minor errors that do not interfere with getting the job done. It shows tact and good judgement. It is well written and well organized. Its main points are developed and supported.

50-64% A document which meets the basic assignment demands. Errors in content, organization, style and tone do not seriously interfere with the communication. It may be an otherwise acceptable document with a major flaw or a number of minor flaws.

0-49% An assignment that fails to meet the basic demands of the assignment, which has a great many minor errors or several major errors, or which has a major, unacceptable flaw. The document would not achieve its intended purpose in the workplace.
