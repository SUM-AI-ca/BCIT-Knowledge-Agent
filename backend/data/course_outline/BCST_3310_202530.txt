### Course Description
This course further develops the skills introduced in BCST 2209 to a higher level. This is a fieldwork course where students will be exposed to, and responsible for, the various key positions relating to the operation of the radio station Evolution 107.9 FM which operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Each job rotation is an important factor in the operation of Evolution 107.9 FM and Evolution 1079.com, allowing students to apply concepts related to media convergence. Students will receive weekly individual critiques from an instructor as they rotate through various shifts across term 3. This fieldwork course prepares students for BCST 4410 - Media Lab 2 in Term 4.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Apply advanced announcing skills on Evolution 107.9 FM and Evolution 1079.com.
- Research and prepare Evolution 107.9 (radio station and web) program content.
- Communicate professionally in all media platforms according to industry standards.
- Comply with primary CRTC regulations.
- Apply high ethical standards as outlined by the Canadian Broadcast Standards Council (CBSC.ca) as well as the Radio and Television News Directors Association (RTNDA.ca).
- Rewrite local and regional news items from the Canadian Press news service.
- Organize and perform a three to four hour radio program with infrequent operational errors, including production for multi-platform environments.
- Collaborate with clients to create radio advertising campaigns.
- Plan and execute a basic multimedia promotional campaign.
- Integrate social media elements into audience interaction.
- Communicate effectively with different demographic groups in all media platforms.
- Apply industry based professionalism in an operational environment.
- Articulate concerns and ideas to others in a positive work environment.
