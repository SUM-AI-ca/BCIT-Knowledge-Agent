### Course Description
The course provides students with theoretical and empirical tools to analyze mining-related problems and design solutions using emerging technologies. The course is divided into a lecture component and a research component. The lecture component covers topics in leading-edge technologies within the mining industry and the research component provides students with the opportunity to investigate areas of leading edge technologies under development or currently being implemented in the minerals industry. As part of the lecture component students will undertake a series of reading and group presentation sessions of cutting edge research papers in order to build research expertise and presentation skills. Students, in small groups, will select a problem to investigate, conduct research into the topic and design a conceptual solution to the problem. Groups will prepare a concept paper that outlines the problem, the research methods employed and the solution(s) proposed. The problem analyzed and proposed solutions will be presented to peers and faculty for review. Some possible areas for research might be: • Remote sensing technology used in the exploration and mining industry • The development of automated load-haul-dump and drilling equipment in mines • The use of expert systems and tele robotics in mining operations • New designs for ground support and ventilation in deep, hot underground mines • Potential future sources of minerals such as recycling of municipal and mine waste (urban mining) • Mining of ocean floor ore deposits (undersea mining) • Future mining of asteroids (space mining) • Advanced sensing/scanning methods for underground mapping • Automated control strategies in mineral process plants, including fundamentals of sensing/control devices, real-time communication and real-time computing • Emerging technologies for control and amelioration of acid mine drainage (AMD) • New methodologies for maximizing the efficiency of mining operations while minimizing their foot-print • Energy efficiency and environmental improvements in mining and mineral processing
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate the effectiveness of emerging technologies in the minerals industry [1,2,4,5,9,11,12].
- Apply theoretical and empirical tools to analyze mining-related problems [1,4,5,11,12].
- Suggest innovative methods to solve current problems in mine operations and design [1,2,4,5,11,12].
- Assess the impact of innovation on society and the environment [1,2,4, 9,11,12].
- Plan and complete a research project from concept to deliverables [1,4,7,8,11,12].
- Actively participate in debates on current mining developments [1,2,6,7,12].
- Prepare and defend a research paper on the area investigated and solution proposed [1,2,4,7,8,11, 12].
