## Course Description
​Vascular Sonography 2 is a continuation of DSON 2005 - Vascular Sonography 1. The emphasis shifts from normal features to abnormal findings in the carotid arteries and peripheral extremity veins through the integration of clinical signs and symptoms, sonographic findings, and hemodynamic consequences of vascular disease.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the requisite protocols and images required for a complete upper extremity peripheral venous DVT study and explain how to optimize patient position for the exam while maintaining patient dignity.
- Evaluate upper extremity venous images for orientation, identification, labelling, image artifact and quality while identifying appropriate technical adjustments to create diagnostic images.
- Recognize and communicate abnormal arterial and venous sonographic findings in provided images and formulate a technical impression using sonographic descriptors and accurate medical terminology and standard abbreviations.
- Describe the appropriate patient management, potential treatments and exam implications when urgent sonographic findings are present during extremity vein and carotid studies, and express the process for investigating further.
- Correlate exam indication with patient clinical history, and explain how to assess for signs and symptoms of vascular disease.
- Explain the effects, on the venous return during provocative maneuvers such as (Valsalva) when v venous disease is present.
- Correlate the variable factors of vascular disease and their associated impact on measured velocity data and associated calculations.

## Learning Resources
Required: High-speed computer access with internet capability.

Optional textbook: The Vascular System (2nd or 3rd Ed.)., Kupinski, A. M. Wolters Kluwer, 2018 or Lippincott Williams & Wilkins, 2022 respectively. Older versions of the textbook are available for two-week loans from the BCIT library.

## Course Goals
This higher-level course will develop the learner's ability to correlate and analyze abnormal arterial and venous hemodynamics associated with peripheral vascular disease using sonographic assessment techniques including 2D, colour and spectral Doppler.
