### Course Description
(UBC BIOT 352) Teaches the biotechnology student the to assist in the management of a biotechnology facility. Emphasizes the importance of Total Quality Management together with Good Manufacturing Practices and Good Laboratory Practices. Shows how to develop a quality management system based on ISO 9000 standards. Discusses government regulations. Teaches management practices appropriate for a biotechnology facility, including basic human resource management, and financial administration.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Discuss the impact of their work activities on quality and regulator compliance in a biotech company.
- Describe the importance of intellectual property and related issues.
- Describe the importance of record keeping in maintaining proof of intellectual property and experimental results.
- Describe and discuss the laws, regulatory agencies and bodies that are responsible for regulating drug development, biotechnology and genetically engineered organisms.
The goal of this course is to provide the students with a background to understand the kinds of regulatory and management requirements under which they will operate in a biotechnology facility.

### Course Goals
Introduces the biotechnology student to issues important in the management of a biotechnology facility. The importance of Total Quality Management together with Good Manufacturing Practices and Good Laboratory Practices will be emphasized. The student will be introduced to ISO 9000 standards for quality management systems. Government regulations related to drug manufacturing and the basics of natural product regulations will be discussed. Manufacturing issues related to gene based drugs will be introduced .
