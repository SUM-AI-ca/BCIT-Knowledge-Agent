## Course Description
Big Data continues to have a significant impact on business. This course serves as an introduction to the key aspects of how business intelligence is used in current business practices. Students will build business intelligence knowledge through the practical hands-on development of data analysis software and other applications and apply this knowledge by analyzing information to make business decisions.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student wil be able to:

- Evaluate the functions and components of a Business Intelligence system.
- Process data sets using a Business Analytics system.
- Plan Business Intelligence and Analytics technology implementations.
- Develop reports and support business decision making using various Business Intelligence tools.
- Assess the functionality of local and Cloud Computing applications for Business Intelligence.
- Evaluate emerging trends in Business Intelligence.

## Learning Resources
This course will require the use of Microsoft Excel and PowerBI software. In our first week we will go over software requirements in more detail and ensure you are able to install and use all required tools for this course.

There is no course textbook and instead online reference articles and resources will be shared on the learning hub each week.

## Course Goals
Business Intelligence (BI) is a big topic with a long history. This course will focus on the introductory BI skills businesses use to make more informed decisions by leveraging data. A large part of this course will focus on using business intelligence tools to bring data to life using various visualization and presentation techniques.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.
