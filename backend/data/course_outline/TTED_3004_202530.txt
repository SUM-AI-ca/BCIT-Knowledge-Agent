### Course Description
This course introduces the basic principles and methods of joining wood, metals, and plastics. Methods covered will include solvent cementing of plastics, gluing and clamping of woods, soldering, brazing, fusion welding and gluing of metals, and an introduction to a range of mechanical fasteners appropriate for use in public school Technology Education facilities.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe and demonstrate the joining of plastics through the process of solvent cementing.
- Describe and demonstrate the joining of woods through the process of gluing and clamping.
- Describe and demonstrate the joining of metals through the processes of fasteners, riveting, soldering, spot welding, brazing, and fusion welding (oxy-acetylene and shielded metal arc welding).

### Course Goals
TTED 3004 is an introductory course aimed at gaining practical experience in materials, tools, equipment and processes associated with the high-school Technology Education experience. Through a number of practical assignments, demonstrations and personal exploration, students will develop knowledge useful in the field.

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Week 1: Midterm exam from TTED 3022, Review course syllabus, Review assignments, Machine demos, hand threading demo, assignment work time, prepare materials for upcoming assignments
Week 2: Demo ABS solvent, Demo wood adhesives, Demo Oxy Acetylene start up and shut down procedures, Demo Oxy-Acetylene welding techniques, Demo Brazing techniques, lab time
Week 3: Class cancelled for Provincial Tech Ed. conference
Week 4: Demo soft soldering, Demo spot welding, Demo riveting, lab time
Week 5: Demo MIG welding, Creative project approval, lab time
Week 6: Demo Arc welding, lab time
Week 7: Completion of all coursework
Week 8: Completion of all coursework and review for Final Exam
### Course topics
Threaded fasteners, Riveting, Wood adhesives, Plastic solvent cement, Epoxy, Cyanoacrilate adhesives, Soft-soldering, Brazing, Spot welding, Oxy-Acetylene fusion welding, MIG welding, Arc welding, Aluminum TIG welding.
