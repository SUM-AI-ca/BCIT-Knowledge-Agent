### Course Description
This course builds on Technical Arts 2, where students developed their skills in designing shaders, procedural materials, and textures. In Technical Arts 3, students focus on advanced technical procedures and camera setup to light and render scenes in 3D applications and game engines. They will create expressions and develop scripts for dynamic lighting, camera movement, and effects. Finally, students will learn advanced rendering techniques: managing and prioritizing data, troubleshooting and optimizing materials, and ensuring the smooth operation of rendering processes. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Construct light and camera nodes in 3D applications and game engines.
- Create dynamic lighting based on the environmental variables.
- Develop a script for a dynamic camera in 3D applications and game engines.
- Optimize shaders and textures for rendering processes.
- Report visual artifacts and incomplete or damaged shaders or images.
- Assess scenes, game levels, and environments for smooth rendering.
- Troubleshoot issues that could hinder the rendering process.
- Diagnose problems arising in different stages of the production pipeline.
- Provide a solution for problems identified at each stage of the production pipeline.

### Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: As set out in BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations , regular attendance in lectures and labs is integral to student success. Therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Students are responsible for communicating with instructors proactively about both potentially approved and unexcused absences. It is the student’s responsibility to work with their instructor to find an effective means to complete work missed due to both approved and unexcused absences. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate . For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation. If absences are the result of a medical or other accommodation, notification from Accessibility Services personnel will be accepted in lieu of a doctor’s note, on an interim basis. A student who has ceased to attend a course without explanation before the withdrawal deadline (two-thirds of the way through the course) will receive a Vanished (V) grade. These requirements are set out in accordance with both BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations and BCIT Policy 5103 Student Evaluation More information regarding withdrawal deadlines is available at Academic Dates & Deadlines . Academic Integrity and Use of Artificial Intelligence : The unauthorized use of Artificial Intelligence tools (such as ChatGPT) for any part of student work in this course is prohibited, and, when identified by your instructor, will result in an academic integrity violation.
