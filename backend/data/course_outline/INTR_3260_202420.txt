## Course Description
This course covers the basics of Autodesk Revit for Windows as used in the Interior Design industry, from design through construction documentation. Students will be introduced to the tools and concepts of working with a fully parametric building model and will complete a small residential project. Delivery of the course will concentrate on real world approaches and methodology rather than step by step procedures.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Compare/contrast the advantages of the BIM process with conventional CAD drafting.
- Demonstrate familiarity with Revit tools for performing the following tasks:
- Create levels and grids
- Work with views and sheets
- Model and render walls, floors, ceilings, windows, doors, furniture, etc.
- Basic family creation
- Produce schedules and legends
- Create colour fill schemes based on rooms and areas
- Detail millwork in drafting views.
- Create a set of residential working drawings in the Revit environment, based on a preliminary/conceptual design.
