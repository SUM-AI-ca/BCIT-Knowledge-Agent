## Course Description
This course is a continuation of the study of building construction estimating and the manual preparation of the contractor-oriented bid. It covers bidding procedures; measurement, pricing, and preparation of an estimate summary for specific items of work; requests for subcontractor quotations and subcontractor bid analysis; a simulated bid closing, and submission of a bid. Measurement in this course is focused on MasterFormat Division 7 (Thermal and Moisture Protection) and Division 9 (Finishes). The course also identifies the role of construction cost accounting in the construction estimating process.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Measure and price items of work in MasterFormat Division 7 and Division 9.
- Describe subcontracting the work, sub-trades, and scope of their work.
- Select, measure, and price appropriate Division 1 - General Requirements pertaining to temporary site facilities and relevant project-related administrative or procedural requirements.
- Use costing references and cost accounting data in determining unit prices.
- Develop rational head office overhead and profit percentages for inclusion in a bid price.
- Prepare and submit a competitive Tender for a simulated Bid Closing.
