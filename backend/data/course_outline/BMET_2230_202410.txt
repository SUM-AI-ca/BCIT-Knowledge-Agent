## Course Description
Students are introduced to the scope and practice in biomedical engineering through: Field trips to various workplaces including hospitals, research labs, medical device companies, and test centers. Presentations and guest speaker sessions on related topics.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the profession of a biomedical engineering technologist.
- Discuss the multi-disciplinary nature and scope of work in biomedical engineering.
- List professional associations and learned societies and outline their relevance for biomedical engineering technologists.
- Apply the professional code of ethics in the field of biomedical engineering.

## Attendance Requirements
Students are expected to complete all course work and attend all classes. Excessive absence may result in failure or withdrawal from this course. Course work or assessments must be completed within the scheduled period, otherwise a zero mark will be assigned. Marks will be deducted for late submission. No make-up will be provided except under excused absence (such as illness or other unavoidable cause as described below). In case of illness or other unavoidable cause of absence, the student must communicate as soon as possible with the course instructor. Prolonged illness must be substantiated by a medical certificate. Absence other than illness must be supported by proper documentation. Based on the information provided, the instructor will determine if the absence is deemed to be excused. If a student, with excused absence, is unable to complete a lab session or an assessment at the scheduled period, the course Instructor will work with the student to determine the next step for accommodation.
