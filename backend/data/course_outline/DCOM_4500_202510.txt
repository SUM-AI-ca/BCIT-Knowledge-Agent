## Course Description
​In this course, students will learn how to install, document, operate, and maintain an advanced wide area network (WAN) with fiber optic links. Students will discuss design considerations and the practical operation of fiber optic and other communications links. In a lab setting, students will program and test fiber optic spans, implement and test Coarse Wavelength Division Multiplexing (CWDM) links, and plan and execute network troubleshooting. Furthermore, they will design, program and observe the operation of automated failover routes to restore services following simulated network disasters. The course will focus on the proper procedures for handling network equipment cards and foster teamwork and communication skills for students to create and manage the orderly build and turn up of networks. Students will also identify selected Outside Plant (OSP), cable television (CATV), and electrical distribution system components in the aerial and underground and create a service-entrance design in CAD.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the operation of WANs.
- Document the interconnection of network elements using AutoCAD and/or other software.
- Describe the operation of an intelligent multiplexer/multiservice provisioning platform (MSPP).
- Design, program and build a voice and data network on an MSPP.
- Identify potential areas of network failure.
- Design a disaster recovery plan for the network.
- Troubleshoot faults on a fiber network.
- Use test equipment to analyze network performance.
- Describe circuit implementations suitable for a multi-city environment.
- Change out a card in networking equipment, while mitigating ElectroStatic Discharge (ESD) and other hazards to the equipment and the technician.
- Identify selected telecom, CATV, and electrical distribution system components in the OSP environment.
- Design an OSP underground service entrance using AutoCAD or similar software.​
