## Course Description
This course is a continuation of COMM 3394. It covers three areas that will prepare you for your further studies and your teaching career: 1. Technical communication - as a technology teacher you need to know several specialized types of technical communication suitable to technology education classes. 2. Communication in the classroom - you will also have to know how to incorporate communication activities into your assignments, particularly because all teachers in BC are mandated to be teachers of literacy in their own specialized areas. 3. Academic communication - in order to succeed in your educational career, you'll need to be able to communicate in an academic context.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Apply principles of technical communication to written and oral assignments.
- Adjust oral and written communication to audience and purpose.
- Apply documentation styles and conventions to academic writing.
- Describe the consequences of plagiarism in an academic setting.
- Produce a plan for a demonstration presentation.
- Deliver a technical demonstration to peers and instructors.
- Write meaningful, organized, and thoughtful feedback to peers.
- Craft a professional job package for technology education teacher postings.
- Prepare and participate in school site visits in a professional manner.
- Write short, informal reports on school site visits.
- Reflect on pedagogical work and activities both orally and in writing.
