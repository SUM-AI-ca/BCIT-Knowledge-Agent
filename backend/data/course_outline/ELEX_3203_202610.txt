### Course Description
This course is primarily focused on the most recent Canadian Electrical Code (CEC) as amended for the Province of British Columbia and the safe work practices specified by the CSA Z462 Workplace Electrical Safety standard. The emphasis will be on identifying key elements of the Canadian Electrical Code and learning how to properly interpret and implement the requirements defined by the Code and the required safe work practices needed to comply with the CSA Z462 standard. The Safety Standards Act of BC, the Safety Standards General Regulations, the Electrical Safety Regulation and the BC Safety Authority Directives will also be reviewed and referenced in context.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain the layout of the Canadian Electrical Code (CEC), Part 1 (including Rules, Tables and Appendices).
- Correctly interpret relevant sections of the CEC as amended for BC.
- Identify specific sections of CEC needed for a particular circumstance.
- Explain how to apply General Sections of the CEC to specific applications.
- Select conductors, demand calculations, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, overcurrent protection based on specific sections of the CEC.
- Calculate as needed requirements for conductors, demand calculations, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, overcurrent protection based on sections of the CEC.
- Interpret the specific requirements for: Class I and Class II circuits, hazardous locations, motors and generators, photovoltaic systems and emergency Systems.
- Identify the key elements of the Safety Standards Act, the Safety Standards General Regulations and Electrical Safety Regulation.
- Summarize safe electrical work practices in accordance with the CSA Z462 standard.
- Describe the relevant BC Safety Authority directives.
