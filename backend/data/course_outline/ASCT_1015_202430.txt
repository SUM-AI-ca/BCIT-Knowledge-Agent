## Course Description
This course provides an introduction to the relationships between construction drawings and building construction. The course goal is to enable students to read and interpret construction drawings, including Floor Plans, Roof Plans, Elevations, Building Sections, Framing Plans and Construction Details. Students will be able to understand the relationship and interdependencies of all drawings represented in contract documents. They will also become familiar with abbreviations, symbols and materials used in construction blueprints.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe project delivery process, parties involved and their roles.
- Explain the principles, purpose & contents of the Construction Contract Document.
- Identify types of drawings comprising a set of contract documents and understand their relationships.
- Properly interpret Industry standards as they relate to symbols, abbreviations, schedules and drawing cross-references.
- Identify components of various types of structural framing systems.
- Identify components of various types of foundation systems.
- Interpret building sections drawings and apply appropriate material patterns.
- Properly organize multi-view drawings.

## Course topics
This course will cover the following key topics:

- Introduction to Blueprint Reading Understanding the purpose and importance of construction drawings in various industries.
- Plans, Sections, and Elevations Interpreting floor plans, sections, and elevation views to understand building layouts and structures.
- Symbols, Abbreviations, and Industry Standards Recognizing common symbols, abbreviations, and conventions used in architectural, structural, and mechanical drawings.
- Roof Plans and Framing Details Understanding roof layouts, structural framing, and interpreting roof plans and sections.
- Building Sections and Foundations Examining foundation plans, cross-sections, and their relationship with structural elements.
- Residential and Steel Framing Interpreting residential wood framing and steel framing plans, focusing on the connections and support structures.
- Schedules (Doors, Windows, Columns) Reading and interpreting door, window, and column schedules in construction drawings.
- Steel Stairs and Connections Understanding steel stair designs, their connections to the structure, and safety elements.
