### Course Description
The Mining Seminar course enables students to present and discuss relevant mining industry topics. Presenters will include students, external faculty and industry representatives.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Discuss advanced engineering topics with industry representatives [1].
- Evaluate new technology and current issues in the mining industry [1].
- Evaluate and discuss the requirements for effective, professional technical presentations. [7]
- Effectively present on a technical subject. [7]

- Students may be required to have a functioning webcam and microphone for use in lectures, assignments and/or exams. If you do not have access to this equipment, please notify your instructor as soon as possible to make alternative arrangements.

Expected Feedback Timelines
The instructor will strive to provide feedback on submitted student work in a timely manner, ideally within 10 business days, or before the next assignment is assigned. The instructor will communicate to students in a timely manner when exceptions to the planned feedback timeline may occur.
