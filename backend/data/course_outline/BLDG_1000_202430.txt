## Course Description
This course is an introduction to various drawing methods, graphic language (conventions, standards, symbols), equipment and media used in the building industry for the purposes of conveying information. Students study two-dimensional drawing systems and practice manual drafting and drawing techniques and lettering. Emphasis is placed on the production of design development drawings. In addition, students are introduced into project develpoment and delivery process. The course is structured as a 3-hour lab session which meets once a week. Most lab session involve lecturing and instructional time. The remaining lab time is available for students to work on assignments and to receive feedback from instructors.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Prepare convincing and professional-looking drawings using the graphic language, conventions, and standards of the building industry.
- Read and interpret the design ideas and technical information that are described in architectural drawings.
- Produce drawings using a variety of media, particularly graphite and ink on vellum.
- Use drawings as a creative problem solving tool when developing plans, sections, and elevations.
- Use drawings as a creative problem solving tool when developing plans, sections, and elevations.
- Document existing building conditions by site-measuring interior spaces and exterior surfaces and generating the appropriate drawings.
- Produce a simplified version of a design development package for a small residential project which includes a site plan, floor plans, sections, elevations, and a detail.
- Identify content of different consultant’s drawings in a construction drawings set: architectural, structural, mechanical and electrical and interpret drawings using industry standard graphic communication methods.

## Course Goals
Drawing is fundamentally a process of visual thinking and graphic expression. Pictorial in nature and information-rich, the process of drawing is utilized to transmit complex messages efficiently, thus becoming the primary communication vehicle used in the field of architectural design and building construction.

By approaching drawing as both a graphic technique and a mental thought process, this course endeavours to enhance:

· the student’s skills of observation, visualization, and delineation

· the student’s ability to understand and “read” building design and construction drawings

through the process of manual drafting

· the student’s ability to draw effectively and efficiently using the graphic language of the industry

· the student’s time management skills by encouraging a pragmatic, disciplined approach to assignments

· the student’s graphic communication skills which are required for other courses in the Architectural and Building Technology program
