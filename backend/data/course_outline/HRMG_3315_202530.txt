## Course Description
Are you in an HR role or a Management role with a large HR component? This on-line course uses a number of interactive, multi-media tools to teach students how to choose, implement and use best practice Human Resource metrics and analysis techniques that are aligned with organizational strategy. It looks at how to evaluate the financial impact of Human Resource initiatives and examines key metrics in areas such as engagement, absenteeism, recruitment and selection, performance management, health and employee development. Students will learn to show how investments in HR initiatives translate to bottom-line benefits

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
The learning outcomes for this course reflect both theory and practise in Human Resources Measurement

Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Provide a compelling argument for the value of strategic human resources measurement.
- Critically evaluate the quality of commonly used human resources measures.
- Explain the advantages and limitations of the major human resources measurement approaches.
- Understand fundamental statistics and research design concepts.
- Employ fundamental economic and finance concepts.
- Assess the financial and strategic impact of HR initiatives.
- Develop a HR measurement plan that is linked to and supports an organization’s strategy.
- Make effective written presentations.
- Work effectively as an on-line team member.

DISCUSSION ASSIGNMENTS GRADING CRITERIA

Details

The discussion assignments are experiential learning activities. Each week you will be given an exercise to complete that is designed to help you learn how to create an HR measurement plan.

Since the discussion assignments are learning activities, you will not be graded on whether you get the answers right or wrong, but rather on the criteria listed below. Your grade will be determined by the quality, frequency, and timeliness of your discussion assignments. To receive full marks for the discussion assignments, you must complete each discussion assignment AND respond to one other student for each discussion assignment.

Each discussion activity is worth 10 points except for the student introduction which is automatically awarded 2 points and the ROI case which is worth 15 points.

Posts are due on Sundays and replies to classmates are due on Tuesdays. Late posts are penalized 10% per day and will not be accepted after 2 days past the due date. Students must submit their discussion assignments by the late deadline to avoid being recorded as absent for that week of the course. Late replies are not accepted.

To receive credit for your discussion assignment, you must use specific concepts and terms from the course text, and other materials in the relevant learning module, in your post and the course terms must be highlighted in bold.

The unauthorized use of outside information sources, including Artificial Intelligence products (such as ChatGPT), for discussion assignments is prohibited, and when identified by your instructor, will result in an academic integrity violation. Refer to policy 5104 - Student Code of Academic Integrity
