## Course Description
Integrating concepts from psychology, sociology, and interpersonal relations, this course is designed to help learners respectfully address the psychosocial needs of patients, and collaborate successfully with other members of the health care team. Empathy and interpersonal communications skills are emphasized; as these provide the basis for crucial employability skills, such as managing stress in both self and patients, reducing the frequency and intensity of conflict, and working respectfully with those from other cultures. Responsible, professional and ethical conduct is underscored.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion the student will be able to:

- Integrate effective interpersonal communication skills in order to foster rapport and trust.
- Apply concept of empathy to create respectful, supportive, and compassionate relations with patients and co-workers.
- Recognize and adapt communication based on the individual needs and nonverbal communication of patients and colleagues.
- Use active listening skills to obtain information from patients and/or support persons.
- Perform various types of assertion according to appropriateness and desired result.
- Implement psychological strategies and skills to prevent distress and uncooperative behavior in patients.
- Given a scenario, develop strategies to deal with harassment and discrimination in the workplace.
- Develop strategies for giving and receiving feedback that emphasize accountability, respect, and professional growth
- Identify types of conflict in the workplace and employ appropriate conflict management techniques to maintain a professional work environment.
- Develop strategies to work with cultural and developmental differences that impact interactions and treatment in a clinical setting.
- Demonstrate empathy and sensitivity to the diversity of individuals
- Identify indicators of abuse and respond within ethical and legal guidelines.
- Explain the impact of stress and factors that generate or increase stress.
- Implement strategies to prevent and reduce distress in both self and patients.
- Describe stress-related disorders and appropriate treatments and interventions.
- Develop an individualized plan to cope with stress, burnout, grief, and loss in the healthcare environment.

## Other information
Midterm Skills Video Assessment (25%): The midterm video skill assessment assesses communication skill understanding and implementation by requiring students to respond to scenario-based prompts with appropriate nonverbal and verbal communication. Students will be given written quiz prompts/scenarios (via Bongo Video Assignment) and asked to respond verbally on camera within a short time limit. Students will receive one chance to respond to each prompt. For exam integrity reasons, the skills assessment will take place at an assigned time. No retakes or late quizzes will be accepted. Students are expected to keep the content of quizzes confidential.

Culture Presentation (25%) + Peer Reviews (10%): Students will complete a recorded digital presentation in small groups. Groups will be assigned by instructor. Students will review peer presentations and complete peer reviews utlilizing course-related feedback skills and demonstrating professional and respectful communication. More information to be provided by instructor.

Final Exam (35%): The final exam will be cumulative and administered via Learning Hub Quiz feature. It will consist of written-response and short-answer questions. Students are expected to arrive on time for exams. Late arrivals will not receive extra time. All personal belongings, including bags and electronic devices, must be left in the designated area. Academic honesty is expected during the final exam. Any form of cheating, plagiarism, or breach of academic integrity will result in disciplinary action as per the institution's academic policies.

Participation (5%): This is an interpersonal communciation skills class. It is essential that students develop interpersonal communication skills by actively participating in class. Participation marks will be awarded at the end of the term at the discretion of the instructor. Students will have the opportunity to reflect upon their own communication strengths/weaknesses, set personal classroom communication goals, and evaluate their own communication skill development throughout the term. This input will be considered, along with instructor participation considerations (below), when determining participation marks at the end of term.

Criteria considered by instructor for when determining participation mark include:

Attendance: Please contact instructor if you are unable to attend class. Missed classes without prior discussion/communication may result in a deduction from your participation grade. Demonstrate respectful and professional conduct by arriving to class on time and communicating with instructor regarding absences and late arrivals.
Role-Plays/Group Work: Full participation in role plays, class discussions, and group work is required for this class.
Questions: Demonstrate active listening by asking questions – others might be wondering the same thing! I invite you to speak up in our classes. Students are also encouraged to ask questions of fellow classmates. Keep the conversation going.
Contribution: Demonstrate your engagement by offering your perspective or answering questions about class materials.
Respectful Conduct: Some topics may be sensitive and/or invite debate of different perspectives. Disrespectful language and/or behaviour will not be permitted. Use verbal and nonverbal communication to promote respect, inclusion, sensitivity, and curiousity.
Use of Skills: Demonstrate skills we have learned in class; show that you are able to apply your learning in a real-life interactive setting. Respond to classmates (and Instructor) using Active Listening Skills. Use the course skills when communicating via email. Incorporate skills into group work and all aspects of the course.
Preparation for Class: Prepare for class by doing your readings ahead of time; it will be easier to absorb and discuss materials discussed in class. Reference the readings in class to demonstrate your preparedness.
Use of Electronics: Please refrain from using your phone or computers for non-course related things during class time. Be mindful of the nonverbal messages being communicated through the presence/use of technology during classtime.
Support your Classmates: Be respectful, attentive, and supportive during class presentations. Give presenters your full attention. Limit your use of electronics during class presentations. Pay attention to your nonverbal communciation. Participate meaningfully in discussions after presentations.
HSIP 0300: Attend and actively participate in HSIP 0300; Utilize course-related skills when working with students from other programs; connect HSIP 0300 material to content and skills we are learning in class.
