## Course Description
This course covers maritime mobile and satellite services. Students will learn the operation and regulatory requirements of the basic equipment in a ship station and will apply their knowledge to the Global Maritime Distress & Safety System (GMDSS). This includes making distress, urgency, and safety calls following safety communications procedures. In addition, students will learn the procedures for general communications and how to use the Fleet Broadband and Iridium A3 satellite communications equipment in simulated real-world scenarios.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Explain the basic features of the Maritime Mobile and Mobile Satellite Service.
- State the operational regulatory requirements related to the GMDSS equipment.
- Demonstrate how to use a ship’s station basic GMDSS equipment and sub-system equipment.
- Demonstrate how to use operational procedures for general communications.
- Demonstrate how to use the Fleet Broadband and Iridium A3 equipment for satellite communications.

## Evaluation Criteria
Criteria: %
Practical: 50%
Final examination: 50%
TOTAL: 100%

In order to pass this course, students must achieve an aggregate grade of at least 70%. In addition, students must achieve a minimum of 50% of the marks allocated for course work and 50% of the marks allocated for the final exam. Students who fail to achieve 50% of the allocated marks in either component cannot receive a final grade greater than 69%. The minimum attendance for this course, in compliance with the Transport Canada requirement, is 90%.

Note that the wording pertains to only two components: (1) the final examination and (2) course work. It is therefore NOT required that each sub-component, such as a quiz, assignment, etc., has to be passed. For course work, it is up to the student, with the instructor’s support, to meet the 50% bar through the sub-components of possible make-up quizzes, alternative assignments, etc.

## Course Specific Requirements
On the first day of class you are required to provide picture identifcation which shows your date of birth.

Two (2) recent citizenship-style photographs measuring 1' x 1.25' (25 mm x 30 mm) are required for the issuance of the Industry Canada General Operators Certificate (GOC).

In order to be eligible to work in the Marine Industry, you must pass a Transport Canada Medical Examination.

Exam Attempts

Students who were unsuccessful in their final evaluation of a course are eligible for one (1) rewrite of their final examination. It is the responsibility of the student to contact the Cadet Program Assistant to schedule their re-examination. A student must book a minimum of ten (10) days prior to the posted re-examination date by emailing marine_exams@bcit.ca.

Upgraders:

Students who are upgrading are provided the opportunity to rewrite a final evaluation within twelve (12) months of the course completion.

Cadets:

Nautical Science diploma students must complete and pass all term courses before enrolling in the next academic term (e.g., students must have passing grades in all Term 1 courses prior to enrolling in Term 3 academic courses).

Students in Term 5 have twelve (12) months from the end of their program to complete and pass a final evaluation rewrite.

Time Limit to Complete a Credential

The time limitation begins on the starting date of the first course towards the credential. The time limit to complete a credential is seven (7) years (BCIT Policy 5103).
