## Course Description
​This course provides the theoretical and practical knowledge necessary to install, maintain, and troubleshoot circuits that employ discrete and integrated semiconductor devices. These include rectifier diodes, Zener diodes, 3-terminal regulator ICs, bipolar junction transistors, field effect transistors, operational amplifiers and switching regulator ICs. Applications of semiconductors as switches, amplifiers, oscillators and regulators are explored. Circuits are built from schematic diagrams and are tested and measured for their operating parameters. Students further develop soldering techniques, including insertion and soldering of components on a printed circuit board, resulting in a complete audio amplifier circuit as a small project.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the internal structure and operation of p-n junctions in semiconductor devices.
- Identify the schematic symbols and physical packages of discrete and integrated semiconductor devices.
- Interpret datasheet specifications for certain discrete and integrated semiconductor devices.
- Explain the operation of small signal, rectifier, Zener and varactor diodes.
- Test various diodes for their operating conditions, for troubleshooting purposes.
- Build, analyze and measure basic diode switching circuits (DC).
- Build, analyze and measure basic rectifier circuits (half-wave and bridge).
- Build, analyze and measure linear voltage regulator stages (Zener diode and 3-terminal IC).
- Build, analyze and measure various bipolar transistor switching circuits.
- Build, analyze and measure various linear amplifier circuits using bipolar transistors (classes A, B and AB).
- Build, analyze and measure various field effect transistor circuits (as switches and linear amplifiers).
- Build, analyze and measure circuits employing operational amplifiers (linear amplifiers and active filters).
- Build, analyze and measure RC and crystal oscillator circuits.
- Build, analyze and measure various switching regulator IC circuits (SMPS).
- Insert, solder and clean through-hole components on a printed circuit board.​
