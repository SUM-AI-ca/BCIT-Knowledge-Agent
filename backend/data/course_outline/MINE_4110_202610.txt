### Course Description
​Students will complete a team project that involves preparing and presenting a technical report on a mining project. This report will be centered around an actual mineral deposit/property and will integrate student-collected field and laboratory data with additional project information provided to the students. Topics to be studied may include aspects of deposit geology, exploration methods, resource estimates, mine planning and design, mineral processing, site assessment and layout, environmental sustainability, community engagement, project cost estimates and economics. Each team is responsible for the preparation of a succinct, well-written, and technically accurate project report. Teams will also complete an oral presentation and discussion of their study results.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Work as members of a team preparing, presenting, and discussing a technical report on a mining project.
- Demonstrate teamwork, project management and research skills.
- Apply interdisciplinary knowledge to solve complex engineering problems.
- Demonstrate engineering knowledge while applying the concepts of health and safety, environmental sustainability, and corporate social responsibility.
- Interpret the geology and describe the mineral potential of a mining project.
- Create and execute a field plan for a mining project site visit.
- Design mine and site layouts for a mining project.
- Evaluate the economics of a mining project.

### Course topics
CHECKLIST
- Take personal needs with you including medication
- Plan for your meals prior to the trip
Safety
- Conduct five-point safety every of field work
- Risk assessment of activities such as surveying, mapping,
- Identify the nearest resources to be used to in case of emergencies.
- Mine Safety protocols
- Mine Safety Culture
- Safety improvements required
Geology
- Geology - mine and surroundings (rock types and mineralisation
- Mapping plus geotechnical
- Core Logging
- Sample collection
Environmental Sustainability
- Environmental studies including issues that could hinder the project and existing ecosystem
- Conduct water quality sampling
- Discuss any potential social or community related requirements (consult people or experts in the areas where possible).
Ventilation
- Establishing vent survey stations with proper labels
- Measure airflow including velocity and volume.
- Measure air quality using air quality monitoring instruments (may touch based with environmental engineering department).
- Calculate air quality Index.
- Produce ventilation survey report (plan view of the mine indicating the direction and the quantity of airflow).
Surveying
- Activity: Drifts survey using surveying instruments students used at BCIT.
- Deliverable: Drift profiles, floor and back elevations, 3D drift views, plan view of the mine and drift sections.
Mine Services:
- Collect and assess information on existing mine services, including ventilation, water management, compressed air, and power & sanitary facilities. Determine if these services meet the requirements for reopening the mine. Identify any necessary rehabilitations or additions to comply with industry standards.
Deliverables: Submission of Technical Report and Oral Presentation
1) The team will deliver a completed study report. It must contain all relevant information generated during the study period about the project and the team’s conclusions on its technical.
2) Each team member(s) will be responsible for producing the section of the report relevant to his/her role on the team. Some sections such as costing may require the contributions of ALL team members.
3) You will be required to give an oral presentation on the results of your study to a panel made up of members of BCIT staff and other invitees.
