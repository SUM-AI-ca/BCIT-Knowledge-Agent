### Course Description
COMM 3478 builds on the skills that you learned in COMM 1178 and 2278. You will update your career package for employment as a biomedical engineering technologist. While writing a technical research report for BMET 4403, you will consult with your COMM 3478 instructor for advice on format and organization. Finally, you will write a technical report documenting the device that you will design and build in BMET 4402.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Design and write an effective resume and application letter for a career-specific position.
- Write a BMET 4403 research paper that has a clear structure, documents sources using a standardized format, and is grammatically correct.
- Write a formal technical report documenting the design process and construction of the BMET 4402 project.
- Use critical thinking skills to evaluate research findings to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete.
