Office Hours | In this course you will learn to apply safe welding practices to perform oxy-acetylene welding, cutting, and shielded metal arc welding operations. You will also learn to identify the tools and the procedures to perform the different types of welding processes.
### Course Description
This course introduces students to welding safety, identification of metals, and procedures for oxy-acetylene welding and shielded metal arc welding (SMAW)
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use welding safety practices
- Perform oxy-acetylene welding and cutting procedures
- Perform shielded metal arc welding procedures

### Safety equipment or protective clothing

CSA-approved safety glasses CAN/CSA-Z94.3-92
Cotton coveralls, preferably high-visibility and fire retardant
CSA Standard Z94.2-02 hearing protection devices
### Course Schedule and Assignments
The student will be required to read Trades Common core Line J: Oxyacetylene cut & weld prior to the start of the course, they must pass a safety related theory exam in class before continuing on to the practical portion of the course where they will perform oxy-acetylene welding and cutting procedures as well as shielded metal arc welding procedures
