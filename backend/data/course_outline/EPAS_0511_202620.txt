### Course Description
​This course supports learners in successfully transitioning into their BCIT programs and strengthening the listening and speaking skills needed for effective participation in academic and workplace settings. Learners will participate actively in experiential activities that they will encounter in BCIT studies and the workplace. Intercultural competence and academic study strategies are embedded throughout the course. The course focuses on developing proficiency in English speaking and listening for presentations, group discussions, conversations, meetings, and interviews. Learners will practice expressing detailed ideas clearly and concisely, while adjusting language, tone, and style for different purposes and audiences. Learners will also improve their ability to listen to lectures, presentations, conversations, and talks to identify key information. They will also improve their ability to communicate with others by reflecting on how culture and assumptions can affect interaction. By the end of the course, learners will increase their understanding of CEFR B2–C1 vocabulary and grammatical features and will use B1 and most B2 features accurately in spoken communication.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate understanding of lively, complex conversations, discussions, and arguments, including speakers' viewpoints and attitudes
- Demonstrate understanding of the essential aspects of lectures, talks, and reports and other forms of complex technical academic presentations
- Communicate complex technical academic ideas and concepts clearly and precisely, distinguishing between those that are similar to each other
- Give a clear, well-structured presentation or argument, highlighting significant points and relevant supporting detail. Respond to interruptions and follow-up questions quickly and without difficulty
- Organize a discussion to decide a course of action with a partner or group, reporting on what others have said, summarizing, elaborating, and weighing multiple points of view
- Carry out an effective, fluent interview of a person in his/her target field of study/work, handling spontaneous questions, following up and exploring interesting replies
- Identify differences in sociocultural traditions or styles of communication, critically reflect on them, and adjust own communication accordingly​
