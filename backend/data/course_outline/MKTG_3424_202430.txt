## Course Description
​This course enables learners to create a competitive strategy and the development of a sales and marketing plan for a business. Learners examine demand factors, trends, travel motivation, market segmentation, branding, advertising and promotion, media relations, and sales tactics. Focus in on developing a sustainable competitive advantage for the business. Learning is assessed through a combination of quizzes, exams, in class activities and a project.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop effective public speaking skills by delivering oral presentations (individual and team) to audiences of diverse sizes.
- Conduct an analysis of the macro and microenvironment in which a business operates.
- Develop a competitive strategy based on market conditions, and opportunities.
- Assess research data to make informed decisions.
- Develop strategies and tactics to achieve marketing goals and objectives.
- Prepare a strategic sales and marketing plan.
- Evaluate the relationship between the marketing plan and the budget.
- Apply marketing and business principles to the marketing planning process.​

## Course Goals
What is your organization's marketing strategy? What is your pricing and distribution strategy? Every successful enterprise needs effective marketing strategies to compete in today's complex business environment. This course is designed to provide a detailed overview of the strategic marketing planning process. Students will develop a logical, step-by-step process of building a marketing plan. Cases and discussions will be used to explore strategic marketing. This course is integrated with the Tourism Venture Creation and Managerial Finance courses. The term project will have elements from all three courses.

## Course Specific Requirements
Preparation for Class: This course is composed of a lecture and lab each week where attendance and participation are expected by all students. For each session, students are expected to be prepared. It is each student's responsibility to understand what is required and to complete the readings and other preparation before each lab. Preparation will usually involve readings each on perusall.com. Participation and reflection of the readings each week on purusall.com will be encouraged and evaluated.

Professional Behavior: In order to create a productive and professional learning environment this course follows these guidelines:

- Students are expected to attend all classes and labs, except in the event of illness or some other very good reason for being absent. In such cases, the students must e-mail the instructor as soon as possible to advise the extent and reason for the absence.
- Cell phones should be turned off during lectures and lab.
- Students will refrain from general, non-class related surfing of the net, playing computer games or reading material not related to the session; e-mail, chat or instant messaging. This is an expected professional courtesy to colleagues and instructors
- While casual attire is generally appropriate, students are expected to wear “business casual” attire when we have guest speakers; no shorts or T-shirts. More formal attire is expected for major team presentations unless other apparel is employed for dramatic effect.
- Students are expected to be prepared for each class, which means being able to add value to discussions, general knowledge or opinion-based input will not be sufficient. Students are encouraged to volunteer to speak during class, the instructor will also call on students at random to ask questions or invite opinions.
- Punctuality is extremely important as students who arrive late disturb all others.
- All students are expected to contribute during class discussions in order to enrich the learning experience for everyone. However, individual student should not monopolize or dominate discussions, precluding or intimidating others from contributing. When someone is talking is expected that everyone will listen and not hold parallel conversations
- In general, students are expected to behave as professionals during lecture and lab time.
- Students are encouraged to bring laptops to class. In some cases, students will be asked to go online in small groups to undertake research and prepare for discussion.
- Students are expected to act ethically and with academic integrity at all times.
