## Course Description
This course provides an overview of the major human resource and industrial relations processes and issues in the BC workplace with emphasis on the value of the worker and the overall effectiveness of modern human resource management. Students will develop an understanding of the skills and abilities required for effective interviewing, performance management, compensation reviews, labour contract negotiations, training and development programs, grievance and collective agreement administration. The course also includes an overview of relevant employment laws.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Outline the programs and processes associated with acquiring and maintaining an effective and efficient workforce.
- Analyze the key human resource management issues facing Canadian organizations.
- Explain the generally accepted procedures of major human resource management processes and programs, including recruitment and selection, compensation, and performance management.
- Interpret employment laws and regulations that apply to employers operating in British Columbia.
- Compare and contrast unionized and nonunionized workplaces and the different relationship management strategies that are appropriate to each.
- Assess the implications unionization has on workplace culture, processes, and roles for employees, unions, and employers.
- Describe the primary functions and principles of collective agreements and contract administration.

## Course Goals
HRMG 6010 will prepare students to:

- Analyze the strategic impact of Human Resource Management on achieving organizational goals and driving success.
- Explore how HR aligns workforce strategies with business objectives to enhance performance.
- Understand the legal side of HR and how it helps organizations manage risk.
- Develop practical HR strategies that tackle compliance, risk management, and resilience.
- Apply ethical principles to HR decision-making and planning.
- Build strong written and verbal communication skills for presenting HR strategies.

A Note on AI

In this course, we emphasize the development of critical thinking and decision-making skills through case study analysis. As senior business students, you are embarking on a journey to refine your ability to dissect complex real-world scenarios, identify key issues and formulate well-reasoned solutions. To foster the growth of these skills, it is important that you engage directly with the course and case materials and engage in lab discussions.

Therefore, use your judgement with regard to the use of AI chatbots, such as ChatGPT. These tools can be helpful for summarizing information and aiding with background research but are highly error-prone and are not a substitute for critical thinking, problem solving, or clear and concise writing. Using AI to write assignments for you is plagiarism and is subject to the normal sanctions for academic misconduct (see above).
