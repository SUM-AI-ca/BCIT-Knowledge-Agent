## Course Description
​Describe and apply basic principles of nerve conduction studies to develop nerve conduction techniques for performing routine motor and sensory nerve conduction studies (NCS) and late responses in accordance to minimal technical standards. Emphasis for learning is on basic normal recording theory, techniques and interpretation. Techniques acquired in the lab assignments provide the technologist with introductory skills required to perform NCS as an entry level technologist, performing routine NCS in a laboratory setting under the direction of a registered technologist. Hands on practice in laboratory classes supplement the theoretical learning. Evaluation includes a range of lab and theory assignments, quizzes, and exams.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe normal nerve and muscle anatomy and function as well as disease states that are most commonly encountered in an electrodiagnostic laboratory.
- Review patient’s chart including test requisition form and previous consultation prior to testing.
- Take relevant patient history to determine which limb(s) needs to be examined and what NCS protocol to use.
- Demonstrate patient handling skills by clearly describing the procedure to the patient and addressing patient concerns.
- Demonstrate basic nerve conduction techniques to record reproducible routine motor, sensory and late responses according to the lab protocol.
- Verify correct nerve stimulation by observing stimulator polarity orientation and appropriate muscle contraction.
- Recognize physiological and non-physiological artefacts and take the appropriate steps to eliminate or reduce them.
- Distinguish (not interpret) the difference between normal and abnormal waveform parameters and calculated conduction velocities.
- Demonstrate how to ensure that the laboratory and testing site adheres to Occupational Health and Safety (OH&S) standards.
- Follow appropriate standard precautions and facility policies.
