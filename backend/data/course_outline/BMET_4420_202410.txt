## Course Description
Enables the student to reinforce knowledge and gain practical working experience in the field of biomedical engineering technology. Allows the student to work under supervision in a hospital, medical device manufacturing facility, research and development laboratory, or technical service and support organization. The work experience is five weeks in duration.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Interpret and use information from operation and service documentations (manuals, circuit diagrams, data books, hazard notices, etc.)
- Use measuring instruments and tools appropriate to the situation.
- Perform system analysis to determine component failures and problems.
- Apply quality assurance and safety standards in the practice of Biomedical Engineering technology.
- Explain equipment operation and usage.
- Follow technical instructions and procedures.
- Work effectively within a professional environment.
- Communicate effectively (verbally and in writing).
