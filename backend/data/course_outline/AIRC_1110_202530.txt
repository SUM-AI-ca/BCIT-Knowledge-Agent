### Course Description
​This course provides students with the specific skills and techniques needed to become successful and effective learners. These fundamental concepts, behaviors, and competencies are useful not only in a learning environment but can also be applied in the workplace environment. Evaluation includes a Student Success assignment and examination.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop a personal SMART goal driven action plan for their study success.
- Analyze personal strengths and learning styles.
- Identify effective time and stress management skills.
- Demonstrate research and note-taking skills.
- Plan and use effective exam preparation and study skills.

Attendance In case of illness or other unavoidable cause of absence, the student must communicate as soon as possible with their instructor or Program/Department Head, stating the reason for the absence. Attempts As stated in BCIT Policy 5103 - Student Evaluation , students must successfully complete a course within a maximum of three (3) attempts at the course. Students with two attempts in a single course will be allowed to repeat the course only upon permission from the Associate Dean. Students who have not successfully completed a course within three attempts will not be eligible to graduate from their respective program. For those courses or programs that have Education Council approval, the number of attempts as stated in the evaluation section of the course outline shall apply.
Academic Integrity It is the responsibility of all students to be familiar with the Student Code of Academic Integrity.
