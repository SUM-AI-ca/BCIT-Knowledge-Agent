### Course Description
This course utilizes Excel’s PowerPivot and PowerBI functionality working on skills to model different types of data and how the data is synthesized into management information. In addition to the these skills, this course will dive into the issues related to big data and analyze big data so information can be integrated into the strategic decision-making process by using data visualization tools. Students will be then able to build custom dashboards to answer management problems.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand Data Modelling:
- Structure data gathering to a business need.
- Utilize systems to store, edit, and retrieve required data.
- Determine best practices in data use.
- Discuss ethical and legal considerations in the use of data.
- Understand Data Visualization:
- Build effective data visualizations to communicate meaning through data.
- Determine correct visual tool to analyze multiple data types.
- Perform quantitative and qualitative analyses as appropriate.
- Select tools to effectively convey meaning.
- Understand Power BI:
- Perfom basic application use.
- Describe Business Intelligence (e.g., Online Analytical Processing or OLAP).
- Perform data analysis using MS Power BI toolset.
- Perform time series analysis (forecasting techniques).
- Apply Power BI to Data Mining and Discovery methods.
- Use single and multiple table data sources in Power BI.

Each student is required to submit a Peer Review.
- The act of submission contributes to the submitting student's PARTICIPATION mark.

The Peer Review requires you to evaluate each member of your group (including yourself) based on the following criteria:
Communication & Collaboration

- Contributes ideas and supports them with relevant information.
- Responds promptly and effectively to group messages or emails.
Reliability & Responsiveness
- Completes assigned tasks on time and meets deadlines.
- Demonstrates a strong work ethic and takes responsibility for their role.

Contribution to Group Work
- Provides valuable insights, ideas, and contributions to the group's work.
- Takes an active role in project planning, organization, and execution.
- Demonstrates a willingness to take on additional tasks or responsibilities.
- Supports and encourages other group members.
Professionalism & Respect
- Maintains a professional and respectful attitude toward group members.
- Upholds ethical standards and treats others with fairness and integrity.
- Resolves conflict constructively and seeks common ground.

You will complete the workbook provided to provide a 0-4 rating for each criterion.
- 0 = Inadequate
- 1 = Needs Improvement
- 2 = Satisfactory
- 3 = Good
- 4 = Excellent

The peer review calculates the re-distribution of effort based on peer feedback and adjusts student grades appropriately. Peer review calculations will be provided to students upon request. All peer-review data will remain anonymous. If you are having significant challenges with a group member during the course, it is your responsibility to try to remedy the situation. If you cannot, you must let your instructor know immediately. Waiting to bring up concerns until the end of term Peer Review limits the ability for a solution to be found.
