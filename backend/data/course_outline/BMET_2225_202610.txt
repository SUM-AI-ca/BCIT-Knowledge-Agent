### Course Description
This course will offer a number of workshops and laboratory sessions in which students are introduced to a range of practical skills that are relevant to the profession and which do not fit into the context of other academic courses. Each topic will be introduced with basic theory, and explored with hands-on skill development exercises that focus on selection of appropriate materials and tools, proficiency and safety. General skills: Correct use of appropriate lubricants and cleaners; Correct use of appropriate adhesives and sealants; Repair and maintenance of pneumatic and hydraulic components and (sub)systems; Systematic disassembly and reassembly of mechanical assemblies; Data analysis and graphing. Special skills associated with handling specific biomedical devices such as:Centrifuge, microtome; Optic components, microscopes.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Select appropriate tools and materials required to maintain typical biomedical equipment.
- Use equipment and materials safely.
- Demonstrate general skills above with adequate proficiency:
- Use systematic methods to disassemble devices in order to facilitate efficient reassembly.
- Correctly reassemble a previously disassembled device.
- Demonstrate the special skills required to handle the equipment listed above.
