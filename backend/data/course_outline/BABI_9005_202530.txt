### Course Description
This course covers the theory of Business Data Analytics, and prepares students to write the Certification in Business Data Analytics (CBDA) exam from the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA).
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify business research questions.
- Source data.
- Analyze data.
- Interpret and report Results.
- Use results to influence business decision making.
- Guide company-level strategy for Business Analytics.
- Prepare to write the IIBA CBDA certification exam.
