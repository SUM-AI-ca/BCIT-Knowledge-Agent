## Course Description
This course presents the study of factors that either influence or are influenced by people at work. Focuses on macro factors such as organizational structure, technology and environment; group factors such as group dynamics, leadership, conflict, change and decision-making; and individual factors such as personality, attitudes, perception and motivation.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Apply group behaviour concepts to achieve team outcomes.
- Illustrate the roles of perception, personality, emotions, attitudes and values.
- Compare and contrast motivation theories.
- Differentiate leadership styles and theories.
- Explain the impact of power and politics in the workplace.
- Demonstrate negotiation styles and conflict management techniques.
- Use decision making and problem solving models.
- Analyze various communication techniques and tools.
- Integrate appropriate organizational concepts to facilitate organizational change.
- Apply open systems and contingency theories to understand and determine effective behaviour in a variety of organizational situations.

## Course Specific Requirements
Discussion topics: These will be explained in the online discussion forum (Go to the activities tab/discussion). It is suggested that you schedule the same time to post your discussion early in the week and, a second time, comment on your classmates' posts. Each topic/week will open on Sat. at 8 AM, and you are expected to have your assignment posted no later than noon on the following Friday, with peer commenting to be made before the 8 PM cut-off.

Once the course is completed, four posts from the various topics will be evaluated as shown below. You will have the opportunity to select 2 of the posts that will be assessed, and I will choose the other two randomly. ALL posts must be completed. Missing a topic’s release time means a zero on that specific discussion (also classified as not attended). You may prepare the discussion in Word, then paste it back into the board before the window closes. Individual submissions are due by NOON, and peer commenting is before the 8 PM cut-off for the week. See the schedule, activities tab, and class activities each week.

Contribution Level One (50-60%): Basic sharing of your thoughts on the question in 2 or 3 paragraphs for a given post; the minimal level expected. This mark will be reduced proportionally if the post is incomplete (content and comments).

Contribution Level Two (60-70%): Level One includes additional use of course concepts and theories closely tied to your posts. Language use and knowledge are critical!

Contribution Level Three (70-80%): Level Two and with deeper discussion with peers above the minimum requirements

Contribution Level Three (80-90%+): Levels One + Two +3 and includes the recommendation of URLs or business articles that you have found related to the topic of discussion.

Instructor feedback/comments: Once the topic has closed, I will use the newsfeed for collective class feedback and email for personalized feedback when available.
