### Course Description
This course is designed to provide your team with the training needed to both develop and maintain a HACCP Plan, an essential component of a food safety system, that is appropriate for your establishment and meets Canadian regulatory requirements. The team will be taken through the 12 steps and 7 principles of HACCP by working with a product and process from your facility. The preventive controls (or Good Manufacturing Practices (GMPs) needed to support your HACCP Plan will also be reviewed. Hazards associated with the product and process will be identified after which the team will be trained to assess their likelihood and severity. From this examination, the CCPs (critical control points) are determined. For each CCP, the team learns how to identify validated critical limits; establish monitoring, corrective action and verification procedures; and to develop the needed documentation, including Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), Work Instructions and Forms, for these procedures. Finally, strategies for implementing and maintaining the HACCP Plan and food safety system are addressed. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain why HACCP Plans are a necessary component of a food safety system.
- Describe the preventive controls or GMPs that are needed to support the HACCP Plan and state why each is important.
- Develop a product description, process flow, and traffic flow diagram for a selected product and process.
- Perform a hazard analysis, assess the likelihood and severity for each identified hazard and determine CCPs.
- Establish appropriate monitoring, corrective action and verification procedures through the development of SOPs, Work Instructions and Forms.
- Identify procedures for implementing and maintaining HACCP Plans and food safety systems.​
