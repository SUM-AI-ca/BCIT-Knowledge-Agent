### Course Description
​This course applies the concepts of physics to geomatics technology. It covers simple machines, gyroscopes, waves and their relationship to distance measurement, hydrographic surveying, basic electronics and magnetism, and electronic distance measurement. Students will learn the topics through lectures and hands-on labs.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply basic concepts of mechanics to the determination of theoretical and actual mechanical advantage of various simple machines.
- Apply the theory of thermal expansion, stress-strain relationships in materials, and pressure-density relationships to calibration of instruments.
- Apply concepts of rotational motion to the understanding of gyroscopes and gyrocompasses.
- Use wave concepts to explain distance measurement in both land- and water-based surveys.
- Analyze basic electric and magnetic forces.
- Use Ohm's Law in the analysis of simple electric circuits.
- Describe the process of electric distance measurement for both lasers and satellite positioning.
- Apply wave principles to solve problems relating to sound.
- Describe the region of the electromagnetic spectrum and how interference and polarization work.
- Apply simple harmonic motion principles to solve problems relating to mechanical oscillators.
- Apply the theory of work and energy to solve problems involving work, energy and their transformations.
- Describe the function of ground-penetrating radar (GPR).​
