## Course Description
​The objective of this course is to develop student's ability to teach the secondary school Automotive Technology curriculum. A major emphasis will focus on the problems encountered in teaching a mechanics curriculum to secondary school students. This course will cover past, present and future trends in the design, construction, mechanical and support system functions of the automobile.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate the personal confidence to guide and assist secondary school students in learning automotive service and repair procedures.
- Demonstrate the necessary supporting knowledge and understanding of how mechanical, hydraulic and electronic systems in automobiles function and interact.
- Demonstrate a concern and responsibility for the environment by appropriately recycling and disposing of waste materials.
- Describe how gasoline and diesel-fuelled vehicles affect the environment.
- Explain how a vehicle's impact on the environment can be reduced.
- Develop appropriate, paper-based and digital learning materials suitable for inexperienced secondary school students.
- Demonstrate the habits of a safe, independent, and cooperative worker in the automotive shop environment.
- Access and effectively use paper and web-based service literature.
