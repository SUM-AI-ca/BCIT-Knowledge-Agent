### Course Description
Topics covered include heat, wave phenomena, electrostatics, electric circuits, magnetism and electromagnetic induction. This course is equivalent to general level second term Physics courses at BCIT.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Apply the concepts of calorimetry to compute temperature changes, heat loss/gain in conduction and radiation processes, thermal expansion and thermal stress in technological contexts.
- Demonstrate how vibrations and wave concepts apply, predict modes of vibration in simple systems, and explain the use of sound waves in non-destructive testing.
- Verify Ohm's Law for DC current and voltage and compute resistivity.
- Analyze electric and magnetic forces and simple electric circuits.
### Learning Resources
Textbook modules, laboratory manuals, lecture slides and videos will be made available via the Learning Hub
You will need a scientific calculator for exams, and access to Microsoft Excel or an equivalent program for data analysis for labs.
### Course Goals
At the end of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe simple circuit components, and predict the behaviour of a circuit containing these components
- Calculate electric and magnetic forces and fields for simple systems
- Describe the connection between energy and heat, and the mechanisms of heat transfer
- Describe quantitative properties of waves and use them to analyze simple systems
- Explain the connections between electricity, heat, waves, and mechanics and synthesize this information to solve problems.
- Make and interpret graphs of relationships between physical variables
- Write laboratory reports, complete with the analysis of data and the prediction of mathematical relationships.

### Course topics
Unit 1: Heat and Heat Transfer
- temperature
- specific heat capacity
- sensible and latent heating
- phase diagrams
- first law of thermodynamics
- linear expansion
- the mole
- gas laws
- the ideal gas law
- thermal conduction
- r-value
- thermal radiation and emissivit
Unit 2: Waves
- wave properties
- equation of a harmonic wave
- phase
- Huygens’ Principle
- refraction
- diffraction qualitatively
- reflection
- intensity
- inverse square law
- superposition
- AM and FM signals
- standing waves
- waves on a string
- sound speed
- Doppler Effect
- decibels
Unit 3.1: Electrostatics
- electric charge
- charging by conduction and induction
- Coulomb’s Law
- electric fields
- Gauss’ Law
- electric potential difference and potential
- electrical energy storage
- capacitance
- parallel plate capacitors
- dielectrics
- energy stored in capacitors
- energy stored in batteries
- emf
Unit 3.2: Circuits
- voltage, current, resistance
- Ohm’s Law
- resistivity
- basic circuits
- series and parallel circuits
- Kirchhoff’s Laws and network analysis
- Basic electrical safety
Unit 4: Magnetism and Induction
- properties of magnets
- magnetic fields
- magnetic force on moving charges and the Lorentz relationship
- magnetic fields due to currents
- Ampère’s Law
- solenoids
- Faraday’s Law
- AC electric generators
- inductance
- inductors
