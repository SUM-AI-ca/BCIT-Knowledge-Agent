### Course Description
In this course, students will apply skills and knowledge developed over the course of the FWR program to assess ecosystems and management options at various landscape scales. Students will be asked to consider multiple perspectives (Indigenous, government, community, applied science, industry) that influence ecosystem stewardship and management decisions. Content covered in the course will include ecosystem functions at the watershed and site levels.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Assess ecosystems at the appropriate scale for protection, management, and restoration using the appropriate tools and information.
- Compare and contrast Indigenous and non‐Indigenous conservation and stewardship values.
- Integrate concepts of hydrology, forest health, ecosystem functions, and fish and wildlife values for a specific site or area.
- Describe ecosystems using adaptive systems theories, including resilience and redundancy.
- Examine and interpret laws and regulations that govern land use, management, and stewardship at a site and watershed level.
- Demonstrate respectful and professional behaviour that meets industry standards and expectations​.
