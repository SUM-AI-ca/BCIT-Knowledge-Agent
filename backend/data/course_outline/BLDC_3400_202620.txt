### Course Description
Through a simulated bid closing and submission of a bid, students will apply bidding procedures, the measurement, pricing, and preparation of an estimate summary for specific items of work, construction cost accounting, requests for subcontractor quotations and subcontractor bid analyses.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate the importance of subcontracting work, subtrades and scope of their work.
- Select, measure and price appropriate Div. 1 – General Requirements pertaining to temporary site facilities and relevant project-related administrative and procedural requirements.
- Develop rational head office overhead and profit percentages for inclusion in a bid price.
- Prepare and submit a competitive Tender for a simulated Bid Closing.
- Discern relevant information from multiple sources and select the applicable data to include in the Tender price.
### Learning Resources
Completion of all courses in the Certificate in Construction Estimating Program is required; course materials from those courses plus the latest edition of the CIQS Method of Measurement of Construction Works is recommended.
