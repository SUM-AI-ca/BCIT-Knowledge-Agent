## Course Description
Directed Studies enables the students to propose an area of study that would lend itself to increased learning through the exploration of study in Selected Topics areas. This Technical Elective course of study may vary from term to term and may include subjects such as: technology and design; environmental ethics; Studies abroad, architectural workshops; Evaluation, Communication and Monitoring of Project deliveries. The student must submit a Statement of Intent which is to be reviewed and approved by the Program Head prior to the student's ability to register for this elective.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Ability to chose a selected area of study currently unavailable in the program.

Enhances the diversity of learning experiences.

Teaches the students effective specific management skills and critical thinking that would apply to their area of interest.

Emphasizes breadth of learning: encouraging cross-disciplinary approaches, ideas, and solutions, as well as discussing connections, comparisons, and continuities between and among fields of knowledge and technologies.

The course requires that students research a specific topic area outside of the required course materials, in order to cultivate research methodology skills. Students may also be required to present their ideas and research findings in an accepted academic format (e.g. research paper/report) which includes proper citation of sources.

The student may explore and participate in diverse socio-cultural learning opportunities related to their area of study (Studies Abroad).

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance is mandatory during all ARSC classes, presentations and learning events. Students are expected to be present for any and all learning activities scheduled during ARSC program hours (8:30-5:30 Monday to Friday). Graded course work is conducted during this time and may be impossible to make up. Partial attendance will be considered cumulative absence. Poor attendance may render the student unable to complete the course.

In case of absence, course instructor/s and Program Head should be notified with a minimum 24 hours notice unless medically unavoidable. Documentation for the date of absence is to be provided via email within one week's time. In case of undocumented absence the following protocol will be followed:

1) first undocumented absence will receive a warning from instructor

2) second undocumented absence will be referred to program head

3) third undocumented absence will be referred to AD

## Course Specific Requirements
Students must complete and submit all assignments. Failure to complete and submit each assignment will result in a final grade of Incomplete. Late submissions will have 10% of grade deducted per day.

This course may include mandatory industry events &/ resources. An additional cost of up to $200 may incur.

A key element of this course is to develop a professional presentation of self on all class platforms and in all interactions. Students are required to familiarize and prepare themselves on an ongoing basis with emerging best practices for hybrid work, as some learning activities may be conducted online. This is an important self directed element of this course.
