## Course Description
This course is intended to provide students with practical experience in conducting house inspections. The course is comprised of a combination of site visits, in class reviews and preparation for the field inspections. The field training is intended to enable the students to learn and improve their skills in conducting residential house inspections.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Apply theory to practice when conducting a home inspection for a client.
- Report safety concerns and apply general awareness of good building practice.
- Acquire information relating to time management and professional conduct.
- Apply effective communication and make appropriate recommendations.

## Course Goals
The course goals are to provide the following: Give students practical experience through fieldtrips along with in class lectures intended for conducting a typical house inspection. The students will also learn the necessary steps to prepare and present a report to their client. The course will also assist the students in preparing for the mentorship training that is required for House Inspection Licensing as per Consumer Protection B.C. (CPBC).

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance is required for the fieldtrips in order to complete the assignments. Student late for the Fieldtrip will be deducted 5 Marks. Students late more then 20 minutes to the Fieldtrip will not be allowed to attend the Fieldtrip resulting in Grade 0.

## Course Specific Requirements
For the fieldtrips the student will need: a Flashlight, Camera, Electrical Tester and a clip board

It is also reccommended to have and bring a reporting system that the students plan to use for their mentorship and later when working in the industry

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Material Covered: Date
Class 1: Getting started: Standards of Practice, Contracts, Reports, Setting up the Inspection, Arriving at Site, Leaving the Inspection & Site Safety,: Jan 9
Class 2: Fieldtrip 1: Inspect the Property &Generate a Short Narrative Report from the Inspection: Jan 11
Class 3: The Inspection Method: Sequence of a typical Inspection, In Class Test: Jan 16
Class 4: Fieldtrip 2: Inspect the Property & Generate a Checklist of Observations Found the Exterior of the House: Jan 18
Class 5: Documentation: Selecting a Reporting System, Filling out the Report, Use of Photos in a Report, Legal Ramifications of the Report, Who may Read your Report: Jan 23
Class 6: Fieldtrip 3: Inspect the Property & Generate a Checklist of Observations Found on the Interior of the House: Jan 25
Class 7: Interacting with the Various Party's at the Property: Owner, Client, Realty Agents, Builder and Others. Starting Your Own Business. In Class Test: Jan 30
Class 8: Fieldtrip 4: Inspect the Property & Generate a Checklist of the Observations for the Entire House: Feb 1
Class 9: Presenting Your Report to the Client: Who Should be Present, Common Questions Asked, Dealing with Call Backs/Client Complaints: Feb 6
Class 10: Fieldtrip 5: Inspect the Property & Generate a Report that Includes a Checklist, Photo's and a Narrative Summary (A Client Ready Report): Feb 8
Class 11: Condominium Inspections, Other Types of Inspections, Role Play :(Inspector & Client): Feb 13
Class 12: Fieldtrip 6: Return to Property of Class 5 Fieldtrip to Review Findings & Report: Feb 15
