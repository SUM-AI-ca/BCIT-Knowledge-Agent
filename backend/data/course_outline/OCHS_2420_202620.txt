### Course Description
Introduces legislation regulating hazardous materials used and transferred in the workplace and the environment. Fully explores workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) and Transportation of Dangerous Goods (TDG) requirements. Investigates lead abatement and asbestos management and control options for their workplace application.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Recognize hazardous materials and emergency situations in the workplace.
- Mark recommendations to reduce those hazards through preparation, analysis, program implementation, and training.
- Deliver effective, skills-oriented training sessions using a variety of techniques.
- Apply WHMIS, TDG, asbestos, and lead legislation to the workplace.
- Prepare dangerous goods for road, rail, air, and marine transport.
- Liaise with local, provincial, and federal services during training activities and chemical emergencies.
- Manage asbestos and lead abatement projects and describe the procedures involved in both processes.
