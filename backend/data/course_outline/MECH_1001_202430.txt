## Course Description
​​This course provides a general overview of the mechanical engineering profession and the mechanical engineering programs at BCIT. Additional introductory lecture topics are presented in study techniques, documenting engineering problem solutions, teamwork, standards and codes, and engineering ethics. Lab sessions will provide individual and team assignments in support of these topics.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Implement efficient study techniques for engineering courses.
- Recognize the importance of teamwork in academic and engineering environments.
- Practice safe working procedures in laboratories.
- Describe the Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS).
- Document solutions to engineering problems.
- Define how mechanical engineers and technologists integrate with other engineering fields.
- Understand process of accreditation and its role in quality assurance​

## Course Goals
This course is intended to be the starting point for all Mechanical Engineering streams. It will cover the concepts, engineering methods, and common tools used by mechanical engineers while introducing the students to a few interesting devices. Students will be introduced to the school of energy (SOE) at BCIT and will be exposed to topics such as time management and problem-solving techniques in engineering. The objective of the course is to promote student success in their educational pathway through mechanical engineering diploma or degree streams.

## Course topics
Upon successful completion, the student will: • Be familiar with basics of mechanical engineering in BCIT and possible options for pursuing their studies in the SOE. • Develop an understanding of resources, course contents, curriculums, evaluation processes and faculty relations. • Gain a preliminary understanding of individual and teamwork ethics in engineering studies and fundamentals of academic integrity obligations at BCIT. • Be introduced to basics of time management and problem-solving techniques. • Learn basic safety regulations at BCIT and obtain Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) certification.
