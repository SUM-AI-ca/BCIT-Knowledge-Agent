## Course Description
This course is designed to provide the forensic investigator the concepts of case management and the application of case management methods in organizing investigative teams, planning investigative actions and analyzing evidence and intelligence.

## Learning Resources
Course material will be provided by the instructor.

## Course Goals
1. Explain the elements of case management in criminal investigations.

2. Apply case management methods in criminal investigations.

3. Develop templates for case management scenarios.

4. Analyze and manage conventional and complex criminal investigations.

5. Describe the capabilities, limitations, use, access and methods of various case management systems.

6. Apply the fundamentals of disclosure requirements in terms of Charter of Rights and due process.

7. Describe the interface of police techniques and Incident Command.

The assignments/presentations are selected by each team. The format is to demonstrate how each team will implement major case management skills, processes, and tools in completing an investigation, applying the course materials.
