### Course Description
​The course guides the student towards the development of achievable research objectives to address a research problem or gap, and propose suitable methodologies, with consideration of resources available and practical limitations.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop achievable research objectives to address a research problem with consideration of limitations.
- Propose suitable methodologies through the literature review, with consideration of resources available.
### Learning Resources
There are no mandatory textbooks for this course. Students are expected to have their own laptops and to co-ordinate with their research supervisors on obtaining the necessary materials and resources to complete their research.
