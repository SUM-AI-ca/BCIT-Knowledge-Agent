## Course Description
This course introduces students to the elements of radio documentary production. During the semester students will work on researching, interviewing, writing, sound-gathering techniques and producing effective proposals. They will assess effective long-form productions. Students will produce a number of small assignments and work with a partner to produce a final six to seven minute final radio documentary.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Assess the elements of a longer form radio production.
- Use research skills to organize documentary content.
- Demonstrate effective field recording and interview techniques.
- Use performance skills to enhance long-form production.
- Use effective entry-level audio production techniques.
- Write a persuasive documentary proposal.
- Write and produce 6-7 minute radio documentary with a partner.
