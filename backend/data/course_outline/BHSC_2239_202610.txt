### Course Description
Integrating concepts from psychology, sociology, and interpersonal relations, students learn to respectfully address the psychosocial needs of patients and collaborate successfully with other members of the health care team. Empathy and interpersonal communications skills are emphasized; as these provide the basis for crucial employability skills, such as managing stress in both self and patients, reducing the frequency and intensity of conflict, and working effectively and respectfully with specific populations and those from other cultures. Responsible, professional and ethical conduct is underscored. Weekly lectures integrate clinical scenarios, role plays, and class discussions. Active participation and engagement with material is expected. Evaluation of learning is based a range of assignments, examinations, and active participation.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Apply concept of empathy to create respectful and supportive relations with patients and co-workers.
- Recognize and adapt communication based on the individual needs and nonverbal communication of patients and colleagues.
- Integrate effective interpersonal communication skills in order to foster rapport and trust.
- Use basic active listening skills to obtain information from patients and/or support persons.
- Identify types of conflict in the workplace and employ appropriate conflict management techniques to maintain a professional work environment.
- Perform various types of assertion according to appropriateness and desired result.
- Develop strategies for giving and receiving feedback that emphasize accountability, respect, and professional growth.
- Develop strategies to work with cultural and developmental differences that impact interactions and treatment in a clinical setting.
- Explain common difficulties encountered by those with disabilities, and approaches for working with such individuals both sensitively and respectfully.
- Demonstrate empathy and sensitivity to the diversity of individuals, including those with head injuries, mental health conditions, substance use, and those who are grieving.
- Explain the impact of stress and factors that generate or increase stress.
- Develop an individualized plan to cope with stress, burnout, grief, and loss in the healthcare environment.

### Course Specific Requirements
Emotional Intelligence Reflections (20%) : Students will complete an emotional intelligence questionnaire and provide a brief reflection of their experience taking the questionnaire and the outcome of the measure. Students will be asked to identify one or two actions they will commit to taking to improve their emotional intelligence, Midway through the course, they will complete a second reflection discussing the impact of their efforts to improve emotional intelligence via the skills they have previously identified implementing.
Exams (Midterm 20%, Final 30%): Exams will be administered during class time on the date and time specified in the course outline. Missed Exams: Failure to take an exam at the scheduled time will result in a grade of “0” for that exam unless a physician’s note is provided or other arrangements were made with the instructor prior to the day of the exam. The instructor reserves the right to utilize an alternative assessment method (which may be different from the original) in the case of a missed exam.
Presentation (20%) + Peer Review (5%) : Students will complete a short presentation in small groups. Groups will be assigned by the instructor. More information about the presentation will be provided in class. Students will review peer presentations and complete peer reviews utilizing course-related feedback skills and demonstrating professional and respectful communication.
Participation (5%) : Participation marks are awarded at the end of the term at the discretion of the Instructor. Criteria considered when determining this mark include:
Attendance | Please contact instructor if you are unable to attend class. Students are expected to attend all classes, arrive on time, and stay for the entire class. Missed classes without prior discussion/approval will impact your participation mark.
Invitation to Engage and Contribute | Demonstrate your engagement by offering your perspective or answering questions about class materials.
Role-Plays/ Group Work | Full participation in role plays. activities, discussions, and group work is required for this class.
Questions | Demonstrate active listening by asking questions of peers during class sessions and posing questions for the instructor.
Contribution | Offer your perspective and/or answer questions about class materials. Take the discussion to the next level.
Respectful Conduct | Some topics may be sensitive and/or invite debate of different perspectives. Disrespectful language and/or conduct will not be permitted. I encourage conversation that is respectful, supportive, inclusive, curious and sensitive to others.
Use of Skills | Demonstration of skills we have learned in class indicates you are able to apply your learning in a real-life interactive setting.
Preparation for Class | Prepare for class by doing your readings ahead of time; it will be easier to absorb and discuss materials discussed in class.
Use of Electronics | Please refrain from using your phone or computers for non-course related things during class time. Be mindful of the nonverbal messages being communicated through the presence/use of technology during classtime.
