## Course Description
A first line management course for people responsible for staff training; members of human resource departments contemplating a training program, or who are involved with on-the-job training will be particularly interested. Develops a solid grounding in current training methodology, techniques and aids. Topics include learning theory, determining training needs, writing objectives, designing training programs using outside resources and evaluation.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of the course participants will be able to:

- Describe methods for conducting training needs assessments.
- Explain the principles of adult learning.
- Formulate objectives for a training session.
- Select appropriate instructional methods.
- Design a training session.
- Use audiovisual supports.
- Deliver a training session.
- Provide feedback to peers.
- Learn to accept feedback from peers.
- Describe techniques for managing group interactions.
- Describe strategies for creating an inclusive training environment.
- Describe processes for assessing training sessions.

## Attendance Requirements
Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from PTS courses is available at https://www.bcit.ca/pts/pts_withdrawals.shtml.

## Course Specific Requirements
Learning Design and Evaluation (LDE) Template - During this course, you will engage with the concepts discussed through hands-on experience using the Learning Design and Evaluation (LDE) template provided by the instructor.

Graded Outputs - There will be four graded assignments in this course. These will be evaluated based on a rubric, which will be reviewed and explained on the first day of class.

Weekly Discussion Posts - Each week, you will participate in discussion posts focused on reflection questions about training and development. These reflections will explore how training and development impact the learning experience of participants.
