## Course Description
​This course provides the opportunity to develop a complete communications plan from the situation analysis to creative solutions. It reviews the role of strategic planning as it applies to media, creative, sales promotion and research, and examines those areas of promotion where each fit in the promotional mix and when would each best be applied.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe a model of the Integrated Marketing Communications process. Explain the steps in developing a marketing communications program.
- Explain how the various decisions of the marketing mix influence and interact with advertising and promotional strategy.
- Explain the importance of advertising and promotion objectives. Differentiate between marketing objectives and communication objectives. Use a comprehensive framework for setting IMC objectives.
- Define advertising creativity. Describe the creative strategy planning process. Assess the effectiveness of various types of message appeals and sources.
- Identify three key decisions for creative tactics. Assess the effectiveness of various creative execution styles.
- Explain the role and functions of specialized marketing communications organizations.
- Explain the reasons for measuring promotional program effectiveness. Evaluate alternative methods for measuring promotional program effectiveness.
- Develop a complete communications plan.
- Describe the advertising regulation system in Canada. Evaluate the ethical perspectives of advertising. Assess the social effects of advertising. Explain the economic role of advertising and its effects on consumer choice, competition, and product costs and prices.
- Cultivate proactive groupthink conduct such as: responsibility, accountability and professionalism that foster team spirit and success.

## Course Goals
This course is online and is asynchronous - there will be no scheduled lectures that students must attend, but there are deadlines for coursework and team assignments.

MKTG 2202 will build on course material covered in MKTG 1102.

MKTG 2202 will provide an opportunity to develop a complete integrated marketing communications plan from the situation analysis and creative brief to developing and presenting creative solutions to the client. The course reviews the role of strategic planning as it applies to media, development of creative solutions, advertising, sales promotion and research and examines those areas of promotion where each of these elements might best fit in the promotional mix. Students will develop, through sequenced deliverables, a strategic Integrated Marketing Communications (IMC) plan in a team-based environment.

Deliverables for the IMC Plan (Team Project):

The deliverables for the IMC plan, the IMC Plan and the pitch to the client are all team-oriented, and students must cooperate and be willing and able to participate fully in a team-centric environment.

Toward the end of week #1, you will be assigned to a small group to work within for the rest of the term. Your team will assume the role of a small but agile, full-service integrated marketing communications agency that's eagerly looking for new business.

Your agency has decided to target one of three companies (Aritzia, TELUS, Sephora). You'll need to pick a product or service or line of products or line of services that the selected company offers and come up with an idea, angle or unique selling point that you can use to develop a creative strategy and marketing campaign that will help your chosen company market that product or service successfully and cause the company to hire your team as the advertising agency of record (AOR).

As part of the campaign development process, you'll need to complete a series of deliverables that will serve as building blocks to creating an IMC plan and to developing and creating a pitch (presentation) to the company you want as a client.

All deliverables, mock-ups, and storyboards, along with your group's oral (pitch) presentation to the client (me) and the written integrated marketing communications (IMC) plan, will be graded.

Students assigned to a group must work within that group to complete all the deliverables, the video pitch presentation, and the IMC plan, due between 12:01 AM on July 14 and 11:59 PM on July 19 (week 13). Late papers and presentations will not be accepted.

Please note that individual students are not guaranteed a full team mark for the project if the individual has not fully participated in all related group discussions, didn't share in the workload or did not satisfactorily complete or provide their input for the project in a timely, professional manner. Teams can request a peer review in which all team members are evaluated. Upon review of the peer evaluation results, the instructor can downgrade the team's assigned group mark for any individual on the team who has failed to meet the group's standards satisfactorily. You must pass the group term project in order to pass MKTG 2202.

Students must cooperate and be willing and able to participate fully in a team-centric environment. You must be willing and able to put in the same level of work, effort and commitment as your fellow group members. You cannot expect others to carry your weight. If you do, your grade will be severely reduced, and you may fail this course.

All absences must be immediately communicated to your fellow team members and the instructor. Don't drop out of sight suddenly or become hard to reach. Please ensure that your teammates are able to contact you. Consider exchanging mobile numbers, and email addresses, and utilize WhatsApp or whatever platform will best enable everyone to be in touch.
