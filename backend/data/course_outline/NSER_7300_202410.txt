## Course Description
Nurses develop and begin to enact their role in emergency nursing by exploring an emergency environment and assessing influences pertaining to patient and family health. Building upon theory and frameworks from the preceding courses, nurses focus on developing comprehensive assessment skills and applying this knowledge in the care of patients and families. Effective clinical decision making in the context of common, less acute patient presentations is another focus of this clinical experience. In addition, nurses will develop and foster collaborative relationships with patients, families, colleagues, instructors, and other health care providers. The development of critical thinking, systematic inquiry, and communication skills will be emphasized throughout this course.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
As a healthcare practitioner, you are expected to follow professional standards of practice throughout all SN programs and courses. In this course, you will have opportunities to use the processes of critical thinking, systematic inquiry, communication, collaboration, leadership, professionalism, and clinical decision-making. Upon successful completion of this course, you should be able to:

- Perform comprehensive emergency nursing assessments on patients experiencing non-complex health challenges.
- Use various sources of knowledge to determine the significance and relevance of assessment findings.
- Articulate assessment findings (both verbally and in writing) in a comprehensive and systematic manner.
- Demonstrate clinical decision making that integrates both appropriate sources of data and the unique perspective of patients and families in emergency care settings.
- Develop and utilize interpersonal skills in order to establish partnerships with patients, families and other health care workers.
- Demonstrate growth in critical thinking skills through reflection on personal practice and learning.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
To achieve a satisfactory grade in NSER 7300, participants must meet the following criteria for clinical competency and complete the following assignments. Criteria for Clinical Competency

- Consistently and independently apply a comprehensive, systematic approach to the process of patient assessment and data analysis in the context of stable patients.
- Demonstrate effective diagnostic and therapeutic reasoning skills in relation to identification of and intervention for patient problems.
- Care for patients in emergency settings in ways that promote connection with patients as unique individuals.
- Communicate salient information in a concise, timely, effective manner in emergency settings.
- Translate classroom acquired skills to the emergency context.
- In the context of stable patient presentations understand common challenges in relation to airway, breathing, and circulation issues.
- Be able to provide care for a minimum of two stable patients independently.

Assignment Details Assignment #1 Case Presentations (Oral) Participants present a case scenario derived from clinical experiences in the emergency setting. Content

- Presentations should include:
- information pertaining to patient and presenting health care concerns are thorough and accurate, i.e., history, assessment, etc.
- patient’s perception and meaning of health and emergency experience are explored.
- use of appropriate sources of knowledge as evidenced by the ability to determine the significance and relevance of data.
- factors influencing clinical decision making, clearly articulated and critically analyzed.
- an understanding of the emergency environment and the role of emergency nurses.

Process

- Presents material in an interactive format.
- Presents knowledge/ideas in an integrated fashion.
- Uses a variety of sources of information to inform presentation.
- Addresses both physical and emotional components of patient case.

Assignment # 2 Course Participation Students are expected to participate fully in the course. This includes engaging in learning activities such as journals, post-conference presentations, and independent research.
