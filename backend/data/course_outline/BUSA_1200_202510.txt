### Course Description
Business Concepts provides broadcast students with a foundation of language and understanding of issues in business, economic and geo-politics. Through lecture, reading, discussion and application, students will gain a foundational knowledge and an understanding of a broad range of business and economics topics and concepts. The course will address strategic planning, operational planning, leading, organizing and controlling for performance. Through actual business scenarios, cases and exercises, students gain experience in decision making and applying theory to real world organizations.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Explain the major economic systems.
- Define major economic indicators.
- Analyze economic indicators and economic data.
- Explain concepts in international business.
- Discuss current issues in international business.
- Explain the forms of business and discuss the pros and cons of each.
- Describe the four functions of management.
- Explain the role of accounting in business.
- Analyze a balance sheet and income statement.
- Discuss issues in business ethics and social responsibility.
- Understand the role of information systems in business.
- Explain the function of human resources management.

### Guidelines for School of Business + Media

Students are responsible for communicating with instructors proactively about both potentially approved and unexcused absences.
