## Course Description
Through the practice & study of Computer applications, this subject area endeavors to develop the Student's: (1) Familiarity with the operation of the personal computer. (2) Use of the various software packages, typically used in the design & construction industry. (3) CAD applications in the context of architectural drafting & design projects. Presentation skills & opportunity to enhance manual skills acquired in previous course materials.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Prepare professional-quality drawings, using AutoCad, & graphic conventions standard to the industry.
- Integrate the use of the computer & software applications into projects, assignments, & presentations.
- Use the computer to use create 3-D documents.
- Use the computer as a creative problem-solving tool when developing & communicating design.
- Use the computer as an analytical & management tool.
- Employ graphic techniques to critically; study, review, and judge alternative concepts.
