### Course Description
​The Mass Timber Connections and Constructability course offers participants practical experience in installing, designing, and repairing mass timber connections. Through an intensive practicum utilizing BCIT's Connections and Constructability Hub—a modular, mobile mass timber mockup structure—participants will assess various connection options, select suitable and buildable connections using detailed drawings, and install a range of connection types. The practicum also provides opportunities to encounter and address common challenges associated with connections, as well as develop effective repair techniques. By the end of the course, participants will gain a deeper understanding of how mass timber materials behave during construction, enabling them to make informed decisions regarding sequencing, tolerances, and the influence of connection design on installation.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the participants will be able to:
- Accurately identify commonly used connections in the industry and describe their key strengths.
- Describe the considerations for connections design, including strength and stability of the connection, as well as speed, esthetic and tolerances, from both a manufacturer and installer perspective.
- Evaluate connection choices from multiple options; describe the impact of connection choices on installation speed and amount of work; and select an appropriate, constructible connection based on detailed drawings.
- Describe how materials react during installation and apply their understanding of the material properties of MT to forecast issues and anticipate adjustments.
- Install a variety of connections effectively, including wood to wood, wood to steel, and wood to concrete connections; troubleshoot common connection challenges and adjust tolerances.
- Identify considerations for constructability from a manufacturer and installer perspective.
- Contribute meaningfully to constructability review for a MT project, and identify detailing considerations, tolerances based on 2D and 3D drawings. ​

### Attendance Requirements
One online course launch + two online interactive session + five days in person bootcamp at the Burnaby campus (6 hours/day)
### Course Specific Requirements
This course is ideal for individuals with prior experience as installers, carpenters, detailers, and engineers/design professionals with experience in the construction industry.
- Completion of Level 2 of Carpentry is recommended.
- Completion of BCIT’s micro-credential -Introductory Studies in Mass Timber Construction is recommended, but not required (XTMB 1001, XTMB1002)
### Safety equipment or protective clothing
For in person practicum: steel toe boots, hi-viz vest, hard hat, safety glasses
