### Course Description
This course has two components. The major part deals with problematic GPS data and troubleshooting. This includes pre-processing preparation such as selection and understanding of base station coordinates and datums, baseline and network processing parameters and techniques, detailed logfile inspection and troubleshooting. The second part is devoted to provide students with the skills to manage a satellite positioning project, including the abilities to plan, estimate the cost of and execute surveys, to meet a predefined set of survey specifications (accuracy requirements, area of interests, etc).
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the concepts and carry out GPS baseline and network processing in a controlled manner.
- Plan the appropriate baseline processing parameters for different baseline types and lengths.
- Evaluate and interpret GNSS processing results.
- Relate knowledge to trouble shoot problematic GNSS data.
- Appraise satellite positioning network adjustments.
- Design and develop satellite positioning project management plan, including.
- Select and establish a geodetic network for the survey.
- Design observation strategy for the survey.
- Estimate the cost and time of the GNSS survey.
- Execute the survey.
- Process the data and evaluate the results.
- Compile the final report.

### Course Goals
This course prepares students to:
- Identify the major steps and associated concerns to design and run a GNSS survey campaign
- Understand and follow GNSS survey standards, specifications and requirements
- Run and manage (resources, time, and cost) a GNSS survey project
- Process, troubleshoot, and assess the quality of GNSS baseline solutions
- Process, troubleshoot, and assess the quality of GNSS network solutions
- Understand and apply horizontal and vertical coordinate transformations of GNSS surveys
- Understand and apply datum transformations of GNSS surveys
