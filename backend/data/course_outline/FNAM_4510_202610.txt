### Course Description
Continuing from FNAM 3500, in this course, students learn advanced skills related to forest road location and design, harvest system selection, and harvest planning and operations. Students will develop strategies for road maintenance and deactivation. Economics of harvesting operations will be discussed. Techniques for dealing with landslide prone terrain will be investigated.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe common resource road and trail maintenance practices and procedures
- Describe common resource road deactivation practices and procedures
- Demonstrate safe field work practices related to forest harvesting and road operations.
- Survey for and design appropriate drainage structures within the limits of a RFT
- Assess the characteristics of soils and surficial materials that affect forest operations
- Relate the importance of terrain stability assessment with forestry operations.
- Prepare work schedules for different job operations and project phases using planning and scheduling software
- Utilize the principles of engineering economics to determine cost effective solutions for forestry operations planning.
- Calculate the hourly operating costs of harvesting and road equipment.
- Calculate the phase costs per m3 for a harvesting operation.
- Calculate optimal solutions using the concept of break-even analysis
- Utilize current practices for the inspection and maintenance of bridges.
- Define bridge terminology and describe and sketch bridge components
- Assess safety issues in bridge maintenance and installation.
- Identify symptoms and modes of bridge hazards.
- Estimate maintenance requirements based on visual inspections.
- Recommend remedial action for a long-term minimum maintenance program.
- Prepare a road deactivation plan and road maintenance inspection.
- Complete a field assessment of a case study area.
- Recognize and recommend remedial action for unstable cuts and fills.
- Locate and orient drainage structures on a plan and in the field.
- Determine the potential for ditch line erosion.
