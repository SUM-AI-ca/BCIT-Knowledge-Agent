## Course Description
Studies basic physical principles and applies them to relevant situations in the OCHS technology. Topics include kinematics, vectors, dynamics, statics, friction, energy, and simple machines. The labs emphasise measurement, data analysis, and experimental techniques as they relate to the lecture material.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Understand some of the basic physical principles underlying your technology.
- Use analytical skills in the solution of technical problems.
- Set up scientific apparatus and make measurements.
- Analyze experimental data and write scientific reports.

## Course topics
The topics of the course are intended to help the student develop the following skills:

- Units and Measurement Convert between units given conversion factors Describe and differentiate random/systematic uncertainties Calculate uncertainty and discrepancy, and propagate uncertainties through a calculation Draw a 1D comparison graph
- Convert between units given conversion factors
- Describe and differentiate random/systematic uncertainties
- Calculate uncertainty and discrepancy, and propagate uncertainties through a calculation
- Draw a 1D comparison graph
- Graphical and Equation Analysis Make a graph from a set of data Read graphs to determine value, slope, and area Use a model fit to a set of data and assess appropriateness of a particular fit Manipulate equations to solve for a desired parameter / relationship
- Make a graph from a set of data
- Read graphs to determine value, slope, and area
- Use a model fit to a set of data and assess appropriateness of a particular fit
- Manipulate equations to solve for a desired parameter / relationship
- Vectors Decompose a vector, or convert x-y components into length and direction Add vectors graphically and algebraically
- Decompose a vector, or convert x-y components into length and direction
- Add vectors graphically and algebraically
- Kinematics Given one of {x, v, a} vs. t plots, estimate the other two Solve for x, v, a, or t using kinematics equations in a 1D problem Solve a projectile motion problem
- Given one of {x, v, a} vs. t plots, estimate the other two
- Solve for x, v, a, or t using kinematics equations in a 1D problem
- Solve a projectile motion problem
- Forces Draw a free body diagram for an object or group of objects Use Newton’s 2nd Law to find net force or acceleration in a problem Characterize force types and calculate values for specific force types Distinguish Newton’s 3rd Law force pairs
- Draw a free body diagram for an object or group of objects
- Use Newton’s 2nd Law to find net force or acceleration in a problem
- Characterize force types and calculate values for specific force types
- Distinguish Newton’s 3rd Law force pairs
- Equilibrium and Stress Draw an extended free body diagram Define equilibrium, and use equilibrium conditions to solve problems Describe and define mathematically three types of stress and strain
- Draw an extended free body diagram
- Define equilibrium, and use equilibrium conditions to solve problems
- Describe and define mathematically three types of stress and strain
- Work, Energy, Power Calculate work done by a force on an object Explain dissipation, and describe where energy conservation does/doesn’t apply Characterize energy types and calculate values for specific energy types Explain connection between energy and power and calculate power for a process
- Calculate work done by a force on an object
- Explain dissipation, and describe where energy conservation does/doesn’t apply
- Characterize energy types and calculate values for specific energy types
- Explain connection between energy and power and calculate power for a process
- Momentum Define and calculate linear momentum, and explain when momentum conservation applies Classify and describe collision types Solve 1D and 2D collision problems using momentum conservation
- Define and calculate linear momentum, and explain when momentum conservation applies
- Classify and describe collision types
- Solve 1D and 2D collision problems using momentum conservation
- Rotation and vibration Calculate parameters for an object undergoing uniform circular motion Define sources of centripetal and centrifugal force Define and use angular variables Explain the meaning of torque and calculate its value by a force about an axis Use torque and the 2nd equilibrium condition to solve a statics problem Explain the connection between torque and rotation and use rotational analogue of 2nd Law Explain the hazards associated with vibration and what occurs when there is a vibrational resonance

- Define sources of centripetal and centrifugal force
- Define and use angular variables
- Explain the meaning of torque and calculate its value by a force about an axis

- Explain the connection between torque and rotation and use rotational analogue of 2nd Law
- Explain the hazards associated with vibration and what occurs when there is a vibrational resonance
