### Course Description
​Develop core skills for professional practice as a Medical Laboratory Technologist, including recognizing signs of individual stress and burnout, conflict and group stress in the workplace. Recognize signs of patient stress and distress, and learn to support special patient cases, such as phobic and anxious patients, and reducing pain perception. Develop skills in coping with stress and de-escalating distress in self and others, conflict resolution, as well as managing the impact of shiftwork. Exercises and activities include discussions, role-play, and a self-reflective approach to examine how one's own interpersonal style influences others. Learning is assessed through a mix of theoretical exams, skills demonstrations and reflections.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Assess the best strategy to employ, given the current situation, in order to minimize distress in patients, colleagues and self, including effectively coping with shiftwork and burnout, and understanding the right to refuse unsafe work
- Describe the impact of stress and factors that generate or exacerbate distress, and pain perception, and understand how to use coping strategies that would alleviate stress in patients, colleagues and self.
- Identify appropriate precautions and interventions with common psychological problems, such as anxiety or depression, particularly patients with needle phobia.

### Course Specific Requirements
- Examinations (2 Exams = 80% of TOTAL MARK) will potentially consist of multiple-choice, true or false, and short answer questions; these will cover material from the textbook and from class. You will be responsible for all material in the readings, even if it is not covered in class. Exams are cumulative. Failure to attend an exam will result in a grade of 0 unless a physician’s note is provided, or other arrangements are made PRIOR to the day of the exam.
- Stress Management Assignment (10%): The ability to understand and manage stress is paramount to health and longevity within the medical field. This assignment will help you expand your knowledge around stress management by allowing you to explore different stress management techniques that may support physical, psychological, and/or emotional wellness. More information on this assignment can be found on Learning Hub.
- Attendance & Participation (10%): Weekly class attendance is mandatory, and you will receive 0.5% for every class that you attend for a total of 5% of your overall grade. Participation marks are awarded at the end of the term at the discretion of the instructor for a total of 5% of your overall grade. Criteria considered when determining this mark include:
Engagement During Weekly Classes | Students are expected to actively participate fully during class sessions whenever possible.
Role-Plays/ Group Work | Are you sitting in class but not involved? Full participation in roleplays, discussions, and group work is required for this class.
Questions | Responding to questions asked in class. Your contribution should be appropriate and allow opportunities for others to give their opinion as well. Lower marks will be assigned to those whose participation is either minimal or excessive to the point of dominating the conversation.
​ Contribution | Offer your perspective and/or answer questions about class materials. Take the discussion to the next level.
Respectful Conduct | Some topics may be sensitive and/or invite debate of different perspectives. Disrespectful language and/or conduct will not be permitted.
Use of Skills | Demonstration of skills we have learned in class indicates you are able to apply your learning in a real-life interactive setting.
Preparation for Class | Prepare for class by doing your readings ahead of time; it will be easier to absorb and discuss materials discussed in class.
