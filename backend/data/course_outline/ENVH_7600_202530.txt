### Course Description
This course examines the role of the Public Health Inspector/Environmental Health Officer (PHI/EHO) in effecting behaviour changes in a particular target audience or end user through environmental health education and advocacy. Students apply the principles of adult education to the design and presentation of an educational program and evaluate the educational programs presented by their classmates. They also design an evaluation process to determine the effectiveness of educational programs in general, recognizing the importance of evidence-based service delivery.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Justify the role of education in promoting healthy lifestyles.
- Explain the importance of effecting behaviour change and the barriers to effecting change.
- Compare and contrast health education to other strategies such as engineering the environment and enforcement of legislation).
- Assess the role of advocacy in addressing environmental public health issues and challenges.
- Assess the need for environmental health education and advocacy taking demographics and culture into consideration.
- Identify the environmental public health concerns surrounding life style issues such as substance abuse (alcohol, tobacco, drugs), injury prevention, healthy eating and exercise.
- Assess the importance of the social determinants of health in educational programs for a given educational program.
- Identify the target audience or end user, stakeholders and potential partners.
- Select appropriate educational options (e.g. TV, radio, print media, social media, etc.) based on the target audience or end user.
- Evaluate the role of community mobilization in educational programs.
- Analyze the issues surrounding resource allocation, e.g. government funding is often tied to evidence-based assessments.
- Design an educational program applicable to environmental health (such as food, sewage, smoking, healthy eating, exercise, injury prevention).
- Explain the principles of adult education
- Outline the advantages and disadvantages of the various instructional techniques which may be used.
- Write goals, objectives and a lesson plan.
- Design an education product (e.g. poster, pamphlet, blog, etc) to be delivered in the selected media (hardcopy or electronically).
- Present an educational program to the target audience or end user.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of the environmental health educational programs as presented to the target audience or end user.
- Use evidence-based data to evaluate the impact of an environmental health educational program on the target audience or end user.
- Outline the strategies used in preparing and presenting a media release and interview on a given topic.

### Course Specific Requirements
AI-based content generation tools, or AI tools , are permitted for out-of-class assignments and project work. However, they are not to be used during in-class work or during formal assessments including both midterm exams, the skills assessment, the practical lab exam or the final theory exam. Note that AI output can include incorrect or misleading information, fabricated authors/dates/publications, faked works or may incorporate discriminatory ideas. It is the student’s responsibility to ensure the quality, accuracy and appropriateness of any work submitted.
If ChatGPT (or a similar tool) is used to prompt ideas and/or partial answers for an assignment and/or to generate any text for a draft or final version of any part of an assignment, students must declare that it thas been used, describing the extent to which it was used. Any generated text must be saved and available to Instructional staff if requested. There is no penalty for using AI tools as specified here, but an instructor may ask for the generated text to assist with grading.
As with other reference materials AI tools must be cited. Undeclared use of AI is a violation of academic integrity as it presents work, ideas created by AI or other people as your own. Failure to fully declare the use of this tool will be considered a violation of BCIT academic policy.
