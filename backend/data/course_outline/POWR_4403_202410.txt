## Course Description
As a continuation of POWR 3303, Power Plant Theory 3, this course prepares the power engineering student to operate power plant equipment within the scope of the Third Class Power Engineer's Certificate. Topics covered include: heating and air conditioning; lubrication and bearings; steam turbines, gas turbines, and gas combustion engines; air compression and refrigeration; pumps, welding, and water treatment.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:

- Describe the main principles of heating and air conditioning.
- Describe the main principles of lubrication.
- Describe the main characteristics of turbines and engines.
- Describe the main principles of air compression and refrigeration.
- Fulfil examination requirements for Part A - Paper 2, Part B - Paper 1, and Part B - Paper 2.
- Operate power plant equipment within the scope of a Third Class Power Engineer's Certificate.
- Describe boiler construction, operation, pumps, water treatment, and control.

## Learning Resources
Required: Pan Global, 3rd Class Part B, ideally Edition 2.5 or later

## Course Goals
At the end of this course the student will be able to:

- Describe the main principles of boiler construction and heat transfer components.
- Describe the main principles of burner designs, draft and flue gas equipment, and boiler control systems.
- Describe the main principles of boiler procedures and water treatment.
- Describe the main characteristics of pumps, steam turbines, and condensers.
- Describe the main principles of gas turbines and internal combustion engines.
- Describe the main principals of cogeneration schemes, wastewater treatment, and compressor designs.
- Fulfil several of the TSBC examination requirements for 3rd Class Part B - Papers 1 and 2.
- Operate power plant equipment within the scope of a Third Class Power Engineer's Certificate.
