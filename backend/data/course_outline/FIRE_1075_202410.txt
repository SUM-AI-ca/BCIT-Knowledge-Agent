## Course Description
Emphasizes the general structure and layout of the BC Fire Code. Provides an overview of the fire safety requirements in and around buildings with an introduction to Division A, Division C and Division B Part 1, 2, 6 and 7. Students must bring a copy of the BC Fire Code and the Fire Services Act to the first class.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Understand and apply sections of the fire code requirements.
- Identify who is responsible for the requirements as described in the fire code.
- Have knowledge of fire safety in and around buildings.
- Identify what is required for the inspection, testing and maintenance requirements of fire protection systems in buildings.

## Learning Resources
The Province has adopted the NFC as the 2018 BC Fire Code (2018 BCFC). A paper copy of the Code is the preferred version for learning the code structure, understanding use and making the BCFC yours (notes, highlights, etc.). However, the BC Office of the Fire Commissioner (the Province) provides access to a free online version of the BC Fire Code.

A copy of the BC Fire Services Act is required for the first session (for access to the online version), see:

https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96144_01

For information about code products and pricing email bccodes@gov.bc.ca

This course is currently provided as an online, virtual classroom – internet access is required, including a laptop / computer with camera and microphone. ## Course Goals
BCFC 1 is intended to provide a general introduction to the layout and structure of the BCFC, including an introduction to the Fire Services Act, OFC Support, NFC (BCFC) Preface, terminology, numbering and general use.

Provides a general overview of Division A, Division C, and Division B, Parts 1, 2, 6, and 7.
