## Course Description
In this course, students will learn basic skills in oxyacetylene cutting and welding. Students will work in a welding shop environment to learn the basics about equipment components and their safe handling.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Identify safety hazards associated with cutting and welding.
- Identify gases, components and component handling procedures for oxyacetylene cutting and welding.
- Assemble and disassemble a portable oxyacetylene unit.
- Utilize oxyacetylene equipment to weld multiple steel plates.
