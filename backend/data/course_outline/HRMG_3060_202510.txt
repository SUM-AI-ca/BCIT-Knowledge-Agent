## Course Description
Provides students with a working knowledge of recruitment and selection performance appraisals, job evaluations and job descriptions.

## Learning Resources
- Canadian Human Resource Management, 13e, Schwind
- Edition: 13e

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

Understand the importance to organizational success of programs and processes associated with attracting, developing and maintaining an effective and efficient workforce.

Students will be able to describe some of the key human resource management issues facing Canadian organizations now and in the foreseeable future. They will also develop a working knowledge of the terminology and generally accepted processes of human resource management activities such as job design, HR planning, recruitment and selection, compensation, terminations, etc; understand basic references to B.C. and federal employment legislation; as well as understand some of their own basic employment rights.
