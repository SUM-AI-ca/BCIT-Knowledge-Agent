### Course Description
This course is the third of three courses that guide students through completion of a comprehensive independent project in a selected area of geomatics. Prior to graduating from the Bachelor of Science in Geomatics program, students will undertake a major independent project in a selected area of Geomatics technology, starting in GEOM 6130 Geomatics Project Planning and continuing through GEOM 7130 Geomatics Project 1. In consultation with industry sponsors and faculty advisors, students will solve technical problems and demonstrate a thorough knowledge of their project topic and present final results through a poster presentation, a final written technical report, and an oral presentation. The final report and poster are continuation of the draft report and draft poster submitted at the end of GEOM 7130. Emphasis should be on the explanation, validation, and verification of the results and a critical appraisal of the processes and techniques used. Students will present their findings and answer questions from peers and faculty during the oral presentation with the project poster as a visual reference.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate an ability to think critically, analyze, and solve a significant technical issue or problem related to the geomatics work environment.
- Apply resources, data, and personal organization techniques to manage a project.
- Maintain effective and constructive communication with industry and faculty on a regular basis.
- Maintain a project schedule, using appropriate project management techniques when faced with unexpected issues.
- Demonstrate a high level of competence in producing a professional report through written and presentation skills.
- Record project observations and results in a logical and concise manner.
- Verify, validate, and discuss the results and information collected.
- Analyze the results and draw meaningful conclusions and suggestions from the research conducted.
- Create a poster that integrates the key elements of the project in a well organized, visually appealing format, using concise language and well-chosen images/graphics.
- Present an overview of the final research project to peers and faculty and defend the methodology and results during an oral presentation with the aid of a poster as a visual reference.
