### Course Description
​This laboratory course focuses on the application of technical knowledge in nuclear medicine and nuclear medicine instrumentation. The laboratory activities in this course are specifically designed to prepare students for competent performance in clinical practice. Students will be given the opportunity to practice critical thinking and decision making skills through various practical and simulated lab activities. The lab activities in this course will primarily focus on specific topics related to radiopharmacy, gamma camera SPECT quality control, gamma camera acquisition, and post-acquisition image processing.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Perform a variety of radiopharmacy lab skills in a safe and efficient manner.
- Critically analyze radiopharmaceutical preparation strategies and quality control results for clinical significance and acceptability.
- Evaluate the technical and diagnostic quality of acquired laboratory data and gamma camera images.
- Perform accurate image processing on acquired nuclear medicine studies using vendor-specific display and processing software.
- Evaluate processed image results for normal or abnormal patterns associated with common pathologies.
- Perform routine SPECT and CT quality control tests
- Evaluate the clinical significance of gamma camera and CT quality control results with respect to image quality and equipment performance
- Assess gamma camera quality control results for acceptability and determine if any further actions are required.
- Evaluate decisions that arise in the lab setting using critical thinking and best clinical practice guidelines

### Evaluation Criteria
Criteria | % | Comments
Pre-Lab Assignments | 5 | Pre-lab quizzes will be set to unlimited attempts for study purposes, but the grade will be based on the first attempt. Pre lab quizzes must be completed satisfactorily (>65%) by the time they are marked, the morning of the lab. Please complete them the night before, to ensure full marks are given.
Radiopharmacy Skills Assessments x 5 | 40 | Assessed using a rubric for radiopharmacy labs. Each RP Assessment is worth 8% Students must satisfactorily achieve ≥ 65% overall on the radiopharmacy skills assessments to pass this course.
Tutorial Quizzes x 4 | 30 | 90% of grade is from the individual quiz and 10% is from the group quiz. Each quiz is worth 7.5%
Final Exam | 25 | Cumulative and comprised of written (multiple choice) worth 15% and practical stations worth 10%
Students must complete all labs. The minimum cumulative passing grade for this course is 65%.
