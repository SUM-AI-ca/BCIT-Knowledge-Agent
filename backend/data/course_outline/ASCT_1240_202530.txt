## Course Description
By employing a variety of architectural graphic techniques, the design (or thinking) process is explored and seen from different points of view. Additionally, the communication of the "design thesis" to its intended audience, is enhanced through the appropriate use of graphics. This subject area endeavors to develop the Student's: (1) Analytical, observation, & thought processes in developing & reviewing architectural concepts. (2) Communication techniques in describing Architectural concepts. (3) To explore methods of problem solving in construction & design. (4) To understand 3-Dimensional form & space.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Employ graphic techniques to analyze spatial concepts, & to creatively problem solve.
- Employ graphic techniques to communicate architectural & construction ideas.
- Employ graphic techniques to critically; study, review, and judge alternative concepts.
- Construct effective 3-dimensional graphics.
