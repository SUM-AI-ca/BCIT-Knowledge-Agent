## Course Description
​This course begins with students learning to formulate and solve problems of weighted parameters, nuisance parameters elimination, and the sequential adjustment model. It continues with the analysis of datum, including inner constraint adjustments, constraint functions, and datum transformations, and then evaluation of local, network, internal and external accuracies of surveys. The course will then discuss major topics such as approximation, Fourier analysis, filtering in observation and frequency domains, and prediction, as well as Kalman filtering and their applications in geomatics. Students will learn through lectures, labs and participation.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Formulate and solve weighted parameters problems.
- Formulate and solve problems with the nuisance parameters elimination model and sequential adjustment model.
- Analyze datum problems in geomatics, including free network (inner constraint) adjustments and datum transformations.
- Determine and evaluate local, network, internal and external accuracies of surveys.
- Apply matrix theory in approximation problems.
- Construct curve or mathematical functions that best fit to series of data points.
- Model time and spatial data series for further analysis.
- Apply the error propagation approach to determine quality of approximated functions.
- Apply regression analysis to geomatics problems, such as time series and non-time-series data.
- Perform spectral analysis.
- Perform filtering in observation and frequency domains.
- Describe the principles of a Kalman filter.
- Relate simple Kalman filtering and prediction methods with the sequential least-squares adjustment method.
- Design and use various filters, including a simple Kalman filter.
- Describe the relationship between spectral analysis and filtering.
