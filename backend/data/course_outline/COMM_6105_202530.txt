### Course Description
Building on the fundamentals of communication from COMM 5105 – Communication 1 for Radiation Therapy, this course focuses on communication in research and career development settings. The research modules cover theory and practice for communicating scientific research; students apply these skills by writing a research article and preparing an oral presentation for peers, instructors and other health care professionals. In the career communication module, students practice job application skills appropriate for their discipline and prospective employers. Throughout the course, students develop oral communication skills for team meetings, project consultations and interviews.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyse the professional literature to identify appropriate content, organisation and style for reporting research.
- Write a scientific article based on the RADT 6109/7109 project, following the Journal of Medical Imaging and Radiation Sciences (JMIRS) guidelines for article manuscripts. Key skills include:
- Developing discipline-appropriate scientific writing style
- Reviewing the literature
- Reporting research methods
- Reporting data and statistics
- Providing critical analysis and conclusions
- Creating and integrating tables and graphics
- Citing sources in APA Style
- Prepare an oral presentation of research project findings in a style appropriate for peers, instructors and other health care professionals.
- Set career goals, conduct a skills inventory and analyse a target employer/site.
- Create a discipline-appropriate job application package (resume, application letter, other as required) and practice job interview skills.
- Identify opportunities to share knowledge and build a professional profile (e.g., presenting to colleagues, publication, conferences, awards).
