## Course Description
​Students learn how to create and present artwork for an online portfolio. Video and still image renders are examined and discussed to students can deliver a result that shows off their art favourably. Proper lighting, staging, camera moves, and titling are all considered when creating a final render. Scheduling and time management are discussed and students need to adhere to this schedule for their final project. Learning how to give feedback as well as to receive feedback is explored to further enhance the professionalism of the student. During the course of the semester, students receive feedback to implement into their ongoing project. The final for this course is a project presented using the skills learned in this course.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Effectively present artwork in a marketable way.
- Create an online portfolio.
- Provide and receive feedback effectively.
- Use basic work flow techniques to work quickly and efficiently.
- Modify and revise portfolio piece based on critiques.
- Create and maintain a schedule.
- Create a final portfolio piece for presentation.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.
