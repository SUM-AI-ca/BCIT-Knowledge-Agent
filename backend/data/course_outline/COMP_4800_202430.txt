## Course Description
​Allows students to work on projects within guidelines specified by faculty. The projects are drawn from a variety of sources, especially from industrial situations, and may require extensive contact with the business community. Students work in teams and seek advice from a faculty member acting as their project supervisor.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Determine project requirements.
- Plan and schedule project work tasks.
- Design a system that meets the documented requirements.
- Implement a system that has been designed.
- Test and deliver a working system to an industry client.

## Learning Resources
- Teams will need to establish their own development, source control and testing environments.
- Students will use their own devices via the internet to work on the project.
- Teams will need to use productivity software, such as Microsoft Office or Google products to complete documentation requirements.

## Course Goals
- To identify risks and create a mitigation plan for the project and to apply Agile methodology.
- To work in teams to design, develop, and deliver a significant application / module for the selected project sponsor.
- To ensure required project documentation is created along with measurement metrics for monitoring progress.

Please Note:

- Any student not actively contributing to the project will be given a failing grade.
- A team member's grade for each sprint may be adjusted if their contribution is deemed unequal or insufficient. Since most students are responsible and contribute fairly, no adjustment is generally needed.
- Each member of the project team must attend meetings with their project sponsor and supervisors.
- At the conclusion of each Sprint, we will conduct Sprint Reviews either during class or as the Supervisor determines.
- Two or more peer evaluations will be conducted before the course concludes to ensure that equal contributions to the project have been made by every team member.
- Without delivering a Minimum Viable Product, a team will receive a failing grade, irrespective of any grades awarded for individual project stages or sprints.
- All projects will follow the Agile method, including Sprint Planning, Backlog adjustments, Sprint Reviews, and Retrospectives.
- Supervisor Consistency Adjustment is evaluated and assigned by the course instructor, to increase consistency of assessment across different Supervisors.

Plagiarism and other forms of cheating will not be tolerated.

- Students are encouraged to work in groups to develop peer-to-peer communication and support. However, each student must complete and submit their own individual work (not copies of the same assignment) and not material found online or recycled from other courses. Assignments and labs are to be completed by each student on an individual basis unless stated otherwise.

Academic misconduct includes providing solutions to other students.

- Students involved in cheating or assisting others in cheating will be recorded. Repeated academic misconduct will result in stricter consequences. These can range from being removed from the course without a refund, failing the course, to permanent expulsion from the program.

Academic Integrity:

Violation of academic integrity, including plagiarism, and dishonesty in assignments, examinations, or other academic performances are prohibited and will be handled in accordance with Policy 5104 - Academic Integrity and Appeals, and accompanying procedures.
