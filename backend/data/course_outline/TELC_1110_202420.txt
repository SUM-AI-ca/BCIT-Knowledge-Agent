## Course Description
This course is a basic primer on the nature of electricity and how to troubleshoot simple systems.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Describe simple electrical theory
- Solve simple electrical circuits
- Use a multimeter to test and verify circuits
- Identify and select basic electrical components
- Use relays and timers in typical security applications

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:

Describe basic electrical theory

Use digital multimeters for measuring and testing

Solve series, parallel and combination circuits

Select, install, and test relays and switches

Service rechargable batteries and battery chargers
