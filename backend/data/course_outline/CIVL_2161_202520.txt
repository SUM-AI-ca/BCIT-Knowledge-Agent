## Course Description
An introduction to the terminology, physics and applications of hydrostatics and hydraulics, this course covers fluids at rest and in motion. Topics include the properties of water, fluid pressure, hydrostatic force and buoyancy, head loss and the application of Bernoulli’s equation to hydraulic systems. Students will use energy conservation methods to solve pipe systems with multiple branches.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Discuss fluid properties and hydrostatic pressure.
- Explain the principle of buoyancy and calculate the buoyant force on a submerged object.
- Apply Bernoulli’s equation to practical pipe flow problems.
- Solve simple hydraulic flow problems using energy conservation methods.

## Course topics
Course Schedule

Week 1 Hydrostatics • Terminology • Units • Properties of liquids • Calculation of pressure • Total hydrostatic force • Center of pressure 1

Week 2 Flow Fundamentals • Terminology • Units • Relationships between velocity, flow rate, area, volume, and time • Continuity principle 2

Week 3 The Energy Equation • The three forms of hydraulic energy • Total energy • Using energy principles to solve flow problems 3

Week 4 Pipe Friction Losses • General relationships • Reynolds number/Moody diagram • Lammar flow vs turbulent flow • The Hazen-Williams, Manning, and Darcy-Weisbach equations • Solving pipe flow problems involving energy losses

Week 5 Local Losses • Loss coefficients • Solving pipe flow problems involving both pipe friction losses and local losses 5

Week 6 Midterm Exam

Week 7 Pipe Networks Hazen-Williams formula , Complex Pipe Networks — Hardy Cross method

Week 8 Pumps

Week 9 Uniform Open Channel Flow

Week 10 Non-uniform Open Channel Flow

Week 11 Fluid Measurements

Week 12: Final Exam
