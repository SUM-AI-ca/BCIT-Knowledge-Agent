## Course Description
This course builds on the skills you learned in COMM 1188. It covers a professional career portfolio, job interview skills, literature reviews, persuasive writing and presentations, and report writing. You will also research, design, and “sell” a proposal for change on an OHS topic.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Compile a professional career package.
- Take part in an interview.
- Take part in a panel.
- Write a report for an investigation (Accident, Causation, and Analysis).
- Write (persuasive) workplace messages for OCHS.
- Conduct a literature search on an OCHS topic.
- Write a proposal.
- Give a persuasive presentation based on the written proposal.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.
