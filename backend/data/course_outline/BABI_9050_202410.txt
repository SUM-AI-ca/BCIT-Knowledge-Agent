## Course Description
Drawing on the knowledge gained in the previous courses, students will assume the role of the Business Analytics Professional in a real-world Industry Project. Students are encouraged to develop the project proposal from their particular sector, and will develop the project from proposal to completion, working closely with the Faculty advisor and their Industry stakeholder. Students will apply their Business Analytics skill set to the solution of the Industry Project. Evaluation will be based on application and integration of Business Analytics to the solution.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Develop a formal Project Proposal including using business analytics methodologies.
- Present initial research findings to peers and Industry stakeholders.
- Communicate analysis results effectively to specify the solution proposed.
- Provide a real-world industry solution using business analytics.
- Develop and produce a written analysis of an Industry Project.
- Present findings and clearly communicate conclusions to peers and Industry stakeholders.
- Effectively identify a business need, perform appropriate research and analysis, and package the results in industry standard reporting formats.
- Plan, manage, and execute a business analytics project.

This course features a major team-based project. All team members usually receive the same mark. However, marks can be redistributed if one or more team members contribute less than their fair share. This may include one or more of the following:

- frequently absent from team meetings or classes
- submitting plagiarized or low effort work
- missing team deadlines or class deadlines
- disruptive behavior during team meetings

Your team will need to have a team charter and document infractions, escalating the issue to your instructor before the assignment is due. The instructor will then guide your team through the mark redistribution process. In this process, the team will have two options:

Option 1: If all members of the group agree that grades should be reallocated, up to 40% will be taken off one student’s score and redistributed to the other students.

Option 2: If group members do not reach agreement on how marks should be reallocated, each team member’s contributions will be marked separately.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
