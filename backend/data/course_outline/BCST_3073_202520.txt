## Course Description
​There is a community and an audience online for just about every story, idea, and concept. The problem is that many people don't know how to create their ideas online, or how to build and engage a community around those ideas. That is what you will begin to build in this course – a StoryWorld around your digital media endeavours, whether for personal branding, business or a project. This will include utilizing WordPress to build a professional website, optimizing SEO to that website and an associated YouTube channel, fine tuning your website and YouTube channel for accessibility, niche audience building, creation of and participation in a digital storytelling campaign, measuring social metrics, crowdfunding, crafting appealing email newsletters, and developing a long term strategy for continued community engagement.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify unique methods of sharing your voice in a creative or business endeavour.
- Describe how storytelling can be used across media platforms to build brand imagery and loyalty.
- Formulate goals to be achieved through digital storytelling.
- Identify the story that will help you reach your goals.
- Develop a professional WordPress site.
- Explore ways to drive more viewers to a site and YouTube channel, and improve a site and channel's SEO.
- Define SEO and identify ways to improve your site and channel's SEO.
- Demonstrate best practices for web accessibility on a website and digital videos.
- Identify your target audience.
- Locate your desired audience in niche digital communities, through various different digital media platforms.
- Engage your desired audience in the digital space.
- Build a StoryWorld around a website, YouTube channel, and your desired digital niche communities.
- Create an engaging email campaign.
- Identify the characteristics of a successful crowdfunding campaign.
- Craft strategy for a crowdfunding campaign of your own.​

## Course Goals
BCST 3073 : Building Your Digital Media Presence is a project based course, designed to help students to build a storyworld around their cross-platform storytelling endeavours (whether for personal branding, business, a cause, creative endeavour or a traditional storytelling project). Our goals in this course are to teach students to utilize WordPress to build a professional website with web accessibility in mind, Web Monetize their sites (if they wish to do so), optimize the SEO to that website and to an associated YouTube Channel in order to target your desired audience in organic searches online, build a niche audience to your storyworlds, create and participate in a digital storytelling campaign, measure social metrics, design a crowdfunding campaign, craft an appealing email newsletter, and develop a long term strategy for continued community engagement.

## Course Specific Requirements
This Course is project based, meaning that the homework and assignments are designed around building you a digital base for something you are creating for the future - a business, your personal brand, your music, a presence for a cause or non-profit you are involved in, your art, or some sort of project or creative endeavour that you are working on.

Come with something in mind or ideas to discuss and flesh out in the first few week of the course.
