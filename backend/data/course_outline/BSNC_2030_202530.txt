## Course Description
This course builds on Practice of Nursing 1, focusing on clinical reasoning and clinical skill acquisition to care for adult clients in an acute care setting experiencing chronic and acute health challenges. Learners will learn and demonstrate related psychomotor skills, and integrate these with client assessment, pathophysiological and pharmacological knowledge to plan nursing care for these clients in acute care situations. Clinical practice guidelines and research evidence are accessed to build learners’ clinical judgments in caring for specific individual clients. Learners will learn and apply the principles of critical inquiry to determine the most appropriate evidence to facilitate prioritizing and planning care for clients. The influence of the determinants of health for adults requiring acute medical intervention will be explored. Knowledge, skills, and attitudes related to client-centered care for adults, teamwork and collaboration, evidence-based practice, quality improvement, safety, and nursing informatics will be considered within the context of nursing practice. This course includes an integrated and applied learning experience in a simulated/practice environment.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will meet the following competencies:

Advocate

- Uses relational practice to implement nursing care that recognizes the unique requirements of each client.
- Recognizes professional and ethical responsibility to advocate for client safety, fairness, and equitable distribution of health care resources within a defined scope of practice.
- Has theoretical and practical knowledge of relational practice and understands that relational practice is the foundation for all nursing practice.
- Incorporates knowledge of the health disparities and inequities of at-risk populations and the contributions of nursing practice into nursing care to achieve positive health outcomes for clients.
- Identifies and respects basic human rights.

Collaborator

- Identifies organizational structures within the healthcare system.
- Forms partnerships and relationships with various members of the healthcare team to optimize outcomes for clients and the healthcare system in defined contexts and situations.
- Identifies roles and scope of practice of healthcare team members.
- Identifies and describes facets of a strength-based approach to collaboration.
- Displays initiative, a beginning confidence and self-awareness in collaborative interactions within the healthcare team.
- Recognizes the impact of communication in supporting safe, high quality, client- centred healthcare.
- Identifies conflict and discusses potential solutions

Communicator

- Documents clinical encounters in an accurate, complete, timely, and accessible manner, in compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
- Uses appropriate nursing informatics, communication modalities and technologies in the practice of nursing care
- Recognizes the impact of communication in supporting safe, high quality, client- centred healthcare
- Questions unclear orders, decisions, or actions that are inconsistent with positive client outcomes, best practices, health safety standards, and client wishes
- Demonstrates elements of assertive communication in practice
- Demonstrates effective group process and presentation skills
- Verifies that clients have an understanding of essential information and skills to be active participants in their own care

Decision Maker

- Reflects on nursing actions and incorporates knowledge into practice
- Draws on knowledge from nursing and other sciences, relational practice and critical inquiry in the practice of nursing care
- Has theoretical and practical knowledge of relational practice and understands that relational practice is the foundation for all nursing practice
- Utilizes a decision making model to guide nursing care planning and delivery
- Applies principles of critical inquiry and relational practice to conduct client- focused assessments that emphasize client input and the determinants of health
- Identifies links between salient client data, knowledge, required nursing care and expected client responses
- Reflects on nursing practice during the provision of client care and adjusts plan of care as necessary
- Prioritizes clinical problems/issues to promote client safety and quality of care

Global Citizen

- Provides culturally safe and equitable care in defined contexts and situations

Leader

- Consults and collaborates with clients and health care team to identify actual and potential client health care needs, strengths, capacities and goals
- Demonstrates knowledge of the role of the nurse as a leader/change agent/follower within a multidisciplinary healthcare team
- Recognizes own scope of practice and collaborates with the healthcare team as appropriate
- Utilizes evidenced based knowledge, and ethical standards to guide practice decisions
- Seeks and accepts feedback to foster own professional growth

Lifelong Learner

- Critically appraises learning resources by systematically examines evidence to ascertain its credibility, value, and relevance in a particular nursing practice context
- Draws upon evidence-based resources to actively develop foundational nursing knowledge
- Articulates the elements and principles of the research process

Professional

- Functions within own level of competence in selected contexts and within a defined scope of practice
- Maintains individual competence within scope of practice and professional standards
- Locates and applies policies and procedures consistent with the standards of the profession
- Demonstrates understanding of the nursing profession as a self-regulating profession mandated by provincial legislation to protect the public
- Maintains appropriate professional boundaries with clients and the healthcare team
- Seeks support when needed from the most appropriate resources and persons
- Demonstrates self-care and personal development to build resilience
- Demonstrates understanding of the role of nursing unions and professional associations
- Is accountable to clients, society and the nursing profession for own actions, decisions, and professional nursing conduct
- Takes action to promote the provision of safe, appropriate and ethical care to clients and seeks support and assistance as necessary
- Demonstrates knowledge of the healthcare system at the micro, meso, and macro levels
- Demonstrates honesty, integrity, and respect in all professional interactions
- Recognizes and discusses personal biases and takes action to minimize the influence of positional power, personal values and beliefs on clients
- Demonstrates consideration of the values, beliefs and practices of clients

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance: As student success is tied to regular attendance of classes, the BSN program strongly recommends that students attend class regularly. Attendance at all nursing theory classes is an expectation of the BSN program.

In all learning environments, students are socialized into their professional role and are thus required to demonstrate knowledge and application of professional nursing standards and ethics. Multiple absences may jeopardize a student’s development of the knowledge base required for competent professional nursing practice and successful completion of course requirements. As adult learners, students are ultimately responsible for their own learning; however, it is also recognized that repeated absences have a negative impact on collaborative learning experiences.
