### Course Description
The students are exposed to an increasing level of complexity for the chromosome abnormalities and increased band resolution for examination. The system for selecting metaphases and analysis is reviewed and stressed. Through the analysis of test prints/slides and assigned cultures, a level of confidence and competence is reached. Pairs and partial karyotypes of pro-metaphase chromosomes as well as special stains and banding are organized. The selection of metaphases with chromosomes of high quality and the accuracy of the subsequent analysis are critical for success in this course. Understanding the relationship of chromosome analysis and karyotyping under the microscope with image reproduction support is crucial in this course and life as a professional cytogenetics technologist. G-banding testing of selected metaphases is a critical skill that all students must work hard to achieve consistent critical success in.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
By the end of this course, learners will be able to:
- Appreciate the need for and function of computer-assisted chromosome imaging.
- Refine technique and criteria for selecting metaphase chromosomes under the microscope.
- Document observations while analyzing chromosomes under the microscope, on reproductions, or on a karyotype.
- Detect simple and complex chromosome abnormalities.
- Assemble a timely cytogenetic report consisting of all metaphases examined and the cytogenetic result.
- Observe special banding and staining procedures such as Q-b, C-b, NOR, and DAP1, and recognize their significance and use.
- Determine the total haploid band number for a particular metaphase by following a specific estimate key.
- Observe bands on prophase chromosomes with special attention to those bands involved in the microdeletion syndromes such as Prader-Willi.
- Use the CytoVision® imaging system.
- Use the 2020 ISCN to describe chromosome abnormalities.

### Course Goals
The goals of this course are to:
· Analyze metaphase chromosomes in different formats, e.g., prints, computer images, slides, and karyotypes and use the ISCN system to describe the chromosome result. To continue using the system for metaphase selection and analysis introduced in CLGT 5502 with increased emphasis on the clinical importance and integrity of each analysis submitted for review by the student.
· Refine skills using CytoVision® for chromosome karyotypes and metaphase image capture.

Assignment Details
- Use of generative AI tools (eg ChatGPT, etc.)
Student analysis of lab data is an important part of student learning. Instructional staff do not believe that generative AI tools like ChatGPT are an adequate substitute for student judgement in analyzing data. Reports to be submitted for marking are modelled after the format used at clinical sites, so there is little opportunity to use generative AI to write these reports. However, if students do choose to use one of these tools, please follow these guidelines:
Cite and number each use, like this [1, ChatGPT, (date)]
Mark all generative AI output, by using “quotes” for using unedited genAI output, and ‘quotes’ for using paraphrased genAI output
For all generative AI content, student must include primary source verification i.e. original research journal articles or chapters from traditionally published textbooks.
