## Course Description
​In Paid Media Planning and Buying, students will explore and evaluate major advertising media, including traditional (broadcast, print, and out-of-home), display, video, audio, native, social media, search, and programmatic. Using data-driven advertising strategies, students plan paid media marketing communications campaigns, including media selection, estimating, and buying. In labs, students will practice targeting, executing, and optimizing campaigns with key advertising platforms, such as Facebook Ads, DSPs, Google Ads, and more. As part of a cross-course team project (with Brand and Campaign Strategy, Consumer Experience Marketing, Owned Marketing Communications, and Earned Marketing Opportunities), students will follow a systematic approach to the analysis of paid media using a current marketing communications case study.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Examine the characteristics and key terms associated with each major advertising medium
- Plan paid marketing communications campaigns, including media selection, estimating, and buying
- Create advertising messaging and appeals
- Collect and evaluate ad performance metrics
- Create, manage, and optimise search engine marketing (Google Ads)
- Plan and execute strategic paid social media marketing (Facebook, Instagram, twitter and others)
- Explore how to monetize web properties (such as with Google AdSense)
- Discuss current trends and issues in advertising
- Analyze the paid media strategies and tactics of a real-world IMC campaign
- Develop team collaboration skills
- Develop strong written and oral presentation skills
