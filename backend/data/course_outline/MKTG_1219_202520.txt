## Course Description
This course provides the fundamental skills, tools and processes of professional selling. The focus is on “relationship” and “need-satisfaction” selling in a business-to-business environment. Through experiential exercises, practical applications and discussions students develop, deliver and evaluate a wide range of selling methods and presentations. This course is ideal for people in business who want to enhance their selling skills, people new to a sales role, and salespeople at any level who do not have formal training in the field. To be successful in this course, students must be able to communicate well in English.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Explain the role of personal selling in a business-to-business environment.
- Describe the characteristics, knowledge and skills necessary to be a successful salesperson.
- Explain the steps in the sales process, and apply effective methods involved with each step.
- Develop a persuasive sales presentation.
- Deliver a persuasive sales presentation.
- Evaluate a sales presentation.
- Develop strategies for dealing with ethical challenges in a variety of sales contexts.

## Course Goals
By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Understand the Role of Professional Selling: Explain the significance of professional sales in the contemporary business environment, including career opportunities and the evolving nature of sales roles.​
- Apply Ethical Principles in Sales: Demonstrate an understanding of ethical considerations in sales and apply ethical decision-making frameworks to build and maintain customer relationships.​
- Analyze Customer Buying Behavior: Assess the psychological and behavioral factors that influence why and how customers make purchasing decisions.​
- Develop Effective Communication Skills: Utilize verbal and non-verbal communication techniques to establish and strengthen relationships with clients.​
- Acquire Product and Market Knowledge: Demonstrate comprehensive knowledge of products, services, and market dynamics to effectively address customer needs and differentiate offerings.​
- Master the Sales Process: Execute the stages of the relationship selling process, including prospecting, pre-approach, approach, presentation, handling objections, closing, and follow-up, to successfully convert prospects into loyal customers.​
- Manage Time and Sales Territories Efficiently: Implement strategies for effective time management and territory planning to optimize sales performance and productivity.
- Utilize Technology and Social Media in Selling: Integrate modern technologies and social media platforms into the sales process to enhance customer engagement and sales outcomes.​
- Adapt Sales Techniques to Various Contexts: Apply relationship selling principles across different industries and sales contexts, including retail, business-to-business, services, and nonprofit sectors.

## Course topics
Below are topics students will cover in this course:

- Understanding the role and career path of a professional salesperson
- Practicing ethical decision-making in sales
- Building and maintaining strong customer relationships
- Applying buyer psychology to influence sales outcomes
- Communicating effectively and persuasively with clients
- Gaining in-depth product, customer, and market knowledge
- Identifying and qualifying prospects using strategic prospecting methods
- Planning and preparing for effective sales meetings
- Approaching clients with professionalism and confidence
- Delivering compelling and needs-focused sales presentations
- Handling objections and overcoming resistance
- Successfully closing sales and recognizing buying signals
- Following up with clients to enhance satisfaction and retention
- Managing time, sales territories, and personal productivity
- Navigating ethical and legal challenges in the sales process
- Understanding diverse selling contexts (e.g., retail, service, nonprofit)
- Demonstrating professionalism and accountability in a business environment
- Leveraging experiential learning through practice, feedback, and role-playing
- Preparing for real-world B2B sales through applied assignments and presentations
