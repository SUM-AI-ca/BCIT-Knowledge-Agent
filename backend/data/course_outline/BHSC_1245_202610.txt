### Course Description
Integrating concepts from psychology, sociology, and interpersonal relations, this course is designed to help learners respectfully address the psychosocial needs of patients, and collaborate successfully with other members of the health care team. Empathy and interpersonal communications skills are emphasized; as these provide the basis for crucial employability skills, such as managing stress in both self and patients, reducing the frequency and intensity of conflict, and working respectfully with those from other cultures. Responsible, professional and ethical conduct is underscored.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion the student will be able to:
- Integrate effective interpersonal communication skills in order to foster rapport and trust.
- Apply concept of empathy to create respectful, supportive, and compassionate relations with patients and co-workers.
- Recognize and adapt communication based on the individual needs and nonverbal communication of patients and colleagues.
- Use active listening skills to obtain information from patients and/or support persons.
- Perform various types of assertion according to appropriateness and desired result.
- Implement psychological strategies and skills to prevent distress and uncooperative behavior in patients.
- Given a scenario, develop strategies to deal with harassment and discrimination in the workplace.
- Develop strategies for giving and receiving feedback that emphasize accountability, respect, and professional growth
- Identify types of conflict in the workplace and employ appropriate conflict management techniques to maintain a professional work environment.
- Develop strategies to work with cultural and developmental differences that impact interactions and treatment in a clinical setting.
- Demonstrate empathy and sensitivity to the diversity of individuals
- Identify indicators of abuse and respond within ethical and legal guidelines.
- Explain the impact of stress and factors that generate or increase stress.
- Implement strategies to prevent and reduce distress in both self and patients.
- Describe stress-related disorders and appropriate treatments and interventions.
- Develop an individualized plan to cope with stress, burnout, grief, and loss in the healthcare environment.

### Attendance Requirements
Because this Human Behaviour course emphasizes applied, practice-oriented learning, regular attendance and active participation are essential. Students are expected to attend all classes and engage fully in discussions, reflections, activities, and role plays. Any absences should be communicated to the instructor in advance by email, demonstrating professional communication. Missed classes may impact Review & Reflect mark.
### Other information
Review and Reflect (Weekly In-Class Assignment) (30%): Most weeks will include a short in-class Review and Reflect assignment designed to reinforce learning and encourage deeper engagement with course material. Dates for these assignments are indicated in the course schedule. These low-stakes activities support understanding, application of concepts, and connections across topics.
Review & Reflect Assignments may include:
- Review Questions: Multiple-choice questions on key concepts from the previous week.
- Reflection & Application Questions: Short written or multiple-choice prompts intended to connect, apply, or reflect on the current week’s material.
Review & Reflect assignments must be completed during class; late submissions are not accepted. A password provided in class is required for access. The Learning Hub system will automatically drop the lowest Review & Reflect grade (one free miss) to accommodate unexpected absences. One make-up assignment may be provided for additional absences due to extenuating circumstances; no further make-ups are available.
Clinical Speaking Micro-Skills Quiz (15%): During class time, students will respond in writing to clinical scenarios, demonstrating effective use of empathic paraphrases, probes, perception checks, I statements, and/or assertive communication.
Culture Presentation (25%) + Peer Reviews (5%): Students will work in assigned small groups to create and record a digital presentation designed to learn about other cultures and practice cultural humility. Students will review peers presentations and provide constructive feedback, applying course-related feedback skills while communicating professionally and respectfully. The instructor will provide additional details.
Final Exam (25%): The cumulative final exam will be administered via the Learning Hub Quiz feature and include written-response and short-answer questions. Students must arrive on time; late arrivals will not receive extra time. All personal belongings, including bags and electronic devices, must be left in the designated area. Academic honesty is expected—any cheating, plagiarism, or breach of integrity will result in disciplinary action according to institutional policies.
