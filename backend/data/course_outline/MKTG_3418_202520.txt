## Course Description
In this course, you will look out how planning a strategy for design work helps build a strong and effective ad. You will learn the stages of design production, copywriting and how to design for print, out of home and interactive ads. While using Adobe InDesign, an industry-standard software, you will develop your own marketing materials and start building a portfolio. The course is ideal for aspiring graphic designers and 'ad creatives' as well as marketers who want to better manage their creative resources.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course you should be able to:

- Analyze and critique the creative strategies employed by advertisers in a wide variety of advertising and communication materials, such as print, Out-of-Home and web ads.
- Develop creative strategies and produce advertising materials that effectively employ and communicate these.
- Describe the fundamentals of creative production, which include copywriting, layout and design, Adobe InDesign, computer graphics, art design techniques, and printing requirements.

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion of this course, you should be able to:

- Analyze and critique advertisers’ and marketers’ creative strategies in various print communication materials.
- Develop creative strategies and produce advertising materials that effectively employ and communicate a message to an audience.
- Describe creative production fundamentals, including brainstorming, research, copywriting, mock-ups, layout, and design.
- Use Adobe InDesign to produce computer graphics, art design techniques, and print materials.

## Course Specific Requirements
This course's content takes place over 12 consecutive weeks. It will be conducted in person using learning techniques: live demonstrations, presentations, discussions, critiques, work sessions, assignments, projects, research, graded Discussions, and meetings with student groups and the instructor.

You will also be expected to work on course material by attending in-person classes and working independently and in groups on assignments and projects. Depending on how quickly you work through the assignments, it is recommended that you allow at least three (3) hours to work on each, and projects might take up to twenty (20) hours to complete.

Course assignments must be completed within a specific period, as each will have due dates. These will be graded, and you will be expected to participate fully. Plan your time wisely, as content in some assignments will require more work time than others. Use the peer-support discussion forums to work through ideas with each other, as other people may have the same questions as you.

Computers are available in the classroom, but you are welcome to bring your personal computers. Whether using a lab or a personal computer, you must have downloaded and installed specific programs by the start of the course: InDesign and Acrobat Reader. The instructor will provide download links to each.

System requirements for Adobe InDesign are:

PC

- Operating system: Microsoft Windows Windows 10 (64-bit)
- RAM: Minimum 8 GB of RAM (16 GB recommended)
- Hard disk space: 3.6 GB of available hard-disk space for installation. Additional free space required during installation (cannot install on removable flash storage devices)
- Monitor resolution: 1024 x 768 display (1920 X 1080 recommended), HiDPI display support
- Internet: Internet connection and registration are necessary for required software activation, validation of subscriptions, and access to online services

macOS

- Processor: Multicore Intel processor
- Operating system: macOS version 12.x (Monterey) or newer
- RAM: Minimum 8 GB of RAM (16 GB recommended)

- Monitor resolution: 1024 x 768 display (1920 x 1080 recommended), Retina display support

- Internet: Internet connection and registration are necessary for required software activation, validation of subscriptions, and access to online services.

## Other information
A basic understanding of computer functions is very important for this course: how to download and save files properly, how to navigate between a browser, desktop, and running applications, as well to work quickly and efficiently. Though no prior experience with InDesign is necessary, students will experience more benefits of the course and its material if they are fairly comfortable using a computer.

It is also very important that students work through the course material in a steady timely manner as there will be technical demonstrations and instruction for each of the weekly exercises.
