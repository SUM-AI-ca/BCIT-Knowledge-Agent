## Course Description
​Introduces tools and approaches for creating an effective Customer Service Framework and strategy for residential home builders; includes elements for creating a responsive customer service culture and an understanding of how the home Warranty Insurance program works in BC, what the responsibilities of all parties involved are. Homeowner Orientation and Walk-through procedures will be discussed including homeowner manuals and the creation of performance standards that will guide employees to deliver a successful project that satisfies customer expectations. Customer dissatisfaction and ways to communicate and deal with conflict will be discussed, along with fulfilling homeowner expectations and how to avoid disputes.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop customer service framework and customer service culture for a residential construction company
- Explain techniques for managing customer expectations
- Demonstrate good communication skills for customer management
- Identify the homeowner Protection Act requirements for Home Warranty
- Setup a management of Home Warranty Insurance program
- Describe Orientation, Walk-Through Inspection, and Turn-Over procedures
- Differentiate dispute resolution techniques
- Describe the elements of Performance Standards and Quality Assurance programs for residential construction
- Develop Homeowner and Maintenance Manuals
- Setup an after turn-over customer service program

## Course Goals
The objective of this course is to equip students with the essential tools and approaches to develop an effective Customer Service Framework and strategy specifically for residential home builders. This course will explore the creation of a responsive customer service culture, the workings of the Home Warranty Insurance program in British Columbia, and the responsibilities of all parties involved. Students will gain insights into Homeowner Orientation and Walk-through procedures, the development of homeowner manuals, and the establishment of performance standards that ensure successful project delivery and meeting customer expectations. Additionally, we will cover strategies for managing customer dissatisfaction, effective communication, conflict resolution, and fulfilling homeowner expectations to prevent potential issues.

## Course topics
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

a. Explain what customer service is

- Describe the 5 factors of service excellence
- Demonstrate an understanding of customer values, behaviours and expectations
- Understand the framework for customer service

b. Understand the framework for customer service

- Explain how to establish customer expectations
- Understand the steps in transferring a home to the customer
- Describe the elements of a walk-through inspection
- Explain the importance of customer orientation
- Understand key components of an effective maintenance manual
- Explain how to plan for warranty service work

c. Understand and identify how to handle difficult situations with customers

- Identify the common causes of disputes and customer dissatisfaction
- Explain techniques for resolving disputes

d. Construct and use a quality customer service action plan

- Represent attributes of an effective customer service strategy
- Identify procedures for identifying customer service problems and solutions

e. Explain how to identify and respond to possible construction defects for the homeowner
