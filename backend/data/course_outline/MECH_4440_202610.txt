### Course Description
Continues from MECH 3340. Covers couplings, brakes and clutches, anti-friction and journal bearings, helical, bevel and worm gearing, power screws, springs and machine assembly topics. Introduction to mechanical vibrations with emphasis on critical speeds of rotating bodies. Introduction to bulk materials handling systems.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze gears and gear reducers. [1, 2, 5]
- Analyze power screws. [1, 2, 5]
- Describe sleeve and anti-friction bearings. [1, 2, 5]
- Describe bearing mounting and dismounting methods. [1]
- Analyze keyed and keyless shaft connections. [1, 2, 5]
- Analyze brakes and clutches. [1, 2]
- Analyze the inertial effects of rotating machines . [1 ,2]
- Calculate critical speeds of shafts . [1, 2]
- Produce detailed drawings of machine assemblies . [2, 4, 5, 7]
- Produce detailed drawings of machine assemblies . [2, 4, 5, 7]
- Analyze coiled and torsion springs. [1, 2]
- Select mechanical components from catalogues for machine applications. [2, 5]
- Apply computer aided engineering tools for design optimizations. [2, 5]

### Course topics
Lecture | Topic
1 | Introduction to mechanical transmissions, Determine Energy, power, torque, rpm.
2 | Spur gears 1. Basic geometry and terminologies, gear ratio.
3 | Spur gears 2: Gear pair interference and contact ratio. Force transmission between gears.
4 | Helical gears - basic geometry, efficiency, gear ratio, Force transmission between gears.
5 | Bevel and worm gears - basic geometry, efficiency, gear ratio, Force transmission between gears.
6 | Gear trains. Design, operational characteristics and applications.
7 | Shaft design and Critical speeds of shafts - Introduction to natural frequencies and resonance in systems. Calculations of critical speeds of simplified lumped-mass shaft assemblies.
8 | Shaft hub connections. Keyed and keyless connections. Stresses in keyed connections. Stresses in interference fits.
9 | Rolling contact bearings - Terminologies and bearing life definition. Calculation of bearing loads. Bearing selection from catalogues. Bearing arrangements, mounting and dismounting methods. Bearing fits and tolerances. Basic designation of bearings. Applications of bearings. Types of plain bearings, Bearing loads and PV factor.
10 | Power screws - Basic geometry and terminology, efficiency, torque and power transmission capacities, screw motion analysis.
11 | Springs. Basic geometry and terminologies. Spring materials, stress and deflections. Fatigue analysis of springs. Selection of coil springs from catalogues. Applications of coil springs.
12 | Clutches and brakes. Basic clutches and brake types, inertial effects in rotating machinery, thermal analysis, self-energizing and self locking brakes
13 | CAE tools used in machine design
