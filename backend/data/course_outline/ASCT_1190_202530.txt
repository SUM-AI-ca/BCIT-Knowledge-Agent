### Course Description
ASCT 1190 covers the relationship between the external loads applied to a deformable body and the intensity of internal forces acting within the body. It also involves studying the deformations of the body and the body's stability when it is subjected to external forces.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Define stress and strain, allowable stress and factor of safety.
- Design simple connections.
- Define Hooke's law and Stress / Strain relationship for various materials.
- Calculate elastic deformation, thermal stress and stress concentrations of axially loaded members.
- Calculate deformations and shear stresses due to bending using Flexure and Shear formulas.
- Design a beam.
- Calculate beam deflections using Deflection formula, Method of Superposition and Moment-Area method.
- Analyze combined loadings: Shear and Bending.
- Analyze column buckling applying Euler's formula.
