## Course Description
The new media internship gives students access to a hands-on 300 hour work experience with an on or off-campus client or host agency. The internship is designed to empower students to complete a real-world project, while developing their professional network and portfolio. Under the direction of an adviser, students will find their own placement/assignment (in some instances, support is provided and approved by the adviser). Students are expected to earn genuine real-world experience while refining the skills and knowledge learned throughout the program and better understand the professional expectations of the position they hold during the project. Students will be assessed by their internship securement, completion of internship documentation and workplace performance provided by client or host-organization. Students are required to submit an internship weekly journal entry and meet during the last week of the term to prepare and present a reflection of their work experience.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Integrate knowledge of critical thinking and ethics with effective business practices in dealings with host agencies, clients, colleagues and suppliers.
- Integrate academic and hands-on instruction with practical experience in an industry sector or field of interest.
- Produce a final, industry-quality digital portfolio project that quantifies an internship experience.
- Use current applications of the Adobe cloud creative suite and current new media development tools.
- Decide which areas of the industry are of particular interest for career direction.
- Demonstrate professional skills that pertain directly to the internship experience.
- Increase networking opportunities and acquire industry contacts leading directly to a new media related job or contract.
- Establish a "Terms of Reference" for the industry projects.
- Build a record of work experience.
- Demonstrate strong communication skills.
- Present a summary of work experience to advisor, faculty and peers.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: As set out in BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations, regular attendance in lectures and labs is integral to student success. Therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Students are responsible for communicating with instructors proactively about both potentially approved and unexcused absences. It is the student’s responsibility to work with their instructor to find an effective means to complete work missed due to both approved and unexcused absences. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate. For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation. If absences are the result of a medical or other accommodation, notification from Accessibility Services personnel will be accepted in lieu of a doctor’s note, on an interim basis. A student who has ceased to attend a course without explanation before the withdrawal deadline (two-thirds of the way through the course) will receive a Vanished (V) grade. The V grade is calculated into the GPA as a 0 (zero). These requirements are set out in accordance with both BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations and BCIT Policy 5103 Student Evaluation More information regarding withdrawal deadlines is available at Academic Dates & Deadlines. Academic Integrity and Use of Artificial Intelligence: The unauthorized use of Artificial Intelligence tools (such as ChatGPT) for any part of student work in this course is prohibited, and, when identified by your instructor, will result in an academic integrity violation. - Required resubmission of the work in question for equal or lower grade value;

- A failing grade on the work submitted; or
