## Course Description
​In this third level of the Marine Engineering Knowledge Motor course, students will study the construction, operation, and maintenance of large-bore, low speed marine propulsion diesel engines in detail. Particular attention will be given to the combustion and lubrication processes, including chemical properties of fuel (Liquefied Natural Gas - LNG) and lubricants. Gas exchange process, combustion air supply, and turbocharger performance will be investigated. Students will deepen their learning about the preparation of main machinery to start up, operation, fault detection, and necessary measures to prevent machinery damage, with the help of Propulsion Plant Simulator exercises. Safety and emergency procedures for the operation of propulsion plant machinery will be included in most of the study topics. Students will also be introduced to ship's vibration.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe with the aid of a simple sketch, the construction and maintenance of bedplates, framing, and holding down arrangements of the low speed propulsion diesel engine.
- Describe with the aid of a simple sketch, the construction and maintenance of crankshafts and running gear of the low speed propulsion diesel engine.
- Describe with the aid of a simple sketch, the construction and maintenance of the liner and pistons of the low speed propulsion diesel engine.
- Describe with the aid of a simple sketch, the construction and maintenance of the cylinder heads and valves of the low speed propulsion diesel engine.
- Assess with the aid of a simple sketch, the construction, operation, and maintenance of the fuel injection system in marine diesel propulsion engines.
- Analyze with the aid of a sketch, typical diesel engine indicator diagrams.
- Demonstrate how to calculate indicated power, using dimensions, r.p.m., m.e.p.
- Compare chemical properties of marine fuel, including LNG, and lubricants.
- Assess the fuel atomization and combustion process in a diesel engine, including LNG.
- Describe with the aid of a sketch, scavenging and turbocharging processes of marine diesel engines.
- Describe with the aid of a sketch, the construction, operation, maintenance, and performance evaluation of axial and radial types of turbocharger for large marine diesel engines.
- Develop the safety and emergency procedures for operation of propulsion plant machinery.
- Classify the preparation, operation, fault detection, and necessary measures to prevent damage for main engine.
- Examine engine vibration fundamentals.
