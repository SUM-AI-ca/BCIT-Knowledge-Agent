### Course Description
Endless career opportunities available for the professional Inside Sales Representative, this course is designed for individuals focused on acquiring the tools and attributes to be successful in this profession. Using lectures, case studies, group discussions, and guest speakers, to teach and refine efficient and practical selling techniques, students and existing inside sales professionals will learn how to sell more in less time.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Distinguish the difference between inside sales and telemarketing.
- Recognize the career opportunities available in Inside Sales, from lumber trading to financial advising to selling advertising space.
- Explain the skills and attributes required to become a professional Inside Sales Representative, using the phone as the primary method of communication.
- Develop a practical and efficient sales presentation, encompassing the complete sales cycle, from initial customer contact to the closing and/or getting an act of commitment.

### Course Goals
The primary goal of this course is to enable the student to develop fundamental selling skills for inside sales toward a business development representative role.
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Distinguish the difference between inside sales and telemarketing.
- Recognize the career opportunities available in Inside Sales, from lumber trading to financial advising to selling advertising space.
- Explain the skills and attributes required to become a professional Inside Sales Representative, using the phone as the primary method of communication.
- Develop a practical and efficient sales presentation, encompassing the complete sales cycle, from initial customer contact to the closing and/or getting an act of commitment.
Other areas we will cover are as follows:
1. Understanding the Fundamentals of Selling
- Explain the role of professional selling in the business environment.
- Differentiate between transactional selling and relationship selling.
- Describe the key characteristics and skills of successful sales professionals.
2. Review of the The Selling Process and How to communicate effectively at each step
- Outline the steps in the personal selling process, from prospecting to closing and follow-up.
- Demonstrate effective prospecting techniques to identify potential customers.
- Develop and deliver persuasive sales presentations tailored to customer needs.
- Utilize active listening and effective questioning techniques to uncover customer needs.
3. Building Customer Relationships
- Identify strategies for building and maintaining long-term customer relationships.
- Understand the importance of trust and ethics in selling.
- Apply relationship management techniques to enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty.
4. Sales Communication and Negotiation (Focus on Cold Calls)
- Utilize verbal and non-verbal communication skills to improve sales interactions.
- Develop strategies to handle objections and resistance from customers.
- Apply negotiation techniques to create win-win solutions for buyers and sellers.
5. Sales Technology and CRM (Customer Relationship Management)
- Explain the role of technology in modern sales, including CRM software and data analytics.
- Utilize digital tools and social media for prospecting, engagement, and sales tracking.
- Analyze sales data to improve sales strategies and performance.
6. Ethical and Legal Issues in Selling
- Recognize ethical dilemmas in sales and apply ethical decision-making frameworks.
- Understand legal considerations related to selling, including contracts and consumer protection laws.
- Apply ethical selling practices to foster trust and credibility in customer relationships.
7. Sales Management and Teamwork (How to work with the internal team)
- Describe the role of sales managers in recruiting, training, and motivating sales teams.
- Explain key performance metrics used to evaluate sales performance.
- Develop teamwork and collaboration skills within a sales organization.
