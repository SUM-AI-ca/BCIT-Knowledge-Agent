## Course Description
Printing multi-colour and four-colour process jobs using single-colour and mid-size sheetfed offset presses are the focus of this advanced imaging course. Students identify press systems, adjust the press for optimum operation, perform maintenance, incorporate troubleshooting techniques, and consider quality control issues as well as prepare and install dampeners, and mix printing inks. Digital Press production will be analyzed for colour gamut comparisons and quality issues relating to colour management and proofing. Digital output will be compared to inkjet proofing to evaluate effectiveness in a production environment. Emphasis is placed on job scheduling and planning to maximize production and complete finishing operations.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Use offset printing presses to produce commercially acceptable jobs.
- Make and install lithographic printing plates.
- Create press-ready digital and inkjet proofs.
- Check proofs against final product for quality.
- Control ink and density settings accurately.
- Check press sheet quality as the press is running.
- Install a blanket on the press.
- Make and install dampeners.
- Use anti-offset spray.
- Produce final litho-printed projects in a team setting.
- Demonstrate effective troubleshooting skills on sheetfed offset press simulators.
- Prepare and manage media for litho, electrophotographic, and inkjet imaging.
- Use densitometry and spectrophotometry to colour-manage all imaging devices.
- Compare production press runs to soft and hard proofs from other media.
- Colour-manage a RIP to simulate lithographic, flexographic, digital, and inkjet imaging.

## Course Specific Requirements
This course requires students to adhere to all instructions and safety protocols while in the lab. Practical evaluations will be conducted throughout the course. Failing any of these evaluations will result in the student being removed from the lab and failing the course.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
The Graphic Communications program utilizes a unique print lab facility, with equipment donated by the generous business owners in our community. Students must always respect the equipment in the lab, and comply with the following rules:

- Loose hair or long beards must be tied back or contained in a hat or hair net.
- Remove jewelry, scarves, coats, or loose clothing.
- Gaming, videos, or loud music that can cause distraction are not permitted.
- Mobile devices are not permitted when working around machinery.
- No food or drink in the lab (closed water bottles are allowed at desks only).
- PPE is required to be worn when working with chemicals.
- No video recording or photography is permitted in the lab.

These rules are for the safety of all students using the equipment in the lab and are overseen at the discretion of all faculty. Failure to comply with lab rules will not be tolerated and may result punitive action including dismissal from class, formal warning, course failure, or withdrawal from the program.
