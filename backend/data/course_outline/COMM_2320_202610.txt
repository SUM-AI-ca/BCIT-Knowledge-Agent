### Course Description
COMM 2320 is an applied course building on the skills learned in COMM 1120. The course will enable you to develop the persuasive writing and the presentation skills you will need to succeed in your program and at work.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Give a persuasive proposal presentation using a variety of visual communication techniques and technologies appropriate to a range of purposes and audiences.
- Write an effective negative message using appropriate patterns of organization, style, and tone.
- Research, write, illustrate, and revise a formal proposal.
- Critically evaluate information to determine accuracy, credibility and relevance.
- Develop group management strategies and allocate roles/responsibilities while working collaboratively as a team to complete a task.
- Communicate collaboratively as an effective member of a team while preparing written or oral group projects.
