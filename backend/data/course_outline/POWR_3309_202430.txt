## Course Description
On this practicum, power engineering students become familiar with the actual operation of boilers, turbines, pumps, air compressors, and other auxiliary and pressure vessel equipment typically found in heating plant or industrial steam plants. This course teaches in greater depth the material taught in the Power Engineer Plant Training courses (POWR 1100/2200). Students will go on various field trips to relevant industrial and commercial plants in the local area, and complete assignments that discuss the operation and maintenance of steam plant lab equipment.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:

- Trace and sketch a detailed schematic of the steam, water and condensate lines in the steam lab area.
- Start up, operate and shut down various boilers and auxiliary equipment including deaerator, pumps, turbines and other related equipment.
- Using water treatment lab equipment, test the boiler water, condensate and feedwater.
- Add applicable chemicals to the boiler water, condensate and feedwater in response to results obtained from testing.

## Course Goals
This course will expose students to various types of equipment and systems that are typically found in small to medium sized steam plants.

Identification and understanding of how steam and heating plant equipment is installed operated and maintained

Hands on training in the operation, maintenance and troubleshooting of equipment in the BCIT steam plant lab

Participation in LOTO (lock out tag out) of equipment

Participation in annual boiler inspection, maintenance

Industry plant tours(s) when available
