## Course Description
(UBC BIOT 210) Studies fermenter design and operation. Discusses instrumentation required for biological process. Assesses various methods of downstream processing of fermentation broths. Analyzes economics of fermentation and downstream processing. Presents waste treatment systems with examples taken from biotechnology.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

Bioprocess Engineering

- Describe the scope of bioprocess engineering.
- Review microbial growth kinetics.
- Mass and Energy Balances.

Fermenters

- Operate lab-scale fermenters.
- Describe fermenter configurations including stirred-tank, air-lift, and continuous bioreactors.
- Explain control parameters.
- Describe vessel construction.
- Explain sterilization procedures.

Bioprocess Instrumentation

- Explain the use of bioprocess instrumentation for data acquisition and the proper control of biological process parameters.
- Describe the use of sensors and measurement techniques.
- Explain the function of controllers.
- Describe control strategies.

Downstream Processing

- Assess the requirements for downstream processing of fermentation broth including the following processes:
- Cell disruption
- Centrifugal separation
- Filtration
- Extraction
- Absorption
- Chromatography
- Evaporation and Crystallization
- Dehydration

Preservation and Packaging of Cultures and Products

- Evaluate techniques for the preservation and packaging of cultures and products.
- Explain various preservation and packaging techniques.
- Select the appropriate technique(s) for a given culture or product.

Fluid Systems

- Explain the handling of fluids in bioprocess systems.
- Explain hydraulic theory.
- Describe the use of pumps, piping, valves in fluid systems.

Bioprocess Economics (time permitting)

- Determine the economics of fermentation and downstream processing.
- Calculate capital investment required for a bioprocess system.
- Calculate operating costs for a bioprocess plant.
- Apply costing procedures to determine equipment costs.

Biomass/Biogas Systems (time permitting)

- Analyze the requirements for effective biogas and biomass production systems.
- Describe requirements for liquid waste management systems.
- Describe requirements for solid waste and bio-remedial systems.

## Course Goals
The goal of the course is to provide students with a sufficient understanding of bioprocess systems, sterile product preparation and some related applied skills which will enable them to assist in the production of cultures and products in a biotechnology facility.

## Attendance Requirements
BIOT 5361 will be in person delivery model. Some of the lectures will be recorded online in real time and will be accessed via the learning hub. If you are sick you may attend lecture online in the virtual classroom.

Students are expected to attend all lecture and lab sessions unless you have a serious illness or other medical condition. In the Biotechnology Program, laboratory attendance is of the highest priority since lab experience is what sets this program apart from other undergraduate programs and what prepares students for co-op work terms. Furthermore, lab work is typically done in pairs and students are therefore expected to be present to support their partners. Students must attend all laboratory sessions.

Please notify your instructor and the Biotechnology program head of any absences.
