### Course Description
​Students will work as a team one day a week on a major project related to tourism marketing. This project typically relates to a specific client company and is carried out under the guidance of assigned faculty members.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completio​​n of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop effective public speaking skills by delivering oral presentations (individual and team) to audiences of diverse sizes.
- Identify and define a practical marketing problem or challenge faced by an individual or organization with whom he or she does not have a pre-existing relationship.
- Define the project objectives, conduct appropriate research, develop conclusions, and formulate recommendations to appropriately address the problem or challenge.
- Create an in-depth written report covering all aspects of the project.
- Deliver a face-to-face presentation to the sponsor and the Faculty Advisor about the project.
- Use effective leadership, teamwork and project management skills while working within a self-managed group of students to complete the Business Consulting project.​

### Course Specific Requirements
Please note - the Business Consultling Project requires consistent and rigorous commitment from all team members. Each team member must commit to the meetings, preparation and other working hours assigned for BCP. If a team member is not meeting expectations of Team Charter or BCP, team member(s) may be asked to leave BCP group. Minimum time set aside each week by each team member for BCP is 8 hours. Accountability of 8 hours a week minimum is required.
