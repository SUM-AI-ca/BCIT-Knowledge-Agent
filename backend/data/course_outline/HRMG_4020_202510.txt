### Course Description
The purpose of this course is to develop skills in the utilization of HR data to support evidence based decision-making at an organizational, departmental and initiative level. Methods for data gathering, analysis and reporting will be explored, to measure HR effectiveness and to assess the financial and strategic impact of HR initiatives. Practical methods in the use of workforce analytics, to help focus future investments in human capital, will be introduced.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe the characteristics of meaningful data
- Critically evaluate the limitations and strengths of common HR metrics
- Identify the metrics that best align with a given strategy – measure the right thing
- Develop processes to measure the appropriate data
- Apply analysis techniques to interpret the data, avoiding common pitfalls and challenging assumptions
- Communicate findings of the analysis to effectively impact business decisions, garnering support and buy-in from the organization
- Employ workforce analytics to identify opportunities for strategic initiatives, that address organizational issues

### Attendance Requirements
The instructor expects every student to attend all classes, including lectures and labs. Students' experiences are beneficial to contextualize theoretical ideas and relating the concepts to real-life contexts. In addition, you can demonstrate active learning by participating in class discussions, completing class prep online, contributing to group work, and supporting your peers. An important goal for this course is learning to listen actively, think critically, and effectively communicate ideas in groups and work as a positive and productive contributor. Therefore, the instructor expects students to read assigned materials and prepare for each class. The instructor will note contributions that add value. In addition, the instructor will note individual verbal contributions in small group work and the class environment. To achieve marks for in-class participation, you must: (1) attend the class regularly, (2) participate in the class discussions (by asking questions or making comments), (3) complete activities in small groups, and (4) contribute positively to the learning environment and be respectful of other students. You must be in class unless you have an excused absence to complete and receive credit for the in-class activities. You must inform the instructor in advance if you cannot attend a class.
