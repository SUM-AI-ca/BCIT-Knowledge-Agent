### Course Description
​This course introduces students to computerized payroll systems, including systems such as Intuit's QuickBooks and Sage 300 ERP. QuickBooks is used by many small businesses to pay their employees, while Sage 300 provides a more robust payroll system that will allow students to learn how to pay employees with more complex payroll requirements. This course builds on the fundamental payroll knowledge students will have gained in FMGT 4915, and shows them how the theory works in practice within organizations that use computerized payroll systems.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Add new employees in a computerized system.
- Set up and change all statutory and non-statutory deductions for employees.
- Create and access a pay period.
- Process payroll, verify reports, and pay employees.
- Change employee status and produce Record of Employment.
- Calculate vacation pay and/or accrued vacation balances.
- Calculate and process infrequent pay types, including termination pay, bonuses, and retroactive pay.
- Create new pay codes/benefit codes for employees.
- Set up payroll for a new company.
- Produce and/or verify payroll entries for the company accounting system.
### Learning Resources
Students will use a combination of instructor materials and industry payroll software to develop practical payroll processing skills.
The instructor will provide:
- Lecture notes and demonstrations explaining payroll concepts and procedures.
- Hands-on assignments and practice exercises completed during and outside of class.
- Step-by-step examples and case scenarios to help students practice payroll setup, processing, and reporting.
These materials will support learning outcomes such as:
- Adding employees to a payroll system
- Setting up statutory and non-statutory deductions
- Processing payroll and verifying reports
- Calculating vacation pay and special payments
- Producing Records of Employment
- Creating payroll codes and payroll accounting entries
Students will gain practical experience using industry payroll systems. Software will be installed and supported by the IT department .
- QuickBooks
- Sage 50
These systems will be used to:
- Add and manage employee records
- Create and process payroll runs
- Generate payroll reports
- Set up deductions and benefits
- Produce payroll journal entries
- Manage payroll adjustments such as bonuses, termination pay, and retroactive pay
Students are encouraged to:
- Review instructor examples and demonstrations regularly.
- Practice payroll procedures using the software during scheduled lab time.
- Ask questions and seek clarification during class activities and assignments.
