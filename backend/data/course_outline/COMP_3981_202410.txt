## Course Description
This course provides an introduction to the various topics in artificial intelligence. Topics to be covered include search, games, constraint satisfaction problems, knowledge and reasoning, and learning. Students will develop an understanding of the basic concepts and algorithms used in artificial intelligence. Application of artificial intelligence to problems in different domains will also be discussed.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Explain the key concepts and techniques in artificial intelligence.
- Describe intelligent agents, knowledge-based systems and their architectures.
- Solve problems using uninformed, informed and adversarial search techniques.
- Define and solve constraint satisfaction problems.
- Explain the concepts of knowledge representation and reasoning.
- Explain the basic concepts of learning and the differences between unsupervised, supervised and reinforcement learning.
