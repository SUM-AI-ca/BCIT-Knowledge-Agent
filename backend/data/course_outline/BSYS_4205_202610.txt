### Course Description
​This is an advanced course in Business Information Systems that builds on the material delivered in BSYS 3205 and serves as an introduction to Machine Learning (ML) and Artificial Intelligence (AI). This is a hands-on project-based course that introduces Big Data analytic concepts such as ML, AI, and NoSQL databases in an open source or cloud platform environment. The focus is to use the latest solutions in Deep Learning (DL) to gain better insights and predictions. This course will utilize the student’s background in databases and the Python programming language.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Build and query schema-less data models using MongoDB.
- Explore the Python environment for machine learning development.
- Practice with top big data solutions from Google and Microsoft.
- Examine image/object recognition algorithms and their applications in the field of artificial intelligence.
- Use cloud platforms such as Microsoft Azure to design machine learning experiments.

All course assessments will be evaluated through a combination of the following approaches
- Some assessments will have a rubric shared in advance through the learning hub. Rubrics will ensure students are not just completing assignments, but taking the time to show critical thinking, problem solving, and effective demonstration of the skills developed during each course module.
- Some assessments will have graded observational components.This will be done in class with your instructor using an approach based on the Socratic technique and question based inquiry (details to be shared in class).This assessment will include examples of effective collaboration, reflection, feedback, critical thinking, preparedness, and frequency of presence during discussions.
- Some group based assessments will have individual peer evaluation components. Each student is expected to contribute effectively and equally to group work so this evaluation will allow for differences in grades between individual members of each group (details to be shared in class).Groups will be formed using the same groups as your Term B BCP class and will likely include between 3 and 5 students.
### Attendance Requirements
Attendance: This course will have a significant amount of in person feedback and participation. While physical attendance is not scored, students are strongly encouraged to attend all labs and lectures to ensure the instructor is able to effectively assess your performance in class discussions, and group activities.
Deadlines: All assignments will have clear deadlines outlined in the learning hub. Students are required to submit all assignments on time, unless arrangements are made with the instructor. If you anticipate difficulty meeting a deadline, you are required to contact the instructor during class time or by email prior to the assignment deadline. Extensions may be granted for cases of illness, emergencies, or other valid reasons.
Late Deductions: Without prior arrangements, assignments submitted up to 4 days late will be subject to a 20% deduction. Assignments will not be accepted beyond 4 days late because the main purpose of deliverables in this class is to give formative feedback that can be incorporated into your future deliverables (this is particularly important on the project).
### Course Specific Requirements
Personal/Lab Computers: This is a BYOD course that requires you to bring your laptop to all classes. Classes will not be held in labs with computers so you must be able to manage and maintain your own software to complete the various activities. The BCIT library can provide access to laptops and lab computers.
Generative AI: In this application based class, the learning outcomes showcase your knowledge and skills built throughout this program. Use of AI, along with other online resources in support of developing ideas, concepts and research is allowed. Students using these tools (including AI) must reference them and be able to demonstrate and include a critical evaluation of these reference tools in their work. Failure to correctly reference or include your critique of the output will result in lower grades on assessments.
