## Course Description
This course covers the basics of Autodesk Revit in an architectural environment, from design through construction documentation. Students will be introduced to the tools and concepts of working with a fully parametric building modeler.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Use a project-based learning approach throughout the major steps of a project design.
- Documenting the design.
- Working with roofs and floors.
- Creating schedules.
- Detailing with Autodesk Revit Building.
- Worksharing with Autodesk Revit Building.
- Understanding workset fundamentals.

## Course Goals
- Provide students with an understanding of BIM concepts and the advantages of BIM over conventional CAD drafting
- Familiarize students with the essential modeling and annotation tools available in Revit, allowing them to be productive members of a Revit project team
- Equip students with the knowledge required to take a project from schematic design through to working drawings in the Revit environment, and the workflow of a typical project

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Session: Outline: Tests + Assignments
1: General Introduction; User Interface; Coordinate Systems: Complete student profile
2: The Revit Process – Creating a Project; Levels and Grids; Revit’s Editing Toolset: Assignment 2.1, 2.2, 2.3
3: Views and Sheets: Midterm Exam 1 Assignment: Adding Views and Sheets to a Project
4: Assemblies, Walls and Curtain Walls: Start term project Assignment: Placing Walls, Windows and Doors
5: Roofs: Footprint + Extruded; Floors: Exercise: Editing a Flat Roof; Creating a Sloped Roof
6: Families: Component and In-Place: Save in window family done in class
7: Stairs, Railings and Ceilings: Assignment: Adding Stairs and Railings
8: Rooms, Areas and Schedules: Midterm Exam 2
9: Topography and Shared Coordinates; Working with CAD; Collaboration; Worksets
10: Annotating and Detailing
11: Rendering and Presentation Graphics
12: Wrap-Up: Final Exam Term Project Due
