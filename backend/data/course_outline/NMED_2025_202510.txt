### Course Description
Examines the chemical, physical and biological characteristics of radiopharmaceuticals, the concepts of biological compartments and biorouting, and common clinical applications of radiopharmaceuticals for diagnostic and therapeutic procedures. Provides an introduction to non-radioactive drugs used for interventive nuclear medicine procedures. Students will practice radiopharmacy procedures in a simulated environment to gain experience in radiopharmaceutical dispensing, quality control procedures, radiation safety, inventory control, record keeping, radiopharmacy lab manager software applications, and laboratory techniques for non-imaging in vivo procedures.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Categorize radiopharmaceutical compounds according to chemical, physical and biological characteristics.
- Discuss the concepts of biological compartments and radiopharmaceutical biorouting.
- Examine the relationship between radiopharmaceutical characteristics, clinical applications and the development of protocols for diagnostic and therapeutic procedures.
- Describe mechanisms of localization, biodistribution and excretion for individual radiopharmaceuticals.
- Recognize clinical image patterns and variants of normal radiopharmaceutical biodistribution.
- Investigate and respond to common adverse reactions to radiopharmaceuticals.
- Identify applications of non-radioactive drugs used for interventive nuclear medicine procedures.
- Relate knowledge of radiopharmaceutical biodistribution to internal dosimetry calculations and the concept of target organ.
- Relate knowledge of radiopharmaceutical biodistribution to promote best practices regarding radiation safety for patient, public and workers.
- Apply principles of safe practice in a radiopharmacy.
- Prepare radiopharmaceuticals in accordance with work schedule requirements.
- Perform and assess quality control procedures on radiopharmaceutical preparations.
- Evaluate dose calibrator performance.
- Appraise decision options based on resource implications.
- Maintain radiopharmacy inventory and records.
- Use radiopharmacy lab manager system software applications.
- Perform lab techniques required for non-imaging in vivo procedures.
