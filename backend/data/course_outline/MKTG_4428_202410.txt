## Course Description
The course provides an overview of current issues facing the professional sales representative. Students will be able to anticipate and adapt to the latest trends and events that will impact their professional lives. Course content will focus on current research, issues, and dilemmas facing the professional sales representative.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop effective public speaking skills by delivering oral presentations (individual and team) to audiences of diverse sizes.
- Describe the current issues affecting professional salespeople.
- Explain several current issues facing major local industries and sales organizations.
- Access a wide range of sources for business news and interpret current events that impact salespeople.
- Discuss the key technologies that support sales efforts.
- Evaluate and apply technologies available for time planning, organization and self-management.
- Expand online business networking using LinkedIn.
- Implement business profiling skills in preparation for employment interviews.
- Apply a variety of communication skills during the employment interview.
- Discuss the newest training and techniques offered to sales organizations by leading sales trainers, authors and professional speakers.
- Describe the latest value creation methods for salespeople.
- Apply telephone communication techniques to a variety of cold calling and selling situations.
