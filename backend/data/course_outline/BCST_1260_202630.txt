### Course Description
Film captures the attention of audiences and brings stories to life. Having a solid foundation in camera skills is essential in this modern media age. This course is designed for students interested in the basics of video and film camera operation; Students will explore professional camera set up, lighting, sound recording and editing of scenes. The art of sequencing your shots to tell a story both technically and aesthetically is also introduced. The hands-on approach enables students to gain experience and confidence using a professional video camera to shoot popular scenes used in news, corporate, online and film production.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate the proper procedures for setting up a camera.
- Shoot a scene using correct white balance and differentiate colour balances.
- Choose (and execute) the proper exposure of the camera given the lighting for a specific shot or sequence.
- Explain how to professionally sequence a scene.
- Use a microphone to capture professional quality audio.
- Use a professional video camera to shoot a professional scene.
- Compare and contrast different video camera types, and select the appropriate video camera given a specific scenario.
- Properly set up a time code in a camera for the appropriate shooting scenario.

### Course Schedule and Assignments
BCST 1260 PROFESSIONAL VIDEO CAMERA OPERATIONS COURSE OUTLINE
Class 01: Introduction to the Course and Video Production
● Introduction to the course and its objectives.
● Overview of the syllabus and assessment methods.
● Importance of storytelling, images and video.
Understanding Cameras and Components
● Evolution of video cameras from analog to digital formats.
● Different types of cameras: camcorders, DSLRs, mirrorless, cinema cameras.
● Components and functions of a camera: lenses, sensors, viewfinders, controls.
● Lab Activity: Familiarization with camera controls and basic settings.
Mastering Exposure Triangle and Color Balance
● Understanding the exposure triangle: aperture, shutter speed, ISO.
● Introduction to color temperature and white balance adjustments.
● In-Class Assignment 01: Hands-on practice with exposure and white balance settings.
Class 02: Role of Audio in Video Production
● Understanding how audio complements visual storytelling.
● Different types of microphones and their applications in different shooting scenarios.
● Techniques for recording clean and clear audio in various environments.
● In-Class Assignment 02: Students practice recording, paying attention to clarity and quality.
Class 03: Focus Techniques and Depth of Field
● Depth of field and its creative applications.
● Focus techniques: manual, auto-focus, and focus tracking.
● Lab Activity: Capturing shots with varying depth of field, applying both manual and
auto-focus techniques.
Principles of Composition and Shot Sizes
● Principles of composition: rule of thirds, leading lines, framing.
● Exploring shot sizes and their impact on viewer engagement.
● In-Class Assignment 03: Capturing a series of images, applying the rule of thirds and
experimenting with framing techniques.
Class 04: Camera Angles, Movement & Shooting Sequences
● Camera angles and movement and their impact on storytelling.
● In-Class Assignment 04: Applying composition techniques in practical shooting scenarios
with different camera angles and basic movement technique.
Class 05: Importance of Lighting and Types of Equipment
● The crucial role of lighting in setting the tone and mood of a scene.
● Types of lighting equipment and their characteristics.
● Lab Activity: Students set up a basic lighting arrangement and compare its effects on the
subject.
Three-Point Lighting Setup and Mood Creation
● Three-point lighting setup: key light, fill light, backlight.
● Using lighting to create different moods and effects: dramatic, soft, and natural.
● In-Class Assignment 05: Students experiment with various lighting setups to create
different moods and atmospheres.
Class 6: Final Project Planning and Filming
● Final Project: Guidelines for filming and capturing footage for the final projects.
● Planning, pre-production, assigning roles and responsibilities for the final projects.
● Lab Activity: Students film their final video projects, applying all the skills they have
learned throughout the course.
● Final Project: Students showcase their final video projects to the class.
● Discussion: Reflecting on the creative choices made during the project, and lessons
learned throughout the course.
