## Course Description
​This course is designed to improve student ability to apply a balanced scorecard approach to increase organizational performance. Students will identify leading and lagging key performance indicators (KPIs) that are commonly measured and contribute to a firm's strategic success. This course moves beyond theory to build on data modelling and business intelligence skills to build and implement a performance management dashboard using Excel & Power BI.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Assess how organizations can profit from strategic agility.
- Evaluate an organization's performance using financial and nonfinancial measures.
- Identify the impact of gaps between a firm's objectives, measures, targets, and strategic initiatives.
- Evaluate the role of performance dashboards in strategic and operational management.
- Identify common key performance metrics that drive performance.
- Analyze the attributes of effective performance measures.
- Recommend and critically defend a balanced approach to strategic management.
- Apply critical thinking to design effective performance management dashboards.
- Develop a performance management dashboard using Microsoft Excel & Power BI.

Communication & Collaboration

Contribution to Group Work

- Provides valuable insights, ideas, and contributions to the group's work.
- Takes an active role in project planning, organization, and execution.
- Demonstrates a willingness to take on additional tasks or responsibilities.

Professionalism & Respect

- Maintains a professional and respectful attitude toward group members.
- Upholds ethical standards and treats others with fairness and integrity.
- Resolves conflict constructively and seeks common ground.
- Demonstrates a positive and cooperative demeanour.

You will complete the workbook provided to provide a 0-4 rating for each criterion.

- 0 = Inadequate
- 1 = Needs Improvement
- 2 = Satisfactory
- 3 = Good
- 4 = Excellent

Peer Reviews for each student will be averaged and will contribute 10% of the Capstone Project evaluation.

If you are having significant challenges with a group member during the course, it is your responsibility to try to remedy the situation. If you cannot, you must let your instructor know immediately. Waiting to bring up concerns until the end of term Peer Review limits the ability for a solution to be found.

Here is an example of how Peer Reviews impact overall Capstone evaluation:
