## Course Description
​The capstone project is a directed study course in which students continue to develop skills required for office administrators in simulated working environments. Students will apply best industry practices to develop documents, spreadsheets, presentations, databases, a website with a focus on employment considerations. To manage tasks and meet deadlines, students will work collaboratively using communication tools adopted by the industry. All students will have scheduled meetings with the instructor to track their progress and receive feedback. Evaluation is based on performance on weekly projects.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Utilize professional skills - such as effective communication, conflict resolution, stress management, and team skills in office environments.
- Create documents, spreadsheets, presentations, databases, and websites.
- Identify possible and preferable future career directions and opportunities within an area of interest.
- Collect a personal library of solutions to industry challenges (“lessons learned").​
