### Course Description
The first of two consecutive courses that cover human anatomy and physiology of relevance to the study and practice of radiation therapy. This course consists of an introductory review of relevant cytology and histology, and comprehensive study of the following systems: the skeletal system, the musculoskeletal system, the nervous system and the endocrine system. This course supplements typical considerations of anatomical structure and function with explorations of relational and sectional anatomy to prepare for the diverse imaging modalities encountered in clinical practice.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the foundational principles of cytology and histology and how they relate to the structure and function of tissues, organs and organ systems
- Describe the anatomy and physiology as well as the relevant relational and sectional anatomy of the following organ systems:
- Skeletal system
- Musculoskeletal system
- Nervous system
- Endocrine system
- Understand the foundational principles of the study and use of sectional anatomy, including how to visualize 2D sections of the body along the three cardinal body planes, and how to view and interpret 2D sections of the body that are represented using various imaging modalities (ex. CT, MRI).
- Relate and apply an understanding of anatomical and physiological principles to radiation therapy practice.

### Course topics
I: INTRODUCTION
- Descriptive Anatomy Terminology
- Review of Biology Essentials (self-directed online module + quiz)
II: HISTOLOGY
- Epithelium
- Connective Tissues
- Serous Membranes
- The Integument
III: SKELETAL SYSTEM
- Long Bone Anatomy, Compact vs Spongy Bone
- Bone Cells
- Processes of Ossification, Bone Growth, Bone Remodeling
- Calcium Homeostasis
- Synovial and Solid Joints, Synovial Joint Classification
IV: MUSCULOSKELETAL SYSTEM
- Skeletal Muscle Tissue, Physiology of Skeletal Muscle Contraction
- The Upper Extremity Major Joints (Glenohumeral, Elbow and Wrist) Major Upper Extremity Muscle Groups Upper Extremity Blood Supply and Drainage Brachial Plexus
- The Lower Extremity Major Joints (Hip, Knee, Ankle) Major Lower Extremity Muscle Groups Lower Extremity Blood Supply and Drainage Lumbosacral Plexus
V. NERVOUS SYSTEM
- Neurons and Neuroglia
- Neuron Physiology
- Anatomy of the Cerebrum, Diencephalon, Brainstem and Cerebellum
- Transmission of Sensory and Motor Information
- Peripheral Nerves, Cranial Nerves and Reflexes
- Autonomic Nervous System
- Protection of the CNS Bony Protection (Skull and Vertebral Column) Meninges and Venous Sinuses
- Blood-Brain Barrier
- Ventricular System
- Cerebral Blood Supply and Drainage
VI. ENDOCRINE SYSTEM
- Hormones
- Anatomy of the Hypothalamus, Pituitary Gland, Hypophyseal Portal System
- Pituitary Gland Hormones and Associated Actions
- Anatomy and Physiology of the Thyroid and Parathyroid Glands
- Anatomy of Physiology of the Adrenal Glands and Endocrine Pancreas
