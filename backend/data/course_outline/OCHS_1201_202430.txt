## Course Description
Introduces concepts of how accidents are caused and provides evidence to support the analysis and investigation of these causes. Analyzes a variety of sources, from historical perspectives to current behavioural theories, and their application to today's workplaces. Discusses accident investigation and interview techniques, and their legal, moral, and ethical implications. Reviews and analyzes a number of accident scenarios throughout the course. (This course is equivalent to OCHS 1200.)

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
After successfully completing this course, you will have sufficient information to:

- Describe the management principles involved in a total loss prevention program.
- Conduct various incident/accident investigations to determine the root causes and recommend corrective measures.
- Describe several common causes of incidents and accidents.
- Develop several approaches to promote safety and behavioural change in the workplace.
- Conduct an effective interview of witnesses in an incident or accident investigation.
- Complete a comprehensive incident or accident report.

## Course Goals
- Introduce the concepts of loss prevention and total loss control management
- Introduce the concepts of how accident are caused and provide evidence to support the analysis and investigation of these causes
- Develop knowledge and skills required to conduct and accident/incident investigation
- Develop effective witness interview techniques
- Provide hands-on application of loss causation models and analysis by means of incident/accident scenario case studies
- Describe the management principles involved in a total loss control management program
- Conduct various incident/accident investigations to determine root causes and provide recommendations and action planning for remedies
- Describe several common causes of accidents and incidents
- Develop knowledge and skills in completing comprehensive accident investigation reports.
