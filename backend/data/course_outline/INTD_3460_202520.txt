## Course Description
​Students will be introduced to Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) as it applies to interior design. ​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- View a given design model in Virtual Reality (VR) with an online resource, based on an industry standard procedure.
- Present a given design model in Augmented Reality (AR) with a smart phone app, based on an industry standard procedure.
- Navigate a given design model in a fully immersive Virtual Reality (VR) environment, base on an industry standard procedure.​

## Learning Resources
Students will be introduced to Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) as it applies to Interior Design. This course will be held in a BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) environment. Students must supply their own mobile device, capable of running Google Cardboard. (Students are advised to back-up their device before class, to avoid the possibility of losing photos, etc.). Students must also have access to a PC, capable of running SketchUp and Revit. (Windows environment required for Revit).
