### Course Description
Big Data continues to have a significant impact on business. This course serves as an introduction to the key aspects of how business intelligence is used in current business practices. Students will build business intelligence knowledge through the practical hands-on development of data analysis software and other applications and apply this knowledge by analyzing information to make business decisions.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student wil be able to:
- Evaluate the functions and components of a Business Intelligence system.
- Process data sets using a Business Analytics system.
- Plan Business Intelligence and Analytics technology implementations.
- Develop reports and support business decision making using various Business Intelligence tools.
- Assess the functionality of local and Cloud Computing applications for Business Intelligence.
- Evaluate emerging trends in Business Intelligence.
### Learning Resources
This course will require the use of Microsoft Excel and PowerBI software which work best on a windows based computer (which will be provied in lab if needed). In our first week we will go over software requirements in more detail and ensure you are able to install and use all required tools for this course.
There is no course textbook and instead online reference articles and open resources (free to use) will be shared on the learning hub each week.
### Course Goals
Business Intelligence (BI) is a big topic with a long history. This course will focus on the introductory BI skills you would use in a business setting to make more informed decisions with data. A large part of this course will focus on using practical business intelligence tools, data modelling, visualization, and communication techniques.
