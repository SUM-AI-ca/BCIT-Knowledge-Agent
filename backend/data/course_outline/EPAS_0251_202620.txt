### Course Description
​This course is designed for students aiming for the Low B2 level of English proficiency. It focuses on helping learners succeed in both their BCIT programs and the professional workplace. By participating in this course, students improve their reading, writing, grammar, and vocabulary while also building the teamwork skills needed to work effectively with others. The course uses hands-on learning, cultural awareness, and specific study strategies to help learners reach their academic and career goals. In this class, learners read a variety of different texts to understand how authors change their writing style for different audiences and purposes. Students then practice using these same techniques in their own writing. They also begin to develop research skills by learning how to find, evaluate, and combine information from several different sources. This helps them create more professional and organized academic work. A major goal of the course is to improve the clarity, organization, and precision of written communication for the professional world. Students practice writing professional emails, short position papers, technical paragraphs, and reports. Throughout this process, learners expand their knowledge of B2 vocabulary and grammar. They also work on ensuring they can use B2-level grammar features accurately in all of their written assignments. By the end of the course, students will be better prepared to handle the communication requirements of a technical or professional environment.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the content and relevance of news items, articles, and reports on a wide range of technical academic topics in order to decide whether the texts have the information needed to complete a task or are worthy of further study
- Demonstrate understanding of articles and reports of up to 850 words on modern-day problems in which writers share particular viewpoints
- Research a topic by reading simple technical and academic texts
- Write an essay or report of up to 300 words which develops an argument, giving reasons in support of or against a particular point of view and explaining the advantages and disadvantages of various options
- Write a short, precise, descriptive report of up to 300 words to promote a product
- Synthesize information and arguments from a number of sources
- Demonstrate understanding of informal and formal emails and messages related to school or work
- Respond to informal and formal emails and messages, related to school or work, clearly responding to the sender's points and arguments
- Identify and reflect on similarities and differences in cultural behaviours and discuss their significance​
### Learning Resources
Students require writing instruments (pens/pencils) and paper for in-class note-taking and assessments, or alternatively a laptop computer for digital notes. A binder or another way to organize printed course materials distributed in class is also required.
### Course Goals
This course develops the English language, academic, and professional communication skills needed for success in BCIT programs and the workplace at a low B2 level of proficiency. Learning activities focus on improving reading, writing, grammar, and vocabulary. Students also develop cultural awareness, study strategies, and the ability to communicate clearly, accurately, and professionally for a variety of academic and workplace purposes.

### Other information
Generative AI Use
Generally, the use of AI is not allowed for assignments or assessments in this course. If the use of AI tools (e.g. Chat GPT) is permitted for a part of an assignment, your instructor will indicate that in the assignment instructions. When AI tools are permitted and you use them, you are responsible for ensuring that the content you submit under your name adheres to the course’s expectations. You must also acknowledge use of AI tools, e.g. I acknowledge the use of [name the tool] in the preparation of my assignment. Using AI tools without authorization is a violation of BCIT’s Student Code of Academic Integrity .
