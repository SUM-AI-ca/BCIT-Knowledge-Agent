### Course Description
Graphical presentation of statistical data including Pareto analysis. Measures of central tendency and variation. Binomial and Normal probability distributions. Confidence limits and hypothesis testing for means and proportions. Regression and correlation. If time permits, chi-squared test for independence. Applications to data and problems relevant to Occupational Health Technology.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Interpret and produce bar, pie, histogram and Pareto charts.
- Compute and understand the meaning of: mean, median, range, and standard deviation.
- Solve statistical problems using the binomial or normal probability distributions.
- Carry out calculations and statistical tests related to sample means, sample proportions, chi-squared distribution, and correlation and regression.
- Apply the above concepts and techniques to topics in the field of occupational health and safety.
- Demonstrate fluency with a calculator for all relevant applications.

### Course Goals
The goal of this course is to provide the student with the mathematical skills required to apply basic statistical methods to problems arising in occupational health and safety. The student will solve problems in the following areas:
- descriptive statistics
- discrete and continuous probability distributions
- statistical estimation and hypothesis testing, including linear regression and correlation and goodness-of-fit tests
