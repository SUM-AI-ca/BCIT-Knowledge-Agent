## Course Description
This course builds on business-to-business selling skills and enables the student to apply methods to develop, manage, and retain business customers. Students will learn and apply contemporary account management methods, including key account selling. Key topics include B2B buyer behaviour, consultative selling, client management (and CRM), time & territory management, sales call planning, selling to the c-suite, request for proposals, and selling in a non-profit environment. A core element of this course will be cold calling for a non-profit fundraising project and managing the relationship of new and existing accounts.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop effective public speaking skills by delivering oral presentations (individual and team) to audiences of diverse sizes.
- Apply the principles of account management in business-to-business sales situations.
- Analyze your selling style and the buying style of the customer.
- Adapt your selling style to the buying style of your customer.
- Explain how to adapt to C-level executives and differentiate between these and other business buyers.
- Differentiate between transactional selling and partnering/ consultative selling.
- Formulate CRM Strategies and techniques for gaining account penetration at higher levels and increasing customer and territory margins.
- Prepare written informal and formal RFP proposals and differentiate with RFQs and RFIs.
- Sell companies in the non-profit environment.
