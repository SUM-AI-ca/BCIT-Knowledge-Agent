## Course Description
Cooperative Education (co-op) integrates relevant work experience (also called sea phases) within the Nautical Sciences Diploma program. Students will enhance their educational experience during the sea phases by applying the knowledge gained throughout the previous term, while meeting the requirements of Transport Canada for the number of hours at sea. Sea phases are conducted with reputable marine companies.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of Co-operative Training 2, the student will be able to:

- Complete the practical tasks as listed in the On Board Training Record Book (ISF) for Officers in Charge of a Navigational Watch (Deck Cadets) to include aspects of navigation, cargo handling and stowage, and ship operations (compliance with applicable regulations, maintenance of life saving appliances, and ship maintenance).
- Integrate and apply the global knowledge acquired during the term in a workplace environment by performing chartwork, outlining navigation safety, interpreting meteorology, analyzing ship construction and cargo operations, and demonstrating general ship knowledge.
- Work productively as a team member.
- Draw various diagrams to depict the outlays of the ship.
- Explain given processes that are carried out onboard a ship.

## Evaluation Criteria
The Cadet’s progress during the sea phase is monitored by the Co-op Coordinator, and their performance on the job is supervised by the Cadet’s employer.

The work term consists of the following three evaluation components:

1. A completed and satisfactory Sea Phase Assignment 2

2. Completed a minimum of 80% of the tasks listed in the ISF Cadet Training Record Book

3. A minimum of 60 days of Deck Cadet sea time

Items 1 and 2 are assessed by the Department Head. Item 3 is assessed by the Co-op Coordinator.

The following course grades may be assigned:

All of the evaluation components above have been submitted and assessed as Satisfactory

One or more of the evaluation components have been assessed as Unsatisfactory or is missing

Unsatisfactory Sea Phase Assignments and Cadet Record Books will be returned to the Cadet with accompanying notes for suggested improvement by the Department Head within three weeks. The Cadet must then re-submit the revised sea-phase assignment and/or Cadet Record Book to the Department Head within seven (7) calendar days of receipt for re-assessment.

A ‘U’ will be assigned if, after 30 days of the end of the sea phase, any component is still deemed to be Unsatisfactory.

## Course Specific Requirements
Note: Transport Canada Marine Safety and Security (TCMSS) requires 100% of the tasks in the Cadet Training Record Book be completed prior to attempting an oral examination for a Certificate of Competency (CoC). It is strongly recommended that the student completes as many tasks in the Cadet Training Record Book as possible during their co-op term(s).

## Other information
Sea Phase Administration and Documentation

Documentation:

You must take the following ORIGINAL documents with you:

1. A Canadian passport (valid 6 months past the sea phase end date)

2. Transport Canada Discharge Book

3. ISF Cadet Training Record Book

4. Transport Canada Marine Medical Certificate (valid past the sea phase end date)

5. Marine Emergency Duties Certificates of Proficiency (CoP):

- Marine Advanced First Aid
- STCW Basic Safety
- STCW Survival Craft

6. Vessel Personnel with Security Duties (VPWSD) – applicable to international voyages

7. Any other Transport Canada certificates you have received

It is strongly recommended to bring photocopies of all these documents with you, and store photocopies in your luggage. Ensure that you keep the originals on you at all times during travel (do not put originals in your checked luggage).

Sea Time:

360 days of Cadet sea time (as assessed by Transport Canada) is the minimum required by Transport Canada to be eligible to obtain a Watchkeeping Mates Certificate of Competency. Transport Canada assesses sea time by reviewing the Sea Testimonials and Discharge Book. It is the Cadet’s responsibility to ensure that the ship properly fills out the discharge book, and to ensure that complete sea testimonials are obtained.

Transport Canada Sea Testimonials:

One of the most valuable documents you must obtain throughout your sea phase is the Transport Canada Sea Testimonials. It is through these documents that the examiner at Transport Canada assesses your sea time which will allow you to proceed to your next certification.

1. Ensure that an entry is made in each empty space available.

2. Ensure that the ship’s stamp is showing on each testimonial.

3. Have a testimonial completed for each Master. If your ship changes Masters, obtain testimonials prior to his/her departure.

Please note that only original copies will be accepted by Transport Canada. If you require more copies of the Transport Canada Testimonials, visit https://wwwapps.tc.gc.ca/Corp-Serv-Gen/5/Forms-Formulaires/search.aspx. The Testimonial Form for Sea Service is 82-0546.

Cadets must ensure completion of sea testimonials and discharge book entries are made prior to disembarking their ships.
