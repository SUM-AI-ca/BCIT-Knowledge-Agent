## Course Description
This course explores new media storytelling for personal, social and corporate branding. Students will learn about structuring effective narrative of digital media through the creation of written, video, web, and interactive projects. Storytelling techniques in video game, web design, and film will be evaluated. Students develop a critical perspective on how to engage and inspire various audiences across several platforms. The course will also prepare students with the means to choose digital media that best expresses their ideas and to create projects that are in accordance with their interests and career goals. Students will be assessed by a combination of group and individual projects, final exam and a term project.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Research and structure narratives for personal, social and corporate storytelling.
- Create narrative projects with digital media that reflect artistic and marketing practices.
- Map and formulate an effective communication strategy to articulate stories from planning to execution.
- Capture and manipulate digital media.
- Discuss and include sequences and transitions, rhythm, repetition and subjectivity in digital storytelling.
- Integrate different media into a seamless interactive environment.
- Critique and offer their own perspective in analyzing different types of digital stories.
