## Course Description
Two essential parts of automated control systems installations are measurement and control. This course will introduce various transducers that are encountered in automated control systems as this foundation is necessary for the installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting of analogue devices and programmable devices. Students will make power, signal, and communication connections for the programmable relay and interpret and write programs. Numerous troubleshooting exercises will be completed. Topics covered include installation, interfacing, closed loop control, trouble-shooting and testing, safety, and an introduction to monitoring.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Select, install, connect, maintain and troubleshoot basic functions of PLCs.
- Configure PLC programming and operating Software.
- Create ladder and function block programs and test them in simulation and connected to lab peripherals.
- Set up communication between a PLC and a PC locally.
- Install, connect, maintain, repair, and troubleshoot Sensors and Transducers in Automated Control systems.
- Install, connect, maintain, repair, and troubleshoot common feedback and communication systems in basic Automated Control Open and Closed Loop Systems.
