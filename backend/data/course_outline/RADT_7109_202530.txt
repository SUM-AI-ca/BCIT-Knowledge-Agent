### Course Description
Continues from RADT 6109, enables completion of research under guidance from committee of advisors. Students submit for evaluation a research paper on the analysis of the results, findings and conclusions. The oral presentation consists of an overview of the final research results.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Refine research skills by completing research methodology and data analysis.
- Formulate conclusions based on their initial research question.
- Complete a research paper that clearly outlines the research project question, research methodology and conclusions.
- Participate in the education of peers and other radiation therapy health care professionals.

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Research projects will be assessed using the following criteria:
Analysis of data/research information collected Data/information gathering methods are concisely described. Data/information gathering methods are appropriate to the address the research question. Analysis of data/information collected is appropriate, accurate and conclusive.
Analysis of reliability/validity of data/research information collected Within scope of research methodology, student strived to ensure consistent and meaningful data was collected. Limitations to research methodology are described. Methods to improve research methodology are suggested.
Statements of summary and conclusions Conclusions are clearly stated, non-contradictory. Conclusions are substantiated by evidence presented & relevant to the question. Impact on radiation therapy practice is identified. Suggestions for further research are identified.
Research paper style & completeness Content of research paper is concise with a logical sequence of information provided. Paper (journal article) is comprehensive with clear & concise description of research question, methodology, data analysis and conclusions Paper is written in proper research format, with adherence to MLA (Modern Language Association) or APA (American Psychological Association) format.
Research skills and Communications: Demonstrates critical thinking in analysis of data/information collected and conclusions made. Demonstrates cohesiveness between written paper and oral presentation. Demonstrates knowledge of topic; can speak to and defend content. Has progressed at a steady pace, has adhered to suggested timelines. Demonstrated effective & regular communications with Advisor(s).
