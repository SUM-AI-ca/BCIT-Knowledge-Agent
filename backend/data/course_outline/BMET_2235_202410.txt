## Course Description
​In this course, students will carry out guided independent investigations to explore various selected topics or to solve a given problem. Students will collaborate in small teams to discuss the issue, design experiments that will provide more information, construct and validate the experimental setup, collect and analyze the data, and report the results. In some cases, the results of an investigation may lead to new questions to be explored further as time permits. The course will combine academic content from allied disciplines, which may provide the context for the investigation, or where the investigation provides the context in which content is applied. For example, topics from Physics, Biology or Chemistry may provide the context for an investigation, while Math and Communications are applied to analyze and communicate the results of the investigation.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Collaborate with others to carry out an independent investigation:
- Formulate specific questions to be answered, based on a given general problem.
- Design experiments to provide the data required to answer the questions.
- Construct the experimental setup required to collect the data.
- Validate the experimental setup.
- Collect required data.
- Analyze the data for validity, relevance and significance.
- Use the results to justify any conclusions or decisions to be made.
- Communicate effectively with others to coordinate team activities.
- Explain findings during a presentation.
- Analyze data using Excel spreadsheets by comparison against theoretical data models.
- Illustrate data using basic chart features in Excel spreadsheets.
- Design and create a poster presentation, which effectively communicates the investigation and its major findings.
- Write an informative, well-structured report documenting the investigation and its major findings.

## Course topics
Students will be assigned four separate investigations to be carried out concurrently:

- Inquiry into the diversity of physiological parameters: Students will examine some of the human physical and physiological parameters required to design a biomedical device. Data will be collected following ethical principles of research, including informed consent, and privacy & confidentiality. Data will be analyzed using basic statistical techniques as needed to establish parameters for device design.
- Exploration of design characteristics that influence mechanical strength of an object: Students will modify an existing design for a simple mechanical object to improve its mechanical strength, subject to certain constraints. Modified designs will be created using SolidWorks, fabricated using 3D printers, and stress tested to failure to evaluate their performance. Students will compare each others' design approaches to draw general conclusions about the impact of mechanical design features on strength.
- Interpret manufacturers' datasheets to fabricate a working circuit: Students will be given the key components of a simple wireless communication system along with corresponding data sheets. Students need to interpret the datasheets to determine the actual wiring diagram and specifications for missing components. Students will assemble and test their circuit to demonstrate a working communication link.
- Solve an assistive device problem: Students will be introduced to the Makers Making Change community and will be presented with a simple problem to be solved based on actual clients' needs. Students will design and fabricate a prototype device to solve that problem. Hopefully, the best solution will be implemented for real clients.

Process:

- Teamwork: students will work in teams of two (or three, to avoid students having to work alone)
- Planning: students will plan their activities and document their plans
- Documentation: students will document their designs, experimental data, analysis and conclusions
- Analysis: students will use appropriate spreadsheet tools to analyze data and extract meaning
- Reviews: documentation will be reviewed by team mates and instructors as early and as often as possible to catch and resolve problems
- Presentation: students will present their accomplishments and findings to their classmates and instructors
