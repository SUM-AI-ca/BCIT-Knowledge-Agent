## Course Description
Explores the general concepts of legislation relevant to the safety field. The history of the Canadian legal system sets the tone for introducing the concepts of workers' compensation, safety regulation, due diligence, consultation, and enforcement. Critically compares OH&S systems in Canada, the United States, and around the world. Introduces Canadian federal legislation and other safety regulations. You need access to a copy of your governing OH&S legislation. (This course is equivalent to OCHS 2100.)

## Course Goals
Provide students with a broad overview of Occupational Health and Safety Legislation in various jurisditions around the word including: how different jurisdictions manage legislation in OHS and compensation. Ensure students have an understanding of basic basic law concepts underlining legislation, common legislation throughout Canada/ western society, legilsation in Canada, United States and the United Kingdom. Provide students with knowledge of similarities and differences in approaches to health and safety legislation which will assist them with development of approaches to managing health and safety compensation effectively.
