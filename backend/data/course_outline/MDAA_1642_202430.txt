## Course Description
​In this course, students will explore the fundamentals of animation using specialized 3D software. They will be introduced to posing, timing, key framing, interpolation, and how they are applied in 3D animation. Through assignments and exercises, students will practice with industry standard software to develop animation for models and props that will help them gain the foundations of animation.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Apply the principles of animation.
- Create, save and edit animations and projects.
- Control the interpolation of animated objects.
- Apply timing and spacing between animation key frames.
- Navigate the graph editor.
- Apply overlap and offset actions and animations.
- Create pose to pose animation.
- Animate an animation cycle.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Week: Topics: Handed Out: Assignment Due
Week 1 - September 5: Introduction to 3D Space and Primitive Assets. World Local Space. Shotgrid
Week 2: Keyframing. Reference. Action Analysis Hierarchy and Animation. Interpolation.
Week 3: Function Curves, Graph Editors, Exporting and Importing Animation, Rigs: Assignment 1
Week 4: The 12 Principles of Animation / Action Analysis. Inanimate Objects
Week 5: The 12 Principles of Animation / Applying. Inanimate Objects 2: Assignment 2: Assignment 1
Week 6: Working with Different Rigs. Planning Animation sequences. Pose to Pose, Straight Ahead. Iteration. Function Curves. Path of Action
Week 7: Exploring animation styles. Weight, Timing and contrast. Working with high quality rigs. Game loops vs progressive: Assignment 3: Assignment 2
Week 8: Utilizing constraints in animation sequences. Baking animation, Layering.
Week 9: Walks. Compare and contrast animation approaches. Treadmill vs progressive. Keys to a walk. Blocking. Planning Strides/ Contacts. Crossovers.
Week 10: Adding weight and gravity to a walk. Exaggerating and generating personality in a walk by exploring the characters motivations.: Assignment 4: Assignment 3
Week 11: Generating Progressive cycles, looping techniques, tangent management. Weighted tangents.
Week 12: Run cycles. The utility of the COG. Understanding Rig functionality.
Week 13: Transitioning between walks and runs. Locking Feet.: Assignment 5: Assignment 4
Week 14: Polishing function curves. Animation Layers. Pose Libraries
Week 15 - December 12: Semester Wrap: Assignment 5
