## Course Description
Endless career opportunities available for the professional Inside Sales Representative, this course is designed for individuals focused on acquiring the tools and attributes to be successful in this profession. Using lectures, case studies, group discussions, and guest speakers, to teach and refine efficient and practical selling techniques, students and existing inside sales professionals will learn how to sell more in less time.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Distinguish the difference between inside sales and telemarketing.
- Recognize the career opportunities available in Inside Sales, from lumber trading to financial advising to selling advertising space.
- Explain the skills and attributes required to become a professional Inside Sales Representative, using the phone as the primary method of communication.
- Develop a practical and efficient sales presentation, encompassing the complete sales cycle, from initial customer contact to the closing and/or getting an act of commitment.

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion of this course, students should be able to:

1. Differentiate between inside sales, outside sales, and telemarketing.

2. Recognize the career opportunities available in Inside Sales, from selling packaged goods to financial advising to selling advertising space, and much more.

3. Explain the skills and attributes required to become a professional Inside Sales Representative, using the phone as the primary method of communication.

4. Develop a practical and efficient sales presentation, encompassing the complete sales cycle, from initial customer contact to the closing and/or getting an act of commitment.

5. Understand the value of CRM software in the sales environment

6. Learn how to effectively apply features, advantages, and benefits in the inside sales of goods and services. “Benefit Selling”.

7. Learn how to adapt to an asynchronous course, which requires individual students to participate every week of the course and utilize strong time management skills.

## Other information
Asynchronous Learning At BCIT

Asynchronous learning at BCIT is any type of learning that a student undertakes on their own with an instructor. It differs from synchronous learning, which can be done online or in-person, and typically requires you and your classmates to attend scheduled classes with an instructor.

What makes learning asynchronous?

When used in the educational context, asynchronous learning refers to courses where students access course materials—lectures, lessons, readings, projects, quizzes, and assignments— with some of it on their own time. Learning takes place at different times for students enrolled in this type of course, because there is no set class time.

Asynchronous learning at BCIT can include:

- Watching pre-recorded lectures/lessons
- Undertaking independent research and writing projects
- Participating in an online discussion forum
- Watching online videos and taking a quiz/exam to evaluate your understanding.
- Completing a guided course project
- Communicating with classmates when completing a team project

While this course is asynchronous, students are expected to complete the assigned work (including all of the above) during the week it is assigned. Failure to login to the Learning Hub to to complete the assigned work will be noted as an absence.
