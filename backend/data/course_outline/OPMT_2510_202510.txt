## Course Description
Understanding and delivering on stakeholder needs is the ultimate objective of all projects. Measuring performance and completeness of deliverables is at the core of project quality management. This must happen in the context of an uncertain environment. The ability to identify and manage this uncertainty is critical to success. This course will examine the methodologies and tools used to manage project quality and risk.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the elements of modern quality management and their use in the project context.
- Differentiate between the use of various quality tools in planning, assuring, and controlling of project quality.
- Identify and evaluate stakeholder needs by applying quality methodologies and tools.
- Describe the elements of a project quality metric system and the use of Cost of Quality.
- Develop a project quality plan to ensure project performance is measured and client needs are met.
- Describe the elements of risk management and their use in the project context.
- Identify and evaluate project risk by applying risk methodologies and tools.
- Develop a risk management plan to identify, assess, and control project risk.

Review project management process groups and knowledge areas.

Connection between Risk and Quality.

Inputs to, and outputs of, risk management

Risk Analysis Tools

The 8 generic approaches to Risk Response.
Understand how contingent response is different from Risk Response.
Contingent response
Constructing a Risk Registry
Tracking and controlling risks in projects
5: Risk Monitoring, Control & Integration: Assignment #1 Due
Connection of risk identification, assessment and response planning in the overall risk management system
Compile all pieces of risk together and link them in one harmonious system
Control and continually re-assess risks
Differentiate between risks and issues
Common risk management errors
6: Midterm Exam (Project Risk Management): Mid-Term Exam
7: Quality Overview
Quality in the context of a project.
Dimensions of quality in goods and services.
Quality from the producer’s and consumer’s perspectives.
Guiding principles of quality in projects.
Classify quality costs.
8: Quality Planning - Part 1: Start working on assignment #2
Basic elements of Quality Management Planning.
Inputs (Company policies, Standards, Regulations) of Quality Planning.
Translate Customer Needs into agreed deliverables/requirements.
Define conformance – how to deploy to process.
Develop Project Quality Plan / Control Plan.
9: Quality Planning - Part 2
The concept of “Plan Quality / Develop Quality Planning”.
The difference between “Perform Quality Assurance” and “Perform Quality Control”.
Understanding the different formats and how to build a “Quality Plan”.
Learning some of the quality methods and techniques that help the project manager and project team to plan for quality and to make decisions.
10: Quality Assurance & Quality Control
Understand the concept of Quality Assurance activities.
Understand the concept of Quality Control activities.
Learning about Quality Audits in projects.
Learn how to apply the 7 classical tools of quality to quality control and improvement.
11: Quality Integration - Putting it all together!: Assignment #2 Due
Describe the different components of Project Quality Management.
Learning how to integrate industry and company systems into the project, then translate VOC into measurable deliverables.
Understand what other quality system elements that could be used in the project and how to import/export between project and organization.
Understand the concept of continuous improvement with the PDCA cycle as an example for that.
Describe how the different elements of the quality system work in the real world.
12: Final Exam (Project Quality Management): Final Exam
