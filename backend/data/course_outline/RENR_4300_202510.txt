## Course Description
​This field-based course is a culmination of the aquatic knowledge and skills students have learned over the course of the FWR program. Students will gain hands-on experience with fish handling, fish capture methods, fish habitat assessment, and benthic invertebrate sampling protocols. Other activities will include fish and fish habitat data entry to the provincial database and water quality sampling. ​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Apply inventory methods to survey fish and other aquatic organisms
- Identify and apply best practices to capture and handle fish
- Assess and document aquatic habitat required to support fish and other aquatic organisms
- Assess and document natural and anthropogenic disturbances
- Measure water quality parameters that affect aquatic health
- Demonstrate safe, respectful, and professional behaviour that meets industry standards and expectations​

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
Mandatory PPE:

- Leak-free chest waders
- Wading boots (if applicable)
- Wading belt
- Hat with a brim
- Polarized sunglasses

Mandatory field clothing:

- Rain gear (preferably Gore-tex or alternative)
- Hiking boots
- Long-sleeve shirts/insulative layers (t-shirts may be permitted on exceptionally warm days, but in most cases you must be wearing clothes that cover as much skin as possible)
- Field pants (preferably quick dry - jeans and shorts are not permitted)
- Warm socks (remember you will be standing in cold water for full days)
- A change of clothes in the event you get wet

Other required equipment/gear:

- Clinometer
- Field notebook
- Water bottle

* Additional mandatory PPE that must be worn in the field will be provided by the program
