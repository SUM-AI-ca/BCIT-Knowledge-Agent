### Course Description
In this practical training, students will apply theory and industry best practices in mass timber construction. They will gain hands-on experience in the on-site installation of a mass timber structure and associated components according to specifications. Students will apply safe practices for tool and equipment operation and site management. They will use project drawings to verify materials and work as a team to install mass timber structural components and connections according to specifications. Students will participate in the safe lifting and handling of mass timber structural components under instructor supervision. They will demonstrate mass timber construction skills including layout, cutting, drilling and inserting fasteners according to specification.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the main activity areas on a construction site.
- Identify the key tools and equipment used for the installation of mass timber.
- Describe the main activities involved in the installation of mass timber.
- Identify Personal Protective Equipment that must be worn by workers on a construction site.
- Work as a team to plan and install mass timber structural components and connections.
- Interpret and use project drawings and specifications to install a mass timber structure.
- Verify mass timber materials, hardware, fasteners, layout and installation according to specifications.
- Demonstrate safe use of tools and equipment for mass timber construction skills.
- Demonstrate safe use of access, rigging, and hoisting equipment for mass timber construction​.

### Course Goals
- Execute a plan for a mass timber project
- Follow safety policy, guidelines, regulations, industry best practices, and written safe work procedures
- Identify mass timber, structural steel, dimensional lumber, and fasteners from a drawing
- Handle mass timber products
- Rig and hoist materials
- Signal a crane
- Position loads within a structure
- Align and level building materials
- Install bracing
- Install fasteners
- Field fabricate NLT panels
- Install building enclosure panels
- Dismantle and store materials
- Fabricate glulam assembly
