## Course Description
​This course is a continuation of BLDG 3200. The course begins with an introduction to acoustic principles and performance and then moves on to examine various larger building construction systems and assemblies including precast concrete or heavy timber, masonry, exterior cladding systems, interior finishes / architectural woodwork and prefabrication. There is an emphasis on particular technical and procedural concerns in the development of working drawings in the context of construction contract documents. Guest lecturers and field trips supplement formal lecture and lab activities

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate ability to draw construction documents applying knowledge of materials, building codes, graphics and software applications (Revit) introduced in other courses.
- Identify acoustic principals and apply to construction assemblies to improve acoustic performance.
- Produce detailed working drawings for interior components and building envelope conditions.
- Identify and compare differing viewpoints, approaches and solutions applied to construction details.
- Apply particular technical and procedural methods in the production of detailed working drawings in the context of contract documentation for construction.
- Develop and demonstrate initiative, self-confidence, and problem-solving ability in the completion of projects.

## Learning Resources
Required: This course makes use of the course handout material, drawings/details, site visits, videos and photographs to teach the subject material. There is a mandatory text. The following materials are required: 1: Course binder with paper for taking notes/ making drawings/ details 2: Manual drawing tools (mechanical pencils/ scales/ ruler etc.) for in-class drawing exercises. 3: CSA approved safety footwear, hardhat, gloves, hi-vis vest and safety glasses for construction site visits.

Required text: “Building Construction Illustrated”, (6th edition) by Francis D.K. Ching, John Wiley & Sons, 2020 Additional resources and references:

“Architectural Graphic Standards”(Student Edition), Ramsey & Sleeper, (12th Ed.), Wiley and Sons, 2017

“Architectural Detailing “, (3rd edition) by Edward Allen & Patrick Rand, John Wiley & Sons 2016 “Architect’s Studio Companion”, Edward Allen & Joseph Iano, (6th Edition), John Wiley & Sons, 2017

“BC Building Code” and BCBC Access Handbook, current editions.
