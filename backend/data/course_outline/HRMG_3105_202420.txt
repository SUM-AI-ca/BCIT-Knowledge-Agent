## Course Description
This course covers theory, practice and standard programs employed by human resource management practitioners in today’s private sector business, government and other organizations. The course covers the major human resource management functions with some emphasis on applied knowledge. Recommended for all persons interested in general management, HR management, and/or supervision.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the programs and processes associated with acquiring and maintaining an effective and efficient workforce.
- Analyze some of the key human resource management issues facing Canadian organizations.
- Demonstrate a working knowledge of the terminology and some generally accepted practices within some of the main human resource management functional areas. E.g.: Recruitment and Selection, Training & Development, Performance Management, etc.
- Identify the various bodies of employment laws and regulations that apply to employers operating in British Columbia.
- Create effective written and oral reports.

## Course Specific Requirements
Once the course is completed, 5 posts from the various topics will be evaluated as shown below. You will have the opportunity to select 2 of the posts that will be assessed, and I will select the other 3 randomly. ALL posts must be completed. Missing a topic’s release time means a zero on that specific discussion (also classified as not attended); You may prepare the discussion in word, then past back into the board before the window closes. Individual posts are due by 12PM (noon) on the due date with peer commenting open until the 8PM cut-off.

o Contribution Level One (50-60%): Basic sharing of your thoughts on the question in 2 or 3 paragraphs for a given post; the minimal level expected. If the post is incomplete (missing content or required comments), that will reduce this mark proportionally and a PI will be seen in the attendance for that week.

o Contribution Level Two (60-70%): Level One and includes additionally…the use of course concepts and theories being closely tied into your posts. Use and knowledge of course language is key!

o Contribution Level Three (70-80%): Level Two and makes a few comments on classmates’ posts.

o Contribution Level Three (80-90%+): Levels One + Two +3 and includes the recommendation of URLs or business articles that you have found that are related to the topic of discussion. Reads and comments frequently on classmates’ contributions. (Comments must be beyond “well done, nicely put” etc.)

Instructor feedback/comments: Once the topic has closed (Fri. 8pm), I will use the newsfeed for collective class feedback. I will do personalized feedback on an as needed basis to make sure you are on track (check your email often). PLEASE post by noon on the due date, so your peers have an opportunity to comment before it closes.
