## Course Description
This course begins with the fundamentals of print estimating, processes, and the development of production standards. Students will work with Microsoft Excel and Management Information System software to create project cost estimates. Topics covered in this course will include: the scope and function of estimating; production standards; order processing—sale through invoice; estimating paper, ink, and buyouts; estimating prepress; estimating sheet fed offset; estimating post-press; and the completion of a number of basic estimates. These principles are then applied to other imaging technologies starting with large format inkjet projects, electrophotography projects and flexographic print processes.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the steps required to assign costs to a print project production plan.
- Describe the differences between marketing, pricing, and profit in the graphic communication industry.
- Create a production standard with MIS software for one pre-press, press and post-press function.
- Estimate paper requirements for straight, stagger cutting and bookwork.
- Calculate the correct spoilage for press and bindery operations.
- Explain the different methods of bookwork impositions.
- Create basic layouts for sheet and bookwork.
- Estimate ink quantity and cost.
- Provide cost estimates for digital pre-press production.
- Estimate sheeted presswork and common post-press production methods.
- Construct requests for quote and produce cost estimates for basic jobs, using MIS software.
- Explain the common methods used to estimate small commercial work.
- Establish tailored prices using cost estimating.
- Explain the principles of budgeted hourly rates and activity-based costing.
- Explain how a production standard is developed.
- Perform an audit on a set of production data.​

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
The Graphic Communication program utilizes a unique print and imaging lab facility. The equipment in the GCOM lab was donated by the generous business owners in our community. Students must respect the equipment in the lab at all times, comply with all rules and take reasonable care of themselves and others:

- Loose hair or long beards must be tied back – or contained in a hat or hair net
- Remove jewelry, scarves, coats or loose clothing
- Do not engage in recreational gaming or web browsing
- Do not bring food or drink into the lab(closed water bottles are allowed at desks only)

These rules are for the safety of all students using the equipment in the lab. Failure to comply with lab rules will not be tolerated. The degree of punitive action may range from being issued a verbal or written warning, to being barred entry to the lab or required to leave (which may result in course failure), to withdrawal from the program.
