## Course Description
This course builds on the foundations taught in ‘GCOM 2110 Managing Graphic Communication Workflows 1’ and provides an overview of output devices fed by electronic imaging workflows. Students are taught to evaluate and apply the required attributes to transform electronic files into tangible output. The course will focus on tools for automating PDF workflows. Students will get hands-on experience with Kodak Prinergy servers and the connection to Kodak Insite web server. Current workflows in soft proofing, web authorizations and eCommerce transactions are explored and evaluated.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Produce a variety of proofs, prints, and plates from devices that support input from Prinergy.
- Differentiate between the appropriate uses of inkjet, electrophotographic and “soft” proofing.
- List the characteristics of the most common output devices driven by PDF workflows.
- Describe the types of media associated with various output devices.
- Apply colour management with ICC or device link profiles in the Colour Control module of Prinergy’s Process Plan for proofing output.
- Map spot-colours to the correct colour library for the best simulation with proofing devices.
- Combine surplus spot colours to a single separation for successful printing plate production.
- Automate a PDF workflow through Kodak Insite server from supplied postscript, soft proof approvals, revision cycles and final output.
- Set and revise trapping characteristics based on expectations of the ultimate printing condition.
- Evaluate other vendor offerings for PDF workflows to various RIP technologies.
- Directly connect output devices, remote machines, or third-party devices.​

## Course Goals
Course Description:

This course will expand on the use of Kodak Prinergy Workshop and its many tools, including VPS Proofing, Preps Imposition, Rule Based Automation, and InSite Online Proofing. Using self-directed projects, we will examine various challenges that can exist in a prepress workflow and how to avoid and correct them prior to file output.

Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies:

Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Create Print-Ready PDF files that meet the technical requirements of different print processes.
- Create and Edit Kodak Preps imposition templates for various products and different output situations.
- Explore Advanced Trapping Techniques.
- Understand the proper use of overprinting and knocking out content.
- Prepare files for digital output using Kodak Digital Print.
- Use the Plate Builder feature to prepare files with post-press treatments such as varnish, opaque white and foils.
- Use Hot Folders and create Rule Based Automation

- Use Kodak’s InSite online proofing software to get offsite client approval.

Safety equipment or protective clothing

The Graphic Communications program utilizes a unique print lab facility, with equipment donated by the generous business owners in our community. Students must always respect the equipment in the lab, and comply with the following rules:

- Loose hair or long beards must be tied back or contained in a hat or hair net.
- Remove jewelry, scarves, coats, or loose clothing.
- Gaming, videos, or loud music that can cause distraction are not permitted.
- Mobile devices are not permitted when working around machinery.
- No food or drink in the lab (closed water bottles are allowed at desks only).
- PPE is required to be worn when working with chemicals.
- No video recording or photography is permitted in the lab.

These rules are for the safety of all students using the equipment in the lab and are overseen at the discretion of all faculty. Failure to comply with lab rules will not be tolerated and may result punitive action including dismissal from class, formal warning, course failure, or withdrawal from the program.
