## Course Description
This course provides the legal, ethical and contextual foundation for nursing practice in Canada. Students will examine the history of nursing practice, the establishment of nursing as a regulated profession, the role of professional, regulatory and union bodies on the practice of nursing, and contemporary nursing issues and trends. The course will explore professional regulation of nurses in depth and will introduce students to knowledge regarding regulatory requirements outlined by the BCCNM that supports the delivery of safe, competent ethical nursing care. This course offers students an opportunity to develop their understanding of the three categories of BCCNM Standards of Practice: Scope of Practice, Professional Standards and Practice Standards. The course will also provide an opportunity to explore the ethical values of registered nurses. BSNC 1020 is a blended course and will occur using active learning strategies and provide students an opportunity to explore nursing issues/trends/theories and present their work to the class. This course includes an integrated and experiential learning experiences.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will meet the following competencies:

Advocate

- Identifies the impact of inequities in determinants of health, genetics that limit client choice and affect health outcomes
- Articulates the role of primary healthcare and the determinants of health in health delivery systems and their significance for population health
- Incorporates knowledge of the origins of health disparities and inequities of Indigenous populations and the contributions of nursing practice into nursing care to achieve positive health outcomes for clients
- Incorporates knowledge of the health disparities and inequities of vulnerable populations and the contributions of nursing practice into nursing care to achieve positive health outcomes for clients
- Recognizes the professional and ethical responsibility to advocate for client safety, fairness, and equitable distribution of health care resources within a define scope of practice

Professional

- Demonstrates knowledge of the health care system at functional aspects of the micro, meso, and macro health care system
- Demonstrates understanding of the role of nursing unions and professional associations
- Demonstrates understanding of the nursing profession as a self-regulating profession mandated by provincial/territorial legislation to protect the public
- ​Recognizes and discusses personal biases and takes action to minimize the influence of personal values, beliefs, and positional power on client care
- Maintains individual competence within scope of practice and professional standards​

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance: As student success is tied to regular attendance of classes, the BSN program strongly recommends that students attend class regularly. Attendance at all nursing theory classes is an expectation of the BSN program.

In all learning environments, students are socialized into their professional role and are thus required to demonstrate knowledge and application of professional nursing standards and ethics. Multiple absences may jeopardize a student’s development of the knowledge base required for competent professional nursing practice and successful completion of course requirements. As adult learners, students are ultimately responsible for their own learning; however, it is also recognized that repeated absences have a negative impact on collaborative learning experiences.
