## Course Description
This is a course in the application of consulting skills and problem solving. The intent of the course is to provide students with a set of tools that will help them solve unstructured business problems using the skills and knowledge learned in the program in a real world setting.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Apply business knowledge to solve a management problem in an organization.
- Introduce stages of consulting process.
- Create a project plan using project planning tools.
- Manage a complex project using appropriate tools such as GANNT and work breakdown charts.
- Design and use primary research instruments; select and summarize secondary research.
- Analyze data from all six functional areas of organization management using appropriate diagnostic tools to develop alternative solutions, and determine a recommendation for the given management problem.
- Introduce change management tools and concepts.
- Demonstrate effective client communication skills through written and oral presentations.
- Apply teamwork and relationship management skills in working with external clients, supervisory faculty, your consulting team, and other stakeholders.

## Course Specific Requirements
Business Consulting Project Requirements:

- Students are ambassadors of BCIT and are expected to conduct themselves accordingly. Students shall adhere to BCIT’s Student Code of Conduct (Policy No. 5102) while working on their Business Consulting Project, working remotely or on-site.
- Students work on the project under the organization's general guidance, and the person in the organization is assigned as the supervisor or sponsor. The BCIT Faculty Advisor will provide support and consultation to the team.
- The organization will determine the hours of work and should total 15 hours per week per student.
- Students will develop a Terms of Reference that contains specific information about their project, including the deliverables, a detailed project plan, a team plan, and other important details. The Terms of Reference must be completed before the commencement of the actual project work. A copy of the Terms of Reference is provided to the faculty advisor and the client for their approval and signatures.
- Students are responsible for submitting a weekly Progress Report to their faculty advisor. These reports will explain any deviations from the agreed-to project plan and include information about each team member’s weekly activities. The timely submission of weekly progress reports and their content is evaluated under “project management.”
- Research and surveys conducted as part of a Business Consulting Project must conform to the relevant sections of BCIT’s research and ethics policies (Policy #6500 and #6600) and the relevant sections of BCIT’s standards for academic integrity (Policy#5104).
- Completed projects include a written report, a presentation, and a project summary.
