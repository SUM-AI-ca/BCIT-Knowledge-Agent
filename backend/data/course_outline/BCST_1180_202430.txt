## Course Description
In today's age of social media platforms and the need to rapidly develop online content, video editing skills are beneficial to many professions and applications. This course introduces students to the artistic and technical platform of video editing using Adobe Premiere, beginning with an overview of the software interface, basic editing tools, and roles of a video editor. Along with learning how to use the software, students will become familiar with editing styles and postproduction workflow through provided handouts and assignments. Upon completion of this course, students will have the basic skillset to build successful and presentable sequences with provided video clips and export them as compressed video files for upload to various online media. ​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Define roles of a video editor.
- Successfully navigate the Adobe Premiere interface.
- Organize desktop media.
- Explain efficient post-production work flow.
- Demonstrate basic sound mixing and sound editing skills.
- Identify correct focal lengths, pacing, and continuity for particular types of editing projects.
- Use basic editing tools.
- Demonstrate basic transitions, effects, and title creations.
- Build successful sequences and scenes.
- Export media in various formats.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
- Introduction to the course and its objectives.
- Editing Requirements: Computer Specs, Hard Drives, Equipment
- Understanding the role of video editing in visual storytelling.
- Overview of Adobe Premiere Pro's interface and basic tools.
- Media Management: Importing media files (video, audio, images) into Premiere Pro.
- Organizing and managing media assets in the Project panel.

- Navigating the timeline: cuts, adding clips, rearranging.
- Basic editing techniques: ripple edit, rolling edit, slip edit.
- Using the Source Monitor and Program Monitor for editing.
- Applying transitions (cuts, fades) to create smooth visual transitions.
- In-Class Assignment 01: Creating a simple montage with basic cuts and transitions.

- Introduction to audio editing in Premiere Pro.
- Understanding audio tracks, levels, and the audio mixer.
- Importing and adjusting audio clips.
- Adding and adjusting audio keyframes for volume control.
- In-Class Assignment 02: Editing a dialogue scene to ensure clear and balanced audio.

- Using keyboard shortcuts for efficient editing.
- Creating split edits (J-cuts and L-cuts) for seamless transitions.
- Applying speed changes and time remapping for creative effects.

- Introduction to color correction.
- Adjusting brightness, contrast, saturation, and color balance.
- Using the Lumetri Color panel for basic color adjustments.
- In-Class Assignment 03: Correcting color to a scene.

- Introduction to color grading.
- Using the Lumetri Color panel for advanced color adjustments.
- Applying cinematic looks with presets and LUTs (Look-Up Tables).
- Matching color between different shots for visual consistency.
- In-Class Assignment 04: Correcting color and applying a specific mood to a scene.

- Exploring advanced transitions (dissolves, wipes, slides).
- Applying and customizing video and audio transitions.
- Introduction to effects and video filters in Premiere Pro.
- Keyframing effects for dynamic animations and changes over time.

- Creating and customizing text layers for titles and lower thirds.
- Adding graphics and logos to video projects.
- Animating text and graphics using keyframes.
- Using the Essential Graphics panel for creating reusable templates.

- Advanced audio editing techniques: noise reduction, audio effects.
- Creating audio transitions for seamless sound changes.
- Adding background music and sound effects for impact.
- Using audio keyframes for precise control over audio changes.

- Understanding export settings and formats.
- Exporting videos for various platforms: YouTube, social media, etc.
- Creating custom export presets for consistent output.
- Exporting high-quality videos while managing file size.
- Final Project’s Guidelines

- Watch trailers: Movies & Series
- Discuss their pre-production and production process
- The post-production process on timeline.

- Peer review and feedback session for final projects.
- Course conclusion: Showcasing final projects and reflecting on the journey of learning Adobe Premiere Pro.

Each week's topic builds upon the previous ones, allowing students to progressively develop their video editing skills using Adobe Premiere Pro.
