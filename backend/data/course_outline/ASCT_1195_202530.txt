## Course Description
Through the study of Building Code, this subject area endeavours to develop the Student's: (1) Awareness of the impact of codes & regulations upon design & construction. (2) Ability to interpret the various intents of the building code. (3) Ability to integrate the structural considerations in design & construction documents.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify the role, contents, & structure of NBC and BC Building Code.
- Know the terminology, symbols and abbreviations used in BCBC.
- Work through Code Analysis procedures & develop appropriate strategies for specific design & construction issues.
- Define dead and live loads.
- Understand site plans, and ground level applications.
- Read tables for uniform live loads for various structures in kPa and lbs/sqft.
- Determine allowable bearing pressure on various soil and rock types.
- Determine minimum dimensions for rooms, doors, stairs, rails, and halls.
- Determine code requirements for persons with disabilities.
