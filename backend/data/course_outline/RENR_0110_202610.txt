### Course Description
​Develop foundational plant identification skills in this field-based course focused on native plant species of BC. Stop seeing a "wall of green" and start recognizing the individual stories of the plants around you. Through hands-on experience, you'll learn how to use field guides, understand scientific names, and apply botanical terminology to identify 10+ native plants (conifers, broadleaf trees, shrubs, ferns, and herbs) in their natural habitats.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify 10+ unique plants native British Columbia including conifer and broadleaf trees, shrubs, ferns, and herbs.
- Use botanical terms to describe identifying characteristics visible in all seasons.
- Build familiarity with both common and scientific nomenclature.
- Effectively use a field guide in plant identification. ​

### Safety equipment or protective clothing
Students must have the following for every field session: hard hat, high-vis vest, sturdy boots, safety glasses and rain gear. Many classes will be partially indoors and partially outdoors, so you will need your field gear for most sessions (unless otherwise informed by the instructor).
