## Course Description
The final course in the series provides details on the selection, implementation and monitoring of risk financing techniques. Subjects include insurance as a risk financing technique; financing property; risk retention including use of captives; risk cost allocation; finite and integrated risk plans and capital market products for risk financing.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:

- Illustrate the main components of risk financing and the importance of risk financing to enterprises.
- Demonstrate how insurance is used effectively in risk financing scenarios, being able to evaluate which types of risk are suited to various insurance alternatives.
- Design an integrated insurance plan that addresses risks to be insured, policy provisions, deductibles, layers, umbrella programs and alternative risk financing techniques.
- Compare and contrast traditional forecasting techniques to estimate accidental losses and risk financing requirements.
- Generate and propose various types of self-insurance plans as appropriate.
- Generate and propose retrospective rating plan structures, pricing and administration procedures.
- Assess the use of reinsurance as part of an integrated risk management program.
- Interpret the effectiveness of captive insurance programs within larger enterprises or groups of enterprises.
- Design finite risk programs as part of an integrated risk management program.
- Evaluate and implement a range of non-insurance contractual transfer of risk strategies.
