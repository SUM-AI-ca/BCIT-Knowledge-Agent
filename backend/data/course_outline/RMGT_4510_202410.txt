## Course Description
Focused knowledge of insurance and professional conduct within the claims domain forms the core of this course. Soft skills and knowledge required to handle claims are blended in the curriculum in order to improve fundamental claims handling techniques. Students will learn how to manage relationships in order to gather critical information in the claims handling process. A step-by-step process delivers the key to policy analysis for coverage evaluations. Students will also learn the fundamentals of investigation, evaluation, negotiation and settlement within the claims process.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Evaluate the loss adjuster's role in the business of insurance.
- Describe the types of information necessiary to evalute vatious claims.
- Describe the framework of the legal systems of Canada in relation to insurance loss adjustments.
- Plan a claims investigation.
- Describe the adjustment process for physical damage automobile claims in each province.
- Handle claims for various automobile accident scenarios.
