### Course Description
Students are introduced to the scope and practice in biomedical engineering through: Field trips to various workplaces including hospitals, research labs, medical device companies, and test centers. Presentations and guest speaker sessions on related topics.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the profession of a biomedical engineering technologist.
- Discuss the multi-disciplinary nature and scope of work in biomedical engineering.
- List professional associations and learned societies and outline their relevance for biomedical engineering technologists.
- Apply the professional code of ethics in the field of biomedical engineering.

### Attendance Requirements
- Students are expected to complete all coursework and attend all classes. Excessive absences may lead to failure or withdrawal from the course.
- Coursework and assessments must be completed within the scheduled period; otherwise, a zero will be assigned. Marks will be deducted for late submission. Make-up opportunities will only be granted for excused absences, such as illness or other unavoidable circumstances, as outlined below.
- In the case of illness or another unavoidable reason for absence, the student must inform the course instructor as soon as possible. Prolonged illness must be verified by a medical certificate, and absences for reasons other than illness must be supported by appropriate documentation. The instructor will determine whether the absence is excused based on the information provided.
- If a student has an excused absence and is unable to complete a lab session or assessment during the scheduled period, the course instructor will work with the student to determine appropriate accommodations.
