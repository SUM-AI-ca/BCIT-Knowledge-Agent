## Course Description
Introduces basic planning issues encountered in building and site design with emphasis on residential projects. Covers the influences of site conditions on building orientation, interior layout, and exterior form. Focus is on planning strategies for achieving good spatial and functional relationships in a dwelling.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon completion of this course, the student will be better able to:

- Apply the 'design process' as an approach to developing thoughtful, creative and responsible solutions to practical site and building design problems.
- Generate design solutions that regard influences, requirements, and constraints as design opportunities rather than design limitations.
- Identify, analyze, and evaluate the factors from the natural and built environments that must be considered and understood when formulating design options.
- Propose design solutions that respond to the influences and changing patterns of the built environment and that reflects the interrelationship of context, site and building.
- Produce a workable design solution to a residential planning problem.
- Illustrate design solution of orthographic and axonometric drawings, using various techniques.

## Evaluation Criteria
Criteria: %: Comments
Assignment 1: 20: Site Analysis Assignment involves site specific information gathering and communicating the site information in a clear organized manner to use this information to assist in making site specific decisions based the findings in the Site Analysis.
Assignment 2: 25: On completing the Site Analysis we begin the design development and integration of the building program into the design focusing on specific weekly tasks to develope the design solution. These loose sketch type drawings (on tracing paper) will be compiled and become the basis of the design portfolio for each students design.
Assignment 3: 55: The Final Design solution is neatly drafted and presented on 2 drawing sheets complete with the compiled design portfolio. In order to satisfy the requirements for course completion the final design assignment must be submitted during the final lab week. Students are required to complete and hand in all assignments. Assignments submitted late will be docked 20% per calendar day late. Students unable to submit the final assignment due to illness must submit a medical certificate to the instructor. The certificate must state the time period in question and state that student was unable to attend class due to medical reasons.
TOTAL: 100%
