## Course Description
Sonography Principles and Instrumentations is a sonography course that teaches students the basic principles in ultrasound physics and machine functioning. The students will learn basic ultrasound concepts such as sound wave physics, the Doppler principle, and different ultrasound modalities. In addition, the students will learn about safety issues in ultrasound, and learn how to perform ultrasound quality assurance tests.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the properties of waves.
- Describe the construction of the different types of ultrasound transducers.
- Describe the process for digital image storage such as the Picture Archiving and Storage system (PACS).
- List the variables in the Doppler principle and describe how each variable is obtained in a sonography exam.
- Describe factors that affect the validity of the Doppler effect, such as the Nyquist limit and the angle of incidence.
- Differentiate between colour, continuous wave, and pulsed wave Doppler.
- Describe the appearance and causes of ultrasound artifacts (4.2e).
- List the biological safety concerns in ultrasound scanning, including thermal heating and cavitation.
- Describe how to monitor output display indices and adjust power output in accordance with "as low as reasonably achievable" (ALARA) principle. (4.2c).
- Describe imaging and instrumentation activities included in quality assurance testing. (4.3a).
- Identify degraded instrument performance. (4.3b).​

This course covers the following modules:
