### Course Description
This course emphasizes the use of decision-making models in business. Students will learn to use quantitative methods to choose alternatives in the decision-making process. Software applications (spreadsheets and databases) will be used to support the decision-making process.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply descriptive statistics and basic probability theory to management science models.
- Compare decision-making techniques including: Decision Analysis, Forecasting Methods including Simple Regression, Time Series, Linear Programming and basic Inventory models.
- Construct and interpret a confidence interval estimates for a single population means or proportions using the appropriate standard normal or t distributions.
- Manipulate data between databases and spreadsheets.
- Carry out a hypothesis test comparing one or two population means or proportions.
- Import data into a spreadsheet and carry out a statistical analysis of the data.
- Manipulate data between databases and spreadsheets.
- Import data into a spreadsheet and carry out a statistical analysis of the data.

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Date | Topic
Week 1 | Introduction to: Data Analytics, Quantitative Methods and Statistical Analysis. Populations and samples, Descriptive Statistics, Measures of central location: Mean, median, weighted mean and mode for listed and grouped data, Quartiles and percentiles for listed and grouped data, Measures of variation: range, Range, standard deviation, and interquartile range for listed and grouped data, coefficient of variation
Week 2 | Statistical Software (Excel), Finding numerical summary statistics using Excel for both listed and grouped data, constructing frequency distributions, obtaining graphical descriptions of data including bar graphs, histograms, scatter plots and box plots
Week 3 | Basic probability rules: Addition + Multiplication, Union, Intersection Mutually Exclusive and Independent Events, Conditional probability, start on 2×2 probability tables, expected value and standard deviation of random variables
Week 4 | Discrete Probability functions Binomial distribution, Hypergeometric distributions, Poisson distribution
Week 5 | Continuous Distributions: Uniform, Exponential, Empirical rule, Normal distribution (applied problems)
Week 6 | Test #1, Thursday June 16, 2026 Point estimation, distribution of population means, central limit theorem (CLT) and Interval estimation of population mean and population proportion
Week 7 | Confidence intervals for proportions Confidence intervals for means for both known and unknown population std dev
Week 8 | Decision Making and Hypothesis Testing: Null + alternative hypotheses, Type I and II errors. One and two-tailed tests
Week 9 | One and two-tailed tests for population mean and population proportion P-values and its applications
Week 10 | Comparing two population means Comparing two population proportions Test #2, Thursday July 14, 2026
Week 11 | Chi-square test of independence,
Week 12 | ANOVA
Week 13 | Regression Analysis: Simple regression model, least square method, coefficient of determination, assumptions. Testing for significance, Regression line for prediction. Using Excel for multivariable regression
Week 14 | Project Presentations and Review for final exam
Week 15 | Final Exam, the date will be Announce by the Program
