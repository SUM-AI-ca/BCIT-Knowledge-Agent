## Course Description
(UBC BIOT 205) Examines microbial diversity and evolution, as well as the history, scope, and relevance of the field of microbiology. Microbial cell structure, growth and metabolism are discussed in depth. The control of microbes by antimicrobial chemotherapy is also covered. In the lab, techniques employed in the growth, enumeration and identification of bacteria are taught. Measurement of antimicrobial activity of antimicrobial drugs is also performed.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Describe microbial diversity and evolution.
- Describe the history and scope of microbiology.
- Describe microbial cell structure and function.
- Discuss microbial growth and metabolism.
- Describe antimicrobial chemotherapy.
- Prepare microbial culture media and grow bacteria in culture using aseptic technique.
- Enumerate bacterial cultures and determine growth rate of a bacterial culture.
- Identify bacterial species based on their biochemical activities.
- Measure the antimicrobial activity of antimicrobial drugs.

## Attendance Requirements
Students are expected to attend all lecture and lab sessions unless they have a serious illness or other medical condition. In the Biotechnology Program, laboratory attendance is of the highest priority since lab experience is what sets this program apart from other undergraduate programs and what prepares students for co-op work terms. Furthermore, lab work is typically done in pairs and students are therefore expected to be present to support their partners. Students must attend all laboratory sessions.

For some lab-based courses, participation marks will be given for completion of laboratory activities and these marks will be worth up to 5% of the student’s final course grade. A student must be on time for start of the lab period to avoid losing participation marks. These lab activities involve generating lab specific materials and/or data, learning, acquiring and/or practicing new skills and/or procedures.

Please notify your instructor and the Biotechnology program head of any absences.

If exams, quizzes, or in-class assignments are missed for reasons of illness, medical notes will be required to reschedule.
