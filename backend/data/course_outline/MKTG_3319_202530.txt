## Course Description
The course builds on fundamental selling skills and enables the student to apply more advanced methods to handle challenging selling situations. Students will also learn and apply contemporary business development methods through common communication channels, including social media. Key topics include: Social Selling, Contact Strategies, Prospect Engagement Methods, Diagnostic Questions, Customer Relationship Management, Visual Selling and Dealing with Difficult Customers. A key element of this course will be the development and delivery of an interactive, persuasive presentation to a new prospect.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the primary inbound and outbound methods and explain key benefits
- Explain the implementation of primary inbound and outbound methods
- Compare and contrast best practices in social selling and reputation management
- Define the terms of lead generation, qualifying, conversion, and cost of customer acquisition
- Detail the components of an effective cold call
- Develop effective outbound email templates for different selling situations
- Present a business case to persuade a prospect in a challenging B2B selling situation
- Identify the steps and methods in the sales process leading to closing
- Differentiate methods of handling objections and closing the sale
- Contrast the sales process with the buying process in B2B selling
- Explain the concept of customer-centric selling and solutions selling
- Explain the benefits of CRM systems and the sales pipeline model in business development.
- Integrate professional, persuasive communication through all steps of the selling process.
- Develop methods for dealing with difficult customers and for overcoming challenging objections.

## Attendance Requirements
Professional business behaviour is expected in this course and therefore attendance at all lectures and labs is required. Arriving more than 5 minutes past the start of class without emailing the instructor in advance will be considered an absence. Excessive absences will be deemed to have missed more than 10% of the lectures and labs. Medical documentation is required if a student has an excessive absence record or misses an assignment, a presentation, or an exam. All other BCIT attendance policies will be applied.

Missing more than 10% of classes without an excused absence will reduce Professionalism & Engagement marks to zero and will require a meeting with the instructor. Professionalism and Engagement marks will be based on a student's attendance and contribution to class discussion and activities on a weekly basis. Also, if a student misses an in-class assignment or activity without an excused absence (such as acceptable medical note) they will not be able to make-up the work.

Completion of All Assignments, Quizzes, Final Exam and Course Work

Successful completion of the course depends on the student attending the course lectures and labs, passing the cumulative of the midterms and final exam, as well as successful completion of the course group projects and any individual assignments.

- Assignments must be submitted on time. There will be a 10% mark deduction for every day a submission is late. After 10 days, you will receive a zero.
- All components of this course must be completed in order to receive a final grade. Students who do not complete assessments will receive a final grade of INC (incomplete) until these are submitted and marked.
