## Course Description
This course prepares students to perform basic sensory evaluation tests associated with product development and quality control. Students will also learn how these sensory methods can be used to monitor and/or verify that food being produced meets food safety, quality and regulatory requirements. Design of experiment (DOE) techniques will be introduced.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Compare trial and error, one-factor-at-a-time (OFAT) and Design of Experiment (DOE) methods for product development and food processing.
- Outline the steps for an OFAT and DOE method.
- Perform appropriate sensory evaluation techniques.
- Statistically evaluate sensory evaluation data.
- Explain how sensory evaluation methods can be used as monitoring and verification tools.
