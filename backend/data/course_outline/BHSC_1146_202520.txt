## Course Description
This course is designed to assist the student to handle common events encountered in health care settings in a responsive and professional manner. Students will learn how to effectively manage stress, diffuse conflict, and negotiate mutually beneficial solutions in interactions with patients and colleagues.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Integrate effective interpersonal communication skills in order to foster rapport and trust.
- Apply concept of empathy to create respectful and supportive relations with clients and co-workers.
- Perform various types of assertion according to appropriateness and desired result.
- Describe the impact of stress and outline factors that generate or increase stress.
- Apply appropriate strategies to prevent and reduce distress in both self and others.
- Analyze approaches to managing negative feelings in self and clients.
- Apply conflict resolution skills.
- Describe strategies for demonstrating respect for diversity amongst patients from various cultures.
- Choose age and development-appropriate communication strategies when working with patients across ages and physical abilities.
- Describe the range of illness experiences from the clients’ and family members’ perspectives.
- Identify adaptations appropriate to the individual needs of dying patients.
- Explain common difficulties encountered by those with disabilities and approaches for working with such individuals both sensitively and respectfully.
- Identify techniques to effectively address various types of harassment and discrimination found in the workplace.
- Discuss ethical and legal implications for practice as a cardiology technologist.

## Course topics
This course is designed to assist the student in handling everyday events in healthcare settings responsively and professionally. Students will learn how to effectively manage stress, diffuse conflict, and negotiate mutually beneficial solutions in interactions with patients and colleagues.

Exams (Midterm 35%, Final 35%): Exams will be administered online on the date and time specified in the course outline. Exams may be administered by BCIT-approved exam invigilation software at the instructor's discretion. Students will be instructed on installing and using exam invigilation software before implementation. All students can take a practice test with the remote invigilation software to familiarize themselves with the technology and system compatibility. Students who fail to complete the practice test will be ineligible for accommodations due to technical difficulties during the exam.

Missed exams and late arrival to exams: Failure to take an exam at the scheduled time will result in a grade of “0” for that exam, unless a physician’s note is provided, or other arrangements were made with the instructor prior to the day of the exam. The instructor reserves the right to utilize an alternative assessment method (which may be different from the original) in the case of a missed exam or in the case of students arriving more than 20 minutes late to a scheduled exam. If a student misses an exam or presentation, she/he must contact the instructor by email no later than the following day. Failure to do so may result in a grade of zero.

Weekly Assignments (15%): Students are to complete 7 individual homework assignments and 1 group assignment. Late assignments will not be accepted unless students have communicated extenuating circumstances with the instructor prior to the deadline.

Video Skill Quizzes (10%): Students will complete 2 video skill quizzes (5% each) via Bongo Video Assignment. Video quizzes are designed to assess skill understanding and implementation in real time. Students will be given a written quiz prompt and asked to respond verbally on camera within a short time limit. Students are expected to keep the content of quizzes confidential. Students will receive one chance to take each quiz. For exam integrity reasons, no retakes or late quizzes will be accepted.

Attendance/Participation (5%): Participation marks are awarded at the end of the term at the discretion of the instructor. Criteria considered when determining this mark include: Attendance at Virtual Class sessions: Students are expected to attend all Virtual Class sessions and stay for the entire class. Missed classes without prior discussion/approval will impact your participation mark.
Engagement during Virtual Class Sessions: Students are expected to actively participate during class. Are you responding to questions posed by the instructor or your classmates? Are you utilizing the tools in the virtual classroom (such as emojis, writing instruments, polls, microphone, video camera) to show that you are actively listening and participating? Your participation in the chat box for each virtual class is quantified and recorded. Verbal responses (using the microphone) feel more authentic and are encouraged.
Role-Plays/ Group Work: Are you sitting in class but not involved? Full participation in breakout rooms, discussions, and group work is required for this class.
Questions: Demonstrate active listening by asking questions of peers during virtual class sessions and posing questions for the instructor.
Contribution: Offer your perspective and/or answer questions about class materials. Take the discussion to the next level.
Respectful Conduct: Some topics may be sensitive and/or invite debate of different perspectives. Disrespectful language and/or conduct will not be permitted.
