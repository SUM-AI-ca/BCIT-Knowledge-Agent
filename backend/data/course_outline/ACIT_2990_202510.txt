## Course Description
The Co-op experience allows students to apply the knowledge learned from the program. This is a paid position acquired through the Co-op Education office.

## Learning Resources
Required for this course is completion of an online pre-employment class, attendance at 5 mandatory pre-employment seminars along with required assignments and coaching sessions as instructed by the Co-op Coordinator. All necessary resources will be provided by the Centre for Workplace Education (CWE) office.

## Evaluation Criteria
The student’s progress on the job is monitored by a BCIT Co-op Coordinator and their performance on the job is supervised on an ongoing basis by the student’s Co-op employer. Students must complete the minimum required work hours to receive credit.
The Work Term course consist of the following evaluation components: The Co-op Work Term Report The Employer’s Evaluation of the Co-op Students Performance: The following course grades may be assigned: - Satisfactory: All course requirements fulfilled. - Unsatisfactory: Course requirements not fulfilled. Students must receive Satisfactory on both evaluation components to pass this course.
