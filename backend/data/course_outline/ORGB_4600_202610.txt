### Course Description
This course looks at planned organizational change, defined as a set of activities and processes designed to change individuals, groups, and organizational processes, systems and structures. A number of models, frameworks and tools are used to identify the need for change and to understand the elements required for implementing an effective change strategy. The course addresses change as non-linear, complex and often messy undertaking, where multiple perspectives and approaches are required.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Assess and understand the need for change from multiple perspectives
- Discuss the principles and processes through which planned change may be accomplished
- Describe the environmental factors that may cause/impact change within an organization
- Develop a communication strategy to articulate the need for change and share a comprehensive change vision
- Apply various change models/tools to determine the what and how of change within the formal and informal structures of an organization
- Examine organizational systems that can foster, impede and/or facilitate the acceptance of change initiatives
- Identify the roles of organizational culture, power dynamics and stakeholder engagement on a change initiative
- Explore the role of resistance to change
- Describe the purpose and competencies of an effective change agent
- Evaluate models used to control and measure the effectiveness of a change initiative
