### Course Description
​This course focuses on the systematic strategic planning approach used at an airline or airport organizations. The goal of strategic planning is to analyze and improve a strategic business plan in terms of the vision, mission, values, strategic goals, balanced scorecard and financial results of the aviation organization. Evaluation includes analyses of a business plan components, SWOT analysis, revision of a strategic plan and creation of an executive summary. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Compare and contrast the concepts of strategic planning with other types of business planning.
- Analyze the values, vision and mission for an airport and airline and identify areas for improvements.
- Analyze the balanced scorecard and financial statements and identify areas for improvement.
- Complete Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats analysis (SWOT).
- Apply management planning tools that support development of the strategic plan.
- Apply financing strategies for capital investment for both airport and airline organizations.
- Revise the existing strategic plan for airport or airline.
- Create the executive summary for an existing airport or airline strategic business plan.
