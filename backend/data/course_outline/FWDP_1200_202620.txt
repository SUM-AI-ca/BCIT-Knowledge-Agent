### Course Description
User Experience (UX) plays a critical role in the success of products and services. This course will focus on the theory of website UX planning, the design process, and the integration of UX into the web design and development process. Students will learn the fundamentals of defining requirements, creating information architecture, and evaluating solutions using usability testing techniques. This course will use a combination of lectures, in-class discussions, hands-on exercises, group work, and tool demonstrations. Evaluation will include a combination of group and individual assignments.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to;
- Recognize the main disciplines of web usability, interface design, and user experience.
- Gather user requirements.
- Implement and manage website/app content inventory by organizing and labelling content.
- Implement and manage website/app content inventory by organizing and labelling content.
- Apply information architecture principles to user experience to enable users to find and manage information successfully.
- Plan the structure and functionality of a website/mobile application.
- Prepare visual documentation, such as user journeys, sitemaps, and wireframes.
- Analyze and select appropriate evaluation methods within an iterative design process to improve user interface designs (UI) and user experience (UX).
- Design and conduct usability testing and web evaluations that include accessibility.
- Analyze the performance of a website on the usability objectives and goals of the website and identify the changes required to improve user performance and satisfaction.
- Prepare a formal report on website performance analysis.
### Learning Resources
Books (foundational) The Design of Everyday Things -Donald A. Norman Don't Make Me Think, Revisited - Steve Krug
There are many other books. However, I would read these blogs first:
Smashing Magazine – https://www.smashingmagazine.com/category/user-experience Smashing Magazine is a website where you will find the best tips to make not only your UX design process but also the experiences you craft, to the next level. A meaningful experience that lasts. Isn’t that what we all want to achieve?
NNgroup – https://www.nngroup.com/ NNgroup (Nielsen Norman Group) is a world leader in Research-Based User Experience. They share their research findings to help designers and teams improve user experience. They are legends and one of their founders – Don Norman coined the term “User Experience”.
UXmatters – https://www.uxmatters.com/ UXmatters provides insights and inspiration to experienced professionals working in every aspect of User Experience, as well as those who are just beginning their journey in the field.
UX magazine – https://uxmag.com/ UX Magazine is a free community resource exploring all facets of experience design. They work closely with practitioners and industry leaders versed in all areas of UX to provide a steady stream of engaging and useful content.
The UX Collective – https://uxdesign.cc/ The UX Collective is a platform built to elevate unheard design voices all over the world. Curated stories on user experience, visual and product design.
Usability Geek – https://usabilitygeek.com/ Usability Geek is a blog that provides practical and useful insights into topics like Usability, User Experience (UX), Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Information Architecture (IA) and related fields.
UX Movement – https://uxmovement.com/ UX Movement is an independent publication that teaches you how to design intuitive user experiences through innovative tips, techniques, and best practices.
UX Myths – https://uxmyths.com/ UX Myths collects the most frequent user experience misconceptions and explains why they don’t hold true. They’ll show you a lot of research findings and articles by design and usability gurus.
UX planet – https://uxplanet.org/ UX planet is the one-stop resource for everything related to user experience. With a useful section for beginners with UX basics.
The UX Blog – https://theuxblog.com/ The UX Blog is a resource for user experience design, user research, and UX strategy. They collaborate with UX Practitioners and thought leaders from a broad array of disciplines to give their audience insightful and actionable advice that has practical application in the workplace.
Usability.gov – https://www.usability.gov/ Usability.gov is the leading resource for user experience (UX) best practices and guidelines, serving practitioners and students in the government and private sectors.
Laws of UX – https://lawsofux.com/ Laws of UX is a collection of the maxims and principles that designers can consider when building user interfaces. It was created by https://jonyablonski.com/
A List Apart – https://alistapart.com/ A List Apart explores the design, development, and meaning of web content, with a special focus on web standards and best practices.
CustomerThink – https://customerthink.com/ CustomerThink is a global online community of business leaders striving to create profitable customer-centric enterprises.
UX Project Checklist – https://uxchecklist.github.io/ UX Project Checklist is a simple tool to keep in mind what you want to consider from a UX point of view while working on a project in an ongoing organisation.
Portfolio Development: https://careerfoundry.com/en/blog/ux-design/ux-design-portfolio-tips/ https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-design-portfolios/
