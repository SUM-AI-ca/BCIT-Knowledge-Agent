### Course Description
Covers survey fundamentals and quantity take-off of civil engineering projects pertaining to roadwork, water, sewer and building construction. Topics include quantity take-off, productivity rates, labour and material costing, construction equipment and machinery, and project cost control. .
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Read and interpret typical civil construction drawings.
- Identify common construction equipment and their applications.
- Identify fundamental construction activities for a typical construction project.
- Prepare portions of a contractor's estimate.
- Use basic project cost control on a project.
### Learning Resources
Please attend the class with the following:
- Scientic Calculator (mobile phone works, but I find them less efficient)
- Ruler (scale is more powerful but not necessary), and/or software that can scale (Bluebeam for example)

Optionally, consider the following
- RS Means Heavy Construction Handbook. This book has North American standardized estimating costs.
- Caterpillar Equipment Handbook. Potentially available for download online.
- The Blue Book Equipment Rental Rate Guide
### Course Goals
By the end of this course you will develop the following skills - Ability to read and interpret civil drawings and associated tender information - Identify equipment required for a typical civil project - Develop basic first principle estimates - Have a general understanding of most costs that will apply to a typical civil tender

### Course Specific Requirements
Please ensure you have awareness of typical geometry, such as area, volume of shapes common in the construction industry. In addition, be able to convert from metric to imperial and vice versa as well as use specific gravity for establishing material weights.
Math and basic financial understanding is key to being an excellent estimator.
