### Course Description
A continuation of PERF 7200, this course will focus on the safe application, maintenance and weaning of Cardiopulmonary Bypass (CPB). The physiological principles of Extracorporeal circulation and its effects on the body will be a major focus of this course. A thorough understanding of metabolism, anticoagulation and the systemic effects of CPB are essential to the practicing Clinical Perfusionist.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the systemic effects CPB can have on multiple organ systems
- Describe pre-operative preparation techniques and patient considerations
- Explain the importance of Coagulation Management while on CPB
- Discuss the application of appropriate anticoagulation techniques during CPB
- List the common tests used to asses anticoagulation during CPB
- Explain the principles involved in the safe initiation of CPB
- Describe the maintenance phase of CPB, and the tools used by Perfusionists to ensure safe and effective care of the cardiothoracic patient during this period of time
- List the steps involved when preparing for separation, or weaning from bypass
- Describe the process of weaning from CPB
- Compare the systemic effects CPB has on various organ systems
- Describe the application hypothermia during CPB

### Course Schedule and Assignments
I have organized the course with on-line discussions and written assignments to help you organize and manage your time appropriately with an incentive to keep up with the material in the modules.
The elements used in this course are:
Discussion Forums & Tutorials
Discussions are focused on cases that you can encounter as a perfusionist and are meant to aid with applying theory to practice. Discussions are also a good platform to work out questions and issues that you may have with the content or to help others with their questions. Your active participation is essential to your learning in PERF 7210 and will be worth 5% of your final mark. More details are contained in the Assessment section.
There are 3 synchronous tutorials which will make up 5% of your overall mark in addition to the tutorial presentation worth 10%.
Module Assignments and Exams
There are two written module assignments in this course and two written examinations, a midterm, and a final exam.
Late assignments will be subject to a 10% deduction per day if submitted past the due date but before the release of the grades. Assignments submitted after the release of the grades will not be accepted and full marks will be lost.
