## Course Description
Analyses business strategy formulation to give the student practical experience and confidence in handling complex business situations. Comprehensive business cases will be selected in fields such as finance, control, personnel, production, marketing, and general management. Acquaints the student with management decision-making and effective verbal and written business analysis. Teamwork and organizational change are addressed as elements of strategy implementation.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Integrate related disciplines and skills in a time sensitive decision-making environment.
- Apply strategic management techniques to public and private sectors.
- Perform standard financial statement and ratio analysis.
- Apply strategic theories to business examples.
- Analyse organizations, thus effecting desired changes within a comprehensive, responsive management system.
- Adapt various decision tools to case studies and projects (i.e. Value Chain Analysis, Economic Value business problems) using a structured decision model.
- Recommend solutions to business problems.
- Identify implementation issues when developing a solution to a business problem.

## Course Goals
Develop Strategic Thinking Skills

- Cultivate the ability to analyze complex business environments using frameworks such as SWOT, PESTEEL, Five Forces, and Value Chain analysis.
- Apply integrative thinking to balance competing stakeholder interests in decision-making.

Integrate Business Knowledge for Strategic Decision-Making

- Synthesize concepts from finance, marketing, operations, human resources, and leadership to form cohesive strategies.
- Demonstrate how cross-functional alignment supports long-term organizational success.

Apply Strategic Frameworks and Tools

- Utilize the models and approaches presented in Strategic Analysis and Action to diagnose organizational challenges.
- Formulate strategic options at the corporate, business, and functional levels.
- Evaluate strategies using criteria such as feasibility, alignment, sustainability, and competitive advantage.

Strengthen Problem-Solving and Critical Analysis

- Examine real-world cases to assess strategic issues and identify opportunities.
- Critically evaluate global and industry-specific dynamics that influence strategic choices.

Enhance Strategic Communication and Leadership Competence

- Develop persuasive arguments and communicate strategic recommendations clearly in written and oral formats.
- Practice leadership and teamwork skills through collaborative case studies and projects.

Understand Ethical, Sustainable, and Global Dimensions of Strategy

- Evaluate the role of ethics, corporate social responsibility, and environmental sustainability in shaping business strategy.
- Recognize the importance of global competition and technological disruption in strategic planning.

## Course topics
Introduction to Strategic Management

- The role of strategic management in organizations
- General management perspective
- Defining strategy and strategic fit

Strategy Concepts and Levels

- Corporate, business, and functional strategy
- Vision, mission, and objectives
- The role of stakeholders in strategy

The Diamond-E Framework

- Linking environment, resources, management preferences, and organization
- Assessing alignment and misalignment
- Applying the Diamond-E to case analysis

Environmental Analysis Tools

- PESTEL analysis (macro-environment)
- Porter’s Five Forces (industry structure)
- BEAM and other analytical frameworks

Strategy–Environment Link

- Assessing opportunities and threats
- Industry and market dynamics
- External fit in strategy formulation

Resource and Capabilities Analysis

- VRIO framework
- Core competencies and distinctive capabilities
- Internal strengths and weaknesses

Organizational Analysis

- Structure, systems, and processes
- Culture and readiness for change
- Linking organization to strategic execution

Management Preferences and Leadership

- Leadership style and decision-making
- Ethical responsibility in strategy
- Strategic choices and biases

Strategic Choice and Strategic Processes

- Evaluating strategic alternatives
- Strategy formulation methods
- Balancing deliberate and emergent strategies

Implementing Strategy I: The Change Agenda

- Leading organizational change
- Strategic execution challenges
- Managing resistance and building alignment

Implementing Strategy II: Guidelines and Action

- Turning strategies into action plans
- Performance measurement and monitoring
- Linking strategy to execution and outcomes

Integrating Strategic Analysis and Action

- Pulling together environment, resources, organization, and leadership
- Synthesizing insights across frameworks
- Capstone case analysis and presentations

Course Wrap-Up and Final Examination

- Review of strategic analysis frameworks
- Integrative decision-making
- Applying concepts to complex business problems
