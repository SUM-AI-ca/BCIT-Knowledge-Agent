## Course Description
Covers areas of fluids, thermal physics, vibrations, waves and electricity, as well as light and properties of radiation. Topics in fluids include fluid pressure, buoyancy, and fluid dynamics. Topics in thermal physics include kinetic theory of gases, specific and latent heat, thermal expansion, and heat transfer. Vibrations and waves covers types of simple vibrations, waves, standing waves and resonance and sound. Topics in electricity include DC and AC circuits and electrical safety. Particular attention is paid to the importance and interpretation of noise measurements in the workplace.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Identify some of the basic physical principles underlying the OCHS technology.
- Use analytical skills in the solution of technical problems.
- Analyze experimental data.
- Write scientific reports.

## Course topics
1. Fluids - Hydrostatic pressure - Safety aspects related to pressure hazards - Bernoulli's Equation

2. Temperature and Heat - Heat and heat energy - Temperature hazards and psychrometric measurement - Ideal gas law - Heat transfer

3. Waves and vibrations - Vibrations and resonance - Waves and the physical nature of sound - Sound safety (decibel scale, hearing damage) - Acoustics

4. Light and Illumination - The physical nature of light - Introduction to light safety - Introduction to photometry

5. Nuclear Physics (basics of Radiation Safety) - Nuclear physics and elementary particles - Safety: types of radiation and shielding

6. Electricity and Magnetism - Electric Fields (power lines, arcing) - Electric Current (safety and current through body) - Basic introduction to DC circuits - Basic introduction to magnetism
