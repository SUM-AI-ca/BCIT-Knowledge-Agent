## Course Description
This course introduces students to the fundamentals of exploration databases and spatial data management. GIS technology will be applied to the mapping, display, and analysis of mineral exploration data. Topics covered include fundamental concepts, definitions, organization of databases and GIS; data integration and conversion; spatial and non-spatial query, analysis, display and reporting. Quality assurance and quality control of exploration databases will be discussed. Labs will develop skills in the use of GIS and database software for managing and analyzing mineral exploration data.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe fundamental concepts of Database management and Geographic Information Systems. (1.7,1.8)
- Identify the concept of geospatial analysis of data. (1.7,1.8)
- Conduct basic database QA/QC procedures.( 1.7,2.1)
- Perform basic analytical operations in GIS. (5.1,6.1)
- Utilize GIS and database software to perform common GIS and database tasks. (4.1,5.1,5.3,6.1)​
