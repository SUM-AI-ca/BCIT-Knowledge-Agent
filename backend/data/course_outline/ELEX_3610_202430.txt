## Course Description
This course provides a basic working knowledge of process measurement and control systems to provide a deep appreciation as to their application in industry and the challenges associated with the networking and network security of them. Building on the concepts developed in the Industrial Systems for Industrial Network Cybersecurity, Calculus for Industrial Network Cybersecurity, and Engineering Fundamentals for Industrial Network Cybersecurity courses in term three, this course prepares students for the Distributed Control and Safety Instrumented Systems for Industrial Network Cybersecurity course in term four. The concepts being developed in this course are closely related to the concepts being developed concurrently in the Embedded Systems and Industrial Network Security courses. Topics include: transducers and instruments used for measuring pressure, level, flow, and temperature; open- and closed-loop control; ON/OFF and Proportional, Integral and Derivative (PID) control; and advanced control strategies including feedforward and cascade. Lab activities will include commissioning a simple control loop. ​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Interpret the ISA symbology used to document simple process measurement and control systems. (3)
- Explain the difference between open and closed loop systems and describe the major elements required for a feedback control system. (2)
- Explain the difference between ON/OFF and continuous control. (2)
- Differentiate between the contributions of Proportional, Integral and Derivative modes in control systems. (2)
- Implement common measurement devices found in process control including instruments for measuring pressure, flow and temperature (install and configure). (3)
- Describe the challenges associated with controlling industrial processes. (2)
- Implement a single loop strategy appropriate for industrial process control. (4)
- Explain the general principles of advanced control strategies including feedforward and cascade. (4)

## Course Goals
Using basic instrumentation terminology and symbology. Calibrating analog and digital electronic transmitters. Analyzing the principles of operation of instruments. Assessing measurement instruments for specific applications and performing basic measurements. Recognizing and appreciating common closed-loop control strategies. Commissioning a closed-loop control system.
