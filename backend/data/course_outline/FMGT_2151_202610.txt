### Course Description
​This course focuses on the context of accounting and the use of accounting information for decision making, planning, and control. It will examine how managers can utilize the tools of economics, accounting/finance, and decision theory to aid in these critical decisions. The course will consider how differences and changes in the internal operating conditions can affect a manager's resource allocation decisions. Topics include worksheet accounting, short-run decision-making and financial analysis.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe and differentiate the three forms of business organization
- Prepare and analyze financial statements
- Apply the basic principles of worksheet bookkeeping to record transactions for both a service and a merchandising business, including period-end adjustments
- Identify capital assets
- Understand, explain, and employ various methods of depreciation
- Define and differentiate between a variety of terms used in cost and managerial accounting
- Apply financial analysis techniques to a company's financial statements
- Analyze and make recommendations in several typical business decision scenarios
- Develop cash budgets
- Identify the components of a master budget and prepare a flexible budget.
- Apply the concepts of responsibility accounting to management evaluation and control.​

### Attendance Requirements
Attendance and participation are integral for success in this course. Students are responsible for reading and understanding BCIT's attendance policy included in this course outline.
Students are required to complete all course requirements at the scheduled times. There will be no make-up assignments or assessments. If you miss an assignment or assessment, you will receive zero marks. Exceptions may be made for approved excused absences and accommodations, in accordance with the information below and BCIT Procedure 5101-PR1.
Excused Absences and Accommodations
A student who misses an assignment or assessment due to a medical reason or other unavoidable causes will be provided with an opportunity to make up for that missed exam or evaluation, but only under the following conditions:
- The student must notify the instructor that they will miss the assignment or assessment as soon as possible, preferably prior to the time when the assignment or assessment is scheduled to occur. Timely notice should be as soon as possible, but never more than 7 calendar days after the missed event.
- For medical-related absences, the student must provide a BCIT-approved medical certificate, obtained at the student’s own expense, to show that an assignment or assessment was missed due to medical reasons. The BCIT Student Medical Certificate is available at the following URL: https://www.bcit.ca/files/healthservices/pdf/studentmedicalcertificate.pdf
- For other unavoidable absences the student should be prepared to provide supporting documentation.
Consistent with these policies, students are advised that in this course:
- The only acceptable medical certificate is the BCIT Student Medical Certificate completed and signed & stamped by their doctor that is available at the following URL: https://www.bcit.ca/files/healthservices/pdf/studentmedicalcertificate.pdf
- Students who fail to notify their instructor or submit to their instructor the BCIT Student Medical Certificate in a timely manner, will be awarded zero marks on the missed assignment or assessment. Timely manner means as soon as possible, but never more than 7 calendar days after the missed event.
- Students who miss an exam due to an excused absence will have to schedule and write a replacement exam at BCIT’s Test Centre. Students are responsible for covering any invigilation fees incurred. This replacement exam must be written within 14 calendar days of the original date of the missed exam.
Accessibility Services (AS)
Students with Individual Accommodation Plans (IAPs) are required to inform Accessibility Services (in the required timeframe) if they plan on taking an assessment at the AS centre. Similarly, students with IAPs wishing to have accommodations provided by instructors for assessments taken outside of AS must inform the instructor of their request at least five (5) days before each assessment is due. Failure to do so for each assessment will result in no accommodations being provided, resulting in students having to take their assessments under the same circumstances as all other students.
