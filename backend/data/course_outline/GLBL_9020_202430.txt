## Course Description
Examine the role of leadership in developing and nurturing an effective global team. You will build on your knowledge of the impact cultural dimensions have on communication styles, working relationships, team performance, and leadership styles. Through collaborative and individual learning activities, you will analyze, practice, and refine the soft skills and strategies needed to create and lead high-performing global teams. Learn to recognize potential cross-cultural challenges, and develop strategies to mitigate them to enhance your leadership capacities and enable you to better manage and motivate cross-cultural teams. Competencies may be demonstrated through a blend of individual and collaborative assignments, skill demonstrations, and application to real world or hypothetical scenarios.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Evaluate pros and limits to various leadership styles.
- Analyze and reflect on personal leadership style and its impact in a work environment.
- Examine team dynamics in cross-cultural and/or global teams.
- Analyze and evaluate barriers to leading in a global context.
- Design effective strategies to manage cross-cultural challenges at the team level.
- Apply different leadership styles and best practices to manage a global team.
