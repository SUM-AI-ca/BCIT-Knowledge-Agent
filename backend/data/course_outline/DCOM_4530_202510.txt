## Course Description
​This course is a work-sharing experience within the digital communications and wireless industries. Students will be placed with a host company to job shadow professional technicians. Students will work in a field environment and have opportunities to apply their knowledge of test equipment and troubleshooting techniques. Activities will be tracked with a daily log book. Students will select a topic based upon the host company's technologies to research and produce a report for evaluation by faculty. This report will also form the basis of a class presentation on their work experience.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate workplace safety practice.
- Demonstrate professional work habits.
- Describe roles and expectations of a technician in the digital communications industry.
- Create workplace correspondence and activity logs.
- Research a selected technology in use by a host company.
- Create a report on a technology used by a host company.
- Deliver a short presentation on the work experience.​

## Attendance Requirements
Regular attendance on the practicum placement is seen as integral to student success, and will be monitored. Students are expected to be punctual for the full duration.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
The student must wear steel-toed boots, if required by their Host Agency. Student will be informed, one month prior to commencment of practicum, if there is a requirement for steeled-toed safety boots.

Other safety equipment will be supplied by the Host Agency. Student must comply with wearing all safety gear required for the work being performed.

## Other information
Student must provide their own means of transportation to and from the Host Agency's location.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
The first two weeks of the course will be spent onsite with the practicum host. The final week will be at BCIT and will consist of an in-class presentation on your experience with the practicum host.
