## Course Description
PERF 7520 builds on the skills learned during Perfusion Lab 1. Students will move toward independent bypass circuit set-up and priming the heart lung machine. Preparation for bypass surgery will be reviewed in-depth and students will be introduced to the process of initiating, maintenance and weaning off of bypass.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate following the procedural safety checklist.
- Identify and resolve common problems when priming the bypass circuit.
- Demonstrate sterile technique during circuit set-up and priming.
- Practice clearly communicating during all stages of bypass surgery.
- Demonstrate the different techniques required during initiation, maintaining and weaning off of bypass surgery.

## Course Goals
This second 1-week applied lab reinforces safe practice within the simulated wet lab setting. This course will continue to build on the procedural policies, safety protocols, and checklists associated with the safe operation of the equipment associated with cardiopulmonary bypass introduced in PERF 7500.

## Evaluation Criteria
Students will be required to complete 3 attempts for each criterion of the procedural circuit safety checklist and demonstrate the associated foundational skills in all of its outlined components needed for the safe operation of the cardiopulmonary bypass equipment. Ongoing and formative feedback will be provided by the instructor to highlight areas for growth (G) and areas of Strength (S).

By the end of this lab week, students must demonstrate strength (S) in all asterisked criteria of the circuit set-up checklist in addition to the required foundational knowledge elements to be considered successful and achieve a Satisfactory mark in PERF 7520. A full list of the asterisked criteria is identified within the course content. Attendance is mandatory. Grading in this course is Satisfactory-S or Unsatisfactory-U
