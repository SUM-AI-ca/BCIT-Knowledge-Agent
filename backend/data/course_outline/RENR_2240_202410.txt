## Course Description
This course provides a broad overview of law in Canada and the province of British Columbia as it applies to resource development and environmental regulation. Students in this course study the legal framework of Canada, key federal and provincial acts, and important court decisions as they relate to the environment and Indigenous people. Students will also learn how to access and use legal resources, review best practices for working with regulators, and the roles and responsibilities of environmental professionals.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Find, read, and interpret laws related to natural resources
- Describe Canadian legal context and jurisdictions
- Discuss examples of key legislation in Canada and BC that relate to resource management and environmental regulation
- Describe best practices for working with regulators
- Discuss Indigenous treaty rights and important court decisions
- Describe roles and responsibilities of environmental professionals
- Explain structure and processes of the criminal justice system as it relates to environmental legislation and enforcement
- Demonstrate respectful and professional behaviour that meets industry standards and expectations

## Course Goals
This course is designed to be a broad introduction to Canadian Environmental Law. It will cover a wide range of federal and provincial legislation relating to the protection of the environment. Topics to be covered include; the origin of Canadian Law, the Constitution Act, division of government powers, common environmental legislation, working with regulators, permit requirements and applications, arrest, search and seizure, conducting inspections and investigations, collection and preservation of evidence, interviewing procedures, and court processes. During class, students will have an opportunity to apply their knowledge in discussions, field scenarios, preparation of court briefs based on field scenarios and case studies and participation in mock court trials.
