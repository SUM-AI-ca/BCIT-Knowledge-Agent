## Course Description
​For any event – regardless of purpose - creating "buzz" or generating attendance is absolutely vital! You will be introduced to marketing fundamentals such as the 4 P's of marketing, SWOT analysis, event branding, and advertising in order to develop a marketing plan for a simulated event that will positively impact event attendance and / or growth. You will be required to develop a comprehensive event or festival marketing plan.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Conduct basic market research, including a situational analysis, to define target market segments.
- Write compelling positioning statements and unique value propositions reflective of the target audiences' needs and stakeholder expectations.
- Determine strategic event pricing based on market research and ensure alignment with event budget, forecast and goals.
- Develop an event marketing plan that meets the goals and objectives of the event stakeholders.
- Distinguish between the relative strengths of print, electronic, and other advertising mediums to enable the development of effective marketing communications.

## Course Goals
Live and virtual events are important tools for businesses to reach target markets. This course covers how to segment and target your event to produce results, and develop tactics that will help communicate your marketing message with live and virtual events. This course looks at how and when to use live and virtual events, and how to market them.

## Course Specific Requirements
Preparation for Class: This course is composed of a lecture and lab each week where attendance and participation is expected by all students. For each session students are expected to be prepared. It is each student's responsibility to understand what is required and to complete the readings and other preparation before each lab. Preparation will usually involve some readings and questions. Students are encouraged to go beyond the supplied resources and find additional research on the topic at hand. There is no textbook for this course and students will need to read from electronic sources in order to understand class content and complete assignments. Participation and reflection will be encouraged and evaluated as part of this course.

Professional Behavior: In order to create a productive and professional learning environment this course follows these guidelines:

- Students are expected to attend all labs, except in the event of illness or some other very good reason for being absent. In such cases, the students must e-mail the instructor as soon as possible to advise the extent and reason for the absence.
- Students will refrain from general, non-class related surfing of the net, playing computer games or reading material not related to the session; e-mail, chat or instant messaging. This is an expected professional courtesy to colleagues and instructors
- While casual attire is generally appropriate, students are expected to wear “business casual” attire when we have guest speakers; no shorts or T-shirts. More formal attire is expected for major team presentations unless other apparel is employed for dramatic effect.
- Students are expected to be prepared for each class, which means being able to add value to discussions, general knowledge or opinion-based input will not be sufficient. Students are encouraged to volunteer to speak during class, the instructor will also call on students at random to ask questions or invite opinions.
- Punctuality is extremely important as students who arrive late disturb all others.
- All students are expected to contribute during class discussions in order to enrich the learning experience for everyone. However, an individual student should not monopolize or dominate discussions, precluding or intimidating others from contributing. When someone is talking is expected that everyone will listen and not hold parallel conversations
- In general, students are expected to behave as professionals during lecture and lab time.
- Students are encouraged to bring laptops to class. In some cases, students will be asked to go online in small groups to undertake research and prepare for discussion.
- Students are expected to act ethically and with academic integrity at all times.
