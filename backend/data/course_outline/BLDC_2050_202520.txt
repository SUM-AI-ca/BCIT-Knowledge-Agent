## Course Description
Continues from BLDC 1050, acquainting students with the properties of various materials and assemblies that are used in MasterFormat Divisions 6 through 9, with an emphasis on the methods in which they are implemented in a construction project.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student should be able to:

- Identify construction materials from the Divisions 6-9 of the MasterFormat and allocate them according to standard practice grouping.
- Understand the chemical and physical make-up of some basic construction materials and assemblies.
- Understand the sequence of installing these basic components with a view to authorities having jurisdiction.

## Course topics
This course introduces basic materials and methods used in construction from Divisions 6 to 9 of the MasterFormat System. Topics include: physical and chemical properties; the manufacturing process of various material and methods and the way they are implemented in a construction project.

Students will be able to utilize their knowledge of the manufacture, properties and purpose of the construction materials and components identified above to:

1. Select materials for the appropriate use.

2. Ensure proper installation of those materials.

3. Communicate vital site process information using and understanding the terminology.

4. Understand and utilize the MasterFormat process for organizing materials in a comprehensive manner.
