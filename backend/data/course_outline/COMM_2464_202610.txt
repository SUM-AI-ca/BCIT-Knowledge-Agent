### Course Description
This course introduces advanced technical writing techniques and principles. In labs, students write industry-oriented reports and give technical briefings. They prepare proposals, progress reports, and documentation describing the project they designed and produced for ROBT 4491 - Robotics Project. They also write a resume and an application letter and hold meetings and interviews.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Produce professional quality job application materials.
- Perform competently at job interviews.
- Deliver effective oral presentations using PowerPoint.
- Structure and write project proposals, progress reports and formal reports.
- Participate effectively in business meetings and write minutes for them.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.
