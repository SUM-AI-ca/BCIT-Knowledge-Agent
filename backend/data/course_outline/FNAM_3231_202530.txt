## Course Description
This course provides the student with the opportunity to become involved with an applied research project of particular interest in the field of Renewable Resources. Experience will be gained in planning a project, working productively with other individuals in a team environment, recording relevant information in a number of different formats (including journals), meeting the stated objectives of the project, and reporting on a renewable resource-related group project. Technical Projects 1 focusses on preparing a comprehensive project proposal.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Select a topic for a technical project.
- Formulate goal statements for technical projects.
- Compose objective statements required to achieve goals for technical projects.
- Determine appropriate data sources for technical projects.
- Analyze the results of research projects in order to make conclusions and/or recommendations relevant to the project.
- Prepare literature reviews for technical projects and describe the purpose of a literature review.
- Document research sources in a manner consistent with accepted practices.
- Develop appropriate strategies to achieve objectives for technical projects.
- Prepare and present a project proposal and work schedule.
- Write concise project progress reports.
- Prepare meeting agenda and minutes.

## Attendance Requirements
Students must consult with their supervisor(s) and project team members if they are going to miss a scheduled meeting, field day or classroom session. Absences without warning may result in penalties on meeting attendance. Supervisors and students will work together to ensure that sickness or other reasons for absence are reasonably accommodated and made up for by the student without undue pressure on group mates.

Attendance/Illness (FWR/FNAM guidelines): If you need to miss class, please (1) notify instructors with as much advanced notice as possible (ideally written notice via email at least 24 hours in advance of class), and (2) arrange for a classmate to provide you with all missed handouts/notes/instructions. Students cannot miss more than 10% of class meetings without prior arrangements with the instructor. A doctor’s note may be required for any illness causing you to miss any assignments/projects/quiz/exam/lab/field trips. At the discretion of the instructor, you may complete the work missed or have the work prorated for excused absences.

Unapproved absence in excess of the prescribed regulations within this document may result in failure or forced withdrawal from the course or program. Please see Policy 5101 - Student Regulations. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT approved medical certificate submitted to the program, substantiating the reason for absence. For other absences, the student should be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation as requested.

## Course Specific Requirements
Group Work Expectations

Group work is a critical part of natural resource professions, and you will need to be able to work as a team effectively. Your learning in this course and program will be impacted if you are not able to collaborate and communicate effectively and respectfully with peers and instructors. Group assignments make up a significant portion of this course, it is expected that everyone contributes their fair share. If a student does not contribute their fair share to group work, instructors reserve the right to penalize their evaluations within reason. Repeated offences (lack of contributions to field or written work, inappropriate communications, distracted or inattentive conduct, etc.) will be treated more strongly.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
Appropriate PPE and gear must be worn in the field - appropriate sturdy footwear (caulks, hiking boots, etc.), hard hat and hi-vis if appropriate or required at site and warm gear for winter.
