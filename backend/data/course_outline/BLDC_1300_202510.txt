## Course Description
Introduction to measurement of construction work, with a brief review of reading construction drawings and specifications. Specific study of methods of measurement techniques applicable to site work (excavation), concrete and masonry quantity take-offs.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Read and interpret information on construction drawings to:

- Prepare estimate work items checklists in accordance with industry MasterFormat Divisions.
- Prepare appropriate preliminary calculations for an estimate.
- Apply principles and techniques of measuring work items on standard quantity sheet forms.
- Extend and summarize work item final quantities in preparation for pricing.
- Comprehend the roles of estimates and estimators in the building development and construction process.

## Course Specific Requirements
Students MUST complete and hand in ALL assignments prior to the final exam. Late Assignments will be penalized with a 20% marks deduction per week.

Students unable to take an exam due to illness must advise the instructor prior to the exam and a medical certificate must be submitted to the instructor. The certificate must state the time period in question and state that student was unable to attend class due to medical reasons.

Most evenings will involve a lecture and practical demonstration of a measurement topic area, followed by a class assignment of the topic for submittal at end of class or for the beginning of the next class. All assignments have to be handed in to complete this course.
