## Course Description
This course is designed to allow the student to assimilate previous academic and practical experience, with CLGT training, and to explore some of the subject matter as outlined in the competencies for clinical genetics published by the CSMLS (January, 2016) . Students will be given assigned readings/case studies, and will use online discussion forums to post individual and group comments, and to create resources for self review. Students will also participate in weekly in-class discussions . The written final examination will consist of questions based on all readings and case studies.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

- Critically analyze scientific literature on topics relevant to the Clinical Genetics Technology profession.
- Write individual feedback based on assigned readings/case studies.
- Write group feedback based on assigned readings/case studies.
- Participate in the course-specific online discussion forum with other class members.
- Generate review material based on assigned readings.
- Organize and communicate information based on the CSMLS competencies for Clinical Genetics (January 2016) and current technology that is of value to other students.
- Participate during in-class discussions using appropriate communication techniques.
- Understand the principles of nucleic acid transfer, and chemiluminescence for the analysis of nucleic acids.
- Describe the roles of members of the healthcare team, including the patient, family and community, and their contributions to safe healthcare.
- Demonstrate respectful communication in collaborative decision-making as part of an interprofessional team.

## Course Goals
The goal of this course is to study selected topics from the CSMLS syllabus (including interprofessional education competencies), current technology, and recent publications to allow the student to process the information for individual or group reflection.
