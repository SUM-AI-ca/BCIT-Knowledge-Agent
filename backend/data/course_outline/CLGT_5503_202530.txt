### Course Description
This course is designed to allow the student to assimilate previous academic and practical experience, with CLGT training, and to explore some of the subject matter for clinical genetics. Students will be given assigned readings/case studies and will use online discussion forums to post individual and group comments, and to create resources for self-review. Students will also participate in in-class discussions . The written final examination will consist of questions based on all readings and case studies.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Critically analyze scientific literature on topics relevant to the Clinical Genetics Technology profession.
- Write individual feedback based on assigned readings/case studies.
- Write group feedback based on assigned readings/case studies.
- Participate in the course-specific online discussion forum with other class members.
- Generate review material based on assigned readings.
- Organize and communicate information based on Clinical Genetics topics and current technologies that is of value to other students.
- Participate during in-class discussions using appropriate communication techniques.
- Understand the principles of nucleic acid transfer, and chemiluminescence for the analysis of nucleic acids.
- Describe the roles of members of the healthcare team, including the patient, family and community, and their contributions to safe healthcare.
- Demonstrate respectful communication in collaborative decision-making as part of an interprofessional team.​
