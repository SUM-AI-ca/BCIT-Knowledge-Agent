## Course Description
This course is a continuation of CLGT 5504 and provides the students with an opportunity to refine digital imaging skills using CytoVision®. Emphasis will be placed on the quality of the finished karyotype/metaphase for band resolution, contrast, and analyzability. It is a critical skill for students to be able to use the software to maximize the resolution of the bands for all karotypes and WM produced for all exercises submitted.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

- Gain further expertise in all aspects of computer karyotype imaging, from specimen banding to image capture and karyotype production.
- Understand how the above-mentioned techniques are used to optimize the quality of the final karyotype/metaphase using the image enhancement features of CytoVision® software.
- Demonstrate professional practice when completing tasks and assignments.
- Communicate and interact with instructor and fellow students in a professional manner.
