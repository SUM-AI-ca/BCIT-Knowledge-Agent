### Course Description
This course engages students in new media project management, team work and freelancing. The students will learn about both the potential benefits and challenges intrinsic in new media projects in a variety of work environments. All aspects of project management and team development are examined: project assessment, negotiation, cost-estimation, planning, documentation, time tracking, meetings and presentations. Students are also introduced to skills that help them to analyze when to get legal and accounting assistance. Students are expected to complete two small new media projects using waterfall and agile systems and will also be assessed by a combination of quizzes and two group projects.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Define the main characteristics of project management methodologies for new media.
- Describe the key roles, skills and techniques in new media project management.
- Perform the main responsibilities of a new media project manager or scrum-master
- Scope out a project, identifying the key components, resources, timeline and budget.
- Write proposals, contracts and other documentation for new media projects.
- Complete a project assessment and develop a production schedule for a new media project.
- Estimate and formulate a budget for a waterfall new media project.
- Recognize the main stages of team/group formation and functioning in new media for agile and waterfall project processes.
- Recognize signs of slippage or potential problems in a new media project and propose appropriate problem solving techniques and methods.
