## Course Description
This course assists students with their industry Safety Program Review (SPR). Students spend one day per week gathering information at the company, conducting interviews, designing and administering a questionnaire, reviewing safety-related documentation, and inspecting workplace conditions. They also research relevant legislation for the company chosen and using this information, students evaluate the program and present their findings to industry sponsors, the instructor, and the OCHS program head. Students work with industry representatives, handle correspondence, and write a formal report. They also implement one of the report's recommendations in the workplace.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Arrange a terms of reference for a safety program review with a client in industry.
- Design, administer, and interpret questionnaires surveying employees and managers.
- Organize and conduct interviews with employees and managers.
- Collect and evaluate relevant documents for the review.
- Write a safety program review report for the client.
- Present review findings to supervisors at BCIT and to management at the company.
- Document client communication and design a log of work time on the project (mock invoice).
- Deliver oral and written progress reports to the client and instructors.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.
- Develop criteria for evaluating the effectiveness of the client's safety communication.
- Participate in meetings at the company.
- Write an article for a corporate or safety newsletter.
