## Course Description
Are you in an HR role or a Management role with a large HR component? This on-line course uses a number of interactive, multi-media tools to teach students how to choose, implement and use best practice Human Resource metrics and analysis techniques that are aligned with organizational strategy. It looks at how to evaluate the financial impact of Human Resource initiatives and examines key metrics in areas such as engagement, absenteeism, recruitment and selection, performance management, health and employee development. Students will learn to show how investments in HR initiatives translate to bottom-line benefits

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
The learning outcomes for this course reflect both theory and practise in Human Resources Measurement

Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Provide a compelling argument for the value of strategic human resources measurement.
- Critically evaluate the quality of commonly used human resources measures.
- Explain the advantages and limitations of the major human resources measurement approaches.
- Understand fundamental statistics and research design concepts.
- Employ fundamental economic and finance concepts.
- Assess the financial and strategic impact of HR initiatives.
- Develop a HR measurement plan that is linked to and supports an organization’s strategy.
- Make effective written presentations.
- Work effectively as an on-line team member.

## Course Specific Requirements
DISCUSSION ASSIGNMENTS GRADING CRITERIA

Details (45% of Course Grade)

The discussion assignments are experiential learning activities. Each week you will be given an exercise to complete that is designed to help you learn how to create an HR measurement plan.

Since the discussion assignments are learning activities, you will not be graded on whether you get the answers right or wrong, but rather on the criteria listed below. Your grade will be determined by the quality, frequency, and timeliness of your discussion assignments. To receive full marks for the discussion assignments, you must complete each discussion assignment AND respond to one other student for each discussion assignment.

Each discussion activity is worth 10 points except for the student introduction which is automatically awarded 2 points and the ROI case which is worth 15 points.

Posts are due on Sundays and replies to classmates are due on Tuesdays. Late posts are penalized 10% per day and will not be accepted after 2 days past the due date. Students must submit their discussion assignments by the late deadline to avoid being recorded as absent for that week of the course.

All outside sources must be properly cited. The unauthorized use of Artificial Intelligence products (such as ChatGPT) for discussion assignments is prohibited, and when identified by your instructor, will result in an academic integrity violation. Refer to policy 5104 - Student Code of Academic Integrity

Very Good to Excellent (8.5 to 10 points)

- Post addresses ALL components of the discussion assignment questions
- Demonstrates strong analysis and insight into the question and the related course materials (text, videos, articles etc.) listed in the relevant learning module
- Relevant terms, theories and concepts from all of the course learning materials are applied as appropriate
- Shares relevant experiences and references current events that relate to the discussion topic
- Replies to classmates add value to the discussion by stating reasons if you agree or disagree with a comment or statement and/or offering insights based on your own experience and knowledge of the course materials.
- Responds to questions posed by other students and/or the instructor

Fair to Good (5 to 8.4 points)

- Post addresses most components of the discussion question
- Demonstrates some analysis and insight into the question and the related course materials
- A few relevant course terms, theories and concepts from some of the course learning materials are applied as appropriate
- Shares a little relevant experience but does not reference current events that relate to the discussion topic
- Reply to classmate is completed but adds limited value to the discussion
- Does not always respond to questions posed by other students and/or the instructor

Unsatisfactory (0 to 4.9 points)

- Post does not address most components of the discussion question
- Demonstrates little to no analysis and insight into the question and the related course materials
- Does not apply relevant course terms, theories and concepts from the course learning materials
- Does not share relevant experiences and does not reference current events that relate to the discussion topic
- Reply to classmate not completed
- Does not respond to questions posed by other students and/or the instructor
- Uses disrespectful and/or discriminatory language when interacting with others
