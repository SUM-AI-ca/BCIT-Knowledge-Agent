## Course Description
This course will introduce students to the process and requirements needed to create construction drawings for a residential project, including plans, elevations, legends, schedules and drawing references. Students will first design and develop freehand drawings to scale and will create a set of construction drawings using AutoCAD, based on their design solutions.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify various types of construction drawings for residential interior design.
- Produce construction plans with dimensions, specifications, legends and schedules.
- Design and dimension a reflected ceiling plan that includes variations in ceiling heights, materials and lighting.
- Produce a lighting schedule with generic specifications.
- Produce building sections showing stairs with appropriate annotation.
- Create attributed title blocks.
- Incorporate attributed blocks for sections, elevations, doors and finish symbols.
- Apply external referencing to create detailed areas in plans and elevations.
- Produce power and communication plan with a legend.
- Recognise appropriate power and communication requirements to comply with building codes.
- Produce finish plans and elevations with schedules, legends, and appropriate specifications.
- Apply appropriate line weights, scale, dimensions and text standards.
- Prepare a set of residential construction drawings with referencing.
