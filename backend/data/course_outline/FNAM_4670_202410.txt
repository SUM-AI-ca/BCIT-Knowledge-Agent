## Course Description
This course introduces the student to the issues related to fire management in the Wildland /Urban Interface. With the spread of home and other development into the forest interface, managers need to have a basic understanding of how to design using some FireSmart principles. There is also a requirement for managers to understand the interaction between local fire department operations and typical wildland fire-fighters. This course will also discuss at the initiative of Community Wildfire Protection Plans.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Describe Wildland/Urban Interface fire management planning.
- Apply FireSmart planning principles that can be used to reduce wildfire hazards to a specific interface area.
- Evaluate the potential interface wildland fire situation.
- Order and deploy the necessary resources in an interface wildland fire situation.
- Apply safe and effective strategy and tactics to minimize property damage in an interface wildland fire situation.
- Describe the Community Wildfire Protection Program (CWPP) as it relates to Wildland /Urban Interface Planning in B.C.
- Predict fire behavior using the Canadian Forest Fire Weather Index System and the Canadian Fire Behavior Prediction System.
- Explain basic Fire Management responsibility based on the Wildfire Act and Regulation for industry and government agencies.
- Discuss current fire management issues relevant to natural resource management.
- Create a CWPP for an area of land within the WUI
