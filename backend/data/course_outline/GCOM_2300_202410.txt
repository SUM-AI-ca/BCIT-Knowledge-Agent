## Course Description
Students will develop a media imaging promotional campaign for an external client. Students will work collaboratively to implement their design skills specific to the media they are producing to deliver a specific marketing message for their customers. The project will stress working effectively on a team to produce high quality media. The students will design and lay out the project using software skills previously taught. Substrate and manufacturing processes will be engineered, and weekly planning and management considerations must be accounted for. When the job is assembled and checked, students will create final media files for electronic distribution and archival. The student team will produce final colour composite proofs that are colour-managed to set proper client expectations. The final product will be graded in comparison to prototype proofs.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Conduct meetings with an external client and incorporate their feedback and ideas into project work.
- Conduct meetings with faculty and incorporate their feedback and ideas into project work.
- Generate production documentation to account for their time and material usage.
- Design and lay out a media campaign with an appropriate imaging media.
- Work as a team to manage the project and meet customer expectations.
- Pre-flight and check all electronic files for graphics, images, and fonts.
- Create colour-managed proofs with all printers’ marks for bleeds and trim.
- Manufacture the project within defined deadline and meet quality expectations.
- Final package their project and present it to the client.​

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
The Graphic Communications program utilizes a unique print lab facility, with equipment donated by the generous business owners in our community. Students must always respect the equipment in the lab, and comply with the following rules:

- Loose hair or long beards must be tied back or contained in a hat or hair net.
- Remove jewelry, scarves, coats, or loose clothing.
- Gaming, videos, or loud music that can cause distraction are not permitted.
- Mobile devices are not permitted when working around machinery.
- No food or drink in the lab (closed water bottles are allowed at desks only).
- PPE is required to be worn when working with chemicals.
- No video recording or photography is permitted in the lab.

These rules are for the safety of all students using the equipment in the lab and are overseen at the discretion of all faculty. Failure to comply with lab rules will not be tolerated and may result punitive action including dismissal from class, formal warning, course failure, or withdrawal from the program.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
