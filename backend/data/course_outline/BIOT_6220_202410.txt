## Course Description
​This course continues from Molecular Genetics 1 in its coverage of the principles of genetic analysis. Includes recombinant DNA technology, gene editing, regulation of gene expression, genetics of the cell cycle, genetic development and extranuclear DNA. Also covers advanced topics in Molecular Genetics, such as its application in cancer biology.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- apply molecular biology to the study of gene regulation.
- describe and discuss genetic engineering, gene editing, and genomics.
- describe the genetic control of development.
- explain the molecular genetics of cancer.
- describe extranuclear inheritance.
- sequence DNA.
- Isolate and reverse transcribe RNA.
- perform real-time PCR and gene editing.
- perform basic bioinformatics techniques.

## Attendance Requirements
Biotechnology Attendance Requirements

Students are expected to attend all lecture and lab sessions unless they have a serious illness or other medical condition. In the Biotechnology Program, laboratory attendance is of the highest priority since lab experience is what sets this program apart from other undergraduate programs and what prepares students for co-op work terms where it is expected that they will have acquired specific competencies. Furthermore, lab work is typically done in pairs or small teams and students are, therefore, expected to work collaboratively and support their lab partners.

Please notify your instructor and the Biotechnology program head of any absences. If exams, quizzes, or in-class assignments are missed for reasons of illness, medical notes will be required to reschedule.

Assignment Details
