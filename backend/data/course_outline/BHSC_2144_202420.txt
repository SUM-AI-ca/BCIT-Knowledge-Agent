## Course Description
Human Behaviour 2 (DSON) is a continuation of BHSC 1144. Student experiences in practicums are used to guide further development of skills necessary for interactions that are compassionate, safe and effective. Unique aspects of care of patients who are pregnant or fearful (i.e. cancer diagnosis, cardiac disease) is emphasised. Student awareness of bias and personal beliefs is explored within the context of patient care that is respectful in all regards.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Integrate effective interpersonal communication to build rapport and trust.
- Demonstrate respect for all patients regardless of their culture, race, gender or beliefs.
- Identify verbal and non verbal signs of stress in clients and respond appropriately.
- Respond to patient needs while working within accepted scope of practice.

WHAT ELSE SHOULD I KNOW TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS COURSE? PARTICIPATION IS KEY! (IT’S WORTH 5% OF YOUR GRADE)

This is an interpersonal communciation skills class. It is essential that you continue to develop your interpersonal communication skills by actively participating in class. Participation marks will be awarded at the end of the term at the discretion of the Instructor. Students will have the opportunity to reflect upon their own communication strengths/weaknesses, set personal classroom communication goals, and evaluate their own communication skill development throughout the term. This input will be considered, along with instructor participation considerations (below), when determining participation marks at the end of term.

Criteria considered by Instructor for when determining participation mark include:

Attendance: Please contact me if you are unable to attend class. Missed classes without prior discussion/approval will result in a deduction from your participation grade. Demonstrate repectful and professional conduct by arriving to class on time. Communication professionally with instructor if you are going to be late.
Role-Plays/Group Work: Full participation in role plays, class discussions, and group work is required for this class.
Questions: Demonstrate active listening by asking questions – others might be wondering the same thing! I invite you to speak up in our classes. Ask questions to demonstrate interest and engagement after class presentations. Keep the conversation going.
Contribution: Demonstrate your engagement by offering your perspective or answering questions about class materials. Engage with the presentations.
Respectful Conduct: Some topics may be sensitive and/or invite debate of different perspectives. Disrespectful language and/or behaviour will not be permitted. Use verbal and nonverbal communication to promote respect, inclusion, sensitivity, and curiousity.
Use of Skills: Demonstrate skills we have learned in class; show that you are able to apply your learning in a real-life interactive setting. Respond to classmates (and Instructor) using Active Listening Skills. Use the course skills when communicating via email. Incorporate skills into group work and all aspects of the course.
Preparation for Class: Prepare for class by doing your readings ahead of time; it will be easier to absorb and discuss materials discussed in class.
Use of Electronics: Please refrain from using your phone or computers for non-course related things during class time. Be mindful of the nonverbal messages being communicated through the presence/use of technology during classtime.
Support your Classmates: Be respectful, attentive, and supportive during class presentations. Give presenters your full attention. Limit your use of electronics during class presentations. Pay attention to your nonverbal communciation. Participate meaningfully in discussions and activities after presentations.
