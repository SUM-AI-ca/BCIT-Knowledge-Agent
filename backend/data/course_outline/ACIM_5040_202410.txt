## Course Description
This course will familiarize students with installation and operational requirements for electrical machines with variable-frequency drives. Students will be introduced to applications of variable-frequency drives (VFDs), including their installation needs, classifications and harmonic considerations. The drives will be utilized on standalone and micro-processor-based systems programmable logic controllers (PLCs)/programmable automation controllers (PACs). A significant part of the course is dedicated to application activities that reinforce the theory.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Connect a variable speed drives in accordance with the Canadian Electrical Code.
- Choose, connect, and program a VFD for a variety of load applications.
- Connect and program microprocessor-based controllers (PLC/PAC) with a VFD.
- Apply a troubleshooting tree to diagnose problems with variable frequency drives that are utilized in PLC systems.
- Describe dynamic and static characteristics and analysis of AC VFDs.
- Troubleshoot with an oscilloscope, using various test points on a VFD.
- Choose and connect shaft encoder types based on application.
- Tune a proportional-integral-derivative (PID) control system that uses a variable frequency drive.
