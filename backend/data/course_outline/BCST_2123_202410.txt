## Course Description
Building on BCST 1223 Production Planning, this course will cover in more detail, and with less direct instructor guidance, the logistical and organizational aspects necessary for any production. Students will prepare a script breakdown identifying the requirements needed to complete the production. They will learn how to prepare and read a production budget, and how to work with contingencies. Students will learn how to create and manage a production schedule. Production contracts, licenses, clearances and releases will be revisited and students will gain a better understanding of which ones are appropriate for specific situations on specific productions.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Create production budgets and prepare invoices
- Create and adjust a workback schedule, a critical path, and a daily shooting schedule
- Prepare script breakdowns and identify production requirements for completion
- Identify the various agreements that govern the relationships involved in a production
- Restate clauses within contracts to demonstrate an understanding of their effect
- Given a set of pre-made production agreements, select and complete contracts appropriate for the various aspects of a specific production
- Determine when licenses, clearances, and releases are necessary and complete the respective applications
