### Course Description
​The purpose of this course is to introduce the working processes and considerations of architectural practice. Students will gain familiarity with the different types of architectural practice organization and basic principles of project management, covering various roles and responsibilities. Case studies are used to illustrate stakeholder dynamics, project sociologistic, political and economics through real-world examples. Students will engage with building industry professionals and professional resources throughout the course.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Discuss architectural practice organizational processes and associated implications for business planning and financial management.
- Explain the key roles and responsibilities of the major players in each phase of an architectural project using various project delivery frameworks, including methods for selecting consultants and project teams.
- Discuss the types and scales of architectural firms.
- Identify key factors in successful project delivery (including risk and professional liability, fees and schedules, communication and technology management, quality assurance, and consultant coordination).
- Discuss emerging trends and implications for the practice of architecture. ​

### Course Goals
- Obtain a realistic vision of the architecture field
- Identify a diversity of paths within the architecture and construction industry
- Analyze and learn from the construction and management of existing projects
- Understand the diversity of routes for future professional development via an AIBC orientation
- Prepare students for workplace culture, teamwork, and the client / Architect relationship
