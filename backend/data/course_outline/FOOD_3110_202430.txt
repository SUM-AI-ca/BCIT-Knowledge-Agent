## Course Description
This course examines food manufacturing processes in the areas of raw food of animal origin (RFAO), fruits and vegetables, cereals, grains, nuts and pulses, and fats and oils with an emphasis on their food safety and quality aspects. Students will also compare the food safety risks associated with these food manufacturing processes. Ingredients and formulations specific to food processing will be introduced and examined. Mass balance calculations will be performed on a given set of formulations as part of learning about the traceability and recall procedures outlined in the Safe Food for Canadians Regulations (SFCR).

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Compare and contrast RFAO food manufacturing processes with respect to their production and processing methods and important food safety and quality points.
- Describe the processing methods and food safety and quality points for a set of processes associated with fruits and vegetables (produce), cereals and grains, nuts and pulses, and fats and oils.
- Explain the need for ingredients that are specifically used by food manufacturers including hydrocolloids, acidulants, colours, and preservatives.
- Examine and perform food formulation calculations for a given set of processes.
- Perform a mass balance for a given formulation and explain why a mass balance approach is needed to demonstrate food product traceability and to perform a recall.
- Write a traceability and recall SOP with the associated Work Instructions.
- Demonstrate the food processing practices associated with a given food process.
- Perform a food safety risk assessment.
- Compare the risks associated with the various food processes introduced in this course.

## Course Specific Requirements
Students will need to purchase a lab manual for the course and will need to have a food grade labcoat and safety shoes in the production labs. Students will need to follow appropriate Good Manufacturing Procedures such as hair/beard nets, absense of jewlery, minimal make-up, no nail-polish etc.
