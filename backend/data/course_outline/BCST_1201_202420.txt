## Course Description
​Have a passion to develop your original story idea into a short film or web series? Adapt an article, a life event or literary material for the screen? Not sure where to begin your journey as a media content creator? The hands-on workshops that make up this course will acquaint you with successful projects and first steps in creating your own screen based content.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Research and write a compelling visual treatment and/or proposal.
- Understand how to access essential resources supporting the development of original screen content.

## Learning Resources
Materials will be provided in class (online)

## Course Goals
The course provides students with an introduction to visual storytelling, research and writing skills for various genres of production including documentary, branded entertainment and narrative scripts.

Upon completion students will be able to,

- Incorporate elements of visual storytelling into their research and writing
- be familiar with story rights, clearances, releases, permits and other production planning considerations
- Identify local, regional, national and international sources of support and financing for content creation
- Access traditional and non traditional marketing, audience engagement and distribution resources

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Week 1/2 Research and writing for various formats and screen genres. Story rights, copyright and chain of title

Week 3/4 Story structure; character development; dialogue;visual treatments, storyboards, pitching.

Week 5/6 Local, regional, national and international sources of support and financing

Week 7 Marketing screen content and moving beyond press kits and press releases. Impact producing and social media resources.Creating traditional and non traditional marketing materials and audience engagement tools

Week 8 Traditional and non-traditional distribution resources
