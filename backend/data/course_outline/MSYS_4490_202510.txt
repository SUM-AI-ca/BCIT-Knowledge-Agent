## Course Description
Overall competency in engineering design, project management, technical communication and teamwork skills is demonstrated by successfully completing a relevant industry project.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Apply the principles of mechanical systems to a practical industrial problem.
- Work as member of a team on the industrial problem.
- Prepare project proposal for approval.
- Produce, analyze and select alternatives solutions most appropriate to the specific problem.
- Communicate progress with the project supervisor.
- Apply the principles of project planning and scheduling to the solution of the practical problem.
- Prepare a technical report and document all technical details on the project.
- Present the result of the project in oral presentation.

## Course Goals
Students will complete a project sponsored by a company in the industry, applying and practicing what they learn in various Systems courses under the guidance provided by the sponsor and the course instructors.
