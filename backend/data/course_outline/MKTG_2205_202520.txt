## Course Description
Marketing Services introduces a Gaps Model of Service Quality that service industries can use to evaluate and drive their ability to listen to the marketplace, consider new innovative ideas, plan service improvement or growth, compete for the best people, and build effective internal and external communication strategies for a service firm. This course also explores the increasing demand for self-service-technology and an artificial intelligence framework that can improve a service firm's scalability, profitability and competitiveness. Students will apply the model and framework to make recommendations to a service firm.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Make primary distinctions between product-based and service-based businesses.
- Create marketing objectives that generate predetermined shifts in customer behaviour.
- Develop a synergistic marketing strategy and put it into action.
- Choose appropriate research methods for services.
- Select appropriate database management and segmentation methods for services.
- Formulate the seven 'P's' of the Extended marketing Mix for services.
- Demonstrate the process of how to communicate and promote services B-to-C and B-to-B.
- Describe the four Customer Value Segments and select the best pricing techniques for each.
- Select the best way to reach customers through person selling, advertising, direct mail, internet etc.

## Course Goals
The service sector can be very profitable and is quickly becoming the largest segment of the world's economy. Yet, the intangible delivery of services can be difficult to manage and market. Even when service organizations get it right, satisfaction levels are quick to slip or be copied by other service providers. Meanwhile, effective, and efficient service fails can make or break a service firm. Technology is having a huge impact of service success!

This course will provide students with the tools, strategies, and approaches for designing and sustaining a successful service centered organization. There is a strong consumer focus in this course. Students will development an awareness of a gaps model that differentiates between what organizations think consumers want - and what consumers really want, while maintaining an eye to a service firms’ financial viability and profitability.

Assignment and Quiz Summary

Late assignments will not be graded and will result in a grade of zero.

Contact Assignment – Service Research Project This is a contact assignment that requires students to visit a service where they will conduct an assessment on how the firm applies the concepts of Gap 1, the Listening Gap; including how they conduct research, identifying the strengths and weakness of their approaches; and make recommendations on how they could further improve their Gap 1 results.

Assignment 2 – Term Project/Consultancy Project Students will apply their learning to a term-based consultancy project. Students are encouraged to apply the concepts of each chapter to their Assignment 2 Consultancy firm project every week. Maintaining this schedule will help students to maintain regular and ongoing attention to the course, and their applied project, reducing stress at the very end when there are multiple deliverables.

Mini Quiz Tips There will be two mini quizzes. One will focus on Gap 2 and the other Gap 3. The mini quizzes will include 2 – 5 questions that specifically reference the student’s consultancy project. For this reason, students are encouraged to: 1. Pre-read Chapters and complete McGraw Hill Connect Assignments for the coming week. 2. Attend classes, review lessons and engage in class discussion. 3. Apply the learning to their consultancy project. 4. Take the mini quiz – take special note of the mini quiz deadlines which have been established to support this process. Mini quizzes have an official end date. Extensions to these dates will not be permitted/No exceptions.

Final Exam Will include gap 4 mini quiz questions and a series of true/false; multiple choice; open ended questions as well as case review/responses.
