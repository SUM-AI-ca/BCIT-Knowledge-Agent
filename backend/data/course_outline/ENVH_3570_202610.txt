### Course Description
The Public Health Inspector/Environmental Health Officer (PHI/EHO) must use strong Human Relations skills to establish rapport, project professionalism and communicate effectively to avoid conflict and achieve compliance by operators/clients. Since human relation skills are critical to the inspection process and to the understanding of complex human behaviors, this course will focus on the interactions between people and methods to avoid conflict, build trust and achieve compliance with a strong focus on inspection, cultural awareness, conflict resolution, negotiation and mediation specific to the role of the PHI/EHO. Role playing exercises and case studies are used to examine effective behaviors and interactive techniques.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Analyze the characteristics of basic communication as applied to the process of communication.
- Identify their communication style as well as the communication styles of others.
- Evaluate verbal and non-verbal communication interactions necessary for PHIs/EHOs to be effective in their practice.
- Assess methods to resolve conflict and their application to the field of Environmental Public Health.
- Outline effective interpersonal skills in dealing with a variety of difficult people.
- Evaluate cross-cultural communication to eliminate misperception and miscommunication.
- Apply conflict resolution, negotiation and mediation strategies specific to the role of the PHI/EHO.
- Employ good human relation skills (such as diplomacy and active listening) in establishing rapport, educating, projecting professionalism and communicating effectively.
- Analyze case studies and commonly encountered professional experiences with respect to effective communication styles and knowledge application.

### Course Goals
Integrate the following CIPHI BOC Instructional Objectives with the Course Outcomes:
Outline the primary objectives of risk communication.
Describe factors affecting risk perception and management, including how social or other barriers can impact ability to manage risks.
Explain the principles of trust and credibility in risk communication.
Explore strategies to build trust, relationships, and effective communication with communities and individuals.
Communicate effectively using risk communication principles: gain feedback, address concerns, establish trust, alleviate fear or anger, address changing or crisis situations, explain information in an effective yet sympathetic manner.
Recognize barriers experienced by the operator/client related to the social determinants of health that may be affecting their ability to comply with regulations.
Describe methods and tools used in enforcement to achieve compliance.
Describe selected policy and program options to address a specific public health issue.
Describe the implications of each option, especially as they apply to the determinants of health and recommend or decide on a course of action.
Use skills such as team building, negotiation, conflict management and group facilitation to build partnerships.
Mediate between differing interests in the pursuit of health and well-being, and facilitate the allocation of resources.
Address population diversity when planning, implementing, adapting and evaluating public health programs and policies.
Apply culturally-relevant and appropriate approaches with people from diverse cultural, socioeconomic and educational backgrounds, and persons of all ages, genders, health status, sexual orientations and abilities.
Communicate effectively with individuals, families, groups, communities and colleagues.
Interpret information for professional, nonprofessional and community audiences.
