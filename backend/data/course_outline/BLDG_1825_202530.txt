## Course Description
​​​Examines Part 9 of the 2024 B.C. Building Code as it applies to multi-unit residential and small commercial and industrial buildings. Covers plan checking and inspection techniques. Prepares students to deal with issues relating to Part 9 of the B.C. Building Code. Some duplication of material covered in BLDC 1500. Students must bring the B.C. Building Code (hardcopy) to the first class.​​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Check plans of buildings for compliance with the regulations in the BC Building Code, at an elementary level.
- Inspect small, single or multi unit buildings relative to compliance with thr regulations in the BC Building Code.
- Deal with question relating to Building Code requirements small, single or multi unit buildings.
- Have a basic understanding of acceptable materials, systems, and methods used in construction.

## Course Goals
To provide the student with an overview of Divisions A, B and C of the 2024 British Columbia Building Code as it relates to small buildings within the scope of Part 9, other than single family dwellings with or without secondary suites.

To provide the student with hands-on experience in the application of Division B, Part 9 of the British Columbia Building Code as it relates to small buildings (other than Single Family Dwellings or Duplexes) through:

- On-line lessons and current, real-life examples;
- Weekly self-quizzes after each module;
- Term plan review project that resenbles a plan review typically conducted by Building Officials working at a local government office.

To facilitate the student’s understanding of currently accepted, industry interpretations of the British Columbia Building Code.

To familiarize the student with standards and documents referenced by the British Columbia Building Code, such as NFPA 13, NFPA 96 and NFPA 101.

## Other information
This course is developed for the student to attend each online class session in person. The expectation is for the student to pre-read weekly course material. Utilizing PowerPoint (PPTX), the instructor will conduct the class lesson which compliments the material the student has pre-read. Where possible, real life examples through construction site photos will be provided. Course lessons are recorded and therefore available for review at any time. The PPTX presentation will be posted prior to commencment of the lesson to allow the student to follow along.

Due to the large amount of new material, the weekly PPTX presentation highlights only the important aspects of the weekly course material. There is opportunity for student interaction duriong the 3 hour weekly lesson for the student ask questions on the course mateirial that they pre-read.

The majority of the material presented in the first 3 weeks reviews course material presentend in BLDC 1500, BC Building Code Part 9, Single Family Dwellings.

Self-quizzes are provided and avaialbe for each course module. The student is execpected to use the quizes as a tool to gauge their understanding of the material. The self-quiz may be taken as often as the student desires. However, the self quizzes are not considered in the studetn's course evaluation. The self-quizzes are only intended as a study aid.

For course delivery, the student should have a computer equipped with speakers.

For course delivery, Google Chrome is the preferred browser although Firefox works. Using other browsers such as Safari will cause glitches.
