## Course Description
Introduces you to the writing, reading, listening, and speaking skills you will need to successfully carry out your professional communication tasks. You will learn how to organize and present technical information, write memos/emails and letters, and prepare and deliver an oral presentation. The first term establishes the principles and basic patterns on which you will build more advanced applications in the second term.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Follow a systematic process in planning and writing technical documents.
- Select and organize information to meet the needs of your readers.
- Use a style and tone appropriate for your purpose and audience.
- Write clear, concise, and correct sentences.
- Make your writing more accessible by using headings, stacked lists, white space, and layout.
- Use effective organizational patterns and conventional formats in writing memos, letters, and reports.
- Prepare and use effective visuals in documents and oral presentations.
- Prepare and deliver an effective oral presentation.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.
