### Course Description
​This studio course permits students to experience the challenges and rewards of integrated design. Projects involve the synthesis of various applied architectural and engineering concepts dealing with architecture, structure and building systems. Students will apply sustainable design principles to schematic design and pursue unprecedented building performance levels, working independently, in teams and with consultants to achieve environmentally responsible and regenerative designs. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to
- Adopt an integrated design process throughout the project.
- Integrate the requirements of architectural, structural, mechanical and electrical systems in the design process.
- Outline individual building systems/components according to their purpose, basic function, nature and characteristics.
- Justify which systems are required in a building project, in relation to local environmental requirements.
- Evaluate design options according to sustainable design principles.
- Interpret performance indicators in critiquing design solutions.
- Design and document an introductory project at various scales.
- Develop an architectural language through the use of space, structure, materials, technology and construction.
- Engage in critique and incorporate feedback.
- Conclude the project through independent and team work.
- Make a professional and informative project proposal through graphic and oral presentation(s).

### Course Goals
This studio course introduces integrated design to the architectural process, applied to a large architectural project with complex systems requirements. A series of exercises will approach the synthesis of architectural principles with engineering concepts to propose architecturally integrated structures and environmental systems with advanced performance benchmarks. Students will work independently, in teams, and with industry consultants to achieve climate-resilient design. Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Apply design theories, methods, and precedents to an integrated design process, including architectural, structural, mechanical and electrical considerations.
- Integrate architectural, structural, mechanical and electrical considerations in the design process.
- Manage the full range of architectural design tools and techniques.
- Analyze program requirements and priorities.
- Evaluate a complex site through documentation and analysis (including current and historical context, ecologies, systems, patterns, urban design, Indigenous territories, and authorities having jurisdiction) to inform project response.
- Design a large building response based on a concept, client, program and complex site systems.
- Justify structural, mechanical, material and environmental systems selected in a project, in response to local requirements.
- Iterate design, material and assembly options according to building performance and sustainable design assessment.
- Illustrate research skills into complex and nested systems using a variety of resources and media.
- Use architectural conventions to communicate through graphics, models and presentations.
- Apply all relevant building codes, regulations and standards including universal design.
- Engage in collaborative critique and iterative design processes.
- Simulate professional best practice individually and collaboratively.
