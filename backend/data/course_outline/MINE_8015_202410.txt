## Course Description
This course is a continuation of MINE 7015 in level 7. Students will continue to engage with industry personnel in a series of presentations and research topics.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Discuss advanced engineering topics with industry representatives [1].
- Evaluate new technology and current issues in the mining industry [1].
- Evaluate and discuss the requirements for effective, professional technical presentations. [7]
- Effectively present on a technical subject. [7]
