## Course Description
Topics included in the lecture portion of GEOM 2010 are an introduction to astronomy, Global Positioning Systems (GPS) for survey applications, the importance of a code of ethics to the profession, industry specific health and safety guidelines and the necessary specific information for the corresponding weekly field lab session. The lab portion of the course will place an emphasis on keeping field notes both for recording data and supplementing electronic data. On-board and external device software routines will be used for survey data collection, resection, traversing and setting out. Practical field labs will also introduce students to both solar and star observations for azimuth determination. Several labs will involve a ‘field to finish’ format taking data from the field followed by manipulating, evaluating and analyzing the results to produce a final plan or summary.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful compleltion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate the ability to use onboard and external device software routines including 'Free Station' and 'Set Out'.
- Assess and calibrate a total station for horizontal collimation / vertical index errors.
- Explain and choose basic total station / computer parameters for electronic data transfer.
- Demonstrate observational, note keeping and reduction methods for traversing.
- Demonstrate two procedures for typical building layout.
- Discuss workplace hazards and safe work procedures.
- Collect, download and reduce electronic data in conjunction with industry software.
- Define and determine magnetic declination.
- Determine directions using a compass and explain magnetic declination.
- Describe the basics of the GPS for low accuracy surveys.
- Collect and manipulate data to produce a topographic plan from raw field measurements.
- Determine azimuth by time and altitude methods from solar and star observations.
- Describe the equation of time, apparent time, sidereal time and Universal Time.
- Use the Star Almanac for Land Surveyors to determine right ascension, declination, R and E.
- Explain and assess relevant Codes of Ethics for the Geomatics profession.
- Identify and explain the purpose of codes and standards applicable to the discipline.
- Develop strategies to work effectively as a member of a work / project team.
