### Course Description
Continues the study begun in COMM 1178 in the writing and speaking skills you will need to successfully carry out your professional communication tasks. You will design and write an effective resume and cover letter, and participate in practice job interviews, as both an interviewer and interviewee. You will also write short reports and participate in productive project meetings.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Design and write an effective resume and job application letter.
- Practice answering BMET job interview questions in role-plays and give and receive feedback from peers.
- Research and write technical reports that have appropriate structure, accurate content, and clear, correct, grammatical expression.
- Prepare and deliver an effective oral presentation on the report’s findings using engaging delivery and appropriate visuals.
- Demonstrate effective teamwork and meeting skills to run productive and positive team meetings to achieve the goals of the report and presentation.
- Describe the roles of health care professionals and the contributions they make to the interprofessional health care team.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and justify the findings.
