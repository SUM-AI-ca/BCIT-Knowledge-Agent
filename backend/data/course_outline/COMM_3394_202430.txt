## Course Description
COMM 3394 is designed to help TTED students to learn, develop, and apply the communication skills and knowledge that will help them succeed at UBC in the Faculty of Education and in the technology classrooms of the BC school system. Technology teachers must know how to communicate effectively with colleagues, parents, students, administrators, outside partners, and stakeholders. Effective safety communication and practice form the foundation of all good technology classroom practice and will be emphasized in this course by learning the techniques of clear, correct, and concise expression. COMM 3394 focuses mainly on technical formats and conventions, with some attention on pedagogical and academic applications. Basic procedures/techniques for organizing and writing business and technical documents are taught; particular emphasis is placed on the various audiences in the school environment. Throughout the course, students practice providing thoughtful and meaningful feedback to peers. Oral and visual communication is emphasized throughout the course.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Adjust oral and written communication to audience and purpose
- Apply page design techniques including lists, white space, graphic highlighting, and headings to technical documents
- Write a set of technical instructions for a shop or technology procedure
- Write units of measurement and numbers correctly
- Write clear, concise, and grammatically correct sentences
- Integrate graphics correctly and effectively in technical documents and presentations
- Write professional e-mails using the pyramid model
- Deliver short individual presentations which are suitable for a technology education classroom
- Reflect on pedagogical work and activities both orally and in writing.
