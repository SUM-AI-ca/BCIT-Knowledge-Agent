## Course Description
Introduction to Cytogenetics Laboratory Technology and Methodology is an entry-level course that utilizes hands-on training through demonstrations, lectures, and laboratories. The principles and methodologies involved in clinical cytogenetics testing in somatic genetics diseases are stressed. The students' background in the sciences is utilized to aid in the explanation of the relationship of results, application, and interpretation. The emphasis is also on lab organization, time management, individual and group activities, and the attainment of specified competencies related to the CSMLS document.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

- Understands and demonstrates the concepts of safe work practices.
- Perform cell culture for chromosome analysis.
- Set up cultures from various tissues, and process for chromosome analysis and be rigorous in the use of tissue culture flow sheets.
- Identify amnion/chorion tissue from placental tissue sample.
- Use the Zeiss Axioscope microscope for routine analysis and karyotyping.
- Troubleshoot tissue culture and banding problems.
- Pretreat dishes/slides for optimal chromosome banding by drying in 90º oven for 1hr.
- Optimize cytogenetic quality and quantity of metaphases by appropriate use of basic cytogenetic elements such as hypotonic, colcemid, media additives, and methanol: acetic acid fix.
- Organize personnel and lab resources for efficient completion of procedures.
- Use effective organizational and time management skills to complete assignments, prepare for quizzes, and prepare for scheduled examinations.
- Care for, troubleshoot, and use microscopes correctly.
- Prepare tissue culture media.
- Understand and use media additives in the recommended concentrations.
- Organize media and additive needs in a timely manner before setup of tissue(s).
- Understand the need for and apply acute documentation techniques for all tissues examined.
- Understand the need for and use good communication, cooperation skills to develop the professionalism needed to be successful at the clinical sites.

The goal of this course is to develop the knowledge and skills (practical and theoretical) involved in cell culturing, to produce metaphase chromosomes for banding,chromosome analysis and karyotyping.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
- Laboratory

Three written technical reports must be submitted in partial fulfillment of the course requirements. These reports — Lab Exercises 1 through 3 (see accompanying schedule) — are based on assays performed and data and results collected during the laboratory sessions. A full description of how these technical reports should be formatted and content they should include will be provided upon commencement of the course.

- Quizzes

The 1 hour quizzes will evaluate student progress to date and consists of multiple choice, short answer questions and data interpretation.

- Final Exam

The final exam will be 3 hours long and take place during Exam Week. This will be similar in format to the quiz consisting of multiple choice, problem solving and data interpretation.

- Lab checklist

The labs in this course will strive to mimic the clinical lab environment. There will be a lab checklist for students to complete as a review of their lab skills. Students will be given a marking rubric in the first week of class to explain instructor expectations.
