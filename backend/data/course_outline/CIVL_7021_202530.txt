### Course Description
This second mechanics of materials course covers advanced topics including composite beams, unsymmetrical bending, plastic bending, beams on elastic foundations, curved beams, shear flow and torsion in closed and open sections, and elastic stability.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Establish equilibrium, kinematic and constitutive relationships and their linkages for bars and beams. [1, 2]
- Determine the stresses that develop in composite beams. [1, 2]
- Determine the stress distribution in beams under elastic-plastic bending. [1, 2]
- Analyze beams subjected to unsymmetrical bending. [1, 2]
- Solve for the internal forces and displacements in Beam on an Elastic Foundation. [2, 3, 5]
- Use principle of Beam on Elastic Foundation in civil engineering applications. [2,3,5]
- Determine the shear flow and locate the shear centre in beams under transverse loading. [1, 2]
- Determine the shear stresses in thin walled members subjected to torsion and bending. [1, 2]
- Solve elastic stability problems involving columns, beam-columns and frames. [2, 3, 5]

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Assignment Details
- Assignments will be primarily assessed on completeness and content. Neatness and clarity of presentation will also be weighted. Incomplete solution of a problem in the assignment will get zero marks.
- Each exercise must outline a clear problem statement and solution methodology.
- Engineering paper is strongly recommended.
- Each problem should begin at the top of a new page.
- List all important assumptions, define all variables, indicate all units, use an appropriate number of significant figures and clearly identify answers by boxing or underscoring.
- Incorporate sketches and graphs that enhance your solutions.
- You may work on assignments as a group. However, any submitted assignment must represent work done by the person who will receive credit. It is not acceptable for students to submit identical copies of an assignment. Also, it is not acceptable for one student to copy work previously done by another.
- Late homework assignments normally receive a zero grade. Exceptions may be granted in two cases: prior consent of the instructor or a medical emergency.
Quiz Details

### Course topics
week | Topics | Note
1 | Review and Elements of Mechanics | Introduction, course outline. Schedule, assessments etc, Review of Mechanics of Material I | Knowledge Retention Test
2 | Elements of mechanics, Bars and Beams | Relationship between equilibrium, kinematics and constitutive properties, Elements of mechanics, axially loaded members, governing differential equations, distributed loads
3 | Non-prismatic members., Temperature effects, misfits, Introduction of nonlinearity | Assignment #1
4 | General equation for beam Beam bending, composite beams, unsymmetrical bending

8 | Applications of BOEF in structural/ geotechnical engineering problems | Assignment # 3
