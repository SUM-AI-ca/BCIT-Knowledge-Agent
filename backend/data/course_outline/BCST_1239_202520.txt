## Course Description
​This course focuses on the fundamentals of Ableton Live for composing, recording and editing music. Ableton Live is recognized as one of the top competitors in digital music creation and is also recognized for its non-linear composition and audio recording environment while also enabling a more traditional linear arrangement. The course examines automation essentials and tools for creating, editing and producing music compositions. Course studies also focus on MIDI programming, composition strategies, use of device racks, live audio recording and editing, sampling, MIDI instrument design, and mixing for production, collaboration and remixing finished works within Ableton Live. Essentials for setting up the digital audio workstation are covered. Use of Ableton Live for live performance is also explored. Prior knowledge of music theory and prior music experience is helpful but is not essential for this course.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Use Session and Arrangement views for non-linear and linear composition and recording with proficiency.
- Apply basic music theory and techniques using tools within Ableton to create songs and audio clips.
- Record and edit MIDI-based music.
- Edit and mix professional sounding audio.
- Record professional sounding audio (low cost recording equipment options will be discussed but are not required for the course).
- Audition and select instruments and voices for MIDI.
- Design sounds with audio samples and instrument racks.
- Use tools within Ableton Live which use music theory to create pleasing harmonies.
- Develop musical dynamic song structures and themes.
- Mix and edit recordings.
- Remix works from other artists or collaborate with other artists using Ableton Live.
- Implement generative strategies to overcome writer's block and to enhance compositions.
- Manage files and optimize configuration for computers.
- Use features to assist with live music performance.​

## Course Specific Requirements
- Prior knowledge of music theory is helpful but is not essential for this course.
- A low-cost MIDI piano keyboard controller with a USB connection is required. (A 25-key minimum is recommended).
- Ableton Live Intro (or higher) is required for this course.
- Ableton Live Push hardware is not required for the course. Push hardware will not be discussed in the course.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Week 1: MIDI Recording and playback introduction. Working in Session View. MIDI programming. Techniques for creating expressiveness. Automation envelopes.: Introduction discussion due by next week. Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week.
Week 2: Introductory music theory and tools for composition. Notes and basic scales. Chords and melody. Creating multi-track harmony with MIDI instruments. Exporting audio.: Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week.
Week 3: Composition structure, motif and sectional contrast. Working in the Arrangement View. Song arrangement. Motif identification and creation. Reference tracks. Enabling dynamic contrast with arrangement and instrument selection.: Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week. Song Creation Assignment 1 due week 7.
Week 4: Audio recording and editing. Audio recording equipment and configuration. Recording audio with Ableton. Warping and editing audio clips and tracks.: Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week.
Week 5: Working with samples. MIDI instrument creation with Simpler. Drum racks.: Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week.
Week 6: Building a sound palette. Instrument racks. Auditioning instruments and audio. Plug-Ins.: Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week.
Week 7: Effects. MIDI and audio effects. Arpeggiators. Effect racks. Filtering. Modulation envelopes.: Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week. Song Creation Assignment 2 due week 12.
Week 8: Mixing. Volume and panning. Reverb. Send and return tracks. Equalization. Compression.: Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week.
Week 9: Generative creation strategies. Follow actions. Random, Pitch, Chord and MIDI effects. Side-chaining. Ableton Grooves.: Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week.
Week 10: Collaboration and file management. Collaborating with others. File management. Remixing.: Skill practice due by next week. Composition activity due by week.
Week 11: Telling a story with music. Stability / instability. Positioning and mixing options. Harmony choices.
Week 12: Performance techniques. Automating live playback. Recording MIDI tracks on the fly. Editing audio from video.
