## Course Description
​In this capstone course students will integrate knowledge, skills, and abilities gained throughout the program towards the inclusion of a minimum of two major design projects in an online graphic design print and web portfolio. Students will self-direct their workflow, employing research, concept ideation, and production skills to refine previously completed program projects. Depending on recently honed abilities and project requirements, students may create new projects. Students will be guided in professional methods and techniques for achieving effective content including the design of a self-brand identity (with stationery), review resumes, and prepare for the interview process and presentations. For ease of access and deployment, students will be instructed and guided to build their portfolio website on an online content management platform. This course should be one of the last taken in the Certificate. ​​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Create a portfolio plan based on research, career path preferences and self-assessment.
- Rationalize your portfolio design strategy based on research data and personal strengths analysis.
- Develop discipline tracks, visual layouts, narratives, and nomenclatures for graphic design portfolios.
- Design a personal overarching theme (visual language) for all portfolio items.
- Apply industry-standard software to demonstrate design skills in developing and assembling a design portfolio.
- Assess graphic design industry employment requirements.
- Prepare print and web portfolios that demonstrate professional skills required for employment.
- Evaluate your own and others' designs for strengths and weaknesses.
- Analyze feedback from stakeholders, customers, or clients for consideration in project refinement.
- Examine and evaluate sample resumes and other employment search documents.
- Demonstrate proficiency in presenting skills.​
