### Learning Resources
Students are required to organize their own 4-week clinical placement in an affiliated OPC Canadian P&O faciltiy and under the supervision of an OPC Certifee in good standing. The students will communicate confirmed facility sites to the clinical coordinator and provide BCIT with a contact person for the student's evaluation. All sites should have current BCIT affiliation agreements in place. Students must complete one prosthetic and one orthotic clinical placement during the program. Students are to select work placements in facilities that they have not had paid work or volunteered in the past, or have future plans for a residency. This is to optimize the student's learning opportunity in new P&O environments.
Specific Guidelines can be found in the Prosthetic and Orthotic Student Practicum Term 4 Document for January 5– January 30, 2026
### Course Goals
Orthotics Placements
- Students should be provided with the opportunity to assess and cast 6 patients
- Under supervision, students should assume total responsibility for the treatment of at least two patients who requires a foot orthosis and one patient who requires an ankle foot orthosis (Three complete projects). For each of these patients the student should;
- Perform an appropriate patient assessment and casting
- Independently fabricate the device
- Demonstrate to the supervisor that the device fits the patient appropriately
Prosthetics Placements
- Students should be provided with the opportunity to assess and cast 4 patients
- Under supervision, students should assume total responsibility for the treatment of at least one patient who requires a transtibial prosthesis and, if available, one patient who requires a symes or partial-foot prosthesis (Two complete projects). For each of these patients the student should;
- Perform an appropriate patient assessment and casting
- Independently fabricate the device
- Fit and align the device to a satisfactory standard
### Evaluation Criteria
Criteria | % | Comments
Clinical Placement report (graded as Satisfactory or Unsastisfactory) | 48% | Clinical supervisors are expected to complete a clinical performance appraisal form in the final week of the student’s practicum with the student. The student is expected to sign that this report was reviewed with them. In addition, the primary clinical supervisor is required to assign the student a SATISFACTORY or UNSATISFACTORY grade and this is to be sent to Yvonne Jeffreys ( yvonne_jeffreys@bcit.ca ) along with the signed practicum appraisal form. All students must attain a satisfactory grade in their clinical placement report to pass this course.
Clinical Reflection Journal (graded as Satisfactory or Unsastisfactory) | 48% | The Journal should provide a daily reflection of specific, unique experiences and observations. When describing client management, the description may include an indication of pathology, aims of prosthetic/orthotic treatment and a reflection on how this case fit into the student’s clinical education the steps taken to complete the procedure. Journal entries should focus on ‘ahha’ moments during the placement or highlights that are ‘notes to self for future consideration. You may also consider specific technical process or lay-ups for future reference. You may also discuss impact of interprofessional interactions (meetings, clinic rounds etc.) and how this contributed (or not) to the P or O treatment and patient outcomes. Reflections may also include your reactions to your experiences throughout the placement (not limited to: patient safety, new viewpoints, surprises or disappointments).. The Journal is to be electronically documented, and submitted in pdf format to Yvonne Jeffreys, Clinical Coordinator by 8:00 am Feb 9 th 2026.The journal will be marked as Satisfactory or Unsatisfactory. based on reflective content. Late submissions for reflective journals are not eligible for grading if received after the deadline unless a request has been discussed and approved by Clinical Placement Coordinator (Yvonne Jeffreys) at minimum 24 hours prior to the due date. All students must attain a satisfactory grade for this Journal to pass this course.
Completed Placement Evaluation and Reflective Journal criteria | 4% | Both clinical placement evaluation and reflective journal must be submitted and graded as satisfactory to pass this course.

### Course Schedule and Assignments
1. Students are required to complete a four-week of clinical placement. The fourth term placement will be completed between January 5 – January 30, 2026 (120 hours minimum). Student must report to clinical site supervisors every day and advise of any missed hours (due to sickness) prior to the start of each work day.
2. Clinical supervisors are expected to complete a clinical performance appraisal form in the final week of the student’s practicum with the student. The student is expected to sign that this report was reviewed with them. In addition, the primary clinical supervisor is required to assign the student a SATISFACTORY or UNSATISFACTORY grade and this is to be sent to Yvonne Jeffreys ( yvonne_jeffreys@bcit.ca ) along with the signed practicum appraisal form (signed by both student and supervisor). Due date: Feb 9, 2026
3. The Refletive Journal must be emailed in a pdf or word format to the Clinical Coordinator by 8:00am Feb 9 th 2026.
4. Virtual check-in via MS TEams: Placement #1 check-in: Thurs Jan 15 th 3:30-5:00 pm PST or Placement #2 check in: Fri Jan 16th 9:00-10:30 am PST. Student are expected to participate in one web-based clinical discussions.
