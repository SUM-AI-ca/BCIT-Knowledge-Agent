## Course Description
This course provides a foundation of study in the theory and practice of quality management in health care through an examination of the literature and case study reviews. Relevant and current TQM theory will be applied to the cancer health care environment with a student assignment to design a survey tool used to measure outcomes of care. Improvement strategies and implementation for the obtainment of quality processes are examined in the context of health care.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify the main principles of Total Quality Management (TQM).
- Justify the need for total quality management in health care.
- Apply the principles of Total Quality Management to the cancer treatment health care environment.
- Examine the relationship between quality improvement implementation and health care facilities as unique organizations in structure and culture.
- Evaluate resource utilization and processes for continuous quality improvement within current constraints of the health care system.
- Assess the problems and issues in implementing quality improvements efforts within a health care organization.
- Examine the role of information management and technology within quality improvement strategies.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
As an online student, your learning is more independent than that of your classroom counterparts. Unlike them, you are not bound by time and space for most activities. Despite this freedom, you may need to be more mindful of how you manage your time. This is not an at your own pace course because there are specific deadlines given for every assignment.

Each week, you will be required to do one or more of the following:

- Complete the reading assignments and conduct further research
- Complete discussion board posts
- Provide substantive responses to discussion board posts
- Complete a written assignment as identified in each module.
- Participate fully in the IPE course as demonstration of interprofessional collaboration and communication

You should plan to spend time online daily. Points are awarded for the quality of your work and the timeliness of your submissions to discussions and the assignment dropbox. Try to get into a rhythm. Set aside time each day to dedicate to your coursework. If you manage your time well, you will keep up with assignments and learn at a comfortable pace.
