### Course Description
In this course, you will explore billing service fee codes and medical billing processes, including data entry for both manual and computer billing in Canadian provincial billing systems and the different billing procedures in physicians’ offices and hospitals. The course will address EMR(electronic medical record systems), billing for visits, supplementary items, and diagnostic procedures carried out in a medical office, a hospital, or home. It is essential that you learn how to accurately process claims and forms related to alternate billing such as what is needed for government services (e.g. WorkSafeBC), reciprocal procedures, and private third party companies. Emphasis is placed on the MSC Payment Schedule and Diagnostic Code Descriptions from the Medical Services Plan website, The Doctors of BC Uninsured Services Fee Guide and other services across Canada. You will create a patient list, perform data entry comprising of patient demographics, service fee codes, and diagnostic fee descriptions. You will generate an electronic patient claim sheet record which includes billing codes, specialist details and billing notes through given outlined medical clinical scenarios. The General Preamble, General Services and Family Medicine sections of the MSC payment schedule provide the foundation for billing a medical service provided to a patient. Focus is on referencing the General Practice fee codes and their significance to age of the patient and type of service/visit provided. Practical application will introduce the learner to processing patient information beginning with registration, insurance verification, health history, and contact information required to create an electronic medical record. Billing visits use specific age-related codes referenced from an MSP Fee Schedule. Attention to detail and accuracy of entries and verification of billing entries prior to posting into batches is essential.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain why ethical practice is required to maintain confidentiality when performing medical billing.
- Describe security protocols and best practices for protecting electronic medical records (EMRs) and other sensitive medical data.
- Complete alternate billing and collection procedures.
- Locate information on eligibility, services covered, uninsured services, fee codes, and diagnostic codes by navigating through Medical Services Plan websites.
- Bill Medical Services Plans by using the Preamble, Fee, and Diagnostic Code Descriptions.
- Follow schedules from appropriate MSPs.
- Follow the steps of submission using Teleplan.
- Reject bills by following the appropriate guidelines.
- Demonstrate how to integrate patient information, perform basic MSP billing (e.g. WorkSafeBC and other services) bill reciprocally, bill private and third-party companies, and interpret explanatory codes when dealing with billing rejections.
- Generate posted medical billing reports that demonstrate accuracy and attention to detail.
- Apply problem solving skills to find and edit medical billing errors.

### Course Goals
## OFFC 1007: Course Learning Goals
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Navigate the Source of Truth: Utilize the MSP General Preamble and the General Practice (Section 7) of the MSC Fee Schedule as the definitive foundation for all medical billing.
- Master Fee-for-Service (FFS) for Family Practice: Accurately identify and reference Section 7 fee codes based on the specific type of service and the age of the patient for primary care providers.
- Ensure Billing Accuracy: Apply a high degree of attention to detail when verifying billing entries to ensure they meet provincial standards prior to posting into batches.
- Analyze Explanatory Codes: Interpret MSP and third-party explanatory codes to troubleshoot and resolve billing discrepancies.
- Execute Specialized Invoicing: Confidently navigate the unique billing requirements and reporting templates for WorkSafeBC , ICBC , and RoadSafety BC .

### Course Schedule and Assignments
## OFFC 1007: Course Syllabus & Schedule
Focus: Mastering the Source of Truth and the legal framework of BC Medical Billing.
- Class 1: Course Orientation & The General Preamble. Introduction to the Rulebook for Fee-for-Service (FFS) billing.
- Class 2: Theory Continuation. Deep dive into Payer roles, patient eligibility, and the DoBC (Doctors of BC) relationship.
- Class 3: Coding Mechanics. Introduction to Diagnostic Coding (ICD9) and common GP Service Codes.
- Class 4: Billing Scenarios & Logistics. Practical application of FFS theory, Uninsured services, and Explanatory codes.
- Class 5: Clinical Procedures. Minor Procedure billing, Tray Fees (Code 00080), and Prefix Letter Codes. Midterm Review.
- Class 6: MIDTERM EXAM (3 Hours). * Format: Comprehensive theory and coding application. Requirement: MANDATORY CAMERAS and microphones.
Focus: Moving beyond standard MSP into ICBC, WorkSafeBC, and RoadSafety.
- Class 7: Midterm Review & Introduction to RoadSafety. Transitioning from standard MSP to RoadSafety BC and the MOA Billing Guide.
- Class 8: WorkSafeBC (WSBC). Understanding WSBC protocols and the Timed WSBC Quiz (Submission same-day).
- Class 9: Advanced Scenarios. Lessons learned from complex billing errors and real-world troubleshoot scenarios.
- Class 10: ICBC Billing. Navigating Invoicing, Report Templates, Vendor Numbers, and the crucial role of Patient Consent.
- Class 11: Final Exam Review & Mock Exam. A non-graded simulation of the final exam. Answers provided on the Learning Hub.
- Class 12: FINAL EXAM (3 Hours). * Focus: Primarily on the latter half of the course (RoadSafety, WSBC, ICBC), while incorporating foundational MSP Theory from Classes 1–11. Requirement: MANDATORY CAMERAS and microphones.
### Course topics
## OFFC 1007: Course Topics & Curriculum Mapping
- The Source of Truth: Mastery of MSP Terminology as defined by the General Preamble .
- Fee-for-Service (FFS/SSS) Mechanics: Understanding how physicians are compensated under the provincial model.
- The Language of Billing: * Diagnostic Codes (ICD-9): Clinical coding for patient encounters. Service Fee Codes: Identifying the correct codes for various patient visits and procedures.
- Billing Entry: Practical application and data entry protocols.
- MSP Explanatory Codes: Decoding the remittance advice to understand why claims are paid, adjusted, or refused.
- Out-of-Province Protocols: Navigating Reciprocal Billing and managing Reciprocal Claims for non-BC residents.
- Federal & Specialized Billing: Procedures for RCMP and Correctional Services encounters.
- Private and Third-Party Billing: Processes for services not covered by the provincial medical plan.
- WorkSafeBC (WSBC): * WSBC Billing Protocols. Navigating WSBC Explanatory Codes and timelines.
- ICBC & RoadSafety BC: ICBC Reports: Understanding the mandatory templates and reporting requirements. ICBC Billing Codes: Specific invoicing for motor vehicle accident claims. RoadSafety BC: Collaborative billing and medical-legal reporting requirements.
