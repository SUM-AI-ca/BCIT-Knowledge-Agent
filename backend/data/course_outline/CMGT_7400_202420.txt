## Course Description
This course takes a look at construction management processes and procedures over the life of a project with a focus on quality control and risk management. Identifying the project phases, the key milestones and deliverables at each phase, is critical to include in the project plans. Tracking, analyzing, and evaluating, progress to the milestones over the project's lifecycle allows the management team to manage and mitigate both quality and risk factors in an organized and timely manner. Best practices and new concepts will be examined in order to enable students to formulate tools and techniques specific to their management styles and types of projects. This course also provides students with opportunities to further develop their presentation and report writing skills to meet industry expectations at the construction manager's level.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Define typical project phases and identity the processes and procedures standard to each phase.
- Explain the relationship between quality control and risk management on construction projects.
- Critically analyze quality control issues and risk factors to determine potential outcomes and outline appropriate mitigative measures.
- Evaluate quality and risk factors from a wider perspective, and over the projects lifecycle.
- Formulate project processes and procedures that will enable you to manage your projects pro-actively, to the quality level expected, while mitigating project risks throughout the project lifecycle.
