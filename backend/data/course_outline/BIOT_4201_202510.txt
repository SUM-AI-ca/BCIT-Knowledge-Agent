## Course Description
(UBC BIOT 206) Discusses microbial genetics, recombination, plasmids and recombination DNA techniques. Describes eucaryotic and procaryotic viruses, and surveys major groups of microorganisms. Also describes the role of microorganisms in the environment, industrial microbiology and biotechnology. Using a microbial system, employs procedures used in the identification of microorganisms, the evaluation of antimicrobial activity, the growth and quantification of phage and detection of mutagens and carcinogens.

## Attendance Requirements
In the biotechnology program, laboratory attendance is of the highest priority since lab experience is what sets this program apart from other undergraduate programs and what prepares students for co-op work terms. Furthermore, lab work is frequently done in pairs and students are therefore expected to be present to support their partners. You are expected to attend all in-person lecture and lab sessions unless you have a serious illness or other medical condition.

For some lab-based courses, participation marks will be given for completion of laboratory activities and these marks will be worth up to 5% of the student’s final course grade. A student must be on time for start of the lab period to avoid losing participation marks. These lab activities involve generating lab specific materials and/or data, learning, acquiring and/or practicing new skills and/or procedures.

Please notify your instructor and the Biotechnology program head of any absences by email as soon as possible.

If exams, quizzes, or in-class assignments are missed for reasons of illness, medical notes will be required to reschedule.

## Course Specific Requirements
Makeup Tests, Exams, or Quizzes: There will be no makeup tests, exams, or quizzes. If you miss a test, exam, or quiz, you will receive zero marks. Exceptions may be made for documented medical reasons or extenuating circumstances. In such a case, it is the responsibility of the student to inform the instructor immediately.

To be eligible to write the final examination, students must complete and submit a minimum of 80% of their assignments and reports.

Assignment Details: Assignments/lab reports must be submitted by the indicated due date or a penalty of 10% per day (24 hour) will be deducted from the assignment grade.

AI use: Any use of AI must align with BCIT academic integrity policies. If AI tools are used to assist in course work (idea generation, assignments etc.) students must clearly acknowledge their use, specifying the tool and it’s contributions. Students are expected to critically evaluate any information or content generated by AI tools to ensure their accuracy. You will not be penalized for using AI tool, however students will be required to submit an additional file with AI generated material. The use of AI to complete tests, exams or quizzes is prohibited. Undeclared use of AI is a violation of the academic integrity policy as it presents work or ideas created by tools or other people as your own.
