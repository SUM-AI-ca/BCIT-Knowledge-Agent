### Course Description
This course introduces participants to key sections of the BC Building Code, with a focus on Part 3 and Part 9 regulations as they relate to guardrails and handrails. Learners will gain foundational knowledge of code requirements concerning fall hazards, climate considerations, fire safety, occupant protection, and accessibility. The course emphasizes practical application of building code compliance throughout various project phases—from planning and design to production, measurement, drafting, and installation—strengthening participants’ ability to ensure safety and regulatory adherence in construction environments.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand general principles of Part 3 and Part 9 of British Columbia Building Code.
- Understand how to check for compliance with the regulations in the BC Building Code as it pertains specifically to guardrails and handrails.
- Establish introductory knowledge around building code requirements (i.e. fall hazard, climate, fire, occupant safety, accessibility, etc.) as it relates to guardrails and handrails.
- Demonstrate an improved knowledge of code requirements at different phases of a project: from planning, shop drawing/designing to production, measuring, drafting, and installation.​
