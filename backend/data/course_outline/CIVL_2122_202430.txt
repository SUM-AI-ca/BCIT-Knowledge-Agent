## Course Description
This course builds on the foundation of CIVL 2121, continuing with a discussion of the relationship between applied loads and the resulting stresses and deformations produced in common structural elements. Topics include beam deflections, combined stresses, stress transformations, Mohr's circle, column buckling, and torsion. A laboratory component is included in the delivery of this course.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Determine the elastic buckling loads of columns with various support conditions.
- Use standard equations in conjunction with the principle of superposition to calculate the deflection of bending members.
- Set up and perform laboratory tests to determine bending stresses and strain distribution and buckling properties of timber specimens.
- Describe the expected failure mode of short, intermediate, and long timber columns.
- Describe the principles that underlie the use of electrical resistance strain gauges and applications of electrical resistance strain gauges in current civil engineering practise.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Topic: Remarks
Week 1: Review of topics covered in Stress Analysis 1, Introduction to Columns and struts
Week 2: Columns and Struts: End Conditions, equivalent length, Euler's Equation, Problems
Week 3: Crippling load for various end conditions from 1st principles, Problems
Week 4: Introduction to Deflection of beams, differential equation for slope and deflection, deflection in cantilever beams for different load conditions from 1st principles; problems
Week 5: Lab Class 1, Exam 1: Exam 1 is of 1.5 hour duration
Week 6: Deflection in simply supported beams; double integration method, derivations from 1st principles for different load conditions: problems
Week 7: Deflection in beams; moment area method, Macaulay's method, problems
Week 8: Statically Indeterminate beams
Week 9: Lab 2, Exam 2: Exam 2 is of 1.5 hour duration
Week 10: Stress and strain analysis: plane stress, principal stress and maximum shear stress
Week 11: Stress and strain analysis: Mohr's circle
Week 12: Final Exam: Final exam will be of 2 hours duration
