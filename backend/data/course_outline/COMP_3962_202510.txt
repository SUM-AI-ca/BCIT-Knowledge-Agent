## Course Description
In this course students learn how to utilize a wide variety of resources and services available on cloud computing platforms. Topics include characteristics of cloud computing; cloud deployment models; cloud platform architectures; cloud computing platforms and comparative analysis; cloud compute resources including virtual machine instances, load balancers, auto scaling groups, and snapshots; cloud networking, with an emphasis on virtual private clouds (VPCs); cloud storage and content delivery, cloud-hosted databases, and cloud security models including user identity, access management, and resource security. Students will build their skills by participating in in-class interactive lectures, hands-on lab activities, and working on comprehensive assignments.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Differentiate the different types of cloud deployment and service models.
- Describe the advantages and disadvantages of cloud computing.
- Analyze the platform architectures of a variety of public cloud computing platforms.
- Configure and deploy virtual machines (instances), load balancers, and auto scaling groups.
- Design and implement VPCs.
- Implement security policies on users and resources, including VPCs and instances.
- Select storage options for resources and applications, including instances, databases and websites.
- Design and deploy cloud-based websites.
- Deploy and configure a variety of database instances.
- Remotely configure and monitor deployed resources.
- Install, configure and use both integrated development environments and command line interfaces to configure and manage resources on public clouds.

## Other information
In this course, students will be using Amazon Web Services (Amazon), Google Cloud Platform (Google) and Microsoft Azure (Microsoft). As BCIT is subject to the BC Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act and in accordance with BCIT Policy 6700 Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy that requires all BCIT employees take steps to protect Personal Information within their custody and control, it is BCIT's responsibility to inform students prior to using Amazon, Google and Microsoft products in this course, that this software may be hosted outside of Canada.

What is Personal information? “Personal Information” is any recorded information that uniquely identifies the student, such as your name, address, telephone number, age, sex, race, religion, sexual orientation, disability, fingerprints, or blood type. It includes information about the student's educational, financial, criminal, employment or health care/medical history. It also includes anyone else's opinions about the student and the student's own views or opinions.

Why is BCIT concerned about where Personal Information resides? Amazon, Google and Microsoft may be operated by vendors that are US owned and any information held on Amazon, Google and Microsoft servers outside of Canada are subject to the USA Patriot Act. Student's personal information may be used by other third parties as is it not protected by Canadian law. The USA Patriot Act gives the US government the right to seize and use any data residing on any server owned and/or controlled by a US or US based company regardless of where that server may be in the world. This increases the risk of unauthorized or improper access, use and disclosure of BCIT student's and instructor's personal information and violation of privacy provisions under section 30.1 of FIPPA which with few limited exceptions requires that personal information in the control or custody of a public body be stored and accessed only in Canada unless the individual about whom the information is about has consented to their Personal Information being accessed or stored outside of Canada.

Notice and Consent. BCIT wishes students to be fully informed prior to their using this software and BCIT is thus also obligated to obtain a student's written consent should the student agree to the use Amazon, Google and Microsoft products. It is also important for a student to know that their consent to agree is optional and may be withdrawn at any time. If a student wishes to opt out of using the Amazon, Google and Microsoft cloud platforms altogether, the student can discuss alternative activities to complete with the instructor. A student may also choose to opt back in at any time.

The nature of the information that may be at risk within the context of the specific software being proposed is the studen'ts name, address, email address, telephone number and credit card information.

If a student agrees to the use of Amazon, Google and Microsoft, BCIT must ask them to agree in writing to do so. BCIT will provide the student with a consent form the first day this course convenes.
