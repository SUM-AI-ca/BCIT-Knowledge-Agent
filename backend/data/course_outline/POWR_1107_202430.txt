## Course Description
This math course focuses on mathematic problems and situations encountered in the trades environment, particularly in power plants. Course topics include basic mathematical concepts, algebraic expressions, linear equations, geometric problems, logarithms, and trigonometry. The course also covers section one (Math) of the 4th Class Inter-Provincial course with preparation for the examination.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:

- Perform simple arithmetic operations.
- Demonstrate knowledge of algebraic principles, terminology and operations.
- Demonstrate knowledge of geometric principles, terminology and operations.
- Demonstrate knowledge of trigonometric principles, terminology and operations.
- Demonstrate knowledge of logarithmic principles, terminology and operations.
- Solve problems and equations by applying the appropriate mathematical principles and formulae.
- Solve mensuration problems involving lengths, areas and volumes in SI and Imperial systems.
- Achieve a minimum mark of 65% on a written mathematics test modelled on the 4th Class Interprovincial examination.

## Course Goals
Chapter 1 - Fundamental Arithmetic

- Define and apply concepts of multiplication, division, addition, and subtraction
- Define and determine lowest common multiples
- Definition and calculation common factors
- Define proper, improper and mixed fractions
- Convert between proper, improper and mixed fractions
- Add, subtract, divide, and multiple fractions
- Given conversion factors, convert between different units of measure

Chapter 2 - Arithmetic 2

- Define and apply concepts of powers and roots
- Calculate ratios
- Define direct and inverse proportionality
- Solve direct and inverse proportionality problems
- Define and calculate percentages
- Define and calculate weighted averages

Chapter 3 - Algebra Fundamentals

- Explain the acronym BEDMAS and how it applies to the order of operations
- Simplify mathematical expressions that involve addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, brackets, and exponents

Chapter 4 - Simple Equations

- Define what constitutes an equation
- Solve equations to determine valuse of any unknowns
- Analyze word problem and extract pertinent information

Chapter 5 - Interpolation and Extrapolation

- Define interpolation and extrapolation
- Explain potential errors created by interpolation and extrapolation
- Use interpolation and extrapolation to determine unknown values

Chapter 6 - Quadratic Equations

- Define quadratic equations
- Use factoring to solve for unknown values in quadratic equations
- Use the quadratic formula to solve for unknown values in quadratic equations
- Recognize other forms of equations that could be solved using quadratic formula methods

Chapter 7 - Simultaneous Equations

- Define simultaneous equations
- Solve a series of simultaneous equations to determine unknown values (limited to two variable systems)

Chapter 8 - Logarithms

- Define logarithms and anti-logarithms
- Differentiate between base 10 logarithms and natural logarithms
- Calculate logarithms and anti-logarithms
- Use logarithms to determine unknown exponent values
- Explain scientific notation

Chapter 9 - Geometry

- Differentiate between degrees and radians
- Convert between degrees and radians
- Convert between angles given in decimal form and those in degrees, minutes, and seconds
- Differentiate between acute, obtuse, and right angles
- Define radius, diameter, sector, segment, chord, and arc for circles

Chapter 10 - Trigonometry

- Define triangles
- Apply the Pythagoras theorem
- Explain the acronym SOHCAHTOA and specify where it can be used
- Apply the sine rule
- Apply the cosine rules

Chapter 11 - Mensuration

- Differentiate bewteen lengths, areas, and volumes
- Define and calculate perimeters of shapes given in the PanGlobal Academic Supplement
- Define and calculate area of shapes given in the PanGlobal Academic Supplement
- Differentiate between cross-sectional areas and surface areas
- State and apply Hero's Formula to calculating the area of a triangle
- Define and calculate volumes of shapes given in the PanGlobal Academic Supplement
- Discuss the concept of similar shapes
