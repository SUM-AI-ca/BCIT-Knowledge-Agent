## Course Description
Human Behaviour 1 (DSON) explores psychological and sociological concepts, research and applications of relevance to sonographers in training and in clinical practice. This course includes skills for interaction and communication; preventing and managing distress in clients and self; working with cultural diversity; group interaction and work teams; dealing with client needs associated with pregnancy, age and abilities, mortality, managing interpersonal conflict, harassment, reporting child abuse and ethics; balancing expectations of patients with requirements of workplace regarding disclosure of information; and recognizing and responding to patient anxiety and depression. Emphasizes improving sensitivity and human relations skills in dealing with both clients and staff. Students in this program have a particular focus on the unique aspects of caring for clients who are pregnant and their families.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Apply concept of empathy to create respectful and supportive relations with clients and co-workers.
- Integrate effective interpersonal communication skills in order to foster rapport and trust.
- Perform various types of assertion according to appropriateness and desired result.
- Employ methods used to resolve conflict to maintain a professional work environment.
- Explain common cultural differences relating to interpersonal communication styles, patient perceptions, experiences and expectations.
- Demonstrate respect for diversity by interacting with patients from various cultures appropriately and respectfully.
- Use recommended strategies to prevent and reduce distress in both self and clients.
- Describe stress related disorders, and appropriate treatments and interventions.
- Analyze approaches to managing negative feelings in self and clients.
- Manage effectively various types of harassment and discrimination found in the workplace.
- Apply knowledge of developmental differences that impact interactions and procedures in a clinical setting.
- Respond ethically and within legal guidelines to situations where abuse is suspected.

## Other information
Midterm Skills Video Assessment (20%): Students will be given written quiz prompts (via Bongo Video Assignment) and asked to respond verbally on camera within a short time limit. The midterm video assessment will assess skill understanding and implementation by requiring students to respond to scenario-based prompts with appropriate nonverbal and verbal communication. Students will receive one chance to respond to each prompt. For exam integrity reasons, the skills assessment will take place at an assigned time, if possible. No retakes or late quizzes will be accepted. Students are expected to keep the content of quizzes confidential.

Culture Presentation (20%) + Peer Evaluations (10%) + Individual Role Paper (10%): Students will complete a recorded digital presentation in small groups. Groups will be assigned by instructor. More information about the presentation and corresponding assignments will be provided in class. After completion of the group assignment, students will write a brief paper reflecting on their role and contribution to the group, skills/strategies they used to navigate group dynamics, and the impact of their skill utlization on the the group.

Final Exam (35%): The final exam will be cumulative and administered via Learning Hub Quiz feature. It will consist of written-response and short-answer questions. MISSED EXAMS: Students are required to notify instructor in advance if they are unable to write the exam at the scheduled time. Failure to take an exam at the scheduled time may result in a grade of “0” for that exam, unless prior approval is arranged (or in the rare care of an emergency). LATE EXAM ARRIVAL: Students are expected to arrive on time for exams. If arrival is later than 30 minutes after the start of the exam, the student will not be permitted to write the exam. The student must contact the BHSC department head to request that an alternative arrangement. It is the sole discretion of the BHSC department head to permit an alternative arrangement. EXAM INTEGRITY: The instructor reserves the right to utilize an alternative assessment method (which may be different from the original) in the case of a missed exam or if the instructor believes the exam has been compromised (after consultation with the department head).

Participation (5%): This is an interpersonal communciation skills class. It is essential that students develop interpersonal communication skills by actively participating in class. Participation marks will be awarded at the end of the term at the discretion of the Instructor. Students will have the opportunity to reflect upon their own communication strengths/weaknesses, set personal classroom communication goals, and evaluate their own communication skill development throughout the term. This input will be considered, along with instructor participation considerations (below), when determining participation marks at the end of term.
