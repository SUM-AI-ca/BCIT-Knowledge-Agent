### Course Description
​This course covers the essential components of an occupational health and safety management system for organizations. Students will examine the legislative landscape, regulations, and standards and understand the frameworks needed to implement and manage effective health, safety, and wellness strategies and programs in the workplace. The course will emphasize strategic thinking, analytics, and contemporary practices and introduce reporting requirements of the various federal and provincial regulators – Work Safe BC. Students will learn practices that encourage wellness, prevention and education to sustain employee and organizational health. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Distinguish between the complementary elements of a human resources professional (CPHR) and an occupational health and safety professional (CRSP).
- Describe the elements of an effective occupational health and safety program.
- Explain the role of the occupational health and safety and workers’ compensation agencies and explore the various prevention, education, and enforcement resources.
- Prepare the various forms required by workers' compensation boards when responding to a workplace accident and compensation claim.
- Identify those points in the process where a human resources department should be involved.
- Describe the participation of HR in the workers' compensation appeals process on behalf of the employer.
- Outline the participation of HR in effective disability management and return-to-work programs.
- Develop wellness initiatives that promote mental health and well-being through structured benefits and ad-hoc programs.
- Assess whether health and wellness programs are inclusive, address diverse employee needs, and promote equity in access to resources and benefits.
- Describe accommodation processes and medical leaves, including how to train and coach managers on handling medical leave and return-to-work processes.​
