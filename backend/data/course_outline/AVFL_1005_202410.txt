## Course Description
AVFL 1005 will cover aircraft inspection reporting techniques such as image capture, PowerPoint presentation of defects, manufacture limit identification and defect limit evaluation per manufacture specifications.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Record aircraft defects
- Use correct reporting forms and parts tags for defect reporting
- Use Microsoft office products to produce defect reports
- Manipulate digital images within Microsoft products
- Review published data to determine serviceability of defects.
