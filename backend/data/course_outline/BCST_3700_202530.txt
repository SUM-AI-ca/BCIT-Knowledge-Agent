### Course Description
In this course, students will expand upon the digital newsroom skills learned in Digital Journalism (BCST 2240) and Journalism Practicum 1 (BCST 2235) and apply those skills in an active daily Digital Newsroom. Participants will work in the field and also in the newsroom managing the BCIT News webpage and social media accounts. As a team, students will work to produce original content created specifically for a digital audience
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Work in a group to manage all digital aspects of the BCIT Newsroom.
- Follow ethical and legal guidelines, specifically relating to online content creation and copyright.
- Write informative online articles, using industry-standard style and format.
- Write engaging headlines using SEO guidelines.
- Recognize appropriate sources for gathering digital content.
- Create multimedia stories using a variety of digital tools.
- Produce content to deadline.

Late assignments *may* be accepted without penalty, on a case-by-case basis with prior communication (and permission) in advance of the due date/time.
Journalistic integrity: In addition to BCIT’s Student Code of Academic Misconduct policy (policy 5104), academic integrity violations within the journalism program may also be defined as…
- Deliberately inaccurate or misleading reporting
- Misrepresentation of facts or individuals
- Conflicts of interest (including but not limited to interviewing family, friends, roommates, or individuals at places you work or accepting a gift or monetary compensation for coverage, excluding exchanges as part of cultural traditions)
- Falsifying audio, images, or video including the use of artificial intelligence
Additional note regarding Lab evaluation:
Evaluation includes a compilation of all work a student submits for vetting or marking while in a specific role. Therefore, penalties for violations of academic or journalistic integrity will be deducted from a student’s overall mark in that role.
