## Course Description
This course builds on the skills developed in MKTG 3309. It focuses on the collection and analysis of market intelligence to facilitate strategic business decisions using state-of-the-art research techniques. Key topics include competitive intelligence, business analytics, marketing and customer metrics, and advanced applied marketing research techniques. Skills developed include: designing a competitive intelligence program; developing a competitive intelligence matrix reporting tool; locating and using both traditional and non-traditional sources of competitive intelligence information; applying marketing KPIs and data visualization techniques to develop effective dashboard reporting and early warning system. Students in this course complete projects applying the major areas of study to real company situations.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Competitive Intelligence:
- Identify intelligence needs relevant to strategic decision-making.
- Analyze the forces that drive strategic risk in a competitive industry.
- Develop and undertake a Competitive Intelligence research program for a real business situation using a variety of data sources and analysis tools.
- Explain the ethical implications related to gathering market and competitive intelligence.
- Business Analytics:
- Explain the strategic role of Business Analytics and outline the types and levels of analytics techniques specifically related to Marketing.
- Explain the applications of Marketing Automation, CRM, and Customer Metrics in Demand Management Strategies.
- Apply Marketing KPIs and Data Visualization techniques to develop effective dashboard reporting and early warning system.
- Applied Marketing Intelligence:
- Identify decisions for the marketing manager and the unique research techniques to facilitate data-driven strategies.
