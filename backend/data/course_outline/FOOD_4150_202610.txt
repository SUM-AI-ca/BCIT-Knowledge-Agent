### Course Description
This capstone course introduces the basic product development process, and students will work in teams to develop a new food product. Techniques used in other food technology courses will be utilized to identify appropriate ingredients and processing equipment, set up experiments, measure product attributes, perform preliminary shelf life testing, develop packaging, and write a Preventive Control Plan (PCP). FOOD 4030 is a required co-requisite that provides with tools to assess a new food product.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain how the food safety of a new food product may be improved through the manipulation of factors.
- Identify and perform appropriate objective measurements for a given product development scenario.
- Discuss the role of nutrition when designing a new food product.
- Create a new food product.
- Integrate a new food product into a food safety and quality program.
- Develop a marketing strategy and costing for the new product.
- Create a presentation to present the new food to interested customers.

### Course Goals
This capstone course introduces the basic product development process, and students will work in teams to develop a new food product. Techniques used in other food technology courses will be utilized to identify appropriate ingredients and processing equipment, set up experiments, measure product attributes, perform preliminary shelf life testing, develop packaging, and write a Preventive Control Plan (PCP). This project is done co-jointly with BUSA 1102.
Upon successful completion, the student while working in a team environment will be able to:
- Explain how the food safety of a new food product may be improved through the manipulation of factors.
- Identify and perform appropriate objective measurements for a given product development scenario.
- Discuss the role of nutrition when designing a new food product.
- Create a new food product.
- Integrate a new food product into a food safety and quality program.
- Develop a business plan, marketing strategy and costing for the new product
- Co-jointly with BUSA 1102 create a presentation and detailed report to present the new food to interested customers.
