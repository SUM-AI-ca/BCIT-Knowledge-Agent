## Course Description
Introduces the field of occupational health and safety for operations managers. Examines a broad range of safety topics and provides opportunity to participate in the application of several safety functions such as an inspection, accident investigation and safety committee meetings. Discusses the requirements for a successful safety program integrated into the company business plan; at the same time explores the legal, economic and humanistic reasons for doing so. Covers additional topics including WHMIS, lock-out, confined spaces, workers' compensation, fire protection, and due diligence.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Apply the principles of accident prevention to daily business planning and operations.
- Explain the function of Workers Compensation Systems, and particularly the WCB of BC.
- Actively participate on a workplace Joint Health and Safety Committee.
- Outline the requirements for effective fire prevention and protection.
- Apply the requirements of Workplace Hazardous Information System (WHMIS) to an industrial setting.
- Actively participate in worksite safety inspections, and accident investigations.
- Apply the principles of WCB Claims Management to effectively administer accident claims.
- Outline the requirements for a basic accident prevention program.
- Apply the principles of 'Due Diligence' as they relate to legal and moral expectations, as well as employer and personal obligations and liability.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
This course is designed to teach Operations Managers an overview to the field of Occupational Health and Safety. The student will learn about Legal requirements specific to the WorkSafe BC Regulations and Act and the application of those regulations to Operations Management. Students will complete a WorkSafe BC Regulations assignment by researching the regulations through the BCIT library. Discussions about successful safety programs and program integration with business plans, along with principles of accident prevention and the Moral, legal and humanistic approaches will be facilitated by the instructor during lectures.

Operations Management students will learn about safety functions in the workplace such as conducting safety inspections, participating in accident investigations and performing claims management for accidents. Students will conduct a hazard evaluation/controls during a workplace safety inspection off campus, participate in an Accident Investigation case study, and facilitate/participate in a Joint Health and Safety Committee Meeting in seminar formats. Finally, students will learn about the details of evacuation planning and Assessing Life Safety concept strategies. Scheduling for assignments during in class seminars and off campus activities will be instructor paced during the 10 week course.
