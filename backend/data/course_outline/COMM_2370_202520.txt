## Course Description
This course will present strategic principles and technical practices for the development of effective resume and cover letter writing skills, as well as networking and interviewing skills. Students will participate in a range of activities including self assessment, tactical job search strategies, and simulation exercises, including an interview. This format will provide current theory, guidance, and practical experience through examples and application. Assessment will be based on the successful development and completion of a comprehensive personal career package of relevant skills and abilities.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Examine and objectively assess educational, professional, personal skills related attributes
- Evaluate current employment market
- Develop job search tactics and strategies
- Develop skills and knowledge required to gain employment
- Prepare effectively written resume and cover letter
- Prepare an online professional summary
- Apply oral (and non-oral) skills to simulated interviews​
