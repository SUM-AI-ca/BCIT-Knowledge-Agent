### Course Description
Presents an overview of the many components that comprise Nuclear Medicine Technology and its role in the health care system. Students are introduced to communication, professional practice and ethical issues relevant to the profession. Students will have the opportunity to work in Interprofessional teams with other health professional students in the acquisition of entry-to-practice Interprofessional competencies. The course also includes a comprehensive study of medical terminology through a word-building approach using systematic construction with roots, prefixes and suffixes.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use a variety of resources to locate and search literature relevant to the profession.
- Present relevant, accurate and complete information on a topic related to the profession.
- Demonstrate a basic understanding of historical, current and emerging topics relevant to imaging and therapeutic technologies.
- Describe the functions, resources and opportunities provided by provincial, national and international professional bodies.
- Describe ‘Nuclear Medicine’ and the various components of practice.
- Outline the requirements for nuclear medicine technologist professional certification.
- Describe basic sequential events in the diagnostic process.
- Describe the roles of members of the healthcare team, including the patient, family and community, and their contributions to safe healthcare.
- Discuss the relevance of nuclear medicine in diagnostic and therapeutic processes.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the shared and differing roles of the medical radiation professions.
- Demonstrate an understanding of legislation, regulations & ethical guidelines related to the profession.
- Relate the CAMRT Code of Ethics to professional practice and performance expectations.
- Reference CAMRT Best Practice Guidelines to inform professional practice.
- Discuss ethical considerations of health research.
- Construct and derive the meaning of medical terms.
- Apply an understanding of medical terminology to communicate effectively in professional practice.
