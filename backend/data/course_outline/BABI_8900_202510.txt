## Course Description
​This course is designed to improve student ability to apply a balanced scorecard approach to increase organizational performance. Students will identify leading and lagging key performance indicators (KPIs) that are commonly measured and contribute to a firm's strategic success. This course moves beyond theory to build on data modelling and business intelligence skills to build and implement a performance management dashboard using Excel & Power BI.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Assess how organizations can profit from strategic agility.
- Evaluate an organization's performance using financial and nonfinancial measures.
- Identify the impact of gaps between a firm's objectives, measures, targets, and strategic initiatives.
- Evaluate the role of performance dashboards in strategic and operational management.
- Identify common key performance metrics that drive performance.
- Analyze the attributes of effective performance measures.
- Recommend and critically defend a balanced approach to strategic management.
- Apply critical thinking to design effective performance management dashboards.
- Develop a performance management dashboard using Microsoft Excel & Power BI.
