### Course Description
This course introduces all the necessary production elements that are required to create a television or video production. Students will be exposed to general business concepts, such as the need for various agreements to govern the relationships between parties involved with a production, the purpose of schedules and budgets and methods for tracking completion of objectives. Students will also experience the uniquely important role communication plays in the planning and production of television and video productions.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify and explain the phases of a television or video production by describing the activities that occur in each phase.
- Under limited supervision, research a potential client’s video production requirements.
- Determine specific production deliverables to achieve a client’s video production requirements.
- With guidance, produce a director’s shot list and/or storyboard for a scene or short script segment.
- With guidance, analyse the director’s shot list and/or storyboard to produce a set of breakdown lists of production elements required to visually tell the story (e.g. production design elements of sets, wardrobe, makeup and hair, lighting, props, etc.).
- Research and document sources for production elements.
- With guidance, create a rudimentary Production Schedule using a variety of project planning methods.
- Identify elements of a production budget including those in the Telefilm Canada budget template.
- With instructor guidance and feedback, create a rudimentary production budget for a given short script or script segment.
- Discuss budget tracking and control processes.
- Coordinate the compilation of all production documentation including script, breakdowns, schedule, budget, and communications.
