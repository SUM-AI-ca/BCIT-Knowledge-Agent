## Course Description
The role of the sales manager in planning, directing and controlling will be the focus of this course including the selection of sales representatives as well as training, supervision, motivation and leadership.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:

- Describe the critical Role of the Sales Manager in an organization.
- Explain the importance of Sales Management Leadership and Vision in today’s competitive market.
- Communicate effectively at all levels of the organization.
- Significantly enhance the effect of sales meetings in accomplishing overall objectives.
- Obtain a broad understanding of Human Resource Management Components.
- Develop a proactive recruiting, hiring and training plan which ensures the availability and development of outstanding sales personnel.
- Understand the critical importance of Strategic Sales Management.
- Significantly improve the tactical (face to face) sales efforts of the sales team by more effectively managing the planning and execution of sales calls.
- Provide effective Management of Sales Performance.
- Coach and motivate the sales force effectively.

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion and application of the course materials, the student will be able to:

- Understand the transition from “Selling to Managing.”
- Understand that in management, “It's all About Communication.”
- Understand Sales Planning: Setting the Direction for the Sales Team.
- Understand Time Management, Territory Planning and Sales Forecasting.
- Become skilled in Recruiting, Interviewing and Hiring the Very Best
- Build an environment for motivation: compensation plans, recognition, and rewards.
- Train, coach, counsel, and know when to apply each of them.
- Step up to be a true leader.

## Course topics
Students will play the role of a newly promoted District Sales Manager from their previous position as a territory sales representative.

Students will communicate with their imaginary sales team of seven sales representatives throughout the province of British Columbia using emails, sales meetings, working with sales representatives, and face-to-face online activities. (The sales representatives are imaginary and intended only for communication parts of the project.)

Students will participate in the recruiting, interviewing and hiring of a new sales representative, replacing a team member who has recently resigned from the company.

Students will participate in lessons regarding exit interviews, resume reviews, interview questions and candidate selection.
