## Course Description
In Portfolio 1 students will compile their best work from terms 1 and 2, as well as personal work performed independently, into a targeted interactive portfolio. During the duration of this course the students will start new projects in specific disciplines to further develop their research, management, design and programming skills in a self-directed format. A personal brand, and self-promotional materials will be defined by each student to reflect their personalities and design styles. The students will be expected to present their portfolios at the end of the course. Students will be assessed by a combination of individual projects and a group peer critique.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Produce an industry-quality digital portfolio and artefacts that demonstrates creative, development and professional skills in new media.
- Plan and implement search engine optimization and metadata for portfolio site and professional social media.
- Research, write, report, document, store and package information in a variety of formats.
- Integrate principles of visual design into the creation and evaluation of one’s own work and the assessment of the work of others. Use the portfolio as a tool for displaying, communicating and promoting and marketing purposes.
- Integrate principles of visual design into the creation and evaluation of one’s own work and the assessment of the work of peers.
- Give and receive constructive feedback on portfolios work.
- Present a portfolio to clients and prospective employers.
- Use current applications of the Adobe cloud creative suite and current new media development tools.
- Recognize differences between freelancing and employment showcase digital portfolios.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
