## Course Description
Through the study of Building Construction assemblies & techniques, this subject area endeavors to develop the Student's: (1) Knowledge of various construction assemblies, issues, & methods. (2) Analytical & technical construction knowledge. (3) Development of the fundamental principles, employed in residential, commercial, industrial, & institutional projects. (4) Ability to develop technical Architectural working drawings.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Communicate effectively in the graphic & verbal language of building construction.
- Effectively recognize and problem solve building construction issues.
- Understand the organization & principles of architectural working drawings.
- Prepare professional quality drawings using the graphic language, conventions, and standards of the construction industry.
- Develop an understanding of the relationship of the drawings to the context of the overall contract documents.
