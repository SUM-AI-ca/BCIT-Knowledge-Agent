### Course Description
Motorcycle technicians work primarily on 2 and 3-wheeled motorcycles and other units such as motor scooters. Topics for level 1 include safety-related functions, routine work practices, tools, equipment and documentation, communication and mentoring techniques, wheels and tires, brake systems, final drive systems, electrical systems and vehicle management systems.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Maintain safe work environment
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE) and safety equipment
- Use trade-related consumables
- Perform periodic maintenance of lubrication systems
- Perform periodic maintenance of cooling systems
- Perform periodic maintenance of bearings
- Perform storage procedures
- Prepare new motorcycles
- Use diagnostic tools and equipment
- Use precision measuring instruments
- Use hand tools
- Use heating/cutting tools and equipment
- Use pneumatic and electric power tools and equipment
- Use shop equipment
- Use documentation
- Maintain tires
- Maintain cast wheels
- Maintain hydraulic braking systems
- Maintain mechanical braking systems
- Maintain final drive chains and sprockets
- Maintain final drive belts and pulleys (sprockets)
- Apply electrical and electronic principles
- Maintain batteries
- Maintain electrical standard and accessory components
- Read diagnostic trouble codes (DTC)
