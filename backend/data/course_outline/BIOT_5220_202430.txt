## Course Description
(UBC BIOT 307) Introduces genetic analysis viewed from the molecular level. Includes Mendel's Laws, chromosome mapping, DNA structure, gene mutation, and the genetic basis for recombinant DNA technology.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe molecular genetics and genomics.
- Discuss the structure of DNA and how DNA can be manipulated.
- Discuss transmission genetics and perform genetic analyses.
- Discuss the chromosomal basis for heredity.
- Analyze and map genes and chromosomes using linkage analysis.
- Describe the molecular organization of chromosomes.
- Analyze human karyotypes and chromosome behaviour.
- Discuss the molecular mechanisms of DNA replication and recombination.
- Describe the genetics of bacteria and their viruses.
- Apply genetics to populations.
- Subclone DNA fragments.
- Apply molecular biology to the study of gene expression.

## Attendance Requirements
Biotechnology Attendance Requirements

Students are expected to attend all lecture and lab sessions unless they have a serious illness or other medical condition. In the Biotechnology Program, laboratory attendance is of the highest priority since lab experience is what sets this program apart from other undergraduate programs and what prepares students for co-op work terms where it is expected that they will have acquired specific competencies. Furthermore, lab work is typically done in pairs or small teams and students are, therefore, expected to work collaboratively and support their lab partners.

Please notify your instructor and the Biotechnology program head of any absences. If exams, quizzes, or in-class assignments are missed for reasons of illness, medical notes will be required to reschedule.
