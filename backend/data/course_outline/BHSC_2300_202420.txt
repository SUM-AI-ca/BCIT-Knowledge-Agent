## Course Description
Develop core skills for effective communication and interpersonal interactions for Medical Laboratory Technologists within the health care team, including concepts such as communication skills, establishing and maintaining a relationship with clients and others, caring, empathy, and courtesy. Uses an experiential and self-reflective approach to develop self-awareness, an increased understanding of self, and how one's own interpersonal style affects others. Respecting and responding sensitively to individuals with cultural or other intersectional diversity is emphasized. Teamwork exercises and learning activities include discussions, role-play, and case study analysis. Learning is assessed through a mix of theoretical exams, skills demonstrations and presentations.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Explain inter-professional collaborative practices with regard to role clarification, communication, and collaboration.
- Explain aspects of defensiveness and identify approaches to minimizing this.
- Demonstrate effective verbal, non-verbal, and written communication strategies.
- Manifest and convey empathy in working with patients and colleagues in a health care setting (e.g., become aware of nonverbal communication skills, active listening, assertiveness training)
- Describe the nature of interpersonal conflicts.
- Create possible ways to resolve conflict leading to win-win situations.
- Apply effective teamwork skills.
- Discuss cultural differences and use approaches to increase understanding and respect between persons of different cultures in clinical situations.
- Illustrate approaches to working effectively with patients where language barriers are present.
- Define confidentiality of healthcare information and understands the importance of informed consent prior to performing a procedure and respecting a patient's right to refuse

## Other information
Please note full details regarding assignments and rubrics will be provided on The Learning Hub

Active Listening Skills Quiz (15%): Students will complete a quiz based on modules 1&2. The quiz is designed to assess completion and comprehension of assigned readings. Students are expected to keep the content of quizzes confidential.

Group Communication Handout (25%) + Journal Reflection (complete/incomplete, 5%): Students will be assigned groups to create a handout regarding skills and strategies from Modules 2-4. Rubric will be provided on The Learning Hub. After completion of the assignment, student will write a brief paper (250 words) reflecting on their role and contribution to the group, skills/strategies they used to navigate group dynamics, and the impact of their skill utilization on the group.

Final Reflection paper (500 words, 35%): Students will write a brief paper reflecting on skills practice outside of class, which can include scenarios from their clinical placement. Students are expected to integrate information from modules 1-6 which will be outlined in a rubric on The Learning Hub. Students will reflect on their values, skills/strategies used, strengths, areas of growth, and the impact of their skill utilization on colleagues and patients.

Participation (complete/incomplete, 20%): This is an interpersonal communication skills class. It is essential that students develop interpersonal communication skills by practicing skills outside of class and reflecting on their progress in the discussion forum. In order to gain full participation marks, students are expected to actively engage on a weekly basis by posting a response to two discussion questions and commenting on a classmate’s response. Participation marks are also awarded based on the completion of a goal setting activity and reflection. Rubric will be provided in The Learning Hub.

Additional Considerations:

Use of skills

You will be asked to try out new skills outside of class and reflect on successes and challenges. Show that you can apply your learning by sharing your reflections with others in the discussion forum. Respond to classmates (and instructor) using active listening skills when writing responses. Use the course skills when communicating via email. Integrate skills into all aspects of the course and into your clinical placement experiences. This class is an opportunity to build strong working relationships with your classmates and colleagues, who are a part of your professional network.

Questions

Demonstrate active listening by asking questions in the weekly discussion forum – others might be wondering the same thing! Students are also encouraged to ask questions of fellow classmates. Keep the conversation going.

Contribution

Demonstrate your engagement by offering your perspective or answering questions about class materials.

Respectful Conduct

Some topics may be sensitive and/or invite debate of different perspectives. Disrespectful language will not be permitted. Use verbal and nonverbal communication to promote respect, inclusion, sensitivity, and curiousity.

Self-disclosure

While your discussion posts and assignments include personal reflection and writing about experiences communicating with others, you may choose your depth of personal self-disclosure. Please consider what details you feel comfortable sharing in an online class setting.

Preparation for weekly modules

Prepare for each week by completing the module readings early so you have time reflect and practice skills before writing your discussion post.

ASSIGNMENTS: Assignments should be submitted to the designated drop box on the Learning Hub on the date and time specified. Late assignments will not be accepted. If extenuating circumstances impact your ability to submit your assignment on time, please contact your instructor to discuss prior to the due date.
