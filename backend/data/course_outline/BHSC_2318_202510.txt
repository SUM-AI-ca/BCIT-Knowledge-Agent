## Course Description
Explore advanced topics related to the peripheral nervous system anatomy and physiology and its immediate relevance to the practice of electroneurophysiology with an emphasis on nerve conduction studies (NCS) and electromyography (EMG). Students deepen their understanding of the normal peripheral nervous system, muscle, and neuromuscular junction (NMJ) anatomy and physiology and learn to apply the neurological exam to assessment of nervous system function. Discussion of related material encountered in the clinic, lab, or other courses is encouraged and active participation in learning will be expected. Evaluation of learning will include quizzes, exams, and self-evaluation of participation.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the individual components of a general neurological exam and relate each to the normal function of the peripheral nervous system that is being assessed.
- Describe normal anatomy, physiology, and development of the peripheral nervous system, including nerves and muscle of the limbs, head, neck, and back.
- Describe basic nerve and muscle physiology as it pertains to electrodiagnostic testing.
- Describe components involved in normal movement, including selected brain and spinal cord tracts.
- Describe peripheral nerves, neuromuscular junction, muscle, and proprioceptive and sensory organs and relevant electrophysiologic methods of assessment.
