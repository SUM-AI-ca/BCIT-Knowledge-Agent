### Course Description
A+ Certified Technicians possess knowledge and skills essential for employment as computer service technicians. This course utilizes the hardware and software technologies used in today's workplace and aligns with the topics examined in the A+ certification exam. The course includes lab activities in which students work with computer hardware and operating systems software to develop troubleshooting skills, assemble and configure computers, add peripherals, connect to networks and customize the operation of the computer. Students are expected to have working knowledge of computers. Students must have access to a Windows based multi-media computer.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the functions of the hardware components of a computer.
- Replace components to repair and upgrade computers.
- Troubleshoot hardware issues on a personal computer.
- Configure networking parameters to establish network connectivity.
- Install and provide basic configuration of a Windows operating system.
- Troubleshoot computer problems within a Windows environment.
- Describe procedures for adding and removing peripheral devices in a Windows environment.

### Course Goals
This course is designed to help students think and work like real IT technicians by following a clear, step-by-step —starting from the physical hardware and moving up to how applications function. Instead of relying on memorization, students learn by doing: building systems, troubleshooting real issues, and understanding how problems at one level can affect everything else. Throughout the course, they develop practical skills in assembling and configuring computers, installing operating systems, connecting devices to networks, and solving technical problems in a structured and confident way. The course also introduces foundational cloud concepts—helping students understand how hardware connects to modern cloud environments and services—so they are prepared not only for CompTIA A+ certification but also for today’s evolving IT roles where on-premise systems and cloud infrastructure work together.

### Other information
This course is delivered through a structured blend of hands-on laboratories, guided simulations, lectures, and applied troubleshooting activities, all designed to develop practical, job-ready skills. Students participate in physical lab exercises, including non-powered PC assembly and disassembly, network cable construction, and the installation, configuration, and testing of Windows operating systems, software, and networks (wireless and wired). These activities reinforce essential real-world networking concepts. Virtualization platforms, such as Oracle VM VirtualBox and VMware Workstation Player, are utilized to support operating system installation and troubleshooting within controlled environments. Additionally, the course introduces foundational cloud computing concepts through platforms such as Amazon Web Services (AWS)/Microsoft Azure, helping bridge traditional hardware knowledge with modern infrastructure practices. A free-tier account is required to access and complete cloud-based activities.
### Course Schedule and Assignments
Week | Topics | Learning Activities
1 | IT Fundamentals and Safety | IT roles, lab safety, ESD, tools
2 | TCP/IP Concepts | IP addressing, protocols, and connectivity basics
3 | Construction and Testing of Cables | Build and test Ethernet cables
4 | Networking Fundamentals | Devices, Packet Tracer simulation, Switches, NICs, Cables
5 | Network Configuration and Connectivity | IP config, troubleshooting, Connecting Computers
6 | Computer Hardware Components | Identify internal components
7 | PC Assembly and Disassembly | Assemble/disassemble with checklist
8 | Storage Devices and File Systems | Install storage, partitions
9 | BIOS/UEFI and Boot Process | Boot sequence, troubleshooting
10 | Power, Peripherals, Cooling | PSU, devices, maintenance
11 | Windows Installation & Management | Install OS in VM, configure
12 | Troubleshooting, Virtualization, Cloud | Diagnostics, VM, cloud intro
