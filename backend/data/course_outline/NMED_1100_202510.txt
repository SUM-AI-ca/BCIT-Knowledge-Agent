## Course Description
This course builds on the concepts introduced in HSIP 1000, HSIP Patient Care to equip the student with specific knowledge and skills that are necessary for working in a nuclear medicine department and general hospital environment. Students will participate in a variety of simulations and hands-on learning activities that emphasize best practice and reinforce patient care theory. Students will be given opportunities to reflect on their nuclear medicine knowledge and skills during a variety of simulated patient care situations.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify appropriate infection prevention and control practices for a given scenario.
- Perform appropriate donning and doffing of personal protective equipment (PPE) for additional precautions using infection prevention and control practice.
- Describe the mechanisms of pharmacodynamics.
- Describe the phases of pharmacokinetics.
- Describe the major drug classifications.
- Investigate and respond to a simulated adverse reaction.
- Prepare a medication for the appropriate administration route.
- Perform venipuncture in a manner that promotes safety and patient comfort.
- Perform venipuncture using syringe, butterfly needle and 3-way stopcock apparatus.
- Safely handle and dispose of sharps and biohazardous materials according to universal precautions.
- Perform and determine a blood glucose level using a glucometer in a manner that promotes safety and comfort.
- Describe the common administration routes used in nuclear medicine procedures.
- Perform a simulated administration of a radiopharmaceutical using the appropriate route.
- Describe the precautions of specialized IV routes (e.g.-central lines) used for radiopharmaceutical administration.
- Perform a simulated IV catheter insertion in a manner that promotes safety and comfort.
- Access and maintain a saline lock in a manner that ensures patient safety.
- Assemble and maintain IV therapy equipment in a manner that ensures patient safety.
- Operate and maintain an electronic infusion device.
- Reflect on nuclear medicine skills and incorporate knowledge into practice.
