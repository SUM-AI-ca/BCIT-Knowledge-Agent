## Course Description
This intermediate course in small litho press operation stresses press maintenance, problem solving and troubleshooting techniques. Students’ skills are improved by printing more complex projects, projects consisting of more than one-piece, multi-coloured projects with proper registration and working in teams to produce a program newsletter. Students prepare specific ink colours and gain confidence by printing projects of their own creation and preparation from other classes. Classes are modular, self-paced, competency-based exercises in all four imaging technologies. Hands-on production skills are stressed in inkjet and digital imaging engine technology. Finally, students proceed to advanced bindery skills​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Print multi-coloured jobs with proper registration (fit and register).
- Cut suitable stock in an efficient manner for the best cut.
- Measure and cut stock to maximize best yield from a sheet.
- Describe the makeup of paper and ink.
- Use standard troubleshooting techniques for ink, plates, paper, and blankets.
- Maintain quality control throughout the press run.
- Clean and preserve a press and printing plates.
- Use standard press densitometry measurement techniques.
- Use an inkjet printer to calibrate a new substrate in a RIP.
- Use a digital imaging engine to duplex image both sides of a substrate.
- Troubleshoot media changes in a digital imaging RIP.
- Produce advanced folded pieces on the folding machine.​

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
The Graphic Communication program utilizes a unique print lab facility, with equipment donated by the generous business owners in our community. Students must always respect the equipment in the lab, and comply with the following rules:

- Loose hair or long beards must be tied back or contained in a hat or hair net.
- Remove jewelry, scarves, coats, or loose clothing.
- Gaming, videos, or loud music that can cause distraction are not permitted.
- Mobile devices are not permitted when working around machinery.
- No food or drink in the lab (closed water bottles are allowed at desks only).
- PPE is required to be worn when working with chemicals.
- No video recording or photography is permitted in the lab.

These rules are for the safety of all students using the equipment in the lab and are overseen at the discretion of all faculty. Failure to comply with lab rules will not be tolerated and may result punitive action including dismissal from class, formal warning, course failure, or withdrawal from the program.

Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class.
