## Course Description
​This course introduces principles of eukaryotic/prokaryotic molecular and cell biology to provide foundational knowledge & support future program courses. Topics include cell and genome organization, DNA structure and replication, NAT pre-analytical considerations​, and genotype-phenotype correlation. Lecture theory is supported by learning activities. Assessment is based on assignments, quizzes and exam.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- ​Describe the main distinguishing features of prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, and all internal structures of each cell type
- Explain each stage of the cell cycle in the correct order
- Describe the structure of nucleic acids and how this lends it to being the hereditary material
- Describe DNA replication and compare the process in vivo and in vitro
- Define genotype and phenotype, and explain their role in the different clinical laboratory areas
- Explain the advantages and limitations of NAAT and how they affect QA/QC
- Explain the pre-analytical considerations of NAAT and how they affect QA/QC
- Use the provided terminology correctly and accurately to facilitate​ effective communication
