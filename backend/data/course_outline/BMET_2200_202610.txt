### Course Description
Provides introduction to AC excited RLC circuits and the behavior of the basic active devices and integrated circuits. Topics include frequency response, RC, RL, RLC circuits, and resonant circuits, 3 phase systems, transformers, power suppliers, semiconductor fundamentals, diodes, bipolar transistor and field-effect transistor fundamentals, BJT and FET amplifier circuits, and the operational amplifier circuits. In Laboratory session, students will build a variety of circuits to confirm the theory and use the common test equipment to analyze and troubleshoot electronic circuits. Corequisites: MATH 2781.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Analyze the frequency response of RC, RL and RLC circuits.
- Apply the knowledge of resonance to analyze the series and parallel resonant circuits.
- Describe the operation of transformers.
- Examine the basic application of transformers.
- Describe the basic of three-phase voltages and currents in phasor forms.
- Analyze the three-phase circuits including the Wye and Delta three-phase generator and load connections.
- Analyze the operation of diode circuits.
- Analyze the basic DC power supply circuit.
- Describe the operating characteristics of transistors (BJT, JFET and MOSFET).
- Analyze DC bias circuit for transistors.
- Analyze single-stage transistor amplifier circuits, and multi-stage transistor amplifiers.
- Analyze the applications of operational amplifier and their operations.
- Analyze the frequency response of active filters.
- Use computer simulation software to simulate and test various electronic circuits.

### Course topics
BMET2200 course syllabus:
- L1- BMET 1100 Review Course Introduction
- L2- Resonance frequency response of series and parallel circuit, selectivity curve, bandwidth, quality factor and cut-off frequency
- L3- Frequency response, transfer function, passive filters
- L4- Transformers: fundamental, voltage, current and power ratio, reflected impedance and impedance matching, applications
- L5- Semiconductor Fundamentals, pn junction, diodes
- L6-Diode Applications, half-wave and full-wave rectifiers, clippers, clampers, special diodes
- L7- Bipolar Junction Transistor (BJT), structure and fundamentals
- L8- BJT amplifiers, CE, CC, CB amplifiers
- L9- Metal Oxide Semiconductor Transistor (MOSFET), structure and fundamentals, CS, CD, CG amplifiers
- L10- Op-Amp Fundamentals, inverting, noninverting, summing and difference amplifiers, active filters, integrators and differentiators,
- L11- Single-phase and three-phase systems
- L12- Course Review
