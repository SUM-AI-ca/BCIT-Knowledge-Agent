## Course Description
This course introduces students to the basic concepts of the project management process: planning, organizing, leading and controlling in preparation for their fourth term projects (GTTM 4411). Students are required to conduct a management consulting project. Students must demonstrate learned skills within set Terms of Reference that are mutually developed between the students, faculty advisors and the host organization.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Identify those activities which are required to successfully complete a project.
- Analyze and evaluate business situations involving management issues and develop appropriate responses to improve the situation by:
- Researching and interpreting business situations
- Applying commonly used models and methodologies for analysis
- Developing appropriate alternatives and solutions
- Develop an effective project plan
- Use the basics of Gantt charts, timelines, project software in the planning, tracking and execution of projects
- Design, prepare and negotiate a Terms of Reference.
- Deliver innovative oral/visual presentation of information using multimedia techniques approaches on an organization
