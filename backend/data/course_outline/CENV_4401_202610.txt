### Course Description
This course consists of two week full-time practicums at participating companies which will provide students with the opportunity to work in industries related to chemical and environmental technology. Potential industries include: assaying, laboratory analysis, chemical processing, environmental consulting and testing, material sciences, metallurgy, mineral processing, research and development, technical sales and waste treatment. In the unlikely event that a suitable industry placement cannot be made for a student, the student will be given the opportunity to undertake an equivalent assignment under the direction of a faculty member.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Have working experience in industries related to their studies at BCIT.
- Relate knowledge and skills learned at school to how these are applied in industry.
- Gain an insight into how works are performed in industry through work shadowing, which is useful for focus on study goal and building work habits.
- Plan for gaining employment after graduation, with an improved awareness of what employers want and expect of employees.
- Establish contacts for potential future employment opportunities.
- Demonstrate confidence in future employment interviews through real hands-on work experience.
- Enhance their job skills and knowledge.
- Carry out assigned tasks more efficiently and confidently at school, based on exposure to priority setting and timelines in a real work environment.
- Recognize the importance of effective communication in a team environment.
