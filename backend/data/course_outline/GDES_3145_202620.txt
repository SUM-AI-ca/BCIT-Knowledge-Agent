### Course Description
​This advanced typography course builds on foundational typographic principles, focusing on the application of typography in both print and digital media. Students will engage in creative problem-solving to develop compelling typographic solutions that ensure visual continuity, legibility, and impact. The course covers flexible and adaptive typography techniques and emphasizes creating accessible and inclusive designs. Students will apply advanced typography skills using vector and layout software to design cohesive typographic systems, translate complex information into engaging visual artifacts, and develop expressive compositions that challenge typographic conventions. Through practical assignments and projects, students will refine their ability to create typographic solutions tailored to specific industries and target markets. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Create compelling typographic solutions for print and interactive media projects.
- Determine proper typeface treatments to match specific industries and target markets.
- Explain and implement visual continuity in typographic designs to ensure impact, legibility, and readability.
- Describe and apply the psychological effects of typography to enhance user experience.
- Develop expressive compositions that challenge and expand typographic conventions.
- Use type to translate complex information into engaging visual artifacts.
- Implement advanced typography techniques, including flexible and adaptive typography.
- Evaluate and apply typography solutions that enhance accessibility and inclusivity for diverse users.
- Design and apply cohesive typographic systems across multiple media and formats.
- Demonstrate advanced proficiency working with vector and layout software for creating and manipulating typographic elements.
