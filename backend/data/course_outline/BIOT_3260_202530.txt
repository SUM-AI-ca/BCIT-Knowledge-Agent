### Course Description
(UBC BIOT 207) This course will compare physiological processes in different animals, including cellular and membrane dynamics, nervous and endocrine system responses, muscular contractions, circulatory and respiratory functions, and ion and water balance. Physiological data acquisition technology and other techniques will be used to measure physiological responses in various animal species.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify animal tissues and organs and understand their functions.
- Understand and measure the following processes:
- Basic cellular and membrane dynamics.
- Nervous and endocrine system responses.
- Muscle contractions.
- Circulatory and respiratory functions.
- Ion and water balance.
- Master the use of physiological data acquisition systems (such as BIOPAC).
- Dissect invertebrate animals and manipulate their physiology.
- Analyze and present physiological data in a concise written format.

Successful completion of both the lecture component and the lab component (50% grade in each) of this course is required for a passing grade. Section 3.4 of BCIT policy 5103-PR1 states that when one component is successfully completed and the other is not, but the average mark of the two components is a passing grade; a failing grade of U (unsatisfactory) is awarded.
Assignment Details
- Assignments and lab reports will be due on assigned dates
- Assignments/lab reports must be submitted by the indicated due date or a penalty of 10% per day (24 hour) will be deducted from the assignment grade
- Assignments must be done on an individual basis unless otherwise specified by the instructor.
- To submit the lab report, a student must participate in 80% of the lab periods associated with the lab report. In the case of illness where a medical note is provided, lab participation marks will not be lost for those missed lab period(s) and will not be counted towards the 80% participation marks required to submit a lab report or assignment. Instructors are not able to re-schedule missed lab periods.
### Attendance Requirements
Students are expected to attend all lecture and lab sessions unless you have a serious illness or other medical condition. In the Biotechnology Program, laboratory attendance is of the highest priority since lab experience is what sets this program apart from other undergraduate programs and what prepares students for co-op work terms. Furthermore, lab work is typically done in pairs and students are therefore expected to be present to support their partners. Students must attend all laboratory sessions.
Please notify your instructor and the Biotechnology program head of any absences.
If exams, quizzes, or in-class assignments are missed for reasons of illness, medical notes will be required to reschedule.
### Safety equipment or protective clothing
Lab coat required for some labs. Disposable gloves will be provided.
