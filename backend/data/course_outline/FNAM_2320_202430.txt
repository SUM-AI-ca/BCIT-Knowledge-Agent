### Course Description
​Students learn to identify species of fish and wildlife occurring in British Columbia. Students will be introduced to their life cycles and habitat requirements. This course introduces common fish and wildlife identification techniques. Students will identify the different government ministries and departments responsible for the management of fish and wildlife species and the respective legislation. Students will be able to describe the main management strategies and recognize major threats to fish and wildlife habitat.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the main freshwater and anadromous fish species occurring in British Columbia
- Identify the main wildlife species occurring in British Columbia (Avian, mammal, reptile and amphibian)
- Describe the life cycles and habitat requirements of the main fish and wildlife species occurring in British Columbia.
- Describe the habitat management strategies for the main fish and wildlife species occurring in British Columbia.
- Recognize major threats to fish and wildlife habitat.
- Describe sampling design components for: Presence/Not detected, Relative abundance, & Absolute Abundance.
- Describe common RISC sampling techniques used to quantify fish and wildlife
- Identify the different government ministries/departments responsible for fish and wildlife
- Access information on legislation and management strategies related to fish and wildlife species
- Recognize the importance of SARA legislation in land/habitat management
- Recognize the risks and precautions while working in wildlife habitat​
### Learning Resources
References include the following:
- Species Identification Guide Books (Birds, Mammals, Fish, Amphbians and Reptiles)
- Provincial RISC Guides and Manuals

- SARA Regulations and Species Lists

### Safety equipment or protective clothing
Field Gear for field labs as directed: As several labs will be in forested and stream conditions you will require the following:
- Cruiser Vest, hard hat, compass, clinometre, diametre tape, related field note books and supplies
