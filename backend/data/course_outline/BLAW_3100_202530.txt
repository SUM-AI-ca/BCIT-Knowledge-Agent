## Course Description
Presents a practical study of Canadian business law, including the legal and administrative systems, torts, contracts, sale of goods and consumer protection, employment, business organizations, and intellectual property. Participation in this course, taught by lawyers, prepares you to recognize and feel comfortable with the legal aspects of doing business.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Describe and demonstrate a basic knowledge of the law and recognize legal issues as they arise.
- Describe and demonstrate a general understanding of business law as it applies to business relationships and transactions.
- Apply the rules of law to business situations and limit their potential legal exposure.
- Describe and demonstrate how to engage in lawful business practices and transactions.
- Handle data and related new technologies in a responsible manner by complying with digital ethics.
- Communicate effectively with lawyers and regulators.

## Course Specific Requirements
Activities for Marks: Late submission / completion of activities for marks will not be acceptable, and assigned a zero mark. Activities for Marks must be done on an individual basis unless otherwise specified by the instructor.

Makeup Tests, Exams or Quizzes: There will be no makeup tests or exams. If you miss a test or exam, you will receive zero marks. Exceptions may be made for documented medical reasons or extenuating circumstances. In such a case, it is the responsibility of the student to inform the instructor immediately.

Passing Grade: In order to pass the course, students require a passing grade (50%) on the weighted average of all Evaluation Criteria.

Learning Hub (LH): Learning Hub is the platform for this course. All announcements and materials related to the lectures will be made available on LH.

Conduct in Classes: Please confine private conversations to breaks. Please refrain from texting, internet browsing, using social media or emailing in class. If you want to communicate with someone other than the instructor, feel free to leave the classroom and return when you are done. If you want to ask or answer a question or speak to the instructor or the class, kindly raise your hand.

Ethics: BCIT assumes that all students attending the Institute will follow a high standard of ethics. Incidents of cheating or plagiarism may therefore result in a grade of zero for the assignment, quiz, test, exam, or project for all parties involved and/or expulsion from the course.

I.D. Required for Exams: In order to write exams, students are required to produce photo identification at the beginning of the exam. Students should have a BCIT OneCard or, alternatively, a government photo I.D. such as a driver’s licence or passport.

Feedback: If you have comments in relation to your instructor or the quality of the curriculum, you are encouraged to communicate them directly to your instructor.
