## Course Description
This course focuses on effective targeted writing for new media applications. Through research, discussion and practice students will create, and prepare content in writing for cross-platform deployment: print, web and mobile. Students will explore how people read screen text in contrast to printed text, view examples of high and low-impact content, and practice adapting traditional print media to new media publications. Students will critically evaluate context, content and audiences in order to define the accuracy, relevancy and effectiveness of written communications. During the course, students will be evaluated through different means: written exam, assignments and projects.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify and apply the basic principles of effective new media writing.
- Apply research techniques to gather textual and visual content for new media applications.
- Employ usability standards for writing new media content.
- Compare and adapt traditional print media for new media applications.
- Work in teams and apply peer and self-evaluations.
- Prepare and utilize visual presentation techniques to persuade an audience.
- Use appropriate procedures and formats for documenting research sources.
- Communicate as an effective team member during internal and external presentations.
- Integrate critical thinking to quantify and qualify information before it is made public.

## Course Specific Requirements
Professionalism: This is determined by: regular attendance, punctuality, a collegial attitude, participation in class exercises and discussions and the ability to meet deadlines and follow instructions. Staying off social media, not working on other projects unless you have finished assignments for this course and have shown them to me, and paying attention during online class is expected.

Students are required to read a variety of websites and to analyze them critically for effectiveness, credibility, copywriting and SEO techniques, and usage of hypertext and multimedia. Accurate research, grammar and spelling are required on all writing assignments.

Assignments: Material sourced from the Internet for assignments must be linked to or properly referenced. Failure to do so could result in a zero grade for plagiarism. You are expected to write assignments IN YOUR OWN WORDS. If you use AI, ChatGPT, Grammarly or any other editing platform, you must cite your sources either in the form of links or MLA-style citations. You will lose marks if you do not do this. If you cannot show me your work, or adequately explain your thought processes, you may receive a zero grade.

Late assignments will not be accepted unless you've made prior arrangements with the instructor. Handing work in on time is a foundation of professionalism and you are professionals in training.

Post assignments in the appropriate Dropbox on D2L before the time stamp at 11:59 the day before class. Assignments posted on D2L must be posted as Pdfs or zipped files and can only be ONE Pdf file. Please do not zip ONE document unless it exceeds 20 MB. If you do, it will not be graded. Course resources and news will be posted on the D2L.
