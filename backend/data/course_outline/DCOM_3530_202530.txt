## Course Description
​In this course, students will explore microwave theory concepts and device applications. The applications of microwave communications within the navigation industry are examined. As the course focuses on the integration of basic land and marine navigation systems in use today, navigation principles , navigational terms, Global Positioning System (GPS) and associated technologies will be covered. Marine navigational information systems will be examined, including Automatic Identification System (AIS), Global Maritime Distress Safety System (GMDSS), and search and rescue (SARS) systems. Tracking and location systems such as Automatic Vehicle Location (AVL) will be investigated.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Explain how microwave devices operate and their applications.
- Discuss the creation/propagation of microwave frequencies used in point-to-point and SATCOM RF links.
- Explain the operating principles of waveguide, dish antennas, microwave devices and microwave transceivers.
- Perform a point-to-point microwave link analysis and link budget.
- Explain basic theory behind radar transceivers and safety concerns.
- Explain the operation of pulsed radar systems and tracking displays.
- Define the basic terms used in navigation.
- Discuss the operational principles of marine navigational systems.
- Explain the operations of the satellites, ground stations and subscriber units in the GPS system.
- Explain the operation of tracking and location systems used on land and at sea.
- Explain the operations of the maritime GDMSS emergency response system and SAR location technologies.​
