### Course Description
​This course discusses the environmental, social, and economic perspectives related to recreation, tourism, and stewardship of protected areas in British Columbia. Students will examine policies governing recreation and parks and protected areas and discuss examples of Indigenous stewardship principles in Indigenous Protected and Conserved Areas. Students will also examine sustainable growth of recreation and tourism through ecotourism and discuss how protected areas interact with industry.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- ​Identify critical gear and equipment used in recreational activities in BC and describe how to travel safely and comfortably in the backcountry.
- Describe key recreation activities in BC and assess their environmental, social, and economic value and impact.
- Identify examples of parks and protected areas at each level of government and explain the policies and regulations in place to manage the area.
- Assess the success of a stakeholder engagement process for a recreation and/or conservation decision.
- Explain how natural resource management issues influence stewardship decisions in protected areas and emerging trends facing decision‐makers.
- Demonstrate respectful and professional behaviour that meets industry standards and expectations.
