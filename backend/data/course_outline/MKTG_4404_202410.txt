## Course Description
Provides field work experience in the sales force of a sponsoring firm. Full evaluation of the on-the-job performance is included along with a student report on the experience.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop effective public speaking skills by delivering oral presentations (individual and team) to audiences of diverse sizes.
- Have practical experience in a sales and marketing environment.
- Be able to understand corporate culture.
- Be able to apply sales and marketing skills to assist an organization.
- Be able to make industry contacts.

## Learning Resources
Any learning resources will be supplied by the practicum sponsor. There is no textbook for this course.

## Course Goals
The primary goal for this course is to enable the student to apply what they have learned in this program to the real world of professional selling.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Submit the completed and signed Industry Practicum Project Approval form by email to your practicum advisor. Also email your advisor stating your learning objectives (at least 3) for your practicum – these must be referred to in your final report. Due: Mar. 6th by 5pm.

March 21: First day of your practicum.

Practicum Progress Report. Review practicum progress with your sponsor, write a 1-page summary of your work and responsibilities to date, have it signed, and submit it to the Learning Hub folder. Due: April 26th by 5pm.

May 17: Final day of your practicum.

Company Report. This is to be a professionally written report that includes the following components and submitted to Learning Hub:

•Executive Summary – an overview with key learning outcomes

•Company Profile – describe your company including history,products/services, markets, customers, competitors, uniqueselling proposition, and mission statement/core values

•Sales Force – a description of the sales team, compensationstructure, training programs, and an organizational chart withsales and marketing management and all sales personnel

•Sales Audit – describe the steps in the sales process and salestactics you observed – also provide three (3) suggestions ofhow this company could improve their sales efforts

•Practicum Activity – describe your role and responsibilities atthe company and what you accomplished (your Daily Journal,as well as reports, etc. should be in the Appendix)

•A copy of a thank you letter (or screen shot of an email) youwill send to your practicum sponsor

•10% will be deducted for each day past deadline

Due: May 21st by 5pm.

Daily Journals. A detailed description of your daily activities (include in the Appendix of your Company Report). Due: May 21st by 5pm.

Sponsor Evaluation. During your final week of practicum ensure your sponsor completes the Sponsor Evaluation form (in Learning Hub) and emails it to the practicum advisor by the due date. Due: May 21st by 5pm.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class.
