## Course Description
​This course is designed for students with no prior knowledge of Spanish. Students will develop the four basic language skills (listening, speaking, reading and writing), gain knowledge of Hispanic cultures, and learn how culture impacts business practices. Through interactive classroom activities and utilizing Spanish first-language multi-media materials, students will learn business terminology and the professional communication skills needed to handle business situations. Students will demonstrate their understanding of the Hispanic business environments through a range of individual and group activities, assignments and examinations.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Apply appropriate phonetic rules and patterns to correctly pronounce Spanish words and sentences.
- Construct and comprehend simple sentences, using basic business vocabulary.
- Read and write basic business correspondence in Spanish.
- Analyze the cultural diversity of the Hispanic world and the aspects that characterize the business environments.
- Compare the similarities and differences between the Spanish-speaking cultures and their own.
- Interact effectively in real-life business scenarios in a culturally appropriate manner with native speakers.
- Develop strategies to enhance life-long learning of other languages and cultures by reflecting on the learning process through a learning portfolio.

## Course Specific Requirements
Participation: Active participation is essential for achieving the course objectives. You must thoroughly study all assigned materials before each meeting to ensure effective engagement during class. Active involvement in classroom activities is also required and will impact your participation grade. To earn the full participation mark each week, you must submit a weekly learning portfolio after the in-person class. Online participation will be evaluated based on your contributions to the forums, particularly, posting a response to the weekly thread and responding to other posts. Simply logging into the forum does not equate to meaningful participation.

Assignments: Late assignments will be penalized 1 point per day and will not be accepted after 5 calendar days past the due date. However, in exceptional circumstances, some flexibility may be considered if the instructor is informed of the situation before the assignment's due date.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media

Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class.
