### Course Description
This course builds on the fundamental knowledge and skills learned in BCST 1120, Television and Video Production 1. This course provides participants with advanced knowledge of the components of television and video production and how they interrelate. Upon completion of BCST 2120, students will have an intermediate working knowledge of professional broadcast equipment such as cameras, switchers, microphones, audio mixers, and media servers. Through several projects, students will learn studio and field audio acquisition and lighting of studio and field productions, including scenes/sets and products, and electronic news gathering/electronic field production equipment. Students will learn to plan and execute both live productions and electronic field productions. Students will operate portable, studio, and control room production equipment.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Execute a short video field production under direct supervision within a production team environment.
- Independently plan and execute short video field productions to visually tell a story.
- Set up, troubleshoot, and use professional broadcast video and audio equipment, with limited supervision in a television studio or on location.
- Use basic lighting equipment to accomplish a desired lighting of a scene or product, with limited supervision.
- Operate BCIT’s field production equipment to industry entry-level standards.
- Commit to a team and share responsibility for completion of team projects, demonstrating strong interpersonal communication skills.
- Collaboratively plan and execute short video field productions to visually tell a story.

### Course Specific Requirements
Instructors will post assignment sheets on the BCIT Learning Hub outlining the objectives, deliverables, and due dates for both individual and group assignments.
These sheets provide the overall framework for each assignment but are not intended to answer every possible question that may come up.
It is the responsibility of students and production groups to contact instructors whenever additional details or clarification are needed, and to regularly monitor the Learning Hub for updates on upcoming assignment and quiz deadlines.
Attention to detail, and accurate, complete adherence to instructions are essential parts of the learning experience in this program. Each assignment includes specific delivery requirements on its assignment sheet. Failure to follow these instructions may result in a deduction of marks, up to and including a grade of 0% for the submitted component.
There will be no makeup tests, exams or quizzes. If you miss a test, exam or quiz, you will receive zero marks. Exceptions may be made for documented medical reasons or extenuating circumstances. In such a case, it is the responsibility of the student to inform the instructors immediately.
Except where otherwise noted all material for all assignments must be new as of the assignment date, originally generated and created independently by the student(s) for the purposes of meeting learning outcomes. Student submissions may be critiqued publically in lectures or labs, and / or posted online for training or promotional purposes.
Except where otherwise noted, all projects and assignments must be completed students on an individual basis.
### Other information
BCIT has an institute policy of no weapons (firearms, edged weapons, explosives, or replicas thereof, etc). Any students who produce or submit any project or assignment materials which violate this policy will be assessed a failing grade of 0% on those projects or assignments. The only exception will be for students who have obtained written permission a minimum of 6 business days in advance of the production date from both BCIT Safety and Security, and the Television Broadcast Faculty.
The material or schedule specified in this course outline may be changed by the instructor. If changes are required, they will be announced in class and/or communicated in writing.
To ensure a timely response, students must copy all correspondence to all instructional staff associated with the course (2 faculty + AI) when seeking information or clarification on course projects, assignments, or materials. Where a request for special access to equipment or facilities is necessary, it should be emailed to the faculty a minimum of 7 days in advance.
IMPORTANT : A lack of familiarity with the regulations cannot be used as a defence by those found to have violated academic integrity policy. It is the student's responsibility to become familiar with the rules.
