## Course Description
This course is a basic introduction to the fundamental concepts of engineering economics related specifically to the building industry. It covers various financial topics such as interest rates, the time value of money, financial decision-making, and capital budgeting. The course also presents the concept of life-cycle cost analysis and the basic principles of market supply-and-demand.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:

- Use simple and compound interest rates to calculate the present value and future value of various cash flows.
- Prepare cash flow diagrams.
- Calculate payments and annuities.
- Distinguish between different types of interest rates and calculate equivalent interest rates.
- Identify basic project financing requirements.
- Describe the concept of life-cycle cost analysis as an alternative approach for determining return on investment for green buildings.
- Prepare project pro-forma statements.
- Identify the basic principles of market supply-and-demand with a focus on land development.
