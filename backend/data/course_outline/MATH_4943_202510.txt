## Course Description
A continuation of MATH 3942. Topics include binary and other number systems; mean and standard deviation; functions and graphs; the straight line; slope and distance; solving inequalities and systems of equations; factoring; solving quadratic equations; solving mixture, investment and other word problems; radians; right-angle trigonometry; oblique trigonometry.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Understand the binary number system and be able to convert between binary and decimal.
- Calculate weighted average, mean and standard deviation, from a given set of data.
- Summarize and display data in a graph, using Excel.
- Find and plot the equation of a straight line given two points or one point and a slope; describe various properties of a straight line.
- Given two points on a graph, calculate the distance between them.
- Appreciate mathematical relations and functions as they apply to technology.
- Solve systems of equations in two variables, graphically and algebraically.
- Understand the rudiments of inequality systems and performance envelopes.
- Solve various problems using quadratic equations as they occur in technology and trades.
- Understand properties of angles; change angle units.
- Solve various right angle triangle problems, including applications.
- Solve various oblique triangle situations from technology.
- Solve various force problems using vectors.

## Statement for prior learning assessment
Applications for course credit or course exemption on the basis of previously completed mathematics courses are assessed on a case-by-case basis by the BCIT Mathematics Dept. taking into account all of the following:

- the correspondence between topics, content and level
- recency (generally no more than 3-5 years)
- the grade (generally at least a C+ or 65%)
- the context (course taken as part of a university or college science or engineering program, rather than, for example, an arts or social science program).

Students wishing to apply for course credit can pick up a Course Credit/Exemption Application form from Student Information and Enrolment Services (SW1-1300). This form must be submitted to Student Information and Enrolment Services within approximately 2 weeks of the beginning of term. Until students know that they have been granted course credit, they should continue to attend classes and work on their assignments.
