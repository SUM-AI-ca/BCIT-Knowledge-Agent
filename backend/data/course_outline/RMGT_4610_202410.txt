## Course Description
This course will develop essential skills for an entry level underwriter. Topic coverage begins with an overview of the underwriter's role as an investor of shareholder capital on behalf of the insurer. Students will learn how that role has evolved and how the underwriter accepts or rejects risk for the insurer within the parameters set by the insurer and imposed by the external environment. Students will consider the types of detailed knowledge, the "hard" and "soft" skills, and the temperament an underwriter needs to succeed. Students will see these characteristics applied in the analysis of individual property, liability, and automobile risks.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Describe the nature of underwriting, the underwriting process; and the tools of an underwriter.
- Discuss the underwriting of risk, including the distinction between pure and speculative risk; the legal principle of contra proferentem, and the significance of frequency and severity in loss analysis.
- Discuss the analysis of insurance, including the need to properly identify the applicant; the characteristics of the applicant; the effective and expiry dates of the applicant's current insurance; the applicant's loss experience; and any prior cancellations or premium discounts.
- Describe risk underwriting in property, liability, and automobile insurance.
- Explain how factors influencing price for various insurance risks.
- Describe an underwriter's internal and external environments; the influences of centralized and decentralized insurers on underwriting authority and practice; the impact on underwriters of technology, privacy concerns, social trends, judicial decisions, and government regulation.
- Discuss the characteristics of the underwriting temperament, the importance of communication skills, and factors that affect communication, including barriers to communication, methods of communication, the potential for conflict, and diversity.
- Perform basic underwriter functions.

- Understand the role of the underwriter in an insurance company

- Explain why its important for the underwriter to etablish good relationships with insurance professionals both inside andoutside the insurance company.
