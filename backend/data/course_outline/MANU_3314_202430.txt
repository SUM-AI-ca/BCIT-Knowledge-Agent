## Course Description
Examines the design of jigs, workholding fixtures, and the use of standard tooling components. 3D CAD modelling software and tool design libraries will be used.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Specify manufacturing processes capable of producing fits, tolerances and surface finishes required for a design.
- Use geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) to dimension component drawings.
- Apply the 3-2-1 principle for part location in a fixture.
- Select appropriate part holding devices for fixtures.
- Design work holding devices for milling and turning.
- Describe simple bending and forming dies.

## Course topics
1. INTRODUCTION TO TOOL DESIGN

- Describe the role of the tool designer.
- Discuss the objectives of tool design.
- Discuss tool design requirements.

2. PRINCIPLES OF LOCATION AND LOCATING DEVICES

- Analyze parts for confining the 6 degrees of freedom
- Utilize the 3-2-1 principle for part location.
- Describe various locating devices.
- Utilize Foolproofing techniques

3. PRINCIPLES OF CLAMPING AND CLAMPING DEVICES

- Describe clamping devices.
- Select appropriate holding devices
- Discuss workholding devices for milling, drilling and turning processes

4. TOOL MATERIALS

- Analyze the requirements of materials used for tooling.
- Describe physical and mechanical properties of tool materials.
- Describe typical uses of ferrous and non-ferrous materials in tool design

5. GEOMETRIC DIMENSIONING AND TOLERANCING

- Introduce Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GDT) and the importance of Datums

6. REVIEW BASIC CONSTRUCTION PRINCIPLES AND COMPONENTS

- Discuss various fasteners, pins and other hardware used in tooling.

7. JIG DESIGN

- Analyze and plan typical jig applications.
- Layout and design a drill jig.
- Select drill jig bushings.

8. FIXTURE DESIGN

- Analyze and plan typical fixture application.
- Layout and design a milling fixture.
- Layout and design a lathe fixture.

9. TOOLING USED IN PLASTIC PART MANUFACTURE

- Analyze thermoforming molds
- Describe and analyze injection molds
- Layout and design an injection mold
