## Course Description
Cardiovascular Perfusion Clinical 4 is a continuation of PERF 8300. The students are expected to improve upon the level of competency gained in previous clinical courses. In this clinical course, students will continue to review and apply the objectives introduced in previous rotations, as well as prepare and apply advanced Perfusion techniques and technologies. Review of CPB case studies will assist the students in understanding various clinical scenarios and conditions that have presented in the past and how they were managed.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Display the safe application of objectives from PERF 8100, PERF 8200 and PERF 8300.
- Describe the indications/evaluations of patients for Heart Transplantation.
- Perform effective and safe management of CPB case during Heart Transplantation.
- Describe indications/case setup and management of Lung and Liver Transplant cases.
- Describe the indications and clinical differences between; Veno-venous Extra Corporeal Oxygenation (VV ECMO), Extracorporeal Life Support (ECLS), Veno-Arterial Extra Corporeal Oxygenation (VA) ECMO, Ventricular Assist Device (VAD).
- Explain ECMO objectives from didactic Year 1.
- Prepare and prime ECMO circuit.
- Assess potential VV ECMO, ECLS, VA ECMO patients and safely prepare associated circuitry and pumps.
- Review the various cannulation and anticoagulation techniques for: VV ECMO, ECLS, VA ECMO.
- Safely perform initiation, maintenance and weaning from ECMO.
- Review indications, contraindications, setup and operation for the various ventricular assist devices (VADS), implantable artificial hearts, and implantable cardiac assist devices.
- Describe physiologic principles involved in the use of VADS.
- Describe and demonstrate safe transport techniques for patients requiring extracorporeal support.
- Describe indications, use and processing techniques for Platelet Gel Therapy
- Describe indications and techniques for Off Pump Coronary Artery Bypass (OPCAB) surgery.
- Describe the setup of Cardiac Robotic cases.
- Prepare or describe the CPB pump and circuit for minimally invasive cardiac surgeries.
- Describe and assist in the cannulation for minimally invasive CPB.
- Evaluate CPB case studies, and correlate clinical history and physiological findings with case management.
- Participate in the evaluation of new equipment and techniques.
- Communicate effectively during the procedure with all members of the health care team.
- Perform post-op CPB evaluation and, reflect on individual performance and set goals for improvement.

## Attendance Requirements
Sick days: A maximum of 5 absences, due to illness is permissible over the duration of all clinical rotations. A physician's note is required when 3 consecutive days are missed.

Special Leave Days: A maximum of 3 special leave days are available over the duration of all clinical rotations. Special leave days require advanced approval from the BCIT Clinical Instructor.

All absences will be submitted via Comptracker to the Clinical Educator and monitored by the program.

Note: Unused sick time or special leave is not considered as owing to the student.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
Students are expected to don all required personal protective equipment (PPE) to maintain safety within the clinical environment and follow departmental safety precautions and policies.

In situations where a student consistently demonstrates unsafe practice in the perfusion lab or while at the clinical site, a Required to Discontinue (RTD) grade will be issued to the student and they will be asked to leave the program effective immediately.
