## Course Description
​In this course, students will be placed into teams to mirror the real life, deadline oriented, team-based environment of work in industry. Students will work on group projects that are focused on different aspects of the 3D industry and create content to support those projects. Students will identify their strengths and areas of interest and will choose the tasks that they want to work on, allowing them to exercise the specific skills that they want to develop. Students must submit their work for weekly review and take note of any revisions that their instructor identifies. These notes should be addressed and implemented in future submissions. Students will be assessed based on the planning and revision of the work done throughout the course.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify their strongest skillset.
- Apply their strengths while working in a team environment.
- Communicate with team members to recognise the project deliverables.
- Deliver on time completed components as required by their team role.
- Apply advanced modeling techniques towards creating 3D characters and objects for game, animation or visual effects project.
- Apply advanced animation methods towards creating character and prop animation for their project.
- Implement feedback, test and retest as needed.
- Present a final project as a team member to an audience.

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance will be taken at the start of class and can be taken again in middle of the class. Students who have not made previous arrangements with their instructors and are not present during attendance check will be given an unexcused absence mark. The instructor also hold the right to give students an unexcused absence mark, if the student fails to meet certain requirements such as bringing required assignment progress, or required equipment for the day.

Students who arrive 15 minutes after the start of class will be marked as late, and 2 late marks will be equivalent to 1 unexcused absence.

Students who have informed their instructors of their absence will be given an excused absence mark, at the discretion of the instructor.

Students are only allowed to have 2 unexcused absences or 3 excused absences for the term, and exceeding this number would result in a V grade (0 GPA) for the course.

All this is implemented for this class on top of the BCIT Attendance Policy detailed at the end of this course outline.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: As set out in BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations, regular attendance in lectures and labs is integral to student success. Therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Students are responsible for communicating with instructors proactively about both potentially approved and unexcused absences. It is the student’s responsibility to work with their instructor to find an effective means to complete work missed due to both approved and unexcused absences.
