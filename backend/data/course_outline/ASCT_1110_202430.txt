## Course Description
ASCT 1110 covers the relationship between applied loads and the resultant support reactions and internal forces developed in statically determinate members and stuructures.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Define types of forces and force systems.
- Graphically and Mathematically solve Concurrent Coplanar Force Systems.
- Calculate Moment of a force for Concentrated and Distributed Forces.
- Produce Shear and Moment Diagrams.
- List the conditions of static equilibrium.
- Construct the Free Body Diagram.
- Solve unknown forces in Static Equilibrium.
- Determine tributary areas for slabs, beams and columns.
- Analyze truss, pulley systems, machines and frames, built-up members.
- Determine section properties: Moment of Inertia, Section Modulus, Radius of Gyration, Center of Gravity, Centroid of a Shape.
