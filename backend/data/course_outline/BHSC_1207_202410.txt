## Course Description
This course presents information on the structure and function of human organ systems under normal conditions and in response to challenges from the environment. Specifically we explore ways in which organ systems respond to challenges in various work environments. Structure and function of lungs, skin, liver, kidneys, endocrine organs, ears and eyes are discussed, and potential occupational hazards impacting these organs and related systems are identified. Finally we discuss abnormal function of these organs arising from their interactions with specific hazards.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Communicate effectively with other health team members in terms of human anatomy and physiology.
- Apply anatomical and physiological principles to the Occupational Health and Safety field.
- Apply appropriate directional and regional anatomical terminology to the description of body parts, body cavities, and organs.
- Relate the functions of body systems to the common goal of homeostasis.
- Describe the four basic tissue types found in organs of the body (epithelia, connective, muscle, nervous tissues) and identify their relative vulnerability to injurious agents.
- Describe the gross and microscopic structure of the skin and explain the skin's roles in protecting underlying structures and in temperature regulation.
- Describe normal structure and functions of special sense organs (eyes and ears) and explain their vulnerability to occupational hazards.
- Explain the normal role of the endocrine system in the maintenance of homeostasis and predict the consequences of endocrine disruption on body function.
- Describe the normal structure and functioning of the lungs and respiratory airways and discuss the consequences of abnormal respiratory function.
- Describe the normal structure of the liver and explain its role in detoxification.
- Describe the structure of the urinary system and explain how the kidneys function in excretion of wastes and regulation of fluids and electrolytes.

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Use the appropriate anatomical terminology to describe body parts and regions, body cavities and organ systemswhen communicating with other health team members.
- Describe the integumentary system, its role in protecting underlying structures and regulating body temperature.
- Explain the structure of the musculoskeletal system and its vulnerability to workplace injuries
- Summarize the vulnerability of the various structures of the respiratory system to environmental hazards
- Predict the effect of environmental toxins on the normal structure and function of the liver
- Summarize the role of the kidneys in the regulation of fluids and electrolytes and the excretion of wastes andtoxins
- Recognize the vulnerability of the structures of the eye and the ear to occupational hazards
- Summarize the role of the endocrine organs of the body in the maintenance of homeostasis
- Predict the effect of endocrine disruptors on the homeostatic regulatory mechanisms designed to regulate normalbody function
