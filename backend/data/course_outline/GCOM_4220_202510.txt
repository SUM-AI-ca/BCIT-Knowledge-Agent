## Course Description
Specialty print finishing is where packaging moves from a simple necessity into eye-catching marketing sensations. This course introduces students to the world of premium print finishing, including die cutting, laser cutting, foil stamping, embossing, and film lamination. Through a combination of hands-on learning, industry tours, and guest speakers, students will gain a comprehensive understanding about producing award winning packaging. Students will gain a comprehensive knowledge of industry suppliers, machinery, materials, and workflows.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Define common finishing terminology.
- Determine the characteristics and manufacturing considerations for industry-standard specialty finishing equipment.
- Determine the workflow and scheduling requirements of projects including specialty finishing.
- Specify steel rule die tooling.
- Describe the interaction between papers, inks, tooling, heat and pressure and the limitations this has on design.
- Articulate the differences between hot, cold, and digital foil applications and their suitable uses.
- Differentiate between different types of hot foil and emboss die tooling and their applications.
- Describe the considerations of foil material releases and their applications on different substrates.
- Determine appropriate depth for emboss/deboss tooling based on design and material selections.
- Differentiate between different film lamination substrates and their interactions with a variety of papers and inks.
- Operate film laminator machinery.
- Perform basic maintenance on film laminator machinery.
- Apply foil decoration on a range of media using digital foil machine.
- Explain the applications of laser cutting and its limitations and challenges. Identify common mistakes in design and prepress and their impact on specialty finishing projects.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
The Graphic Communications program utilizes a unique print lab facility, with equipment donated by the generous business owners in our community. Students must always respect the equipment in the lab, and comply with the following rules:

- Loose hair or long beards must be tied back or contained in a hat or hair net.
- Remove jewelry, scarves, coats, or loose clothing.
- Gaming, videos, or loud music that can cause distraction are not permitted.
- Mobile devices are not permitted when working around machinery.
- No food or drink in the lab (closed water bottles are allowed at desks only).
- PPE is required to be worn when working with chemicals.
- No video recording or photography is permitted in the lab.

These rules are for the safety of all students using the equipment in the lab and are overseen at the discretion of all faculty. Failure to comply with lab rules will not be tolerated and may result punitive action including dismissal from class, formal warning, course failure, or withdrawal from the program.
