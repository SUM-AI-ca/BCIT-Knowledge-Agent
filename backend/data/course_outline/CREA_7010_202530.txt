## Course Description
​This course introduces students to project management in the creative industries. It enables students to gain a comprehensive understanding of the various aspects of project management in a creative industries context, such as initiation, planning, budgeting, financing, implementation/execution, cost monitoring/controlling, and closing, as well as scheduling, purchasing, contracting, and reporting. Students will learn how to manage a creative project effectively through all phases, develop a project strategy, and report results to inform investors and stakeholders.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe how creative industry projects exist within a system for value delivery
- Interpret the relationship between project management and product lifecycle in a creative industries context
- Discuss various project pricing models used in the creative industries
- Explain how to close a project in the creative industries and transfer knowledge and ownership to the client Describe leadership styles appropriate for the chosen project management approach​
- Choose the correct project management approach for the given objectives of a project in the creative industries
- Evaluate the internal and external environment of a project in a creative industry setting
- Apply project management practices in alignment with the creative workflow
- Explain the 12 principles of project management
- Model different project tools and techniques used in creative industries​

- submitting plagiarized or low effort work

Your team will need to have a team charter and document infractions, escalating the issue to your instructor before the assignment is due. The instructor will then guide your team through the mark redistribution process. In this process, the team will have two options:
