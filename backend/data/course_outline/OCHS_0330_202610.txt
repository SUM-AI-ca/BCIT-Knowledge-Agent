### Course Description
​​This course is offered as a 1-day in person refresher at the BCIT Burnaby Campus or as a 1- week ONLINE course. The course provides refresher theory and hands-on training required to conduct hearing tests on noise-exposed workers in industry, counsel workers on the results of their hearing tests and advise workers about hearing protection, and administer hearing conservation programs in industry. For the 1-day in person course, students must bring their own audiometers and for the 1-week ONLINE course, students will need a computer with internet access and a camera. Successful, qualified students are eligible to be reauthorized by the BC Workers' Compensation Board as an industrial audiometric technician in BC.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Conduct industrial hearing tests.
- Categorize industrial audiograms.
- Counsel workers regarding hearing protection.
