### Course Description
This course builds on the listening, speaking and writing skills acquired in COMM 1184. Students will focus research, scientific writing, reporting, and a technical research communication project. Emphasis is on communication applications in the prosthetics/orthotics field.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the genre of scientific writing for prosthetics and orthotics.
- Write with conventions of scientific writing.
- Use conventions to structure a research report based on a unique research design and self-compiled data.
- Critically research, evaluate, and summarize technical documents and scientific articles.
- Write a research / technical report to industry standards.
- Edit and revise all documents to an acceptable industry standard.
- Prepare and deliver effective informal and formal oral presentations.
- Use PowerPoint to enhance oral presentations.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.
- Participate in effective group meetings.

### Other information
Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools: AI (artificial intelligence) tools may be used in a limited way to complete assignments in this course. See each assignment for details on acceptable use (e.g. brainstorming, editing) or inquire with your instructor in advance of submission. Assignments are designed to help you build skills and understanding. Please note that AI tools cannot learn for you
AI tools offer benefits when used wisely. You must use AI tools responsibly and ethically and acknowledge use in all work submitted for a grade. Please note that AI output may include incorrect or misleading information, fabricated authors/dates/publications, or faked works. You are always responsible for accuracy and quality. Material directly copied from any source (e.g. websites, books, AI tools) must be cited. Other uses (e.g. ideas, brainstorming, designing, paraphrasing) must be acknowledged including the sources. Undeclared use of AI is a violation of the academic integrity policy as it presents work or ideas created by tools or other people as your own.
Late Submissions: Any work submitted late will be penalized at 10% per day; no work will be marked if more than 5 days past the due date. Any extensions will be on a case-by-case basis with the instructor but must be identified at least 2 days prior to the original due date.
