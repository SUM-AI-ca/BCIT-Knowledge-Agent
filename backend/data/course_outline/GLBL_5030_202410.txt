## Course Description
This course is designed for students with no prior knowledge of French. Students will develop the four basic language skills (listening, speaking, reading and writing), gain knowledge of the French culture, and learn how culture impacts business practices. Through interactive classroom activities and utilizing French first-language multi-media materials, students will learn business terminology and the professional communication skills needed to handle business situations. Students will demonstrate their understanding of the French business environment through a range of individual and group activities, assignments and examinations.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Apply appropriate phonetic rules and patterns to correctly pronounce French words and sentences.
- Construct and comprehend simple sentences, using basic business vocabulary.
- Read and write basic business correspondence in French.
- Analyze the French culture and the aspects that characterize the business environment.
- Compare the similarities and differences between the French culture and their own.
- Interact effectively in real-life business scenarios in a culturally appropriate manner with native speakers.
- Develop strategies to enhance life-long learning of other languages and cultures by reflecting on the learning process through a learning portfolio.

## Course Specific Requirements
Participation: Active participation in labs and discussion forums is vital to accomplish the course objectives. All assigned chapter materials must be carefully reviewed and prepared prior to each synchronous lab session, and students are expected to actively participate in lab exercises. This will contribute to the participation mark. Participation during the online portion of the course will also be evaluated based on forum participation. Forum participation includes creating threads and responding to other student threads. Showing up in the forum is not the same as participating in a discussion thread.

Assignments: Late assignments will be penalized 20% per day and will not be accepted after 5 calendar days past the due date. In exceptional circumstances, flexibility and discretion will be used implementing this policy, provided the instructor is advised of the circumstances before the due date of the assignment.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media

Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class.
