### Course Description
​This course introduces the student to interpretation of engineering drawings used in public works construction, including the correlation between construction layout, specifications and standard drawings. Prior completion of PUBW 1001 is recommended.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Utilize engineering scale rules to determine distances on plans and drawings.
- Identify the basic components of a set of engineering drawings.
- Identify and utilize various plans and drawings common to municipal operations.
- Explain and use terminology related to roadworks, sewerage, drainage, and water distribution.
- Interpret plan and profile information, grades and slopes as shown on engineering drawings.
- Interpret construction information such as roadway alignments and geometrics, utility features, etc., as found on engineering drawings.
### Learning Resources
Required:
- Metric scale rule with 1:100, 1:200, 1:250, 1:300, 1:400, 1:500 scales (ie. Staedtler-Mars 987 19-4)
- Basic calculator with trig functions (sine, cosine, tangent)

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Session Number | Outcome/Material Covered | Reference Reading | Quiz
1 | Overview of typical maps and plans; engineering drawings and maps used in municipal operations. Introduction to symbols and typical legend used in the interpretation of maps and plans. Introduce Key Plan drawing.
2 | Introduction to roadworks plan & profile drawings, typical cross-sections, MMCD standard details, use of scale rule, and related terminology.
3 | Continuation of roadworks drawings, determination of slopes and grades, use of scale rule for both horizontal and vertical distances; relationship to storm drainage design. | Quiz #1 – Types of plans and roadworks drawings.
4 | Introduction to storm drainage plan & profile drawings, MMCD standards details, sewer grades, and related terminology.
5 | Continuation of storm drainage infrastructure, service connections, contours, and relationships to other utilities. | Quiz #2 – Storm drainage drawings
6 | Introduction to sanitary sewerage plans, MMCD standard details, related terminology, and relationship to other utilities.
7 | Continuation of sanitary sewerage plans, MMCD standard details, related terminology, and relationship to other utilities. | Quiz #3 – Sanitary sewer drawings.
8 | Introduction to Lot Grading plans, relationship to stormwater management and other municipal plan & profile drawings, Record Drawings, digital drawings.
9 | Introduction to street lighting system; BC Hydro, TELUS, and cable service drawings. Overview of regional services and other buried pipeline systems | Quiz # 4 – waterworks drawings.
10 | Final Test – covering all drawings, handouts, assignments and presentation material.
