### Course Description
​​Drawing on the knowledge gained in the previous courses, you will assume the role of a Professional in Business. You will concentrate on real-world Industry challenges by engaging either in primary or secondary research that will provide a professional learning experience. You will apply your Business skill set to the solution of the Industry challenge by working closely with a Faculty advisor and/or an Industry stakeholder. You will create a graduate level major report and presentation and evaluation will be based on application and integration of key Graduate Certificate in Business Administration concepts to the solution.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop a formal project proposal.
- Carry out a critical literature review, refining project methodology, and data analysis approach.
- Conduct primary or secondary research using qualitative and/or quantitative methods.
- Analyze primary or secondary research data.
- Plan, manage, and execute an applied graduate project.
- Provide a real-world industry solution using key business concepts.
- Communicate findings clearly to peers and/or Industry stakeholders.
- Create a graduate level academic major paper.
- Give a graduate level presentation to a panel of instructors and peers.​

### Course Goals
COURSE GOALS
Upon successful completion of GLBL 9200 graduate students will be able to:
- Develop a formal Capstone Project Proposal.
- Create a graduate level academic paper using applied research that demonstrates application of the key concepts, methods, and approaches in the graduate program, and demonstrates ability to apply these to graduate business administration problems.

- Communicate analysis results effectively to specify the solution proposed.

### Course topics
The Capstone Course is an applied research paper completed in 20 weeks. The paper is a capstone undertaking completed with guidance from an academic CP supervisor, usually in coordination with an industry client. The Capstone Course involves a review of the literature and the systematic application of frameworks, models, concepts, and theories from Graduate Business Administration Courses to a specific business problem or situation, for which Graduate Candidates develop practical recommendations. The Capstone project will be either a qualitative, quantitative or action research paper.
The Capstone Course research typically involves an exploration of a leadership topic, issue, or problem. The Capstone Course includes:
- A review of the relevant project management literature on lessons learned and a critical assessment of a business administration project in terms of shortcomings, best practices, and lessons learned with respect to Managerial Economics, Accounting for Managers, Marketing Management, Business Finance, or Systems and Operations Management.
- A review of the relevant graduate business administration literature on competences and the application to a company’s needs.
- Both Quantitative Research (positivistic research) or Qualitative Research (interpretive/constructivist research) may be used.
- A review of the relevant literature and the application of internal and external environmental analyses to a company’s graduate business administration requirements as undertaken by the CP.

- Required resubmission of the work in question for equal or lower grade value;
- A lower grade on the work submitted (i.e. a penalty applied);
- A failing grade on the work submitted; or
