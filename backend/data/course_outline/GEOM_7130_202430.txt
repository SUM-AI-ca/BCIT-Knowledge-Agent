## Course Description
This is the second of three courses that guide students through completion of a comprehensive independent project in a selected area of geomatics. In consultation with industry sponsors and faculty advisors, students will solve technical problems, demonstrate thorough knowledge of the project topic, exercise critical thinking, and present an overview of the project they planned during GEOM 6130 Geomatics Project Planning. In Geomatics Project 1, students will complete background research, finalize detailed planning, execute required field work, and draft their initial findings into a technical report and poster prepared according to specific guidelines. Students will critique previous posters to understand the primary organization, visual appeal, and flow characteristics necessary for an efficient poster design. Students will track their progress at monthly meetings where they set short-term goals for each stage of project delivery. They will also prepare and present a mid-term progress report for assessment.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Conduct an independent research project/study and demonstrate an ability to think critically, analyze, and solve a significant technical issue or problem related to the geomatics work environment.
- Apply resources, data, and personal organization techniques to manage a project.
- Manage a project schedule to meet milestone deadlines using effective record-keeping skills.
- Critically appraise the organization, visual appeal, and flow of previous degree project posters.
- Present a written progress report on project status using effective report-writing skills.
- Present a written progress report on project status using effective report-writing skills.
- Prepare a draft report containing the initial research results.

- Students will have regular 20-minute one-on-one meetings (dates TBD based on student term workloads) with their instructor to report progress against milestone deadlines as part of the project management grade. Marks will be awarded based on essential management including project organization, progress, and preparedness.
- The project management mark consists of 2 x 5%, 1 x 10% status update (3 total) meetings.
- Students will work in pairs to critique posters from earlier years for organization, flow, visual appeal and design to mirror the marking scheme used for the final poster presentation in GEOM 8130.
