### Course Description
​This course introduces the fundamentals of project management including development approaches and activities and tools used at various stages of a project. The course covers the full project lifecycle and provides an opportunity to apply a range of management methods necessary to deliver value. Projects are unique and uncertain environments. Managing this uncertainty and tailoring the project approach is critical to a successful project. The course is consistent with the Project Management Body of Knowledge and the Standard for Project Management.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
​Project environment
- Describe project characteristics, project management practices and guiding principles.
- Explain the importance of organizational structures and culture and their impacts on managing project teams and delivering project value.
Initiating
- Apply common project frameworks such as adaptive (agile) and predictive (waterfall) and their uses.
- Use project screening, selection and governance methods.
- Construct a Project Charter.
Planning
- Define stakeholder value and deliverable requirements.
- Develop scope, schedules and budget for both predictive and iterative projects using appropriate tools (WBS, Network diagram, user stories and product backlog)
- Plan resource utilization and apply resource leveling.
- Apply risk management and quality planning.
Delivery
- Explain the importance of managing the release of work to a project team.
- Create project tracking and measurement systems.
- Demonstrate techniques to analyze and control project performance.
- Apply risk management, problem solving and quality management techniques.
Closing
- Explain the role of project governance processes in closing out projects.
- Describe project closing activities.
- Evaluate project team performance using lessons learned.
Software
- Apply project management techniques using project management software.

Participation Grading
Each session, students will be evaluated for participation and scored out of 2. During the lecture portion, students will receive a worksheet to complete and hand in at the end of the session, graded for completeness and effort rather than correctness. During lab and activity portions, students will be assessed on timeliness, attention, engagement with assigned work, and whether they are contributing positively to the classroom environment. A score of 2 reflects genuine effort on the worksheet and active engagement throughout the session; a score of 1 reflects weakness in one area; a score of 0 is recorded for absence or lack of meaningful participation.
### Course Specific Requirements
Team Based Project
This course features a major team-based project. All team members usually receive the same mark. However, marks can be redistributed if one or more team members contribute less than their fair share. This may include one or more of the following:
- frequently absent from team meetings or classes
- submitting plagiarized or low effort work
- missing team deadlines or class deadlines
- disruptive behavior during team meetings.
Your team will need to have a team charter and document infractions, escalating the issue to your instructor before the assignment is due. The instructor will then guide your team through the mark redistribution process. In this process, the team will have two options:
- Option 1: If all members of the group agree that grades should be reallocated, up to 40% will be taken off one student’s score and redistributed to the other students.
- Option 2: If group members do not reach agreement on how marks should be reallocated, each team member’s contributions will be marked separately.
