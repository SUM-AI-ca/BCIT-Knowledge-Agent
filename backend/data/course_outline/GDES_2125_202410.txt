## Course Description
Brand is the persona of a company and it must portray a focused message in a consistent manner to be recognized and remembered in the marketplace. This course establishes how brand equity is maintained through the implementation of a vital and compelling corporate identity program. Students explore the intricacies of brand creation, implementation and maintenance creating a consistent identity that can be flexibly applied to many applications. Broader brand issues will be discussed in class and through research assignments.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Explain how brand equity is critical for a company’s success.
- Distinguish between the terms “brand” and “identity.”
- Conduct research to determine an organization’s brand message.
- Communicate a brand message through a design concept.
- Explore visual components of a brand message using symbols.
- Represent a brand message by designing a logo or wordmark.
- Create a coordinated visual language (design elements, colour scheme and typographic family and composition) from the logo design.
- Apply this visual language to a basic corporate standards guide.
- Explain design issues inherent in tailored brand identity creation and maintenance.
- Interpret visual elements from written criteria and concepts.
- Using your visual language elements, apply the brand message to elements of corporate business identity.
