### Course Description
This course focuses on applying the marketing concepts from the introductory marketing course. Marketing strategy will be discussed as an essential requirement for business success. It emphasizes the development of personal selling skills and customer relationships.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain the importance of marketing in Organizations and organizational strategy.
- Explain the effects of social, legal, ethical, and technological environments on marketing decision-making.
- Perform market forecasting in the insurance industry.
- Evaluate market forecasting data.
- Given case information identify marketing problems and opportunities.
- Prepare a formal Marketing Plan for an insurance brokerage.
### Learning Resources
There is no textbook for this course. Readings and videos will be assigned, as listed in relevant week’s material within the Learning Hub course site.
### Course Goals
- Apply core marketing concepts to the insurance industry Develop a working knowledge of foundational marketing principles such as STP (Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning), the 4 P’s, and consumer behavior, and apply them to real-world insurance contexts.
- Analyze and evaluate external and internal factors affecting insurance marketing strategies Use frameworks like PESTEL, SWOT, and Porter’s 5 Forces to assess the strategic marketing environment and identify opportunities and threats specific to insurance organizations.
- Develop and present a comprehensive marketing plan for an insurance provider or brokerage Research, prepare, and deliver a formal marketing plan that includes customer analysis, branding, promotional strategy, and sales tactics.
- Demonstrate effective communication, sales, and presentation skills Build personal selling and persuasion skills in B2B and B2C contexts.
- Interpret and apply market forecasting techniques to inform marketing decisions Evaluate marketing data and use forecasting methods to support strategic decision-making in a dynamic and competitive insurance marketplace.

### Attendance Requirements
Successful completion of the course depends on the student attending the course lectures and labs, passing the cumulative of the midterms and final exam, as well as successful completion of the course group projects and any individual assignments.
Professional business behaviour is expected in this course and therefore attendance at all lectures and labs is required. Arriving more than 5 minutes past the start of class without emailing the instructor in advance will be considered an absence. Excessive absences will be deemed to have missed more than 10% of the lectures and labs. Medical documentation is required if a student has an excessive absence record or misses an assignment, a presentation, or an exam. All other BCIT attendance policies will be applied.
Missing more than 10% of classes without an excused absence will reduce Professionalism & Engagement marks to zero and will require a meeting with the instructor. Professionalism and Engagement marks will be based on a student's attendance and contribution to class discussion and activities on a weekly basis. Also, if a student misses an in-class assignment or activity without an excused absence (such as acceptable medical note) they will not be able to make-up the work.
