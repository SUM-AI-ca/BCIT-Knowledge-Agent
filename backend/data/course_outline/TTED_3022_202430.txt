## Course Description
This course introduces students to basic hand tools and related processes used to work with metal. Jeweller's saws, sheet metal machines, drill press, abrasive belt sander and mechanic's hand tools are also included. Students will learn accurate layout procedures, selection, set up and safe use of these tools and machines for specific operations. As well, this course introduces students to the basic operation and use of metal lathes and milling machines. Students will learn how to select appropriate procedures for specific operations and to develop logical sequences for the production of a project. Safe work practices will be emphasized and lecture content will support hands-on, practical work.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Conduct themselves safely to the standard of safety expected in school shops.
- Identify basic metalworking, sheet metal and mechanic's hand tools.
- Identify key features of the drill press and abrasive belt sanding machine.
- Demonstrate the use of various metalworking, sheet metal and mechanic's hand tools.
- Demonstrate the use of the drill press and abrasive belt sanding machine.
- Demonstrate a reasonable standard of hand skill accuracy, fit and finish in making projects.
- Use metalworking lathes and milling machines to produce project solutions.
- Identify metalworking machine tools.
- Make basic calculations required to perform machining operations.
- Describe the essential angles required on a metal cutting tool bit.
- Grind a tool bit for machining aluminium.
- Perform simple hardening and tempering operations.
