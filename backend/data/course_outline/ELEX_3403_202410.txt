## Course Description
The focus of this course will be on the renewable energy resources, their present-day technologies and operation principals. This course builds upon general knowledge in mathematics and natural sciences as well as electrical concepts learned in the first year of the program. The course is designed to give students the required background to evaluate renewable energy systems. The class will explore solar, wind, small hydro and tidal energy systems in general and will be given the opportunity to specialize in wind or solar systems. Software tools will be used for modelling and simulation throughout the course.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Describe the primary renewable energy systems and technologies.
- Describe basic electrical components and system architecture of renewable energy systems.
- Compare energy resources and technologies using the concept of energy demand.
- Evaluate renewable energy systems for residential and commercial applications.
- Create a project proposal for a residential renewable energy systems.
- Use engineering software to model and design renewable energy systems.
- Apply Canadian Electric Code sections to design a residential renewable energy system.

## Course Goals
The class will focus on solar and wind energy systems, providing the basics of energy conversion and the factors affecting the efficiency of the conversion.

Software tools will be used for modelling and simulation of solar and wind energy systems.

Students will be exposed to hands on experiments on solar panels to find the typical IV and PV curves of panels.

The role of power electronics converters in renewable energy applications will be studied throughout the course.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Week 1:

- Introduction to solar cells, panels, arrays.
- Lab 1: Simulink simulation of PV panels

Week 2:

- Introduction to IV and PV curves and Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT)
- Lab 2: Simulink simulation of MPPT and partial shading

Week 3:

- Introduction to basics of renewable wind system
- Lab 3: Double-Fed Induction Generators

Week 4:

- Introduction to different classes of Wind Turbine Generators (WTG)
- Exam

## Course topics
Weeks 1 and 2: Solar systems

Weeks 3 and 4: Wind energy systems
