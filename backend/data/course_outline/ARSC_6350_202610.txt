### Course Description
The purpose of this course is to apply building science knowledge and skills to propose a high-performance sustainable project for Design Studio 4: Comprehensive. Through a series of lectures and assignments, students will execute holistic climate and site analysis, develop design strategies for building resilience, and set performance goals and drivers for the comprehensive project. A laboratory approach is used, developing iterations to improve process and results while promoting whole-building systems thinking.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the benefits of an integrated design process.
- Run daylight simulation and analyze results.
- Conduct a life-cycle assessment.
- Analyze whole-building energy use from an architectural perspective.
- Practice data-driven design with Building Information Modeling (BIM) to improve building performance.
- Demonstrate professional best practice individually and collaboratively.
