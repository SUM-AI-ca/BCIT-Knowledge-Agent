### Course Description
Provides both the theory and hands-on training required to conduct hearing tests on noise-exposed workers in industry, counsel workers on the results of their hearing tests and advise workers about hearing protection. After successfully completing this four-day classroom course, qualified students are eligible to be authorized by the BC Workers' Compensation Board as an industrial audiometric technician in BC.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Verify the need for hearing conservation programs.
- Conduct industrial hearing tests.
- Counsel workers regarding their hearing test results.
- Advise workers regarding hearing protection.
- Maintain facility of testing.​

### Course Goals
To train individuals to become certified as Industrial Audiometric Technicians within the province of BC as regulated and defined by WorkSafeBC.
Industrial Audiometric Technician (IAP) certification information available on the WorkSafeBC website at https://www.worksafebc.com/en/health-safety/education-training-certification/industrial-audiometric-technician

Comments:
The passing Final Grade for this course is 70%.
A passing Final Grade is dependent on a student completing all three (3) of the criteria (assignment and exams). If all 3 criteria are not completed, a Final Grade of ‘U’ (Unsatisfactory) will be given.
A passing Final Grade is dependent on a student obtaining 70% on each of the three (3) criteria (assignment and exams). If less than 70% is obtained on any one of the three (3) criteria, a Final Grade of ‘U’ (Unsatisfactory) will be given.
A Final Grade of ‘V’ (Course discontinuance / Vanished) will be given to a student who does not complete any of the criteria, and does not officially withdraw from the course.
