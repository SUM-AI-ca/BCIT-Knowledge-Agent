## Course Description
This course provides you with managerial foundations, frameworks, and tools used by businesses to make sound financial decisions. Building on a basic understanding of accounting, this course will teach you how to assess a company’s financial situation, forecast future financial requirements, determine how new investment opportunities are evaluated, and determine how companies manage their finances.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Discuss the role finance plays in the economy and in the operations of a business.
- Analyze corporate financial statements (using standard tools and techniques): identify trends, possible issues, and give investment recommendations.
- Demonstrate the ability to write financial statement analysis in a professional format (clear, precise, concise).
- Demonstrate the ability to identify relevant financial data and present these in an effective way.
- Assess a company’s short-term and long-term financing requirements.
- Explain the relation between risk and required rate of return.
- Justify new corporate investment opportunities using various evaluation techniques.
- Discuss major issues affecting how a company finances its operations.
- Assess how various financing sources affect a company’s risk and value.
- Estimate a company’s cost of capital.
- Assess the issues underlying how a company manages its short-term financing needs.
