### Course Description
​This course will prepare professionals in understanding core functions and concepts of the history and development of modern international education. Policies and trends in the internationalization of higher education in recent decades will be reviewed and critiques of the field will be explored. This course will provide foundational knowledge for those interested in employment in the field and a pre-requisite for recent practitioners pursuing professional development. ​An emphasis will be placed on the Canadian context.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Summarize foundational concepts of international education.
- Summarize core professional functions of the current practice of international education.
- Explain the influence of historical contexts and key frameworks of international education.
- Apply methods to critically examine the field of international education.
- Identify and describe the effect of current and emerging trends in the field on the work of international education practitioners​.

### Course topics
Module | Title | Brief Description
1 | Situating ourselves in our work | In the first module we will be developing a foundation for learning, you will have a chance to assess your own knowledge in this space, begin to understand the inter-relational nature of international education and we will go over the objectives and expectations of the course.
2 | Internationalization in Theory and Practice | In the second module we will explore foundational concepts and core professional functions of the current practice of international education.
3 | How did we get here? A broad overview of the history of International Education | In the third model we will discuss the influence of historical contexts and how these affect our work as international educators today.
4 | A critical examination of International Education | In the fourth module we will explore different concepts and methods to critically examine the field of international education.
5 | Current and emerging trends in IE | In the fifth, and final module we will review some current and emerging trends in the field of international education. We will also spend time in class reviewing what we have learned.
