## Course Description
​An intensive, full-time, 35 week long clinical placement in one or more settings enables the real-world application of theoretical knowledge and laboratory practice gained throughout the program. All competencies required for entry-level practice, and credential examination are developed in live settings. Safety, quality control, and accurate testing and documentation are expected without exception. Professional competencies of collaboration, ethical decision making, problem solving, communication, and scope of practice are expected and evaluated on site. Documentation of clinical activities and performance are evaluated by clinical educators (preceptors) and overseen by institute faculty.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Analyze genuine patient samples.
- Interpret and accurately report clinical findings in Hematology, Anatomical Pathology, Transfusion Science, Microbiology and Clinical Chemistry.
- Apply principles of safe practice
- Demonstrate proper use of personal protective equipment
- Perform accurate documentation of errors
- Use standard operating procedures (SOP's) and standard precautions
- Recognize areas of potential risk
- Employ ethical standards of practice modelling professional conduct expected of a Medical Laboratory Technologist
- Utilize effective communication skills to transfer information and facilitate understanding in a healthcare setting
- Describe and participate in routine Quality Management initiatives adhering to established laboratory protocols
