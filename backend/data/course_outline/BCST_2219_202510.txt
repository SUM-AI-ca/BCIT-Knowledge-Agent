## Course Description
This course introduces students to the production of a local television newscast. With limited supervision, television students will work in conjunction with Broadcast and Online Journalism program students to produce, shoot, and edit local news stories as well as produce a live recorded newscast and an on-air graphics package. Under direct and constant supervision, students will engage in activities that simulate a real-world newscast within a local television station.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Use electronic news-gathering cameras to shoot news stories (with limited supervision).
- Use non-linear editing systems to edit news stories (with limited supervision).
- Demonstrate the relationships among the various roles involved in producing a local television newscast, through participation in a production crew (with direct supervision).
- Prepare (under direct supervision) the technical facilities of a television studio to produce a local television newscast.
- Coordinate (under direct supervision) television field production equipment to ensure equipment availability for production operations.
- Perform (under direct supervision) several entry-level crew positions required to produce a local television newscast.
- Develop (with limited supervision) graphical elements that provide a unique on-air look for a local newscast.
- Follow code-of-conduct guidelines to work effectively in a team environment with other students to produce a local newscast.
- Demonstrate good interpersonal skills in working as a team member with other students to produce a local newscast.

## Course topics
Cinema Camera

Television & Video Production students will utilize a cinema camera with prime lenses to become familiar with the manipulation of camera and lens settings to acheive various effects.

Multicamera

Television & Video Production students will utilize broadcast facilities to produce a live production in a live, or “live to tape” format. A production team of students will occupy the primary production roles necessary to coordinate and produce the program. Students will learn the proper technical setup and roles necessary to run studio operations, audio, graphics, teleprompter (where necessary), server, etc.

ENG Story

- Production Shooting

Television & Video Production students will utilize high-definition broadcast cameras packages to capture the elements necessary to build a cohesive and well-sequenced story. Proper audio capture and lighting of subjects is also an important component of this assignment.

- Production Editing

Television & Video Production students will utilize non-linear broadcast editing systems to build a cohesive and well-sequenced story out of the visual and audio elements captured by students performing the Production Shooting assignment. Ingest and export of media, utilizing digital video servers, and proper audio mixing are important components of this assignment.

Single Camera Production

Television & Video Production students will utilize cinema camera packages to produce a short narrative video, including lighting, camera movement, sound capture, and post production.
