### Course Description
​In this course, the learner will be able to utilize social media analytics to influence engagement and build an online marketing strategy to increase opportunities for revenue generation. Through the understanding of data collection methodologies and the process of analysis the learner will be skilled at utilizing analytics to leverage information with the objective to optimize brand development. The learner will be able to utilize these to expand SEO’s, determine and develop engaging content, grow page likes, increase followers and post engagements along with find new customers in an on line environment. The relationship between client, customer, and business will be explored in connection to the social media realm.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop strategic marketing plans to integrate social media data for brand identification.
- Define social media behaviour and influences.
- Understand types of social media measurements i.e. engagement rate, impressions, post clicks, post reach, page likes.
- Utilize various social media analytic tools i.e. Google Analytics, Sprout Social, BuzzSumo, Snaplytics etc.
- Collect and Process Data: Data mining and analysis to leverage social media data.
- Understand the three main steps in analyzing social media: data identification, data analysis, and information interpretation.
- Use different tools for collecting analyzing, and exploring social media data for research and development purposes.
- Understand data identification: structured data, language, venue, time, ownership.
- Perform data analysis: sentiment, sentiment drivers, demographics.
- Develop a data model: organize data elements into a cohesive document to inform marketing decisions.
- Create a visualization/graphics of data analysis​.

### Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the student will:
- Gain a comprehensive understanding of the current social media environment, its revolutionary impact, and how it's shaping consumer behaviour.
- Learn how to conduct effective social media audits, interpret key metrics, and understand their significance in shaping social media strategies.
- Explore strategic uses of social media in business, including content marketing principles and applications, to effectively engage with target audiences.
- Engage in hands-on learning through workshops, guest lectures, and case studies, focusing on real-world applications like personal branding on Instagram and YouTube SEO.
- Be equipped with the skills and knowledge necessary for successful careers in social media marketing, preparing them for industry practicums and future professional roles.
