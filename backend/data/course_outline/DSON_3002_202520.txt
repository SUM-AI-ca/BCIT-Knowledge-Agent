## Course Description
​Women's Health Sonography 3 is a continuation of DSON 2002. The emphasis in this course is on more complex pathology and pathophysiology of the female breast, pelvic structures, and all stages of pregnancy as assessed with sonography. Scan techniques to thoroughly assess and document these findings are discussed. Critical thinking skills are developed through the correlation of sonographic findings with clinical history and alternate imaging modalities. Topics such as assisted reproduction, complications in multiple gestations, and prenatal screening are included, along with advances in technology such as 3D/4D and Spatial-Temporal Image Resolution (STIC). The theory taught in this course will be integrated with laboratory practice in DSON 3011 through both live and simulated practice.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- ​Recognize, describe and differentiate 2D images of intrauterine and extrauterine maternal and fetal anatomy, breast anatomy, anomalous and pathologic conditions as visualized by ultrasound or schematic diagrams and articulate their spatial anatomic relationships. (Appendix C)
- Given a clinical scenario explain how to investigate abnormal findings on obstetrical and breast sonographic exams.
- Given a clinical scenario correlate etiology, clinical features and patient characteristics of pathologies and conditions of obstetrical and breast health patients.
- Explain how to modify the examination based on the provided patient symptoms, clinical history, laboratory results, and sonographic findings while integrating knowledge of disease processes.
- Correlate embryonic development with sonographic findings as visualized by ultrasound
- Describe the requisite protocols, images and measurements required to assess and document anomalous and pathologic conditions for obstetrical and breast ultrasounds.
- Evaluate 2D, M-Mode, Doppler ultrasound images and breast ultrasound images for quality while identifying appropriate technical adjustments to create diagnostic images.
- Explains how to modify the examination based on the provided patient symptoms, clinical history, laboratory results, and sonographic findings while integrating knowledge of disease processes.
- Formulates and communicates an accurate technical impression and differential diagnosis using sonographic descriptors and medical terminology based on provided sonographic images.
- Describe the patient's background, clinical history and procedures involved with Artificial Reproductive Technologies (ART).
- Explain the applications when 3D / 4D and STIC ultrasound is beneficial
- Given a sonographic image explain how to determine the number of fetuses and chronicity. (Appendix A)
- Explain the importance and how to acquire the nuchal translucency and Biophysical Profile (Fetal breathing, fetal movement and tone). (Appendix A)
