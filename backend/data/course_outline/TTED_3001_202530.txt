### Course Description
This course introduces students to the basics of design and hand drawing using an integrated approach. Students will apply industrial design methods to practical constructions in this and subsequent courses throughout the TTED program. Techniques for producing pictorial drawings and sketches are developed. The course also focuses on the conventions of mechanical drawing to detail design solutions for the purpose of product manufacture.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply basic design principles and elements in two and three dimensional contexts.
- Apply the key stages of a formal design process.
- Critique design solutions using standard nomenclature.
- Reflect on the social and environmental impacts surrounding the creation of new products.
- Manipulate the relationships of form, function and structure in practical work.
- Prepare design briefs and display materials to communicate design solutions.
- Create two and three dimensional hand drawings to communicate design ideas, form and shape.
- Communicate and apply the principles of multi-view working drawings.
- Apply ANSI standards and conventions to working drawings.
- Identify how design and draw activities can be applied to the high school context.

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Week 1 -- Introduction to course and participants, discussion, detail Design Activity 1, detail Draw activity 1. Lecture / Discussion on Design Process, Issue discussion article, Lecture on Drawing. Design time for Design Activity 1.
Week 2 -- Discuss reading, In-class build time for Design Activity 1 (Build time is 2.5 hours, Demonstration (Competition) time is 1.5 hours). Summary report for Design Activity 1 is due end of day Friday of week 3.
Week 3 -- In class time to work on Draw Activity 1. Introduce Design Activity 2. Discussion on Design Principles as related to teacher practice. Issue Design Activity 2. Draw Activity 1 due end of week 4.
Week 4 -- In class Form and Function experimentation for Design solutions to Design Activity 2. Be prepared to present scaled concept models for Design Activity 2 in last half of class.
Week 5 -- In class time to work on Draw Activity 2. In class construction of full-scale solution for Design Activity 2. Revise and/or complete full-scale mock-up for Design Activity 2.
Week 6 -- Complete Design Activity 2 if needed. Issue Draw activity 3.
Week 7 -- Presentation of Design Activity 2. Draw Activity 2 due.
Week 8 -- In Class work on Design Activity 3.
Week 9 -- In-class work on Design Activity 3. Design Activity 3 presentations. Issue Draw activity 4.
Week 10 -- Lecture on Orthographic drawings and working drawings. Issue Design Activity 4.
Week 11 -- In-class work on Design Activity 4 and Draw Activity 4. Small group critique sessions on Design Activity 4.
Week 12 -- In-class work on Design Activity 4 and Draw Activity 4.
Week 13 -- In-class work on all assignments.
Week 14 -- Shop clean-up. Review. Any work outstanding is due.
