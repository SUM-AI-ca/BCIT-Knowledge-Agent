## Course Description
Continues from BLDG 2800, this course covers the advanced concepts of Autodesk Revit in an architectural environment. Gives students greater familiarity with the breadth of Revit Architecture's feature set.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Allows students to design and develop their own 3D and 2D objects (families) from the provided templates.
- Incorporates functions such as error trapping, and nesting.
- Teaches students to customize common object types such as railings and stairs.
- The course will give students the essential methods of administrating Revit in an architectural office environment.
- Delivery will concentrate on real world approaches and methodology rather than step by step procedures.

## Course Goals
- Give students greater independence with Revit, and extend their exposure to Revit’s wide feature set
- Improve modeling skills: from basic objects like walls and roofs, to custom content like furniture and windows
- Develop students’ ability to lead multi-user projects, and administrate Revit in their office
- Provide solutions to real-world challenges in modeling architectural and structural components

## Course Specific Requirements
This course require students to work on assignments and projects outside of class time. Having your own computer that can run Revit is required. BCIT students are eligible for an educational licence of Revit at no cost. Revit is only available on the Windows platform. Please check Autodesk’s website for minimum hardware and operating system requirements for Revit. Please check the BCIT Learning Hub at https://learn.bcit.ca and log into your course for a handout on acquiring Revit for your use in this course.
