## Course Description
This course is designed to allow the student to continue to assimilate previous academic and practical experience, with CLGT training, to explore some of the subject matter as outlined in the competencies for clinical genetics published by the CSMLS (January, 2016). Students will be given assigned readings/case studies, and will use online discussion forums to post individual comments. Student-led In-class discussions will occur for review and to practice teamwork skills. The written final examination will consist of questions based on all readings and case studies.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

- Critically analyze scientific literature on topics relevant to the Clinical Genetics Technology profession including CCMG guidelines for record retention.
- Write individual feedback based on assigned readings/case studies.
- Participate in the course-specific online discussion forum with other class members.
- Generate review material based on assigned readings.
- Organize and communicate information based on the CSMLS competencies for Clinical Genetics (January 2016) and current technology that is of value to other students.
- Understand the principles of cryopreservation and recovery.
- Demonstrate effective teamwork skills.
- Participate during in-class discussions using appropriate communication techniques.
- Practice collaborative leadership and conflict management as part of an interprofessional healthcare team.

## Course Goals
The goals of this course are to:

- use selected topics from the CSMLS syllabus, current technology, and recent publications and allow the student to process the information for individual and group response.
- develop the skill of preparing a summary of theoretical/practical information that is understandable and conveys understanding.
- ask and answer critical questions related to the topic individually and/or as a group
