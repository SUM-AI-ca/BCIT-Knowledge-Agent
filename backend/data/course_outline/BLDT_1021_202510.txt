## Course Description
This course provides an introduction to the relationships between drawings and building construction. Emphasis is on practical application of learned skills in a lab environment. These skills are essential for further studies in site processes, construction procedures and estimating. The course goal is to enable students to interpret construction drawings. Students will be able to derive necessary information from graphical construction documents in conformance with general practice in the industry.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Interpret construction drawings using industry standard graphic communications.
- Understand the relationship and interdependencies of all consultant drawings and read drawing sets proficiently.
- Explain the relationship between construction drawings and the related specification, contracts and other construction documents.
- Interpret written regulations and codes with respect to construction drawings.

## Course topics
1. Introduction

- Project delivery, intro to process/parties involved.
- Relationship & type of drawings involved
- Scale of drawings & information. Line types.
- Sheet layout/title block information, page/ detail referencing

2. Site Plan / Survey Plans

- Industry standards, Graphic/reference symbols.
- Interpreting legal survey & site information: Elevations, legal description, PL., Setbacks, Bldg. Grade, service utilities, etc.

3. Architectural Plans

- Industry standards, Graphic/reference symbols.
- Analyzing the plan; organization of drawings, architectural/consultant information.

4. Architectural Reflected Ceiling Plans and Schedules

- Reflected Ceiling Plans.
- Use of schedules to convey information.
- Coordination issues.

5. Architectural Elevations and Sections

- Analysis of Interior and Exterior Elevations & Various scales and drawing information required.
- Analysis of Elevations & Section drawings. Various scales and drawing information required.

7. Consultant Drawings / Information: Structural Drawings

- Industry standards, Graphic/reference symbols.
- Analyzing the plan; organization of drawings, overview of structural framing systems
- Schedules

8. Consultant Drawings / Information: Mechanical Drawings

- Overview of Mechanical systems / information
- Schedules
- Coordination of Architectural, Structural & Mechanical drawings

9. Consultant Drawings / Information: Electrical Drawings

- Overview of Electrical systems / information
- Schedules
- Coordination of Architectural, Structural, Mechanical & Electrical drawings

10. Building Code

- BCBC Code examples; Part 3 vs. Part 9
- Code related information on Drawings

11. Construction Contract Documents / Specifications (Project Manual)

- Principles, purpose & contents of the contract documents
- Overview of the Project Manual & Specifications;(master format), types of specifications, organization & references
