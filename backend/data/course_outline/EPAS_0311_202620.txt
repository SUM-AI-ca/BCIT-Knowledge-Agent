### Course Description
​This course is designed for students who have an upper-intermediate level of English proficiency. It aims to help learners improve their listening, speaking, grammar, and vocabulary while also teaching them how to work effectively with others in BCIT programs and in the workplace. Students will develop a better ability to participate in conversations in both academic and workplace situations. They will practice understanding various academic and technical materials, such as lectures, talks, and news reports. After listening to these sources, learners will work on their ability to give clear, accurate, and well-organized spoken summaries of the content. In addition to summarizing information, students will learn how to deliver and respond to oral presentations. student will begin to use technical and academic language and specific communication techniques during their speeches and formal discussions. This helps them become more capable of interacting with people at BCIT and in the workplace. Finally, the course helps students expand their knowledge of B2-level vocabulary and grammar features. While they learn these new words, they will also focus on using intermediate grammar correctly and naturally when they speak. By the end of the term, learners will be better prepared to communicate in a wide variety of technical, workplace, and academic environments.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate understanding of a variety of somewhat complex social exchanges, interactions related to school, and discussions on familiar topics
- Demonstrate understanding of talks and lectures on familiar technical academic topics
- Demonstrate understanding of informational and argumentative content of audiovisual reports on familiar technical academic topics
- Give an oral summary of up to 6 minutes to accurately communicate ideas, news, concepts, or information about a familiar general or technical academic concept
- Give a clear, prepared 6-minute proposal presentation that covers key points, provides a rationale, and makes a recommendation
- Participate effectively in extended discussion that involves the sharing of information and views, sharing and discussing technical content, and putting forward and responding to arguments​

### Attendance Requirements
I take attendance in each class (including online classes). Be ready to speak in groups and respond to teacher questions in every class. If you arrive more than 20 minutes late to a class or leave early, you will be marked absent.
If you have a valid medical or personal reason for missing class, the relevant documents must be submitted to me or the Program Head (consult with me first, as it depends on the length of absence and what you have missed).
### Other information
Generally, AI is not allowed for assignments in this course. If the use of AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) is permitted for part of an assignment, I’ll indicate that in the assignment instructions. When AI tools are permitted, and you use them, you are responsible for ensuring that the content you submit under your name adheres to the course’s expectations. You must also acknowledge the use of AI tools, e.g. I acknowledge the use of [name the tool] in the preparation of my assignment. Using AI tools without authorization is a violation of BCIT’s Student Code of Academic Integrity .
I may videotape lessons if we need to do an emergency online class. I will not give students access to the videos unless there is an emergency with proper documentation. All students enrolled in this class accept this videotaping requirement. While a laptop or tablet is an ideal device for a virtual class, some students manage with their smartphones. I would tailor any emergency lesson with the understanding that some people lack access to a laptop or tablet at home.

Evaluation deadlines are important in this class and are enforced to the minute. Deductions may range from 10% to 100%, depending on the assessment and lateness. You will need to use PowerPoint and Word, which are free to all BCIT students; please get them if you do not have them.
