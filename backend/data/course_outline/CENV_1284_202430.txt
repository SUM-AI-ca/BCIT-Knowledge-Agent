## Course Description
The course provides basic coverage of the structures, properties and applications of common engineering materials with emphasis on those used in prosthetic and orthotic devices. Concepts such as tensile and yield strength, fatigue, hardness and deformation will be explored both in theory and in the testing laboratory.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Describe basic mechanical properties of materials including UTS, yield strength, ductility, impact strength, tough-to-brittle transition temperature, elastic modulus, hardness, creep resistance, fatigue properties.
- Carry out simple stress calculations.
- Describe the effects on properties of materials of processing variables.
- Understand structures, properties and failure mechanisms of common engineering materials including:Metals; Ceramics; Plastics and Composite materials.
