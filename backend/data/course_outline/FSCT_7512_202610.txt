### Course Description
​This course introduces students to the concepts of cloud computing, forensics and security. The course will touch on advanced cloud concepts, including security and privacy, and discuss the best methods to store, control, manage and access private and corporate data in the cloud while addressing issues such as risk management and disaster recovery. The course will discuss the modern cloud forensics approaches and challenges with preserving, collecting and processing cloud-based data and the best methods and tools to commence a forensics investigation in the cloud.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Differentiate the concepts of cloud computing and cloud security.
- Describe the layout and the infrastructure of various cloud service providers.
- Explain the importance of cloud data confidentiality and security.
- Demonstrate how to access, store and extract data from various cloud sources.
- Perform forensics acquisition and analysis of cloud-based data.
- Develop a cloud based forensic lab/service.
- Assess the challenges and perspectives of cloud-based digital forensics.

### Course Goals
Students will be introduced to first steps in the Digital Forensics workflow, Identification, and Preservation. A Digital Forensics Practitioner must be able to enter a location and manage the unique set of conditions that could present themselves. The many different types of devices that one could encounter, determining how to approach each acquisition, and the importance of documenting this process.
Every type of device encountered could provide different challenges. Students will learn about the many different tools and methods involved in the evidence collection process as well as the best ways to utilize them. We will also discuss the various file systems, Operating System types and digital media carriers, which will expand the student’s knowledge of the process.
Note-taking in Digital Forensics is essential for several reasons, as it underpins the integrity, accuracy, and reliability of an investigation. A proper note-taking procedure will be discussed and implemented.
Finally, students will practice the proper techniques to acquire a sound forensic image from a variety of digital devices, including live and dead systems.

### Course topics
Students will be introduced to the most popular cloud providers on the market (AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud), as well as the basic structure and concepts that they use.
Students will set up and navigate the most common data-storage services in the cloud to understand the security protocols they use.
Students will then be introduced to the various scenarios that can jeopardize the integrity and security of the data stored in the cloud as well as how to mitigate those risks.
Students will then learn how to properly access, preserve, collect and analyze cloud-based data and logs.
We will discuss the modern challenges and approaches that the field of Cloud Forensics presents.
Finally, students will acquire hands-on experience with various tools and methods to properly preserve and analyze cloud data.
