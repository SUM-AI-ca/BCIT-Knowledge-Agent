## Course Description
This course builds on the fundamental knowledge and skills learned in BCST 1120, Television and Video Production 1. This course provides participants with advanced knowledge of the components of television and video production and how they interrelate. Upon completion of BCST 2120, students will have an intermediate working knowledge of professional broadcast equipment such as cameras, switchers, microphones, audio mixers, and media servers. Through several projects, students will learn studio and field audio acquisition and lighting of studio and field productions, including scenes/sets and products, and electronic news gathering/electronic field production equipment. Students will learn to plan and execute both live productions and electronic field productions. Students will operate portable, studio, and control room production equipment.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Execute a short video field production under direct supervision within a production team environment.
- Independently plan and execute short video field productions to visually tell a story.
- Set up, troubleshoot, and use professional broadcast video and audio equipment, with limited supervision in a television studio or on location.
- Use basic lighting equipment to accomplish a desired lighting of a scene or product, with limited supervision.
- Operate BCIT’s field production equipment to industry entry-level standards.
- Commit to a team and share responsibility for completion of team projects, demonstrating strong interpersonal communication skills.
- Collaboratively plan and execute short video field productions to visually tell a story.

## Course Specific Requirements
Instructors will be providing assignment sheets outlining objectives, deliverables, and due dates for both individual and group assignments on the BCIT Learning Hub.

These assignment sheets form the general outline of each assignment, but are not designed to comprehensively answer every assignment-related question which may arise.

It is the responsibility of students and/or production groups to initiate conversations with instructors where additional detail or clarification on assignments and criteria may be necessary as well as monitoring the learning hub for information about upcoming assignment and quiz due dates.

There will be no makeup tests, exams or quizzes. If you miss a test, exam or quiz, you will receive zero marks. Exceptions may be made for documented medical reasons or extenuating circumstances. In such a case, it is the responsibility of the student to inform the instructor immediately.

Except where otherwise noted all material for all assignments must be new as of the assignment date, originally generated and created independently by the student(s) for the purposes of meeting learning outcomes. Student submissions may be critiqued publically in lectures or labs, and / or posted online for training or promotional purposes.

Except where otherwise noted, all projects and assignments must be completed students on an individual basis.
