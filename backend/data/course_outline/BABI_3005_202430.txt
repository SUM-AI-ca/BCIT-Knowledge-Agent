## Course Description
​This course explores the fundamentals of data governance and its crucial role in enabling effective and ethical data analytics. Students will learn the principles, frameworks, and best practices for managing and governing data assets in organizations, with a focus on supporting analytics initiatives. The course combines theoretical concepts with practical applications.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Successful completion of Year 1 in the BITMAN program.

- Demonstrate the importance of standard data governance in business analytics.
- Communicate the impact of general data quality on executive decision-making.
- Implement industry-standard data governance policies and procedures in a business setting.
- Apply current data management techniques to ensure data is accurate, complete, and consistent in a business setting.
- Use common data profiling, cleansing, and standardization tools on real-world data.
- Analyze the accepted ethical and legal implications of data governance in industry.
- Identify relevant data privacy concerns to comply with data regulations in the organization's realm of operation.
- Use current data visualization and reporting tools to communicate data governance metrics to stakeholders.​

## Course Schedule and Assignments
This schedule will definitely change!

Week 1: Introduction to Data Governance for Analytics - Understanding data governance in the context of analytics - Key concepts and terminology - Real-world examples and case studies - Concepts around submissions / views / IP / storage Week 2: Data Governance Frameworks - Overview of data governance frameworks (e.g., DAMA-DMBOK) - Selecting a data governance framework Week 3: Data Governance Roles and Responsibilities - Defining data governance roles (e.g., Data Stewards, Data Owners) - Governance committees and decision-making processes Week 4: Data Governance Policies and Standards - Developing data governance policies and standards - Ensuring data quality and security through governance Week 5: Data Catalogs and Metadata Management - Importance of data catalogs in data governance - Metadata management tools and practices Week 6: Midterm Exam - Covers material from Weeks 1 to 5 - Review session prior to the exam Week 7: Data Privacy and Compliance - Data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) - Ensuring compliance through data governance Week 8: Data Governance Tools and Technologies - Overview of data governance software and tools - Hands-on experience with data governance solutions Week 9: Data Governance in Analytics Projects - Integrating data governance into analytics projects - Case studies on successful data governance in analytics Week 10: Data Governance and Data Warehousing - Data governance in data warehousing environments - Ensuring data governance in ETL processes Week 11: Data Governance Metrics and Measurement - Defining and tracking data governance KPIs - Assessing the effectiveness of data governance Week 12: Data Governance Challenges and Future Trends - Emerging trends in data governance - Addressing challenges and adapting to changing data landscapes Week 13 and 14: Catch up, because this is an ambitious schedule!
