### Course Description
​In this course, students will continue to work on the Integrated Entrepreneurial Project (IEP) started in BSYS 3000 Cloud Business Development 1. Students will take the business web application developed in BSYS 3000 and build advanced functionality. The fully functional website will be tested and implemented in a live server environment using existing Cloud-based providers such as Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Microsoft Azure. After implementation and testing, students will develop and apply a web marketing plan. Online advertising and analytics, social media, affiliate programs, and planning for mobile applications and devices will also be covered. Upon completion of the course the student will have a viable Cloud-based e-commerce business.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Create advanced functionality for an online business web application.
- Test and implement an operational Cloud-based ecommerce website.
- Create a web marketing plan.
- Implement search engine optimization (SEO) and online advertising tools.
- Plan for the integration of mobile applications and devices into their IEP.
- Measure impressions and sales conversions using real-time Internet marketing analytics.
- Connect with customers, build social networks, and employ affiliate marketing programs.
- Plan for web application security.
- Manage scalability, growth, and sustainability for a viable Cloud-based ecommerce enterprise.

### Course Specific Requirements
Students will be expected to keep up with all required readings, projects, and assignments. Late assignments will not be accepted for marking. If a student is ill, a medical note will be required to obtain credit for the missed assignment. Assignments must be done on an individual basis unless otherwise specified by the instructor.
This course features a major team-based project. All team members usually receive the same mark. However, marks can be redistributed if one or more team members contribute less than their fair share. This may include one or more of the following:
- frequently absent from team meetings or classes
- submitting plagiarized or low effort work
- missing team deadlines or class deadlines
- disruptive behavior during team meetings
Your team will need to document infractions, escalating the issue to your instructor before the assignment is due. The instructor will then guide your team through the mark redistribution process. In this process, the team will have two options:
Option 1: If all members of the group agree that grades should be reallocated, up to 40% will be taken off one student’s score and redistributed to the other students.
Option 2: If group members do not reach agreement on how marks should be reallocated, each team member’s contributions will be marked separately.

### Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: As set out in BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations , regular attendance in lectures and labs is integral to student success. Therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Students are responsible for communicating with instructors proactively about both potentially approved and unexcused absences. It is the student’s responsibility to work with their instructor to find an effective means to complete work missed due to both approved and unexcused absences. A student who has ceased to attend a course without explanation before the withdrawal deadline (two-thirds of the way through the course) will receive a Vanished (V) grade. These requirements are set out in accordance with both BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations and BCIT Policy 5103 Student Evaluation More information regarding withdrawal deadlines is available at Academic Dates & Deadlines .
