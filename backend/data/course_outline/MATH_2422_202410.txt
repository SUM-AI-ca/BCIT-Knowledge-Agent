## Course Description
Introduction of the integral with its many practical uses in engineering and science type applications; overview of different integration techniques.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Differentiate implicit functions. [1]
- Graph functions using their first and second derivatives and asymptotes. [1]
- Relate the concept of the integral to that of the derivative. [1]
- Integrate algebraic and transcendental functions. [1]
- Apply the indefinite integral to problems involving motion, shear force, bending moment, deflection. [1, 2]
- Calculate areas and volumes of irregular shapes using the trapezoid rule or Simpson’s rule. (Applications include area and volume of materials, lakes, reservoirs, areas under hydrographs.) [1]
- Use definite integrals to find area, volume, centroid, arc length, surface area, fluid pressure and work. [1, 2]
- Recognize situations which require the use of l’Hôpital’s rule. [1]
- Apply the different integration techniques appropriately. [1]
- Use successive integration to solve linear differential equations. [1]
