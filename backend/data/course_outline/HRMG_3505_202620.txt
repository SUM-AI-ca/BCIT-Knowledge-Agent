### Course Description
A first line management course for people responsible for staff training; members of human resource departments contemplating a training program, or who are involved with on-the-job training will be particularly interested. Develops a solid grounding in current training methodology, techniques and aids. Topics include learning theory, determining training needs, writing objectives, designing training programs using outside resources and evaluation.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of the course participants will be able to:
- Describe methods for conducting training needs assessments.
- Explain the principles of adult learning.
- Formulate objectives for a training session.
- Select appropriate instructional methods.
- Design a training session.
- Use audiovisual supports.
- Deliver a training session.
- Provide feedback to peers.
- Learn to accept feedback from peers.
- Describe techniques for managing group interactions.
- Describe strategies for creating an inclusive training environment.
- Describe processes for assessing training sessions.

### Course topics
Skill Practice 1 – Needs Analysis
This is an individual assignment. Students are required to prepare a written report (approximately 3-4 pages) that describes and provides a critique of the performance management process of an organization. The scope of the report should include, but is not limited to:
- A brief description of the organization, its mission and business strategy (1/2 page; 10 marks)
- A clear description of the objectives or purpose for their performance management process (1/2 page; 10 marks)
- A needs assessment of the organization describing the current needs of the employees using interviews, a review of any engagement surveys, glassdoor, news articles, and/or an audit of their operational procedures and human resource policies (1 pages; 30 marks)
- A gap assessment of their current performance management process and training needs relative to their organizational goals (1 to 2 page(s); 30 marks)
- The organization and presentation of your ideas, insights and quality of the written report (20 marks
Skill Practice 2 – Training Design
This is an individual assignment. Students are required to prepare a written report (approximately 3-4 pages) that describes and provides a critique of the performance management process of an organization. The scope of the report should include, but is not limited to:
- An outline of training requirements to address the gap assessment from the previous Needs Analysis (1 page; 20 marks)
- An outline of the pros of cons of various training design options available to the organization (1 page; 30 marks)
- A recommendation on the best method(s) for the training design (1 page; 30 marks)
- The organization and presentation of your ideas, insights and quality of the written report (20 marks)
Skill Practice 3 – Training Plan
This is an individual assignment. Students are required to prepare a written report (approximately 2-3 pages) that describes and provides a critique of the performance management process of an organization. The scope of the report should include, but is not limited to:
- A step-by-step guide on how the organization should execute its training plan, audience participation, stakeholder involvement, and sustainment approach (2 pages; 50 marks)
- A schedule outlining each step of the training design (1 page; 30 marks)
- The organization and presentation of your ideas, insights and quality of the written report (20 marks)
Skill Practice 4 – Training Presentation
This is an individual assignment. Students are required to prepare a presentation to leadership on your overall performance management process for the organization. The scope of the report should include, but is not limited to:
- A powerpoint presentation using graphics, visuals, notes, and an audio element (either recorded via zoom, MS Teams, or embedded audio)
- The organization and presentation of your ideas, insights and quality of the written report (20 marks)
