### Course Description
Modified from NSNN 7400, for nurses enrolled in a Compressed Time Frame program. Nephrology Nursing Certificate requires completion of NSNN 7410 Part A as well.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
As a healthcare practitioner, you are expected to follow professional standards of practice throughout all SN programs and courses. In this course, you will have opportunities to use the processes of critical thinking, systematic inquiry, communication, collaboration, leadership, professionalism, and clinical decision-making. Upon successful completion of this course, you should be able to:
- Describe the principles of peritoneal dialysis and the underlying physiological impacts.
- Identify psychological and social impacts of dialysis as it varies over the life span and with individuals.
- Explain the efficacy of dialysis using clinical data.
- Interpret clinical data to recognize complications of dialysis.
- Apply knowledge of dialysis to develop care plans for renal patients.
- Create a personal action plan together with a renal patient, using the principles of adult education and self-care management.

### Course topics
## First Module: Living with Peritoneal Dialysis (PD)
In this module, you will have the opportunity to explore the theory surrounding the principles of peritoneal dialysis, related modality, equipment and technology, and PD-dialysis access. The psychological and physiological issues and complications that arise in the context of peritoneal dialysis are also explored. As in the previous courses, you will find client narratives and case‑based approaches are utilized to emphasize the experiences of clients living with peritoneal dialysis and the related treatment complications.
## Second Module: Integrating Self-Management & Self-Management Support into Practice
In the last module, the basic principles and concepts of self-care management and adult teaching/learning in a home dialysis setting will be introduced. Integration of self-care management and support into nephrology nursing practice will be explored utilizing case-based scenarios. The emphasis will be on empowering clients on dialysis to self-manage through collaborative goal setting.
