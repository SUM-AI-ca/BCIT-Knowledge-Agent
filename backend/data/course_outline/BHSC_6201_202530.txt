### Course Description
This course is designed to assist students in managing workplace challenges in professional practice, and developing communication and other interpersonal skills. Students will learn teamwork, collaborative troubleshooting, and the importance of cooperation and respectful behavior towards fellow Clinical Genetics technologists and other health care professionals.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
By the end of this course, learners will be able to:
- Effectively communicate with clients and other health care professionals by using the following communication skills:
- Active listening
- Verbal communication
- Non-verbal communication
- Identifying barriers to effective communication.
- Demonstrate interpersonal skills when interacting with clients and health care professionals, including dealing with issues arising from cultural differences.
- Develop professional relationships with other health care professionals by:
- Recognize role of other health care professionals and demonstrate inter-professional collaboration in dealing with other health care professionals.
- Clarify the role and responsibilities of the clinical genetics technologist to other health care professionals when necessary.
- Take responsibility for professional behaviour in these relationships.
- Recognize signs of individual and group stress and demonstrate empathy in assisting colleagues to deal with stress.
- Demonstrate the use of conflict resolution skills.
- Utilize effective teamwork skills by learning how to assess team dynamics and functions, and respond accordingly.
- Demonstrate knowledge of change management strategies as well as anticipate, contribute to and work effectively in a changing environment.
- Demonstrate effective time management strategies, and prioritize tasks accordingly.
- Utilize information to make informed decisions, particularly when dealing with situations that call ethics into question.
- Understand standards of practice and importance of maintaining these.
- Make appropriate use of the technologist’s right to refuse to participate in potentially dangerous situations.
- Recognize how moral and ethical issues in health care may affect their decisions.
- Keep welfare and confidentiality of the patient paramount at all times.

### Course Specific Requirements
Review and Reflect (Weekly In-Class Assignment): Most weeks, students will complete a short in-class “Review and Reflect” assignment designed to reinforce learning and encourage deeper engagement with the course material. Review and Reflect assignment dates are indicated on course schedule below. These assignments collectively provide a low-stakes way to check your understanding, practice applying concepts, and build connections across course topics. Review and Reflect assignments will include:
- Review Questions : Multiple-choice questions covering key concepts from the previous week’s material.
- Reflection & Application Questions : Short written prompts that ask you to connect, apply, or reflect on the current week’s material.
Review and Reflect assignments must be completed during class time and cannot be submitted outside of class , as they are meant to promote active participation and immediate engagement with the material. A password will be provided in-class to access the assignment. Do not share this password with others who may not be in attendance that class period.
To allow for unexpected absences, the lowest “Review and Reflect” grade will automatically be dropped (one free miss). In addition, one make-up assignment will be available for students who miss more than one session due to extenuating circumstances. No additional make-up assignments will be provided.

A NOTE ABOUT COURSE ENGAGEMENT & PARTICIPATION...
This is an interpersonal communciation skills class. We can ALL get better at communicating. To get the most out of this class, students are encouraged to actively participate and contribute in classroom discussions, presenations, role plays, and activities. Consider the following aspects of course participation:
Attendance | Please contact me if you are unable to attend class. Demonstrate repectful and professional conduct by arriving to class on time. Communication professionally with instructor if you are going to be late.
Role-Plays/Group Work | Full participation in role plays, class discussions, and group work is expected for this class.
Questions | Demonstrate active listening by asking questions – others might be wondering the same thing! I invite you to speak up in our classes. Ask questions to demonstrate interest and engagement after class presentations. Keep the conversation going.
Contribution | Demonstrate your engagement by offering your perspective or answering questions about class materials. Engage with the presentations.
Respectful Conduct | Some topics may be sensitive and/or invite debate of different perspectives. Disrespectful language and/or behaviour will not be permitted. Use verbal and nonverbal communication to promote respect, inclusion, sensitivity, and curiousity.
Use of Skills | Demonstrate skills we have learned in class; show that you are able to apply your learning in a real-life interactive setting. Respond to classmates (and Instructor) using Active Listening Skills. Use the course skills when communicating via email. Incorporate skills into group work and all aspects of the course.
Preparation for Class | Prepare for class by doing your readings ahead of time; it will be easier to absorb and discuss materials discussed in class.
Use of Electronics | Please refrain from using your phone or computers for non-course related things during class time. Be mindful of the nonverbal messages being communicated through the presence/use of technology during classtime.
Support your Classmates | Be respectful, attentive, and supportive during class presentations. Give presenters your full attention. Limit your use of electronics during class presentations. Pay attention to your nonverbal communciation. Participate meaningfully in discussions and activities after presentations.
