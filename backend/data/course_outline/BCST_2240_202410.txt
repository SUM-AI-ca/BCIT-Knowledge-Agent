## Course Description
This course is a broad exploration of Digital Journalism. Throughout the term, students will explore the current digital landscape, with a focus on web writing, design, social media, and online story delivery. Classes will be a mix of topical lectures/discussions and hands-on learning. Through course work, students will explore a variety of digital tools and platforms currently being used in industry. The course will also investigate the important topic of verification and look at tools to combat misinformation in the digital landscape. At the culmination of this course, students will bring together everything they have learned to create an in-depth, cross-platform multimedia story. ​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Demonstrate industry standard web writing techniques to build online stories.
- Identify the basics of Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and apply that knowledge to effective headline writing.
- Identify the ethical and legal parameters related to digital content creation.
- Use a variety of digital tools to build engaging online content.
- Demonstrate the principles of design to effectively create informative graphics.
- Identify the fundamentals of data journalism and utilize basic visualization tools to enhance data storytelling.
- Demonstrate the use of multimedia production skills to build engaging online stories.
- Develop original news and feature stories for multi-platform delivery.
- Identify the principles of verification and utilize those skills to combat mis-information in the digital landscape.
- Demonstrate effective use of social media to create journalism focused content.
- Work effectively in a group to create an in-depth multiplatform story.

## Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence.
