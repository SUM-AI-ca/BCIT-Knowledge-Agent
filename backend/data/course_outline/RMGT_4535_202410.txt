## Course Description
This course provides the student with the selling and communications skills essential to success in a financial profession. The course covers the marketing of financial products and services and, in addition, it will introduce the student to the institutional environment in which many financial professionals operate.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Compare the concept of relationship selling to other sales approaches.
- Explain the steps in the relationship selling process.
- Develop sales presentations for various clients.
- Deliver sales presentations for various insurance products.
- Critique a videotape of their own sales presentation.
- Evaluate the techniques available for maintaining and building client relationships.
- Demonstrate effective verbal, non-verbal and written communication skills.
