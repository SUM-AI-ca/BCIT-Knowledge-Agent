## Course Description
The course content is based upon the IMO Model Course, ISPS Code, the Marine Transportation Security Regulations, Transport Canada's Marine Security requirements and guidelines for a Ship Security Officer. The techniques and skills required to address the qualifications and duties of Ship (Vessel) Security Officer: (a) the security of a ship, (b) implementing and maintenance of a ship security plan, and (c) liaising with a Company Security Officer (CSO) and a Port (Marine) Facility Officer (PFSO/MFSO), will be provided. Emphasis is placed on awareness of terrorist threats and methods of operation as well as obtaining current intelligence, threat assessments, and the importance of training and vigilance. The course provides reference to the pertinent Acts, Regulations, Codes, International Resolutions, Administrative Guides, and Industry Standards to be in compliance with the ISPS Code and Transport Canada Marine Security's requirements.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Maintain and supervise the implementation of the Ship Security Plan.
- Propose modifications to the Ship Security Plan.
- Assess security risk, threat, and vulnerability.
- Undertake regular security inspections of the ship.
- Coordinate the security aspects of the handling of cargo and ship's stores.
- Ensure that security systems and equipment are properly operated, tested, and calibrated.
- Report to the Company Security Officer on deficiencies and non-conformities during audit.
- Report all security incidents.
- Enhance security awareness and vigilance on board.
- Ensure adequate training has been provided to shipboard personnel.

## Evaluation Criteria
Criteria: %
Practical exercise checklist: 25%
Assignment: 25%
Final examination: 50%
TOTAL: 100%

Successful students must have 100% attendance, and achieve an aggregate mark of not less than 70%. Students must achieve 70% on a written test and 50% on the practical exercise in class.

In order to pass this course, students must achieve an aggregate grade of at least 70%. In addition, students must achieve a minimum of 50% of the marks allocated for course work and 50% of the marks allocated for the final exam. Students who fail to achieve 50% of the allocated marks in either component cannot receive a final grade greater than 69%. The minimum attendance for this course, in compliance with the Transport Canada requirement, is 90%.

Note that the wording pertains to only two components: (1) the final examination and (2) course work. It is therefore NOT required that each sub-component, such as a quiz, assignment, etc., has to be passed. For course work, it is up to the student, with the instructor’s support, to meet the 50% bar through the sub-components of possible make-up quizzes, alternative assignments, etc.

## Course Specific Requirements
In order to be eligible to work in the Marine Industry, you must pass a Transport Canada Medical Examination.

Exam Attempts

Students who were unsuccessful in their final evaluation of a course are eligible for one (1) rewrite of their final examination. It is the responsibility of the student to contact the Cadet Program Assistant to schedule their re-examination. A student must book a minimum of ten (10) days prior to the posted re-examination date by emailing marine_exams@bcit.ca.

Upgraders:

Students who are upgrading are provided the opportunity to rewrite a final evaluation within twelve (12) months of the course completion.

Cadets:

Nautical Science and Marine Engineering diploma students must complete and pass all term courses before enrolling in the next academic term (e.g., students must have passing grades in all Term 1 courses prior to enrolling in Term 3 academic courses).

Nautical Science and Marine Engineering diploma students in Term 7 have twelve (12) months from the end of their program to complete and pass a final evaluation rewrite.

Time Limit to Complete a Credential

The time limitation begins on the starting date of the first course towards the credential. The time limit to complete a credential is seven (7) years (BCIT Policy 5103).
