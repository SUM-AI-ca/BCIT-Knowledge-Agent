## Course Description
Deals with key concepts such as machine construction, NC v. CNC, coordinate systems and reference points.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Identify the components of the NC machine and CNC machine.
- Understand the basic G codes and M codes.
- Operate basic handle mode and manual data input.
- Create, input and run basic lathe programs on a Haas simulator.

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance:

Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, and will be monitored. Students are expected to be punctual and attend class for the full duration. Late arrivals or early departures may be considered as absences. Absence from more than 10% of the scheduled course hours will result in the issuance of a warning. If additional scheduled sessions are missed, withdrawal or course failure may be recommended.

Attendance will be taken at the beginning of each session. Students not present at that time will be recorded as absent.

It is the responsibility of the student:

- In the case of illness or other unavoidable absence, to notify your instructor, indicating the reason for the absence as soon as possible.
- To obtain and present to the Department Head an official BCIT Student Medical Certificate substantiating an absence of three or more consecutive days or any absence from an evaluation such as an assignment, lab, exam or other component.

Students who are absent for any reason are responsible for determining what work was missed and the details of any assignments. In the event of extenuating circumstances (e.g. documented illness) the instructor may consider an extension.
