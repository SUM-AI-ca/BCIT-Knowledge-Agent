### Course Description
Introduces students to the fundamental principles important to the discipline of personal Financial Planning. Students will acquire an understanding of the concepts and applications associated with financial planning, and the analysis of various financial situations. This course covers the basic issues to be considered in building a sound program to achieve long term financial goals. Topics include cash and debt management, insurance, investments and portfolio management, wills, estates, and tax planning.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of FMGT 3830, the student will be able to:
- Identify the process of setting personal financial goals and outline how this fits into the personal financial planning process.
- Describe, in detail, the steps in the personal financial planning process.
- Evaluate the role of the key components of cash and debt management, risk management, retirement planning, estate planning, tax planning, and investment planning within a financial plan.
- Assess the framework within which to assemble a personal financial plan.
- Outline the regulatory, ethical and legal framework affecting the practice of personal financial planning.

### Course Schedule and Assignments

1 September 4 | Chapter 1 & 3 | Financial Planning Basics Describe the FPSC and the CFP designation Describe the six components of a comprehensive financial plan Describe the six step financial planning process Understand the concepts of net worth and budgeting Prepare and analyse a personal cash flow statement and balance sheet.
2 September 11 | Chapter 2 plus Supplementary notes | Financial Math Locate the main TVM keys on a financial calculator Perform compounding operations for lump sums and annuities Perform discounting operations for lump sums and annuities Solve future sum type problems Calculate NPV and IRR [not in text] Solve unknown interest rate type problems [not in text] Calculate effective annual interest rates Calculate real, after-tax rates of return [not in text]
3 September 18 | Chapter 5, 6 & 7 | Cash and Debt Management Describe the money management process. Compare the different financial institutions and their savings alternatives. Describe credit bureaus and credit scores. Understand the risks of identity theft Describe the personal loan process and calculate the real cost of borrowing. Discuss the common forms of consumer financing, including credit cards, and assess debt capacity Describe the concept of leverage Describe the home purchase process and the common forms of mortgage financing Describe the alternatives open to the struggling debtor
4 September 25 | Chapters 4 | Tax Planning Describe the structure of a personal tax return and the eight steps in completing a tax return. Calculate income tax payable on the three main types of investment income Explain the importance of and calculate marginal tax rates Describe and give examples of tax planning strategies
5 October 2 | Chapter 8 | Risk Management Explain risk management and how insurance works can be a part of a risk management program. Explain the basics of insurance company operations. Describe the basic concepts, terminology and typical policy provisions of automobile insurance Describe the basic concepts, terminology and typical policy provisions of home insurance
6 October 9 | Chapter 9 | Risk Management II Calculate how much life insurance a family needs Explain how life insurance premiums are calculated Describe the types of life insurance and standard terminology Describe the pros and cons of term versus whole life insurance Describe the basic concepts, terminology and typical policy provisions of health insurance Describe the terminology and issues associated with disability coverage and long term care insurance.
