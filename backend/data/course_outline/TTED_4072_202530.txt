## Course Description
Provides an introduction to a wide range of topics related to teaching technical and vocational subjects in schools. Includes the development and preparation for delivery of an activity that will satisfy the outcomes of the provincial technology education Instructional Resource Packages or the new Workplace Program Guides.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the professional behaviour of practising secondary school teachers and demonstrate appropriate behaviour when observing and teaching in public schools.
- Describe and assess teaching techniques, classroom activities and instructional management observed in secondary Industrial/Technology Education classes.
- Describe Technology Education (TE), and explain how it relates to Industrial Education (IE).
- Work individually and in teams to create appropriate Technology Education learning activities and resources based on the Provincial Curriculum document for Technology Education public school courses.
