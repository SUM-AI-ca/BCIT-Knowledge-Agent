### Course Description
​Vascular Sonography 1 introduces students to imaging of the carotid arteries and lower extremity veins. The course begins with vascular architecture, anatomy, hemodynamic principles then shifts to sonographic applications. The focus in this course is on normal flow patterns as obtained with 2 dimensional, color, and pulsed wave Doppler assessment of the extracranial arteries and peripheral extremity veins. The integration of sonographic appearances with hemodynamics and physical principles is emphasized. Etiologies of stroke are introduced.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Recognize normal carotid artery and extremity vein anatomy, anomalous conditions, and their spatial anatomic relationships as visualized by ultrasound or schematic diagrams.
- Describe the requisite protocols and images required for complete carotid artery and extremity venous exams and how to set up the ultrasound room and optimize patient positioning to maintain their dignity.
- Evaluate carotid and lower extremity venous images for orientation, identification, labelling, image artifact and quality.
- Identify proper technical adjustments to create diagnostic images based on image evaluation.
- Explain the principles of normal arterial and venous hemodynamics and their correlation with Doppler techniques.
- Correlate exam indication with given laboratory and diagnostic imaging results, patient clinical history, and presentation.
- Formulates and communicates an accurate technical impression using sonographic descriptors and medical terminology based on provided sonographic images.
- Explain the effects, on the venous return during provocative maneuvers such as (Valsalva). (3.3d)
- Differentiate the anatomy and function of the lower extremity veins as listed in NCP 6.1 Appendix D.
- Analyze carotid velocity data as seen in a calculation package for accuracy and explain the variable factors that directly impact calculations.
- Compare and contrast carotid velocity data as seen in a calculation package for accuracy and explain the variable factors that directly impact calculations. ​
