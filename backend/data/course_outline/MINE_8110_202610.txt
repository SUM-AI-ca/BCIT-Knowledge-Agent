### Course Description
Note: This description should be read in conjunction with the description for MINE 7110 – Capstone Project I This course is the sequel to the Capstone Project I course in level 7. It involves completion of the prefeasibility study project and oral presentation begun in level 7. Each team will work to complete the execution of the engineering work plan that was commenced in MINE 7110 by preparing a prefeasibility report and presenting their results. The course draws on expertise in engineering theory, design and project management developed earlier in the program, and brings their capstone engineering design project to an appropriate conclusion. This course will require the involvement of faculty from a number of disciplines principally in mining, geology, mineral processing, economics and technical communication.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Execute a project management plan based on an identified project management process [1,6,10,11].
- Complete a complex, multi-disciplinary engineering design task working with other team members [4,6,8,10].
- Manage time and activities to ensure that prescribed milestones are achieved in a timely manner [2,6,8,10].
- Evaluate alternative design concepts [4,7,9,11,12].
- Perform engineering and economic analyses to identify an optimal solution from a series of trade-off alternatives [2,4,7,9,10,].
- Write a comprehensive report to summarize results of a prefeasibility level study on a mining project [1,7,10,11,12].
- Deliver an oral presentation on the results, conclusions and recommendations of the study to an audience of their peers, professors and specialists from the mineral industry [7,8,10].

### Course topics
This course is the sequel to the Capstone Project I course in level 7. It involves completion of a comprehensive engineering study project and oral presentation begun in level 7. Each team will work to complete the execution of the engineering work plan that was commenced in MINE 7110 by preparing a prefeasibility-level report and presenting their results. The course draws on expertise in engineering theory, design and project management developed earlier in the program, and brings their capstone engineering design project to an appropriate conclusion. This course will require the involvement of faculty from a number of disciplines principally in mining, geology, mineral processing, economics and technical communication.
