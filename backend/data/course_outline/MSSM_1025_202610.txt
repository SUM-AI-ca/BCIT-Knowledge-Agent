### Course Description
Students will learn the basics of first aid, rescue equipment, and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) within the context of marine environments. Topics include responding to emergencies; dealing with shock, unconsciousness, and choking; treating wounds and fractures; performing artificial respiration; and using an automated external defibrillator (AED) to respond to sudden cardiac arrest.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate and explain emergency scene management in relation to a responsive and unresponsive casualty.
- Describe how to deal with shock, unconsciousness, and fainting in a casualty.
- Demonstrate and explain the first aid procedures for a conscious choking adult, choking adult who becomes unconscious, choking adult who is pregnant or larger than the rescuer, choking adult found unconscious, and self-administered abdominal thrusts.
- Demonstrate and explain the procedures for performing artificial respiration on an adult, child, and infant using a jaw thrust with and without head tilt.
- Demonstrate and explain cardiovascular emergencies on an adult (1-Rescuer CPR, and 2-Rescuer CPR).
- Explain and demonstrate how to use an automated external defibrillator.
- Discuss different potential medical conditions.
- Describe rescue equipment.
- Demonstrate how to make narrow and broad bandages.
- Demonstrate and explain how to control severe bleeding on a casualty.

Successful students must have 100% attendance. A minimum grade of 70% is required to pass this course.
This two day course will be delivered in a blended format through the Academy of Emergency Training (AET).
Day 1 – Online training will be taught by AET. Prior to the course, students will be emailed the details on how to access the online training. This training must be completed prior to taking the in-class and examination portion of the course.
Day 2 – Students will be required to take the in-class two hour course and examination at AET (address located under Course Specific Requirements). Students will be notified in advance of their exam date and time.
Successful students will receive a Transport Canada Marine Basic First Aid training certificate.
