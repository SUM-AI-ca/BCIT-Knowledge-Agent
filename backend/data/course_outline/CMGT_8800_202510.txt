## Course Description
The goal of this course is to provide the student with an opportunity to work independently on an applied research project associated with a construction industry issue or procedure. Students work closely with an industry sponsor, under the mentorship of a faculty advisor to execute the research proposal that was developed in CMGT 8700. The students' pre-approved proposal outlines the project scope, goals, methods, and deliverables that forms the basis for this course. This course is intended to assess the students' ability to integrate the skills and knowledge gained from the Program with their individual work experience and apply these in a real-world context. In conjunction with an industry sponsor, the student solves a technical problem relating to construction. The research project must contain elements that are innovative, experimental, or exploratory in nature. A faculty advisor will supervise the progress of the project, provide guidance and direction where appropriate and evaluate the final report and its presentation. The faculty advisor submits their evaluation and the final grade is determined collaboratively with the Department.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Outline the current state of the specific construction issue identified in the approved proposal.
- Research the topic, systematically analyzing relevant construction methodologies.
- Propose and justify the recommended changes, efficiencies or resolution(s) with research-based arguments and data.
- Present the findings in a comprehensive professional management report.

## Evaluation Criteria
The grading system for the project will be:

S - Satisfactory (Pass) or U - Unsatisfactory (Fail)

The grade will be assigned after review of the deliverables identified in your proposal.

The review will include a report from the Faculty Advisor, comments from the Industry Sponsor and a final review by the Program Department.

## Course Specific Requirements
Final Project Deliverables - Important Notes:

- Final project deliverables will be accepted until the last day of the students assigned course dates. Submissions received after this date will not be graded.

- Final projects will be reviewed when all project documents, including the industry sponsor and faculty advisor reports, have been submitted to the Department.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
This course schedule outlines key milestones for the final industry project. Students are expected to build these into their project plans and adjust the timeframes as needed.

TARGET MONTH: KEY MILESTONES
1st Month: Project Kick-off Meeting: Separately meet with the industry sponsor and faculty advisor to discuss: project objectives; methodologies; schedules; deliverables; effective presentation options and formats; and personal project goals.
Project Progress Memos & Meetings: Define and schedule the timing of at least 2 progress meetings with the faculty advisor and industry sponsor. Provide a progress memo prior to each meeting for discussion.
4th Month: Preliminary Draft Submission (50%-60%): Allow time in project schedules for advisor review (1 month). Feedback from industry sponsors and faculty advisors is critical for project success (think consultants and clients). If feedback is not incorporated review rational with sponsor and advisor.
7th Month: Final Draft Submission (90%): Allow time in project schedule for advisor review (1 month). Review content and formatting in detail with both the industry sponsor and faculty advisor. Ensure formatting, title, labels, references, etc. all meet APA standards and are in accordance to project report guidelines used in the BTech CM program.
9th Month: Final Professional Technical Report: Final submission with all deliverables as outlined in the project proposal and any additions or amendments as discussed and approved by the industry sponsor and faculty advisor.
