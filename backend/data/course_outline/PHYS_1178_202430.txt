## Course Description
A general level course with emphasis on the application of basic physics principles to biomedical engineering technology. Topics covered include measurement techniques and reporting, equilibrium mechanics, stress and strain relationships, fluid statics and dynamics, wave motion with application to sound and ultrasound, and applied optics.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course, the student will be able to:

- Define basic terms for the concepts covered this term.
- Describe biophysical/biomedical examples related to the concepts covered.
- Solve single and double concept problems.
- Demonstrate correct measurement and data acquisition skills in a laboratory environment.

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

Define basic physical terms for the concepts covered this term. These include equilibrium mechanics, stress and strain relationships, fluid statics and dynamics priniciples, general wave descriptions with specific application to sound, ultrasound, and applied geometric and physical optics.

Use physical principles to describe biophysical/biomedical applications.

Follow a systematic, clearly presented approach to problem solving.

Acquire data acquisition, presentation, and analysis skills through laboratory practice sessions. These labs will be help to reinforce material covered in the lecture portion of the course.

Explain what a Joule is, how many Joules the average Canadian uses in a year, how many Joules the human population uses in a year and how many Joules the sun supplies the earth in a year so that they may view our current climate situation from an energy input/output perspective.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Assignments: The assignments are available using the Mobius system which you access through our learn.bcit.ca page.

Mobius will generate a random assignment for each student and will continue to generate random questions in the assignment until you get 100%. An assignment may only have 2 questions but Mobius could be selecting those questions from a set of 20 different questions. Once you get 100% on a question Mobius will bank that result and stop generating a new question for you to try. There is no penalty for generatling new questions. You are encouraged to use Mobius as a learning tool. Genearate an assignment. Try the first question, then simply grade the assignment without trying the remailning questions. View details to learn from any mistakes you made. Repeat until you get 100% on the whole assignement. If you want more practice then there are (Practice) versions of the assigments that will keep generating new assigments even after you get 100%.
