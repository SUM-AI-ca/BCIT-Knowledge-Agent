### Course Description
Through a series of video notes this course explores the wealth of statistical data Google Analytics provides, to track how visitor activity can help you streamline your design and guide your marketing strategies. By reviewing the data in Google Analytics reports, you will learn how to optimize different aspects of your site to increase visitors, maximize Conversion Rates, and reduce your Costs of Acquisition. The course also teaches the importance of your online persona and how to synchronize your brand across all Social Media outlets.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Set up Google Analytics and install tracking code.
- Manage user accounts.
- Defining your Marketing goals.
- Identify Key Performance Indicators.
- Study visitor behavioral data for device, location, operation system and point of entry.
- Apply filters and remove internal traffic.
- Identify key landing pages.
- Measure your Search Engine Optimization results.
- Manage Social Media Campaigns, including Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube.
- Generate and navigate demographic reports.
- Calculate Conversion Rates.
- Minimize the Cost of Acquisition.
- Improve your online persona.
- Synchronize with AdWords campaigns.
- Use the collected data to influence design and code changes.

Assignment 5: Search Engine Optimization | 10 | Optimize your site for a specific set of keywords and set up Structured Data for the different elements in your site to maximize the effectiveness of your keyword campaigns.
