### Course Description
This online course will provide the students with the theory required to engage in common Computed Tomography (CT) scan examinations. Emphasis is placed on fundamental principles of CT imaging, patient care and positioning, routine procedures and system maintenance. Students will understand in depth theory of intravenous contrast media use in CT and safety considerations of its use, including contraindications and reactions. Procedural focus on basic CT scan protocols for the head, neck, chest, abdomen and pelvis, spine and extremities are foundational to understanding CT in the clinical environment along with commonly occurring pathologies associated with the exam indications. Image reconstruction processes and methods that include anatomical considerations of axial, sagittal, and coronal planes will be included in learning outcomes.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain essential CT components using common terminology for image acquisition and processing.
- Describe the patient preparation, positioning and care for CT exams introduced of the head, spine, thorax, abdomen, and extremities.
- Recognize clinical indications for CT exams introduced and apply to the appropriate protocol selection.
- Recognize essential communication which promotes an efficient and safe environment.
- Prepare contrast agents for various clinical procedures​
- Outline the appropriate patient management procedures for contrast media contraindications and adverse reactions​
- Recognize important patient counselling information related to pre and post procedural care.
- Describe and evaluate protocol rationale and technical parameters for selected CT exams.
- Evaluate images in axial, coronal, and sagittal planes for diagnostic acceptability.
- Identify common pathologies on CT images related to protocols introduced.
- Identify resultant radiation exposure readings and exposure reduction methods in keeping with current safety standards.
- Identify common artifacts and recommended solutions.
- Identify commonly used accessory imaging equipment.
- Recognize drug therapy commonly used in CT.

### Course Goals
This course is dedicated to providing the foundational knowledge you will require to prepare you for your CT clinical rotations next semester. Throughout this course, we will be learning about various aspects relating to CT. This includes the principles and components of CT, how data is obtained and processed, patient care associated with CT exams, and common procedure and protocols for the different body areas.
