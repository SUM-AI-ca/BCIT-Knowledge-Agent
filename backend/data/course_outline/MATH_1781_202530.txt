## Course Description
Covers systems of linear equations and determinants with application to electrical, networks, logarithmic and exponential functions including the study of electrical transients, dB gain, logarithmic and semilog graphing, trigonometric functions and the graphs of the sinusoidal functions - right triangle geometry with application to impedance and admittance diagrams, complex numbers, rectangular/polar conversion and AC circuit applications, number base conversion and binary number operations with a brief introduction to Boolean logic and Karnaugh mapping.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Write systems of equations for multi-loop electrical networks.
- Solve system of equations using the method of determinants.
- Solve exponential equations relating to electrical transients.
- Solve logarithmic equations with application to decibels.
- Make effective use of log-log and exponential graphs with application to Bode plots.
- Sketch sinusoidal waveforms and demonstrate an understanding of phase lag / lead.
- Solve simple trigonometric equations.
- Use complex numbers to perform calculations using rectangular, polar, exponential forms and vector like calculations.
- Understand the complex frequency spectrum, an application of Euler's formula.
- Use complex numbers to solve ac circuit problems.
- Work with binary, octal, and hexadecimal number systems.
- Set up logic problems using Boolean algebra and use methods for simplifying Boolean functions.

## Course topics
Unit 1 - Algebra Review and Linear Circuit Analysis Topics

- A brief review of algebra
- The basic algebraic fundamentals for setting up electrical circuits
- Solving systems of linear equation using determinants
- Properties of determinants
- Using loop equation for solving electrical circuits

Unit 2 - Exponential and Logarithmic Equations Topics

- Working with logarithms - any base
- Solving logarithmic equations
- Application of logarithms - calculating decibels
- Solving and manipulating exponential equations
- Exponential decay/growth - circuit time constant
- Semilog and log-log graphs with application to Bode plots

Unit 3 - Complex Numbers Theory and Trigonometry Topics

- Radian measure and the properties of the sine, cosine and tangent functions
- The inverse trigonometric functions - solving simple trigonometric equations
- Graphing y = cos(x + φ), y = sin(x + φ), and y = tan(x)
- Representing sinusoidals as functions of time - amplitude, period, frequency, phase angle, lag/lead
- Graphing y = Asin(ωt + φ) and y = Acos(ωt + φ)
- Introduction to imaginary numbers and the complex plane
- Addition, multiplication, division of complex numbers using the rectangular form
- Other representations of the complex number: - The polar form - The exponential form - Working with real/imaginary parts
- The complex conjugate and absolute value of a complex number
- Representing sinusoidals as rotation vectors in the complex plane - the phasor
- Representing alternating voltages and currents as phasors in both the rectangular and polar form
- The complex frequency spectrum, an application using Euler's formula
- Application of complex numbers to AC circuits

Unit 4 - Number Systems and Binary Logic Topics

- Writing numbers using decimal, binary, octal and hexadecal forms
- Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division using different base representation
- two's complement representation of binary numbers
- Fundamentals of digital logic and design of simple logical devices
- Using Boolean algebra and Kaungh mapping to simply logic designs
