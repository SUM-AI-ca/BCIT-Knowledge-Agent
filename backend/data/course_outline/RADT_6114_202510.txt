## Course Description
The second in a series of courses examining the fundamentals of radiation physics and the principles and concepts of dosimetry in the application of beam data to obtain the optimal treatment plan for each patient. Examines the physical applications and concepts relevant to teletherapy and brachytherapy equipment, photon and electron interactions, and beam data collection. Lectures are reinforced with numerical examples and students solve numerical problems where appropriate.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe the production of x-rays from kilovoltage machines.
- Describe the production of x-rays from linacs.
- Describe brachytherapy sources.
- Define PDD and apply in mathematical problem sets.
- Calculate equivalent squares.
- Calculate dose to a point using given data.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Week: Outcome/Material Covered
Week 1: Review of Physics I Atomic structure X-ray production Photon interactions
Week 2: Basic Concepts in Radiation Physics Introduction to dosimetry Kerma and absorbed dose Dose measurement
Week 3: Cavity Theory Introduction to Cavity Theory Practical applications
Week 4: Clinical Radiation Generators Machine types Linear accelerator components
Week 5: Radiation Protection Fundamentals of radiation protection Monitoring and regulation Structural shielding design
Week 6: Particle Accelerators Types of accelerators Heavy particle therapy
Week 7: MIDTERM I (20%)
Week 8: Radiation Dosmietry Systems Dosimeter types Key properties Calibration and standards
Week 9: Photon Beam Dosimetry Dose distributions Visualization Clinical applications
Week 10: SPRING BREAK
Week 11: Photon Beam Dosimetry Percent depth dose Beam profiles and symmetry Isodose distributions
Week 12: Electron Beam Dosimetry Percent depth dose Beam profiles and symmetry Isodose distributions
Week 13: MIDTERM II (20%)
