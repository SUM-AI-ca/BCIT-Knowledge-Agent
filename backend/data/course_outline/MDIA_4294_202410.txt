## Course Description
Focusing on PHP and MySQL, students will develop and integrate a Content Management System (CMS) into a website for inclusion in their portfolio. Students will also install and modify an industry standard off-the-shelf CMS system, such as WordPress or Drupal. Knowledge and ability to hand code HTML and CSS is required. Students will be assessed by lab exercises, quizzes, and individual projects.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop and manage static and dynamic websites using programming standards, content management systems (CMS) and versioning control frameworks.
- Development of a starter theme, exploring various WordPress specific PHP functions.
- Install and manage extensions (plug-ins), of an industry standard CMS system.
- Explore workflow in commercial theme development.
- Identify market standards and current offerings from third party developers.
- Managing and securing CMS driven sites, including sever migrations.

## Course Specific Requirements
Students will require current installations of Google Chrome or Firefox, for testing local and remote installation of CMS applications. Local installation of a virtual server (MAMP or Xampp required) along with an FTP application. The ability to open and review PDF documents is highly recommended.
