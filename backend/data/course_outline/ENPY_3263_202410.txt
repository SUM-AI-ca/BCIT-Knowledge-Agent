## Course Description
Learn the knowledge about the correlations between clinical presentation and electroneurophysiologic findings of the most common neuromuscular diseases. These include: Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS), Charcot Marie Tooth (HMSN Type I)/CMT, Chronic Inflammatory Demyelinating Polyneuropathy (CIDP), Guillain-Barre Syndrome/Acquired Inflammatory Demyelinating Polyneuropathy (AIDP), Lambert Eaton Myasthenic Syndrome (LEMS), Myasthenia Gravis, Werdnig-Hoffman (SMA) and Kugelberg-Welander (adult onset SMA), Diabetic peripheral neuropathy, and other peripheral nerve injuries and disease processes that may be present. Correlate patient history and clinical symptoms in order to select appropriate nerve conduction studies for patient’s peripheral nerve evaluation. Students engage in evaluation of clinical findings and analysis of combined electrophysiologic pattern of abnormalities on nerve conduction studies in most common neuromuscular diseases. Evaluation of learning is conducted through a range of online activities, case study presentation, quizzes, and examination.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Recognize a range of clinical symptoms of common neuromuscular diseases.
- Select appropriate nerve conduction study protocol for patient’s peripheral nerve evaluation
- Analyze obtained nerve conduction study results and, if abnormal, characterize the findings on NCS and localize the lesion
- Differentiate between common neuromuscular diseases by recognizing electrophysiologic patterns of abnormalities on nerve conduction studies and describe how they correlate to patient clinical symptoms.
- Collaborate on case studies with peers to check accuracy of conclusions regarding correlations of patient’s clinical presentation and combined patterns of abnormalities on nerve conduction studies (motor, sensory, late responses, repetitive nerve stimulation and needle EMG).
- Distinguish (not interpret) the difference between normal and abnormal waveform parameters and calculated conduction velocities.
