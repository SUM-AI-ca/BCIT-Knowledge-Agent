## Course Description
Develops personal problem-solving skills, with emphasis on role playing, teamwork and analysis by students acting in a variety of supervisory/management situations. Participant have the opportunity to practice skills that will enhance their effectiveness in working with people. These skills include effective listening, assertive communication and conflict resolution. Upon completion of the course, students should have a clear appreciation of the interpersonal skills required for effective supervision and management.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
The following is representative of the key skills we will be working on throughout the course:

- Self Awareness
- Listening Skills/Communication Skills
- Group Dynamics/Teamwork
- Goal Setting/Time Management
- Politics/Negotiations
- Assertiveness
- Conflict Resolution
- Stress Management

Role-playing, case studies and written assignments will play a major role in this course. Individual classes will include instructor presentations, videos, role-plays, group projects and the presentation and analysis of case studies.

## Course Specific Requirements
DISCUSSION ASSIGNMENTS GRADING CRITERIA

Details

The discussion assignments are experiential learning activities. Each week you will be given an exercise to complete that is designed to help you develop your interpersonal skills.

Since the discussion assignments are learning activities, you will not be graded on whether you get the answers right or wrong, but rather on the criteria listed below. Your grade will be determined by the quality, frequency, and timeliness of your discussion assignments. To receive full marks for the discussion assignments, you must complete each discussion assignment AND respond to two other students for each discussion assignment.

Each discussion activity is worth 10 points except for the student introduction which is automatically awarded 2 points and the Coffee Across the Divide exercise which is worth 20 points.

Posts are due on Sundays and replies to classmate are due on Tuesdays. Late posts are penalized 10% per day and will not be accepted after 2 days past the due date. Students must submit their discussion assignments by the late deadline to avoid being recorded as absent for that week of the course. Late replies are not accepted.

To receive credit for your discussion assignment, you must use specific concepts and terms from the course text, and other materials in the relevant learning module, in your post and the course terms must be highlighted in bold.

The unauthorized use of outside information sources, including Artificial Intelligence products (such as ChatGPT), for discussion assignments is prohibited, and when identified by your instructor, will result in an academic integrity violation. Refer to policy 5104 - Student Code of Academic Integrity

Very Good to Excellent (8.5 to 10 points)

- Post addresses ALL components of the discussion assignment questions
- Demonstrates strong analysis and insight into the question and the related course materials (text, videos, self-assessments, articles etc.) listed in the relevant learning module
- Relevant terms, theories and concepts from all of the course learning materials are applied as appropriate
- Shares relevant experiences and references current events that relate to the discussion topic
- Replies to classmates add value to the discussion by stating reasons if you agree or disagree with a comment or statement and/or offering insights based on your own experience and knowledge of the course materials.
- Responds to questions posed by other students and/or the instructor

Fair to Good (5 to 8.4 points)

- Post addresses most components of the discussion question
- Demonstrates some analysis and insight into the question and the related course materials
- A few relevant course terms, theories and concepts from some of the course learning materials are applied as appropriate
- Shares a little relevant experience but does not reference current events that relate to the discussion topic
- Reply to classmate is completed but adds limited value to the discussion
- Does not always respond to questions posed by other students and/or the instructor

Unsatisfactory (0 to 4.9 points)

- Post does not address most components of the discussion question
- Demonstrates little to no analysis and insight into the question and the related course materials
- Does not apply relevant course terms, theories and concepts from the course learning materials
- Does not share relevant experiences and does not reference current events that relate to the discussion topic
- Reply to classmate not completed
- Does not respond to questions posed by other students and/or the instructor
- Uses disrespectful and/or discriminatory language when interacting with others
