## Course Description
This course covers the principles and systems of manufacturing and construction, using wood and synthetic materials. Using design, planning and processing skills, students will produce individual as well as group solutions to manufacturing challenges appropriate for public school Technology Education programs. Appropriate safe work and material handling procedures will be required throughout the course.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course the student will:

- Demonstrate a clear understanding of the standard of safety required in school shops.
- Employ correct terminology when discussing a range of woodworking tools and machines.
- Design a process plan for a wood manufacturing challenge.
- Demonstrate the proper operation of woodworking tools, machines and processes in a range of tasks.
