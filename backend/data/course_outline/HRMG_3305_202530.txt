## Course Description
This is an advanced course for students specializing in human resource management. The learning outcomes address both the knowledge and skills to design and implement effective recruitment and selection processes and/or to critique and improve existing processes. The outcomes also address the specific skills to conduct selection interviewing. The course materials are designed to be relevant for a human resource professional in either an organizational or consulting capacity.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:

- Develop a comprehensive understanding of the processes and systems for effective recruitment and selection of employees.
- Prepare for and conduct behavior based selection interviews and panel interview.
- Research and understand current issues related to the recruitment and selection of employees.
- Appreciate the application of statistical concepts as they relate to recruitment and selection.
- Understand the relevant legal framework in both unionized and non-unionized settings.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of an organization's recruitment and selection process and make improvements where indicated.

## Course Specific Requirements
Skill Practice 1 – Environmental Assessment

- This is an individual assignment. Students are required to present the organization that they will be using for the duration of the course. This organization will be used for the next three skill practices. Effectively building off the previous skill practice.
- An overview of the five most critical issues that you see affecting recruitment and selection over the next five years. This can be a summary of existing or new trends that you see positively or negatively affecting recruitment. Your summary should provide examples and arguments on why you see these five as the most important (10 marks)
- An introduction of the organization of choice. This should also include trends that are affecting this organization that may be unique to its own challenges (5 marks)
- The organization and presentation of your ideas, insights and quality of the written report (5 marks)

Skill Practice 2 – Recruitment Strategy

- This is an individual assignment. Students are required to prepare a written report (approximately 3-4 pages) that develops a recruitment strategy for a real organization of the students choosing. This organization must be unique from other students. The scope of the report should include, but is not limited to:
- Incorporating your skill practice 1, prepare the recruitment strategy and job analysis using Chapter 1 to 4 (15 marks)
- The organization and presentation of your ideas, insights and quality of the written report (5 marks)

Skill Practice 3 – Job Design

- This is an individual assignment. Students are required to prepare a written report (approximately 2-3 pages) that describes and provides a critique of the performance management process of an organization using Chapter 5. The scope of the report should include, but is not limited to:
- An outline of the competency model for the role (Chapter 4), how the organization will measure the performance of the job (Chapter 5) (10 marks)
- A step-by-step guide on how the organization recruit for the role using Chapter 6 (5 marks)
- The organization and presentation of your ideas, insights and quality of the written report (5 marks)

Skill Practice 4 – Selection and Recruitment Design

- This is an individual assignment. Students are to outline the recommended selection and interview process using content from Chapter 7, 8 and 9. The scope of the report should include, but is not limited to:
- Include the screening process, recruitment and selection process, avoiding discrimination, improving interview effectiveness, removing selection errors, and your reliability and validation strategies for the hiring process (10 marks)
- The skill practice should also include presenting a strong argument as to why these approaches were taken over others (5 marks)
- The organization and presentation of your ideas, insights and quality of the written report (5 marks)
