### Course Description
​This course is designed to help students further develop the professional attributes needed to be a successful teacher in BC middle and high schools. Faculty will again meet with each student to assess and discuss their current proficiency with the professional attributes needed to be a successful teacher and offer suggestions for continued growth.​​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the attributes needed for successful teaching
- Reflect on personal practice and behaviours as they relate to the stipulated attributes
- Act on advice provided by faculty
- Meet the expectations in the professional attributes rubric used in this course​

TTED 4444 & 5555 Assessment Rubric
Professional Qualities | Not yet meeting expectations - U | Approaching expectations - S | Meeting expectations - S
1. Demonstrates enthusiasm and positive attitude for teaching and learning | demonstrates a negative attitude or lacks enthusiasm for teaching/learning | generally demonstrates a positive attitude and/or enthusiasm for teaching/learning | consistently demonstrates a positive attitude and enthusiasm for teaching/learning
2. Demonstrates a commitment to high professional and ethical standards | demonstrates very little professionalism in attitude, appearance and/or demeanor has difficulty behaving in a professional and ethical manner | generally demonstrates a professional attitude, appearance and demeanor behaves in a professional and ethical manner, but September need support | demonstrates professionalism in attitude, appearance and demeanor at all times behaves in a professional and ethical manner at all times
3. Is dependable, punctual, proactive and responsible | is not dependable, punctual and responsible does not initiate action independently, even when prompted | is usually dependable, punctual and responsible needs prompting before initiating any actions | is consistently dependable, punctual and responsible initiates action independently without reminders
4. Is respectful and cooperative with classmates, instructors and staff | is frequently disrespectful and/or uncooperative | is respectful and cooperative most of the time | is consistently respectful and cooperative in all interactions
5. Communicates effectively and appropriately | does not show initiative to communicate even when prompted does not communicate in an effective or appropriate manner | communicates infrequently, or only after repeated reminders needs support to communicate in an effective and appropriate manner | initiates regular communication communicates effectively and appropriately
6. Contributes to the classroom and Tech Ed community | does not contribute to the Tech Ed community | contributes to the Tech Ed community when approached or in a very limited capacity | actively seeks to participate in, and contributes to, the Tech Ed community
7. Demonstrates effective time management throughout the program | does not manage time effectively | adequately manages time | manages time effectively in all areas of the program
Inquiry & Reflective Practice | Not yet meeting expectations - U | Approaching expectations - S | Meeting expectations - S
8. Is open to and acts on advice to improve professional practice | is defensive or argumentative when receiving constructive criticism does not act on feedback from instructors | is generally open to receiving constructive criticism has difficulty implementing feedback effectively and/or in a timely manner | accepts constructive criticism, without defense implements changes effectively, in a timely manner
9. Is reflective in their practice | unable to identify strengths or areas that require improvement unable to articulate reflective process (self-questioning and action) | is able to identify strengths and areas that require improvement, with guidance able to articulate reflective process if asked or with support | consistently and accurately self-identifies strengths and areas that require improvement able to articulate reflective process
10. Engages in constructive conversations with instructors | is a passive participant in conversations with instructors | at times is able to provide feedback and/or commentary during conversations | is an active and involved participant in all conversations with instructors
Classroom Climate | Not yet meeting expectations - U | Approaching expectations - S | Meeting expectations - S
11. Develops rapport with classmates, instructors and staff | demonstrates very little rapport with peers, or shows rapport with a very limited number of students interactions with students September be inappropriate or negative | is beginning to develop rapport with (more) peers interactions with peers are becoming more appropriate and positive | has developed a strong rapport with most peers interactions with peers are consistently appropriate and positive
12. Works safely at all times | does not demonstrate appropriate/safe classroom procedures and routines | demonstrates appropriate/safe classroom procedures and routines on a somewhat consistent basis | effectively demonstrates safe classroom procedures and routines contributes to shop safety
Diversity, Inclusion, Communication & Self-Regulation | Not yet meeting expectations - U | Approaching expectations - S | Meeting expectations - S
13. Shows an understanding of all people through inclusive language and practices Sensitivity to ability, language, culture, religion, gender and sexual orientation | is not aware of the individual differences in all people | shows awareness of the individual differences of all people | demonstrates understanding of the individual differences of all people
14. Models appropriate written and oral language at all times | does not model appropriate written and oral language when communicating with others communicates with frequent errors | needs the occasional reminder to model appropriate written and oral language when communicating with others communicates with occasional errors | effectively and consistently models professional written and oral language when communicating with others communicates without errors
15. Can self-regulate emotions. | no real ability to consistently regulate emotions. there is little evident ability to recognise specific emotions as they arise there is also a low understanding of how one might better manage one’s emotions. | only emerging ability to modulate strong emotions. self-awareness is only beginning to become evident, and this is not consistent in all contexts. productivity in other areas are often impacted detrimentally because of low ability to regulate emotions or take emotional responses out of the equation to enable better focus or interactions. | clear emotional strengths can handle own emotions able to recover from disappointment, challenging situations and move forward confidently and positively.
