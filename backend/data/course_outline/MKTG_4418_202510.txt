## Course Description
Students will work off-campus one day a week on a major project related to their marketing field of study. This project typically relates to a specific client company and is carried out under the guidance of assigned faculty members.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop effective public speaking skills by delivering oral presentations (individual and team) to audiences of diverse sizes.
- Identify and define a practical marketing problem or challenge faced by an individual or organization with whom you do not have a pre-existing relationship.
- Define the project objectives, conduct appropriate research, develop conclusions, and formulate recommendations to appropriately address the problem or challenge.
- Create an in-depth written report covering all aspects of the project.
- Deliver a face-to-face presentation to the sponsor and the Faculty Advisor about the project.
- Use effective leadership, teamwork and project management skills while working within a self-managed group of three students to complete the Directed Studies project.

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Identify and define a practical marketing problem or challenge faced by a BC-based organization in either the private or public sector and whether it operates for profit or not.
- Develop a work plan that includes substantial marketing research, the results from which will be used to develop a ‘business development plan’ for the client.
- Demonstrate, through application, clear knowledge of marketing research skills learned in MKTG3309.
- Demonstrate the ability to turn research results into recommendations that form the basis of a ‘business development plan’ designed to move a client’s organization forward.
- Deliver a face-to-face presentation to the sponsor and the Faculty Advisor about the project.
- Use effective leadership, teamwork and project management skills while working within a self-managed group of three students to complete the Business Consulting Project.
