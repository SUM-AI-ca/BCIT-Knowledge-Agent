### Course Description
​This course provides the comprehensive knowledge and practical skills necessary for ensuring the safe and responsible use of radiation in the field of nuclear medicine. Students will gain an understanding of federal and provincial regulations governing the use of radiation, licensing requirements, and the importance of adhering to radiation safety protocols. Additionally, the effects of radiation on cells will be explored, along with strategies for minimizing radiation exposure and effectively communicating risks to patients, staff, and the public. Practical aspects of radiation protection, such as the appropriate use of protective equipment, implementation of personal protective measures, and the application of the ALARA (As Low As Reasonably Achievable) principle, will also be covered. Students will learn the necessary steps to respond to incidents involving radioactivity, ensuring a prompt and efficient handling of such situations.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply the federal and provincial requirements for the safe use of radiation and licensing. (1.1.2, 2.2.4, NM.2.1, NM.2.4, NM.2.5)
- Discuss the effects of radiation on cells. (NM.1.6)
- Develop basic communication skills to summarize the risks of radiation exposure and strategies for radiation protection. (2.1.6, 5.2.4)
- Demonstrate appropriate use of radiation protection equipment. (NM.1.8, NM.2.3)
- Ensure a safe working environment by implementing personal protective equipment and using proper body mechanics. (NM.1.1, NM.1.2, NM.1.3, NM.4.1)
- Using the ALARA principle, demonstrate appropriate radiation protection measures, such as dosimetry, shielding, distance, and time, to minimize radiation exposure. (NM.1.5, NM.1.7)
- List the steps to respond to incidents involving radioactivity. (NM.2.6, NM.2.7, NM.1.10, 5.4.1)​

### Course Schedule and Assignments
Week 1 Introduction and units
Week 2 Radiation detection equipment
Week 3 Spill Response, contamination checks and emergencies
Week 4 Decay and shielding calculations, 99m Tc/ 99 Mo Generators: receiving, storage and dismantling
Week 5 Regulations
Week 6 Midterm #1
Week 7 Regulations (continued)
Week 8 Biological interactions and cellular responses to radiation
Week 9 Tissue and organ response, effects on embryo and fetus
Week 10 Midterm 2
Week 11 Practical means of radiation protection, dosimeters and accessory equipment
