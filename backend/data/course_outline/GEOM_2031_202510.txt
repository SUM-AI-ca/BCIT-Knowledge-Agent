## Course Description
​This course focuses on skills in an integrated coordinate geometry and drafting environment, with an emphasis on MicroSurvey and Autodesk Civil 3D software applications. Through lectures, labs and assignments, students will import and process survey data to produce industry-standard survey plans. When possible, field data from concurrent field surveying courses will be used as example data sets.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Use required software applications.
- Upload and download electronic field survey data.
- Process collected field data to create industry-standard survey plans through a series of field-to-finish steps.
- Output processed field measurements for other key industry-specific needs, including survey control files and ASCII import/export through the creation of a survey database.
- Enter raw field traverse data and adjust through specialized software routines including least-squares adjustments and Helmerts transformation.
- Create drawing templates that align with land title drafting standards and other industry standards (including the creation of proper symbols, lines, texts, annotations, and so on).
- Produce industry-standard 2D and 3D legal and engineering drawings.
- Build extensive 3D surface models and create topographic survey plans.
- Undertake land parcel subdivision and area calculations using COGO functions.
- Fit building layouts to property lots with setback requirements.​

## Attendance Requirements
It is the policy of the BCIT Geomatics program to not accept late assignments. Students failing to submit weekly labs or assignments on time will receive a mark of zero for the missed labs or assignments.

Assignments and weekly labs are often performed in teams or as groups. Failing to attend without prior notice puts your team members in a difficult position and may prevent them from completing their work on time.
