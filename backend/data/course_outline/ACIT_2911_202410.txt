## Course Description
This course provide students with hands-on experience developing application software using an Agile methodology such as Scrum. Collaboration and teamwork are used to continuously develop software over a series of iterations. Students learn to cope with changes, to identify and manage multiple work tasks, and to focus on maintaining a potentially releasable product throughout the project.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe a modern agile development framework.
- Describe the roles and responsibilities on an agile development project.
- Plan and schedule work using agile development techniques.
- Create the artifacts associated with an agile development project.
- Maintain a potentially releasable product throughout agile project.
- Use modern version control applications to manage project source code and related artifacts.
- Use continuous integration with unit testing, to maintain software quality.
- Use functional testing tools to assure proper project behaviour.
- Use modern collaboration software throughout an agile development project.
- Participate in the various project meetings associated with an agile development project.

## Course Goals
Students will undertake a team project, extending previous technology and adding skills for the development of large-scale solution systems. They will apply the core technical knowledge acquired in the program so far, completing the project by collaborating with their team members.

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance in lectures, labs, team meetings and presentations (supervised/unsupervised) is mandatory. Students are required to attend lectures, supervisor meetings and presentations with your team members during regular school hours. Attendance will be taken in all scheduled online lectures (no makeup lectures). Students are required to attend and participate all scheduled meetings with team members.

## Course Specific Requirements
This course includes mandatory group activities. Lack of participation may result in failure of the course.

Course Website: All course materials are posted on the Learning Hub. Please monitor it for announcements, submission dates and assignments.

Group: Each team will have a maximum of 6 members. All teams are pre-assigned by instructors at the beginning of the course and posted on the Learning Hub. Students are required to stay within their assigned team unless withdrawing from the course. Instructors reserve the right to move students into other groups to ensure successful execution of the project.

Group Communication: Each group is responsible for creating an online communication channel, using tools such as Discord or Slack, to enable remote collaboration among team members.

Quizzes: There are four quizzes based on the online learning materials, lecture materials, and the lab practice. Students will receive a grade of zero if they miss the quiz. Note: There are no makeup quizzes.

## Course topics
Project topics and technologies will be covered in instructional seminars every week. Students are required to attend each instructional seminar (Check your weekly schedule on the Learning Hub) on time. All teams will conduct a weekly check-in meeting with their respective supervisors. In addition, the students will have additional unsupervised collaboration labs to complete their group tasks.

The project that the students undertake will be completed using Agile development practices. There will be five one-week iterations that produce required project artifacts or feature deliverables starting in Week 2.

The individuals and teams will conduct iteration reviews between iterations, and a project review at the end of the course. These will be assessed by the instructors. Additional comments and guidance will also be provided.
