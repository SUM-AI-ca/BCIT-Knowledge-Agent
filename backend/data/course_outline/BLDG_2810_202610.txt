### Course Description
This course covers the basics of Autodesk Revit in an architectural environment, from design through construction documentation. Students will be introduced to the tools and concepts of working with a fully parametric building modeler.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use a project-based learning approach throughout the major steps of a project design.
- Understand the overall concept of Revit.
- Organize a project within Revit: setup templates, create new project and views.
- Create basic building envelope and use components families such as walls, floors, stairs, roofs & ceiling.
- Generate basic schedules (door, finishes, room, etc.)
- ​Produce basic 3D views​.

### Course Goals
- Provide students with an understanding of BIM concepts and the advantages of BIM over conventional CAD drafting
- Familiarize students with the essential modeling and annotation tools available in Revit, allowing them to be productive members of a Revit project team
- Equip students with the knowledge required to take a project from schematic design through to working drawings in the Revit environment, and the workflow of a typical project

### Course Specific Requirements
This course require students to work on assignments and projects outside of class time. Having your own computer that can run Revit is recommended but not necessary. BCIT students are eligible for a year educational licence of Revit at no cost. Revit is only available on the Windows platform. Please check Autodesk’s website for minimum hardware and operating system requirements for Revit.
