### Course Description
This course presents a general survey of codes and regulations affecting the design and construction of buildings. It focuses on the British Columbia Building Code, latest edition, with particular reference to Part 3: Fire Protection, Occupant Safety and Accessibility, and the control of fire hazards. Lab exercises provide students with an opportunity to practice code applications for a variety of building conditions. Students completing this course will be expected to carry out a basic building code plan review analysis for complex Part 3 buildings.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe the content and layout of BC Building Code, latest edition.
- Classify a building according to occupancy and determine the relevant construction requirements for different uses.
- Classify multiple use buildings and determine relevant construction requirements for different uses.
- Determine fire separations between spaces, including firewalls, shaft requirements, and service spaces.
- Determine areas of openings, spatial separation, construction requirements, and other requirements for exposing building face.
- Describe fire and life safety related code requirements for building design.
- Determine fire fighting provisions along with features and systems.
- Determine means of egress in buildings and calculate requirements for corridors, stairs, doors, and ramps.
- Determine vertical transportation, service spaces, health requirement and plumbing facilities to meet BC Building Code, latest issue.
- Determine code requirements for persons with disabilities and design accordingly.
- Discuss a broad range of codes, bylaws and statutes which affect construction in BC.
- Discuss the limitations in scope of practice as related to the Registered Professionals.​

### Course Goals
The course goals are to make students aware of the impact that codes and regulations have on the design, construction, and ongoing use of buildings. A more detailed analysis of Part 3 in the BCBC, latest edition, is conducted through the lectures and the laboratory exercises. The laboratory exercises also provide experience and practice in code applications for a variety of situations.

### Course Specific Requirements
The course provides an understanding of the requirements of Part 3 of the BC Building Code and includes the basic skills required to understand and apply fundamental building code concepts for design and construction.
The student will have an:
- Awareness of the relevant codes, regulations, and statutes that have an influence on the design and construction of buildings in British Columbia, and
- Understanding of the rationale for determining the fire protection, occupant safety, and accessibility requirements of the British Columbia Building Code, specifically Part 3.
The student will be expected to provide clear explanations and applicable building code references in order to support all answers and/or solutions on assignments and exams. Students are expected to participate in classroom discussions and workshop sessions to demonstrate a working knowledge of the building code.
