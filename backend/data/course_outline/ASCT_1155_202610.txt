### Course Description
This course presents information required to develop a set of construction drawings for a single family wood-frame structure. It introduces students to materials, methods and details specific to residential construction. The course is delivered through in-depth review of drawing sets for a single family wood-frame house and project work on floor plans, elevations, roof plans, foundations, schedules, and details.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Properly use symbols and abbreviations commonly used in wood-frame construction documents.
- Properly utilize dimensions, layers, line types and hatching patterns.
- Prepare detailed working drawings for residential structure using the graphic language, conventions, and standards of the construction industry.

### Course topics
Week 1: Introduction to Construction Drawings and Standards
- Symbols, line weights, notes
- Borderline, title block
- Setback, Floor Area Ratio (FAR)
Week 2: Wood Frame Floor Plans
- Wood frame details
- Joints, components, and assemblies
Week 3: Wood-Framed Roofs
- Types of roofs
- Roof pitch, trusses, rafters
Week 4: Sections and Elevations of Wood Frame Residential House
- Creating sections and elevations
- Details, hangers
Week 5: Stairs
- Calculating risers and treads
- Stringers, total run and rise
- Handails
Week 6: Details and Finishes in Wood Frame Residential Building
- Materials
- Assemblies
Week 7: Building Codes and BCBC
- Part 9 of BC Building Code
Week 8: BC Building Permit Requirements
- Drawing scales
- Sheet size, scale, and details
Week 9: Exam Preparation and Final Review
- Reviewing final submission
- Exam materials
Week 10: Final Exam and Submission
