## Course Description
​In this course, students apply learned information technology skills in a relevant external industry environment. They spend 15 weeks working part-time under the direction of a workplace supervisor in an information technology role within an external organization. There they actively participate in the various aspects of day to day operations that are related to the CIT program.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Actively participate in a company’s day-to-day information technology operations according to industry standards.
- Solve common problems and respond to day-to-day challenges working both independently and in collaboration with others.
- Communicate effectively as a team member.
- Take direction from supervisors and show initiative in the work place
- Apply a multitude of skills effectively, appropriate to the demands of the given tasks, timelines and workplace environment.
- Complete assigned tasks in a timely fashion while conforming to relevant policy and regulations.

## Evaluation Criteria
The student’s progress on the job is monitored by the instructor and their performance on the job is supervised on an ongoing basis by the student’s practicum employer. Students must complete the minimum required work hours to receive credit.
The practicum course consists of the following evaluation components: Midterm employer’s evaluation A practicum report End-of-term employer’s evaluation: The following course grades may be assigned: - Satisfactory: All course requirements fulfilled. - Unsatisfactory: Course requirements not fulfilled. Students must receive a Satisfactory on all evaluation components to pass this course.
