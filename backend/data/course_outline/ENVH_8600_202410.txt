## Course Description
Focuses on the application and integration of environmental health concepts and principles in a real-life work situation (off-campus practicum). Enhances skills in critical thinking, problem solving, decision-making, communication and interpersonal relationships through participation in a variety of programs within an environmental health or other approved agency. Twelve weeks long, requires hours of work as assigned by the agency.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Complete site inspections, facility evaluations and assessments, and other assigned duties independently.
- Carry out investigative field activities necessary to analyze and assess environmental health concerns.
- Utilize appropriate reporting methods to recommend resolution of potential or confirmed environmental health risks.
- Work within the culture of the organization.
- Communicate effectively with clients and members of the organization.
- Employ appropriate interpersonal skills in dealing with clients and members of the organization.
- Utilize the environmental health information systems currently in use by the host agency for day-to-day inspectional activities.
- Demonstrate entry-level competence in a range of environmental health-related activities.

## Evaluation Criteria
Total hours as specified by the Agency - 420 hours is required (7 hrs X 5 X 12).

Evaluation will be based on the CIPHI BOC Final Evaluation form (Form C) received from the Student Coordinator responsible for monitoring the student’s practical experience. Students achieving 65% or more on their final evaluation will receive a Satisfactory and successfully complete the course. The Midterm and Final Evaluation forms (both Form C) are provided on the Learning Hub.

## Attendance Requirements
Students must complete 12 weeks (420 hours) of practicum.

## Course Specific Requirements
• Students are responsible for the costs associated with relocation to the practicum site (if applicable), daily travel to and from the agency and accommodation during the practicum period.

• Students are expected to work the hours specified by the practicum site.

• Students have the opportunity to complete the reports required by the Board of Certification (see the Board of Certificate Candidate Information package).

• In the extraordinary circumstance where it is not possible to complete this practical experience, documentation satisfactory to the BCIT Practicum Coordinator must be immediately submitted to justify this circumstance.

Note: All documentation must be submitted by the last day of the practicum period.
