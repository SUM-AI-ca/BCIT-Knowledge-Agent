## Course Description
(UBC BIOT 231) This course will introduce you to the writing, speaking, research and presentation skills you will need to successfully carry out your professional communication tasks. You will learn how to organize and present technical information in memos and reports. In COMM 3343, your research on a controversial biotechnology topic will be incorporated into both a formal written report and an oral presentation. As industry requires high literacy levels, we will continue to focus on English grammar and editing skills.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Follow a systematic process in planning and writing technical documents.
- Select and organize information to meet the needs of your readers.
- Use a style and tone appropriate for your purpose and audience.
- Write clear, concise, and correct sentences.
- Make your writing more accessible by using headings, stacked lists, white space, and layout.
- Use effective organizational patterns and conventional formats in writing memos, letters, and reports.
- Prepare and use effective visuals in documents and oral presentations.
- Prepare a formal research report.
- Prepare and deliver an effective oral presentation.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.

## Course Goals
Prepare to be a competitive applicant in the biotechnology job climate:

- Craft a polished resume, cover letter and email summary tailored to a biotech job posting
- Develop a LinkedIn profile and understand how to use it for networking and job search

Enhance scientific research and writing abilities:

- Formulate a precise research question
- Conduct thorough literature searches using advanced techniques and tools
- Compile and evaluate scientific sources for a literature review

Refine modern workplace communication techniques:

- Write and evaluate persuasive workplace messages utilizing both traditional and AI tools
- Participate in peer evaluations to improve skills in giving and receiving feedback
- Structure and deliver an effective oral presentation on your literature review topic
- Design visual aids, such as slides and infographics, to communicate field-specific information effectively

Demonstrate professional conduct through an engaged class presence, collaborative teamwork, and adherence to deadlines
