## Course Description
Project teams complete an industrial mechatronics project which involves integrating various skills learned throughout the program such as mechanical design and fabrication, electronics, and programming. Teams develop and present written and oral presentations of their proposed and completed designs.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Apply mechatronics principles to an industrial problem.
- Work as a member of a team on a practical industrial problem.
- Conduct a needs analysis to establish project objectives.
- Establish manufacturing considerations.
- Develop alternative solutions.
- Select the solution to be implemented.
- Prepare status reports and communicate status to the client and project supervisor.
- Plan and schedule the solution of an industrial problem.
- Prepare a final report documenting the project and analyzing the results.

## Learning Resources
In this course, students are assigned a project. The students should design, build and demonstrate this project. The students are encouraged to use whatever resources (datasheets, application notes, books e.t.c.) needed to demonstrate their knowledge and abilities.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
Steel toed boots and safety goggles are required in any of the Mechanical Depertment shops. The permission of an instructor of this course is required to use power equipment . The protocol to use the shops will be explained in the first lecture of the course.

Students are expected to prioritize safety when working in the labs.
