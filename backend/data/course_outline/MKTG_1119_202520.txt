## Course Description
Examines the full breadth of sports marketing and sponsorship, its use in the marketing plans of corporations and its function inside amateur and professional sport. This project based course will appeal to sports administrators and event organizers as well as promotion, community relations and marketing personnel who address sports marketing in their occupation.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Explain sport marketing, its industry and participants, and the unique socio-economic culture it operates in.
- Identify the challenges that face professional sports franchises in general and Canadian Franchises in particular.
- Identify the different sources of revenue and expenses in the world of professional sports.
- Describe the elements that go into a successful sports sponsorship program.
- Identify how sponsorship effects major international events.
- Explain the history of the NBA in Canada.

## Course Goals
To most people professional sports are a passionate interest and pleasant pastime to view. But they are more than that. The total revenues from all sports related activity in North America makes it the 11th largest industry. The goal of this course is to help students understand the role that sponsorship, ticket sales, broadcast revenue and licenced merchandise sales play in fueling the world’s different professional sports leagues and major events. The instructor draws upon his 30 years’ experience in the NBA, NHL and Olympic Games to illustrate not only the revenue side of sports but the expense side too. Upon completion of the course students will begin to view sports in a different way, as a lucrative, ever changing and growing business.

1) Have a general understanding of sport marketing, it's industry and participants, and the unique social economic culture it operates in

2) Identify the challenges that face professional sports franchises in general, and Canadian franchises in particular

3) Understand the business side of all professional sports and be able to identify their different sources of revenue and expenses

4) Have a general understanding of the elements that go into a successful sports sponsorship program

5) Know how sponsorship effects major international sports events

6) Be conversant on the history of the NBA in Canada

7) Understand the IOC selection process for choosing Olympic Host cities

8) Understand the business aspects of organizing an Olympic Games

9) Be able to identify the different departments of an Olympic Organizing Committee

10) Understand the operation, funding and marketing of International Marathons

## Course Specific Requirements
This is a lecture-based course that uses case studies, group projects, class presentations and textbook reading assignments as its method of instruction. The lectures are backed up by power point presentations made available to the students. The instructor draws upon his 25-years of experience in the Olympic Movement and the NBA and NHL to illustrate many of the principals of International Sports Marketing. Guest speakers will enhance the course.
