## Course Description
​This course covers economic and non-monetary considerations for making decisions on public and private sector civil and electrical engineering systems. Topics include rational decision making, cost concepts, time-value of money, cost-benefit analysis, and risk and uncertainty.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Describe, in general terms, the engineering decision making procedure. [11]
- Evaluate the Time Value of Money (TVM) using interest rates. [11]
- Evaluate project economics using various methods including: present value (PV), future value (FV), annual worth (AW), net present value (NPV), and internal rate of return (IRR). [11]
- Identify differences between mutually exclusive and independent projects. [11]
- Utilize modified Internal Rates of Return. [11]
- Interpret, analyze, and construct financial statements. [11]
- Incorporate asset depreciation and taxes in economic analyses. [11]
- Integrate inflation in economic analyses. [11]
- Apply cost-benefit analyses to public projects. [11]
- Manage uncertainty using sensitivity and break-even analysis. [11]
- Perform risk assessment in economic analyses. [11]
- Utilize spreadsheet software and Monte Carlo simulation in economic analyses. [11]
