## Course Description
Explore effective communication, both in-person and remotely, with customers, colleagues and stakeholders from different cultures. You will enhance your understanding of different communication styles that will reduce misunderstandings in a multicultural context. Through team based and individual learning activities, you will reflect critically on your own cultural identities, biases, attitudes and cross-cultural experiences and discuss how these factors may impact your effectiveness in a cross-cultural work environment. Learning objectives will be demonstrated through individual and collaborative assignments that will challenge you to analyze, reflect and evaluate your own identities and apply this knowledge to real world scenarios.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Critically reflect on the different components of culture, personal cultural identities, biases, attitudes and cross-cultural experience.
- Analyze the aids and barriers to intercultural competencies.
- Evaluate different communication models, theories, and terms.
- Critically analyze and reflect on self and how one's identity impacts a cross-cultural work environment.
- Develop an awareness of effective intercultural communication skills.
- Enhance personal intercultural communication skills with others who have differing values, experiences and beliefs.
- Assess the impact of cultural dimensions in the work environment, including on interpersonal communication.

Attendance

Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.
