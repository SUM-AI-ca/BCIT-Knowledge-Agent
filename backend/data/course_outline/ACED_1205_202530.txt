### Course Description
​Students will be provided with an introduction to Revit Fundamentals, including graphic communication for architecture, essential modelling and annotation tools available in Revit, how to identify and manipulate basic building components in a building model, and the workflow of a typical construction project. Students must have a computer to download & install Autodesk Revit for this course. ​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain Graphic Communication for architecture.
- Perform Essential modeling and annotation tools available in Revit.
- Identify and manipulate basic building components in a building model.
- Describe the Workflow of a typical construction project.

### Course Specific Requirements
Session | Outline | Tests + Assignments
1 | General Introduction; User Interface; Coordinate Systems. Revit’s Editing Toolset | Complete student profile
2 | The Revit Process – Creating a Project; Levels and Grids; Assemblies; Views; & Revit View Range Tutorial. | Assignment 2.1, 2.2, 2.3 Assignment: Adding Views and Sheets to a Project. Start term project
3 | Roof, Floor & Ceilings; Roof by Extrusion | Exercise: Editing a Flat Roof; Creating a Sloped Roof Midterm Exam 1
4 | Introduction to Families & Understanding Groups. | Assignment: Placing Walls, Windows and Doors
5 | Stairs, Railings | Exercise: Creating Stairs. Hand in – Term Project for Review.
6 | Room, Area and Schedules. Presentation Drawings-Colour Fill. | Midterm Exam 2
7 | Annotating and Detailing; Topography and Shared Coordinates; | Assignment: Hand-in Term Project for Review.
