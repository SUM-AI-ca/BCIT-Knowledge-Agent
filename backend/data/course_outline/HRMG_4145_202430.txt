## Course Description
This course introduces systems and procedures associated with human resource information collection and storage, with emphasis on use for strategic and organizational human resource planning purposes. The course provides an overview of technology and Human Resources Management Systems, as well as current trends.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of the course, students will be able to:

- Understand the evolution of information technology with respect to HRMS.
- Identify the purpose, components, and benefits of a HRMS.
- Understand the strategic relevance of HRMS to organizations.
- Understand HRMS/ERP systems in general including key functionality and limitations.
- Understand key risks and opportunities associated with the HRMS analysis, planning, acquisition, and implementation process.

## Attendance Requirements
Weekly assignments/activities will be used for attendance tracking purposes. Not participating in more than two week's worth of activites will result in course failure.

Participation & Lab Work: Your grade for participation will be determined by regular attendance, the extent to which you are prepared for class, completion of in-class activities and assignments, your contribution to class discussions and the extent to which you facilitate and enable the participation of others. Your ability to demonstrate appropriate workplace behaviours – both in lecture and labs will also be considered when determining your grade for participation.

Excellent participation (16-20): Attends all labs, consistently participates and moves class discussions forward. Contributes high quality analysis. Helps others with understand course concepts. Helps others participate effectively. Punctual and attentive. Constructive engagement.

Satisfactory participation (12-14): Misses no more than one lab. Arrives prepared, contributes good observations and analysis when called upon. Understands assigned readings and is able to explain the concepts in the text. Participates in a manner that is not dominating or overwhelming of others. Does not distract others.

Unsatisfactory participation (0 – 9): Not prepared when called upon; misses classes; presents poorly reasoned observations and analysis; frequently “off task” in lab activities. Does not contribute to large class discussions or small group activities. Dominates class or small group discussions. Treats others in a way that is intimidating, discriminatory or otherwise inappropriate. Generally, demonstrates inappropriate workplace behaviours.
