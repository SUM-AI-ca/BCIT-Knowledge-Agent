## Course Description
Introduces students to the basic principles and techniques for planning and design of manufacturing, processing, and distribution facilities. Key aspects of the planning and design process from functional concept to architectural and engineering design are reviewed. Topics covered includes relationship programming, systematic layout planning, lean concepts, line balancing, workstation design, materials handling, building systems, WCB and Building Code requirements, and project management and budgeting.

## Learning Resources
Fred E. Meyers and Matthew P. Stephens (2013). Manufacturing Facilities Design and Material Handling. 5th Edition. West Lafayette (IN). Purdue University Press.

NOTE: Supplementary content will be presented by the instructor, Brian Amouzegar, to reinforce the learning process and introduce new concepts. Students’ regular attendance, and note-taking, is essential as printed handouts are not available to cover all supplementary content.

## Course Goals
At the end of this course, the student will be able to: Describe the basic steps in industrial facility planning and design Analyse data to determine process and storage requirements Understand common methods of industrial materials handling and select appropriate methods for various applications Understand the main principles of systematic layout planning Describe how safety and building codes impact on the planning of industrial facilities Understand the role of planners and designers with respect to implementation of new facilities Identify principle components and systems of new buildings Learn how inter-departmental relationships affect facility design layout
