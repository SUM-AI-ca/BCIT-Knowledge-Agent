## Course Description
​​Drawing on the knowledge gained in the previous courses, you will assume the role of a Professional in Business. You will concentrate on real-world Industry challenges by engaging either in primary or secondary research that will provide a professional learning experience. You will apply your Business skill set to the solution of the Industry challenge by working closely with a Faculty advisor and/or an Industry stakeholder. You will create a graduate level major report and presentation and evaluation will be based on application and integration of key Graduate Certificate in Business Administration concepts to the solution.​

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Develop a formal project proposal.
- Carry out a critical literature review, refining project methodology, and data analysis approach.
- Conduct primary or secondary research using qualitative and/or quantitative methods.
- Analyze primary or secondary research data.
- Plan, manage, and execute an applied graduate project.
- Provide a real-world industry solution using key business concepts.
- Communicate findings clearly to peers and/or Industry stakeholders.
- Create a graduate level academic major paper.
- Give a graduate level presentation to a panel of instructors and peers.​

## Course Goals
Upon successful completion of GLBL 9200 graduate students will be able to:

- Develop a formal Capstone Project Proposal.
- Create a graduate level academic paper using applied research that demonstrates application of the key concepts, methods, and approaches in the graduate program, and demonstrates ability to apply these to Graduate Business Administration Areas.
- Effectively identify a business need, perform appropriate research and analysis, and package the results in industry standard reporting formats.
- Transition from the proposal to the Capstone Project paper stage by developing clear and concise research questions, conducting a thorough critical literature review, refining research methodology, and analyzing data.
- Plan, manage, and execute an industry-based Capstone Project.
- Provide a real-world industry solution using key concepts from the Graduate Business Administration Program.
- Develop and produce a written analysis of the Industry Capstone Project.
- Present applied research findings and clearly communicate conclusions to peers and industry stakeholders.
- Communicate analysis results effectively to specify the solution proposed.

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance: The attendance policy as outlined in the current BCIT Policy 5002 will be enforced. Students are required to attend all classes. Attendance will be taken in lectures and seminars. Students who miss more than 10% of classes may be required to withdraw from the course, thereby receiving a failing grade. If students are sick, a medical note is required to avoid these absences from counting towards the 10% limit. Email or telephone contact with the Instructor is required for any illness causing you to miss class. At the discretion of the Instructor, you may complete the work missed. ## Guidelines for
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.

In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.

A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.

These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations. More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
