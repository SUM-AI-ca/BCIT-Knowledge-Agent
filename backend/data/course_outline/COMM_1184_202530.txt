### Course Description
​This course helps student clinicians develop essential written and verbal communication skills for professional settings. Students will also refine job-seeking abilities by creating tailored application documents and engage with professional literature to support evidence-based practice.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate written materials — their own and others' — to ensure clarity, conciseness, and correctness at the sentence and paragraph levels.
- Apply structural and visual design strategies to enhance the accessibility and readability of documents.
- Achieve a positive, persuasive, and reader-oriented tone across various types of professional communication.
- Critically read and assess professional literature to extract and synthesize key information.
- Write clear, effective instructions and descriptions tailored to diverse audiences.
- Compose and refine professional emails for a range of purposes, such as requests, replies, and patient communication, adapting tone and structure to suit the context and audience.
- Respond to potentially uncomfortable situations using indirect messaging strategies, both verbally and in writing.
- Verbally summarize their own projects with clarity, develop confidence in delivering verbal reports, and integrate natural storytelling techniques to engage their audience.
- Produce concise, well-structured progress reports that clearly articulate the development of their capstone projects.
- Prepare for job applications by analyzing job ads, tailoring resumes to align with employer expectations, and developing skills for informational and professional interviews.

### Course Goals
This course aims to help student clinicians:
- Develop professional communication skills essential for working with colleagues, patients, and other healthcare professionals.
- Understand and apply strategies to ensure written communication is clear, concise, and accessible in clinical and professional contexts.
- Build confidence in presenting project updates and clinical information verbally, using storytelling techniques to enhance engagement.
- Cultivate the ability to assess and refine communication materials to meet industry standards and align with audience expectations.
- Strengthen job-seeking skills through the analysis of employment materials and the development of tailored application documents.
Engage critically with professional literature to support evidence-based practice and lifelong learning.

### Attendance Requirements
If you anticipate needing to miss a class for any reason, I expect you to communicate with me in advance and make suggestions for how you will make up the lost class time and in-class activities.
Attendance requirements are consistent with the Prosthetics and Orthotics Program expectations below:
- When sick or away, you are responsible for making up for missed in-class work. Activities that can be made up will be accepted at the discretion of the instructor.
- It is strongly discouraged to make plans to be away from class for any reason - this includes travel plans and appointments. However, when sick, you are encouraged to stay home and your instructor will support you as best as possible.
- Regular attendance and participation in lectures, seminars, laboratory, and clinical sessions are integral to student success. Similarly, regular participation via prescribed forms of electronic communication may be required in any virtual and/or online aspects of the course. In case of illness or other causes of absence, students must notify their instructor AND program head.
