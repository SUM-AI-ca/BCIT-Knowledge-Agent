## Course Description
The purpose of this course is to learn all of the sonography imaging components of a complete routine echocardiogram study, including the assessment of heart structure and function. Students will build on the 2D imaging and technical skills learned in DSON 1012 and learn to assess cardiac structures/regions of interest using 2D, CDI, spectral Doppler, and basic quantification methods. Topics of this course include CDI, spectral Doppler, assessment, and quantification of the heart structure and function using the American Society of Echocardiography (ASE) guidelines. Students will perform echo sonography hands-on skills on a lab partner with instructor guidance. Students are introduced to Inter-Professional Education (IPE) through shared activities with other BCIT SOHS students.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Question the patient to obtain relevant information regarding history and condition. (1.1f)
- Demonstrate proficiency/competence in setting up, monitoring, and analyzing a 3-lead ECG in the lab setting. (3.3f)
- Perform uncomplicated cardiac sonograms in a timely manner, including 2D assessment, m-mode, Doppler and measurements (when applicable) on the structures of interest described in NCP 6.1 Appendix E. Note: structures of interest include ventricles, atria, heart valves, wall segments, septa, coronary vessels, hepatic veins, outflow tracts, great vessels and extra-cardiac structures such as pleural or abdominal spaces. (3.1h, Appendix E)
- Ensure all applicable components of the examination are complete. (5.3l)
- Communicate departmental reporting procedures to the patient. (1.1g)
- Identify and respond to exceptions from established protocols and procedures. (2.2g)
- Identify and prioritize differential findings. (5.4e)
- Formulate and communicate an accurate technical impression based on patient history and sonographic findings.
- Recognize and document equipment and technical limitations. (5.3m, 5.3n, 5.4f)​

## Course Goals
Cardiac Sonography Lab 2 will build on the 2D imaging and views performed in Cardiac Sonography Lab 1. Students will learn to assess heart structure and function and incorporate colour Doppler imaging (CDI) and spectral Doppler techniques into the echocardiography (echo) scan routine. After this term, students will be able to perform a full, uncomplicated, routine echo examination study on a patient.

## Evaluation Criteria
Grading: Weight
Quizzes: 10 %: There are five quizzes with three attempts of each quiz permitted. All quizzes must be completed.
Imaging Assignments: 40 %: Assignments have a pass mark of 65 %. Assignments that do not receive a passing mark can be resubmitted by the student up until the course end date.
In-Lab Assessments: 40%: Assessments have a pass mark of 65%. Assessments must be performed within the specified time limit during instructor-guided lab sessions. Unsuccessful assessments can be repeated with the oversight of a cardiac lab instructor to monitor for time efficiency. Students should contact the lab lead instructor to arrange for re-assessment.
Lab Performance and Professional Conduct: 10 %: Ongoing instructor feedback will be provided to students throughout the course. Students' preparation, participation and professionalism in the lab environment are expected and follow the Sonography Canada Professional Practice Guidelines.
Final grading for this course is Satisfactory or Unsatisfactory (S or U)

## Attendance Requirements
Attendance is required. As a courtesy to others, students are expected to arrive on time. Late arriving students will be the sonographer at the outset of the scan session so their fellow students' scan time is not impacted. Both attendance and late arrivals will be monitored. Students should attempt to find a DSON student replacement for scheduled labs, where possible.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
Professional behaviour and attire must be adhered to at all times. The required dress code for the lab is scrub pants, a patient gown, and closed-toe and closed-heel shoes. Long hair must be tied back. No food, gum chewing, or scented products are permitted in the lab. Water bottles are permitted but must be kept away from the ultrasound machines. If required, students are expected to don all required personal protective equipment (PPE), such as a surgical face mask, to maintain safety in the lab environment.
