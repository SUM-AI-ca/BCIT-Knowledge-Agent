## Course Description
​Students in this course gain hands-on experience in the settings of cardiac sonography. Students who successfully complete this course will be able to perform skills satisfactorily in more difficult or complex cases with assistance. Essential core competencies are emphasized and assessed through this course. Examples of these include effective communication, safe practice, patient care, and integration of academic knowledge. For a complete list of core competencies and clinical proficiency levels see the associated clinical course shell in the Learning Hub.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- ​Demonstrate professional behaviour at all times and in accordance with Sonography Canada's code of ethics and scope of practice. (2.1f, 2.1e, 2.1c, 2.2d, 2.2e, 2.2i, 2.3a, 2.3b, 2.3h)
- Demonstrate effective verbal and non-verbal communication with patients, their support persons, and other healthcare professionals. (1.1a, 1.1b, 1.1d, 1.1e 1.1g, 1.1h, 1.1i, 1.1j, 1.1k, 1.3a, 1.3b, 2.3f)
- Obtain and record accurate and relevant patient history based on clinical indications. (1.2b, 2.2b, 2.2c, 4.1c, 5.1a)
- Implement safe practice to include appropriate patient care and own physical safety. (2.2a, 2.1e, 2.2f, 2.2h, 3.1c, 3.1d, 3.1e, 3,1f, 4.2d, 6.2a, 6.2b, 6.2c)
- Formulate scanning strategies incorporating relevant clinical information, patient conditions, and knowledge of disease processes while applying problem-solving skills consistent with the level of experience. (2.2c, 2.2d, 2.2e, 2.2f, 2.2g, 2.2h, 2.2i, 3.1b, 5.1c, 5.1d, 5.1e, 5.3b, 5.3c, 5.3k)
- Produce accurate and diagnostic images, in a timely manner, demonstrating findings using spatial reasoning and according to departmental protocols of structures listed in Sonography Canada NCP 6.1 appendices. (4.1a, 4.1b, 4.1c, 4.2a, 4.2b, 4.2d, 4.2g, 4.2e, 4.2f, 4.2g, 5.3a, 5.3b, 5.3c, 5.3d, 5.3e, 5.3f, 5.3h, 5.3i, 5.3l, 5.4a, 5.4d)
- Identify and respond to urgent sonographic findings aligned with best practices and site protocols. (2.2g)
- Formulate and present physicians with an accurate technical impression based on findings and the limitations of exam quality in a timely manner. (1.2c, 1.2d, 1.2e, 2.3g, 5.4b, 5.4f, 5.4g)
- Appraise performance critically and outline goals for self-improvement. (2.4a)
- Demonstrate a collaborative interprofessional approach to deliver patient-centred care, using interprofessional education (IPE) competencies as a guide. (2.3c, 2.3d)​

## Attendance Requirements
Sick days: A maximum of 4 absences, due to illness, is permissible for the duration of the entire 24-week clinical practicum. A physician's note is required when 3 consecutive days are missed.

Special Leave Days: A maximum of 3 special leave days are available over the duration of the 24-week clinical practicum. Special leave days must meet a specific set of criteria, and advance approval is required from the BCIT Clinical Coordinator.

- Students exceeding the above limits may be required to make up clinical days after the official program end date. This decision is at the discretion of the program.
- This decision is at the discretion of the program.
- Unused sick or special leave is not considered as owing to the student.​

Punctuality: Students must be ready to commence scanning by their assigned start time.

- A pattern of late arrivals will not be tolerated and will be addressed on an individual basis. Extra clinical time may be required.

Clinical days are typically 8 hours including lunch and breaks. Clinical days will vary and may include evening and/or weekend days. Assigned days will be at the discretion of the clinical departments if not previously arranged with BCIT. Overtime may be allowed, and only with program permission.

- All absences and late arrivals must be logged in Comptracker.

## Safety equipment or protective clothing
Infection control policies and procedures must be followed diligently. Specifically, students are expected to don all required personal protective equipment (PPE) to maintain safety in the clinical environment, such as;

- Gloves
- Gowns
- Surgical Masks
- Face Shield/Eye protection

Safety equipment will be provided to Sonography students in the clinical environment as mandated by the provincial student practice policies.
