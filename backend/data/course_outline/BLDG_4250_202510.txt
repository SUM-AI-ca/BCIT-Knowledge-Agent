### Course Description
(Formerly known as BLDG 3350) This course is an introduction to construction specifications and the role they play in the building design and construction industry. As an essential component of the contract documents, the specifications must be clearly and accurately written in order to be effective and enforceable. Principles and procedures of specification writing are covered, as well as practical applications in locating, retrieving and interpreting technical information found in the specifications. General topics include the relationship of specifications to other contract documents, specification organization and format, language and style, specifying techniques, and product research and evaluation.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the basic principles, methods, and forms of construction specifications.
- Define the role of specifications with respect to drawings and other contract documents.
- Identify and describe the components of the Project Manual.
- Identify and outline the contents of a specification for a particular building type using MasterFormat.
- Organize and present technical information using the industry standards of MasterFormat, SectionFormat, and PageFormat.
- Locate and retrieve technical information within the contract documents.
- Identify style and language problems within the construction specifications through case studies
- Locate sources for collecting reliable and current technical information and develop systems for filing and retrieving that information.
- Perform basic procedures to research, analyse, evaluate, and select construction products based on building type and product characteristics and requirements.
- Recognize different specifying techniques used for products and workmanship to satisfy project criteria.
- Use a preliminary technical section of the specifications for a particular building type.
