## Course Description
Delivers the clinical application of dosimetry and treatment planning concepts in order to obtain optimal beam arrangements. Examines the application of dosimetric principles in teletherapy and brachytherapy and relates them to clinical practice. Provides practical dosimetry skills through completion of mathematical problem sets for teletherapy and brachytherapy techniques.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Analyze beam data for dose variation due to changing depth and different beam energies.
- Apply dosimetric principles for photon and electron beams used in teletherapy techniques.
- Apply dosimetric principles for radioactive isotopes used in brachytherapy techniques.
- Perform manual calculations given a set of treatment conditions for teletherapy techniques using photon and electron beams.
- Perform manual calculations given a set of treatment conditions for brachytherapy techniques for a variety of isotopes.
- Discuss the effects of inhomogeneities and irregular field shapes on dose distribution.
- Perform dosimetric calculations to correct for inhomogeneities and irregular field shapes.
- Compare different field arrangements to provide for dose uniformity across the PTV while limiting the dose to normal structures.
- Analyze the effect of beam energy and patient separation on composite isodose distributions.
- Assess the advantages and disadvantages of beam energy, arrangement and weighting to treat specific tumour sites.
- Describe the principles of computer algorithms as the basis of treatment planning software.
- Contrast the use of 3-dimensional computer planning and co-planar treatment planning.
- Discuss the application of new technologies and techniques as it relates to the objective of radiation therapy.
- Recognize the challenges of implementing new technologies and techniques into clinical practice.

## Course Schedule and Assignments
Week: Outcome/ Material Covered
1: External beam therapy - definitions, geometry, beam shaping devices
2: Dosimetric functions – TPR/TMR, inverse square law.
3: Dosimetric functions – PDD, PSF, CF, SF.
4: Radiation therapy treatment planning I – single beams. Normalization conventions, tumour dose, axis dose, extended SSD.
5: Radiation therapy treatment planning II: multiple beam geometry – Normalization conventions, relative weighting, and treatment time calculations.
6: Dosimetric functions – DVH.
7: Review
8: Midterm exam
9: Dose calculation within the patient corrections for patient geometry and heterogeneity. Computerized methods – photon beam modelling, Monte Carlo
10: 3D conformal, IMRT, VMAT; IGRT
11: Electron beam therapy I – physical characteristics of electron beams, single field treatments, corrections for beam obliquity
12: Electron beam therapy II – missing tissue and inhomogeneities, bolus, field shaping, extended SSD, field matching
13: Brachytherapy
14: Treatment plan evaluation tools
15: Review
16: Exam
