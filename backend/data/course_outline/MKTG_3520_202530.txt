## Course Description
​In this course, students will look at opportunities for owned marketing communications channels to increase customer acquisition, conversion, retention, and advocacy as part of an integrated marketing communications (IMC) campaign. Students will identify resources and tools to audit, plan, produce, distribute, and manage effective marketing assets that develop strong customer relationships and deliver a solid return on investment. Students will explore and apply best practices and tactics for a variety of current, relevant owned media channels—including websites and landing pages, email marketing, and content marketing (blogs, infographics, whitepapers, video, collateral, etc.)—for both B2B and B2C organizations. As part of a cross-course team project (with Brand and Campaign Strategy, Consumer Experience Marketing, Earned Marketing Opportunities, and Paid Media Opportunities), students will follow a systematic approach to the analysis of owned media using a current marketing communications case study.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Perform website and content audits
- Evaluate tools and resources to create, manage, publish, and optimize marketing assets, including CMS platforms, email service providers, search engine optimization tools, and digital asset managers
- Explore ideation and storytelling techniques for marketing
- Produce owned media assets, including graphics, video, and written materials, that fit with a brand strategy and the customer journey
- Measure and optimize owned marketing communications media for conversions
- Design a marketing automation plan
- Discuss current trends and issues in owned media
- Analyze the owned media strategies and tactics of a real-world IMC campaign
- Develop team collaboration skills
- Develop strong written and oral presentation skills
