## Course Description
Continues from BLDG 2915. This course demonstrates how to analyze the major components/systems in existing houses, with special emphasis on identification of problems inherent in these systems. The course includes a field trip to be announced in class.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Identify incorrect installations of the main home systems in compairison to code requirements and good tradesman practices. The students will be able to report to their clients deficiencies especially any safety issues and make appropriate recommendations.

## Course Goals
The course goal is to provide students with an understanding of the methods of inspecting existing houses through a basic knowledge of the various components that make up a house. It covers the remaining systems that are required to be inspected by the Standards of Practice. The other systems are covered in BLDG 2915 (House Inspection 1).This is the second of a series of courses that meet the academic requirements of the Applied Science Technologists and Technicians of B.C. (ASTTBC) for their certification as well as the qualifications for Home Inspector Licensing Regulations under Consumer Protection BC (CPBC). These courses are also valuable for those individuals in the housing industry who do not wish to pursue a career as a house inspector but want to know more about identifying issues related to houses.
