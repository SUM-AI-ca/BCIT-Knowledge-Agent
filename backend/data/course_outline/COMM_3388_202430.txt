## Course Description
This course prepares students for the Safety Program Review (SPR) completed at the end of the second year. Students write proposals, design questionnaires, negotiate a Term of Reference, deliver progress reports, and conduct an evaluation of a few elements of a safety program. They also present their findings to industry sponsors, the OCHS program head, communication instructor, and classmates.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Set communication objectives and identify the needs of employees, managers, and clients.
- Participate as an effective member of a health and safety audit team.
- Plan and write a 'Terms of Reference' for a review of parts of a safety program.
- Identify relevant documents and evaluate the company's written safety program materials.
- Design and administer questionnaires and interviews for employees and managers.
- Design scoring tables and other graphics for the audit report.
- Write a formal report explaining the review's findings.
- Communicate the principal findings to management in an oral presentation using appropriate visuals.
- Locate a site/sponsor and write a proposal for the term four audit.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments.
