## Course Description
This course focuses on the legal and economic constraints associated with structuring a business in the field of new media. Topics examined include the most appropriate business entity given specific legal and tax considerations, improving negotiation skills, establishing a binding media/publishing contract, protecting intellectual property, licensing and distributing creative work, and how to apply a "hands-on" approach to problem solving, acquiring media, and the negotiating and the drafting of contracts, with special emphasis on ethics and critical thinking. Students will be assessed by a combination of quizzes, team assignments, individual projects and written exam

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Identify the basic legal issues surrounding the various forms of intellectual property and other intangible rights.
- Identify and discuss various elements of, and potential issues around, basic contract law both online and offline.
- Differentiate between intellectual property rights of works created by employees vs. independent contractors.
- Contrast the different obligations arising on both sides for all parties in the creation of an employment relationship vs. an independent contract.
- Identify and assess potential acts of defamation when publishing information, in order to avoid potential liability.
- Compare and contrast the advantages and disadvantages of conducting business under a sole proprietorship, partnership or corporation.
- Explain the basic legal issues that govern the use of domain names and brands online.
- Demonstrate knowledge of tort issues and other basic legal problems that may cause liability when operating a new media business.
- Demonstrate knowledge of and assess potential acts that may infringe on rights of publicity and privacy when publishing information.
