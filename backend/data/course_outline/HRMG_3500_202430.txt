### Course Description
This course examines the role of training and development within an organization and focuses on the contribution it makes to the success of the organization. Using a training and development model, students will explore the design, delivery and evaluation of training. The course examines the principles of adult learning and builds an awareness of the budgeting processes necessary to support the training and development function as well as the return on investment provided by a strong training and development program.

### Course Goals
Welcome to our class!
You will quickly learn that I am fascinated by how we discover and make sense of the world, our interactions, and how we speak and listen to one another.
We all have different motivations for our actions, behaviours, and contributions. I believe that each of our behaviours is motivated to satisfy a need of ours, some known and some more deeply embedded in our conscience. Many of my studies have focused on when things go wrong, conflict, and disagreement. That can be an important component of training and development, but such awareness also helps us to be attentive to one another and good communication practices.
In our class conversations, I will share some personal and professional experiences, including my interests and opinions, what inspires and fulfills me and what kinds of development pursuits I chose for myself. I ask that you be open to sharing your life experiences, perspectives, and curiosity about our topics, and one another.
You, alongside your classmates are equally responsible for making this class worthwhile and a generative learning experience. I invite you to share your experiences, perspectives, ideas, cases, news stories related to our topic of study, and questions.
I am looking forward to meeting each of you and our exploration together.

### Guidelines for School of Business + Media
Attendance: Regular attendance in lectures and labs is seen as integral to student success, therefore, attendance in class is monitored. Unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for this course may result in the assignment of a failing grade. Attendance may be taken at any time during class. A student not present for attendance will be marked absent. A student who leaves class for a period of time deemed excessive by the instructor may be considered absent regardless of whether they are present when attendance is taken.
In case of illness or other unavoidable absence, students must communicate with the instructor, or the appropriate Program Head, as soon as possible (preferably in advance) indicating the reason for the absence. Students who are seeking accommodation for a medical absence must have a BCIT-approved medical certificate ). For other absences, students must be prepared to provide appropriate supporting documentation.
A student who has unexcused absences in excess of 10% of the time prescribed for the course by the Withdrawal Deadline is considered to have ‘Vanished’ and will be assigned a grade of ‘V’. In these cases, no further work will be graded.
These requirements are set out in accordance with BCIT Policy 5101 Student Regulations . More information regarding withdrawals from Flexible Learning courses is available at Refunds and Withdrawals
