### Course Description
​Students in this course will focus on agile project methodologies for software development. Web and mobile technologies require face paced development in an environment where requirements evolve and change frequently. Students in this course will be introduced to the history and rationale of agile project management, as well as the required roles and environment necessary for agile teams to be successful. Student in the course will learn how agile project operate, the impact of agile on project areas such as scope, schedule, cost, quality and risk, including the reporting and resources required to manage agile projects. Finally, students will develop an understanding of the benefits of agile project management when applied in an appropriate setting.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the rationale for agile methodologies in software development project.
- Describe the Agile Manifesto and the Twelve Principles of Agile Software.
- Identify the project framework and roles required to create an agile environment.
- Work on an agile project, specifically Scrum Methodology.
- Explain how agile methodologies affect project scope, schedule, cost, communication, quality and risk.
- Identify keys to creating an environment for agile success.
- Describe the benefits, metrics and resources required for agile environments.

### Course Goals
Course Learning Outcomes
At the conclusion of the course the student will be able to:
Understand the core principles and values of the Agile methodology, including its focus on iterative development, customer collaboration, and flexibility. Explain the roles and responsibilities within an Agile team, such as the Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Development Team. Describe the various Agile frameworks and practices, such as Scrum, Kanban, Extreme Programming (XP), and Lean Software Development. Apply Agile techniques for project planning, including creating user stories, estimating story points, and prioritizing the product backlog. Demonstrate the ability to conduct Agile ceremonies, such as daily stand-ups, sprint planning, sprint review, and retrospectives. Utilize Agile tools and techniques for tracking progress, managing work, and promoting transparency, such as burndown charts, task boards, and continuous integration. Understand the importance of continuous delivery, automated testing, and refactoring in Agile software development. Recognize the challenges and potential pitfalls in adopting Agile methodologies, and learn strategies for overcoming them. Appreciate the cultural and mindset shifts required for successful Agile adoption, including fostering collaboration, embracing change, and empowering self-organizing teams. Identify opportunities for continuous improvement and adapting Agile practices to fit the specific needs of a project or organization. Develop skills in effective communication, conflict resolution, and facilitation within an Agile team environment. Understand the relationship between Agile methodologies and other project management frameworks, such as Waterfall and hybrid models
