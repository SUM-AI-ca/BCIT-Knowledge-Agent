### Course Description
Advanced production procedures are followed in this hands-on course designed to increase the student proficiency in press operation, troubleshooting and independent decision-making skills. Printed documents include individual and teamwork projects and actual print documents for program promotion. Emphasis is placed on four-colour process, evaluating registration and consistency for accurate and predictable results. Students create their own proofs with inkjet and electrophotographic imaging technologies and colour-manage the proofing process to make sure they can match colour on the litho and flex presses. Students will review a cross-media campaign that spans all four imaging technologies and troubleshoot the colour management issues inherent with using a variety of substrates with a variety of manufacturing processes. Students consider the importance of planning and scheduling and the finishing techniques to professionally complete a live project.​
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Produce commercial quality printed work including process and spot colour jobs with varnishes.
- Set up feed, register and delivery systems on litho, flex, and digital presses.
- Adjust ink and water for clean printing.
- Determine use of standard process ICC profiles for creating a proof.
- Schedule press time.
- Use troubleshooting techniques and perform basic press maintenance.
- Recognize pleasing colours and colour density for correct colour reproduction.
- Measure colour and light with a spectro-densitometer.
- Create colour-managed proofs with one imaging technology and match a production run with an alternate technology (e.g., inkjet proof for a litho press run).​

### Safety equipment or protective clothing
The Graphic Communications program utilizes a unique print lab facility, with equipment donated by the generous business owners in our community. Students must always respect the equipment in the lab, and comply with the following rules:
- Loose hair or long beards must be tied back or contained in a hat or hair net.
- Remove jewelry, scarves, coats, or loose clothing.
- Gaming, videos, or loud music that can cause distraction are not permitted.
- Mobile devices are not permitted when working around machinery.
- No food or drink in the lab (closed water bottles are allowed at desks only).
- PPE is required to be worn when working with chemicals.
- No video recording or photography is permitted in the lab.
These rules are for the safety of all students using the equipment in the lab and are overseen at the discretion of all faculty. Failure to comply with lab rules will not be tolerated and may result punitive action including dismissal from class, formal warning, course failure, or withdrawal from the program.
