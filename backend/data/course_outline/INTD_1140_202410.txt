## Course Description
​This course introduces students to drawing as a means of visual communication. Developing freehand drawing skills for interior design applications is the focus of the program.

## Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

- Create a perspective using various drawing mediums, including pencil and ink.
- Create drawings using different drawing techniques.
- Create interior renderings that illustrate the elements and principles of design composition.
- Create drawings showing the relationship between negative and positive space.
- Record images quickly in simple line drawings.
- Create quick freehand sketches of interiors and/or furniture.
- Create drawings that relate to interior design applications.
- Draw perspectives which illustrate the principles of descriptive geometry (perspective).
- Convey depth in a drawing through the use of shade and shadow.

2. Pencil lead pointer or sharpener;

3. White block eraser

4. Grey kneadable eraser

5. Thumb tacks or low tack tape for fixing paper sheet to the drawing board

6. 20” x 26” light weight, rigid drawing board

7. 3/16 dia x 24” long dowel wooden stick or bamboo 12 skewer

8. 2-3 sheets of 18” x 24” 70-90 lb. white drawing paper per session (no newsprint acceptable)

9. 8½”x11” min 40 pages sketch book – plain bond paper with spiral coil

10. Portfolio to store and carry the drawings

Required later

11. Black drafting marker with 0.3 tip

12. 4 sheets of 18” x 24” Vellum paper

13. 12 or 14” wide roll of trace paper

14. 2 sets of matboards and 2 backing cardboards + self made carrying portfolio/envelope.
