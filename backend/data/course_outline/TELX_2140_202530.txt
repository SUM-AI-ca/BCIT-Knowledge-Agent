### Course Description
​In this course, you will learn how and where to research electrical contractors as potential employers. You will also write a resume and apply for some jobs. You may also practice interview skills.
### Course Learning Outcomes/Competencies
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Research and find potential employers.
- Apply for jobs via email.
- Write a precise and error-free resume.
- Prepare for formal job interviews.
