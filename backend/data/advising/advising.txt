## Welcome To Advising
## Plan your next steps
Whether you’re a prospective student exploring your options, or a current student seeking guidance, contact Program Advising for support. Advisors are equipped with the knowledge and resources to assist you in making informed decisions about your academic path.

### Flexible Learning Advising
### Support available with:
Admission and Academic Planning
- Guidance on entrance requirements, prerequisites, and registration.
- Options to strengthen your application for a competitive program.
- Exploration of available upgrading options.
Program Information
- Navigation and comparison of BCIT programs and courses.
- Detailed information on programs, workload, and career prospects.
- Advice on skills and attributes for success in each BCIT program.
Transfer and Pathway Opportunities
- Guidance on the transfer credit process and requirements.
- Advice on advanced placement and transfer options.
- Direction on laddering opportunities post-graduation.
