## Shape your learning journey

In Flexible Learning you can take a single course or work towards a certificate, diploma, or degree, all at a pace that’s right for you. BCIT offers over 1,000 courses during the day, evening, and weekends, as well as online options. And with new learning options like microcredentials and free online courses, building your resume has never been easier. Whether you’re looking to get your foot in the door, upskill in your career, or planning a career shift, Flexible Learning is designed for you.

## Email advising

Flexible Learning program email response time is approximately 1 week. Please use the virtual advising service for time-sensitive questions.

Send an email to program_advising@bcit.ca with the BCIT program or course name in the subject line. Please do not send duplicate emails as this only causes a further delay.

## How we support you

BCIT’s Flexible Learning Program Advising is here to provide expert guidance tailored to your needs:

- Academic planning: We help you create a personalized study plan that fits your schedule and goals.
- Program guidance: We provide information to help you plan your courses and explore pathways to advanced credentials.
- Credit for prior learning: We provide guidance on opportunities for course exemptions based on your prior knowledge.
