## Contact Full-time Cohort Advising
## Empower your academic journey
Our full-time programs are taught in cohorts. These are small groups of students that you’ll work with throughout your program. As a full-time student, you are automatically registered into all the courses you need to complete your program, so you never have to worry about scheduling. You can focus completely on your studies—and entering the workforce when you graduate.

## How we can help
BCIT’s full-time program advising team is here to provide expert guidance tailored to your needs.
- Academic planning: We guide you to choose a program that aligns with your career goals.
- Upgrading: We help you find the best upgrading option if you are missing courses required for entrance or if you have the required course, but your percentage grade is not high enough.
- Support for success: We can provide resources and knowledge that set you up for success in your studies.
