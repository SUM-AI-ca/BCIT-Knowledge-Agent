## Architectural and Structural CADD and Graphics Technician (Architectural Option)
Certificate Full-time School of Construction and the Environment
## Overview
Delivery: in person. See details .
The Architectural and Structural CADD and Graphics Technician program offers two distinct areas of specialization:
- Architectural CADD and Graphics Technician
- Structural CADD and Graphics Technician
Applicants must indicate their area of specialization when applying.
Architectural and Structural CADD and Graphics Technician programs at BCIT offer training for a variety of applications in architecture, building construction, and industrial applications. CADD (Computer-Aided Drafting and Design) is an integral part of all our drafting programs.
Classes and program activities are typically scheduled between 7:00 AM and 2:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
All students begin in September and must complete a drafting and CADD core and specialty. Total program length: 40 weeks.
### The Program
The Architectural CADD and Graphics Technician specialty focuses on drafting and computer applications (BIM and CAD) with hand drafting / sketching related to architectural building systems and construction. Students develop skills in preparing architectural documents from Schematic Design phase drawings and illustrations to technical drawings focusing on the Construction Documentation phase. Producing these documents follows industry standards, applying building code and zoning restrictions to mimic workplace activities.
Training includes architectural drafting standards, building construction applications, Computer-Aided Drafting and Design (CADD), and Building Information Modelling (BIM) using industry-standard software applications commonly used in the architecture, engineering, and construction industries.
The program incorporates applied and experiential learning components throughout the curriculum. Students receive training to help them secure practicum placements and prepare for professional workplace expectations. Practicum opportunities are typically between two weeks and one month in duration. The program also includes experiential learning activities such as field and site visits, as well as industry guest speakers.
Graduates may pursue opportunities in areas such as architectural drafting, building design support, assistant site superintendent, BIM modeling, and related technician roles within architecture, engineering, construction, and consulting firms.
