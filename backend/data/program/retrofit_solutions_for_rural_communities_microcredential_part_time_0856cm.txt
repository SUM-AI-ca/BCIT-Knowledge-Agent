## Retrofit Solutions for Rural Communities
Microcredential Part-time School of Construction and the Environment
## Overview
Delivery: blended. See details .
The Retrofit Solutions for Rural Communities microcredential is for learners to expand their knowledge of upgrading and retrofitting existing homes in a rural community.
### About the program
The microcredential—designed to enhance knowledge of essential building science, zero emissions buildings, and retrofit strategies—will cover concepts such as ‘house-as-a-system’, building envelope, heating, cooling, and ventilation, and design for culture, comfort, and affordability. It consists of two courses using practical examples of residential retrofits in rural regions. Through applied activities, learners will identify affordable solutions to improving performance and practice applying these techniques through hands-on activities.
Upon completion, learners will understand key building science concepts and approaches to maintaining and upgrading buildings to achieve improved comfort, energy efficiency, and durability goals.
They will also be able to:
- Describe the context for reducing emissions of the building sector.
- Explain the fundamentals of building science related to residential building retrofits.
- Describe how the ‘house-as-a-system’ approach can be applied to retrofit decisions.
- Identify the strategies for evaluating emissions retrofits to improve occupant comfort, efficiency, and durability.
- Apply building science fundamentals in hands-on construction and sequencing of specific building retrofits and upgrades.

## Courses
### Program matrix

Required Courses: | Credits
XZEB 1114 | Building Science for Retrofits ​This course introduces the fundamentals of building science for retrofits and upgrades for homes in rural communities. The course will outline the contexts for reducing emissions in homes, fundamental building science concepts, solutions for residential building retrofits and upgrades, and strategies to reduce energy usage and improve comfort and building durability. The course presents multiple case studies from communities in different regions of BC. Key topics include House-as-a-System: air, water, moisture and heat management; retrofit solutions and programs; culturally appropriate design; and best practices for retrofit, envelope, and mechanical upgrades. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 1.5
XZEB 1185 | Implementing Retrofit Solutions for Rural Communities The course will introduce approaches to above and below-grade assembly solutions to achieve energy efficiency, durability, and comfort. Learners will apply their building science knowledge in hands-on construction and sequencing of specific building upgrades. By the end, they will be able to compare typical strategies based on local material availability, durability, application, and available funding. Key topics include House-as-a-System approaches, envelope upgrades, mechanical upgrades, sequencing, constructability, and durability. This course will be offered in person in the community or at the BCIT High Performance Lab. Learners should be prepared for light construction and wear steel-toe footwear for lab sessions. Tools will be provided onsite. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 2.0
