## Architectural and Structural CADD and Graphics Technician
## Work in the Drafting Field in Less Than Ten Months!
The Architectural and Structural CADD and Graphics Technician program offers two areas of specialization: Architectural CADD and Graphics Technician and Structural CADD and Graphics Technician . Applicants must indicate area of specialization on their application.
These programs at BCIT offer training for a variety of applications in architecture, structural engineering, building construction and industrial applications. Program integrates drafting and computer applications with design skills necessary for production of complete sets of working drawings.
