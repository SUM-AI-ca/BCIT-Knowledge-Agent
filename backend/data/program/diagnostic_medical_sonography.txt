## Diagnostic Medical Sonography Programs
## Pursue a Career in Diagnostic Medical Sonography
Whether you’re starting your journey in ultrasound technology or advancing your skills as a practicing sonographer, this program empowers you to excel in a vital healthcare field. Use high-frequency sound waves to capture detailed images of the abdominal, blood vessels, heart, obstetrics and organs—making a meaningful impact on patient care and diagnosis.
## Why Choose Sonography at BCIT?
BCIT’s sonography programs offer hands-on training, clinical placements, and industry connections to prepare you for a rewarding career as a medical sonographer. With practical skills and access to cutting-edge facilities, BCIT ensures you’re ready for success in this growing healthcare field.
### Cardiac Sonography
Focuses on the heart and is typically performed in cardiology or diagnostic imaging departments of hospitals.
### General Sonography
Encompasses abdomen, blood vessels, obstetrical, and pelvis. This type of sonography is conducted in both hospital settings and outpatient clinics.
## Choose your Sonography Path
BCIT offers various sonography programs designed to cater to different career paths. Whether you are looking for a specialized diploma, a broader dual diploma, or advanced training in a niche area, our programs prepare you for success in the field.
## Program Pathways:
### Dual Sonography Diploma
Best for: Those who want training in both general and cardiac sonography, with no prior post-secondary education.
This dual program provides training in both General Sonography and Cardiac Sonography. It’s ideal for those looking to gain comprehensive expertise in both areas of sonography.
### General Sonography Diploma
Best for: Those focused on general sonography, looking for a shorter path to start working, with no prior post-secondary education.
This diploma focuses on abdomen, blood vessels, obstetrical, and pelvis ultrasound. It prepares students for careers in hospital diagnostic imaging departments and outpatient imaging clinics.
### Cardiac Sonography Advanced Diploma
Best for: Those who already have a degree or diploma and want to specialize in cardiac ultrasound.
This advanced diploma is designed for those specifically interested in cardiac sonography. It provides in-depth training in the specialized field of cardiac ultrasound.

## Sonography FAQ
The word “ultrasound” can refer to the machine, physical principles or technology, whereas “sonography” is the medical practice of using it, and “sonogram” is the resulting image. Often these terms are used interchangeably. A sonographer may also be called an ultrasound tech.
Radiology is a general term that covers all medical imaging technology while sonography is a specific imaging modality that uses ultrasound or sound waves to view inside the body. Sonography does not use radiation. Radiography refers specifically to X-ray imaging.
Echocardiography and cardiac sonography are two terms for the same thing and are often used interchangeably. Both describe ultrasounds on the heart but cardiac sonographer is the more modern term.
Key competencies include:
- Applicants should possess both physical and mental endurance
- Proficient fine motor control and manual dexterity
- Outstanding communication skills
Candidates must also demonstrate the ability to:
- Visualize objects in three dimensions
- Accurately distinguish between shades of grey, various colours, and sounds
- Work effectively both independently and as part of a team
- Adapt to and manage stressful, fast-paced work environments
- Safely move and transfer patients from wheelchairs to stretchers and washrooms
- Assist patients with toileting needs
- Multitask with precision and efficiency
- Apply problem-solving skills across diverse situations
Yes, a Medical Sonographer is listed as a High Opportunity Occupation in the 2025 BC Labour Market Outlook report (Appendix 5). There are 610 projected job openings over the next 10 years, with 320 due to expansion and 290 due to replacement.
Diagnostic medical sonography graduates write the Sonography Canada registration exam and are then able to work around British Columbia in hospitals and medical imaging clinics.
Sonography can scan any soft tissue area or organ in the body. This includes the abdominal and pelvic organs, the cardiovascular system, including the heart, arteries and vein, and superficial structures, like the breast, scrotum or thyroid gland. Sonography cannot scan areas filled with air like the lungs.
Sonography can be used to detect a wide range of pathologies. Examples of conditions that may be identified include, but are not limited to:
- Cardiac ultrasound: valve disease, congenital heart defects, heart muscle dysfunction, coronary artery disease, and blood clots.
- General ultrasound: gallstones, kidney stones, liver disease, thyroid nodules, breast masses, muscle and ligament tears, assessment of fetal health, and ovarian cysts.
