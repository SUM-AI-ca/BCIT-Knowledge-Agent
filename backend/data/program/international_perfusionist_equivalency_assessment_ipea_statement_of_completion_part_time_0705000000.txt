## International Perfusionist Equivalency Assessment (IPEA)
Statement of Completion Part-time School of Health Sciences
## Overview
Delivery: blended. See details .
### About the program
The International Perfusion Equivalency Assessment (IPEA) Statement of Completion is a collaboration with the Canadian Society of Clinical Perfusionists (CSCP) and is not intended to replace the Cardiovascular Perfusion Advanced Certificate program . This collaborative initiative is to address the ongoing need and critical shortage in the industry.
### Who it’s for
Prospective applicants to the IPEA Statement of Completion must receive pre-approval through the CSCP and apply through the CSCP website. This program is intended for internationally trained perfusionists with current Canadian employment offers.
For more information on this opportunity, please visit the CSCP website.
## Entrance Requirements
- Applicants must receive pre-approval through the Canadian Society of Clinical Perfusionists ( CSCP ). Please visit the CSCP website for more information. CSCP will require the following for the pre-approval: Certification of graduation from an accredited perfusion program. Actively employed as a Cardiovascular Perfusionist in the country they are applying from English language proficiency: Category 1 – English Studies 12 (73%) or equivalent Proof of eligibility to work in Canada. Offer of employment with a Canadian healthcare institution including: Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) Requirement to complete the IPEA process followed by successful certification of CSCP Certification and in good standing with one of the following associations: European Board of Cardiovascular Perfusion (EBCP) Australian and New Zealand College of Perfusionists (ANZCP) Society of Clinical Perfusion Scientists of Great Britain & Ireland (SCPS) Comprehensive credential assessment report from one of the following: World Education Services (WES) International Credential Evaluation Service (ICES) Processing fee: Non-refundable application fee must be paid in CAD to CSCP . BCIT tuition is separate and must be paid in addition to the CSCP fees.
Note: Once an applicant has been accepted into the IPEA program, CSCP will inform BCIT that all IPEA entrance requirements have been met.

IPEA 7610 | International Perfusionist Equivalency Assessment 1 IPEA 1 is the first of three mandatory one-credit courses in the International Perfusion Equivalency Assessment (IPEA) process. Each course includes a blend of self-assessments and a comprehensive final exam. A minimum score of 60% on the final exam is required to advance to IPEA 2. IPEA 1 examines applicants on the essential and requisite knowledge for clinical perfusionists, focusing on the normal physiology and pathophysiology of body systems, including the circulatory, cerebral, pulmonary, renal, hepatic, hematological, immune, and endocrine systems. It also assesses understanding of metabolism and temperature regulation. Additionally, it will test the concepts and applications of pharmacology as they pertain to the clinical practice of cardiovascular perfusion, as well as cardiac anesthesia and the care of cardiac patients in the operating room setting.​ Prerequisite(s): Acceptance approval from the Canadian Clinical Society of Perfusionists (CSCP). course outline | 1.0
IPEA 7620 | International Perfusionist Equivalency Assessment 2 ​IPEA 2 is the second of three mandatory one-credit courses in the International Perfusion Equivalency Assessment (IPEA) process. Each course includes a blend of self-assessments and a comprehensive final exam. A minimum score of 60% on the final exam is required to advance to IPEA 3. ​ IPEA 2 assesses the applicant's requisite and essential knowledge required to deliver safe cardiopulmonary bypass during all phases of cardiothoracic surgery, including initiation, maintenance, and weaning. It will examine their understanding of the physiological principles of extracorporeal circulation, its systemic effects, and crucial aspects of metabolism, anticoagulation, and CPB impacts. Additionally, it covers hemodynamic monitoring for cardiovascular and pulmonary status, pediatric perfusion techniques, and the embryological development of the heart. Prerequisite(s): 60% in IPEA 7610 course outline | 1.0
IPEA 7630 | International Perfusionist Equivalency Assessment 3 ​IPEA 3 is the third and final mandatory one-credit courses in the International Perfusion Equivalency Assessment (IPEA) process. Each course includes a blend of self-assessments and a comprehensive final exam. A minimum score of 60% on the final exam is required to advance to the clinical component of the IPEA Statement of Completion. This final component of the knowledge assessment will evaluate applicants’ mastery of the program learning outcomes, with a focus on critical thinking, problem-solving, and the integration of knowledge. Emphasis will be placed on applying theoretical concepts to practical scenarios and making informed clinical decisions to deliver high-quality, safe, person-centered care during cardiopulmonary bypass and related procedures. ​ Prerequisite(s): 60% in IPEA 7610 and 60% in IPEA 7620 Acceptance approval from the Canadian Clinical Society of Perfusionists (CSCP). course outline | 1.0
IPEA 8600 | International Perfusionist Equivalency Clinical Assessment ​This is the final component of the IPEA Statement of Completion and will be comprised of 6-8 weeks of clinical training within the employer site. Applicants will be immersed within the clinical setting to assess their competence in the delivery of safe high-quality person-centered care essential to the practice of clinical perfusion. Over a maximum of 8 weeks, twenty satisfactory cases must be pumped and no more than 4 non-critical failures are permissible. BCIT clinical criteria, aligned with the CSCP National Competency Profile will be used for evaluation purposes and accessible through Comptracker. Clinical assessment will be conducted by an experience Clinical Perfusionist within the site of employment, except for the final evaluation, which will be overseen by an external evaluator appointed by the CSCP Board of Directors. Prerequisite(s): 60% in IPEA 7610 and 60% in IPEA 7610 and 60% in IPEA 7630 course outline | 9.0

## Program Details
### Program length
12 weeks.
### Grading
Students must achieve 60% on all theory-based examinations and will be required to complete 20 satisfactory clinical cases.
### Program delivery
Blended: This program is delivered partly on campus and partly online.
All courses can be completed online except for the clinical. The clinical placement must be in Canada.
### Program location
Distance and online learning
