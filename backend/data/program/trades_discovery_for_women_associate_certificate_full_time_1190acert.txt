## Trades Discovery for Women
Associate Certificate Full-time School of Transportation
## Overview
Delivery: blended. See details .
The Trades Discovery for Women program is open to eligible, self-identified women who want to explore exciting, hands-on careers in the skilled trades. Delivered through a partnership between BCIT and YWCA Higher Ground , the program offers tuition-covered training, personalized support, and extensive access to BCIT’s trades and technical facilities, giving participants the practical experience, confidence, and preparation needed to pursue further training or step directly into employment.
### Program information
Delivered by BCIT, this hybrid, 16-week full-time program gives students hands on experience in up to fifteen different trades disciplines through a blend of in-shop and online learning. Participants take part in activities such as layout and fabrication, general piping, carpentry framing, welding, wiring, and working on mechanical systems in automobiles, aircraft, and heavy equipment. A work experience component offers a real look at day-to-day skilled trades workplaces and career pathways, supported by industry connections and wraparound services designed to help students succeed.
### Trades explored
Students will explore a variety of programs from the following industry sectors:
- Construction
- Manufacturing
- Transportation and warehousing
- Utilities
During the program, students will have an opportunity to earn workforce certificates** such as:
- Basic First Aid (OFA Level 1)
- Confined Space Entry and Monitor
- Fall Arrest Safety
- Lift Truck Operator
- Mental Health First Aid
- Mobile Elevating Work Platform (scissor lift training)
- Respirator Fit Test
- Workplace Hazardous Material Information System (WHMIS)
** workforce certificates subject to availability
### Tuition cost
The Trades Discovery for Women program is free for eligible YWCA Higher Ground participants, with tuition fully covered and access to personalized supports throughout the program. To learn more about eligibility and participation requirements, visit the YWCA Higher Ground program page .

### Program matrix
Required Courses: | Credits
TEXP 1000 | WorkSafeBC Occupational First Aid Level One A basic one day/8 hour course covering the essential ABC's - airway, breathing and circulation. How to deal with obstructed airways, breathing distress and control bleeding. One person CPR, minor wound care and records / reports are also part of the course. A 3 year certificate will be issued at the end of the course upon successful completion. Prerequisite(s): 16 years of age. course outline | 0.5
TEXP 1002 | Workforce Certificates ​Students will have an opportunity to earn recognized workforce certificates (subject to availability); Fall Protection, Confined Space Entry and Stand by Attendant, Lift Truck Operator, Mobile Elevated Work Platform. Students must earn at least three workforce certificates. ​ Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 1.0
TEXP 1110 | Interpret and Sketch Blue Prints This course is an overview of interpreting and sketching blueprints. Students will be able to identify the alphabet lines, explain the three most commonly used orthographic views, use isometric drawings, working drawings, and make three way sketches of simple objects. The course includes lecture, as well assignments and quizzes. Prerequisite(s): Entry in to Trades Discovery course outline | 1.5
TEXP 1120 | Career Preparation A key component of the program is an integrated trade’s research project directed by the student within their chosen field or industry: their own search for knowledge, expertise and individual contacts allows the student the opportunity to realistically assess the actuality of their educational and/or employment choice. Comparison studies reveal the complexities of the market trends and their impact and response to external and economic pressures. Upon completion of this course, students will be able to complete a market research paper on their chosen trade. Students will develop a job search portfolio that includes “blue collar” resume, cover letter and reference statements. Effective and efficient communication skills are essential elements in acquiring and retaining a career in a highly competitive job market environment. This course is designed to increase a student’s ability to prepare and practice for trade specific job applications and interview processes. Prerequisite(s): Entry in to Trades Discovery course outline | 2.0
TEXP 1130 | Work Experience - Industry Tours Trades Discovery students participate in an approved unpaid work experience module. The work experience component ensures each student experiences first-hand the day- to- day realities of the work situation. Industry Tours/Lectures are scheduled to provide a bigger picture of the needs of Industry and possible opportunities for employment. Prerequisite(s): Students failing TEXP 1150 Safe Work Practices, TEXP 1120 Career Preparation and/or 1 or more courses in Trades Discovery will not be allowed to participate in the TEXP 1130 Work Experience. course outline | 2.5
TEXP 1140 | Use Oxygen and Acetylene Equipment This course covers the operation of oxy-acetylene torches, including safety, ignition, flame types, shut down and storage of equipment. Applications of the oxy-acetylene torch for heating, cutting and welding will be covered during practical exercises. Sessions will include an introduction to SMAW (Shielded Metal Arc Welding) and MIG (Metal Inert Gas) welding. This course includes assignments, homework, and tests. Prerequisite(s): Entry in to Trades Discovery – Must pass an Oxygen and Acetylene Safety Exam prior to any shop/practical work course outline | 2.0
TEXP 1150 | Trades Specific Skills A series of in class sessions as well hands on in the shop training that encompass approximately twenty different Trades Training/Technical Studies programs taught at BCIT campuses. Lessons in safety, care and use of hand tools, power tools, and shop machinery will be taught and Applied Science will be covered. Students are placed in a workshop environment with a journeyed instructor where they will perform a number of tasks to gain experience in each trade. Each area will include an overview of the industry covering, apprenticeship and career opportunities. Prerequisite(s): Entry in to Trades Discovery course outline | 9.0
TEXP 1160 | Applied Mathematics This course consists of sessions in applied basic mathematics; students are expected to complete math assignments in the following areas: whole numbers, fractions, decimals, measurements, ratios, conversions, percentage, exponents, equations, perimeter, area, volume and geometry. Students are required to complete hands-on applied math sessions as well as self-paced assignments and tests. Prerequisite(s): Entry in to Trades Discovery course outline | 1.5
TEXP 1180 | Apply Safe Work Practices This course provides an overview of WorkSafeBC and how Workers Compensation Regulations apply to industry. An in-depth look into safety culture in the workplace; focusing on safety precautions and procedures. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 1.5
Total Credits: | 21.5
