## Medical Office Assistant
Associate Certificate Part-time School of Business + Media
## Overview
The part-time Medical Office Assistant Associate Certificate provides practical, relevant knowledge in healthcare and office settings, preparing you for a career in the field. With a practicum for hands-on experience, the program covers essential areas such as anatomy, medical documentation, billing, clinical procedures, transcription, and more.
Delivery: online. See details .
Medical Office Assistants work as administrators in the health care sector, helping to make the entire office run smoothly. They possess strong client-service skills, as well as excellent organization and communication skills. This program is designed to give you applied, relevant knowledge about health care and the office setting to prepare you for work in the field. The Medical Office Assistant Associate Certificate includes a practicum to gain real-work experience and is one of BCIT’s more popular programs, with classes filling up quickly.

CLRK 1015 | Healthcare Communication Medical administrators require excellent communication skills as their roles require them to interact with the public, colleagues and other members of a healthcare team. This course introduces professional healthcare communication skills customized to build appropriate skills. You will learn how to effectively use tone in written and oral communication. You will also learn to use language correctly when interacting with patients and families, external partners, and a healthcare team including physicians, nurses, technologists, and other hospital staff. The course covers communication required for specific administrative tasks and addresses the collaboration skills needed when working with team members. OFFC 1001 | Anatomy and Terminology 1 This course focuses on the medical terminology of anatomy and physiology. You will study the terminology of the digestive, urinary, reproductive, nervous, cardiovascular, and respiratory systems. For each system, you will learn about the organs, the functions of each component, common disease processes and symptoms, and anatomical terminology. You will build expertise in the use of common prefixes, suffixes, and terminology related to disease processes of specific body systems. Anatomy and Terminology 1 will cover approximately 50% of the human body systems and lead into Anatomy and Terminology 2. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 3.0
OFFC 1007 | Medical Billing Practices In this course, you will explore billing service fee codes and medical billing processes, including data entry for both manual and computer billing in Canadian provincial billing systems and the different billing procedures in physicians’ offices and hospitals. The course will address EMR(electronic medical record systems), billing for visits, supplementary items, and diagnostic procedures carried out in a medical office, a hospital, or home. It is essential that you learn how to accurately process claims and forms related to alternate billing such as what is needed for government services (e.g. WorkSafeBC), reciprocal procedures, and private third party companies. Emphasis is placed on the MSC Payment Schedule and Diagnostic Code Descriptions from the Medical Services Plan website, The Doctors of BC Uninsured Services Fee Guide and other services across Canada. You will create a patient list, perform data entry comprising of patient demographics, service fee codes, and diagnostic fee descriptions. You will generate an electronic patient claim sheet record which includes billing codes, specialist details and billing notes through given outlined medical clinical scenarios. The General Preamble, General Services and Family Medicine sections of the MSC payment schedule provide the foundation for billing a medical service provided to a patient. Focus is on referencing the General Practice fee codes and their significance to age of the patient and type of service/visit provided. Practical application will introduce the learner to processing patient information beginning with registration, insurance verification, health history, and contact information required to create an electronic medical record. Billing visits use specific age-related codes referenced from an MSP Fee Schedule. Attention to detail and accuracy of entries and verification of billing entries prior to posting into batches is essential. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 3.0
OFFC 1008 | Medical Documentation This course teaches you how to create professional medical documents. There is an emphasis on letter formatting and accuracy. You will be introduced to ergonomics, transcribing rules, and medical abbreviations in preparation for medical transcription. You will also produce professional documents in styles appropriate for different audiences. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 1.5
OFFC 1015 | Medical Office Practices This course will prepare you with the basic knowledge needed in medical office administration and procedures, and professional conduct. You will develop skills in appointment scheduling, reception and communication, filing, referrals, and pharmacology. You will learn how to order inventory and supplies used in a medical office. There will be a review of issues related to patient rights. You will be introduced to medical abbreviations and to using resources such as the BC medical directory. You will also be introduced to medical services [E-Comm (911), non-emergency health advice (811), and (711)] and medical authorities [Regional Health Authorities, First Nations Health Authority, Provincial Health Services Authority, sub-agencies]. Through office scenarios, you will apply problem solving, critical thinking, decision making, and conflict resolution skills. Role playing will help you develop the confidence needed for dealing with concerns relating to the duties of a medical office assistant. Prerequisite(s): 65% in OFFC 1001 course outline | 3.0
OFFC 1045 | Clinical Procedures In this course, you will be introduced to the clinical procedures and tests commonly performed in medical settings. You will learn relevant theory about clinical procedures and develop a beginning competency in selected clinical skills. You will gain knowledge of the basic instruments, equipment, supplies and procedures used in medical settings. This course will help you develop the skills needed to assist physicians, prepare patients for tests and examinations and keep accurate records. You will learn how to set up an examination room with the appropriate equipment and supplies for examinations. You will also learn the correct procedures for sterilizing medical equipment, properly disposing of bio-hazardous materials as well as storing, maintaining and ordering supplies. In addition, you will be introduced to the basic role of health services, WorkSafe organizations, medical service plans and other medical organizations across Canada. OFFC 1048 | Medical Transcription Skills In this course, you will be introduced to the words and phrases of medical practice with the assistance of a software program. Medical terms that you previously studied will relate to those used by the software. During this course, you will develop entry-level transcription skills for transcribing dictated materials in an accurate and complete manner and follow the documentation requirements used in different areas of the medical field. This course is offered in the Medical Office Assistant program and is also open to medical office personnel wishing to upgrade their medical transcription skills. OFFC 1055 | Electronic Medical Records Healthcare administration is rapidly changing and electronic files are replacing paper files. The Electronic Medical Records (EMR) course explores key aspects of software systems used by healthcare providers for scheduling, referrals, requisitions, patient management and record keeping. You will be introduced to the Collaborative Health Record (CHR) software system and other Electronic Medical Records (EMR) software. This course includes lectures, exercises and hands-on practical software use for administration and clinical application in physicians’ offices and clinics. You will configure, organize, and navigate the CHR system to input and retrieve electronic medical data. This course is a required component of the Medical Office Assistant program and may also be taken as a stand-alone course by MOA graduates and other healthcare workers. ## Program Details
In the Medical Office Assistant Associate Certificate program, you will take courses carefully designed to provide you with the skills and resources necessary to be successfully employed in a variety of roles in health care. This popular program works around your schedule by offering classes on weekday evenings and occasionally on weekends.
Courses in the program cover:
- anatomy and terminology,
- medical documentation,
- medical billing,
- clinical procedures,
- transcription, and more.
By developing the key skills for a range of tasks, you’ll be prepared for responsibilities that can arise in any medical office, and therefore broadening your appeal to potential employers.
You’ll also participate in an 80-hour practicum to gain practical, hands-on work experience in a medical office setting.
### Important practicum information!
Students must have the following information on file with BCIT to qualify for the Practicum:
- Up-to-date immunization records .
- A Criminal Record Check (CRC). Students must pass a CRC working with children and vulnerable adults
If you are currently employed as a Medical Office Assistant, with permission of the Instructor, you may be able to complete the practicum at your workplace.
### Program goals
Upon successful completion of the Medical Office Assistant Associate Certificate, you will be able to:
- Perform medical office tasks professionally and ethically including scheduling appointments, preparing bills, maintaining medical records and ensuring patient confidentiality while managing time effectively.
- Assist physicians and prepare patients for examinations, procedures and tests.
- Manage the Electronic Medical Records (EMR) system used in doctors’ offices, and clinics.
- Manage medical billing documentation as dictated by Medical Services in Canadian provincial systems.
- Create medical documents and communications formatted according to professional and Health Information Standards.
- Transcribe medical dictation for a variety of different purposes.
- Apply critical thinking and problem-solving techniques to resolve issues that occur in medical offices.
- Demonstrate effective, accurate and professional communication skills as appropriate in the healthcare environment.
- Demonstrate effective interpersonal and team skills and communicate with the empathy, compassion, and sensitivity appropriate for interaction with the public, including Indigenous Peoples and those with special needs.
- Demonstrate an understanding of cultural health considerations that may impact services.
- Apply ethics, security, and privacy standards to comply with Canadian PIPEDA and provincial privacy protection regulations for healthcare environments.
- Demonstrate computer literacy and cloud software skills required to manage medical records, locate critical information, support communication and collaboration across the healthcare environment.
- Employ the medical and anatomical terminology and abbreviations used in healthcare communication.
