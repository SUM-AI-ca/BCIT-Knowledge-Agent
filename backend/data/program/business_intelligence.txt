Overview text

In today’s data-driven world, analytics have become essential for making informed decisions and staying competitive. The Associate Certificate in Business Intelligence (ACBI) is designed to serve the growing need for analytic skills across all industries and sectors. The program promotes data literacy by providing professionals with expertise in data analysis, statistical methods, data visualization, and storytelling. As a result, this program enables knowledge workers across various disciplines and sectors to leverage tools and techniques to extract and communicate meaningful insights from data, supporting informed business decisions. In an era where organizations increasingly rely on data to drive strategy and innovation, data literacy has become a critical competency across all sectors. The ACBI program equips professionals with the analytical, interpretive, and decision-making skills needed to navigate a data-rich environment, enhancing their career potential and meeting employers’ demand for adaptable, insight-driven talent. By emphasizing the connection between data analysis and business decision-making, the ACBI makes data analytics accessible and relevant to professionals who aim to generate actionable insights by gaining sufficient technical and programming expertise. Who should apply for the Associate Certificate in Business Intelligence? If this sounds like you, apply today on the Entrance Requirements page.



## Courses

Introduction

You must be accepted into the program before you can register in any courses. Courses must be taken in the order identified in the ACBI Course Flow [PDF] . Please email Ruth MacKay , Program Assistant, to register.

### Required courses

BABI 3100 - Business Analytics Theory

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi3100/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course introduces students to the theory of Business Data Analytics and prepares students to write the Certification in Business Data Analytics (CBDA) exam from the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA).

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

BSYS 3105 - Information Technology 1

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/bsys3105/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This is an introductory course on the development and management of relational database applications and business analytics. This course covers all of the phases of the enterprise database web application life cycle, and builds a hands-on system development using Database Management Systems. Appropriate technologies are used to build user interfaces for the database applications. Multi-dimensional data modeling is discussed, and Microsoft Power BI technologies are used to acquire, model, and visualize data.

Prerequisites: BSYS 2000

BSYS 3205 - Business Intelligence 1

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/bsys3205/

Credits: 4.0

Description: Big Data continues to have a significant impact on business. This course serves as an introduction to the key aspects of how business intelligence is used in current business practices. Students will build business intelligence knowledge through the practical hands-on development of data analysis software and other applications and apply this knowledge by analyzing information to make business decisions.

Prerequisites: BSYS 2000

BSYS 4205 - Business Intelligence 2

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/bsys4205/

Credits: 2.5

Description: This is an advanced course in Business Information Systems that builds on the material delivered in BSYS 3205 and serves as an introduction to Machine Learning (ML) and Artificial Intelligence (AI). This is a hands-on project-based course that introduces Big Data analytic concepts such as ML, AI, and NoSQL databases in an open source or cloud platform environment. The focus is to use the latest solutions in Deep Learning (DL) to gain better insights and predictions. This course will utilize the student’s background in databases and the Python programming language.

Prerequisites: BSYS 3205

OPMT 1500 - Business Analysis Fundamentals

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/opmt1500/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course introduces you to the basic principles and concepts of Business Analysis. Designed to provide a foundation of the subject matter required for other courses in business analysis, this course covers key knowledge areas, including planning, business need identification, requirements gathering, analysis and management and solution approach and evaluation. It also introduces key tasks and techniques that support these knowledge areas. You will learn these topics through lectures, exercises and discussions, case studies and/or scenario-based problem solving, both individually and working in teams. It is mandatory that this course be taken first in the program. If one other course is taken at the same time, it is recommended to take an elective or OPMT 2520 Needs Assessment and Planning. The course also includes writing the Entry Certificate in Business Analysis™ (ECBA™) exam through the International Institute of Business Analysis™ (IIBA®).

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

About IIBA IIBA is a professional association dedicated to supporting business analysis professionals to deliver better business outcomes. IIBA connects almost 30,000 members, over 100 chapters, and more than 500 training, academic, and corporate partners around the world. As the global voice of the business analysis community, IIBA supports recognition of the profession, networking and community engagement, standards and resource development, and comprehensive certification programs.
