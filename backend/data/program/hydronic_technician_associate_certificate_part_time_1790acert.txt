## Hydronic Technician
Associate Certificate Part-time School of Construction and the Environment
## Overview
The Hydronic Technician Program is designed to provide those working in the construction industry with the technical knowledge and installation skills required to install and service complete Hydronic Systems (hot water heating).
Delivery: in person. See details .
Many tradespersons working in the Hydronic Heating Industry have taken Apprenticeship in the traditional fields of Plumbing, Pipefitting, Gas Fitting or Air Conditioning/Refrigeration but lack the high degree of specialized skills and technical knowledge needed to work in the Hydronic field.
This program is designed to provide those skills and is fully endorsed by the RHWHA who is actively promoting this training within their association and industry. Many components of this program will also be of interest to those involved in Building Technologies such as Hydronic Design, Hydronic Installation and Cross Connection.

## Courses
### Program matrix
Check current availability of courses for this program.
Required Courses: | Credits
PPGS 1041 | Basic Drafting for Hydronic Technicians Provides instruction in the production and interpretation of orthographic and isometric drawings as they apply to the hydronic system, and flow diagrams and general assembly drawings. Formal drafting techniques including board skills will be covered. Related terms, symbols and call-out bubbles will be discussed. An introduction to AutoCAD will be included. Passing Grade is 70% Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 3.0
PPGS 1140 | Hydronic System Design Trains those working with hot water heating to properly design a residential hot water heating system to meet the guidelines established by the Residential Hot Water Heating Association of BC. After successful completion of the Certification Exam, students can, for a fee, register with the TECA as being Certified in Hydronic Systems Design. Passing grade is 70%. Prerequisite(s): Student should be working with residential hot water heating systems or a related field. course outline | 3.0
PPGS 1142 | Basic Electrical for Hydronic Technicians Provides a practical understanding of direct and alternating current, series and parallel circuits. Covers the analyzing of circuits using ohms law and multimeters. Ladder diagrams and electrical code will also be covered. Passing grade is 70%. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 3.0
PPGS 1143 | Installation Practices for Hydronic Systems Provides a practical introduction to the installation of hydronic systems; including installation planning, mechanical room layout and installation of heat transfer units. Safe work practices will be applied to proper piping procedures and techniques. Passing grade is 70%. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 3.0
PPGS 1144 | Controls for Hydronic Technicians A practical introduction to the installation of controls involved with hydronic systems including control strategies, installation procedures and techniques, safe work practices, and commissioning. This course includes instruction on how to install and commission a functioning hydronic control system. Passing grade is 70%. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 3.0
PPGS 1145 | Servicing and Troubleshooting Hydronic Systems Designed for tradespersons actively working in the residential hydronic heating industry. Helps prepare them to service and troubleshoot systems that have been designed and installed to meet the guidelines of the Residential Hot Water Heating Association. Emphasis will be on hands-on servicing in the training lab. Prerequisite(s): Basic knowledge of the concept of hot water heating. course outline | 3.0
PPGS 1150 | Domestic / Commercial Gas Fitter "B" Certificate of Qualification Prepares students to write the examination to qualify for the Gas Fitter "B" Certificate of Qualification and is a requirement of the BC Safety Authority. To become eligible to write the exam, approval from the BC Safety Authority is required and recommended before enrolling. A Provincial Class B Examination fee is additional. Passing grade is 70%. Prerequisite(s): Student must be the holder of an industry training credential (TQ, IP) in plumbing, steam fitting, refrigeration or sprinkler fitting. course outline | 7.0
PPGS 1155 | Backflow Assembly Tester Certification This water related course teaches the hydraulic principles of backflow combined with testing of backflow prevention assemblies, it is recommended that you are employed in a water or health related field. On completion of the course you have the option of taking the BCWWA examination. There is an additional examination fee payable to BCWWA. For more details please contact BCWWA at 604-433-4389. Passing grade is 75%. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 4.0

## Graduating & Jobs
### Job opportunities
Hydronic heating in the domestic/commercial market has experienced rapid growth in the last few years primarily due to the aggressive marketing of manufacturers and suppliers and is expected to continue to grow for years to come. It is the heating system of choice for custom housing and many types of commercial building. There is a growing demand by the heating contractors of British Columbia for highly skilled trades people for both the installation and service sectors of this industry. The growth of the residential/commercial market will ensure a continued growth in the hydronic market and the workforce needed to provide installation and service.
