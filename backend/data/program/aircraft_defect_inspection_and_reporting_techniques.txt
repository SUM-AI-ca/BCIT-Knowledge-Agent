Title: Aircraft Defect Inspection and Reporting Techniques

================================================================================

## Overview

Delivery

in person

Overview text

This micro credential will allow aviation employees to upskill to perform inspector related activity in their current role with the goal of upgrading to the inspector level within their organization. Working with key industry players the goal is to utilize the current hands-on skills of the inspectors and give them skills to properly document performed inspections using industry related reporting tools. Software use and image capture methods have changed drastically since most of these inspectors completed their training and by giving them tools to expand their current industry skills into emerging processes will ensure their viability while helping to advance the current workforce.

### Required Courses

AVFL 1000 - Aircraft Structural Inspection Techniques

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/avfl1000/

Credits: 2.0

Description: AVFL 1000 will cover aircraft structural inspection techniques such as scratch identification, damage measurements, defect types and defect measurements.

Prerequisites: This course is for Aircraft Maintenance Engineering graduates, individuals employed in the aviation field, and aircraft owners who would like to expand on their inspection knowledge and methods.

AVFL 1005 - Aircraft Inspection Defect Reporting

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/avfl1005/

Credits: 2.0

Description: AVFL 1005 will cover aircraft inspection reporting techniques such as image capture, PowerPoint presentation of defects, manufacture limit identification and defect limit evaluation per manufacture specifications.

Prerequisites: S in AVFL 1000
