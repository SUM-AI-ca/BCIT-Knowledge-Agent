Title: Spanish Language and Culture Fundamentals

================================================================================

## Overview

Delivery

in person and online options

Overview text

The Spanish Language and Culture Fundamentals (SLCF) Microcredential is a fast and cost-effective way for business people, students and other travellers to receive a high-quality language training credential that will support the successful international and intercultural transitions and other short-term expat experiences. This Microcredential program builds learners’ capacity for short-term work, study abroad, and internship overseas. The curriculum is tightly focused on developing the essentials these travellers will need to be confident and competent in the common interactions encountered on short-term sojourns.



## Courses

### Required Courses:

SLCF 1150 - Spanish Language and Culture – Working Foundations

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/slcf1150/

Credits: 1.5

Description: This Level 1 of the course, “Spanish Language and Culture – Working Foundations,” will introduce students to Spanish grammar and pronunciation and build awareness of informal and formal exchanges relevant to the Spanish culture. The course presents the learners with the foundations to develop a Spanish vocabulary as well as useful expressions for working and travelling. This convenient format supports the learner’s ability to quickly gain speaking and listening competencies while building vocabulary and conjugation foundations.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

SLCF 1155 - Spanish Language and Culture – Practical Exchanges

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/slcf1155/

Credits: 1.5

Description: This course builds on the Level 1 Spanish Language and Culture – Working Foundations course by building on more advanced Spanish vocabulary. Participants will extend their ability to express themselves while working and travelling in a Spanish-speaking environment. This class supports the learner's knowledge of conjugating the present tense of regular ar, er, and ir verbs. It will also help students learn about cultural differences in professional and travel settings.

Prerequisites: 50% in SLCF 1150

## Program Details

Program length

The program requires a total of 36 hours. Students who complete the two required courses will receive a Spanish Language and Culture Fundamentals Microcredential.

Grading

Two courses must be taken in order. The minimum passing grade for each course is 50%.

Program delivery

This program is offered in the following delivery modes: In person: This program is delivered on campus. Online: The entire program is delivered fully online.

Program location

Courses may be offered at the following locations: Burnaby Campus 3700 Willingdon Avenue Burnaby, BC Distance and online learning
