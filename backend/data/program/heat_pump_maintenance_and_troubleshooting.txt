## Courses

### Required Courses:

HVAX 3200 - Heat Pump Installation- Basic

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hvax3200/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course introduces the fundamental principles and practices involved in installing heat pump systems. On line lectures for basic refrigeration, electrical, and piping procedures will be covered before attending the practical portion of the course, this will allow more hands-on time in our lab. The intent of the course is to introduce the student to proper procedures on how to install a heat pump, helping them understand and learn in the field with a qualified individual.

Prerequisites: Students must hold the following certifications: 1) Red Seal: HVAC *or* Level One/Foundations program in HVAC and Refrigeration from BCIT or another recognized institute in Canada. AND 2) Environment Canada ODS Certificate.

HVAX 3210 - Heat Pump Installation- Advanced

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hvax3210/

Credits: 2.0

Description: Building upon the foundational knowledge gained in the basic heat pump installation course, the Advanced Heat Pump Installation course delves deeper into the intricacies of designing, installing, and optimizing complex heat pump systems. This course is designed for experienced HVAC technicians, engineers, and professionals seeking to enhance their expertise in heat pump technology.

Prerequisites: 70% in HVAX 3200

HVAX 3220 - Heat Pump Maintenance and Repair

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hvax3220/

Credits: 2.5

Description: The Heat Pump Maintenance and Repair course is designed to equip HVAC technicians and maintenance professionals with the knowledge and skills necessary to effectively maintain, troubleshoot, and repair heat pump systems. Heat pumps play a crucial role in providing heating, cooling, and humidity control in residential, commercial, and industrial settings, making it essential to ensure their proper functioning through regular maintenance and timely repairs.

Prerequisites: 70% in HVAX 3200 and 70% in HVAX 3210
