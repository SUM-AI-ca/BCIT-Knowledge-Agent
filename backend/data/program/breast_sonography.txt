### Required courses:

XDMS 5002 - Breast Sonography Skill-up

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/xdms5002/

Credits: 2.0

Description: Students in this introductory course will learn how to identify normal breast anatomical structures and identify variations in tissues due to age, pregnancy, and breast density on ultrasound. They will also learn the sonographic features of benign conditions and common pathologies. Students will also discuss the unique patient care and patient management strategies including targeted vs whole breast ultrasounds, mammograms, MRI, breast biopsies, and the ACR – BI-RADS® reporting system.

Prerequisites: This specialty course is intended for a cohort group of registered generalist sonographers (CRGS, RDMS) employed in British Columbia, Canada.

XDMS 5012 - Breast Sonography Clinical First-Steps

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/xdms5012/

Credits: 4.0

Description: Students in this course gain entry-level hands-on experience in the settings of Breast sonography. Students who complete this course will be able to perform skills satisfactorily in more difficult or complex cases. Essential core competencies are emphasized and assessed through this course. Examples of these include effective communication, safe practice, patient care, and integration of academic knowledge.

Prerequisites: Must be a BC resident, registered, employed, and working Sonographer as breast sonography upskilling and clinical first-steps are supported through a BCIT partnership with the provincial Health Authority. For example, potential students need to have graduated from an accredited sonography training program and hold the Registered Diagnostic Medical Sonographer (RDMS - AB/OB/GYN) or Canadian Registered Generalist Sonographer (CRGS) credentials to qualify for BCIT breast sonography specialty training.



## Entrance Requirements

introduction

Students are chosen by their employers through a selection process. Students must be British Columbia (BC) residents, credentialed, employed, and working for the BC Health Authority or local industry partner. A student will need to have graduated from an accredited sonography training program and hold the Registered Diagnostic Medical Sonographer (RDMS – AB/OB/GYN) or Canadian Registered Generalist Sonographer (CRGS) credentials to qualify for the BCIT breast sonography specialty training. Proof of Sonography Canada or American Registry for Diagnostic Medical Sonography membership is required.
