## Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)
## Advance Your Career in MRI with BCIT
Unlock your potential with a career in Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI). As a vital branch of medical imaging, MRI professionals play a critical role in healthcare by using advanced technology to capture precise images of internal body structures. BCIT’s program equips you with hands-on experience, practical skills, and the confidence to excel in a high-demand, fast-paced medical environment.
## Why choose MRI at BCIT?
BCIT’s MRI programs help you develop the skills needed for success through expert instruction, hands-on clinical experience, and flexible learning pathways. All faculty are currently practicing MRI technologists, ensuring students benefit from instruction informed by current practices and emerging trends in the field. MRI is an excellent career choice for those who thrive in both technical and patient-focused roles. If you have the following strengths, this program could be a great fit:
### Patient Well-Being & Care
You are compassionate and driven to make a meaningful impact in healthcare by providing quality and safe patient care.
### Mix of Technical & Patient Interaction
You enjoy a well-balanced mix of working with advanced imaging technology and engaging directly with patients.
### Opportunities in a Growing Field
You want a stable and in-demand career in the expanding healthcare sector.
## Choose Your Path in MRI
BCIT offers three distinct MRI training paths designed to match your experience level and career aspirations.
## Find Your Path
### Diploma Program
Ideal for students starting their journey in medical imaging, the Diploma in MRI provides a comprehensive foundation in both theoretical knowledge and hands-on technical skills. Emphasizing real-world clinical experience, students develop expertise across all facets of the MRI modality through in-person instruction, simulation labs, and supervised practicum placements.
Who it’s for: Students without prior healthcare or imaging experience seeking a full-time, on-campus training program.
### Advanced Certificate Program
Designed for healthcare professionals looking to specialize or upskill, the Advanced Certificate in MRI offers the opportunity to be dual-modality trained, while working in your current imaging field. This program is ideal for those already in medical imaging who want to expand their expertise and increase their career opportunities.
Who it’s for: Professionals with an existing medical imaging background seeking advanced training.
### Bachelors Program
The Bachelor of Health Science in Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) credential incorporates the MRI Advanced Certificate, with the addition of several core component courses to complete a Bachelor’s Degree. The core component courses provide a strong foundation in health leadership, clinical preceptor skills, statistics, research, and liberal studies.
Who it’s for: Medical Imaging technologists seeking to be trained in MRI, while obtaining a Bachelor’s degree.

## Find the right MRI program for you
BCIT offers three MRI program pathways designed to fit your career goals—whether you’re starting fresh, building on existing skills, or advancing your education. Choose the program that’s right for you and take the next step in medical imaging.
Bachelor of Health Science (MRI)
The Bachelor of Health Science in Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) credential incorporates the MRI Advanced Certificate, with the addition of several core component courses to complete a Bachelor’s Degree.
Who it’s for: Medical Imaging technologists seeking to be trained in MRI, while obtaining a Bachelor’s degree.
What to expect: Part time program consisting of a series of online theory courses and a full-time 16-week MRI clinical term. This program must be completed within 5-years.
MRI Advanced Certificate
Designed for healthcare professionals looking to specialize or upskill, the Advanced Certificate in MRI offers the opportunity to be dual-modality trained, while working in your current imaging field. This program is ideal for those already in medical imaging who want to expand their expertise and increase their career opportunities.
Who it’s for: Professionals with an existing medical imaging background seeking advanced training.
What to expect: It is recommended that students complete the theory courses for the program within two years (six terms) and that students enroll in the 16-week clinical term upon completing their last theory course. The maximum time to complete the program is three years.
MRI Diploma
Ideal for students starting their journey in medical imaging, the Diploma in MRI provides a strong foundation in both theoretical knowledge and hands-on skills. With a focus on real-world clinical experience, students gain expertise in all facets of the MRI modality through in-person instruction and simulation labs.
Who it’s for: Students without prior healthcare/imaging experience looking for a full time on-campus, training program.
What to expect: A structured two-year curriculum, including a full clinical placement year with health authority partners, ensuring graduates are fully prepared for the workforce.

As there is no ionizing radiation used for imaging, this is a safe exam for patients. However, since MRI uses a strong magnetic field, the MRI technologists use a thorough screening process to ensure a patient is safe to have their exam, including making sure they have no metal on or in their body that could be reactive to the magnetic field.

Similar to all medical imaging modalities, there is a great need for MRI technologists.
MRI Technologists work in community and high acuity hospitals or clinics.
MRI Technologists enjoy a full schedule working with a variety of patients each shift. Technologists are involved with the patient’s MRI exam, from beginning to the end by prepping a patient for their exam, positioning patients, properly placing the MRI coil according to what is being imaged and by ensuring safety during the MRI exam. MRI procedures often require continuous engagement with patients and clear communication. MRI technologists provide reassurance, enhance comfort, and facilitate cooperation during lengthy or complex scans. Every shift technologists use their expertise of anatomy, pathology, physics and patient care to optimize MRI diagnostic images to provide the best result possible.
To be successful in MRI, students must have good communication skills, strong critical thinking and a collaborative mindset.
Having a Bachelor’s degree increases a technologists opportunities to grow your profession and allows you to move into leadership roles. Professional Development is vital in healthcare and obtaining a degree allows you to be more effective in your profession.
