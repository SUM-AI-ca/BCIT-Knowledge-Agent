## Overview

Delivery

blended

Overview text

The Trades Discovery programs are designed to allow participants extensive access to the available trades and technical training at BCIT campuses. These highly practical and relevant training programs prepare participants to successfully enter and complete skilled trades/technical training or find employment. Students gain hands-on experience in up to fifteen different trades disciplines. Some examples of in-shop activities include: layout and fabrication, general piping, carpentry framing, welding, wiring and working on mechanical systems in automobiles, aircraft, and heavy equipment. A work experience component allows the participants to experience the day-to-day realities of skilled trades workplaces, partnering education with industry and community. ### Required Courses

TEXP 1000 - WorkSafeBC Occupational First Aid Level One

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/texp1000/

Credits: 0.5

Description: A basic one day/8 hour course covering the essential ABC's - airway, breathing and circulation. How to deal with obstructed airways, breathing distress and control bleeding. One person CPR, minor wound care and records / reports are also part of the course. A 3 year certificate will be issued at the end of the course upon successful completion.

Prerequisites: 16 years of age.

TEXP 1002 - Workforce Certificates

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/texp1002/

Credits: 1.0

Description: Students will have an opportunity to earn recognized workforce certificates (subject to availability); Fall Protection, Confined Space Entry and Stand by Attendant, Lift Truck Operator, Mobile Elevated Work Platform. Students must earn at least three workforce certificates.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

TEXP 1110 - Interpret and Sketch Blue Prints

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/texp1110/

Credits: 1.5

Description: This course is an overview of interpreting and sketching blueprints. Students will be able to identify the alphabet lines, explain the three most commonly used orthographic views, use isometric drawings, working drawings, and make three way sketches of simple objects. The course includes lecture, as well assignments and quizzes.

Prerequisites: Entry in to Trades Discovery

TEXP 1120 - Career Preparation

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/texp1120/

Credits: 2.0

Description: A key component of the program is an integrated trade’s research project directed by the student within their chosen field or industry: their own search for knowledge, expertise and individual contacts allows the student the opportunity to realistically assess the actuality of their educational and/or employment choice. Comparison studies reveal the complexities of the market trends and their impact and response to external and economic pressures. Upon completion of this course, students will be able to complete a market research paper on their chosen trade. Students will develop a job search portfolio that includes “blue collar” resume, cover letter and reference statements. Effective and efficient communication skills are essential elements in acquiring and retaining a career in a highly competitive job market environment. This course is designed to increase a student’s ability to prepare and practice for trade specific job applications and interview processes.

Prerequisites: Entry in to Trades Discovery

TEXP 1130 - Work Experience - Industry Tours

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/texp1130/

Credits: 2.5

Description: Trades Discovery students participate in an approved unpaid work experience module. The work experience component ensures each student experiences first-hand the day- to- day realities of the work situation. Industry Tours/Lectures are scheduled to provide a bigger picture of the needs of Industry and possible opportunities for employment.

Prerequisites: Students failing TEXP 1150 Safe Work Practices, TEXP 1120 Career Preparation and/or 1 or more courses in Trades Discovery will not be allowed to participate in the TEXP 1130 Work Experience.

TEXP 1140 - Use Oxygen and Acetylene Equipment

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/texp1140/

Credits: 2.0

Description: This course covers the operation of oxy-acetylene torches, including safety, ignition, flame types, shut down and storage of equipment. Applications of the oxy-acetylene torch for heating, cutting and welding will be covered during practical exercises. Sessions will include an introduction to SMAW (Shielded Metal Arc Welding) and MIG (Metal Inert Gas) welding. This course includes assignments, homework, and tests.

Prerequisites: Entry in to Trades Discovery – Must pass an Oxygen and Acetylene Safety Exam prior to any shop/practical work

TEXP 1150 - Trades Specific Skills

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/texp1150/

Credits: 9.0

Description: A series of in class sessions as well hands on in the shop training that encompass approximately twenty different Trades Training/Technical Studies programs taught at BCIT campuses. Lessons in safety, care and use of hand tools, power tools, and shop machinery will be taught and Applied Science will be covered. Students are placed in a workshop environment with a journeyed instructor where they will perform a number of tasks to gain experience in each trade. Each area will include an overview of the industry covering, apprenticeship and career opportunities.

Prerequisites: Entry in to Trades Discovery

TEXP 1160 - Applied Mathematics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/texp1160/

Credits: 1.5

Description: This course consists of sessions in applied basic mathematics; students are expected to complete math assignments in the following areas: whole numbers, fractions, decimals, measurements, ratios, conversions, percentage, exponents, equations, perimeter, area, volume and geometry. Students are required to complete hands-on applied math sessions as well as self-paced assignments and tests.

Prerequisites: Entry in to Trades Discovery

TEXP 1180 - Apply Safe Work Practices

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/texp1180/

Credits: 1.5

Description: This course provides an overview of WorkSafeBC and how Workers Compensation Regulations apply to industry. An in-depth look into safety culture in the workplace; focusing on safety precautions and procedures.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.
