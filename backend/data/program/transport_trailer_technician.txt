## Overview

Overview text

The Transport Trailer Technician (TTT) apprenticeship program requires time spent on the job and in-school training. A Transport Trailer Technician trainee must complete a two-year program including workplace and in-school hours of training as follows: After completion of training, a passing grade on the interprovincial exam will result in the SkilledTradesBC Certificate of Qualification (Transport Trailer Technician) and Certificate of Apprenticeship and the Inter-Provincial Red Seal Endorsement. ## Courses

### Level 2 (4 weeks)

ATTT 2GAP - Transport Trailer Technician Apprentice Level 2 Final Grade

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/attt2gap/

Credits: 10.0

Description: This SkilledTradesBC Transport Trailer Technician (ATTT) level 2 course has 5 subjects in which you will learn to service, diagnose, repair, align, and demonstrate safe work practices, while conducting activities of a heavy duty equipment technician in a shop and field service role. In this level 2 course you will learn to service, repair, and perform diagnostic procedures on brakes, hydraulic, electrical, frames, and suspension systems, align steering, and repair cab and body structures.

Prerequisites: Students must be a sponsored apprentice.
