## Accounting Diplomas
Set the stage for a prosperous career in business with an Accounting diploma program at BCIT. By training with knowledgeable, experienced instructors who are respected professionals in the accounting industry, you’ll learn how accounting is the backbone of businesses big and small. This highly experiential program will prepare you to step into a corporate office or head back into the classroom to complete your bachelor’s degree. The road to CPA designation is an easy one with accounting programs at BCIT.
Whether studying in our full-time or part-time option, you can expect:
- Hands-on experience: applied activities to reinforce learning
- Small class sizes: receive individual attention and get to know your peers
- Connections to industry: learn from instructors and guest speakers working in the field and benefit from the real-world experience of accounting practitioners
Choose from our full-time and part-time options:
- Full-time option: about seven courses per term and completed in 2 years
- Part-time option: evening courses at your own pace and recommended if you are working full-time
