Title: Motorcycle Technician

================================================================================

## Overview

Overview text

The harmonized Motorcycle Technician Apprenticeship requires time spent learning on the job and in the classroom. The apprenticeship is four years in duration, including 6,720 workplace hours and 600 in-school hours of technical training which comprises four levels. After completion of training, a passing grade of a minimum 70% on courses within BCIT, and the Ministry of Advanced Education exams, will result in the BC Certificate of Apprenticeship, BC Certificate of Qualification, and the inter-provincial endorsement, also known as Red Seal. This program delivers technical training required for employment as a motorcycle technician in BC. For those currently sponsored by an employer, the harmonized apprenticeship program includes the following outcomes: servicing two and four stroke gasoline and diesel engines, basic electrical, starting and charging, fuel and ignition, brakes, chassis, wheels and tires, manual and automatic transmissions, final drives, new unit assembly, computer controls and engine management systems. Please visit the SkilledTradesBC website for more details about the program. Note: For those who are not currently sponsored into an apprenticeship, successful completion of the BCIT Motorcycle Technician Foundation program will qualify for credit towards level 1 of technical training required in the provincial Apprenticeship program.

### Level 1 (5 weeks)

AMCT 1GAP - Motorcycle Technician Apprentice Level 1 Final Grade

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/amct1gap/

Credits: 10.0

Description: Motorcycle technicians work primarily on 2 and 3-wheeled motorcycles and other units such as motor scooters. Topics for level 1 include safety-related functions, routine work practices, tools, equipment and documentation, communication and mentoring techniques, wheels and tires, brake systems, final drive systems, electrical systems and vehicle management systems.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

### Level 2 (5 weeks)

AMCT 2GAP - Motorcycle Technician Apprentice Level 2 Final Grade

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/amct2gap/

Credits: 10.0

Description: Motorcycle technicians work primarily on 2 and 3-wheeled motorcycles and other units such as motor scooters. Topics for level 2 include using tools, equipment and documentationm. Maintaining chassis and components, suspension systems, two-stroke and four-stroke engines, clutches and primary drives, transmissions, electrical systems, and fuel and exhaust systems.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

### Level 3 (5 weeks)

AMCT 3GAP - Motorcycle Technician Apprentice Level 3 Final Grade

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/amct3gap/

Credits: 10.0

Description: Motorcycle technicians work primarily on 2 and 3-wheeled motorcycles and other units such as motor scooters. Topics for level 3 include using tools, equipment and documentation. Maintaining chassis and components, suspension systems , wheels and tires, two-stroke and four-stroke engines, transmissions, final drive systems, electrical systems, fuel and exhaust systems.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

### Level 4 (5 weeks)

AMCT 4GAP - Motorcycle Technician Apprentice Level 4 Final Grade

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/amct4gap/

Credits: 10.0

Description: Motorcycle technicians work primarily on 2 and 3-wheeled motorcycles and other units such as motor scooters. Topics for level 4 include performing work practices, using communication and mentoring techniques. Maintaining brake systems, two-stroke and four-stroke engines, transmissions, vehicle management systems, fuel and exhaust systems, and electric motorcycles.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.



## Entrance Requirements

Pathways

There are two ways of beginning your new career as a motorcycle technician apprentice: Take our Motorcycle Technician Foundation program. By working through a series of practical projects and assignments and theory assignments you'll build a solid foundation of knowledge and skills to make you more employable in an entry-level position. By successfully completing the program, you will also receive credit for the in-school portion of Level 1 of our Motorcycle Technician Apprenticeship training. Find employment under a motorcycle technician who will register you with SkilledTradesBC . In this option, you are indentured to a company as an apprentice, learning basic skills while you are employed. The advantage here is that you earn while you learn.

Entrance requirements

You must be an active apprentice registered with SkilledTradesBC to register for apprenticeship training at BCIT.

## Program Details

Program length

The total amount of in-school, technical training for a motorcycle technician apprenticeship is 600 hours. There are four levels of technical training; each level is five weeks in duration.
