### Required Courses (20.5 credits):

OPMT 1106 - Quality Management Fundamentals

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/opmt1106/

Credits: 3.0

Description: Quality impacts every aspect of the operations of a company, therefore managing processes and the quality of their outputs is an essential responsibility of any organization that wants to succeed in business and provide true customer satisfaction. This course introduces the basic concepts and tools of quality management systems for manufacturing and service industries. Students learn a general overview of quality management topics and process approach: basic concepts of quality control and quality assurance; quality systems and their impact in an organization to ensure consistency of product and/or service reliability and quality; process definition and control; problem solving; standards for quality management systems; inspection operations; lean tools introduction; and quality costs. Using real case studies via in-class exercises and assignments, students will have the opportunity for practical application of concepts.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

OPMT 1130 - Business Statistics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/opmt1130/

Credits: 5.5

Description: Includes descriptive statistics, including numerical and graphical presentation of data, measures of central tendency, dispersion and elementary probabilities. Introduction to several discrete and continuous probability distributions . Introduction to inferential statistics through selected topics such as sampling, confidence limits of the mean, hypotheses testing, simple linear regression and the chi-squared test for independence.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

OPMT 1710 - Introduction to Lean Concepts

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/opmt1710/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course is the first of 2 courses (also OPMT2710 - Lean Leadership) that will give the student the basic understanding of lean. Combined the two courses will deliver the Lean Body of Knowledge that prepares the student to write the ASQ/AME/Shingo Institute/SME Lean Bronze Certification exam. Lean used to be referred to as "Lean Manufacturing". It has since been proven that Lean is not just a "shop floor" thing. Lean applies in every type of business. This first course introduces the student to Lean Enterprise concepts, how and why it evolved and many of the tools used within a Lean Environment.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

OPMT 1740 - Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt Certification

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/opmt1740/

Credits: 3.0

Description: The Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt certification course is a crucial stepping stone for individuals and professionals seeking to enhance their understanding of the Lean Six Sigma methodology. This certification validates your skills in the fundamental principles of Lean Six Sigma and empowers you to contribute effectively to process improvement projects within your organization. BCIT's Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt (LSSYB) Certification Course offers individuals and professionals an in-depth understanding of how to excel in process improvement and operational efficiency. This course introduces fundamental Lean Six Sigma principles, basic quality management tools, and essential techniques for data-driven decision-making. Lean Six Sigma is a globally recognized approach that combines Six Sigma concepts and tools to the Lean manufacturing /Lean enterprise philosophy, striving to eliminate waste of physical resources, time, effort, and talent, while assuring quality in production and organizational processes. Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt is the starting point of Six Sigma qualifications and is aimed at those who are new to the world of Six Sigma and are keen on developing basic knowledge about the methodology. This is an entry-level certification for professionals who are looking to get an overview of Six Sigma and the Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, and Control (DMAIC) process. The knowledge gained through this certification helps the workforce in understanding the concepts and potential of Six Sigma tools and process improvement methodologies within the enterprise. This course follows the Body of Knowledge (BOK) of the International Association of Six Sigma Certifications (IASSC). Certification A grade of 60% is required to pass this course and obtain the Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt Certification. Upon successful completion of the certification requirements, a formal certificate confirming that the student has achieved the Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt practitioner level will be issued within 3 weeks.

Prerequisites: 50% in OPMT 1710You may qualify to have the prerequisite waived for this course if you have equivalent experience in Lean methodology. Please contact the Flexible Learning coordinator Juan_Azmitia@bcit.ca for approval.

OPMT 2740 - Lean Six Sigma Green Belt Certification Part 1

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/opmt2740/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course develops an understanding of Lean and how it is applied across a variety of settings. In addition to understanding the core Lean concepts, tools and practices, participants will gain the capability and skills to facilitate improvement initiatives. Participants learn excellent techniques for creating value; improving processes and implementing change; as well as capabilities that are highly transferrable across any organization. This course follows the Body of Knowledge (BOK) of the International Association of Six Sigma Certifications (IASSC) and the Association for Manufacturing Excellence (AME).

Prerequisites: 60% in OPMT 1740 and 50% in OPMT 1106 and 50% in OPMT 1130

OPMT 2750 - Lean Six Sigma Green Belt Certification Part 2

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/opmt2750/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course, which is the 2nd part of the Lean Green Belt Certification, is purely project based. Using the notions learned in the Lean Green Belt Part 1 course, you will be tasked to do a full Lean analysis on the Current and Future state using the project templates supplied. The Lean Green Belt Part 2 certification course will provide you the practical knowledge on how to streamline processes, improve business performance and establish you as a go-to problem solver for any organization. The Green Belt course focuses on how process efficiency can be improved and how processes can be designed to deliver customer or client quality. This course follows the Body of Knowledge (BOK) of the International Association of Six Sigma Certifications (IASSC) and the Association for Manufacturing Excellence (AME).

Prerequisites: 60% in OPMT 2740

## Program Details

introduction

Lean Six Sigma is a managerial concept combining the problem-solving techniques of Lean and Six Sigma. Both Lean and Six Sigma focus on reducing unproductive processes in organizations with the goal to improve operational effectiveness. Lean, also known as Lean Manufacturing, Lean Enterprise, or Lean Production, is a process-improvement methodology that identifies and eliminates non-value-adding activities in business processes. Value-adding activities are what the customer is willing to pay for and non-value-adding activities are considered waste and unproductive areas. Six Sigma strives to improve the quality of service and manufacturing business process outputs by identifying and eliminating the causes of defects and minimizing variability and how to apply it in your current or future position. By training closely with working professionals and managers, you’ll gain the key concepts and abilities of Lean and Six Sigma so you can effectively manage the operations of many types of organizations.
