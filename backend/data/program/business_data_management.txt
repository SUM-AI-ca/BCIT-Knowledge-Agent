## Overview

Delivery

blended

Overview text

The demand for data-savvy professionals spans various industries, including finance, healthcare, marketing, retail, and manufacturing, where data-driven decision-making is critical to maintaining a competitive edge and driving innovation. The Associate Certificate in Business Data Management (ACDM) is designed to meet the growing need for data management skills across all industries and sectors, enhancing data literacy by providing professionals with the skills to govern and manage data quality, enabling them to leverage tools and techniques to ensure accurate and reliable data for analysis. As organizations increasingly rely on data to drive strategic decision-making, the need to ensure data integrity, privacy, and compliance has never been greater. The ACDM program prepares students to manage data as a strategic asset—harmonizing disparate systems, upholding governance standards, and enabling accurate, reliable insights that support informed decisions. Designed to meet the growing demand for data management and governance skills, this program empowers learners to thrive in a data-driven world while helping employers meet the need for innovative, competitive talent. Research from the Centre for International Governance Innovation reveals that many countries are still in the early stages of developing effective data governance frameworks, which creates significant challenges in ensuring accountability and reliability, particularly as data increasingly powers artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) systems. The ACDM program directly addresses this gap, equipping graduates with the knowledge and skills to navigate the complexities of data governance in the age of AI, thereby positioning them as valuable contributors to organizations that strive to leverage data responsibly and strategically. Who should apply for the Business Data Management Associate Certificate? If this sounds like you, apply today on the Entrance Requirements page.

### Required courses

BABI 3000 - Data Quality Approaches for Business

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi3000/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course explores the critical role of data quality in contemporary business environments. Students will learn various approaches and techniques for ensuring data quality and how it impacts decision-making, operational efficiency, and strategic planning within organizations. Practical applications and case studies will be used to reinforce concepts.

Prerequisites: Successful completion of Year 1 in the BITMAN program.

BABI 3005 - Data Governance for Business Analytics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi3005/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course explores the fundamentals of data governance and its crucial role in enabling effective and ethical data analytics. Students will learn the principles, frameworks, and best practices for managing and governing data assets in organizations, with a focus on supporting analytics initiatives. The course combines theoretical concepts with practical applications.

Prerequisites: Successful completion of Year 1 in the BITMAN program.

BABI 3100 - Business Analytics Theory

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi3100/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course introduces students to the theory of Business Data Analytics and prepares students to write the Certification in Business Data Analytics (CBDA) exam from the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA).

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

BABI 4000 - Applied Data Management for Analytics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi4000/

Credits: 5.5

Description: This course will introduce students to the fundamentals of data management for analytics. Students will learn how to use Microsoft Power Automate and other data management tools to collect, store, clean, and transform data. They will also learn how to integrate data from multiple sources and prepare data for analysis using various techniques. Students will have the opportunity to work with real-world datasets and build hands-on skills in data management and analysis.

Prerequisites: 50% in BABI 3000

BABI 4005 - DQM with Python

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi4005/

Credits: 2.0

Description: This course will introduce students to the concepts and techniques of data quality management (DQM) using the Python programming language. Students will learn how to use Anaconda and Python libraries most suited for DQM, such as pandas, NumPy, seaborn, and SciPy. They will learn how to assess data quality, identify data anomalies, and develop strategies for improving data quality. Students will have the opportunity to work with real-world datasets and build hands-on skills in data quality management using Python.

Prerequisites: 50% in BABI 3005
