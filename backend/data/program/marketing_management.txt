## Marketing Management for International Students
Between now and 2022, over one million jobs will become available across British Columbia, Canada . Many of these jobs will require marketing professionals with international experience.
Creative new companies start-up every year, while larger global firms expand into the Vancouver area, which has quickly become a booming technology hub.
That’s where BCIT comes in with exceptional Marketing Management Diploma programs. A BCIT credential is more than just book-learning. Our undergraduate programs combine theory with practice. You get the best of both worlds, with a recognized academic qualification and practical workplace training. Remarkably, 98% of our degree graduates are employed.
## International Info Sessions
Attend an Info Session specifically designed for international students in your own time zone. You will learn more about these full-time marketing programs, including entrance requirements, tuition and job opportunities.
