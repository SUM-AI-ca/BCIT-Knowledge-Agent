## Data Development Technologies
### Thousands of students have learned data technologies at BCIT.
BCIT Computing Flexible Learning offers four separate data-related credentials, course by course at night and on the weekends. With elective planning, these programs can be taken as a series of laddered credentials.
ACIS starts with becoming an expert end-user of a Windows 11 PC.
ACIS is aimed at two audiences:
- Those currently working in information systems with no formal credentials, and
- Those interested in foundational aspects of Computing and IT before moving on to further education.
ACIS g raduates acquire a solid understanding of the technical foundations of information systems, and may decide to move on to the laddered credential path to the Computer Systems Certificate ( CSC ) and the CST FLEX Diploma or move on to Applied Database Administration and Design ( ADAD ) or Applied Data Analytics Certificate ( ADAC ) programs.
ADAD is aimed at two audiences:
- Those who want to design and build RDBMS, and work as Database Administrators (DBAs) who manage access and policies for secure and scalable business information systems.
- Those who also want to explore Business Intelligence (BI), Data Mining, and Reporting tools.
Hands-on courses provide experience with data modelling, relational database design, SQL programming, data quality and task automation. Students install, configure and manage a Microsoft SQL Server, and choose from a variety of industry electives, including data visualization.
ADAD graduates with additional prior education and experience have career prospects in Database Administration, and also have significant course credits toward ADAC .
ADAC is aimed at those who have math skills and want to develop, build, and use data analytics systems.
Hands-on courses provide experience with data modelling, relational database design, and SQL programming. Students choose from elective courses in Information Technology and Business Intelligence reporting systems, or tools to create and manage systems for evidence-based decision-making.
ADAC graduates are equipped to help organizations across all sectors make informed and actionable decisions.
You can apply to receive a microcredential after completing 3 courses.
- Data Visualization with Microsoft Power BI
Earn a badge after completing just one course.
- COMP 2256 -Introduction to Visual Analytics with Tableau.
