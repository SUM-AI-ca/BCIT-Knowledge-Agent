## Marketing Management Diploma
## Kickstart your career in two years
In just under two years, you’ll transition from classroom learning to hands-on experience in the workplace. This program is a great choice for those seeking a career in the dynamic world of marketing or sales.
Whether you’re a recent high school graduate, an international student, or someone looking to switch careers, our Marketing Management Diploma program is your gateway to a successful future in the ever-evolving world of marketing and sales.
Take the first step towards an exciting and rewarding career – apply now!
## Start here
Begin your journey as a Marketing Management Diploma student at the BCIT School of Business + Media.
In the first year, you’ll receive a foundational education in business and marketing, delivered by industry experts. Build a strong foundation in your field through hands-on training in industry-standard marketing tools and techniques.
Take advantage of valuable networking opportunities through our events and partnerships with industry leaders, connecting you with professionals in your chosen field.
## Specialize in year two
Take your marketing and sales skills to the next level as a second-year Marketing Management Diploma student. Build on your strong foundation from first year and gain practical experience. Choose from one of our five specializations and take your expertise to new heights.
### Digital Marketing
Immerse yourself in digital marketing and brand strategy, and advance your career.
### Entrepreneurship
Gain practical skills and knowledge to manage your own businesses.
### Real Estate
Gain knowledge from seasoned real estate professionals and connect with industry experts.
### Professional Sales
Join one of Canada's top sales programs and earn in-demand B2B selling skills.
### Tourism Marketing
Launch your dream career in tourism with BC's only business-focused marketing and sales program.
Email Study_Marketing@bcit.ca for general, industry, and media inquiries.
## Launch your career
According to Glassdoor statistics, the average salary for a Marketing Graduate in Canada is $64,659 per year.
Establish a strong foundation for your future career in marketing, professional sales, public relations, digital media, entrepreneurship, tourism, or real estate through our rigorous program. Explore career options including:
- Marketing Coordinator
- Account Manager & Business Developer
- Brand Strategist
- Owner/Operator
- Commercial/Residential Brokerage
- Hotel Manager
- Conference Coordinator
## Ladder up to a degree
Ladder your Marketing Management diploma program into BCIT’s Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) degree program, which you can complete full-time in the day, or around your new career as part-time in the evenings.
