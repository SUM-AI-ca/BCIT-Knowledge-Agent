## Ironworker Generalist
Apprenticeship Full-time School of Construction and the Environment
## Overview
Note: To be eligible for apprenticeship training you must be registered as an apprentice. Go to the BCIT Apprenticeship website for more information.
Delivery: in person. See details .
Ironworkers rig, erect, and permanently join structural members fabricated from steel, concrete, and timber to form the frameworks of buildings and infrastructure. Their work spans the construction of high-rise buildings, bridges, dams, industrial facilities, and renewable energy structures. Ironworkers collaborate closely with other trades and engineers, using specialized tools and cranes to safely assemble complex frameworks. This physically demanding trade suits individuals who enjoy teamwork, problem-solving, and working outdoors, often beyond standard work hours to meet critical project timelines.
An ironworker apprentice must complete a three-year program including 4260 workplace hours and 600 in school hours of training. Level 1 – 300 hours (10 weeks) or completion of the Ironworker Foundation program (23 weeks); Level 2 – 180 hours (6 weeks), this level is under review by SkilledTradesBC; and Level 3 – 180 hours (6 weeks), this level is under review by SkilledTradesBC. After completion of training, a passing grade on exams will result in the B.C. Certificate of Apprenticeship, the B.C. Certificate of Qualification, and the Interprovincial Standard Endorsement, also known as Red Seal.

## Entrance Requirements
### Pathways
There are two ways of beginning your new career as an ironworker generalist apprentice:
- Take our Ironworker Foundation program. By working through a series of practical projects and assignments and theory assignments you'll build a solid foundation of knowledge and skills to make you more employable in an entry-level position. By successfully completing the program, you will also receive credit for the in-school portion of Level 1 of our Ironworker Generalist Apprenticeship training.
- Find employment under an ironworker who will register you with SkilledTradesBC . In this option, you are indentured to a company as an apprentice ironworker, learning basic skills while you are employed. The advantage here is that you earn while you learn.
### Entrance requirements
You must be an active apprentice registered with SkilledTradesBC to register for apprenticeship training at BCIT.

### Register for Apprentice Training
To register for apprentice training:
