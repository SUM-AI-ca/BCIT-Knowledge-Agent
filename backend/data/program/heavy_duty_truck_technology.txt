Title: Heavy Duty Truck Technology

================================================================================

## Overview

Delivery

in person

Overview text

With four technical training terms plus one 15-week paid co-op work term, the Heavy Duty Truck Technology program provides more time spent per topic than traditional heavy duty truck apprenticeship training. Courses are delivered in-class, online, and in the shop, just as they are by major employers in the industry. This innovative program includes management courses to ensure you are ready for high-demand positions as well as advancement opportunities. Major manufacturers ensure students learn on the latest technologies and support our training by sharing their online course materials as well as specific engines and products. In-depth diagnostic skills are achieved through this program, going beyond the National Red Seal Occupational Standards and BC curriculum with courses including electric vehicle propulsion, fuel cells, power generation, A/C motor control, and mobile refrigeration. Upon successful completion of the program, students will receive a Heavy Duty Truck Technology Diploma and qualify for credit towards the first three levels of the technical training requirements in the provincial common core heavy mechanical trades apprenticeship: heavy duty equipment technician, truck transport mechanic, transport trailer technician, diesel engine mechanic, and up to 900 hours of time in trade requirement. ### Term 1 (15 weeks)

HDTT 1001 - Occupational Skills

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt1001/

Credits: 8.0

Description: This course has a number of learning outcomes in which you will apply and demonstrate safe work practices and use tools and shop equipment. You will also use fasteners and fittings, lift and support loads, operate equipment, and service wire rope, bearings and seals. In this occupational skills course you will identify lubricants, and use welding equipment, electronic media and record keeping practices.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

HDTT 1010 - Brakes

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt1010/

Credits: 4.0

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to successfully service and repair hydraulic brake systems, service and repair hydraulic power brakes, and service and repair air brakes.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 1020 - Hydraulics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt1020/

Credits: 4.0

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to successfully describe hydraulic system principles, components and system types, and to service hydraulic components.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 1030 - Electrical

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt1030/

Credits: 5.0

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to successfully describe electricity, magnetism, and circuit components; interpret wiring diagrams and symbols, and use electrical testing instruments, service batteries, charging systems, starting systems, and electrical circuits.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 1040 - Frames, Steering, Suspension

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt1040/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to successfully service steering systems, service, diagnose, and repair suspension systems, remove and install undercarriage, and diagnose and repair frames.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 1050 - Trailer

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt1050/

Credits: 2.0

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to successfully service landing gear and trailer accessories, service and repair coupling systems, and service, diagnose, and repair trailer body components, heating systems, and refrigeration systems.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 1060 - Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt1060/

Credits: 2.0

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to successfully identify heating and air conditioning components, describe the construction and operation of heating and air conditioning systems, describe the impact of CFCs on the environment and apply legislated procedures when dealing with systems containing CFCs.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 1070 - Structural Components

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt1070/

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt2050/

Credits: 2.0

Description: This course will provide you with the basic skills for effective communication in a heavy duty truck shop, including a discussion of relevant working conditions and business practices. Written and oral skills to produce a job package will prepare you for successful employment, together with an explanation of the Truck Transport Mechanic (TTM) apprenticeship process. On-the-job communication among employees, management and customers will be practiced through presentations.

### Term 3 (15 weeks)

HDTT 3001 - Powertrain

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3001/

Credits: 12.0

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to describe gear ratios and power transfer principles and methods, the operation, diagnosis and repair of clutches, manual, automated, automatic and power shift transmissions. In this course you will also learn the principles, operation, repair and diagnosis of the drivelines, drive axles, final drives, driveline retarders, along with power take-offs and transfer cases.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 3010 - Detroit Diesel Engines

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3010/

Credits: 1.5

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to describe then perform the basic tune-up and preventative maintenance procedures of the DD13/15 Detroit Diesel engines. This course includes an overview of after-treatment devices (ATD), electrical components and new emission components.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 3020 - Detroit Diesel Diagnostics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3020/

Credits: 1.5

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to learn how all the components of the fuel system, from the fuel tank to the injector, work together on a properly-running engine. Based on that knowledge you will learn to use the latest electronic service routines and to diagnose fault code or symptom-based issues with the fuel system. This course will cover the evolution of the DD engine fuel system from its initial EPA07 release through the latest GHG17 release.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 3030 - Detroit Diesel After-Treatment

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3030/

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to diagnose, service and repair Detroit Diesel after-treatment systems.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 3040 - Power Generation

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3040/

Credits: 1.5

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to describe alternating current (AC) generators. You will also learn to identify components and perform maintenance and diagnostic procedures on AC generators.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

HDTT 3050 - Cummins Engines

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3050/

Credits: 2.5

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to describe Cummins engines, fuel systems and the EGR/ SCR after-treatment systems with VGT. This course will include the mechanical and electrical sub-systems, cooled EGR and exhaust after-treatment system.

HDTT 3060 - Paccar MX Engines

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3060/

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to describe the Paccar MX engines. In this course you will also learn the Davie diagnostic software, exhaust turbochargers, engine braking assemblies, and fuel and exhaust after-treatment, lubrication, and cooling systems of the MX engines.

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3070/

Credits: 2.5

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to successfully communicate in writing and verbally within the heavy duty truck industry with managers, supervisors, government inspectors or with the public. You will complete record keeping through the use of appropriate forms, documents and media, following service manual procedures and manufacturer technical bulletins.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

HDTT 3080 - Service Management Financial Concepts

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3080/

Credits: 2.5

Description: This course will introduce you to fundamental accounting principles and processes used by Service Managers to assess the performance of service operations. Referencing a business plan, you will explore fixed and variable operating costs including equipment purchase or renewal, insurance coverage, labour needs and building overhead. Pricing strategies for business sustainability and growth will be evaluated through the job costing approach.

Prerequisites: HDTT 1001

### Co-op Work Term (15 weeks)

HDTT 3990 - Co-op Work Term

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt3990/

Credits: 15.0

Description: Cooperative Education (Co-op) involves enhancing the educational experience by integrating traditional academic studies with relevant work experience. You will work full time at the employer's work site for the duration of the Co-op work term period. The Co-op work term is a full time paid position where you will complete productive tasks that relate to the heavy duty truck industry. The Co-op position is approved by the Co-op Coordinator. Students in the program attend a pre-employment course to enhance their employability prior to their Co-op work term and compete for jobs posted during the first academic term. During the work term, students are monitored by BCIT.

Prerequisites: HDMT 1001 and completion of the BCIT HDTT term 1 and term 2 courses, and the co-op pre-employment workshops

HDTT 4001 - Emerging Technologies

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt4001/

Credits: 13.0

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamentals of emerging and advanced technologies in the heavy duty truck industry. You will acquire fundamental skills in working with the latest technologies and alternate fuel / propulsion systems including: compressed natural gas (CNG), liquid propane gas (LPG), fuel cell technology, autonomous controls and guidance systems, electric vehicles and high voltage systems.

HDTT 4010 - Research Technology Projects

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hdtt4010/

Credits: 4.0

Description: This course will introduce you to the fundamentals of the fuel, combustion and emission chemistry as it applies to ignition and compression engines, and applied research as it relates to over-the-road transportation. You will participate in the Emissions Reduction and Research Test Hub (ERRTH) engine emissions testing using portable emissions measuring system (PEMS), dynamometers and advanced equipment. You will also engage with industry supplied projects such as electric vehicles, or participate in conversions of internal combustion engines to fully electric propulsion and energy storage systems.
