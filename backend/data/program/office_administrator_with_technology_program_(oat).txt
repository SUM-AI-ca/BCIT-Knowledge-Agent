## Courses

Class hours

9:00 am to 4:00 pm, Monday to Friday.

### Required Courses:

OATP 1000 - Communication Skills

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/oatp1000/

Credits: 5.0

Description: This course has been designed to assist students in the application and development of professional skills, necessary to succeed in industry. Topics covered include, but are not limited to: interview skills, career planning, time management, team building and conflict resolution skills.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

OATP 1200 - Business Development

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/oatp1200/

Credits: 6.0

Description: This course has been designed to assist students in the application and development of business skills to function effectively in an administrative role. Topics covered include, but are not limited to financial reporting, document design, social media and presentation skills

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

OATP 2000 - Information Technology

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/oatp2000/

Credits: 24.0

Description: This course has been designed to give students advanced skills in the most recognized and used computer applications in business. Topics covered include, but are not limited to: Microsoft Office, computerized accounting, and website development.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

OATP 3000 - Work Place Education

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/oatp3000/

Credits: 11.0

Description: This workplace education provides students with the opportunity to augment their classroom training and demonstrate learned technical and social skills. Students will acquire and further refine their skills through real-world work experiences working in an administrative related position.

Prerequisites: OATP 1000 and OATP 1200 and OATP 2000

OATP 3001 - Capstone Project

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/oatp3001/

Credits: 11.0

Description: The capstone project is a directed study course in which students continue to develop skills required for office administrators in simulated working environments. Students will apply best industry practices to develop documents, spreadsheets, presentations, databases, a website with a focus on employment considerations. To manage tasks and meet deadlines, students will work collaboratively using communication tools adopted by the industry. All students will have scheduled meetings with the instructor to track their progress and receive feedback. Evaluation is based on performance on weekly projects.

Prerequisites: 65% in OATP 1000 and 65% in OATP 1200 and 65% in OATP 2000



## Entrance Requirements

Application processing

This program is open to applications: *or next business day Intakes may fill before the deadline. Applications are considered for a specific intake.

Entrance requirements

Applicants must meet the following entrance requirement and will be accepted on a first-qualified basis as long as space remains: Read more about how to meet BCIT’s entrance requirements English language proficiency: Category 3 – English Studies 12 (50%) or equivalent

Recommended for success

Applicants are recommended to take COMP 1002 – Applied Computer Concepts before applying to this program if they have limited experience with a computer operating on Windows, particularly for tasks like managing personal files.

International applicants

The in-person delivery option is available to international applicants. The online delivery option is available to international applicants who will complete the program from outside Canada or who currently have a valid status in Canada other than study permit. Study permit: A valid BCIT study permit is required prior to starting the in-person delivery program. International students completing the program fully online are not eligible for a study permit. A co-op work permit is also required for the in-person delivery program.

The BCIT student outcomes report presents summary findings from the annual survey of former students administered by BC Stats one to two years after graduation. These reports combine the last three years of available results for the 2022-2024 BCIT Outcomes Surveys of 2021-2023 graduates and for Degree 2020-2022 graduates.
