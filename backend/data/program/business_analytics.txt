## Courses

Introduction

Drawing on BCIT’s applied approach to teaching and learning, the courses in this program incorporate a blend of case study, problem-based learning, collaborative team-based learning, and individual project assignments. In the last course – the Business Analytics Capstone Project – you will participate in all the steps of a real-world analytics assignment, as well as develop your project management skills. Please view the 2025/26 schedule [PDF] .

Class hours

With the exception of BABI 9005 and BABI 9050, each course is completed over a seven week period, with students in the classroom one evening a week, from 5:30 pm to 9:00 pm, at BCIT’s Downtown Campus. The balance of the week’s work is completed online using BCIT’s learning management system, The Learning Hub. BABI 9005 Theory of Business Data Analytics is entirely online and runs over the first 14 weeks of the program. BABI 9050 Business Analytics Capstone Project is completed in the second term, over 11 weeks. Please review the course delivery schedules on the Program Entry page carefully before applying to the program. Ensure that you are able to attend all classes and complete the online component each week for the intake that you are applying to.

### Term 1:

BABI 9005 - Theory of Business Data Analytics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi9005/

Credits: 3.0

Description: This course covers the theory of Business Data Analytics, and prepares students to write the Certification in Business Data Analytics (CBDA) exam from the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA).

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

BABI 9010 - Foundations of Applied Business Analytics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi9010/

Credits: 3.0

Description: The field of Business Analytics is growing in every industry sector. Business decisions are now made with the support of data and methodologies to develop strategic advantage. This course explores the lifecycle of applied business analytics and develops the skills needed to be successful in a business analytics role. Students will learn to conduct business analytics projects from start to finish, including developing business needs, requirements gathering and developing a hypothesis, data preparation and exploration, identifying metrics, using best practices in report development, performing ethical and legal use of data, and effectively communicating analytics results. Students will complete real-world studies using business analytics techniques.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

BABI 9020 - Utilizing Data Structures for Business Analytics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi9020/

Credits: 3.0

Description: Insight without relevance is meaningless in Business Analytics. Relevance comes from a deep understanding of the data and the methods used to store and access it. This course enables students to perform complex tasks associated with data in the business environment, from storage to access. Using current Business Analytics database technologies, students will explore the different storage systems and constructs, the use of analytics structures to define data cubes, and the tools used to perform ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) operations on existing datasets to support Business Analytics. An integral part of the course will be a project in which students will build and demonstrate a fully integrated analytical model—from entering data into an existing Relational Database Management System (RDBMS) and using ETL tools to get relevant data into the analytical database (cube), to running analytical queries on the cube to provide meaningful insight into the data.

Prerequisites: BABI 9010

### Term 2:

BABI 9030 - Quantitative Methods for Business Analytics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi9030/

Credits: 3.0

Description: Effective business decisions begin with a sound understanding of the analytical techniques and methods used to make sense of the data underlying the business. This course increases students’ expertise in the mathematical and quantitative analysis methods used to perform Business Analytics in a real-world setting. Students will gain increased knowledge of quantitative methods for decision making, including forecasting, hypothesis testing, choice models, optimization, regression modelling, and break-even analysis, and will learn to use these tools effectively to solve complex analytical problems. Students will perform choice of tools, selection and manipulation of data, and presentation of complete solutions in a quantitative analysis project setting.

Prerequisites: BABI 9020

BABI 9040 - Analytic Models for Business Decisions

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi9040/

Credits: 3.0

Description: Effectively communicating business analytics results requires knowledge of the intended audience as well as insight into the data. Knowing the questions to ask is supported by the skills to choose the correct modelling technique. Students will access a dataset, analyze it using quantitative methods, and select, format, and explore the data to discover trends and correlations. Multiple model scenarios will be explored, and students will learn to differentiate and effectively choose the correct model, and justify their choice within the framework of Business Analytics. Students will also expand on their knowledge of the ethical uses of data and the legal framework around freedom of information and protection of privacy.

Prerequisites: BABI 9030

BABI 9050 - Business Analytics Capstone Project

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/babi9050/

Credits: 4.0

Description: Drawing on the knowledge gained in the previous courses, students will assume the role of the Business Analytics Professional in a real-world Industry Project. Students are encouraged to develop the project proposal from their particular sector, and will develop the project from proposal to completion, working closely with the Faculty advisor and their Industry stakeholder. Students will apply their Business Analytics skill set to the solution of the Industry Project. Evaluation will be based on application and integration of Business Analytics to the solution.

Prerequisites: BABI 9040

## Program Details

introduction

Upon successful completion of the Graduate Certificate in Business Analytics, you will be able to: Over the six courses, you will develop case analysis skills, practical data analytic skills, and project management skills through a number of mini-projects. You will complete a range of key assessments, such as the development of databases and case-based projects, culminating with the final capstone industry project and presentation. You will be encouraged to retain these assessments in the form of a digital portfolio that can be used to demonstrate your business analytics skills to current and future employers. If you are looking for a BCIT Computing program that provides the technical foundations to build and manage data analytics systems and tools – consider the Applied Data Analytics Certificate (ADAC) , which is an undergraduate program with course-by-course registration.

Program length

Nine months, full-time, cohort program It is intended that students will complete the program with their cohort in nine months, following the course delivery schedule for each intake. Only under extenuating circumstances, with the approval of the program head and on a space-available basis, will students be able to resume their studies with another cohort if they are unable to complete the program with the intake that they are accepted into. The maximum amount of time allowed for completion of this program is three years.

Grading

Courses are graded on a percentage scale. The passing grade for each course is 60%.

Program delivery

Blended: This program is delivered partly on campus and partly online. This program is a full-time, six-course graduate certificate, delivered in a cohort model. Once you are accepted into the program, you will complete all six courses with your cohort. The courses are delivered over two terms (9 months). This program uses blended course delivery. Courses are delivered partly in the classroom and partly online using BCIT’s online learning management system, The Learning Hub. This is a Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) program. You will require your own laptop. Please check the program-specific BYOD requirements listed on the Program Entry page and ensure that you are able to meet these requirements before applying to this program. Note: Courses in this program are not available for individual course registration. Seats are only available to students that have applied and been accepted to the program.

Program location

Downtown Campus 555 Seymour Street Vancouver, BC Online Learning Courses are taught in a blended delivery model, with classroom-based sessions held at the BCIT Downtown Campus and the balance of coursework completed online using BCIT’s online learning management system, The Learning Hub.
