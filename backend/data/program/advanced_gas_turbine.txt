Title: Advanced Gas Turbine

================================================================================

## Overview

Overview text

BCIT’s School of Transportation at the Aerospace Technology Campus (ATC) proposes the creation of an Industry Partnership Certificate (IPC) for advanced gas turbine overhaul through applied learning, to provide an agile and rapid response to economic recovery in the aviation gas turbine sector. Initially, the IPC will be established with MTU Maintenance Canada , one of the largest networks of companies overhauling gas turbine engines in the Lower Mainland, but partnerships in the sector will continue to be pursued by BCIT as a way to expand this type of educational provision. The proposed IPC will be delivered at the industry partner’s worksite, in an accelerated format of 8 hours per day to model the typical industry work schedule, over the course of eight weeks. Graduates will earn a total of 19.5 credits. Potential students include graduates of any Aircraft Gas Turbine Technician certificate program from a recognized institution in Canada, including the BCIT Aircraft Gas Turbine Technician Certificate.

Class hours

### Required Courses:

AVGT 1200 - Safe Operation of Equipment

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/avgt1200/

Credits: 1.5

Description: In this course, students will learn how to safely perform engine maintenance practices while at the industry partners' facility. Students will become familiar with the facilities' safety infrastructure: they will learn the location of the first aid room, where fire extinguishers are stored, where the escape plan is posted including the location of muster stations. Furthermore, they will learn all relevant safety precautions to follow in the event of an emergency at the industry partner facility. As part of their safety training, students will study foreign object damage procedures in order to safely work on gas turbine engines and they will select and operate the appropriate lifting device while working with heavy objects in a shop environment.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

AVGT 1210 - Engine Familiarization

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/avgt1210/

Credits: 2.5

Description: Students will be introduced to specific gas turbine engines to be repaired and overhauled by local industry sector employers. Students will get an overview of the engine, the engine's systems and the engine aircraft interface, along with the engine layout and characteristics. Students will learn to identify engine component location, detect normal and abnormal system operations, determine routine service and/or maintenance requirements and describe basic troubleshooting of the engine systems. The course will also cover relevant safety precautions to follow during normal maintenance procedures.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

AVGT 2200 - Disassembly Techniques

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/avgt2200/

Credits: 2.5

Description: This course will provide students with the knowledge and practical hands-on experience to disassemble turbine engines and their sub-assemblies using specialty tooling and manufacturers' instructions. Based on given work orders, students will be able to identify and select the proper hand tools and gas turbine tooling in order to safely disassemble gas turbine engines. Students will also work with relevant industry standard work orders and follow standard procedures to accomplish the task efficiently and effectively. The course will also cover relevant safety precautions to follow during normal maintenance procedures.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

AVGT 2220 - Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) Inspection

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/avgt2220/

Credits: 5.5

Description: In this course, students will learn to interpret technical data to determine the serviceability on gas turbine parts. Topics covered include the use of software to track parts by part number and serial number during the repair and overhaul process. Students will select the part, interpret the OEM Overhaul manuals for inspection, and then route the part to its proper disposition to determine if the part is serviceable, unserviceable (replace), or can be routed for rework (repair). Students will also have the opportunity to interpret OEM service bulletins (SBs), verify the modification status of parts, and identify if the SB has been complied with. The course will also cover how to locate Airworthiness Directives (AD's) issued by the appropriate governing body and determine its applicability to any of the parts that they are inspecting.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

AVGT 2230 - Advanced Precision Measuring

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/avgt2230/

Credits: 2.5

Description: This course will enable students to assess the serviceability of gas turbine parts utilizing various precision measuring devices. Students will locate and verify manufacturers' tolerances regarding dimensions of mating parts, perform dimensional checks and calculate running clearances to suit gas turbine OEM fits and clearances. Students will experience activities performed in a coordinate measuring machine (CMM) room and observe and then analyze the dimensional inspection of complex parts.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

AVGT 3200 - Assembly Techniques

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/avgt3200/

Credits: 2.5

Description: This course will provide students with the knowledge and practical hands on experience to assemble gas turbine engines and their sub-assemblies working with specialty tooling and manufacturers' instructions. Based on work orders, students will learn to identify and select the proper hand tools and gas turbine tooling in order to safely disassemble gas turbine engines. Students will work with relevant industry standard work orders and follow standard procedures to accomplish the task efficiently and effectively. The course will also cover relevant safety precautions to follow during normal maintenance procedures.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

AVGT 3220 - Advanced Borescope Inspection

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/avgt3220/

Credits: 2.5

Description: In this course, students will learn to diagnose and assess the serviceability of gas turbine engines utilizing a borescope. Students will locate and verify manufacturers' tolerances regarding engine inspection criteria, and perform a borescope inspection at multiple locations on a turbine engine.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.
