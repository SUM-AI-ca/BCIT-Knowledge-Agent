Title: Heavy Mechanical Trades Foundation

================================================================================

## Overview

Delivery

in person

Overview text

This 36-week foundation program provides you with the theoretical understanding as well as practical skills needed to become proficient in basic mechanical maintenance and repair of heavy mechanical trades. You will become familiar with servicing hydraulic systems, air brakes, winches, blades and accessories.



## Courses

Class hours

0630 - 1430, Monday to Thursday or Tuesday to Friday, depending on the start date.

### Required Courses:

HMTF 1001 - Workplace Safety

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1001/

Credits: 2.0

Description: This course introduces students to personal safety, occupational health and safety, and environmental practices.

Prerequisites: No prerequisites are required for this course.

HMTF 1002 - Employability Skills

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1002/

Credits: 1.5

Description: This course introduces students to employment readiness, communication skills using electronic media, and industry business practices.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1003 - Tools and Equipment

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1003/

Credits: 7.5

Description: This course introduces students to the selection, use, and maintenance of hand, measuring, and power tools, fasteners, shop equipment, and reference resources. This course also introduces students to job action preparation and diagnostic procedures.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1004 - Welding

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1004/

Credits: 3.5

Description: This course introduces students to welding safety, identification of metals, and procedures for oxy-acetylene welding and shielded metal arc welding (SMAW)

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1005 - Operation of Equipment

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1005/

Credits: 5.0

Description: This course introduces students to the safe operation of lifting and mobile equipment.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1006 - Brakes

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1006/

Credits: 12.0

Description: This course introduces students to the principles of operation, repair, and service procedures performed on hydraulic and air brake systems. This course also introduces students to service and repair procedures performed on bearings, seals, tires, wheels, and hubs.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1007 - Hydraulics

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1007/

Credits: 4.5

Description: This course introduces students to the principles of hydraulics and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on hydraulic systems and components.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1008 - Electricity

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1008/

Credits: 7.5

Description: This course introduces students to the basic principles of electricity and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on electrical components and circuits.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1009 - Suspension and Steering

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1009/

Credits: 9.5

Description: This course introduces students to the basic principles of suspension and steering and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on suspension systems and components.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1010 - Trailer

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1010/

Credits: 4.0

Description: This course introduces students to the basic principles of trailer landing gear, accessories, trailer bodies, and refrigeration and heating systems and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on hydraulic systems and components. Prior to the refrigeration component of this course, students must obtain an Ozone Depleting Substances Handling Certificate from the Heating, Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Institute.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1011 - Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1011/

Credits: 2.0

Description: This course introduces students to the principles of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems and components. Prior to the practical component of this course, students must obtain an Ozone Depleting Substances Handling Certificate from the Heating, Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Institute.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1012 - Engine and Supporting Systems

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1012/

Credits: 6.5

Description: This course introduces students to the basic principles of internal combustion engines and their supporting systems and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on engines and their supporting systems and components.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1013 - Powertrain

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1013/

Credits: 6.0

Description: This course introduces students to the basic principles of powertrain and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on powertrain systems and components.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

HMTF 1014 - Structural Components and Accessories

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/hmtf1014/

Credits: 0.5

Description: This course introduces students to the basic principles of structural components and accessories and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on structural components and accessories.

Prerequisites: HMTF 1001

introduction

Note: This trade program is in high demand. Visit Trades Training BC to estimate a future start date.

Application processing

Applications are accepted throughout the year.

Entrance requirements

Applicants must meet all entrance requirements and will be accepted on a first qualified basis as long as space remains. When available intakes are full, qualified applicants are wait-listed. Applicants must meet all entrance requirements prior to applying to this program. If you are uncertain or don't meet the requirements we strongly recommend you complete any applicable Trades Entry Assessments before applying. Read more about how to meet BCIT’s entrance requirements English language proficiency: one of: Category 3 – English Studies 12 (50%) or equivalent or BCIT English Trades Entry Assessment Category 3 – English Studies 12 (50%) or equivalent or BCIT English Trades Entry Assessment Math: one of the following: Foundations of Math and Pre-Calculus 10 (50%) or Workplace Math 10 (50%) or Other acceptable BC and Yukon courses or BCIT Math Trades Entry Assessment Foundations of Math and Pre-Calculus 10 (50%) or Workplace Math 10 (50%) or Other acceptable BC and Yukon courses or BCIT Math Trades Entry Assessment

Continuation requirements

Students who wish to attend apprentice technical training must register as an apprentice with SkilledTradesBC . Foundation program students can register in the Heavy Mechanical Trades Apprenticeship programs.

Program delivery

Continue your education

Students who complete the Heavy Mechanical Trade Foundation program will receive technical training credit for Apprenticeship level 1 and 450 work-based hours credit toward completion of the Heavy Duty Equipment Technician apprenticeship program.



## Graduating & Jobs

Graduate employment outcomes

The BCIT student outcomes report presents summary findings from the annual survey of former students administered by BC Stats one to two years after graduation. These reports combine the last three years of available results for the 2022-2024 BCIT Outcomes Surveys of 2021-2023 graduates and for Degree 2020-2022 graduates. The reports are organized into three-page summaries containing information on graduates’ labour market experiences and opinions regarding their education. More detailed information can be accessed at the BC Student Outcomes website. To view these results, you may need to have the Adobe Acrobat Reader installed in your Web browser.
