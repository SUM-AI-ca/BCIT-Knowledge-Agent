## Flexible Learning that Works around Work
## Marketing is Evolving. Help Your Team Upskill, Reskill and Advance.
The marketing landscape is shifting quickly. Skill gaps are growing, and your team doesn’t have time to step away from work. BCIT helps employers strengthen employee retention, increase productivity, and close capability gaps, without disrupting operations or inflating training budgets.
Upskill your team with flexible, applied marketing and sales education. Employees learn while continuing to work. No downtime. No workforce disruption.
- Online and evening options
- Applied, job-ready learning
- Start with one course and scale as needed
- January, April and September start dates
- Optional BCIT credentials for employees who want to advance
## BCIT is a Trusted Workforce Development Partner
BCIT has supported thousands of working professionals in advancing their careers while continuing to work. Flexible Learning in marketing and sales is built for real business environments, not theoretical classrooms.
- Trusted BC brand with strong employer credibility
- Industry instructors who teach what they practice
- Employees apply job-ready skills immediately
- Courses aligned to real business outcomes
- Low-risk learning: pay per course, no long-term commitment

## Real Marketing and Sales Skills. Real ROI.
BCIT courses focus on applied learning that employees can use immediately:
- AI integration in marketing workflows
- Digital marketing strategy and execution
- Analytics, search, content, and social media
- Sales strategy, negotiation, and relationship building
- Sales force management
- Business development and revenue growth
- Strategic event planning and sponsorship
- Sustainability integration
- Nonprofit and fundraising strategy
- Tourism marketing and hospitality
Applied learning accelerates capability building and delivers measurable business impact. Employees can take individual courses or build toward a BCIT marketing or sales credential over time.

## Build Capacity without Disrupting Operations
Flexible Learning at BCIT is ideal for organizations that want to build internal capability, rather than rely on external hiring.
We support:
- High-potential employees preparing for expanded roles
- Marketing and sales teams adapting to digital transformation and AI
- Succession planning and talent pipeline development
- Cross-functional employees moving into marketing-related responsibilities
- Cost-effective workforce development initiatives
Whether you lead human resources for a large organization or manage talent development in a growing company, Flexible Learning provides a structured yet flexible way to strengthen performance without disrupting operations.
