### About the program
The demand for data-savvy professionals spans various industries, including finance, healthcare, marketing, retail, and manufacturing, where data-driven decision-making is critical to maintaining a competitive edge and driving innovation. The Associate Certificate in Business Data Management (ACDM) is designed to meet the growing need for data management skills across all industries and sectors, enhancing data literacy by providing professionals with the skills to govern and manage data quality, enabling them to leverage tools and techniques to ensure accurate and reliable data for analysis.
As organizations increasingly rely on data to drive strategic decision-making, the need to ensure data integrity, privacy, and compliance has never been greater. The ACDM program prepares students to manage data as a strategic asset—harmonizing disparate systems, upholding governance standards, and enabling accurate, reliable insights that support informed decisions.
Designed to meet the growing demand for data management and governance skills, this program empowers learners to thrive in a data-driven world while helping employers meet the need for innovative, competitive talent.
Research from the Centre for International Governance Innovation reveals that many countries are still in the early stages of developing effective data governance frameworks, which creates significant challenges in ensuring accountability and reliability, particularly as data increasingly powers artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) systems.
The ACDM program directly addresses this gap, equipping graduates with the knowledge and skills to navigate the complexities of data governance in the age of AI, thereby positioning them as valuable contributors to organizations that strive to leverage data responsibly and strategically.

## Program Details
### Program aim
The ACDM aims to equip individuals with the skills and knowledge to organize and manage data for meaningful analysis. The program focuses on data governance, quality, and integration, ensuring the reliability, accuracy, and completeness of data for impactful business outcomes.
### Program goals
Graduates of the program will:
- Leverage data governance frameworks to ensure that data is utilized effectively for analytics and meets the standards of privacy, security, and ethics.
- Explore the critical role of data quality in contemporary business environments, learning approaches and techniques to ensure data accuracy and reliability.
- Recognize and enhance the quality of data in various business scenarios.
- Utilize advanced tools to facilitate the cleaning, storage, transformation, and integration of data from diverse sources, thereby supporting business analytics.
- Plan for and use data analytics for strategic business purposes.
- Plan for and support the evolution of the mature data organization.
### Program length
The program can be completed in one year, based on completing two courses per term for two terms. The program must be completed within 5 years.
