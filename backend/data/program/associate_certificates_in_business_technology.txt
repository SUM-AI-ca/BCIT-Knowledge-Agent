## Entrance Requirements
### Who Qualifies
All three Associate Certificates share the same entrance requirements . If you meet them, you can apply to any of the three programs.
These programs are designed with working professionals in mind — part-time, blended delivery means you can build credentials without stepping away from your career. If your job is already touching data, analytics, or AI and you want structured, recognized skills to back it up, these certificates are built for you.
These courses are commonly completed as part of BCIT’s Business Diploma programs. Graduates of other post-secondary business programs may have equivalent courses that qualify.
Important: BCIT applies a five-year relevancy window to prior coursework. Courses completed more than five years ago may need to be retaken or supplemented.
English proficiency requirement: Category 2 — English Studies 12 (67%) or equivalent.
### Required Prerequisite Courses
BSYS 2000 – Applied Data Analytics in Excel (or equivalent)
- Minimum grade: 50%
- Required for: ACAI, ACBI, ACDM
BSYS 2065 – Business Systems Programming (or equivalent)
- Minimum grade: 50%
- Required for: ACAI, ACBI, ACDM
OPMT 1130 – Business Statistics (or equivalent)
- Minimum grade: 50%
- Required for: ACAI, ACBI, ACDM
OPMT 3197 – Management Science OR OPMT 3301 – Quantitative Methods for Business
- Minimum grade: 50%
- Required for: ACAI only
## From Where You Are to Where You Want to Be
A typical journey from where you are now to your BCIT Associate Certificate.
### Review Your Prerequisites
Review the required courses against your transcripts and check the five-year relevancy window to confirm what counts toward your application.
- Transcript review
- 5-year relevancy window
### Fill Any Gaps
If you’re missing one or more prerequisite courses, BCIT offers them as part-time flexible learning courses. Prerequisites run year-round, so you can complete them on a schedule that works for you.
- BSYS 2000
- BSYS 2065
- OPMT 1130
- OPMT 3197
- Year-round availability
### Apply to Your Chosen Certificate
Applications are accepted year-round. Apply early — intakes fill before the deadline. Choose ACAI, ACBI, or ACDM based on your career goals. Applications open through EducationPlannerBC.
💡 You may not need to wait. It may be possible to apply and begin coursework while you are still completing missing prerequisites.
### Start Your First Course
September marks the official program launch, but you can begin at any point once you’re admitted. Blended delivery means a mix of online and on-campus sessions designed to fit around a full-time work schedule.
- Blended delivery
- Part-time
### Graduate with Your Associate Certificate
Complete all required courses and graduate with a BCIT Associate Certificate — a credential recognized by BC employers in finance, healthcare, retail, marketing, and tech.
