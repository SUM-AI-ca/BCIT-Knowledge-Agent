## Heavy Mechanical Trades Foundation
Certificate Full-time School of Transportation
## Overview
Delivery: in person. See details .
### The program
This 36-week foundation program provides you with the theoretical understanding as well as practical skills needed to become proficient in basic mechanical maintenance and repair of heavy mechanical trades. You will become familiar with servicing hydraulic systems, air brakes, winches, blades and accessories.
## Entrance Requirements
### Application processing
Applications are accepted throughout the year.
### Entrance requirements
Applicants must meet all entrance requirements and will be accepted on a first qualified basis as long as space remains. When available intakes are full, qualified applicants are wait-listed.
Applicants must meet all entrance requirements prior to applying to this program. If you are uncertain or don't meet the requirements we strongly recommend you complete any applicable Trades Entry Assessments before applying.
- English language proficiency: one of: Category 3 – English Studies 12 (50%) or equivalent or BCIT English Trades Entry Assessment
- Math: one of the following: Foundations of Math and Pre-Calculus 10 (50%) or Workplace Math 10 (50%) or Other acceptable BC and Yukon courses or BCIT Math Trades Entry Assessment
Read more about how to meet BCIT’s entrance requirements
### Continuation requirements
- Students who wish to attend apprentice technical training must register as an apprentice with SkilledTradesBC .
- Foundation program students can register in the Heavy Mechanical Trades Apprenticeship programs.
### Recommended for success
- High school graduation is recommended by SkilledTradesBC. Please refer to the SkilledTradesBC website for current apprenticeship prerequisite information.
- A driver's licence is not required to enter the program. However, it is strongly recommended in order to move vehicles in and out of the shops.
- Potential students with medical or physical disabilities should contact Accessibility Services to arrange 
an interview with the institute's rehabilitation specialist at 604-451-6963 (telephone interview is acceptable for out of town applicants).

### Program matrix
Required Courses: | Credits
HMTF 1001 | Workplace Safety This course introduces students to personal safety, occupational health and safety, and environmental practices. Prerequisite(s): No prerequisites are required for this course. course outline | 2.0
HMTF 1002 | Employability Skills This course introduces students to employment readiness, communication skills using electronic media, and industry business practices. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 1.5
HMTF 1003 | Tools and Equipment This course introduces students to the selection, use, and maintenance of hand, measuring, and power tools, fasteners, shop equipment, and reference resources. This course also introduces students to job action preparation and diagnostic procedures. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 7.5
HMTF 1004 | Welding This course introduces students to welding safety, identification of metals, and procedures for oxy-acetylene welding and shielded metal arc welding (SMAW) Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 3.5
HMTF 1005 | Operation of Equipment This course introduces students to the safe operation of lifting and mobile equipment. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 5.0
HMTF 1006 | Brakes This course introduces students to the principles of operation, repair, and service procedures performed on hydraulic and air brake systems. This course also introduces students to service and repair procedures performed on bearings, seals, tires, wheels, and hubs. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 12.0
HMTF 1007 | Hydraulics This course introduces students to the principles of hydraulics and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on hydraulic systems and components. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 4.5
HMTF 1008 | Electricity This course introduces students to the basic principles of electricity and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on electrical components and circuits. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 7.5
HMTF 1009 | Suspension and Steering This course introduces students to the basic principles of suspension and steering and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on suspension systems and components. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 10.5
HMTF 1010 | Trailer This course introduces students to the basic principles of trailer landing gear, accessories, trailer bodies, and refrigeration and heating systems and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on hydraulic systems and components. Prior to the refrigeration component of this course, students must obtain an Ozone Depleting Substances Handling Certificate from the Heating, Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Institute. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 4.0
HMTF 1011 | Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning This course introduces students to the principles of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems and components. Prior to the practical component of this course, students must obtain an Ozone Depleting Substances Handling Certificate from the Heating, Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Institute. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 1.0
HMTF 1012 | Engine and Supporting Systems This course introduces students to the basic principles of internal combustion engines and their supporting systems and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on engines and their supporting systems and components. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 6.5
HMTF 1013 | Powertrain This course introduces students to the basic principles of powertrain and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on powertrain systems and components. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 6.0
HMTF 1014 | Structural Components and Accessories This course introduces students to the basic principles of structural components and accessories and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on structural components and accessories. Prerequisite(s): HMTF 1001 course outline | 0.5
Total Credits: | 72.0

The reports are organized into three-page summaries containing information on graduates’ labour market experiences and opinions regarding their education.
