## Bachelor of Science – Specialty Nursing Degree
## Is this Program Right For You?
This program is for diploma-prepared RNs with a current Canadian license and acute care experience. LPNs and RPN are not eligible. Undergraduate BSN students may take certain theory courses as electives with the permission of the program.
## Transform your RN Diploma into a Bachelor of Science in Nursing Degree (BSN)
The only program in BC designed specifically for working diploma RNs who want to advance their careers through specialty nursing education. Earn your Bachelor of Science in Specialty Nursing while gaining advanced clinical expertise in critical care, emergency medicine, pediatrics, or other specialized areas.
## Your Pathway to a Bachelor of Science in Specialty Nursing
Designed exclusively for diploma-prepared RNs with current Canadian licensure. This unique degree pathway combines specialty nursing education with academic coursework to advance your career while you continue practicing.
If you completed your nursing diploma outside of Canada and are looking to build your understanding of Canadian nursing practice, check out NSIN 7110 – Nursing in Canada: Legal, Ethical, and Equity Perspectives .
## Your Starting Point: Diploma RN
### Choose Your Specialty
Advanced Certificate
Choose from specialty areas such as Critical Care, Emergency, Pediatrics, Perioperative, or combination tracks. Earn your advanced specialty certification while continuing to build toward your nursing degree.
### The BSN Pathway
Academic Bridge
Complete core nursing theory courses, liberal education requirements, and targeted electives that strengthen your specialty foundation and expand on your clinical experience.
### Specialty BSN
Bachelor of Science in Nursing
Earn your degree while integrating advanced specialty certification and academic coursework to support career advancement and long-term professional growth.

## Find the right Specialty Nursing Program for you
Choose your specialty area within the Bachelor of Science in Specialty Nursing degree program. Each option combines advanced specialty certification with academic coursework designed specifically for diploma RNs advancing to bachelor’s level credentials. Select a specialty below to learn more about requirements and career outcomes.
Program | Who’s it For
Critical Care Nursing Specialty The BCIT Critical Care Nursing Specialty is an exciting and dynamic program that prepares registered nurses (RNs) with the knowledge, skills and abilities required to work in all levels of intensive care units (from community/rural to tertiary and quaternary), coronary care and cardiac surgery intensive care units, and post anesthetic care units. | Diploma RNs
Program | Who’s it For
Emergency Nursing Specialty This highly niched program prepares you to work in the fast-paced, dynamic emergency care environment. No two shifts are the same, with patients who change constantly and have many different reasons for needing emergency care. Emergency nurses take on leadership, support, and decision-making roles to provide the best patient care possible. | Diploma RNs
Program | Who’s it For
High Acuity Nursing Specialty High acuity applies the foundational medical-surgical nursing skills you learn as a registered nurse (RN) to an acute care setting. A niche area of patient care, high acuity nurses work in inpatient units supporting acutely ill adults with multiple health challenges. No two patients are the same and each requires individualized care. | Diploma RNs
Program | Who’s it For
Neonatal Nursing Specialty We equip nurses to care for infants who are in the first month of their lives and need hospitalization in neonatal nurseries and neonatal intensive care units (NICUs). Our program trains you in the technical and emotional skills to effectively support infants and their families during this challenging time. | Diploma RNs
Program | Who’s it For
Nephrology Nursing Specialty Nephrology nurses work closely with patients who require ongoing care or life-sustaining treatment. Using dialysis, renal transplantation, and other treatment methods, nephrology nurses manage illnesses such as chronic kidney disease and end-stage renal disease. Their work directly impacts quality-of-life and end-of-life care for renal patients. | Diploma RNs
Program | Who’s it For
Pediatric Nursing Specialty Pediatric nursing is dedicated to the health and care of young people, ranging from babies to teenagers. Children, as patients, have unique healthcare needs that require specialized knowledge and expertise in their growth, illness, and injury. | Diploma RNs
Program | Who’s it For
Perinatal Nursing Specialty Perinatal RNs work in partnership with childbearing women and their families, providing education and support to empower their patients with skills to care for their families. In a dynamic work environment, perinatal nursing requires technical expertise, sensitivity, and excellent communication skills to effectively support the women they care for. | Diploma RNs
Program | Who’s it For
Perioperative Nursing Specialty The BCIT Advanced Certificate in Perioperative Nursing prepares students to practice as novices in a variety of Perioperative settings. Students explore factors influencing Perioperative Nursing in today’s changing health care environment. | Diploma RNs
