## Overview

Overview text

The Truck and Transport Mechanic (TTM) apprenticeship program (formerly Commercial Transport) requires time spent on the job and in-school training. A Truck and Transport Mechanic trainee must complete a four-year program including workplace and in-school hours of training as follows: After completion of training, a passing grade on the interprovincial exam will result in the SkilledTradesBC Certificate of Qualification (Truck and Transport Mechanic), Certificate of Apprenticeship and the Interprovincial Red Seal Endorsement. Students work on mechanical and electronic components of vehicles. This includes most vehicle systems such as fuel, brake, steering and suspension systems, transmission, emission control and exhaust systems, engines, and electrical, cooling and air conditioning and refrigeration. ## Courses

### Level 4 (4 weeks)

ATTM 4GAP - Truck and Transport Mechanic Apprentice Level 4 Final Grade

Outline URL: https://www.bcit.ca/outlines/attm4gap/

Credits: 10.0

Description: This SkilledTradesBC Truck and Transport Mechanic (ATTM) level 4 course has 5 subjects in which you will learn to service, diagnose, repair, align, and demonstrate safe work practices, while conducting activities of a heavy duty equipment technician in a shop and field service role. In this level 4 course you will learn to service, repair, and perform diagnostic procedures on brakes, hydraulic, electrical, frames, and suspension systems, align steering, and repair cab and body structures.

Prerequisites: Student must be a sponsored apprentice.

## Graduating & Jobs

Graduate employment outcomes

The BCIT student outcomes report presents summary findings from the annual survey of former students administered by BC Stats one to two years after graduation. These reports combine the last three years of available results for the 2022-2024 BCIT Outcomes Surveys of 2021-2023 graduates and for Degree 2020-2022 graduates. The reports are organized into three-page summaries containing information on graduates’ labour market experiences and opinions regarding their education. More detailed information can be accessed at the BC Student Outcomes website. To view these results, you may need to have the Adobe Acrobat Reader installed in your Web browser.
