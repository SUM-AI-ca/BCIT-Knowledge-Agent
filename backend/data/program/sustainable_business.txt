## Sustainable Business
Are you passionate about social and environmental issues? Are you looking for a rewarding career building a more just and sustainable world for all? Our sustainable business programs equip you with the applied skills necessary to create a job path that’s fulfilling and rewarding for you, your community, and the planet. Our courses are offered in the evening and online to enable you to upskill in the rapidly growing field of sustainable business. Join us and help businesses and organizations build a better world. Watch an example of a Sustainable Business Leadership graduate who did just that.
In our sustainable business credentials, you can expect:
- Hands-on training: case-based learning and applied activities focused on relevant job skills training
- Small class sizes: receive individual attention and get to know your peers
- Connections to industry: learn from instructors and guest speakers working in the field and benefit from the real-world experience of sustainability practitioners
Choose from our one full-time and two part-time credentials:
- Full-time Sustainable Business Leadership Advanced Diploma (46.0 credits): four courses per term for three terms (fall, winter, spring); complete in 10 months; includes a two-term sustainable business consulting project ; recommended if you are working part-time or less
- Part-time Sustainable Business Leadership Advanced Diploma (46.0 credits): take evening courses at your own pace; have up to 4 years to complete; includes a two-term sustainable business consulting project , recommended if you are working full-time and want to gain additional leadership skills along with your sustainable business studies
- Part- time Sustainable Business Advanced Certificate (27.0 credits): begin with a shorter credential that ladders into the part-time Advanced Diploma; take evening courses at your own pace; have up to 4 years to complete; includes a one-term industry project ; recommended if you are working full-time and want to start with a smaller credential
