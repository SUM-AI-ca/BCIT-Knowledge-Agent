## Global Leadership
Graduate Certificate Full-time School of Business + Media
## Overview
The Global Leadership Graduate Certificate equips students with essential leadership skills and intercultural competencies, essential for navigating the global marketplace and workforce. This full-time 9-month cohort program also fosters the growth of a robust professional network. You'll develop effective communication and negotiation skills.
Delivery: in person. See details .
In the Global Leadership program at BCIT, you will gain invaluable leadership skills and intercultural competencies that are instrumental for today’s business environment. You will become responsive to the needs of both a global marketplace and workforce, while factoring in the needs and perspectives of internal and external stakeholders. Ultimately, this graduate certificate program will promote the global mindset required to lead in an increasingly diverse business world.
### About the program
The program incorporates a blend of assessments, including case studies, problem-based learning, collaborative group work, self-reflections, and individual projects. Students are exposed to real-world leadership issues throughout their coursework, culminating in a capstone project where students will be given the opportunity to work on solving real problems facing industry.
- Two terms to complete: Six graduate level courses taught over nine months (September to May)
- Blended course delivery: Evenings at the Vancouver campus combined with online learning
- Cohort program: You will work through this full-time program with your cohort, sharing your experience and expertise, and building a professional network that will last long after you have graduated
- Global leadership capstone project: Students assume the role of a professional in global leadership and develop a report and presentation, addressing solutions to real-world industry challenges
- Laddering options: Articulations with partners are being developed so that alumni can ladder to international master degree programs
### Who should apply for the Graduate Certificate in Global Leadership?
This program is designed for students who have completed a recognized bachelor degree and are looking to continue their education at the graduate level, or for employees looking to up-skill. If this sounds like you, visit the Entrance Requirements page to apply today.
### What grads can do
The Global Leadership program prepares graduates for careers in management and leadership roles across a range of industries, from health care to construction, and education to financial and business services. Learn more about the career opportunities open to graduates on the Graduating and Jobs page.
### MBA Degree Opportunities
The BCIT Graduate Certificate in Global Leadership program is structured to meet the requirements for direct entry and advanced placement in MBA and other master degree programs. For more information, please contact globalleadership@bcit.ca .

### Program matrix
Term 1: | Credits
GLBL 9010 | Working Effectively Across Cultures Explore effective communication, both in-person and remotely, with customers, colleagues and stakeholders from different cultures. You will enhance your understanding of different communication styles that will reduce misunderstandings in a multicultural context. Through team based and individual learning activities, you will reflect critically on your own cultural identities, biases, attitudes and cross-cultural experiences and discuss how these factors may impact your effectiveness in a cross-cultural work environment. Learning objectives will be demonstrated through individual and collaborative assignments that will challenge you to analyze, reflect and evaluate your own identities and apply this knowledge to real world scenarios. Prerequisite(s): Acceptance into the Graduate Certificate in Global Leadership program. course outline | 3.0
GLBL 9020 | Leading Global Teams Examine the role of leadership in developing and nurturing an effective global team. You will build on your knowledge of the impact cultural dimensions have on communication styles, working relationships, team performance, and leadership styles. Through collaborative and individual learning activities, you will analyze, practice, and refine the soft skills and strategies needed to create and lead high-performing global teams. Learn to recognize potential cross-cultural challenges, and develop strategies to mitigate them to enhance your leadership capacities and enable you to better manage and motivate cross-cultural teams. Competencies may be demonstrated through a blend of individual and collaborative assignments, skill demonstrations, and application to real world or hypothetical scenarios. Prerequisite(s): Acceptance into the Graduate Certificate in Global Leadership program. course outline | 3.0
GLBL 9050 | Leading Global Strategy Evaluate global strategic concepts that will enable successful leadership in a complex and rapidly changing global environment. You will analyze company strengths and weaknesses, assess risks and opportunities, and develop strategies for international companies. Through collaborative and individual learning activities, you will apply behavioural models to implement strategic change and overcome resistance to organizational strategic change. The objective of this course is to enhance leadership of strategy and to provide tools to develop effective business strategies and global goals to become an effective leader of strategic change. Competencies may be demonstrated through a blend of individual and collaborative assignments, skill demonstrations, and major papers. Prerequisite(s): Admission into the Graduate Certificate in Global Leadership. course outline | 3.0
Term 2: | Credits
GLBL 9030 | Leading Innovation Innovation drives ongoing improvements and has the potential to unleash a new idea that can serve as a competitive advantage for an organization. It is thus important for business professionals to be able to identify the need and context for innovation, the roles key stakeholders play, how resistance will be managed, the communication styles that will be required and finally, the stages or timescale of innovation. Examine and evaluate the fundamental concepts of innovation management. You will be presented with the practical tools for developing an innovative mindset, with an emphasis on building and fostering a culture of creativity and innovation. Learn the skills needed to drive innovative initiatives in the workplace and cultivate awareness of the impact of change to individuals and organizations. Competencies may be demonstrated through a blend of individual and collaborative assignments, skill demonstrations, and quizzes. Prerequisite(s): Acceptance into the Graduate Certificate in Global Leadership program. course outline | 3.0
GLBL 9040 | Conflict Management and Negotiations ​Explore the key elements of effective negotiation and conflict management. You will gain a deeper understanding of tools and processes that can be used to negotiate in a range of situations with internal and external stakeholders in a multicultural business context. Through collaborative and individual learning activities, you will develop strategies to resolve conflict in business and determine strategies that will increase team satisfaction and strengthen relationships. Competencies may be demonstrated through a blend of individual and collaborative assignments, skill demonstration, and exams. Prerequisite(s): Admission into the Graduate Certificate in Global Leadership program. course outline | 3.0
GLBL 9100 | Global Leadership Capstone Project Drawing on the knowledge gained in the previous courses, you will assume the role of a Professional in Global Leadership. You will concentrate on real-world Industry challenges by engaging either in action or secondary research that will provide a professional learning experience. You will apply your Global Leadership skill set to the solution of the Industry challenge by working closely with a Faculty advisor and/or an Industry stakeholder. You will create a graduate level major report and presentation and evaluation will be based on application and integration of Global Leadership key concepts to the solution. Prerequisite(s): Acceptance into the Graduate Certificate in Global Leadership program plus completion of Term 1. course outline | 4.0
Total Credits: | 19.0

## Program Details
In the Global Leadership program at BCIT, you will gain invaluable leadership skills and intercultural competencies that are instrumental for today’s business environment. You will become responsive to the needs of both a global marketplace and workforce, while factoring in the needs and perspectives of internal and external stakeholders. Ultimately, this graduate certificate program will promote the global mindset required to lead in an increasingly diverse business world.
### Program goals
In the Global Leadership program, you will learn to:
- Demonstrate leadership skills to develop and optimize successful global teams.
- Determine successful global strategies that will position an organization to capitalize on opportunities while being responsive to new and emerging situations.
- Cultivate a culture of trust and integrity, influence others, form teams, and build coalitions, in a cross-cultural context.
- Demonstrate effective and culturally-aware communication and negotiation skills both in direct and technology-enabled communications.
- Create clear objectives that identify competitive strategic advantages and integrate motivational strategies for employees in global teams.
