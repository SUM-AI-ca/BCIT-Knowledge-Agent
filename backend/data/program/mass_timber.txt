## Mass Timber Training Hub
## Mass Timber: The Skills Shaping the Future of Construction
At BCIT’s Mass Timber Training Hub, we are shaping the future of sustainable construction by providing cutting-edge education and hands-on training in mass timber design and construction. Tailored to meet the evolving needs of the industry, our programs focus on applied learning, equipping students with the practical skills and knowledge necessary to excel in the growing mass timber sector. Accelerate your career in the design-build industry while contributing to a sustainable tomorrow.

### Course Overview
This industry-aligned course provides an overview of mass timber design, fabrication, and construction, covering the project lifecycle from early concept development to installation. Participants will explore sustainability principles, engineered wood and mass timber products, structural systems, fabrication aspects, construction practices, and the value of off-site construction.

### What You’ll Learn
Across six modules, you will explore:
- Forests, Carbon & Sustainability – Carbon capture, lifecycle thinking, and certification systems.
- Wood Science & Engineered Wood Products – Behaviour of timber, manufacturing characteristics, and product variability.
- Mass Timber Design Considerations – Gravity and lateral systems, detailing, fire safety, and code considerations.
- Mass Timber Fabrication Considerations – Supplier expectations, quality control, deliverables, and offsite workflow.
- Mass Timber Construction Considerations – Tolerances, connections, moisture management, and site leadership.
- Off-site Construction & Industry Value – An exploration of the value of off-site construction principles for designers, builders, developers, and owners.

Digital Project Delivery for Mass Timber Construction is a stand-alone part-time course delivered over a 9-week timeframe. This course explores the impact of digital modelling on processes, methods and tools used in mass timber construction. Students will learn how digital modelling can transform project planning and execution from early schematic design to commissioning and occupancy. ### Course Overview
In the face of a changing climate and the urgent need for low-carbon solutions, forests and wood products play a critical role in reshaping how we design and build. This mini-course explores the intersection of climate change mitigation, sustainable forestry, and the use of mass timber in high-performance buildings.
Through a life cycle lens, participants will learn how sustainably managed forests and bio-based materials contribute to decarbonizing the built environment. The course also covers how to evaluate and recommend material strategies that balance embodied carbon, biodiversity, and health considerations.
Emphasis is placed on wood’s contribution to indoor environmental quality, biophilic design, and occupant wellness, alongside applications of leading green building certifications like LEED®, Built Green®, and Passive House.
Whether you’re an architect, builder, engineer, or policy advisor, this course provides the knowledge to make informed, climate-smart material decisions for today’s evolving construction landscape.
### Why take this Course?
By the end of this course, you will be able to:
- Describe the role of forest ecosystems, certification systems, and sustainable material selection in optimizing carbon storage and supporting climate-resilient, low-impact wood construction.
- Demonstrate the environmental performance of mass timber construction by applying life cycle principles, carbon uptake and environmental product declarations to inform low-carbon design and material selection strategies.
- Discuss the impact of material choices, particularly wood, on indoor environmental quality, occupant health, and building performance, including aspects of fire safety and acoustics.
- Explain how mass timber can contribute to meeting the criteria of major Canadian green building certification systems, including LEED®, Built Green®, and Passive House.

## Modern Methods of Construction Education
Modern Methods of Construction Education (MMC Edu) is the outcome of a national initiative led in partnership with Natural Resources Canada and BCIT’s Mass Timber Training Hub. MMC Edu connects educators, industry, and institutions to address training gaps in mass timber and off-site construction.
Through collaboration and applied learning, it has developed a mass timber training framework and supporting teaching resources to advance education and workforce readiness across Canada. Explore the resources and join the network.
