## International Applicants
## Start your studies in Canada at BCIT
Learn new skills and acquire an education and credential in Canada. Become qualified to apply for a study permit and a post-graduation work permit after graduation in eligible programs.
The three main ways to enter BCIT as an international student are through the following options:
- Full-time Cohort Learning
- Flexible Learning
- English Language and Academic Upgrading
## Full-time Cohort Learning
Select from a variety of program options using BCIT’s cohort model that puts a strong focus on team-based learning.
- Students are automatically registered into their courses each term
- Have a chance to earn your credential as quickly as possible with a full-time course load
- Immerse yourself in BCIT campus life by studying day classes throughout the week
- Create lasting connections with fellow students in your set as you progress through the program together
## Flexible Learning Studies
Choose from a variety of course and program options and register into courses directly each term.
- Flexibility to self-register for a full-time or part-time course load
- Start in one of three intakes: winter, spring/summer, or fall
- Work part time while maintaining a full-time academic status
- Flexible course offerings with evening, day, weekend options
- Qualified to apply for a study permit in eligible programs
