## Industry Services

BCIT International bolsters industry services to enhance human capital for international clients and stakeholders.

- BCIT’s international programing and knowledge transfer expertise are well developed through successful delivery of international projects to international stakeholders;
- BCIT International provides train-the-trainer and capacity building for off-shore institutions by leveraging BCIT’s experience built through many decades through the Learning & Teaching Center and BCIT’s wholly owned subsidiary TTA Technology Training Associates;
- TTA’s expertise in education consulting, international business development, and project management capacity fosters BCIT’s global footprint and influence. It has mobilized partnerships with organizations within government, business, and education; and carried out approximately $40 million in development projects for Canadian International Development Agency, the World Bank, and the Asian Development Bank.
