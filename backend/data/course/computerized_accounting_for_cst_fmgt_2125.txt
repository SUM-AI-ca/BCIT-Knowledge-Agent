## Computerized Accounting for CST FMGT 2125
Financial Management Course
## Course Overview
This is the last in a series of three accounting courses designed to provide first year Computer Systems Technology students with a general understanding of accounting. This particular module introduces the student to computer based accounting systems and illustrates the application of such a system in small business.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in FMGT 1100
