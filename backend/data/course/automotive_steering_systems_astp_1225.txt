## Automotive Steering Systems ASTP 1225
Automotive Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces you to the fundamental skills required to diagnose, service, and repair steering systems and perform wheel alignments.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Service steering columns.
- Describe manual steering systems.
- Describe power steering systems.
- Service power steering systems.
- Service steering linkage.
- Describe suspension and steering geometry.
- Perform wheel alignments.
