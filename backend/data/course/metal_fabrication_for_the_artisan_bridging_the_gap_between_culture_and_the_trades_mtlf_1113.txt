## Metal Fabrication for the Artisan: Bridging the Gap between Culture and the Trades MTLF 1113
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
​This course focuses on the development of basic shop skills as a metal fabricator and the practical application of skills in the pursuit of artistic endeavors and other functional steel items.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
10.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Work safely in a basic shop environment using all PPE and identifying hazards of working a shop.
- Interpret basic shop drawings and visualize how these may be assembled.
- Transfer information from drawings onto physical materials using metric & imperial measurements.
- Operate a variety of metal fabrication hand tools (Grinder, etc.).
- Operate a variety of metal fabrication shop equipment (GMAW Welding, Hand Plasma, Plate Shear, Ironworker, Plate Rolls etc.)
- Form, fit, assemble, and finish steel components into final products.
