## Creative Economics & Market Dynamics CREA 4060
Creative Industries Course
## Course Overview
​How do creative products and services create, capture, and scale value in rapidly evolving markets? This course examines the unique economic principles governing creative industries, from traditional media to emerging digital platforms. Students will explore how creative works are valued, monetized, and distributed in global markets, while analyzing innovative business models that are reshaping creative commerce. Through market analysis and practical explorations, students will learn to identify opportunities, assess market viability, and develop strategies for launching and scaling creative ventures in an increasingly complex digital economy.​​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze market dynamics and economic patterns specific to creative industries using current market data and industry case studies
- Compare diverse creative industry market structures (e.g., platform economies, sharing economies, subscription models) and their implications for business strategy
- Develop pricing and monetization strategies for creative works that reflect both market conditions and value propositions
- Create evidence-based forecasts of market opportunities in specific creative industry segments using economic data and trend analysis
- Assess the impact of technological disruption, including AI and automation, on creative industry business models and market structures
- Create strategies for scaling creative ventures across different markets and platforms while maintaining a sustainable competitive advantage
- Evaluate different business models for creative ventures through analysis of successful and failed business cases​​
