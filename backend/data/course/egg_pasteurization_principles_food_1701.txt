## Egg Pasteurization Principles FOOD 1701
Food Technology Course
## Course Overview
The principles of pasteurization of liquid egg products for companies involved in egg processing operations. Topics include: microbiology and the egg, thermal processing principles, pasteurizer principles, pasteurizer controls, and cleaning and the pasteurizer. Emphasizes the fulfilment of CFIA requirements for producing safe liquid egg products. www.bcit.ca/study/programs/6340acert
