## Team Dynamics and Stress Management CLCR 1060
Call Centre Course
## Course Overview
A one-day course highlighting how to be an effective team member; problem solving and decision-making methods; strategies to prevent and reduce stress in a call centre environment; and creative stress management techniques.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
