## Youth Explore Trades Skills Preparation TTED 0100
Technology Teacher Education Course
## Course Overview
This course will provide the participants with the essential skills and knowledge required to teach the Youth Explore Trades Skill curriculum. Carpentry, electrical and plumbing modules with be taught with an extensive hands-on focus.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of a recognized technology teacher education program.
### Credits
0.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
After completing this course, participants should be able to:
- Select and use personal protective equipment.
- Select and demonstrate safe use of hand and power tools.
- Select materials for wood frame construction.
- Construct floors, support systems and wood frame walls.
- Rough-in residential electrical systems.
- Rough-in residential plumbing systems.
- Install residential plumbing fixtures.
