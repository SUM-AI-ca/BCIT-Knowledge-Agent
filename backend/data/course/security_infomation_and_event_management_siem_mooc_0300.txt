## Security Infomation and Event Management (SIEM) MOOC 0300
Massive Open Online Courses Course
## Course Overview
In this course, you will learn what a security information and event management (SIEM) is and how FortiSIEM is different from other SIEMs. You will also learn about FortiSIEM architecture and some initial configuration. You will also learn how to collect performance information and aggregate it with Syslog data to enrich the overall view of the health of the environment. *Note: Thank you for your interest in a BCIT – Free Online Learning course. ## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Identify SIEM products in the market
- Describe how FortiSIEM differentiates itself from other SIEMs
- Identify FortiSIEM’s seven key strengths
- Connect logs to the SIEM
- Explain FortiSIEM
- Connect different devices to FortiSIEM
