## Basic Refrigeration Systems 3 HVAC 0106
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
This is the third in a five-part program covering heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems. This course covers: types of systems, the psychrometric chart for studying air properties, proper HVAC installation procedures, troubleshooting, and tune-ups. Preventive maintenance is discussed and basic troubleshooting skills are performed on computer simulators/window air conditioning units in shop. Steel Toed Boots required.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in HVAC 0104 and S in HVAC 0145
