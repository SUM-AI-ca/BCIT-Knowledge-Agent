## 134A Retrofit Regulations (Ministry Approved) AUTO 0107
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Upgrades technicians, service advisors and shop administrators in the procedures and regulations of air conditioning 134A retrofit. This theory awareness course provides current information on the impact of the Ozone Depleting Substances and Other Halocarbons Regulation on customer vehicles. Successful participants will receive Air Conditioning Retrofit certification. Training is recognized as an ICBC c.a.r. shop upgrading course and counts as four hours towards your accreditation status.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be:
- Provided with general directions for air conditioning repair technicians to perform R12 to R134a Class II retrofits.
- Able to understand the service procedure for retrofitting a car system.
- Able to understand the integrity of the retrofit and the troubleshooting of 134a system.
