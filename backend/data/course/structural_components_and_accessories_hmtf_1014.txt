## Structural Components and Accessories HMTF 1014
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the basic principles of structural components and accessories and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on structural components and accessories.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify protective structures
- Service cab structures
