## Digital Illustration for Interior Design INTD 1360
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
​This course will provide students with basic knowledge and skills to use SketchUp Layout software for the purposes of producing interior design illustrations for presentations. Students will create a presentation encompassing their acquired skills showcasing a completed project.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 65% in INTD 1100 and 65% in INTD 1140 and 65% in INTD 1180 and 65% in INTD 2150 and 65% in INTD 2160 and 65% in INTD 2300
### Credits
3.0
### Domestic fees

International fees are typically 3.4 times the domestic tuition. Exact cost will be calculated upon completion of registration.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Create 3D conceptual models in SketchUp.
- Organize the model with Groups and Layers.
- Create custom materials and apply materials to surfaces.
- Import and edit a component.
- Create a walk-through animated presentation of the model and export to other file formats.
- Create scaled plan, elevation, and axonometric views in Layout.
Effective as of Fall 2019
