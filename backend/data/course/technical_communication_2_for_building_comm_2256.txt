## Technical Communication 2 for Building COMM 2256
Communication Course
## Course Overview
COMM 2256 continues to develop the skills required for effective communication in other courses of the program, and in the workplace. Students learn different types of short reports based on industry-related case studies. (Formerly: Part 1 of COMM 2255 )
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMM 1140
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Plan and conduct effective meetings.
- Produce effective resumes.
- Write effective letters of application.
- Plan, organise, and write professional informal reports.
- Design effective presentations, and deliver them with confidence.
Effective as of Winter 2012
