## Overview

In today’s creative industry, designers must do more than create visuals—they must align design with business strategies to achieve success. This course covers essential aspects of design within a business context, including research, concept development, and implementation. Students will explore client relations, contract management, and project scope, with a focus on both freelancing and in-house roles. Key topics include contract negotiation, proposal writing, budgeting, and the legal and ethical responsibilities of a designer. Through a major campaign project, students will apply strategic design thinking and manage projects from concept to execution, gaining critical skills for independent and collaborative work. You will also analyze and evaluate work through discussion, critiques, and verbal and written presentations.

## Learning Outcomes

- Identify and utilize appropriate research approaches and resources to effectively establish and support project goals.
- Define a project's goals and target audience, ensuring alignment with the business’s overall objectives.
- Write a professional business research report.
- Write and design a creative proposal for presentation, including effective delivery techniques.
- Work effectively with a client to develop creative concepts that align with business marketing strategy and achieve project goals.
- Analyze, evaluate, and implement design concepts and business practices within a strategic campaign, including developing targeted key messages.
- Identify and explain the rights and responsibilities of a designer when working independently or as part of an organization, including legal and ethical considerations such as intellectual property rights, confidentiality, and project expectations.
- Apply project management strategies, including budgeting, time tracking, and client communication, to ensure successful project execution and relationship management.
- Develop contracts, quotes, and proposals, as well as create professional invoices for both freelance and client-based work, while managing project scope and client billing.
