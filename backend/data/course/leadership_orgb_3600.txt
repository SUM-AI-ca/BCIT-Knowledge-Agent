## Leadership ORGB 3600
Organizational Behaviour Course
## Course Overview
Provides a clear understanding of the role of a leader at a personal, team and organizational level. An understanding of the traditional and recently developed leadership theories provides a foundation from which to examine personal approaches to leadership. The student is encouraged to explore opportunities for personal development and growth as a leader, recognizing the importance of integrity and values based decision-making in challenging and complex situations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ORGB 1100 or ORGB 1105
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the key elements of leadership and the leadership skills required to influence at the individual, team and organizational levels.
- Discuss the role of moral development and ethical behaviours within a leadership context
- Critically analyse organizational practices that foster ethical/unethical work environments
- Analyse the relationships among negotiation and conflict, influencing tactics, power and politics
- Examine the roles of effective communication, coaching and conflict resolution as critical leadership skills
- Evaluate the roles of charismatic, transformational, and strategic leadership styles in various situations
- Apply diagnostic tools to explore individual leadership strengths, blind spots and developmental opportunities
- Model personal values and demonstrate personal growth as a leader
