## Advanced Scripting TECA 6500
Technical Arts Course
## Course Overview
This course focuses on advanced object-oriented programming techniques in Python. It builds on the skills developed with scripting and data structures. Students will create and develop scripts and expressions that control node attributes within 3D applications and that connect nodes to influence one another. In addition, students will create and implement pre- and post-render scripting options to automate time-consuming processes. Students will also develop tools that provide flexibility and ease of modeling, texturing, shading, rigging, animation, and rendering processes throughout different stages of the production pipeline. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Modify an object’s attributes through expressions and scripts.
- Connect node attributes across different objects.
- Change attribute values of other objects from one node.
- Write rigging scripts to enhance the animation capability of 3D characters and props.
- Manipulate lights and camera effects based on the scene and an object’s animation.
- Enhance effects and dynamics with expressions.
- Create tools in 3D applications to manipulate attributes across 3D objects, curves, and shaders.
- Develop pre- and post-rendering scripts to streamline the rendering process.
