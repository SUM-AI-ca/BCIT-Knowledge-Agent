## Interpretation of Drawings for Fabricators MTLF 1210
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
Metal fabricators use blueprints and sketches and drawings during the fabrication process. In this course you will learn to identify basic lines, views and symbols from drawings. You will also learn to draw a detailed sketch and create a bill of materials based on information in a blueprint. As you work through the course you will use drawings to fabricate structural and plate projects.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1205

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Draft simple objects using orthographic projection.
- Draft simple objects using isometric views.
- Identify various views on construction drawings.
- Identify line types, symbols, and miscellaneous notes on metal fabrication drawings.
