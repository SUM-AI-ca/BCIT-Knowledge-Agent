## PowerPoint Workshop - Level 1 COMP 0282
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
COMP 0282 and COMP 0283 have been replaced by COMP 2384 . This intensive, hands-on seminar uses MS PowerPoint 2007 and introduces the new Ribbon interface that applies to MS Office 2007. In just one day you will cover the fundamental principles and practical skills required to produce professional looking presentations. Topics include: creating and editing a presentation,formatting text, working with graphics, creating tables, creating charts, adding transitions and applying animations.This non credit one day workshop prepares students to move on to MS PowerPoint 2007 Level 2.
