## Portfolio Development COMP 2102
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This hands-on course helps students develop and present a portfolio designed to showcase their talents using their own IT projects. Participants will evaluate their own experiences to identify future developmental areas and to help them establish their career directions and goals. Computing faculty work with each student individually to help them package and effectively present their work both in print and online. This course is equivalent to ACIT 2101 in the Full-time CIT Diploma, admission is by department approval only.
### Prerequisite(s)
- CIT Level 1 completion.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify and highlight the student's marketable skills and accomplishments.
- Identify personal developmental areas and set career goals.
- Develop an effective portfolio as a base for exploring employment opportunities, career enhancement and further training.
- Package and present technology projects for print media.
- Package and present technology projects for online media.
