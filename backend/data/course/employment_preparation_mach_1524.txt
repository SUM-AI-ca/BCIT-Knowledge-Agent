## Employment Preparation MACH 1524
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
This course prepares students to find employment in the machinist trade. Students will learn how to write a cover letter and resume to detail their experience and learning accomplishments.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe business and labour organizations.
- Describe expectations and responsibilities of employers and employees.
- Identify legislation affecting employment.
- Write a cover letter and resume in preparation for job search.
Effective as of Winter 2016
