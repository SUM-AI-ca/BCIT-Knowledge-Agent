## Technical Art Foundations: 3D Applications TECA 5100
Technical Arts Course
## Course Overview
Understanding the 3D environment is fundamental for technical artists. This course will cover the basics of 3D applications, animation, materials, and scripting, as well as polygon modeling techniques for simple 3D objects. The course will introduce students to Maya ® , one of the most popular 3D applications used by the visual effects, animation, and game industries. Students will navigate different environments within the Maya ® application, becoming familiar with modeling menus, Hypershade, and render settings. Students will complete a series of 3D projects, creating basic objects, materials, character rigs, and scripts in a 3D environment.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Navigate through different Maya ® panels, windows, and menus.
- Create simple 3D objects.
- Apply key frame animations on 3D props.
- Create character rigs for 3D characters.
- Develop shaders for 3D objects.
- Integrate textures to materials.
- Experiment with lights and camera in 3D scenes.
- Configure render settings for rendering a 3D scene.
- Write scripts for automation in a 3D environment.
