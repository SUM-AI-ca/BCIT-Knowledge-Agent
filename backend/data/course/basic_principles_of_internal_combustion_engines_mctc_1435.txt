## Basic Principles of Internal Combustion Engines MCTC 1435
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces the students to two- and four-cycle internal combustion engine design, commonly encountered in the Motorcycle service industry. Students will also become familiar with lubrication-systems and cooling system operation of the internal combustion engines.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCTC 1400
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe 2-and 4-stroke engine principles.
- Describe basic ignition systems.
- Describe basic fuel delivery systems.
- Describe basic engine management systems.
- Describe basic exhaust systems.
- Describe basic engine emissions and emission controls
- Maintain lubrication systems on 2- and 4-stroke engine
- Maintain lubrication systems on 2- and 4-stroke engine
