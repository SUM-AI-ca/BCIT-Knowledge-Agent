## Air Brakes for Drivers CTMX 0106
Commercial Transport Course
## Course Overview
This ICBC-approved air brake course will prepare you for an air brake endorsement on your BC driver's licence. Course coverage will include: principles of air brakes, components of an air brake system, how the basic system works, trailer systems, dual air systems, anti-lock brake systems, automatic slack adjusters and pre-trip inspection. On successful completion of the practical pre-trip inspection test, you will receive credit for 90 days toward the ICBC Air Brake Endorsement examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the basic components of an air brake system
- Describe the operating principles of a basic air brake system
- Perform a brake adjustment
- Perform a pre-trip inspection
