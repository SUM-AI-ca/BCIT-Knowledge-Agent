## 3D Studio VIZ 1 AICO 4020
Computer Aided Design & Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Introduces computer aided modeling and visualization techniques. Prepares students to generate photo realistic images for architectural and engineering applications. Explores the user interface, some related AutoCAD features and essential VIZ concepts. Hands-on practice and step-by-step procedures for creating and animating 3D scenes.
### Prerequisite(s)
- AICO 3001 or AICO 3042 or equivalent.
