## Plumbing MSYS 3980
Mechanical Systems Course
## Course Overview
Encompasses a study of principles and practices of plumbing systems with a strong emphasis on achieving an acceptable level of proficiency in understanding components, materials and design layout as applied to potable water, storm and sanitary systems in buildings.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Delineate work practices for the installation of plumbing systems and the choice of plumbing fixtures in buildings.
- Describe potable water supply and distribution systems based on average pressure loss method.
- Apply the fundamentals of hydraulics – flow and pressure characteristics applicable to plumbing.
- Describe piping materials used in plumbing, including fittings, such as cleanouts, sanitary wye and tee.
- Compare and select types of hot water heaters for buildings.
- Describe the means to prevent backflow and water hammer.
- Describe storm drainage systems.
- Describe sanitary drainage systems.
- Describe onsite private sewage treatment systems under different soil conditions.
