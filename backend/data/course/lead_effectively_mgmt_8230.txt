## Lead Effectively MGMT 8230
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates leadership skills, such as building shared vision, leading persuasively, and focusing on outcomes and performance.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Building Shared Vision
- Foster the development of a common vision.
- Link the teams mission to that of the organizations broader goals.
- Examine the impact of influence
- Examine Motivation, Satisfaction and Performance
- Examine task interdependence, motivation, satisfaction and their impact on job performance
- Examine the elements of risk, courage and change in effective motivation
- Focus on Outcomes and Performance
- Provide direction and define priorities
- Measure outcomes against team goals and vision.
- Communicate performance results and acknowledge accomplishments.
- Situational Leadership
- Demonstrate various leadership styles appropriate to the situation.
- Demonstrate the ability to analyze situations and determine the appropriate leadership styles within the context of the organizations culture.
