## Transportation Planning Part 2 TSYH 4582
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
Covers the transportation analysis process and the theoretical basis for highway planning, travel demand management, and evaluation of choices.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Traffic Volume Forecasting
- Describe and perform trip assignment.
- Describe and perform capacity analysis.
- Describe the transport behaviour of individuals.
- Describe computer forecasting models.
## Module 2 - Planning and Impact Studies
- Perform impact studies.
- Describe the highway planning process.
- Describe travel demand management.
- Describe and perform the evaluation of choices.
