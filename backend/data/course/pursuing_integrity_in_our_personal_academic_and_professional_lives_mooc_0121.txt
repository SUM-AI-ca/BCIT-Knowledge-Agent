## Pursuing Integrity in our Personal Academic and Professional Lives MOOC 0121
Massive Open Online Courses Course
## Course Overview
This course builds on established research and writing on the topic of Academic Integrity by providing tools you can use in your education, career and everyday life. The value Academic Integrity brings to your life starts with understanding, both of the topic, and your personal values. *Note: Thank you for your interest in a BCIT – Free Online Learning course. You are about to register for the course. If you are new to BCIT, you will first need to create a BCIT ID. Then, log in with your ID and password to register. Once registered, log in to learn.bcit.ca with your BCIT email address and password to access your online course. If you are new to BCIT, it may take up to 24 hours for your course access to become active. Because these are not credited courses, and you are not paying a fee, there may be student resources you come across that you are not entitled to access. This would include Student Aid and other financial assistance. Enjoy your learning and we’re happy to support your lifelong learning journey.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain Academic Integrity
- Understand the importance of Academic Inegrity in their leanring, career and life.
- Develop a personal standard for Academic Integrity.
