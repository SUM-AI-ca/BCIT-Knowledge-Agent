## Diesel Engines for Trailer Refrigeration Units CTMX 0131
Commercial Transport Course
## Course Overview
This course will upgrade you to service, diagnose and repair diesel engines that are used in trailer refrigeration units. You will learn to service and diagnose engine lubrication systems, engine fuel systems, and engine support systems as well as remove and replace major engine and ancillary components.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Have a better understanding of how a diesel engine works;
- Service and diagnose engine lubrication systems;
- Service and diagnose engine fuel systems;
- Service and diagnose engine support systems;
- Remove and replace major engine components; and,
- Remove and replace ancillary components.
