## S-100A Basic Fire Suppression and Safety Annual Re-Certification RENR 0533
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
The S-100A Basic Fire Suppression and Safety Re-Certification course is for those who already possess a valid, British Columbia, S-100 Basic Fire Suppression and Safety Certificate (Red Card). This course fulfills the requirements of the Forest Fire Prevention and Suppression Regulation and WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Part 26.3.1(1) for people working in a forest environment. The course includes wildland fire behaviour, fireline safety and fire control techniques required to control small fires.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate a basic understanding of wildland fire behaviour through knowledge of:
- The basic structure of forests and fuels in the wildland fire environment
- The three elements of the fire triangle
- The three key factors that influence fire behaviour; the 30/30 Cross concept
- The main components of the Canadian Forest Fire Danger Rating System
- The three fire types; candling and spotting
- The Fire Intensity Ranking System
- The parts of a fire.
- Demonstrate a basic understanding of fireline safety, through knowledge of:
- When you should receive a briefing and what should be in it
- LCES safe work practice
- Fireline evacuation proc
- Personal Protective Equipment used on the fireline
- The five basic fireline hazards
- Fire entrapment, fire entrapment avoidance and fire entrapment survival
- Wildland/Urban Interface hazards
- Danger tree marking system, no work zones and danger tree significant hazards
- Firefighter fitness and health.
- Demonstrate a basic understanding of wildland fire suppression through knowledge of:
- The basic fire suppression principles
- The principles of fireline construction
- The three attack methods
- The principles of mop-up, cold trailing and inspection and patrol.
