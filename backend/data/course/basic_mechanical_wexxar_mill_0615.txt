## Basic Mechanical - WEXXAR MILL 0615
Millwright Course
## Course Overview
Basic Mechanical – WEXXAR course is designed for entry level Wexxar employees. The training utilizes tools, materials and concepts directly related to the employees' on the job tasks. Students will complete classroom sessions and then utilize the knowledge gained in the shop environment to complete pre-set practical projects.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon Successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Select and use hand, power and measuring tools by application
- Describe metal properties and select metals
- Identify and apply fasteners by application
- Describe bearing loads
- Identify and select bearings by application
- Describe lubricants
- Identify and select lubricants by application
- Use plans and sketches
- Identify and install belts drives
- Describe pneumatic components
- Design and assemble basic pneumatic circuits
