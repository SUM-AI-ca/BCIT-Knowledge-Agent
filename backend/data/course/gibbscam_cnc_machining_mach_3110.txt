## GibbsCAM CNC Machining MACH 3110
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
Focuses on the creation and modification of 2D geometry and 2D CNC machining tool path in GibbsCAM 2014. The basic 2D tool paths include milling processes for CNC milling machine operations and turning processes for CNC lathe operations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 1 year CNC machining experience or successful completion of Machinist Foundation program
