## CMA Exam Preparation FMGT 5230
Financial Management Course
## Course Overview
This 40-hour course concentrates on the case-writing skills that will prepare a student to succeed on the case study that makes up Day 2 of the CMA National Entrance Exam. These skills of Strategic Thinking, Analysis, Integration, Judgement and Written Communication will be emphasized by means of case simulations and mock examinations. There will also be one session devoted to a simulation of the Day 1 exam. The class will take place on Monday evenings as well as on some weekends.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
