## Applied Physics 1 for OCHS PHYS 1288
Physics Course
## Course Overview
Studies basic physical principles and applies them to relevant situations in the OCHS technology. Topics include kinematics, vectors, dynamics, statics, friction, energy, and simple machines. The labs emphasise measurement, data analysis, and experimental techniques as they relate to the lecture material.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
5.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Understand some of the basic physical principles underlying your technology.
- Use analytical skills in the solution of technical problems.
- Set up scientific apparatus and make measurements.
- Analyze experimental data and write scientific reports.
