## Millwright CQ Refresher MILL 0101
Millwright Course
## Course Overview
Prepares tradespersons to write the examination for the Red Seal (Interprovincial) endorsement / Certificate of Qualification as a Millwright. The course reviews the theoretical knowledge of the trade. Topics include: general fitting practices, hydraulics, pneumatics and lubrication, material handling, machine components and machine installation. Contact SkilledTradesBC at www.skilledtradesbc.ca or 1-866-660-6011 to determine eligibility requirements for writing the certification examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Documented MILLWRIGHT related work experience of 9,900 hours

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the process for challenging a trade certification examination.
- Identify the tasks that will be tested on the Red Seal examination,
- Self-assess preparedness to write the Certification of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination.
