## Manufacturing Automation Systems MTEC 8050
Manufacturing Course
## Course Overview
This course explores the use of automation equipment in the production environment. Students will analyse applications and select and program suitable automation equipment, design workholding and part presentation devices and investigate sensing systems used in automated workcells.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MTEC 7045 and 50% in MECH 4450
