## Overview

This course introduces students to the language and documentation standards used in medical practice, supported by transcription-based learning tools. Medical terms that students previously studied will connect to those encountered in written physician orders. Through this course, students will develop entry-level transcription skills to accurately and completely process medical documentation, following established hospital protocols and workflows. This course also supports medical office personnel looking to enhance their medical transcription and order processing skills.

## Learning Outcomes

- Transcribe a patient's physician's orders.
- Navigate a hospital system to transcribe orders for the correct department.
- Identify which departments perform which tests.
- Transcribe a patient's test appointments or preparations in advance of treatment.
- Record patient information provided by another department and transcribe instructions.
- Access a patient's current chart to find information.
- Access a patient's history.
- Spell generic and brand-name drugs.
- Spell medications, medical terminology, and abbreviations by memory and by consulting reference books.
- Transcribe a variety of medical orders from different departments, including diagnostic radiology, X-Ray, MRI, and medical consultations.
- Photocopy, fax, and distribute reports.
