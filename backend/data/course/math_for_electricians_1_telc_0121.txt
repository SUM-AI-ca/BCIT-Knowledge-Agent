## Math for Electricians 1 TELC 0121
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for students planning to attend level one technical training for the electrical apprenticeship program. Course topics include basic electrical concepts, fundamental electrical laws and mathematical expressions, algebra refresher, DC electricity and resistive circuit analysis.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MATH 10
