## Critical Thinking and Ethics in Media MDIA 4700
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Critical Thinking and Ethics in Media focuses on the role of the media in society from practical and theoretical perspectives. This course begins with the notion that what we read, watch and hear in the media plays an important role in how we construct meaning. Learners will discuss several media issues focussing upon the production of news and advertising and will be encouraged to actively read, listen and view the media in order to discuss ongoing issues. This course will also discuss ethical frameworks to apply in the production of print, online, video and broadcast media content. These areas will include diversity, gender an tolerance.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Discuss relative strengths and weaknesses of the news media in communication.
- Identify prominent contributors to communication theory and analyze their work.
- Critically examine ethical content in the electronic, social media and print media.
- Identify current issues in Canadian media.
- Evaluate issues in Canadian private and public broadcasting.
- Assess the role of media regulation in Canada.
- Discuss the use of images in media.
- Analyze the application of media by the public relations industry.
- Discuss various frameworks used for ethical decision making.
