## Lead From Within: Sharpen Your Edge XLED 1012
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​​Leadership begins with understanding yourself. This course focuses on developing self-awareness as the foundation for effective leadership. Participants will explore how perceptions, assumptions, and biases shape their leadership style and identify personal strengths and challenges. Through reflection and planning, participants will create actionable strategies to foster growth and realize their leadership potential in today's evolving workplace.​​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain key leadership attributes required for success in today's dynamic workplace.
- Analyze personal strengths and leadership style to gain insight into areas for growth.
- Reflect on perceptions, assumptions, and biases to evaluate their impact on leadership effectiveness.
- Design an action plan to meet the identify development goals and enhance personal leadership potential.​
