## Introduction to Political Science TDMT 2310
Transportation and Distribution Course
## Course Overview
Through discussion and individual research, students will learn to analyse some of the social, cultural and political considerations when dealing with export markets. Students prepare a research paper on political event(s) that shape the strategies and opportunities of international trade.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Develop international political arguments in terms of Canadian business.
- Identify and explain moral and ethical dilemmas.
- Justify personal and ethical standards.
- Compare different ethical and moral value systems.
- Relate global political events to business decisions.
- Justify business decisions based on moral and ethical arguments.
- Develop effective search criteria using secondary research materials.
