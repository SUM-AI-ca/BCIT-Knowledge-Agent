## Call to Adventure VENT 0085
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
Uncover your next adventure! Are you graduating from post secondary education, considering a career transition, starting a business or just plotting your next life chapter? Then join us for this two day action packed workshop. Discover your passions, your strengths, your values and your purpose. Then learn how to put this knowledge to work in a personalized plan to gain the courage to take your next big step! Workshop disclosure: "Careful what you wish for!"
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Understand their strengths and passions.
- Create a purpose statement.
- Create a personalized plan to turn their learning into action.
