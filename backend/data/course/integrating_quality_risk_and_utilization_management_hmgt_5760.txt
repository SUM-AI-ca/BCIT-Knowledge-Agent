## Integrating Quality, Risk and Utilization Management HMGT 5760
Health Management Course
## Course Overview
Presents the key theory and current practice of quality, risk and utilization management with specific application to health services. Focuses on demonstrating the integrated nature of quality assessment, quality planning, risk and utilization management and how the application of these quality systems and tools are essential for today's health organization. Covers quality management assessment, client satisfaction and provider competence, risk management tools, utilization management (accessibility, effectiveness, efficiency and appropriateness) and tools and quality management monitoring and reporting.

## Learning Outcomes
This course introduces the theory and application of quality programs, risk management, utilization management, quality monitoring and reporting tools.
These will be summarized from each modules learning objectives.
- Module 1: Quality Management Introduction
- Quality Management defined as a program consisting of systems and tools of quality assessment, quality planning/CQI, risk management and utilization management
- Definitions of quality assessment, quality planning, risk management, utilization management
- Demonstration of the inter-connectedness and the A-P-D-C-A-M cycle
- Organization wide focus versus clinical focus
- Module 2: Quality Management Programs: Assessment
- The use of established quality frameworks and criteria - CCHSA, Baldrige, NQI, QCBC
- Using these standards of performance to assess organizations/programs/departments
- Identifying opportunities for improvement
- Developing quality plans
- Dimensions of quality and definitions related to CQI/RM/UM
- Module 3: Client Satisfaction and Provider Competence (Acceptability)
- Methods of collecting data on patient satisfaction
- Using patient satisfaction tools to identify opportunities for improvement
- Process for ensuring provider competence and continuing education
- Credentialing, human resource issues related to quality, CME
- Role of professional staff
- Module 4: Risk Management (Safety)
- The processes of risk management
- Identification of risk
- Risk assessment
- Taking actions to manage risks
- Evaluation of risk management activities
- Elements of a risk management program
- Module 5: Risk Management Tools
- Incident reporting
- Complaints management
- Collection of data to analyze and identify opportunities for improvement
- Module 6: Utilization Management (Accessibility, Effectiveness, Efficiency, and Appropriateness)
- Focus on clinical quality
- UM defined as two components: utilization review and utilization analysis
- What UM is, does, accomplishes
- Prospective, retrospective, concurrent reviews
- Module 7: Utilization Management Tools
- Approaches and methodology in use e.g. MCAP, InterQual, AEP
- Using the tools to identify opportunities for improvement
- Module 8: Utilization Management - Clinical Management Tools
- Clinical practice guidelines
- Clinical pathways
- Protocols
- Case management - continuity with other care provides and organizations, discharge planning, etc.
- Module 9: Quality Management Monitoring and Reporting
- The use of performance indicators
- The use of benchmarks and targets
- Routine measurement and monitoring to identify opportunities for improvement
- Ensuring statistical significance prior to action
- Reports for Board, Executive, Management, medical staff, etc.
