## Essentials of Internet Search for Investigators FSCT 0038
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
This one-day seminar condenses the best content of BCIT's popular "Internet for Investigations" course into an intensive, hands-on roller coaster ride through the deep web. Learn the search strategies and tactics of the pros. Find out how to protect yourself online and how to assess reliability. Intensive and interactive, this seminar is only recommended for those who already have some Internet experience and want to take their search skills to the next level.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Outline the internet search strategies and tactics of the pros.
- Apply search skills for effective and deeper information search.
- Exercise search online protection.
- Assess the reliability of search information.
- Use search information appropriately.
