## Landing Gear, Wheels and Brakes AVAM 2101
Aircraft Maintenance Course
## Course Overview
A course designed to provide you with the theoretical understanding and practical skills needed to test, troubleshoot, repair, and adjust landing gear components including: wheels, tires, brakes, anti-skid, and gear extension/retraction.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the learner will be able to:
- Demonstrate aircraft chocking.
- Describe wheel types.
- Describe wheel maintenance.
- Describe repair of tires and tubes.
- Perform tire servicing and inflation.
- Perform install and balance of wheel.
- Explain aircraft braking systems.
- Perform brake shoe replacement and testing.
- Explain anti-skid brakes.
- Describe maintenance.
- Describe purpose and types of landing gear.
- Perform disassembly, servicing and reassembly of an oleo.
- Describe the operation retractable landing gear.
- Describe aircraft floats.
- Describe repair of floats and skis.
