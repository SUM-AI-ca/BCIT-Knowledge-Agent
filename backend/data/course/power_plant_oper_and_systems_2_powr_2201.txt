## Power Plant Oper and Systems 2 POWR 2201
Power Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This course discusses the various types of industrial power plants and the major processes used in these plants. Topics include pulp and paper mills, sawmills, oil refining, natural gas processing, food preparation/refrigeration, fluidized bed units, steam turbine power generation, and gas turbine power generation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in POWR 1101
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Interpret diagrams and drawings used for instrumentation work.
- Compare different types of water systems.
- Compare the processes used in pulp and paper mills and sawmills.
- Explain the role of the power engineer in workplaces such as pulp and paper mills, sawmills, oil refineries, natural gas plants, and food processing plants.
- Describe the processes used in oil refineries and natural gas plants.
- Compare the uses of steam and refrigeration for food processing.
- Describe the operation of hot oil systems, including how oil flows through a typical system and any special safety considerations.
- Explain the purpose and operation of fluidized bed units.
- Demonstrate the start-up operations of steam turbine and gas turbine power generators.
