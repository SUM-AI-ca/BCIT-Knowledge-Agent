## Capstone Project FWDP 4500
Front-End Web Developer Course
## Course Overview
​This course is the culmination of the knowledge and learning in the Front-End Web Development program and the practical application and development of skills gained in earlier courses. This course will introduce students to the professional practice of working on a small web development team to build a mid-sized custom dynamic website. Throughout the supervised project, students will use industry best practices to develop a website based on specific business and functional requirements. To manage their tasks and meet deadlines, students will work collaboratively using communication and management tools common in the industry. All teams will have scheduled meetings with the instructor to track their progress and will present tutorials to the class on topics they research during their projects. Evaluation will be based on the project. Evaluation will be based on the project.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to;
- Act as a functional member of a small development team.
- Use collaboration, project management, and development tools to increase efficiency to meet deadlines on a fast-paced project.
- Conduct a client discovery meeting to gather the requirements.
- Analyze and understand client requirements.
- Use the learned user experience processes to make design decisions.
- Plan the custom development process based on the business, functional and design requirements.
- Develop a custom website using a CMS that is built for the client's ease of use.
- Use testing to optimize the performance of a website.
- Tutor project stakeholders on the use of their custom website.
- Launch the client's website.
