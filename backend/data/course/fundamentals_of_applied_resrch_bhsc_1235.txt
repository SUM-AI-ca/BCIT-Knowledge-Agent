## Fundamentals of Applied Resrch BHSC 1235
Basic Health Sciences Course
## Course Overview
Research methodology as a scientific process of inquiry will be discussed with reference to problem selection and statement, review of the literature, research design, data collection and data analysis, and communication of research results. Also, applied research issues including the nature of bio-physical measurements, ethical considerations, grant applications, the nature and sources of funding, etc. will be explained.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
