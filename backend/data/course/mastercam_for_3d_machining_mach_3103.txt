## Mastercam for 3D Machining MACH 3103
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
Focuses on the creation of 3D wireframe, 3D surface, 3D modeling and 3D tool paths using Mastercam. Topics include complex geometry used in creating the surface, models, and toolpaths. Students will test the modeling and the CNC programs on CNC machines.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in MACH 3102
