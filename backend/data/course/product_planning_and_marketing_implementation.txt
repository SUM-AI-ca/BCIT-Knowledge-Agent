## Overview

This course will enable candidates to develop a product marketing plan which will enable them to manage the delivery of a commercially viable product. The course provides a complete and proven methodology of bringing a product from conception to full commercial development.

## Credits

1.0

## Cost

$375.44

## Prerequisites

- 50% in TMGT 7111

## Learning Outcomes

- Lead a focused idea generation or brainstorming session that considers the underlying needs of the customer.
- Prepare a product commercialization plan for both marketing and technical departments of a company.
- Determine the roles and tasks of both marketing and technical personnel throughout commercialization.
- Critically evaluate the role of the “product champion” in product innovation.
- Understand different product delivery strategies and the product adoption process through case studies.
