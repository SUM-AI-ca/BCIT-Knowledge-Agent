## Mastercam For Machinists 1 MACH 3100
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
Focuses on the creation of 2D geometry for preparing of machining operations featured in Mastercam version 9.0. Explores the techniques and methods that can be used to design 2D drawing.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
