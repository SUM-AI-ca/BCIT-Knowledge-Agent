## Respirator Protection and Fit Tester Training for Safety Officers CNST 0157
Construction Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for workers who risk being exposed to air contaminants above the permissible exposure limits (PEL's) or suggested exposure levels.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the legislative requirements for respirators.
- Describe roles and responsibilities.
- Conduct hazard identification.
- Perform respirator selection, use, and APF.
- Explain the types of respirators and filter ratings.
- Perform pre-medical screening and medical evaluation.
- Maintain a respiratory fit test record.
- Describe cartridge classifications.
- Perform fit tests.
