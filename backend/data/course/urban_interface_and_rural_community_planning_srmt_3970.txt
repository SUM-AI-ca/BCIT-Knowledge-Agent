## Urban Interface and Rural Community Planning SRMT 3970
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
The purpose of this course is to introduce students to resource management issues that occur in the interface areas between urban and rural environments. The responsibilities and interactions between local and regional governments, and resource management agencies for planning, management, and emergency response will be an important focus for the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- SRMT 1900 and SRMT 2910 and SRMT 3900
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Assess the levels of interaction between local fire departments and MOF in developing fire plans and emergency response in interface areas.
- Describe how geohazard assessments, fire related issues and hydrological principles can be integrated in development and subdivision plans.
- Assess how agricultural and industrial activities can impact groundwater resources.
- Describe strategies and preferred conditions for local solid waste disposal.
- Describe how developments such as Independent Power Projects can influence the management of other resources (roads, water rights, fish, forestry access etc.).
- Explain how visual landscape management principles can be included in development and subdivision design.
- Explain how the intense recreational use of interface areas can influence subdivision and resource management planning.
- Review management strategies for public safety issues in interface areas.
- Review management related to wildlife management in interface areas.
