## Performance Measurement OPMT 2290
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
This is an introductory, applications course to work measurement. Using the principles of work study, methods study, motion study and time analysis techniques, the student is well equipped to solve work study problems. Time measurement techniques such as stop watch, M.T.M (Methods Time Measurement), M.O.S.T. (Maynard Operating Sequence Technique) will be discussed. This course will not license students as work study practitioners but will give them a basic understanding of the principles of work study, work methods and work measurement techniques.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in OPMT 1198
