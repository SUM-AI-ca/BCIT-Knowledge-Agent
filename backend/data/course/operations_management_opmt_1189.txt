## Operations Management OPMT 1189
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
Presents broad interpretation of operations management and details some of the problems faced by different types of enterprises (private and public) in the management of their production systems. The student studies the nature, purpose and processes associated with operations management; the relevance of systems design, resource allocation, operations planning and control to the individual firm; how to identify and solve operational problems using quantitative methods.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
