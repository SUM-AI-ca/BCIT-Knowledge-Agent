## Projects in Industry FMGT 4910
Financial Management Course
## Course Overview
Students will work in small teams on a project provided by an organization, developing problem solving, reporting and presentation skills. Five classroom hours are scheduled.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Form a working team to complete an industry project chosen from options provided.
- Meet with the organization (client) and prepared a project plan.
- Follow and successfully completed a project plan.
- Analyze and evaluated the area designated by the client and developed reports or processes.
- Deliver face-to-face presentation to client and faculty advisor about the project
- Create and provide a written report to both the client and the advising faculty member.
