## Manage Financial Resources - Modified 2 MGMT 8422
Management Course
## Course Overview
Modified version of 8420.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in FMGT 1100 or 50% in FMGT 1152
### Credits
5.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Determining Direct and Full Costs
- Predict both the direct and full costs of an activity by breaking down its cost elements and their behaviors.
- Define and classify costs as to fixed, variable or mixed.
- Define and give examples of differential, opportunity and sunk costs.
- Cost Modelling
- Develop a model/method to predict both the direct and full costs of an activity by breaking down its cost elements and their behaviors.
- Calculate the costs of a job using a job order costing system.
- Value Costing
- Develop a model for predicting both the direct and full costs of an activity by breaking down its cost elements and the behaviors of the cost elements.
- Evaluate your organization's value costing by calculating the costs of a job using a 'process' costing system.
- Income Statement Models
- Identify information which an organization's financial recording and reporting systems must generate in order to enable managers to make rational decisions.
- Analyze how your company separates mixed costs into fixed and variable components through the high-low method, and through regression analysis (manual or computer model).
- Determine how to prepare an income statement in the contribution margin format.
