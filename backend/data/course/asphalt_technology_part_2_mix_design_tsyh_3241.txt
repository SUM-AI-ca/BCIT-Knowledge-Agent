## Asphalt Technology Part 2: Mix Design TSYH 3241
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
Commences with the proportioning of asphalt mixes including the analysis and calculations required to determine the physical properties of the mix. Step-by- step procedures are given for mix design including the methodology, test specimens, procedures and interpretation of test data for two mix design methods. Fundamentals of manufacturing hot-asphalt mixes (storage, mixing methods, mixing plants, production, inspection) are covered, including hot-mix recycling and special mix designs.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Properties of Asphalt
- Specify the desired mix properties.
- Outline the asphalt mix design procedure.
- Perform specific gravity and density-voids calculations.
## Module 2 - Marshall Method of Mix Design
- Outline this method of mix design.
- Define the procedure for the preparation of test specimens.
- Identify the steps in the test procedure.
- Prepare and interpret the test data.
## Module 3 - Superpave Mix Design
- Describe the selection of binder and aggregate materials.
- Describe the Superpave gyratory compactor and test procedure.
- Analyze the results of a mix design.
## Module 4 - Manufacturing Process
- Identify methods of asphalt production.
- State methods of aggregate storage and determine cold aggregate feed supply.
- Illustrate methods of hot aggregate preparation and storage.
- Demonstrate methods of asphalt storage and circulation.
## Module 5 - Asphalt Plants
- Identify the types of asphalt plants.
- Describe the materials handling and types of cold aggregate feed.
- Outline the aggregate drying, screening and grading process.
## Module 6 - Batch and Continuous Mix Asphalt Plants
- Discuss components common to Batch and Continuous Mix plants.
- Describe the operation of a Batch Plant.
- Describe the operation of a Continuous Mix Plant.
- Outline the procedures for Batch Plant calibration.
- Outline the procedures for Continuous Mix Plant calibration.
## Module 7 - Drum Mix Asphalt Plants
- Explain the operation of the drum mixer components.
- Outline the procedures for drum mixer calibration.
- Demonstrate the sampling, testing, recording and reporting techniques for asphalt plants.
## Module 8 - Hot Mix Recycling
- Describe the procedures in preparing a recycled hot mix design.
- Outline the production of recycled hot mix asphalt.
