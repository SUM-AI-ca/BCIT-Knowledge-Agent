## Heating, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration MMTF 1100
Marine Mechanical Technician Course
## Course Overview
​In this course you will learn about cabin heating systems and the installation considerations. This course also introduces you to air conditioning theory, its components, and the refrigeration systems. In this course you will demonstrate the servicing of an Air Conditioning (AC), heating and refrigeration system.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in MMTF 1000
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe types, selection considerations and components of natural draft, forced air and hot water cabin heating systems
- Demonstrate proper installation considerations and procedures for installing natural draft, forced air and hot water cabin heating systems
- Describe the principles of AC and refrigeration theory, components of AC and refrigeration systems and refrigeration equipment commonly found on recreational vessels.​
