## Intro Tech Comm for Mech Tech COMM 1105
Communication Course
## Course Overview
teaches the basic skills needed to become effective writers and speakers in the mechanical industry. Students learn the layout, content and graphic techniques of technical writing as well as research methods and employment application techniques. Students write technical memos, letters and descriptions, and give an oral report. A section on English language fundamentals is included.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
