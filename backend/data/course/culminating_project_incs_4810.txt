## Culminating Project INCS 4810
Industrial Network Cybersecuri Course
## Course Overview
This project cements the knowledge learnt in the program by taking the steps necessary to analyze an industrial network, identify vulnerabilities, perform a risk analysis, recommend actions that can be taken to reduce the risk, and create an incident response plan in accordance with the ISA-62443 standard. Students work in teams and seek advice from a faculty member acting as their project supervisor. At the end of the project students submit an individually prepared formal report and participant in a group presentation. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- ACIT 4421 and ELEX 4610 and INCS 4310 and INCS 4320
- COMM 2230 ‡ ( ‡ must be taken concurrently)

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze an industrial network in accordance with ISA-62443. (4)
- Identify vulnerabilities in accordance with ISA-62443. (4)
- Perform a risk analysis in accordance with ISA-62443. (4)
- Recommend mitigating actions that can be taken to reduce the risk in accordance with ISA-62443. (4)
- Create an incident response plan in accordance with the ISA-62443. (4)
- Prepare a formal report for the plant manager responsible for the Operational Technology and an office manager responsible for the Information Technology. (4)
- Provide an oral presentation prepared for the plant manager responsible for the Operational Technology and an office manager responsible for the Information Technology. (4)
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2021
