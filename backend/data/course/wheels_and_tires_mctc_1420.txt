## Wheels and Tires MCTC 1420
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to tire construction; procedures for changing and repairing tubes and tubeless tires, wheel balancing and procedures. The students will also perform basic service procedures on hubs and bearing assemblies as encountered in the Motorcycle repair industry.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCTC 1400
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe Tire Construction and service techniques.
- Perform tire services.
- Describe wheel assemblies.
- Describe bearing design and construction.
- Service Hubs and Bearings​.
