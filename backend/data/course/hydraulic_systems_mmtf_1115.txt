## Hydraulic Systems MMTF 1115
Marine Mechanical Technician Course
## Course Overview
​In this course you will learn the theory of hydraulic principles. You will also learn hydraulic system components as well as hydraulic safety. In addition, this course introduces you to hydraulic steering systems and components. In this course you will describe and demonstrate hydraulic system operation, install system components and diagnose hydraulic issues.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in MMTF 1000
### Credits
5.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe hydraulic theory, hydraulic system components, hydraulic oil and power distribution systems
- Describe the function and components of hydraulic steering systems, and steering system layouts for single and multiple station installations
- Install and service hydraulic steering systems, selecting and making up lines, bleeding and diagnosing
- Perform repair procedures for correcting typical hydraulic system problems
- Perform proper procedures for installing and servicing hydraulic motors and cylinders for typical marine applications​
