## Mobile Photography/Videography BCST 0105
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Want to use your phone to take stunning photos? Want to learn how to shoot, edit and export professional looking videos in a few hours only using your smartphones? Nowadays, phones are capable of shooting high definition photos and videos, editing and processing high quality footage and exporting to multiple online platforms instantly. This course shows you how to use your mobile device to its maximum capacity. Additionally, you will have a chance to use various mobile photo/video production accessories such as external lenses, stabilizers etc. to improve your production value significantly. We also simulate a real professional photo/ video industry scenario by allowing students to work with professional models/actors for their hands-on in class projects.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Compose, shoot, re-touch photographs using various mobile applications
- Use the best mobile applications for shooting professional looking videos
- Utilize stabilizers/external lenses to smoothen/improve video shots
- Use natural environment lighting effectively to enhance photo/video quality
- Apply best practices for recording good quality audio
- Direct your models/actors to get the best result in photo/video sessions
- Apply the best 'on the fly' editing practices using mobile apps
- Explore different ways of reaching out to mass audiences around the globe
