## Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning HMTF 1011
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the principles of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems and components. Prior to the practical component of this course, students must obtain an Ozone Depleting Substances Handling Certificate from the Heating, Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Institute.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe heating and air conditioning fundamentals
- Repair air conditioning system components
