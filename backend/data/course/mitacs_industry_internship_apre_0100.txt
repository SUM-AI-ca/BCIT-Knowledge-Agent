## Mitacs Industry Internship APRE 0100
Applied Research Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed to allow students to participate in Mitacs Industry Internships at BCIT as a registered student. All internships require an industry partner as well as a faculty supervisor. Students wishing to enroll in this course must obtain prior approval from the BCIT Applied Research Liaison Office (ARLO) email: research@bcit.ca . For more information on Mitacs Internships please see www.mitacs.ca/en/programs#student . Students not wishing to participate in a Mitacs Industry Internship as a registered BCIT student can contact Mitacs directly for other opportunities.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Students are required to secure an industry partner, faculty supervisor and Mitcas funding before enrolling in this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze engineering, scientific and business publications.
- Properly define project scope.
- Select an appropriate research method.
- Prepare a research proposal including the research topic background information, research methods, and project milestones.
- Apply critical & creative thinking to a technical problem.
- Relate knowledge of the related technical discipline and private sector problem.
- Present results in a logical and professional manner.
