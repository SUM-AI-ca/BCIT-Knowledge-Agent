## Application Area Directed Studies COMP 9190
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
​Students will study a research topic in an application area, under the supervision of a BCIT faculty member. This faculty member may be from the Computing Department, or they may be from an external department. The student will undertake a literature review of relevant research related to a particular topic, under the instructor's supervision. The student will then complete individual assignments demonstrating mastery of this topic. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Program Head Approval
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Critically analyse current scholarly literature in a specific domain of application.
- Explain how applied computing can solve problems in the application area.
- Apply the skills of critical thinking, decision making, and problem solving.
- Demonstrate an ability to work independently and meet deadlines for individual work. ​
