## Drafting and Design 1: Introduction to Architectural Drafting and Design BLDG 1700
Building Course
## Course Overview
Basic architectural drafting techniques and skills. Graphical communication required for the preparation of building development permit drawings. A study of the various approving authorities and their influence over architectural design services.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
