## Advanced Topics in IT Infrastructure ACIT 3495
Computer Information Technology Course
## Course Overview
The CIT Diploma program provides training in four key areas of IT: IT Infrastructure, Web Services, Enterprise Software, and Technology Support. This elective course allows students to study advanced concepts related to IT Infrastructure. Topics are drawn from a variety of sources, with a focus on current industry standard tools and techniques related to the architecture, design, deployment, and management of IT Infrastructure in organizations.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- ACIT 2420 and ACIT 2620
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Discuss various approaches and techniques used to build customized IT infrastructure solutions that meet the operational needs of a business or organization.
- Evaluate, compare, and recommend IT Infrastructure solutions to solve a specified business problem or meet a specified organizational goal.
- Implement and deploy IT Infrastructure solutions that utilize industry standard and/or emerging technologies.
Effective as of Fall 2018
