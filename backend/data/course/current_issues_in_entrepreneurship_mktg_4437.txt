## Current Issues in Entrepreneurship MKTG 4437
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
Provides an overview of current issues facing entrepreneurs. Annual subject matter shifts as do the issues covered by the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MKTG 3345
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop effective public speaking skills by delivering oral presentations (individual and team) to audiences of diverse sizes.
- Identify and explain contemporary issues in entrepreneurship.
- Recognize and examine opportunities in contemporary entrepreneurship trends.
- Create and implement strategies to take advantage of these opportunities.
- Explain and differentiate current financing issues in entrepreneurship.
- Understand current financing issues in entrepreneurship.
- Describe how to tune up an existing growing business.
- Explain business cycles and propose how to adapt.
Effective as of Winter 2025
