## Municipal Recruitment & Onboarding XLED 2021
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​This course explores effective recruitment, selection, and onboarding practices for municipal leaders working within municipal and unionized environments. Participants will learn how to plan equitable and transparent hiring processes, develop competency-based selection tools, and design onboarding strategies that foster engagement and retention.​
