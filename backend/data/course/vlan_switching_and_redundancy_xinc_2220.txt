## VLAN Switching and Redundancy XINC 2220
Cybersecurity Professionals Microcredentials Course
## Course Overview
XINC 2220 introduces students to redundant switch configuration. The course will focus on the configuration of Virtual Local AreaNetworks (VLANs) and trunking between switches. Redundancy protocols such as Spanning Tree Protocol (STP) and RapidSpanning Tree Protocol (RSTP) will be discussed. Students will then configure EtherChannel to allow high-speed networkcommunication between switches.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Configure initial settings on a switch and router,
- Explain switching concepts such as frame forwarding and switching domains,
- Implement VLANs and trunking in a switched network,
- Configure and troubleshoot inter-VLAN routing on Layer 3 devices,
- Explain how Spanning Tree Protocol enables redundancy in a Layer 2 network,
- Configure and troubleshoot EtherChannel on a switched network.
