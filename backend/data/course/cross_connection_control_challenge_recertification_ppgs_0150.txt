## Cross Connection Control Challenge Recertification PPGS 0150
Piping, Plumbing, Gas Course
## Course Overview
This challenge exam provides certification through the BC Water & Waste Association (BCWWA). This is not for students with expired tickets. To check eligibility contact the BCWWA at 604-433-4389. Registration is required two weeks prior to the exam date; register first with BCIT and then go online at the BCWWA website at www.bcwwa.org to register and pay for the exam. Passing grade is 75%.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
