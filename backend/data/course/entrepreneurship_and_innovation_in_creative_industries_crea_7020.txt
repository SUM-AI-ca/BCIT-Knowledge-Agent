## Entrepreneurship and Innovation in Creative Industries CREA 7020
Creative Industries Course
## Course Overview
​The digitization of our world has fundamentally changed the ways that businesses are creating value and connecting with consumers—particularly within the creative industries. This course will introduce students to the principles and practices of entrepreneurship within creative industries including the preparation of a business plan. Students in this course will study concepts and techniques, including individual creativity, innovation in the workplace, design thinking, entrepreneurship, leadership, problem identification, implementation of ideas and other practical methods that they can apply to their studies, work, and personal lives. Taking on the role of an entrepreneur with a new creative business concept, students will learn how to build the idea into a viable business venture. As part of the course, students are introduced to creative ways to solve problems in addition to traditional decision-making methods and formal critical-thinking models.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the role of the creative industry in the economy
- Describe the nature and diverse range of entrepreneurial activity in the creative industries
- Evaluate the role of the entrepreneur in creative industries
- Research and evaluate various business models in relation to the creative industries
- Propose an opportunity for innovation in the creative workplace
- Develop a data-driven opportunity analysis of a new creative venture
- Critically examine the paradigm diffusion of creative industries
- Develop a pricing model aligns with a cost analysis, business goals and market dynamics.
- Prepare a business plan for reaching investors and consumers for creative industry businesses
- Integrate design thinking into creative industries' practices
- Analyze the stages of transforming a creative idea into a commercial product or service
- Propose a creative venture to a variety of stakeholders ​
