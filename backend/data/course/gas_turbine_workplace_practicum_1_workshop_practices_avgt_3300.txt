## Aviation Inspection and Maintenance Practices AVGT 3300
Gas Turbine Course
## Course Overview
​Students will secure an industry practicum at a turbine engine overhaul, or component shop facility. The student will learn and demonstrate hangar shop safety practices, workflow, and quality processes and procedures to assist and perform gas turbine engine inspection and maintenance.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in AVGT 2004
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply employment search skills to secure a practicum with an industry partner
- Apply aerospace industry safety practices
- Describe aerospace industry workflow and quality assurance practices
- Perform aerospace workshop cleaning practices
- Maintain a workstation to industry standards
- Assist a technician to perform inspection of equipment
- Perform basic maintenance procedures under supervision​
