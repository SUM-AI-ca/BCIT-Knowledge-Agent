## Technical and English Training (Short Term) INTL 0010
International Education Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for international trainees to receive specialized a short-term technical training experience in an English environment that introduces them to Canadian culture. Each program is customized to provide unique content that focuses on the needs and preferences of each group. This course places emphasis on foundational technical English and skills required within the selected technical training. It has a strong focus on collaborative learning, an essential principle in regular BCIT programs, and it employs a task-based approach for students to acquire and use skills in a variety of group projects. ​Furthermore, course content emphasizes cultural awareness, allowing students to obtain a basic understanding of cultural norms commonly occurring in Canada. Students will: study in classrooms, labs and work-shops; gain hands-on experience through practical projects; transfer acquired skills and strategies into real work contexts and participate in field trips and company visits.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Improve foundational technical English and skills required by the subject matter
- Collaborate in small groups to solve problems and complete projects
- Develop practical knowledge and technical skills in the selected area of training.
- Apply technical knowledge and skills in laboratory, project-based, and workplace-related activities
- Become familiar with Canadian culture
