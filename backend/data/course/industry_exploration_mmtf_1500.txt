## Industry Exploration MMTF 1500
Marine Mechanical Technician Course
## Course Overview
​In this course you will learn about the recreational marine industry which could include service and repair, parts supplies, sales and customer service. This course introduces you to the recreational marine service industry to gain knowledge about the working environment. In this course you will explore the marine industry by either job shadow, doing a research project or an assigned workshop project.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in MMTF 1000
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the recreational marine industry sectors
- Describe Service Department roles and responsibilities.​
