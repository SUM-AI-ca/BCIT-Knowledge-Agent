## Hydraulics 2 MILL 1506
Millwright Course
## Course Overview
This theory/shop course will include a review of Hydraulics 1 ( MILL 1505 ) and will also cover contamination, graphic symbology, diagnostic instruments, circuit and control analysis, component functions and failures, systematic troubleshooting procedures, and repair of fluid power circuits and components. Upon successful completion of Hydraulics 1 ( MILL 1505 ) and Hydraulics 2 ( MILL 1506 ) students will receive a Statement of Completion in Hydraulics. Steel-toed safety boots and safety glasses are required for this course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in MILL 1505
- Hydraulics 1 ( MILL 1505 ).
