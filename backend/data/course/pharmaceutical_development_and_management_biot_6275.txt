## Pharmaceutical Development and Management BIOT 6275
Biotechnology Course
## Course Overview
Teaches the biotechnology student to assist in the management of a pharmaceutical development project and a biotechnology facility. Explores the production of Biological Drugs and Cell and Gene based Therapeutics. Emphasizes the importance of Total Quality Management and ISO 9000 standards together with Good Manufacturing Practices and Good Laboratory Practices. Discusses government regulations of GMO and gene therapy. Teaches management practices appropriate for a biotechnology facility, including basic human resource management, financial administration and inventory control.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- BIOT 3250
