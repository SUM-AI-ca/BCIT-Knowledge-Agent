## Building Science Design Project ARSC 8355
Architectural Science Course
## Course Overview
​This course holistically integrates previously learned tools and processes to inform and refine the graduating project. Through a series of lectures and studio assignments, students will pursue transformative architectural solutions based on course-specific performance goals. Students will self-evaluate building performance on an ongoing basis throughout the course.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- ARSC 7350 and ARSC 7006
- ARSC 8005 ‡ ( ‡ must be taken concurrently)

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate strategic architectural decision-making to optimize full-spectrum sustainability and occupant comfort.
- Propose appropriate building materials based on multi-solving climate, performance, socio-ecological and architectural drivers.
- Synthesize whole building energy analysis into comprehensive architectural solutions.
- Integrate passive, renewable and energy-efficient systems for climate response and community resilience.
- Apply green building rating and certification tools, metrics and systems.
- Demonstrate professional best practice individually and collaboratively.​
Effective as of Winter 2026
