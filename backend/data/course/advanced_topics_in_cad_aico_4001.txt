## Advanced Topics in CAD AICO 4001
Computer Aided Design & Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Explores software solutions that extend the core CAD software for discipline specific tasks. Mechanical Desktop, Architectural Desktop and 3D Studio Viz are typical of the discipline specific software solutions that could be introduced.
