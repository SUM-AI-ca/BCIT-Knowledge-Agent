## Computer Graphics Portfolio Development MDAA 3000
3D Modelling, Art & Animation’ Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides diploma and certificate graduates with the tools, access, and structured environment needed to deepen their creative and technical skills in 3D art, animation, and related technologies. Students engage in ongoing practical work supported by continued software access, enabling them to refine workflows, troubleshoot complex production challenges, and expand their technical capabilities. Through iterative project development, mentorship, and collaboration, students build versatile, industry-relevant portfolios while developing adaptability across a range of pipelines and tools. The course prepares graduates for evolving roles in animation, visual effects, games, and interactive media.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Diploma/certificate from 3D Animation, Game Design or VFX program

## Learning Outcomes
​​Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Produce a cohesive, industry-relevant portfolio demonstrating versatility and technical proficiency.
- Receive, analyze and implement feedback.
- Communicate technical concepts and workflows clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Track progress based on the proposed plans.
- Critically evaluate and iterate on their work to improve quality and align with industry standards.
- Demonstrate readiness for professional roles in animation, visual effects, games, and interactive media. ​
