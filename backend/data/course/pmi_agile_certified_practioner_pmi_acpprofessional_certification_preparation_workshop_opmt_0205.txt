## PMI Agile Certified Practioner (PMI-ACP)®Professional Certification Preparation Workshop OPMT 0205
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
PMI‐ACP® Certification from the Project Management Institute is the industry standard for demonstrating competence and a solid foundation of agile principles and your skill with agile techniques. This workshop prepares participants to write the PMI Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI-ACP)® certification exam and provides the 21 hour of educational credits towards meeting the PMI application requirements. The workshop provides an overview of the PMI Agile framework as set out in A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide) and the PMI Agile Practice Guide.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply agile key concepts, terminology, organizational influences, and methodologies
- Describe the agile principles and practices
- Apply agile techniques for managing dynamic projects
- Apply agile project sizing and estimation
- Apply test‐taking techniques for writing the exam
- Understand the PMI-ACP Certification Process from PMI
- Understand the PMI application process to write the PMI-ACP Exam
- Be prepared to write the PMI-ACP Certification Exam
