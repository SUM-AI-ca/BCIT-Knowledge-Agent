## Electrical Technology 2 MEOC 2202
Marine Engineer Officer Cadet Course
## Course Overview
This course explores magnetism, electromagnetism, electrostatics, electromagnetic induction, single-phase alternating voltage, and single-phase transformers. Emphasis will be on application of principles to determine torque and forces on conductors in magnetic fields, and the generation of alternating current sine waves as conductor or magnetic field moves. Capacitors connected in series and parallel, calculating charge on capacitor, the relationship between capacitance and dimensions of capacitor, and types of dielectric. Charging and discharging capacitors and time constant τ, and electric field between capacitor plates. A small section of the course will cover electrical cable installations through bulkheads and types of marine cables approved by classification societies for use on vessels and offshore installations.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Sketch and describe a magnetic field and the B-H curve for different types of magnetic materials used in electrical machines and transformers.
- Build a simple electromagnet and calculate: field intensity, magnetomotive force, and flux density.
- Calculate the voltage induced in a conductor when there is relative motion between the magnetic field and the conductor, using Faraday's Law of Electromagnetic Induction.
- Calculate force on a current carrying conductor situated in a magnetic field.
- Calculate torque on a moving coil instrument given current, dimensions of coil, and strength of magnetic field.
- Calculate the torque developed in an armature given the dimensions of the armature and current in the armature conductors.
- Calculate the voltage on plates of a capacitor and charge on a capacitor when capacitors are connected in series and/or parallel to a DC source.
- Calculate the charging and discharging voltages across a capacitor in series with a resistor at a given time.
- Describe electric flux lines.
- Calculate the force exerted on a charge (Coulombs) placed in an electric field.
- Find the list of approved marine electrical cables using TP 127E.
- Sketch a multi-cable transit used on ships when cable runs through watertight bulkheads.
- Sketch a single phase step-down transformer.
- List the components needed to build a single phase step-down transformer.
- Calculate the turns ratio on step-up and step-down single phase transformers when you give the primary and secondary side voltages.
- Describe the no-load and full-test transformer tests.
