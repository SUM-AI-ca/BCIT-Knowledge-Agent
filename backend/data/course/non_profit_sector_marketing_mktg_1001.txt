## Non-profit Sector Marketing MKTG 1001
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
This course examines the fundamentals of marketing in the non-profit sector. Emphasis will be on the application of the marketing plays within the non-profit organization. Lectures, case studies and class discussions will be used to analyze marketing management within a wide range of organizations including social service agencies, cultural societies, educational institutions and public corporations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
