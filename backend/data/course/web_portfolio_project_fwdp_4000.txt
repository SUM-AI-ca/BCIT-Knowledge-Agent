## Web Portfolio Project FWDP 4000
Front-End Web Developer Course
## Course Overview
The focus of this course is to plan a front-end web development-related career, and at the same time, prepare to showcase a body of digital work created in and outside the program. This includes personal and professional development designed to support career success. Students will research trends, employment, and entrepreneurship to create a career plan. They will also create a fully functional, publicly accessible, professional-level portfolio website based on after-program career goals and industry expectations. At the end of the course project, students will have a portfolio website that demonstrates their skills using HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, JavaScript frameworks, and optionally server-side programming and/or content management systems. Students will also develop their personal brand, create a competitive resume and cover letter and learn advanced interview skills. Evaluation will include the project and workshop participation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- FWDP 1000 and FWDP 2200

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Recognize differences between freelancing and employment web portfolios.
- Plan the content and information architecture of a portfolio website based on individual career goals.
- Design and develop a professional-level portfolio website.
- Secure a professional custom domain and an appropriate hosting package to publish a secure portfolio site on a public web server.
- Apply career planning and research to identify trends, enhance employability, and entrepreneurial competitiveness.
- Explain the importance of a unified personal brand when seeking employment and freelancing.
- Compile the necessary elements of a unified personal brand.
- Prepare a competitive resume and cover letter that leverage new skills and experience to get employers' attention.
- Learn advanced interview skills for an effective job search.
