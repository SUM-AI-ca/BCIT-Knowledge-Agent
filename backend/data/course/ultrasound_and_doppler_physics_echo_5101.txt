## Ultrasound and Doppler Physics ECHO 5101
Adult Echocardiography Course
## Course Overview
This distance education course prepares students for the theory and practicum courses in echocardiography by outlining the underlying physical principles of ultrasound. It includes modules on waves, transducers, beam formation, the pulse echo diagram, artifacts, real time ultrasound principles, image processing, quality assurance and bio-effects. The latter portion of the course has an emphasis on Doppler principles.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Perform simple algebraic calculations.
- Describe the underlying principles of 2 dimensional (2-D), m-mode and Doppler ultrasound.
- Describe techniques to improve 2-D image quality.
- Correlate the effect of technical changes with image quality and Doppler sensitivity.
- Describe the physical principles that create image artifacts.
- Describe the ALARA principle and indices that are used to create awareness of the potential for bio-effects.
