## Presentation Skills for Broadcast and Business Success BCST 3251
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
​​Shine in front of any audience. Elevate your message. Leave a lasting impression in interviews, presentations and everyday conversations. Stand out confidently whether it's to a group, a client or your circle of friends and family. Be assured you have effectively communicated your message for the biggest impact on your listeners. Using age-old techniques passed down from masters in performance, you will gain a solid foundation to tackle any message or conversation to inspire and inform others in a meaningful way. Through personal exploration and technical exercises, you'll gain an inner confidence that sets you above the mundane and enables you more personal comfort and enjoyment for all interaction, whether personal or business.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Present a compelling delivery of any message, written or improvised; conversation or formal presentations.
- Influence and inspire any audience with your desired outcome.
- Improve the meaning behind your chosen words using subtexts.
- Assess whether you have captured and maintained the attention of the audience.
- Improve presentation abilities through acting tricks and improvisation.
- Elevate your personal voice to become your most powerful tool.
- Successfully interpret direction/directing words to refine delivery.
- Apply different vocal techniques and exercises to speak in front of any audience with ease
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2024
