## Intermediate Quality Control Methods OPMT 2242
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
Helps candidates prepare for the certification examination of the ASQ. This course is intended for people who have previous knowledge of statistics. Topics will include the use of distributions and tests of QC applications. The development of control charts and calculations for basic models.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in OPMT 1130 or 50% in OPMT 1197
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- apply the concepts of control charts and acceptance sampling in industrial situations
- choose the correct control charts for processes
- perform all necessary calculations for standard charts
- calculate capabilities for processes
- apply standard acceptance sampling plans
- perform basic reliability calculations
- apply probability distributions
Effective as of Winter 2018
