## Structures 2 for Building BLDG 2600
Building Course
## Course Overview
This course presents the elementary principles of mechanics of materials and an introduction to wood design. Topics include concepts of axial stress and strain, section properties of structural shapes, bending and shear stresses in beams, deflection of beams, column buckling, BC Building Code gravity and wind loads, limit states design philosophy, and preliminary sizing of wood bending members using the Canadian Wood Council Wood Design Manual. Students participate in a team-oriented, hands-on, design-build project which involves a preliminary structural design and construction of a small wood-frame structure.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Calculate the section properties of structural shapes.
- Determine internal stresses, strains, and deformations produced by forces acting on simple axial members and bending members.
- Explain British Columbia Building Code (BCBC) loading on structural members.
- Develop preliminary designs for wood joists, beams, and columns.
- Proposed a structural framing layout for a residential house.
- Use span tables for joists and studs.
