## Basic LNG Marine Bunkering Operations MSSM 0120
Marine Safety and Seamanship Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides training for personnel sailing on vessels (subject to relevant parts of the IGF Code) who are not immediately responsible for the care and use of fuel-related systems on board.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful co​​mpletion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Contribute to the safe operation of a ship, subject to the IGF Code.
- Take precautions to prevent hazards on a ship, subject to the IGF Code.
- Apply occupational health and safety precautions and measures when working aboard vessels, subject to the IGF Code.
- Respond to emergencies and carry out firefighting operations on a ship, subject to the IGF Code.
- Take precautions to prevent pollution of the environment from the release of fuels found on ships, subject to the IGF Code.​
