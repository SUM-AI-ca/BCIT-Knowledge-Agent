## MRAD Pre-program Preparation MRAD 0120
Medical Radiography Course
## Course Overview
​This course is intended to familiarize students with the program structure and to equip them with the resources available at BCIT to support their academic studies. Topics covered include, program orientation details, student support services and learning strategies and resources.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the program structure and delivery methods
- Access to services and academic resources
- Identify their learning style and how to best utilize this information for academic success
- Create a study schedule
