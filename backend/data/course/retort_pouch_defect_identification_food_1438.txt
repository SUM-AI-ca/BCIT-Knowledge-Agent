## Retort Pouch Defect Identification FOOD 1438
Food Technology Course
## Course Overview
For anyone who processes and inspects retort pouches. This workshop uses lectures, demonstrations, and labs to cover retort pouch background, material characteristics, processing, evaluation procedures and inspection requirements, and defect identification. BCIT issues a statement of completion to each participant who successfully completes this workshop with a minimum final grade of 70%. www.bcit.ca/study/programs/6340acert
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe pouch characteristics, including laminate materials, pouch types, and pouch terminology.
- Describe the specific considerations in processing of retort pouches, including filling and exhausting, seal formation, handling of closed pouches, and thermal processing.
- Describe the tests used in evaluation of retort pouches, including burst test and residual air test (destructive and non-destructive).
- Identify and classify retort pouch defects.
