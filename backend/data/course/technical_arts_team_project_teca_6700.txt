## Technical Arts Team Project TECA 6700
Technical Arts Course
## Course Overview
This course is the first of two courses in the team project sequence. The goal of the team project sequence is to prepare students for the deadline-oriented, results-driven, team-based environment that technical artists work in. Students will have the opportunity to use project management skills developed in Term 1. Students will be placed in teams to pitch, prototype, and deliver a technical art project, such as developing and optimizing tools, dynamic assets, procedural shaders and/or textures. Students will experiment with scripting, user interfaces and modeling techniques, and will apply technical and artistic skills specific to the project they are completing. Each week teams will discuss their progress with their instructor and receive feedback through team meetings and online sessions. The instructor will sign off on each milestone as the teams present their work.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Execute a project plan and manage tasks in order to meet deadlines.
- Complete project deliverables according to the deadlines outlined in the project plan.
- Conduct tests such as design, scripting, and prototyping at different stages of development.
- Perform regression tests in order to Incorporate feedback and test and retest as needed.
- Conceptualize and propose a technical art project.
- Develop a project plan, identify project deliverables, and estimate time requirements.
- Develop and execute a communications plan for the project.
- Develop and apply the skills required to work effectively in an interdisciplinary team.
- Develop scripts and expressions to create the elements of the proposed project.
- Present a final project in teams for instructor and peer evaluation.
Effective as of Fall 2019
