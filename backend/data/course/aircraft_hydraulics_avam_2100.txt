## Aircraft Hydraulics AVAM 2100
Aircraft Maintenance Course
## Course Overview
A course designed to provide you with the theoretical understanding and practical skills needed for the maintenance, troubleshooting and repair of hydraulic and pneumatic systems and components used in aircraft.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the learner will be able to:
- Discuss the importance of aircraft fluid systems.
- Fabricate rigid tube lines and fittings.
- Describe fluid line fittings.
- Inspect and repair fluid lines and fittings.
- Describe flexible hose fluid lines and fittings.
- Fabricate flexible hose fittings.
- Install, inspect, and test flexible hose lines.
- Discuss hydraulic seals.
- Discuss hydraulic fluid properties.
- Identify types of hydraulic fluid.
- Install seals and test components.
- Explain the behaviour of fluids.
- Explain work, power, and mechanical advantage in hydraulic systems.
- Explain system operation.
- Explain independent, dependent, and auxiliary systems.
- Explain system design.
- Explain operation of emergency backup systems.
- Hydraulic maintenance and servicing.
- Test, troubleshoot, repair, adjust, remove and replace hydraulic systems.
