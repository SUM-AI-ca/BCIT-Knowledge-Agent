## How to Launch an Online Business BTCW 0154
Business Workshops Course
## Course Overview
The Internet continues to offer opportunities and attract entrepreneurs. This 1-day session is designed to map out the process and procedures necessary to plan, launch and manage an online business. The workshop is designed to provide a framework, highlight issues and necessary resources. Topics include but are not limited to: strategy and planning, marketing and promotion, design and development, hiring and working with a design / development team, privacy and security issues for Canada, payment systems and software tools. If you are an individual or small business considering an online business or are in the planning process, this workshop is for you.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon completion of this workshop, participants will be able to:
- Identify the necessary steps and important topic areas in planning, launching and managing and e-Commerce-enabled business.
- Access an array of resources need to launch an online business.
- Understand the array of systems and process required for launching an online business including planning, design, development, marketing and managing.
- Understand the planning, logistical and management issues.
- Plan an e-Commerce business.
- Develop a framework and path on which to launch an online business.
