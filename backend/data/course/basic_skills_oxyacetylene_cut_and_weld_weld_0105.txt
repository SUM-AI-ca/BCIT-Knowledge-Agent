## Basic Skills: Oxyacetylene Cut and Weld WELD 0105
Welding Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the student to oxyacetylene welding. This course will familiarize the student with the safe use of gas welding equipment, types of welded joints, and will demonstrate preferred techniques for oxyacetylene welding. There are two aims for this course. The first aim is to introduce students to oxyacetylene welding safety, fundamentals and equipment. The second is to ensure that students can demonstrate the safe use of oxyacetylene welding equipment, and can correctly demonstrate welding and testing techniques.

## Learning Outcomes
- Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Identify and demonstrate safe workshop practices.
- Identify workshop hazards.
- Identify and describe BCIT workshop policies.
- Identify and demonstrate the use of personal safety equipment.
- Identify and describe oxyacetylene safety.
- Identify and describe oxyacetylene fundamentals.
- Identify and describe oxyacetylene equipment.
- Identify and describe oxyacetylene welding techniques.
- Identify and describe oxyacetylene cutting techniques.
- Identify and describe oxyacetylene welding faults.
- Identify and describe oxyacetylene cutting faults.
- Identify and describe oxyacetylene weld inspection procedures.
- Perform fillet welds.
- Perform welds on corner, lap and tee joints.
- Perform vertical welds
- Perform free-hand cutting techniques
- Perform guided cutting techniques.
- Perform brazing techniques.
