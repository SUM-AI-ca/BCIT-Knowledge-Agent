## Applied Research in Healthcare RSCH 4200
Applied Research in Health Course
## Course Overview
In this course, students will explore and critically evaluate published research on chosen topics in the field of medical imaging. Students will also learn various research methodologies, how to employ an evidence-based approach to healthcare and the principles of research ethics. Students will conduct their own systematic literature review to apply the theoretical knowledge learned from this course. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMM 3200 and MIMG 4000
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify and explain the research processes used in evidence-based practice implemented in health care settings.
- Identify basic principles of research ethics (Tri-Council Policy Statement)
- Critiquing and analyzing research characteristics.
- Search for relevant sources related to research topic using appropriate databases.
- Critically evaluate, summarize, and synthesize research sources relevant to research topics
- Apply discipline-specific citation style when writing to avoid plagiarism.
- Produce a systematic literature review.
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2024
