## Welding 2 ABOD 2501
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to set-up procedures for welding aluminum with metal inert gas (MIG) welding equipment. Students will perform various types of welds on sheet aluminum for the practical portion of this course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- WELD 1401 and WELD 1404
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, students will be able to:
- Describe set-up procedures for MIG welding aluminum
- Perform various aluminum MIG welds
