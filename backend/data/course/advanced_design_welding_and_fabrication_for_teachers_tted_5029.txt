## Advanced Design, Welding and Fabrication for Teachers TTED 5029
Technology Teacher Education Course
## Course Overview
Students will learn SMAW, GMAW, TMAW and oxy/acetylene welding. They will also cut and braze using oxy/acetylene. Drawing from those skills, students will engage in a design process to develop a fabrication project appropriate to students' age/grade and at nominal cost e.g. $30. Alternately, students may fabricate a fixture(s), jig(s) or machine that can be used in a technology education class without welding facilities. Additionally, students will design and fabricate a tool or device appropriate to their school/grade level. As an example, this might include a fixture table to support student work in the shop. This table will be multi-use and adjustable to support work in a multitude of situations. Finally, all instruction and work will be performed with an emphasis on safety, and the development of practices that encourage safe operations throughout the typical high school shop. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of a recognized Technology Teacher Education Program.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Fabricate artifacts with Multi-position SMAW and GMAW.
- Weld artifacts using flat-position TMAW.
- Demonstrate oxy/acetylene welding.
- Use oxy/acetylene for cutting metal.
- Braze items using oxy/acetylene.
- Apply Design principles for fabrication.
- Apply fixturing principles to support work precisely in the process of fabrication.
