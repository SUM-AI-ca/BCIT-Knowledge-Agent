## Sheet Metal GAP Training Level 2-HT ASHM 0000
Sheet Metal Apprentice Course
## Course Overview
This course is to provide GAP training to students who need to complete missing competencies for transition into the harmonized program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ASHM 1GAP
- Sheet metal apprentice level 1
