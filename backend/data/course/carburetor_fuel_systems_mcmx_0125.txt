## Carburetor Fuel Systems MCMX 0125
Motorcycle Mechanic Course
## Course Overview
This course provides motorcycle owners and enthusiasts with the skills needed to perform various diagnostic and maintenance procedures on carburetor fuel systems. Lectures are combined with demonstrations and hands-on training. BCIT motorcycles of various types are provided for demonstrations and hands-on activities.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCMX 0115

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe carburetor fuel system maintenance requirements on common types of motorcycles.
- Identify differences between aftermarket and factory replacement components.
- Perform carburetor fuel system cleaning and adjustment.
- Perform carburetor fuel system jetting, tuning, and synchronizing.
