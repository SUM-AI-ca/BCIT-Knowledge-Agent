## Social Media Strategies MKTG 1552
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
​In this course, you will explore how to develop social media marketing initiatives designed to meet business objectives. With a strong emphasis on real-life use cases, best practices, and data-driven techniques, you will learn how to create a strategically focused social media marketing plan that integrates with marketing, sales, and HR activities, and contributes to an effective online presence beyond social channels.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze the effectiveness of your company’s and competitors’ social media programs.
- Design social media programs that directly support business and marketing goals.
- Develop social marketing tactics that integrate with other online and offline marketing channels and programs.
- Discuss current trends and practices in social marketing.
- Implement a process for planning social media marketing activities.
- Evaluate Artificial Intelligence (AI) and GenAI use cases for social media planning and content creation, including assessing their benefits, limitations, and appropriate applications.
- Identify how to effectively use standard social marketing techniques, such as user-generated content (UGC), influencer marketing and viral marketing.
- Measure the effectiveness of social marketing activities, both organic and paid, and calculate ROI.
- Use tools to manage social media marketing programs.
Effective as of Winter 2026
