## Imaging Anatomy and Physiology 2 MRAD 3235
Medical Radiography Course
## Course Overview
This course is the second of two courses providing foundational anatomy and physiology required for the Medical Radiography Technology program. The course emphasizes the anatomy of the skull, as well as the anatomy and physiology of structures of the nervous, endocrine, cardiovascular, lymphatic, urinary and reproductive systems relevant to Medical Radiography Technology. These anatomical structures along with those introduced in MRAD 2235 will be revisited in the context of regional cross-sectional anatomy (head, neck, thorax, abdomen, pelvis, upper and lower extremities), for the purposes of developing visualization skills required for viewing anatomical structures on CT and MRI images in the three cardinal body planes. Course content is delivered in a hybrid format of lecture, lab and online learning. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the bones and anatomical landmarks of the cranium.
- Describe the anatomy and physiology of the organs included in the following systems:
- Nervous system
- Endocrine system
- Cardiovascular system
- Lymphatic system
- Urinary system
- Reproductive System
- Identify anatomical structures and organs of the following organ systems as viewed on CT and MRI images:
- Skeletal system (skull, vertebral column, upper and lower extremities)
- Nervous system
- Endocrine system
- Cardiovascular system
- Respiratory system
- Digestive system
- Urinary system
- Reproductive system
- Understand the relational and cross-sectional anatomy of structures related in the head, neck, spine, thorax, abdomen, pelvis, and upper and lower extremities as viewed on CT and MRI images. ​
