## A Systems Approach to Collaboration XLED 1100
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​No person, no idea, and no department are an end to itself. This workshop will take a systemic approach to collaboration. Through understanding your role as part of the system, and how the system functions, you will gain insight and develop an approach to moving initiatives through the system in which you operate.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand system structure and describe common patterns and perspectives.
- Identify system stakeholders and assess approaches to engage them.
- Activate your personal and group influence to develop a systemic collaborative approach​.
