## Photoshop Texturing for Animators 1 MDIA 1175
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course provides an introduction to the principles of texturing using Photoshop. Topics covered will include a review of the Photoshop tools including modifying images, exploring color techniques, as well as studying and recreating various textures through different painting techniques. Students will review image size, pixel depth and resolution, different selection techniques including Lasso, Color range, Quick mask, Magic wand, Mask logic and the Pen tool. Maya will be introduced towards the end of the course and the principles learned in Photoshop will be applied to the creation of 3D textures.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Good working knowledge of current versions of Photoshop and Maya.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate use of different Photoshop selection tools.
- Apply tonal range changes with curves and levels.
- Create and modify natural media brushes.
- Adjust image color with the color range tools to create special effects.
- Modify images seamlessly together by creating a layer mask.
- Review different Photoshop networks.
- Paint textures with different Photoshop brushes.
- Apply details and effects to textures.
- Create diffuse and normal maps with Zbrush.
