## Aircraft Gas Turbine Systems AVAM 3102
Aircraft Maintenance Course
## Course Overview
A course designed to familiarize you with gas turbine engine systems. Activities focus on learning to inspect and service engine instrumentation, ignition, fuel control, and lubrication systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
6.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the learner will be able to:
- Describe thrust augmentation systems.
- Describe types and operation of thrust reversers.
- Describe gas turbine internal air systems.
- Explain the purpose, function and types of engine gear boxes.
- Components driven by gear boxes.
- Describe the types and requirements of turbine oil.
- Explain engine lubrication system principles.
- Describe turbine engine oil lubrication system maintenance.
- Describe drive train lubrications systems.
- Explain fuel systems.
- Describe maintenance and adjustments of fuel control units.
- Describe types of turbine ignition systems.
- Describe types of gas turbine starting systems.
- Describe gas turbine instrumentation.
- Describe gas turbine inspection and maintenance.
