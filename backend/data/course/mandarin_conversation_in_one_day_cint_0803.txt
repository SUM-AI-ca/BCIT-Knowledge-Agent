## Mandarin Conversation in One Day CINT 0803
BCIT International Course
## Course Overview
For those who have no prior knowledge in Mandarin, but want a quick start in the language, this one-day immersion class is the most effective. The course content and its delivery method will help you to communicate in Mandarin as rapidly as possible. The course will introduce several practical topics and engage participants in activities where they are required to apply what they learn to express themselves and trouble shoot. The topics for each session vary and students are encouraged to take multiple sessions to build up their confidence in Mandarin and to proceed to a higher level.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Able to use simple expressions around certain scenarios.
- Have a fundumental knowledge of certain aspects of the Chinese culture.
- Be familiar with Mandarin pronouciation system.
