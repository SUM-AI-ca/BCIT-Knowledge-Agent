## Teaching Electronics 1 TTED 4060
Technology Teacher Education Course
## Course Overview
This course will investigate aspects of electronic components, power supplies, digital and linear electronic systems which are essential to teaching electronics in schools. It will begin at an introductory level and will take students to an intermediate level. Students will be involved in project design, theory and testing, circuit board and project construction. Appropriate safety and presentation of electronics information in school programs will be emphasized.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TTED 4010

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate safe work practices suitable for teaching high school electronics classes.
- Identify hazards present in an electronics shop and describe ways to address them.
- Design, assemble and troubleshoot electronics projects.
- Create functional enclosures for electronics projects.
- Describe the function of a selection of components and circuits typically used in high school electronics classes.
- Demonstrate proficiency using tools and test equipment typically found in a high school electronics shop.
