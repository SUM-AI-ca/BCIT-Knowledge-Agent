## Food Safety: the HACCP System FOOD 0160
Food Technology Course
## Course Overview
Individuals responsible for ensuring the safety and quality of food, i.e. potential HACCP plant coordinators from areas such as QC, production, sanitation and management will benefit from this workshop. It will focus on demystifying HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point) and the role it plays with ISO 9000 and TQM. Participants will be taught the skills necessary to implement a HACCP program to meet both their company's needs and government regulations. Before attending the workshop, participants will be required to review introductory information on HACCP and complete a company floor plan. Those not currently employed in the industry will be given an alternative assignment to be completed prior to the start of the workshop. A Certificate of Completion will be issued to those who have successfully completed the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Some knowledge of food processing industry and factors relating to safety of foods.
