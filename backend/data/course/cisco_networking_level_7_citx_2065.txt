## Cisco Networking Level 7 CITX 2065
Computer Information Technology Electronics Course
## Course Overview
This course aligns with the Cisco 642-832 (CCNP - TSHOOT) certification exam and provides training for students plan and perform regular maintenance on complex enterprise routed and switched networks. Students learn to use technology-based practices and a systematic ITIL-compliant approach to perform network troubleshooting.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of CITX 2063 AND CITX 2064 , or equivalent Certifications. (6 credits)
### Credits
6.0

## Learning Outcomes
Topics in this course align with the Cisco certification called TSHOOT. Upon successful completion, the student will have had exposure to:
- Common network maintenance tasks and tools.
- Troubleshooting models.
- Cisco IOS? troubleshooting commands and features.
- Troubleshooting Cisco Catalyst? Switches and STP.
- Troubleshooting BGP, OSPF, and EIGRP routing protocols.
- Route redistribution, security, and router performance troubleshooting.
- IP services and IP communications troubleshooting.
- IPv6 troubleshooting.
- Large enterprise network troubleshooting.
