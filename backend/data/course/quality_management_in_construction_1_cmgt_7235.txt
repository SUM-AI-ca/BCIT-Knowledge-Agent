## Quality Management in Construction 1 CMGT 7235
Construction Management - CMGT Course
## Course Overview
Management of quality is mandatory for quality organizations. This is the first course of a three part series on Quality Management in Construction introducing the Fundamentals and Definitions per ISO 9000-2000, Requirements per 9001-2000, and an extensive component on Auditing. This course includes an introduction to ISO and preparation of Quality Plans.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CMGT 7155 and 50% in CMGT 7120
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the noted course, the student will be able to:
- Recognize the standards for specification of quality in the construction industry.
- Examine the concepts of ISO 9001-2000 and present one or two Quality Elements and their function.
- Identify ISO 9001 systems tailored for the multi-family residential construction industry.
- Prepare a Quality Plan for his/her company or for a project relevant to his/her industry.
The student will also practice communication skills, both written and verbal, through assignments, classroom interaction, and presentations.
