## Microsoft PowerPoint Complete COMP 1184
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
COMP 1184 has been replaced by COMP 2384 . This hands-on course is the combination of MS PowerPoint Levels 1 and 2. It provides a thorough look at this presentation package and its approach to creating professional on-screen slides and shows with animation and sound. Students progress from beginner to expert in only 12 sessions. With a combination of lectures, labs and projects, students are provided with the practical skills needed to create advanced presentations by using the Auto Content. As an added bonus, this course helps students prepare to challenge a Microsoft Certification exam.
### Prerequisite(s)

## Learning Outcomes
- Create an Electronic Presentation complete with slide transitions and text animations.
- Edit, format and print On-Screen Presentations, Slides (Overheads), Speaker Notes, Outlines and Audience Handouts.
- Enter text; apply font formats, bullets, text styles, and copy formats by using the Format Painter.
- Work with Templates, Masters and Colour Schemes.
- Incorporate Slides from other Presentations and text from other files.
- Use the Spell Checker.
- Insert Clipart to enhance visual appearance.
- Insert sound and animation to enhance special effects.
- Import text and export presentations
- Use text styles and copy formats by using the Format Painter.
- Download images and sounds from the internet (outside of class)
- Insert sound and animation to enhance special effects.
- Apply Custom animation.
- Identify and use ClipArt, Pictures, Images, WordArt and Diagrams appropriately.
- Identify and use Drawing Objects, 3-D Effects, Sound and Video Clips.
- Link to other presentations and applications such as Word and Excel.
- Create hyperlinks, action buttons and comments.
- Create Summary slides to a presentation.
- Prepare and deliver an on-screen presentation complete with printed audience handouts and speaker notes.
