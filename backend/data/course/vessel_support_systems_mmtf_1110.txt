## Vessel Support Systems MMTF 1110
Marine Mechanical Technician Course
## Course Overview
​In this course you will learn the characteristics of propane and safety considerations, regulatory codes and standards, and proper installation. This course also introduces you to davits, hoists and windlasses, fire suppression systems and electric and mechanical steering systems. In this course you will describe and demonstrate the safety considerations of propane fuel, precautions of installing and operating davits, hoists and windlasses, and the vessel fire suppression systems. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the characteristics of propane gas, regulatory codes, standards and safety considerations
- Describe safe installations of propane storage tanks and fuel supply lines
- Describe the selection, location and installation requirements and considerations for installing davits, hoists, power hook ups and rigging in FRP, metal or wood vessels
- Describe the function, installation techniques and selection of electric and hydraulic anchor windlasses and power/control hook-ups for anchor windlasses
- Describe typical fire suppression equipment and associated safety hazards with fire suppression equipment on recreational vessels and common inspection procedures
- Describe fire suppression equipment safe lock out procedures
- Describe the function and operation of rudders, quadrants, tubes, glands and typical cable operated steering systems
- Describe diagnosing, servicing and repair of rudders, glands and cable systems
- Diagnose, service and repair mechanical steering gear
