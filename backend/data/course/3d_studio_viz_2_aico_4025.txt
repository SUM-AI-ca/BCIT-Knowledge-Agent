## 3D Studio VIZ 2 AICO 4025
Computer Aided Design & Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Provides advanced learning, additional skills and insights for using 3D Studio VIZ more productively. Topics such as AutoCAD Linking, the Materials Editor and advanced modeling techniques are covered extensively. Presents the knowledge and techniques for generating professional models, scenes and animations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in AICO 4020
