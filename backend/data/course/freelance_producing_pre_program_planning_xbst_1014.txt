## Freelance Producing: Pre-Program Planning XBST 1014
Broadcast and Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Freelance Producing will appeal to individuals who want to develop and share their own content for live or live to tape broadcasts and businesses looking for an online presence to promote and distribute company information, branding, and community outreach. ​This course explores how to layout the structure of a broadcast or social media program and how to build a creative team to launch your project. Explore interviewing subjects and develop interviewing skills for a virtual show and or broadcast.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in XBST 1013
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the elements for the pre-program launch
- Develop hosting skills for all media platforms
- Build a network of contacts in all aspects of freelance production
