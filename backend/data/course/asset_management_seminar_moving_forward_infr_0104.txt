## Asset Management Seminar: Moving Forward INFR 0104
Municipal Infrastructure Management Course
## Course Overview
This one day seminar provides an educational opportunity to hear from a global asset management leader with an update on the state of asset management and new government financial guidelines in Australia, a case study in rural Oregon, and our AM approaches to smaller communities. Hear from Infrastructure Canada on where programs are heading and receive an update from our Provincial Government. Having trouble communicating the infrastructure asset management message? Hear from a Canadian asset management leader on what the City of Edmonton has done and how they did it. Bring your issues to the table for discussion with our panel, and network with your peers. The seminar is co-hosted with the Local Government Asset Management Working Group (an initiative of the BC Ministry of Community and Rural Development).
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Articulate the current state of international asset management.
- Describe the current state of asset management in Canada.
- Understand the direction that Canada and British Columbia are heading with regard to infrastructure asset management.
