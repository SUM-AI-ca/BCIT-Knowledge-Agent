## Structural Analysis 1 CIVL 4070
Civil Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the student to various exact and approximate methods of analysis for statically indeterminate structures.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CIVL 2020 and 50% in CIVL 2070
### Credits
2.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Calculate beam deflections under general loading conditions.
- Establish the stability and determinacy of a structure.
- Qualitatively evaluate the structural response of beams and frames.
- Perform approximate structural analysis of statically indeterminate trusses, beams and frames.
- Determine support reactions and internal forces for statically indeterminate beams and frames using the Method of Consistent Deformations.
Effective as of Winter 2006
