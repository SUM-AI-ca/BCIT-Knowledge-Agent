## Physics 11 Challenge Exam for Sonography DSON 0006
Diagnostic Medical Sonography Course
## Course Overview
This challenge exam is designed for Sonography applicants who have surpassed the 5 year BCIT recency requirement and are well versed in the topics covered in the BC Physics 11 curriculum. Applicants can review the Physics 11 curriculum at www.bced.gov.bc.ca . Deadline for completion and submission of this grade is January 5.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Physics 11 or an acceptable equivalent course.
