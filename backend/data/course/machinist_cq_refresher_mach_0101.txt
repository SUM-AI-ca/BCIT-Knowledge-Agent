## Machinist CQ Refresher MACH 0101
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
Prepares tradespersons for the examination for the Machinist Certificate of Qualification with an Interprovincial IP (red seal) endorsement, and refreshes theoretical knowledge of the trade. Topics include: safety; use of hand tools; blueprint reading; grinders; lathes; milling machines; vertical and horizontal boring mills; planers and slotters; drill presses; cutoff and contour saws and CNC machining.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Minimum of 5 years experience in the machinist trade.
