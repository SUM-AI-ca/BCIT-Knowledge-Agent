## Preventing and Responding to Sexual Violence MOOC 0103
Massive Open Online Courses Course
## Course Overview
​Throughout this self-paced course you will learn about the meaning and impact of sexual violence as well as examine some of the reasons why sexual violence happens. You will learn about the importance of consent in all relationships and how to intervene to prevent sexual violence. You'll also learn about how to find support for yourself or how to support someone else who has experienced sexual violence.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the meaning and impact of sexual violence.
- List some of the reasons why sexual violence happens.
- Explain the importance of consent in all relationships.
- Intervene to prevent sexual violence.
- Find support for yourself or support someone else who has experienced sexual violence.​
