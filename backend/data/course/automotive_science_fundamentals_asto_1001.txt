## Automotive Science Fundamentals ASTO 1001
Auto Service Tech & Operations Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the required math, physics, electrical characteristics, and fluid mechanics that are required to ensure student success in the automotive industry.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate basic math concepts
- Illustrate technical measurements
- Describe properties of fluid dynamics
- Employ principles of electricity to series, parallel, and series-parallel circuits
- Demonstrate intermediate math concepts
