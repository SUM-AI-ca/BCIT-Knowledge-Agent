## Mill Field Trip 2 WOOD 3141
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
This course is a five-day workshop consisting of a series of field trips to various wood processing facilities. Its purpose is to supplement the classroom-based learning and provide topics for further discussion.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of Tier 3 courses for those enrolled in the IWPM program.
### Credits
1.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Analyze various manufacturing processes and select the most appropriate for a given log diet and product profile.
- Describe and evaluate mill processing equipment.
- Prepare mill flow diagrams.
- Identify differing management styles and contrast them company to company.
