## Computer Crime Investigative Techniques FSCT 8511
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
This course is a continuation of FSCT 8510 and will examine in-depth concepts in computer evidence collection and preservation, and applications of contemporary commercial forensic investigative software (EnCase, I-Look, Smart, ASR-Forensic Tool Kit).
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in FSCT 8510
### Credits
3.0
