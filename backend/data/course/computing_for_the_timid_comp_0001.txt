## Computing for the Timid COMP 0001
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This is a short, non-credit course for those who have never used a computer, particularly those who are afraid of them. It explains common terminology and the major parts of the computer, keyboard, disks. A very basic introduction to PC's that gives hands-on experience to familiarize students with IBM compatible personal computers. Anywho who has hands on experience with PC's does not need to take this course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Absolutely none - this course is designed for people who have never used a PC and are afraid to do so.
