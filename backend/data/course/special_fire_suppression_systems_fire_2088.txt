## Special Fire Suppression Systems FIRE 2088
Fire Protection and Inspection Course
## Course Overview
Fire 2088 provides an overview of the techniques, routines and solutions for test, inspect and maintenance on special fire suppression systems. Included in this course are a variety of extinguishing agents and the appropriate hardware that stores and issues the agents upon being triggered by a fire situation. The systems utilize extinguishing agents such as carbon dioxide, dry chemical, wet chemical, low, medium and high expansion foam, and water mist. Discussion and quizzes will reinforce the learning outcomes as the course progresses. Upon completion, students will be eligible to register at the Applied Science Technologists and Technicians of BC (ASTTBC) in pre-engineered special suppression systems. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe what constitutes a special fire suppression system
- Explain fire development theory and fire behaviour.
- Differentiate various fire suppressions systems
- Recognize the different types of fire suppression agents
- Recognize the appropriate applications for fire suppression systems
- Apply appropriate installation standards for suppression system detectors
- Explain the various codes and standards that govern installation
- Appreciate the importance of testing and inspection of fire suppression systems
- Examine the associated hazards and dangers of the fire suppression agents
