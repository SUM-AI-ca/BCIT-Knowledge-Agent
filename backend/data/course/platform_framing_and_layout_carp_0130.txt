## Platform Framing and Layout CARP 0130
Carpentry Course
## Course Overview
Covers residential wood-frame construction techniques. This course is hands-on as well as theoretical and is a pre-requisite to CARP 0140 Roof Framing and CARP 0125 Stair Construction. Steel toed boots and safety glasses required.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
