## Forest Road Engineering SRMT 4510
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
This course applies basic road construction knowledge and survey and data collection skills to low-volume forest road location and design. Forest transportation systems, road construction methods, and drainage management are expanded on. Student crews complete road reconnaissance and field surveys. Each student prepares a preliminary geometric forest road design. Additional topics related to road costing, road maintenance and road deactivation are introduced.
### Prerequisite(s)
- SRMT 3500
### Credits
4.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Complete a Geometric Road Design for a forest road based on current industry and Ministry of Forests standards, utilizing RoadEng engineering software.
- Recommend appropriate road construction equipment and techniques for given conditions.
- Discuss considerations for road location, design and construction that will minimize environmental impacts.
- Evaluate low-volume road costs utilizing general accounting and B.C. Forest Appraisal methods.
- Describe raw forest product transportation systems, focusing on road transportation from landing to log storage facilities.
- Describe current practices for the inspection and maintenance of major drainage structures.
- Prepare a road and drainage structure maintenance inspection.
- Recommend deactivation techniques for a road on stable terrain.
