## Interview Skills: Get the Job You Want! CLCR 1080
Call Centre Course
## Course Overview
A two-day course highlighting how to analyze job postings, to identify job content, practicing ways to present your skills and experience, and responses to common interview questions. Identify questions to ask employers and participate in a videotaped interview.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this Interview Skills workshop, you will be able to:
- Analyze a job posting to identify job content.
- Evaluate your own skills and experience.
- Produce effective responses to common interview questions.
- Determine which questions to ask employers in an interview.
- Conduct an effective job interview.
