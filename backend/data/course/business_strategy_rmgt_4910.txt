## Business Strategy RMGT 4910
General Insurance & Risk Management Course
## Course Overview
In this course students will integrate what they have learned in prior courses on the various functional areas of managing a brokerage. Topics include Strategic Leadership, Financial Management, Human Resources, Inside the Brokerage, Sales and Service, Marketing Strategies, Strategic Communication, and Growth, Valuation and Perpetuation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- RMGT 3110 and RMGT 3535
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Analyze a particular setting with respect to local and regional factors from the perspective of the establishment of an insurance brokerage.
- Develop a comprehensive strategic plan for an insurance brokerage, including the effects on the financial statements, human resources, and valuation.
- Communicate the strategic plan to the stakeholders involved, including:
- Documents for distribution,
- Presentation materials.
Effective as of Winter 2009
