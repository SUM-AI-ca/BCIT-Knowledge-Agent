## Learning Skills for Electrical and Computer Engineering Technology ELEX 0304
Electrical and Computer Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This is a six hour, non-credit course designed to help students cope with the extensive workload and provide additional information about the Electrical and Computer Engineering programs. Topics covered include introduction to technology, teamwork, time management, study skills, safety, the coop program, and diploma and degree options.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe principles for success in learning in a post-secondary program and lifelong learning. [12]
- Explain safety procedures in the school and work environment. [6]
- Select educational options in line with goals and interests in electrical engineering. [12]​
Effective as of Winter 2024
