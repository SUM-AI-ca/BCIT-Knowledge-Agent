## Integrative Project HMGT 5800
Health Management Course
## Course Overview
You must be currently working in an area directly related to the academic program to consider this option for your final course. Complete your certificate by identifying and analyzing a practical problem or opportunity at your workplace. By conducting a systematic review of the major issues and best practices, you will be able to develop appropriate alternatives and present a fully justified recommendation for solving the problem or advancing the opportunity. To prepare for this course, consult with your employer and your program head to identify a project plan that meets the learning objectives.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
This course provides each student with an opportunity to complete a comprehensive project that incorporates all learning and courses in all areas covered in your course of studies at BCIT. Each student will apply a problem solving model to a project at their organization.
This course allows each student to develop a specific integrated project that promotes an evidence-based approach to decision making and improves the outcomes for the sponsoring organization.
## Course Goals
- Research a potential change management initiative.
- Research, analyze and organize information.
- Apply problem-solving skills to analyze problems & issues.
- Determine appropriate personnel & processes to resolve problems & issues as required.
- Researching and recommending alternatives.
- Recommend how project learning can be integrated into ongoing organizational systems & processes.
- Develop relevant solutions to organizational issues for implementation options.
Upon successful completion, you will be able to:
- Demonstrate mastery of the issue selected for the project.
- Provide the organization with a 'value added' critical analysis of an important issue.
- Demonstrate proficiency in applying the problem-solving approach to the topic selected for the project, including identification of relevant benchmarks.
- Produce an analysis that demonstrates an ability to use evidence to support decision making.
