## Anatomy & Physiology 1 (NMED) BHSC 1108
Basic Health Sciences Course
## Course Overview
​This is the first of two consecutive courses that provide a comprehensive study of normal anatomy and physiology of the human body, relevant to Nuclear Medicine. This course includes essential principles of biochemistry, cytology, and histology that serve as the foundation for the study of human organ systems. The course also covers the integumentary system, skeletal system, nervous system essentials, muscular system essentials, cardiovascular system and lymphatic system. The next anatomy and physiology course, BHSC 2108 , covers the remaining organ systems that are relevant to Nuclear Medicine.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- ​Apply​ foundational principles of biochemistry, cytology, and histology in relation to the structure and function of organs and organ systems
- Use descriptive anatomy terminology to communicate the locations of anatomical structures in relation to other anatomical structures
- Describe the relational anatomy and physiology of the following organ systems relevant to Nuclear Medicine clinical procedures and anatomical landmarking:
- ​​​Integumentary system
- Skeletal system
- Nervous system essentials (additional nervous system content covered in BHSC 2108)
- Muscular system essentials
- Cardiovascular system
- Lymphatic system​​
