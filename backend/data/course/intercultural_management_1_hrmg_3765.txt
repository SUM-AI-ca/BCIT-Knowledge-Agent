## Intercultural Management 1 HRMG 3765
Human Resource Management Course
## Course Overview
This course focuses on culture as the central construct of diversity and provides theoretical perspectives for working and managing cross-culturally. It examines theories of intercultural communication and management, the effects of culture on teams and organizational behaviour and ways of building on the strength of team members and creating culturally synergistic approaches of team work.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
