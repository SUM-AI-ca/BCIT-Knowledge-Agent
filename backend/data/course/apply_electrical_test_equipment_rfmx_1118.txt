## Apply Electrical Test Equipment RFMX 1118
Refrigeration Mechanic Course
## Course Overview
This course covers the operation and use of basic electrical test equipment such as voltage ohm and amp-meter. Students will gain a greater awareness of their operation and acquire the skills to use them effectively and safely. In addition students will apply the above electrical meters to operational control circuits to determine proper electrical operation of circuits.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe operating principles of meters.
- Use voltmeter to measure volts accurately and safely.
- Use current meter to measure amps accurately and safely.
- Use ohm meter to measure ohms accurately and safely.
