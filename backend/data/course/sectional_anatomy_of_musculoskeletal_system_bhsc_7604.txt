## Sectional Anatomy of Musculoskeletal System BHSC 7604
Basic Health Sciences Course
## Course Overview
Designed for MR and CT Technologists requiring knowledge of sectional anatomy of the musculoskeletal system.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Graduation from approved program in diagnostic medical radiography/nuclear medicine therapy.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the completion of this course you should be able to:
- Evaluate the anatomy of the musculoskeletal system necessary for optimal imaging of the structures.
- Evaluate the normal anatomic and some pathologic features presented in the musculoskeletal system with emphasis on the sectional appearance of the images in various planes.
- Outline the imaging methods and techniques used in magnetic resonance imaging of the musculoskeletal system.
