## Aircraft Reciprocating Engines AVAM 1102
Aircraft Maintenance Course
## Course Overview
A course designed to provide you with the fundamental concepts of aircraft reciprocating (piston) engine operation, maintenance and repair. Activities focus on learning to identify engine components and their functions, to assemble and disassemble engines using appropriate manuals, and to complete required documentation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
8.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the learner will be able to:
- Explain the theory of aircraft reciprocating engines.
- Identify and explain the purpose of engine components.
- Practice techniques needed to inspect an aircraft engine.
- Use of manuals to determine correct procedures.
- Demonstrate shop practices.
- Explain field maintenance.
- Determine parts serviceability.
- Explain documentation.
