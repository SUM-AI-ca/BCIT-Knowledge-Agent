## Sonography Clinical 1 DSON 2004
Diagnostic Medical Sonography Course
## Course Overview
​Sonography Clinical 1 is the first clinical course and is designed to immerse the student into the clinical environment. Students in this course gain hands-on experience in the settings of abdomen, pelvis, obstetrics, vascular, superficial, and cardiac sonography. Emphasis is placed on patient care, inter-professional collaboration, communication, and safe practice. Students will reflect on typical departmental workflow, sonographer tasks, and the role of other health care professionals in the environment. Course goals will be achieved through guided scanning practice, application of feedback, image review, and job shadowing opportunities in other departments.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate professional behaviour at all times and in accordance with the Sonography Canada code of ethics and scope of practice. (2.1f, 2.1e, 2.1c, 2.2d, 2e, 2.2i, 2.3a, 2.3b, 2.3h)
- Demonstrate effective verbal and non-verbal communication with patients, their support persons, and other healthcare professionals. (1.1a, 1.1b, 1.1d, 1.1e, 1.1g, 1.1h, 1.1i, 1.1j, 1.1k, 1.3a, 1.3b, 2.3f)
- Obtain and record accurate and relevant patient history based on clinical indications. (1.2b, 2.2b, 2.2c, 4.1c, 5.1a)
- Implement safe practice to include appropriate patient care and own physical safety. (2.2a, 2.1e, 2.2f, 2.2h, 3.1c, 3.1d, 3.1e, 3,1f, 4.2d, 6.2a, 6.2b, 6.2c)
- Formulate scanning strategies incorporating relevant clinical information, patient conditions, knowledge of disease processes while applying problem-solving skills consistent with the level of experience. (2.2c, 2.2d, 2.2e, 2.2f, 2.2g, 2.2h, 2.2i, 3.1b, 5.1c, 5.1d, 5.1e, 5.3b, 5.3c, 5.3k)
- Produce accurate and diagnostic images, in a timely manner, demonstrating findings using spatial reasoning and according to departmental protocols of structures listed in NCP 6.0 appendices A-E. (4.1a, 4.1b, 4.1c, 4.2a, 4.2b,4.2d, 4.2g, 4.2e, 4.2f, 4.2g, 5.3a, 5.3b, 5.3c, 5.3d, 5.3e, 5.3f, 5.3h, 5.3i, 5.3l, 5.4a, 5.4d)
- Identify and respond to urgent sonographic findings aligned with best practices and site protocols. (2.2g)
- Formulate and present physicians with an accurate technical impression based on findings, limitations of exam quality in a timely manner. (1.2c, 1.2d, 1.2e, 2.3g, 5.4b, 5.4f, 5.4g)
- Appraise performance critically outline goals for self-improvement. (2.4a)
- Demonstrate a collaborative interprofessional approach to deliver patient-centered care, using the IPE competencies as a guide. (2.3c, 2.3d)
