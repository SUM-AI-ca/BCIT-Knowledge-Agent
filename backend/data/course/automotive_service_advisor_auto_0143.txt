## Automotive Service Advisor AUTO 0143
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Upgrades technicians and enthusiasts in automotive service advising. Describes various automotive repair shop management and structure, and identifies responsibilities of employees. Covers specific responsibilities of service advisors and outlines procedures for dealing with customer concerns for both retail and warrantee types of repair orders. Flat rate schedules, price quotations, marketing, customer follow-up, and total quality management will be discussed.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe various automotive service management organization and structure.
- Describe service advising responsibilities and job description / expectations.
- Describe customer sales and relation issues.
- Describe various forms and procedures for retail and warranty repair orders.
- Practice customer satisfaction strategies.
- Provide written job estimate and quotations for various automotive repair operations.
