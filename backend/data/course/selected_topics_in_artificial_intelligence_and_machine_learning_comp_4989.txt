## Selected Topics in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning COMP 4989
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
In this course, students will explore different practical applications of artificial intelligence and machine learning through a series of in-depth case studies. The case studies to be covered will reflect current trends in the industry. Topics may include spam filtering, product recommendation, price prediction, information retrieval, speech recognition and autonomous driving. Students will also work together to design and develop a product using real-world data sets.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 3981 and COMP 3760 . Corequisites: COMP 4983

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify techniques used in existing artificial intelligence and machine learning applications.
- Identify potential applications of artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to real-world problems.
- Design and develop artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions in a project team.
- Assess limitations and performances of artificial intelligence and machine learning systems.
- Plan and manage product development using an iterative and incremental software development framework.
- Create, troubleshoot and maintain a development environment.
