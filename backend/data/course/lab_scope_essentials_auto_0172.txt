## Lab Scope Essentials AUTO 0172
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
A lab scope enables a technician to accurately view and analyze high speed electrical signals to diagnose electronic systems on cars, motorcycles and heavy trucks and equipment. This seminar develops your skills required to configure and interpret lab scopes in order to enhance electrical diagnosis of anti-lock brake system, network and powertrain management systems. Lab scopes will be provided but students who have their own are encouraged to bring them for use during the workshop activities.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Experience in basic electrical circuit operation & diagnosis, ABS and engine management operating principles.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Define the terminology used with lab scope waveforms.
- Demonstrate effective lab scope configuration.
- Analyze the typical signals found in ABS, network, and powertrain management systems.
