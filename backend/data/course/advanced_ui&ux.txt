## Overview

Building upon foundational concepts from the Introduction to UI/UX Design course, this advanced-level class delves into the complexities of the design process, equipping students with advanced techniques and practices. The course emphasizes creating accessible and inclusive digital environments, focusing on interaction design principles, design systems, and responsive design methods. Students will develop and analyze UI/UX case studies, create high-fidelity prototypes, and use metrics to refine their designs. Emphasis will also be placed on integrating user feedback and navigating ethical considerations to address real-world design challenges.

## Learning Outcomes

- Apply interaction design principles to create intuitive interfaces that ensure seamless user interactions and improve overall usability.
- Utilize design systems to ensure consistency and scalability in designs, creating reusable UI components.
- Craft designs that are responsive and accessible, providing a seamless experience across multiple devices and adhering to inclusive design standards.
- Collaborate effectively in multidisciplinary teams, employing iterative design processes and incorporating user feedback.
- Evaluate designs based on usability, aesthetics, and overall user experience, utilizing user research and testing.
- Recognize and navigate ethical considerations in UI/UX design, adhering to legal obligations and guidelines.
- Develop compelling UI/UX case studies demonstrating application of design principles and effective communication of design solutions.
- Effectively incorporate user feedback into design iterations to improve user experience and address usability issues.
- Develop high-fidelity prototypes to test and validate design concepts before final implementation.
- Use metrics and analytics to assess the effectiveness of UI/UX designs and make data-driven improvements.
