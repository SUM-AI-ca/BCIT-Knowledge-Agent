## Practicum 1 BCST 2209
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course offers students their first opportunity to practice their skills in both a field, and studio environment. Students work together on a scheduled basis over four weeks as they produce program content for broadcast.
### Prerequisite(s)
- BCST 1107 and BCST 1111 and BCST 1113 and BCST 1320 and MDIA 2305
### Credits
7.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Collaborate effectively, observing ethical considerations, as part of a broadcast team.
- Demonstrate proficiency in the compilation and presentation of a local news content.
- Demonstrate basic news line-up ability for broadcast delivery.
- Produce material for broadcast use that conforms to the guidelines of professional associations and regulatory bodies.
- Produce pre-recorded material for broadcast use according to industry’s entry level standards.
- Provide and receive constructive criticism of lab work in a safe learning environment.
- Research and conduct a live and/or pre-produced interview.
- Use BURLI, MTS, or EZNews software in a broadcast environment.
- Demonstrate proficient use of professional broadcast field and studio production and post-production equipment.
- Demonstrate proficiency in the compilation and production of content.
- Develop media elements which can be used to provide a foundation for local news content.
Effective as of Winter 2014
