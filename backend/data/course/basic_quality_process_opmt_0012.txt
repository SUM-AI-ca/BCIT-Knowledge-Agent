## Basic Quality Process OPMT 0012
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
Provides a proven model for team problem solving and specific skills for improving any process, with an emphasis on how to sustain continuous quality improvement. These are basic skills that managers,team leaders and key contributors should know if they are being asked to participate in any performance improvement efforts.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
