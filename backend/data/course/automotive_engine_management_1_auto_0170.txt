## Automotive Engine Management 1 AUTO 0170
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Updates technicians with a practical approach to the operation and basic diagnosis procedures for 1996 and newer vehicles equipped with OBD II. Covers: fuel injection and ignition system components, computer operation and basic diagnosis of engine management systems. Provides an opportunity for basic practical experience in scan tool interfacing, data retrieval and interpretation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Mechanical and electrical testing experience.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe fuel injection and ignition system requirements.
- Describe input sensors and computer operation.
- Describe output actuators.
- Describe open and closed loop modes.
- Perform basic sensor testing procedures.
- Perform scan tool analysis to read fault codes and data stream information.
