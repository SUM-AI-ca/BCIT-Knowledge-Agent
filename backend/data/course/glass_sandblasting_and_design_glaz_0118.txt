## Glass Sandblasting and Design GLAZ 0118
Glazing Course
## Course Overview
Sandblast your own designs on various types of glass. No glass exprience necessary. Enjoy working in a well equipped shop and learn the basics of this art form.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify and use safe work procedures.
- Apply a resist to a piece of glass.
- Transfer a stencil onto the resist.
- Cut the stencil in the correct sequence.
- Sandblast the design in the correct sequence.
