## Multivariable and Vector Calculus MATH 4620
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
This course begins with partial derivatives with associated applications involving concepts such as extrema and Lagrange multipliers. Multiple integrals and applications in various coordinate systems are then covered. Theory and applications are then extended to vector functions and operators, line and surface integrals and integral theorems such as Stokes', Green's, and Gauss' theorems. Emphasis will be placed on applications in electromagnetism.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Outline the fundamental mathematical principles of single and multiple integrals and partial derivatives necessary for engineering analysis. [1]
- Apply principles of calculus to solve optimization and constrained optimization problems using the methods of Lagrange multipliers. [1,2]
- Perform vector calculus operations, both differential and integral, in suitable curvilinear coordinate systems. [1]
- Supply physical interpretations of the gradient of a scalar and a vector field, the normal vector, the directional derivative and Laplace's equation. [1,2]
- Identify conservative and non-conservative vector fields and simplify calculations using this knowledge. [1]
- Interpret the divergence and curl of a vector field using physical examples such as electromagnetic fields and the motion of a fluid. [1,2]
- Evaluate line and surface integrals for general surfaces and curves. [1]
- Use vector theorems - Gauss' Divergence, Green's and Stokes' theorems - to transform between different vector integral calculations in order to solve most efficiently. [1]
