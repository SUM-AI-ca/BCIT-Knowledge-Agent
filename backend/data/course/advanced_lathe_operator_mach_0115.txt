## Advanced Lathe Operator MACH 0115
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
A continuation of MACH 0105 . This course is designed to allow the student to gain more experience on the machine tools. The projects used in this course are of a more difficult and challenging nature than those encountered in the first courses. The concept of competency learning is still used in this program and care is taken to ensure that the selected projects are within the student's capabilities. Steel toed safety boots and safety glasses required.
### Prerequisite(s)
- S in MACH 0105
