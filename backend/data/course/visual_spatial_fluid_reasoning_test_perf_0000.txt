## Visual-Spatial, Fluid Reasoning Test PERF 0000
Cardiovascular Perfusion Course
## Course Overview
Cardiovascular Perfusion requires a number of individual skill sets that contribute to successfully completing the program, as well as success as a practicing Perfusionist. Potential candidates should be able to demonstrate a strong working memory, excellent verbal comprehension and communication skills, strong visual-spatial abilities, and excellent fluid (nonverbal logical) reasoning abilities. Furthermore, candidates must be able to demonstrate these abilities in a fast-paced and often stressful environment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- The Visual-Spatial, Fluid Reasoning Test is only available for applicants who have been short-listed for the Cardiovascular Perfusion Advanced Certificate.
