## Statistics for Construction Management 2 CMGT 7155
Construction Management - CMGT Course
## Course Overview
In this follow-up course on construction statistics, participants will be shown the relevance of sampling and estimating, linear regression and correlation with particular emphasis on the interpretation of construction test results. The application of these methods and techniques to business forecasting and quality management will complete this course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CMGT 7145
### Credits
1.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to intelligently read and interpret statistical material and use the statistical tools to carry out a variety of commonly used statistical calculations and tests.
