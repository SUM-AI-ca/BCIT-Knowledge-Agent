## Field Surveying 2 SURV 2264
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
Covers UTM traverse and computations; different field methods of angle measurements; trigonometric leveling; topographic surveys by various methods and instruments; road location; cuts and fills; setting out circular curves; building site layout; and, total stations. This course must be taken concurrent with SURV 2261
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in SURV 1164 and 50% in SURV 1161
