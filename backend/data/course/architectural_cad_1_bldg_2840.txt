## Architectural Cad 1 BLDG 2840
Building Course
## Course Overview
Introduction to CAD for architects, building designers and builders. The course is designed for familiarize the student with the operation of AutoCAD in the Architectural Environment utilizing the most recent release of the software.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Basic drafting knowledge and computer literacy.
