## E-commerce for Printers GTEC 3360
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course is an introduction to the many opportunities presented by E-commerce and online connectivity in the printing industry. It will prepare the student to devise, select, implement, maintain, and grow an online presence for a print company. Emphasis will be on using web technology successfully.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the different components of a print company's online presence.
- Define the criteria for a valuable online presence.
- Contrast the needs of the client with process improvements for the print company.
- Categorize online needs based on the positioning of the print company.
- Analyze an existing web presence for effectiveness.
- Outline needs for high-speed data communication.
- Identify criteria for choosing tech partners.
- Identify company departments (stakeholders) to be included in planning and development.
- Develop a business plan for implementing a complete online presence for a mid-sized print company.
