## Industrial Wood Processing Orientation WOOD 0010
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
This class will prepare students to use the online learning environment throughout the Industrial Wood Processing program, as well as introduce them to instructors and their fellow classmates. Students in this orientation will be provided with an overview of each course in the program and review math skills that will be used in some of the class content.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
