## Wood Gluing WOOD 2010
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
Includes descriptions of the various type of adhesives used in the manufacture of glued wood products, and the selection of the correct adhesive for each product. Methods of application and curing, and factors involved in obtaining high quality joints will be discussed.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in WOOD 1040 and 50% in WOOD 1060
