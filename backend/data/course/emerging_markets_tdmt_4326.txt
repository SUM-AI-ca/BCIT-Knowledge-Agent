## Emerging Markets TDMT 4326
Transportation and Distribution Course
## Course Overview
Through discussion and individual research, students will gain insight and understanding of emerging economies and the role they play in global commerce. By applying the concepts and understanding the issues, students will prepare a research paper on an emerging economy and the opportunities and risk that are inherent.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TDMT 3305
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Identify the main characteristics of an emerging market.
- Analyze the issues and common mistakes made by firms in attempting entry to emerging markets.
- Predict the effect of internal resources and external forces on the working environment.
- Build a business model for emerging markets.
- Identify the role of ethics in emerging markets.
- Assess the competitive position of a company in a global context.
