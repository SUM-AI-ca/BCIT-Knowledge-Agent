## Audio for Animation MDIA 3195
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
The focus of Audio for animation lies in working with ProTools LE. Time will be spent importing, editing and producing basic audio pieces for projects. The work created in this course can be used in multimedia, media projects and demo reels. Students will also be exposed to basic audio connections and cables and learn basic concepts about digital audio. By using practical projects the students will experience the process of audio production and post-production.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon succesful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the major audio formats and their uses.
- Explain broadcast and release medium standards.
- Use advanced industry standard software tools for special sound effects.
- Create a sound track using the basic steps in the audio process.
- Import and edit a basic soundscape.
- Edit and produce a basic music remix with pre-recorded files.
- Control audio pitch sound through mixing controls.
