## Autodesk Survey GEOM 7115
Geomatics Course
## Course Overview
Teaches you how to efficiently collect and process survey field data through the establishment of data collection and processing standards using Autodesk Survey. This course includes a short field project but mainly focuses on the implementation of data collection standards, downloading/importing of data, and the adjustment/analysis of field measurements.
### Prerequisite(s)
- GEOM 7105
### Credits
1.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Establish survey settings for a LDD project.
- Reduce manual survey notes using the Survey Command Processor.
- Create survey figures from a field book file.
- Use coordinate geometry.
- Reduce digital data from a variety of data collectors.
- Perform traverse adjustments.
