## Forest Engineering and Operations 2 FNAM 3400
Forest & Natural Areas Mgt Course
## Course Overview
​This course equips students with the essential skills and knowledge to plan and execute sustainable forest operations, building upon the observational and problem-solving skills developed in FNAM 2400 . Students will learn to assess harvesting opportunities and constraints using photo and topographic interpretation, digital tools, and resource databases. They will gain proficiency in industry-standard surveys and data collection techniques for various forest operations, including hazard assessments, road layout, and stream crossing design. Furthermore, students will develop the expertise to prepare comprehensive harvest plans that align with current permits, legislation, and policies in British Columbia. The course also emphasizes evaluating the effectiveness of remedial actions for roads and crossing structures, considering their long-term impact on forest management objectives and sustainability. Finally, students will analyze operational costs for various forestry activities using engineering economics principles to determine cost-effective solutions.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Interpret aerial photos and topographic maps to identify terrain features, potential harvesting opportunities, and operational constraints for forest operations.
- Perform industry-standard field surveys and data collection techniques for various forest operations, including hazard assessments, road layout, deflection lines, and stream crossing design.
- Prepare detailed harvest plans that incorporate environmental considerations, safety protocols, and adhere to current permits, legislation, and policies in British Columbia.
- Assess the effectiveness of existing roads and crossing structures, and design new infrastructure within the scope of forest technologists that minimizes environmental impact and promotes long-term sustainability.
- Apply engineering economics principles to analyze operational costs for various forestry activities and identify cost-effective solutions for forest operations planning.​
