## Introduction to Intelligence; Theory & Practice FSCT 8435
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to contemporary techniques and applications of intelligence used within the enforcement, regulatory and compliance community. The subject will provide the basis for advanced study in intelligence analysis and strategic intelligence.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Admission to a Forensic credential program or permission of the Program Coordinator.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Introduction to Intelligence is an examination of a field of study in which there is a diversity of perspectives and practices. In this subject you will be introduced to a variety of contemporary applications and techniques used within the enforcement, regulatory and compliance community. You will reconstruct and develop your own ideas as you work through the study material. The outcome of this process will depend on your background and the extent to which you apply your understanding. The assessment items are designed to facilitate this process. The subject will provide the basis for more detailed study in intelligence analysis and strategic intelligence.
