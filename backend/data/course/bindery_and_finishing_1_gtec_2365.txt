## Bindery and Finishing 1 GTEC 2365
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course provides students with an orientation to bindery and finishing. Students will learn how to do basic set-up and running of the cutter, folder and stitcher. Students will also learn to read job dockets, plan work processes, run machines to production standards, and consistently monitor work quality. Students will gain hands-on bindery and finishing experience and learn basic set-up and running of a Perfect Binder, including the 3-knife trimmer. Students will also set-up and run more complex folding, cutting and stitching jobs.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the basic bindery and finishing functions.
- Plan the necessary work processes, with packaging, for completion of the binding or finishing tasks.
- Explain the functions of a job docket and identify the necessary elements for bindery/finishing.
- Perform basic job set up of Cutter, Folder and Stitcher.
- Perform basic maintenance on the Cutter, Folder and Stitcher.
- Produce a cut job to defined job standards.
- Produce a folded job to defined job standards.
- Produce a finished book to defined job standards, using Stitcher and Trimming techniques.
- Complete all bindery and finishing tasks safely.
- Maintain a clean and safe workplace.
- Identify and explain complex bindery and finishing functions and operations.
- Plan the necessary work process for completion of a perfect binding project.
- Complete job layouts that clearly optimize the bindery equipment and meet customer's needs.
- Lead and operating crew in completion of a bindery or finishing project.
- Perform complex job setup of the Folder and Stitcher.
- Perform basic setup of the Perfect Binder.
- Carry out basic maintenance on the Perfect Binder and other bindery and finishing machines.
- Produce a bound job to defined job standards.
- Produce a complex job (double gate or map fold) to defined standards.
- Produce a finished book (stepped or indexed, with line-ups and/or crossovers) to job standards.
