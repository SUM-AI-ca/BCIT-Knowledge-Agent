## Organizational Influence and Persuasion ORGB 2455
Organizational Behaviour Course
## Course Overview
This course develops leadership skill in the application of influence and persuasion methods to communication in organizational settings. Participants develop personal influence and persuasion capability for one-to-one and team environments. Leadership communication skills are refined to incorporate effective methods to present and market change messages to individuals and to diverse stakeholder groups.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
As a result of satisfactory completion of this course, participants will be able to:
- Demonstrate conceptual understanding of communication factors associated with presenting, marketing, and influencing change.
- Demonstrate the skills associated with selecting an approach, appropriate media, mechanisms to gain support, presentation techniques, ans sensitivity to stakeholder needs.
- Demonstrate selling organization change methods application to external individuals and groups.
- Increase effectiveness in personal and professional communication.
- Demonstrate the ability to give, receive, and utilize feedback to improve communication style and delivery.
