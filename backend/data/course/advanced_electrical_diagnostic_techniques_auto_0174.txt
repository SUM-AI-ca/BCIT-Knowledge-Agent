## Advanced Electrical Diagnostic Techniques AUTO 0174
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Move beyond standard electrical resistance and available voltage testing procedures. Think outside of the workshop manual and develop your advanced diagnostic skills utilizing alternative circuit testing methods, lab scope and network system diagnostic techniques. This course is ideal for both professional technicians and apprentices.
### Prerequisite(s)
- AUTO 0173 or minimum Automotive Service Technician 1 Apprentice.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will:
- Apply alternative electrical testing methods.
- Produce an effective diagnostic strategy.
- Demonstrate diagnostic ability in both electronic and interconnected network systems.
