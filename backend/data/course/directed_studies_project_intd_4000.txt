## Directed Studies Project INTD 4000
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
Allows student to incorporate all material from previous courses into a major project. Prerequisite for INTD 4000 ; 65% minimum in all courses in the Interior Design Certificate Program. Before registering in the Senior Certificate program, student must have 65% in INTD 4000 . Student must bring transcripts to first class.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the student will be able to:
- Prepare a complete Interior Design project, including floor plan, furniture layout, reflected ceiling plan, section, elevations, millwork detail drawing, graphic presentation, perspective drawing, furniture selections, and colour/material selections. The design solution will have a strong design concept, continuity, be appropriate, and presented in a clear and professional format.
