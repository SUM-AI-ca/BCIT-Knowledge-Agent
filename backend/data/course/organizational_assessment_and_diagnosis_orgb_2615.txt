## Organizational Assessment and Diagnosis ORGB 2615
Organizational Behaviour Course
## Course Overview
This course develops leadership skill in assessing and diagnosing organizational effectiveness. Analytic and consultative skills are applied to focus organizational purpose and principles, align stakeholder perspectives, and clarify the organization's purpose, mission, and vision through understanding of various mental models and a diagnostic reading for an organization of choice.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in ORGB 2605 † ( † may be taken concurrently)
