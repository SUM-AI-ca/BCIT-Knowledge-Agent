## Ethics and Professional Practice MRAD 4205
Medical Radiography Course
## Course Overview
This course will provide you the context and tools necessary to be an effective member of the health care system as students and professionals. You will often act as a translator of technical information for patients, giving them instructions and explanations about imaging technology and exams. You will also contribute to the Imaging Department's team by making suggestions for improving patient care and departmental efficiency. Skills in assessing the needs of audiences and situations are fundamental in deciding what information to provide and how to say it in a way that will be understood and well received. As a member of an Imaging department and MRT profession, you will share information and best practices with colleagues and may collaborate in applied research to advance the practice. You will also develop an understanding of what it means to be a health care professional. You will have the opportunity to learn about codes of ethics and descriptions of practice as set out by regulatory bodies and national associations. This will guide you into competent collaborative practices with an interprofessional healthcare team.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify factors in the clinical environment that may impact the delivery of care.
- Recommend evidence-based changes to current practice to improve delivery of care.
- Engage in activities to support or promote the profession.
- Develop strategies to prioritize workflow to optimize patient care and resources.
- Outline quality improvement processes.
- Identify changes and emerging issues impacting the practice environment.
- Given a scenario, evaluate clinical behaviors according to the Code of Ethics and Standards of Practice as outlined by regulators and professional associations.
- Identify the different organizations involved in certification, accreditation, and regulations and collective agreements.
- Describe the requirements for attaining and maintaining professional certification.
- Describe the role of an imaging technologist as an educator in clinical practice.
- Explain the implications of the use of social media as a health care professional.
- Create a professional profile appropriate for health care professionals.
- Demonstrates collaborative leadership when engaging with a team member.
- Resolve interprofessional conflicts/disagreements taking onus on personal actions to produce positive outcomes.
- Apply ethical theories for professional practice in accordance with legislation, regulations and ethical guidelines related to the profession.
- Identify key drivers for organizational change in health care environments.
- Describe the application of resource management principles.
- Define and discuss the purpose and key characteristics of a self-governing health profession.
- Outline the purpose and benefits of other imaging modalities.
