## Urban Forest Assessment Level 2 RENR 1070
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
​The Urban Forest Assessment course provides training in ecosystem values and assessment protocols for urban forests and the application of this information in regional land management. This knowledge is increasingly relevant in municipal planning and in the assessment and management of natural capital assets. i-Tree software provides ecological and economic values for urban forests and includes species diversity, pollution mitigation, avoided runoff, carbon sequestration. The data from i-Tree Eco provides valuable information for forecasting planting, pest and storm event impacts on forest canopy ecosystem benefits and structure. This course include an understanding of the basics of urban forest assessment, the ability to sample larger areas or patches of urban trees, and compile, prepare and submit data to the i-Tree system. Students will also have the opportunity to review a typical i-Tree report. The Urban Forest Assessment course is applicable for students studying ecological restoration, forestry, resource management, arborists, planners, consultants, stewardship groups and teachers. The course will consist of two days of combined classroom and field work.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe function of i-Tree Eco including attributes for tree observations
- Practice collection of tree observations with i-Tree Eco attributes
- Create an i-Tree Eco project
- Identify study area and navigate to the site using a variety of tools
- Apply i-Tree Eco data collection method for complete inventory survey in the field
- Prepare field data for i-Tree Eco submission
- Use i-Tree Eco to error check data and trouble shoot issues
- Review reports generated by i-Tree
