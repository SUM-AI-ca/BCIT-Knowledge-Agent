## AVID 3D Animation and AfterFX BCST 2083
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course will teach the student how to create basic 3D animation with Avid 3D software as well as video and graphic compositing with Adobe AfterFX. Students will utilize video from Avid Xpress Studio applications within their projects as well as importing their finished composition back into Avid.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BCST 1180
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will:
- Learn how to create basic 3D animation with Avid 3D software.
- Create video and graphic compositing with Adobe AfterFX.
- Use Avid Xpress Studio applications.
- Import finished composition back into Avid.
