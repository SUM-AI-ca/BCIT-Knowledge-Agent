## Process Measurement and Control 2 ELEX 0221
Electrical and Computer Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Discusses various types of flow and temperature measurement equipment. Calibrate and evaluate typical industrial transmitters. PI, PD, and PID control systems are tuned by the student.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in ELEX 0215 or 50% in ELEX 0216
- ELEX 0215 OR ELEX 0216
