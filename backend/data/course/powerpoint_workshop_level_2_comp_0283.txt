## PowerPoint Workshop - Level 2 COMP 0283
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
COMP 0282 and COMP 0283 have been replaced by COMP 2384 . This intensive, hands-on seminar uses MS PowerPoint 2007 and builds on the Level 1 workshop. In just one day you will cover intermediate and advanced PowerPoint topics including: design template customization, setting up masters, various types of diagrams and smartart graphics , special effects, custom slide shows, collaboration functionality, more on custom animation, and advanced presentation delivery. This workshop is only for those students who have the level 1 knowledge and who already understand the new MS Office 2007 Ribbon interface.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 0282

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Insert and edit graphs, clip art, tables and organization charts.
- Link information from other programs.
- Create effects and slide transitions & timings.
