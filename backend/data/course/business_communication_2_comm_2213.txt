## Business Communication 2 COMM 2213
Communication Course
## Course Overview
Students will propose, research, write, and produce a multimedia personal digital story. they will plan the project using a systematic process. They will create a storyboard and script, research the topic (primary and secondary), integrate references and cite sources, and write a research report. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMM 1113
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply pitch techniques learned in term one to propose a multimedia personal digital story.
- Compare and contrast linear, non-linear, and interactive structures of stories, storytelling, and conventions.
- Plan a story using a systematic process.
- Create a storyboard and script for a personal digital story.
- Apply a variety of technologies to create a personal digital story.
- Conduct research (both primary and secondary) for digital stories and industry-related research reports.
- Integrate references and cite sources using APA .
- Write a research report.
- Produce and present a multimedia personal digital story.
Effective as of Winter 2020
