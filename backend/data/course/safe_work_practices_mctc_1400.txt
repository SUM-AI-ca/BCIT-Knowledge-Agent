## Safe Work Practices MCTC 1400
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to the Workplace Hazardous Materials Management System (WHMIS) and WorkSafeBC regulations. Students will apply the regulations to shop safety, hazardous material safety, fire safety, safe work practices, and proper body mechanics in their work environment. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe personal safety equipment
- Apply WHMIS legislation to the workplace
- Describe WorkSafeBC regulations
- Demonstrate safe work practices​
