## Introduction to Net Zero Construction XZEB 0100
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides an introduction to net-zero construction, covering the principles of green building technologies, energy-efficient design, and mechanical, electrical, and carpentry applications. Students will gain a foundational understanding of how climate change is driving the adoption of net-zero practices and explore hands-on applications of these technologies in the trades.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the impact of climate change on skilled trades and the built environment.
- Describe the role of building envelopes in net-zero construction.
- Understand the fundamentals of photovoltaic (PV) systems and energy-efficient lighting.
- Understand the role of other systems, such as heat pumps, heat recovery ventilation, etc.
- Apply net-zero construction principles through hands-on mechanical, electrical, and carpentry exercises.
- Recognize government and private incentives available for skilled trades workers and green technology contractors.​
