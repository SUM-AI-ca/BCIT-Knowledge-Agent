## Information Technology Concepts For Forensic Investigation 1 FSCT 5103
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the students to the various aspects of Information Technology used in today's businesses with a focus on potential security threats. It will include a review of business processes and the relevance of IT in business operation, computer systems hardware and architecture, operating systems and systems analysis, design and development.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon completion of this module, the student will have sufficient knowledge to advance to more specialized courses that require a good understanding of the various IT concepts, applications and terminologies. More specifically, the student will be able to:
- Explain technological changes including technological obsolescence, Moore's Law exponential change, and security challenges.
- Describe how the forensic process relates to the business environment.
- Explain the basic hardware architectures and components.
- Compare and contrast various computer operating systems.
- Explain the differences between an Open Source environment and the strictly proprietary systems.
- Describe basic web development technology and its applications.
- Explain basic business operations in an organization and how Information Technology fits in these operations.
- Explain how software tools can be used to solve problems in a business environment.
