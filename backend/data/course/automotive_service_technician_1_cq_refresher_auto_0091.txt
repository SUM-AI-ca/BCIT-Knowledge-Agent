## Automotive Service Technician 1 CQ Refresher AUTO 0091
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Refreshes theoretical knowledge of the trade in preparation for the Automotive Service Technician 1 Certificate of Qualification examination. Topics covered will include: basic electrical systems, brake systems, general automotive maintenance, general automotive practices, steering systems, suspension systems, tools and equipment, wheels and tires, and workplace safety. Contact the SkilledTradesBC (tel. 778-328-8700 or toll free within BC 1-866-660-6011) to determine eligibility and to schedule an examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Working experience or prior training on automotive brakes, steering, suspension, and electrical/electronic systems

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be:
- Prepared to write the Certificate of Qualification examination for Automotive Service Technician 1.
- Able to perform practical tasks with a better understanding of component operation.
- Able to write trade exams of various formats.
