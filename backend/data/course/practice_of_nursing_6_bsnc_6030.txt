## Practice of Nursing 6 BSNC 6030
Nursing Course
## Course Overview
This course builds upon all previous Practice of Nursing courses, with a focus on clients facing complex and/or unstable health challenges in acute care settings. It includes a rotation in an acute medical/surgical setting, emphasizing clinical reasoning as essential for planning and delivering safe, high-quality care. Factors such as life span considerations, cultural diversity, and legal aspects of care form the framework for ethical decision-making in client care choices within the acute medical/surgical setting. The course also incorporates integrated and applied learning experiences in simulated and real-world practice environments.​​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will meet the following competencies:
Advocate
- Collaborates with clients to facilitate navigation through health care services effectively
- Uses knowledge of relational practice to impact health outcomes of individuals, families, communities, and populations
- Examines and promotes the equitable allocation of health care resources considering the health disparities of at-risk populations
- Incorporates knowledge of social justice, population health, environmental and global health issues in the nursing care of clients
- Advocates for informed decision making and preservation of dignity on behalf of clients
- Incorporates knowledge of the unique needs of specific populations into nursing care
Collaborator
- Identifies hierarchical structures and power dynamics within the healthcare system
- Recognizes situations for potential and actual conflict and with guidance, identifies and initiates potential solutions to conflict in defined contexts and situations
- Solicits and accommodates multiple perspectives in any given situation to support safe, high quality, client-centred care
- Implements a strength-based approach to collaboration in defined contexts and situations
- Participates in cooperative communication and decision-making with healthcare team members to optimize outcomes for clients and the healthcare system
- Facilitates linkages between resources and clients
- Promotes a strength-based approach when collaborating with clients and healthcare team members
Communicator
- Communicates effectively with clients and healthcare team members in increasingly complex contexts or situations to support safe, high quality, client-centred health care with minimal assistance
- Demonstrates a strengths-based participatory approach to developing and maintaining therapeutic partnerships
- Engages in relational practice through a variety of approaches that demonstrate caring behaviours appropriate for clients
- Assists clients to identify and access health and other resources in their communities
- Demonstrates effective public speaking
- Educates clients to maintain and promote health
- Uses information and communication technologies to support information synthesis in accordance with professional and regulatory standards in the delivery of client care
Decision Maker
- Uses critical Inquiry and relational practice to incorporate knowledge of a particular client, client patterns of responses; personal clinical knowledge of similar clients, and empirical knowledge, in the planning and provision of client care
- In relation to specific clients, identifies credible, accurate, appropriate and current information, resources, services and/or relevant technologies
- Identifies implications for nursing practice related to health information and technologies
- Aligns care with quality criteria, legal frameworks, and economic and ecological principles
Global Citizen
- Uses critical perspectives to analyze social justice and equity issues in healthcare
- Incorporates global awareness in nursing practice
- Incorporates cultural humility and social consciousness in nursing practice
- Plans care for clients that incorporates consideration of determinants of health and social justice
- Provides care to clients that promotes social justice and equity in healthcare
- Uses moral questioning to identify the socio-political, economic and historical factors that lead to inequities among individuals, communities and nations
Leader
- Incorporates evidence-based knowledge, courage and ethical standards to challenge the status quo
- Demonstrates appropriate use of expertise/influence/credibility to positively influence client care outcomes
- Promotes and incorporates the perspectives and expertise of each member of the healthcare team
- Promotes a practice environment that encourages creative thinking and innovation and proposing alternative actions as appropriate
- Explores and develops a range of possible alternatives and approaches for care with clients
Lifelong Learner
- Uses evidence-based resources to promote better practices in client care
- Critically evaluates research and its contribution to nursing practice
- Incorporates interprofessional perspectives so as to optimize client outcomes
- Presents knowledge and critical analysis in a scholarly manner
- Engages in learning activities designed to augment nursing knowledge, skill and attitudes to enrich their contribution to quality healthcare
Professional
- Uses knowledge of the nursing role and the roles of other healthcare professions to consult with the client, family and the healthcare team to develop a plan of care to meet the client’s needs
- Consults with others to identify an appropriate ethical reasoning and decision-making process to address ethical dilemmas and situations of ethical or moral distress
- Promotes a professional culture that is responsive, supportive and collegial
- With guidance, assesses own practice and undertakes activities to improve practice and meet identified learning goals
- Respects and preserves clients’ rights based on the ethical standards of the nursing profession
