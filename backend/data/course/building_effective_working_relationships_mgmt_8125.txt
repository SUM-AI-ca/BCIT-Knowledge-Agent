## Building Effective Working Relationships MGMT 8125
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates effective relationships skills, such as treat people with respect, display a personable and professional style of interacting, and manage conflict in the workplace.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 80% in MGMT 8010
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Build Relationships
- Treat people fairly and with respect, avoiding favoritism, explaining decisions, and providing opportunities.
- Display personal integrity, credibility and sensitivity.
- Display a Personable and Professional Style of Interacting
- Display sociable, approachable, accessible and non-defensive behavior.
- Display tact and sensitivity to personalities and circumstances and avoid criticizing.
- Manage Conflict
- Select among various strategies to manage conflict.
- Deal with conflict collaboratively and seek 'win-win' solutions.
- Minimize recurring conflicts.
