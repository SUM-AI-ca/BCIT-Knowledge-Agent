## Aircraft Maintenance Practices AVAM 4104
Aircraft Maintenance Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed to advance your knowledge and skills through practical experience. You will have an opportunity to perform troubleshooting and maintenance procedures on engines and aircraft. You will become familiar with the procedures for configuring aircraft for floats and skis, and the impact of human factors on aircraft maintenance.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
8.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the learner will be able to:
- Discuss the operation of an AMO.
- Perform service and maintenance procedures on aircraft.
- Perform integrated theory and practical projects on aircraft.
- Discuss human factors in aircraft maintenance.
