## Transfer Safety for Service Personnel from Launch Boat to Vessel Deck MEDI 0105
Marine Emergency Duties Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed to provide an understanding to service personnel to safely board/disembark vessels they are servicing, including boarding techniques, safety equipment, and emergency response.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Physical fitness/Clearance certificate
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the use of safety equipment such as life jackets, helmets, shoes, and working suits.
- Understand survival methods in the water.
- Understand safe boarding and disembarking from transfer launch.
- Understand response in emergencies such as MOB and abandon ship.
- Understand the use of SART, EPIRB, PLB, life buoys, MOB, and personal survival training.​
