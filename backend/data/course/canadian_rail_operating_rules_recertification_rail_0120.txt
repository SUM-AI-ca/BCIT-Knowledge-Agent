## Canadian Rail Operating Rules Recertification RAIL 0120
Railway Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for those working in the railway industry (conductors, locomotive engineers, yard supervisors). The course will provide railroaders a review of the Canadian Rail Operating Rules (CROR) that governs federally regulated railway operations in Canada and a review of the Transportation of Dangerous Goods Act and Regulations (focusing on their impact on Canadian railways).
### Prerequisite(s)
- Previous CROR card (TDG included)
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Review and explain Canadian Railway Operating Rules (including signals, OCS, CTC and Interlocking)
- Review and explain Transportation of Dangerous Goods Act and Regulations
