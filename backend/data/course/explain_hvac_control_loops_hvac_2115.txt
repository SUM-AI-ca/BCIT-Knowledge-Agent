## Explain HVAC Control Loops HVAC 2115
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
A comprehensive knowledge of electrical control loops used in the Air Conditioning industry is essential for the HVAC Technician. System control changes occur on a regular basis from year to year and this course will help you to stay up-to-date with the newest control loops in use in the HVAC industry.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HVAC 2114
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain basic operation of Series 90 control loops as they apply to damper modulation and control
- Explain basic pneumatic control systems as they apply to damper control
- Explain terminology and operational sequence of low voltage controls in:
- Roof top AC
- Residential
- Commercial Central Systems
- Troubleshoot electrical control loops
Effective as of Fall 2011
