## Imaging Technologies 2 GTEC 2464
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This intermediate course in small litho press operation stresses press maintenance, problem solving and troubleshooting techniques. Students’ skills are improved by printing more complex projects, projects consisting of more than one piece, multi-coloured projects with proper registration and working in teams to produce a program newsletter. Students prepare specific ink colours and gain confidence by printing projects of their own creation and preparation from other classes. Classes are modular, self-paced, competency-based exercises in all four imaging technologies. Hands-on production skills are stressed in inkjet and digital imaging engines and flexographic press technology. Press simulators are introduced to build real world production values.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Print multi-coloured jobs with proper registration (fit and register).
- Cut suitable stock in an efficient manner for the best cut.
- Measure size needed; cut stock maximizing yield from sheet.
- Describe the makeup of paper and ink.
- Use standard troubleshooting techniques with ink, plates, paper and blankets.
- Maintain quality control (ink coverage, paper path, cleanliness).
- Clean and preserve a press and printing plates.
- Use standard press densitometry measurement techniques.
- Use an inkjet printer to calibrate a new substrate in a RIP.
- Load and unload substrates on a flexo web press.
- Mount flexo plate cylinders and print single colour web work.
- Use a digital imaging engine to duplex image both sides of a substrate.
- Troubleshoot media changes in a digital imaging RIP.
