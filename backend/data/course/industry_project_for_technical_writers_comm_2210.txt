## Industry Project for Technical Writers COMM 2210
Communication Course
## Course Overview
This course partners students with industry sponsored projects, under teacher supervision, to provide students with a portfolio piece, real-world experience, and with industry contacts and references.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in XCOM 1007 and 70% in XCOM 1008 and 70% in COMM 1009 and 70% in XCOM 1110 and 70% in COMM 1260 and 70% in XCOM 2040 and 70% in COMM 2206 and 70% in COMM 2207 † and (70% in XCOM 1000 or 70% in XCOM 1005 or 70% in XCOM 3110 or 70% in XCOM 3840 ) ( † may be taken concurrently)
- And one eligible 3-credit COMP or MDIA elective.
