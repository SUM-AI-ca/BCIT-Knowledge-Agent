## Freelance Producing: Developing Program Concepts XBST 1013
Broadcast and Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Freelance Producing will appeal to individuals who want to develop and share their own content for live or live to tape broadcasts and businesses looking for an online presence to promote and distribute company information, branding, and community outreach. ​This course explores concepts that lend themselves to a variety of broadcast and digital audiences. Create the content to reach your target followers through the use of social media platforms.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Successfully create content for target audiences
- Share and identify the many types of content distributed on social media platforms
- Build a target audience through social media channels and connections in the industry
