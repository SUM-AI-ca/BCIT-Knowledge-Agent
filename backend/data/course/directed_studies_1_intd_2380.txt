## Directed Studies 1 INTD 2380
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
Students will complete a total package for a small design project.
### Prerequisite(s)
- All 1000 and 2000 series courses with a minimum of 65% in each course (except COMM 1120 , INTD 1360 , INTD 2335 , and INTD 2340 ). INTD 2330 may be taken concurrently otherwise must be completed before INTD 2380 .
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course students will do a design project in which they.
- Produce drawings which show furniture plans, elevations, and sections from different perspectives.
- Select furnishings, including colour and materials to be used.
- Produce five millwork drawings using AutoCAD to illustrate aspects of the underlying concepts of their projects.
- Produce a presentation of their completed project using their multi-media skills, employing PowerPoint, Photoshop, and Word.
