## Laboratory Quality Assurance CHSC 7826
Chemical Sciences Course
## Course Overview
Basic theory and practice of quality assurance in the analytical lab. Topics: basic descriptive statistics, control charts, quality terminology, documentation, accreditation and registration for ISO 17025. Ideally suited to analytical and environmental chemists, analysts, technicians, lab supervisors, project managers and other responsible for submitting samples to analytical labs.
### Prerequisite(s)
- A recognized diploma of technology or degree (must be in an engineering or science discipline) or mature student with extensive and relevant work history.
