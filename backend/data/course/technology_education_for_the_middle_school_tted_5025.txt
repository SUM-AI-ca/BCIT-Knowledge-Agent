## Technology Education for the Middle School TTED 5025
Technology Teacher Education Course
## Course Overview
This course focuses on content and possible teaching methods used to deliver a broad based technology education curriculum specifically addressing the learning outcomes of the grades 8-10 Tech Ed Instructional Resource Package (IRP). A major emphasis will be placed on classroom tested teaching techniques. Hands on activities will include building sample projects as well as jigs and fixtures.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
