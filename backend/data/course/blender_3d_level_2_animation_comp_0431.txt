## Blender 3D, Level 2 - Animation COMP 0431
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
A follow-up to COMP 0413 , this hands-on course introduces students to 3-D modeling and animation. Students who have taken Blender 3D, Level 1, learn to add physical properties like wind, water and gravity to the models that they created in the first course. This material is designed to be fun and these two courses work together to provide a insight into 3-D modeling and animation. Topics include: partial generation, soft bodies and fluid simulation. Recommended for aspiring software and web developers.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 0413

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Animate the models created in level 1.
- Apply wind, water and gravity to a scene.
- Apply physical properties to models in a scene.
- Render a scene to different web ready formats.
