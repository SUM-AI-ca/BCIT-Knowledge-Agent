## Welding HMTF 1004
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to welding safety, identification of metals, and procedures for oxy-acetylene welding and shielded metal arc welding (SMAW)
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
3.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use welding safety practices
- Perform oxy-acetylene welding and cutting procedures
- Perform shielded metal arc welding procedures
Effective as of Fall 2014
