## Trigonometry for Renewable Resources MATH 1044
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the theory and application of trigonometric functions; right angle trigonometry; oblique triangles and vectors.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Math 12 Pre-Calculus or equivalent OR BCIT MATH 0001 .
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - The Trigonometric Functions
- Define an angle.
- Define the trigonometric functions.
- Apply the principles of right angle trigonometry to practical problems.
## Module 2 - Trigonometric Functions of Any Angle
- Calculate the trigonometric functions of any triangle.
- Calculate radian measure.
- Apply the principles of radian measure to practical problems.
## Module 3 - Vectors and Oblique Triangles
- Define vectors in a plane.
- Apply the principles of vector addition to the solution of right angle and non-right angle triangles.
- Apply the laws of sine to the solution of oblique triangles.
- Apply the laws of cosine to the solution of oblique triangles.
- Calculate the area of oblique triangles.
- Calculate the area of right angle triangles.
