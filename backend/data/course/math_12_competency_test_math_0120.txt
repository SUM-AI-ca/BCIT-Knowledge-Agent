## Math 12 Competency Test MATH 0120
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
This test allows students to demonstrate the required mathematics knowledge and skills at the grade 12 level. Topics include basic algebra, geometry, trigonometry, and problem solving. For more information on the test, visit www.bcit.ca/cas/math/mathcompetencytesting.shtml . The Math 0120 fee is non-refundable.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
### Registration requirements
Departmental approval is required to register for this course. You must have approval from the department to register. Students accepted into a program can register directly.
### Domestic fees
$135.00
International fees are typically 3.4 times the domestic tuition. Exact cost will be calculated upon completion of registration.
