## Manufacturing Information Systems OPMT 7026
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
This course reviews various computer applications used in a manufacturing enterprise and the integration of these systems. Students will design information flow, so that appropriate data is delivered to the correct responsibility level, use information systems to gather data on cost, quality and delivery to schedule, derive from the information system economic justification for improvements.
