## Field Safety in Bear County RENR 0500
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
Field Safety in Bear Country builds on the basic safety concepts introduced in the Safety in Bear Country (BEAR10) fundamentals course. Participants will revisit standard strategies utilized to prevent human-bear conflicts as well as procedures for handling both bear encounters and attacks. Participants will also become familiar with Safe Operating Procedure (SOP) policies. The advanced training offers the theoretical and practical field components related to the safe use of bear spray and other deterrents for individuals who work or recreate in bear habitat. Introduces potential bear hazards, bear behavior, methods to avoid bear encounters, and procedures and equipment to employ for self defense if confronted with a bear.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Utilize proper avoidance strategies to prevent human-bear conflicts.
- Execute appropriate safety procedures when dealing with bear encounters and bear attacks.
- Explain safety protocols as outlined in Safe Operating Procedure (SOP) policies.
- Identify key health and safety factors when using both inert bear spray and active bear spray as stated in manufacturer's MSDS documents.
- Demonstrate the safe and proper use of bear spray as a wildlife deterrent.
