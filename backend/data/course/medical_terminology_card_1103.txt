## Medical Terminology CARD 1103
Cardiology/Cardiovascular Course
## Course Overview
​Aimed to familiarize the students with medical terminology commonly encountered in cardiology and other disciplines. Designed specifically for allied health professionals, the text/workbook introduces learners to Latin and Greek roots, prefixes, and suffixes. The online Evolve platform supplements the learning experience with quizzes, case studies, and an interactive pronunciation guide. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the meaning of prefixes, suffixes, and roots, and use them to form medical terms
- Use and interpret terminology specific to cardiology and other health disciplines
- Identify anatomical planes, regions and organs within them
- Name common cardiac procedures based on their abbreviations
- Accurately interpret cardiology patient records
- Identify medical specialists and common procedures within their practice​
Effective as of Fall 2025
