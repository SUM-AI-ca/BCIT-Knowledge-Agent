## Introduction to Solar PV Systems for Renewable Energy Projects XZEB 0052
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
​This introductory course focuses on exploring careers and trades in energy efficient construction through a community-based solar photovoltaic (PV) project. Learners will engage in hands-on activities around grid-connect solar PV technology and systems to understand the process of designing and implementing integrated solar PV projects. The course is designed to be delivered in community in tandem with a community solar PV project for a greater understanding of the trades and career pathways in renewable energy technologies.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the role of solar PV in clean energy projects and building operations
- Identify and describe their purpose of basic solar system components of a grid connected solar system under net-Metering, including:
- PV modules and arrays
- Inverter(s)
- Racking
- Cabling
- Communications and monitoring equipment
- Explain best practices for site preparation of a solar installation
- Describe the characteristics and operation of PV panels
- Apply solar PV safety practices in supervised installation tasks, including:
- Building and installing solar PV interconnection cables
- Measuring site solar access and shading
- Assembling solar PV racking systems
- Mounting and interconnecting solar PV modules
- Connecting solar PV modules to inverters and testing grid-connected operation
- Monitoring and confirming live solar PV system electrical performance
