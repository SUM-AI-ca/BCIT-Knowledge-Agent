## Business and Technical Communication 1 for Industry COMM 0103
Communication Course
## Course Overview
COMM 0103 is offered to industry professionals in their workplace. COMM 0103 is an applied communication course that teaches practical techniques for planning, organizing, selecting, and presenting information in business or industry. It covers routine memos, letters, request and reply letters and emails, oral presentations, and short reports. Extra language support is provided to address the needs of English as an additional language students.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Follow a systematic process in planning and writing business and technical documents.
- Select and organize information to meet the needs of their readers.
- Write clear, concise, easily readable and correct sentences.
- Use a positive, reader-oriented tone in written and oral communication.
- Make written information more accessible through appropriate headings, stacked lists and white space.
- Use the most effective strategies and patterns in organizing routine memos, letters and short reports.
- Prepare and deliver an effective oral presentation on a work-related topic.
