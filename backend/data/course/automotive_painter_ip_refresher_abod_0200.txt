## Automotive Painter IP Refresher ABOD 0200
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course will provide a theoretical review of the trade in preparation for the Automotive Painter Inter-Provincial (IP) examination. Topics include: safety in the workplace, tools and equipment, documentation, use of repair and refinishing materials. Contact the Industry Training Authority (tel. 778-328-8700 or toll free within BC 1-866-660-6011) to determine eligibility and to schedule an examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Experience in the automotive painter trade.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe safety related functions
- Identify the maintenance of tools and equipment
- Describe the use of documentation
- Describe the steps of planning work
- Identify the steps of preparing surfaces
- Describe the use of repair materials
- Describe the use of equipment
- Describe how to use refinishing materials
- Describe pre-delivery tasks
