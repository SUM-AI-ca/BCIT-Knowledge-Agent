## Turbine Engine Testing and Operation AVGT 3011
Gas Turbine Course
## Course Overview
​Students will participate in the operation of a gas turbine engine and be able to describe safety procedures and reasons to test engines. Students will also have the opportunity to tour a local industry jet engine testing facility.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in AVGT 2004
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Carry out engine ground-run
- Describe the safety procedures to operate an engine
- Describe reasons engines require testing​
