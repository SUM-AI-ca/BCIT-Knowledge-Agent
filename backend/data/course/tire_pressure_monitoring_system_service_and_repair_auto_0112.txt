## Tire Pressure Monitoring System Service and Repair AUTO 0112
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Provides skills to service and repair tire pressure monitoring systems (TPMS). Covers tire mount / dismount, sensor service, system calibration, TPMS service information, and system diagnosis / repair using the latest diagnostic equipment and service information.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain the operation of different types of TPMS systems.
- Mount and dismount tires equipped with TPMS sensors.
- Perform system calibration on North American, Asian, and European manufactured vehicles.
- Diagnose and repair TPMS systems.
