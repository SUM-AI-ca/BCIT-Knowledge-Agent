## Pavement Construction Part 1: Construction Methods TSYH 4440
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the site organization, equipment and methods utilized in the placement of Asphaltic Concrete Pavement, Portland Cement Concrete Pavement, and Asphalt Surface Treatments. A prior knowledge of the basic properties of concrete and asphalt is required.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
