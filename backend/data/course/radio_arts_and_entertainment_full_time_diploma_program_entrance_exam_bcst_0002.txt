## Radio Arts & Entertainment Full-time Diploma Program Entrance Exam BCST 0002
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Radio Arts and Entertainment full-time diploma program applicants must successfully complete this exam with a minimum score of 50% in order to be eligible to apply to the program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
This examination assesses the following program-related criteria:
- English competency/writing skills
- General knowledge
- Geography
- Current events
- Broadcast Industry awareness and aptitude
