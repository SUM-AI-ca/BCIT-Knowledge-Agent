## Technical Issues in Software Development Part 2 COMP 4081
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This course and Comp 3081 provide students who have not completed the BCIT CST / CIT diploma program the technical knowledge to complete Comp 7081 through extended and expanded coverage of critical technical methods for software development. The course is oriented towards the industry practitioner and covers both structured and object-oriented methodology. Comp 3081 and Comp 4081 are designed to provide students with additional time and resources to explore additional topics in preparation specifically for the topics normally covered in Comp 7081, and to establish essential connections between software development techniques normally practiced in North America and other countries. Comp 3081 focuses on software modelling, and Comp 4081 focuses on software quality and new trends in software engineering.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 3081

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to critically evaluate and apply:
- Software quality methods.
- Testing methods for different types of software.
- Software metrics.
- New developments of software engineering principles.
