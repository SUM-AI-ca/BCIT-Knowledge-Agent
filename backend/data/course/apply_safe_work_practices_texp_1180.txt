## Apply Safe Work Practices TEXP 1180
Trades Exploration Program Course
## Course Overview
This course provides an overview of WorkSafeBC and how Workers Compensation Regulations apply to industry. An in-depth look into safety culture in the workplace; focusing on safety precautions and procedures.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Apply safe work practices
- Describe the causes and results of accidents
- Describe role of the Workers Compensation Board
- Describe the workplace responsibilities supervisors, and workers
- Identify the Occupational Health and Safety regulations that apply to shop situations
- Obtain WHMIS Certification
- Use safety precautions and procedures relating to personal apparel and general housekeeping
- Describe emergency equipment and facilities
- Describe how workplace safety policy is established
- Describe lock out and tag out procedures and shut off systems
- Describe electrical safety
- Describe safety in confined spaces
- Describe use of barriers and safety devices
- Use shop safety equipment
- Use proper lifting techniques for trades specific materials
- Describe how fires start (fire triangle)
- Identify the equipment used to extinguish small fires
- Describe proper methods for preventing fires
- Recognize safe use of ladders and scaffolds
- Define step ladder safety inspection
- Explain WCB minimum requirements for a fall protection work plan
- State fall protection requirements
- Identify components of a personal fall arrest system
Effective as of Fall 2016
