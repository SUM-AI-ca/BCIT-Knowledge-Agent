## On-Air Performance BCST 1128
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
​Announcing techniques, news reading skills and basic concepts of effective voice usage are introduced in this course. You will explore articulation, pronunciation, inflection, and breathing techniques to help you develop your voice to its maximum potential. During the course daily practice is required and coaching will be provided both individually and in groups.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate proper vocal technique for professional news broadcasting.
- Distinguish the major elements that contribute to successful news anchoring and reporting.
- Analyze and apply constructive criticism to help improve professional performance.
- Read television newscasts effectively using a teleprompter.
- Perform the following broadcasting functions to professional standards:
- Radio news and sportscasts.
- Television reporting stand-ups.
- Television newscasts.​
