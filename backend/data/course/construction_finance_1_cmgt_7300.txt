## Construction Finance 1 CMGT 7300
Construction Management - CMGT Course
## Course Overview
The challenges of determining if a project is staying within budget are critical in today's fiercely competitive environment. This course illustrates the transition of the project estimate for bidding purposes to the cost control system required to ensure profitability. It is designed for those who want to develop methods for monitoring and controlling costs of construction projects while emphasizing the importance of a proactive approach to corrective actions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CMGT 7160

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Implement an effective, cost control system into the daily management of projects.
- Prepare budgets/estimates that are conducive to proper job cost control.
- Develop a cost control system using cost codes.
- Utilize cost baselines to aid in updating and projecting cash flow and balances.
- Identify methods for measuring work progress, i.e., units completed, incremental milestone, lost ratio.
- Understand the pricing sensitivity of construction work.
- Understand the difference between mark up and mark down.
- Understand the key elements of cash flow.
