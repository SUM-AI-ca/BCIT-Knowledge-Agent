## Music & Media Business BCST 2214
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course explores the business aspects of the music industry and its relationship to broadcasting and media. BCST 2214 looks at fee and royalty structures commonly found in the music industry. Students will analyze past patterns in popular music to identify and predict business practices in the media and music industries. The course also examines music as a culture-industry and its historical impact on youth culture.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Discuss the role of performing rights organizations.
- Describe how recording artists, writers and publishers are compensated, including changing royalty structures as influenced by the Internet.
- Compare and contrast performing rights and reproduction rights.
- Differentiate between the roles of a booking agent and concert promoter.
- Evaluate the strengths and weaknesses associated with different recording agreements.
- Discuss historical patterns of the role of radio and convergent media in popular music and youth culture.
- Identify several models of artist management.
- Discuss the role of American radio formats in the development of Canadian broadcasting.
