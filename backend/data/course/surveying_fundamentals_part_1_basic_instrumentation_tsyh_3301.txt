## Surveying Fundamentals Part 1: Basic Instrumentation TSYH 3301
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
A fundamental course in surveying which introduces commonly used survey equipment. After completion, students will be able to identify and describe the equipment, and its applications in the field.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Basic Principles of Surveying
- State and define the term 'surveying' and describe its relevance to land development, engineering and construction.
- Identify the types of surveying typically employed in the industry.
- List the basic categories of survey measurement
- State and discuss kinds of errors, precision and accuracy as related to surveying.
## Module 2 - Basic Surveying - Mathematics and Geometry
- Determine the trigonometric functions of an angle, using function tables or a scientific calculator.
- Calculate the lengths of the sides of a triangle, using available angles and distances.
## Module 3 - Distance Measurement - Equipment and Field Procedures
- List and describe five types of distance measurements and the types of equipment used for each.
- Describe the general principles of electronic distance measurement.
## Module 4 - Elevation Measurement - Equipment and Procedures
- Define the terms used in levelling.
- Describe the differential levelling procedure.
- Identify the equipment used in levelling and elevation measurement.
- Describe the use of different hand levelling instruments.
- Identify and 'read' different levelling rod faces.
## Module 5 - Angle Measurement - Equipment and Procedures
- Add, subtract, multiply and divide angles expressed in degrees, minutes and seconds, as well as degree and decimals of a degree
- State and define magnetic declination, compass bearings, and read compass bearings given the compass face view.
- Discuss the field measurement of vertical and horizontal angles.
- Calculate horizontal distances based on vertical angle and slope distance.
- Calculate elevation differences based on vertical angle and slope or horizontal distance.
- Read or determine measured angles as displayed by a theodolite.
## Module 6 - Global Positioning Systems
- Describe the history of the global positioning system (GPS).
- Describe the basic operation of the global positioning system including use of satellites, system geometry and accuracy, GPS user equipment and components, and what is required in the initial unit setup.
