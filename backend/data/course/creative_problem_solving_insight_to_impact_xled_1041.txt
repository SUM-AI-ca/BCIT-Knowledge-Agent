## Creative Problem Solving: Insight to Impact XLED 1041
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​Creative problem solving is a critical skill for navigating complex challenges in today's dynamic workplace. This course introduces participants to problem-solving methodologies that help identify root causes, generate innovative ideas, and implement effective solutions. Through interactive exercises and real-world applications, learners will practice structured approaches such as brainstorming, evaluation techniques, and decision-making frameworks to enhance their ability to solve problems creatively and collaboratively.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the principles and stages of the creative problem-solving process.
- Differentiate between problems and symptoms.
- Analyze underlying causes of problems using structured tools.
- Generate innovative ideas for solutions using brainstorming and other creative techniques.
- Evaluate potential solutions against established criteria to select the most effective option.
- Apply problem-solving methodology to real-world scenarios for effective implementation.​
