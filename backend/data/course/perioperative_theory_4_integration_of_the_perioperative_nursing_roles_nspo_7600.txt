## Perioperative Theory 4 Integration of the Perioperative Nursing Roles NSPO 7600
Nursing Specialty Perioperative Course
## Course Overview
Expands knowledge from the previous courses by examining perioperative nursing care for individuals with increasing acuity, who are undergoing complex surgical procedures and/or anesthesia, and recovering from anesthesia. Focuses on integration of the perioperative roles, recognition and appropriate response to threatening and rapidly changing conditions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- S in NSPO 7500
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Learners will have opportunities to use reflective critical thinking, systematic inquiry, communication, collaboration and professionalism to:
- Integrate knowledge necessary to provide care for high acuity perioperative patients undergoing and recovering from complex anesthetic and surgical interventions.
- Critically analyze how patients, family members and perioperative nurses perceive and influence the context of perioperative experiences and the perioperative environment.
- Challenge aspects of perioperative nursing practice related to collaborative approaches to caring for perioperative patients.
- Integrate personal knowledge, professional literature, practice frameworks, research and experiential knowledge to further develop competence and confidence in perioperative nursing practice.
- Develop a personal vision of professionalism as a perioperative nurse.
