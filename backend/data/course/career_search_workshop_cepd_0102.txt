## Career Search Workshop CEPD 0102
Career Exploration Course
## Course Overview
Explore your career options! Complete a series of standardized tests and research occupations, current job market resources, and educational options to make better informed career decisions and plans. This twelve-hour course is conducted by a professional counsellor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Students will discover their interests and occupational preferences via a process of self-assessment. Students will learn methods of applying their preferences, and conducting research into, occupational options.
