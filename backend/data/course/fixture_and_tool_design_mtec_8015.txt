## Fixture and Tool Design MTEC 8015
Manufacturing Course
## Course Overview
Furthers knowledge of tooling methods used to increase manufacturing productivity. Covers modern methods of prototype manufacture and the use of currently available techniques to reduce manufacturing cycle times.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MTEC 7045 and 50% in MTEC 8012
