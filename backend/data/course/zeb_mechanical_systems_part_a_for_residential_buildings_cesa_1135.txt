## ZEB Mechanical Systems: Part A (for Residential Buildings) CESA 1135
Centre for Energy Systems Applications Course
## Course Overview
​This course focuses on the knowledge and skills needed by general contractors and HVAC and plumbing contractors working on residential buildings, where contractors play a large role in determining design details and construction approaches. Through interactive learning and hands-on activities, students will become familiar with high performance ventilation, space heating, cooling, and domestic hot water principles and solutions that will play an important role in the BC Energy Step Code. Course focus is on the skills and understanding needed by the contractor or tradesperson related to achieve best practices and ensure overall project energy performance.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the value of various high performance heating, ventilation and cooling strategies (passive and active) and interactions between systems in air-tight buildings.
- Compare heat generation (including domestic hot water) and distribution systems (life cycle costs, performance, space-saving strategies, installation requirements, commissioning, maintenance, etc.)
- Explain best practices and challenges with equipment sizing calculation methodologies
- Explain ventilation requirements, issues, best practice strategies and concept
- Compare common air distribution systems (location, installation paths, performance, air sealing, access for maintenance)
- Explain ductwork design best practices (sizing, fittings, air sealing, fir and smoke dampers, acoustics and vibration)
- Predict construction challenges associated with various HVAC+R systems (e.g. frost protection, maintenance access, fire safety).
- Demonstrate installation of ductwork, pipes, electrical conduit commonly required for mechanical systems without compromising integrity of building enclosure for thermal performance or airtightness.
- Demonstrate construction coordination and checklist best practices specific to mechanical systems.
