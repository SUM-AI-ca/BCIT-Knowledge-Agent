## Recreational GPS SURV 0129
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
GPS treasure hunting - Geocaching, Degree Confluence Project, and Letterboxing. Covers recreational GPS, hardware interfacing, commercial and shareware mapping packages and their integration into your personal navigation tool set. Incorporating a personal GPS to your hiking, fishing, orienteering or urban exploring will add a new level of adventure and security.

- No prerequisites are required for this course.
