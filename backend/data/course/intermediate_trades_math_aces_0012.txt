## Intermediate Trades Math ACES 0012
Access to Trades Course
## Course Overview
Covers ratio, proportion, percent, precision measurement, signed numbers, roots and exponents. Delivery allows learners to participate from home, work or school. Internet or print access to instructor solutions and direction is available. A personal tutor is assigned to each learner.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:
- Work with ratio, proportion and percent.
- Work with signed numbers.
- Work with exponents and roots.
- Use algebra to solve problems with formulas.
- Solve trade-related word problems involving these skills.
