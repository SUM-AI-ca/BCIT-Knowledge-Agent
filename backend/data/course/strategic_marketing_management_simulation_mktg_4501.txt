## Strategic Marketing Management Simulation MKTG 4501
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
Provides students with a multi-dimensional learning environment through lectures, reading, discussions and simulation exercises. The role and importance of planning and information for marketing decision-making is the underlying precept. Simulations are intended for application of knowledge and practical experience. This course demands that students draw heavily from marketing expertise gained in previous courses.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MKTG 4340 and 50% in MKTG 2203
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course you should be able to:
- Explain the marketing decision-making process.
- Examine selective techniques, which aid in marketing decision-making.
- Integrate theoretical concepts with practical marketing problems.
- Explain group decision-making dynamics.
- Apply the marketing concepts learned in other courses in a competitive business environment.
- Explain the impact of strategy and tactics as they apply to corporate resources.
