## Inspectors' Core Education I Workshop FSCT 0077
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
This course is for new or potential inspectors who have been designated to conduct inspections of inquiries/complaints filed with the professional colleges in British Columbia. Participants will be provided with the beginning knowledge and skills required to investigate inquiries/complaints in accordance with appropriate laws and policies under the Health Professions Act of BC. A theoretical core body of knowledge is provided followed by guidance into the investigation process in order to provide the participants with beginning skills needed to become an inspector.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the current legislation applicable to the investigation process.
- Describe the policies and procedures of Colleges applicable to the investigation process.
- Define the steps in the complaint/inquiry process.
- Outline the preparation for an investigation by defining the investigative tools pertinent to inspectors' role.
- Detail the information gathered during an investigation.
- Outline the basics of writing a final report with analysis and conclusions of an investigation
