## CNC Operator Level 3 MACH 1132
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
Focuses on advanced G-code programming. Includes production milling and turning. Study manufacturing process design, job estimate and cost analysis. Canned cycles and macro programming, production fixture design and tooling. Practical projects on CNC lathe and milling machine. Introduce multiaxis machining and Computer Aided Machining (CAM) software.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MACH 1130 and 50% in MACH 1131
