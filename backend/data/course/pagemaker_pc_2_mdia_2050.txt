## PageMaker PC 2 MDIA 2050
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Learn how to create documents in readiness for sending files to a service bureau thereby reducing pre-press costs. Students will learn how to create colour separation files.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MDIA 1050
