## Roadworks Maintenance 2 PUBW 2121
Public Works Course
## Course Overview
​This is a continuing course in municipal roadworks maintenance. Topics to be covered include utility location and adjustments for reconstruction and pavement widenings, master municipal specifications, road rehabilitation techniques, trenchless technology, drainage and spill containment, sidewalks, curbs and traffic control devices.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in PUBW 1121
