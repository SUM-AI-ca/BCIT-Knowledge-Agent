## Automatic Sprinkler Part 1 MECH 4081
Mechanical Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This course will introduce many of the basic considerations in designing a sprinkler system. This will range from an introduction to sprinkler systems, both wet and dry systems, valving, piping and tubing, and alarm systems. It ends with the message that record-keeping on paperwork is very important in the maintenance and operation of a system.
