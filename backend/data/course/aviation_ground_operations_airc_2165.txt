## Aviation Ground Operations AIRC 2165
Airport/Airline Core Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides students with a basic understanding of aviation ground operations through four modules of learning. Students will learn about ramp safety and security procedures, aircraft ground deicing and anti-icing procedures, dangerous goods regulations, and air cargo regulations. Evaluations include quizzes, discussions and a case study.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the basic safety and security procedures associated with ramp activities.
- Describe the proper procedures for aircraft ground de-icing and anti-icing.
- Identify types of dangerous goods in an aviation environment.
- Describe safe handling procedures for all types of dangerous goods.
- Identify different types of cargo and how to process these in compliance with regulations.
- Describe the proper procedures for accepting and tendering cargo.
- Analyze and recommend solutions to industry problems related to ground handling.​
