## Journalism Practicum 2 BCST 4435
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
In this course students apply learned skills in a relevant external fieldwork environment (industry). They spend four weeks working under the direction of a workplace mentor at a media related organization, where they may actively participate in the various aspects of day-to-day operations or specialize in specific areas of interest, related to the program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Successful completion of all other Level 1, Level 2, Level 3 and Level 4 courses or Program Head's approval.
### Credits
7.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this practicum with an approved media organization, the student will be able to:
- Actively participate in a media company’s day-to-day operation
- Solve common problems and respond to day-to-day challenges working both independently and in collaboration with others.
- Communicate effectively as a team member.
- Take direction from supervisors and show initiative in the workplace.
- Apply technical and soft skills learned in the program to the workplace environment.
- Use technology effectively to assist in the production and delivery of content across various platforms.
- Complete assigned tasks in a timely fashion while conforming to relevant policy and regulations.
Effective as of Winter 2025
