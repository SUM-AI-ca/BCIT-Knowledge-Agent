## Applied Artificial Intelligence 1 COMP 3970
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Covers the Common LISP language and its CLOS object-oriented extension. This course prepares courses ( COMP 3900 , 4900, and 4975) by exploring several AI application paradigms.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of first year or permission from the instructor.
