## Blueprint Reading for Carpentry CARP 0110
Carpentry Course
## Course Overview
Introduces students to development of drawings, visualization and gradual understanding of orthographic and isometric drawings as applied to the construction industry. Architectural, structural and mechanical drawing will be covered with a focus on cross-referencing and understanding the application of specification language.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
