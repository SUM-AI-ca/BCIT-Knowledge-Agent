## Portfolio Production MDIA 3480
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course emphasizes the importance of team work. Students will apply the work flow techniques and will organize their work and get their assets together before making their portfolio presentations to the class and to the industry. Students will be encouraged to problem solve while receiving and taking critique of their work. Based on feedback given by classmates and industry professionals students will revise their portfolio. Students will then present their projects to faculty and industry for critiques and feedback. In the final 4 weeks of Portfolio Production the students will discuss their final presentation for their industry night.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all term 1, 2 and 3 courses

3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Work effectively in a team.
- Use the basic work flow techniques to work quickly and efficiently.
- Modify/revise final portfolio demo reel based on critiques from clients.
- Apply project management for project development and production.
- Prepare and present a final portfolio for industry review.
- Gather equipment and resources for their final presentation.
- Create final portfolio for presentation.
