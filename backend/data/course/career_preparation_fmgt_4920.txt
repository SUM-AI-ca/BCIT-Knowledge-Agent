## Career Preparation FMGT 4920
Financial Management Course
## Course Overview
Through a series of guest lectures, presentations, research, and informational interviews, students will gain an understanding of the variety of careers in the accounting profession and will improve their written and verbal communication skills. Topics may include: networking in a professional setting, interview skills, resume preparation and career skills in the accounting profession.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Third level of the Accounting Diploma.
### Credits
4.0
### Domestic fees
$677.53
International fees are typically 3.4 times the domestic tuition. Exact cost will be calculated upon completion of registration.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Gain a better understanding of the career options in their chosen field of study,
- Conduct informational interviews,
- Use social media to create a professional presence,
- Prepare a professional resume which markets themselves to targeted employers,
- Perform online and in person career research.
- Successfully network with professionals in a formal setting.
Effective as of Winter 2015
