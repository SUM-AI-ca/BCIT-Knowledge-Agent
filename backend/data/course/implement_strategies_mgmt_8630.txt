## Implement Strategies MGMT 8630
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates skills in defining stategic goals, demonstrating goals and then summarizing improvements or changes to resources (human, plant, technology, and equipment) in order to carry out goals.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Evaluate the strategic vision, mission and objectives
- Analyze your organization's strategy
- Create strategic goals and objectives
- Develop a strategy for your department
- Implement Strategic Decisions
- Assess current resources (including technology, plant and equipment).
- Link strategic goals to operating goals
- Understand how your organization goes about implementing their strategic plans.
- Create a plan to match resources with needs and implement your strategies
- Evaluate Strategic Plans
- Understand how your organization evaluates their strategic plans.
- Create an plan to effectively evaluate strategic plans.
