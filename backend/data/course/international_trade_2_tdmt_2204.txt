## International Trade 2 TDMT 2204
Transportation and Distribution Course
## Course Overview
Focuses on the importance of the customer. As a continuation of International Trade 1 ( TDMT 1104 ), the student will be exposed to the comprehensive planning necessary for international markets. Topics include ownership, marketing, transportation, technology transfer and intercompany linkage strategies.
