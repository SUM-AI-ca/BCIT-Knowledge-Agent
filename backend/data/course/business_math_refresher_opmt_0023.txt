## Business Math Refresher OPMT 0023
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed to review and strengthen fundamentals of mathematics in preparation for business math courses at the diploma leve for 1st semester diploma students.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Be comfortable with the basics of algebra, geometry, polynomial, and logistic functions.
- Apply basic methodology for solving applied mathematical problems.
- Understand concepts found in business mathematics courses.
