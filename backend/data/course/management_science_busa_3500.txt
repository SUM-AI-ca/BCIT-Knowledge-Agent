## Management Science BUSA 3500
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
Introduces quantitative methods. Students will be introduced to some of the more common techniques in the Management Science field and will use microcomputer software to solve problems. Emphasis is placed on the formulation of problems and the interpretation of calculated results.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in OPMT 1110 and 50% in OPMT 1130
