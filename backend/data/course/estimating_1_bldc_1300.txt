## Estimating 1 BLDC 1300
Building Construction Course
## Course Overview
​​Introduction to measurement of construction work (CIQS Standard Method of Measurement), with a brief review of reading construction drawings and specifications. Specific study of methods of measurement techniques applicable to site work (excavation & backfill ), concrete, formwork, sundries & finishes, rebar, and masonry quantity take-offs (MasterFormat Divisions 31, 3 and 4).​

- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Read and interpret information on construction drawings to:
- Prepare estimate work items checklists in accordance with industry MasterFormat Divisions.
- Prepare appropriate preliminary calculations for an estimate.
- Apply principles and techniques of measuring work items on standard quantity sheet forms.
- Extend and summarize work item final quantities in preparation for pricing.
- Comprehend the roles of estimates and estimators in the building development and construction process.
Effective as of Fall 2003
