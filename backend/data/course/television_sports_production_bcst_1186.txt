## Television Sports Production BCST 1186
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course teaches basic production techniques of covering sporting events for television and how to apply these through practical work in television studios, control rooms and mobile production trucks. It is designed to introduce students seeking a career in television sports broadcasting and video production to the attractive and diverse career opportunities available (including those looking to work during the Olympics), and to provide a solid practical basis for further study.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be:
- Able to understand the process of producing a television sports production, including camera operator, graphic operator, VTR playback, audio operator, director, and producer.
- Be capable of assuming entry-level assistant work for a Remote Production such as the Olympics, Canucks, BC Lions, Vancouver Giants, Vancouver Whitecaps, and other local live events.
