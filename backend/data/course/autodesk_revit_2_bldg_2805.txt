## Autodesk Revit 2 BLDG 2805
Building Course
## Course Overview
Continues from BLDG 2800 , this course covers the advanced concepts of Autodesk Revit in an architectural environment. Gives students greater familiarity with the breadth of Revit Architecture's feature set.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BLDG 2800
### Credits
3.0
### Domestic fees

International fees are typically 3.4 times the domestic tuition. Exact cost will be calculated upon completion of registration.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Allows students to design and develop their own 3D and 2D objects (families) from the provided templates.
- Incorporates functions such as error trapping, and nesting.
- Teaches students to customize common object types such as railings and stairs.
- The course will give students the essential methods of administrating Revit in an architectural office environment.
- Delivery will concentrate on real world approaches and methodology rather than step by step procedures.
Effective as of Fall 2009
