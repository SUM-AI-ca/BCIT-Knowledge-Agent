## Term 2 Job Placement GTEC 2400
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
Employed at a commercial business, the student will be required to work as assigned in one or more departments such as: office work for creating a job docket and estimating; assembling and pre-flighting files in prepress; pressroom for loading plates; and wash-up and post-production for folding and stitching processes. Students will be placed in diverse business environments that span commercial applications of all imaging technologies covered in the first year of study. All students are required to make a 10-15 minute presentation to their cohort at the end of their experience in order to expand all student knowledge of the diversity of the Graphic Communications Industry in our community.
### Prerequisite(s)
- All term 1 and 2 courses
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Examine and interpret internal job docket instructions to produce the final job.
- Be aware of all the steps involved to produce the final client job.
- Adhere to employment standards and workplace expectations.
- Complete a final check to ensure the job is produced to correct specifications.
- Articulate the consolidation of the learning during the job placement and throughout the first year with a presentation to the cohort.
