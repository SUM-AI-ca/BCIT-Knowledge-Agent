## Sawmill Technology WOOD 2140
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
WOOD 2140 Sawmill Technology covers large-scale industrial sawmilling in depth, from log preparation to sorting, including sawmill automation systems, scanners, and optimization.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Analyze equipment and processes used in the manufacture of lumber, including log preparation, log breakdown, and secondary sawmill processes.
- Identify scanning systems and select appropriate application.
- Identify key performance indicators and apply them for continuous improvement planning.
- Evaluate lumber manufacturing practices, appropriate technology by log category, and end product.
- Identify problems associated with lumber manufacturing practices.
- Communicate effectively using appropriate industry vocabulary with production and maintenance personnel.
