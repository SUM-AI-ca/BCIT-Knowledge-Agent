## Chainsaw & Brushsaw Maintenance & Use in Silviculture RRRM 1220
Renewable Resources Resource Management Course
## Course Overview
Chainsaw & Brushsaw Maintenance and Use in Silviculture provides basic training in chainsaw and brushsaw maintenance and in safe and productive use of chainsaws and brushsaws in silvicultural applications. It also provides training in supervision of worker safety, work quality, worker productivity, environmental quality, fire protection and Employment Standards.
### Prerequisite(s)
- RRFW 1140 and RRFW 1143 and RRRM 1130 and RRRM 1222
### Credits
4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Maintain a chainsaw and a brushsaw in safe, efficient operating condition.
- Operate a chainsaw or a brushsaw safely while performing silvicultural treatments.
- Organize work for safety, fire protection, environmental quality, production and Employment Standards.
- Monitor and correct as necessary to achieve acceptable safety, fire protection, environmental quality and Employment Standards.
- Assess work quality and productivity and give feedback to workers.
- Maintain daily records as required for worker payment.
- Assess work quality and payment for each contract unit.
- Assess crew productivity.
