## Advanced Thermal Power Plant Simulation POWX 3110
Power Microcredential Course
## Course Overview
This course incorporates simulator activities delivered via a cloud-based solution accessible through a web browser. Upon successful completion, participants will attain an advanced level of proficiency. This proficiency enables them to navigate simulated thermal power plant operations, evaluate control loops in a simulated environment, summarize the effects of process modifications through manipulation of the Thermal Power Plant model, articulate strategies for enhancing economic plant performance, and test and modify control logic in dynamic environments. T hese ac quired skills not only empower participants within their professional roles but also contribute significantly to improving safety standards in the energy industry. Furth ermore, they play a pivotal role in supporting Canada's transition to a low-carbon economy. Evaluation will be based on individual simulation lab work, with a passing mark being a prerequisite for obtaining the Microcredential.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Operate a simulated thermal power plant and complete advanced plant operating sequences
- Solve plant upset conditions using trouble-shooting skills
- Evaluate control loops in a simulated environment
- Summarize effects of modifications to the process by altering the Thermal Power Plant model
- Explain how the economic performance of the plant could be improved
- Test and modify control logic in a dynamic environment
Effective as of Winter 2024
