## Plumbing IP Refresher PPGS 0103
Piping, Plumbing, Gas Course
## Course Overview
Prepares trades persons for the Plumbing Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination. Review is provided in National Plumbing Code, math, science, pumps, heating, safety, rigging, drafting, plumbing materials rural sewage and other examined subject areas.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Students enrolling in this course must be approved by the ITA to write the Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination and must prove that they have successfully completed an apprenticeship in Plumbing.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Read and interpret the Nation Plumbing Code.
- Solve trade related math problems.
- Solve trade related science problems.
- Pumps.
- Hydronic heating.
- Safety.
- Rigging.
- Drafting.
- Piping Materials.
- Rural sewage.
