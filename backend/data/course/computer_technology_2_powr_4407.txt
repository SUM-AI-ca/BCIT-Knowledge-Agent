## Boiler Plant Computer Simulation 2 POWR 4407
Power Engineering Course
## Course Overview
​ POWR 4407 is designed to train 2nd Year Power and Process Engineering students in simulated thermal power plant operations and Distributed Control Systems. This includes plant start-up, shutdown, emergency operation, troubleshooting, and energy conservation. As stated in the Application for Third Class Power Engineer’s certificate of qualification (B.C. Safety Standards Act): “A one-time, three-month credit towards the qualifying time requirement will be granted to you, if you have received boiler plant computer simulation training at an educational facility approved by a provincial safety manager.”​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in POWR 3307
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Operate a simulated thermal power plant.
- Demonstrate the process dynamics.
- Solve plant upset conditions using trouble-shooting skills.
- Explain how the economic performance of the plant could be improved.
- Summarize effects of modifications to the process by altering TPP (Thermal Power Plant) model.
- Evaluate control loops in a simulated environment.
Effective as of Fall 2022
