## Human Resources for Small Business - Hiring and Retaining Employees VENT 0043
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
Tired of watching your human resources investments end up at other companies? Wondering why employees leave their jobs to seek employment elsewhere? Your small business is only as good as its employees. Learn how to effectively navigate common HR issues and implement tactics that will not only help you retain more employees but also keep your valued employees happier and more productive.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:

- Learn techniques to tap into the true potential of a variety of employee types.
