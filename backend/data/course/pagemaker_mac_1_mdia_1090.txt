## PageMaker MAC 1 MDIA 1090
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Introduces and teaches design, layout and production of professional publications, using the Adobe PageMaker assembly software program. Learn to create projects, advertisements, brochures, bulletins, flyers and forms. Students will have their own workstation using a Macintosh computer for this hands-on course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
