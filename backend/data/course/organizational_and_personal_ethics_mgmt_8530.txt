## Organizational and Personal Ethics MGMT 8530
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates skills in understanding social responsibility and managerial ethics.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Examine various underlying aspects of business ethics and societal judgements
- Examine ethical decision making frameworks.
- Understand the four basic views of ethical conduct.
- Differentiate between social responsibility and ethical conduct.
- Determine Methods to Encourage Employee Ethical Behaviours
- Identify potential organizational responses to ethical issues.
- Understand how your organization deals with ethical issues.
- Develop a plan for integrating ethical tools and decision-making in your department.
