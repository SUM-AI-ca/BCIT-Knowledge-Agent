## Physics for Chemical Sciences 1 PHYS 1141
Physics Course
## Course Overview
Covers a wide range of physical principles, with special relevance to Chemical Sciences Technology. First term topics include kinematics, dynamics, friction, statics, energy, power, circular motion, properties of solids, and fluids.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
5.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the student will be able to:
- Define basic terms for concepts covered this term in Physics.
- Correctly choose, manipulate and apply Physics formulae to the solution of Physics problems, including formula manipulation.
- Solve single and double concept Physics problems by using a rigorous problem-solving technique.
- Demonstrate correct measurement and data acquisition skills in a laboratory environment.
- Write a laboratory report in accordance with a prescribed format.
