## Teaching Electronics 2 TTED 5060
Technology Teacher Education Course
## Course Overview
This course further prepares students to teach secondary school electronics. Students research, design, fabricate, and troubleshoot a variety of electronics projects that incorporate linear, digital, and microcontroller technology. Projects will be prepared and documented for delivery to secondary school students.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in TTED 4060
### Credits
8.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Create electrical schematic diagrams.
- Design, build and program electronic control systems integrating sensors, microcontrollers, and motors.
- Evaluate mobile electric vehicle designs for stability, traction, top speed and pushing force.
- Fabricate electronic projects.
- Troubleshoot circuits using test equipment.
- Demonstrate safe work practices.
- Use standard units in electronics calculations.
- Research reference data and resource materials to develop student activities.
- Create learning activities for secondary school students
Effective as of Winter 2016
