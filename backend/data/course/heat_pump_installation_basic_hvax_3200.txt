## Heat Pump Installation- Basic HVAX 3200
Heat Pump Maintenance and Trou Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces the fundamental principles and practices involved in installing heat pump systems. On line lectures for basic refrigeration, electrical, and piping procedures will be covered before attending the practical portion of the course, this will allow more hands-on time in our lab. The intent of the course is to introduce the student to proper procedures on how to install a heat pump, helping them understand and learn in the field with a qualified individual.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Students must hold the following certifications: 1) Red Seal: HVAC *or* Level One/Foundations program in HVAC and Refrigeration from BCIT or another recognized institute in Canada. AND 2) Environment Canada ODS Certificate.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- ​Understand the basic principles of heat transfer and thermodynamics relevant to heat pump operation.
- Identify different types of heat pumps and their respective applications.
- Demonstrate knowledge of the components and parts required for a heat pump system installation.
- Perform proper refrigerant handling and charging procedures in compliance with environmental regulations.
- Evaluate site conditions and factors affecting heat pump performance, such as insulation and airflow.
