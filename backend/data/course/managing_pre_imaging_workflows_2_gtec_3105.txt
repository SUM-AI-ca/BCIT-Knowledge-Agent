## Managing Pre-Imaging Workflows 2 GTEC 3105
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course builds on the foundations taught in GTEC 2005 : Managing Pre-Imaging Workflows 1 and provides an overview of output devices fed by electronic imaging workflows. Students are taught to evaluate and apply the required attributes to transform electronic files into tangible output. The course will focus on tools for automating PDF workflows. Students will get hands-on experience with Kodak Prinergy servers and the connection to Kodak Insite web server. The course connects to the eCommerce course by building web pages that link to the Insite server. Current workflows in soft proofing, web authorizations and eCommerce transactions are explored and evaluated.
### Prerequisite(s)
- GTEC 2105 and BUSA 1005

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Produce a variety of proofs, prints and plates from devices that support input from Prinergy.
- Differentiate between the appropriate uses of inkjet, electrophotographic and “soft” proofing.
- List the characteristics of the most common output devices driven by PDF workflows.
- Describe the types of media associated with various output devices.
- Apply colour management with ICC or Device Link profiles in the Colour Control module of Prinergy’s Process Plan for proofing output.
- Map spot colours to the correct colour library for the best simulation with proofing devices.
- Combine surplus spot colours to a single separation for successful printing plate production.
- Automate a PDF workflow through Kodak Insite server from supplied postscript, soft proof approvals, revision cycles and final output.
- Set and revise trapping characteristics based on expectations of the ultimate printing condition.
- Evaluate other vendor offerings for PDF workflows to various RIP technologies.
- Configure workflows: directly connect output devices, remote machines or third party devices.
