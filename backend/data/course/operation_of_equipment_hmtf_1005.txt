## Operation of Equipment HMTF 1005
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the safe operation of lifting and mobile equipment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
5.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply WorkSafeBC Regulations to lift and move loads
- Use and maintain lifting and blocking equipment
- Perform pre‐start and walkaround inspections
- Safely operate equipment
- Inspect and service wire rope used on winches
- Apply math and science principles to area, volume, weight/mass, and safe working loads
Effective as of Fall 2025
