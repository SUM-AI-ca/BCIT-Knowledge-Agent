## Avid Non-Linear Level 2 BCST 2280
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Teaches importing and exporting graphics, advanced effects, layering techniques and on-line and audio post preparation. Hands-on Avid time is maximized for additional practice of editing techniques, and includes class review and critiques of cuts.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BCST 1180
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Manage and manipulate media files efficiently and effectively on the media drives.
- Carry out procedures for importing and exporting files, creating title builds and advanced effect layering.
- Properly prepare media for audio and video post production.
- Navigate and work effectively in an organized manner within the AVID project workspace.
- Work respectfully with other editors as a multiple user on the AVID workstation.
