## Advanced Engine Management Supplemental ASTP 0530
Automotive Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This supplementary course will provide additional development training for students that have participated in ASTP 4530 Advanced Engine Management Systems. Engine Management theory will be reviewed and connected with shop exercise to further develop the student's capacity to achieve the ASTP 4530 course objectives. Successful completion of this supplemental course will be considered as a supplemental assignment enabling a 70% pass grade in the ASTP 4530 course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- S in ASTP 4530

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the diagnostic approach.
- Describe diagnostic routines, symptoms, systems, and test equipment.
- Describe engine management systems.
- Test engine management input sensors.
- Test engine management output actuators.
- Analyze on-board diagnostic system data.
- Describe new technology engine control systems.
