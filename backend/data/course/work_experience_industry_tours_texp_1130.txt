## Work Experience - Industry Tours TEXP 1130
Trades Exploration Program Course
## Course Overview
Trades Discovery students participate in an approved unpaid work experience module. The work experience component ensures each student experiences first-hand the day- to- day realities of the work situation. Industry Tours/Lectures are scheduled to provide a bigger picture of the needs of Industry and possible opportunities for employment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Students failing TEXP 1150 Safe Work Practices, TEXP 1120 Career Preparation and/or 1 or more courses in Trades Discovery will not be allowed to participate in the TEXP 1130 Work Experience.
### Credits
2.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use networking techniques to research trades employment opportunities to secure and participate in an approved unpaid work experience. The Work experience employer/supervisor will evaluate the students.
- Participate in Approved Industry tours
