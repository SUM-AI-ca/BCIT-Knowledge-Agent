## Imaging Technologies 3 GTEC 3464
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
Printing multi-colour and four-colour process jobs using single-colour and mid-size sheet-fed offset presses are the focus of this advanced imaging course. Students identify press systems, adjust the press for optimum operation, perform maintenance, incorporate troubleshooting techniques and consider quality control issues as well as prepare and install dampeners, and mix printing inks. Problem solving is emphasized with experiences on the large press SHOTS simulator and the FLEXstar simulator for Flexographic Press simulation. Hands-on experience with the flex press will give students experience with mounting plate cylinders, changing media, and registering 2-colour inks. Digital Press production will be analyzed for colour gamut comparisons and quality issues relating to colour management and proofing. Digital output will be compared to inkjet proofing to evaluate effectiveness in a production environment. Emphasis is placed on job scheduling and planning to maximize production and complete finishing operations.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Use offset printing presses to produce commercially acceptable jobs.
- Make and install lithographic printing plates.
- Work as part of a team to complete the press make-ready.
- Perform multiple checks for creating press-ready digital and inkjet proofs.
- Fully understand how to control ink and density settings.
- Continuously check press sheets as the press is running.
- Install a blanket on the press.
- Make and install dampeners.
- Use anti-offset spray.
- Participate in a team to produce final litho printed projects.
- Demonstrate effective troubleshooting skills on flex and sheet-fed offset press simulators.
- Prepare and manage media for flexo, litho, electrophotographic and inkjet imaging.
- Use densitometry and spectrophotometry to colour-manage all imaging devices.
- Compare production press runs to soft and hard proofs from other media.
- Change roll media on a flex web press.
- Install plate cylinders, apply ink, and register a 2-colour flex web production job.
- Colour-manage a RIP to simulate lithographic, flexographic, digital and inkjet imaging.
