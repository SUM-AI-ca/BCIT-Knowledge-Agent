## Aerial Photo TSYH 3340
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
An introduction to aerial photography presented in a 3- day seminar format. Topics include aerial photos, geometry, maps and aerial photos, engineering applications. Use of stereoscopes, photo indexes, conversions of scale, height determination and photo map point transfers.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Introduction to Aerial Photography
- List the five essential requirements for producing an air photo.
- List six advantages of using air photos.
- List the three factors used to determine the orientation of flight lines.
- List the four factors used to determine the spacing of flight lines.
## Module 2 - Aerial Photo Indexing
- Identify the individual components of an air photo number including those flown for the BC government and federal government.
- Explain how specific air photos are selected from an air photo index.
- List common sources for obtaining air photos at the local and provincial levels.
## Module 3 - Characteristics of Aerial Photos
- Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of wide-angle photography to narrow-angle photography.
- Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of panchromatic and infrared film.
- List the three classifications of aerial photos based on the camera angle.
- List the four classifications of aerial photos based on the camera focal length.
- List eight characteristics of good aerial photos.
## Module 4 - Introduction to the Photographic Process
- Describe the process of creating images on film.
- List the factors affecting the quality of a photographic image.
- Describe the process of developing photographic films.
## Module 5 - Geometry of Single Vertical Aerial Photos
- Define the three types of photo centres.
- Define the terms: camera angle, focal length, angle of coverage, distortion and displacement.
## Module 6 - Aerial Photo Scales, Measurements and Calculations
- Calculate the distance on air photos.
- Calculate ground distance given photo distance and photo scale.
- Calculate the scale of an air photo given the distance between two points on the ground and the same two points on an air photo.
- Calculate the scale of an air photo given the camera focal length and the flying height of the aircraft.
## Module 7 - Stereoscopy in Aerial Photography
- Define the theory of stereoscopy.
- Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of the lens and mirror stereoscopes.
- List the basic rules of care for a stereoscope.
- List the steps to align vertical aerial photos.
- List five causes of eye fatigue when viewing air photos.
## Module 8 - Geometry of Paired Aerial Photographs
- Calculate the height of objects on an aerial photo.
- Measure absolute parallax given a pair of consecutive aerial photos.
## Module 9 - Slope Estimation from Aerial Photos
- Use objective and qualitative methods to estimate slope from aerial photos.
- Estimate the vertical exaggeration of an aerial photo.
