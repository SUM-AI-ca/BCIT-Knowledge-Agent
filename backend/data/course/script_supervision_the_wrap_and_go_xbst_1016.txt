## Script Supervision: The Wrap and Go XBST 1016
Broadcast and Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
​In this final segment of Script Supervision, along with detailed review, there'll be a practical hands-on mock shoot to really immerse yourself in the position. Then it'll be insight into the industry with union discussion and overview. You'll be able to see possible paths forward of where to go from here.​​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in XBST 1009 and 50% in XBST 1010 and 50% in XBST 1011 and 50% in XBST 1012
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
​Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- ​Experience the intricacies and review the position of Script Supervisor in real time
- Prioritize script and practice continuity skills in a mock shoot
- Identify industry pathways and IATSE film union structure ​
