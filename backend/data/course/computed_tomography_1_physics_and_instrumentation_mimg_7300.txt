## Computed Tomography 1: Physics and Instrumentation MIMG 7300
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
Through readings and assignments, deals with the physics and technologic aspects of conventional and spiral/helical CT, including digital image processing, radiation attenuation, data acquisition, and image reconstruction. In addition, describes the major components of a CT scanner (computers, array processors, display, recording and storage devices) and discusses the factors affecting image quality and radiation dose to the patient. A major section deals with Multislice CT technology and its application such as CT Fluoroscopy, Three-Dimensional CT, CT Angiography and Virtual Reality Imaging. Also discusses image artifacts, quality control and mobile CT scanning. To register in this course, students must be registered technologists in Medical Radiography, Nuclear Medicine or Diagnostic Medical Sonography.

## Learning Outcomes
On completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Outline the significant events in the history of CT.
- Assess concepts of computing and digital image processing as they relate to CT.
- Explore the physics necessary for an understanding of the problem in CT with respect to data collection and image reconstruction.
- Categorize the components of a CT scanner with respect to data acquisition, data processing and image display, recording, storage and communication systems.
- Evaluate the factors affecting image quality in CT and radiation dose to the patient during a CT examination.
- Analyze the types of CT artifacts and methods to eliminate or reduce artifact production.
- Outline the concepts of quality control in CT.
- Outline the principles and concepts of three-dimensional CT imaging.
- Describe the advances in volume data acquisition and assess the fundamental difference between the 'old' and the 'new' technologies for CT scanning.
- Outline the principles of multi-slice CT scanning including the data acquisition and image reconstruction concepts.
- Discuss the technical factors involved in CT fluoroscopy.
- Describe the technical elements of CT angiography and outline its post-processing techniques and visualization tools.
- Outline the basic concepts, software tools for virtual reality imaging with specific applications in CT.
