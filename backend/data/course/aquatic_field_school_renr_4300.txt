## Aquatic Field School RENR 4300
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
​This field-based course is a culmination of the aquatic knowledge and skills students have learned over the course of the FWR program. Students will gain hands-on experience with fish handling, fish capture methods, fish habitat assessment, and benthic invertebrate sampling protocols. Other activities will include fish and fish habitat data entry to the provincial database and water quality sampling. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CHEM 1150 and 50% in RENR 3350 and 50% in RENR 3360 and 50% in RENR 4310
- All Term 1, 2, 3 courses.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply inventory methods to survey fish and other aquatic organisms
- Identify and apply best practices to capture and handle fish
- Assess and document aquatic habitat required to support fish and other aquatic organisms
- Assess and document natural and anthropogenic disturbances
- Measure water quality parameters that affect aquatic health
- Demonstrate safe, respectful, and professional behaviour that meets industry standards and expectations​
Effective as of Winter 2025
