## Leading A Team In The Digital Age BUSA 1170
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
This is a highly topical and pragmatic course that will provide emerging leaders with the skills needed to lead their teams through disruptive technological changes that can quickly transform entire markets.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze and understand managing a tech-based team and leading organizational change.
- Analyze and understand data analysis and business intelligence.
- Analyze and understand the cloud and web applications.
- Analyze and understand analytics essentials.
