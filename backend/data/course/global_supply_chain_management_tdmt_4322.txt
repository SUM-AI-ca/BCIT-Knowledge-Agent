## Global Supply Chain Management TDMT 4322
Transportation and Distribution Course
## Course Overview
This course develops an understanding of relationship management within the global supply chain sectors. Topics are delivered based on international influences, value creation, aligning incentives, creating partnerships, sharing resources and finding mutual benefits within the supply chain. An emphasis will be based on building flexible and strategic partnerships and managing changes and shifts in strategies. These methods are aimed at driving supply chain profitability and competitive advantage.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TDMT 3301

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Explain the impact of cultural and international standards on a firm’s strategy.
- Demonstrate the use and benefit of strategically aligning the global supply chain to best fit a firm’s objectives.
- Analyze customer service levels of satisfaction and perceptions to meet both vendor and customer expectations.
- Identify and demonstrate tools and techniques designed for improvement and change management.
- Apply enhanced value-chain planning through information sharing, IT infrastructure, and Key Performance Indicators (KPI).
- Demonstrate supply chain management through partner certification and approval.
- Explain the importance of relationship sustainability, integration and collaboration techniques.
- Develop skills for effective negotiation and business relationship management.
