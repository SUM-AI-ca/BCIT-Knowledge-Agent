## Introduction to Career Planning CEPD 0100
Career Exploration Course
## Course Overview
Considering BCIT? Undecided about your career choice? Learn more about career planning. This two-hour course introduces the steps in the career planning process, exploring interests and researching career/ educational options. Taught by a professional counsellor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
