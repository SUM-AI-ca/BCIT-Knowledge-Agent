## Leading Change: From Plan to Impact XLED 2141
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​Building on concepts from XLED 1141 , this course equips participants with advanced tools and strategies to map, implement, and sustain change. Learners will develop actionable plans, manage resistance, measure progress, and ensure long-term success.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- S in XLED 1141
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop a clear and compelling change purpose statement connected to a real initiative.
- Apply change management tools and tactics—including communication planning and resistance management—to support stakeholder adoption.
- Translate a change purpose into a practical, actionable plan with clear milestones and responsibilities.
- Design and use metrics to track adoption, progress, and success of change initiatives.
- Propose strategies to reinforce positive change and sustain long‑term results, including methods for recognizing and celebrating success.​
