## Business Statistics OPMT 1121
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
Covers fundamental statistics used in business and industry. Topics include descriptive statistics, probability theory and major distributions, sampling, estimation, tests of hypotheses, correlation and linear regression. Calculation is done using spreadsheets.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
