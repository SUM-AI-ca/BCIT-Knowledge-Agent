## Surveying for Building SURV 1120
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
Introduces measurements; electronic distance measuring devices; angular measurements; theodolites; differential and trigonometric leveling; GPS; surveying procedures in high-rises; traverses; triangulation; trilateration; elementary computations; coordinate systems, cadastral plans in B.C.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
