## Overview

This course covers the updates to the Safety Standards Act, the Safety Standards General Regulation, the Electrical Safety Regulation and the Directives. This course also includes a review of all revisions to the BC Electrical Code based on the 2024 Canadian Electrical Code - C22.1:24, Part I, Twenty-sixth Edition, Safety Standard for Electrical Installations. This course is required for electricians renewing their FSRs certificates of qualification.

## Learning Outcomes

- Review the changes to the Electrical Safety Regulations (ESR) and understand the implementation of the Master FSR renewal training program.
- Understand that the Canadian Electrical Code, by ministerial order from the provincial government, is adopted as the BC Electrical Code for use in the Province of British Columbia.
- Navigate and access the most current versions of Electrical Directives, Bulletins, and Safety Orders within the province of BC.
- Review and compare the changes made in the 2024 edition of the Canadian Electrical Code, Part I, with those in the 2021 edition.
- Complete and review the 2024 Canadian Electrical Code quiz.
