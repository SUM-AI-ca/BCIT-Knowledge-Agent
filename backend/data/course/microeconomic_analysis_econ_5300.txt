## Microeconomic Analysis ECON 5300
Economics Course
## Course Overview
The study of consumer behaviour, production, exchange, equilibrium of the firm under different market structures, factor markets and economic welfare.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in ECON 2100 and 50% in MATH 2011
