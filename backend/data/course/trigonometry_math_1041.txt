## Trigonometry MATH 1041
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
A distance education course in trigonometry. Topics include theory and application of trigonometric functions, right angle trigonometry, vectors, trigonometric graphs, identities and equations, compound and double angle formulae and inverse functions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Math 12 Pre-Calculus or equivalent OR BCIT Math 0001.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Module 1 - The Trigonometric Functions
- Define an angle.
- Define the trigonometric functions.
- Apply the principles of right angle trigonometry to practical problems.
- Module 2 - Trigonometric Functions of Any Angle
- Calculate the trigonometric functions of any triangle.
- Calculate radian measure.
- Apply the principles of radian measure to practical problems.
- Module 3 - Vectors and Oblique Triangles
- Define vectors in a plane.
- Apply the principles of vector addition to the solution of right angle and non-right angle triangles.
- Apply the laws of sine and cosine to the solution of oblique triangles.
- Calculate the area of right angle and oblique triangles.
- Module 4 - Graphs of Trigonometric Functions
- Sketch graphs of all 6 trigonometric functions plus composite functions.
- Identify amplitude, period and displacement of a sine or cosine function.
- Identify period and vertical asymptotes for tangent, cotangent, secant and cosecant functions.
- Apply graphical techniques to describe behaviour of physical systems.
- Module 5 - Additional Topics in Trigonometry
- Explain the difference between a conditional equation and an identity.
- Work with a number of trigonometric identities including simplifying expressions and solving equations.
- Describe properties and graphs of inverse trigonometric functions.
