## CNC Fundamentals MACH 0020
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
​Introduces the concepts of Computer Numerical Control (CNC) programming. Students are exposed to simulators as well as demonstrations of CNC vertical milling machines. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify safety hazards associated with the operation of CNC machines.
- Identify the advantages and disadvantages of using CNC machines.
- Describe different types of CNC machines.
- Explain the purpose and operation of CNC machines.
- Identify the major components and tooling of CNC machines.​
