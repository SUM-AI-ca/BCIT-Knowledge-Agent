## Visual Communication 2D INTD 1180
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
​The focus of this course is to introduce freehand drafting techniques as they pertain to interior design. Students will produce schematic two-dimensional drawings for interior design applications, through implementation of scale, line weights, lettering, dimensions, and layouts.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Interpret architectural plans, and elevations.
- Apply imperial and metric scales in drawings.
- Create orthographic drawings of objects and interior space.
- Produce scaled freehand drawings using appropriate line weights, layouts, and lettering techniques.
- Explore various dimensioning applications and terminology.
- Apply dimensions to floor plans.
- Measure interior components and spaces to produce scaled plans, and elevations.
- Demonstrate freehand techniques to represent graphically interior components within two-dimensional drawings.
- Apply referencing to a set of drawings.
- Draw floor plans and elevations to scale using symbols and conventions.​
Effective as of Fall 2025
