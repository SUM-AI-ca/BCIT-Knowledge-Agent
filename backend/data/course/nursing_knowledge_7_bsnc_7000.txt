## Nursing Knowledge 7 BSNC 7000
Nursing Course
## Course Overview
This blended course focuses on promoting synthesis of nursing knowledge with clinical reasoning and reflective processes to enable learners to recognize and respond to context-dependent clinical situations across the continuum of care. Increased emphasis is placed on integration and application of nursing therapeutics and pharmacological approaches, and advanced nursing skills of prioritization and delegation. Learners have opportunities to apply clinical judgment to prioritization frameworks and to practice prioritization and delegation of care for multiple clients across the lifespan. Learning occurs through review of complex cases where students are required to apply and articulate their critical reasoning through group process and communicate client needs in a timely, safe, and effective manner. This course is intended to assist learners in structuring and focusing their own specific knowledge and theory review needs. It does not provide an in-depth content review for all content areas provided in previous nursing knowledge courses. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will meet the following competencies:
Collaborator
- Works to prevent and resolve conflict, and negotiate solutions in interactions with clients and the healthcare team
Decision Maker
- Independently plans nursing care with clients, within the context of critical inquiry and relational practice that integrates knowledge from nursing, health sciences and other related disciplines as well as knowledge from practice experiences, client knowledge, patterns and preferences, and influences within the health care context
- Effectively manages complex clinical scenarios and time sensitive situations
- Articulates clinical reasoning process in support of nursing practice
Leader
- Promotes teamwork and garners commitment and participation of others that will optimize nursing practice and client care
Lifelong Learner
- Contributes to the learning experiences of colleagues through support and teaching
Professional
- Displays initiative, confidence and self-awareness in nursing practice
- Provides knowledge translation to support the professional development of other learners and healthcare team members
- Participates in professional activities to keep abreast of current trends and issues in nursing
