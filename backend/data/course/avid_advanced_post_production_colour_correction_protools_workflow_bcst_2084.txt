## AVID Advanced Post-Production, Colour Correction, Protools Workflow BCST 2084
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course will teach the student the proper post-production workflow for a completed video project, including proper use of the Avid colour correction tools, as well as exporting Protools sessions for sound designers and importing the completed audio sessions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BCST 1180
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will:
- Learn post-production workflow for a completed video project.
- Use Avid colour correction tools.
- Export Protools sessions for sound designers.
- Import completed audio sessions.
