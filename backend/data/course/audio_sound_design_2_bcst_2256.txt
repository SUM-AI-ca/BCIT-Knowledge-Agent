## Audio Sound Design 2 BCST 2256
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course is a hands-on introduction to sound production for different types of video. Students will explore recording location sound for documentaries, feature films and live TV, and creating sound effects and voice-over tracks for animations and web commercials. They will also examine common industry pre-production planning considerations, practices of capturing location sound in various production set-ups, post-production software editing and sound design techniques.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BCST 1256
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify sound pre-production requirements.
- Demonstrate location sound capturing techniques.
- Distinguish the useful ambient sound from unwanted environment noise.
- Demonstrate advanced dialogue and sound effects editing techniques.
- Design and edit Foley tracks.
- Arrange ADR and Dubbing sessions.
- Demonstrate mixing and Syncing sound for video.
