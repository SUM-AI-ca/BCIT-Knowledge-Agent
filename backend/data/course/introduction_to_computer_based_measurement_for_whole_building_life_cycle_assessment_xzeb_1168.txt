## Introduction to Computer-Based Measurement for Whole-Building Life Cycle Assessment XZEB 1168
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
This course covers computer-based measurement competencies for the review of design information and quantity take-off for use in whole-building life cycle assessment. Intended for students taking the Whole-Building Life Cycle Assessment microcredential with minimal or no prior experience in estimating, take-off and measurement, to gain basic skills necessary to prepare quantity takeoffs. The course covers the basic use of PlanSwift, a take-off and estimating software, to prepare quantities for use in whole-building life cycle assessment. Delivery format: This course is intended to be a hybrid of online independent work (using software) and live lectures delivered in 2-hour units per week by a qualified Professional Quantity Surveyor. ​This optional course is designed to support the skills in Whole-Building LCA. To take this course, pre-requisite courses XZEB 1160 and XZEB 1165 must be completed with a passing grade of 60%.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe quantity takeoffs related to Master Format.
- Explain the key functions of the PlanSwift software.
- Explain how to set up projects.
- Perform on screen quantity take-offs and mark-up of drawings.
