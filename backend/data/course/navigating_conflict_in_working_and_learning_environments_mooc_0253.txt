## Navigating Conflict in Working and Learning Environments MOOC 0253
Massive Open Online Courses Course
## Course Overview
​In this course you will learn about the importance of conflict, conflict styles and tools to navigate conflict in the workplace and learning environments. We all have a responsibility for our role in conflict and how we interact with one another. This training will help to empower you to have productive conversations moving forward.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe types of conflict.
- Describe values, needs and identities as they relate to conflict and tools to foster better communication.
- Explain how to avoid making assumptions in conflict and how to disagree respectfully.
- Explain conflict styles.
- Describe the DEAR Model, a tool to address conflict as well as other conflict resolution techniques.​
