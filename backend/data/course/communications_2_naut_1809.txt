## Communications 2 NAUT 1809
Ocean Navigation Course
## Course Overview
This course covers communication by Morse code using flashing light or sound signals.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0
### Domestic fees
$643.11
International fees are typically 3.4 times the domestic tuition. Exact cost will be calculated upon completion of registration.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Recognize Morse code alphabets and numerals by flashlight.
- Send and receive Morse signals by flashlight (SOS).
- List single-letter signals which may be sounded in compliance with the Collision Regulations.
- Understand the recommendations on sound signalling.
Effective as of Fall 2016
