## Building Envelope Solutions BLDG 0137
Building Course
## Course Overview
Sponsored and developed by the Canadian Home Builders Assoc. of BC, Canada Mortgage and Housing Corp. and the Homeowner Protection Office of BC. This course provides knowledge of the background building science that allows durable building envelopes to be constructed or restored. Includes final open book examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
