## Tires and Wheels MCMX 0120
Motorcycle Mechanic Course
## Course Overview
This course provides motorcycle owners and enthusiasts with the skills needed to replace tires, repair tires and inner tubes, and balance wheels. Lectures are combined with demonstrations and hands-on training. BCIT motorcycles of various types are provided for demonstrations and hands-on activities.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCMX 0115

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe tire and inner tube maintenance requirements on common types of motorcycles.
- Identify differences in tools and techniques for tire service procedures and repairs.
- Perform tire and inner tube repair and replacement.
- Perform wheel balance.
