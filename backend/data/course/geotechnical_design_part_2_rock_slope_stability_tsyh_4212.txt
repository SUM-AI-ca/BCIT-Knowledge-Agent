## Geotechnical Design Part 2: Rock Slope Stability TSYH 4212
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
Expands on the concepts of soils technology and covers the basic mechanics of rock slope failures and the principles needed to analyze planar failures. Topics include methods of rock slope stabilization, rockfall protection and detection, required calculations and design applications.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of each module, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Introduction and Basic Fundamentals of Rock Mechanics
- Recognize and understand the economic implications of rock slope failure.
- Differentiate between a soil mass and rock mass.
- Identify the role that geologic structure or discontinuities have on rock slope stability.
- Define the terms 'friction,' 'cohesion' and 'shear strength.'
- Define the term 'factor of safety.'
## Module 2 - Shear Strength and Rock Slope Stability Analysis
- Define the term 'shear strength of rock.'
- Explain the influence of water on shear strength of planar discontinuities.
- Define the term factor of safety and calculate a factor of safety for given slope conditions involving planar failures.
- Describe and recognize the following failure modes:
- A planar failure
- A wedge failure
- A circular failure
- A ravelling failure, and
- A toppling failure.
## Module 3 - Groundwater
- Explain the effect of water on the shear strength of discontinuities.
- Explain groundwater flow in rock masses.
- Describe techniques used to obtain water pressure measurements.
- Describe techniques used to reduce the effects of groundwater.
## Module 4 - Geological Data Collection and Presentation
- Describe what information must be collected in order to analyze discontinuities in a rock mass.
- Describe mapping techniques used to obtain geological structural information.
- Explain the purpose and use of stereonets.
- Graphically represent geological structural data.
## Module 5 - Rock Slope Stabilization Methods
- Describe how slope design interrelates to geological structural site conditions and economics.
- Describe and explain the purpose and function of the following remedial rock work techniques:
- Scaling
- Rock bolting
- Excavation
- Meshing
- Shotcrete
- Rock dowelling
- Buttress.
## Module 6 - Rockfall Control, Warning and Monitoring Methods
- Explain the purpose and function of catchment areas as a method of rockfall protection or control.
- Explain the purpose and function of wire mesh curtains and catch fences as methods of rockfall control.
- Explain the purpose and function of walls, rock sheds and tunnels as methods of rockfall protection.
- Explain the purpose and function of electric fences and trip wires as methods of rockfall warning.
- Describe two tension crack monitoring systems.
- Graph and interpret monitoring data.
## Module 7 - Blasting Effects and Design
- Explain the method of rock fracturing by blasting.
- Define the terms spacing and burden, and calculate appropriate spacing and burden dimensions given information for typical blast conditions.
- Define and explain smooth wall blasting techniques.
- Describe and explain the purpose of blast vibration monitoring.
