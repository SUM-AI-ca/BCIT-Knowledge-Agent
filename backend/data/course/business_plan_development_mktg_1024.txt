## Business Plan Development MKTG 1024
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for entrepreneurs looking to start a business and who require a detailed professional business plan to show investors. Students will examine all aspects of this “road map” to develop a solid plan which can help secure credit, time and expense allocation, action items and setting goals for the future. Topics include: Marketing survey & plan, financial plan, competitive analysis & selling strategies plus basic operational requirements (licenses etc.)
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Develop a complete business plan with an Executive Summary & Operations Plan
- Complete a marketing survey to determine product/service viability
- Create a financial plan determining revenue projects and expenses over 3 years
- Understand value proposition through competitive analysis
