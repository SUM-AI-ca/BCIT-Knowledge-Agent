## Video Production and Editing Final Project BCST 3005
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed to integrate and apply all the skills attained in prior Video Production and Editing Associate Certificate courses. Students will work on a self-directed project (or projects) guided by the instructor, from concept to finished video. Students will benefit from on-going consultation with the client to execute a video production - including planning, writing, story-boarding, shooting, editing, and final revisions to meet the client's needs.​
### Prerequisite(s)

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Create a short project, documentary, promotional or educational video.
- Consult with a client to determine finished project expectations and goals.
- Plan a video production, including shooting and editing requirements and scheduling.
- Write a script for the chosen project.
- Develop a storyboard that shows the elements needed to support the story/message.
- Shoot video footage that captures the essence of the story/message.
- Create digital assets and graphics that enhance the story/message.
- Compose and/or mix audio elements that support the production.
- Edit video and audio to effectively communicate the story.
- Finesse the finished video project to meet client's needs and expectations.
- Prepare the finished video so it may be viewed on various formats, including social media platforms.​
