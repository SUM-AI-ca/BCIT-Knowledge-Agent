## Mobile Product Planning MDIA 1649
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Why do some mobile apps succeed when most fail? Strategic planning. Mobile Internet use is exploding and so is the need for skilled mobile product strategists. This course teaches you how to develop successful mobile apps by applying basic principles of product strategy. Learn how to assess market trends, exploit opportunities, and manage risks when developing mobile apps and websites. The course includes special presentations from industry professionals, and real business cases are used to test your ability to apply the strategic techniques you learn. Class discussions are supplemented by readings from some of the most popular technology blogs and online videos covering everything from product demos and interviews to customer rants and news clips.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will:
- Understand what product development is and its role in mobile product strategy.
- Know how to apply the product development process to achieve product innovation.
- Have the ability to gather and assess market data.
- Be able define a product plan that is aligned with business goals & objectives.
- Know how to exploit market opportunities and manage risks.
- Understand the characteristics of different mobile monetization models.
- Have the ability to develop a product road map.
- Be able to define and present product recommendations.
