## Shielded Metal Arc Welding and Safety WELD 0188
Welding Course
## Course Overview
​This is a refresher course for students who were unsuccessful in completing the Shielded metal arc welding modules in their service class.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon success completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the SMAW process.
- Identify the main components of a SMAW station.
- Assemble, start up, shut down and maintain SMAW welding equipment.
- Identify SMAW electrodes and their uses.
- Identify materials that can be welded with the SMAW process.
- Identify the thermal effects of distortion on joint.
- Identify safety precautions for SMAW.
- Perform SMAW exercises on mild steel sheet and plate.
