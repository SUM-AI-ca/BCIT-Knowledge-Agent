## Broadcast and Online Journalism Entrance Assessment BCST 0001
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
​This assessment evaluates English competency and writing skills, current events awareness, and motivation/suitability for the program. The passing mark is 60%. This exam is delivered on-line and is a mandatory requirement for application to the program. Upon registration, applicants will receive an automated message with full details and dates specific to writing the exam. Assessment results will be posted within ten business days, on the applicant's student account at my.bcit.ca
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
