## Technical Writing and Communication Skills BLDT 1050
Construction Operations/Supervision Course
## Course Overview
This course will familiarize the technical writer with some basic formats and conventions of documents used in the construction industry. Topics covered will include memos, short letters, facsimiles, agendas and minutes or notes of job-site and office meetings.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Grade 10 English and grade 10 Math or Pre-approved program.
