## Advanced Topics in Computer Graphics COMP 8511
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This course is a directed study in computer graphics providing opportunity for additional study in one or more specialized areas such as advanced rendering techniques, ray tracing and radiosity, optimization techniques for real-time systems, optimization techniques for off-line rendering, non photo-realistic rendering.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in COMP 8011
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Part of the main course project involves setting individual learning objectives based on the student's interest. The learning outcomes will be related to researching and applying advanced computer graphics concepts to useful applications.
