## Mining Exploration MINE 1102
Mining Course
## Course Overview
Discusses the roles of participants, the sources of funding, the objectives, and the strategies employed in mining exploration. The course describes various techniques, (including lithochemistry, geochemistry, geophysics, diamond drilling, and prospecting) and the details of their application.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
