## CAPM Certification Exam Preparation OPMT 0203
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
This program is specifically designed for students wanting to obtain an industry credential in project management to complement their academic learning. Project Management Institute (PMI) credentials demonstrate to employers that you have achieved a level of project management competence with the ability to apply these specialized skills to your area of work, for all sectors of industry. A PMI credential will distinguish you from others in your job search and will also help with career advancement in existing jobs. The course prepares participants to write the Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM) certification exam. In addition, the course provides PMI approved educational credits required for new applications to write the exam. Participants receive comprehensive reference binder covering all exam topics organized by subject with review questions with answers for each topic. 23 PDU Credits / Approved Educational Credits are earned from PMI. www.bcit.ca/files/business/pdf/opmt0203.pdf ​​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply the project management key concepts, terminology, organizational influences, and project life cycle.
- Describe the Project Management Fundamentals.
- Describe the Predictive, Plan-Based Methodologies.
- Describe the Agile Frameworks/Methodologies.
- Describe the Business Analysis Frameworks.
- Apply test-taking techniques for writing the exam.
Effective as of Fall 2023
