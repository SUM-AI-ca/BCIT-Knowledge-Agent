## Conflict Resolution XLED 1050
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​We all encounter people we find difficult to deal with. If not properly managed, these situations can escalate into confrontations - personally stressful and seldom productive. Developing conflict resolution skills helps to reduce stress and improve results. Conflict can be productive and build connection when stakeholders are able to clearly communicate needs and identify common interests. There are time-tested approaches that can defuse conflict, increase communication, and shift the focus from conflict to problem solving. This workshop will provide participants the skills and knowledge to allow them to deal effectively with difficult people and situations.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand what ‘conflict’ and ‘conflict resolution’ mean.
- Define and understand all six phases of the conflict resolution process and how to apply it to defuse conflict.
- Develop interpersonal communication skills to assist with productively navigating conflict.
