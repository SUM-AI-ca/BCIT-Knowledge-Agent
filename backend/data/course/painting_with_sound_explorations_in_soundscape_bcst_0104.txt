## Painting with Sound – Explorations in Soundscape BCST 0104
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Ever been scared by a horror film? I bet it was the scraping metal, thumps and creeks of the soundscape that made it believable. In this hands-on workshop we develop your skills to manipulate mood and control emotion using sound. Your team will organize dialogue, music and sound effects to record and edit a custom soundscape. You'll explore how to transform a video scene from comedy to tragedy using the power of creatively chosen and well-edited sound.
