## Pumps: Sanitary PUBW 2201
Public Works Course
## Course Overview
Covers the operation and maintenance of dry well and wet well wastewater pumping facilities including submersible pumps, seals, impellors, suction and discharge lines, valves, hangers, and couplings. Dismantling and reassembling of pumps will be demonstrated to clarify maintenance requirements and procedures. Odour control and related tasks will be discussed.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in PUBW 1201
