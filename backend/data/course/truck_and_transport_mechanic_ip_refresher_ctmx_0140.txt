## Truck and Transport Mechanic IP Refresher CTMX 0140
Commercial Transport Course
## Course Overview
This course is for current Truck and Transport Mechanic Level 4 apprentices. The course will provide a theory refresher of Levels 1, 2 and 3 content in preparation for the Truck and Transport Mechanic Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Current Commercial Transport Mechanic Level 4 apprentice
### Credits
0.0
### Registration requirements
Departmental approval is required to register for this course. You must have approval from the department to register. Students accepted into a program can register directly.
### Domestic fees
$379.68 - $387.80 See individual course offerings below for actual costs.
International fees are typically 3.4 times the domestic tuition. Exact cost will be calculated upon completion of registration.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Write the Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination.
Effective as of Fall 2016
