## Introduction to Numerical Controlled Press Brake SMTL 0130
Sheet Metal Course
## Course Overview
This course will provide students the necessary skills to safely operate the N.C. Press Brake. Topics covered will be safety procedures press brake controls and their functions, programming and set-up and correct usage of measuring devices.
### Prerequisite(s)
- One year experience in metal trades. Some familiarity with computers would be an asset.
