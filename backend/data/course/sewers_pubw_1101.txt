## Sewers PUBW 1101
Public Works Course
## Course Overview
The basic techniques for the installation and maintenance of storm and sanitary sewers are very similar. This course will build on the basic terminology and introduce knowledge of the overall sewer systems, factors affecting their maintenance, other utilities, safety aspects and typical installation/repair techniques commonly found on a day to day basis.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in PUBW 1001
