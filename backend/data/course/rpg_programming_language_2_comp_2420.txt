## RPG Programming Language 2 COMP 2420
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Continues the study of RPG beyond that covered in COMP 1420 . Covers interactive programming concepts such as inquiry, inquiry with update, display lists ("subfiles"), and display lists with selection. Students develop several interactive programs on the IBM AS/400 using the RPG/400 programming language and the screen design Aid (SDA) utility.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in COMP 1420
