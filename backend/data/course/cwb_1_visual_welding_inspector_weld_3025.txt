## CWB 1 Visual Welding Inspector WELD 3025
Welding Course
## Course Overview
Students who participate in this course will study CSA codes W47.1 and W59, welding symbols, welding process theory, weld symbols, quality control systems, standards and specifications, NDE, and metallurgy of steel. This course will prepare students to challenge and pass the CWB level 1 visual inspector examinations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- WELD 3020
### Credits
6.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate in-process weld joints and finished weldments to code of construction requirements.
- Write and pass the CWB Level 1 Visual Inspector examinations.
