## Architectural Graphics ARSC 5000
Architectural Science Course
## Course Overview
This course develops a range of architectural graphics skills and strategies for various design communications. The course is structured around a series of manual drawing exercises, exploring two- and three-dimensional representation techniques and digital post-processing to produce effective architectural graphics for different purposes. The course emphasizes graphic communication as a means of exploring architectural potential, articulating design intent, and documenting meaningful project information.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Illustrate ideas/concepts using a variety of graphic techniques.
- Sketch the natural and built environment.
- Apply line, shade, shadow, colour and texture to communicate form and materiality.
- Demonstrate accurate perspective drawing techniques.
- Construct composite graphics, merging a variety of media.
- Organize a graphic set into effective storyboards and poster boards for presentation.
- Use available resources to research graphic precedents in a variety of graphic styles.
- Simulate professional best practice individually and collaboratively.
