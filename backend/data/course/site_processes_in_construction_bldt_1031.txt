## Site Processes in Construction BLDT 1031
Construction Operations/Supervision Course
## Course Overview
Enables students who successfully complete this course to; identify and layout the site general requirements, understand the basic contractual and organizational arrangements of a typical construction project; undertake in the planning for the efficient management of a construction site in the areas of material procurement, equipment selection, layout and scheduling. 3.0 credits 12 weeks.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
### Domestic fees
$564.42 - $620.01 See individual course offerings below for actual costs.
International fees are typically 3.4 times the domestic tuition. Exact cost will be calculated upon completion of registration.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify and lay out site requirements.
- Describe the basic contractual and organizational arrangements of a typical construction project.
- Plan the efficient management of a construction site in the areas of material procurement, resource allocation, layout, and scheduling.
Effective as of Fall 2003
