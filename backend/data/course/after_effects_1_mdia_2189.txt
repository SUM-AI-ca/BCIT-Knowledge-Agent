## After Effects 1 MDIA 2189
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Provides a working knowledge of some of the software used for video production. Create portfolio-quality Quick Time movies that can be integrated into multimedia projects digital non-linear video editing and Adobe After Effects for composing, animation and effects. Topics include video production process, compression for animation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
