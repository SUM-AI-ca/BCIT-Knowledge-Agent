## Canadian Employment Readiness Boot Camp CERM 4020
BCIT International Course
## Course Overview
​Canadian Employment Readiness Boot Camp course is designed for students with activities that enables them to gain skills engaging Canadian industry and employers' expectations. The course provides students with hands-on learning experiences. Students will deepen their knowledge and abilities to develop interview and professional networking skills, and create the foundation for a resilient personal career path. The perspectives and skills they gain in this course will contribute to their early career success as they adapt to and grow capacity in the Canadian workplace. ​​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CERM 4010 † ( † may be taken concurrently)
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate communication and interview skills through mock interview
- Develop professional networking skills
- Apply knowledge that creates the foundation for a resilient personal career path​​
