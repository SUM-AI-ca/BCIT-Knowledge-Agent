## Presentation Skills MKTG 1023
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed to improve one’s overall ability to give presentations in front of any audience. Students will cover all the key aspects of giving a presentation including tips about understanding an audience and increasing credibility with complex information. Students will be videotaping themselves presenting to an audience. This presentation will be evaluated. This Presentation Skills course provides the skills and the methods to improve the planning, structuring and delivery of formal and informal presentations for productive outcomes.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Create a positive first impression with an audience.
- Develop ideas for a presentation.
- Structure a presentation to achieve defined objectives.
