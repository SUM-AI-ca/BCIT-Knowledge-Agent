## Millwork Drawing and Furniture Design INTD 4350
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
The focus of the course will be on designing and detailing freestanding and built-in furniture, for commercial spaces. Course material will be enhanced with hands-on experience in the joinery shop, through JOIN 4350 , Joinery Fundamentals for Interior Design, which may be taken concurrently.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
4.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify a variety of standard millwork construction materials and their appropriate applications.
- Analyze client needs to design an appropriate and functional piece of custom millwork.
- Explore creative design solutions for custom millwork.
- Assess whether a specification or detail is applicable and/or required.
- Produce detailed drawings for a variety of commercial interior millwork pieces.
- Make appropriate materials and hardware selections for specific functional considerations and usage.
- Prepare complete specifications for custom millwork.
