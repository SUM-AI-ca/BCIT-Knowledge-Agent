## Introduction to Importing MKTG 1325
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
Provides students with importing business basics. Discussion covers methods of sourcing overseas suppliers, assessing market potential, payment mechanisms and foreign exchange. Students will learn how to set up their own import businesses.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MKTG 1102

3.0
