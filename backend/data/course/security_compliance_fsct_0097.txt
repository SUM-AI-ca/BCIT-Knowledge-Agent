## Security Compliance FSCT 0097
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
​Security compliance focuses on protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information within an organization. This workshop will delve into the importance of security compliance, how organizations use compliance to benefit the organization and its members, and specific certifications that are common in the compliance industry.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Define compliance
- Describe the difference between security and compliance
- Explain how compliance programs are implemented in organizations
- Define and explain how the CIA triad relates to compliance
- Explain the importance of compliance
- Describe its benefits to an organization
- Describe its benefits to an organization's members
- Describe its benefits to an organization's customers
- List and describe compliance certifications:
- SOC2
- ISO
- FedRAMP
- PCI
- Cyber Essentials
- Understand how compliance affects YOU as a member of an organization​
