## CNC Operator Level 1 MACH 1130
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
Focuses on the identification of conventional and CNC machines: Terminology, axis of motion and coding. Includes the study of the coordinate system of CNC machines, CNC operation, and maintenance, tooling of CNC machine, basic programming skill for turning center and machine center, as well as canned cycles.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 3 years of machine shop experience and department pre-test.
