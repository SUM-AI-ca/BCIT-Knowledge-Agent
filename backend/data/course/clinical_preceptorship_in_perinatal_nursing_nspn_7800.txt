## Clinical Preceptorship in Perinatal Nursing NSPN 7800
Nursing Specialty Perinatal Course
## Course Overview
Presented as a clinical preceptorship, provides additional clinical experience. Bases learning intentions and evaluation strategies on individuals' learning needs, negotiated among you, the clinical tutor and the clinical site staff.
### Prerequisite(s)
- S in NSPN 7300 and S in NSPN 7500
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
## Learning Intentions
- Explore a selected area of perinatal nursing practice, identifying specific rationale for selection of this area of practice.
- Build on knowledge of childbearing women and their families, concepts of health and growth, family-centered care, and partnership.
- Employ processes of critical thinking, systematic inquiry, communication, collaboration, professionalism, and growth throughout the clinical preceptorship.
## Learning Outcomes
- Demonstrate growth in knowledge and skills in relation to a selected area of perinatal nursing practice.
- Demonstrate increased knowledge of childbearing women and their families, concepts of health and growth, family-centered care and partnership in relation to a selected area of perinatal nursing practice.
- Demonstrate growth in ability to engage in critical thinking, systematic inquiry, communication, collaboration, and professional with respect to a selected area of perinatal nursing practice.
