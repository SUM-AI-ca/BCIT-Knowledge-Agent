## Capstone: Leadership in Action CORP 2000
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed to empower students to apply the leadership and project management skills gained from the first two Microcredentials in a real-world team or departmental context. Students will complete an individual capstone project to diagnose organizational challenges or opportunities using proven leadership frameworks, design targeted interventions and develop actionable plans. They will select a focus area for their project—such as strategic planning, team culture development, productivity enhancement, or conflict resolution —and create professional artifacts alongside a comprehensive project plan.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- S in XLED 1012 and S in XLED 1022 and S in XLED 1061 and S in XOPM 1011 and S in XOPM 2011 and S in XCOR 1201 and S in XLED 1052 and S in XLED 1111
- or approval from the Program Head.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Diagnose organizational challenges within a team or department using relevant leadership frameworks.
- Design targeted interventions that address identified challenges and align with strategic objectives.
- Create professional artifacts such as project charters, measurement tools, process maps, and change management assessments to document and measure leadership initiatives.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of interventions through feedback protocols and outcome mapping.
- Deliver a presentation that articulates project outcomes, strategic recommendations, and organizational impact.​
