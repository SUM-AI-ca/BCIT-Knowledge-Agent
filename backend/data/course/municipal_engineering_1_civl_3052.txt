## Municipal Engineering 1 CIVL 3052
Civil Engineering Course
## Course Overview
​This course focuses on the general concepts of municipal engineering including subdivision planning, development servicing, environmental sustainability and cost analysis. Subdivision layout, off-site services such as water distribution, storm drainage, sanitary sewage, solid waste, roadways and environmental protection forms a major component of the course. Subdivision process, re-zoning, official community planning as well as relevant legislation and local government bylaws are discussed. Servicing costs, engineering economics, life cycle costing and asset management are also covered in the course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Prepare a subdivision plan for a residential development in compliance with the regulatory requirements. [4, 6, 9].
- Prepare a site servicing cost estimate. [4, 9].
- Lay out roadways for proper access to the lots in the subdivision. [4, 9, 11].
- Describe the municipal approval process for subdivisions and rezoning with an awareness of the applicable Acts and regulations. [8].
- Prepare a conceptual site servicing plan (roadworks, water, storm, sanitary) for a residential development. [4, 6, 11].
- Identify best practices in integrated stormwater management and environmental protection. [1,2].
- Identify best practices in sustainable solid waste management. [1,2].
- Conduct hydrograph routing analysis for sizing a storm retention/detention facility. [2,4] .​
