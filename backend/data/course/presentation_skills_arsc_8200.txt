## Presentation Skills ARSC 8200
Architectural Science Course
## Course Overview
The ability to make an effective presentation is now more essential than ever for individuals wanting to advance their career. This course will allow participants to analyze audience needs and integrate a variety of presentation techniques to deliver an effective oral presentation. The skills acquired in this course will also be part of the overall evaluation for the submission and presentation of a term project. It is important to note that this course adopts the assumption that practice and experience are the only ways to develop presentation skills. Therefore students are required to attend and actively participate in all classes and complete all assignments, however little to no reading will be assigned.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to
- Deliver effective professional presentations.
- Develop presentations through rehearsal.
- Be comfortable delivering presentations in front of an audience.
- Create presentations that clearly communicate complex ideas.
- Have a working knowledge of appropriate formats for professional presentations and when to use them.
- Speak with confidence.
- Energize yourself and the audience through eye contact, gestures and pacing.
- Create and use visual material in ways that support architectural presentations.
- Develop strong introductions and conclusions.
- Manage questions from the audience.
