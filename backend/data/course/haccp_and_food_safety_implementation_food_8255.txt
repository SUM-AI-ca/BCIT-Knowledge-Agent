## HACCP and Food Safety Implementation FOOD 8255
Food Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course looks at the implementation of HACCP and Food Safety systems from the paperwork side to the logistics of implementation, including team building and management/employee commitment to the system.
### Prerequisite(s)
- FOOD 6050 and FOOD 7030 and OPMT 7170
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Develop and implement a full HACCP plan that is effective across all business units in a given situation.
- Develop and implement a Quality System also as above.
- Coordinate the team functions required for the development, implementation and maintenance of a HACCP program or a Quality System based on a given situation.
- Audit the HACCP program or Quality System to validate and verify its effectiveness.
