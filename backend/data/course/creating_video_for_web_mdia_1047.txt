## Creating Video for Web MDIA 1047
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course deals with the procedures and processes involved in creating video material for the Internet. The course will cover camera shooting techniques, editing using iMovie, and delivery of your finished product to YouTube, and on a DVD using iDVD. Students will video tape, capture, edit and deliver a finished product online and on DVD.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Effectively shoot video with good composition and capture good location sound.
- Edit video using effects and transitions.
- Mix audio in post-production to sound more professional.
- Upload video files to the internet.
- Create a DVD with menus.
