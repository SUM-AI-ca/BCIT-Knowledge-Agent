## Manual Spray Finishing SCAF 1012
Surface Coating and Finishing Course
## Course Overview
A comprehensive, hands on program designed to give an individual knowledge and practical skills when setting up and using; air spray, HVLP systems, airless spray, and air-assist airless spray will be covered. Safety boots and glasses required.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in SCAF 1010
### Credits
5.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
To provide mechanical knowledge and practical skills to allow a student to successfully work in a finishing shop or production area.
The student will have knowledge and skills in the three basic types of spray equipment (Air Spray, Airless Spray and Air-assist Airless Spray), how they work and how they are set up for use. Proper spray techniques will be covered along with trouble shooting and repairs.
