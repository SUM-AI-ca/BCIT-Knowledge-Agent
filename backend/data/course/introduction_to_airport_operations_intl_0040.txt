## Introduction to Airport Operations INTL 0040
International Education Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed to introduce international trainees to the fundamental skills of Airport Operations. The content may vary depending on the focus and preferences of customized needs. International clients will study in classrooms and labs to gain experience in Airport Operations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Improve foundational technical literacy for the Airport Operations program.
- Introduction to airport and airside operations.
- Introduction to the airline industry.
- Apply basic skills required in Airport Operations.
