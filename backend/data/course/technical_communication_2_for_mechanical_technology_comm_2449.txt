## Technical Communication 2 for Mechanical Technology COMM 2449
Communication Course
## Course Overview
Applies techniques from COMM 1149 to produce a project proposal, evaluation or comparison report, a progress report and a major formal report. Meetings, interpersonal communication techniques and oral reports are integral parts of this term 4 course and technology project course must be taken concurrently.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in COMM 1149
