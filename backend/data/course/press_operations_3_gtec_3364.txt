## Press Operations 3 GTEC 3364
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
Printing multi colour and four-colour process jobs using single-colour and mid-size two-colour sheet-fed offset presses are the focus of this advanced printing course. Students identify press systems, adjust the press for optimum operation, perform maintenance, incorporate troubleshooting techniques, and consider quality control issues, as well as prepare and install dampeners and mix printing inks. Emphasis is placed on job scheduling and planning to maximize production and complete finishing operations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in GTEC 2364

3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use offset printing presses to produce commercially acceptable jobs.
- Make and install lithographic printing plates.
- Work as part of a team to complete the press make ready.
- Perform multiple checks for creating press ready proofs.
- Fully understand how to control ink and density settings.
- Continuously check press sheets as the press is running.
- Install a blanket on the press.
- Make and install dampers.
- Use anti-offset spray
- Participate in a group to prepare printed projects.
