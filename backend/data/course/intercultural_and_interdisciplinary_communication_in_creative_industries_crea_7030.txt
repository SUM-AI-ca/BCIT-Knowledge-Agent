## Intercultural and Interdisciplinary Communication in Creative Industries CREA 7030
Creative Industries Course
## Course Overview
​This course is an introduction to the dynamic forces that enhance effective communication between people from various cultural backgrounds. Students will learn how to facilitate intercultural and interdisciplinary communication effectively and mindfully in the creative industries workplace. Through the application of specific interpersonal techniques and theoretical perspectives, communication in the creative industries will be examined through the lenses of different cultural value systems. Power imbalances and their impact on intercultural communications will also be examined. Students will develop a critical perspective and analyze how to adjust and apply strategies and techniques for communicating more effectively regardless of cultural differences and perceived barriers. By helping students develop a truly global perspective, this course encourages intercultural communication competency (ICC). Due to rapid globalization, ICC is not an option, but a necessity.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the complex components of culture (such as traditions, customs, rituals, narratives, celebrations, artistic traditions, symbol, language, beliefs, norms, and values) in a creative industries' context.
- Identify perceptual barriers to intercultural competence in a creative industries' context (such as ethnocentrism, stereotyping, prejudice, racism, and discrimination) and the strategies to overcome them
- Develop intercultural competence and intelligence through knowledge, mindfulness, and skills (KMS)
- Apply theoretical perspectives of intercultural communication competence (ICC) in an intercultural and/or international creative industry setting, including group projects
- Analyze the importance and integration of global Indigenous histories and perspectives in the creative industries to de-colonize dominant cultural norms
- Differentiate between diverse global communication styles in relation to the creative industries
- Apply communication models, theories, and terms to professional communications in the creative industries, including facilitation of remote team collaboration and online meetings
- Create well-informed and interculturally competent digital media (presentations/infographics) to effectively address barriers to intercultural communication
- Apply knowledge of the dimensions of intercultural and interpersonal communication to assess a given culture and create a presentation for peers on best practices for business communication in this culture
- Integrate intercultural and interdisciplinary communication strategies that promote diversity, inclusion, and intercultural competence in the creative workplace
- Evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete, and to use critical thinking to develop strong arguments
- Apply strong technical writing and research skills to submissions including integration of appropriate referencing standards (APA). ​
