## CNC Operator Level 2 MACH 1131
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
Focuses on the operation of the CNC turning center and CNC milling center. Focus on a hands-on approach; students will expand on skills acquired in level 1. Students will study setting up machines, basic tooling and fixtures, manual programming, editing programming, and basic inspection method for a first-off work piece.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MACH 1130
