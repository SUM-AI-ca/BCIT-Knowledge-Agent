## Blueprint Reading MTLF 1240
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the basic principles of job specifications and blueprint reading. As you work through the course, you will study line types, isometric views, orthographic projections, dimensioning methods and general notes. Topics include using multi-page blueprints to locate information and solve problems in the shop, using shop drawings by correlating specifications and use math associated with blueprints.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1205

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Interpret shop drawings and site.
- Identify the components of a blueprint.
- Identify notes and dimensions on blueprint drawings.
- Interpret symbols and abbreviations found in blueprints.
- Construct three-dimensional objects from two-dimensional drawings.
- Layout copes, block cuts, bolt holes and clips based on information interpreted from drawings.
- Assemble structural components according to a shop drawing.
- Interpret technical information given on schedules and specifications on drawings.
