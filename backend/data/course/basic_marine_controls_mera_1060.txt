## Basic Marine Controls MERA 1060
Marine Engine Room Assistant Course
## Course Overview
​The course will ensure that students become familiar with the shipboard control circuits, major components and their function. The students can use these acquired skills to safely perform basic troubleshooting and maintenance on control systems aboard commercial vessels.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Transport Canada Candidate Document Number (CDN); Minimum 18 years of age at the start of the course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
​Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the basic principles of relays and Programable Logic Controllers (PLC's).
- Identify the main components of marine control systems, along with their function.
- Perform basic programing of PLC's.
- Perform basic troubleshooting and of marine control systems.​
