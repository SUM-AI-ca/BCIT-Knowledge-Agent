## Leveraging a Multi Generational Virtual Workplace CORP 0350
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
Generations are not defined as much by date of birth as they are about a collective group of individuals’ shared experiences that have shaped how that particular generation specifically views the world. Research calls these shared experiences imprints-- marks left by positive and negative pressures that influence behaviours, and both intrinsic and extrinsic motivations for each generation. This webinar explores how imprints impact the virtual world of work. If you understand imprints, then you can understand what motivates each generation in the new and dynamic multi generational virtual workplace.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this webinar, the participant will:
- Gain an understanding of several generations lasting imprints that translates into the virtual working environment
- Learn each generations Request, Work Credo and life Approach
- Explore what matters most and how it can enhance collaboration and engagement in a world of working remotely
