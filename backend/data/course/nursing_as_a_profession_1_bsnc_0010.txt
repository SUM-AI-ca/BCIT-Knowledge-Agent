## Nursing as a Profession 1 BSNC 0010
Nursing Course
## Course Overview
This course welcomes students to the BCIT BSN program and provides an overview of learning within a concept and competency-based curriculum. This course emphasizes foundational knowledge required to transition into a professional nursing program. Students will explore how to apply an indigenizing lens to concept and competency-based learning, and be introduced to approaches and strategies to help support success in the BSN program. This is a blended course, both online and in-class and will employ active learning strategies.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explore how to apply a decolonizing lens to concept and competency-based learning in the BSN program.
- Articulate how to be successful in a professional nursing program.
- Describe BSN concept and competency-based curriculum.
- Describe best practice approaches to concept and competency-based learning across different BSN learning environments.
Collaborator
- Identify roles and scope of practice of nurses and healthcare team members
Decision Maker
- Summarize features and benefit of a concept-based curriculum.
- Draw on knowledge from nursing and other sciences, humanities, research, ethics, spirituality, relational practice and critical inquiry in the practice of nursing care.
Leader
- Demonstrate knowledge of the role of the nurse as a leader/change agent/follower within a multidisciplinary health care team.
Professional
- Recognize and discusses personal biases and takes action to minimize the influence of positional power, personal values and beliefs on clients.
- Demonstrate self-care and personal development to build resilience.
- Maintain own physical, psychological and emotional fitness to practice.
