## People Resources at Airports AVAO 4480
Airport Operations Course
## Course Overview
This human resource course provides essential information for future employees working in the aviation environment. In addition to an overview of current human resource practices and programs students will learn about regulatory requirements surrounding recruitment, employment equity and harassment. Students review practical ways to conduct a personal job search including: job search strategies, cover letters, and resumes, and interview skills. Evaluations include a cover letter, resume and quiz.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the general function of human resources and its application in the airport environment.
- Describe the recruitment and employee orientation process.
- Describe reward/recognition programs and their importance in motivating employees.
- Describe the human resources administration functions.
- Discuss the labor relations environment in an aviation organization.
- Discuss appraisal systems and job search strategies.
- Create an effective cover letter and resume.
