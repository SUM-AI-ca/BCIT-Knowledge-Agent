## Effective Business Writing Workshop VENT 0039
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
Writing effectively is a skill you can learn and is crucial in today’s business environment. It is critical to communicate your thoughts clearly and concisely when writing to colleagues and customers. Whether participants want to refresh their memories or learn new techniques, this one-day workshop offers a practical approach to effective, day-to-day business writing.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify the purpose of a document and the intended audience.
- Define key messages by reviewing the mechanics and explore effective writing techniques.
- Proofread and edit a document for clarity and conciseness.
- Identify and correct common errors in grammar, spelling and punctuation.
- Write letters and emails with more confidence.
