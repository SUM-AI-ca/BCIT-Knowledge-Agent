## Gas Appliance Certificate of Qualification Program PPGS 0120
Piping, Plumbing, Gas Course
## Course Overview
The Gas Appliance C of Q Preparation course is designed to prepare people in the Appliance Service Technician trade for writing the British Columbia Safety Authority Gas Appliance Service Certificate of Qualification Examination (Formerly the Class “C” C of Q). This course will prepare students to service residential and light commercial gas appliances up to an input of 82kW, while employed by a licensed gas contractor or under an appropriate permit. It does not prepare students to do work not covered under the Gas Appliance C of Q such as installation of new or replacement appliances, the replacement of heat exchangers, the installation of gas piping or the repair or installation of venting.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe gas properties and combustion.
- Describe gas pipe sizing requirements.
- Describe combustion air and vent sizing.
- Perform meter clocking and orifice sizing.
- Describe the operation of gas pressure regulators.
- Describe atmospheric burner operation.
- Define the function of the Gas Safety Branch.
- Describe Gas Code/Regulations pertaining to permits, license and inspections.
- Describe safety practices for natural and propane gases.
- Apply regulations and safety practices.
- Describe the operation of electrical components on gas appliances.
- Describe the operation of gas controls.
- Describe the operation of gas appliances.
- Perform gas appliance service and repairs.
