## Overview

Formerly Distribution 2. Provides an overview of physical distribution, contracts and bills of lading, damage prevention and claims, dangerous goods transportation, marine cargo insurance, Canadian Customs, INCOTERMS and the North American Free Trade Agreement, (NAFTA), transportation computer applications, Canadian transportation regulations and an introduction to the field of logistics. Course fee includes the CITT exam fee. (Textbook not included)
