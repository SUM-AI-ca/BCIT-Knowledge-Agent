## Starting Your Own Business VENT 0100
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
BCIT's Starting Your Own Business program is suitable for those who are interested in examining the possibility of starting their own business, or for those who have already started but realize the importance of having a business plan. This course covers entrepreneurial characteristics, developing a marketing strategy, conducting market research, operating your business, financial projections, risk reduction, business plan development and much more. Based on the successful Venture Program, which has been developed over the past twenty years, this condensed course concentrates on need-to-know, street-level information. At the end of the course, participants will have the information required to allow them to create a plan for their business which will be reviewed by members of the Peter Thomson Centre for Venture Development team at the BCIT School of Business.

## Learning Outcomes
- Understand components/purpose of the Business Plan.
- Understand basic accounting skills.
- Acquire an ability to develop and analyze Financial Statements/Cash Flow.
- Understand what banks need from business owners in order to lend money.
- Develop a marketing strategy: product/service definition, market research, pricing, direct marketing.
- Understand and use negotiation and relationship selling as a tool to sell themselves and their products/services.
