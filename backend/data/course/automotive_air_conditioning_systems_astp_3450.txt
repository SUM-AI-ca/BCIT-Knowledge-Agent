## Automotive Air Conditioning Systems ASTP 3450
Automotive Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces you to the fundamental skills required to diagnose, service, and repair air conditioning systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe the principles of refrigeration.
- Describe the construction and operation of an automotive refrigeration system.
- Repair A/C system components.
- Perform A/C performance testing procedures.
- Discharge and charge A/C systems.
- Describe basic A/C and heater control systems.
- Test and service electronic climate control systems.
