## Advanced Concepts in MRI Imaging MIMG 7202
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
​Advanced Concepts in MRI Imaging is designed exclusively for MRI students who have completed foundational coursework and are ready to deepen their technical and clinical understanding of MRI. This course builds on core MRI physics and system knowledge to explore advanced signal encoding concepts, parameter optimization, artifact recognition and correction, and specialized MRI applications. Emphasis is placed on practical decision-making, problem-solving, and protocol optimization to support high-quality, efficient, and clinically relevant MRI examinations.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the principles of MRI signal encoding and decoding, including the role of Fourier transforms and k-space in data acquisition.
- Identify common MRI artifacts and analyze their underlying causes.
- Apply practical strategies to minimize or correct MRI artifacts through parameter adjustment, hardware selection, and patient management.
- Evaluate MRI parameter trade-offs involving signal-to-noise ratio, contrast, spatial resolution, and scan time.
- Optimize MRI protocols for specific clinical indications while maintaining image quality and efficiency.
- Describe the principles and clinical applications of vascular MRI techniques, including time-of-flight, phase contrast, and contrast-enhanced MR angiography.
- Explain the basic principles and clinical relevance of functional MRI and other specialized imaging techniques such as diffusion, perfusion, and spectroscopy.
- Apply quality assurance and quality control principles to support safe MRI operation and reliable diagnostic performance.​
