## Revit for Interior Design INTD 3260
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
​This course covers the basics of Autodesk Revit for Windows as used in the Interior Design industry, from design through construction documentation. Students will be introduced to the tools and concepts of working with a fully parametric building model. As part of this course, the student will complete a small commercial project. Delivery of the course will concentrate on real world approaches and methodology rather than step by step procedures.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Compare/contrast the advantages of the BIM process with conventional CAD drafting.
- Demonstrate familiarity with Revit tools for performing the following tasks:
- Create levels and grids
- Work with views and sheets
- Model walls, floors, ceilings, windows, doors, furniture, etc.
- Basic family creation
- Produce schedules and legends
- Create colour fill schemes based on rooms and areas
- Worksharing with Revit and understanding workset fundamentals
- Detail millwork in drafting views
- Detail millwork in drafting views.
- Create a set of commercial working drawings in the Revit environment, based on a preliminary/conceptual design.
