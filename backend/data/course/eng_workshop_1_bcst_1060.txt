## ENG Workshop 1 BCST 1060
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Build a solid foundation of technical and storytelling skills. Understand how to videotape a news story and learn what it takes to become a photojournalist. Hands on camera work will be critiqued on a one on one basis.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BCST 1162
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
- Understand the basic operation of an ENG camera.
- Learn how to cover news and current affairs.
- Gather sound for news stories.
- Light an interview subject.
- Work with a reporter.
