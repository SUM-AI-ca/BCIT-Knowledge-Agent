## Directed Studies BUSA 4905
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
Directed Studies is a project based course, designed to enable teams of students to apply the management concepts which they have learned directly to real work industry situations. Students will be provided with a Directed Studies package and a project which guides students through their role as "consultants: for the business organization in question. A faculty advisor will be assigned to each project team. Usually offered as the capstone or final course in the Management Systems or Human Resource Diploma Programs.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BUSA 4900 † ( † may be taken concurrently)
- BUSA 4900 or have written approval from Associate Dean.
