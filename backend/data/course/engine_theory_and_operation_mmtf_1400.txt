## Engine Theory and Operation MMTF 1400
Marine Mechanical Technician Course
## Course Overview
​In this course you will learn the history, theory and operation of the basic 2 stroke and 4 stroke internal combustion engines. In this course you will learn spark and compression ignition engines and their operating principles. Also in this course you will perform routine inspections on various 2 and 4 stroke gasoline and diesel engines.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in MMTF 1000
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the history, theory and operation of basic internal combustion engines
- Describe and identify the types of engines commonly in use in the modern marine industry
- Describe the routine inspection of engines and engine function
- Perform routine inspections of engines and engine function​
