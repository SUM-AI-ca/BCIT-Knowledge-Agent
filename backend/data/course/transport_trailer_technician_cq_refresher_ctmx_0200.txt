## Transport Trailer Technician CQ Refresher CTMX 0200
Commercial Transport Course
## Course Overview
Refreshes theoretical knowledge of the trade in preparation for the Transport Trailer Technician Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination. Contact the SkilledTradesBC (tel. 778-328-8700 or toll free within BC 1-866-660-6011) to determine eligibility and to schedule an examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Minimum of four-and-a-half years work experience in the trade
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be:
- Prepared to write the Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination;
- Able to perform practical tasks with a better understanding of component operation; and
- Able to write trade exams of various formats.
