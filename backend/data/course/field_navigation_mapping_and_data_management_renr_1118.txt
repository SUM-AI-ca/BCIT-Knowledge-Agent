## Field Navigation, Mapping and Data Management RENR 1118
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
​Field Navigation, Mapping and Data Management introduces students to various map series and imagery commonly used in BC resource management. The focus for the course is to develop introductory level skills related to the interpretation of thematic maps, contour maps and aerial photographs for a given field site to enable field navigation and data collection. Students will also practice using navigational grade GPS and pdf maps to identify the field location and features and record feature attribute information. Introductory GIS tools will used to map, and present field data. An integrated field project will include a mapping and digital presentation component, and a brief report describing the procedures used to create the map.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify common information that is found on contour, thematic, planimetric and cadastral maps.
- Recognize common topographic features on maps, aerial photographs, and imagery.
- Calculate distances, directions, slopes and areas on maps.
- Determine true north, magnetic north, and grid north for an area.
- Orient maps and imagery for planning and field applications.
- Use compass and map to complete field navigation tasks.
- Identify potential control (tie) points and/or monuments on aerial imagery and maps.
- Locate control (tie) points and/or monuments in the field.
- Plot map locations using common coordinate systems.
- Interpret common topographic features, linear features, and land use patterns from aerial imagery.
- Obtain maps and aerial imagery for a given area.
- Describe map projections, map datums and coordinate systems frequently used in resource mapping.
- Describe how map projections, map datums and coordinate systems impact field work and mapping.
- Complete digital file management tasks.
- Create spreadsheets to record, summarize, and chart data.
- Use mapping software in conjunction with navigational grade GNSS data.
- Use the National Topographic System (NTS) & British Columbia Geographic System (BCGS) indexing systems to acquire maps & data related to area.
- Use a navigational grade GNSS devices to navigate and collect data.
