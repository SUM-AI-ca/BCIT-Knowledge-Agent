## Surveying Highways Part 1: Horizontal Alignment TSYH 3311
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
The first of three courses covering the theory and computations involved in highway surveying computations and layout. Topics include horizontal alignment procedures and calculations, simple circular curves, compound, reverse and transitional curves.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 ¾ Layout Procedures for Control Points
- Define and describe layout procedures for control points, including:
- Offset method
- Polar method
- Intersection method with distances
- Intersection method with direction.
## Module 2 ¾ Introduction to Horizontal Curves
- Define and describe the types of horizontal curves.
## Module 3 ¾ Simple Circular Curves
- Define the elements of a circular curve.
- Define and discuss the layout procedures for circular curves, including:
- Circular curves and stationing
- Deflection angle method
- Offset method from a tangent
- Polar method from control stations.
- Identify and perform special computational problems for circular curves, including:
- A curve tangential to three given lines
- A curve tangential to two given lines passing through a point
- A curve tangential to a line and passing through two points
- A curve passing through three given points
- Intersection points of two curves
- Intersection of a curve and a straight line
- Areas within circular boundaries.
- Define the elements of compound and reverse curves and perform computations for:
- Compound and reverse curves between an existing straight line and a circular arc
- Compound and reverse curves between two existing circular curves
- Compound curves through a fixed point with given radii
- Reverse curves between parallel tangents
- Reverse curves between non-parallel tangents.
## Module 4 ¾ Compound and Reverse Curves
- Compound and reverse curves between an existing straight line and a circular arc.
- Compound and reverse curves between two existing circular curves.
- Compound curves through a fixed point with given radii.
- Reverse curves between parallel tangents.
- Reverse curves between non-parallel tangents.
## Module 5 ¾ Physical Foundations of Transition Curves
- Define and discuss the physical foundations of transition curves, the Mathematical Theory of Clothoid, the Elements of a Spiral and Non-symmetric Spirals.
- Employ the formula and perform computations for transition curves.
