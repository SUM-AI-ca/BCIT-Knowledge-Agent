## Design Studio 4: Comprehensive ARSC 8005
Architectural Science Course
## Course Overview
​The purpose of this studio is to execute comprehensive design for a large mixed-use high-performance building. Based on their pre-design brief, students will work through a series of exercises to develop industry-standard deliverables. The course models professional integrated design practices, including engagement with industry consultants and stakeholders. Students will demonstrate the ability to synthesize and apply knowledge and skills developed throughout their studies, applying leading-edge tools and techniques for high-performance building to a holistically sustainable architectural design, resolved from concept through to construction details.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Model a comprehensive integrated design process (including architectural, structural, mechanical and electrical) informed by appropriate architectural theory and best practice.
- Synthesize appropriate architectural precedents to inform design work.
- Combine design tools and techniques to produce an appropriate level of detail in each phase of the project.
- Develop a complex program considering client and user needs, appropriate precedents, and all applicable requirements.
- Leverage building design to contribute to a safe, rich and contextual public realm.
- Design a large mixed-use high-performance building based on a concept, client, program, and complex site.
- Synthesize structural, mechanical, material and environmental systems into a comprehensive high-performance building.
- Develop materiality, assemblies and architectural details to optimize building performance and sustainable design.
- Produce industry-standard documentation of the comprehensive building design project (including architectural, structural, mechanical, code, universal design, urban design) to an advanced level of detail.
- Apply site analysis to inform architectural form and fabric (including passive design strategies).
- Use architectural conventions to communicate through graphics, models and presentations.
- Apply all relevant building codes, regulations, and standards, including universal design.
- Engage in collaborative critique and iterative design processes.
- Demonstrate professional best practice individually and collaboratively.​
