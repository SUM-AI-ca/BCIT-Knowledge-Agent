## Code Calc for Tformer/Capctors TELC 0110
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
Includes conductor sizing, selection of overcurrent devices for single and three phase transformers. Also covering protection and sizing of conductors for capacitors.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
