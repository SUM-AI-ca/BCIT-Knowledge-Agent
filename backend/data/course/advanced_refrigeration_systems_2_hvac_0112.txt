## Advanced Refrigeration Systems 2 HVAC 0112
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
Last of a five-part series of HVAC systems courses. Tradespeople will sharpen their troubleshooting techniques on air conditioning systems and commercial refrigeration equipment by using computer simulations of a vast array of malfunctions commonly found in equipment today. Previous computer skills not required.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Four years work experience or HVAC 0106 Basic Refrigeration Systems 3.
