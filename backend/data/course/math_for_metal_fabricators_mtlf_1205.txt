## Math for Metal Fabricators MTLF 1205
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
In this course, you will learn how to use basic mathematics concepts and their use in metal fabrication such as, whole numbers, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, proportions, metric imperial conversions, Pythagoras Theorem, use of calculator, perimeter, area, volume basics. In addition, you will learn how to apply Geometric Line theory to develop parallel and intersecting lines for layout.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
- Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Solve problems with whole numbers.
- Solve mathematical problems using ratio and proportion.
- Calculate bend allowances when forming metal.
- Calculate area, mass, and volume of various shapes and sizes.
- Solve problems involving triangles and angles for various geometric construction, using the Pythagoras theorem and trigonometry
- Calculate weights of miscellaneous plate and structural shapes.
- Calculate costs for estimating a project.
