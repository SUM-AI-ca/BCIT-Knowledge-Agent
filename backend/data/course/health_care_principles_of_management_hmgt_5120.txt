## Health Care Principles of Management HMGT 5120
Health Management Course
## Course Overview
Discusses roles and functions of management. Provides the knowledge and skills required for planning, organization and control in health care agencies. Addresses such issues as organizational culture, multiculturalism, downsizing, total quality management and creativity. Uses the process of problem-solving throughout.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

3.0

## Learning Outcomes
The goal of the course is to introduce the technique of problem solving within the context of the management functions.
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the process of management and the roles of the manager.
- Identify the elements and the applications of the various schools of management thought.
- Analyze the external environment to determine factors that will influence managerial performance.
- State the elements of problem solving and apply them in the case study method.
- Describe the principles of planning and their application.
- Identify the components and approaches to strategic management and planning in organizations.
- Explain the functions of leadership, authority, responsibility and delegation.
- Explain the types of organizational structures and their associated advantages and disadvantages.
- Describe approaches to effective coordination factors affecting the span of management and approaches to organizational design.
- Explain the process of control and the various control methods that managers can use effectively.
- Identify and describe various types of financial control methods used in organizations.
