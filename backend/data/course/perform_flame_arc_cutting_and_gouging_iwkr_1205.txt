## Perform Flame, Arc Cutting and Gouging IWKR 1205
Ironworker Course
## Course Overview
When you receive materials at the site, the materials often need field modifications to be installed correctly. This could be due to incorrect fabrication in the shop, or a revision made in the field and the material needs to be re-fabricated because of the change. Regardless, the material has to be made to fit. To do this, you may need to cut the piece or gouge the welded material. This course will provide you with the skills to cut and gouge steel. Commonly you will be working with oxy-fuel as it can be used to cut and gouge carbon steels efficiently and precisely. In addition to the oxy-fuel process you will focus on working techniques for other metal-cutting processes, including Air Arc Cutting (AAC) and Plasma Arc Cutting (PAC).

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Describe different methods of cutting and gouging metals
- Describe safety considerations for cutting and gouging processes
- Describe how to operate and maintain cutting and gouging tools
- Demonstrate the set up and breakdown of oxy-fuel equipment
- Cut steel plate and structural shapes to dimension using oxy-fuel equipment
- Gouge bolted and welded material using oxy-fuel, AAC, and PAC processes
