## MRI Clinical 1 MIMG 4000
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
​This is the first of three MRI Clinical courses. MRI Clinical 1 allows the student to apply the knowledge from terms 1-3 in the MRI environment. In MRI Clinical 1, students will begin their orientation to the magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) environment. Students will be introduced to patient preparation, screening, and scanning in the MRI environment, as well as learning how to work effectively in the clinical environment while following safe practices. Students will also spend some time observing in a variety of other diagnostic imaging modalities.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Successful completion of all term 1, 2 and 3 courses

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
1. Practice in accordance with legislation, regulation, professional expectations and ethical guidelines.
The following concepts, skills, and issues are used to support this Outcome:
- Practice patient care that protects the patient's legal rights
- Comply with federal and provincial legislation and regulations affecting the practice of medical radiation technology
- Practice within provincial regulatory or national association scope of practice and code of ethics
- Demonstrate professional behavior and judgement
- Appraise decision options and take responsibility for decisions and actions
2. Practice safety protocols and guidelines
The following concepts, skills, and issues are used to support this Outcome:
- Provide a safe environment to minimize the risk of adverse events to patients and staff
- Demonstrate infection control protocols including routine and transmission-based precautions
- Verify completion of screening forms including screening and educating all personnel entering the MR environment
- Determine suitability of objects and items entering the MR environment
- Monitor and respond to potential bio-effects of static magnetic, gradient, and Radio Frequency (RF) fields
- Implement emergency procedures for quench, projectiles, and fire in the MR environment
3. Assist with the setup and disposal of Intravenous (IV) supplies and assess patients for suitable venipuncture sites.
The following concepts, skills, and issues are used to support this Outcome:
- Use aseptic technique
- Follow standardized procedures for handling and disposing of sharps, and contaminated and biohazardous materials
4. Operate current magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) equipment and follow quality control protocols.
The following concepts, skills, and issues are used to support this Outcome:
- Apply knowledge and utilize magnets, coil systems, cooling systems, image processors, computers, and ancillary equipment
- Assess and respond to the performance of RF coils, cryogen, magnet, and ancillary equipment
- Participate in quality assurance including applying principles of risk management
5. Analyze the relationship between MRI and the other Diagnostic Imaging (DI) departments.
The following concepts, skills, and issues are used to support this Outcome:
- Demonstrate basic knowledge of current and emerging issues in health
- Demonstrate basic knowledge of current and emerging practices and technological developments in the field of medical radiation technology
- Apply knowledge of other imaging modalities
6. Perform positioning of patients for MRI exams utilizing various coils and immobilization techniques.
The following concepts, skills, and issues are used to support this Outcome:
- Safely use equipment based on the patient's physical and cognitive status and resources available
- Safely use equipment based on the patient's physical and cognitive status and resources available
- Employ proper body mechanics to prevent harm to self and patient
- Use immobilization techniques based on age, physical and cognitive status of the patient and type of procedure
- Adapt procedures based upon patient's physical and cognitive condition
- Select optimal RF coil and prepare and position patient for procedure​
7. Perform scanning protocols for basic MRI exams.
The following concepts, skills, and issues are used to support this Outcome:
- Correlate clinical information, reports and previous imaging studies
- Select optimal RF coil and prepare and position patient for procedure
- Determine limit and extent of image coverage and utilize optimal imaging plane
- Select appropriate imaging parameters and options
- Apply specific absorption rate reduction practices
- Verify accuracy and completeness of acquired data​
