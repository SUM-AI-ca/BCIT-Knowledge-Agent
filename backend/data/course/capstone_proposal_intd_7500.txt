## Capstone Proposal INTD 7500
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
​In this course, students produce a programming document for an interior space of approximately 20,000 square feet, which will become the basis for a multi-purpose capstone project of social and sustainable merit. Students begin with research to develop original project statements on a specific topic related to interior design. Topics may include environmental, social, cultural, and economic concerns. In consultation with faculty and topic advisors, students will apply a variety of research methodologies to evaluate the needs of a specific demographic. Students will then develop parameters for an innovative and feasible design solution through a programming process that includes a functional space program and building & site analysis. Mentorship from a registered interior designer or registered architect will be sought for ongoing engagement during INTD 8500 Capstone Project. The course will conclude with a final written, verbal, and visual presentation to peers, faculty, and advisors, demonstrating a thorough understanding of the research and programming phase.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop clear, concise project statements that will become the basis for the capstone project.
- Apply creative, critical & analytical thinking as it relates to individual topics/projects.
- Demonstrate knowledge and relevance of contextual issues and human factors as they relate to individual topics/projects.
- Analyze research findings to create a functional space program.
- Demonstrate the ability to build upon critiques/findings through discussions with faculty, topic advisors, and peers.
- Review the functional space program to assess and select a suitable building for the proposed interior design project.
- Propose a location that supports the proposed program.
- Communicate through graphic and written documentation an understanding of the theories and principles of universal design, BC Building Code, life safety, and sustainability.
- Produce a schematic design proposal that incorporates a thorough synthesis of all data collected.
