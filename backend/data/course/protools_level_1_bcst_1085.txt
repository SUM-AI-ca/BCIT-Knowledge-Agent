## Protools Level 1 BCST 1085
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course will teach the student the fundamentals of Protools audio editing. Students will learn how to import audio sessions, create simple multitrack sessions, manipulate them within Protools, and export the finished project upon completion of the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Learn the fundamentals of Protools audio editing;
- Import audio sessions;
- Create simple multitrack sessions and manipulate them with Protools;
- Export the finished project.
