## Capstone Integrative Case FMGT 4900
Financial Management Course
## Course Overview
This is a capstone course which provides the opportunity to integrate various accounting disciplines including financial accounting, auditing, managerial accounting, finance and taxation through the use of an integrated case simulation. The objectives of this course are to provide students with: scenarios that are likely to be encountered and tasks that may be required to be completed upon graduation, opportunities to demonstrate effective time management and team skills, opportunities to apply the skills and knowledge they have obtained at BCIT in an integrative approach, and confidence that they have much to offer to a potential employer. This course will require students to work collaboratively in groups.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of Level 3.
### Credits
2.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Perform appropriate analysis for a given business situation
- Identify and rank issues that would be important to various users including decision makers
- Integrate information including the results of quantitative and qualitative analyses to evaluate alternative solutions and conclusions
- Recommend and justify solutions or conclusions based on an integrative view of information for the given situation
- Adapt communications to meet user and decision maker needs
- Exhibit professionalism in written communications and team interactions
