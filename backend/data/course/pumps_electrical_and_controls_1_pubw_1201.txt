## Pumps: Electrical and Controls 1 PUBW 1201
Public Works Course
## Course Overview
Covers electrical and controls systems commonly used in pumping stations, electrical supply, starters, switches and lock-out procedures. Hands-on sessions cover motors, bearing and maintenance procedures. Various control devices for level and flow control are demonstrated. Aspects of record keeping, public relations, safety, and some design considerations complete the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Grade 10 Math.
