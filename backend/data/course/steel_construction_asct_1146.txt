## Steel Construction ASCT 1146
Architectural and Structural CADD and Graphics Course
## Course Overview
The primary objective of this course is to broadly cover the subject of structural steel drafting, emphasizing the process of preparing structural steel design drawings and details for commercial and industrial building applications. The topics are chosen so as to provide the student with the knowledge and skills in working drawing conventions and techniques, construction systems and details, and building code requirements in regard to structural steel members. The course will also present an overview of basic structural steel design calculations through the examples of typical design calculations which will demonstrate the type of routine technical mathematic the entry-level structural drafter and designer can expect to encounter on the job. Students will be introduced to tables and reference information found in the Steel Construction Manual and Detailing for Steel Construction as well as Standard Specification Load Tables for Steel Joists and Joist Girders published by the Steel Joist Institute.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Produce anchor bolt plans and details, floor/foundation/roof framing plans and details, building sections, column base plate details and column schedules.
- Determine and apply to design drawings bolt size, spacing, end gauge, GOSL, cuts and copes
- Develop appropriate beam-to-beam and beam-to-column connection details such as sloped beam connection, skewed connection, moment-resisting connection, beam-to-girder seated connection, beam and joist pocket connection.
- Develop appropriate connection details in tilt-up construction.
- Create reinforcement plans and details for tilt-up panels.
- Use CISC and ASD manuals.
- Apply both ASD and LRFD methods of analysis and design of steel members.
- Design column base plates and framed-bolted connections.
- Select Open Web Steel Joist (OWSJ) and steel decking for a roof system.
