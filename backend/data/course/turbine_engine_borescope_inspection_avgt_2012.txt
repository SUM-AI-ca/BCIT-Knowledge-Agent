## Turbine Engine Borescope Inspection AVGT 2012
Gas Turbine Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed to advance your knowledge and skills acquired from AVGT 1006 . Activities focus on learning to borescope Gas Turbine Engines to determine serviceability.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- AVGT 1006
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Perform a borescope inspection on a turbine engine​
