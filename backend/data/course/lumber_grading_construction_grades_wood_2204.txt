## Lumber Grading - Construction Grades WOOD 2204
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
WOOD 2204 is a lumber grading course leading to attainment of an industry Lumber Grading Ticket (Lumber class) from a CLSAB accredited grading agency.
### Prerequisite(s)
- WOOD 1204
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Apply grade rules to Grade NLGA Para 112, 113 or 114 Boards.
- Apply grade rules to Grade Dimension Lumber to NGR Rules Para (121, 122, 124).
- Apply grade rules to Grade Dimension Lumber to NGR Rules Para (121, 122, 124).
Apply grade rules to Grade BS 4978.
