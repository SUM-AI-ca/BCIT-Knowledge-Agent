## Fundamentals of Electricity and Electronics TELC 1110
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
This course is a basic primer on the nature of electricity and how to troubleshoot simple systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TELC 1105
### Credits
10.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe simple electrical theory
- Solve simple electrical circuits
- Use a multimeter to test and verify circuits
- Identify and select basic electrical components
- Use relays and timers in typical security applications
