## Television & Video Production Full-time Diploma Program Entrance Exam BCST 0003
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Television & Video Production full-time diploma program applicants must successfully complete this exam with a minimum score of 50% in order to be eligible to apply to the program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

This examination assesses the following program-related criteria:

- Broadcast Industry awareness and aptitude
