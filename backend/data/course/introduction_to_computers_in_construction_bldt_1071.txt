## Introduction to Computers in Construction BLDT 1071
Construction Operations/Supervision Course
## Course Overview
Introduces basic understanding and use of computers. Students will learn to use Windows operating system for; file management and storage. Introduction to Mircrosoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint. Prerequisite, familiarity with Windows, and keyboarding skills. 1.5 credits.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
