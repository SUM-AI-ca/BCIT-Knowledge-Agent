## 3D Studio Max for Engineering 2 AICO 4015
Computer Aided Design & Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Advanced topics in object creation including lofting, boolean and other modifiers are presented. Elements of lights, cameras and material/texture definitions are incorporated in development of photo realistic scenes. Advanced rendering and animation techniques, as well as basic video post concepts are included.
### Prerequisite(s)
- AICO 4010 and familiarity with Microsoft Windows.
