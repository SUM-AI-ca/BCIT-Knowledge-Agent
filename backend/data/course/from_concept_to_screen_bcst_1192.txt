## From Concept to Screen BCST 1192
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
If you have an idea or concept for a TV show, mini-series, film, or series for the web, and want to turn your concept into a mini-bible, treatment, and/or script that you can pitch to producers, broadcasters, and funders, this course is for you.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze the theme, or core message, of their story.
- Interpret their central characters and their motivations.
- Assess what makes for good drama and identify the critical turning points of their story.
- Generate a treatment for a series for television, the web, or for a feature film.
- Plan the basic elements required before approaching producers, broadcasters, and funders.
- Develop the basics of good dialogue, which reflects character and sounds natural.
- Construct correctly formatted scenes and/or scripts for episodic entertainment or feature film.
