## What's New in Office 97 COMP 0262
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Presented for those who have or will be upgrading to Microsoft Word and Excel '95. Combines lectures and hands-on to focus on the new and improved features of Word and Excel '95 and discusses conversion issues.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Extensive experience in Excel and Word '95.
