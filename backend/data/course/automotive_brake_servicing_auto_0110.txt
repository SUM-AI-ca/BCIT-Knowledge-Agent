## Automotive Brake Servicing AUTO 0110
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Upgrades mechanics in brake servicing. Covers inspection and repair of disc/drum brake assemblies.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Mechanical experience. Steel-toed footwear and safety glasses required.
