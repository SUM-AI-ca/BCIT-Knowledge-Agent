## Customer Service Skills for Technicians AUTO 0162
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Upgrades entry-level automotive service technicians with customer relations skills. Focuses on essential communication skills to represent the employer in a positive and professional manner. Covers: customer expectations, professional conduct, customer interaction and satisfaction; conflict resolution; brand image, manufacturer warranty situations, and diagnostic questions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Represent the employer's vision and mission statement to the customer.
- Describe the impact that service procedures have on customer satisfaction.
- Use the telephone effectively and professionally.
- Support the service consultant by providing clear technical communication with the customer.
- Design and use diagnostic symptom charts in a customer process.
