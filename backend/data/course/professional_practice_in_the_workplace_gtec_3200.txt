## Professional Practice in the Workplace GTEC 3200
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course focuses on moral issues arising in the world of business, including businesses' responsibilities to employees, employees' responsibilities to employers, honesty in business and environmental responsibility. Emphasis will be placed on practical skills to assist in improving moral decision-making in the workplace. The importance of corporate social responsibility (CSR) and sustainability will also be communicated.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify with the ethical issues relevant to individuals, within the printing industry.
- Recognize the ethical issues relevant to the environment, within the printing industry.
- Utilize ethical reasoning skills to make sound decisions.
- Develop their own ethical decision making skills and apply them to recent cases.
- Assist others in improving moral decision making in the workplace.
