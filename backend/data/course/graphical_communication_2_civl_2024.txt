## Graphical Communication 2 CIVL 2024
Civil Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Through the medium of computer-aided drafting software, students continue to reproduce graphics examples from the civil engineering field. AutoCAD and other design software will be applied as a graphical tool to perform engineering modeling, solve civil engineering design problems, and produce design drawings. Applications in various civil engineering fields (municipal, structural, etc.) will be studied and investigated.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Produce engineering drawings using computer-aided drafting software. [5]
- Annotate drawings with more advanced text and dimensions commands. [5]
- Apply layers, line types and colours to enhance the presentation/appearance of drawings. [7]
- Employ knowledge of AutoCAD and Revit Structures to solve engineering design problems and perform engineering modeling. [5]
- Use blocks, attributes and external references to manage drawing files, organize design processes and collaborate with others. [5, 7]
- Create 3D objects and models. [5]
- Use AutoCAD and Revit 3D models to generate plans, elevations, and sections. [5, 7]
- Generate data (e.g. bill of materials, schedule of quantities and material takeoff) from 2D drawings and 3D models. [5]
- Use scanned images to facilitate the design process through accurate manipulation of rasterized data. [5]
- Transfer and exchange data between various engineering software packages. [5]
- Apply Building Information Modeling (BIM) concepts in engineering modeling and design. [5]
Effective as of Fall 2018
