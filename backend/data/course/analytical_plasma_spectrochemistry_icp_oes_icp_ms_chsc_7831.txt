## Analytical Plasma Spectrochemistry (ICP-OES/ICP-MS) CHSC 7831
Chemical Sciences Course
## Course Overview
An introduction to plasma spectrochemical techniques for chemical analysis; principally inductively coupled plasma optical emission spectrometry (ICP-OES) and inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry (ICP-MS). Applications to environmental, pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, biochemical, agricultural, geological and industrial materials are discussed.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Graduation from University or Institute of Technology in a science program or by special permission based on consideration of work experience and post secondary education.
