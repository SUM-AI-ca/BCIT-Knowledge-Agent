## Manage Financial Resources MGMT 8420
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates financial management skills, such as formulating financial analysis, making financial decisions by understanding financial models, managing costs and financial operations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in FMGT 1100 or 50% in FMGT 1152
