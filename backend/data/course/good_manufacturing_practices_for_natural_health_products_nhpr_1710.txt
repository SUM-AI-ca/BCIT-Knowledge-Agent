## Good Manufacturing Practices for Natural Health Products NHPR 1710
Natural Health Products Course
## Course Overview
This course will present GMP concepts and their practical application in the natural health product industry. With a focus on production, operations, validation and the product specification aspects of GMP requirements, this practical course will be an excellent guide to assist companies in complying with Health Canada's new NHP regulations. Attendees will receive practical knowledge of the NHP GMPs and the regulatory requirements for the manufacture, distribution, importation and packaging of NHPs in Canada.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will:
- Understand NHP GMP requirements.
- Be able to utilize the basic principals of GMP in the activities in their work place.
- Be able to apply methods and techniques to handle GMP inspections and audits.
