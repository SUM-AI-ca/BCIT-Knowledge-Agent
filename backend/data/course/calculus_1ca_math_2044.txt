## Calculus: 1Ca MATH 2044
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
Covers differentiation and integration of trigonometric, logarithmic and exponential functions. Does not cover all basic methods of integration nor any differential equations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MATH 2042 or approved equivalent.

1.5
