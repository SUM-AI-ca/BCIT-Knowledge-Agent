## Pulp and Paper Summer Institute VENT 0300
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
The Pulp and Paper Technology Summer Institute is a five-day program designed to provide an overview of the process and technology of pulp and paper manufacture together with current innovations in technology. Speakers have been drawn from pulp and paper and related industries. Format will be presentations on topics including principles and methods of manufacture and other special topics, in the form of lectures, mini-papers, product displays, technical bulletins audiovisual presentations, question and answer sessions, and mill tours. Attendees are pulp and paper professionals, as well as non-technical personnel and students. Visit the website at www.pulp.bcit.ca .
