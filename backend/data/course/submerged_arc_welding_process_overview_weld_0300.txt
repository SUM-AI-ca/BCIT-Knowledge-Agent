## Submerged Arc Welding Process Overview WELD 0300
Welding Course
## Course Overview
Students will explore comprehensive material on the submerged arc welding process (SAW), which will include an overview of single, twin, and tandem submerged arc welding processes, SAW metallurgy, waveform controlled AC SAW, SAW electrode and flux, SAW defects, SAW welding of stainless and nickel alloys, joint design for SAW, and electroslag cladding.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the effect of variables for the SAW process.
- Explain electrode and flux selection.
- Identify SAW defects and implement corrective action.
