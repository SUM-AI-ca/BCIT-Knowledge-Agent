## Electronic Diesel Fuel Management AUTO 0129
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Introduces automotive and light duty truck electronic diesel fuel management systems. Covers: safety precautions; electronic diesel fuel system theory of operation; diagnosis; and servicing. Coverage includes vehicles, built from 1994 - present, using electronic diesel fuel injection.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Minimum 3rd year apprentice or B.C. TQ in Automotive.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the theory of operation of electronic diesel fuel management systems.
- Diagnose and repair electronic diesel fuel management system components.
- Identify and demonstrate the operation and maintenance of electronic diesel fuel management systems.
- Perform routine maintenance to electronic diesel fuel management systems.
