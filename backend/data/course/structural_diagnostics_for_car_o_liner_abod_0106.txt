## Structural Diagnostics for CAR-O-LINER ABOD 0106
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for automotive collision repair technicians who have some prior experience working with CAR-O-LINER equipment. The concept of damage analysis will be covered in the classroom and explained in greater detail using the CAR-O-LINER measuring system. The technician will have an opportunity to set up a vehicle using the CAR-O-LINER bench rack and analyze damage using the EVO 1, 2 and 3 systems. Training will also include set-up and use of the Vision2 X3. Training is recognized as an ICBC c.a.r. shop technician upgrade course and counts as twelve hours towards your accreditation status.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify the different components of the vehicle's programmed energy management system and explain how they affect vehicle repairs.
- Identify and describe the different classifications for collision damage.
- Identify and explain the three dimensions of measuring as well as how CAR-O-LINER develops its data sheets and the components of the data sheet.
- Identify and demonstrate the proper clamping techniques for both unitized and full frame vehicles using CAR-O-LINER clamping components.
- Identify the different components of the Car-O-Tronic measuring system as well as proper centering and measuring techniques for both under and upper body measuring.
- Demonstrate how to center and measure a vehicle without a data sheet.
- Demonstrate how to make a diagnosis of the damage using Car-O-Tronic software.
- Identify the components of EVO 1, 2 & 3 and demonstrate their use when extra holding is required.
