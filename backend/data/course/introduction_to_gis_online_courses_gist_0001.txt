## Introduction to GIS Online courses GIST 0001
Geographic Information Systems Course
## Course Overview
Introduces GIS online students to the BCIT GIS online learning environment with Desire2Learn and Appsanywhere.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Connect with online courses within Desire2Learn.
- Resolve connection and technical issues.
- Take self-test and quizzes.
- Upload assignments.
- Post and read messages in discussions.
- Connect to Appswhere and use GIS software.
