## Sustainability Practices for Graphic Communications GCOM 3040
Graphic Communications Course
## Course Overview
By designing and producing a sustainability report, students will learn sustainable practices, the sourcing of ethical materials, and the environmental certifications in the graphic communications industry. Gathering information from the GCOM Lab environment, students will analyze data from the building, manufacturing equipment, and material selections. Safety in the graphic communication industry will be introduced, using the lab to identify proper workplace safety practices, and identifying potential risks. Students will study ethical decision making and how it affects the individual, the company, the community, and how these decisions shape a company’s moral compass as well as our own legacy.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Gather relevant and reliable data for a GHG Report.
- Analyze data to be used in a sustainability report.
- Create a sustainability report.
- Describe the ethical implications of waste streams in the Graphic Communications industry?
- Compare ethical campaigns and how they can be a differentiating factor in business.
- Differentiate and choose between sustainable material options.
- Make sustainable choices for paper products based on Environmental Certifications used in the Graphic Communications Industry.
- Define the rights, roles, and responsibilities of employees, companies, and government.
- Identify risks and hazards in the workplace.
- Articulate how ethical and environmental business decisions can impact the individual, the employer, and their community
