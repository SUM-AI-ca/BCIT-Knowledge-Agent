## Lamination Techniques for Prosthetics PROR 1030
Prosthetics and Orthotics Course
## Course Overview
​​In this course, students will learn technical skills related to lamination ​for the fabrication of prosthetic devices. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the principles and purpose of lamination in prosthetic socket fabrication and their impact on socket performance.
- Describe common lamination materials, equipment, and their properties, uses, advantages, limitations, and associated safety protocols.
- Select and justify appropriate lamination materials and lay-up approaches based on prosthetic design scenarios, including transtibial and transfemoral applications.
- Describe and sequence the steps required to prepare a socket model and complete the lamination process according to established fabrication & safety protocols.
- Evaluate laminated prosthetic sockets for structural integrity and quality based on visual and descriptive evidence.
- Identify common lamination defects and recommend appropriate corrective strategies based on case-based examples. ​​
