## Essentials of Residential Electrification Planning and Pathways XZEB 1155
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
​This course focuses on the decision-making for electrification in both new construction and existing Part 9 Buildings homes. Learners will explore efficient electrification solutions and apply knowledge to consider electrification pathways for 'smart electrification' of new and existing homes in a variety of Canadian contexts. Topics introduced include calculated vs. demonstrated (measured) home electrical service capacity in the Canadian Electrical Code, power requirements and energy use of various technologies and home energy management solutions. Learning will cover a range of high-efficiency solutions including electric space heating, cooling, domestic hot water, electrified consumer appliances, electric vehicle charging, energy and load management systems, and on-site renewable electricity generation and storage. Through applied examples and real project data, participants will compare electrification technologies and solutions to practice how to evaluate and develop home electrification pathways tailored to the unique needs of homes and homeowners across Canada to maximize value for residential electrification projects.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the regulatory and market drivers influencing home electrification
- Compare the differences in electrical loads in existing homes and Net Zero Homes including space conditioning, domestic hot water, electrified consumer appliances and other connected loads, and electric vehicle charging
- Discuss how residential electrical fundamentals and service components relate to electrical service upgrades and system sizing
- Contrast Canadian Electrical Code (CEC) electrical service sizing concepts to electric utility distribution design practices, including:
- How qualified persons calculate remining service capacity and allowable conventional approach (calculated loads)
- How historical load measurement approach (demonstrated load) can be applied in existing homes in some jurisdictions
- Compare home electrification technologies and solutions on home performance and electrical service requirements, including consumption and load capacity through case studies and examples
- Communicate electrical service sizing requirements and the importance of:
- Reducing and shifting electrical loads
- Selecting smaller, more efficient equipment
- Using load demand control strategies (load share devices, circuit pausers, smart electrical panels)
- Discuss early new construction design or retrofit planning information and decisions that are required to support 'smart electrification' to provide lowest cost and best value pathways to electrification today or in the future​
