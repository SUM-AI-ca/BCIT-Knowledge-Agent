## Graphic Communications Industry Overview GCOM 1000
Graphic Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the graphic communications industry, its terminology, production processes, and trends and provides for some first-hand knowledge and experience through guest lectures and visits to modern production facilities. Students will gain an understanding of the local marketplace and begin to develop their professional network. Finally, potential roles and career paths in the graphic communications industry will be explored.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the evolution and history of the graphic communications industry.
- List the various occupations within the graphic communications industry.
- Describe the many and varied imaging technologies within the industry.
- Describe a varied and broad range of employment opportunities within the industry.
- Define the key terms in the industry.
- Describe the products used in the manufacture of print such as paper, ink, and technology.
- Develop a database of imaging industry contacts for professional networking.
- Explain why different departments, within a printing company, need to communicate with each other.
- Describe the processes of a printed piece from design concept through to a final finished product.
- Describe the importance of estimating to the overall success of a printing operation.​
