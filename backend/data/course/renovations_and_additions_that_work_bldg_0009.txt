## Renovations and Additions That Work BLDG 0009
Building Course
## Course Overview
Prepares homeowners to plan and execute a successful residential renovation. Focuses on realistic assessment of the project scope, costs, and scheduling. Other topics include ensuring practical design, choosing a contractor, selecting building materials, and avoiding common pitfalls. Includes industry-expert guest speakers.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify their project goals and limitations.
- Recognize and ensure functional design.
- Confidently select building materials and finishes.
- Choose contractors and contracts wisely.
- Expect the unexpected.
