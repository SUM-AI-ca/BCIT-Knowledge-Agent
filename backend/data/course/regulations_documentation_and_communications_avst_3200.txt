## Regulations, Documentation, and Communications AVST 3200
Aircraft Structures Course
## Course Overview
​Aircraft Structural Maintenance Engineers must understand and interpret documentation published by both aircraft manufacturers and government regulatory bodies. This course offers an in-depth examination of these critical documents. Effective communication in the workplace is essential, as miscommunication can lead to serious or even fatal consequences. This course also equips graduates with the skills necessary for clear and precise oral and written communication in a professional aviation environment.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe various aircraft technical records and perform applicable data entries
- Interpret Airworthiness Directives and Service Bulletins and assess their impact on maintenance procedures.
- Describe the purpose and structure of manufacturer's Structural Repair Manuals
- Use Type Certificate Data Sheet specifications to verify aircraft limitations and configuration compliance pertaining to Supplementary Type Certificates
- Describe the Canadian Aviation Regulations as they pertain to an AME
- Communicate technical information clearly and professionally.​
