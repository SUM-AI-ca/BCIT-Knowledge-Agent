## Pattern Development MTLF 1245
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
Plate development is a geometrical method of developing flat, two dimensional patterns for cylindrical and rectangular shaped objects. These objects can be in the form of piping, vessels, tanks, ducting, etc. In this course, you will learn to develop patterns using parallel and intersecting lines in the creation of templates for headers, branches, miters, tees, elbows, and copes and apply those templates to shop projects.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1205 and MTLF 1210 and MTLF 1240

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Perform calculations necessary to develop template.
- Determine the dimensions required to develop a given template..
- Develop templates for shop fabrication projects.
