## Construction Estimating 2 BLDG 3300
Building Course
## Course Overview
This course covers measurement and pricing of various items of work (concrete, structural steel, wood framing, roofing, and finishes), pricing of work using productivity references, supplier and subcontractor bids, and own-forces cost. Green material alternatives are presented and their effect on construction estimating is discussed.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BLDG 2200 and 50% in BLDG 2300
### Credits
4.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Measure and price items of work in:
- Division 3 - Concrete
- Division 5 - Structural Steel
- Division 6 - Rough Carpentry
- Division 7 - Roofing
- Division 9 - Gypsum Board
- Use Cost References and Cost Accounting techniques to determine unit prices for various items of work.
