## Interior Finishing Carpentry for Renovators CARP 1020
Carpentry Course
## Course Overview
Intended for those who wish to enhance their Interior Finishing abilities. Will feature instruction on numerous Interior Finishing topics, including: (1) the installation of doors and windows (2) crown and baseboard moulding (3) trim, and (4) wainscotting.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
