## Student Success Survive/Thrive NTRY 0303
Technology Entry Course
## Course Overview
Prepares students to manage the BCIT workload and pace. This 30-hour course will help you learn more efficiently and effectively. It offers success strategies in reading, note taking, memory improvement, time and stress management, test writing, and working in groups. The practical ideas and techniques will benefit current and future students-at BCIT and in the workplace.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
