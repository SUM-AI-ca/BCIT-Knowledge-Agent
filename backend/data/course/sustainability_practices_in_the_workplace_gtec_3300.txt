## Sustainability Practices in the Workplace GTEC 3300
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
Sustainability Practices in the Workplace focuses on moral issues arising in the world of business, including businesses’ responsibilities to employees, employees’ responsibilities to employers, honesty in business, and environmental responsibility. Emphasis will be placed on practical skills to assist in improving moral decision-making in the workplace. The importance of Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) and sustainability will also be communicated. Students will discuss and evaluate several case studies in modern business settings to practice sustainable decision making in a business setting. This course focuses on the importance of communicating effectively with customers utilizing Customer Relationship Management techniques and principles.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Identify with the ethical issues relevant to individuals, within the printing industry.
- Recognize the ethical issues relevant to the environment, within the printing industry.
- Utilize ethical reasoning skills to make sound decisions.
- Develop their own ethical decision making skills and apply them to recent cases.
- Assist others in improving moral decision making in the workplace.
- Evaluate CRM software and maintain a professional CRM database for their own professional networking.
