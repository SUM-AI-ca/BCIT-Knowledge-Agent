## Business Computer Skills COMP 1900
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Uses lecture and hands-on computer time to develop skills in Windows-based file management, word- processing, spreadsheets, and presentation graphics. Also includes using the Internet as a gateway to computer conferencing.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
