## Portfolio Production MDIA 2095
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Participants consolidate their experience, learning, and talent by compiling a portfolio that focuses on individual interest and strength, and showcasing their work. Prepare an effective medium to assist in furthering their careers, as well as identify professional development needs and set future goals. Recommended last course in Certificate.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
