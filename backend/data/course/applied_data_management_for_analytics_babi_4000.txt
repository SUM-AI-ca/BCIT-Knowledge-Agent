## Applied Data Management for Analytics BABI 4000
Business Analytics & Business Intelligence Course
## Course Overview
This course will introduce students to the fundamentals of data management for analytics. Students will learn how to use Microsoft Power Automate and other data management tools to collect, store, clean, and transform data. They will also learn how to integrate data from multiple sources and prepare data for analysis using various techniques. Students will have the opportunity to work with real-world datasets and build hands-on skills in data management and analysis. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- BABI 3000
### Credits
5.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the fundamentals of data management and its importance in the IT industry.
- Use Microsoft Power Automate and leading data management tools to collect, store, and manage data in an IT environment.
- Apply the latest techniques for cleaning and transforming data to prepare it for analysis in a business environment.
- Integrate multi-source data and identify data quality issues in an IT environment using current techniques.
- Analyze business operational data using current techniques and tools.
- Build professional visualizations and dashboards to communicate insights from data in real-world settings.
- Use best practices for data management and analysis to ensure data quality and reliability in industry.​
