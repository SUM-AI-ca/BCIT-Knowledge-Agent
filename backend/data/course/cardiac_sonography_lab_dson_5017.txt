## Cardiac Sonography Lab 2 DSON 5017
Diagnostic Medical Sonography Course
## Course Overview
​ DSON 5017 prepares the student for entry into the clinical environment. This course is a lab course with an emphasis on patient care and performing full echo examinations. In the patient care portion of the course, the students will be introduced to health and safety measures, learn how to complete a variety of basic healthcare procedures, and develop competency in the use of equipment commonly used in various healthcare settings. In the cardiac scan lab, students will complete full echocardiograms, including patient histories, modifications required to assess for pathology, and documentation of findings. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the Sonography Lab component, the student will be able to:
- Complete a full echocardiogram including 3D imaging, in a timely manner, including assessment of medical history and health status, formulation of scanning strategies, and modifications based on history and findings. (5.1b, 5.1c, 5.1d, 4.2h)
- Recognize and modify the examination based on sonographic evidence, abnormal findings, clinical information, resource implications, and other contextual factors. (5.3k, 5.3j)
- Identify and respond to urgent sonographic findings. (2.2h)
- While scanning in the lab, use appropriate body language, respond to non-verbal behaviours, and apply conflict resolution strategies. (1.3a, 1.3b, 1.1l)
- Verify patient identification and ensure the requested procedure correlates with the patient's clinical history. (2.2a, 2.2b)
- Evaluate patient preparation for the exam, patient's ability to tolerate the exam, and contraindications to the procedure. (2.2c, 2.2f, 3.1b)
- Ensure all applicable components of the exam are complete, diagnostic, and accurately documented. (5.3l,5.4a)
- Ensure that documentation is timely, accurate, concise, and complete. (1.2d)
- Use medical terminology and standard abbreviations in oral communication. (1.1k)
- Provide and receive feedback in a professional manner. (2.3f)
Upon successful completion of the Patient Care Lab component, the student will be able to:
- Measure blood pressure. (3.3a)
- Using proper body mechanics, ensure safe and comfortable patient transporting, transferring, and/or positioning. (6.2a, 3.1a)
- Engage in practices to promote own physical and environmental safety. (6.2c)
- Recognize, respond to, and disclose adverse events and emergency situations. (2.3l, 3.1j)
- Provide care for a patient in a manner that is respectful of individual diversity. (2.3b)
- Maintain awareness of patient's accessory equipment and act as required. (3.1i)
- Employ sterile technique. (3.1c)
- Perform IV starts on a mannequin arm. (3.3a)
- Follow standardized procedures for handling and disposing of sharps and contaminated and biohazardous materials.
- Demonstrate knowledge of Basic Life Support training or equivalent. (3.1K)
- Perform all required images and measurements of a cardiac ultrasound examination on simple, technically straightforward patients with some problem-solving, minimal guidance, critical thinking and time management. In addition to all structures learned prior to this course, students will now also understand how to assess abdominal situs, cardiac position, the chest and thorax (Appendix E).​
