## Advanced Digital Darkroom MDIA 3012
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Advanced Digital Darkroom Production covers the fundamentals of digital images acquired from digital images, masking, correcting, and compositions. The focus of this course is applying real world techniques used in fashion, flyer, and catalogue production by many companies marketing their products. Focus is on hi quality production results using Adobe Photoshop CS6.0. Topics include colour theory as it relates to various media delivery systems, resolution, digital image formats, and the masking & colour correction process. The course will be provided in a lecture/lab format with in-class exercises providing hands-on experience.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MDIA 1170 and MDIA 2012 and MDIA 2270

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Grasp the understanding of how to qualify and adjust image dynamic range, color and contrast
- Achieve the best image quality for a commercial production environment.
- Explain the difference between additive and subtractive colour and make appropriate choices for colour based on the media delivery systems.
- Mix specific colours using RGB, CMYK, LAB, Pantone values, to targeted commercial results.
- Analyse and use different selection tools and alpha channels to create quality production ready masks in Photoshop
- Use the channels and layers panels to manipulate colour, and image composition
- Paint and colour correct selections within images using a variety of tools and brushes
- Use the technique of adding touch plates, a custom ink to CMYK images to expand the colour gamut or increase the impact of selected portions of marketing images on a printing press.
- Combine images seamlessly together with layer masks, and channels
- Use the Hue & Saturation, Selective colour, Curves and Levels to adjust colour
- Retouch images with blemishes, repair colour and flaws from old damaged photos from scanned material
- Make appropriate colour management settings in Adobe Creative Suite 5x.
- Close crop, mask and replace backgrounds, rotate, straighten and resize digital images.
- Perform basic retouching tasks, removing blemishes, reducing wrinkles, balance flesh tone colours
- Maximize the tonal range in a digital image, correct and repair images with bad casts, and poor exposer
- Establish correct neutral tones in a digital images, choose appropriate GCR for best reproduction based on the main focus of each image to ensure best results in a production environment
- Image manipulation starting with Adobe Camera Raw
- Introduction to why colour management, rendering intents, process control
- Applying ICC Profiles for different production requirements including digital press technologies, wide output format and digital media
