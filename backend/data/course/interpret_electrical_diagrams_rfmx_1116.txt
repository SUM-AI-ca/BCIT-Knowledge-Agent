## Interpret Electrical Diagrams RFMX 1116
Refrigeration Mechanic Course
## Course Overview
This course covers the basic concepts of electrical circuits and symbols which are found on all HVAC and refrigeration systems. A complete understanding of electrical diagrams and the ability to interpret them is of utmost importance for successful career in the industry. As a result, students spend a lot of time working with a variety of diagrams.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify and describe the features of simple drawings for electrical circuits, including:
- Pictorial diagrams
- Block diagrams
- One-line diagrams
- Wiring diagrams
- Schematic diagrams
- Read and interpret electrical diagrams.
- Troubleshoot electrical circuits.
