## Kiln Building Training CARP 0104
Carpentry Course
## Course Overview
Through this special on-site, two-week training course, selected community members from First Nations communities will gain the essential practical skills and hands-on experience to build the value-added dry kiln.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
