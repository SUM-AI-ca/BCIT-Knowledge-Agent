## Certification Exam Preparation MRAD 4103
Medical Radiography Course
## Course Overview
This course builds upon the core curriculum and provides the opportunity for students to integrate and synthesize the knowledge, skills, judgments, and attitudes gained through the various BCIT Medical Radiography courses and clinical practice sessions. Students will use a variety of academic learning tools to solidify knowledge and further develop critical thinking skills towards making informed decisions in the clinical setting. Through guided self‐study and assessments, students will develop effective study skills to prepare for the CAMRT certification examination and improve the necessary analytical skills required in the clinical setting.
### Prerequisite(s)
- All MRAD Term 5 courses
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Analyze the CAMRT competency profile requirements and relate them to the certification process.
- Develop a personal study work plan based on CAMRT competencies and program curriculum.
- Apply program curriculum in the analysis of various patient scenarios.
- Attempt comprehensive competency exams based on program curriculum.
- Develop a personal knowledge profile through reflective practice indicating curriculum strengths and content requiring further study.
Effective as of Fall 2023
