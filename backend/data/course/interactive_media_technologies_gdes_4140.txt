## Interactive Media Technologies GDES 4140
Graphic Design Course
## Course Overview
​This course explores the forefront of interactive media and emerging technologies, emphasizing their innovative application in graphic design. Students will engage with a variety of advanced tools and techniques, learning how to integrate these technologies into their design projects to create interactive, engaging, and user-centric experiences. The skills and knowledge gained in this course will empower students to expand their creative projects, utilizing cutting-edge technologies to push the boundaries of traditional design practices. Through both theoretical study and practical projects, students will be equipped to stay ahead of industry trends and adapt to new technological advancements in the field of interactive media. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Design and implement interactive media projects using emerging technologies and tools to innovate traditional graphic design practices.
- Create immersive experiences that enhance storytelling and user engagement using advanced interactive media technologies.
- Critically assess new technologies for their potential applications and limitations in graphic design.
- Demonstrate project management skills by planning, executing, and presenting interactive media projects within set deadlines and specifications.
- Identify the legal and regulatory frameworks relevant to emerging technologies, ensuring compliance in design projects.
- Develop prototypes for interactive media projects, conducting iterative testing and refining based on user feedback.
- Incorporate stock assets to develop original content, integrating them seamlessly into interactive media projects to enhance design and functionality.
