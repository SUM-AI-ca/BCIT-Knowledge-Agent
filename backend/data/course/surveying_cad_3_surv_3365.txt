## Surveying CAD 3 SURV 3365
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the object based CAD drawing environment. Students will create and edit a variety of object styles; import, edit and display digital data from a variety of sources; use coordinate geometry tools; create scaled plots.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in SURV 2265
