## English for Professionals-CAD COMM 0013
Communication Course
## Course Overview
Learn how to write clear and correct letters, memos, and e-mail. This course is designed for professionals using CAD who speak English as an additional language and who would like to imporve their written communication skills. You will practice your writing skills by composing and revising typical documents found in the Canadian workplace.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
