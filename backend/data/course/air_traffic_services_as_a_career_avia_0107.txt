## Air Traffic Services as a Career AVIA 0107
Aviation Course
## Course Overview
This 3 hour introductory session is designed to tantalize individuals who have an interest in Air Navigation, but do not have sufficient knowledge of the opportunities available in this exciting and dynamic industry. Participants will be introduced to areas of practice and future careers available in Air Traffic Services such as Air Traffic/ Area Controller and Flight Service Specialist.

- No prerequisites are required for this course.
