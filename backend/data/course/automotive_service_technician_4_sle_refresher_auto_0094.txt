## Automotive Service Technician 4 SLE Refresher AUTO 0094
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
​Refreshes theoretical knowledge of the trade in preparation for the Automotive Service Technician 4 standardized level examination (SLE). Topics covered will include: diesel engine support systems, driveline systems, electrical systems, HVAC and comfort control systems, restraint systems, body components, accessories and trim, and hybrid and electric vehicles. Contact the SkilledTradesBC (tel. 778-328-8700 or toll free within BC 1-866-660-6011) to determine eligibility and to schedule an examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Working experience or prior training on diesel engine support systems, driveline systems, electrical systems, HVAC and comfort control systems, restraint systems, body components, accessories and trim, and hybrid and electric vehicles.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be to:
- Prepare to write the standardized level examination for Automotive Service Technician 4.
- Perform practical tasks with a better understanding of component operation.
- Be familiar with trade exam question formats.
