## Practicum WOOD 2090
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
Directed studies in a field selected by the student relating to their specialty. A written plan of the methodology, progress reports and a final report are expected. The project could be part of the student's duties at their place of employment. Work is to be carried out under the supervision of a faculty supervisor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all required course work in the certificate program.
