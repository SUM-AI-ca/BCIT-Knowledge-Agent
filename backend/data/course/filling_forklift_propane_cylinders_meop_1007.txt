## Filling Forklift Propane Cylinders MEOP 1007
Mobile Equipment Operator Course
## Course Overview
Provides both theory and practical training in filling forklift propane cylinders by volume. Successful participants will receive Propane Training Institute certification valid for 36 months. Course materials and certification fee included.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Fill propane cylinders by volume in a safe manner as per CAN/CGA-B149.2-M95 Propane Installation Code and Provincial regulations.
