## Municipal Governance in British Columbia XLED 2031
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces municipal leaders to the structures, systems, and processes of municipal governance in British Columbia. Participants will gain a comprehensive understanding of how local governments operate, make decisions, and interact with provincial authorities, with a focus on systems thinking and practical governance processes.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the core principles and legal frameworks that shape municipal governance in BC.
- Analyze key governance processes, including policy development, financial stewardship, relationships with local First Nations, and strategic planning.
- Apply systems thinking to understand how municipal departments, committees, and external stakeholders interact.
- Evaluate governance effectiveness using best practices and assessment tools.​
