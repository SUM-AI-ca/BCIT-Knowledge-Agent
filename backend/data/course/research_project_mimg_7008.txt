## Research Project MIMG 7008
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
Leads through the main components of a research project. In the period of a semester, you choose a research topic or question, review the literature about the chosen topic, further refine the research question, design a survey instrument, administer the survey, analyze the collected data and report your finding. Promotes survey research methods to accommodate the limited time-frame. If the research project is unsuitable to survey methods, provides guidance in alternative research methods. Course modules must be completed sequentially so that the research project evolves in a logical fashion.

## Learning Outcomes
When you have completed this course you will have demonstrated that you can:
- Choose a research question.
- Seek sources of research topics.
- Tentatively select a research topic.
- Evaluate the quality of the question in regards to feasibility, usefulness, relevance and practicality.
- Consider the variables to be studied.
- Estimate the resources necessary to carry out the study including equipment and research subjects.
- Prepare a literature review.
- Search the library catalogue for relevant material.
- Access this material.
- Read and interpret the material.
- Keep records of relevant material.
- Write a literature review.
- Refine the research question.
- Rework the question.
- Become more familiar with the question.
- Make a causal modal based on the research question.
- Consider ethical issues.
- Define and utilize informed consent.
- Be aware of the responsibilities of a researcher.
- Design a measuring instrument.
- Develop items designed to measure the variables of your research question.
- Define validity and reliability.
- Consider random and systematic error.
- Be aware of several assumptions we make as researchers.
- Utilize a checklist to evaluate your survey questions.
- Prepare an introductory letter for a questionnaire.
- Format a questionnaire
- Administer a questionnaire.
- Define population, sample frame and sample.
- Select an appropriate sampling method.
- Generate a sample for your research project.
- Analyze the data.
- Code the data for computer entry.
- Maintain a code book.
- Check data entry for entry errors.
- Discuss table etiquette.
- Process the data from your returned surveys.
- Write the report.
- Utilize the processed data.
- Construct the appropriate sections of a research report.
