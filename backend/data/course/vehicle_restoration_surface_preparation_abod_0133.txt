## Vehicle Restoration: Surface Preparation ABOD 0133
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the enthusiast to the equipment and techniques used to prepare a vehicle for refinishing. The student will learn to analyse vehicle surface, sand surface in preparation for paint, and apply undercoats to vehicle surface. Coverage includes mask techniques.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe safety procedures / personal protective equipment (PPE) used to prep.
- Describe types of vehicle surfaces.
- Describe materials and equipment used to prep.
- Describe various techniques used to prep.
- Describe materials and techniques used to mask.
- Prepare a previously painted surface.
- Prepare a non-previously painted surface.
- Apply undercoats / primers.
