## Innovation and Engagement XLED 1070
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​An engaged and innovative workplace is one that: brings out the best in each person, breaks from the status quo and ‘the way we’ve always done things’ to create solutions that are more effective, sustainable, and inclusive. It is also a workplace that encourages learning from mistakes, invites all voices to be heard and develops solutions with the whole system in mind. Through fostering a culture of innovation, your organization is well positioned to not only tackle stubborn recurring problems, but also emerging challenges. This workshop is designed to provide tools and insights for creating a workplace culture that nurtures high-level engagement and innovation in teams.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the nature of a ‘culture of innovation’ and how it applies to their workplace.
- Implement a process to test, refine, validate and launch an idea (Creativity to Innovation).
- Develop a personal action plan and assessment tool to apply the learning in their unique context​.
