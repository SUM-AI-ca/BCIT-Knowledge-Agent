## Technical Project 1 (Forestry) RENR 3180
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
Allows the student to select, organize and carry out an approved project in the field of natural resource management. The project will normally be carried out in teams and it is expected that in carrying out the project, students will draw on skills developed in first year courses as well as third term concurrent courses.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in RENR 2106 and 50% in RENR 2107 and 50% in RENR 2116 and 50% in RENR 2135 and 50% in RENR 2141 and 50% in RENR 2150 and 50% in COMM 2245 and 50% in MATH 2453
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Recognize and carry out the critical steps required in initiating and carrying a resource related project through to completion.
- Work within a team environment to solve project related problems.
- Provide outlines and reports conveying project related information in an approved technical format.
- Establish contacts with appropriate agencies to obtain helpful information.
- Record important data clearly in a recognized journal format.
