## Success Strategies For The New Decade BTCW 0172
Business Workshops Course
## Course Overview
This unique, solution focused two day workshop is designed to specifically address the challenges, opportunities, strategies and tools needed for managers at all levels to adapt to the new economic realities faced by organizations. This workshop will link key success factors in the changing global economy to the dynamics of organizational adaptation through a focus on innovation, and integration of applied IT and organizational change.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Acquire new analytical techniques to adapt your organization to the economic realities of the coming decade.
- Develop innovative approaches to organizational change.
- Understand and apply new strategic models to performance improvement.
