## Project Communications and Stakeholder Management OPMT 0213
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
The ability to communicate efficiently is a critical skill for today’s projects. Success in motivating teams, delegating, organizing, problem solving, selling ideas, obtaining/providing information, reporting on performance, managing organizational transition, and implementing actions depends heavily on your ability to communicate and influence. In addition, the seminar focuses on managing project stakeholders as project decisions impact their expectations and satisfaction. This course provides comprehensive skills development training in communications for all skill levels. Students are provided with practical communications strategies, tools, best practices along with plenty of real life examples of their application all an interactive format to enhance learning. 15 PDU credits from PMI are earned. www.bcit.ca/files/business/pdf/opmt0213.pdf

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Create a project organization.
- Resource, manage and motivate a project team.
- Manage team conflicts.
- Negotiate personnel terms and conditions.
- Manage project communications.
- Manage stakeholders.
