## Videography BCST 1108
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course combines journalism critical thinking skills with the technical elements of shooting and editing visuals and audio for news and information programming as a solo Video Journalist. Students will explore both pre-production planning and field techniques including the basic elements of lighting. They will also use pacing, structure and sequencing for effective video storytelling.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use a digital video camera to shoot video content
- Select and use the appropriate microphone effectively in the field
- Use Video Journalist skills to record on-camera content.
- Edit video footage using digital editing software
- Use an effective workflow in a digital editing environment from ingest to export
- Record and mix audio, including voiceover, for professional video production
- Explain basic lighting principles
- Use lighting effectively in video production.
Effective as of Fall 2024
