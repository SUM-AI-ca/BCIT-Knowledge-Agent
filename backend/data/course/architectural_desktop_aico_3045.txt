## Architectural Desktop AICO 3045
Computer Aided Design & Engineering Course
## Course Overview
How to use the features of AutoCAD Architectural Desktop software in a typical architectural design project. Topics include conceptual design, modeling, 2D drawing layout, annotations and design features. Customization techniques and project management are also discussed.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in AICO 2000
- AICO 2000 or equivalent.
