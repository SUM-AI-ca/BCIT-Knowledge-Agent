## Practicum OFFC 2000
Office Skills Training Course
## Course Overview
This course provides work experience for students who have successfully completed the BCIT Medical Office Assistant courses. The course includes six hours of classroom time: three hours of instruction on the first week of class; and three hours on the last week of class, which includes instruction and student presentations. A 60-hour practicum placement will be organized by the instructor for work experience in a physician’s office, hospital department or related health-care field. The placement allows students to communicate with patients, physicians and other members of the health care team, thus providing a foundation for specific job skills. Students will apply MOA skills and concepts in the workplace.
### Prerequisite(s)
- OFFC 1001 and OFFC 1002 and OFFC 1006 and OFFC 1007 and OFFC 1020 and OFFC 1040 and OFFC 1050 and OFFC 1060

- Write an effective up-to-date resume and cover letter.
- Be prepared for a MOA job interview.
- Gain confidence in the MOA work environment.
