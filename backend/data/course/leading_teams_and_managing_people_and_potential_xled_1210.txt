## Leading Teams and Managing People & Potential XLED 1210
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​Leading Teams is an interactive, applied workshop on leading teams using a combination of group interactions, role-plays, lectures,and experiential activities to enhance participant engagement and learning. This workshop covers the important factors in building teams including leadership communications skills needed in a team setting. Also, participants will increase their self-confidence and leadership abilities and establish a foundation for further training in supervision and management.

- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop and apply a team charter including performance ground rules and accountabilities.
- Develop or refresh communications skills in the practice of giving and receiving feedback.
- Acquire a technique to develop effective goal setting using SMART goals.
- Describe the importance of enlisting others in achieving goals within a shared vision and shared culture.
- Understand the nature of supervision including the role of a supervisor and the competencies they are expected to possess and use.
- Identify barriers and solutions to increase productivity, describe ways to create and maintain a motivating environment.
- Identify the steps to deal with a conflict and address typical difficult employee behaviors and situations.
- Effectively delegate tasks – describe the steps and conditions necessary for delegation.​
