## Lumber Grading 1 WOOD 1204
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
The course contains the theory material fundamental to succeed in a full lumber grading course. It includes tree growth, wood structure, characteristic identification and classification, classification of end-use products, species identification, and moisture content calculations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MATH 1462 and MATH 2462 and COMM 1135 and OPMT 1600 and OPMT 2651
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe tree growth and wood structure for local commercial softwood.
- Identify species and species grouping for commercial softwoods both rough and surfaced.
- Classify natural, manufacturing, and seasoning characteristics as defined by the R List and NLGA grading rulebooks.
- Classify lumber samples into product classes.
- Describe key water wood relationships.
- Calculate per-cent moisture content.
