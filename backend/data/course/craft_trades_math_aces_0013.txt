## Craft Trades Math ACES 0013
Access to Trades Course
## Course Overview
Covers geometry, formulas and problem-solving relating to various trade applications including metal fabrication, piping, construction, automotive and aircraft related trades. Delivery allows learners to participate from home, work or at school. Internet or print access to instructor solutions and direction is available. A personal tutor is assigned to each learner.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, learners will be able to:
- Use algebra to solve problems with formulas.
- Work with scientific and engineering notation.
- Work with plane (2D) geometry.
- Work with solid (3D) geometry.
- Solve trade-related word problems involving these skills.
