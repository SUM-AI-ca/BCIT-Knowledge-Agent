## Industry Practicum MKTG 4515
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
This course provides practical work experience that is relevant to your marketing-related career path. You will spend the equivalent of 20-working days with an industry organization performing various tasks associated with the firm's marketing efforts. You will submit written reports, and your performance will be assessed by an organization representative and BCIT faculty.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all program courses except MKTG 4435 Directed Studies.

3.5
### Registration requirements

### Domestic fees

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Apply your knowledge and skills to complete marketing-related tasks within an existing industry organization.
- Demonstrate your ability to attend and perform effectively in a real-life work environment.
- Assess your performance in an industry working environment.
