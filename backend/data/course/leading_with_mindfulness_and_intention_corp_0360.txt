## Leading with Mindfulness and Intention CORP 0360
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
This webinar will help you to become more aware of your own experience and be intentional in how you lead. Participants will learn how to observe their thoughts and feelings, to enable purposeful action instead of reactive response. Being able to clearly communicate what you are experiencing is a powerful skill that can be leveraged to strengthen connection within your personal and work relationships. Organizations are relationships-join us to explore how cultivating mindfulness can fuel your leadership intentions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this webinar, the participant will be able to:
- Define mindful leadership and explore the practice of leading with intention
- Explore how our thoughts and feelings influence decision and action
- Learn how to move beyond responding reactively to purposeful action
