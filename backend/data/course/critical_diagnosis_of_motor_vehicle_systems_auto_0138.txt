## Critical Diagnosis of Motor Vehicle Systems AUTO 0138
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
This course develops transferable diagnostic ability within a creative and collaborative classroom training environment. Designed for technicians and apprentices in the transportation trade sector, this course contains generic applications of critical diagnoses within the domains of mechanical, hydraulic, electrical, and internal combustion diagnosis and repair. By generalizing the content in the scenarios or by providing alternative resources based on specific transportation trades, the student will be able to focus on developing a flexible, diverse and transferable diagnostic reasoning ability.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TQ or apprentice in Automotive, Commercial Transport, Heavy Duty, or Motorcycle Mechanic.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the learner will have developed an understanding of:
- Diagnostic process.
- Diagnostic strategy.
- Assumptions in diagnosis.
- Mechanical diagnostic reasoning.
- Electrical diagnostic reasoning.
- Hydraulic diagnostic reasoning.
- Internal combustion diagnostic reasoning.
