## Sexually Transmitted Infections Management Modified NSPN 7735
Nursing Specialty Perinatal Course
## Course Overview
​This 12-week online, asynchronous and self-paced course provides students with baseline theoretical knowledge for diagnosing and treating various sexually transmitted infections (STIs). Students will apply the theory learned to the development of clinical skills, within the scope of certified nursing practice, with clients experiencing or at risk for STIs. It is recommended that learners spend an average of 4 – 6 hours per week of study time. Comprehensive assessment integrates history or present illness and review of systems into the sexual history when appropriate. Registered Nurses seeking certification in Reproductive Health – STI Management with the British Columbia College of Nurses and Midwives (BCCNM) must complete both NSPN 7735 theory course & NSPN 7736 – Sexually Transmitted Infections Management Clinical Course within 6 months in order to be eligible to apply for certified practice designation. Students must be BCCNM registered to be eligible to take the practicum component NSPN 7736 . Students who successfully complete both 7735 & 7736, will apply to be placed on the BCCNM Certified Practices Register, apply for prescribing authority within their certified practice area and be eligible to use the certified practice designation. ​

## Learning Outcomes
As a healthcare practitioner, you are expected to follow professional standards of practice throughout all SN programs and courses. In this course, you will have opportunities to use the processes of critical thinking, systematic inquiry, communication, collaboration, leadership, professionalism, and clinical decision-making. Upon successful completion of this course, you should be able to
- Describe the scope of practice for RNs/RMs in STI Management in relation to BCCNM Certified Practice regulations.
- Develop a comprehensive sexual health assessment framework with a primary focus on socio­economic determinants of health.
- Apply knowledge of reproductive health to the practice of STIM.
- ·Analyze issues related to reproductive health and STIM using systematic inquiry and critical reflection.
- Demonstrate knowledge of administrative strategies for STIM.
- Integrate legal, ethical, and professional standards with comprehensive assessment, evidence-informed decision-making, safe prescribing practice​s, accurate documentation, and effective interprofessional collaboration to prescribe medications in alignment with BCCNM Prescribing Standards. ​
