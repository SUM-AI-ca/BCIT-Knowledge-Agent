## Hospital Practice Systems 1 CLRK 1001
Healthcare Unit Clerk Course
## Course Overview
This course teaches the use of Cerner hospital administration software. Cerner is a cloud-based EHR software program used by health organizations of various sizes across the world and to streamline operations and provide greater healthcare. It offers Quick-Select charting, detailed scheduling dashboard, powerful integration, patient education resources, dynamic clinical documentation tools, auto-texting, revenue management, and health analytics.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use Quick-Select charting for tracking patient data.
- Arrange appointments and transfers using the detailed scheduling dashboard.
- Use powerful integrations for enabling inter communication between medical devices and healthcare applications.
- Provide patients with pertinent education resources.
- Use the patient portal to track patient history and data.
- Examine patient details with the dynamic clinical documentation tools.
- Use auto-texting for quickly populating a note with information from a chart to a physician.
- Navigate screens for patient address, care card, family doctor, emergency contacts, phone number and insurance information.
- Use hospital software to acknowledge different doctors' orders.
- Use Cerner FormFast to access medical forms such as consent forms, referrals, and discharge transfers forms.​
