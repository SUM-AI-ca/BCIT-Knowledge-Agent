## Small Business Growth Workshop VENT 0052
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
A series of independent workshops focusing on Small Business Development with expert instructors/facilitators designed specifically for the established, establishing and yet to be established entrepreneur. All Workshops will highlight on improving business skills and current business trends and indicators.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Improve/Upgrade business skills.
- Develop strategies to take advantage of current business trends.
