## Energy Step Code Fundamentals - Zero Energy/Emissions Building Envelope Focus CESA 0020
Centre for Energy Systems Applications Course
## Course Overview
​This course covers the fundamentals of for zero energy/emissions buildings, with focus on the requirements of the BC Building Code and the changes for building enclosures – with a focus on Part 9 buildings. The course will help learners understand how the new Code requirements will affect building science and building enclosure design and construction. Learners will gain understanding of Code compliance requirements and the evolving design and decision-making practices as project teams identify the best solutions to meet more stringent energy and emission performance requirements. An introduction to emerging practices, building materials, and embodied carbon of materials will be provided. Key topics include: BC Energy Step Code, Zero Carbon Code, energy efficiency performance metrics, building science, integrated design, low-TEDI and airtight enclosures, and material embodied carbon.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the BC Energy Step Code and Zero Carbon Step Code in the context of new construction
- Explain the compliance requirements and process of the BC Energy Step Code
- Describe the key information and decisions during early design process that will guide the performance of and design details of the home and building enclosure
- Describe the 6 principles of high-performance buildings and the "envelope-first approach" to near Zero Energy Buildings
- Explain fundamentals of building science as it relates to heat, air, and moisture transmission
- Understand emerging issues related to materials and assemblies
- Describe the common mechanical and electrical systems found in Net Zero Energy and Passive House buildings (heating, cooling, domestic hot water, ventilation with heat recovery)
