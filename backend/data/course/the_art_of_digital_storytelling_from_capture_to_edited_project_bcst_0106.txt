## The Art of Digital Storytelling – from Capture to Edited Project BCST 0106
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Everyone has a story to tell, but most aren't sure where to start or how to tell that story visually. The ability to take video has never been easier; we all have a versatile, highly technical camera built into our smartphones, but crafting those visuals into something worth watching requires a new level of understanding. During this hands-on, week-long workshop, you will learn how to tell the best visual stories possible. You will conceive, shoot and edit your story using techniques today's professionals use every day. You will learn "tricks-of-the-trade" that will help bring your stories to life and allow you to entertain and inform. Whether that story is a family vacation or a marketing video for your organization, this course will provide you with the tools you need to make invigorating, entertaining and informative digital stories.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Select compositions and camera angles and camera moves to enhance storytelling
- Apply the basics of lighting principles to add drama and enhance technical quality of your video
- Recognize the various shots and their purposes
- Use sequencing and time compression to enhance storytelling
- Apply continuity concepts to a visual production to maintain story continuity and logic
- Use your smartphone functions effectively to maximize technical and creative control of your video capture
