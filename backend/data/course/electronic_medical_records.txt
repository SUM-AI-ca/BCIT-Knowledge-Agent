## Overview

Healthcare administration is rapidly changing and electronic files are replacing paper files. The Electronic Medical Records (EMR) course explores key aspects of software systems used by healthcare providers for scheduling, referrals, requisitions, patient management and record keeping. You will be introduced to the Collaborative Health Record (CHR) software system and other Electronic Medical Records (EMR) software. This course includes lectures, exercises and hands-on practical software use for administration and clinical application in physicians’ offices and clinics. You will configure, organize, and navigate the CHR system to input and retrieve electronic medical data. This course is a required component of the Medical Office Assistant program and may also be taken as a stand-alone course by MOA graduates and other healthcare workers.

## Learning Outcomes

- Accurately perform tasks using Collaborative Health Record (CHR) industry software.
- Describe the core functions of an electronic medical record system.
- Explain the key uses and benefits of Electronic Medical Records (EMR) software.
- Organize and configure an EMR system.
- Configure an electronic appointment book.
- Manage EMR clinical documents.
- Manage clinical tasks in business software.
- Enter patient medical information in business software.
- Manage incoming and outgoing referrals.
- Generate accurate medical invoices with fee codes.
- Manage electronic clinical records and administrative documents.
- Use security measures, such as password protection, data encryption, and access controls, to safeguard information.
- Follow security protocols when working with electronic medical records (EMRs) and other sensitive medical data.
