## Shop Practical Projects MTLF 1260
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
In this course, you will apply safe work practices to the fabrication of shop projects. The course will require integration of knowledge of shop tools and equipment, blueprint reading and interpretation as well as template development to fabricate a variety of shop projects, such as ring to ring, J hook, machine guard, mounting bracket, Jack stand, load divider, three-piece elbow complete with branch. The course also incorporates the production of a capstone project.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1200 and MTLF 1205 and MTLF 1210 and MTLF 1215 and MTLF 1220 and MTLF 1225 and MTLF 1230 and MTLF 1235 and MTLF 1240 and MTLF 1245
### Credits
12.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use blueprints and shop equipment to produce components for an assembly.
- Assemble components based on a given blueprint.
- Perform assigned tasks in accordance with quality and production standards required by industry.
- Fabricate assembly jigs to facilitate more efficient production.
