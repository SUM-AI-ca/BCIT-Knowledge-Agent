## Techniques in Biotechnology BIOT 5800
Biotechnology Course
## Course Overview
BIOT 5800 - Techniques in Biotechnology: Provides practical training in many areas of Biotechnology. This is a 21 credit course divided into units over a 13-week period. This is the only course required to complete the Associate Certificate in Biotechnology.
### Prerequisite(s)
- English 12 (B or better) or English Language Proficiency ( COMM 0015 + COMM 0033 or TOFEL (with TWE and TSE) are acceptable). Recent Bachelor of Science degree in a molecular-based life science or equivalent.

21.0
