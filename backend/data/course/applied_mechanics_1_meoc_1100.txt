## Applied Mechanics 1 MEOC 1100
Marine Engineer Officer Cadet Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to elementary applied mechanics principles. The course consists of lectures solving applied engineering problems in the following areas: vectors, Bowes notation, reciprocating engine mechanism, framed structures, trusses, velocity and acceleration, relative velocity, operations with forces, Atwood's machine, momentum, turning moment, work, power, and transmission of power.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the student will be able to:
- Solve problems involving vectors and vector diagrams.
- Solve problems involving triangle and polygon of forces using Bowes notation.
- Solve problems involving slings, jibs, and reciprocating mechanisms.
- Solve problems involving framed structures.
- Solve problems involving linear velocity, acceleration, and time graphs.
- Solve problems involving general laws of linear motion.
- Solve problems involving gravitational acceleration.
- Solve problems involving angular velocity and acceleration.
- Solve problems involving relationships between angular and linear motion.
- Solve problems involving change of velocity due to change in direction.
- Solve problems involving relative velocities.
- Solve problems involving mass, accelerating force, torque, and momentum using Newton's laws.
- Solve problems involving work, power, and energy including graphical methods.
- Solve problems involving "g" using Atwood's machine.
- Solve problems involving conservation of linear and angular momentum.
- Solve problems involving lifting loads, work done, and power.
- Solve problems involving rotary power.
- Solve problems involving PV diagrams.
- Solve problems involving kinetic and potential energy (impact testing m/c).
