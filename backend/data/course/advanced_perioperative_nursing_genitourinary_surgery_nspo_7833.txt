## Advanced Perioperative Nursing: Genitourinary Surgery NSPO 7833
Nursing Specialty Perioperative Course
## Course Overview
​In this badged course, learners will expand their urological perioperative nursing education, advancing beyond novice practice, focusing on immediate practical knowledge application. Within this badged course, learners will have the opportunity to explain in depth the perioperative nursing role for urology procedures and develop a plan of care for persons undergoing these procedures in both the scrub and circulating roles. Clarification of scope of practice for LPNs and RNs in urology procedures will be outlined as well as adherence to hospital policies when on-site. To prepare for the clinical environment and knowledge uptake, low-stakes assessments, including quizzes and small written case study assignments will be completed.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of Perioperative Specialty Nursing Program. Perioperative Nurses (RN or LPN/RPN) from any Health Authority or Private Surgical Center or equivalent practice setting.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- describe the anatomy of the organs involved in genitourinary surgical procedures
- describe the physiology of the organs involved in genitourinary surgical procedures
- summarize the genitourinary surgical procedures, including the common indications, equipment, approaches, and complications
- create a plan of care for persons undergoing genitourinary surgical procedures​
