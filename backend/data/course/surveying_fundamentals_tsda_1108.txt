## Surveying Fundamentals TSDA 1108
Transystems Development Approval Course
## Course Overview
A fundamental course in surveying which introduces commonly used survey equipment. After completion, students will be able to identify and describe the equipment, parts and applications in the field, including fields and office methods used for measuring and recording surveying data. Topics include booking and plotting notes, measuring angles and distances,basic computations for angles, level note reduction, simple circular curves, simple traverse plots, plans, profiles, cross sections and slope staking.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of each module, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Basic Principles of Surveying
- Define surveying and discuss its relevance to the land development and development approvals process.
- Identify the types of surveying typically employed in the industry.
- State the basic categories of survey measurement.
- Discuss the types of errors encountered in survey work, and the precision and accuracy in surveying and calculation.
## Module 2 - Basic Surveying Mathematics and Geometry
- Add, subtract, multiply, and divide angles expressed in degrees, minutes, and seconds as well as degree and decimals of a degree.
- Determine the trigonometric functions of an angle, using function tables or a scientific calculator.
- Calculate the lengths of the sides of a triangle, using available angles and distances.
- Discuss azimuth and quadrant bearings.
- Calculate the azimuth and quadrant bearings of lines given the deflection angle between lines.
## Module 3 - Distance Measurement - Equipment and Procedures
- State and define the types of linear measurements made and the types of equipment used in the measurement of distance and length.
- Describe the use of horizontal and slope measurements and distances.
- Discuss the merits and relative accuracy of survey chains and tapes, and their respective uses.
- Discuss the conditions and corrections which apply to accurate chaining.
- State and define the general principles of electronic distance measurement.
## Module 4 - Elevation Measurement - Equipment and Procedures
- State and define the terms used in levelling, including horizontal plane, elevation, datum.
- State and define the basic concepts of differential levelling.
- State and define the basic types of equipment used in levelling and elevation measurement.
- State and explain the use of various hand levelling devices and their operation.
- Identify and 'read' various levelling rod faces given the instrument cross-hair location on each rod.
- Calculate instrument heights and elevations from Benchmark information and rod readings.
## Module 5 - Angle Measurement - Equipment and Procedures
- State and define magnetic declination, compass bearings, and read compass bearings given the compass face view.
- Discuss the field measurement of vertical and horizontal angles.
- Calculate horizontal distances based on vertical angle and slope distance.
- Calculate elevation differences based on vertical angle and slope or horizontal distance.
- Read or determine measured angles as displayed by a theodolite.
## Module 6 - Traverses and Curves
- Describe an open and closed traverse.
- Discuss how a traverse might be commonly used in land development survey work.
- State and define co-ordinates.
- Calculate the co-ordinates for various points along a traverse.
- State the elements of a simple horizontal roadway curve.
- Calculate subtangent and arc lengths for simple horizontal roadway curves.
## Module 7 - Office Methods and Procedures
- Determine map and plan distances utilizing various types of scales.
- Plot and interpret contours given the field topographic data.
- State and define the terms contour, slope, grade, profile, cross-section.
- Plot profiles, cross-section, and calculate grades and slopes from contour plans.
## Module 8 - Site Surveys and Construction Layout
- Discuss the process and requirements for the development of a site survey of existing features.
- State and define the terms and general requirements for the layout and survey of construction work and structures.
- Identify the marking of survey stakes for construction purposes.
- State and define the purpose of slope staking and the methods for determining location of placement.
