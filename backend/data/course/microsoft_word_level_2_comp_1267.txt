## Microsoft Word Level 2 COMP 1267
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
COMP 1267 is no longer being offered. Please see COMP 2361 or COMP 2369 . Continues from Level 1 and covers advanced MS Word features. Expert topics include: create, format and use templates, fill-in form templates and on-screen forms. Students learn to record and edit macros, customize Word's environment, integrate and link or embed data from Excel, perform mail merges, create cross-references, hyperlinks, table of contents and index. Course also includes an introduction to XML. Time saving tips, tricks, techniques and shortcuts discussed and demonstrated.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in COMP 1266 or 50% in COMP 1268
### Credits
1.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use, create, and edit templates and form templates.
- Customize the word environment.
- Record, edit and run simple Word macros.
- Insert document references such as footnotes, endnotes, bookmarks, and cross-references.
- Create an index and table of contents.
- Link data from other Office applications into a Word document.
- Perform a mail merge.
- Be familiar with XML concepts.
- Create a web page.
