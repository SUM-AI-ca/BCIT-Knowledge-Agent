## Graphic Presentation 1 INTD 2320
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
Students will create colour presentation drawings of interior spaces and furniture. They will use markers in combination will other mediums to render plans, elevations and perspectives drawings.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 65% in INTD 1100 and 65% in INTD 1150 and 65% in INTD 1310 and 50% in COMM 1120
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Create depth through the use of line weight variation.
- Describe form through the use of shading.
- Combine colours to represent various building materials and textures.
- Add dimension with the use of shadow, highlights, and reflections.
- Create one and two point perspectives using various drawing mediums.
- Create freehand sketches of interiors and/or furniture.
- Employ various mediums to enhance presentation techniques.
