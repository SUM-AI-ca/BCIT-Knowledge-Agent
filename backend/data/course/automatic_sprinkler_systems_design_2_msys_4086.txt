## Automatic Sprinkler Systems Design 2 MSYS 4086
Mechanical Systems Course
## Course Overview
Advanced detailed instruction for persons involved in fire service, engineering design, supervision or inspection of automatic sprinkler systems in commercial and industrial buildings.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MSYS 4080
- MSYS 4080 .
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Select the appropriate deluge valve for a sprinkler system.
- Calculate the hydraulic loss in a complex sprinkler system.
