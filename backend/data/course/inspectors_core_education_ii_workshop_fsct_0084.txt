## Inspectors' Core Education II Workshop FSCT 0084
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
This course is for inspectors who have been designated to conduct inspections of inquiries/complaints filed with the professional colleges in British Columbia. Participants will be provided with advanced knowledge and skills required to investigate inquiries/complaints in accordance with appropriate laws and policies under the Health Professions Act of BC. Guidance by an experienced inspector into the investigation process utilizing examples of specialized tools and case studies will provide additional resources and references for the inspector.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe advanced investigative techniques for the inspector.
- Outline obstacles to the investigation of inquiries/complaints.
- Describe possible solutions to the investigative obstacles.
- Analyze case examples and case studies related to investigations.
- Detail advanced report writing skills.
