## Advanced Structured Cabling CSST 2150
Computer Systems Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
Exposes students to a wide variety of network cabling topics, including structured premises cabling systems, industry standards, media characteristics, connectors, transmission, electrical concepts, safety, pulling terminating cable and testing. Also provides students with the necessary hands-on skills to troubleshoot network wiring and cabling utilizing industry standard Fluke test equipment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in CITX 1150

3.0
