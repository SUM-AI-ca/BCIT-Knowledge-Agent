## Suspension Systems MCTC 1430
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to the fundamental skills required to diagnose, service, and repair various types of suspension systems and components, commonly encountered in the Motorcycle service industry. Students will also learn basic frame and chassis design principles, handling and stability problems related to various Motorcycles, including All Terrain Vehicles.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCTC 1400

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe basic frame designs.
- Describe suspension types.
- Inspect frame and suspension systems.
- Describe Suspension geometry principles.
- Describe Handling and Stability problems.
