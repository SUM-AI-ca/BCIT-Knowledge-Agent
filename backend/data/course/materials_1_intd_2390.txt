## Materials 1 INTD 2390
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
This course will cover interior finishing specifications, characteristics of fabrics and of other materials for floors, walls, ceilings and windows. Environmental issues will be stressed, including LEED. Students will be introduced to local suppliers and manufacturers through guest lectures and field trips.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in INTD 1350
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Research and source materials used as base building finishes.
- Identify the characteristics and suitability of various base building finishes and know about installation of them.
- Create a budget estimate for a project, including for materials and for installation.
- Write specifications using master format.
- Incorporate sustainable design practices into a given space.
- Critically analyse furniture and materials installations of existing sites.
- Identify characteristics of furniture and materials in terms of long term appearance retention.
