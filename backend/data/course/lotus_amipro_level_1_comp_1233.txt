## LOTUS AmiPro Level 1 COMP 1233
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Covers the basic features of this powerful word processor package, version 3.1 by combining lectures and hands-on exercises. Covers basic editing features, page layout, spell check, thesaurus, grammitik, headers and footers, mailmerge, file and print management.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in COMP 1006 or 50% in COMP 1212
