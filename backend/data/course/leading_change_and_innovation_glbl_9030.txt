## Leading Innovation GLBL 9030
Global Business Leadership Course
## Course Overview
Innovation drives ongoing improvements and has the potential to unleash a new idea that can serve as a competitive advantage for an organization. It is thus important for business professionals to be able to identify the need and context for innovation, the roles key stakeholders play, how resistance will be managed, the communication styles that will be required and finally, the stages or timescale of innovation. Examine and evaluate the fundamental concepts of innovation management. You will be presented with the practical tools for developing an innovative mindset, with an emphasis on building and fostering a culture of creativity and innovation. Learn the skills needed to drive innovative initiatives in the workplace and cultivate awareness of the impact of change to individuals and organizations. Competencies may be demonstrated through a blend of individual and collaborative assignments, skill demonstrations, and quizzes.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Design practices for leading and innovation to achieve strategic outcomes while factoring in perspectives of global stakeholders.
- Examine organizational systems that can foster, impede and/or facilitate the acceptance of innovation.
- Analyze strategies to stimulate innovation in a business environment.
- Apply the most useful tactics to motivate team members to change behaviors that hinder sustainability within the organization.
- Illustrate the impact of innovation processes on the individual, team and organizational level.
- Motivate, enable and facilitate collaboration towards innovation.
