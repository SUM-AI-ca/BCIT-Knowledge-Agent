## MTEC Seminar MTEC 0100
Manufacturing Course
## Course Overview
This session consists of a plant tour and presentations. The seminar gives all students an opportunity to observe applications of manufacturing practices and processes by visiting industrial plants. It also provides graduating students a forum to present and discuss their graduating capstone projects. The session enables students to learn about various manufacturing-related developments from guest speakers.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Visit an industrial plant
- Identify practical implementations of equipment and processes discussed in program courses
- Discuss and critique projects of graduating students
