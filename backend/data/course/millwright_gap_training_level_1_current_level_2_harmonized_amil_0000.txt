## Millwright GAP Training Level 1 Current - Level 2 Harmonized AMIL 0000
Millwright Apprentice Course
## Course Overview
This course is to provide GAP training for students who have missed the ITA deadline for completing this training. Upon completion students will receive ITA credit for the L1C --> L2H GAP Training.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Millwright Apprentice Level 1 Current
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Meet the GAP training requirement to move into the harmonized level 2 intake.
