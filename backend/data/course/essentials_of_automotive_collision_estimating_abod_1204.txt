## Essentials of Automotive Collision Estimating ABOD 1204
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
​​This course will continue from ABOD 1202 ​​ as the second course of a two-part program leading to a BCIT Microcredential and Statement of Completion in Automotive Collision Estimating. Content will include: advanced vehicle systems, insurance regulations, collision damage diagnosis and customer service. An emphasis will be placed on creating estimates using collision estimating software platforms such as Mitchell and other related software.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Interpret original equipment manufacturer (OEM) repair procedures
- Describe flat rate times and sequencing
- Describe the refinishing process
- Identify damage to Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS)
- Identify damage to hybrid, electric and fuel cell vehicles
- Demonstrate effective estimate writing using Mitchell software​​
Effective as of Winter 2024
