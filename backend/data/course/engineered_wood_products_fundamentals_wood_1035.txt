## Engineered Wood Products Fundamentals WOOD 1035
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
Engineered Wood Products Fundamentals is a foundation course that introduces plywood/engineered wood products (OSB, particleboard, MDF, LVL, OSL, PSL - Parallam, Com-ply) and their manufacturing technologies. Students will be introduced to a variety of plywood, engineered wood products, and their related applications.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe the Plywood and Engineered Wood Products manufacturing industry and the products it produces.
- Identify various wood panel products and engineered wood components.
- Outline the characteristics of the major product lines manufactured in the various Plywood/ Engineered Wood Products sectors and their uses.
- Identify the various processes and manufacturing technologies applied within the Plywood/ Engineered Wood Products industry.
- Describe the key components of each manufacturing process discussed and outline their importance to other production procedures.
- Identify the critical quality control factors within each production process discussed.
