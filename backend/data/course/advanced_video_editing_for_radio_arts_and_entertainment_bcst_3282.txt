## Advanced Video Editing BCST 3282
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
​Building on the introductory video editing course in Level 2, this course provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the features and tools of video editing software, including Adobe Premiere Pro. Students will explore advanced editing practices, layering and blending techniques, green screen, multicamera editing and work with more advanced effects and graphics.​​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BCST 1180
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Perform advanced video editing techniques.
- Demonstrate basic multi-cam editing techniques.
- Edit green screen and chroma key footage.
- Execute nesting techniques.
- Manipulate audio effects to build and enhance soundscapes.
- Apply effective speed changes such as slow motion and freeze frames to video projects.
- Select appropriate codecs, formats, and resolutions for publishing social media videos.
- Use advanced techniques in applying text, titles, and captions, plus images to video projects.​
Effective as of Fall 2023
