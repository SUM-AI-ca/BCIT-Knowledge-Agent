## Highway Construction Part 5: Rock Drilling and Blasting TSYH 4405
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
An introductory course to the terminology and concepts of explosives used for rock removal in highway construction. Topics include properties of rock, explosive types, detonation, safety, and basic blast design.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Nature and Origin of Rocks
- Describe the major classifications of rock and explain the methods used in rock identification and classification.
- Describe the processes that take place during the formation of igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks.
- Describe the purpose and use the sight recognition and rock identification tables and figures utilized by the Ministry of Transportation and Highways.
- Describe the various components of the structure of an existing body of rock.
## Module 2 - Rock Excavation: Ripping
- Identify and describe the common equipment used in rock excavation by ripping and discuss the general specifications related to rock excavation.
- State and define the general rock classifications and physical characteristics which favor ripping.
- Discuss seismic analysis related to rock ripping and determine the rippability of rock based on seismic wave velocities and ripper performance.
- Discuss the basic operations of ripping, equipment selection, direction of travel, depth and typical ripping techniques.
- Estimate ripper production given the appropriate data.
## Module 3 - Rock Drilling Equipment & Techniques
- Determine the common terms used to describe rock drilling equipment and techniques.
- Define and discuss the methods for rock drilling and the selection of appropriate equipment based on the application and rock type.
- Calculate rock drilling production rate given the appropriate data.
## Module 4 - Explosive Products
- Define the terms used to describe the primary explosives used in highway construction.
- State and define the properties of explosives and their relationship to rock blasting.
- Describe the difference between electric and non-electric initiating devices and the use of primers for explosive detonation.
## Module 5 - Explosive Storage, Handling & Transportation
- List the sources of regulations and control for the safe handling of explosives.
- State in general terms the considerations for safe storage, handling, and transporting of explosives.
- State the requirements for obtaining a blasters certificate.
## Module 6 - Blasting Effects and Design
- Explain the method of rock fracturing by blasting.
- Define the terms spacing and burden, and calculate appropriate spacing and burden dimensions given information for typical blast conditions.
- Define and explain smooth wall blasting techniques.
- Describe and explain the purpose of blast vibration monitoring.
