## BCIT Instructor Orientation BCIT 0110
BCIT Generic Course
## Course Overview
This course provides information and direction for instructors to BCIT.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Be familiar with all BCIT services.
- Be able to set lesson plans for the 1st night of class.
- Be knowledgeable on how to engage students.
