## Schnitzer Steel Two Post Hoist Safe Operation AUTO 0013
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides Schnitzer Steel employees with the safety considerations and procedures required for operation of their own two post vehicle hoist.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe safe operation of a two post hoist.
- Conduct inspection of two post hoist for defects.
- Demonstrate two post hoist operation.
- Complete ALI (American Lift Institute) exam.
