## Listening TRPT 0012
Trades Entry Assessment Course
## Course Overview
The listening test assesses the examinee's ability to hear and understand oral information. The examinee will respond to questions about a conversation between two workers and a classroom lecture. For more information on trades pre-entry tests,visit www.bcit.ca/tlc/pretest/ . Students must present valid Government issued photo identification and proof of payment to the test administrator, and provide their own pen/pencil and eraser. Students will not be permitted to write the test without the required documents and the test fee will be forfeited.
