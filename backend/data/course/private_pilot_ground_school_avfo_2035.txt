## Private Pilot Ground School 2 AVFO 2035
Airline and Flight Operations Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed to meet the knowledge requirements of a Private Pilot License. It is the primary ground school for the Private Pilot License and includes lessons on Canadian Aviation Regulations, aerodynamics, theory of flight, weather, aircraft engine and systems, flight instruments, navigation, radio and electronic theory, human factors, flight operations and licensing requirements.
### Prerequisite(s)
- AVFO 1035 and AVFO 1145
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Obtain a full pass on the Transport Canada Written Examination
- Describe Canadian Aviation Regulations as it pertains Private Pilot license
- Describe aerodynamics, theory of flight, weather and navigation
- Describe aircraft engine and systems
- Identify flight instruments as it pertains to Private Pilot license
- Identify radio and electronic theory in an aviation context
- Describe human factors at the Private Pilot licensing level
- Meet the requirements of flight operations and licensing to meet Transport Canada standards
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2023
