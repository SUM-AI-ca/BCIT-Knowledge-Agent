## Powertrain HMTF 1013
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the basic principles of powertrain and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on powertrain systems and components.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
6.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Service clutches
- Service manual transmissions
- Service torque converters and dividers
- Service power shift and automatic transmissions
- Service drivelines
- Service drive axles
- Service final drives
- Remove and install transmissions
- Remove and install drivelines and differentials
- Remove and install final drives
Effective as of Fall 2014
