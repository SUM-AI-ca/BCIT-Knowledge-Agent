## MS Word Workshop 3 COMP 0268
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Following on from COMP 0266 plus COMP 0267 , participants are shown advanced topics for using Word in this intensive, hands-on seminar. Lectures and labs go into greater detail around advanced MS Word topics including: collaboration features using comments, track changes, comparing and combining document; adding references such as table of contents, table of figures, cross-references, index, bibliographies; creating and using electronic forms. Importing and exporting data to and from Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint are also covered. Successful participants in all three One-Day MS Word workshops will acquire a solid understanding of how to use MS Word for a variety of advanced tasks.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 0266 and COMP 0267 or equivalent knowledge of MS Word.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Integrate and link data from other Office programs into MS Word.
- Use track changes, comments, compare and combine documents.
- Insert a table of contents, table of figures, bibliography and index.
- Create electronic forms.
