## Access Development 2 COMP 1274
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Continues from and builds further on COMP 1273 by exploring and working with the programming language, Data Access Objects (DAO), and handling errors. Discusses in-depth coverage of Events, Modules, Procedures and Structures.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in COMP 1273
