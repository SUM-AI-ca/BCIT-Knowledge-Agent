## Overview

This advanced course in Adobe InDesign focuses on mastering the tools and techniques needed for creating professional-level designs, layouts, and digital documents. Students will explore advanced features such as parent pages, text formatting, styles, templates, and interactive tools for crafting digital publications. The course also emphasizes best practices for project management and collaboration, including the creation and importation of assets like vector and raster graphics. By the end of the course, students will be equipped to build sophisticated, publication-ready layouts and documents, demonstrating a comprehensive understanding of InDesign's advanced capabilities.

## Learning Outcomes

- Apply advanced principles of composition and layout to develop complex and engaging page layouts suitable for both print and digital distribution.
- Create and implement a comprehensive system of parent pages, character, paragraph and object styles, showcasing proficiency in managing and formatting long documents.
- Design and produce interactive layouts that incorporate hyperlinks, buttons and multimedia, for enhancing user engagement.
- Utilize advanced InDesign features to export documents to interactive formats with a focus on ensuring cross-platform functionality, accessibility, and optimal viewing experiences.
- Demonstrate proficiency in creating and integrating complex assets such as vector illustrations and raster graphics, into InDesign projects.
- Package and prepare a multi-page document for print or digital output, ensuring it meets specific supplier requirements, including printer spreads or reader spreads.
- Collaborate effectively with team members and clients, utilizing InDesign's collaborative features and adhering to project timelines and standards.
- Conduct preflight checks and packaging to ensure all assets and fonts are included, verifying documents meet print and digital standards.
- Apply advanced typography techniques, including the use of OpenType features, glyphs, and advanced type controls, to enhance the visual appeal and readability of the designs.
