## Global Positioning System SURV 3576
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
Students will be able to use GPS high precision geodetic equipment to conduct static, kinematic and real-time surveys and process the collected survey data. This course will cover topics such as datum transformation from GPS to North American datums, scale factors in GPS surveying, network adjustment using GPS data and reference station networks in North America.
### Prerequisite(s)
- SURV 3366
