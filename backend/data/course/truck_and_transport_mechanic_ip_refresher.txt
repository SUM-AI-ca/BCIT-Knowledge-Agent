## Overview

This course is for current Truck and Transport Mechanic Level 4 apprentices. The course will provide a theory refresher of Levels 1, 2 and 3 content in preparation for the Truck and Transport Mechanic Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination.
