## Systems Analysis and Design Concepts COMP 2615
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
COMP 2830 replaces both COMP 2615 and COMP 3615 . COMP 2830 - Systems Analysis and Design with UML. In order to properly develop and maintain commercial information systems, business and systems analysts need to understand industry standard concepts, methodologies and tools. This foundation course introduces the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and follows the standards defined by the IEEE group in the SWEBOK (Software Engineering Body of Knowledge). Topics include the first five steps of the software development life cycle: project initiation, problem analysis, requirements analysis, logical design and decision analysis. Participants learn to work in teams and practice analysis using software engineering concepts such as Structured Analysis, Information Engineering and Object Oriented Analysis. This includes development of process, data and object models along with domain UML notation. The SDLC is completed in COMP 3615 , using the tools taught in this course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- PC Literacy and COMM 1103 , or equivalent ability to write and present business materials in a group environment, making oral presentations.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply the major alternative methodologies used in developing information systems and understand the considerations involved in choosing alternative methodologies.
- Produce the requisite systems documentation at each point in the analysis phase of an information system.
- Analyze a business need for information and develop an appropriate strategy to solve the problem and provide the required information service.
- Apply various information-gathering techniques for eliciting user information requirements and system expectations.
- Develop and interpret a variety of system description documents, including data flow diagrams, entity-relationship diagrams and object-oriented UML models.
- Analyze the feasibility of alternative system solutions.
