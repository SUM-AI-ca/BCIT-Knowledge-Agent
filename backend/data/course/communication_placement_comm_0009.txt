## Communication Placement COMM 0009
Communication Course
## Course Overview
COMM 0009 includes both a writing and listening assessment to determine students' eligibility for registration into a Professional English Language Development course ( COMM 0071 , COMM 0003 , COMM 0004 , COMM 0005 / COMM 0008 ), COMM 0029 , and COMM 0030 . If you have never taken a BCIT Professional English Language Development course, we need to assess your English skills so you will be placed in the appropriate courses for your level of English. Note: COMM 0009 results are valid for 1 year from the assessment date. The COMM 0009 assessment fee is non-refundable.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
