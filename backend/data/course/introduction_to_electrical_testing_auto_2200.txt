## Introduction to Electrical Testing AUTO 2200
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Introduces electrical testing to professional technicians with limited experience in electrical and electronic testing. Topics include electrical theory, circuits, diagnosis, testing and maintenance. Safety glasses and steel toed footwear required.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Be competent at testing a variety of electrical problems with state of the art electronic test equipment,
- Be able to interpret wiring diagrams, and
- Develop diagnostic skills necessary to repair electrical circuits on modern vehicles.
