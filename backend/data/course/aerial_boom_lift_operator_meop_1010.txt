## Aerial Boom Lift Operator MEOP 1010
Mobile Equipment Operator Course
## Course Overview
Provides both theory and practical training in the operation of aerial boom lifts. Successful participants will receive Aerial Boom Lift Operator Certification. This program meets current WCB guidelines and CSA standards for aerial boom lift operators. Workbook included.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Driving experience.
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Operate aerial boom lifts in a competent and safe manner within WCB Regulations.
