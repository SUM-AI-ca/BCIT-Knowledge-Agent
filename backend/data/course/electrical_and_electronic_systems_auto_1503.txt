## Electrical and Electronic Systems AUTO 1503
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the principles of electricity, electrical schematics, diagnostic tools andprocedures, power sources, and hybrid and electric vehicle safety.
### Prerequisite(s)
- AUTO 1500
### Credits
9.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Interpret Basic Wiring and Electrical Systems
- Diagnose and Service 12-Volt Batteries
- Implement Safety Protocols for Hybrid & Electric Vehicles
- Describe Electronic Circuits
- Repair Lighting Systems
- Use Advanced Electrical Test Equipment
Effective as of Fall 2021
