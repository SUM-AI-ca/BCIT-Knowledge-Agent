## DE-Calculus for Mechanical Part 1 MATH 1490
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
This course is similar to MATH 1491 . It introduces differential and integral calculus of polynomial functions with supporting topics from technical mathematics. Emphasises application in technology and engineering.
### Prerequisite(s)
- recent Math 12 with C+ or better, or 65% or higher in MATH 0001 .
