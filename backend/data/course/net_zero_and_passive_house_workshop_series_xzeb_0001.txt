## Net Zero and Passive House Workshop Series XZEB 0001
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
​The Zero Energy / Emissions Buildings Learning Centre provides a collection of workshop topics to support industry adoption of the BC Energy Step Code, BC Zero Carbon Step Code, low-energy and/or emission standards (e.g. Passive House Institute's Passive House standard and CAGBC’s Zero Carbon Building), and other regulations (e.g. Vancouver Building By-Law's, energy, emission, and embodied carbon requirements). Workshop topics cover six key areas: (1) energy and emission codes and standards, (2) airtight and low-energy building enclosures, (3) high efficiency and low-carbon energy systems, (4) integrated electrical solutions including photovoltaics, EV charging, resiliency, (5) embodied carbon emissions in construction materials, and (6) integrated design practices for new construction and building retrofits. ## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the requirements of various energy and emission requirements in the BC Building Code, voluntary construction standards, building certification programs, and local government by-laws or policies.
- Apply the key design features and installation approaches of a low-energy and low-carbon building enclosures, mechanical and electrical systems​.
- Describe approaches and activities from design to construction that optimize cost, value, and reduce risk when designing new low-energy, low-emission buildings.
