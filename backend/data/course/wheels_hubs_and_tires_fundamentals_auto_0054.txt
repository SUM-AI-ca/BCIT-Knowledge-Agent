## Wheels, Hubs and Tires Fundamentals AUTO 0054
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Strengthen your fundamental understanding of tires and wheel service, puncture repair, and non-friction bearing Service This course is suitable for professional technicians, apprentices, and enthusiasts.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use a tire machine to mount and dismount tires
- Locate and repair tire punctures
- Balance a tire
- Service non-friction bearings
