## Brake Systems MCTC 1425
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to the fundamental skills, operational principles and service procedures for brake systems that are required to diagnose, service, and repair various types of braking systems including: disc-brakes, drum brake assemblies, and brake hydraulics. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCTC 1400
### Credits
3.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe Brake designs and operation.
- Describe Anti-Lock-Braking Systems.
- Service Tubing and Fittings.
- Service Drum Brake systems.
- Service Disc Brake systems.
- Service Brake Hydraulic systems.
