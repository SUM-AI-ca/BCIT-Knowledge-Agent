## Introduction to Food Technology FOOD 1091
Food Technology Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the field of food science and technology, the sciences used to provide knowledge for food technology and the importance of food in providing proper nutrition. Topics include: chemical and physical properties of foods; issues pertaining to safety, nutritive value and consumer acceptability; government regulations pertaining to food safety; quality and additives; and preservation techniques. www.bcit.ca/study/programs/6340acert

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Describe the various activities of the food industry in BC with respect to the food processing industry in Canada and the global economy and marketplace.
- Describe the importance of the major food sectors in the BC economy (i.e., meat, poultry, seafood, fruit and vegetables, dairy and egg, beverages, etc.).
- Compare the effect of consumer demand on food product development.
- Describe food safety considerations.
- Describe the major constituents of food (i.e., protein, lipid, carbohydrate, mineral, vitamin).
- Describe the nutritional importance of the major food constituents to the proper functioning of the body.
- Explain the importance of a well-balanced diet.
- Discuss the principle of water activity as applied to the preservation of foods and discuss the use of environmental control for controlling food degradation and limiting contamination.
- Demonstrate dehydration practices and explain the principles involved in the dehydration of foods.
- Describe fermentation practices and explain the principles involved in the bioconversion of foods.
- Describe the heat processing of foods.
- Describe freezing practices and explain the principles involved in the cold preservation of foods.
- Describe the principles involved in food preservation by the use of sugar, salt and preservatives.
- Describe the principles involved in microwave and ionizing radiation for the preservation of foods.
- Describe the techniques of genetic modification of foods and the regulatory issues involved as well as the use of nutraceuticals as they relate to diet and chronic disease.
