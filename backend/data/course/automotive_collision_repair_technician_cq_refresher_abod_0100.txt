## Automotive Collision Repair Technician CQ Refresher ABOD 0100
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
Refreshes theoretical knowledge of the trade in preparation for the Automotive Collision Repair Technician Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination. Contact the Industry Training Authority (tel. 778-328-8700 or toll free within BC 1-866-660-6011) to determine eligibility and to schedule an examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be:
- Prepared to write the Certification of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination.
- Able to perform practical tasks with a better understanding of component operation.
- Able to write trade exams of various formats.
