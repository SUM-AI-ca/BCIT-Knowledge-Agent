## International Business TDMT 1353
Transportation and Distribution Course
## Course Overview
Introduces an understanding of the international operating environment. This course will scan the current global trading environment and provide base skills required by international firms to identify risk and analyse opportunities.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
- The student will become familiar with the pedagogy of writing international business reports:
- By having to develop and write an international business analysis
- By researching outside sources in the business community
- The student will develop critical thinking, communication and teamwork skills:
- By having to work in groups.
- Developing a comprehensive perspective of strategic objectives
- The student will learn to integrate and apply skills and concepts:
- By creating a functional country analysis outline.
- Discussions in labs relating to application of course material.
