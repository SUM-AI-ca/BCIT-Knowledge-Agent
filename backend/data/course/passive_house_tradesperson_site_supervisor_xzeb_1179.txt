## Passive House Tradesperson – Site Supervisor XZEB 1179
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
​This course will prepare students who have already completed XZEB 1001 , XZEB 1120 or XZEB 1121 , XZEB 1129 , and XZEB 1171 with the outstanding body of knowledge required to earn additional designation as a Certified Passive House Tradesperson – Site Supervisor. Learners will apply prior knowledge to site supervision and construction management activities on Passive House construction projects. Upon successful course completion, students will earn additional designation as a Certified Passive House Tradesperson - Site Supervisor, registered with the Passive House Institute.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in XZEB 1001 and 60% in XZEB 1120 and 60% in XZEB 1129 and 60% in XZEB 1146 and 60% in XZEB 1171

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the importance of airtightness, thermal bridge mitigation, and quality assurance in ensuring Passive House compliance during construction phases.
- Demonstrate the ability to inspect and assess on-site conditions to ensure proper installation of Passive House-certified windows, doors, and insulation materials.
- Evaluate common deficiencies in construction practices, such as improper insulation of HRV ducting, and propose corrective measures to maintain Passive House standards.
- Compare the effectiveness of site supervision strategies, such as the use of mock-ups or coordination meetings, in maintaining quality and compliance with Passive House principles.
- Describe how to develop a comprehensive construction management plan that integrates quality assurance, coordination of trades, and proper documentation to achieve Passive House certification.​
