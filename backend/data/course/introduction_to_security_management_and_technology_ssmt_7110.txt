## Introduction to Security Management and Technology SSMT 7110
Security Systems Management Course
## Course Overview
This course provides students with knowledge and skills necessary to identify and reduce employee, client, and personal exposure to risks. Students will develop a working knowledge and understanding of the elements and components of security management and technology. Components of this course introduce the skills and expertise necessary for emergency planning and preparedness.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Admission to a Forensics credential program or permission of the Program Head.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify hazards and threats posed to organizations, its people, property, and physical assets.
- Describe the diversity and magnitude of the security management industry.
- Identify areas of general and specialized interest within the various streams of the Security Management & Technology certificate.
- Describe the importance and obligations of industry and business to create and manage emergency preparedness plans that include business continuity.
- Develop remedies and solutions to maintain public safety and business continuity within their organization and or community following a critical incident.
- Develop policy and procedures for application to their roles and functions related to ethical behaviour and management.
