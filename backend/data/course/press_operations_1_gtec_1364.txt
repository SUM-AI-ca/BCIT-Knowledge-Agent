## Press Operations 1 GTEC 1364
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course identifies and describes the main functioning parts of small printing presses in the offset printing industry. Students set-up and adjust presses to print their projects and use basic bindery procedures to achieve professional results. Students will prepare printing plates and operate the presses under supervision to produce a variety of printed material. Students are shown different paper characteristics, such as formation, color brightness and grain direction which all help give a better printed product. Safety and environmental issues, within the printing industry, are covered including process and pressroom chemistry, inks and toners.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Adjust all functioning parts of the feeder, register and delivery, inking, water and cylinder systems.
- Set the feeder, register and delivery system for consistent and continuous printing.
- Adjust the press based on the thickness of paper.
- Recognize the differences between lithography, flexo and various print methods.
- Observe steps of producing a simple job from concept to finishing.
- Practice safety procedures on a small format press.
- Operate the different power components of a small format press.
- Perform basic maintenance on a small format press.
- Select the best paper for a defined print job.
- Review and understand the importance and purpose of good safety manuals.
- Observe good safety practices.
