## Advanced Motor Control TELC 0166
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
Covers principles of advanced Motor Control and the features of 'soft-start' controllers for those working in a modern industrial environment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TELC 0130
### Credits
0.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Develop ladder logic diagrams from schematic diagrams.
- Describe the operation and characteristics of smart (programmable) relays.
- Utilize smart relays to provide process control.
- Describe operation and features of 'soft-start' controllers.
- Connect and operate 'soft-start' controllers.
- Describe basic troubleshooting procedures.
