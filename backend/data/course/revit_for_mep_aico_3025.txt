## Revit for MEP AICO 3025
Computer Aided Design & Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Revit for MEP (mechanical, electrical and plumbing) uses 3D parametric models for both design and construction documentation. This course introduces the basic HVAC, electrical and plumbing components of the software. Architectural models are linked. The expectation is that students have current training/experience with Computer Aided Design. Students should also have exposure to building systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Link architectural files.
- Create and modify views.
- Create spaces and zones.
- Analyze heating and cooling loads.
- Use the HVAC module to add HVAC systems.
- Add piping and plumbing components.
- Add electrical circuits and conduits.
- Create and annotate construction documents.
Effective as of Fall 2011
