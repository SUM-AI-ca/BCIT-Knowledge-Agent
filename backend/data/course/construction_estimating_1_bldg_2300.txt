## Construction Estimating 1 BLDG 2300
Building Course
## Course Overview
Covers general theories of measurement and pricing of construction work. Introduces: recognition of work, specific methods of measurement, estimating forms and common techniques. Sources of cost data and bidding procedures are examined.
### Prerequisite(s)
- BLDG 1050
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Break down quantities of work according to the 16 Division MasterFormat format.
- Utilize standard estimating forms to process a number of items, their measurements and extensions, and input them for future pricing and evaluation.
- Use systematic methodologies of data evaluation and input to minimize the possibilities for error in estimating.
