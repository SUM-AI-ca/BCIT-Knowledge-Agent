## Electricity HMTF 1008
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the basic principles of electricity and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on electrical components and circuits.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
7.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe electricity
- Use electrical testing instruments
- Service batteries
- Service charging systems
- Service starting systems
- Service electrical circuits
