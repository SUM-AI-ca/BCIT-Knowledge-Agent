## Overview

This test allows students to demonstrate the required mathematics knowledge and skills at the grade 11 level. Topics include basic algebra, geometry, trigonometry, and problem solving. For more information on the test, visit www.bcit.ca/cas/math/mathcompetencytesting.shtml . The Math 0110 fee is non-refundable.
