## Injection Molding Process PLAS 1020
Plastics Course
## Course Overview
Covers the basic elements of the Injection Molding Process including the molding cycle, plasticating systems, clamping units, backpressure and a general description of the mold elements. The reciprocating screw system is discussed along with a description of how polymer melting occurs. The course also covers machine specifications and their meaning.
### Prerequisite(s)
- PLAS 1002 or permission of the instructor for those students in the Injection Molding Industry or those with sufficient previous experience.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will have knowledge of:
- Conversion factors.
- Introduction to the basic molding cycle and process.
- Advantages and disadvantages of Injection Molding.
- Basic elements:
- Injection unit - Melting systems, screws, backpressure.
- Molds - Terminology.
- Clamping unit - hydraulic, toggle.
- Machine specifications.
- Basic process:
- Molding cycle.
- Basic molding factors.
- Safety.
