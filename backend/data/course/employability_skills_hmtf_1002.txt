## Employability Skills HMTF 1002
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to employment readiness, communication skills using electronic media, and industry business practices.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate employment readiness skills.
- Demonstrate communication skills.
- Describe business practices.
