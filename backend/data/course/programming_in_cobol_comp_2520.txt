## Programming in Cobol COMP 2520
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the COBOL language. Uses the structured, modular programming techniques developed in COMP 1510 to solve practical business problems. Topics include subroutines, record and file processing, tables, business reports, and data validation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in COMP 1710 and 50% in COMP 1510
