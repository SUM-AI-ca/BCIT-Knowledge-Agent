## Environmental Drone Applications RPAS 0110
Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to Transport Canada RPAS regulations, site assessment, mission planning and a wide range of RPAS applications. Students will collect color, multi-spectral and thermal photogrammetric images and apply structure-from-motion processing to generate sparse and dense point clouds, 3D textured models, digital surface models (DSM) and rectified orthomosaics.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon sucessful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Recall main RPAS components and their function.
- Understand RPAS history and main applications.
- Understand and implement Transport Canada regulations to the safe operation of RPAS.
- Recall pertinent laws and regulations relating to RPAS operations.
- Recall normal and emergency RPAS procedures pertinent to mission planning.

- Demonstrate on how to setup an RPAS for image acquisition.
- Identify the main components of a site survey.
- Use the appropriate software applications to help conduct a site survey.

- Understand electro-magnetic radiation theory as it pertains to cameras and sensors.
- Differentiate between passive and active sensors.
- Describe characteristics common to multi-spectral sensors.

- Understand how multi-spectral and thermal sensors are applied in industry for a variety of uses.
- Differentiate between non-radiometric and radiometric thermal images
- Recall NDVI equation and applications

- Recognize the steps required to import and align drone imagery.
- Distinguish between the various point filtering methods.
- Explain the basics of structure from motion.
- Articulate how depth maps and dense clouds are created.
- Recall the steps to generate digital surface model (DSM) from depth maps or dense clouds.
- Differentiate between digital surface and digital terrain (DTM) models.
- List the input requirements for orthophoto generation.
- Recall the steps required to generate a regular and true orthophoto.
- Identify the main types of orthophoto problems and their solutions.
- Review thermal energy, sensor, and image characteristics.
- Describe thermal image calibration & standardization.
- Describe thermal image calibration & standardization.
- Differentiate between non-radiometric and radiometric thermal images.

- Describe the functionality of a raster calculator.

- Explain hill shade models and their applications.
