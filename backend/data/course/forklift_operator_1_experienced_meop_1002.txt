## Forklift Operator 1 - Experienced MEOP 1002
Mobile Equipment Operator Course
## Course Overview
​This course will provide the experienced forklift operator with both theory and practical training in the operation of counterbalanced lift trucks. On successful completion, you will receive Mobile Equipment Operator Certification for Class 1, 4 and 5 counterbalanced lift trucks. Certification is valid for two years. This course meets current WCB guidelines and CSA Standards for lift-truck operators.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Forklift operation experience.

1.0
