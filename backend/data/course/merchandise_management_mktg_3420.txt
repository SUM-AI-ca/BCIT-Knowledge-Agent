## Merchandise Management MKTG 3420
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
The course is an introduction to the concepts, fundamental steps, and tools and techniques, of developing a robust retail assortment plan. It is designed to illustrate, step by step, how to develop a category assortment plan to address the needs of an identified business target and consumer segment. The retail assortment plan will also work to solidify brand equity and secure market competitiveness, all while maximizing return on invested capital. The process helps to define the role of each product, or class of products within the company from, revenue, image, profit, and traffic points of view based on Category Role. The fundamentals and concept of the course can be directly applied to wholesale, retail and manufacturing businesses.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the concept, role, and importance of assortment planning in improving company’s bottom-line by increasing inventory turn, revenue, average gross margin, and improving brand image.
- Learn the process and steps involved in the development and evaluation of an Assortment Plan.
- Define category types and tactical implications across operating units.
- Develop a product hierarchy and category segmentation to facilitate SKU analysis across the four pillars of market, consumer, competitive and corporate financial data.
- Identify the role of each product within the assortment as a traffic, revenue, profit generator, or image enhancement.
- Conduct a “What If Analysis” to assess impact of retail price and cost changes on unit sales and gross margin to optimize the balance between return on investment and market share.
- The tools and methodology to assess the importance of each segment to the business by analyzing its market attractiveness and company’s readiness.
- Identify key price points within each category by creating a price ladder to achieve profit and market share objectives.
- Develop a Keep, Drop, and Shop list.
- Develop negotiation strategy and tactical plan with suppliers.
- Outline a clear product transition plan to reduce markdown.
