## Railway Yard Safety RAIL 0126
Railway Course
## Course Overview
This course will give those interested in working in the railway industry the opportunity to learn essential skills required for safety in a railway yard and working on freight equipment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe rules related to walking in railway yards.
- Describe rules related to walking in front of or around freight equipment.
- Describe safely entraining, crossing over and detraining stationary freight equipment.
- Explain situational awareness related to railway operations.
- Explain safety factors while in a railway yard.
