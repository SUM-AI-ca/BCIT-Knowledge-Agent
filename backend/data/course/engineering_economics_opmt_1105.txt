## Engineering Economics OPMT 1105
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
Emphasizes the importance of making sound economic decisions when faced with alternative methods of solving technical problems. Provides the basic skills and concepts required to analyze comparative costs and to understand the time value of money (interest), inflation, depreciation, running costs, salvage value and tax considerations.

- No prerequisites are required for this course.
