## Thermal Cutting Equipment & Processes MTLF 1230
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
In this course you will learn how to cut ferrous metals using oxy-fuel processes and non-ferrous metals with the plasma arc cutting processes. You will apply the theoretical concepts explained in the class in the practical projects to fabricate structural and plate components. This will include setting up oxy-fuel and plasma cutting equipment in order to cut, pierce and bevel various thickness of metal plate, cope structural shapes for fit-up, and use flame straightening.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1200

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use oxy-fuel cutting equipment for cutting ferrous metals.
- Apply safety procedures for handling and storing oxy-fuel components.
- Identify safety devices in an oxy-fuel system.
- Use plasma arc cutting processes on ferrous and non-ferrous metals.
- Apply safety procedures for plasma arc cutting.
Effective as of Fall 2017
