## E-Commerce with Shopify: How to start functional business website XMKT 1044
Marketing Microcredential Course
## Course Overview
​Leave this five-module course with a fully functional e-commerce website to launch and grow your business.T his course is most valuable to those who already have a business idea or have an existing business and now need an e-commerce website. You need your new website up and ready like yesterday. But life is busy, especially when you run a business, so it has fallen to the back-burner more than once. Together we're going to start and finish your website in just five modules, giving you a fully functional website ready to launch.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify elements of a successful business website, including focus, layout, and functionality
- Design and populate a customized business website using Shopify
- Demonstrate knowledge and application of automation tools within Shopify platform
