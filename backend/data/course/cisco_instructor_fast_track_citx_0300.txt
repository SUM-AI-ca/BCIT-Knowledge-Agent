## Cisco Instructor Fast Track CITX 0300
Computer Information Technology Electronics Course
## Course Overview
This course provides fast track accreditation to Cisco Networking Academy instructors who have relevant industry certifications. Enrolment must be approved by BCIT’s Networking Academy manager, students must be sponsored by a Cisco Networking Academy, and listed as an instructor for that academy. In addition, registrants must have current industry certification appropriate to the course to be accredited for as an instructor. Students prepare for and then write an on-line exam followed by a skills based assessment. These exams must be coordinated and invigilated by the sponsoring academy. This course does not provide any training on the subject area. Candidates are assumed to have gained the technical skill set through previous training and experience.
### Prerequisite(s)
- CompTIA A+ Certification, or Cisco CCENT Certification, or Cisco CCNA Certification.
