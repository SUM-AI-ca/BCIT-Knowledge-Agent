## Health Labour Relations HMGT 7260
Health Management Course
## Course Overview
The ability to effectively apply contracts, resolve disputes, handle grievances and participate in negotiations is critical for all health care managers. This course introduces the development of industrial relations in the public sector and health care union and management relations. It examines labour legislation and the function of bargaining units, collective bargaining, the application of contracts and resolving disputes. Grievance handling, the arbitration process, and alternative dispute resolution processes are also introduced. This course takes an experiential approach to explore the concepts and theories and explore the different approaches to negotiations. Students will have numberous opportunities to participate in discussions, negotiations, case studies and to apply what they learn.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Discuss why individuals join unions and the development of public sector and health care unions.
- Describe labour legislation (using BC's as an example).
- Describe the purpose of the Labour Relations Code and how it sets a legal context to Employer/Union relationships.
- Apply labour legislation to current labour/management issues.
- Analyze the difficulties of applying/administering collective agreements.
- Explain the grievance process and third party processes.
- Understand the role of arbitration and mediation.
- Explain how bargaining units are formed.
- Explain the collective bargaining process.
- Define roles and responsibilities of effective team members.
- Write proposals and counterproposals for presentation during bargaining process.
- Effectively communicate proposals to opposing team.
- Apply negotiation skills during bargaining process.
- Apply problem-solving skills to labour relations issues.
