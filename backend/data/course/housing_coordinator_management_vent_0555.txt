## Housing Coordinator Management VENT 0555
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
BCIT's Housing Coordinator Management program is being offered to Aboriginal Housing Coordinators. The program provides 8 weeks of part time, key skill training in housing operations, budgeting, accounting, collections and building and basic contract quotes. The program offers a combination of traditional classroom training and live/interactive webinars. Personal coaching will also be provided via phone and internet throughout the 8 week program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
