## Fall Protection CNST 0121
Construction Course
## Course Overview
Reviews all aspects of fall protection as it applies to the construction industry and in accordance with the recent changes in the WCB Regulations (2005). Students learn pre-planning and proper application of fall protection systems, restraining systems, fall arrest systems and various personal fall protection systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
