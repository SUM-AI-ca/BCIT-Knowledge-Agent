## Cardiovascular Clinical 3: Circulating Technologist Techniques CARD 6333
Cardiology/Cardiovascular Course
## Course Overview
A 4-week full-time clinical rotation performed in a BCIT approved Cardiovascular Laboratory at a health care facility. Participate in Cardiovascular Patient Care (Circulator) duties, first delivered in CARD 3360 and CARD 4330 . Assist in selection of surgical supplies for catheterization, the opening and passing of sterile supplies onto a sterile field, proper patient placement on the x-ray table, response to patient needs and delivery of patient teaching as it applies to Cardiovascular manoeuvres and testing. In addition, you may be asked to assess the patient and document patient data, prepare and operate a contrast injector, operate a cardiac defibrillator, and administer medications as directed by the physician. Requires a successful evaluation from the clinical site before BCIT awards credit.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Successful completion of Cardiovascular theory courses plus program head approval. (6 credits)
