## Lumber Grading - Uppers Grades WOOD 2104
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
WOOD 2104 is a lumber grading course leading to attainment of an industry Lumber Grading Ticket in grading clear and shop class lumber from a CLS accredited grading agency.
### Prerequisite(s)
- WOOD 1204
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Apply grade rules to grade Clears under R List (all species by WRC).
- Apply grade rules to grade Clears under NLGA Para 108, 109.
- Apply grade rules to grade Shop Lumber under NLGA.
- Apply grade rules to grade Flitch Lumber under NLGA.
- Apply grade rules to grade Door Stock under NLGA.
- Apply grade rules to grade Window Stock under NLGA.
