## Overview

This course focuses on the medical terminology of anatomy and physiology. You will study the terminology of the digestive, urinary, reproductive, nervous, cardiovascular, and respiratory systems. For each system, you will learn about the organs, the functions of each component, common disease processes and symptoms, and anatomical terminology. You will build expertise in the use of common prefixes, suffixes, and terminology related to disease processes of specific body systems. Anatomy and Terminology 1 will cover approximately 50% of the human body systems and lead into Anatomy and Terminology 2.

## Learning Outcomes

- Analyze and divide medical words into component parts.
- Build medical words using common suffixes, prefixes, and combining forms.
- Name the organs of the digestive system.
- Describe the functions of the components, common disease processes and symptoms related to the digestive system.
- Name the organs and use terminology related to the organs of the urinary system.
- Describe the common disease processes and symptoms that affect the urinary system.
- Describe common symptoms and disease processes related to the organs of the urinary system.
- Name the organs and use terminology related to the organs of the male and female reproductive systems.
- Describe common disease processes and symptoms that affect the male and female reproductive systems.
- Name the organs and use terminology related to the nervous system.
- Describe the function of the component parts, common disease processes and symptoms that affect the nervous system.
- Name the organs of the cardiovascular system.
- Describe the function of the components, common disease processes and symptoms that affect the cardiovascular system.
- Name the organs of the respiratory system.
- Name the organs and use terminology related to the organs and diseases of the respiratory system.
- Describe the functions, common disease processes and symptoms that affect components of the respiratory system.
