## Creating and Marketing your Own Web Series BCST 0108
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
With the rise in popularity of short form video content on the web, it is an exciting time to start creating web series content of your own. During this hands-on week-long workshop, you will get started on that journey - developing ideas; scripting, storyboarding and shooting a trailer or pilot episode on your smartphone or camera; and sketching out the story world and campaign ideas for its' release. Explore platforms for delivery, debate strategic rollout, navigate the festival market, and discuss sustainable funding models for continued production.
### Prerequisite(s)

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop an idea for a web series
- Film a teaser or pilot episode for a web series
- Strategize over web series marketing and roll out
- Explore case studies pertaining to sustainable funding models
