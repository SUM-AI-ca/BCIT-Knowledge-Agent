## Introduction to the Airline Industry AIRC 1009
Airport/Airline Core Course
## Course Overview
​This course will provide a broad understanding of all aspects of the airline industry within Canada and globally. It will cover the regulatory compliance for both Canadian and International airlines, the structure of the airline industry, airline economics, strategy, structure, technology, and environmental issues. Evaluation includes in-class activities, presentation assignment, final project, and a final exam.​​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the current global airline environment and the impact of the airline industry on the national and world economy.
- Identify and use aviation terminology.
- Explain the basic components of the theory of flight.
- Describe the historic development of the aviation industry from conception to modern day.
- Explain the structure and organization of the airline industry.
- Identify the regulators of the national and global airline industry.
