## Math for Electricians 3 TELC 0123
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for students planning to attend level three technical training for the electrical apprenticeship program. Course topics include three phase circuit analysis and three phase transformer calculations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in TELC 0122
