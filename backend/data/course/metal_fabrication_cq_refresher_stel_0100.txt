## Metal Fabrication CQ Refresher STEL 0100
Steel Fabrication Course
## Course Overview
Prepares participants for the Steel Fabrication Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) and refreshes theoretical knowledge of the trade. Contact the Industry Training Centre to determine eligibility and to schedule an exam.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Minimum of six years experience in the trade.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- A-1 Describe safe work practices in the trade.
- A-2 Describe welding processes.
- A-3 Describe methods for controlling distortion.
- A-4 Describe and use plate and pipe development.
- A-5 Make patterns for shop construction.
- A-6 Solve trade related mathematical problems.
- A-7 Describe Metallurgy.
- A-8 Describe production methods.
- A-9 Identify various cutting methods.
