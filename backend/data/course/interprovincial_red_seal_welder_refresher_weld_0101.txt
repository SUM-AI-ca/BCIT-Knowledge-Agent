## Interprovincial Red Seal Welder Refresher WELD 0101
Welding Course
## Course Overview
​This is a condensed four module course providing the necessary theoretical material and review for those welders interested in attempting the Interprovincial “Red Seal” examination. Participants are required already has a minimum “B-Level” or Welder Apprentice Level 3 endorsement in their Welders Log Book prior to taking the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Participants must possess a minimum “B-Level” or Welder Apprentice Level 3 endorsement in their Welders Log Book prior to taking the course.

0.0

## Learning Outcomes
- Prepares students to write the Interprovincial Red Seal Examination which allows mobility of employment to all provinces in Canada.
- Following successful completion of the Red Seal examination, a welder could supervise future apprentices through new and existing provincial apprenticeship programs.
- An excellent review package for apprentices challenging their certification examination.
