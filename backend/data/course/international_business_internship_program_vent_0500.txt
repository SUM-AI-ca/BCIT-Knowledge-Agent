## International Business Internship Program VENT 0500
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
The International Business Internship Program is designed specifically for International students. The program combines studies in general business with an internship. Upon completion of the program, students will have the business knowledge, confidence and understanding necessary to gain employment in the business field or evaluate the opportunities available to start their own business.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will:
- Understand business concepts.
- Have the confidence and understanding necessary to gain employment in the business field.
- Evaluate the opportunities available to start their own business.
