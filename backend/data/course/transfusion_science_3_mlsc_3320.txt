## Transfusion Science 3 MLSC 3320
Medical Laboratory Science Course
## Course Overview
Builds upon information covered in MLSC 1320 -Transfusion Science 1 and MLSC 2320 – Transfusion Science 2. Introduces special transfusion situations, transfusion therapy and complex patient presentations through case studies. Covers perinatal testing and hemolytic disease of the fetus and newborn. Learning is assessed through a combination of exams, quizzes, assignments, and laboratory work. complex patient presentations, and hemolytic disease of the newborn.
### Prerequisite(s)
- All Level 2 courses and MLSC 2320
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Perform investigation procedures used for identification of irregular antibodies.
- Perform a transfusion reaction investigation.
- Describe blood components and fractions with consideration of donor issues and special treatment of blood products.
