## Digital Arts Discovery Bootcamp MDIA 0888
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Join us for a fun four week Discovery Bootcamp. If you are creative and have a passion for computers and art and would like to experience a sampling of Digital Arts programs including: new media, animation, design and digital printing this exciting summer program may be for you! Students will have a hands-on experience using leading edge software including; Photoshop, In-Design, Final-Cut Pro, Dreamweaver and Maya. Work in teams to build a website, shoot video, create audio, dabble with animation and design and produce a color brochure.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Create a website.
- Shoot & edit video for website.
- Animate a model provided by the instructor.
- Design, create & layout a brochure.
- Edit and print brochure.
