## Equipment Maintenance - Wood WOOD 2060
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
Presents and overview of electrical and mechanical equipment in the wood products industry focusing on the transmission, application and control of power. Topics include: drive systems, types of controls, lubrication and bearings, protection and controlling of electrical equipment, industrial electrical power systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Senior Secondary school science and math.
