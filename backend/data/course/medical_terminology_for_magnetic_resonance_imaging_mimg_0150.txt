## Medical Terminology for Magnetic Resonance Imaging MIMG 0150
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
​This course is intended to familairize students with medical terminology and equip them them to be proficient in interpreting and effectively using that terminology. Topics covered include an overview of medical terms specific to Magnetic Resonance Imaging and well as the root words, prefixies, suffixes and acronyms for many other common medical terms.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
