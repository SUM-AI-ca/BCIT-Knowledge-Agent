## Industry Operations GTEC 1000
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the printing industry, its terminology, production processes, and trends and provides for some first-hand knowledge and experience through guest lectures, visits to modern production facilities, and an opportunity to work through the Graphic Arts Technical Foundation Print Scape audio visual program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the evolution and history of the printing industry.
- List the various occupations within the printing industry.
- Describe the many and varied components or sectors within the industry.
- Explore varied and broad range of employment opportunities within the industry.
- Define the key terms in the industry.
- Describe the products used in the manufacture of print such as paper, ink and technology.
- Explain the administration of a printing operation.
- Explain why different departments, within a printing company, need to communicate with each other.
- Describe the processes of a printed piece from design concept through to a final finished product.
- Appreciate of the importance of estimating to the overall success of a printing operation.
