## Quality Auditing in Construction CMGT 7255
Construction Management - CMGT Course
## Course Overview
Provides a deeper understanding of Quality Systems and Plans, and enables participants to become an Internal Auditor within an organization and better prepare for an external audit. Those who obtain a minimum of 70% will be issued with a Certificate of Successful Completion, recognized by the International Register of Certificated Auditors as a critical step in becoming an IRCA registered Internal Auditor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CMGT 7246
### Credits
1.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Develop an audit plan.
- Prepare an audit checklist.
- Conduct an audit including the opening and closing meetings.
- Write non-conformance and audit reports.
- Evaluate an auditee's conformance with its Quality System.
- Monitor their own organization's quality performance.
- Support their own organization in preparation for an external audit.
