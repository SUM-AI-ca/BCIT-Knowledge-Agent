## Blogging for Bloggers MDIA 2112
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Upon completion of this course, participants will have a better understanding of the technology, tools and philosophies behind the blogosphere. Additionally, they will refine their blog finding, reading and writing skills. We will also cover Online Communities, Personal Portals and emerging practices in audio and video blogging.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MDIA 1112
### Credits
1.5

## Learning Outcomes
- Personal portals and communities.
- Blogging and the arts: Web 2.0 Independent Publishing fo Business and Artists.
- Building your community and traffic, connecting with your tibe in the blogosphere.
- How to make money with your blog.
- In-depth look at aggregation, blogging tools and technology.
- Emerging trends including Podcasing and Video blogging.
