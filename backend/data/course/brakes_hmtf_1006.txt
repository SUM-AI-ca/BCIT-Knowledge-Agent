## Brakes HMTF 1006
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the principles of operation, repair, and service procedures performed on hydraulic and air brake systems. This course also introduces students to service and repair procedures performed on bearings, seals, tires, wheels, and hubs.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
12.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Service bearings and seals
- Service tires, wheels, and hubs
- Identify lubricants
- Service hydraulic brakes
- Service hydraulic power brakes
- Service air brakes
