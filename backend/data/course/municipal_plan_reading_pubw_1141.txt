## Municipal Plan Reading PUBW 1141
Public Works Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces the student to interpretation of engineering drawings used in public works construction, including the correlation between construction layout, specifications and standard drawings. Prior completion of PUBW 1001 is recommended.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Utilize engineering scale rules to determine distances on plans and drawings.
- Identify the basic components of a set of engineering drawings.
- Identify and utilize various plans and drawings common to municipal operations.
- Explain and use terminology related to roadworks, sewerage, drainage, and water distribution.
- Interpret plan and profile information, grades and slopes as shown on engineering drawings.
- Interpret construction information such as roadway alignments and geometrics, utility features, etc., as found on engineering drawings.
Effective as of Fall 2010
