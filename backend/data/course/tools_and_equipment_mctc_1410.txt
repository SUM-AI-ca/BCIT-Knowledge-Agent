## Tools and Equipment MCTC 1410
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed to provide students with the theoretical understanding and practical skills related to tools and equipment, the application and safe handling of the tools. All coursework reflects the type of skills encountered in the Motorcycle service industry.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCTC 1400
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify hand tools.
- Identify shop power tools.
- Describe fastening devices.
- Use shop tools and equipment.
- Describe threading and thread repair tools.
- Use measuring tools and instruments.
Effective as of Fall 2022
