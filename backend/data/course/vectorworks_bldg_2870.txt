## VectorWorks BLDG 2870
Building Course
## Course Overview
This course will cover fundamentals of Architectural drawing in VectorWorks. Topics include: 2D and 3D object creation and editing; snap constraints; dimensioning; file organization-classes, layers, and sheets; 2D and 3D walls, windows, doors and roofs. 3D modeling and rendering. Course materials are provided.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use most of VectorWorks 2D, 3D drawing module efficiently and will have a general understanding of the steps required to set up drawing files, and produce VectorWorks drawings.
