## Aviation Communication Skills – Crew Resource Management AIRC 1100
Airport/Airline Core Course
## Course Overview
​This course is the foundation for effective communication skills for an aviation environment. It includes the following topics: the fundamentals of interpersonal communication, effective listening and questioning skills, the effect that diversity has on communications, the challenges of communication in aviation operations workplace, presentation skills so that your message is understood and remembered and maximizing the impact of meetings. Learners will develop the written and oral communication skills to enable them to become effective communicators in the working environment. Evaluation includes a presentation and examination.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the impact of effective and ineffective communication in the aviation environment.
- Identify different channels to communicate verbally and non-verbally.
- Describe the foundations of interpersonal skills and how they influence face-to-face communication.
- Evaluate personal individual preferred communication style.
- Explain how the challenges of intercultural communication and diversity impact communication in aviation.
- Explain how to use a structured meeting process using the required model.
- Explain the steps of presentation planning.
- Deliver a presentation.​
