## Technical Writing Review NAUT 0251
Ocean Navigation Course
## Course Overview
The course provides the students with a comprehensive view and basic understanding of the wide range of types and means of communications existing in the maritime world. Emphasis is placed on developing and improving the student’s abilities to produce written work that is accurate, brief, and clear.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Apply concepts of effective communication in an industrial setting.
- Construct a factual report based on all information gathered.
- Understand the human factor to better reach and inform the intended audience.
