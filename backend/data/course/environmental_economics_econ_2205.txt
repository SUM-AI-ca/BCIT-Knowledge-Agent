## Environmental Economics ECON 2205
Economics Course
## Course Overview
This course offers the student a survey of current topics in environmental economics and regulation. Specifically, the course will look at the different methods used to protect the environment and the inherent difficulties and costs associated with each method. Further, the course will citically evaluate current Canadian and global environmental policy.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
