## Introduction to Construction Management for Mass Timber TMBR 1007
Mass Timber Construction Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides basic principles of construction management including construction planning.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in TMBR 1000
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the main stages of a project lifecycle from planning to occupancy (pre-design, design, construction, occupancy phases).
- Identify the critical aspects of each phase of a construction project.
- Describe the roles of various parties involved in each construction phase.
- Describe the main terms used in scheduling projects for mass timbers.
- Describe the main stages and components in a schedule.
- Describe the field worker's scope in terms of the construction management schedules.
