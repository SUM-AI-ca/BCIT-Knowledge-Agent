## Radiation Risks and Protection MIMG 7009
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
Philosophy of radiation protection through recommendations of ICRP 60. Practical radiation protection training. For those needing to design and perform QA programs, answers questions such as "How do I apply the justification of a practice' principle in my department?" and many more. Introduction to non-ionizing radiation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

3.0

## Learning Outcomes
On completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Outline the essential concepts of radiation physics, chemistry and biology relevant to the study of radiation risks and protection.
- Describe dose response models of radiation risks and outline the models recommended by ICRP.
- Describe the quantitative estimates of the consequences of radiation exposure.
- Outline the concepts of risk assessment and discuss the public's perception and acceptance of the risks of radiation.
- Evaluate the risks associated with various imaging procedures.
- Critically evaluate departmental radiation protection programs.
- Outline sources of radiation exposure and describe various radiation quantities and their units.
- Trace the development of radiation protection criteria and standards.
- Evaluate the objectives of radiation protection; outline the elements of radiation protection and discuss basic radiation safety criteria.
- Outline current radiation protection standards.
- Analyze various methods from monitoring radiation doses in radiology.
- Assess radiation safety criteria and protection standards as applied to dose reduction technologies in modern x-ray systems.
- Outline equipment protection specifications and calculate radiation-shielding requirements for a medical imaging facility.
- Analyze the protection consideration in Magnetic Resonance Imaging.
- Outline the hazards associated with the use of non-ionizing radiation.
