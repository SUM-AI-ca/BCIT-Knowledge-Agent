## Construction Estimating 1 BLDG 2305
Building Course
## Course Overview
This course covers general theories of measurement of construction work, emphasizing sound estimating procedures and accuracy. It introduces identification of work items, specific methods of measurement, estimating forms, and common techniques used for quantity take-off with a focus on concrete, earthwork, masonry, structural steel and wood frame items.
### Prerequisite(s)
- BLDG 1050

4.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Break down items of work according to the CIQS Method of Measurement.
- Utilize standard estimating Quantity Sheets to describe and measure items of work.
- Minimize the risk of estimating errors by using systematic methodologies of data evaluation and input.
- Perform quantity take-offs related to:
- Division 3 Concrete
- Division 4 Masonry
- Division 5 Metals
- Division 6 Wood, Plastics and Composites
- Division 31 Earthwork
