## Aboriginal Ventures Program VENT 1002
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
The BCIT Aboriginal Ventures Program is an entrepreneurial skills training program based on 15 years of delivering practical, need-to-know business planning skills and risk reduction techniques. The program offers comprehensive training targeted toward individuals in the planning and launch phase of a new business venture or community project. The program discusses specific challenges and opportunities that Aboriginals face doing business on and off reserve. Participants will gain the ability to research a business concept and produce an operational business or project plan, practical entrepreneurial/business skills to start and operate a business or business unit within an organization or community and valuable, easy-to-learn computer modelling skills for forecasting and analytical purposes.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Aboriginal ancestry, including Status, Non-status, Metis or Inuit, career preference to operate a business, technical skills and experience appropriate to the business/project that is planned, business concept or project in mind, basic computer skills.

## Learning Outcomes
- Understand components/purpose of the Business Plan.
- Gain a working knowledge of computers as a business tool: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Internet.
- Understand basic accounting skills.
- Acquire an ability to develop and analyze Financial Statements / Cash Flow using a computer model.
- Understand what banks need from business owners in order to lend money.
- Develop a marketing strategy: product/service definition, market research, pricing, direct marketing.
- Understand and use negotiation and relationship selling as a tool to sell themselves and their products/services.
- Understand and apply Human Resource tools such as time and stress management, goal setting, labour standards, legal aspects of starting a business.
- Directed Studies: independent study and development of business plan.
