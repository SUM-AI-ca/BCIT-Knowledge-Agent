## Overview

This course is one of two practicum courses that will provide practical work experience that is relevant to your field specific career path. You will spend approximately 120 hours with an industry organization performing various tasks associated with the firm's marketing efforts. You will be required to submit written reports and/or a work journal as well as regular update meetings with faculty advisor. Your performance will be assessed by an organization representative and BCIT faculty.

## Learning Outcomes

- Apply your knowledge and skills to complete field-related tasks within an existing industry organization.
- Demonstrate your ability to attend and perform effectively in a real-life work environment.
- Assess your performance in an industry working environment.
