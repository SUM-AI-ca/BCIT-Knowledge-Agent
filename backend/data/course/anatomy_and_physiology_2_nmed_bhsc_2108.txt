## Anatomy & Physiology 2 (NMED) BHSC 2108
Basic Health Sciences Course
## Course Overview
​This is the second of two consecutive courses that provide a comprehensive study of the anatomy and physiology of the normal human body in the context of nuclear medicine. This course covers the respiratory system, digestive system, urinary system, nervous system and endocrine system.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 65% in BHSC 1108
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the relational anatomy and physiology of the following organ systems relevant to Nuclear Medicine clinical procedures and anatomical landmarking:
- ​Respiratory system
- Digestive system
- Urinary system
- Nervous system
- Endocrine system
