## Portfolio Design & Presentation MDIA 2098
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
During this course students will design and create a series of tightly defined documents for presentation to prospective employers. Students will prepare an effective medium to assist in furthering their careers by creating documents professionally while using the Adobe Creative Suite including InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop and Acrobat. The instructor will provide guidance regarding the portfolio content. All students will produce a print portfolio as well as a pdf electronic portfolio. This course is recommended to be the last course in the certificate.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MDIA 1040 and ( MDIA 1049 and MDIA 2049 ) and MDIA 1140 and MDIA 1105 and MDIA 1900 and ( MDIA 1180 or MDIA 1170 ) and ( MDIA 2180 or MDIA 2270 ) and MDIA 2012 and MDIA 1144 and ( MDIA 1025 or MDIA 1026 ) and ( MDIA 1160 or MDIA 1260 ) and ( MDIA 2260 or MDIA 2261 ) and MDIA 2088

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Conceptualize and draw a series of logos for each portfolio project.
- Create a series of magazine adverts with common elements and branding.
- Prepare branded marketing materials for a financial services group.
- Produce a stationery system for a small business.
- Design and format a bilingual folded menu and wine list.
- Create a master template for presenting portfolio work.
- Design and produce a series of large format branded posters.
- Create and format a two-sided z-fold marketing brochure for a realty group.
- Design and produce a custom size retail travel advert using advanced tools.
- Revise and polish materials for portfolio presentation.
- Prepare a professional portfolio with high resolution pictures from image banks.
- Create a professional resumé using InDesign to accompany the portfolio.
