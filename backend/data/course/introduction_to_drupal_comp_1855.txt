## Introduction to Drupal COMP 1855
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
COMP 1855 has been replaced by COMP 1854 . Drupal is an open source (free), modular content management system (CMS) for the web. This hands-on course is designed for students who have completed COMP 1850 or those with some understanding of HTML. Participants will learn how easy it is to keep web page information up to date with Drupal. Drupal is a great choice for small businesses, charities and non-profit organizations who need to accommodate multiple authors. Topics include: site structure, modules, security and simple customization of menus and templates. By the end of this course, students will have created a live project and be familiar with Drupal's administrative interface.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Configure a personal instance of Drupal.
- Configure custom site security levels for different modules and users.
- Define a CMS and detail the options available to users and administrators.
- Download, install and configure preselected modules.
- Customize simple interfaces such as menus, log in and template pages.
- Utilize online resources for beginner tasks with Drupal.
- Understand how workflow systems operate with Drupal.
- Integrate other open source technologies such as Blogs, Forums and RSS.
