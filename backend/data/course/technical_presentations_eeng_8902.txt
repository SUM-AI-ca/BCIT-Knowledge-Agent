## Technical Presentations EENG 8902
Environmental Engineering Course
## Course Overview
The ability to make an effective presentation is now more essential than ever for individuals wanting to advance their careers. This course will allow participants to analyse the needs of your audience and then integrate your objective to the content and audience. Video feedback will be used extensively to provide immediate and practical comments as you develop comfort and confidence in business presentations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Diploma or Degree in Engineering or Science.
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, participants will be able to:
- Present a variety of topics to general and technical audiences using prepared overheads from a computer-generated presentation application and/or laptop along with other appropriate visual support materials if necessary.
- Define objectives through the use of specific formats.
- Organize handouts and notes.
- Energize themselves through eye contact, gestures and pacing.
- Channel nervousness.
- Create and use effective visuals.
- Motivate different types within an audience.
- Develop strong introductions and conclusions.
- Demonstrate knowledge and skill in interpreting technical concepts to inform.
- Answer questions about material presented in a professional manner.
