## Welding for Automotive WELD 0185
Welding Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to welding through the use of oxy fuel and GMAW equipment. Students are expected to first learn safe working procedures associated with their welding tasks, and will then learn about oxy fuel and GMAW welding through practical tasks.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the basic safety requirements for oxy fuel equipment.
- Prepare and use oxy fuel equipment for practical tasks.
- Identify the safety requirements for GMAW equipment.
- Prepare and use GMAW equipment for practical tasks.
