## Whole-Building Life Cycle Assessment Final Project XZEB 1169
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
This prepares you to conduct whole-building life cycle assessment (LCA) and responsibly report results in an environmental building declaration. In this project-based course, students will practice completing a high-quality whole-building LCA and preparing an environmental building declaration. Prior knowledge of building design and construction is recommended for success in this course. Upon completion of this and the prerequisites ( XZEB 1160 , XZEB 1165 , and XZEB 1166 ), students can receive a microcredential certifying their skills in whole-building LCA. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- XZEB 1160 , XZEB 1165 and XZEB 1166 or equivalent experience

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Conduct a high-quality whole-building life cycle assessment.
- Demonstrate how to responsibly interpret and use wbLCA results.
- Prepare an environmental building declaration​​.
