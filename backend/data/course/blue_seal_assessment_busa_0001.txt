## Blue Seal Assessment BUSA 0001
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
​This course assesses eligibility for Blue Seal recognition for Red Seal and certified Journeymen. This is an S/U assessment. Instructions will be found in the learning hub. Upon registration, students will be contacted by the instructor or program assistant. The BUSA 0001 assessment fee is non-refundable.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Student must hold a valid Red Seal in any trade.
