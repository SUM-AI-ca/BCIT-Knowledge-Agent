## Script to Screen for the Independent Producer BCST 0102
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Have a passion to develop your original story idea into a short film or web series? Adapt an article, a life event or literary material for the screen? Not sure where to begin your journey as a media content creator? This hands-on workshop will acquaint you with successful projects and first steps in creating, budgeting, financing, and distributing your own screen based content, be it documentary, drama or branded.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Research and write a compelling visual treatment and/or proposal
- Know how to access essential resources supporting the development of original screen content
