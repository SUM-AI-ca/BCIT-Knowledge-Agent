## Formation Evaluation PETR 3304
Petroleum Course
## Course Overview
This course describes the various tools and principles used for reservoir formation evaluation while learning the basic method of anlyzing wireline logs, cores, and drill stem tests.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in PETR 2200 and 50% in PETR 2203 and 50% in PETR 2204
- PETR 2200 and PETR 2203 and PETR 2204
### Credits
5.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Calculate the water saturation in oil & gas reservoirs using openhole logs.
- Evaluate reservoir permeability and wellbore damage using DST and welltest.
- Calculate the initial oil and gas in place volumes in a reservoir.
- Calculate the initial reservoir pressure, formation damage, and permeability of a reservoir.
- Explain why oil and gas is suspected in a formation from various data collected.
