## AutoCADMap 2 ACGS 3000
AutoCAD/GIS Course
## Course Overview
Explores the advanced issues of AutoCAD map and utilizes complex tool sets. The emphasis is on GIS definitions, Topology (spatial relationships), advanced queries and property alteration and spatial analysis.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in ACGS 2000
