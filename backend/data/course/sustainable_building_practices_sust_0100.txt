## Introduction to Sustainable Building Practices for Trades SUST 0100
Sustainability Course
## Course Overview
This course looks at the ways that construction impacts the environment at every stage of it's design and build and the main considerations at each of these stages. The choices made in materials, building design, and the way cities and towns are constructed all have an environmental impact. These modules will introduce a number of concepts in sustainability and how they apply within the construction sector.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify key environmental issues in the built environment.
- Define main concepts in sustainability.
- Describe environmental challenges though out the building process.
- Be aware of some options for increased sustainability in the construction field.
