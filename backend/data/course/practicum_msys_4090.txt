## Practicum MSYS 4090
Mechanical Systems Course
## Course Overview
To demonstrate proficiency as a mechanical systems designer. During this time, the student would produce an industry standard systems design to the specification of the industry sponsor and BCIT instructor. To be taken on completion of all other credits in Certificate Program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all other credits on Associate Certificate program in Mechanical Systems.

3.0
