## Visual Communication 3: Drafting Graphics INTD 1345
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
Students will create two and three dimensional presentation style drawings mechanically and freehand. Three-dimensional techniques to produce one and two point perspective and praline drawings will be covered. Students will create a presentation package, which will include floor plans and elevation with 3D components.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 65% in INTD 1100 and 65% in INTD 1245 and 65% in INTD 1310
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Produce 3D manual drawings with accurate line weights to show depth and detail.
- Create a paraline drawing of a furnished interior space Create 3D drawings, both manually-drafted and freehand drawn.
- Produce one and two-point perspective drawings using conventional drop-down methods using floor plans and elevations.
- Incorporate architectural elements in 3D drawings, such as stairs, fireplaces, windows and doors.
- Apply appropriate conventions to interior finishes, furnishings and foliage.
- Demonstrate the ability to graphically render 3D drawings to show the design intent realistically.
