## Biesse XNC Programmer JOIN 0217
Joinery Course
## Course Overview
This course builds on the skills acquired in JOIN 0215 (Biesse Operator/Programmer). Emphasis is on programming drilling and routing operations using the XNC controller software. Students will gain proficiency with sub-programs and parametric programming. The course objective is to increase productivity by providing expanded programming knowledge. Students are given access to individual computer stations for the software instruction.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
