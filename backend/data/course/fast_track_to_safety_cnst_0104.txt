## Fast Track to Safety CNST 0104
Construction Course
## Course Overview
Designed for Construction Safety Officers and, safety supervisors but also valuable to anyone who requires training in Confined Space Entry and Rescue, Fall Protection Rescue and Train the Trainer level in Respiratory Protection and Fit Testing. Knowledge and understanding of WorkSafe BC Requirements is covered. The successful student will receive three separate Statements of Completion. This one week course is intended as extra training for students who have completed the CNST 1100 : Construction Safety Officer Training but is not mandatory. Due to the physical demands of this course students need to be in good physical condition. For further information please contact the Program Assistant at (604) 412-7564. Students will require CSA approved steel toes boots, a half or full face respirator, PF100 HEPA filters and fall protection harness. No textbooks required.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to receive:
- Certification in Confined Space Entry and Rescue.
- Certification in Fall Protection.
- Train the Trainer in Respirator Protection and Fit Test.
- Understanding and knowledge of WorkSafe BC Requirements.
