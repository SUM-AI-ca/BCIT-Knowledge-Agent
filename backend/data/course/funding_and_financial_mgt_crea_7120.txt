## Funding and Financial Mgt CREA 7120
Creative Industries Course
## Course Overview
​Strong financial management is critical to the success of all enterprises, including those in creative industries. Regardless of whether you are preparing a business plan to launch a new venture or preparing a budget in a well-established firm, you will need a solid foundation in practices and principles of financial management. This course will provide an overview of core financial concepts such as forecasting, budgeting, financing, cash flow management and understanding financial statements which contribute to the sustainability and the growth of the creative enterprise.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the various forms (proprietorship, partnership, and corporation) of business organization which are relevant to new creative enterprises.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the regulatory requirements relating to business startups.
- Explain the purpose and relevance to stakeholders, of financial statements and the relationships between them.
- Use spreadsheet software to prepare and analyze budgets, forecasts and other financial information.
- Use financial information to determine appropriate pricing for goods and services.
- Use data from financial statements to assess the performance of a firm from different perspectives (liquidity, productivity, profitability, financial leverage, valuation).
- Identify optimal sources of financing available to creative enterprises considering their specificities, size, and stage of development (ex. traditional lenders, crowdfunding, venture capital)
- Prepare financial data needed to produce a business plan and help secure financing needed to support a creative enterprise's operations and growth.
- ​Interpret and communicate financial information, needs and decisions effectively to various stakeholders.
