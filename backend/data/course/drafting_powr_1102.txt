## Drafting POWR 1102
Power Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This course provides students with fundamental knowledge and skill in drafting. Learning activities include applying basic drafting skills, including creating piping drawings.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate the application of basic drafting skills including:
- Two dimensional representation of three dimensional objects.
- Principle of scale.
- Orthographic and Isometric projection.
- Dimension basic drawings using standard convention and terminology.
- Interpret basic piping and instrumentation drawings.
- Produce block diagram and piping layout diagrams of simple process.
- Make simple sketches of mechanical parts.
- Create basic drawings using AutoCad.
Effective as of Winter 2008
