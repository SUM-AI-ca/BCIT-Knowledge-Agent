## Field Safety 2 SRMT 2100
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
This course continues the development of the safe workplace ethic started in Field Safety Skills 1. The course includes a number of provincially or nationally recognized safety certification modules. This training is recommended or required by employers and is applicable to field lab conditions in the SRM program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the role of the Provincial SAR and PEP programs.
- Discuss boater safety as it applies to: being prepared, staying safe and surviving while operating a power boat.
- Demonstrate basic chainsaw maintenance and safe operation.
- Demonstrate the safe operation of a 4x4 vehicle.
- Demonstrate wilderness survival skills by participating in an overnight exercise in a "simulated remote" field setting.
