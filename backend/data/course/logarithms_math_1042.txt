## Logarithms MATH 1042
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
A distance education course in exponential and logarithmic functions. Topics include the exponential and logarithmic functions, graphs of these functions, the properties of logarithms, and the solution of log and exponential equations. The use of log-log and semi-log graph paper is included.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Math 12 Pre-Calculus or equivalent OR BCIT MATH 0001 .
### Credits
1.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate the important features of a graphing calculator.
- Understand the exponential function and its graph.
- Translate logarithmic forms to exponential form and vice versa.
- Use the Laws of Logarithms to expand and contract logarithm expressions.
- Understand logarithms of different bases including base 10 and base e.
- Transform logarithms from one base to another.
