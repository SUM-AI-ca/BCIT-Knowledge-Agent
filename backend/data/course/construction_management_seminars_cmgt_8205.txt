## Construction Management Seminars CMGT 8205
Construction Management - CMGT Course
## Course Overview
Through presentations from seasoned construction professionals, participants will gain knowledge of strategies and construction techniques that have been used on a variety of construction projects. Experts representing project managers, engineers, consultants and contractors will present both technical and managerial challenges and solutions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CMGT 7840
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze the issues related to the topics discussed in the presentation.
- Summarize the oral presentations in written form.
- Distinguish a wide array of construction projects characteristics as presented by practising professionals.
- Perform the duties of the audience in a seminar.
