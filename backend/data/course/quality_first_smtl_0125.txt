## Quality First SMTL 0125
Sheet Metal Course
## Course Overview
Instructs how to produce a system for residential heating using forced air guidelines. Topics covered include heat loss calculations, select proper appliances, and duct sizing for residential heating.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Sheet Metal Journeyperson or Apprentice or currently working in the Sheet Metal Trade.
