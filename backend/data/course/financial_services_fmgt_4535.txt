## Financial Services FMGT 4535
Financial Management Course
## Course Overview
Provides the financial planning student with the marketing and communications skills essential to success in the financial planning profession. The course covers the marketing of financial planning products and services and, in addition, it will introduce the student to the institutional environment in which many financial planners operate.
### Prerequisite(s)
- FMGT 3830
### Credits
2.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Understand the concept of relationship selling.
- Explain the steps in the relationship selling process.
- Plan for, develop and deliver a sales presentation.
- Critique a videotape of their own sales presentation.
- Understand the techniques available for maintaining and building client relationships.
- Demonstrate effective verbal, non-verbal and written communication skills.
- Understand the current trends and practices in financial services direct marketing.
Effective as of Winter 2007
