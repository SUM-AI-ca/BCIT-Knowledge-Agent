## Building Science Design Project ARSC 8350
Architectural Science Course
## Course Overview
This course holistically integrates previously learned tools and processes to inform and refine the graduating project.. Through a series of lectures and studio assignments, students will pursue transformative architectural solutions based on course-specific performance goals. Students will self-evaluate building performance on an ongoing basis throughout the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ARSC 7350 and ARSC 8280
- ARSC 8800 ‡ ( ‡ must be taken concurrently)
### Credits
4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Select appropriate tools and processes to inform architectural design decision making.
- Synthesize whole building energy analysis into architectural solutions.
- Propose design material choices based on life-cycle assessment.
- Demonstrate holistic architectural design decision-making.
- Use an integrated Building Information Modeling (BIM) approach in the architectural design process.
- Develop appropriate architectural solutions that integrate performance objectives for the graduating project.
