## Applied AI Management Topics BSYS 4005
Business Systems Course
## Course Overview
In this course, students will apply the artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML) management skills developed in BSYS 3001 Artificial Intelligence Management 1. This is a practical project-based course where the students will have the opportunity to build ML use cases in various fields such as health care, self-driving cars, and banking. The focus is to use the latest solutions in Deep Learning (DL) in Supervised, Unsupervised, and Reinforcement Learning (RL) platforms. This course will use Python programming language and leading AI/ML research platforms.
### Prerequisite(s)
- BSYS 3001

2.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate artificial intelligence/machine learning (AI/ML) business models
- Utilize and practice Python for data science
- Implement ML solutions using leading AI platforms
- Design ML experiments for supervised/unsupervised scenarios in functional business areas
- Construct object/image recognition models
- Explore and utilize Reinforcement Learning (RL) algorithms in automation use cases
- Develop MI models in a team environment for real-life business scenarios
