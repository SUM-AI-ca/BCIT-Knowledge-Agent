## Sound Gathering and Editing FILM 2211
Filming Course
## Course Overview
Sound theory and sound design basics, the role of the sound in film, sound jobs in film, basic recording techniques, Foley and sound FX editing using Pro Tools and Steinberg Nuendo/Cubase.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Understand the use of specialized sound devices, foley, non-linear sound editing devices.
- Location sound and digital recording.
- Be more knowledgeable in location sound and digital recording.
