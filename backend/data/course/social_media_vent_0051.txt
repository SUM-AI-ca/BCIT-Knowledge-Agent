## Social Media VENT 0051
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
Companies need a roadmap to realize the value and impact of social media and web 2.0 services such as Facebook, YouTube and Twitter. How can these technologies add value to your business and affect future growth potential? In this interactive webinar, learn how to develop an integrated media presence and track the results of your efforts.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the participant will be able to:
- Understand social media and web 2.0 and how they relate to business.
- Understand how to develop an integrated media presence and track its effectiveness.
