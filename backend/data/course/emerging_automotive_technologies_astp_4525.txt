## Emerging Automotive Technologies ASTP 4525
Automotive Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course gives you some insight into emerging vehicle technologies.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe the operating principles of hybrid vehicles.
- Explain the high voltage battery safety precautions during service.
- Describe M85 and E85 fuel systems.
- Describe fuel cell technology.
