## Plastics Processes and Materials MTEC 7041
Manufacturing Course
## Course Overview
Covers the three main processes used to shape and form thermoplastic materials: Injection Molding, Blow Molding and Extrusion. The information presented focuses on the recent technological advances in machines and molds, process innovations, commodity and specialty materials and troubleshooting processing problems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in PLAS 3440
