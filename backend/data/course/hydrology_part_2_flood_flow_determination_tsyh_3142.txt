## Hydrology Part 2: Flood Flow Determination TSYH 3142
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the primary methods of determining flood flows through the analysis of existing and obtainable data, including the Rational Method, streamflows, point flow frequencies and snowmelt. Method selection, probability calculation, determination of return periods, and probable flood flows based on the analysis of existing data are presented for practical use in the design of drainage structures based on estimated flood flow magnitudes.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Introduction to Methods
- State and define the term flood and the three primary flood types.
- State and define the primary methods of flood flow projection including data base requirements and applications of each.
- State and define the term return period, for flows of various magnitudes and for structure design purposes.
## Module 2 - Frequency and Probability
- Define the terms frequency and probability, to state the relationship of the two to the probability of occurrence of an event and to determine the probability of occurrence for any given return period.
- Determine the probability of non-occurrence of design life failure risk by calculation and from a risk nomograph.
- State and define the annual, full and partial series data bases.
## Module 3 - Introduction to the Rational Method
- State the rational equation in both metric and imperial forms and define the components in terms of units.
- State the assumptions of the rational method in design peak flow determination and the limitations of the rational method for major structure design flood flow determination.
- Calculate design peak flows based on the rational equation and given criteria.
## Module 4 - Streamflow Analysis
- Define and plot a streamflow runoff hydrograph given the appropriate data.
- Plot precipitation data relative to a storm discharge hydrograph.
- Define the segments of a stream discharge hydrograph.
- Graphically separate stream baseflow from total flow to determine storm runoff flows.
- Define the four determinations that can be calculated from the analysis of a stream discharge hydrograph relative to a precipitation event.
- Reduce the stream discharge hydrograph to a unit hydrograph and derive the unit projections from this hydrograph.
## Module 5 - Point Flow Frequency Analysis
- Define and select the data base required to complete a point flow frequency analysis.
- Rank the yearly peak flow values and calculate the return period corresponding to each ranked flow value.
- Plot the ranked flows vs calculated return periods on the appropriate probability graph paper.
- Interpret the graph results and determine flood flow values for given return periods.
## Module 6 - Snowmelt Runoff Analysis
- State and define the elements in the general snowmelt equation and the units utilized.
- Determine the total number of melting degrees or degree days given the appropriate data and calculate the prior melt volume in storage, given the required data.
- Calculate the snowmelt factor given the equation, temperature and streamflow data for a particular basin.
- State the relationship between rainfall and snowpack and the conditions under which additional storage or additional runoff will result.
