## S-185 Wildfire Safety and Entrapment Avoidance RENR 0540
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
​S-185 is a general knowledge course on both wildfire safety and entrapment avoidance. This course takes approximately 4 hours and consists of both a lecture and then an exam. Upon successfully passing the exam (70% or higher), the candidate will receive an S-185 Fire Entrapment Avoidance & Safety Certification. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
​S-185 is a general knowledge course on both wildfire safety and entrapment avoidance. This course takes approximately 4 hours and consists of both a lecture and then an exam. Upon successfully passing the exam (70% or higher), the candidate will receive an S-185 Fire Entrapment Avoidance & Safety Certification. ​
- Explain fireline safety - The Safety First Attitude
- Explain the basic procedures of fire entrapment avoidance
- Explain the role of crew members and supervisors in fire entrapment avoidance
- Describe safe work procedures and LCES
- Demonstrate situational awareness for fire entrapment avoidance
- Describe procedures for vehicle, heavy equipment and building entrapments
- Explain emergency escape procedures
- Explain possible fire entrapment survival actions
- Explain desirable qualities of survival zones
