## Plan Quality Change MGMT 8320
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates plan quality change skills, such as creating a vision and developing a change plan with improvement objectives and measurable outcomes.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Preparing for Change
- Analyze a change opportunity and create a vision.
- Identify forces for and against change, including stakeholder reactions, and issues of differentiation.
- Prepare for organizational support and resistance to change.
- Developing a Change Plan
- Develop a plan for a specific change initiative.
- Write improvement objectives and measurable outcomes.
- Plan for the phases of the change.
- Identify the resources required for the change initiative.
