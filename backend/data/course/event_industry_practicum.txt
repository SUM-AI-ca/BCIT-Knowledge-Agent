## Overview

The student will spend 75 hours in an organization to gain practical work experience in the sector. During the practicum the student is expected to use their acquired event marketing and management knowledge to gain valuable experience, insights, and connections for work in the events industry. On-the-job performance is evaluated by the employer as well as self-reflection and practical application of acquired knowledge and skills. The practicum includes training, mentoring and hands-on project work. Pre-approved host organizations will be identified for the student. The practicum is contracted by both the student and the organization, with oversight provided by a BCIT advisor.

## Prerequisites

- Completion of the Required Courses in the Event Marketing and Management Strategy Certificate or with Program Head Permission.

## Learning Outcomes

- ​Apply knowledge and skills to complete event marketing-related tasks within an existing industry organization.
- Demonstrate the ability to attend and perform effectively in a real-life work environment.
- Assess performance in an industry working environment.
- Create professional networks to advance the career.
- Develop personal and professional goals and strategies to compete in a global industry.
