## Low Energy Electrical Worker TELC 0144
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
​This course Covers the Canadian Electrical Code requirements for the installation, alteration, repair or maintenance of Class 2 circuits limited to rated output of 100-volt amperes. This includes installation of 120 volt branch circuit wiring for the Class 2 power supplies. This course is an eligibility requirement and prepares students to write the Class Low Energy Systems (LO) certification exam from the Technical Safety BC (www.technicalsafetybc.ca).​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Experience with Class 2 circuits.
