## Basic Field Astronomy TSYH 3335
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
An introduction to astronomy, the course presents the terms, symbols and calculations to compute the azimuth of a line and latitude of a point based on polar and solar observations. The course manual provides the conceptual and theoretical basis for completing an assignment which requires computations from local observations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
