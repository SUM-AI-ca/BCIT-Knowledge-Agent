## Tools and Equipment HMTF 1003
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the selection, use, and maintenance of hand, measuring, and power tools, fasteners, shop equipment, and reference resources. This course also introduces students to job action preparation and diagnostic procedures.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
7.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use hand tools
- Use measuring instruments
- Use power tools
- Use fasteners
- Use shop tools and equipment
- Prepare job action
- Use reference resources
- Describe diagnostic procedures
