## Introducing the IBM AS/400 COMP 1632
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the AS/400 concepts and architecture as it applies to practical business requirements. Some topics are libraries and objects, AS/400 relational database and data management, user profiles and AS/400 security.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 0001 or equivalent knowledge.

3.0
