## Structural Diagnostics ABOD 0103
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course upgrades collision repair technicians in structural diagnostics and three-dimensional measurements. The student will learn how to make structural measurements, use a frame specification manual, set up and read various electronic measuring devices, and diagnose structural damages by using measuring devices. Both theory and hands-on training will be applied. Training is recognized as an ICBC c.a.r. shop technician upgrade course and counts as eight hours towards your accreditation status.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe three-dimensional measuring.
- Describe the various frame misalignments.
- Identify structural misalignments from electronic measuring worksheets.
- Perform measurements using a variety of manual / electronic measuring devices.
- Access and use a frame specification manual.
