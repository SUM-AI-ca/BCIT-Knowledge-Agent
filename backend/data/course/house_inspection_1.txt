## Overview

Provides practical and theoretical information required to inspect existing houses for visible defects. This covers 6 systems of a house that must be inspected as per the standards. It is the first of a series of courses that meet the academic requirements for house inspection and the qualifications for House Inspector Licensing Regulations under Consumer Protection BC (CPBC).

## Credits

3.0

## Cost

$767.94

## Prerequisites

- English 12 "50% (Pass)" or equivalent www.bcit.ca/admission/upgrading/englishproficiency.shtml or COMM 0015 score of 50 or better.

## Learning Outcomes

- Have a basic knowledge about the first 6 systems in a house to be reviewed as per the “Standards of Practice" for a house inspector.
- Understand the responsibility of a house inspector and their limitations.​
