## Woodlot and Community Forest Management SRMT 4970
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
This course explores the role that Woodlot and Community Forest Tenures have in the sustainable management of forest lands in BC. This course provides a background of the history of woodlots and community forests and their respective associations. Students are introduced to the concepts of ecological, social and economic sustainability as it applies to these forms of tenure. Students will review the current application and public consultation processes for these forest tenures. Students identify the typical timber and non-timber forest products generated by these tenures and how to best market them. This course introduces students to practical management practices for small scale forestry.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the historical context of woodlots and community forests.
- Describe the legislative background of woodlots and community forests.
- Explain the application and public consultation processes for woodlots and community forests.
- Explain the social, economic and environmental benefits of local control of forest resources.
- Describe the range of typical timber and non-timber forest products and their markets.
- Describe the plans and permits required to manage operations.
- Describe employee and contractor management in a small scale forest operation.
- Identify the regulatory agencies and processes that impact woodlot and community forest management practices.
- Discuss practical management practices for small scale forestry.
