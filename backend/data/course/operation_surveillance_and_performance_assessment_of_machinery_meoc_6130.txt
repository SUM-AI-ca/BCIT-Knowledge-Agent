## Operation, Surveillance & Performance Assessment of Machinery MEOC 6130
Marine Engineer Officer Cadet Course
## Course Overview
​In this course, students will study all aspects of operation , surveillance , performance assessment and maintaining safety of propulsion plant and auxiliary machinery. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Transport Canada, STCW Fourth-Class Motor Marine Engineering Certificate of Competency

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Safely start up and shut down main and auxiliary machinery, including associated system
- Operate propulsion plants within appropriate limits.
- Carry out safe and efficient operation, surveillance, performance assessment of propulsion plant and auxiliary machinery
- List the parameters defining the operating limits of the main propulsion.
- Demonstrate the operation of the automatic control of main propulsions drives: diesel engine, steam and gas turbines.
- Demonstrate the correct procedures for the emergency operation of the diesel engine.
- Demonstrate the operation of the automatic control of all auxiliary machinery.
- Safely and effectively manage fuel, lubrication and ballast operations.
- Describe the procedures and guidelines for refrigeration and air-conditioning operating, maintenance and surveillance.
- Using sketches, explain the starting and reversing, engine safety, engine emergency operating system of a marine diesel engine. ​
