## A Systems Approach to Collaboration CORP 0280
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
​No person, no idea and no department is an end to itself. Looking beyond what appears to be isolated and independent incidents requires us to look deeper at the connections between things so that we are better able to understand and influence them. This workshop will take a systemic approach to collaboration. Through understanding your role as part of the system, and how the system as a whole functions, you will gain system sight and develop an approach to moving initiatives through the system in which you operate.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- See patterns which drive events
- Develop systems sight, a basis for clearer, wider and different perspectives, an ability to see exactly what is happening
- Describe predictable patterns in a system, and understand its structure
- Demonstrate influence in the system
- Develop more effective ways of dealing with problems and better thinking strategies
- Develop a systemic collaborative approach
