## Crime and Science Seminar FSCT 0016
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
This highly anticipated annual event features forensic experts who provide insight on forensic techniques, tools and skills necessary to do their jobs. This public forum is the perfect opportunity for all members of the community to learn more about the fascinating and important role of Forensic Investigation in solving crimes.
