## Canada’s Ozone Layer Protection Awareness Program for HVAC and Refrigeration HVAC 0145
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
Teaches environmental awareness on ozone depleting substances. Covers environmentally correct equipment design and proper handling of ozone depleting substances. Upon successful completion, participants receive an HRAI/Environment Canada/B.C. Environment Certificate in CFC/HCFC/HFC Controls, and a certificate number for refrigerant handling as required under current BC regulations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
