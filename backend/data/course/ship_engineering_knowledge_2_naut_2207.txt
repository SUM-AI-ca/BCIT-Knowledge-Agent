## Ship Engineering Knowledge 2 NAUT 2207
Ocean Navigation Course
## Course Overview
The course content covers theoretical knowledge of operating principles and design of marine power plants including: diesel engine, propeller, shafting, boilers, steering gear, thrusters, stabilizers, bridge control, and instrumentation. The course also provides the required knowledge for operating the machinery when navigating in ice conditions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Successful completion of Term 5

4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the construction and operation of marine diesel engines and their systems.
- Distinguish between water tube and fire tube boilers.
- Describe an auxiliary and waste heat boiler and their feed systems.
- Describe the construction and operation of the ship’s auxiliary equipment such as steering, bow thrusters, and stabilizers.
- Describe engine transmission systems, including propeller shafts, bearings, and propellers.
- Describe bridge control systems and instrumentation.
- Explain machinery operation consideration when navigating in ice.
