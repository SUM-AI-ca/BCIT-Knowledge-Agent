## WordPress.com for beginners 1 MDIA 0101
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Learn how to make a professional looking website using the WordPress blogging platform with little to no coding required. This Level 1 workshop gives instruction on how to use WordPress.com to build dynamic, elegant and professional looking websites quickly and easily. Learn the settings required to turn a WordPress.com blog into a website, how to tune it for search engines, and how to choose the right theme for your website. This class is suitable for beginners with basic Internet skills (using browsers, web-based email, creating documents online). Students may also wish to attend the Level 2 ( MDIA 0102 ) session which builds on the Level 1 workshop and focuses on installing WordPress yourself and customizing how themes look and feel.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Set up a WordPress.com-based blog or website.
- Know how to organize information on a website.
- Using pages vs posts for the best fit to the content.
- Basics of blogging and posting.
- Adding links, images, and video to posts and pages.
- Choosing a theme for your site that matches your audience.
- Use sidebar widgets to add interactivity and navigation to the blog/site.
- Making a blog theme look like a website.
