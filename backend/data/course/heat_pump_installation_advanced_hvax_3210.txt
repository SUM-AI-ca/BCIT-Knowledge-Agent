## Heat Pump Installation- Advanced HVAX 3210
Heat Pump Maintenance and Trou Course
## Course Overview
​Building upon the foundational knowledge gained in the basic heat pump installation course, the Advanced Heat Pump Installation course delves deeper into the intricacies of designing, installing, and optimizing complex heat pump systems. This course is designed for experienced HVAC technicians, engineers, and professionals seeking to enhance their expertise in heat pump technology.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in HVAX 3200
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate the latest technological advancements in heat pump components and controls for energy efficiency and performance optimization.
- Design and implement sophisticated control strategies for managing heat pump systems in various building applications.
- Assess and troubleshoot issues related to advanced heat pump installations, including refrigerant management and system balancing.
- Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams to design and implement customized heat pump solutions for specific project requirements.
Effective as of Fall 2024
