## Servicing Gas Forced Air Furnaces PPGS 0121
Piping, Plumbing, Gas Course
## Course Overview
Covers installation instructions, service information, sequence of operation, control drawings; testing, equipment, and troubleshooting of gas fired forced air furnaces.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Must have current Gas Appliance Certification, B, or A Gas Fitting Licence.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Understand applicable code requirements.
- Understand service requirements.
- Recognize the various types of gas valves.
- Understand ignition systems.
- Understand basic heat requirements for buildings.
- Calculate Furnace efficiency.
- Understand electrical circuits.
- Understand the various control strategies.
- Test the various components of a forced air furnace.
- Troubleshoot the components of a forced air furnace
