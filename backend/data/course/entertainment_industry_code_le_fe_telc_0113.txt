## Entertainment Industry Code (LE/FE) TELC 0113
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
​Designed for those persons doing electrical work in the entertainment industry. This course follows the Technical Safety BC guidelines and prepares the student to write the Limited Entertainment (LE) or Full Entertainment (FE) certification exam. Information pertaining to the eligibility requirements for writing the exam can be obtained from the Technical Safety BC website at www.technicalsafetybc.ca ​.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Know and find needed Sections, Rules and Tables in the code and other regulations so that students may write the Class LE or FE certification exam. Exam eligibility requirement can be found at the BC Safety Authority website:https://safetyauthority.ca/licences-certificates/exams-certificates/electrical
A statement of completion certificate will be issued to students who successfully complete the course.
