## Electrical and Electronic Systems MCTC 1440
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces the students to basic electrical principles, electrical circuits, magnetism, and electrical generation. Students will learn how to interpret electrical diagrams, use digital and analog meters, service batteries, charging system, starting systems and component operation. Students will also be introduced to specific electronic diagnostic equipment and retrieve diagnostic trouble codes as well as perform basic service procedures for various electrical components and systems related to Motorcycles.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCTC 1400

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe principles of electricity.
- Describe principles of electronics.
- Interpret wiring diagrams
- Maintain batteries.
- Describe charging and starting systems.
- Repair wiring and connectors.
- Repair wiring and connectors.
- Use basic electrical systems diagnostic equipment.
- Describe EV high voltage electrical systems.
