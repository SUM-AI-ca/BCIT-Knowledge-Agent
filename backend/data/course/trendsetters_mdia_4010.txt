## Trendsetters MDIA 4010
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
The future of websites will change with the emergence of new broadband capabilities. Businesses will be demanding richer graphics with eye catching animation, movies and sound tracks. Students will need to be keenly aware of these rapid changes in the industry. This course is focused on researching these new and emerging trends in the industry. Students will participate in group discussions and case studies regarding the implementation of ever-changing software and hardware as it relates to multimedia.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Research new and emerging hardware technology, explaining how it will affect the industry.
- Research new and emerging software, explaining how it will affect the industry.
- Explain how technology is changing the way business advertises.
- Explain how technology trends are being applied in new media.
- Research a case study and present findings to support how an emerging technology or trend will address particular issues in industry.
- Practice professional development to keep multimedia skills current.
