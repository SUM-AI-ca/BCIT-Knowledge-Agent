## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Have an enhanced understanding of the operational, quality, and technical aspects related to EK Motor and EK General subject matter assets.
- Understand the basic and advanced predictive maintenance diagnostics related to vessel machinery.
- Successfully prepare for on-time regulatory and compliance requirements.
- Diagnose and detect technical issues, operational readiness, and quality structure, therefore increasing fleet utilization.
- Demonstrate an understanding of increased technical and operational acumen related to existing vessel fleet, and related customary practices.
