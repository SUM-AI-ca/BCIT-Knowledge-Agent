## Overview

The OPMT 0198 Business Math Assessment test is meant for students who do not meet the math standard for admission to Business programs but have acquired additional math skills since their last math course or do not have access to a transcript of marks. The skills may have been acquired through employment or through taking another course or courses with a strong math component. STUDENTS ARE NOT ALLOWED TO REGISTER INTO OPMT 0198 TWICE. YOUR SECOND ATTEMPT WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED FOR ACCEPTANCE INTO THE SCHOOL OF BUSINESS. View list of topics covered in the test at: www.bcit.ca/files/business/pdf/opmt0198topics.pdf before registering for the test. If you have difficulty with these topics it is recommended you register for OPMT 0199
