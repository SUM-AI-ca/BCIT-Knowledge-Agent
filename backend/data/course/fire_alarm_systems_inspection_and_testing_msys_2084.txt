## Fire Alarm Systems: Inspection and Testing MSYS 2084
Mechanical Systems Course
## Course Overview
Reviews the components, functions and operational principles of fire alarm systems. Covers the requirements for the inspection and testing of fire alarm systems in accordance with applicable codes, standards and regulations. Completion of inspection and test reports will be covered. This course will include field trips.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in TELC 1139
- TELC 1139 or general knowledge of fire alarm wiring, basic electricity and basic electronics.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will have a basic, working understanding of:
- Describe fire alarm systems components, functions and operation.
- Understand codes, standards and regulations applying to fire alarm systems, and:
- Identify and describe applicable inspection and test procedures.
- Conduct inspections and test to ensure compliance.
- Complete appropriate documentation and reports.
