## Fire Warden Training MOOC 0314
Massive Open Online Courses Course
## Course Overview
​This self-paced course is for those who volunteer to be a BCIT designated Fire Warden. In this course you will learn the roles an responsibilities of BCIT Fire Warden. You will also learn procedures during an evacuation, Fire Drills, using fire safety plans, fire safety equipment and​​ signage, Fire Warden equipment and gear and additional resources.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Familiarize with BCIT Fire Safety Program
- Understand the role of a Fire Warden
- Learn Fire Warden's Immediate Response
- Know your area of responsibility
- Learn how to use Fire Extinghishers
- Identify Fire Safety Signage
- Access Fire Warden's Kit​
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2024
