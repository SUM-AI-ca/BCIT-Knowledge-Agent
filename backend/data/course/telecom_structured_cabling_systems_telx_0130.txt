## Telecom Structured Cabling Systems TELX 0130
Electronics Technical Course
## Course Overview
Hands-on installation, testing of data and voice circuits using unshielded twisted pair cable (U.T.P.), RJ-11, RJ-12, and RJ-45 connection as well as BIX, R-66 and 110 type terminal blocks level three, four and five data circuits will also be installed. Testing using state-of-the-art, FLUKE, WAVETEK and MICROTEST equipment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
