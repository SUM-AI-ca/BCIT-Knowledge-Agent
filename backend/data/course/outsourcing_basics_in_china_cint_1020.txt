## Outsourcing Basics in China CINT 1020
BCIT International Course
## Course Overview
Provides fundamental knowledge of IT outsourcing in China. Covers market and labour analysis, business partner development, and implementation plans. Main topics focus on IT and manufacturing outsourcing, including development of outsourcing companies, human resource management, related policies and regulations of the Chinese government, quality control and coordination with end users.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

2.0

## Learning Outcomes
- Develop an awareness of outsourcing in China.
- Prepare a preliminary plan for establishing outsourcing operations in China.
- Develop basic research capabilities for identifying outsourcing opportunities.
