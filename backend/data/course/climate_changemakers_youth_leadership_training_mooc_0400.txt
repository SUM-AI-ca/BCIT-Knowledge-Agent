## Climate Changemakers Youth Leadership Training MOOC 0400
Massive Open Online Courses Course
## Course Overview
As has been demonstrated by the Fridays for Future climate strikes, youth can galvanize millions by their passion and energy. It is important to move beyond supporting the strikes and engage young men and women as emissaries to their communities. As part of the Climate Changemaker training, youth participants would be trained as Climate Changemaker Emissaries. Over the period of the training, participants will both build their leadership capacity while advancing their knowledge on climate change in the region and globally. During the workshop, they will also be connected to an online platform that supports them and continue to advance their learning. The climate changemaker workshop is presented to the youth that unpack and promote a carbon neutral “one planet” lifestyle. The Climate Changemakers Leadership curriculum will provide engaged young leaders with the skills, attitudes, knowledge, and experience needed to increase their capacity to influence positive change in their communities and unpack and promote a carbon neutral “one planet” lifestyle. *Note: Thank you for your interest in a BCIT – Free Online Learning course. ## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Recognize sustainability challenges
- Identify SDGs and a framework for action
- Understand how to initiate change
- Identify leadership attitude for the long haul
- Explore the future //unfurl BHAG's
- Map energy and engagement
- Embrace ‘gray thinking’
- Explore S.A.K.E.© and map a way to increase effective leadership
- Get access to resources including virtual sources
- Apply learned skills to increase leadership abilities as they relate to effecting social change and climate change
- Review federal policies, procedures and communications competencies and practice the skills necessary to be a strategic communicator with officials
