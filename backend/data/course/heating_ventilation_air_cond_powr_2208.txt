## Heating/Ventilation/Air Cond POWR 2208
Power Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This course discusses heating, ventilation, and air conditioning. Topics include properties of air, air conditioning systems, absorption refrigeration systems, and auxiliary building systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
6.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Discuss the psychrometric properties of air.
- Describe air conditioning components.
- Describe air conditioning systems and heat recovery systems.
- Discuss the design of combined air conditioning systems such as constant air volume systems with perimeter heating; fan coil units with make-up air system; hydronic heat pump systems; variable air volume systems with perimeter heating; compartmental variable air volume systems.
- Explain the concepts of heat gain and heat loss.
- Describe the operation and maintenance of absorption refrigeration systems.
- Describe auxiliary building systems, including lighting systems, building water supply systems, and sanitary drainage systems.
