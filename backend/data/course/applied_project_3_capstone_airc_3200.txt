## Applied Project 3/Capstone AIRC 3200
Airport/Airline Core Course
## Course Overview
​This is the last course in a series of three courses that collectively support the completion of an applied capstone project. Across the three courses, students will learn fundamental project management concepts and skills, practice developing project management tools and documents, develop a project management plan for a project completed with an industry partner, execute the project, and evaluate and report on project outcomes. Throughout the series, students will also develop teamwork skills and the ability to engage in structured self reflection. In this course, learners focus on executing the remainder of their applied capstone project in collaboration with an industry partner. Working in teams, learners monitor and control project progress, manage changes, and complete planned project deliverables. Throughout the course, teams produce regular project status reports and participate in structured 360-degree feedback to support accountability and continuous improvement. The course concludes with learners reporting on project outcomes through a formal capstone presentation and reflective analysis of individual and team performance. Evaluation includes assignments and 360-degree feedback.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Execute and complete an applied project in accordance with an approved project management plan.
- Monitor and control project performance by tracking progress against scope, schedule, and budget and documenting results in regular project status reports.
- Manage project changes by updating project plans and adjusting execution strategies as required.
- Collaborate effectively within a project team over an extended period, demonstrating accountability, professionalism, and responsiveness to peer feedback.
- Report on project outcomes by preparing and delivering a capstone presentation that communicates project results, challenges, and lessons learned.
- Reflect on individual contributions, team dynamics, and project performance to support personal and professional development.​
