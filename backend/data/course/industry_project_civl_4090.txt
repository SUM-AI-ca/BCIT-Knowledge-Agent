## Industry Project CIVL 4090
Civil Engineering Course
## Course Overview
​After submitting an acceptable project proposal in COMM 3342 , the student is assigned a faculty advisor. The student is required to meet periodically with the faculty advisor and/or the industry contact, and submit the finished project to both the industry contact and faculty advisor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMM 4442 ‡ ( ‡ must be taken concurrently)
- Completion of Level 3 Civil Engineering courses.
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Work independently on a realistic, industry supported technical engineering problem. [4]
- Identify approach and demonstrate the transition in thinking from an academic perspective to real-world engineering practice. [4]
- Integrate information, ideas and concepts from previous courses into a comprehensive design effort. [4]
- Produce a high-quality, written report identifying the problem(s) and recommended solution(s). [7]
Effective as of Fall 2024
