## Perform Quality Control MTLF 1265
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
In this course, you will learn to identify and apply codes and standards encountered in the metal fabrication trade, interpret architectural information found on drawings, use measuring instruments to inspect finished products, describe different non-destructive testing methods, and use weld measuring equipment. Special focus will be on identification of weld discontinuities and the use of Liquid Penetrant Inspection, Magnetic Particle inspection, etc.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1200 and MTLF 1205 and MTLF 1210 and MTLF 1215 and MTLF 1240 and MTLF 1245
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use codes and specifications to determine tolerance on a given project.
- Use precession equipment to verify that standards have been met on a given project.
- Perform visual inspection of welds
- Create a remediation report based on identified deficiencies of a project.
