## Electrical Advanced 1 DELX 2100
Diesel Engine Electronics Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed to provide you with the theoretical understanding of electricity and electromagnetism related to batteries, charging, and starting systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TQ, apprentice, or ELTT graduate in Commercial Transport, Heavy Duty or Diesel Engine Mechanic. Steel-toed footwear required.
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Understand the basics of electricity and electromagnetism. Topics include batteries, charging and starting systems.
