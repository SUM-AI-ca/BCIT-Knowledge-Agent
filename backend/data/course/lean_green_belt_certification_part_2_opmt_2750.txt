## Lean Green Belt Certification Part 2 OPMT 2750
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
This course, which is the 2nd part of the Lean Green Belt Certification, is purely project based. Using the notions learned in the Lean Green Belt Part 1 course, you will be tasked to do a full Lean analysis on the Current and Future state using the project templates supplied. The Lean Green Belt Part 2 certification course will provide you the practical knowledge on how to streamline processes, improve business performance and establish you as a go-to problem solver for any organization. The Green Belt course focuses on how process efficiency can be improved and how processes can be designed to deliver customer or client quality. This course follows the Body of Knowledge (BOK) of the International Association of Six Sigma Certifications (IASSC) and the Association for Manufacturing Excellence (AME).

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Learn the tactical deployment and applications of Lean philosophy, concepts and methodologies within a work cell, work group or across a value stream.
- Implement Lean processes to eliminate waste, optimize operational efficiencies and drive measurable performance improvements.
- Create Value Stream Maps and understand their function in a Lean System
- Leverage Lean as a cultural enabler for leadership, teamwork, and human empowerment.
- Become better able to train other team members in Lean concepts and methods.
- Get applied practice in conducting a small improvement project.
- Achieve a formal Lean Green Belt Certification​
