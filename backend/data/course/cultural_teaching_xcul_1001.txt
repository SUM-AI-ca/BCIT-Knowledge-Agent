## Cultural Teaching XCUL 1001
Cultural Studies Course
## Course Overview
This course provides an opportunity to explore St’át’imc cultural teachings, language and stories connected to the content of the microcredential. Humour with storytelling will be the primary mode of instilling the teachings. Participants will learn about how to carry their St’át’imc ways of being and values into everyday life.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Introduce oneself in the language and in a cultural way.
- Learn about storytelling teachings.
- Understand the differences between Western and Indigenous worldviews.
- Demonstrate an understanding of how ways of being forms cultural identity (ie., language, storytelling, cultural practices).
- Learn how to utilize the St’át’imcets keyman keyboard for Windows programs.
- Demonstrate how to incorporate cultural teaching into daily life and business practices.
