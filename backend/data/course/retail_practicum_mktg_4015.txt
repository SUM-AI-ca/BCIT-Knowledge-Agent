## Retail Practicum MKTG 4015
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
This course provides practical work experience that is relevant to your retail career path. You will spend the equivalent of 20-working days within industry performing various tasks associated with the firm's retail efforts. You will submit written reports, and your performance will be assessed by an organization representative and a BCIT Faculty Advisor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all program courses.
### Credits
3.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Gain practical experience in a retail environment.
- Understand the retail culture.
- Apply retail skills to assist an organization.
- Make industry contacts.
