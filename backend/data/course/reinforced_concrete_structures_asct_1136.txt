## Reinforced Concrete Structures ASCT 1136
Architectural and Structural CADD and Graphics Course
## Course Overview
ASCT 1136 presents theory and code requirements necessary for detailing of reinforced concrete structural elements. The course provides students with the knowledge and skills necessary to develop a set of construction drawings from concept sketches, design information, codes and specifications. Students will gain knowledge about more complex foundation and floor systems, variety of types of concrete slabs, beams and columns, and about producing both engineering and placing drawings. The course is delivered through project work on floor plans, foundation and roof plans and details, sections, beam/column/footing schedules, bar lists and connection details.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Properly use symbols and abbreviations commonly used in reinforced concrete detailing and design.
- Produce engineering and placing drawings for deep and shallow foundations, foundation walls, slab-on-grade and two-way slabs, reinforced concrete beams and columns.
- Develop sections and connection details specific to reinforced concrete structures.
- Select standard reinforcing details from the ACI Manual for a given situation.
- Properly apply bar bends and standard hook details for a given beam/column/slab type.
- Produce and interpret column, beam and footing schedules
- Identify commonly used types of deep foundation systems: piles, pile walls, diaphragm walls and caissons.
- Identify commonly used types of shallow foundation systems: rafts, mat slabs and slab-on-grade.
- Identify variety of construction methods, including cast-in place, pre-cast, pre/post-tensioning
- Detail concrete beam and column reinforcement, including stirrups/ties, dowels and lap splices.
- Create reinforcement detailing drawing for reinforced concrete beams, columns, stairs
- Produce bar bending schedules and reinforcing bar lists.
- Conduct preliminary design of concrete footings and column base plates.
- Interpret the Master Format – Division #3: Concrete.
