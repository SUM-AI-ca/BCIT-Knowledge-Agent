## Pediatric Advanced Life Support Renewal NSER 0650
Nursing Specialty Emergency Course
## Course Overview
​Our PALS Provider Renewal course tests and strengthens your high-level skills and knowledge involving core PALS concepts in simulated cardiopulmonary arrest or cardiovascular emergencies to help you in your practice. For healthcare providers who are confident in their PALS skills and use them regularly, and who require limited practice to renew their credentials. To be eligible for the PALS renewal course, students must have current Heart & Stroke Basic Life Support (BLS) Provider Certificate and c​urrent Heart & Stroke PALS Provider Certificate. Students will be required to complete PALS Precourse Self-Assessment Certificate (passing score = 70%)
### Prerequisite(s)
- To be eligible for the PALS renewal course, students must have current Heart & Stroke Basic Life Support (BLS) Provider Certificate and current Heart & Stroke PALS Provider Certificate.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of the course, you will be able to:
- Improve outcomes for pediatric patients
- Identify and intervene in pediatric patients presenting with respiratory emergencies, shock and cardiopulmonary arrest
- Develop high performance team dynamics and high-quality individual skills
- Develop high performance communication skills
