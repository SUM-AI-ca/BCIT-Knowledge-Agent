## Overview

This course introduces students to drawing as a means of visual communication. Developing freehand drawing skills for interior design applications is the focus of the course.

## Credits

2.5

## Cost

$593.82 - $630.47

## Prerequisites

- Entrance requirements.

## Learning Outcomes

- Create a perspective using various drawing mediums, including pencil and ink.
- Create drawings using different drawing techniques.
- Create drawings showing the relationship between negative and positive space.
- Practice simple, quick line drawings of objects and interiors.
- Create quick freehand sketches of interiors and/or furniture.
- Draw perspectives which illustrate the principles of descriptive geometry (one-point and two-point perspective).
- Convey depth in a drawing through the use of shade and shadow.​
