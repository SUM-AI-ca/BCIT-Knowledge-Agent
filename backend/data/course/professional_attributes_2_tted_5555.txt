## Professional Attributes 2 TTED 5555
Technology Teacher Education Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed to help students further develop the professional attributes needed to be a successful teacher in BC middle and high schools. Faculty will again meet with each student to assess and discuss their current proficiency with the professional attributes needed to be a successful teacher and offer suggestions for continued growth.​​
### Prerequisite(s)
- all term 3 courses
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the attributes needed for successful teaching
- Reflect on personal practice and behaviours as they relate to the stipulated attributes
- Act on advice provided by faculty
- Meet the expectations in the professional attributes rubric used in this course​
Effective as of Fall 2024
