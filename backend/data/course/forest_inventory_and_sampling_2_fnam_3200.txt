## Forest Inventory and Sampling 2 FNAM 3200
Forest & Natural Areas Mgt Course
## Course Overview
Building upon the foundational skills developed in FNAM 2200 , this course provides higher level sampling and measurement techniques used in the British Columbia inventory system. Students will gain practical experience in forest inventory, timber cruising (including Loss Factor, CGNF, and VRI methods),, log scaling, and waste and residue assessments. Additional topics include the Provincial Market Pricing System, including stumpage rate determination. This course emphasizes planning, data collection, compilation, analysis, and reporting according to current provincial standards and procedures. Students will collect field data and learn how it is reported through various provincial and federal systems. The Timber Pricing System manuals and the Provincial Vegetation Resource Inventory (VRI) ground sampling procedures form the basis of the applications practiced in this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the multi-phase design of the provincial inventory system.
- Explain the role of timber cruising, waste and residue surveys, and log scaling in the appraisal system.
- Design statistically sound sampling plans for various forest inventories, adhering to provincial standards. o Perform precise measurements of trees and forest stands using appropriate tools and techniques for timber cruising, log scaling, waste and residue assessment, and biomass estimation.
- Perform precise measurements of trees and forest stands using appropriate tools and techniques for timber cruising, log scaling, waste and residue assessment, and biomass estimation.
- Compile forest inventory data according to established guidelines and reporting systems, including the Timber Pricing System and VRI databases.
- Explain the differences between "statutory log grades" and "market log sorts".
