## Advanced Perioperative Nursing: Gastrointestinal Surgery NSPO 7811
Nursing Specialty Perioperative Course
## Course Overview
​This badged course is designed for perioperative nurses seeking advanced knowledge in gastrointestinal (GI) surgery. The course provides a comprehensive overview of key procedures involving the stomach, pancreas, liver, spleen, and lower GI tract. The esophagus, although part of the GI tract, will not be part of this badge because of its location in the thoracic cage. Additionally, you’ll dive into the surgical management of hernias, a common but critical aspect of abdominal surgery. Tailored for perioperative nurses, this badged course will expand your understanding of anatomical considerations, common surgical techniques, and the role of perioperative care in optimizing individual outcomes. Use the Course Outline to understand and reference the course elements.Through interactive modules, case studies, and reflective exercises, you will gain the knowledge needed to assist with complex GI surgeries, ensuring safe and effective care for those undergoing these essential procedures. By the end of the course, you’ll be equipped with the information to navigate various surgical challenges and contribute to the success of GI surgeries.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of Perioperative Specialty Nursing Program. Perioperative Nurses (RN or LPN/RPN) from any Health Authority or Private Surgical Center or equivalent practice setting.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this badged course, you should be able to:
- describe the anatomy of the organs involved in gastrointestinal surgical procedures
- describe the physiology of the organs involved in gastrointestinal surgical procedures
- summarize the gastrointestinal surgical procedures, including the common indications, equipment, approaches, and complications
- create a plan of care for persons undergoing gastrointestinal surgical procedures ​
