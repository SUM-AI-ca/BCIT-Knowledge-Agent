## Arc Welding Processes MTLF 1235
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the fundamentals of arc welding, which includes the various welding processes, equipment, and accessories. The course provides hands-on shop training in SMAW (shielded metal arc welding) and GMAW (gas metal arc welding). As you work through the course, you will have many opportunities to use the various welding processes and equipment in the shop, under the supervision of the instructor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1200

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe a variety of welding processes (GMAW, SMAW, MCAW, FCAW).
- Describe safety considerations for welding.
- Describe how to operate and maintain welding tools and equipment.
- Identify weld joints and positions.
- Describe weld discontinuities and their causes.
- Perform a tack test using GMAW.
- Perform single pass welding using GMAW and SMAW.
- Perform multi-pass welding using GMAW and SMAW.
