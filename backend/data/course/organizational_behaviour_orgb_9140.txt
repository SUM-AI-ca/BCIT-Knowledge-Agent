## Organizational Behaviour ORGB 9140
Organizational Behaviour Course
## Course Overview
Management and organizations are viewed in an environmental context rather than as isolated activities in a self-supporting system. An organization continually has to adapt to changes in the competitive dynamic environment or lead the change. Its effectiveness depends on the motivation and behaviour of the workforce talent, the organization’s greatest asset. To make effective business decisions, organizational leaders and managers need to maintain a clear sense of the organizational culture in its environmental web and the interrelationships and interdependencies with which it operates. Knowledge and understanding of the organizational framework, individual and group human behaviour, are essential prerequisites for effective and efficient organizational leadership.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Make effective management decisions through analyzing a company’s dynamic internal and external environment and applying best practices in organizational leadership.
- Effectively lead and manage individuals who are lower in the company and influence individuals who are equal or higher in the company to achieve organizational goals.
- Motivate individuals through applied motivational techniques, such as job re-design, engagement, empowerment, and performance evaluation.
- Evaluate a company's talent to create opportunities for employee development that is aligned with organizational strategic goals.
- Facilitate effective team work.
- Critically evaluate a company's leadership through applying leadership theories.
- Effectively manage workplace conflict and show emotional intelligence.
- Evaluate a company's internal communication channels and implement improvement.
- Analyze management problems in organizations and execute working solutions.
- Critically evaluate the need for organizational change and apply the most effective organizational change model.
