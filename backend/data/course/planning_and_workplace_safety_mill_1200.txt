## Planning and Workplace Safety MILL 1200
Millwright Course
## Course Overview
The Planning and Workplace Safety course trains students to a skill level equivalent to Level One Apprenticeship in Safe Work Practices. Students become familiar with: WorkSafeBC – Occupational Health and Safety Regulations (OHS), Environmental Regulations, Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), Safe Work Areas, Fire Prevention and Control, and Ergonomic considerations. The content of the course is aligned with SkilledTradesBC’s 2014 Competency Profile Chart and includes the following competencies: A1, A2, A3, A4, A6 and A7.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain Federal/Provincial Occupational Health and Safety Regulations as it applies to millwright trade
- Explain and apply Environmental Regulations as they apply to the millwright trade
- Use Personal Protective Equipment while in the millwright shop
- Follow Safe Working Practices in the millwright shop
- Interpret plans and sketches pertaining to the millwright trade
- Use reference resources pertaining to the millwright trade
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2015
