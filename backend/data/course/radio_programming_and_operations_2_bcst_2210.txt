## Radio Programming and Operations 2 BCST 2210
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Continues from BCST 1110 . Major emphasis is on honing the technical operations skills learned in the first term. Analog and digital commercial production, radio station operations, audition tapes and the use of lightweight, portable equipment are topics for instruction in this term. Emphasis is placed on practical applications of theory.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BCST 1110
