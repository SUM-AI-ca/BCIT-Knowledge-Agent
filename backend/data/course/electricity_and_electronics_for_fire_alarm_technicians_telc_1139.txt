## Electricity and Electronics for Fire Alarm Technicians TELC 1139
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
Covers basic electrical and electronic theory, basic electrical calculations, schematic and blueprints, instruments, equipment operations: specifications, work orders, Canadian Electrical Code.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Basic electrical and electronic theory.
### Credits
4.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Basic electrical theory, components and be able to perform basic electrical calculations.
- Basic electronic and semi-conductor theory, and be able to recognize components within a Fire alarm system or Emergency light system.
- Correct use of multimeters, how to avoid errors in testing.
