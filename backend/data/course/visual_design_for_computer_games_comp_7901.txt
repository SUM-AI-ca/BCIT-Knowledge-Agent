## Visual Design for Computer Games COMP 7901
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
The objective of this course is to introduce students to the methods and materials that non-programmers, such as Artists, Modelers, and Animators, would use to create to create visual assets for computer games.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Acceptance into the Bachelor of Technology in Computer Systems Degree program.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
During the course students will acquire basic knowledge and skills in the following topics:
- Object Modeling.
- Organic/Mesh Modeling.
- Materials and Textures.
- Lighting and Shading.
- Animation.
- Rigging.
- UV Texturing.
