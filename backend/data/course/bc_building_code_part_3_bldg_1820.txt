## BC Building Code: Part 3 BLDG 1820
Building Course
## Course Overview
Examines the purpose, scope and contents of Part 3 of the B.C. Building Code, Use and Occupancy. This course will be of special interest to persons in design, drafting, construction, inspection and financing of buildings. Students must bring B.C. Building Code to the first class.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Some knowledge of the B.C. Building Code.
