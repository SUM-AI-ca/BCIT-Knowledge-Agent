## Entertainment Reporting BCST 1058
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Learn how entertainment reporters do their jobs from story selection to developing story ideas to story presentation to preparing for celebrity interviews. CBC's Karen Tankard will pass on tips for people wishing to work in one of the most sought after jobs in broadcasting. Class guests bring years of experience interviewing actors, authors and celebrities.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

0.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Better understand how entertainment is reported in the media and how celebrity interviews are arranged.
