## Marine Power Systems 2 MEOC 2180
Marine Engineer Officer Cadet Course
## Course Overview
In this second level of the Marine Power Systems course, emphasis will be on the construction details, operation, and maintenance of diesel propulsion engine structures and the optimizing and maintenance of fuel injection and scavenging systems. In auxiliary boiler systems, students will study the construction, operation, and maintenance of auxiliary boilers, boiler mountings, boiler defects, and repairs.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the construction and maintenance of slow and medium speed propulsion diesel engine bed-plates, bed plate chocks, and holding-down bolts.
- Describe the construction and maintenance of A-frames and cylinder blocks of slow and medium speed propulsion diesel engines.
- Describe the construction, operation, and maintenance of cylinder liners of slow and medium speed diesel engines.
- Describe the construction, operation, and maintenance of cylinder heads of slow and medium speed diesel engines.
- Describe the construction and design features of main engine crankshafts.
- Describe the construction, operation, and maintenance of cross-head bearings and guides of diesel propulsion engines.
- Describe the construction, operation, and maintenance of 2-stroke and 4-stroke main engine pistons, piston rings, piston rods, and glands.
- Describe the construction, operation, and maintenance of the timing and valve actuating gear of slow and medium speed diesel engines.
- Describe the basics of fuel combustion in diesel engines and the operation of fuel injection systems in diesel propulsion engines.
- Explain how efficient fuel injection is achieved in slow and medium speed marine diesel engines.
- Describe the basic operational requirements and maintenance of a typical 2-stroke marine diesel propulsion engine scavenging system.
- Describe the construction, operation, and maintenance of diesel engine turbo-chargers.
- Describe the construction and working principles of an auxiliary steam generation plant and exhaust gas boiler.
- Describe the construction, routine operation, and maintenance of the combustion system and safety components of the auxiliary steam generation plant.
- Describe the construction and operation of safety valves in auxiliary boilers.
- Describe the construction and operation of auxiliary boiler water level indicators.
- Identify the various boiler defects that arise from overheating and corrosion. State methods of repair.
- Describe the routine boiler operations, preparation for inspection, and lay-up procedures.
