## Communications for Natural Resources COMM 2147
Communication Course
## Course Overview
This course will build on the skills you learned in COMM 1147 to be an efficient and professional communicator at work. You will write effective reports, deliver a persuasive oral presentation, and participate effectively in meetings and group work. You will prepare a formal report and present it orally and in writing. The theme of sustainability will be integrated into your course activities and assignments. The course consists of a one-hour lecture and a two-hour lab each week. The lectures provide the theory and demonstrations; the labs provide opportunities to apply the theory. You will be expected to demonstrate your understanding of both theory and applications in the labs, and in assignments.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMM 1147

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Present an SRM-related topic as part of a team.
- Cite professional literature in a variety of ways.
- Use graphics effectively.
- Collaborate as part of a team to accomplish work-related communication tasks; develop techniques for starting and managing group projects.
- Write informal technical and scientific reports.
- Prepare a scientific report or write a funding proposal.
- Reflect on their communication skills development.
