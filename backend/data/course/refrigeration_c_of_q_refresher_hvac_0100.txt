## Refrigeration C of Q Refresher HVAC 0100
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
Prepares tradespersons to write the examination for the Red Seal (Interprovincial) endorsement / Certificate of Qualification as a Refrigeration Mechanic. The course reviews the theoretical knowledge of the trade. Topics include: refrigeration theory, reciprocating compressors, condensers, evaporators, flow control devices and accessories. Contact SkilledTradesBC at www.skilledtradesbc.ca or 1-866-660-6011 to determine eligibility requirements for writing the certification examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Documented trade related work experience of 10,830 hours.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the process for challenging a trade certification examination.
- Identify the tasks that will be tested on the Red Seal examination,
- Self-assess preparedness to write the Certification of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination
