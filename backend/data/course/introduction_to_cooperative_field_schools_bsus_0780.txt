## Introduction to Cooperative Field Schools BSUS 0780
Sustainable Business Course
## Course Overview
Introduction to Italian Cooperatives is a course offering students applied learning, rich interactive site visits, and immersion in the Cooperative and Italian culture as part of the BCIT School of Business Executive Cooperative Field School. Students will be exposed to the Italian Cooperative Model - a model that demonstrates how co-operative relations among small firms can be leveraged to serve global markets. Students will learn the principles of co-operation and how the adoption of reciprocity and mutual benefit can be operationalized in communities. Students will observe how government policies can actively promote co-operative relations among firms. They will learn to develop sector-specific strategies that aid the growth and competitiveness of small and medium-sized firms. Student will discern the impact of a member based shared services model that can be applied to research and development, education and training, marketing and distribution, financing, technology transfer, workplace safety, environmental regulation, and a host of other services to help small and medium sized firms compete. Finally students will learn how to apply the co-operative model to the broader commercial economy by promoting the involvement of individual firms in collective ventures that benefit all participants and associated sectors.
