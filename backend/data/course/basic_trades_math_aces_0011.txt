## Basic Trades Math ACES 0011
Access to Trades Course
## Course Overview
Covers whole numbers, fractions, decimals and direct measurement. Delivery allows learners to participate from home, work or school. Internet or print access to instructor solutions and direction is available. A personal tutor is assigned to each learner.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the learner will be able to:
- Work with whole numbers.
- Work with fractions.
- Work with decimal numbers.
- Work with fraction and decimal measuring systems and tools.
- Solve trade-related word problems involving these skills.
