## Environmental and Administrative Law SRMT 4930
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
The focus of this course is for students to obtain the skills necessary for researching and reviewing recent court and administrative board decisions that have the potential to impact their day to day work activities.
### Prerequisite(s)
- SRMT 4920
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use legal research tools to find current updates to statutes and regulations.
- Research judgements related to sections within statutes and regulations, and key legal concepts related to sustainable resource management.
- Prepare case briefs of court and administrative board decisions.
- Discuss the significance of recent decisions that directly impact day to day work activities in sustainable resource management.
- Discuss relevant legislation related to Sustainable Resource Management.
