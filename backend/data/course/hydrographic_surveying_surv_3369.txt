## Hydrographic Surveying SURV 3369
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
Includes tides and water levels, obtaining, positioning and reducing soundings, an overview of electronic positioning, electromagnetic wave propagation and positioning systems; propagation of sound in water; acoustic positioning and devices used in hydrography; types and uses of nautical charts.
### Prerequisite(s)
- SURV 1161
