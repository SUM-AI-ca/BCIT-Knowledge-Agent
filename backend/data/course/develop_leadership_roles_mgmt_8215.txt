## Develop Leadership Roles MGMT 8215
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates leadership role, such as understanding leadership and management functions by identifying the characteristics of successful leaders and managers, understanding followers and followership, and examining the impact of leadership ethics and values.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 80% in MGMT 8010
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Understand Leadership and Management Functions
- Identify the characteristics of successful leaders.
- Identify the characteristics of successful managers.
- Differentiate between the roles of manager and leader.
- Differentiate between the roles of informal and formal leader.
- Develop Leadership Skills
- Develop formal and informal leadership skills.
- Understand the importance of leadership development
- Develop key leadership skills
- Examine the link between leadership styles and organizational and individual needs.
- Determine the appropriate style of leadership for different situations.
- Understand the role of influence on leadership effectiveness.
- Understand Followers and Followership
- Understand characteristics of followers and followership.
- Determine as a leader when the leader and follower roles are interchangeable.
- Understand the difference between charisma, transformational, transactional leadership.
- Examine the Value of Ethics and Values on Leadership
- Examine your own ethics and values as they apply to a leadership position.
- Examine the ethics and values of your organization and their impact on organizational culture and performance.
- Understand the impact of attitudes of leadership and followership.
- Examine the Impact of Diversity on Leadership
- Understand the impact of diversity issues on leadership and followership.
- Examine the diversity of your organization and its impact on performance.
