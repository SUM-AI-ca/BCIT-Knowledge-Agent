## Automatic Sprinkler Part 2 MECH 4083
Mechanical Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This course follows the basics taught in Automatic Sprinkler Pt. 1. It describes the various types of deluge valves and covers the hydraulic calculations required in Sprinkler design. The calculations range from basics to the more complicated and detailed Loop and Grid calculations. This course ends with information on fire pumps.
