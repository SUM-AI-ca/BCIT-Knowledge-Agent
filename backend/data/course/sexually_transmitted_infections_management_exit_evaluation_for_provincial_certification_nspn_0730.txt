## Sexually Transmitted Infections Management Exit Evaluation for Provincial Certification NSPN 0730
Nursing Specialty Perinatal Course
## Course Overview
A 2 hour long examination designed to test applicants knowledge in the field of Sexually Transmitted Infection Management. Successful completion of this exam is required in order to pass provincial certification through the College of Registered Nurses of British Columbia. (0 Credits). Before enrolling, please visit BCIT's information site for Reproductive Health certification (please link to www.bcit.ca/health/industry/reproductivehealth/ ). This website contains vital information and resources for all registered nurses who need to be certified before October 31, 2009.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
