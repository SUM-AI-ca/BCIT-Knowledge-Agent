## Theory of Business Data Analytics BABI 9005
Business Analytics & Business Intelligence Course
## Course Overview
This course covers the theory of Business Data Analytics, and prepares students to write the Certification in Business Data Analytics (CBDA) exam from the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA).
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify business research questions.
- Source data.
- Analyze data.
- Interpret and report Results.
- Use results to influence business decision making.
- Guide company-level strategy for Business Analytics.
- Prepare to write the IIBA CBDA certification exam.
Effective as of Fall 2022
