## 1998 BCBC Update: Multi-Unit BLDG 0007
Building Course
## Course Overview
Covers changes and updates to the BC Building code. Emphasis on part 9, multi-unit and small buildings. Practical application of code will be emphasized in lab.
### Prerequisite(s)
- BLDG 1825 or working knowledge of part 9 of the BC Building code.
