## Ecological Restoration Work Experience RENR 0000
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
The required work experience component is paid employment, either temporary or permanent, in which students do productive work that is directly related to the core competencies within the B.Tech. degree in Ecological Restoration. The intent of the paid work term is to provide experiential learning for the purpose of complementing and supplementing the various theories and topics covered throughout the course of studies. The student will apply skills, knowledge, and ability in an ecological restoration setting. Assessment of the work term will be based on a final report, which will summarize the work performed, its relationship to knowledge and skills gained during the course of academic studies, and lessons learned during the work term. This report will be evaluated by the program faculty. Performance during the work term will be graded as satisfactory (S) or unsatisfactory (U), according to BCIT Policy 5004 (Student Regulations Policy).
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all required courses in Core & Applied Science, Integrated Resource Management, and Specialty Courses in the degree program.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of the work term, students will be able to:
- Work productively as a member of a team in an ecological restoration setting, or in a discipline that utilizes techniques similar to those performed in a restoration capacity.
- Plan and perform duties that integrate and apply theories and classroom learning directly related to their course of study.
- Apply the skills of critical thinking, decision making, and problem solving.
- Monitor, assess, and adjust management options for restoring ecosystems.
- Interpret, evaluate, and solve complex ecological problems.
- Report results in a manner consistent with accepted standards and practices in their discipline.
- Utilize work experience in the development of communication skills, both oral and written.
