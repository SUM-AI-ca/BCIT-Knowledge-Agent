## Construction Materials and Assemblies BLDT 2050
Construction Operations/Supervision Course
## Course Overview
An introductory course that acquaints students with materials and assemblies encountered in project work from Divisions 1 through 10. Reviews the physical properties of the materials, how these properties are implemented in the construction project, selection, handling, and purchasing materials as part of the project technical/management team.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Successful completion of Construction Operations program or department approval.
