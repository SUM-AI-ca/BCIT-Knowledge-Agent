## Repair and Overhaul Practices2 AVGT 2004
Gas Turbine Course
## Course Overview
A course designed to familiarize you with the function of bearings, seals and gaskets. Activities focus on learning to inspect, test, and adjust gas turbine engine vibration and balance.
### Prerequisite(s)
- AVGT 2002
### Credits
15.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain handling and inspection procedures of gears
- Explain the use of seals and gaskets
- Describe the function, handling and inspection of components requiring special care
- Interpret aviation publications and documents
- Perform the disassembly, inspection and assembly of a turbine engine subassembly
- Describe the various anti-stall systems currently in use
- Describe engine anti-ice systems
- Describe starter systems
- Describe ignition systems
- Describe engine instrument systems
- Describe engine fire detection and extinguishing systems
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2023
