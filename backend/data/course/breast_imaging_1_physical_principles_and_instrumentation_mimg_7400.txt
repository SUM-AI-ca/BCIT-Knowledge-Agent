## Breast Imaging 1: Physical Principles and Instrumentation MIMG 7400
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
Traces the evolution of film-screen mammography and describes the physics and technology of breast imaging including digital mammography. Discusses radiation dose and risk considerations, radiation protection, quality assurance/control and the Mammography Quality Standards Act. Includes current research in breast imaging including other techniques such as ultrasound, MRI, digital tomosynthesis, laser CT imaging and nuclear medicine.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Trace the history of mammography from the 1960s to the 1990s.
- Outline the status of mammography with respect to its technical developments.
- Explain the physics of the radiation spectrum and its interaction with tissues in mammography.
- Describe the equipment components which meet the imaging requirements of mammography
- Compare and contrast two dedicated mammography systems.
- Outline the factors affecting image quality in mammography.
- Differentiate the various types of film-screen systems used in mammography and explain how optimum film processing can be achieved.
- Describe the essential characteristics of digital mammography.
- Outline the principles of stereotactic breast biopsy systems and describe patient positioning and sampling techniques with emphasis on the role of the mammographer during the procedure.
- Outline the factors affecting dose in mammography.
- Assess the impact of dose on the benefits and risks of mammography and summarize the results of several studies demonstrating benefits of screening mammography.
- Analyze Canadian (or US) recommendations for the safe use of x-rays in mammography.
- Outline quality assurance concepts and quality control procedures in mammography.
- Outline the findings of current research studies and activities in breast imaging.
