## Technical Communication 3 for Industrial Network Cybersecurity COMM 2230
Communication Course
## Course Overview
This course provides support for preparing a formal report and delivering an oral presentation for the Culminating Project. Building on the skills developed in COMM 2130 Technical Communication 2 for Industrial Network Cybersecurity, students will: prepare a formal report and deliver an oral presentation for the Culminating Project; practice teamwork skills; and participate effectively in meetings and group work. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMM 2130
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Participate in meetings productively. (3)
- Monitor teamwork effectiveness. (3)
- Assess teamwork effectiveness. (4)
- Prepare a written formal report. (4)
- Present an oral formal report. (4)
- Develop critical thinking skills for analyzing information and building strong arguments (4)
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2021
