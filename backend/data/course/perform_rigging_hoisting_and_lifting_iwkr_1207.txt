## Perform Rigging, Hoisting, and Lifting IWKR 1207
Ironworker Course
## Course Overview
Ironworkers work with large materials on a daily basis, most of which are too large to move by hand and require the use of various types of rigging, hoisting, and lifting systems. Consequently, you need to learn how to employ systems involving fibre or wire rope, either prepared for connection to a crane or reeved through blocks and sheaves. You also need to work with auxiliary hoisting equipment such as tugger winches, hand winches, chain hoists, or come-a-longs. Sometimes, it is more efficient to use mechanical moving equipment, such as jacks, and rollers. No matter the size of the object or material to be moved, with the correct equipment, knowledge, and planning you will learn how to put it in the correct place. In IWKR 1207 , you will problem solve material handling scenarios to position construction materials. You will also calculate forces and determine matching rigging capacities, while maintaining best practices for load control, inspection, care, and maintenance of rigging.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Use and maintain fibre and wire rope
- Demonstrate how to tie knots, bends, and hitches
- Demonstrate set up of rigging using various slings in multiple configurations
- Demonstrate how to use rope for hand lines and load control
- Demonstrate use of hoisting and rigging equipment
- Demonstrate how to use auxiliary hoisting equipment
- Demonstrate the storage, care, and inspection requirements on various hoisting and rigging equipment
- Demonstrate how to use various types of mechanical moving equipment
- Demonstrate how to move materials using hoisting and rigging equipment
- Perform calculations to determine:
- the weight of a variety of objects
- sling tension on multi leg bridles
- the holdback tension needed to support a hoisted weight
- the mechanical advantage of a simple and compound reeved system
- how to compensate for friction loss in reeved or redirected line systems
- the lead line pull in a reeved system needed to hoist a weight
- the center of gravity (mass) of an object
- Select lifting point(s) or lifting location(s) of various objects
- Demonstrate load control of a variety of objects
