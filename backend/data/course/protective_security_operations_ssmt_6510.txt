## Protective Security Operations SSMT 6510
Security Systems Management Course
## Course Overview
This is a practically oriented, skills-intensive course for law enforcement officers and those with constable status employed in protective security duties for public figures and witness protection operations. Course content is focused on the covert techniques and procedures used by a small security detail in the conduct of a protective security operation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Operational member of federal/provincial law enforcement agency/department AND Completed basic training with that agency/department AND Permission of the Program Head or Instructor.
### Credits
6.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the organization, role, and duties of the elements of a security detail.
- Plan and conduct a security survey.
- Demonstrate tact and professional conduct in protocol.
- Describe methods of VIP attack and demonstrate appropriate counter-measures.
- Demonstrate target indication methods.
- Demonstrate employment of dismounted formations and body protection drills.
- Demonstrate rapid casualty extraction drills.
- Demonstrate security procedures for VIP residences and places of work.
- Demonstrate search and secure procedures for accommodation and vehicles.
- Demonstrate security advance party procedures.
- Describe security protocols related to transportation.
- Demonstrate issuance of operational orders.
- Demonstrate spot code system and command post procedures.
