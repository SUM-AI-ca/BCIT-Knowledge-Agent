## Blueprint Reading for Metal Fabricators STEL 0115
Steel Fabrication Course
## Course Overview
Covers all aspects of blueprint reading for welding.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Some knowledge of steel fabrication. CSA approved steel toed work boots required.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Upgrade your blueprint reading skills, structural, tank, and detail drawings.
- Advance your trade math.
- Expand your knowledge of shop layout and terminology.
- Prepare to challenge the Interprovincial Red Seal Exam.
