## Plant Identification and Perspectives RENR 1125
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
This course provides students with opportunities to hone their observational skills related to plants and to communicate this knowledge to others. Students will develop skills to use different identification tools and become competent in how to approach identifying any unknown plant. This course braids together Western and Indigenous ways of knowing plants and provides opportunities to examine our relationships with plants more closely.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Analyze unknown plants and predict their identity using an array of tools, including books, keys, classification systems, and terminology
- Recognize and describe important common local plant species
- Differentiate between plants and their roles within the environment
- Discuss different perspectives and worldviews relating to plants, including Indigenous ways of knowing, and relate this to plants and the natural world in an expanded relationship of reciprocity
Effective as of Fall 2023
