## Calculus: 1B MATH 2042
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
A distance education course in elementary integral calculus. Topics include anti-differentiation, indefinite and definite integrals with application to areas, volumes and centroids, and the trapezoidal rule.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MATH 2041

3.0
