## House Renovations BLDG 0002
Building Course
## Course Overview
Covers the basic requirements for starting a home renovation. Who is involved? How to begin? Where can we add floor area to a house? When will it be finished? Cost? This course consists of 5 classes and a field trip.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Some familiarity of house construction.
