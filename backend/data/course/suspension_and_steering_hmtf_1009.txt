## Suspension and Steering HMTF 1009
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the basic principles of suspension and steering and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on suspension systems and components.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
10.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Service steering systems
- Service, diagnose, and repair suspension systems
- Remove and install undercarriage
- Diagnose and repair frames
