## Transportation Planning Part 1 TSYH 4580
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
The first of the two transportation planning courses, it provides the general introduction of transport planning, trip assignment, trip distribution and modal choice.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Introduction and Trip Generation
- Describe the various modes of transportation systems.
- Describe current transportation issues.
- Describe the transportation analysis and the sequential demand-forecasting models.
- Describe the trip generation model and perform trip generation analysis.
## Module 2 - Trip Distribution and Modal Choice
- Describe and apply the trip distribution methodology as a part of the sequential-demand forecasting process.
- Describe and apply the trip distribution methodology as a part of the sequential-demand forecasting process.
- Describe and apply the gravity model.
- Calibrate a Gravity Model.
- Apply modal choice models in trip generation and distribution.
