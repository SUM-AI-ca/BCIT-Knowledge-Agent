## AVID Non-Linear Editing Level 2 BCST 2281
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
A comprehensive one-weekend course which is a required course for the BCIT Part Time Studies Associate Certificate in Professional Editing. Advanced topics such as importing and exporting, advanced effects and layering techniques and online and audio post preparation are covered, providing students with even more tools to make the most of their editing experience. Most students taking this course are those pursuing the Associate Certificate in Professional Editing as well as those who are looking for more practice using the software, following the successful completeion of BCST 1180 Non Linear Editing.
### Prerequisite(s)
- BCST 1180
### Credits
1.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student:
- Will possess the skills & knowledge to be able to perform media management, importing & exporting functions.
- Be able to efficiently prepare their program for audio and video post-production sessions.
