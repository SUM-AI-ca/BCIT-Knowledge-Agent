## Sustainability as a Business Opportunity CORP 0372
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
Many leaders and professionals bring sustainability into their business as a personal value. The challenge that often arises is reconciling sustainability with competing business goals and priorities. This workshop offers an applied look at sustainability as a business opportunity, with the goal of helping participants understand its many value propositions.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Recognize sustainability as a business opportunity and articulate its value propositions
- Analyze the impacts of sustainability through a business case lens
- Apply strategies for communicating sustainability to employees, clients, and partners
