## Asphalt Technology CIVL 2224
Civil Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Teaches students how to select suitable materials for asphaltic concrete design using SHRP EQUIPMENT as well as the Marshall method. Topics include plant and paving quality control, asphaltic cement testing, and calculation for asphaltic design.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CIVL 1580
