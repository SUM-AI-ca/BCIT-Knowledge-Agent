## Automotive Fuel and Emissions Chemistry ASTP 3403
Automotive Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces you to fuel, combustion, and emission chemistry as it applies to both spark ignition and compression ignition engines. This is hypostatic to an in-depth understanding of some of the other courses which comprise the AST program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Discover matters, atoms, and the periodic table.
- Explain chemical bonds and molecule shapes.
- Discover hydrocarbons.
- Summarize oil refinery processes.
- Discover fuel characteristics.
- Apply mole concepts.
- Solve stoichiometry problems involving balanced equations.
- Analyze combustion equations.
- Explain fundamental combustion principles.
- Explain reaction mechanisms and emissions.
- Discover gasoline and diesel properties.
- Discover alternate fuels and applications in vehicle powertrain.
- Discover fuel cell theories and applications in vehicle powertrain.
