## Safe Work Practices MPET 1200
Motorcycle & Power Equipment Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the Workplace Hazardous Materials Management System (WHMIS) and WorkSafeBC regulations. Students will apply the regulations to shop safety, hazardous material safety, fire safety, safe work practices, and proper body mechanics in their work environment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe shop safety
- Describe personal equipment safety
- Describe fire safety
- Apply WHMIS legislation to the workplace
- Describe WorkSafeBC regulations
- Demonstrate safe work practices
