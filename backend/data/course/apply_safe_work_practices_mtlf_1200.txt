## Apply Safe Work Practices MTLF 1200
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
Safe work practices follow a set of guidelines on how to perform specific tasks in the shop and should be observed whenever working in the shop to prevent hazards before they happen. In this course, students will learn the theoretical and practical components of what constitutes safe work practices, as outlined in the WorkSafeBC guidelines.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Perform a specific task safely and efficiently from start to finish.
- Operate metal fabrication equipment responsibly according to manufacturer guidelines.
- Operate hand and power tools responsibly according to manufacturer guidelines.
- Mitigate workplace hazards once they have been identified.
- Perform assigned tasks in accordance with quality and production standards required by industry.
Effective as of Fall 2017
