## S-100 Basic Fire Suppression and Safety RENR 0530
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
This course fulfills the requirements of the Forest Fire Prevention and Suppression Regulations and the Occupational Health and Safety Regulations for people working in a forest environment. The course includes instruction in wildland fire behavior, fireline safety and fire control techniques and leads to eligibility for a Wildland Fireline Certificate as an emergency fire fighter.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
