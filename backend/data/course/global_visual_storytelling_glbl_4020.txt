## Global Visual Storytelling GLBL 4020
Global Business Leadership Course
## Course Overview
​The focus of this course is to explore effective visual storytelling techniques through digital media in a global context. Students will learn about the mechanics of visual storytelling and consider a range of visual styles found in global media. They will practice executing the techniques through written and visual elements with the aim of effectively communicating their story, drawing an audience, and developing their sense of visual storytelling.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Acceptance to the School of Business + Media field school program or permission from the International Program Coordinator
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Define and analyze the elements of good storytelling via digital media
- Diagnose the pros and cons of using social media for storytelling
- Understand the entire ecosystem of a social media platform (e.g. post, reels, stories, live) and utilize the right tools for storytelling to draw an audience
- Recognize how to add effective text, graphics, music, and video to complement visual media
- Know where to look for storytelling trends from various accounts (brands, personalities) and how to effectively incorporate them into storytelling
- Define and execute various photographic styles (product, lifestyle, selfie, documentary) using a smartphone​
