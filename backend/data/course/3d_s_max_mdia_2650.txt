## 3D S Max MDIA 2650
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
3ds Max is a professional 3D solution offering the 3D tools required for creating eye-catching visual effects, cutting-edge games and distinct design visualizations. This course teaches the fundamentals of 3D using 3ds Max, including modeling, animation, materials, advanced lighting and rendering tools. 3ds Max is commonly used in games, film, design visualization and special effects.

- No prerequisites are required for this course.
