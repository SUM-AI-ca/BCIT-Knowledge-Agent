## Hazard Recognition and Control OCHS 1003
Occupational Health and Safety Course
## Course Overview
Includes the requirements for workplace inspections, identification, control and evaluation of hazards, and a segment on how to use the BC WCB Occupational Health and Safety Regulation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
