## Overview

This course provides participants with diverse opportunities to expand and strengthen their business writing skills across a variety of communications platforms. With an emphasis on effective writing strategies, students will understand the importance of meeting their audience’s needs through numerous mediums including online discussion forums, emails, a technical report work plan, digital infographic, and the major components of a formal proposal.

## Learning Outcomes

- apply writing strategies to build relationships with diverse audiences around the world
- select the appropriate communications medium to achieve desired results
- write and design effective business and technical communications that reflect professionalism and industry best-practices
