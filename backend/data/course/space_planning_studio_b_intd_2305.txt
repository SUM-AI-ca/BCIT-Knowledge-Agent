## Space Planning / Studio B INTD 2305
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
This course will further examine the space planning process and will deal with commercial spaces.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in INTD 1330 and 65% in INTD 1350 and 65% in INTD 1345 and 65% in INTD 1310 and 65% in INTD 2300
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Complete a commercial design project following a design process.
- Formulate a design solution that incorporates relevant research.
- Present design project to the class .
- Produce drawings which include plans, elevations, sections, isometrics, reflected ceiling plans.
- Render drawings to demonstrate concept and material finishes.
- Produce a concept package.
