## Basic Electrical for Hydronic Technicians TELX 1142
Electronics Technical Course
## Course Overview
Provides a practical understanding of direct and alternating current, series and parallel circuits. Covers the analyzing of circuits using ohms law and multimeters. Ladder diagrams and electrical code will also be covered.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
