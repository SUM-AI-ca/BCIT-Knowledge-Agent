## Scissor Lift Operation Fundamental MEOP 1015
Mobile Equipment Operator Course
## Course Overview
​Provides the theory and practical training in the operation of scissor lift for BCIT students currently attending a Trades Foundation program. Successful participants will receive Scissor Lift Operator Certification. This program meets current Worksafe and CSA guidelines for scissor lift operators.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Driving experience
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Operate scissor lifts in a competent and safe manner within Worksafe Regulations
- Describe safe work practices
- Preform pre-shift inspections
- Describe hazard risk assessments
