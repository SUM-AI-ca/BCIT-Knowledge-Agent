## Airline and Flight Operations TRPT 0017
Trades Entry Assessment Course
## Course Overview
The Airline and Flight Operations assessments are designed to measure mechanical and abstract reasoning abilities. The mechanical reasoning assessment measures your ability to understand basic mechanical principles of machinery, tools, and movement. The assessment items represent simple principles that involve reasoning rather than special knowledge. The abstract reasoning assessment is a non-verbal measure of reasoning ability. It requires the ability to think logically and to perceive relationships in abstract figure patterns within a specified time limit.
