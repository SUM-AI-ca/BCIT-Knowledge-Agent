## Web Projects 1 IDSP 2380
Interdisciplinary Student Proj Course
## Course Overview
​This capstone course is a culmination of the knowledge and applied learning obtained from the first two terms of the program. Students work in interdisciplinary teams in this five-week term where projects are ideated, pitched, validated, designed, built, tested, and finally presented to faculty and class peers. The purpose of this course is to expose students to the interdisciplinary and collaborative environment of design and engineering while committing to a project within an intense and fast-paced timeframe.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of terms 1 and 2A,

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Work in an interdisciplinary team to incorporate the best from the design team and the development team.
- Demonstrate quick and pivoting ideation of project scope and direction within a short time-frame.
- Generate a task list for a given project context and manage the completion of tasks.
- Pitch a project within a strict and limited amount of time a project that the team will commit to design and build.
- Follow UX design procedures for a project and iterate frequently over design decisions.
- Develop a software product that adheres to a UX design.
- Tightly integrate the agile methodology with the UX design and UX development of the product.
- Demonstrate successful pivoting of project design based on user feedback.
- Plan and perform user tests on each iteration of the project.
- Create and adhere to a team charter that is inclusive to all members of the team.
- Plan and produce regular iterations that adapt to the changes based on user testing.
- Perform user surveys and analyze the results for incorporation into the project scope.
- Present the final product to peers and faculty for review and critique.
- Perform precise and successful testing on each iteration and demonstrate changes with no foreseeable errors.
- Demonstrate fairly accurate estimation of time frames based on scope throughout the project lifecycle.
- Integrate into cross-functional groups within a team and demonstrate group cohesion.
- Validate product features based on surveys and personas.
- Create personas and user stories based on surveys.
- Create Analyze user test results and pivot based on user feedback.
- Write, carry out, and assess usability benchmarks and record the results for project analysis.
- Develop strategies to make effective use of interdisciplinary team skills.
