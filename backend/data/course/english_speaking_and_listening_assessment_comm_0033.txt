## English Speaking and Listening Assessment COMM 0033
Communication Course
## Course Overview
​The Speaking and Listening assessment is designed to determine if an individual's English speaking and listening skills are at a level necessary to meet the entrance requirements of BCIT full-time programs. Assessments are based on the individual’s ability to speak English and understand spoken English effectively. Please visit the COMM English Assessments web page at www.bcit.ca/computing-academic-studies/communication/english-assessments/ for more information. The COMM 0033 assessment fee is non-refundable.
