## External and Government Relations SSMT 6430
Security Systems Management Course
## Course Overview
Students will be introduced to the Private Investigators and Security Agencies (PISA) Act as well as the powers bestowed to governing bodies of the security industry. They will develop a working knowledge of the purpose to perform inspections ensuring compliance with the Act; understand the roles and responsibilities surrounding the administration and compliance of licensed employees; monitor and communicate changes and amendments to the act; identify duties and powers of the registrar's office in regard to licensing; and establish methods to work with government to influence recommended changes to legislation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Admission to a Forensics credential program or permission of the Program Head.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the private investigator's role as outlined in the PISA Act.
- Identify operational responsibilities in the management of employees holding security licences.
- Develop and conduct inspections ensuring compliance to the PISA Act.
- Describe the compliance requirements as outlined in the PISA Act.
- Describe the responsibilities and liabilities of licensed employees when performing services for client organizations.
- Define common terms outlined in the PISA Act.
