## Building and Delivering Winning Real Estate Proposals REAL 6700
Professional Real Estate Course
## Course Overview
"Making a Pitch", the marketing or market search mandate is the end goal of a real estate professional's relentless prospecting for new business leads and opportunities. The objective of the pitch is to win the trust of the prospective client (buyer, a seller, a landlord, a tenant, a lender, or a municipal official) and be granted the exclusive mandate to represent the client's interests in the market place. Creating a sustainable business career as a real estate professional requires master skills in building and delivering winning proposals. In this course students work together in teams to evaluate approaches to making a pitch for different prospective clients. Then, individually students hone skills in developing targeted, competitive pitches as well as demonstrating the ability to make effective pitch presentations that are at industry-professional competency standards, including meeting any BC regulatory requirements and industry ethics standards.
### Prerequisite(s)
- REAL 5300 Real Estate Economics, REAL 5400 Sales Generation in Real Estate

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Articulate and evaluate components of an effective pitch for specific prospective clients in each of the residential and commercial market segments to match their different and unique needs.
- Strategize for the development of a competitive pitch.
- Construct an effective, competitive presentation targeted at winning the exclusive mandate of a prospective client, while meeting any BC regulatory requirements and industry ethical standards.
- Deliver an industry-standard, winning presentation to a prospective client in each of the residential and commercial marketplaces.
- Both individually and as part of a Pitch Team, execute a winning in-person Pitch.
