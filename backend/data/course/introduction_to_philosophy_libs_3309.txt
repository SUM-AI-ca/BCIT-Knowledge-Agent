## Introduction to Philosophy LIBS 3309
Liberal Studies Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to many fundamental philosophical issues: the existence of God, the nature of knowledge, the minds relation to the body, as well as the nature of morality. Through reading, discussion groups and written assignments, students will develop the basic philosophical skills of logical and conceptual analysis needed to address these questions.

## Learning Outcomes
After successfully competing this course, a student will be able to:
- Distinguish and evaluate deductive, inductive and abductive arguments.
- Explain some of the central problems of philosophy.
- Identify major themes, theories and contributions of some noted philosophers.
- State standard objections to leading philosophical theories.
- Analyse a philosophical text.
- Identify what makes an argument rationally defensible in a philosophical text.
- Deliver precise criticisms of philosophical arguments discussed in class and in course readings.
- Produce short, rational, and clearly written essays about some central problems in philosophy.
