## Creative Software for Image Manipulation and Colour Management GTEC 2280
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course begins by reviewing the Adobe Photoshop toolbox and customizing a work space in a document. Students begin by using the File Browser to sort images and then explore different ways of creating selections and masks. Students will create a series of projects including photo collages, retouching and filters all used to alter and enhance images. Student will use a variety of tools to adjust colour casts and hues. The course provides an opportunity to see how Photoshop manages colour modes between RGB, CMYK and LAB. Students are introduced to Spectrophotometers and Colorimeters and use them to colour manage LCD monitors.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- View, sort and rank images with Photoshop’s file browser and Adobe Bridge.
- Use different selection tools in Photoshop.
- Use the channels and layers palettes.
- Paint and colour images with a variety of tools and brushes.
- Combine images with layer masks.
- Use the Hue & Saturation, Selective colour, Curves and Levels tools to adjust colour.
- Retouch images utilizing a variety of professional strategies and techniques.
- Utilize Photoshop’s history palette and batch processing imaging techniques.
- Use the photo filter gallery and blending effects in Photoshop.
- Describe the physics of light and colour.
- Recognize how the eye-brain system affects colour perception.
- Calibrate, Profile, and Colour Manage an LCD monitor.
