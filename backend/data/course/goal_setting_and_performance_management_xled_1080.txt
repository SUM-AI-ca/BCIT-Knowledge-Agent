## Goal Setting and Performance Management XLED 1080
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​The Performance Management process ensures that employees and the organization are focused on the same priorities, while developing everyone to their fullest potential. Participants will explore tenets of goal setting theory, motivation, and appraisal to support employee goal achievement.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop a personal goal and success metric template
- Establish performance expectations for employees and learn to provide developmental guidance and feedback to create an environment with clear and achievable expectations.
- Understand and apply different motivational techniques to develop employee confidence and enhance performance​.
