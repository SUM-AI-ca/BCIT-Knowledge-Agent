## Introduction to Bookkeeping VENT 1823
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
This course will introduce students to the basics of bookkeeping, including the related terminology. Students will learn how to perform the daily tasks of recording bank deposits, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and payroll, all according to Canadian legal requirements. The month end, quarterly and year end activities will be discussed, and checklists will be introduced for all of these tasks. An introduction to QuickBooks will be included where students will learn how to set up a company and perform all of the above tasks as well as developing and interpreting balance sheets and income statements.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Acceptance into the program.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Describe basic bookkeeping concepts and terminology.
- Perform basic bookkeeping functions.
- Utilize QuickBooks accounting software to perform basic bookkeeping functions.
