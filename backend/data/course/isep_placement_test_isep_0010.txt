## ISEP Placement Test ISEP 0010
International Student Entry Course
## Course Overview
This online placement test is used to place students into an ISEP level. The test will assess a student's writing, reading, listening, grammar, and vocabulary skills. Note: this test cannot be used to meet the English requirements of other BCIT programs.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
