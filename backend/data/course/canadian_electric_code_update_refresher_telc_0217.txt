## Canadian Electric Code Update/Refresher TELC 0217
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
​This seminar will update learners on the General sections of the Canadian Electrical Code including conductor ampacities, grounding and bonding, capacitors, transformers and motors. The course will also cover the Safety Standards Act and associated provincial regulations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
