## Directed Studies 2 INTD 4380
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
Each student is given an in-depth project to complete using the knowledge and research techniques learned in all previous courses.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 65% in all Level 3 and 4 courses, except INTD 4360 and INTD 4370 which may be taken concurrently.
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, students will be able to:
- Apply knowledge learned in level 1 & level 2 of the program to complete an interior design project.
- Present an appropriate design idea for their project to the instructor.
- Develop a concept and maintain it throughout the project execution.
- Prepare a set of working drawings.
- Select appropriate materials and finishes.
- Create a journal/sketchbook to show research, concept and project development.
- Select appropriate furniture demonstrating an understanding of human factors.
- Present an appropriate lighting scheme demonstrating power smart knowledge.
