## Prolog Prog Lang COMP 2465
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Explains PROLOG, chosen for the Japanese fifth generation computer project. This popular language for Artificial Intelligence/Knowledge Systems (AI/KS) work is a "logic based" languge, which encourages elegant and concise solution to many difficult programming problems. PROLOG origins and uses in industry, language features, and various AI programming techniques (e.g. meta-interpreters) are examined. Students will build PROLOG applications.
### Prerequisite(s)
- IBM micro experience and successful completion of at least one level 1 programming language, or permission of the instructor.
