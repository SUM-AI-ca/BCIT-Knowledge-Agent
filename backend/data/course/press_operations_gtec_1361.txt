## Press Operations GTEC 1361
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course identifies and describes the main functioning parts of small printing presses in the offset printing industry. Students set up and operate presses under supervision, learning to produce a variety of printed products. Safety and environmental issues in the pressroom are covered including inks, fountain solutions and other chemistry.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Observe good safety practices for all press types.
- Review steps for producing a simple job from concept to finishing.
- Acknowledge the importance and purpose of good safety manuals.
- Adjust all functioning parts of the feeder, register and delivery, inking, water and cylinder systems.
- Set the feeder, register and delivery system for consistent and continuous printing.
- Calculate and adjust packing thickness and cylinder pressures.
- Examine ink tack, ink laydown sequence, and trapping issues.
- Review different print technologies such as sheet-fed or web offset and flexo printing.
- Perform basic maintenance on a small format press.
- Examine different types of paper and how it will affect a print job.
