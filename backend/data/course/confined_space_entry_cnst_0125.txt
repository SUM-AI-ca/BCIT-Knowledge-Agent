## Confined Space Entry CNST 0125
Construction Course
## Course Overview
Theory based training covers the mandatory requirements of the WCB Occupational Health and Safety Regulations (2005). Includes approved lockout procedures, work permit systems and personal protective equipment. Suitable for all related construction trades.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Minimum one year construction or trade related experience.
