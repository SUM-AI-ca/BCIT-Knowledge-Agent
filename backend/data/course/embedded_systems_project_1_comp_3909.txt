## Embedded Systems Project 1 COMP 3909
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
The practicum allows students to work on medium and large projects within guidelines specified by faculty. The projects are drawn from a variety of sources, especially from industrial situations, and may require extensive contact with the business community. Students work in teams and seek advice from a faculty member acting as their project supervisor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 2510 and COMP 2721 and COMP 2730 and COMM 2216
### Credits
5.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Determine project requirements.
- Plan and schedule project work tasks.
- Design a system that meets the documented requirements.
- Implement a system that has been designed.
- Test and deliver a working system to an industry client.
