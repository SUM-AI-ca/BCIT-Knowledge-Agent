## Business of Sawmilling Orientation WOOD 0020
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
​This class will prepare students to use the online learning environment throughout the Business of Sawmilling program, as well as introduce them to instructors and their fellow classmates. Students in this orientation will be provided with an overview of each course in the program and review math skills that will be used in some of the class content. Students will also receive an introduction to the Learning Hub.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
