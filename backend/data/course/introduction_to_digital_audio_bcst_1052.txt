## Introduction to Digital Audio BCST 1052
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Gain insight into the exciting world of Digital Audio. In this practical course students work in studios on a mixing console and PC. Learn voice recording, sound editing, effects and mixing on diverse software packages including Adobe Audition and Pro Tools.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, a student will be able to:
- Learn voice recording
- Practice sound editing, effects and mixing on Adobe Audition and Pro Tools
- Work on mixing console and PC
