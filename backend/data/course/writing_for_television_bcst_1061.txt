## Writing for Television BCST 1061
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course will give the student experience in screenwriting for both an existing TV show and creating an original TV series. The student will learn how to plot both half-hour and one hour TV shows and construct a compelling TV show premise. Students will write 60 pages of a first draft of a TV script upon completion of the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Create story ideas for an existing TV show.
- Learn how to pitch, outline and develop a spec script.
- Develop an original TV show premise.
- Write a pilot episode.
