## General Electricity 2 POWR 2204
Power Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This course provides more of a hands-on approach to working with electrical equipment using the theoretical knowledge base established in General Electricity 1. Topics include magnetism and electromagnetism, electrical metering devices, electrical distribution circuits, motors, generators, and transformers.
### Prerequisite(s)
- POWR 1104
### Credits
4.5

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Define terminology used for magnetism and electromagnetism, including quantities and units of measurements.
- Explain how current affects magnetic fields, flux, and forces.
- Describe the relationship between electrical and magnetic circuits.
- Describe Faraday's Law of Induction as applied to motors and generators.
- Describe Flemming's rules for direction of force, and induced EMF.
- Identify various electrical measuring instruments.
- Measure voltage, resistance, continuity, and electrical discharge using designated equipment.
- Construct, test, and troubleshoot basic electrical circuits.
- Wire basic electrical circuit diagrams.
- Describe the operating principles, characteristics, and starting methods of various types of A.C. and D.C. motors.
- Isolate electrical devices safely and put these devices back in operation.
