## Risk Management OCHS 3420
Occupational Health and Safety Course
## Course Overview
Examines the specialized field of risk management, which is devoted to minimizing the adverse effects of accidental loss on an organization. Explores the five steps of risk management identifying exposures to accidental loss, examining risk management strategies, selecting the best risk management technique, implementing the chosen risk management technique, and monitoring the results. Discusses purchasing insurance as one means of managing risk. Also explores the recent legislative trend of requiring workplace risk assessments.
### Prerequisite(s)
