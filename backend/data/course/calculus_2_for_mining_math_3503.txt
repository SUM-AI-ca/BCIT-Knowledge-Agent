## Calculus 2 for Mining MATH 3503
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
This course covers the topics of: definite and indefinite integrals; integration techniques; applications of integration; Riemann sums and integrals; the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus; improper integrals; first-order differential equations; Taylor Series. Applications to the mining industry will be utilized. Problem-solving skills and solution presentation will be emphasized.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MATH 2502
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Evaluate integrals involving a diverse range of functions. [1]
- Choose appropriate techniques and apply them to finding and evaluating definite integrals. [1]
- Calculate areas and volumes of irregular shapes using the trapezoid rule or Simpson’s rule.[1]
- Apply the definite integral to finding area, volume, centroid, arc lengths, fluid pressure, work and moments of inertia. [1, 2]
- Determine if a given sequence converges or diverges.[1]
- Apply concepts of absolute and conditional convergence of a series.[1]
- Approximate a differentiable function by a Taylor polynomial, determine the remainder term and compute the error.[1]
- Solve first-order differential equations.[1]
- Apply the techniques of first-order differential equations to practical problems. [1, 2]
- Use a computer algebra system to find integrals and to do practical problems. [5]
