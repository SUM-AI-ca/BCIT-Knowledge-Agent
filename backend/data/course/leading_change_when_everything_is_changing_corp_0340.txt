## Leading Change When Everything is Changing CORP 0340
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
As organizations and businesses face uncertainty and are fast-tracked into a digital world, this webinar will provide you with some principles on leading change focusing on the people impact. At the end of this webinar, you will be able to use a tool to assess the impact of a change and a framework to understand the phases of individual change. While we navigate unplanned and unprecedented change, your business and change strategy going forward doesn’t need to be.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this webinar, the participant will be able to:
- Explain key principles for leading change
- Understand phases of a change framework and the impact on individuals
- Illustrate the impact of a change on your people
