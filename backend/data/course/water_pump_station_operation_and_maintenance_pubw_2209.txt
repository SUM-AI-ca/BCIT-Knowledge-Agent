## Water Pump Station Operation and Maintenance PUBW 2209
Public Works Course
## Course Overview
The course covers fundamentals of distribution and collection system hydraulics and system curves, pump electrical and controls, and operation of pump to suit a system curve. The student will develop a small distribution system and measure head loss to size a pump. Various types of pumps maintenance and pump components including impellers, packing shaft and seals will be discussed.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Read and interpret pump and system curves.
- Calculate impeller periphery velocity and determine head.
- Understand affinity law the relationship between head, flow and horse power.
- Use nomographs to calculate piping head loss.
- Understand electricity and pump control logic, pump repair & maintenance.
- Receive, inspect, install and commission new pump in a existing system.
