## Math 1 ASTP 1200
Automotive Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
This introductory math course provides you with basic mathematical principles and skills to solve practical automotive problems. It also prepares you for the advanced math course, ASTP 2300 Math 2.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Assess basic mathematical concepts.
- Demonstrate operations of algebra.
- Apply special products and factoring.
- Compute trigonometric functions.
