## Communications 2 ASTP 3400
Automotive Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces you to the fundamental skills required to communicate in writing and in person, within the automotive industry, as managers or supervisors, clients, government inspectors, or with the public.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Communicate successfully to automotive professionals and customers.
- Write an effective cover letter and revised resume.
- Plan and conduct business meetings.
- Plan and conduct technical briefings.
- Plan and conduct sales presentations.
- Write persuasive proposals and defend them in stand-up presentation settings.
- Communicate effectively across cultures.
