## Planning in Times of Uncertainty CORP 0330
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
In times of uncertainty, it has become even more important to adjust how we plan and forecast. One of the toughest challenges in planning has always been trying to predict the future based on past experience. The difficulty we face now is that we have entered into unchartered territories and cannot rely on what we know to plan for tomorrow. In this webinar, learn how case scenario analysis and planning tools prepare organizations that are looking to build resiliency and increase their state of readiness in a rapidly changing and unpredictable environment.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Step by step process for Case Scenario Analysis and Planning
- Explore four case scenarios emerging for BC based on current trends
- Perform case scenario analysis for your organization by identifying potential outcomes and impacts
- Map out key actions for each case scenario and improve organizational readiness to adjust as the situation evolves
