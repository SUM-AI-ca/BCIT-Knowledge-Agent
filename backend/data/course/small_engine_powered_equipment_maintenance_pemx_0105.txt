## Small Engine Powered Equipment Maintenance PEMX 0105
Power Equipment Mechanic Course
## Course Overview
Covers maintenance and general engine repairs on lawn mowers, chainsaws, generator units and other utilities powered by air-cooled two- and four-stroke engines. Steel-toed footwear required.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Apply the basic principles of mechanical technology as they pertain to small engine;
- Carry out preventive maintenance, basic servicing, and rebuild procedures for selected machinery; and,
- Identify and carry out basic troubleshooting procedures on selected machinery.
