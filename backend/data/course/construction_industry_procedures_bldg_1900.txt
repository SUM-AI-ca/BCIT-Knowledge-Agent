## Construction Industry Procedures BLDG 1900
Building Course
## Course Overview
"Presents and overview of the established methods and procedures vital to the construction industry today. Topics include the basis of building development, bidding and contracting construction contracts, specifications and estimating."
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
