## Custom Food Safety Training FOOD 0165
Food Technology Course
## Course Overview
​An instructor will work with you and your company to identify deficiencies in the food safety plan and come up with a specific action plan to address these deficiencies.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
### Registration requirements

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Review the food safety plan
- Identify deficiencies
- Create an action plan
