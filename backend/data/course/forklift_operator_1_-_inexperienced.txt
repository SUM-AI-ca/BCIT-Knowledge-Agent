## Overview

This course will provide the inexperienced forklift operator with both theory and practical training in the operation of counterbalanced lift trucks. On successful completion, you will receive Mobile Equipment Operator Certification for Class 1, 4 and 5 counterbalanced lift trucks. Certification is valid for two years. This course meets current WCB guidelines and CSA Standards for lift-truck operators.
