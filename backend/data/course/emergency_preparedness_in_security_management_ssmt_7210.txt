## Emergency Preparedness in Security Management SSMT 7210
Security Systems Management Course
## Course Overview
Security management is instrumental to the success of emergency planning for all hazards within organizations. This course will focus on elements of emergency preparedness, mitigation, response, and recovery. Fieldwork and case studies are designed to complement and integrate lecture material.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Admission to a Forensics credential program or permission of the Program Head.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the legal responsibility of security management in emergency planning.
- Define key elements of emergency planning and business continuity.
- Describe the differences between emergency planning and business continuity.
- Explain the principles of planning, mitigation, preparedness & recovery.
- Explain the phases of emergency planning, mitigation, preparedness, response, recovery & business continuity.
- Identify the resources required for effective emergency response.
- Explain the principals of business continuity.
- Develop an emergency plan for a given organization.
- Evaluate the emergency response plans as well as the supporting to determine effectiveness and probability of resistance.
