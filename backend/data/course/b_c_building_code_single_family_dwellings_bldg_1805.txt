## B.C. Building Code: Single Family Dwellings BLDG 1805
Building Course
## Course Overview
Gives students a working knowledge of Part 9 of the 1998 BC Building Code for single family dwellings and accessory buildings. Prepares students to check plans, inspect buildings and deal with questions relating to Part 9 of the BC Building Code. Covers acceptable materials, systems, and methods used in housing construction. Students must bring BC Building Code to the first class.
### Prerequisite(s)
- BLDG 1970 and BLDG 2970 or knowledge of building construction.

3.0
