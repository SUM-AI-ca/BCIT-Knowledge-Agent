## Plastics & Composites, and Sheet Metal Repair ABOD 0201
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This is a supplementary course for MVBR Level 2 students who missed Plastics & Composites, and Sheet Metal Repair modules due to Covid.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify fundamentals of plastics and composite panels and components
- Prepare plastic and composite panels and components for repair
- Remove plastic and composite panels and components
- Repair plastics and composite panels and components
- Install plastic and composite panels and components
- Identify fundamentals of vehicle construction, metal and damage
- Repair metal panels and components
