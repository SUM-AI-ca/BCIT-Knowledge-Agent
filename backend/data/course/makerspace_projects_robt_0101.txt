## Makerspace Projects ROBT 0101
Mechatronics and Robotics Course
## Course Overview
Through self-directed study, students learn how to create 3D CAD models and use rapid prototyping technologies such as 3D printing. This course is an opportunity for students to bring their ideas to life and collaborate with other creative and technical people.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Create basic 3D models
- Describe 3D rapid prototyping technologies
- Design parts for rapid prototyping
- Build models using rapid prototyping technologies
