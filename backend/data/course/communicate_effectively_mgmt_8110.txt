## Communicate Effectively MGMT 8110
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates communication skills, such as speaking effectively, listening actively, writing clearly, and conducting meetings.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Listen Effectively
- Demonstrate that the whole message has been received.
- Demonstrate facilitating behaviors.
- Reduce the chance of misunderstandings by using various strategies (e.g., seek feedback, confront discrepancies, and analyze problems that do occur).
- Avoid disruptive behaviors.
- Foster Open Communication
- Interact openly and directly, sharing information and welcoming diverse perspectives, and encourage others to do the same.
- Work to establish a climate that values constructive criticism, the exchange of ideas and encourage multidirectional communication.
- Foster remote personal computer communications (e.g., using on-line chat forums, e-mail, forums, etc.).
- Speak Effectively
- Speak clearly and concisely.
- Strive to be understood and to maintain interest.
- Use an appropriate style and avoid distracting behaviors.
- Demonstrate sensitivity to audience and respond to non-verbal cues.
- Prepare Effective Written Communications
- Generate various forms of business writing (e.g., letters, memos, and reports) that demonstrates the principles of good writing.
- Demonstrate clarity, organization, a sense of purpose and an engaging style in written work.
- Conduct Effective Meetings
- Analyze the needs and interests of the participants and the opportunities and limitations of the meeting.
- Identify the meetings objective and plan its content and format of meetings.
- Conduct an effective meeting.
