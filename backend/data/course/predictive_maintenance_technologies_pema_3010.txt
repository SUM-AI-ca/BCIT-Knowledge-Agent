## Predictive Maintenance Technologies PEMA 3010
Maintenance Management Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the various predictive maintenance technologies and discusses their impact and benefits. Topics include fault code analysis (FCS), wear particle analysis, vibration analysis, laser alignment systems, nondestructive testing (NDT) and infrared thermography.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in PEMA 1000
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Explain how predictive maintenance dovetails into the maintenance effect.
- Know what type of predictive maintenance and condition based maintenance (CBM) should be used for optimum effect.
- Understand the fundamentals of fault code analysis.
- Understand the fundamentals of wear particle analysis.
- Understand the fundamentals of vibration analysis.
- Understand the fundamentals of laser alignment systems.
- Understand the fundamentals of N.D.T. & ultrasonic detection.
- Understand the fundamentals of infrared thermography.
