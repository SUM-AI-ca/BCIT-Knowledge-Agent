## Occupational First Aid - Level 1 CTMX 1301
Commercial Transport Course
## Course Overview
Covers basic first aid with rescuer C.P.R. Successful participants will receive WorkSafeBC Occupational First Aid Level 1 Certification. Personal identification must be presented to the instructor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will receive:
- Level 1 W.C.B. Occupational First Aid Attendant certification.
