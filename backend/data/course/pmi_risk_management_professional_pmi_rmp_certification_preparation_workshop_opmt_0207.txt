## PMI Risk Management Professional (PMI-RMP)® Certification Preparation Workshop OPMT 0207
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
PMI‐RMP® Certification from the Project Management Institute is the industry standard for demonstrating competence and a solid foundation of skills to identify and assess project risks, mitigate threats and capitalize on opportunities. This workshop prepares participants to write the PMI-RMP® certification exam and provides 30 hours of educational credits towards meeting the PMI application requirements. The workshop provides an overview of the PMI risk management framework for project management as set out in the PMI Standard for Risk Management in Portfolios, Programs, and Projects and A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide)

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply the project risk management key concepts, terminology, organizational influences, and methodologies
- Describe the project risk management processes
- Apply the risk management techniques as defined in A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide)
- Apply the risk management techniques as defined in the PMI Standard for Risk Management in Portfolios, Programs, and Projects
- Apply test‐taking techniques for writing the exam
- Understand the PMI-RMP Certification Process from PMI
- Understand the PMI application process to write the PMI-RMP Exam
- Be prepared to write the PMI-RMP Certification Exam
