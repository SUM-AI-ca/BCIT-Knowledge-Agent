## Computer Aided HVAC Design MSYS 4179
Mechanical Systems Course
## Course Overview
Prepares HVAC designers to perform HVAC load analysis & air handling system design and to simulate building energy consumptions using Carrier Hourly Analysis Program (HAP3.12) A case study is also included during the interactive workshop.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MSYS 4079

1.5
