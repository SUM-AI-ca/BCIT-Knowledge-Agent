## Vehicle Restoration: Refinishing ABOD 0134
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the enthusiast to equipment, materials and techniques used to refinish a vehicle. The student will learn the application of conventional style paints as well as the application of waterborne products in a state of the art spray booth.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ABOD 0133
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe safety procedures / personal protective equipment (PPE) used to paint.
- Describe types of paints, including waterborne products, used in vehicle manufacturing past and present.
- Describe materials and equipment used to refinish a vehicle.
- Describe various refinishing techniques.
- Refinish selected panels, using single stage and base coat / clear coat finishes.
