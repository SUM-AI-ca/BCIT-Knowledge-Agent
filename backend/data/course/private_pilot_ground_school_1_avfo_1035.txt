## Private Pilot Ground School 1 AVFO 1035
Airline and Flight Operations Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed to meet the knowledge requirements of a Private Pilot Permit. The course includes topics such as Canadian Aviation Regulations, aerodynamics, theory of flight, weather, aircraft engine and systems, flight instruments, navigation, radio and electronic theory, flight operations and licensing requirements.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe Canadian Aviation Regulations as it pertains to Private Pilot license
- Describe aerodynamics, theory of flight, weather and navigation
- Describe aircraft engine and systems
- Identify flight instruments as it pertains to Private Pilot license
- Identify radio and electronic theory in an aviation context
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2023
