## Vehicle Restoration: Sheet Metal Fabrication ABOD 0130
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the enthusiast to sheet metal fabrication and repair techniques. The student will learn to shape, shrink, and stretch sheet metal used in vintage year automobiles, then apply the techniques learned in a typical shop environment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe general shop safety procedures.
- Describe various types of metals.
- Describe tools used to fabricate and repair sheet metal.
- Describe techniques used to fabricate and repair sheet metal.
- Use tools to fabricate and repair sheet metal.
- Apply techniques used to fabricate and repair sheet metal.
