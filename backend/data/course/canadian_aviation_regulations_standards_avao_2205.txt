## Canadian Aviation Regulations/Standards AVAO 2205
Airport Operations Course
## Course Overview
​The focus of this course is to develop a solid understanding of aviation regulations and the related standards as required by Transport Canada to maintain a certified aerodrome or air operator. This includes Canadian Aviation Regulations and Standards (CARS) Part III and Part VII and the related standards and advisory circulars. Evaluation includes quizzes, and a case study report and presentation (with a team).
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in AIRC 1004 and 70% in AIRC 1009
### Credits
4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe CARS Part III (Airport and Aerodromes) and the associated regulations, standards and advisory circulars.
- Describe the CARS Part VII (Air Operators) and associated regulations, standards and advisory circulars.
- Apply the regulations in CARS Part III and VII and the associated regulations, standards and advisory circulars.
- Apply the guidelines for developing an Airport Operations Manual or Air Operators manual as per the Aerodrome Advisory Circular. ​
Effective as of Fall 2022
