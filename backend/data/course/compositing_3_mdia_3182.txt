## Compositing 3 MDIA 3182
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Students build on skills and knowledge gained in Compositing 1 and 2, and explore more advanced topics and techniques in the fields of Visual Effects and Digital Compositing. Editing the final demo reel and proper online delivery is the focus. Students examine 3D camera tracking and its applications in compositing live and CG footage, set extensions, as well as a method for achieving planner tracking. They also explore keying green or blue screen shots and how to render a CG scene in passes in order to successfully integrate it with keyed shots. Variant useful Effects are used to achieve the desired final look for demo reels. Editing and colour correcting demo reels are examined, as well as final output formats for successful online delivery (such as to Vimeo or Youtube).
### Prerequisite(s)

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- List the steps needed for 3D camera tracking.
- Integrate text or CG elements in a live footage.
- Create and integrate set extensions within a video.
- Identify the steps needed in production to achieve a correct key.
- Apply variant keying tools in After effects to a blue or green screen shot.
- Explain the advantages of rendering a CG scene in passes.
- Utilize colour correction as a tool for editing a demo reel.
- Utilize variant effects in After effects to achieve a visual style.
- Edit a demo reel using Final Cut Pro and/or After effects.
- Output and compress a demo reel for online delivery.
