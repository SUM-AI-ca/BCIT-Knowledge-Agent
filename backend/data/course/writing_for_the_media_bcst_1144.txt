## Writing for the Media BCST 1144
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Grow your potential for employment in communications, media relations, and the news media. Explore how to write an engaging blog and develop the skills and confidence to work in any media newsroom. You’ll get much writing practice in this course and become well-grounded in the principles of journalism. Skills learned dovetail with BCST1148 'Interviewing for the Media' and, combined, offer the opportunity to vigorously stimulate your communication skill set.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Define elements of effective media writing.
- Apply effective writing elements to all media writing formats, such as news, reviews, and commentaries.
- Increase your writing speed.
- Organize information in a logical fashion for presentation to a target audience.
- Construct a newscast and its components.
- Adapt online media content for traditional broadcast and vice versa.
- Create and maintain a blog.
