## Safety Skills for Field Technologists RENR 1112
Renewable Resources Course
## Course Overview
This course provides a foundation for a safety work ethic both for the completion of studies at BCIT and when on the job as a field technologist. With a focus on safety oriented training for technologist working in remote outdoor settings, students will obtain 6 core safety certifications: 1) WCB Occupational First Aid, Level 1, 2) WCB Transportation Endorsement, 3) Chain Saw Safety, 4) S-100 Basic Fire Suppression and Safety, 5) Bear and Cougar Awareness and Safety, 6) WHMIS (Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System).
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate First Aid Skills that meet Worksafe BC OFA Level 1 certification.
- Demonstrate First Aid Skills that meet Worksafe BC TE certification.
- Demonstrate basic chainsaw maintenance and safe operation.
- Describe the fundamentals of wildland fire fighting, fire safety and behaviour.
- Describe the appropriate response to bear and cougar attacks; including avoidance procedures, encounter procedures and procedures used while being attacked.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System in BC.
