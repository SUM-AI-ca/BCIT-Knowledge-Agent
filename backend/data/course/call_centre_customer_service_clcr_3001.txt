## Call Centre Customer Service CLCR 3001
Call Centre Course
## Course Overview
At the end of the module the student will be able to identify practices that lead to improved customer satisfaction, identify customer needs, use problem solving techniques and handle difficult customer.
### Prerequisite(s)
- College level courses in Communications, Business Windows 95.
