## Interior Design Drafting 2 INTD 2340
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
Students will become familiar with two-dimensional drafting techniques and drafting terminology to produce construction plans, elevations and section drawings for interior design applications. Appropriate legends, schedules and cross-referencing will be covered and included in drawings. The focus will be on creating legible, accurate construction drawings using manual drafting techniques.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in INTD 1340 and 65% in INTD 1345
### Credits
2.5

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Create drawings using manual drafting equipment with a pencil.
- Identify various types of plans for interior design applications.
- Produce construction plans, power and communication plans, and reflected ceiling plans, showing appropriate dimensions and cross-referencing.
- Produce construction elevations, sections and details with appropriate cross-referencing shown.
- Produce construction drawings with appropriate legends, including partition legends, and schedules, including ones for doors, plumbing, and finishing work in both imperial and metric scales.
- Draw 'as-built' spaces.
- Explain reasons for the placement of stairs in a plan and the elevations used.
- Produce drawings using appropriate line weights, layout and lettering techniques.
- Cite appropriate sections of the Building Code of BC, including residential electrical codes.
