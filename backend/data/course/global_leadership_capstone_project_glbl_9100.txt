## Global Leadership Capstone Project GLBL 9100
Global Business Leadership Course
## Course Overview
Drawing on the knowledge gained in the previous courses, you will assume the role of a Professional in Global Leadership. You will concentrate on real-world Industry challenges by engaging either in action or secondary research that will provide a professional learning experience. You will apply your Global Leadership skill set to the solution of the Industry challenge by working closely with a Faculty advisor and/or an Industry stakeholder. You will create a graduate level major report and presentation and evaluation will be based on application and integration of Global Leadership key concepts to the solution.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Acceptance into the Graduate Certificate in Global Leadership program plus completion of Term 1.
### Credits
4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Develop a formal project proposal.
- Carry out a critical literature review, refining project methodology, and data analysis strategy.
- Conduct primary or secondary research using qualitative and/or quantitative methods.
- Analyze primary or secondary research data.
- Plan, manage, and execute a major project.
- Provide a real-world industry solution using key concepts from Global Leadership.
- Communicate findings clearly to peers and/or Industry stakeholders.
- Create a graduate level academic major paper.
- Give a graduate level presentation to a panel of instructors and peers.
Effective as of Fall 2021
