## Photography 2 MDIA 1010
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course improves knowledge of handling 35mm Single Lens Reflex equipment and accessories. Topics include composition, technique and lighting. Students will carry out assignments using their own equipment, get hands-on experience in portraits, still life's, landscapes, and macro photography.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate basic understanding of the following:
- Camera controls continued from MDIA1099
- Basic lighting for photography
- Composition
- Share ideas in class and group environments
- Work in groups to solve problems
- Participate in in-class discussions
- Present ideas and projects to the class
- Explore more in-depth ideas and aspects of photography
- Discuss different filters and accessories for cameras
- Night photography and other unusual lighting techniques
- Demonstrate understanding of basic camera controls through assigned projects
- Understand the process of getting a good digital print
- Learn how to create great B&W photos
- Beginner portraiture
- Learn how to create good compositional elements in an image
- Learn how to shoot macro photography
