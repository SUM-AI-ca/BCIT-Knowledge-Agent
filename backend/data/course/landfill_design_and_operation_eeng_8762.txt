## Landfill Design and Operation EENG 8762
Environmental Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This course will examine landfill site selection, landfill capacity analysis, landfill construction and operations, environment systems overview of leachate generation and landfill gas.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in EENG 8761 † and 50% in EENG 7741 † ( † may be taken concurrently)
- EENG 8761 and EENG 7741 may be taken concurrently.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the various aspects of solid waste management and the multidisciplinary tasks associated with practical landfill management.
- Discuss regulations pertaining to solid waste management in BC.
- Illustrate landfill planning and the factors that affect operations, siting and environmental impacts including:
- Predicting population increase and solid waste generation volumes.
- Designing the appropriate facility based on predicted incoming tonnage and volume.
- Determining the size and layout of the facility.
- Locating a suitable site based on the incoming tonnage and layout requirements.
- Develop landfill design requirements, input and regulatory criteria.
- Develop landfill infrastructure, layout and engineering requirements.
- Describe operational criteria and requirements, including practical ways to achieve these.
- Outline cover requirements, engineering design and emplacement.
- Plan landfill economics and costing.
- Develop an operational plan for efficient and appropriate landfill closure.
