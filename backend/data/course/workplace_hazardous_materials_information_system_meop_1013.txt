## Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System MEOP 1013
Mobile Equipment Operator Course
## Course Overview
Covers Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS) which ensures that workers using hazardous materials have the necessary information to safely handle, store, and dispose of controlled substances.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify the purpose and components of WHMIS.
- Identify classifications of controlled products.
- Create workplace labels.
- Identify and use material safety data sheets.
