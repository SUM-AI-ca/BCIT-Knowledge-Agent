## Course Overview
English is a somewhat unique language in that many of its words come from other languages. Many words are also built in ways that enable those with a basic understanding to figure out their meaning. This course will provide you with an understanding of the English language though it's history and how the language evolved in ways that make it more easy understood. ## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain how the history of England shaped the English language.
- Explain how English words we use today were created through the influence of many other languages over thousands of years.
- Analyze English words to understand their meaning.
