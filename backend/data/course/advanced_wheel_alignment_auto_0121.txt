## Advanced Wheel Alignment AUTO 0121
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Upgrades technicians in advanced wheel alignment techniques using John Bean 3-D wheel aligner. Provides a brief review of wheel alignment angles and procedures then progresses to effects of suspension and wheel/tire modifications and issues that impact handling and tire wear.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe wheel alignment angles (both adjustable and non-adjustable);
- Describe issues of suspension and tire / wheel modifications and the impact on wheel alignment / tire wear; and,
- Describe strategies to maximize tire life and good vehicle handling.
