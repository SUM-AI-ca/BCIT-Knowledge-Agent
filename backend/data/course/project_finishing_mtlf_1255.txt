## Project Finishing MTLF 1255
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
Preparing metal surfaces for finishing requires attention to specification and adherence to quality standards. Metal products can be finished in a variety of methods, including galvanized, anodized and specialty epoxy finishes. In this course, you will learn how to apply codes and standards for finishing shop projects, including surface preparation and application of primers and paints.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1200 and MTLF 1205 and MTLF 1210 and MTLF 1215 and MTLF 1240
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify types of metal cleaning equipment and their components.
- Describe different procedures and applications required to complete painting process.
- Demonstrate safe work practices while preparing metal surfaces for painting.
- Apply WHMIS and WorkSafeBC regulations and standards for the handling and application of paint finishes
- Prepare metal surfaces for painting.
- Paint and finish shop project.
Effective as of Fall 2017
