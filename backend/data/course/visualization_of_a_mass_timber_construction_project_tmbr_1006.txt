## Visualization of a Mass Timber Construction Project TMBR 1006
Mass Timber Construction Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides visualization of a mass timber construction project and includes a narrated tour.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in TMBR 1000
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify components of mass timber construction on a construction site including structural elements, connections, building envelope, etc.
- Explain considerations related to a mass timber construction site in terms of equipment needs (crane), safety, construction waste, number of workers, etc.
