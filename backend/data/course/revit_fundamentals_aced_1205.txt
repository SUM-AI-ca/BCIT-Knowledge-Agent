## Revit Fundamentals ACED 1205
Architectural, Construction and Engineering Education Course
## Course Overview
​Students will be provided with an introduction to Revit Fundamentals, including graphic communication for architecture, essential modelling and annotation tools available in Revit, how to identify and manipulate basic building components in a building model, and the workflow of a typical construction project. Students must have a computer to download & install Autodesk Revit for this course. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in ACED 1200 and 50% in ACED 1210 and 50% in ACED 1220
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain Graphic Communication for architecture.
- Perform Essential modeling and annotation tools available in Revit.
- Identify and manipulate basic building components in a building model.
- Describe the Workflow of a typical construction project.
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2024
