## Mill Field Trip 1 WOOD 2141
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
WOOD 2141 is a three-day workshop consisting of a series of field trips to various wood processing facilities. This course supplements the classroom-based learning and provides topics for further discussion.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of Tier 1 courses.
### Credits
0.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Compare various manufacturing processes.
- Select the most appropriate manufacturing processes for a given log diet and product profile.
- Describe and evaluate mill processing equipment.
- Prepare mill flow diagrams.
- Analyze differing management styles and contrast them company to company.
