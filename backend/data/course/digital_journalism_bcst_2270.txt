## Digital Journalism BCST 2270
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This is a hands-on course which introduces students to the fundamentals of writing and posting digital news articles. A combination of lecture/lab, students will learn by publishing content to a live website. Topics explored include: structuring a compelling news article, writing headlines to capture readers' interest, turning a news release into an engaging story, posting creative, captivating photos to enhance story-telling, making your content stand out in the digital landscape and an introduction to the basic concepts of SEO – search engine optimization.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Write an engaging online news story
- Explain the components of a compelling headline
- Provide examples of his or her own digital writing published online
- Recognize basic photography concepts to enhance storytelling
- Understand the basics of digital journalism ethics and responsibility
- Utilize the concepts behind search engine optimization
