## Digital Signage and Plotters GTEC 3366
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course concentrates on learning about the different types of plotters (medium, large and grand) as well as the applications of each including: inks (dye, pigment, solvent and eco solvent); the relationship between file, application, ink, media and resolution; how the finishing affects the printing; indoor and outdoor applications; how to tile large outputs; and, how to print on two sides. Students will gain a basic understanding of digital plotter technology and produce a colour managed contract proof to large signage. Topics also include Drop On Demand (DOD) inkjet technology, how inkjet printers work and how to select equipment.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Correct file formats.
- Use Raster Image Processing (RIP) software internal and external. PC or Mac.
- Work with inks: dye, pigment, solvent, eco solvent and applications.
- Work with media applications: paper (matte, glossy, semi-gloss), vinyl, polyester, canvas, cotton, film (back and front lit), etc.
- Setup up profiles.
- Adjusting and/or substituting manufacturer's profiles.
- Create profiles.
- RGB versus CMYK.
- Describe the function of each major subcomponent of a plotter.
- Perform basic diagnostics tasks.
- Perform printer adjustments.
- Compare and describe inkjet RIP technologies.
- Describe available plotter media.
- Understand piezo and thermal inkjet imaging systems.
- Plotter calibration and proof. Single poster and continuous running take-up reels.
