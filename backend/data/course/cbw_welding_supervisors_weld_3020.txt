## CBW Welding Supervisors WELD 3020
Welding Course
## Course Overview
This class will prepare students to challenge the CWB welding supervisor's examination by studying CSA codes W47.1 and W59, welding symbols, quality control techniques, weld faults, and welder and procedure testing requirements.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the requirements for shop certification, welder testing, and procedure testing.
- Describe the requirements of "pre-qualification" and inspection of a welded product.
- Explain weld symbols, weld faults and how to identify and prevent them.
- Describe quality control practices related to welding and common inspection methods.
- Prepare and read welding procedure data sheets and use them in a welding shop.
- Write and pass the CWB welding supervisor's examination.
