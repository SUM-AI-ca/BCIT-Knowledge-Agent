## Management of Construction Enterprise 1 CMGT 8430
Construction Management - CMGT Course
## Course Overview
Participants will be introduced to the primary elements of the management of a construction enterprise, including the initial set-up of a construction company, the company profile and managing day-to-day operations. Includes developing a marketing plan and fiscal plan, including procurement, cash flow and risk analysis, and submitting and negotiating a construction bid.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in CMGT 7420 and 50% in CMGT 7320 and 50% in CMGT 7255
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Compare various business entity formats.
- Assess one's competitive capabilities in the light of industry characteristics.
- Categorize and assign the tasks involved in operating a construction enterprise.
- Identify and manage risk.
- Recognize issues to be addressed in the development of a business plan.
