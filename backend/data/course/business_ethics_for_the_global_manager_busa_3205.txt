## Business Ethics for the Global Manager BUSA 3205
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
Emphasizes the moral, ethical aspects of businesses interacting in society, to acquaint the student with matters of personal and corporate decision-making while incorporating ethical outcomes that will better serve all stockholder groups. The global economy demands a knowledge of business ethics for the 21st Century Manager.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
