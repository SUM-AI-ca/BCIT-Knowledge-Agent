## Safety Legislation BLDT 2071
Construction Operations/Supervision Course
## Course Overview
Defines the contractor's and owner's role in construction as it pertains to site safety and accident prevention.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
On successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- Understand the requirements of the W.C.B. Regulation and ensure compliance with them.
- Understand the safe layout and safe equipment for construction sites.
- Understand and implement the important issues of programs to prevent accidents, injuries and industrial disease.
- Implement safe systems of work and the safeguarding of machinery.
