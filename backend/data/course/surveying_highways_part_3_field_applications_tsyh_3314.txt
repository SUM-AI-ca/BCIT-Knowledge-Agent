## Surveying Highways Part 3: Field Applications TSYH 3314
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
The last of three courses on highway surveying computations and layout, this course allows students to obtain credit for their skill in applying theory learned in TSYH 3311 and 3312. Students will be required to demonstrate, to the satisfaction of an approved evaluator, competency in a series of surveying tasks related to vertical and horizontal survey computations and layout.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate basic field instrumentation practices and calculations involved in horizontal alignment control including the layout simple circular, compound, reverse and transition curves.
- Demonstrate basic field instrumentation practices and calculations involved in vertical alignment control including symmetrical and non-symmetrical vertical curves.
