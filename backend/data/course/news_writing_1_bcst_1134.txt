## Introduction to Broadcast Writing BCST 1134
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course trains and prepares students to write stories for a variety of platforms. This course combines basic writing theory, broadcast formats, and writing content to deadline. This course relies heavily on in-class practice, writing analysis and critique.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use industry writing standards and structure.
- Select leads which capture the essence and immediacy of a story.
- Write clearly and concisely for different platforms using proper spelling and grammar.
- Use broadcast format and terminology including Canadian Press Style.
- Identify the news value of a story based on the news determinants.
- Apply Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion principles to written content, including the use of culturally sensitive reporting practices when telling Indigenous stories.
- Write to deadlines.
Effective as of Fall 2024
