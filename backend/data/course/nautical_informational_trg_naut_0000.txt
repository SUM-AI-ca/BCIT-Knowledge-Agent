Ocean Navigation Course

For Industry Services partnership to provide training.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Have an enhanced understanding of the operational, quality, and technical reliability of existing float assets.
- Understand the basic and advanced predictive maintenance diagnostics related to vessel machinery.
- Successfully prepare for on-time regulatory and compliance requirements.
- Diagnose and detect technical issues, operational readiness, and quality structure, therefore increasing fleet utilization.
- Have an understanding of increased technical and operational acumen related to existing vessel fleet and related customary practices.
- Understanding client asset characteristics to leverage technical, operational, and quality aspects.
