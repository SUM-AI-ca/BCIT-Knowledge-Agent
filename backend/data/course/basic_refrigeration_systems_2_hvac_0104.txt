## Basic Refrigeration Systems 2 HVAC 0104
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
This is the second in a five-part program covering basic electrical theory, alternating current fundamentals, single-phase motor theory, practical exercises in interpreting elementary electrical diagrams, demonstrations on use of electrical test instruments, electrical diagrams on computer simulators and hands-on shop projects.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in HVAC 0102 and S in HVAC 0145
- HVAC 0102 and HVAC 0145 or equivalent. Safety footwear with steel toes required.
