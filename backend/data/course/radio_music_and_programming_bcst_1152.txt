## Radio Music and Programming BCST 1152
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Students learn how radio programming is developed, how music formats are designed and maintained, what is the role of news and information in radio programming, how and why radio is regulated. This is a focussed course designed to familiarize students with the primary programming elements needed for radio stations to attract audiences.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
