## AC Circuit Analysis TELX 2131
Electronics Technical Course
## Course Overview
​In this course, you will apply trigonometric functions towards vector addition and sine wave calculations. You will also use an oscilloscope to measure voltage, frequency, and relationships involved in alternating current circuits.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TELX 1154
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Solve problems involving right angle triangles and vectors
- Analyze alternating current waveforms and characteristics including associated terminology (Peak, RMS, Average, Period and Frequency)
- Measure voltage and frequency using an oscilloscope
