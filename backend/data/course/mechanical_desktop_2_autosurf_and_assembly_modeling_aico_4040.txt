## Mechanical Desktop 2: Autosurf and Assembly Modeling AICO 4040
Computer Aided Design & Engineering Course
## Course Overview
A continuation of AICO 3040 , the AutoSurf component of Mechanical Desktop allows for the creation of 3D curves and complex surface models. When combined with Designer, parametric solid models with free-form surfaces can be created. With Designer's assembly modeling tools, assemblies can be created and documented complete with exploded views, balloons and bills of materials.
### Prerequisite(s)
- AICO 0050 or familiarity with Windows 95 and AICO 3040 .
