## Advanced Study of Net Zero Energy Ready Construction and Site Supervision for Part 3 Buildings CESA 0571
Centre for Energy Systems Applications Course
## Course Overview
​This course will provide an advanced study of Net Zero Energy Ready Construction for Part 3 Buildings to help site supervisors, general contractors, tradespeople, and consultants develop their construction knowledge. Students will learn from industry experts and develop an applied understanding of the Energy Step Code, building science, airtight construction, thermal-bridge free and highly insulated assembly details, and an introduction to construction stage considerations for ventilation and mechanical systems. Students will develop a solid understanding of performance metrics, certification requirements, construction best practices processes, and quality control best practices. Through hands-on construction and lab activities students will develop applied understanding of airtight and low-TEDI enclosure construction, trades training, quality control, construction management, and commissioning activities for Net Zero Energy Ready construction. This course is offered in-person with industry expert instructors providing online lectures and in-person construction activities and demonstrations at the BCIT High Performance Building Lab.

## Learning Outcomes
​Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the BC Energy Step Code requirements and impact on construction
- Explain the BC Energy Step Code performance metrics and processes.
- Identify the unique construction and compliance requirements for projects built in compliance with the BC Energy Step Code.
- Develop applied construction knowledge of airtight assemblies, airtightness testing requirements, and construction best practices
- Apply building science concepts of air, moisture, and heat in enclosures designed for the upper step of the BC Energy Step Code.
- Interpret air barrier and thermal barrier details on drawings
- Provide feedback to project team on air barrier system design details to ensure constructability
- Install and explain technical installation details for correct installation of an air barrier in a number of assembly details
- Evaluate air barrier solutions for problematic construction details
- Develop applied construction knowledge of low-TEDI and thermal bridge-free assemblies and construction best practices
- Identify construction details and materials that will mitigate moisture and water risks with various highly insulated assemblies
- Develop a construction plan for the sequencing and installation to mitigate risks
- Explain and evaluate construction details and solutions for various split-insulated assembly details and interfaces
- ​Be aware of the responsibilities, skills, and best practices for construction management of Net Zero Energy Ready construction sites
- Explain the impact of various construction decisions on project performance and compliance.
- Recognize construction documentation needed for compliance.
- Explain requirements to prepare for and support airtightness testing activities
- Be aware of approaches to plan, schedule, and coordinate work on construction sites to ensure quality constructing and performance verification of air barrier, insulation, and thermal bridging, and mechanical systems
- Explain the best practices for managing construction quality for airtightness and enclosure thermal performance (from design to construction and testing)
- Identify best practice approaches for onsite training and engagement with trades and workers who will impact project performance and compliance
