## Lean 5S Housekeeping Strategies BTCW 0182
Business Workshops Course
## Course Overview
This one day module will give you the information you need to participate in implementing the 5S method of housekeeping for your organization. Learn what the 5 Pillars of the Visual Workplace are and why 5S is the cornerstone of Productivity Improvement activities and a good starting point of your company’s Lean Journey.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon completion of this workshop the participant will be able to:
- Understand the reasons for 5S.
- Identify the 5 Pillars of 5S.
- Have in-depth understanding of the 5 Pillars: Sort – The first Pillar; Set in Order – The second Pillar; Shine – The third Pillar; Standardize – The fourth Pillar; Sustain – The fifth Pillar.
- Review common resistance to 5S programs.
- Compare with examples of 5S successes in companies.
- Understand how to begin with the end in mind.
