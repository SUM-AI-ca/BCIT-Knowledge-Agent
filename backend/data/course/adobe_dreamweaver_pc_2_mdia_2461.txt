## Adobe Dreamweaver PC 2 MDIA 2461
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This level two course builds upon the skills learned in level 1. Students will learn some of the more advanced features of DreamWeaver including working with templates and libraries, cascading style sheets, dynamic HTML and JavaScript. The course will also cover working with forms and plug-ins.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MDIA 1461
### Credits
1.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use behaviours.
- Create templates.
- Understand CSS.
- Use video and audio with Dreamweaver MX.
- Understand how to implement previously created material in other programs (like Flash and Shockwave).
- Effectively create dynamic content in Dreamweaver MX like slideshows and timeline animation.
