## Research Project BSCH 8000
Bachelor of Health Science Course
## Course Overview
This course provides for the formulation of your research topic or question, review and critique of relevant literature, and obtaining approval from project advisor(s) before commencing research methodology. It requires you to prepare an independent proposal based on the research question. The course examines the principles of clinical trials as a method to advance medical practice and other forms of experimental design commonly found in medical research, and the importance of research in professional development. This course enables you to complete research under guidance from committee of advisors. You will submit for evaluation a research paper, poster or other works on the analysis of the results, findings, and conclusions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MATH 6150

3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, you will be able to:
- Construct a research question that addresses a current health-care issue
- Prepare a literature review
- Justify the need for further research
- Refine the research question
- Design a practical research methodology
- Develop a proposal for research approval
- Conduct the research
- Analyze the data
- Formulate conclusions based on your research question
- Complete a research paper that clearly outlines the research project question, research methodology, and conclusions
