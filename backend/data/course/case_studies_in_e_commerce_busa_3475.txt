## Case Studies in E-commerce BUSA 3475
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
Examines current case examples of Internet trade and E-commerce. The course combines research and projects and is based on team participation and case studies. Case Studies will include success stories, failures and new and evolving business. (It is recommended that students have already completed BUSA 3485 Online Business Management).
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BUSA 3455
