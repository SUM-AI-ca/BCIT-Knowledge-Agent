## Plastics SPC 2 PLAS 1071
Plastics Course
## Course Overview
Continues from PLAS 1070 . Covers frequency curves, numerical methods of describing data with required statistical calculations, measures of variability, and the development of control charts. Discusses the detection of out-of- control data and handling such data.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in PLAS 1070
