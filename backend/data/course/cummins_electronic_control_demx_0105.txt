## Cummins Electronic Control DEMX 0105
Diesel Engine Technician Course
## Course Overview
Combines electrical and electronic theory with hands-on use of diagnostic tools and troubleshooting manuals for Cummins diesel engines. Prepares the individual to repair, troubleshoot and diagnose electronic systems for Cummins engines.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TQ or apprentice in Commercial Transport, Heavy Duty or Diesel Engine Mechanic.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Comprehend Cummins electronic information system
- Demonstrate safe work practices
- Demonstrate use of troubleshooting programming and procedures
