## Dynamics MECH 2245
Mechanical Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Presents kinematics: basic equation of motion, motion diagrams, trajectories. Kinetics: Newton's Laws, inertia, rectilinear and rotational kinetics, systems of bodies. Work, energy, power and efficiency. Introduction to mechanisms.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MECH 1140
