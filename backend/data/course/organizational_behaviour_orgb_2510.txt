## Organizational Behaviour ORGB 2510
Organizational Behaviour Course
## Course Overview
Explores the importance of harmonious relationships and the skills and methods for achieving them. Broadcasting involves unusually close interaction among its participants who work together to provide information, entertainment and revenues.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Apply group behaviour concepts to achieve team outcomes.
- Illustrate the roles of perception, personality, attitudes, and values.
- Apply motivation theories to organizational situations.
- Differentiate leadership styles and theories.
- Explain the impact of power and politics in the workplace.
- Demonstrate conflict management techniques.
- Analyze various communication techniques and tools.
- Integrate appropriate interpersonal and organizational concepts to facilitate organizational change.
- Apply appropriate interpersonal and organizational concepts to understand and determine effective behaviour in a variety of organizational situations.
