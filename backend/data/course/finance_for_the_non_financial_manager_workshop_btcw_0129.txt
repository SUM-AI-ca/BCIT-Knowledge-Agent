## Finance for the Non Financial Manager Workshop BTCW 0129
Business Workshops Course
## Course Overview
Money matters can be intimidating for even the smartest people. However, having a solid understanding of basic financial terms and methods is crucial to your career. When terms like ROI, EBIT, GAAP, and extrapolation join the conversation, you'll want to know what people are talking about, and you'll want to be able to participate in the discussion. The Finance for the Non- Financial Manager workshop will give participants a solid foundation in finance. We'll cover commonly used terms including financial statements, budgets, forecasting, purchasing decisions, and financial legislation.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of the workshops participants should be able to;
- Identify financial terminology.
- Understand financial statements.
- Identify how to analyze financial statements.
- Understand budgets and to make budgeting easy.
- Understand how to manage the budget.
- Understand advanced forecasting techniques.
- Identify how to make smart purchasing decisions.
- Identify the legal aspects of finances.
