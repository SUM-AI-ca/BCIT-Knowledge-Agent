## Mammography Clinical Practicum MIMG 6400
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
Provides the clinical experience necessary of the CAMRT Certificate in Breast Imaging and the BCIT Breast Imaging Certificate. May be accessed after completion of all required Breast Imaging Certificate courses. Requires completion of a Record of Clinical Experience under the supervision of a qualified assessor at a pre-arranged clinical site during the three-month (or equivalent) practicum. BCIT arranges the clinical practicum on an individual basis. BCIT provides a Compressed Time Frame (CTF) Clinical Practicum for practitioners.

## Learning Outcomes
On completion of the clinical experience, you will be able to:
- Communicate with the patient in a knowledgeable and compassionate manner during the examination.
- Provide patient care.
- Care for the patient using the necessary infection control methods and safety measures.
- Clinically assess the patient, take a complete history and document all appropriate information.
- Know and be able to utilize all the necessary components of the dedicated mammographic unit, film/screen combinations, and mammography processing system.
- Select appropriate exposure parameters and understand the factors affecting dose technically and anatomically.
- Perform all basic and special mammographic positions, including positioning of the augmented breast and breast specimens.
- Identify and perform optimal positioning using creative solutions to demonstrate all breast anatomy.
- Describe interventional procedures.
- Identify radiographically: breast anatomy, parenchymal patterns, asymmetry and pathology.
- Determine and apply solutions for optimal breast imaging of the difficult patient.
- Perform quality control test procedures, determine the corrective action to be taken by the technologist and understand the importance of a preventative maintenance program.
- Provide patient care using a knowledge of legal, ethical and confidential guidelines and/or legislation.
- Demonstrate the role of the technologist as a member of a multi-disciplinary approach to breast health promotion, diagnosis, and care.
- Apply the practical application of radiation safety for the patient and for personnel.
