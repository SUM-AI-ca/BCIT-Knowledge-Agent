## Training Skills for Aviation AIRC 4410
Airport/Airline Core Course
## Course Overview
This course will focus on the principles of the “systematic approach to training” as it applies to aviation technical information. Topics will include development of a lesson plan, delivery techniques, use of training aids and learning evaluation. Evaluations include lesson plan, lesson delivery and an examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop a lesson plan in support of performance/learning outcomes and includes appropriate:
- learning techniques;
- learning materials to enhance the learning process;
- practical (informal) assessment/evaluation methods;
- feedback to support performance;
- effective questioning techniques to check understanding.
- Facilitate the lesson.
Effective as of Fall 2022
