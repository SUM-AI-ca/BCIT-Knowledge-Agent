## Customized Industry Corporate Training CLCR 0100
Call Centre Course
## Course Overview
Monthly workshops related to industry-driven training and development needs. Specific workshop details provided in the 'Notes' section below.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the participant will be aware of:
- Communication style and the impact style has on communication.
- Phrases, body language and approaches that can influence communication.
- Alternatives to handling objections and resolve possible communication conflicts.
- Evaluating telephone strengths, customer service strengths, and areas of improvement.
- Being an effective team member.
- Problem-solving and decision-making methods.
- Prevention and reduction of stress in the work environment.
- Customer needs, problem solving and creative thinking techniques to find solutions.
