## Technical Communication 1 for SRM COMM 1148
Communication Course
## Course Overview
This course familiarizes the technical writer with some basic formats and conventions of documents used in the field of sustainable resource management. It also includes a review of basic English grammar and sentence structure. Emphasis is placed on writing with clarity, directness, and brevity. NOTE: COMM 1103 or COMM 1106 may be taken instead of COMM 1148 .
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Communicate in clear, correct, concise sentences.
- Make professional decisions regarding communication content, tone, and delivery.
- Compose routine correspondence.
- Critically read/observe and summarize.
- Deliver and follow clear and correct technical instruction.
- Write acceptable incident or accident reports.
- Write an effective set of Minutes for a meeting.
