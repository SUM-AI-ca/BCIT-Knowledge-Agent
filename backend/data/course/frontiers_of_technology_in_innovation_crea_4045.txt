## Frontiers of Technology in Innovation CREA 4045
Creative Industries Course
## Course Overview
​This course explores the cutting-edge technologies and emerging trends that redefine the creative industries. With a focus on the future of work and society, students will gain insights into transformational innovations across rapidly evolving frontiers, equipping them with the foresight to lead in a tech-driven world. Rather than delving into specific tools or current trends, this course encourages design thinking, critical thinking, adaptability, and a deep curiosity about what’s next​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze emerging technological trends and their potential impacts on the future of creative industries and beyond.
- Evaluate the broader societal, cultural, and ethical implications of advancements.
- Cultivate a forward-thinking mindset to anticipate and adapt to new technologies and paradigms.
- Develop skills in speculative thinking to envision how evolving technologies might shape the future of work, society, and culture.
- Engage in informed discussions about technological trends and their intersections with creativity, business, and innovation.​
