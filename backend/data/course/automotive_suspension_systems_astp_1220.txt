## Automotive Suspension Systems ASTP 1220
Automotive Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces you to the fundamental skills required to diagnose, service, and repair various types of suspension systems and the replacement of component pieces.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe frames.
- Describe front and rear suspension systems.
- Describe suspension service precautions.
- Service suspension systems.
