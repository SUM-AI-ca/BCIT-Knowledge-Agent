## Automotive Service Technician 3 CQ Refresher AUTO 0093
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Refreshes theoretical knowledge of the trade in preparation for the Automotive Service Technician 3 Certificate of Qualification examination. Topics covered will include: electrical and electronic systems, fuel delivery systems, electronic ignition, engine management systems, and emission control systems. Contact the SkilledTradesBC (tel. 778-328-8700 or toll free within BC 1-866-660-6011) to determine eligibility and to schedule an examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Working experience or prior training on electrical/electronic systems, fuel delivery systems, electronic ignition, engine management systems and emission control systems.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be:
- Prepared to write the Certificate of Qualification examination for Automotive Service Technician 3;
- Able to perform practical tasks with a better understanding of component operation; and
- Familiar with trade exam question formats
