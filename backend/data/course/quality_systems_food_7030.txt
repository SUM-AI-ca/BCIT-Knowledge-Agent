## Quality Systems FOOD 7030
Food Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course examines TQM, ISO 9000, ISO 14000, ISO 22000, GFSI, ASQ, SQF and BRC requirements as quality systems. It also covers the implementation of these systems and the effect on various units within the company.
### Prerequisite(s)
- FOOD 6255 and OPMT 6343
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Explain the standards and elements of various quality systems.
- Apply the appropriate quality system based on customer demand.
- Write Standard Operating Procedures to address the applicable elements.
- Develop interactions with business units to actually use / work within the standards of the quality system chosen.
- Audit the appropriate food safety system chosen to its applicable standards.
