## Trailer HMTF 1010
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the basic principles of trailer landing gear, accessories, trailer bodies, and refrigeration and heating systems and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on hydraulic systems and components. Prior to the refrigeration component of this course, students must obtain an Ozone Depleting Substances Handling Certificate from the Heating, Refrigerating and Air Conditioning Institute.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
4.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Service landing gear and trailer accessories
- Service and repair coupling systems
- Service, diagnose, and repair trailer body components
- Service, diagnose, and repair trailer heating and refrigeration systems
