## Automotive Service Technician 2 CQ Refresher AUTO 0092
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Refreshes theoretical knowledge of the trade in preparation for the Automotive Service Technician 2 Certificate of Qualification examination. Topics covered will include: advanced electrical systems, heating/ventilation/air conditioning systems, engines and support systems, and hybrid vehicle safety. Contact the SkilledTradesBC (tel. 778-328-8700 or toll free within BC 1-866-660-6011) to determine eligibility and to schedule an examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Working experience or prior training on advanced electrical systems, heating/ventilation/air conditioning systems, engine and support systems, and hybrid vehicle safety

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be:
- Prepared to write the Certificate of Qualification examination for Automotive Service Technician 2;
- Able to perform practical tasks with a better understanding of component operation; and
- Familiar with trade exam question formats.
