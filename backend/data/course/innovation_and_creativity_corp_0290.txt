## Innovation and Creativity CORP 0290
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
​The critical element for developing innovation in organizations is the "people" side of the equation. It is empowered, creative people that develop a culture of innovation. This workshop will introduce some of the tools, techniques, and methods that engender creativity and innovation. This introduction to the basics of creativity and innovation in the workplace is intended to capture the imagination of participants and encourage seeing the possibilities to enhance organizational results. Innovation requires implementation and, awareness of change leadership and change management tools are included in this workshop.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the difference between innovation and creativity in a workplace setting
- Develop greater confidence in seeing themselves and others as creative
- Acquire information on some tools and techniques to both generate and implement fresh ideas to enhance results
- Practice creative thinking
- Understand modern change management and change leadership theories as they relate to implementing innovation
