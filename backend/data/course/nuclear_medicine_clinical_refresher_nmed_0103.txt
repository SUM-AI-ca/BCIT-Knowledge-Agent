## Nuclear Medicine Clinical Refresher NMED 0103
Nuclear Medicine Course
## Course Overview
This 12 week clinical refresher is designed for graduates of an accredited Nuclear Medicine Technology Program who have not worked in the field within the past 5 years upgrade their clinical skills. Entry into this clinical refresher is by permission of the Program Head and a clinical site (or sites) affiliated with the BCIT program. Expected outcomes are based on the CAMRT clinical competency profile for Nuclear Medicine Technologists.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Graduation from an accredited Nuclear Medicine Technology Program. Applicants will be assessed individually and didactic upgrading may be required. Worker's Compensation and liability coverage will be provided for individuals registered in this BCIT clinical course. Prior to acceptance to a clinical area, applicants will be required to provide evidence of current CPR (level C) certification which must be valid throughout the duration of the clinical refresher. A criminal record search may be required at the applicant's expense. Immunizations must be up-to-date (including the COVID-19 and influenza vaccines); evidence to be supplied by the applicant. Upon acceptance into this course, students must have a valid N95 respirator fit-test before entering a clinical setting. The fit test must be performed in accordance with CSA Standard CAN/CSA-Z94.4.02 and students are responsible for any N95 fit test fees.
