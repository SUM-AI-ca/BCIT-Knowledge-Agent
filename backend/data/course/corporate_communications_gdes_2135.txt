## Corporate Communications GDES 2135
Graphic Design Course
## Course Overview
A strong brand builds equity by resonating with its target audience, and corporate communications are essential in conveying a company's strategy, structure, and vision across multiple platforms. This course explores how business strategies form the foundation for effective design strategies in both internal and external corporate communications. Students will develop and enhance a cohesive brand message by building a visual language and applying it to various corporate identity applications, such as logo mock-ups, corporate typography, colour schemes, and other visual assets. Through hands-on projects, students will learn to create consistent and impactful communication materials that support brand equity and organizational goals.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Research and analyze communication strategies to identify factors that contribute to their success or failure.
- Build and refine a visual brand strategy that consistently conveys a client-focused message aligned with the company’s values, mission, and communication objectives.
- Conduct a design audit to identify communication, design, and budget needs, and draft a corporate communications plan based on the findings.
- Apply a brand identity to support specific communication goals, such as increasing public awareness, retaining employees, and driving company growth
- Demonstrate how different companies use a variety of communication tools and platforms to convey distinct messages.
- Explain the differences in messaging needs between internal and external communications within a company.
- Develop and expand the corporate visual language by establishing visual standards for applications, including annual reports, templates, and marketing materials.
