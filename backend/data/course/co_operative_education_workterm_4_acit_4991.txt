## Co-operative Education Workterm 4 ACIT 4991
Computer Information Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course is a co-operative education work placement (co-op work term). Work placements allow students to apply their classroom knowledge and develop new skills in a professional setting. Students are paid for their work experience and are evaluated by the employer and BCIT. Work placements allow students to network within their industry, which helps in finding employment after graduation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 75% GPA
### Credits
16.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Integrate and apply skills and knowledge gained from BCIT in a workplace environment
- Apply skills acquired in the Co-op position
- Work productively as a member of a team
- Write a Co-op Work Term Report
- Acquire a better understanding of the career options in their field of study
- Evaluate personal interests and career goals
- Apply job search strategies in this industry​
