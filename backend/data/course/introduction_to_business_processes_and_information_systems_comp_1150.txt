## Introduction to Business Processes and Information Systems COMP 1150
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed to provide students with an understanding of the major business processes in a typical business organization, and the impact on the efficiency and effectiveness of these processes through the deployment of information systems. Hands on experience with SAP software is provided. The course also introduces students to some of the principles of electronic commerce. The topics of infrastructure, security, marketing, payment systems and supply chains, on the internet are introduced.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the functional areas, the principle activities, and the primary information flows within a business environment.
- Define common business processes such as sales and distribution, procurement and materials management, production planning, financial accounting and human resource management.
- Describe business processeses using a modeling technique.
- Discuss ways in which information technologies can be used to enhance business processes.
- Identify the enabling technological components for enterprise systems and e-commerce/e-business systems.
- Describe the functional modules and components of an e-commerce/e-business system.
- Identify the security and other issues of building an e-commerce/e-business system.
