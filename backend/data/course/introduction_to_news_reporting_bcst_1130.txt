## Introduction to News Reporting BCST 1130
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the student to the basic principles of news gathering and production. The course will give students a grounding in the systems, issues, and practices of news reporting and will prepare them for the more detailed and involved aspects of the broadcast and online journalism industry.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify the news value of stories based on the news determinants.
- Describe and discuss weekly current events.
- Identify the principles of general assignment reporting, including news conferences and municipal council.
- Use basic skills to effectively interview news makers.
- Assess ethical issues in broadcasting.
- Apply basic newsgathering skills including research and notetaking.
- Apply Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion principles to news coverage.
- Use storytelling techniques to create focused news stories.
- Develop local angles from national and international news stories.
- Demonstrate knowledge of safe, trauma-informed reporting practices.
Effective as of Fall 2024
