## Leadership and Team Management in Creative Industries CREA 7110
Creative Industries Course
## Course Overview
​The course focuses on the role of team managers and leaders in creative organizations. Comprehensive development of personal leadership potential is provided through guided reflection, networking, executive coaching, interaction with peers, learning from seminars, guest speakers, academic literature, and case studies, all based on current leadership theories. You will examine the role of leadership and organizational culture in developing, nurturing, and managing effective teams in a creative industry setting. Among the topics addressed are theories of leadership, values, ethics, vision, conviction, and courage; leadership principles and styles; building relationships with stakeholders and shareholders; team building, group dynamics, and creative collaboration; delegation; conflict resolution; stress and change management; communication; character and power; and leadership techniques, tools, and strategies.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply leadership theory within the Creative Industries
- Discuss theories of motivation and personality within the Creative workplace
- Identify effective and ineffective leadership styles and behaviours in Creative Industries
- Apply evidence-based principles of influence and change management in a creative industry setting
- Demonstrate competence in applying ethical considerations to leadership and team management in a creative industry context
- Apply evidence-based principles of team development and conflict management in a creative industries context
- Practice communicating as a leader in Creative Industries to drive team performance
- Demonstrate how and when to utilize feedback and coaching in team development in the creative industries
- Employ creativity and innovation to drive performance in a creative team
- Practice aligning organizational culture with team behaviour
- Demonstrate strategic networking in a creative industry context to drive business results
- Apply an agile approach to leadership and team cohesion within a hybrid or global workforce
- Practice evidence-based principles of decision making in a creative industry context ​
