## Administering Lotus Notes COMP 2677
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the concerpts and techniques in planning and administering Lotus Notes database and applications. Designed for administrators who need to have a thorough understanding of how to set up a Lotus Notes database, and to maintain the usage and evolution of the database.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 1675
- COMP 1675
