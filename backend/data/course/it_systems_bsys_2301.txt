## IT Systems BSYS 2301
Business Systems Course
## Course Overview
Information Technology (IT) is one of the largest investment categories for many firms. Adopting new IT infrastructure can result in substantial competitive advantages or catastrophic failures with the potential to bankrupt a company. The course takes a practical approach to understanding how firms of various sizes from a cross-section of industries build and manage IT systems to best deliver economic value including e-Commerce and contemporary solutions. The course addresses these IT challenges by defining modern day networks and e-Business solutions and finally by teaching students how to value their investment in information technology.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Configure basic networks, topologies and associated hardware.
- Apply e-business solutions including security.
- Value the productivity gains on investment.
- Understand the application of Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Systems within a Supply Chain Management ,(SCM) and E-Commerce environment.
- Make investment decisions based on Business Intelligence Tools.
