## Talent Acquisition and Onboarding HRMG 3350
Human Resource Management Course
## Course Overview
​This course builds upon foundational Human Resources principles and provides students with the knowledge and practical skills for managing the end-to-end recruitment and onboarding process. Students will learn to align hiring strategies with business needs, use relevant sourcing techniques, conduct effective interviews, and ensure a positive candidate experience. This course also covers legal considerations, diversity/equity/inclusion (DEI) factors in creating a diverse workforce, modern recruitment tools, and the critical role of talent acquisition professionals and hiring managers in onboarding. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain full-cycle recruitment and onboarding processes to create a high-Performance workforce.
- Explain why hiring goals must align with business needs, managers’ requirements, and other considerations such as budget constraints and organizational culture.
- Analyze how a talent brand communicates an employee value proposition to attract talent.
- Explain the difference between passive and active sourcing strategies for attracting talent.
- Apply various interview techniques to evaluate the candidate’s knowledge, skills, attributes and other competencies and their overall fit to the organization.
- Develop a legally defensible recruitment and selection plan (including accommodation as required), keeping the candidate experience in mind throughout the various stages of the recruitment process (talent attraction, screening, shortlisting, interview preparation, stakeholder management, job offer presentation, and onboarding).
- Explain the importance of creating legally compliant and effective job descriptions based on a thorough job analysis.
- Develop talent acquisition activity metrics to align the target hiring timeline with activity inputs.
- Describe effective Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), leveraging AI, and using social media and other recruitment tools in talent acquisition.
- Explain the importance of background checks, differences between official and unofficial reference checks and how to avoid discriminatory practices.
- Describe the critical elements of an onboarding plan and the importance of new employee orientation.
- Create an onboarding plan that includes activities for socializing the new hire to the organization’s culture.
- Explain the best diversity, equity and inclusion practices for the recruitment and onboarding process for creating a diverse workforce. ​
