## Calculus: 1C MATH 2043
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
Covers differentiation and integration of trigonometric, logarithmic and exponential functions. Same text as MATH 2041 and 2042.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MATH 2042 † ( † may be taken concurrently)
- MATH 2042 or approved equivalent.
