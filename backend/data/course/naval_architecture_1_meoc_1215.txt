## Naval Architecture 1 MEOC 1215
Marine Engineer Officer Cadet Course
## Course Overview
Students will learn the effect of hydrostatics related to flotation and ship stability, hull coefficients, and centre of pressure. They will also study the effect of a change in the ship’s displacement on its centre of gravity, load diagram and centre of pressure, Archimedes’ principle and laws of flotation, coefficient of form, calculation of area and volume for ship structure using Simpson’s rules.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Calculate the positions of vertical centre of mass and longitudinal centre of mass of ships given various masses, and their locations on board ship.
- Calculate the change in position of a ship’s centre of mass due to a change in a ship’s displacement.
- Calculate the pressure exerted on an immersed plane by a liquid head.
- Calculate the centre of pressure and load on bulkheads, using the centre of pressure equations.
- Draw the load diagram of an immersed plane under a head of liquid.
- Calculate the density and relative density of floating bodies based on equations derived from Archimedes’ principle and laws of flotation.
- Calculate the form coefficients of vessels based on their dimensions.
- Find the wetted surface area of similar geometric shapes based on the ratio of their similar dimensions.
- Calculate the area and volume of a ship’s structure based on ratios of similar dimensions.
- Explain the effect of density on draft and displacement.
- Solve numerical problems based on ship’s deadweight, load lines, TPC, sinkage, FWA and DWA.
- Calculate areas and volumes of irregular shapes using Simpson’s 1st and 2nd rules.
