## Zero Energy Building Enclosures: Assemblies Lab (for Residential Buildings) CESA 1121
Centre for Energy Systems Applications Course
## Course Overview
This course has been replaced by XZEB 1130 Airtightness and Low-TEDI Enclosures Lab for Zero Energy/Emissions and Passive House Buildings. This lab course will allow students to practice construction of various high performance assembly details to meet the requirements of the Upper Steps of the BC Energy Step Code and the Passive House standard. Students have already developed a foundational understanding of high performance assemblies through pre-requisite courses. With eight hours in the BCIT High Performance Lab, students will work through a number of hands-on lab ZEB: Assemblies Labstations to build, test, or evaluate various assemblies using a range of approaches and materials. Lab activities will cover various approaches and details for foundations, above and below grade walls, roofs, interfaces, windows, balconies, penetrations, and more. An introduction to working with low-embodied carbon insulation materials will be provided. Builders, tradespeople, insulators, designers, building officials, and energy advisors would benefit from this course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Prior learning on airtightness and air barriers systems is required. Students must be registered for or have already completed any one of the following: CESA 1120 , CESA 1150 , CESA 1155 , CESA 1160 , CESA 1500 , CESA 1501 , CESA 1505 .

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Compare material properties (insulation, fasteners, thermal bridging components, etc.) which are part of the thermal barrier.
- Identify construction details that will help protect the thermal barrier over the life of the building.
- Identify construction details and materials to mitigate moisture and water risks with various highly insulated assemblies.
- Interpret details on drawings.
- Develop a construction plan for the sequencing and installation of a thermal barrier system.
- Demonstrate the technical skills to execute correct installation of various split-insulated assembly types and complete exterior finishing details.
- Be familiar with thermal bridging properties of materials and their potential role in addressing problematic construction details.
