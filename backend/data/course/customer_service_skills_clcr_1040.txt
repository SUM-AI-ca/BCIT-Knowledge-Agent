## Customer Service Skills CLCR 1040
Call Centre Course
## Course Overview
A two-day course highlighting: how to elicit customer needs; using problem solving and creative thinking techniques to find solutions; how to handle difficult customers; and how to evaluate your own customer service strengths to find solutions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
