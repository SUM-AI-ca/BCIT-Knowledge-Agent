## Work Practicum AUTO 1511
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
​This work practicum allows students the opportunity to gain practical experience at a sponsoring automotive repair facility. This real life, automotive-related work experience reinforces materials presented in the program and enhances skills and abilities through interaction with experienced tradespeople. This 2 week work practicum may not be consecutive weeks. The time frame of the work practicum will be determined by the instructor through consultation with industry partners.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply skills and knowledge gained from the Automotive Technician Program in a workplace environment.
- Work productively as a member of a team.
- Acquire a better understanding of the career options in their field of study.
- Evaluate personal interests and career goals.
Effective as of Fall 2018
