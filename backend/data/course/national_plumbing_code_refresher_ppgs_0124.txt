## National Plumbing Code Refresher PPGS 0124
Piping, Plumbing, Gas Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed to review the National Plumbing Code for a better understanding of design and installation of plumbing systems in BC. the course is designed for plumbers, engineers and mechanical designers.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify and describe Plumbing Code terminology and definitions.
- Identify and describe drainage and venting systems.
- Identify and determine venting components, rules and regulations.
- Identify and determine sanitary components, rules and regulations.
- Identify and determine storm and combined drainage system components, rules and regulations.
- Identify and determine wet venting rules, regulations and sizing requirements.
- Identify and determine circuit venting rules, regulations and sizing requirements.
- Identify and size sanitary, storm and combined systems.
- Identify and describe potable water systems.
- Identify and size potable water piping systems to code requirements.
