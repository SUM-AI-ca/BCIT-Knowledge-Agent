## Learning Outcomes
Upon completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Explain why protection of privacy and cyber security are important.
- Identify personal information and personal identifying information.
- Explain the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FIPPA).
- Explain the Collection, Transmission, Processing and Storage of personal information in a cloud-based environment.
- Explain Use and Disclosure of personal information.
- Identify security threats.
- Use strategies to improve the security of your personal data.
- Understand the implications of a data breach.
