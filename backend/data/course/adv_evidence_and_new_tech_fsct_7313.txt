## Adv. Evidence & New Tech FSCT 7313
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
This course expands upon the theories and applications of evidence handling covered in Crime Scene Investigation (Level II) with additional, specialized topics related to forensic biology, entomology and anthropology. This course also offers an opportunity for students to learn about and assess the latest technologies in the ever-changing field of crime scene investigation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in FSCT 7312
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Plan proper evidence seizure techniques of insect evidence for the forensic entomologist.
- Plan proper evidence seizure techniques of skeletal/human remain evidence for the forensic anthropologist or pathologist.
- Plan proper evidence seizure techniques of DNA evidence for the forensic biologist
- Evaluate new technologies related to evidence and crime scene analysis.
