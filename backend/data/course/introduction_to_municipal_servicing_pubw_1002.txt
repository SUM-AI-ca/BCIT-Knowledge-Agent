## Introduction to Municipal Servicing PUBW 1002
Public Works Course
## Course Overview
An introduction to municipal servicing: topics include construction methods, quality control process, materials and equipment used in the construction and operation of municipal services. Students will be exposed to decision making process used in construction based on a good understanding of applicable regulations and field conditions often encountered during construction.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
