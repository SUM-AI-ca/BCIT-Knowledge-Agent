## AVID DVD Authoring and Post-Production BCST 2082
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course will teach the student how to prepare a professional DVD including multiple-menu layouts including scene selections, audio and subtitle choices, and how integration with Avid Xpress Studio allows transfer between applications.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BCST 1180
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Prepare a professional DVD.
- Include multiple-menu layouts, scene selections, audio and subtitle choices.
- Integrate with Avid Xpress Studio and transfer between applications.
