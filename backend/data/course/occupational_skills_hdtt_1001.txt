## Occupational Skills HDTT 1001
Heavy Duty Truck Technology Course
## Course Overview
​This course has a number of learning outcomes in which you will apply and demonstrate safe work practices and use tools and shop equipment. You will also use fasteners and fittings, lift and support loads, operate equipment, and service wire rope, bearings and seals. In this occupational skills course you will identify lubricants, and use welding equipment, electronic media and record keeping practices.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
8.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use Safe Work Practices
- Use Hand Tools, Power Tools and Shop Equipment
- Use Fasteners and Fittings
- Lift and Support Loads
- Operate Equipment
- Use Shop Resources and Record Keeping Practices
- Service Winch Wire Rope
- Identify Lubricants
- Service Bearings and Seals
- Use Electronic Media
- Use Cutting and Welding Equipment
- Describe Diagnostic Procedures
- Use Environmental Practices
- Apply Occupational Health and Safety.
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2020
