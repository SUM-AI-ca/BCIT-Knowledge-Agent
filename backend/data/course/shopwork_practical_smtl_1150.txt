## Shopwork Practical SMTL 1150
Sheet Metal Course
## Course Overview
This course gives the student the opportunity to put into practice all the theory learned in previous courses. Students will demonstrate their skills through a number of practical projects. Students will be given flexibility in terms of how they complete these projects as long as they meet the basic criteria for each one.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in SMTL 1141 and 70% in SMTL 1143 and 70% in SMTL 1144 and 70% in SMTL 1149
### Credits
10.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use safe work practices.
- Use and maintain hand tools.
- Safely use portable power tools.
- Safely use shop equipment.
- Fabricate basic cutout projects as per specifications.
- Fabricate various types of seams and edges as per specifications.
- Fabricate various types of round tees as per specifications.
- Fabricate various types of round elbows as per specifications.
- Fabricate various types of round offsets as per specifications.
- Fabricate inside and outside gutter miters as per specifications.
- Fabricate round on center tapers as per specifications.
- Fabricate two-piece funnel as per specifications.
- Fabricate three-piece funnel as per specifications.
- Fabricate tapered bucket as per specifications.
- Fabricate flat roof jack as per specifications.
- Fabricate pitched roof jack as per specifications.
- Fabricate storm collars as per specifications.
- Fabricate china caps as per specifications.
- Fabricate various types of square to rounds as per specifications.
- Fabricate various types of oblique tapers as per specifications.
- Fabricate duct fittings and straight duct as per specifications.
- Solder any projects required as per specifications.
