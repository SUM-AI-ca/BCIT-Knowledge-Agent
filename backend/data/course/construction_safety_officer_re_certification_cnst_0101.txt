## Construction Safety Officer Re-Certification CNST 0101
Construction Course
## Course Overview
For Construction Safety Officers who require renewal of their certificate. This is the open book final exam given to our CNST 1100 students. The minimum passing grade is 75% for a BCIT Statement of Completion to be issued.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Proof of previous certification as a CSO, a letter from employers to prove a minimum of twelve months combined experience as a CSO within the previous three years and six months of that time must have been accumulated over the previous eighteen calendar months.
