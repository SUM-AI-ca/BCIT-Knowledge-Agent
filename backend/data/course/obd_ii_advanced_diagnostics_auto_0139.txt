## OBD II Advanced Diagnostics AUTO 0139
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Updates technicians with a practical approach to diagnose and repair OBD II systems. Covers: AirCare III testing procedures; OBD II enhanced features including drive cycles and readiness monitors; and, diagnostic strategies using generic scan tools. Provides an opportunity for practical experience in scan tool interfacing, data retrieval and interpretation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Automotive Service Technician TQ or Apprentice.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the features of enhanced OBD II.
- Describe component drive cycle and readiness monitors.
- Describe the strategies necessary for diagnosing and repairing OBD II equipped vehicles.
- Describe the OBD II test modes.
- Perform technical interpretation of PID data and failure criteria.
