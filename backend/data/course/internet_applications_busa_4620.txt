## Internet Applications BUSA 4620
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
Strengthens the understanding of the role of the Internet in a business setting. Continues the development of web building skills and looks at the benefits and limitations of the Internet and its supporting software. A major component of the course is the planning and development of a small business web site.
### Prerequisite(s)
- BSYS 3100
### Credits
2.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply basic HTML
- Apply basic CSS
- Develop a basic website for small business
- Practice developing a website with a website builder tool
- Use the appropriate technology terms in a conversation with a web developer
