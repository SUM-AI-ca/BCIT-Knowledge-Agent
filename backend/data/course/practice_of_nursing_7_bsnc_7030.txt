## Practice of Nursing 7 BSNC 7030
Nursing Course
## Course Overview
​​This course builds on Practice of Nursing 6, focusing on adult and older adult clients experiencing complex and chronic health challenges through the lens of continuum of care. Learners' clinical reasoning and clinical judgment increases in depth, and with minimal guidance, demonstrated through increasingly accurate and refined health assessments, and identification and prioritization of multiple client needs. Through development and strengthening of effective time management skills, including organization, prioritization, and delegation, learners develop therapeutic relationships with individuals and families, and communicate and collaborate effectively with the health care team to ensure safe, appropriate and timely client care. Students continue to evolve their professional identity in relation to becoming a registered nurse and exhibit autonomous scope of practice by self-assessing strengths and needs to take appropriate actions to meet course competencies. This course includes an integrated and applied learning experience in a simulated/practice environment. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
Advocate
- Examines and challenges the influence of power within organizational, social, economic and political contexts that influence health and well-being within the context of nurses’ role in the delivery of care
- Advocates for policies and programs that effectively address the determinants of health
- Promotes acceptance of diversity and supports client empowerment to reduce stigma
Collaborator
- Works to prevent and resolve conflict, and negotiate solutions in interactions with clients and the healthcare team
Decision Maker
- Effectively manages complex clinical scenarios and time sensitive situations.
- Monitors the effectiveness of clients’ outcomes to inform future care planning within all contexts and situations
- Articulates clinical reasoning process in support of nursing practice
Global Citizen
- Practices culturally safe and equitable nursing care in diverse contexts and situations
- Recognizes and addresses factors that influence the socio-political, economic and historical factors that lead to inequalities in healthcare
- Integrates knowledge of emerging community and global health issues, population health issues and related research into nursing practice
Leader
- Collaborates with and acts as a resource for members of the healthcare team to meet client needs
- Promotes teamwork and garners commitment and participation of others that will optimize nursing practice and client care
Lifelong Learner
- Collaborates with the interprofessional team to implement evidence based practice
Communication
- Communicates effectively with clients and healthcare team members to support safe, high quality, client-centred healthcare in diverse situations and contexts
Professional
- Displays initiative, confidence and self-awareness in nursing practice
- Provides knowledge translation to support professional development of other learners and healthcare team members
- Independently assesses own practice and undertakes activities to improve practice and meet identified learning goals
