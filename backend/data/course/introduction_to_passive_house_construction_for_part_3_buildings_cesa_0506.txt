## Introduction to Passive House Construction for Part 3 buildings CESA 0506
Centre for Energy Systems Applications Course
## Course Overview
This course will provide knowledge to help site supervisors, general contractors, tradespeople, and designers or consultants develop a working understanding of Passive House construction. Students will learn from industry experts and develop an applied understanding of Passive House buildings, including the Passive House Standard requirements, building science, airtight design and construction, thermal-bridge free and highly insulated assembly details. The course includes an introduction to ventilation and mechanical systems for Passive House buildings. Students will develop a solid understanding of the Passive House standard requirements, processes and quality control specific to their work. Through hands-on construction or lab activities students will develop applied understanding of airtight and low-TEDI enclosure construction, trades training, quality control, construction management, and commissioning requirements for Passive House construction. This course is offered in a variety of formats tailored to the needs of individual clients or project teams. Industry expert instructors provide a combination of in-person or online lectures with in-person construction activities and demonstrations at the BCIT High Performance Building Lab. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the Passive House Standard performance metrics and requirements.
- Identify the unique construction and compliance requirements and processes for projects built in compliance with the Passive House Standard.
- Apply building science concepts of air, moisture, and heat in enclosures designed for the Passive House Standard.
- Describe low-TEDI enclosure solutions and Describe airtightness solutions and testing requirements for Passive House construction.
- Considerations for construction (e.g., mitigating thermal bridging, cost, constructability, etc.)
- Describe airtightness solutions and testing requirements for Passive House construction.
- Be aware of the design and construction considerations for mechanical systems in Passive House buildings.
