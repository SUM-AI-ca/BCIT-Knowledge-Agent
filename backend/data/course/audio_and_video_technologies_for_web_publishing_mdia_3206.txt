## Audio and Video Technologies for Web Publishing MDIA 3206
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
With over 2 hours of video notes, spread between 21 movies, this course examines every aspect of audio and video for use on the web. You will explore how to resample and edit your audio and video clips, apply audio filters, use masks and poster images with your movies, and combine multiple audio and/or video tracks into a single presentation. We will also look at different options for mounting your content on a website for delivery.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MDIA 1205 and MDIA 2205
### Credits
2.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Resample audio and video clips.
- Edit audio and video clips.
- Apply audio filters to samples.
- Combine multiple audio and/or video tracks.
- Apply compression codecs to audio and video samples.
- Use masks and poster frames with videos.
- Mount audio and video content on a website.
