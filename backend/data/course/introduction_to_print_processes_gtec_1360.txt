## Introduction to Print Processes GTEC 1360
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course provides a solid review of Web Offset Printing, Flexographic Printing and Silkscreen Printing. It will be a combination of in-class theory, take home assignments, investigative assignments and evaluation by the management in the industry. The Student will study the different types of printing, and how they can work together in a printing plant.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe the many and varied components or sectors within the industry.
- Define the key terms in the industry.
- Describe the products used in the manufacture of print such as paper, ink and technology.
- Explain the administration of a printing operation.
- Describe the progress of a printed piece from design concept to a final finished product.
- Explain the importance of estimating to the overall success of a printing operation.
- Describe Web, Flexographic, Silkscreen, and Offset Printing processes.
- Choose the appropriate printing process for different types of jobs.
- Apply the standards of press production.
- Provide practical solutions on printing and related problems.
