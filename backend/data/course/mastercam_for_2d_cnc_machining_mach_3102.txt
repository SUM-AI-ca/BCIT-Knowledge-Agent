## Mastercam for 2D CNC Machining MACH 3102
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
Focuses on the creation of CNC programs for 2D machining. Students will use Mastercam to create contouring, pocketing, drilling, engraving, and threading for CNC milling center and turning operations for CNC lathe. The course also explores 2D drawing file conversion and introduces post processor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 1 year CNC machining experience, or successful completion of Machinist Foundation program.
