## Composites AVAM 3415
Aircraft Maintenance Course
## Course Overview
​This course will introduce the students to composite materials and explore their use in aviation structures. Composite repairs and inspection will be completed.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- All term 1, 2 and 3 courses
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe materials, process, and handling procedures for composite materials and structure
- Explain reinforcement fibers, matrix materials, core materials, and composite safety
- Explain manufacturing techniques, methods of curing of pre-preg, temperature and pressure applications
- Explain inspection, damage assessment, and repair procedures
- Perform composite inspections and repairs. ​
