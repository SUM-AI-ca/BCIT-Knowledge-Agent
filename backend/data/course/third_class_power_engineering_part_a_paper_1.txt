## Overview

Take the next step in your journey toward becoming a certified 3rd Class Power Engineer! This course is designed to build on your practical experience and provide the advanced knowledge you need to confidently challenge the 3rd Class, Part A, Paper 1 certification exam under provincial regulations. Through engaging, industry-relevant training, you'll deepen your understanding of essential topics, including applied mathematics, mechanics, thermodynamics, and applied science—all critical components of power engineering. By combining this course with POWR 1330 , POWR 1340 , POWR 1350 , and the required qualifying work experience, you will be well-prepared to pursue your 3rd Class Power Engineer certification through your provincial regulatory body. Enroll today to enhance your expertise, build your confidence, and accelerate your career in power engineering!

## Prerequisites

- 4th Class power engineering certification or department approval.

## Learning Outcomes

- Master Key Concepts for Success:
- Be fully prepared to meet the Interprovincial examination requirements for 3rd Class, Part A, Paper 1. Topics include applied mathematics, mechanics, thermodynamics, and applied science, enhanced with interactive video demonstrations to make learning clear and practical.
- Solve Real-World Problems with Confidence:
- Learn to confidently apply mathematical and physics concepts like trigonometry, mensuration, applied mechanics, and elementary thermodynamics, supported by engaging video demonstrations and practical examples.
- Explore Chemistry in Action:
- Dive into essential chemistry principles and their applications in power engineering, including combustion, boiler water treatment, metallurgy, and corrosion prevention.
- Discover the Steelmaking Process:
- Uncover the connection between steelmaking and power engineering, gaining insight into a critical process that impacts the industry.
- Bring Technical Drawings to Life:
- Develop skills to create and interpret technical drawings, including orthographic and isometric projections, making design and engineering concepts easy to understand.
- This course combines interactive learning, real-world applications, and cutting-edge tools to give you the knowledge and confidence to excel in the power engineering field!
