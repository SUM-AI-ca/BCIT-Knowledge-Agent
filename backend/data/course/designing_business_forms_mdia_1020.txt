## Designing Business Forms MDIA 1020
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Every business needs its own special look! This course will introduce the topic of designing business cards and letterhead. The students will also learn how to design forms that make sense and are legible for internal and external use.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MDIA 1050 or 50% in MDIA 1090 or 50% in MDIA 1150
