## Green Building Construction Practices BLDT 4081
Construction Operations/Supervision Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces Green Building Construction concepts and practices. Topics include green building definition, guidelines, regulations and standards. Evaluation of the cost/benefit of green construction methods and green construction rating systems. Relevant case studies will be reviewed. Two site visits will be included.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply green building concepts and approaches.
- Illustrate the benefits of sustainable construction.
- Describe economic, environmental, and social imperatives for action to improve building environmental performance.
- Recognize the site practices required for sustainable construction.
- Categorize materials and resources selection and management for green construction.
- Interpret relevant codes and regulations for green building.
- Analyze core issues in sustainable construction.
