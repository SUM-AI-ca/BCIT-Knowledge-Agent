## Sheet Metal and Fasteners AVCC 1115
Aviation Common Core Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides the theoretical understanding and practical skills needed to inspect, fabricate and repair: sheet metal, tube and composite material structures. Aviation sheet metal fasters will be explained and used to perform repairs and modifications.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the use of sheet metal tools
- Explain the difference between structural and non-structural repairs
- Select, handle, layout, and fabricate sheet metal to maintain aircraft structures
- Describe standards and specifications used to related fasteners
- Explain the application and installation/removal of special fasteners
- Select special purpose fasteners based on application
- Explain the inspection processes for welds
- Perform tasks utilizing sheet metal hand and power tools
- Perform sheet metal repairs, including cutting, bending, forming, and fabrication
- Perform damage inspection on sheet metal
- Perform the installation inspection, and removal of solid rivets
- Perform installation, and removal of standard fasteners
