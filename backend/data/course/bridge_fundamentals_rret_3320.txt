## Bridge Fundamentals RRET 3320
Renewable Resources Engineering Technology Course
## Course Overview
Provides an overview of all aspects of bridge planning, design, construction and inspection. Topics covered include: legal implications, environmental controls site factors that influence design and construction, plus the basic engineering principles that apply to costing, stream hydrology, hydraulics and structural analysis.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Introduction to Bridges
- Describe the history of bridges and their components.
- Describe the benefits of different bridge material types.
- Define the specific controls on bridges through various pieces of legislation.
## Module 2 - Bridge Components
- Identify the component parts of bridges and their material make-up.
- Describe the failure mechanisms of various bridge components.
- Describe how bridge components are designed or fabricated to prevent failure.
## Module 3 - Site Data for Bridge Design
- Analyze the site information needed for bridge design.
- Describe the components of good and poor bridge sites.
- Compute the basic stream hydrology and hydraulics calculations.
## Module 4 - Design Standards and Analysis
- Describe basic bridge design and materials specifications.
- Evaluate bridge loadings and stresses.
- Apply techniques and processes to allow materials to withstand applied loads and stresses.
## Module 5 - Construction
- Compare and contract construction practices for various bridge components.
- Describe effective as-built records that need to be kept after construction.
- Describe how the construction processes are impacted by outside influences, and what steps can be taken to overcome obstacles.
## Module 6 - Bridge Inspection
- Describe the needs for bridge inspections, the basic tools, and the results of those inspections.
- Describe the purposes of carrying out a load rating in conjunction with inspection.
- Explain how the proper application of suitable materials can prevent future maintenance problems.
- Describe preventative measures that could be applied to a problem brought to light through an inspection, so as to prolong the life of the structure.
