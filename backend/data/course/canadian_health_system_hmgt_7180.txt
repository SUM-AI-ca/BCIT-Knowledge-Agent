## Canadian Health System HMGT 7180
Health Management Course
## Course Overview
Examines current issues in the delivery of health care in Canada's Health System with some reference to other health care systems in the world. Critical thinking and evaluative skills applied to investigation, analysis and discussion of various issues.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain the relationship of health determinants and the health care delivery system.
- Summarize the organizational structure, financing, and governance of the Canadian health system.
- Analyze the public/private divide in health care in Canada.
- Using evidence, evaluate the sustainability of the Canadian health system.
- Discuss the position of health professionals and the public on whether there is a crisis in our health system.
- Analyze and justify whether user fees would help sustain the system.
- Summarize some of the challenges in chronic disease management and relate these to your local health system.
- Explain the issues that affect the delivery of acute care services.
- Analyze and discuss the issues around waiting lists and the implementation of solutions.
- Summarize the problems/issues that health authorities are facing with regard to community/home care.
- Evaluate the evidence for what works in health promotion/ prevention of illness.
- Using evidence, develop an argument for and against the view that a physician shortage exists.
- Analyze how the work of delivering health services can be organized differently by examining scope of practice.
- Analyze the evidence of reporting of errors in the health system.
- Summarize the challenges to improving quality and how they might be dealt with.
- Research a Canadian health authority, and evaluate its governance, restructuring efforts, and level of integration of health service delivery.
- Compare and contrast several health systems in the world, and relate findings to the Canadian health system.
- Identify strategies that are needed to manage nursing shortages in the short term and in the future.
- Develop an informed opinion about the kinds of reforms that are needed in the Canadian health system.
- Critically reflect upon changes to your views about the organization of health systems.
- Research, critically analyze, and write an academic paper on an issue that relates to the health care system.
- Participate in online discussion with colleagues, as discussion initiator/leader, contributor, and/or team membe.
