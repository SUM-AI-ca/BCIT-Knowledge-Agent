## Value-Added Wood Entrepreneur WOOD 1041
Wood Products Course
## Course Overview
Designed to help you establish your own entrepreneurial potential. It explores the essential and unique entrepreneurial traits, studies the risks and rewards, and reviews the simple essential business skills required to make it work. Topics include marketing, sales, finance, professional support roles (i.e. lawyers, accountants & consultants), innovation and interpersonal skills.
