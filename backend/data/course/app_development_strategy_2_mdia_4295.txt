## App Development Strategy 2 MDIA 4295
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course will introduce students to advanced design and development of applications on mobile platforms. Android will be used as a basis for exploring the design and programming techniques associated with the development of standalone applications and mobile interfaces. Students will implement and publish the app designed in MDIA 3295 . Students will be assessed by a combination of lab exercises, quizzes, group and individual projects.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MDIA 3295
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Integrate content strategy with information architecture (IA), user experience (UX) and interface design (ID).
- Install and configure Android application development tools.
- Save state information across important operating system events.
- Apply entry level programming concepts and use developing tools to Android application development.
- Simulate app publishing in alpha or beta version.
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2020
