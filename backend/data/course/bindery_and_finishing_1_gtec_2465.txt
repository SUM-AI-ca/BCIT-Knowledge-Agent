## Bindery and Finishing 1 GTEC 2465
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course provides students with an orientation to bindery and finishing. Students will learn how to do basic setup and running of the Cutter, Folder and Saddle Stitcher for lithographic post production. Students will also learn to read job dockets; plan work processes; identify production standards; and consistently monitor work quality. Students will gain hands-on bindery and finishing experience and learn basic setup and running of a Gang Stitcher including observation of a Three-Knife trimmer. Students will also be able to identify the challenges of more complex folding, cutting and stitching jobs. In Large Format post production students will learn substrate mounting and laminating techniques. Digital Bindery operations including collating, coil binding, drilling and perfect binding will be demonstrated. Students will build a portfolio of their own custom finishing projects.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Identify the basic bindery and finishing functions.
- Plan the necessary work processes, with packaging, for completion of the binding or finishing tasks.
- Explain the functions of a job docket and identify the necessary elements for bindery/finishing.
- Perform basic job setup of the Cutter, Folder and Saddle Stitcher.
- Perform basic maintenance on the Cutter, Folder and Saddle Stitcher.
- Produce a cut job to defined job standards.
- Produce a folded job to defined job standards.
- Produce a finished book to defined job standards, using the Saddle Stitcher and Three-Knife Trimmer.
- Complete all bindery and finishing tasks safely.
- Maintain a clean and safe workplace.
- Identify and explain complex bindery and finishing functions and operations.
- Plan the necessary work process for completion of a perfect binding project.
- Complete job layouts that clearly optimize the bindery equipment and meet customer’s needs.
- Lead an operating crew for a Saddle Stitching job.
- Identify the challenges of complex Folding and Saddle Stitching jobs.
- Perform basic setup of the Perfect Binder.
- Carry out basic maintenance on the Perfect Binder and other bindery and finishing machines.
- Produce a bound job to defined job standards.
- Produce a finished book to defined job standards.
- Apply cold lamination to Large Format inkjet projects.
- Collate digital print runs; drill and coil bind a finished digital project.
- Explain WHMIS standards.
