## Graduation Project/Internship 3 FSCT 8630
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
The student will conclude the project/internship proposed and conducted in FSCT 8610 and FSCT 8620 respectively. A written final report will be submitted and presented orally to the advisory team.
### Prerequisite(s)
- S in FSCT 8620

## Learning Outcomes
The student will conclude the project/internship proposed and conducted in FSCT 8610 and FSCT 8620 respectively. A written final report will be submitted and presented orally to the advisory team.
- Demonstrate the knowledge and skills acquired in the program to complete the proposed project/internship.
- Plan, organize and write a comprehensive final report on the project/internship.
- Defend the findings and the conclusion of the project/internship.
