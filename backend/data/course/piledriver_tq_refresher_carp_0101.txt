## Piledriver TQ Refresher CARP 0101
Carpentry Course
## Course Overview
Provides students with the necessary instruction to enable them to write the provincial trade qualification examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- A minimum 5 years experience in the trade. To become eligible to write the exam, approval from the Ministry of Advanced Education, Training and Technology is required and recommended before enroling.
