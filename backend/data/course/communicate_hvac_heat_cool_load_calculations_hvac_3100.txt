## Communicate HVAC Heat/Cool Load Calculations HVAC 3100
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
Load calculations are indispensable for HVAC system design. You will learn how to calculate cooling and heating loads for buildings using manual and computer-based methods. This knowledge is applied by completing a Load Project, where calculations are recorded, equipment selected, and costs are estimated.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Calculate building cooling requirements using manual and computer-based methods.
- Calculate building heating requirements using manual and computer-based methods.
- Complete a Load Project.
