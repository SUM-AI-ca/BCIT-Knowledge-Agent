## Video Editing BCST 1118
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
​This course teaches students how to edit video footage, still images and audio using professional editing software to create a variety video content. It also introduces basic video graphics and some creative editing techniques.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Set up a video project and sequence using professional video editing software.
- Import video footage and images from various sources into a project.
- Demonstrate the ability to edit video, still photos, and audio on a timeline.
- Assess and troubleshoot problematic footage.
- Produce basic video graphics.
- Use a variety of video transitions and effects
- Demonstrate the ability to export media for a variety of platforms.
Effective as of Fall 2024
