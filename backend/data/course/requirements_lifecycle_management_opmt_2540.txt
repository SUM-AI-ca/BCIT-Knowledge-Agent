## Requirements Lifecycle Management OPMT 2540
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
​Requirements and potential design solutions are core elements and key deliverables of the business analysis process. This is often iterative work that involves confirmation and modification as the project or work progresses. Therefore, requirements and subsequent designs need to be managed throughout the lifecycle from elicitation to retirement. This course covers the complete requirement lifecycle and the activities that control this process. The organization, stakeholders, solution requirements and designs must be aligned and maintained for the project to provide value. You will learn these topics through lectures, exercises and discussions, case studies and/or scenario-based problem solving, both individually and working in teams.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the requirements lifecycle from elicitation through to fulfillment
- Explain the tasks required to manage the requirements lifecycle
- Analyze a system to manage requirements and designs including:
- Identifying relationships within and between requirements and designs
- Ensuring that requirements and designs are traceable to an organizational need
- Ensuring that proposed changes are tracked, assessed and approved
- Ensuring that requirements and designs are prioritized
- Ensuring that all requirements and designs are approved​
