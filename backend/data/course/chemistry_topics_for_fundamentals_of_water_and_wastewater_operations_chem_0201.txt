## Chemistry Topics for Fundamentals of Water and Wastewater Operations CHEM 0201
Chemistry Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides foundational chemistry topics fundamental for the study of Water and Wastewater Operations. The materials in this course can be used as a resource for students in the Water and Wastewater Operations program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- ​Recognize chemistry elements and compounds, basic naming and formulas.
- Evaluate safety concerns in handling of compounds used in water treatment.
- Evaluate safety concerns in handling of compounds used in water treatment.
- Describe properties of mixtures and solutions pertinent to water and wastewater chemistry.
