## Foundations of Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems RPAS 0120
Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems Course
## Course Overview
​This course prepares students to write and successfully pass either the Basic or Advanced Transport Canada RPAS Certification online test. Transport Canada regulations for safe and legal RPAS use are reviewed along with a brief history of drones and their common applications.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Outline the history of RPAS use.
- Describe common commercial applications of RPAS.​
- Recall the regulatory requirements for operating a RPAS as described by Transport Canada, including relevant air law, systems, human factors, meteorological rules, navigation, operation, theory and radiotelephony. ​
