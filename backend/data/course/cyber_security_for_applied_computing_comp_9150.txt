## Cyber Security for Applied Computing COMP 9150
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces student to concepts, topics, issues, and methodologies related to Artificial Intelligence. Students will develop the skills required to evaluate, design, develop and improve Artificial Intelligence (AI) algorithms through hands-on projects and problem solving. Participants are expected to develop a portfolio of Al projects during this course.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain representations, algorithms, and techniques used in artificial intelligence.
- Apply/evaluate and design new AI techniques for a given computing problem.
- Articulate the basic processes that a machine learning agent must perform – from perceiving its environment to making decisions on how to act and perform those actions.
- Apply machine learning techniques, in particular to draw inferences from data and help automate the development of AI systems and components.
- Design, implement and test the AI-based software.
- Work productively as a member of a team in an applied computing setting. ​
