## Air Distribution Arrangement for HVAC HVAC 2113
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
This course explains different Air distribution arrangements used in conjunction with heating and cooling equipment. This is essential for any further studies into HVAC System design and service.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HVAC 2112
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain HVAC Central Systems:
- Single Zone control types
- Multi Zone Control types
- Describe types of variable air volume systems and their application
- Describe packaged systems:
- Fan coils
- Terminal Units
Effective as of Fall 2011
