## Protools Level 2 BCST 2085
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course carries on from the introductory Protools level 1 course, to expand on the sophisticated uses of Protools, including integration with video projects (such as from Avid, Final Cut, etc). Students will create a soundscape for a short video project at the completion of this course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BCST 1085
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Expand on the sophisticated uses of Protools.
- Integrate with video projects from Avid, Final Cut, etc.
- Create a soundscape for a short video project.
