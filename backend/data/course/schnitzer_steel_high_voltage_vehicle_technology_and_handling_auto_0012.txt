## Schnitzer Steel High Voltage Vehicle Technology & Handling AUTO 0012
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
This course provides Schnitzer Steel employees with the safety considerations and procedures required for the safe inspection and dismantling of Battery Electric and Hybrid Electric vehicles.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe electric vehicle high voltage systems.
- Apply high voltage safety procedures.
- Demonstrate high voltage tools and equipment.
- Inspect high voltage components.
- Demonstrate high voltage component removal.
