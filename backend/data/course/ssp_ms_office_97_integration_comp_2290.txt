## SSP MS Office 97 Integration COMP 2290
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Teaches students via hands-on projects to decide which desktop application to use for specific tasks. Learning to effectively integrate and link information from other products such as Word, Excel, Access, Powerpoint and Outlook.
