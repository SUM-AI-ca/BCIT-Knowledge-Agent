## Finance 2 Administration FMGT 4565
Financial Management Course
## Course Overview
Builds on the concepts and techniques learned in FMGT 3560 . Topics include capital budgeting, capital markets, capital structure and dividend policy.
### Prerequisite(s)
- FMGT 3560
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Discuss the financial and economic environment, including financial securities, institutions, and markets and their importance to a business.
- Apply time value of money calculations to determine present and future value lump sums, annuity and perpetuity values, returns, growth rates, and borrowing costs.
- Evaluate long-term investment decisions using net present value and internal rate of return calculations.
- Assess managerial performance measures that can be useful to rewarding wealth creation.
- Outline the characteristics and variables which determines an option’s value; and discuss how employee stock options may be used to motivate wealth creation.
