## Growing A Business VENT 1821
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
The course will introduce learners to the fundamentals of growing an existing business in Canada. Topics will include strategic options for growth, estimating revenue and costs associated with growth, special considerations involved in growing a family business and environmental factors influencing growth capabilities and avenues for expanding Canadian companies.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Prepare a plan for growth of a business
- Analyse and describe resource requirements for a growing business
- Analyse and describe environmental factors influencing company growth capabilities
- Assess the pros and cons of various strategic options for growth of an expanding business
- Assess pre-growth considerations such as the amount of financing required and the stage of development of the business seeking to expand
