## CNC Placement Theory Exam MACH 0123
Machinist Course
## Course Overview
A theory examination related to Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machine programming, machine set-up, machine operation and the mathematics application. The examination questions and answers in a Learning Management System (LMS) electronic format.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Provide assessment of CNC theoretical knowledge of programming, set-up, and operation for students and their sponsor.
