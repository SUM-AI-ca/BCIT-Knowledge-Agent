## Ship Cargo 2 NAUT 2210
Ocean Navigation Course
## Course Overview
This course builds up on Ship Cargo 1 and covers the safe stowage, securing, carriage, and discharge of specialized categories such as deck, liquid bulk, hazardous, and refrigerated cargoes.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Term 1.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use International and Canadian regulations for safe stowage, securing, and carriage of deck cargoes.
- Use Code of Safe Practice for Cargo Stowage and Securing of under deck and deck cargoes.
- Outline the design and operation of oil tanker piping and pumping arrangements.
- Perform oil cargo calculation for weight: ullages, volume, and densities.
- Use International and Canadian regulations for safe stowage, securing, and carriage of dangerous, hazardous, and harmful cargoes.
- Outline preparation, safe stowage, carriage, and discharge of refrigerated cargoes.
- Describe the principles of a hatch cover, the construction of hatch covers, and the critical areas of hatch covers.
- Describe the purpose of ballast tanks, their construction, and the critical components of ballast tanks.
- Outline the design and operation of ships carrying dangerous liquid chemicals.
- Outline the design and operation of ships carrying liquefied gases in bulk.
