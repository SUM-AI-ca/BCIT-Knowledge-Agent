## Storytelling Techniques in Games COMP 7904
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This course teaches students to evaluate and analyze storytelling techniques through the study of games and films. Students will apply the concepts and develop a team-based prototype of a 2D/3D interactive experience. The course will focus on introducing students to the concepts, methods and materials used to build a rich narrative in games. The focus will be on applying concepts for game design such as storyboarding, plot structure, themes, 3D models, textures, model rigging, cinematographic techniques, and principles of sound design to create mood and feeling in games. The course is designed as a combination of a series of interactive lectures, discussions based on assigned film viewings and video games, group projects and individual assignments such as case studies, game pitches, storyboards and asset development.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Assess narrative in film and interactive devices.
- Describe the basics of plot structure.
- Identify elements of linear storytelling that can be imported into interactive media.
- Analyze visual effects and characters using techniques like modeling, texturing, rigging and animation.
- Analyze player agency, authorial intent, and AI-controlled non-player characters, and how they interplay.
- Assess different audio techniques for creating a mood.
- Generate elementary graphic objects such as characters and backgrounds to be incorporated into computer games.
- Produce 2D/3D art assets for computer game development using an iterative method.
- Plan convincing theme music-to-character mappings.
- Design engaging story sound themes for games.
- Apply different camera shots and perspectives.
- Utilize cinematographic styles for more engaging gameplay.
- Develop narratives in interactive media.
