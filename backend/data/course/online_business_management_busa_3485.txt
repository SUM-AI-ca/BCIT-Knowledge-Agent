## Online Business Management BUSA 3485
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
This is a research and project course where students will prepare a business proposal for a new company that wishes to market products or services on the Internet. A complete strategy for online business will include market research, a cost-benefit analysis, coverage of technical and operational issues such as logistics and promotion, legal requirements, and human impact issues.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BUSA 3455
