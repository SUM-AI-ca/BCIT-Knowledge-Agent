## Overview

Students will acquire advanced skills in digital illustration using SketchUp and rendering software through the application of advanced techniques in lighting, 3-D modeling and texturing to create photo-realistic images of interiors. Students will exhibit their proficiency of a professional presentation using an interior design project.

## Credits

3.0

## Cost

$1,033.64

## Prerequisites

- 50% in ENGL 1177 and 70% in COMM 1120
- Entrance Requirements for the Diploma.

## Learning Outcomes

- Build detailed digital models of interior environments using advanced techniques in SketchUp Pro and supplementary software.
- Incorporate human forms to convey the scale of the space.
- Create visually-enhanced materials, colours and surface textures.
- Apply advanced lighting techniques to create photo-realistic interior illustrations and contextual surroundings.
- Create dynamic professional presentations that incorporates 2-D and 3-D drawings and animation.
