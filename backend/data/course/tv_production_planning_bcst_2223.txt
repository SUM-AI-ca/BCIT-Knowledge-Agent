## TV Production Planning BCST 2223
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Covers the techniques and methods used in managing and organizing the details of pre-production, production and post-production activities in a studio or location television or video production. Students acquire skills in budgeting, production, scheduling, crewing and other elements of successful productions. This course is creditable to Television day school.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Plan all the elements necessary to guarantee that the production will meet the professional standards of the television and/or video production industries.
- Organize and conduct meetings as the Producer and/or Director of a proposed production.
- Organize production company objectives.
- Research the requirements for producing a video for a potential client.
- Negotiate the terms of a client production.
- Analyze time management and budget for a production.
- Develop the production book.
- Design communication log forms.
- Write production scripts.
- Design story boards.
- Draft production contracts.
- Identify the chronological communications patterns between their production company, the client and all related sub-contractors.
- Demonstrate effective presentation skills.
- Communicate positive and constructive evaluation of peer production company projects.
