## Work Practicum SRMT 2200
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
The course provides opportunities for students to work in a supervised field setting on a project or in the workplace. Students can explore different work place experiences or on the job training that they may or may not encounter in their usual course of studies. This experience may be achieved through students following their own placement and / or through directed placements.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMM 2248 and SRMT 2360 and SRMT 2500 and SRMT 2610 and SRMT 2700
### Credits
5.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Work productively in a Natural Resources industry environment.
- Apply skills learned during their first year of studies.
- Demonstrate the development of skills acquired during the practicum.
- Demonstrate an appropriate professional approach to the workplace.
- Maintain a daily time log.
- Record events and results of assigned duties/tasks to an acceptable standard in a journal format.
- Write a report summarizing the work performed , reflecting on their work experience and the relationship to their training and career path.
