## Yachtmaster Offshore (Theory) Part 1 YMTC 1020
Yachtmaster Coastal Course
## Course Overview
Yachtmaster Offshore certificate is a Master's certificate of competency for operating commerical yachts up to 200 tons within 150 nm of a safe haven overseas. This certification involves 66 hours theory (part 1) at the Marine Campus.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
