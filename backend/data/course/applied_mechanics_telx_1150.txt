## Applied Mechanics TELX 1150
Electronics Technical Course
## Course Overview
This course provides the foundation of the required skills for solving practical problems in science for Electricians. Clear demonstration on the advantages of simple machines including: pulleys, levers, gear drives, and inclined planes are provided for practice.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
8.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Solve equations and word problems using whole numbers, fractions, decimals, percent, roots and powers, ratio and proportions, graphs.
- Convert between Metric and Imperial units, and between Engineering prefixes and Scientific notation.
- Solve problems involving perimeter, area, and Length, Volume, Temperature, including circular mils and square mils.
- Demonstrate the ability to Substitute and Transpose formulas.
- Apply trigonometry to solve problems involving vectors.
- Solve problems involving Time, Mass, Velocity, and Acceleration.
- Apply the concepts and principles to Work, Power, Energy, and Efficiency problems.
- Demonstrate and solve problems involving simple machines and mechanical advantage using inclined planes, Levers, Jacks, Pulleys, and Gear drives.
