## Propane Fuel Sys.for Vehicles AUTO 0131
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides the necessary theoretical instruction required to write the Technical Safety BC certification examination. Practical training provides the skills required to identify, inspect, diagnose, service, and repair propane-powered vehicles. Contact Technical Safety BC (www.technicalsafetybc.ca) to determine eligibility and to schedule an examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Minimum Third Year Apprentice or B.C. TQ in Automotive, Commercial Transport, or Heavy Duty Mechanic

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be:
- Prepared to write the Technical Safety BC certification examination
- Inspect propane (LPG) systems
- Service and repair propane (LPG) vehicles
- Troubleshoot propane (LPG) vehicles
