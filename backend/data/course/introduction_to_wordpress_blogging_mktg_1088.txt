## Introduction to WordPress Blogging MKTG 1088
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
In this course you will explore the WordPress blogging platform from a writer and blogger’s perspective. Upon completion of the course you will have created a live, functioning, and customized WordPress blog on a niche topic of your choice. You will focus on developing your blog’s strategy with tactics such as, search engine optimization (SEO), marketing and monetization, voice, and branding.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Develop a niche market blog
- Discuss the importance of blog strategy and tactics
- Explain blog ethics, professionalism and best practices
- Create a pitch for your blog to businesses and brands
