## Net Zero Energy and Zero Emissions Buildings – A Practical Intro for Modular Home Manufacturing CESA 0104
Centre for Energy Systems Applications Course
## Course Overview
​This workshop introduces the fundamentals of net zero energy and zero emissions, with focus on wood-frame modular residential construction. The workshop will help attendees develop their knowledge of the BC Energy Step Code, zero energy/emissions buildings principles, science, systems and materials. During the workshop, BCIT experts will demonstrate the construction of various air barrier systems and assembly details for zero energy/emissions factory built buildings to meet the requirements of the BC Energy Step Code.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain what is the BC Energy Step Code, Passive House standard and performance metrics for Part 3 Buildings with focus on wood-frame modular residential construction.
- Describe the BC Energy Step Code and zero energy/emissions buildings principles.
- Identify various air barrier systems and assembly details for zero energy/emissions​.
