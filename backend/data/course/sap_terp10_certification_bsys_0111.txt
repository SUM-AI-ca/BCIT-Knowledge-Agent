## SAP TERP10 Certification BSYS 0111
Business Systems Course
## Course Overview
This course use SAP ERP Software to introduce students to ERP processes. Students will be provided with detailed explanations of each business process. They will examine process step alternatives and customization for each process area covered during the 10 day course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Define organizational structures used in various business processes.
- Create key master data required to execute each business process.
- Complete ERP transactions needed to complete each business process cycle.
- Establish key integration points between different business disciplines in each business process cycle.
- Produce key reports for each business process model.
