## Survey Instrument Operations: Levels SURV 1101
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
Introduction to differential leveling; terms used in leveling; field note-keeping; peg-test; bench mark leveling; level note reductions and adjustment. Leveling applications - sewer-line survey and building site survey; earthwork volume calculations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5

International fees are typically 3.4 times the domestic tuition. Exact cost will be calculated upon completion of registration.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Carry out differential leveling - bench mark and detail leveling.
- Take and reduce field notes.
- Adjust level loops.
- Carry out a peg test to determine if the level instrument is in good adjustment or not.
- Calculate grades and invert elevations sewer-lines.
- Cut and fill; earthwork volume determinations.
Effective as of Winter 2007
