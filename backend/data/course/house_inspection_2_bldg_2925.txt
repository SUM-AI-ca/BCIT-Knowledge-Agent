## House Inspection 2 BLDG 2925
Building Course
## Course Overview
​​Provides practical and theoretical information required to inspect existing houses for visible defects. This covers the other remaining systems of a house that must be inspected as per the standards. It is the second of a series of courses that meet the academic requirements for house inspection and the qualifications for House Inspector Licensing Regulations under Consumer Protection BC (CPBC).​​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BLDG 2915 † ( † may be taken concurrently)
- English 12 "50% (Pass)" or equivalent www.bcit.ca/admission/upgrading/englishproficiency.shtml or COMM 0015 score of 50 or better. BLDG 2915 may be taken currently with BLDG 2925 .

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Have a basic knowledge about the last 6 systems in a house to be reviewed as per the “Standards of Practice" for a house inspector.
- Understand inspecting to the “Standards of Practice" versus industry standards.​​
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2024
