## Electrical Apprenticeship Level 4 Theory Review TELC 0178
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for students planning to attend Level 4 technical training for the Electrical Apprenticeship. This course will concentrate on a review of the technical training for Levels 1 to 3 as well as a review of the mathematics utilized in Level 4.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon completion of this course, the student will:
- Be aware of the mathematics used during technical training for Level 4 of the Electrical Apprenticeship.
