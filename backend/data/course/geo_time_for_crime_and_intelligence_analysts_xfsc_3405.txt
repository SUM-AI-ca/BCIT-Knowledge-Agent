## Geo-Time for Crime and Intelligence Analysts XFSC 3405
Forensics Microcredentials Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed for Crime and Intelligence Analysts who are employed in either law enforcement or regulatory agencies, as well as analysts employed in related fields. In this course you will learn how to use Geo-Time to analyze the geospatial information related to vehicles, phones, and trackers. The course will focus on applications to crime investigation and will introduce uses for intelligence purposes.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate use of the various features of Geo-Time software
- Conduct geo-spatial analysis of vehicle and phone movement using Geo-Time software
