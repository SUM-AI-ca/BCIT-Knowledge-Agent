## Overview

Students use the Premiere Pro interface to create multi-layered movies by syncing audio, video, and imagery seamlessly to produce a video. The course covers adding styled titles, applying filters and transitions, and colour correcting the entire video to ensure a professional product from start to finish. The course explains how to use Premiere Pro's workflow and integrate with Adobe Photoshop, Audition, and After Effects to edit audio, imagery, and add complex effects that update in real time. Students will explore the best options for exporting video content to mount on a website, or through video sharing sites such as Vimeo, Youtube, and Vine. The course will be delivered through a series of video notes.

## Learning Outcomes

- Use the Premiere Pro interface
- Edit Video content on a single layer
- Add audio tracks to a presentation
- Combine multiple video and audio sources together
- Colour correct a presentation
- Apply filters and effects to enhance a presentation
- Work with styled text and titles
- Describe the workflow integration between Premiere Pro,  After Effects, Audition, and Photoshop
- Demonstrate the strengths and weaknesses of the current file formats, sizes, and video settings
- Export files for optimized web delivery
- Mount audio and video files on a website
- Produce compressed video files for Youtube, Vimeo, and Vine
