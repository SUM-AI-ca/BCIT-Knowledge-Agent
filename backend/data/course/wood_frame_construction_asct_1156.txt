## Wood Frame Construction ASCT 1156
Architectural and Structural CADD and Graphics Course
## Course Overview
This course presents theoretical foundation and code requirements necessary to design and detail wood-framed structures. Through the study of building construction assemblies & techniques, this subject area endeavors to develop students’ knowledge of various construction assemblies, issues, methods, analytical & technical construction knowledge, and capacity to implement fundamental principles employed in single and multi-family residential construction.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ASCT 1010 and ASCT 1015 and ASCT 1155

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Communicate effectively in the graphic & verbal language of building construction.
- Effectively recognize and problem solve building construction issues.
- Describe the organization & principles of structural and architectural working drawings.
- Prepare professional quality drawings using the graphic language, conventions, and standards of the construction industry.
- Explain the relationship of the drawings to the overall context of the contract documents.
- Verify compliance with specifications.
- Produce floor/foundation /roof framing plans and details, building sections, connection details.
- Identify types of wood products commonly used for main structural members in wood-frame construction.
- Identify fasteners used in wood frame construction.
- Produce wood framing details.
- Implement Master Format – Division #6: Wood.
