## Develop & Layout Templates MTLF 1225
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
In this course you will learn to use drafting principles and applications in the Metal Fabrication industry. As you work through this course, you will engage in extensive lab work to apply triangulation and parallel line development techniques as well as the alphabet of lines (various kinds of lines) to create templates from blueprints. Parallel line development is a geometrical method of developing flat, two dimensional patterns for cylindrical and rectangular shaped objects. You will also learn the principles of the various types of pattern development.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1205 and MTLF 1210

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the terminology used in various blueprint applications.
- Describe the methods of developing patterns and templates.
- Determine the dimensions required to develop a given pattern.
- Perform the calculations necessary for the development of patterns.
- Make templates and patterns for structural and plate projects.
