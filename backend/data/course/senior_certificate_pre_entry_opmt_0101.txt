## Senior Certificate Pre-entry OPMT 0101
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
Designed to orientate prospective participants to BCIT and the Operations Management Senior Certificate Program. Provides participants with a realistic view of the program through identification, problem solving, inventory and human relations techniques.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
