## Business Communication for Designers MDIA 1515
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course will help you develop the verbal and written skills required to communicate effectively with clients, suppliers, and co-workers within a professional environment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, you will be able to:
- Compose a business letter that follows the standard conventions of correct grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
- Create business documents that demonstrate basic principles of design and layout.
- Proofread written documents following standard conventions of correct grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
- Use professional, jargon-free communication in written and oral formats.
- Adapt communication styles for different media (email, text, voice mail, and social media).
- Demonstrate professional etiquette skills based on provided criteria.
- Conduct a conversation, which is at the appropriate level of formality, using effective speaking and listening skills.
- Deliver a verbal presentation.
- Create a professional slideshow presentation.
- Discuss the impact of non-verbal communication on how you will be perceived in a professional environment.
