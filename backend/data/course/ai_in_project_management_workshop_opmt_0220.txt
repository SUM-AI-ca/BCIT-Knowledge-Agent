## AI in Project Management Workshop OPMT 0220
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
​This intensive three-day workshop equips project managers with practical skills to integrate artificial intelligence into their project delivery workflows. Structured around the five project management process groups: Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring & Controlling, and Closing. The workshop demonstrates how AI tools can enhance decision-making and productivity at every phase. Topics are grounded in predictive (waterfall) methodology, with a dedicated session covering AI applications in Agile environments.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply AI tools across all stages of the project management lifecycle
- Use AI to support smarter initiation, planning, and execution decisions
- Leverage AI for cost estimation, resource allocation, and schedule optimization
- Implement AI-driven monitoring systems for proactive project control
- Select the right AI platforms and tools for specific project challenges
- Integrate AI practices into both waterfall and Agile delivery environments
