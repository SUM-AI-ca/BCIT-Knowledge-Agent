## Summer Workshop Access - BCIT Racing Club MECH 0206
Mechanical Engineering Course
## Course Overview
In this course, the students as part of a team, will be able to design, fabricate, and assemble parts for their Baja race vehicle using machine shop equipment. On their own, the students will complete the logistics of preparing the team and equipment for their upcoming Society of Automotive Engineering competition.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Be part of a successful team
- Design the parts required for their vehicle
- Fabricate the parts based on their design
- Assemble the parts that they designed and fabricated into a Baja race vehicle
