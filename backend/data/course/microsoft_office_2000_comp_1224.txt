## Microsoft Office 2000 COMP 1224
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This is a transition course and was designed specifically for experienced users of MS Office 97, 95 (or earlier). We will examine in detail the rich assortment of new features and changes (particularly Web based) made to Word 2000, Excel 2000, Access 2000, PowerPoint 2000 and Outlook 2000. Upgrading issues will also be discussed. Students are required to have strong keyboarding and file management skills as well as a working knowledge of word processing, spreadsheet and database software.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
