## Making Meetings Effective BTCW 0152
Business Workshops Course
## Course Overview
Through discussion, exercises, activities and practice this dynamic and participatory workshop will provide participants with the strategies, skills and confidence to organize and lead effective meetings.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the structure and elements of an effective meeting.
- Evaluate meeting opportunities and alternatives.
- Explore skills and strategies for creating climate and context.
- Understand critical participant roles for effective meetings.
- Discuss strategies and skills to promote participation and achieved desired 
outcomes.
- Apply strategies for intervention and managing disruptive and/or challenging behaviours.
- Explore the function of record keeping.
