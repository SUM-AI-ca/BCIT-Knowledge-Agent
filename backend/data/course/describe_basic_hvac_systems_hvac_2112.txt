## Describe Basic HVAC Systems HVAC 2112
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
This course provides a basic introduction to various HVAC systems. It includes component operation, location and application of various system arrangements. Success in subsequent HVAC courses is dependent on a good basic understanding of HVAC systems and is vital for a successful career as a HVAC technician.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HVAC 1114
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain HVAC Functions and Applications:
- Describe Function of A/C Systems
- Explain Types of A/C Systems
- Explain Equipment Arrangements
- Explain System Components and Operation:
- Explain Fans
- Explain Air Quality
- Explain Filtration
- Explain Air Volume Control
- Explain Humidification
- Explain Dehumidification
- Describe Ducting
Effective as of Fall 2011
