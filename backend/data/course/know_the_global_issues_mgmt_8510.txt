## Know the Global Issues MGMT 8510
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates skills in understanding the complex environment and its significance for the organization.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Determine the Significance of a Dynamic Global Economy on your Organization
- Identify the key forces for increased competitiveness and change in the global economy.
- Describe the effect of external forces on your industry.
- Explain the specific impact (direct or indirect) of global trends on your organization/department.
- Develop a plan to ensure your department stays attuned to global changes and their potential effect on the department/organization.
