## Sheet Metal IP Refresher SMTL 0100
Sheet Metal Course
## Course Overview
Provides students with the necessary instruction to enable them to write the provincial trade qualification examination. To become eligible to write the exam, approval from the Ministry of Advanced Education, Training and Technology office nearest your residence is required and recommended before enroling.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 5 years experience in the trade.
### Credits
0.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will have:
- Ability to challenge the Sheet Metal Red Seal examination.
- Knowledge of tools and equipment.
- Sheet metal fabrication.
- Air and material handling systems.
- Roofing, Architectural metal and speciality products.
- Maintenance and repair.
- Welding.
