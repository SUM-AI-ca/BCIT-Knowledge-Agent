## Telescope Making SURV 0125
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
For those who ever wanted to view the skies through a powerful telescope. This course will show you how to build and use an 8 inch (20 cm) Newtonian Reflector. The job is easy sailing in this all-wood construction.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
