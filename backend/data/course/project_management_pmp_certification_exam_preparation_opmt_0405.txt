## Project Management PMP Certification Exam Preparation OPMT 0405
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
PMP Certification from the Project Management Institute (PMI) is the industry standard for demonstrating competence and a solid foundation of project management skills. This exam preparation workshop provides students with strategies and methods needed to improve their overall exam score. All PMP exam topic areas are covered, including the PMI knowledge areas, the PMBOK and many other reference texts. Participants evaluate their existing knowledge base, determine what is missing and create a study plan to fill the gaps. Sample exam questions on every topic area, a complete exam simulation and a review of answers and references are provided. The workshop also provides the 35 hour education credits required by all new applicants. www.bcit.ca/files/business/pdf/opmt0405.pdf

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Develop the skills and knowledge to take the Project Management Professional (PMP)® certification exam from the Project Management Institute (PMI)®
- Describe the PMI® project management framework, concepts, definitions, life-cycles, phases, and terminology.
- Define the PMBOK® Guide project management process groups, knowledge areas, inputs, tools & techniques, and outputs.
- Explain the PMI® certification process, including the application process.
- Identify the styles and types of exam questions found in the exam.
- Apply test taking techniques to interpret and answer exam questions to maximize test score.
- Obtain the full 35 hours of project management education required to write the PMP® exam.
