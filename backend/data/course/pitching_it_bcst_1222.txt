## Pitching It BCST 1222
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to television and film pitching techniques. Students will be introduced to their projects selling process, pitching tools and terminology and the relationship needed to secure financing, development and creative ideas when working with film and television agents, distributors and broadcasters.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Pitch and explain their projects to various segments of the film and television industries.
- Conduct an effective business meeting by developing a strategy for selling their projects and hone their one on one skills and passion for the projects.
- Deliver a pitching presentation.
- Learn how to write a log line, synopsis and package their material.
- Learn who to target and recognize who their audience is.
