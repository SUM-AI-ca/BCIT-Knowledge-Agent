## GxP Audits Vs Inspections FSCT 0068
Forensic Investigation Course
## Course Overview
This course deals primarily with regulatory controls, auditing/inspecting techniques which are applicable to any function of chemical/biopharmaceutical/nutraceutical and contract testing laboratories. Specific examples will cover auditing of certain aspects of operations for compliance with GMP, GCP and GLP.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Define FDA, EU & Health Canada regulations applied in chemica/biopharmaceutical/nutraceutical/Analytical testing and contract research laboratories.
- Identify the main audit cycle stages and inspection process.
- Outline the necessary techniques to plan, organize and conduct audits and inspections.
- Interpret the FDA viewpoint on specific aspects of auditing/inspecting for GLP, GMP and GCP compliance.
- Apply the methodology needed for writing effective audit/inspection reports.
- Utilize the systems required for following up on Corrective Actions resulted from Audits/inspections.
