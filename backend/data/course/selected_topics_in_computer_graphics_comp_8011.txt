## Selected Topics in Computer Graphics COMP 8011
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This course focuses on selected topics in computer graphics. Students will work on independent projects allowing supervised research in areas such as 2D/3D graphics for computer games, visual simulation, data visualization and animation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in COMP 7011
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Part of the main course project involves setting individual learning objectives based on the student's interest. The learning outcomes will be related to researching and applying advanced computer graphics concepts to useful applications.
