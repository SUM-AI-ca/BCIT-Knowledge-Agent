## Technical Operations and Quality AVAM 4103
Aircraft Maintenance Course
## Course Overview
A course designed to familiarize you with issues important to aircraft maintenance such as quality assurance, the establishment and operation of an Approved Maintenance Organization (AMO), aircraft handling, aircraft inspection, weight and balance. A unit is also included on aircraft interiors and equipment such as upholstery, seats, cargo compartments and restraints, emergency and life saving equipment, and galleys.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
8.0

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the learner will be able to:
- Explain the operation of an Approved Maintenance Operation (AMO).
- Perform duties of key personnel within an AMO.
- Explain MSG concepts.
- Describe quality assurance programs.
- Outline the AME's responsibilities for maintenance release.
- Perform completion of documents for maintenance records.
- Perform aircraft inspection.
- Perform weight and balance process.
- Perform configuration change (landing gear).
- Perform engine removal/ installation.
- Perform ground run-up.
- Perform inspection and maintenance of aircraft interior and emergency equipment.
- Solve problems in aircraft systems.
