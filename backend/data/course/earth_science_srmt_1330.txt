## Earth Science SRMT 1330
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
Earth Science is an introduction to surficial geology, hydrology and geomorphology. Topics include the hydrologic cycle, surface and subsurface flow of water, stream channel hydraulics, evapotranspiration, the rock cycle, erosion and deposition. Recognition and interpretation of landforms, soil formation, soils of Canada, interpretation of soil and terrain maps, and land use impacts on soils and watersheds are also covered.

## Learning Outcomes
- Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify common, important landforms found in British Columbia using aerial photos and site characteristics.
- Describe how water moves through a wildland watershed, including how streamflow is generated.
- Describe the structure and utility of the B.C. Terrain Classification System.
- Read and interpret terrain maps and reports.
- Name and describe the major soil taxa occurring in Canada and in B.C. and the environments in which they occur.
- List the impacts of various land uses on soils and on surface and ground waters.
