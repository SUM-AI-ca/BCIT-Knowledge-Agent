## Math for Electricians 2 TELC 0122
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for students planning to attend level two technical training for the electrical apprenticeship program. Course topics include basic trigonometry, alternating current (AC) concepts, single phase AC circuit analysis and single phase transformer calculations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TELC 0121 or Electrical Apprenticeship Level 1 or Electrical Foundation program.
