## CMA Fast Track Program FMGT 5220
Financial Management Course
## Course Overview
The Certified Management Accountants of British Columbia's CMA FastTrack Program is a seven month program that will enable you to challenge the CMA National Entrance Exam in less than a year. Topics include financial accounting, management accounting, operations management, strategic management, human resource management, corporate finance, international business, marketing, taxation, and internal control.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in FMGT 1100 and 50% in FMGT 2100 and 50% in FMGT 3110 and 50% in FMGT 4110 and 50% in FMGT 3210 and 50% in FMGT 4210 and 50% in FMGT 3510 and 50% in FMGT 4510
### Credits
12.5

## Learning Outcomes
- Possess a strong technical grounding in the core subject areas of financial accounting, management accounting, corporate finance, taxation and information systems along with the ability to apply this knowledge in order to successfully attempt the CMA Entrance Exam.
- Possess a 'survey' level of knowledge of the other subject areas (operations management, marketing, human resources, international business and strategic management) in order to successfully attempt the CMA Entrance Exam.
