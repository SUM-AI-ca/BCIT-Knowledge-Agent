## Fine Art Photography BCST 0103
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
If you are enticed by the art of photography, if you are tempted to capture the pure essence of the moment, if you are daydreaming about boosting your creative approach & widening your vision, there is an opportunity for you to explore this journey. This provocative workshop will take you through the basics of photography; such as the exposure triangle, metering, composition and balance as well as post touching in Adobe Lightroom. This adventurous three-day class is a combination of theory, lab, model photo shoots (in natural light), analysis and multiple field trips. By the end of this workshop, you will be able to have a fine art portfolio on hand; with which to build your future artistic goals. Basic photography knowledge is an asset.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Set camera exposure manually and use different lenses & manage their efficiencies
- Apply compositional techniques to enhance your photographs
- Widen your vision with the art of photography
- Develop your original story idea into a photo series and create an engaging portfolio
- Find charming props & striking locations for a photoshoot
- Manage models with natural poses
- Professionally edit photos in Adobe Lightroom
