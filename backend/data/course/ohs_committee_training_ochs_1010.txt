## OHS Committee Training OCHS 1010
Occupational Health and Safety Course
## Course Overview
Interactive online course covers OHS committee functions and requirements for several industries in BC and Alberta. You choose a jurisdiction and industry to customize your course. Included legislation, inspections, accident investigations, reporting, OHS programs and how these topics relate to OHS committees.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
