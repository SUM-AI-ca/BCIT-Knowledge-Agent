## AutoCAD Map 1 ACGS 2000
AutoCAD/GIS Course
## Course Overview
Covers the basic technical skills required in spatial data collection and management. AutoCAD Map fundamentals with an emphasis on CAD issues, the Workflow model, and map spatial data tools are discussed.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in AICO 1000
