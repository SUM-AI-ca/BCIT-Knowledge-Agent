## Supervisor Safety Management OCHS 1005
Occupational Health and Safety Course
## Course Overview
Includes the specific Occupational Health and Safety requirements for BC supervisors including supervision of a safe work environment, safety inspections, accident investigations, and due diligence. Course available to industry clients on request.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
