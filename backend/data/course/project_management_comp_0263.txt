## Project Management COMP 0263
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Provides a workshop covering the key concepts of project management while using MS Project as a tool to communicate, determine schedules and costs before beginning a project, plan ahead for upcoming tasks and resource needs, anticipate the impact of missed dates, and to think of issues ahead of time. Presents Project Definition (Scope, Approach, Rates, and Constraints, and introduces Microsoft Project (Define Tasks, Schedule tasks, Assign resources, Report projects, evaluate and refine the Plan).
### Prerequisite(s)
- Extensive experience with projects.
