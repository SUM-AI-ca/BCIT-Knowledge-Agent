## Noise, Vibration & Harshness AUTO 0156
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Provides technicians with a working knowledge of test procedures and diagnostic test equipment, including a road force balancer and electronic vibration analyzer, to service customer complaints due to vehicle noise, vibration, and harshness problems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
