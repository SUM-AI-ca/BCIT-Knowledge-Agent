## Online Store MDIA 4150
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course will cover multiple platforms to create and launch an online store. Software reviewed will include plugins for content management systems (CMS) such as WordPress, third party templates and app integration. Students will review typical components of an online store and what is required for the store to operate successfully and securely, review the configuration settings for product samples, price options, and manage shipping options. Students will be assessed by a quiz and individual projects.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Determine best shopping platforms for specific projects.
- Plan and implement components of an online tool (website or App).
- Define security and payment gateways prerequisites.
- Configure, test and troubleshoot online store setup and configuration.
- Create images and metadata for catalogue setup
- Manage product catalogue, integrate pricing tactics and promotions.
- Configure payment gateways and shipping options.
- Customize without coding app or store interfaces.
- Edit and create new store section templates.
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2020
