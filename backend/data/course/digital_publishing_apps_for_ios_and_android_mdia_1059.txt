## Digital Publishing Apps for iOS and Android MDIA 1059
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course uses the Digital Publishing Suite and Adobe InDesign DPS tools to design, create and publish mobile apps to iOS and Android smartphones and tablets. The course covers the Digital Publishing workflow including: basic concepts, the design process, creating articles, creating both single-folio and multi-folio apps, app distribution, the Digital Publishing Suite Dashboard, interpreting analytics, adding interactivity including; multi-state objects, buttons, hyperlinks, panoramas, pan and zoom, scrollable frames, image sequence, audio and video and HTML5 web content. Students will setup their identities and accounts and provision their mobile devices for previewing and testing their apps. The course concludes by using DPS App Builder software to build a final app ready for approval and distribution by different app stores.
### Prerequisite(s)

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Create accounts, setup identities and provision mobile devices for iOS and Android.
- Determine how to divide an app into logical parts.
- Use InDesign to create the content for one or more parts of an app.
- Create an article for each of the InDesign files created.
- Construct horizontal and vertical alternate layouts using liquid layout rules.
- Add interactivity and media with a set of overlay creator tools.
- Preview multi-folios on mobile devices.
- Create app viewer icons with exact specifications.
- Preview and test apps in Apple iTunes, Google Play Developer Console and the Amazon Appstore.
- Prepare a final app ready for approval and distribution with App Builder software.
