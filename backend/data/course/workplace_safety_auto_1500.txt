## Workplace Safety AUTO 1500
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the Workplace Hazardous Materials Management System (WHMIS), WorkSafeBC regulations, proper use of personal protection equipment, procedures for using fire extinguishers and the use of vehicle hoisting, and lifting equipment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe a safe work environment
- Apply safe work practices
- Use a Hoisting and Lifting Equipment
