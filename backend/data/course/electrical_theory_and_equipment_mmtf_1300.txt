## Electrical Theory and Equipment MMTF 1300
Marine Mechanical Technician Course
## Course Overview
​In this course you will learn about the principles of electrical theory, both alternating and direct current electricity and do electrical circuit calculations. In this course you will learn to read electrical schematics and use diagnostic and measuring equipment. In this course you will complete circuit calculations and use diagnostic meters and equipment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in MMTF 1000
### Credits
4.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe AC and DC theory
- Apply Ohm's law and relationships between voltage, current and resistance and calculate voltage drop in typical circuits
- Identify and describe symbols used in schematic diagram for switches, lights, motors, fuses/breakers, alarm senders/detectors, solenoids and gauges
- Trace electrical circuits in a schematic diagram and describe circuit function
- Describe where, why and how to use electrical diagnostic meters such as continuity testers, test light, multi-meters and amprobes
- Demonstrate the use of continuity testers, multi-meters and amprobes to measure and analyze electrical circuits​
