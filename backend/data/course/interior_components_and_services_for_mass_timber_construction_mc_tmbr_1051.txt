## Interior Components and Services for Mass Timber Construction (MC) TMBR 1051
Mass Timber Construction Course
## Course Overview
​This course builds upon concepts presented in the microcredential, Introductory Studies in Mass Timber Construction. Students will learn about the installation methods and components used for interior floor, wall and ceiling systems in mass timber construction. They will learn best practices for fire protection and sound insulation in mass timber buildings. Students will consider strategies for the installation of mechanical, electrical and plumbing services and troubleshoot installation problems.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in TMBR 1021 and 60% in TMBR 1041

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the installation of interior wall, floor and ceiling systems and components in mass timber construction.
- Describe best practices for fire protection in mass timber buildings, including encapsulation and firestopping.
- Describe the different types of sound insulation products used in wall, floor, ceiling, and window applications in mass timber buildings.
- Describe best practices for the installation of acoustic products for wall, floor, window, and ceiling applications in mass timber buildings.
- Explain considerations for the installation of mechanical, electrical and plumbing services in mass timber construction.
- Troubleshoot mass timber installation problems.​
