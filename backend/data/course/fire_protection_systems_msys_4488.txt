## Fire Protection Systems MSYS 4488
Mechanical Systems Course
## Course Overview
Includes fire protection systems regulations and codes of practice; fire hazard classification; detection, alarm and communication systems, stand pipe and sprinkler systems for buildings with an overview of HVAC Systems Fire and Smoke Control. Applications will be applied to design assignments.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MECH 3325
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify various classes of fire.
- Select appropriate extinguishing agents.
- Use portable fire extinguishers.
- Design fire protection systems, selecting sensors, alarms and communication systems.
- Assess fire protection requirements based on the BC Building Code and the NFPA Codes.
- Explain fire department use of standpipe systems.
- Design standpipe and hose systems.
- Determine water storage requirements for sprinkler systems.
- Design a sprinkler system.
- Describe requirements for openings through building fire separations.
