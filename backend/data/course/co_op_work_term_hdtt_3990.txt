## Co-op Work Term HDTT 3990
Heavy Duty Truck Technology Course
## Course Overview
​Cooperative Education (Co-op) involves enhancing the educational experience by integrating traditional academic studies with relevant work experience. You will work full time at the employer's work site for the duration of the Co-op work term period. The Co-op work term is a full time paid position where you will complete productive tasks that relate to the heavy duty truck industry. The Co-op position is approved by the Co-op Coordinator. Students in the program attend a pre-employment course to enhance their employability prior to their Co-op work term and compete for jobs posted during the first academic term. During the work term, students are monitored by BCIT.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HDMT 1001 and completion of the BCIT HDTT term 1 and term 2 courses, and the co-op pre-employment workshops

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Integrate and Apply Technical Skills and Knowledge Gained into a Workplace Environment
- Apply Skills Acquired in the Co-Op Position
- Work Productively as a Member of a Team
- Write a Co-Op Work Term Report
- Acquire a Better Understanding of Career Options in his/her Field of Study
- Evaluate Personal Interests and Career Goals
- Apply job search strategies in this industry
Effective as of Winter 2025
