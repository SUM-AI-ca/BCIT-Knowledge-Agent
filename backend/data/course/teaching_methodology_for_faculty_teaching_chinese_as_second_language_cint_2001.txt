## Teaching Methodology for Faculty Teaching Chinese as Second Language CINT 2001
BCIT International Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for instructors teaching Chinese as a second language. The course will cover the curriculum development and interactive teaching methodology. Upon successful completion of the course, the individuals will be able to develop curriculum and teaching plans for students whose Chinese is a second language. Students will also receive a certificate from the Confucius Institute at BCIT with recognition from Beijing headquarters as a junior level instructor.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop awareness of teaching Chinese as a second language on a learner-centred basis.
- Compare differences between learning Chinese as a first language versus second language.
- Prepare sample course outlines.
- Develop a sample course curriculum.
