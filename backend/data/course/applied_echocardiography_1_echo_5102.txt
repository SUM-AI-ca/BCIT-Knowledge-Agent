## Applied Echocardiography 1 ECHO 5102
Adult Echocardiography Course
## Course Overview
This distance education course introduces cardiac anatomy and performance as assessed with echocardiographic techniques. The course includes patient preparation, imaging windows and views, image orientation, instrumentation, and two-dimensional, m-mode Doppler echocardiography. The final chapters correlate cardiac hemodynamic changes with sonographic changes.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in ECHO 5101 † and 60% in BHSC 2219 † ( † may be taken concurrently)

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Define rationale for performing echocardiograms.
- Describe patient preparation and positioning.
- Describe echocardiographic windows and scan planes.
- Correlate normal cardiac anatomy and physiology with echocardiographic images (two dimensional, m-mode and Doppler) as obtained from transthoracic (external) and transesophageal windows.
- Describe routine measurement protocols.
- Define normal values for adult heart measurements.
- Perform simple calculations to assess left ventricular performance.
