## Term 2 Final Project GTEC 2010
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
Working in teams, the students are required to produce 5 products which are all parts of an integrated design campaign. The students design and produce high resolution files, and will create a professional design for their customer’s branding and identification. The files will require revision changes and file editing to meet customer approvals. Teamwork will be emphasized and analyzed. Each team’s project will have a unique look.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Estimate the First Year, multi-component, imaging campaign.
- Engineer the parts for maximum cost efficiency.
- Organize and designate the applicable raw materials in inventory for each part.
- Provide proofs and track changes in a CRM for customer sign off (Instructor to provide approval).
- Manufacture each part to acceptable proven quality control standards.
- Accurately track time and materials for all parts.
- Provide a detailed report of Cost versus Actual of all parts of the imaging campaign.
- Provide a teamwork analysis through a peer review process.
- Use a database for Customer Relations Management to record and manage customer requests.
