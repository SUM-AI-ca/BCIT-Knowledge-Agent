## Leading Teams XLED 1020
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​Leading teams in today’s evolving workplace requires a broad array of skills to enable the potential of your people and resources. Building on the personal leadership style and strengths developed in Leading Self, participants will apply their skills in the context of work in teams. This workshop introduces tools such as Team Charters and techniques such as feedback to foster team building and build trust among team members.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand stages of team development with approaches to facilitate effective teams.
- Develop a team charter for how a group will work together to create vision and clarity
- Practice giving and receiving feedback to build trust with others in teams​.
