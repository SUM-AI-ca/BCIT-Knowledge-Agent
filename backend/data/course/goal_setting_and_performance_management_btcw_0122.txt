## Goal Setting and Performance Management BTCW 0122
Business Workshops Course
## Course Overview
In this one day Goal Setting and Performance Management workshop, learn the skills and techniques to ensure the employee and the organization are focused on the same priorities, seeking improved employee performance, productivity and effectiveness.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Define performance management.
- Understand how performance management works and the tools to make it work including goal setting, pay for performance, training and development, career management and when necessary, disciplinary action.
- Learn the three phases of performance management and how to assess each.
- Discuss effective goal-setting.
- Learn how to give feedback on performance management.
- Recognize the importance of motivation.
- Develop a performance journal and performance plan.
