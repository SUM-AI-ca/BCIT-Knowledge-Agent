## 3D Animation 1 MDIA 1640
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course explores the fundamentals of animation using cutting edge 3D software. Students will cover a mixture of topics such as posing, timing, key-framing, interpolation, transformations and rendering. Through assignments and exercises, the students will build experience, setting the stage for more advanced topics and exercises in the second semester.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Create, save and edit animations and projects.
- Control the interpolation of animated objects.
- Blend constraints.
- Animate a walk cycle.
- Create straight-ahead and pose to pose animations.
- Overlap and offset actions and animations.
- Frame characters and create appealing camera compositions.
