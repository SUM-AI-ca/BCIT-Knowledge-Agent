## Structures and Strength of Materials TTED 4044
Technology Teacher Education Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the basic design of structures, strength of materials and stress analysis at an appropriate level for pre-service Technology Education teachers. Principles of math, science, technology and engineering are learned in an integrated way. Besides receiving formal instruction, students will solve statics problems as well as design, build and test scale structures.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TTED 3044

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the concepts of strengths of materials.
- Explain the effect of buckling and slender columns.
- Define the term: structure.
- Explain the purpose of structures.
- Explain the terms used in the design and analysis of structures (e.g. equilibrium, resultant, reactions, etc.).
- Explain the three broad categories of structures.
- Explain the different types of loads.
- Explain the properties of forces.
- Explain the types of support and the internal forces in plane frames.
- Create free body diagrams.
- Calculate trigonometrical solutions for forces.
- Calculate moments & couples.
- Perform a variety of stress analysis calculations.
- Design structures for specific applications.
- Determine the size of material necessary for a given design application.
- Develop structure activities and problems suitable for inclusion in a High School.
