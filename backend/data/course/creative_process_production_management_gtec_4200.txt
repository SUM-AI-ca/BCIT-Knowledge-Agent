## Creative Process Production Management GTEC 4200
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
Students will examine the relationship between the print buyer and printer. Referencing an industry MIS (Management Information Systems) software, students will be exposed to sales fundamentals, account management, job specifications and project management. The basics of CRM (Customer Relationship Management) will also be covered. Students will be exposed to contractual issues, trade practices and copyright law.
### Prerequisite(s)
- GTEC 2010
### Credits
2.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Create, manage and update a customer relation management database utilizing standard CRM software.
- Define the relationship between the print buyer, printer and creator.
- Create specifications to fit the imaging job to the budget and end-use requirements.
- Identify and explain contractual issues, common trade practices and the basics of copyright law as it applies to the printing industry.
- Process a job from an estimate to a final job docket; set up purchases for substrate and subcontracted work through shipping and billing.
- Provide examples of job specification changes that will optimize production and add value for both the company and customer.
- Use pricing strategies, such as price lists, program structures, and rebate offerings that are common in the industry.
