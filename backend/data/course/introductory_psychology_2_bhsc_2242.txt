## Introductory Psychology 2 BHSC 2242
Basic Health Sciences Course
## Course Overview
This 15 week (3 hours a week) course is a continuation of BHSC 1142 , which focusses on the psychologist's approach to problems and issues in contemporary psychology. Current research on personality, psychological disorders and treatment, health psychology, social behavior, and interaction and group processes are covered.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BHSC 1142
