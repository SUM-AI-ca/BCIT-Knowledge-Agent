## English for Professional and Academic Success - Listening/Speaking 3 EPAS 0311
English for Professional and Academic Success Course
## Course Overview
​This course aims to improve English skills in listening, speaking, grammar, and vocabulary at the intermediate level while also developing the collaborative skills needed to participate effectively in BCIT programs and the workplace. You will practice following instructions and listening to academic or professional presentations to find main ideas and specific details. The course focuses on accuracy and logic when speaking in professional settings It’s recommended that learners bring their own devices to every class (laptop/earphones). Weekly requirements: Courses includes two hours of online asynchronous class time. Prepare to spend 3-5 hours on homework studying, or assignments. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Submit results of an approved language assessment, course(s), or credential(s). These results will decide your EPAS level. Please check the EPAS placement webpage for the language assessments or courses that are accepted.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate understanding of a variety of somewhat complex social exchanges, interactions related to school, and discussions on familiar topics
- Demonstrate understanding of talks and lectures on familiar technical academic topics
- Demonstrate understanding of informational and argumentative content of audiovisual reports on familiar technical academic topics
- Give an oral summary of up to 6 minutes to accurately communicate ideas, news, concepts, or information about a familiar general or technical academic concept
- Give a clear, prepared 6-minute proposal presentation that covers key points, provides a rationale, and makes a recommendation
- Participate effectively in extended discussion that involves the sharing of information and views, sharing and discussing technical content, and putting forward and responding to arguments​
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2026
