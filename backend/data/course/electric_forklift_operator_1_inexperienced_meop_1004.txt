## Electric Forklift Operator 1 - Inexperienced MEOP 1004
Mobile Equipment Operator Course
## Course Overview
​This course will provide you with both theory and practical training in the operation of powered pallet trucks and narrow aisle forklifts including electric walkies, end-riders and reach trucks. On successful completion, you will receive Mobile Equipment Operator Certification for Class 1, 2 and 3 electric lift trucks. Certification is valid for two years. This course meets current WCB guidelines and CSA Standards for electric lift-truck operators.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Driving experience
