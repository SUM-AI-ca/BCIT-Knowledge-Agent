## Visual Storytelling Workshop BCST 1196
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
How do you take your story idea and make it work successfully in the video realm? There are many important theories that apply when shifting stories between media. You will explore the essentials of how to find and tell a compelling visual story from conception to initial edit.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
Recognize the elements that make up a good visual story.
- Find, research and prepare visual stories.
- Write a broadcast-style script.
- Maintain focus and structure in story-telling.
- Successfully write to pictures.
