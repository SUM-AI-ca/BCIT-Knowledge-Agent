## Handle Materials MTLF 1250
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
This course covers the safe practices for lifting and moving objects, the use of personal protective equipment (PPE) and the use of crane or hoist hand signals to direct movements of a load. In this course you will learn to apply math to calculate mass of structural shapes and plates in both imperial and metric units using the structural handbook and mass tables, calculate loads and tensions, center of balance, and identify rigging components to safely hoist and handle a load. Apply WorkSafeBC standards and regulations for loads being hoisted as well as the use of forklift, dollies, jacks, and rollers.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1200 and MTLF 1205

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Use knots, bends, and hitches to attach rope for load control.
- Identify the working load limits of rigging equipment.
- Calculate the working load limit of rigging equipment.
- Use slings to safely hoist a load.
- Calculate the mass of a given load in order to plan safe approach to rigging.
- Describe the safe use of rigging hardware.
- Execute safely a rigging plan.
- Select the correct rigging equipment for moving or hoisting loads.
- Apply communication procedures for hoisting a load.
Effective as of Fall 2017
