## Landslide Prone Terrain Management RRET 2272
Renewable Resources Engineering Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course provides an overview of recognition and prescriptive options for timber harvesting and road construction on steep and sensitive slopes. Topics covered include: recognizing the types of landslides and hill slope processes; preparing a preliminary terrain stability assessment; measures for control and management of unstable terrain; preparing an outline for a watershed rehabilitation plan.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
## Module 1 - Locate Relevant Information in Handbook 18
- Describe commonly held ideas and perceptions about landslides.
- List the purposes for managing landslide-prone terrain.
- Locate the four main topics described in Handbook 18.
- List two topics described in the Forest Practices Code that relate to topics in Handbook 18.
## Module 2 - Recognize Types of Landslides and Hillslope Processes
- Define the six dominant types of landslides.
- Describe the setting in which landslides occur.
- Describe why landslides occur.
- Describe the factors affecting slope stability.
- Describe the factors affecting downslope transport.
## Module 3 - Prepare a Preliminary Terrain Stability Assessment
- Identify landslide-prone terrain on air photographs, terrain maps and slope stability maps.
- Identify landslide-prone terrain in the field.
- Identify the mitigating factors that would require assessment by a professional (i.e., P. Eng., P. Geo.).
## Module 4 - Measures for Control and Management of Unstable Terrain
- Describe measures that will avoid the unstable situation.
- Describe measures that will prevent destabilization of marginally stable slopes.
- Describe measures that will stabilize unstable slopes.
- Describe measures that will protect downslope resources when unstable situations cannot be corrected.
## Module 5 - Outline a Rehabilitation Plan
- Prepare an erosion and sediment control plan.
- Describe techniques for forest road deactivation.
- Prepare an outline of a rehabilitation plan.
