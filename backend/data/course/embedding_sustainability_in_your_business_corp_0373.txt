## Embedding Sustainability in Your Business CORP 0373
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
The world of sustainable business is changing fast. CSR, ESG, SDGs, and planetary boundaries have all become buzzwords that can be challenging for business owners, managers, and professionals to navigate. This workshop reviews key trends in sustainable business and explores approaches for businesses to define and embed sustainability goals and practices.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand key concepts and trends in sustainable business
- Review how business and organizations are setting goals around sustainability
- Investigate approaches to embed sustainability in business processes, practices, and cultures
