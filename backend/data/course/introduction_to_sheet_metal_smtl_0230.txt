## Introduction to Sheet Metal SMTL 0230
Sheet Metal Course
## Course Overview
This program will provide an introductory level of exposure to sheet metal. Students will focus on shop safety and basic operations of tools/equipment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Navigate BCIT’s campus and feel confident about attending a post-secondary institution.
- Describe the important aspects of shop safety.
