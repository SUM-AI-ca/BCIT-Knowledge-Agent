## Understanding UNIX COMP 1060
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the major concepts and tools of the UNIX operating system to a beginner. Topics include UNIX's operating system concepts, system adminstration tools and strategies, shell programming and UNIX's roles in business and the Internet.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in COMP 1006
