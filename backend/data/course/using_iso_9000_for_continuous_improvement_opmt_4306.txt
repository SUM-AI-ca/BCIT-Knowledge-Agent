## Using ISO 9000 for Continuous Improvement OPMT 4306
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
In today's competitive marketplace, quality is increasingly seen as a necessary prerequisite to supply. The ISO 9000 Standards for Quality Management are having an every increasing impact in Canadian and International markets. Persuing registration can be reqarding journey. However, the most progressive and successful organizations have discovered that registration is just beginning. ISO 9000 registration can provide an essential building block for national competitiveness. However, it is what comes after registration that allows an organization to maintain its competitive edge and to reap the full rewards from its investment. This course will build on an understanding of the ISO 9001 requirements and focus on the using and enhancing an ISO 9000 based quality system to create quality improvement.
### Prerequisite(s)
- OPMT 3306
- OPMT 3306 : ISO 9000 Standards for Quality Systems (or equivalent).
