## Leading Self XLED 1010
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​Leadership is the act of leading a group of people. Learning how we, as individuals, lead others to reveal opportunities for lifelong development and growth. A key skill to develop our ability to grow our leadership is self-awareness. Self-awareness improves our leadership by revealing the role our perceptions, assumptions and biases play in creating our sense of reality. Participants will identify their strengths and challenges and create an action plan to fuel their development to realize their leadership potential.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the key leadership attributes for success in today’s evolving workplace.
- Develop awareness and insight of your strengths and personal leadership style.
- Identify goals with a feed-forward plan to grow and develop your personal leadership potential.
