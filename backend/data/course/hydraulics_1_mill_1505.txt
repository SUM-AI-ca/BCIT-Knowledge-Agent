## Hydraulics 1 MILL 1505
Millwright Course
## Course Overview
MILL 1505 is the first of two courses offered in industrial hydraulics. Upon successful completion of Hydraulics 1 ( MILL 1505 ) and Hydraulics 2 ( MILL 1506 ) students will receive a Statement of Completion in Hydraulics. MILL 1505 provides basic fluid power theory; the description and application of hydraulic components, and basic circuit design. During the course students are required to design a basic circuit of their own design. They are also required to design and assemble several circuits to perform basic jobs often found in industry. Steel-toed safety boots are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the principles and terms relating to fluid power including:
- Pascal's Law
- Bernoulli's principle
- Law of conservation of energy
- The relationships between Force, Pressure and Area.
- Explain the relationship of flow and flow paths.
- Identify and use basic hydraulic schematic symbols.
- Describe the function of hydraulic components within a hydraulic circuit.
- Calculate actuator forces and speeds.
- Assemble basic hydraulic circuits in a training environment.
- Troubleshoot basic hydraulic circuits in a training environment.
