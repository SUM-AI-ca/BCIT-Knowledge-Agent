## Overview

This course is optional for students that have been accepted into the Aircraft Maintenance Engineering - Category M (Maintenance) Program. Students will begin to develop hand skills by manufacturing metal objects using hand tools. Students will also learn time management techniques, study skills, and note taking strategies. It is recommended that students accepted to the AME-M program take this course to better prepare for the rigor and expectations of post secondary education.
