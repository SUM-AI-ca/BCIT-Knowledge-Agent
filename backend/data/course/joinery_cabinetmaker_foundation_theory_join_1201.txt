## Joinery (Cabinetmaker) Foundation Theory JOIN 1201
Joinery Course
## Course Overview
The Joinery Theory course is structured to align with the Create Shop Drawings course (Join 1271) and the Practical Projects course (Join 1276). As students work through their shop drawings and practical projects they are assigned a series of related reading components and tests. At the end of each practical project students will receive a Project Final Exam, which summarizes the theory components assigned to that project.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the use of safe work practices.
- Solve mathematical problems.
- Apply layout techniques.
- Describe the care and use of hand tools.
- Identify woodworking joints.
- Describe portable power tools.
- Describe the use of woodworking machines.
- Identify materials.
- Describe using machining and assembly techniques.
- Describe applying a finish.
- Describe installing millwork.
- Use computerized software.
- Prepare for employment.
