## Applied Math Workshop OPMT 1101
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
This workshop is intended to remediate difficulties in numbering systems, binary arithmetic, and elementary Boolean Algebra as it relates to the operation of digital computers. The workshop will also cover linear quadratic and exponential functions, including logarithms.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Math 12 and previous experience in Boolean algebra.
