## Technical Communication 1 COMM 1149
Communication Course
## Course Overview
Teaches the basic skills for effective writing and speaking in industry. Students learn the layout, content and graphic techniques for technical writing and speaking, as well as job-search documents. Students write instructions, letters, reports, and give an oral report.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
