## Technical Communication 2 for Industrial Network Cybersecurity COMM 2130
Communication Course
## Course Overview
This course develops the skills necessary to be an efficient and professional communicator in the INC field. Building on the skills learned in the Technical Communication 1 for Industrial Network Cybersecurity course in the first term, this course prepares students for the formal report and oral presentation portion of the Culminating Project. Topics include how to: conduct an effective job search and write the accompanying resume, LinkedIn profile, and cover letter; develop strategies for persuasion and for delivering negative messages; and write an effective proposal, identify the parts of a formal report, and deliver an oral presentation. Students will practice teamwork skills and participate effectively in meetings and group work. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMM 1130

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Write an effective LinkedIn profile, resume and cover letter. (3)
- Perform an informational interview (prepare and conduct). (3)
- Deliver an effective “elevator” pitch to use in networking. (4)
- Choose effective strategies for persuasion. (2)
- Write informal recommendation and progress reports. (4)
- Plan and develop a structure and outline for a formal report. (4)
- Explain proper citation methods to reference sources and avoid plagiarism. (2)
- Identify the elements of effective teamwork. (3)
- Present a persuasive oral presentation (choose persuasive strategies, prepare, and deliver). (4)
- Participate in meetings productively including giving and taking constructive feedback and handling negative messages. (3)
- Evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete and use critical thinking to develop strong arguments. (4)
