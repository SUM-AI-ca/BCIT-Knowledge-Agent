## Communications for Biotechnology COMM 3343
Communication Course
## Course Overview
​(UBC BIOT 231) This course will introduce you to the writing, speaking, research and presentation skills you will need to successfully carry out your professional communication tasks. You will learn how to organize and present technical information in various industry related documents. In COMM 3343 , your research on a specific biotechnology topic will be incorporated into both a literature review and an oral presentation. As industry requires high literacy levels, we will continue to focus on foundational writing and editing skills.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Follow a systematic process in planning and writing technical documents.
- Select and organize information to meet the needs of your audience.
- Use a style and tone appropriate for your purpose and audience.
- Write clear, concise, and correct sentences.
- Make your documents more accessible using headings, stacked lists, white space, and layout.
- Use effective organizational patterns and conventional formats in writing scientific documents.
- Prepare and use effective visuals in documents and oral presentations.
- Prepare an industry-relevant job application package.
- Prepare a literature review.
- Prepare and deliver an effective oral presentation.
- Critically evaluate information to determine if it is accurate, relevant, and complete.
