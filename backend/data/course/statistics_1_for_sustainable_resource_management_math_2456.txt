## Statistics 1 for Sustainable Resource Management MATH 2456
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
The goals of this course are to provide the student with the statistical and exponential/logarithmic function skills needed to solve problems, which will be encountered in the Sustainable Resource Management programs and in future employment in related industrial settings. The student will be exposed to methods for effective use of calculators and the Excel spreadsheet software in some hands-on computer labs. The course will involve: 1. the application of exponentials and logarithms to rates of growth and other situations 2. introductory level statistics. Includes descriptive statistics; measures of central tendency, variation and skewness; probability laws and distributions; inferences from one and two samples; estimation of sample size; estimation of parameters from various sampling designs. These methods are applied to examples chosen from the Sustainable Resource Management field.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Solve exponential and logarithmic equations and apply these concepts to applications like growth and decay.
- Present an organized summary of the basic statistical properties of a data set, and draw appropriate conclusions from it regarding a related population.
- Demonstrate the application of statistical methods to problems of estimation in the Sustainable Resource Management field.
- Explain the meaning and importance of the underlying assumptions behind the statistical procedures commonly used in the Sustainable Resource Management field.
