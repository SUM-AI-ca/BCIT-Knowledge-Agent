## Online Learning - Computer and Study Skills for Success MOOC 0120
Massive Open Online Courses Course
## Course Overview
​This light-hearted independent study course will cover two areas we all need to be more competent and confident in the world of computers and online learning: 1) Computer basics, and 2) Skills for online learning success. The course is designed for you to complete as a whole, or to pick and choose the areas you feel you need the most help. Self-assessments will help you identify the areas you need to focus on, and learning activities throughout the course will help you practice both your computer skills, and your learning skills as you use the computer to learn about learning using the computer to learn. Think about it! *Note: Thank you for your interest in a BCIT – Free Online Learning course. You are about to register for the course. If you are new to BCIT, you will first need to create a BCIT ID. Then, log in with your ID and password to register. Once registered, log in to learn.bcit.ca with your BCIT email address and password to access your online course. If you are new to BCIT, it may take up to 24 hours for your course access to become active. This would include Student Aid and other financial assistance. Enjoy your learning and we’re happy to support your lifelong learning journey.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify hardware and software requirements
- Describe browsers and web sites
- Describe Learning Management Systems (LMS)
- Work with LMS tools
- Search the web effectively and efficiently
- Understand the importance of critical thinking when on the Internet
- Develop effective independent learning strategies
- Develop effective class participation strategies
- Work with interactive learning activities
