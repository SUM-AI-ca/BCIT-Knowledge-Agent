## Overview

The Business of Sawmilling Math Readiness Tool is intended for students interested in applying to the Business of Sawmilling Associate Certificate Program, and provides them with the opportunity to assess and receive feedback about their comfort with the various types of math they will encounter during the program. The purpose of this tool is not to exclude people, but rather to give them information about either their readiness to tackle the program, or to identify areas of weakness that it would be beneficial to brush up on prior to entering the program.
