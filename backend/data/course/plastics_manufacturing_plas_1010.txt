## Plastics Manufacturing PLAS 1010
Plastics Course
## Course Overview
Introduces plastics manufacturing. Information is presented on the scope of the plastics industry, the basic composition of plastics materials, health, safety and environmental considerations. The various forms of plastics and ingredients added to plastics are reviewed. The plastics families, their properties and how to use those properties are determined and discussed in detail. An overview of the various plastics manufacturing processes is presented.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Comprehend the scope of the plastics industry.
- Describe the nature and characteristics of polymers including the basic reactions forming a polymer and how polymer structures differ.
- Identify and mitigate the health and safety risks in processing polymers.
- Discuss the environmental implications using and recycling plastics.
- Select and specify a variety of additives for differing end use requirements.
- Describe the various raw material and finished product forms of plastics.
- Carry out a variety of tests for plastics and explain their relevance.
- Describe the various techniques used to process and manufacture plastics.
