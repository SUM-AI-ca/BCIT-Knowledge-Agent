## Nurse Practitioner CRNBC Clinical Practice Evaluation for BC Nurse Practitioner Certification NSNP 0100
Nurse Practitioner (Adult) Course
## Course Overview
This clinical practice evaluation course has been designed for RNs from outside of BC who have formal NP education but have not graduated at a Master's level in their NP studies and are applying for CRNBC registration as a nurse practitioner (Family), NP (F). Individuals must be currently participating in the CRNBC Nurse Practitioner Competency Assessment Process (NP CAP) and have demonstrated successful completion in the case study and oral interview NP CAP requirements. The course is a supervised clinical practice experience of a minimum of 80 hours in a family practice setting that allows the student to demonstrate they are able to work with a range of clients at the level and scope required for practice as a nurse practitioner in BC. The course focus is on the demonstration and evaluation of the competencies required for practice as an NP in BC, not on the acquisition of new learning or required clinical hours.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Phase one and two of the College of Registered Nurses of British Columbia Nurse Practitioner Competency Assessment Process.
