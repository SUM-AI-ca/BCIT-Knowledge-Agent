## Quality Assurance in Diagnostic Radiology MIMG 7010
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
Through readings, internet activities, assignments, and teletutoring support, addresses the major concepts and issues surrounding continuous quality improvements (CQI) in diagnostic radiology. First, outlines quality management concepts and procedures, followed by an analysis of quality control (QC) testing for radiology and fluoroscopy systems, including digital radiography and fluoroscopy systems. Additionally, reviews QC testing for film processing, conventional tomography and mammography systems. Concludes with an evaluation of a repeat film analysis and examines radiation protection as a significant part of a radiology CQI program.

## Learning Outcomes
On completion of this course, you will be able to:
- Outline and apply the concepts of total quality management (TQM) to quality assurance (QA) and quality control (QC) in diagnostic radiology.
- Evaluate the results of selected QC test procedures for radiographic imaging systems.
- Outline the general principles of fluoroscopic imaging systems.
- Evaluate the results of selected QC test procedures for fluoroscopic imaging systems.
- Describe QC test procedures for digital radiography fluoroscopy systems, including laser cameras and the PACS QC process.
- Describe basic QC test procedures for the dark room and radiographic film processors.
- Analyze major parameters and QC monitoring of conventional tomography systems.
- Outline QC activities designed to monitor and improve the technical aspects of mammography.
- Outline, conduct, and evaluate a reject-repeat film analysis.
- Outline the administrative responsibilities elements of radiation protection inherent in a CQI program for diagnostic radiology.
- Outline the major considerations and elements of a radiation safety program for diagnostic radiology.
