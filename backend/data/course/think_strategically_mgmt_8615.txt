## Think Strategically MGMT 8615
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstates skills in developing a strategic perspective and understanding strategic models.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Develop a Strategic Perspective
- Define the term strategic perspectives.
- Enhance your strategic thinking skills.
- Understand Strategic Models
- Summarize strategic analysis models and select the one(s) most useful in assessing the strategic planning process in your workplace.
- Assess your organization's approach to strategic planning using selected models.
