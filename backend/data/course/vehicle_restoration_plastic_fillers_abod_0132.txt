## Vehicle Restoration: Plastic Fillers ABOD 0132
Automotive Collision Repair/Refinish Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the enthusiast to the equipment and techniques used to apply / shape various types of fillers used in the auto body trade. The student will learn to mix, apply and sand polyester fillers. The student will also learn to use various fibreglass repair materials.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe safety procedures / personal protective equipment (PPE) used when working with fillers.
- Describe various types of plastic fillers.
- Describe equipment and techniques used to apply / shape fillers.
- Describe the equipment and techniques used to apply / shape fibreglass.
- Apply and shape fillers.
- Apply and shape fibreglass.
