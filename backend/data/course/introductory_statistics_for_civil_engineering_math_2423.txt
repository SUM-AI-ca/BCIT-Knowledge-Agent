## Introductory Statistics for Civil Engineering MATH 2423
Mathematics Course
## Course Overview
​An introduction to statistical analysis with topics including the organization and graphical presentation of data, basic summary statistics such as mean, standard deviation. Elements of probability theory as well as Normal distribution are introduced. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MATH 1422
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Organize quantitative experimental data into tables and produce histograms. [1, 3, 5]
- Compute common statistical measures and explain their application. [1]
- Apply basic formulas of probability. [1]
- Perform calculations involving the standard and general normal distributions. [1]​
Effective as of Fall 2025
