## Security Management for IT Professionals Level 1 SSMT 7230
Security Systems Management Course
## Course Overview
This Level 1 course provides an overview of the major areas of information security management. It covers topics such as security management practices; security architecture and models; law, investigation, liability and ethics; business continuity and disaster recovery planning; and physical security. This course provides added value by demonstrating the real world application of the concepts and principles discussed. It lays a firm foundation for subsequent study in the information security field and provides the underpinnings and background for the field of information security management. The practitioner is left equipped with a range of methodologies and tools they can bring to bear on practical information security issues they may come across in their careers. The practical hands-on labs and homework assignments will provide students with direct experience of the tools and techniques discussed.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Admission to a Forensics credential program or permission of the Program Head.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain the four main types of threats to a given organization's information assets.
- Explain the role of the IT security manager with respect to identifying and securing an organization's information assets to prevent electronic intrusion
- Describe the impact of privacy legislation on IT security policies and procedures.
- Evaluate an existing security model in terms of its confidentiality and integrity.
- Explain the legal and privacy requirements for the protection of information assets, both locally and internationally.
- Discuss how ethical behaviour contributes to the security of information.
- Develop a Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) for a given organization.
- Develop a Business Continuity Plan (BCP) for a given organization.
- Explain how physical and personnel security contribute to the safeguarding of digital assets.
