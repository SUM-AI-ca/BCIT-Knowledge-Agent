## Basic AutoCAD AICO 1001
Computer Aided Design & Engineering Course
## Course Overview
An introduction to AutoCAD including basic 2D drawing creation, editing, view manipulation, text, dimensioning, hatching, plotting, blocks and digitizing. Also includes a review of computer fundamentals and is intended for those with limited computer experience.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Basic drafting knowledge
