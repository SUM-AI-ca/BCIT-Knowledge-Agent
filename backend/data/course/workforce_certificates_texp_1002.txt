## Workforce Certificates TEXP 1002
Trades Exploration Program Course
## Course Overview
​Students will have an opportunity to earn recognized workforce certificates (subject to availability); Fall Protection, Confined Space Entry and Stand by Attendant, Lift Truck Operator, Mobile Elevated Work Platform. Students must earn at least three workforce certificates. ​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
Confined Space Entry and Stand by Attendant
- Identify safety and health hazards of confined spaces,
- Identify and perform hazardous atmospheres and testing procedures
- Identify control methods for hazardous atmospheres; and/or safety and health hazards
Fall Protection
- Comprehend Fall Protection Fundamentals & Regulations
- Understand Methods of Eliminating the Fall Hazard
- Identify the Components of Personal Travel Restrain & Fall Arrest Systems
- Understand Equipment Specifications, Limitations, Care & Inspection
- Understand Methods of Proper Planning When Controlling Fall Hazards
- Understand the Need for Rescue Planning & Preparedness
Lift Truck Operator
- Identify hazards, preform pre-operational checks,
- Follow safe operating procedures for Sit Down Counterbalance Lift Trucks Class1, 4, and 5​
Mobile Elevated Work Platform
- Demonstrate appropriate pre-use checks on an MEWP and complete a checklist.
- Use the correct anchor point on an MEWP and don an appropriate safety harness.
- Maneuver an MEWP safely – raising, lowering, traversing appropriately.
Effective as of Winter 2026
