## Automotive Welding Refresher AUTO 0021
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
This course reintroduces students to the fundamental skills related to handling heating and welding equipment, including safety, equipment shutdown and storage, oxyacetylene torches, and metal inert gas. Completing this supplementary course will help students enrolled in automotive programs meet the welding requirements of their program. ​​​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in ASTO 1020 or 70% in AUTO 1500

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe welding and cutting safety procedures
- Demonstrate oxyacetylene welding and cutting procedures
- Describe gas metal arc welding procedures
- Perform gas metal arc welding
