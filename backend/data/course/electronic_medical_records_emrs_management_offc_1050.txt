## Electronic Medical Records (EMRs) Management OFFC 1050
Office Skills Training Course
## Course Overview
With many physicians converting their practices over to electronic medical records, taking the Electronic Medical Records (EMRs) Management course will train students in all aspects of EMR software using Wolf Medical Systems. This course will teach students how to implement, use and be familiar with EMRs. Students will be taught the skills and given hands-on practical experience, both from an administrative and clinical perspective necessary to navigate EMRs with ease and manage the input and retrieval of electronic data with confidence. This course is part of the MOA Program but may be taken on its own by former MOA graduates and other interested healthcare workers.
### Prerequisite(s)
- OFFC 1001 , OFFC 1002 , OFFC 1006 , OFFC 1007 , OFFC 1060 ; must also be computer literate, proficient in basic Microsoft Word, proficient in English and typing speed of 35 wpm or better.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Apply computer skills using Wolf Medical Systems software
- Describe the core functions of an electronic medical record system
- Analyze an EMR system
- Organize an EMR system
- Implement an EMR system
- Generate accurate medical billing using Wolf Medical Systems
- Configure an electronic appointment book
- Manage EMR administrative documents accurately
- Manage EMR clinical documents accurately
- Increase typing speed with 5 minute timings
