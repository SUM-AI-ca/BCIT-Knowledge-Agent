## Community and Public Relations for Aviation AIRC 3105
Airport/Airline Core Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces public relations techniques and strategies in an airport/airline operations environment. Topics included are issue management, media relations, and speaking to the media. Evaluation includes a report, media package and examination.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe information management techniques for community and public relations.
- Analyze theories and practices of public relations.
- Produce a public relations and media package.
- Describe community relations as it relates to the aviation environment.
- Describe community relations as it pertains to crisis management. ​
Effective as of Fall 2022
