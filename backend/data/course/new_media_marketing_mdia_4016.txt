## New Media Marketing MDIA 4016
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course is an overview of web, mobile and social media marketing. The emphasis will be on developing a cost-effective web and social media marketing campaign for small businesses. Special focus will be placed on marketing tactics and advertisement programs for mobile, web and social media. Students will develop a general awareness of diverse advertising campaigns including the understanding of storytelling, metadata, search engine promotion, on-line branding, domain names, blog monetarization, ads standards, microsites, email advertising, and trends in these fields. Students will be assessed by a combination of quizzes, group and individual projects.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the important role of Web marketing tactics in Web design
- Assess and evaluate new media marketing/advertising requirements.
- Explain the adjustments of traditional marketing principles for interactive media communications.
- Identify target markets and the forces affecting web, mobile and social media communication.
- Evaluate advertising programs for web, mobile and social media.
- Develop tactics for optimizing content to be noticed by major search engines.
- Recognize the importance of traditional and interactive marketing outputs.
- Explain the benefits and the drawbacks of advertising on the web, mobile and social media.
- Plan and develop a cross platforms marketing campaign that will effectively promote products and services.
