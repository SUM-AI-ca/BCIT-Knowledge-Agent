## Employment Skills Upgrading VENT 0140
Venture Development Centre Course
## Course Overview
BCIT's Business Skills Training program will help you attain a hands-on practical business education that will allow you to immediately make a positive impact in the small and medium sized business sectors, the recognized engine of BC's economy. You will acquire relevant business skills, receive up-to-date training with computer based business applications, and gain an understanding of what an appropriate career path might be. Whether you're considering establishing a career in business, contemplating a career change, or wanting to update your skills, the BCIT Business Skills Training program will help you meet your business training goals.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be have and/or be able to;
- Understand the fundamentals of running a small to medium sized business in today's business environment.
- Acquire essential business skills, including marketing plan development and market research.
- Learn how to assist in the development and implementation of a marketing strategy detailing competition and positioning, promotion, pricing, and placement.
- Acquire a solid understanding of bookkeeping that will enable you to assist in the preparation and interpretation of financial statements.
- Enhance your written and verbal business communication skills.
- Gain a working knowledge of the computer as a business tool using Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and the Internet.
- Understand and apply human resource tools such as time and stress management, goal setting, labour standards, and business law.
- Use the knowledge and tools acquired throughout the program to develop and implement a relevant career strategy.
