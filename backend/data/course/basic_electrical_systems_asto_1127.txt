## Basic Electrical Systems ASTO 1127
Auto Service Tech & Operations Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the fundamental skills required for the service, repair, and diagnosis of basic automotive electrical systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ASTO 1120
### Credits
5.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Interpret basic wiring and electrical systems.
- Diagnose, repair, and service 12-volt batteries.
- Inspect and replace exterior lamps.
- Demonstrate basic scan tool functions.
