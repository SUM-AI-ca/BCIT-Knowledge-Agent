## Creative Industries Capstone CREA 8200
Creative Industries Course
## Course Overview
​This capstone course provides students with the opportunity to integrate and synthesize their learning from previous terms. Working in groups or individually, students will create a business plan for their own creative business, which can be initiated during the course or executed directly after graduation. Students will analyze their own business model, management system, strategic direction, and strengths and weaknesses in relation to the creative industry cluster in which they plan to operate. A student's project may include industry initiatives in collaboration with a local or international organization. As part of this capstone project, the students may specialize in a single discipline or bridge between technical specializations to develop original products and/or services.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- All courses in Terms 2 & 3 must be successfully completed prior to starting this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify business opportunities related to personal career goals that drive innovation and develop commercially valuable projects and businesses
- Demonstrate knowledge of strategic planning, competitive analysis, and ethical standards in a culturally diverse environment
- Design value propositions and business models based on business development, branding, marketing, and socio-economic principles
- Prepare a comprehensive business plan including business, financial, operations and marketing strategies for a creative business
- Research and analyze local and global markets to generate ideas for segmenting, targeting, and positioning their own businesses/projects
- Create strategies for launching and marketing credible products and services
- Integrate feedback from peers, faculty and industry when conceptualizing and carrying out a creative project
- Demonstrate leadership and creativity when anticipating problems related to project execution
- Prepare a product or service with constructive and support feedback from faculty and classmates
- Demonstrate effective and persuasive communication using a variety of communication skills and tools to express ideas, findings, data analysis, and recommendations ​
