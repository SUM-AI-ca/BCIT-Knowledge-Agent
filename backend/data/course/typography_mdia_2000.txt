## Typography MDIA 2000
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course covers typographic development, typesetting terminology, printers' measurements and font formats. Using Adobe Indesign, students will create industry-standard documents.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MDIA 1049 and 50% in MDIA 2049
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Comprehend type terminology: x-height, small caps; ligatures; accents; etc..
- Use standard measurements and printers' measurements: Imperial; Metric; Pica; Agate.
- Control font size, leading, tracking, kerning, word spacing, to produce aesthetically pleasing documents.
- Apply typesetting rules and relationships: measure; paragraph spacing; indentation; subheads, etc.
- Track and custom kern headline type.
- Choose fonts suitable to subject.
- Use proofreading marks.
