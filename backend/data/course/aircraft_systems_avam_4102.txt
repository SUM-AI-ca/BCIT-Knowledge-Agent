## Aircraft Systems AVAM 4102
Aircraft Maintenance Course
## Course Overview
A course designed to provide you with the theoretical understanding and practical skills needed for the operation and inspection of fire detection and suppression, ice and rain protection, environmental control, pressurization, oxygen, and fuel systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
6.0

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course, the learner will be able to:
- Explain the procedure used to test, troubleshoot, repair, adjust, remove and replace airframe and related systems in small and large aircraft.
- Explain aircraft system operation to the component level.
- Explain function of thermal switches, thermocouples, continuous loop fire detection and pressure type fire detection system.
- Describe operation, routing, installation and inspection of sensors.
- Discuss fire suppression systems.
- Perform fire protection and detection system installation and operational tests.
- Inspect, test and maintain fire protection and detection systems.
- Explain ice detection and anti-icing systems operation and maintenance.
- Locate and identify ice control system components.
- Operate, troubleshoot, clean and carry out maintenance of ice control system.
- Explain rain control systems.
- Explain the operation, maintenance and servicing of pneumatic systems.
- Explain the fundamentals of heating, cooling, and ventilation systems.
- Identify components, inspect, test and maintain heating systems.
- Explain electrical/ electronic equipment cooling systems.
- Describe air conditioning servicing procedures.
- Describe maintenance, operation and control of pressurization system components.
- Perform the inspection and servicing of environmental control system.
- Explain aircraft fuel types, properties and additives.
- Explain airframe fuel system configurations and component functions.
- Describe fuel system operation.
- Describe multi-engine and helicopter fuel systems.
- Perform a function check on a fuel quantity indicating system.
- Explain standard practices and operational checks, inspection and certification of aircraft systems.
