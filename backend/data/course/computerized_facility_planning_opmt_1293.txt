## Computerized Facility Planning OPMT 1293
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the principles and practices of computerized facility planning techniques. The programs include: Automated Layout Design Program (ALDEP), Computerized Relationship Layout Planning (CORELAP), Computerized Relative Allocation of Facility Techniques (CRAFT), Computerized Facilities and Design (COFAD), and Plant Layout Evaluation Technique (PLANET).
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Know the categories of computer programs used for facility layout.
- Understand the advantages and disadvantages of computerized layout.
- Explain the theory of operation of computerized layout techniques.
- Know the input and output requirements for the construction and improvement algorithms used with computerized techniques.
