## People Analytics and Technology HRMG 4500
Human Resource Management Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces people analytics, artificial intelligence (AI) tools and human resources technology as a strategic advantage in HR management, equipping students with the skills to harness data and technological tools for strategic HR partnership. The focus extends to the broader landscape of HR technology and its application in data-driven decision-making. Students will learn to identify their organization's right human resources information system (HRIS), assess relevant AI tools, generate insightful reports, and leverage analytics to drive organizational success. Emphasis will be placed on understanding key metrics, predictive analytics, and the latest HR technology trends to prepare future strategic HR professionals.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify the fundamentals of people analytics and their role in strategic HR management and decision-making.
- Describe the latest trends and advancements in HR technology, including being open to AI developments and their impact on HR processes and strategies.
- Effectively assess and select an HRIS while considering the organization's size, HRIS maturity, and specific business needs.
- Make data-driven HR decisions that enhance business outcomes.
- Describe predictive analytics in HR decision-making to uncover insights and trends in employee data.
- Identify key HR metrics aligned with organizational goals to drive strategic initiatives and measure efficiency.
- Identify the importance of data security, privacy regulations, and best practices in managing critical employee data within HR systems.
- Apply diversity, equity, and inclusion principles into people analytics, ensuring that HR systems accommodate diverse identities and support accessibility.​
