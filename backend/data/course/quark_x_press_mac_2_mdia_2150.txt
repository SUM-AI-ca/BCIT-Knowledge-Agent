## Quark X Press MAC 2 MDIA 2150
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Teaches advanced publishing techniques using QuarkXpress including integration with work processing and graphics software and the use of spot and process colours. Students will have their own workstation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MDIA 1150
