## Air Barrier Installation Fundamentals XZEB 1135
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
​This course establishes a practical, field-ready training program for insulation and sheathing sub-trades, as well as other trades whose work impacts the air barrier in residential buildings. The program develops: Technical understanding of air barrier systems, hands-on installation competency, awareness of common failure points, ability to contribute to airtightness targets and blower door readiness and coordination awareness across trades. The course supports builders in achieving consistent airtightness performance and establishes a recognizable competencies that can be used for hiring.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- ​Explain why airtightness matters in residential construction, including impacts on moist​ure, durability, energy use, comfort, and occupant health.
- Distinguish clearly between air barrier and vapour barrier functions.
- Describe exterior and interior air barrier approaches.
- Install air barrier membranes, transitions, and penetrations in accordance with best practices and manufacturer requirements.
- Test installations with common site tests (smoke and infrared).
- Identify common site deficiencies that contribute to blower door test failures.
