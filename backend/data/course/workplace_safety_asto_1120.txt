## Workplace Safety ASTO 1120
Auto Service Tech & Operations Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to safe work practices in the automotive shop, including Workplace Hazardous Materials Management System (WHMIS) and WorkSafeBC regulations.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe a safe work environment.
- Apply safe work practices.
- Describe hybrid and electric vehicle safety.
