## Electric Vehicle Technology AUTO 0171
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
Introduces electric vehicle technology to technicians and hobbyists interested in electric vehicles. Provides a basic understanding of the components, systems and technology in both production and converted electric vehicles.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Have knowledge of the hazards and dangers of working with high-power electric drive and battery systems.
- Identify and understand the basic functions of electric vehicle components.
- Understand different battery and electric motor technologies.
- Understand the different performance characteristics between gasoline and electric drive trains.
- Make an educated decision whether to buy a new production electric vehicle or convert a gas vehicle.
