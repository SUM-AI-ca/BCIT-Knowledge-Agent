## Communicating through Conflict CLCR 1070
Call Centre Course
## Course Overview
From the serious to the not-so-serious, and from the straightforward to the complex and difficult, conflict is a fact of life - it's really just another way we communicate. In this workshop, you will raise your awareness of phrases, body language and approaches that can escalate conflict in person and over the telephone. You'll reflect on your communication style, and the impact your style has on communication in conflict, and learn a seven-step process to resolve conflict through communication.
