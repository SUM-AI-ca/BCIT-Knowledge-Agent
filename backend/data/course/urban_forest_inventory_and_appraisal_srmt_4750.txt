## Urban Forest Inventory and Appraisal SRMT 4750
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces inventory techniques commonly used in urban forest environments. General methods and techniques for sampling greenbelt areas, riparian forests, urban forests and street trees will be presented. The course includes an integrated project. Students will conduct tree inventories in various environments such as the BCIT Woodlot, the BCIT Burnaby Campus and Queen Elizabeth Park.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze the principles for the valuation and appraisal of trees and forests.
- Perform tree replacement appraisal methods.
- List the multiple values in urban forest environments .
- Create an inventory design that reflects specific management values and objectives .
- Apply different sampling methods such as point sampling, 3-P and line transects.
- Apply estimate techniques for tree and other vegetation cover.
- Calculate tree volumes, biomass and forest fuel loading.
- Compare the uses of photographic and other remote sensing techniques commonly used in urban inventories.
- Assess wildlife trees and hazard trees for safety and biodiversity.
