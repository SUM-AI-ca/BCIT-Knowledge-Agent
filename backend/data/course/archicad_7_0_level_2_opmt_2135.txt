## ArchiCAD 7.0 Level 2 OPMT 2135
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
The Level 2 course will cover advanced modeling, rendering and construction drawing creation. The students will learn how to create custom objects, explore animation and texturing, and to properly import and export to other file formats. A set of construction working drawings will be created with ArchiCAD's companion software, Plotmaker. Personal exploration of ArchiCAD will be encouraged through a final project of the student's choosing.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in OPMT 1135
