## Civil Tech Project TSYH 4195
Transystems Course
## Course Overview
Students are required to contact a registered professional in the Civil Engineering / Construction industry and formulate an industry applicable project. The project should involve the investigation of a current relevant problem for the industry client. The Student is required to submit a project proposal for approval.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
