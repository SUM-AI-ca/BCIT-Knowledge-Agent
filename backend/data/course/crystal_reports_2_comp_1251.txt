## Crystal Reports 2 COMP 1251
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This hands-on, 6 week workshop continues from COMP 1250 and builds upon the basic end-user understanding of Crystal Reports v2011. Students who already understand how to design and create reports are shown many options for enhanced formatting with Crystal Reports built-in features. They also learn valuable lessons that apply to multiple versions and distributions of Crystal Reports. Topics include: Cross-tabs, Charting, Dynamic Cascading Prompts, Sub-reports, and advanced drill down techniques. By the end of this course, successful participants will know how to produce a variety of different charts and ways to depict data, plus be prepared to start COMP 1253 , Crystal Reports Advanced Workshop. Those who plan to take COMP 1250 in the same term should instead register for the complete 12 week course COMP 2252 , Business Intelligence with Crystal Reports, which covers both fundamental and advanced topics.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 1002 and COMP 2010 ; or an equivalent understanding of MS Windows 7 and MS Office 2010 or 2013.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use functions, operators, variables and arrays.
- Build a template without the data source.
- Define and create parameters.
- Build and format a cross-tab.
- Use the Running Total Expert.
- Create a form letter.
- Build a report with alerts.
- Build a Top N report.
