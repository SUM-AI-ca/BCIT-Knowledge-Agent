## Integrated Land Management SRMT 4910
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
This course reviews the interactions among different government agencies, industry, and public interest groups in the planning and land management process. It introduces integrated resources planning as an instrument of natural resource policy. The course reviews the purpose of integrated resources planning for major industries in the province, typical steps in planning processes, and the benefits and challenges to planning. The course surveys the history of natural resource planning in BC and describes in detail, strategic and operational planning. Current and emerging changes are reviewed, and examples of strategic and operational level planning initiatives are discussed. The relationships between various plans and their roles in monitoring performance will be highlighted. Various information sources related to planning and management processes will reviewed, as well as an overview of common planning tools. Students will have the opportunity to work in teams to prepare portions of plans, to participate in mock planning processes, and to critically analyze examples of plans.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the current planning initiatives and legislation related to planning for natural resources management.
- Identify the key strategic and operational planning tools used in natural resources management.
- Describe the inter-relationships between high level, strategic and operational plans, and the importance of these relationships for effective resource management.
- Develop an operational level plan with effective goals, objectives, and strategy statements.
- Provide an overview of the key planning issues related to resource management sectors such as forestry, mining, recreation, tourism, agriculture, range, wildlife, fisheries.
- Critically analyze various types of plans to assess whether the plan effectively addresses key resource issues.
- Explain some of the challenges of planning for multiple resource values on the same land-base through participation in role play exercises.
- Research and analyze existing information related to key resource management issues for strategic and operational planning levels.
- Use GIS technology to produce maps and reports supporting strategic and operational level plans.
