## Corporate Communications CDEP 3130
Design Essentials Program Course
## Course Overview
This course builds upon CEDE 310: Introduction to Brand Identity. It provides an overview of the many facets of corporate communications. Effective corporate communications require a visual strategy that maintains the visual personality defined through corporate identity and branding, as well as conveys specific messages. Students will apply their design skills to respond to internal and external communications needs.
### Prerequisite(s)
- CEDE 310: Introduction to Brand Identity.

2.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, you will be able to:
- Build upon the systematic visual language developed in CEDE 310 and apply it across a range of media.
- Build upon the systematic visual language developed in CEDE 310 and apply it across a range of media.
- Apply a brand identity to specific communication objectives such as public awareness, customer loyalty, staff and employee retention, increased sales, and company growth.
- Determine various communication vehicles and their distinct messaging.
- Assess specific communication needs through a design audit.
- Develop a corporate communications plan.
- Apply visual standards to an expanded family of corporate communications.
- Distinguish the messaging needs between an organization’s internal and external audiences.
- Deconstruct communication strategies to determine what makes them successful/unsuccessful.
