## CIT Program Fundamentals ACIT 1100
Computer Information Technology Course
## Course Overview
The course covers topics of importance to new and continuing students on how to balance school and related activities to enhance chances of success in their academic careers. In this course students will learn a variety of different techniques to improve study habits, note taking, and time management skills. The class will also present other topics of interest to CIT students and will introduce students to various support systems at BCIT.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Determine alternate paths to completion for the CIT program.
- List several job opportunities for CIT graduates.
- Make an informed decision about applying to the co-op program.
- Find available BCIT and online resources.
- Identify and follow BCIT policies.
- Apply time management techniques to the CIT schedule.
- Explain the strengths and weaknesses of different critical thinking strategies and techniques.
- Demonstrate multiple note-taking techniques.
- Identify how to apply different test taking techniques and strategies.
