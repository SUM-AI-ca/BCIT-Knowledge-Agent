## ASCM 2000 Planning and Inventory Management, Certified in CPIM 9.0 ASCM 2000
Asstion for Supply Chain Mgmt Course
## Course Overview
CPIM 9.0 combines key concepts from the field of operations management. It details nine domains of operations management. These include: [1] Supply Chains and Strategy, [2] Sales and Operations Planning, [3] Demand, [4] Internal Supply, [5] External Supply and Key Support Functions [6] Inventory, [7] Detailed Schedules, [8] Distribution, [9] Quality, Business Investments, and Continuous Improvement. This comprehensive training course provides the student with the information and techniques to thoroughly understand the APICS body of knowledge for the Certiﬁed in Planning and Inventory Management (CPIM) exam. This course is the only single resource available that is designed specifically to help candidates pass the ASCM/APICS CPIM 9.0 exam. The APICs learning system materials provided in this course include printed handouts, knowledge progress checks, practice quizzes, practice exams and case studies.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- ​Identify the topics covered in the ASMC/ APICS CPIM 9.0 c ertiﬁcation exam.​
- Use the ASCM/APICS CPIM online learning portal to comprehend the CPIM exam topics
- Identify the topics covered in the ASCM/APICS CPIM 9.0 version Understand the business environment in which an organization operates, align business and functional strategies, and make decisions to support competitive advantage.
- Evaluate supply and demand at an aggregate level, reconcile product/service portfolios, demand, supply, and financial plans while considering trade-offs.
- Understand the markets in which an organization chooses to operate, and how to align an organization's resources and business strategies.
- Learn how to manage inventory in support of an organization's resource availability, business and functional strategies, and tactical planning.
- Understand how to implement both material and capacity plans, including the adjustment of schedules, queues, and work prioritization to meet demand and inventory goals.
- Learn how to design a distribution network while considering the various investment cost and service trade-offs, and competitive priorities.
- Understand the use of appropriate technology tools and emerging technologies that support supply chain processes.
