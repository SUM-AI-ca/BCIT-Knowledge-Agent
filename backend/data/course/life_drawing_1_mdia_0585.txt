## Life Drawing 1 MDIA 0585
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This is a foundation Life Drawing course in which students will explore various art and media. Students will draw the human form and be given instruction in how to apply these skills to design, traditional and digital animation, digital modeling and game art.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

0.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explore the importance of the gesture.
- Draw a solid gesture.
- Understand the importance of a professional working sketch book.
- Study general human proportions and foreshortening.
- Strengthen observation and measurement skills.
- Explore and apply line, shadow and texture to a life drawing.
- Gain basic knowledge of drapery.
- Create a professional life drawing portfolio.
- Improve overall drawing ability.
