## Adobe Photoshop Power User Tips and Tricks MDIA 2280
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This 6 hour workshop shows you how to unlock the hidden power of channels. Learn how to make your images go from flat to fantastic in just seconds, make impossible selections and intricate masks, repair damaged, noisy and problem images, use sharpening techniques in LAB mode and create special effects that can only be created using channels and filters. Students will also learn how to use the new camera raw features, the channel mixer command and other advanced Photoshop tips and techniques.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ( MDIA 1180 and MDIA 2180 ) or ( MDIA 1170 and MDIA 2270 )

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explore the revolutionary vanishing point features.
- Create smart objects for performing non-destructive scaling and rotating.
- Work with multiple layer control.
- Master the camera raw features for adjusting tonal and exposure changes.
- Create packaging mock-ups and other dimensional effects by wrapping an image around a shape.
- Adjust luminance, brightness and contract independently in the LAB mode.
- Polish digital photographs with advanced noise correction tools.
- Create and edit 32-bit images, and combine multiple exposures int a single, 32-bit image with an expanded tonal range from the deepest shadows to the brightest highlights.
- Use the power of channels to colour correct images.
- Create customable workspaces and menus.
- Retouch photographs with tonal and exposure controls with the spot healing brush.
- Create accurate and precise paths.
- Apply and adjust gradients to shapes layers while allowing for opacity changes.
