## Heavy Duty Equipment Technician IP Refresher HDMX 0140
Heavy Duty Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
This course is for current Heavy Duty Equipment Technician Level 4 apprentices. The course will provide a theory refresher of Levels 1, 2 and 3 content in preparation for the Heavy Duty Equipment Technician Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Current Heavy Duty Equipment Technician Level 4 apprentice
### Credits
0.0
### Registration requirements
Departmental approval is required to register for this course. You must have approval from the department to register. Students accepted into a program can register directly.
### Domestic fees
$379.68 - $387.80 See individual course offerings below for actual costs.
International fees are typically 3.4 times the domestic tuition. Exact cost will be calculated upon completion of registration.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be:
- Prepared to write the Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination.
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2017
