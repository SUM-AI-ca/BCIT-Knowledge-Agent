## Fundamentals of Cybersecurity Threats and Attacks XINC 2120
Cybersecurity Professionals Microcredentials Course
## Course Overview
The badge explores the physical and logical structure of a computer network. Students will learn to identify the devices and connections used in a network and will be able to explain the operation of the network. Several types of network attacks will be explored, and students will learn how to classify these attacks for effective retrieval, mitigation, and communication.​
