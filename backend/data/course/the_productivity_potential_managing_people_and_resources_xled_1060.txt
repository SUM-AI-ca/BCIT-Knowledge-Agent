## The Productivity Potential-Managing People and Resources XLED 1060
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
People thrive when they can see how their work contributes to the purpose of the organization in a meaningful way. When managing people and resources, supervisors need to have a clear understanding of the role of the employee, a process for task identification and delegation, and common approaches to problem-solving and decision-making. By consistently utilizing your processes and practices, employees will be able to predict and understand expectations. This workshop will help you define the role of a supervisor and how you can leverage the “Productivity Potential” by aligning the work, people, and resources.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the nature of supervision, the role of a supervisor and the competencies that spark and fuel productivity.
- Identify barriers and solutions to increase productivity
- Describe elements that create and maintain a motivating environment.
