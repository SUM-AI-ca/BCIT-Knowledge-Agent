## Introduction to Cost Estimation for Timber Construction TMBR 1002
Mass Timber Construction Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to the basic principles of cost estimation for mass timber construction.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in TMBR 1000
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Define the concept of estimating.
- Describe the role of estimating in construction projects.
- Identify the basic tools for estimating construction projects.
- Compare basic types of estimates used in the different stages of project design.
- Describe the basic estimating process.
- Identify the role of cost estimating for deciding between mass timber and other materials.
- Define and compare basic life cycle costs for wood frame, mass timber, steel and concrete.
- Explain the basic steps to compile a complete estimate.
- Explain the key considerations for pricing mass timber.
- Describe the major cost drivers for mass timber.
- Compare the main cost differences between mass timber, steel and concrete structures.
- Identify basic cost considerations associated with construction projects.
- Describe ways to achieve cost efficiencies in mass timber projects.
- Identify the basic procurement routes for mass timber projects.
- Discuss the long term costs and sustainability of mass timber projects.
