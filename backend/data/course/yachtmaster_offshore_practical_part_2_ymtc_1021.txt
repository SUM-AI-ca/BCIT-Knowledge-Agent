## Yachtmaster Offshore (Practical) Part 2 YMTC 1021
Yachtmaster Coastal Course
## Course Overview
Yachtmaster Offshore certificate is a Master's certificate of competency for operating commercial yachts up to 200 tons within 150nm of a safe haven overseas. This training involves a 5 day practical aspect (part 2) live-on-board a power or a sail yacht. Applicants must obtain both certificates offshore theory (part 1) and offshore practical (part 2) within 12 months.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
