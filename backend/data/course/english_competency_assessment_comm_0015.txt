## English Competency Assessment COMM 0015
Communication Course
## Course Overview
​This computer-based assessment is designed to help determine if an individual's English language skills are at a level necessary to meet the entrance requirements of BCIT full-time programs or Communication courses. The assessment reflects an individual's skill in reading comprehension, understanding of appropriate sentence structure, and clarity in written composition. Please visit the COMM English Assessments web page at www.bcit.ca/computing-academic-studies/communication/english-assessments/ for more information. The COMM 0015 assessment fee is non-refundable.
