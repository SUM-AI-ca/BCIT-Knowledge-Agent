## Basic Drafting for Hydronic Technicians PPGS 1041
Piping, Plumbing, Gas Course
## Course Overview
Provides instruction in the production and interpretation of orthographic and isometric drawings as they apply to the hydronic system, and flow diagrams and general assembly drawings. Formal drafting techniques including board skills will be covered. Related terms, symbols and call-out bubbles will be discussed. An introduction to AutoCAD will be included. Passing Grade is 70%
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
