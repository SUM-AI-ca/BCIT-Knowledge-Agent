## Advanced Computer Graphics for Architecture BLDG 4425
Building Course
## Course Overview
The course covers advanced techniques for modelling/materials/lighting and rendering using mental ray and 3D Studio MAX, as well as post-production concepts using Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator to create high quality professional Architectural presentation media.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BLDG 3405
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Have a better understanding of advanced tools and techniques in 3D Studio MAX for use in architecture.
- Understand the relationship between different computer applications in 2D and 3D and how to apply them to professional presentation media.
- Explore other graphic applications and use various drawing and presentation techniques that enhance the architectural design process.
