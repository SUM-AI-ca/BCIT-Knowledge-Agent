## Overview

Refreshes theoretical knowledge of the trade in preparation for the Heavy Duty Equipment Technician Certificate of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination. Contact the SkilledTradesBC (tel. 778-328-8700 or toll free within BC 1-866-660-6011) to determine eligibility and to schedule an examination.

## Credits

0.0

## Cost

$924.95

## Prerequisites

- Minimum of six years experience in the trade

## Learning Outcomes

- Prepared to write the Certification of Qualification with Red Seal Endorsement (IP) examination;
- Able to perform practical tasks with a better understanding of component operation; and
- Able to write trade exams of various formats.
