## Advertising Techniques for Radio and Television BCST 1138
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces the fundamentals of advertising for Radio and Television. Explore the rules and regulations governing advertising, the basic structures of commercials, various appeals and common approaches, production elements and presentation. Theories of advertising, audience attitudes and marketing principles will be examined as well, we will explore the relationship between the cause and effects of techniques. Students completing this course will have a thorough grasp of fundamental principles and practices required to develop advertisements for Radio/TV. Practical application of all skills taught will result in industry standard advertisements.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain how and why advertising works.
- Develop commercials to apply the basic principles of writing for Radio/TV.
- Develop copy using a variety of styles.
- Create copy using a variety of appeals.
- Create copy for a variety of formats and target demographics.
- Create copy that uses language and advertising principles correctly and effectively.
- Work effectively as a team, incorporating agency style philosophies.
- Evaluate copy written by peers and industry writers to uncover cause and affect relationship of a variety advertising techniques.
- Develop listening skills to aid in evaluation.
