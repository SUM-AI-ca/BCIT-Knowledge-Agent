## Motorcycle Maintenance 2 MCMX 0117
Motorcycle Mechanic Course
## Course Overview
Provides maintenance skills to motorcycle owners and enthusiasts. Continues from MCMX 0115 . Focuses on engine design, clutches, transmissions, and driveline principles. Combines lectures, demonstrations and hands-on training to present material.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCMX 0115 OR basic understanding of motorcycle mechanics.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Identify and compare engine components;
- Identify types of failures; and,
- Replace types of components.
