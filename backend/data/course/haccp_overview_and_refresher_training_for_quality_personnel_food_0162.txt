## HACCP: Overview & Refresher Training FOOD 0162
Food Technology Course
## Course Overview
​This course, offered as a contract between your company and BCIT, is customized to address the needs of your company's specific HACCP Program. Through group learning activities, company personnel examine how a HACCP-based food safety program operates and what their role is within the program. Participants interact with the company's own HACCP Plan, Preventive Controls or GMPs, and Standard Operating Procedures during the training session. This course is suitable for people being introduced to HACCP Programs and for people requiring refresher training. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Provide rationale for Prerequisite Programs and HACCP Plans.
- Outline how the Hazard Analysis affects company procedures and policies.
- Justify the role of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and Work Instructions in implementing the food safety program.
- Describe the food safety program responsibilities of quality assurance and quality control personnel.
- Explain how Corrective Action procedures enable continuous improvement.
- Examine SOPs for verification of Prerequisite Programs, HACCP Plans, and the HACCP Program.
