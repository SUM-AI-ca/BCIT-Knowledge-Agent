## Business Consulting Projects TDMT 4411
Transportation and Distribution Course
## Course Overview
To apply principles of market research, international business, transportation and distribution systems, economics, problem solving, inventory control, financial/cost accounting, and all other topic areas studied at BCIT to a project within an operating business or agency.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Successful completion of Levels 1, 2 and 3, but may be short one course except TDMT 3402 .
