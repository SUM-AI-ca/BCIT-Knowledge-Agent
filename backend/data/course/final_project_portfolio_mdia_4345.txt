## Final Project - Portfolio MDIA 4345
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course is the culmination of the Certificate in Web Technologies. The main goal of this course is to draw together all of the knowledge and skills that you have acquired in the Associate Certificate and Certificate programs and present them in terms of a personal online portfolio. You should consider this course in the same class as a visual artist’s design portfolio. There will be 10 assignments (portfolio pages) and each assignment should focus on a specific skill or technique, although you will also want to include other design elements to create a well-balanced page. You should approach this course in terms of creating a marketing tool you can use demonstrate your background and skillset to prospective clients. There will be no notes for this course but you may feel free to draw upon the expertise of our entire team of instructors for assistance. Assignments for this course will be assessed by a minimum of 3 instructors from the Web Technologies program. The instructors chosen to do the assessment will be selected based upon the special skills/techniques required for each assignment submission.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of the Associate Certificate or equivalent, and completion of all courses for the Certificate, or equivalent.

3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Develop an online personal portfolio using skills gained in all courses in this program
- Demonstrate their skillset using individual pages in their portfolio
- Organize the portfolio pages to display their skills to best advantage
- Develop a strategic marketing strategy for their skills
- Market these skills using their online personal portfolio
