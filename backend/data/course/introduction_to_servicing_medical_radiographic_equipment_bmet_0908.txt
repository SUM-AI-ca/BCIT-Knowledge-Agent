## Introduction to Servicing Medical Radiographic Equipment BMET 0908
Biomedical Engineering Course
## Course Overview
A three-day hands-on workshop introducing the principles and practice of servicing medical radiographic equipment and systems. Emphasizes practical laboratory sessions to teach basic operation, quality and assurance and preventive maintenance of the x-ray system.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
