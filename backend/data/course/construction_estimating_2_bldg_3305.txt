## Construction Estimating 2 BLDG 3305
Building Course
## Course Overview
This course is a continuation of the study of construction estimating and the manual preparation of a contractor-oriented bid. It covers bidding procedures, take-off, pricing, and preparation of an estimate summary for a specific scope of work. It includes requests for sub-contractor quotations, sub-contractor bid analysis, a simulated bid closing and submission of a prepared bid.
### Prerequisite(s)
- BLDG 2200 and BLDG 2305
### Credits
4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Measure and price items of work related to Division 3, 6, 7 and 9.
- Calculate material, labour and equipment unit prices using quotations, historical data and independent publications.
- Describe sub-contracting work, sub-trades, scope of their work and analysis of sub-trade quotations.
- Price Division 1 – General Requirements items.
- Develop rational head-office overhead and profit percentages.
- Prepare and submit a competitive tender for a simulated Bid Closing.
Effective as of Fall 2025
