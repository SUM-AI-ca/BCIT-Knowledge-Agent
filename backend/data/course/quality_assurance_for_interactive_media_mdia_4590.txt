## Quality Assurance for Interactive Media MDIA 4590
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
The Quality Assurance for Interactive Media has been engineered for students testing trials of newly-developed websites, apps or interactive media. The primary objective of this course is to detect bugs and performance issues before a new media product reaches the client or final consumer. The students will learn how to develop and run a test plan, cooperate with other team members and designers and create progress and final reports. Students will be assessed by a combination of assignments, quizzes and term project.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Specify the overall testing goals and strategy for the functionality of a new media product being tested.
- Produce testing plan for a live new media product.
- Select, obtain and assign the appropriate resources to complete predefined tasks.
- Organize and lead teams for bug detection tests.
- Justify decisions and provide adequate reporting information where applicable for improving new media products.
- Execute plan tests on multiple browser and mobile platforms.
- Review and analyze UI elements and compare expected test results against actual application behaviour.
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2020
