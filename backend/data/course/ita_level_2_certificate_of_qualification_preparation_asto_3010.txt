## SkilledTradesBC Level 3 Standardized Level Exam Preparation ASTO 3010
Auto Service Tech & Operations Course
## Course Overview
This course prepares students to successfully write the Automotive Service Technician Standardized Level Exam (SLE) examination, Level 3.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ASTO 1020
### Credits
1.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Prepare for challenging the Industry Training Authority (ITA) Level 2 outcomes
