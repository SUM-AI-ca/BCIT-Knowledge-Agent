## Overview

This Red Seal based refresher course helps prepare Electricians, both Construction and Industrial, to write their Interprovincial (IP)/Certificate of Qualification (TQ) examination. Information pertaining to the eligibility requirements for writing the IP/TQ exam must be obtained from SkilledTradesBC at www.skilledtradesbc.ca or 1-866-660-6011.

## Learning Outcomes

- Fundamentals of Electricity
- Meters and Metering
- Motors (DC and AC)
- Motor Controls
- Generators and Alternators
- Industrial Electronics
- Electro Magnetism and Transformers
- Canadian Electrical Code and application
