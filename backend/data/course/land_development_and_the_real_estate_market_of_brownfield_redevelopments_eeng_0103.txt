## Land Development and the Real Estate Market of Brownfield Redevelopments EENG 0103
Environmental Engineering Course
## Course Overview
This course examines the topics of land development and real estate sales and marketing in the context of brownfield redevelopment. (This is one course in a series of 5 brownfield redevelopment courses under joint development and instruction by BCIT and UBC.)
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Outline the necessary steps involved in the redevelopment of a brownfield site within the relevant political and public frameworks.
- Understand the unique features of selling/marketing brownfield redevelopment sites.
- Develop effective communication strategies to sell/market a brownfield redevelopment project to politicians and the community.
- Discuss the professional ethics related to brownfield redevelopment projects.
- Describe approvals from all three levels of government and their interrelationship.
- Discuss methods and approaches to minimize approval time for brownfield redevelopment projects.
- Describe appropriate public and meeting facilitation skills.
