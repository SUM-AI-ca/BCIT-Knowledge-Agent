## Training Skills for Trainers BTCW 0117
Business Workshops Course
## Course Overview
Covers all aspects of training, with an emphasis on facilitation skills. Designed for those who may have training responsibilities as part of their job. Topics include learning to pinpoint training needs, principles of effective course design for effective skill transfer, importance of "learner motivation" and how to develop it, key principles of adult learning, presentation skills, and significance of group involvement.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
