## e-Commerce for Creative Imaging GTEC 3460
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course is an introduction to the many opportunities presented by e-commerce and online connectivity in the Graphic Communications industry. It will prepare the student to devise, select, implement, maintain and grow an online website for a commercial Graphic Imaging company. Emphasis will be on using current technology for website creation with links to PDF workflow systems and online estimating and transaction services.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Explain the different necessary components of a Graphic Imaging company’s online presence.
- Define the criteria for a value added web to print service.
- Compare and contrast the needs of the client with imaging production processes.
- Categorize online needs based on the positioning of the Graphic Imaging company.
- Analyze an existing web presence for effectiveness.
- Outline needs for high-speed data communication.
- Identify criteria for choosing tech partners.
- Identify company stakeholders to be included in planning and development.
- Develop a business plan for implementing a complete online website for a mid-sized company.
- Create a functioning web page with links to Kodak Insite and Prinergy servers.
