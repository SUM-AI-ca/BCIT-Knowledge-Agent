## Customer Relations and Sales ASTP 2302
Automotive Service Technician Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces you to the principles of service sales, follow up, and complaint resolution necessary to represent your employer effectively in a positive and professional manner.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe the key characteristics of winning organizations.
- Design an effective customer service/relations program for an automotive service organization.
- Discuss the importance of non-verbal communication.
- Interview for information.
- Use the telephone effectively.
- Use the principles of effective selling.
