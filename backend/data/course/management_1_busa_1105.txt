## Management 1 BUSA 1105
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
An insight into the basic nature of business problems and the administrative process involved in handling them, with emphasis on the personnel management function. Study and discussion is undertaken of actual business situations illustrating problems frequently met in industry which require managerial analysis, decision and action.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
