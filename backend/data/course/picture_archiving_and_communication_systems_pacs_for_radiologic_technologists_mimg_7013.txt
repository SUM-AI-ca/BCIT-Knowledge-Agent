## Picture Archiving and Communication Systems (PACS) for Radiologic Technologists MIMG 7013
Medical Imaging Course
## Course Overview
Through readings, assignments, and telephone tutor support, this course covers the fundamentals of Picture Archiving and Communication Systems (PACS). First, the history of PACS is outlined followed by a description of various image acquisition modalities connected to the PACS. Secondly, the major components of a PACS, related topics, and PACS Workflow are described. The third major section of this course examines the integration of PACS with HIS and RIS as well as covers the basics of computer networking, digital image display and storage technologies. An overview of DICOM and HL7 standards, IHE, and the FDA and HIPAA requirements for PACS is provided. In addition, technical elements of image acquisition modalities, concepts of digital image quality and workflow integration are studied. The roles, responsibilities and tasks of a PACS system administrator are also discussed.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- State the meaning of PACS.
- Outline the history and evolution of PACS.
- Outline the technical elements of digital image acquisition systems for PACS.
- Outline the technical aspects of the major systems components for PACS.
- Discuss image quality, image compression, and speciality viewing applications for PACS.
- Outline workflow integration and workflow issues related to PACS.
- Explain computer networks, network topologies and communication protocols.
- Outline functions of common network components for connectivity.
- Identify common image display and storage technologies in PACS and their characteristics.
- Outline the functions of PACS, RIS and HIS and their interrelationships.
- Explain the purposes and structure of HL7 standards, DICOM standards and IHE information model.
- Identify FDA requirements for medical devices and HIPPA requirements to address security and privacy of patient health information.
- Discuss the roles, responsibilities and tasks of a PACS Administrator.
