## Design Studio 1 ARSC 5005
Architectural Science Course
## Course Overview
The purpose of this studio is to introduce architectural design as an iterative process of inquiry and experimentation. A series of exercises will provide a structured approach to architectural process and methods (including physical models, concept diagrams, precedent studies and presentation drawings). Students will build on skills acquired in previous architectural and building technology diploma courses to implement first principles of architectural design on small site-specific projects. Detailed context analysis will be conducted to inform meaningful and sustainable architectural solutions.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply architectural design principles, methods and precedents.
- Conduct a detailed case study of a contemporary architectural project, and present findings using a variety of resources and media.
- Document and analyze an urban site (including current and historical context, ecologies, systems, patterns, Indigenous territories and authorities having jurisdiction) to inform project response.
- Identify the full range of requirements for an architectural program.
- Design small building response(s) based on a concept, client, defined program and flexible site.
- Select appropriate structural system(s) and materials and illustrate construction intention.
- Outline building components, materials and assemblies according to their purpose, basic function, nature and characteristics.
- Analyze local ecological systems to propose building solutions that enhance healthy ecosystems.
- Use a range of design tools, including 2D and 3D representation techniques, hand and digital drawings, and physical and virtual modeling.
- Use architectural conventions to communicate through graphics, models and presentations.
- Apply all relevant building codes, regulations, and standards, including universal design.
- Engage in collaborative critique and iterative design processes.
- Simulate professional best practice individually and collaboratively.
