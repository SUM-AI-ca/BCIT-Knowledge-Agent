## Portfolio Design GDES 4300
Graphic Design Course
## Course Overview
​This course guides students through the process of crafting a standout digital portfolio that effectively showcases their diverse skills and expertise in graphic design and interactive media. Students will engage in a thorough curatorial process, selecting and refining their best work to reflect a cohesive and professional identity. Emphasis will be placed on integrating user experience principles to create a portfolio that is intuitive and engaging. Additionally, students will strategize on enhancing their professional presence and visibility through optimized content and presentation techniques. The course culminates in a presentation of the final portfolio to industry professionals, offering valuable feedback for refinement and demonstrating students' ability to stay current with design trends and practices. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Curate a portfolio that effectively showcases expertise across multiple design disciplines.
- Justify the selection of each project in the portfolio, providing rationale for how each piece demonstrates the student's diverse skills and strengths.
- Apply advanced design techniques to refine and update previous projects, ensuring their relevance, currency, visual appeal, and alignment with current design trends.
- Demonstrate consistency in personal branding ensuring that all portfolio materials reflect a unified and professional identity.
- Plan and implement strategies for enhancing the visibility of the portfolio and professional presence, including optimizing content for discoverability and engagement.
- Present and defend the final portfolio to industry professionals, prospective employers, or clients, and critically analyze feedback to make informed improvements.
- Integrate user experience principles into portfolio design, ensuring that the presentation of work is intuitive, engaging, and accessible to a diverse audience.
- Demonstrate adaptability and innovation by incorporating emerging design trends and techniques, showcasing a forward-thinking approach in the portfolio.
