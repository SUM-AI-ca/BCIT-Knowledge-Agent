## Practicum TELC 2220
Electrical Trades Course
## Course Overview
This course is a work experience practicum with a licensed security company. Over four weeks (totalling 160 hours), students will work alongside a qualified technician and have exposure to a typical month as an installer/technician. The final week students return to the classroom for 30 hours to prepare for the qualification exam for the trade of Security Systems Technician.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TELC 2230
### Credits
7.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Install, maintain and repair security systems
- Communicate with other technicians and other trades
- Summarize and report on weekly activities
- Pass a sample C of Q exam
