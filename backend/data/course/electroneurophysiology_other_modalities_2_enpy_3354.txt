## Electroneurophysiology - Other Modalities 2 ENPY 3354
Electroneurophysiology Course
## Course Overview
This course will be a continuation of ENPY 2254 – Electroneurophysiology Other Modalities 1. It will provide an introduction to some of the advanced subspecialties in the field of electroneurophysiology including an introduction to the concepts and techniques of polysomnography and intraoperative monitoring.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ENPY 2254
### Credits
4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate the ability to obtain a relevant patient history for polysomnography studies.
- Describe the steps involved in obtaining a polysomnography recording including:
- The various sleep stages.
- Modalities used.
- Respiratory, cardiac and movement monitoring.
- Machine settings.
- Define sleep disorders and their polysomnographic correlates.
- Define sleep study scoring.
- Describe the steps and uses of a multiple sleep latency test.
- Prepare a concise and complete technical interpretation/report of a sleep study.
- Analyze and describe PSG normal and abnormal waveforms seen on previously recorded and real-time recorded EP's during assignments and reading sessions.
- Define the technologist’s role in the operating room.
- Define the roles of each member of the surgical team.
- Describe techniques used to maintain sterility in the operating room.
- Describe the uses and techniques of the following monitoring modalities in terms of pre-operative, intraoperative and post-operative phases:
- EEG
- VEP
- ABR
- SEP
- EMG
- NCS
- MEP
- Describe how anaesthetic techniques and medications are used in the operating room and how they affect intraoperative monitoring.
- Describe how non-physiologic and physiologic factors that are common in the operating room affect intraoperative monitoring.
- Define alarm criteria for alerting the surgical team of monitoring changes.
