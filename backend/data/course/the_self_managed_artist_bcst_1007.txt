## The Self-Managed Artist BCST 1007
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
​Led by a successful active female musician, this comprehensive course is designed to meet self-managed artists where THEY ARE AT at any stage of their career and provide the day to day tools needed to bridge the gap between emerging and established. By exploring every facet of the modern music business—from social media and DSP strategies to marketing, touring, radio, and legal fundamentals—working musicians will gain the strategic insights necessary to break through plateaus and scale their professional presence. You will hear from experts in these fields and implement a revamped framework to elevate your career.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Distinguish your unique brand identity and arrange your sustainable content strategy​
- Outline a professional content calendar
- Assemble a pitch deck
- Define and engage your audience through algorithms, ads, and analytics
- Differentiate between revenue streams to identify your income pathways
- Implement a project budget tailored to your next release.
- Explore strategies to harness balance between personal well-being and managing career demands
- Analyze insights into commercial, college, and satellite radio promotion for maximizing revenue
- Identify international and domestic booking and showcasing opportunities
- Identify tax write offs for your business
- Arrange a final project curated for your personal music business goals
