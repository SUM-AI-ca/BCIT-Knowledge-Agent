## Routine Inspection and Maintenance Work Practices MCTC 1450
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to the fundamental skills related to the use of lubricants and fluids, procedures for measuring levels and condition of fluids, maintenance requirements, identifying sources of leaks, replacement of gaskets and seals, and performing pre-delivery and pre-storage servicing related to Motorcycles.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCTC 1400
### Credits
6.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe Maintenance schedules.
- Maintain Controls and Actuators.
- Maintain Gaskets and Seals.
- Perform Storage preparation Services.
- Prepare New Motorcycles​.
