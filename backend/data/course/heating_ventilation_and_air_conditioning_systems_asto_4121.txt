## Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning Systems ASTO 4121
Auto Service Tech & Operations Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the fundamental skills required for the service, repair, and diagnose of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ASTO 1120 and completion of ASTO Terms 1 & 2
### Credits
3.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Diagnose, repair, and service heating and ventilation systems.
- Diagnose, repair, and service air conditioning (AC) systems.
- Complete the HRAI ODS Handling Course.
