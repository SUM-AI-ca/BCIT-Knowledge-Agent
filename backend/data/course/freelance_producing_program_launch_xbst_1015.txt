## Freelance Producing: Program Launch XBST 1015
Broadcast and Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Freelance Producing will appeal to individuals who want to develop and share their own content for live or live to tape broadcasts and businesses looking for an online presence to promote and distribute company information, branding, and community outreach. ​This course explores how to launch your program online and how to distribute your program to various media platforms.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in XBST 1013 and 50% in XBST 1014
### Credits
1.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply all the elements from concept to show planning to post-production for a successful program delivery
- Create a show outline and script for each episode
- Collect and apply google analytic statistics to increase distribution reach
