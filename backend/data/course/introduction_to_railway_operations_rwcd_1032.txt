## Introduction to Railway Operations RWCD 1032
Railway Conductor Course
## Course Overview
This course will give learners a basic knowledge of the history of the railways (evolution, interaction with other industries, modern organization structure) and main infrastructure aspects (track, railcars, locomotives and their maintenance, technological advancements).
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe organizational (corporate) structure as it relates to railway operations.
- Describe railcars as they relate to railway operations.
- Describe locomotives as they relate to railway operations.
- Describe track and equipment as it relates to railway operations.
- Describe track maintenance as it relates to railway operations.
- Describe the evolution of technology used on Canadian railways.
