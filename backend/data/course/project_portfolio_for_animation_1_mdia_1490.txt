## Project/Portfolio for Animation 1 MDIA 1490
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
In Project/Portfolio students further develop their traditional drawing and design skills in the first half of the term. Students will work with traditional mediums and learn how to develop ideas, create concepts using rules of composition and perspective, and develop and design original characters. Students will also be exploring the art of film making through the creation of their own storyboards. In the second half of the term students will be collecting artworks from their digital and traditional classes and create a digital portfolio for critique and assessment.

- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate effective presentation skills.
- Work within tight deadlines.
- Create visually appealing art work.
- Arrange a picture using rules of composition.
- Draw a picture using 3D perspective.
- Design appealing characters .
- Work in a team environment.
- Give and receive constructive criticism on projects and make changes accordingly.
- Analyze projects and identify problem areas.
