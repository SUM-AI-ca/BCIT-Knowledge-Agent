## Computer Applications in Engineering CIVL 1100
Civil Engineering Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the use of Q BASIC programming language to solve engineering problems. Each student will have the use of a computer and will be able to successfully write Q BASIC programs at the completion of the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
