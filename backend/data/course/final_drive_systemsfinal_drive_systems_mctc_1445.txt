## Final Drive Systems MCTC 1445
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces the students to design and operation of chain, belt and gear drive systems, as well as final drives and differential systems related to various Motorcycles and All-Terrain vehicles.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCTC 1400
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Maintain Chain Drive systems.
- Maintain Belt Drive systems.
- Maintain Shaft Drive systems.
- Maintain Final Drive Differential systems.
- Describe EV Drive systems​.
Effective as of Fall 2022
