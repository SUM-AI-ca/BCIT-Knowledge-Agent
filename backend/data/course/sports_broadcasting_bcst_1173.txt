## Sports Broadcasting BCST 1173
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to the fundamentals of sports media and broadcasting, combining theoretical knowledge with practical applications. Students explore career opportunities in the sports media industry, develop essential research and writing skills for sports broadcasting, and learn the art of announcing and interviewing. Through engaging assignments and hands-on exercises, including mock interviews, live event analysis, and sportscast production, students build the skills necessary to excel in sports media. Industry guest speakers and real-world simulations provide insight into industry expectations and best practices, preparing students for this dynamic field.​​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain the role of sports in media.
- List a wide variety of potential careers within the sports media industry.
- Demonstrate essential sports researching and writing skills.
- Use basic announcing skills in a sports story performance.
- Conduct an interview for a sports story.
- Identify the different elements required for a local, regional, and national sportscast.
- Construct a contemporary sportscast for the electronic media.
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2013
