## Backflow Assembly Tester Certification PPGS 1155
Piping, Plumbing, Gas Course
## Course Overview
This water related course teaches the hydraulic principles of backflow combined with testing of backflow prevention assemblies, it is recommended that you are employed in a water or health related field. On completion of the course you have the option of taking the BCWWA examination. There is an additional examination fee payable to BCWWA. For more details please contact BCWWA at 604-433-4389. Passing grade is 75%.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
4.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate the knowledge and ability to choose the appropriate backflow prevention assembly and test and troubleshoot backflow prevention assemblies using the following criteria:
- Causes of backflow
- Recommended backflow prevention procedures
- Application of backflow prevention procedures
- Types of backflow preventers
- Recommended installation practices
- Recommended testing procedures
- Definitions and abbreviations
- Backflow prevention assembly performance standards
- Safety
Effective as of Fall 2003
