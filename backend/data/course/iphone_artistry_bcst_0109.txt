## iPhone Artistry BCST 0109
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
What is the simplest tool for creating beautiful photographic images and art? The iPhone of course! No mess, easily positioned for any shot, versatile, lightweight and a complete camera and art kit in one. In this workshop you will learn about iPhone photography and how to maximize your potential as an artist. You’ll also learn how to take the best pictures so you have a foundation for the best art. Beginners are welcome.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Maximize iPhone shooting, editing, storage options and photo organization skills
- Explore photography composition and lighting
- Learn special techniques for shooting portraits and panoramas
- Create stunning photographic images and fine art using apps suitable for the iPhone
- Apply creative touches to your photos including stacking images, making texture backgrounds, making collages and adding text
- Create stunning travel photos, book pages, art and poetry/essay books, cards and coasters
