## Maya 1 MDIA 2600
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This is a hands-on introductory course in 3D computer graphics using Maya software. The focus of this foundation course will be on software concepts, interface, modelling, and rendering.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
