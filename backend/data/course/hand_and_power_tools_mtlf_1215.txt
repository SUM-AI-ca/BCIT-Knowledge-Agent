## Hand and Power Tools MTLF 1215
Metal Fabricator Foundation Course
## Course Overview
In this course, you will learn to identify and use metal fabrication hand and power tools such as grinders, drills, sanders, threaders, etc. Apply WorkSafeBC standards and regulations, shop specific standard operating procedures, and maintenance for all tools and equipment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MTLF 1200
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Operate high speed abrasive equipment to cut materials.
- Operate drilling equipment during fabrication of products.
- Operate metal fabrication hand tools from a bench and power equipment according to safe working practices.
- Use bench and hand grinders following safe work procedures.
- Use polishing equipment to finish projects.
Effective as of Fall 2017
