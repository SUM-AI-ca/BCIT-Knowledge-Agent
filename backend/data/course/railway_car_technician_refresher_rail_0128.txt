## Railway Car Technician Refresher RAIL 0128
Railway Course
## Course Overview
This course prepares students to write the ITA certification exam and give participants information that is fundamental to performing the job of a railway car technician in accordance with specifications and standards of the Railway Car Technician Program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Must be a registered Railway Car Technician with the Industry Training Authority.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the need for schematics, blue prints, and assembly drawings.
- Identify freight car types, superstructure parts, under frame parts
- Identify safety appliances and inspection points.
- Identify couplers and uncoupling levers.
- Identify draft gear assembly parts, cushioning devices, center plates and side bearings.
- Identify parts for freight car brake system, truck and components.
- Explain how to use the A.A.R. Field Manual of the Interchange Rules.
- Describe precision measuring tools used in fabrication.
- Explain the importance of workplace safety precautions.
- Inspect hand and power (electrical and pneumatic) tools.
- Explain lock out regulations, shop protection and blue flag rules.
- Describe types of car bodies, defects, and repairs.
- Explain the Transport Canada pre-departure test for car bodies.
- Describe oxy-acetylene welding and cutting equipment, welding related safety.
- Describe gas metal arc welding and equipment.
