## Generator Sets MMTF 1315
Marine Mechanical Technician Course
## Course Overview
​In this course you will learn the function of self-contained Alternating Current (AC) generators and the criteria to be considered when installing a new system. You will learn to install a generator and how to diagnose generator faults.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 70% in MMTF 1000
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe AC genset function and installation criteria
- Install genset in vessel to manufacturer's and ABYC specifications
- Diagnose electrical malfunctions and service genset generator components​
