## Emergency Lighting Systems Refresher FIRE 0277
Fire Protection and Inspection Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed and offered only to Red Seal Electricians. It provides a very compact overview of the inspection and testing requirements for unit equipment for emergency lighting systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Electrical BCTQ
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify different types and sizes of batteries.
- Determine an equivalent replacement battery for an emergency lighting unit.
- Perform the tests and measurements required by building codes of emergency lighting units.
- Use Ohm's and Joules Law to confirm emergency lighting units are installed correctly.
- Repair emergency lighting units.
- Confirm installation meets Canadian Electrical Code and Building Code requirements.
