## Event Industry Practicum TOUR 2030
Tourism Course
## Course Overview
The Event Industry Practicum is designed to provide students with a work experience position in the event management industry. On-the-job performance is fully evaluated by the employer as well as self-reflection and practical application of acquired knowledge and skills. Students will work up to 4 days per week for 12 weeks with an approved event management organization (the minimum required will be 200 hours during the Practicum).
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all program courses
### Credits
8.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the stdudent will be able to:
- Explore corporate culture and the professionalism expected in the event industry.
- Apply event management knowledge to assist an organization in successfully producing an event or implementing an event marketing or promotional plan.
- Create professional networks to advance the career as an event manager.
