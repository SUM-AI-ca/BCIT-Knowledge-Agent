## Manage Operational Performance MGMT 8435
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates operational performance skills, such as managing the activities of an operating unit to achieve desired results, managing projects successfully, applying the principles of Total Quality Management, and analyzing processes and design improvements which are cost/benefit justified.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 80% in MGMT 8010
### Credits
4.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Apply Total Quality Management Principles in the management of an operations
- Identify who your customers are.
- Establish goals or critical success factors for the operation.
- Establish continuous improvement objectives for an operation.
- Understand the basic requirements for ISO 9000.
- Create a human resources environment conducive for a continuous improvement culture.
- Apply Systematic Process Design Methods to Achieve Improved Operational Performance.
- Identify processes critical to the effectiveness of an operation.
- Assess the effectiveness of a process.
- Lead process redesign and implementation activities to produce a cost/benefit justified improvement.
- Apply a structured problem-solving process (plan, do, check, act).
- Effectively Plan and Manage Projects
- Develop a project plan
- Create an effective performance monitoring process.
- Manage and coordinate a project through to completion.
