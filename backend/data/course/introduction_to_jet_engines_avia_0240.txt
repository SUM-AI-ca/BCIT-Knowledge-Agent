## Introduction to Jet Engines AVIA 0240
Aviation Course
## Course Overview
A course designed to familiarize you with the principles of jet engine operation and engine systems. Focus is on learning the fundamental concepts associated with engine construction, operation, and systems, including engine instrumentation, ignition, fuel control, and lubrication systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
