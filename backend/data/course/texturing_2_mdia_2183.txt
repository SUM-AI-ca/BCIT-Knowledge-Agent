## Texturing 2 MDIA 2183
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
In this course students will build upon the principles of texturing from Term 1, using specific techniques to create believable and stylized surfaces. Texturing will be explored in more depth with an emphasis placed on details. Students will use photographic reference images to enhance the believability of their work. Through working with industry standard sculpting applications, students will create color and detail textures for organic and solid objects.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all term 1 courses

3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyze methods for unwrapping UVs and laying out efficient maps.
- Prepare images by painting and applying textures.
- Practice different techniques to apply texture detailing.
- Describe how lighting may impact textures.
- Apply different skills for manipulating photographic images.
- Alter photographic images with painting tools for applying dirt and wear and tear to objects.
- Use online support networks and help systems to expand knowledge of industry standard image creation and editing software applications.
- Generate an animated texture in a software program.
- Construct a toon texture in a three dimensional program.
