## The Building Permit Process BLDG 0003
Building Course
## Course Overview
The overall permit approval process required by all lower mainland municipalities. Includes development permit, building permit, rezoning, board of variance, design panel, and council approval.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
