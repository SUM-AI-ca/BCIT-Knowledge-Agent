## International Construction Management MSc. Residential Seminars CMGT 9000
Construction Management - CMGT Course
## Course Overview
The International Construction Management MSc. Residential Seminars are designed to provide several key functions that are important to distance learning students. These include: meeting peers, academic tutors, and student support staff; receiving lectures and tutorials from unit tutors, academics and practitioners; and undertaking workshop and group work sessions. Registrants must have approval from the International Construction Management MSc. Program Head.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
