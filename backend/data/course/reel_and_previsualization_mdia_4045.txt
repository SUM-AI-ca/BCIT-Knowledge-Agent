## Reel and Previsualization MDIA 4045
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course focuses on the techniques and methodology for drafting and prototyping a 3D art form and demo reel creation. Under the direct supervision of their instructor, students will create a rough draft piece that shows the alignment of their demo reel to their story board with proxy placement and animations. Students will present their storyboard to their instructor and receive feedback and guidelines for the pre-visualization and ultimately their capstone project. This course also cross-collaborates and shares some assignments with the portfolio production course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all term 1, 2 and 3 courses

3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course the student will be able to:
- Create and finalize storyboard for their demo reel
- Identify key objects and animation components in the project
- Analyze each scene of the storyboard and identify proper proxy objects
- Create animations for objects and camera for Pre-Vis project
- Incorporate feedback received from instructors into their demo reel
- Present a final Pre-Visualisation of expected demo reel
