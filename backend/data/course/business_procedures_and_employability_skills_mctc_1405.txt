## Business Procedures and Employability Skills MCTC 1405
Motorcycle Technician Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to the administrative skills, knowledge, and practice necessary to contribute to the operation of an efficient shop. Areas of study include staff responsibilities/roles within a service facility, service department operations, forms and procedures, customer relations and service information systems utilization. This course also provides students with the practical skills required to find employment. Topics covered include resume and cover letter preparation and job search strategies.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- MCTC 1400
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe workplace skills.
- Describe General Shop administration.
- Describe parts inventory records and controls.
- Describe service department record keeping
- Describe customer relation skills.
- Utilize service information.
- Demonstrate Employment readiness skills.
- Describe what Respect, Diversity and Inclusion means in the workplace​.
