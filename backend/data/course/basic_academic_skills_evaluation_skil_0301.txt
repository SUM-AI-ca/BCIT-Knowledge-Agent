## Basic Academic Skills Evaluation SKIL 0301
Study Skills Course
## Course Overview
This review and evaluation presents incoming BCIT students with some of the basic math and physics skills they require to be successful at BCIT, followed by an online evaluation of those skills. Students can then decide if they need to refresh those skills in preparation for attending their BCIT program, either on their own, or through a MATH 0004 or PHYS 0304 refresher course. Depending on the level of academic skills, students may need 2-20 hours to work through this material. NOTE: If you’re looking to polish your study and student success skills before starting your program, you might be interested in SKIL 0303 .
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Solve basic word problems using algebra, geometry and trigonometry.
- Solve basic physics problems related to one and two dimensional kinematics and dynamics.
