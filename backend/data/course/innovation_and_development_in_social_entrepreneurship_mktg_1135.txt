## Innovation and Development in Social Entrepreneurship MKTG 1135
Marketing Management Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to social innovation involved with developing new ideas that resolve social, cultural, economic and environmental challenges for the benefit of people and planet. It is designed to inspire social innovation and development from individuals, groups or organizations that can take place in the for-profit, non-profit and public sectors. The course helps students develop an understanding of innovative organizational strategy, the specific processes involved in social innovation, and how to assess an organization’s readiness for socially entrepreneurial activity. Students will be able to demonstrate fundamentals of creating an innovative climate within an organization, which is an essential ingredient in entrepreneurial innovation. This is a hands-on course where students will spend 50% of class time exploring innovative ideas and applying course concepts to develop a social innovation blueprint.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Define Social Innovation, identifying ways in which systems-changing social innovation occurs.
- Describe the process of Corporate Innovation and what an I-Organization is.
- Demonstrate how to implement elements of creativity and innovation in order to achieve a more socially innovative organization.
- Identify innovation and show key elements that a socially innovative development strategy should provide.
- Explain the process for converting a business opportunity into a social innovation.
- Design an innovation plan and “sell” the social innovation story for your organization.
