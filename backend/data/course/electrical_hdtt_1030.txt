## Electrical HDTT 1030
Heavy Duty Truck Technology Course
## Course Overview
​This course will introduce you to the fundamental skills required to successfully describe electricity, magnetism, and circuit components; interpret wiring diagrams and symbols, and use electrical testing instruments, service batteries, charging systems, starting systems, and electrical circuits.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HDTT 1001
### Credits
5.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe Electricity
- Use Electrical Testing Instruments
- Service and Diagnose Batteries
- Service Charging Systems
- Service Starting Systems
- Service Electrical Circuits
- Apply Math and Physics for Trades
- Demonstrate Safe Work Practices.
Effective as of Spring/Summer 2020
