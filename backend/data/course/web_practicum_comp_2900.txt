## Web Practicum COMP 2900
Computer Systems Course
## Course Overview
This practicum is the final piece of the Associate Certificate in Web Development. Students propose, specify and develop a web project from start to finish, within the guidelines specified by a faculty advisor, using the methodology learned from the required courses and the tools or technologies that they have chosen from the electives. Projects may be drawn from a variety of sources, especially from industrial situations, and should include extensive contact with the business community. Instructor to determine meeting times, this is not a regular weekly class.
### Prerequisite(s)
- COMP 1850 , COMP 2615 , COMP 1950 , COMP 2712 plus at least 7.5 credits from the technology electives.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Address a real business need based on an initial proposal.
- Create solutions that will be intuitive, functional and optimized for current technology.
- Interact with third parties to plan, design, develop and deliver a dynamic, data driven web project.
