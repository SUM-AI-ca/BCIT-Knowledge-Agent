## Television and Video Production Kick Start BCST 0030
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course provides an overview of television and video production with emphasis on single camera and multi-camera studio production techniques. Students will explore equipment operation in a hands-on learning environment. Students will complete exercises that will provide them with practical skills for television studio and control room operations. The course will give students a solid foundation for further study in the television and video production program. Students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the full-time Television and Video Production Program including requirements for successful completion.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
- Effectively shoot video with good composition and sound
- Demonstrate how to plan/create and shoot a video sequence
- Execute the process of producing a short television segment
- Recognize and become familiar with individual roles within a production team, including control room and studio positions
- Operate under supervision studio production equipment including cameras and audio equipment
- Operate control room production equipment under supervision, including video production switchers
