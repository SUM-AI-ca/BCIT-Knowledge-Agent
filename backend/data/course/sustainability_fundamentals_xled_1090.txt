## Sustainability Fundamentals XLED 1090
Building Capacity Course
## Course Overview
​The world of sustainable business is changing fast. CSR, ESG, SDGs, and planetary boundaries have all become buzzwords that can be challenging for employees and organizations to navigate. This workshop explores established and emerging sustainable business concepts and trends and how you can plan to act on sustainability in your organization.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand key concepts and trends in sustainable business.
- Review how business and organizations are setting goals around sustainability
- Investigate approaches to embed sustainability in business processes, practices, and culture
- Develop a sustainability action plan​.
