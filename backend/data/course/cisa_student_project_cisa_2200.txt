## CISA Student Project CISA 2200
Computer Info Systems Admin Course
## Course Overview
This course allows students the opportunity to work in small groups to complete case studies which provide a review of the concepts and topics covered in previous courses. Students are also encouraged to write the industry certifications and have full access to the laboratories and instructor expertise to work through related case exercises. Working in groups, the students are also required to create a presentation on an industry related topic. In addition students develop a personal portfolio that includes a resume, career goals, and notes on interview preparation. Teamwork and interpersonal skills are key elements emphasized in this course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Successful completion of CISA Level 1 with minimum 60% grade point average.
### Credits
8.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate project management skills
- Perform basic research on technical topics
- Write and develop presentation materials on a technical topic
- Present a topic related to the industry to a technical audience
- Work productively within a team environment
- Identify career goals
- Prepare for an interview
- Create a personal resume
- Demonstrate ability for self-directed learning
