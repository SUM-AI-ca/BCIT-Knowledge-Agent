## Information Systems in Health Care HINS 7200
Health Information Systems Course
## Course Overview
Introduces health information systems and provides the health care professional with fundamental knowledge of how the delivery of patient care be improved through the appropriate application of health management information systems. Discusses how to apply skills and knowledge to assist in the development and implementation of health information management systems in their organization.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
- Upon successful completion of this course, the students will be able to:
- Describe and explain the role of health management information system (HMIS) in the health care system.
- Describe the relevance of system's thinking and theory in providing a paradigm for thinking effectively about health services delivery
system in today's health care environment.
- Describe the fundamentals of information concepts.
- Describe and explain the essential management functions and how they interrelate with systems theory to assist in the development of a management performance system.
- Describe and explain the evolution of the primary systems development methodologies.
- Apply health management information systems strategic planning principles and methods to practical issues in today's health care organization.
- Understand the management of health information as a resource.
- Outline the steps in and variables affecting the implementation of a health information system.
- Identify the principles and methods of total quality improvement and explain their integration into health information management systems.
