## Applied Project for Site Supervision of Zero Energy/Emissions and Passive House Construction XZEB 1173
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
​The goal of this course is to provide an opportunity to practice skills and knowledge learned throughout the BCIT Supervising Net-Zero and Passive House Construction micro credential. In this course, students will work on an applied project associated with supervising the construction of a Net-Zero, Net-Zero Ready, or Passive House, or equivalent building. Under the supervision of an advisor, students will develop and present a report to demonstrate their understanding and competency in supervising Net-Zero, Net-Zero Ready, Passive House, or equivalent high-performance construction. Examples of this project could be a report on developing an airtightness training program or on planning and scheduling construction work to meet airtight and low-TEDI performance metrics. This course is offered online through the BCIT Learning Hub.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in XZEB 1001 and 60% in XZEB 1120 and 60% in XZEB 1146 and 60% in XZEB 1171

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Develop and present a report to demonstrate their understanding and competency in supervising Net-Zero, Net-Zero Ready, Passive House, or equivalent high-performance construction, including but not limited to:
- A report on their experience developing an envelope quality control program
- A plan coordinating and sequencing air barrier detail construction
- A process for quality control of construction material substitutions
- A process for collecting and managing important documentation and data for compliance
- An airtightness training plan
- A plan and construction work schedule to ensure airtight construction details and performance verification
