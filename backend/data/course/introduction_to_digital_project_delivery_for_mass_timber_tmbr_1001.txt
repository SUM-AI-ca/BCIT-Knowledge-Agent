## Introduction to Digital Project Delivery for Mass Timber TMBR 1001
Mass Timber Construction Course
## Course Overview
​This course Introduces students to digital manufacturing with wood and digital project delivery. Includes the basic principles of construction modelling for mass timber construction.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in TMBR 1000
### Credits
0.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe digital project delivery in the context of mass timber construction.
- Explain usage and particularities of each construction model as it pertains to builders /installers/ construction managers /cost estimators.
- Explain basic interface options between models.
- Define the role and goals of BIM in construction projects.
- Define the role and goals of DfMA in construction projects.
- Explain the value of simulations.
- Identify types of data sharing.
- Describe pro/cons of each type of data sharing.
- Describe analyses: clash detection , constructability analysis, clearance detection.
