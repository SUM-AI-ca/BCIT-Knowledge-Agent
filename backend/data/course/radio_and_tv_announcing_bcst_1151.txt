## Radio and TV Announcing BCST 1151
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
This course is designed for the person eager to learn what it takes to be a professional broadcaster. Whether you're curious about radio and television broadcasting or interested in becoming a Youtuber, a podcaster or exploring other media, you will have the opportunity to learn the announcing skills and techniques than can be transferred to all these platforms – including how to break down a commercial script, interview preparation and execution, basic recording equipment usage, developing characters for animation and much more. The course also includes industry professional speakers who will share their announcing journey and provide tips and guidelines on how to break into the industry. In class participation is a key component of this course, as you will be asked to present to the class on more than one occasion. Bring your curiosity, enthusiasm and willingness to have fun to this course.​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Prepare a commercial script before voicing.
- Determine what style of announcing is required for various scripts.
- Practice effective interview skills.
- Perform technical operations required to record your voice for a commercial.
- Utilize various techniques required for a memorable and convincing on-camera editorial.
- Recognize the importance of your target audience and how to develop that audience.
- Demonstrate the use of correct tone, pitch, pronunciation, etc. for news and sportscasts.
- Record and present an on-camera newscast while working with a teleprompter.
- Develop strong listening skills for interviews and multi-voice commercial scripts.
- Gain insights into the current media landscape and how to thrive within it.​
