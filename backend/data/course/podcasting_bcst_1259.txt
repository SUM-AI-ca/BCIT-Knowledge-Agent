## Podcasting BCST 1259
Broadcast Communications Course
## Course Overview
Everyone can have a digital presence nowadays. Audio and video forms of podcasting continue to gain popularity, and they're easier and more affordable to create than ever before. This course will cover content preparation, interviewing techniques, writing, basic editing and the technical aspects of getting a podcast on the air. We'll also learn about portable studio assembly and the plethora of great apps available to facilitate podcast-making. All you need is a smartphone to get started. Much of what we'll cover is also applicable to people pursuing broadcast careers.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Conceive of and design a podcast.
- Identify technical requirements, equipment and applications necessary for podcast creation.
- Create conversational written material for podcast audiences (writing for the ear) and digital channels (writing for the eye).
- Create effective lines of questioning.
- Conduct guest interviews with confidence.
- Organize information in a logical fashion for presentation.
- Construct a simple, free website for a podcast.
- Identify ways to promote a podcast using social media channels.
- Understand the basics of audio and video recording, and digital editing.
- Apply performance techniques for hosting a podcast.
