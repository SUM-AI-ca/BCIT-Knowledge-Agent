## AI in Project Management OPMT 3505
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
​This comprehensive 12-week course teaches project managers how to leverage artificial intelligence throughout the project management lifecycle. Organized around the five project management process groups (Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring & Controlling, and Closing), the course demonstrates how AI tools enhance each phase of project delivery. The curriculum focuses on predictive (waterfall) methodology with a session on Agile approaches.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Leverage the use of AI through the project management process groups and lifecycle.
- Apply AI tools throughout each project phase from initiation to closing.
- Use AI for project planning, estimation, and optimization.
- Implement AI-powered execution and monitoring systems.
- Leverage predictive analytics for proactive project control.
- Select appropriate AI platforms for different project phases.
