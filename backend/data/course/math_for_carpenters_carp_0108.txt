## Math for Carpenters CARP 0108
Carpentry Course
## Course Overview
Prepares trades persons for the Carpentry Apprenticeship Program and Red Seal Endorsement (IP) Exam. Mathematics can be challenging for carpentry students throughout their apprenticeship and careers. This course will assist students in achieving math skills that will allow them to perform accurately and effectively. Recommended for all levels of apprenticeship. Students will require a calculator . Texas Instrument T1-30XA is preferred.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Perform metric and imperial conversions.
- Indentify and use trigonometric functions.
- Calculate concrete quantities.
- Estimate construction materials.
- Identify and demonstrate building layout.
- Perform stair and roof calculations.
- Use building code tables.
- Apply basic geometry principles.
