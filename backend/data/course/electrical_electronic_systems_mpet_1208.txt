## Electrical/Electronic Systems MPET 1208
Motorcycle & Power Equipment Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to basic electrical principles, electrical circuits, magnetism, and electrical generation. Students will learn how to interpret electrical diagrams, use digital and analog meters, service batteries, charging systems, starting systems, and component operation. Students will also perform basic service procedures for various electrical components and systems related to motorcycles and power equipment.
### Prerequisite(s)
- MPET 1200
### Credits
6.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe principles of electricity
- Describe principles of electronics
- Interpret wiring diagrams
- Describe starting and charging systems
- Service batteries
- Service exterior lamps
- Describe basic electrical systems diagnostic equipment
