## Audio MDIA 2196
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
In this course students will work with audio for New Media using Adobe Audition. The focus of the course is importing, editing and producing basic audio pieces that can be used in multimedia, web and other media projects. Students will also be exposed to basic audio connections and cables in conjunction with an introduction to basic microphone techniques. By using practical projects the students will experience the process of audio production and post-production. This will enable students to communicate with audio producers and artists as well as making basic audio products themselves.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Explain broadcast and release medium standards.
- Use advanced industry standard software tools for special sound effects.
- Create a sound track using the basic steps in the audio process.
- Perform a post production edit and mix for video.
- Edit and produce a basic music mix with pre-recorded files.
- Control audio pitch sound through mixing controls.
- Choose and use the correct microphone for a given recording task
