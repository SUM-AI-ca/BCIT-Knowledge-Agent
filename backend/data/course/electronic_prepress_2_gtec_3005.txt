## Electronic Prepress 2 GTEC 3005
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course builds on the foundations taught in Electronic Prepress 1 and provides an overview of output devices fed by electronic prepress workflows. Students are taught to evaluate and apply the required attributes to transform electronic files into tangible output.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in GTEC 2005
### Credits
3.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Produce a variety of proofs, film and printing plates from devices that support input from Prinergy.
- Differentiate between the appropriate uses of inkjet and half-tone proofing devices.
- List the characteristics of the most common output device families relating to electronic prepress.
- Describe the types of media associated with various output devices.
- Apply colour management with ICC or Device Link profiles in the Colour Control module of the Process Plan for proofing output.
- Map spot colours to the correct colour library for the best simulation with proofing devices.
- Combine surplus spot colours to a single separation for successful printing plate production.
- Select the appropriate screening parameters based on production requirements for half-tone proof, film or plate output.
- Set and revise trapping characteristics based on expectations of the ultimate printing condition.
- Configure workflows: directly connect output devices, remote machines or third party devices.
