## Dairy Processing 1 FOOD 2150
Food Technology Course
## Course Overview
Review of FOOD 1150 Dairy Processing (correspondence course). Includes additional material such as product safety, cleaning, sanitizing procedures, processing and pasteurization techniques. Successful completion of this course enables the dairy plant worker to apply for provincial licensing. BCIT issues a statement of completion to those who achieve a pass grade of 50%.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 60% in FOOD 1150
- FOOD 1150 . https://www.bcit.ca/study/programs/6340acert
### Credits
2.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Understand and apply the Milk Industry Act of British Columbia.
- Understand the basics of dairy chemistry.
- Understand the principles of microbiology that apply to dairy products.
- Understand the issues of food safety.
- Understand the principles of thermal processing with respect to heat transfer and holding time.
- Operate and test and HTST pasteurizer.
- Apply the principles of sanitation and CIP cleaning to a HTST pasteurizer.
