## Perioperative Theory 3: The Nurse in the Scrub Role NSPO 7400
Nursing Specialty Perioperative Course
## Course Overview
Explores individuals' experiences of common, less complex surgeries. Expands upon the concepts of partnership, stability and safety, and introduces the principles of surgery and healing. Explores the role and practice of the scrub nurse.
### Prerequisite(s)
- S in NSPO 7300
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Integrate knowledge of principles of perioperative practice, anatomy, biophysics, asepsis and healing and relate them to the provision of care and maintenance of an individual's physical stability during the intraoperative period.
- Integrate effective verbal and non-verbal communication techniques that foster collaboration between nurses and members of the surgical team and facilitate efficient and competent care based on perioperative standards of practice, throughout the intraoperative phase of the individual's perioperative experience.
- Critically analyze the concept of caring, focusing on the components of conscience, confidence and competence in relation to the role of the scrub nurse.
- Develop and articulate patient-centred care plans for individuals undergoing common, less complex surgeries.
