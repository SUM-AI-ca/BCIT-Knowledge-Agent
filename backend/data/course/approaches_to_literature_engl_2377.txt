## Approaches to Literature ENGL 2377
English Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the principles of university-level literary study and provides them with the skills to think and write critically about literature. The purpose of the course is to expand the students' understanding of literature and ideas through the study of poetry, drama and fiction, making specific reference to the works studied in the course. University transferable.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in ENGL 1177
