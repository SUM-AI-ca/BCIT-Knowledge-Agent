## Occupational Health Nursing: Practice Experience 1 NSOH 7255
Nursing Specialty Occupational Health Course
## Course Overview
This practice experience supports and focuses learning in the co-requisite theory course, NSOH 7250 Work and Work Environments 2. Follows a directed study plan to complete practice experience with a selected organization. Requires critical analysis of observations using a health promotion framework to apply principles of industrial hygiene, safety, ergonomics and job demands analysis. Starts in week seven of the term and uses NSOH 7250 tutor as a mentor for the experience. Corequisite: NSOH 7250 .
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in NSOH 7200
- NSOH 7250 ‡ ( ‡ must be taken concurrently)
- NSOH 7200
