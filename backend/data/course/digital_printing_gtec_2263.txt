## Digital Printing GTEC 2263
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This course commences with the basics of digital printing, including the principles of xerography and other related technologies. There will be a review of different manufacturer's equipment, their capabilities, and quality. Media issues will all be discussed as well as printer's speeds and job costing features. Students will practice troubleshooting different media and calibrate the printer to ensure consistent colour reproduction. The course also covers many of the other considerations for reproducing files correctly on a digital printer.

## Learning Outcomes
At the end of this course the student will be able to:
- Describe the principles of Xerography.
- Explain the different technologies encompassed within digital printing market.
- Choose the appropriate printing equipment for a job.
- Distinguish between particular printer capabilities.
- Choose the appropriate media for a job.
- Troubleshoot different media issues.
- Explain how printer speeds, waste and running costs effect the total job cost.
- Configure calibration software to ensure consistency in colour reproduction.
- Setup jobs for output media size and related finishing options.
- Structure and package files for output to a digital printer, both internally and from remote location (i.e.: create pdf, and PostScript files).
- Use text and fonts including Open Type.
- Consider design issues including colour crossover, trapping, solid files and graduations.
- Organize pages of a design with imposition software.
- Define an ICC profile and describe its application in digital printing.
- Print with total ink coverage for optimum results.
