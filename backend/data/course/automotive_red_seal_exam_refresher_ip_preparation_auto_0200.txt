## Automotive Red Seal Exam Refresher (IP Preparation) AUTO 0200
Automotive Mechanics Course
## Course Overview
​This course is designed to prepare recent graduates and experienced Automotive Service Technicians to challenge the Red Seal Examination (RSE). Participants will receive a comprehensive review of the Red Seal Exam requirements, an in-depth overview of the theoretical content covered on the Red Seal Written Exam. Students will be guided to schedule their written exam with SkilledTradesBC. ​​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Prior experience in automotive mechanical repair or apprenticeship related training in automotive service and repair is recommended.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Explain the requirements of the Red Seal examination for Automotive Service Technicians.
- Identify and review key knowledge areas and competencies assessed in the Red Seal Written Exam.
- Apply theoretical knowledge aligned to Red Seal standards.
- Develop personalized study strategies towards achieving success in the Red Seal exam.
- Navigate the process for registering and scheduling the written exam with SkilledTradesBC.​
