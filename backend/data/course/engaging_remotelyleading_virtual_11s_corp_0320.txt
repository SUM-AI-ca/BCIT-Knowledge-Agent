## Engaging Remotely:Leading Virtual 1:1's CORP 0320
Corporate Training Course
## Course Overview
The sudden introduction to remote work for many organizations has been challenging for leaders and team members alike. Disruption of normal workplace practices and communication channels requires people to adapt to these changes. This webinar will prepare you to use simple behaviour styles knowledge to engage your team and support ongoing performance.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this webinar, the participant will be able to:
- Identify your own and your employees' unique style & needs
- Recognize individual needs and challenges based on behaviour style
- Apply your knowledge of individual styles to build trust and understanding
- Increase engagement and productivity through tailored connection with your team
