## Industry Practicum PLAS 1091
Plastics Course
## Course Overview
Provides students with a 36 hour practicum to gain experience in the chosen process option. The work must be at an appropriate technical level dealing with an investigation of an existing process, the study of a new process, a study of a technical process improvement or a design project for a specific system or product. A technical report on the work performed is to be submitted to the sponsor and BCIT.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all other credits required for the Associate Certificate program in Plastics Technology.
