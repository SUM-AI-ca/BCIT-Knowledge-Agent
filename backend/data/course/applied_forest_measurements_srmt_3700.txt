## Applied Forest Measurements SRMT 3700
Sustainable Resource Mgt Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces forest sampling techniques related to forest inventory and appraisals. Emphasis is placed on an introduction to the current: provincial inventory and appraisal cruising systems, logging waste surveying and log scaling. The principles of double sampling, count/measure and enhanced plot sampling, 3-P sampling and value added sampling techniques are also introduced. The course reinforces the sampling and measurement principles and skills required of the technologist to practice in the professionally regulated revenue risk B.C appraisal system.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Perform as a `cruiser in training' using accepted sample principles and techniques.
- Perform elementary call grading and net factoring using statutory grades.
- Explain the role of cruise data in both interior and coastal appraisal systems.
- Explain uses and limitations of sampling procedures and collected data.
- Prepare and compile variable plot cruise data using provincial compilations procedures and interpret results.
- Apply variable plot sampling techniques.
- Apply the concepts of 3-P sampling.
- Describe the multi phase design of the provincial inventory and uses of the inventory attributes.
- Develop an acceptable sampling plan from data collected in a field reconnaissance.
- Prepare thematic maps from field data.
- Calculate stumpage rates and estimated profit & loss using project data.
- Apply logging waste measurement techniques using provincial standards.
- Apply log measurement techniques using provincial scaling standards.
- Determine statutory log grades to the 'sort' level for selected coastal species.
