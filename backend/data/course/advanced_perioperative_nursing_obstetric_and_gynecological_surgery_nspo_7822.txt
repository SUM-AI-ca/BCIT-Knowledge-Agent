## Advanced Perioperative Nursing: Obstetric and Gynecological Surgery NSPO 7822
Nursing Specialty Perioperative Course
## Course Overview
​In this badged course, learners will expand their obstetric and gynecological (ob-gyn) perioperative nursing education, advancing beyond novice practice, focusing on immediate practical knowledge application. Within this badged course, learners will have the opportunity to explain in depth the perioperative nursing role for ob-gyn procedures and develop a plan of care for persons undergoing these procedures in both the scrub and circulating roles. Clarification of scope of practice for LPNs and RNS in ob-gyn procedures will be outlined as well as adherence to hospital policies when on-site. To prepare for the clinical environment and knowledge uptake, low-stakes assessments, including quizzes and small written case study assignments will be completed.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of Perioperative Specialty Nursing Program. Perioperative Nurses (RN or LPN/RPN) from any Health Authority or Private Surgical Center or equivalent practice setting.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Understand the anatomy of the organs involved in gynecologic and obstetric surgical procedures.
- Understand gynecologic and obstetric pathology.
- Understand gynecologic and obstetric surgical procedures.
- Apply the knowledge of perioperative nursing care to support gynecologic and obstetric surgical procedures.​
