## Bindery and Finishing 2 GTEC 3365
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
This senior-level course provides students with advanced bindery and finishing skills. Students will learn how to do complex (e.g. working with diverse materials) set-up and running of a Perfect Binder. Students will continue to build on previously learned machine operating and specialty skills and learn to handle plant floor organization.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in GTEC 2365
### Credits
4.5
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Identify and explain complex bindery and different finishing functions and operations.
- Plan the necessary work process for completion of a job that requires a minimum of 4 processes (e.g. stitching, folding, cutting, binding, perforating, scoring, foil stamping, die cutting, etc.).
- Set up the Perfect Binder and perform complex job set ups.
- Produce an assigned job with a minimum of 4 processes to defined job standards.
- Complete job lay-outs that clearly optimize the bindery equipment and meet the customer's needs.
- Lead an operating crew in completion of a complex bindery or finishing project.
- Perform complex maintenance of the Folder, Stitcher, Cutter and Perfect Binder and other equipment.
- Complete all bindery and finishing tasks safely.
- Maintain a clean and safe workplace.
- Identify the degree of complexity of substrates to be bound and/or finished and recommend the best (most suitable) substrate for the intended binding and/or finishing work.
