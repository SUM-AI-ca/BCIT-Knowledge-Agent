## Practicum MANU 4090
Mechanical Manufacturing Course
## Course Overview
Students who have completed all course requirements of the Associate Certificate in Manufacturing complete a Computer Aided Manufacturing (CAM) related project, preferably sponsored by an employer, to demonstrate the practical applications of the knowledge gained from CAM courses.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Completion of all other credits on the Associate Certificate in Computer Aided Manufacturing program.
### Credits
3.0
