## Basic Drafting for Hydronic Technicians DRFT 1041
Drafting Course
## Course Overview
Provides instruction in the production and interpretation of orthographic and isometric drawings as they apply to the hydronic system, and flow diagrams and general assembly drawings. Formal drafting techniques including board skills will be covered. Related terms, symbols and call-out bubbles will be discussed. An introduction to AutoCAD will be included.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
