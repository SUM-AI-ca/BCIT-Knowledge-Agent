## Pumps: Electrical and Controls 2 PUBW 2205
Public Works Course
## Course Overview
This first part of this continuing course in pump control devices will explore application, operation and I/O interaction of PLC's. Topics will include logic concepts, programming devices, implementing and programming a PLC system together with installation, start up and maintenance. The second half of the course will cover SCADA systems involving communication data, RTU's, alarm conditions and event messaging. Aspects of output displays, routine operations, keyboard operations and system constraints together with many other topics will conclude the course.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in PUBW 1201
