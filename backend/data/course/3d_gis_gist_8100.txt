## 3D GIS GIST 8100
Geographic Information Systems Course
## Course Overview
​This course provides theoretical exposure to fundamental 3D GIS tools and data structures. ​Students will also gain a practical understanding of 3D GIS computer applications. Topics covered include BIM and object file formats, data conversion and display, feature visualization, and 3D operations.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in GIST 7100 and 50% in GIST 7130
- Course registration is restricted to current students registered in a BCIT GIS program.
### Credits
3.0
### Registration requirements

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student should be able to:
- Complete 3D GIS applications using a current industry standard software.
- Describe the structure of BIM data.
- Discuss approaches for 3D feature visualization.
- Identify the requirements for 3D model generation.
- Research and present a GIS technical topic to an advanced level GIS audience.
- Compare the problems in various GIS issues by listening to presentations by GIS researchers.​
