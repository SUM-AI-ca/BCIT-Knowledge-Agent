## Naval Architecture - Hydrostatics 1 MEOC 1115
Marine Engineer Officer Cadet Course
## Course Overview
Students will learn the effect of hydrostatics related to flotation and ship stability, hull coefficients, first and second moments of area and volume, centre of pressure, and basic ship stability. They will also study the effect of a change in the ship’s displacement on its centre of gravity, load diagram and centre of pressure, Archimedes' principle and laws of flotation, coefficient of form, calculation of area and volume for ship structure, and calculation of transverse and longitudinal stability of ship.
### Prerequisite(s)
- Term 1.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Calculate the centre of gravity of various shapes and ship's centre of gravity.
- Calculate the effect of change in ship's centre of gravity due to change in ship's displacement.
- Calculate the pressure exerted by liquid.
- Find the centre of pressure and load on a bulkhead.
- Draw the load diagram.
- Explain the Archimedes' principle and laws of flotation.
- Explain density and relative density.
- Calculate the coefficient of forms.
- Find wetted surface area of similar figures.
- Calculate the area and volume of ship's structure.
- Calculate first and second moments of area of ship structure.
