## Anatomy and Physiology Nurs BHSC 1105
Basic Health Sciences Course
## Course Overview
A survey of the basic structure and function of human body systems. An introduction to the basic principles of genetics is also included. This is a guided learning course and is equivalent to BHSC 1140 in the BCIT General Nursing Program.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
