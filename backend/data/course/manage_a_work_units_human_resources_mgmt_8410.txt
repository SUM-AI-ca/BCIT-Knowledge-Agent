## Manage a Work Unit's Human Resources MGMT 8410
Management Course
## Course Overview
The learner demonstrates human resource skills, such as planning, acquiring and managing the performance of human resources.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
4.0
Retired: This course has been retired and is no longer offered. Find other Flexible Learning courses that may interest you.
## Learning Outcomes
- Manage within the applicable legal and organizational framework
- Demonstrate knowledge of relevant legislation, regulation, policies, procedures and collective agreements.
- Align the management of human resources in a manner consistent and in compliance with relevant legislation, regulations, policies, procedures and collective agreements.
- Gain an understanding of the complexity of laws relating to employee management.
- Manage the performance of employees
- Review and assess performance management tools.
- Identify performance measurement expectations and criteria.
- Communicate performance expectations and criteria.
- Conduct a performance management assessment.
- Develop a plan to improve performance
- Manage employee relations
- Identify opportunities to improve employee relations in union and non-union environments.
- Evaluate your organizations progressive discipline process.
- Manage diversity within the workplace.
- Plan for future human resources requirements
- Identify required staffing levels and the general knowledge, skill, abilities and behaviours that will be required of employees.
- Review existing staff to identify gaps between their current knowledge, abilities and skills and those that will be required in the future.
- Evaluate the supply and demand for current and future staffing requirements in your department.
- Identify training and development needs to meet current and future skills and abilities requirements to meet business objectives.
- Plan effective recruitment and selection strategies
- Analyze the criteria for selecting (hiring) staff based, on job-related knowledge.
- Participate in internal and external recruitment activities to identify and attract qualified applicants.
- Assess applicants in a fair, objective manner, using a range of selection techniques and pre-determined, job related criteria.
- Conduct a selection interview using behavioral questions.
