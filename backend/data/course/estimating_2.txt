## Overview

Continues from the studies introduced in BLDC 1300 and covers the construction details found in more sophisticated structures. Expands on the topics of BLDC 1300 and introduces sections 5, 6 and 7 covered by the CIQS Standard Method of Measurement in preparation for the CIQS Exam 202.

## Credits

3.0

## Cost

$564.42 - $607.36

## Prerequisites

- 50% in BLDC 1300

## Learning Outcomes

- Measure the structural and architectural work required for tendering on Stipulated Sum Contracts.
- Differentiate the Work into General Contractor's and the various Sub-trades.
- Prepare appropriate checklists, preliminary calculations, and take-off of the General Contractors work.
- Prepare appropriate checklists, preliminary calculations, and take-off of the work of the required sub-trades.
- Apply the principles and techniques of measuring work items on standard quantity sheet forms in a systematic approach.
- Confidently write the CIQS Method of Measurement Exam.
