## Survey Engineering 1 SURV 1150
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
Principles of differential leveling using automatic levels through practical projects. Bench mark and detail leveling procedures, industry booking formats and note reductions. Ideal for the municipal engineering and construction industry.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
3.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Perform an instrument peg test to determine instrument error.
- Perform both loop and connecting differential leveling circuits.
- Perform detail topographic leveling using intermediate foresights.
- Record and reduce level field notes.
- Perform adjustment of level circuits.
- Calculate grades from level readings.
- Measure invert elevations.
- Calculate drainage pipeline cut depths.
- Calculate earthwork volumes using grid method.
- Perform calculations and and carry out field work to layout a given elevation.
- Measure inverted rod readings as part of a level circuit to determine overhead heights.
