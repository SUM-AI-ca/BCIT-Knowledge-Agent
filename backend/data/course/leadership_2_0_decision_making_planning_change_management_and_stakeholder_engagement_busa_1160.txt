## Leadership 2.0 - Decision Making, Planning, Change Management, and Stakeholder Engagement BUSA 1160
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
This dynamic and engaging BCIT course will assist managers and emerging leaders with developing strategies and skills to enhance critical thinking, make better decisions, conduct productive meetings and facilitate organizational change. Students will enjoy this highly interactive class utilizing lecturettes, group discussions, practice sessions, and online assignments. www.bcit.ca/files/business/pdf/vbotleadership2.pdf

- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Improve Decision Making
- Assess personal and organizational barriers and traps to effective decision making
- Identify individual decision making styles and their potential impacts
- Understand when to utilize individual or group decision making
- Refine Planning and Productivity skills and strategies
- Identify and develop strategies to address procrastination and other common productivity barriers
- Construct and apply strategies for effective meetings
- Identify and develop planning tools and processes to effectively respond to organizational priorities and environmental changes
- Facilitate Change Management
- Identify individual change response styles and their impact on change leadership
- Evaluate change theories and describe processes for facilitating organizational change
- Analyze a current change initiative within an organization and identify opportunities to enhance the success change
- Understand Stakeholder Engagement
- Discuss an action plan for organizations to effectively and ethically deal with its stakeholders
- Debate the changing nature of the relations between the organization and its stakeholders
- Develop action plans to minimize the adverse consequences of crisis situations to business organizations and their stakeholders
- Apply issues management concepts to analyze the actual and potential challenges faced by business organizations
