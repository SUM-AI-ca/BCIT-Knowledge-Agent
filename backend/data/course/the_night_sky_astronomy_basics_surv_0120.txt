## The Night Sky - Astronomy Basics SURV 0120
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
Features of the night sky, major stars, constellations and galaxies. Classroom sessions in the BCIT planetarium and a field trip to SFU Observatory (students are responsible for transportation). Make and use a planisphere to locate stars. Celestial motion concepts, planet identification and star clusters will be shown and discussed. Hosted by one of Vancouver's best-known astronomers - Mr. Bill Burnyeat.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
