## Marine Pilot Preparatory Course MPPC 0100
Marine Pilot Preparatory Course
## Course Overview
The Pilot Examinations Preparation course will support the candidate to prepare for the examination process leading to a career as a marine pilot.
### Prerequisite(s)
- As established by the Pacific Pilotage Authority.
### Credits
0.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate an advanced knowledge and understanding of chartwork.
- Demonstrate a knowledge of meteorology.
- Understand the fundamentals of ship stability.
- Accurately state the rules of the International Convention for the Prevention Of Collisions at Sea with Canadian Modifications.
- Be conversant with pertinent Marine Acts and Regulations.
- Demonstrate an advanced knowledge and understanding of electronic navigation instruments.
- Demonstrate and understanding and interpretation of general seamanship knowledge.
