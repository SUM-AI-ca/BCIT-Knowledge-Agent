## Brake Systems ASTO 1124
Auto Service Tech & Operations Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the fundamental skills required for the service, repair, and diagnosis of foundation/base brake systems.
### Prerequisite(s)
- ASTO 1120
### Credits
3.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe hydraulic brake systems.
- Service brake tubing.
- Service hydraulic brake systems.
- Describe power assist systems.
