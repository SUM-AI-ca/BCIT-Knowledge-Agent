## Introduction to Photovoltaic (PV) Installation XZEB 0150
Net-Zero & Passive House Course
## Course Overview
​This course introduces students to photovoltaic (PV) system installation, including key electrical concepts, safety procedures, wiring methods, and code compliance. Through hands-on training at a live site, students will learn how to install, wire, and connect solar panels while following Canadian Electrical Code and local building bylaws.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Describe the basic principles of photovoltaic (PV) systems and solar energy generation.
- Understand the key components of a solar panel system, including inverters, batteries, and wiring methods.
- Follow Canadian Electrical Code and local bylaws for PV installation.
- Safely use ladders, scaffolding, and personal protective equipment (PPE) during installations.​
