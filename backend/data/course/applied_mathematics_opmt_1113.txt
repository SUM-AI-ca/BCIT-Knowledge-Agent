## Applied Mathematics OPMT 1113
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
Splits material covered into three sections. The first portion of the course includes positional numbering systems, binary arithmetic and an introduction to Boolean Algebra as it relates to operation of digital computers. The second phase reviews and applies the concepts of linear, quadratic and exponential functions, including logarithms. These will be applied to financial and industrial problems. The third phase will cover matrices and determinants.
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
