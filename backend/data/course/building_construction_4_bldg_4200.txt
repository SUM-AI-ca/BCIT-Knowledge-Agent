## Building Construction 4 BLDG 4200
Building Course
## Course Overview
This course is a continuation of BLDG 3200 - Building Construction 3. The course begins with an introduction to acoustic principals and construction responses and then examines various larger building construction systems including precast and tilt-up concrete, masonry as well as components and assemblies such as interior finishes, architectural woodwork, roofing, exterior cladding systems, and significant accessory components. The topics of prefabrication, sustainable building and building systems integration are also touched on. There will be an emphasis on applying learning from other subjects and the particular technical and procedural concerns in the development of technical drawings in the context of construction contract documents. Integrated learning opportunities for students include guest speakers, field trips and project work.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Demonstrate ability to produce technical drawings applying knowledge of graphics, materials, building codes, and software applications (Revit) introduced in other courses.
- Understand basic principals of acoustics and apply to construction assemblies to improve acoustic performance.
- Produce detailed technical drawings for interior components and building envelope conditions.
- Identify and compare differing viewpoints, approaches and solutions applied to construction details and industry situations.
- Develop particular technical and procedural methods in the production of detailed technical drawings in the context of contract documentation for construction..
- Develop and demonstrate personal initiative, problem-solving ability, and self-confidence in the completion of projects.
