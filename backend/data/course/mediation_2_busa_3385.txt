## Mediation 2 BUSA 3385
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
Provides an opportunity to act in a variety of workplace (role play) situations in the capacity of the mediator, party to a dispute and observer, providing effective and appropriate feedback. Instruction will be provided by mediators and business professionals including lawyers who act as mediators. This course will review a number of recognized dispute resolution organizations' standards, code of ethics and guidelines for practice. At the successful conclusion of this course, the student will be able to demonstrate a range of skills required in mediation as well as have an understanding of how to proceed and progress in the mediation field.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BUSA 3300
