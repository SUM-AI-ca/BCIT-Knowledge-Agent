## English for Professional and Academic Success - Reading and Writing 3 EPAS 0351
English for Professional and Academic Success Course
## Course Overview
​This course aims to improve English skills in reading, writing, grammar, and vocabulary, at the Intermediate level while also developing the collaborative skills needed to function effectively in BCIT programs and the workplace. In this course, you will study different types of writing to understand how they are organized and formatted. You will practice writing clear and accurate reports or essays while learning how to paraphrase information correctly. You will also learn how to navigate textbooks, websites, and professional documents to find main ideas and specific details. This course will help you improve your overall reading and writing skills for both school and work. It’s recommended that learners bring their own devices to every class (laptop/earphones). Weekly requirements: Courses includes two hours of online asynchronous class time. Prepare to spend 3 - 5 hours on homework studying, or assignments. ​

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Read and evaluate websites, containing short texts of multiple genres
- Demonstrate understanding of technical academic articles and secondary academic literature/textbooks, of up to 900 words, that include a variety of structures (i.e. factual information, cause-effect relationships, processes, arguments)
- Demonstrate understanding of the details of long complex instructions or procedures
- Write notes, short or long responses, or short descriptions or summaries in order to communicate facts or ideas
- Write a report or an essay of up to 350 words that provides background information and evaluates, develops and justifies a recommendation or argument
- Demonstrate understanding of more than one worldview, providing evidence of interaction with people from different cultures​
