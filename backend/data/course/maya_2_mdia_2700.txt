## Maya 2 MDIA 2700
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
This hands-on course builds upon the modelling and rendering experience gained in MDIA 2600 and provides an introduction to animation techniques such as key framing, motion paths, skeletons, and inverse kinematics. or permission from the instructor.
