## Quark X Press MAC 1 MDIA 1150
Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Introduces the most powerful page layout program currently available. Gives a complete overview of the abilities of QuarkXpress, providing a solid understanding of text importing, formatting and manipulation, graphics integration and pagination. Students work at their own workstation.
### Prerequisite(s)
- A solid working knowledge of the Macintosh.
