## Environmental Legislation BLDT 3081
Construction Operations/Supervision Course
## Course Overview
​Defines the contractor's and owner's roles in construction as they pertain to site environment accountability.​
### Prerequisite(s)
- No prerequisites are required for this course.
### Credits
1.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Interpret Environmental regulations and implement their meaning into construction operations.
- Implement proactive programs, to include the handling of construction waste.
- Demonstrate due diligence, inspections, and investigations.
- Co-ordinate clean-up of spills and contaminated sites.​
Effective as of Winter 2026
