## Coaching Skills for Managers BTCW 0127
Business Workshops Course
## Course Overview
With workplaces being more sophisticated and employees more knowledgeable and educated new skills are required to get the performance both the organization and the employee want.For years successful teams have discovered the value of coaching for maximum performance and now these concepts are proving successful in motivating, guiding and encouraging employees to reach their peak potential. This innovative workshop will give you the tools to turn problem employees into productive winners and encourage your productive employees to achieve bigger goals.
