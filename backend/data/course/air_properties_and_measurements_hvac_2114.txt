## Air Properties and Measurements HVAC 2114
Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (Technician) Course
## Course Overview
This course provides students with a complete understanding of the Psychometric Process along with Air measurement and balancing procedures. This knowledge is essential for the HVAC Technician in performing proper system installations and repairs.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HVAC 2113
### Credits
2.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Use psychometric charts
- Plot state point air properties
- Plot mixed air and HVAC processes:
- Heating process
- Heating/ humidification process
- Cooling process
- Cooling / heating process
- Cooling / dehumidifying process
- Describe Air Flow and Air Measuring Devices:
- Anemometers
- Velocity meters
Effective as of Fall 2011
