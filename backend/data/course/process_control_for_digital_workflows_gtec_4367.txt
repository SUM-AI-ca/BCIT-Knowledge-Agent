## Process Control for Digital Workflows GTEC 4367
Graphic Communications Technology Course
## Course Overview
Students will gain a basic understanding of several digital technologies and produce colour managed contract proofs for large format signage and variable data digital print work flows. Students will get hands on implementation of several Process controls to integrate all the digital equipment in the facility. Particular attention will be paid to digital print as a proofing technology for offset and flexographic print workflows.
### Prerequisite(s)
- GTEC 3367
### Credits
2.5

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, the student will be able to:
- Correct file formats for digital workflows.
- Use Raster Image Processing (RIP) software internal and external, PC or Mac.
- Setup up profiles adjusting and/or substituting manufacturer’s profiles.
- Create profiles.
- Convert color spaces RGB versus CMYK.
- Compare and describe inkjet RIP technologies.
- Compare and describe digital press RIP technologies.
- Implement Plotter calibration.
- Implement color proofing for digital print, offset print, and flexographic print technologies.
