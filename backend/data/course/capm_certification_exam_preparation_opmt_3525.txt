## CAPM Certification Exam Preparation OPMT 3525
Operations Management Course
## Course Overview
​This course concentrates on exam content from the Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK® Guide) and other resources. The program is designed for preparing you to write the Project Management Institute (PMI) Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM®) certification exam. You will prepare for the exam through a comprehensive review of the PMI project management fundamentals and core concepts, review of the predictive and agile methodologies, and review of business analysis frameworks as they relate to examination. The course includes an outline of the certification process and exam format, along with practice exam questions, a simulated exam, and practical test-taking techniques.​
### Prerequisite(s)

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply the project management key concepts, terminology, organizational influences, and project life cycle.
- Describe the Project Management Fundamentals.
- Describe the Predictive, Plan-Based Methodologies.
- Describe the Agile Frameworks/Methodologies.
- Describe the Business Analysis Frameworks.
- Apply test-taking techniques for writing the exam. ​
