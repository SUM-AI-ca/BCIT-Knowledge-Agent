## Visual Communication 3D INTD 1250
Interior Design Course
## Course Overview
The focus of this course is to create three-dimensional drawings to visually communicate the concepts and details of interior spaces. Further development of line weights and freehand drawing techniques for interior spaces will be explored. Students will produce one-point and two-point perspective drawings and will continue to employ the elements and principles of design to create three-dimensional compositions.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 65% in INTD 1100 and 65% in INTD 1150 and 65% in INTD 1310 and 50% in COMM 1120
### Credits
3.0

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Analyse and select the most appropriate perspective view.
- Demonstrate an awareness of the process used to create one and two-point perspectives using the traditional drop-down method.
- Accurately create one-point and two-point perspectives using the grid method.
- Produce three-dimensional manually-drafted and freehand drawings with accurate line weights to show depth and detail.
- Visually articulate architectural elements such as fireplaces, level changes, lighting, doors & windows in three-dimensional drawings.
- Incorporate standard architectural conventions to illustrate typical interior finishes & materials.
