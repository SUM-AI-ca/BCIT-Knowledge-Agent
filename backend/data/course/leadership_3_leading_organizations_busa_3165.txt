## Leadership 3 - Leading Organizations BUSA 3165
Business Administration Course
## Course Overview
Provides the skills required in organizations that are fostering a collaborative environment. Gives managers, supervisors, team leaders and individual contributors the skills they need to successfully interact with others and assist their organization in realizing their organizational goals while pursing continuous improvement and development.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in BUSA 3155
