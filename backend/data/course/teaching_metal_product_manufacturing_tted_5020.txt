## Teaching Metal Product Manufacturing TTED 5020
Technology Teacher Education Course
## Course Overview
This course covers the equipment, materials and skills necessary to teach metal work at the senior secondary level. Casting, forming, conditioning, advanced and non-traditional machining will be covered. Consideration will be given to the role of the machine shop in public school Technology Education programs.
### Prerequisite(s)
- TTED 4022
### Credits
8.0
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Fabricate metal items to a high standard using welding, machining, and casting operations.
- Develop solutions to open-ended projects.
- Describe the teacher's role in regard to the safe and efficient operation of a metal shop.
- Describe the importance of the metalwork discipline within Technology Education.
- Safely manage students in a high school metal shop.
Effective as of Fall 2015
