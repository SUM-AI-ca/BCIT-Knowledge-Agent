## Hydraulics HMTF 1007
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the principles of hydraulics and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on hydraulic systems and components.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
4.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Describe hydraulic systems
- Service hydraulic systems
