## Adjustment of Surveying Measurements SURV 4663
Surveying Course
## Course Overview
Covers definitions and classification of errors, measures of precision; propagation law of standard errors; weights and propagation of weights; principle of least squares (in matrix notation); adjustment by variation of parameters; conditional observations; combined adjustments; adjustments of triangulation, trilateration nets and traversing post adjustment global and local tests.
### Prerequisite(s)
- 50% in MATH 3511 and SURV 2261

4.0
