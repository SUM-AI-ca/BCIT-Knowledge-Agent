## Professional Feedback System POLY 1035
Polytechnic Teaching Course
## Course Overview
​In this workshop, learners will explore professional feedback systems and will review 360-degree reviews, discuss the benefits to self and others of the peer review process, complete a 360-degree assessment with their students and peers, and provide a written self-assessment on the outcomes. Examples of 360-degree reviews used at BCIT will also be discussed (e.g. FSA Performance Development System and GEU end-of-course survey model).​
### Prerequisite(s)
- S in POLY 1005 and 60% in POLY 1010 and 60% in POLY 1015 and 60% in POLY 1020 and S in POLY 1025 and 60% in POLY 1030

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- List 3 benefits of professional feedback systems.
- Create a plan for 360-degree review implementation.
- Assess instructional competencies within your Teaching Practice.
- Identify opportunities for professional growth within your Teaching Practice.
- Communicate effectively and work collaboratively with peers and learners.
- Demonstrate professionalism appropriate to the role of an educator.
