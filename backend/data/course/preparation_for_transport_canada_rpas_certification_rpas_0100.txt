## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Recall the main regulations pertaining to Canadian Air Regulations Part IX regarding safe RPAS operations.
- Explain the various systems and components of a RPAS.
- Describe basic aerodynamic principles governing safe flight.
- List the important human factors relating to safe RPAS operation.
- Define the main terms relating to CARs Part IX General Provisions
- Describe the different types of Canadian airspace.
- Memorize Canadian air law, air traffic rules and procedures.
- Interpret various meteorological factors to determine whether it is safe to proceed with an operation.
- Identify the main elements pertaining to safe flight operations.
- Describe the important functional elements of airframes, power plants, propulsion and systems.
- Select the correct radiotelephone phrase to use when communicating over an air-band radio.
