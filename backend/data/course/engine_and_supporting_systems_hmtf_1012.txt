## Engine and Supporting Systems HMTF 1012
Heavy Mechanical Trades Fdn Course
## Course Overview
This course introduces students to the basic principles of internal combustion engines and their supporting systems and the safe service, diagnostic, and repair procedures performed on engines and their supporting systems and components.
### Prerequisite(s)
- HMTF 1001
### Credits
6.5
Not offered this term: This course is not offered this term. Notify me to receive email notifications when the course opens for registration next term.
## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion, the student will be able to:
- Service engine support systems
- Service diesel fuel supply systems
- Service gasoline fuel systems
- Remove and install diesel engines
- Service, diagnose, and repair electronic ignition systems
