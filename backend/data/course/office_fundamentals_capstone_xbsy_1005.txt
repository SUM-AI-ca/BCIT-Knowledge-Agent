## Office Fundamentals Capstone XBSY 1005
Business Systems Course
## Course Overview
​The Office Fundamentals Capstone gives participants the opportunity to use the knowledge they have acquired thus far, in a realistic setting. Participants will put their understanding of Microsoft Office (Word, PowerPoint, Excel) and Microsoft Teams to work as they demonstrate their readiness to take up new projects and are prepared to use these skills in the business world.

- No prerequisites are required for this course.
