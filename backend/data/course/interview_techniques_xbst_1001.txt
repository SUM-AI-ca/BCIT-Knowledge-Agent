## Interview Techniques XBST 1001
Broadcast and Media Communications Course
## Course Overview
Discover how searches, sources, statistics, and interview subjects can be a treasure trove of valuable material. During his 35-year career, TV & Radio journalist and BCIT graduate, Mike Killeen interviewed hundreds of people. Join him to learn strategies for interviewing that apply to a broad range of areas. Whether it's in-person, by email, on the phone or with a microphone or camera, students will learn how to get the most from interview subjects, guests and experts and apply that to their field of interest. Guest speakers include Canada's top interviewers.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:
- Apply essential interviewing techniques
- Demonstrative effective interviewing techniques
- Interview with greater understanding and confidence
- Describe what to avoid in interview situations
